Several main findings held in this data: pyramidal cell rates are lower in nonREM than in waking with a slope below 1, and they fall across sleep, most in the fastest cells. The effects of intervening microarousals, waking and REM on rates from one nonREM packet to the next did not reproduce, most likely because the dataset has no microarousal labels and the derived ones were about half the paper's count. The UP/DOWN state and spindle findings could not be tested because their detection methods are only in the unretrieved supplement, and the movement result could not be tested because the dataset has no movement data. Only 22 of the 27 sessions are in the dataset, and two outcomes depend on a few sessions.
