The main finding held up only in part: sharp wave-ripple rate predicted a later fall in glucose in 18 of the paper's 30 rats, under a minority of analysis choices. It beat circular shuffles and was stronger for clustered ripples, but the phase reset of the glucose rhythm did not reproduce. The optogenetic and lateral septum findings could not be tested because the dataset lacks stimulation times and CNO or cohort labels. Among minor claims, the glucose oscillation and the ripple refractory period did not reproduce. The paper does not say how ripple counts were aligned to glucose readings, and the main result depends on that choice.
