Only the cortical findings of Figure 4 could be tested, because the dataset holds nothing else. Neuronal expression of eBRIC held up, and the steady baseline held in three of four recordings. Whether stimulation raises bioluminescence in all four electrode and current conditions could not be settled, since the paper gives no response window or test and the files do not label electrodes or currents.
