The behavioural findings and the share of visually selective cells held up, but the memory-selective and dual-selective cell counts did not. Recognition AUC, asymmetric ROC curves, higher accuracy at high confidence and the Figure 4 example neuron all reproduced. Memory-selective cells numbered 110 against the paper's 146, because the paper's code tests correct trials only, which the Methods do not describe. The Figure 3 example neuron could not be tested, as its session is absent from the dataset. Minor metadata claims partly failed, including a new/old label coding that is the reverse of the stated one.
