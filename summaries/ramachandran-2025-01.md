Most main findings did not hold in this data, one held only on an assumption, and the sham and cell-type sorting findings could not be tested. Time-locked RSU responses peaked at 3000 Hz, not 1500 Hz (107.7 to 108.7%). FSU time-locked responses differed across PRF, and awake FSU delayed onsets were not later than anesthetized ones. Anesthesia lowered delayed responses in both cell types only if one recording per rat and condition is assumed, which the paper does not state. The sham, the second batch of rats and the waveforms are missing, and the anesthetized data come from two rats, not ten.
