Only part of the main findings held up in this data. The drop in light-induced spiking during sharp-wave ripples, the larger gain inside place fields and the share of light-responsive cells reproduced, but the theta-trough gain and the place fields unmasked in non-place cells did not. The light-intensity, intracellular, decoding, assembly and replay results could not be tested, because data were missing or the Methods were unavailable. Light pulse times exist for only 2 of 28 sessions, so every light test rests on one mouse, and most pulses are missing from those files.
