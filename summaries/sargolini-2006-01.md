Two of the three findings tested held up in this data and one held only in part, but this record is deprecated because it was written from the abstract alone. Head-direction and conjunctive cells in the deeper layers but not in layer II held, as did the rise of firing rate with running speed. Layer II having mostly grid cells held only in part: 54% at a grid score of 0.3, but 31% against shuffled spike trains, level with layer III. The attempt used a stricter grid criterion than the paper's, and five other findings were never tested.
