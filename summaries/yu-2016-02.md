Some main findings held, some did not, and some could not be tested. Phase coupling to whisking in VPM and fast-spiking cells held, and so did the larger effect of touch than of whisking on excitatory cell membrane potential. VPM touch responses were weaker than reported (0.40 against 0.6 spikes per touch). Excitatory cells fired more during whisking, where the paper found no change. Suppressing PV cells raised excitatory firing as reported, though at higher rates. The nerve-cut experiments could not be tested because no file marks the cut mice. The depolarized-inhibition and thalamic multiunit recordings are not in the dataset. Thalamic drive of fast-spiking cells could not be tested because only 5 cells qualified.
