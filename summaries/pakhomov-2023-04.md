The paper's main claims could not be tested for the most part, and the three that could be tested did not hold in this data. The dandiset holds only the Figure 8 experiment, so the single-cell uptake results (Figure 4), rotation and packet-number effects (Figures 5 and 6), amplitude and array-size independence (Figure 7) and the electrode-gap effect (Figure 9) are untested, because their data are not in the dataset. For Figure 8, Packet 2 did not show the best central focusing: its center was 83% of the periphery, against 183% in the paper, while Packet 3 reached 129%. The three packets also did not give equal central uptake, and Packet 3 was not stronger than Packet 2 near the electrodes. The Packet 2 and 3 labels in the files may be swapped relative to the paper, and the data cannot settle this.
