Most of the paper's main findings held up in this data. VPM and fast-spiking cells rose with whisking (VPM 4.3 to 11.9 spikes/s), their spikes were phase-locked to whisking, and cutting the infraorbital nerve removed fast-spiking touch and whisking responses. Thalamic photoinhibition lowered fast-spiking rates in direction, but the percentages differed from the paper's. Layer 4 excitatory cells did not show the reported lack of a whisking effect (p about 0.02), and the eNpHR touch-detection findings did not reproduce, although one of them flips with how passive first touches are handled. The inhibition seen at depolarized potentials, the thalamic multiunit photoinhibition and the rabies tracing could not be tested because those recordings are not in the dataset. Several latency and amplitude values were off, and the files do not say which tracked whisker is the principal one.
