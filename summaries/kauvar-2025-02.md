Most of the paper's main findings held up in this data. Ketamine cut late but not early eye closure in humans and mice, lowered persistent activity along the coding dimension in humans and mice, and shortened the human intrinsic timescale. The brain-wide spectral factors and the network and region changes also reproduced. Human beta phase locking fell under ketamine, but by more than the paper reports. In mice, the late drop along the emotion-like dimension failed with the Methods window, though the code's different window gives it, and the claimed lack of change in fast activity failed. Verbal reports and licking could not be tested because the dataset lacks them.
