Of the paper's main findings on membrane charging by 2-us pulses, two held here, the pooled time constants did not, and the shape and alignment effects could not be tested without shape labels in the dataset. Polarization at the electrode-facing poles held (13 of 14 experiments), as did equal charging at both poles, though that test has little power. Pooled discharging times came out longer than reported, and the paper's ROIs and fit settings are not given. Sustained firing partly held and the Di-8-ANEPPS control did not, under a 5% level set for another dye. The sine wave excitation and threshold findings are in other dandisets and were not tested for lack of time.
