Few of the main findings held up cleanly in this data. Higher match rate and performance in WT pairs appeared under one reading of the repeated-measures ANOVA and not the other, which the paper does not specify. Lower WT choice entropy held only at 50% reward, while higher exploration efficiency, shorter arrival and departure lags and greater Fmr1 reliance on follower gaze did not reproduce. Equal leadership and rising follower gaze over a trial held. Mixed pairs, sensory deprivation and the cue and virtual-agent tasks are missing from the dataset.
