Of 47 claims, 9 held, 9 held in part, 23 did not and 6 could not be tested. The 3-6 Hz waking rhythm with its largest power in layer 4 and trough-locked units held, as did stronger waking firing in deep cells. The eight unit clusters did not hold, because the silhouette never favoured eight. The layer-specific firing, monosynaptic connection and DOWN-state results built on those clusters mostly failed, with about twice the paper's connection counts. The CSD landmark and flash-evoked claims could not be tested, because the paper places landmarks by eye and flash times exist for only 2 of 13 mice. Most laminar results rest on depth aligned to one landmark, which was an assumption.
