No central finding was cleanly confirmed in this data. The dorsal-to-ventral layout of the articulators could not be tested, because the dataset lacks electrode positions relative to the central sulcus and Sylvian fissure. The staggered timing of articulator activity and the early peak of consonant separability did not reproduce. The state-space clustering results and the count of about 30 active electrodes stayed undecided, since the paper does not fix the baseline, z-scoring or syllable set. Mixed single-electrode tuning held, but under a test that barely discriminates. It needs to be written there.
