The behavioral findings and most of the neural population findings held up, but the cluster counts and the reward analyses did not. Rats discriminated danger, uncertainty and safety, and the Threat Inhibited, Danger Excited and Bidirectional Valence firing patterns reproduced, though several effect sizes differed. The five-cluster counts and the Threat Excited and Non-Selective results failed, because the paper's population labels are not deposited. Reward and nose poke cessation findings failed, since recordings are paused between trials. Pre-surgery acquisition could not be tested, and the dataset has 90 sessions, not 95.
