The behavior, the unit and session counts and the stimulus and delay activity held up in this data, but the central finding that the Gain model fits best could not be tested. Fitting the paper's encoding models, which also underlie the gain links to accuracy and reaction time, needs far more compute than the run allowed. Position selectivity and decoding held while identity selectivity and decoding did not, and the Ring signal-noise trend held for one monkey only. Triangle signal and noise correlations were undecided because the Methods and the code define signal correlation differently. Decoder orthogonality held for synthetic and Ring data but not for Triangle neural data (106.7 degrees).
