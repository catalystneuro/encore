The paper's membrane recovery findings mostly hold in this data, which covers only its two-pulse experiment, so most other findings could not be tested. Test-pulse charging stays cosine-shaped and unchanged after mild conditioning pulses and falls after the strongest ones, as reported. The fall differs in size from the paper's at the cathodic pole, and at the anodic pole it is not significant after the weaker of the two strongest pulses. Full resealing after mild pulses and residual leak after strong ones hold, but the test pulse also finds a pole difference after the strongest pulse. These results depend on an automatic membrane trace, and the dye calibration, escalation, Gd3+ and calcium findings are absent from the dataset.
