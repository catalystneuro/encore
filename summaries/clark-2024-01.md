None of the paper's main findings could be tested in this data: the task-anchored and task-independent grid cell firing classes, the trial-by-trial switching between them, the agreement among simultaneously recorded cells, and the link between task-anchored firing and successful reward localisation. The dandiset holds only raw tetrode signal and position, with no sorted units, and every finding rests on units the authors sorted with MountainSort. Re-sorting was not attempted, because streaming the raw signal alone would take about 2.3 hours against a 60-minute notebook limit. Even with units, the unnamed open arena position columns and an arena extent that differs from the stated size in some sessions could block grid cell classification.
