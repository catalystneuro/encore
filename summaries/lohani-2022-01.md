Most of the paper's main findings hold in this data, which covers 4 of the 6 mice. Facial movement as the best predictor of ACh and calcium, the inverted anterior-posterior gradients, and the rise in between-area correlations with facial movement and fall with locomotion all reproduced. Cortical transients at movement onset and sustained-state calcium effects held only in part, with calcium amplitudes above the paper's ranges. The claim that ACh-calcium coupling falls with locomotion only in frontal cortex did not hold, since the decrease was broad. The ECoG, scopolamine, hemodynamic correction, sensor validation and axon findings could not be tested because that data is not in the dandiset.
