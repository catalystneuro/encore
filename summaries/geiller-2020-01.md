Findings on locomotion coupling mostly held in this one mouse, while those on spatial tuning mostly did not, and several could not be tested. Most interneurons correlated positively with velocity, CCK cells were enriched among the negatively correlated ones, and subtypes differed. Weaker modulation of IvC/NGFC, the fraction of place-selective cells, position decoding from them and the greater stability of PVBC did not reproduce, and the paper's place-field rule selected 1.0% of cells against 17.5%. Depth, ripple, novel-belt, reward and VIP findings could not be tested because the dataset lacks those recordings. The dataset holds one of 16 mice, and the imaging rate had to be refitted for two sessions.
