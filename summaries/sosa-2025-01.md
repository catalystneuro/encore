Most main findings held in this data: reward-relative place cells remap with the reward zone, grow near reward with experience, and keep their firing order across the switch. Recruitment of other cells into this population across days did not reproduce, most likely because the files hold no cross-day cell matching and the attempt built its own. The decoding, GLM and remap-timing findings were not tested for lack of run time. The over-represented region extended further than reported, and the minor place-field results failed. A later dataset version was used because the cited one has mismatched behaviour.
