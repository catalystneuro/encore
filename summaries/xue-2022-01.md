Most main findings held in this data, but the acceptance count and the OUT/IN ratios did not. The recordings gave large F-scores, best-R values near Table 1 for most spines, and the negative link between spike contrast and F-score. Acceptance of connections gave 7 spines instead of 9, probably because the background regions used for the noise estimate are not in the dataset. Accepted spines still had higher best-R than rejected ones, though best-R alone did not separate them. OUT/IN matched Table 1 in only 6 of 19 recordings, probably because the paper does not say how it was computed, and its correlation with F-score was weaker than reported.
