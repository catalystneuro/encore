Most main findings held up in this data. Light evoked widespread dopamine increases, the tone drove increases in the posterior tail and decreases anteriorly, both latency gradients appeared, and release from one stimulated fiber fell with distance. Two parts did not hold: light increases were not less prevalent in the tail, and tone decreases were more ventral but not more lateral. Chronic stability could be tested only to week 8, because the task variables for later sessions are missing. The freely moving and jaw movement results were not tested, because their data are only on Zenodo. Latency means matched the paper only when counted as its code counts them.
