None of the main findings reproduced in full, and several could not be tested. Trajectory selectivity, odor-dependent firing and the fall of connection probability with distance appear in this data, but with higher fractions of selective units than the paper's. The cell classes from connections, facilitation and depression, and supralinear summation did not match. Position-dependent coupling could not be tested because its methods are in the supplement, which was unavailable. Task performance, where trajectories diverge and the example session could not be tested because error trials, the y coordinate and that session are missing from the dandiset.
