Some main findings held, some did not, and the central rigid versus plastic cell results could not be tested. Sequence scores during running exceeded those in pre-sleep, and fast-firing cells dominated pre-sleep coactivation. Post-sleep scores did not exceed pre-sleep in the pooled test, although they did in all 8 sessions taken separately. The slow-firing cells' coactivation and replay results did not appear. The rigid and plastic analyses depend on the paper's per-cell significance test, which sits in supplementary methods that were unavailable. Place cells numbered 417 against 491.
