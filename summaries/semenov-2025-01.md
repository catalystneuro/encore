Most of this paper's main findings could not be fully tested in this data. The dandiset itself holds none of the paper's data, so the checks ran on the nine dandisets the paper lists, using an assumed soma region. Reproduced: pure sine waves fire more action potentials than modulated ones, and firing locks to the beats of modulated waves. Partly reproduced: AP peak height held but duration did not, and modulated thresholds came out higher with only one of three tests significant. Not reproduced: the excitation block at the highest fields. Blocked: the 20 kHz versus 2 kHz threshold ratio, which needs voltage calibration that is not deposited, and the low-conductivity thresholds, whose sessions are missing. The Figure 4 to 6 membrane-charging findings were also blocked, because the strobe files hold no ROIs or cell identities.
