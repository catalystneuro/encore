Most of what the paper describes held up in this data. That includes the counts of mice, sessions, video frames, neural sessions and neural hours, the task rules, and the video and neural timebases. The stated 18,514 trials did not hold, as the files contain 32,350. Choice time as the first lick, the ignore-trial definition and the inclusion criterion on labeled contacts also did not hold. Tracking accuracy, neural-to-video synchronization and behavioral clock drift could not be tested because the curated labels, sync channels and logs are not deposited. The 9 s timeout and the 90-trial minimum depend on readings the paper does not specify.
