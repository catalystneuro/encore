Few of the paper's main findings could be confirmed in this data, which holds one monkey's 100 trials over 27 of the 108 maze conditions. The weak positive correlation between preparatory and peri-movement tuning did not reproduce (mean r -0.079 against 0.13). The claim that a preferred direction in the peri-movement space best captures preparatory tuning only partly held, with no significant gaps between spaces. The linear-dynamics simulation reproduced. The findings for monkeys A and B, single-electrode recordings and EMG could not be tested because those data are missing.
