Only the finding that granule and semilunar cells lose their electrical differences in the V321L mutant held in these recordings. The wild-type differences in resting potential, spike width and downstroke went the paper's way but were not significant (p = 0.08 to 0.35), and the separation of wild-type cell types by PCA and a trained classifier also failed. The morphology, soma position, Golgi and RNAScope findings could not be tested because those data are not in the dandiset. The sequencing findings on Penk+ cells and pseudotime could not be tested either, because the kept nuclei and cluster labels are not deposited and the pipeline needs R. The embryonic expression of the semilunar-cell genes held, judged by eye on Allen atlas images.
