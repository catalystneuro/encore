Most of the paper's central findings held up in this data, but the optogenetic screen could not be tested. Decoding of tongue length, velocity, angle, sequence identity and sequence time across S1TJ, M1TJ, ALM and S1BF reproduced in order and size, as did canonical correlation, zigzag context decoding and target position coding in ALM. Backtracking decoding held only in part, since S1TJ and S1BF also decoded it. The inhibition effects of Figure 2 could not be tested because the files do not name the inhibited region. Some minor behavioural counts did not reproduce, and the earplug, air flow and numbing controls lack condition labels.
