The core electrophysiology held up. L5 ET neurons had higher resonant frequency, lower input resistance and faster spikes than other cells, and motor cortex differed from temporal cortex in resonance, afterhyperpolarization and input resistance. The transcriptomic findings could not be tested because the sequencing data are not in the dataset and were not processed in the run. Corticospinal neurons grouping with L5 ET cells in the physiology map did not reproduce, nor did the ranking of resonance and input resistance as the largest effects. Classifier and subthreshold oscillation findings held only in part, the latter on a pipeline rebuilt without the authors' code. Spike-width ratio and rheobase claims failed.
