Five of the paper's central findings held up in this data, including the voltage-sensitivity and half-activation-voltage (V0.5) differences between pigmented and albino cells and the fit of V0.5 against charge density (Fig. 3a). Several of the model's quantitative claims, such as the sensor-area range and the two capacitance limits, did not reproduce, and the sequencing and cross-species claims could not be tested. Most of these rest on a per-cell lateral-wall capacitance that the dataset does not store, so the agent rebuilt it from a cited method. Evidence for two hyperpolarized states is mixed: the slope near -1/2 holds, but the mean slope and the capacitance limits fall just outside tolerance. The XE991 voltage-clamp improvement, the V0.5 length gradient and the equality of the two colonies' V0.5-capacitance slopes also failed. The sequencing, protein-model and other-species claims need data outside the dataset.
