Most of the paper's brain-wide findings held up in this data, but the region-level ones could not be tested. Correlations with forward and turning velocity, the mirror symmetry of the forward map, model weights stereotyped across flies, and ipsilateral voxels leading contralateral ones all held. The share of correlated voxels (31% against 39%) and the prediction of behaviour from principal components came out lower, and exact peak times depend on per-plane acquisition times the files lack. The contralateral decrease during turning did not reproduce, since the opposite side rose too. All neuropil-level results were untestable because the dandiset holds no neuropil labels for its atlas space.
