Most of the paper's main findings held up, tested on its own deposits (dandisets 000021 and 000022) because 000253 holds a different experiment. Response latency, receptive field size, modulation index and response decay timescale each tracked anatomical hierarchy, and cross-correlogram directionality did too. Two findings did not reproduce: the absence of the hierarchy in spontaneous activity (borderline) and the sharp-peak count (21,962 against 16,119). The likely reason is that the paper does not state which units, window or jitter function it used. Behavioural performance could not be tested because those sessions are not deposited, and several pairwise area tests failed.
