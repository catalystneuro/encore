The sparse, non-columnar shape clusters and the widespread contrast-invariant tuning held up in this data. The exact counts of clustered neurons and the texture findings did not. Clustered neurons were a minority and levelled off in well-sampled penetrations (21.5% here), but no analysis choice matched the paper's counts, probably because its code is unpublished and its reliability rule is ambiguous. Texture clustering did not reproduce, with a higher clustered fraction than the paper reports (26% to 52%), and the records give no clear cause. The current-source, orientation, curvature and gyral-penetration findings could not be tested, because the files hold only spike times, with no shape images or grid coordinates.
