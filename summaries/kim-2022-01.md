Of the paper's main findings, one held in part, three did not, and most could not be tested. YO-PRO-1 uptake rose with field for every second-phase amplitude, but the strongest field differed significantly from the weakest for only three of five amplitudes. The exponential rise with an unchanged slope, the cancellation to about 20% of unipolar uptake (54.5% here), and its independence of field were not reproduced. The dandiset holds only the 5-pulse experiment of Fig. 2, so the findings on pulse number, duration, repetition rate, membrane charging and heating could not be tested. The unipolar group has two dishes because one dish's image is a copy of another's.
