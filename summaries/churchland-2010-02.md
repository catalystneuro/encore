Most main findings held in this data, but the preparatory and peri-movement tuning correlation only partly did, and its time course did not. Preferred directions captured preparatory tuning best in the peri-movement space, and the oscillator simulation behaved as reported. The tuning correlation was positive but larger than reported (mean r 0.195 against 0.13), and it was still 0.218 at movement onset, not near zero. The single-electrode datasets and the EMG are not in the dandiset, so two main findings could not be tested. Several results depend on analysis details the paper does not state, such as whether the PCA space is recomputed for each left-out condition.
