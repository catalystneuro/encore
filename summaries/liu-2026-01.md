Some main findings held in this data, two did not, and the central von Economo neuron (VEN) claims could not be tested. VENs mapped to the L5 ET and L5/6 CT clusters, and L2/3 IT neurons had the shortest membrane time constant. The lower firing rate of VENs did not reproduce, and neither did the correlation between tau and transcriptomic distance (r 0.053 against 0.53). The saddle-shaped phase plot could not be tested, because the hand-placed peaks are not described well enough to automate. The VEN versus L5 pyramidal action potential comparison and the VEN subtype comparisons need recordings that are not in this dataset.
