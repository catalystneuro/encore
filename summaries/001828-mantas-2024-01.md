Of the paper's main findings, the slower locomotion of Anxa1-silenced mice held in this data, and so did the negative correlation of cCP dopamine with speed. The ramp of Anxa1 and DAT axon signals to the water-drop touch did not reproduce, and neither did the positive correlation of Anxa1 axon activity with speed (p = 0.064 to 0.21). The loss of Anxa1 neurons in early Parkinson models, the anatomy and the motif analyses could not be tested, because the dandiset holds only behaviour and photometry and not the paper's 37-motif labelling. Procedural learning was mixed: controls improved at reaching and silenced mice did not, but Arch mice never differed significantly, and maze speed did not reproduce. Evoked dopamine release in dCP and cCP held.
