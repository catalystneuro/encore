Only one of this paper's main findings could be tested, and it held: the median electric field in the 20-minute 40 Hz experiments came out near the reported 1.65 mV/mm (1.83 and 1.53 under two readings), assuming the channels sit row by row in the 4 x 4 array. Everything else could not be tested. The 1-minute files hold only the stimulation command, with no recordings, so the firing-rate, entrainment and LFP findings and the sham control are untestable. The finite element model is not public. The 20-minute spike findings need re-sorting that runs about 85 minutes against a 60-minute limit.
