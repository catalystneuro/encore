Of the findings that could be tested in this one array session, most hold up, but the paper's central claim of only weak agreement between preparatory and movement-period tuning did not reproduce. In the dataset the mean tuning correlation was 0.051 (Pearson) and 0.072 (Spearman), against 0.13 and 0.11 in the paper, with no test significant. Only 17 neurons passed the selection, and the paper's unit and trial selection is not recorded. The preparatory space capturing preparatory tuning best, the time course of the correlation, the controls and the model simulations all reproduced, but dimensionality results did not (best performance at 14 dimensions with no decline, and 15 to 18 components for preparatory activity against 7 to 10). Speed-task, single-electrode and EMG findings could not be tested because that data is not in the dandiset, and the anterior-posterior findings lacked unit locations.
