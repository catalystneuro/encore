The central finding held: beta sharpness, steepness and phase-amplitude coupling are higher off than on medication at sensorimotor electrodes, though each had one statistic outside tolerance. The bipolar check, the temporalis nulls and the dominance of sharp-peaked, steeply decaying waveforms also held. Higher steepness in patients off medication than in controls did not reproduce (corrected p 0.053), nor did the current source density result, the spectral peaks or the paper's quadrant patient counts. The likely reason is that the paper's manual artifact marks and excluded electrodes are missing from the dataset, so no artifact rejection was applied.
