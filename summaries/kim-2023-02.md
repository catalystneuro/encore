The no-difference findings held in this data, the 770 kHz enhancement did not, and the Figure 2, 4 and 5 findings could not be tested. Co- and cross-directional pulses gave similar dye uptake at 1 Hz, cross-directional pulses at 770 kHz matched 1 Hz, and uptake did not depend on membrane area. Co-directional pulses gained 1.96-fold at 770 kHz on average but not significantly (p = 0.16), and did not significantly beat cross-directional ones. With few dishes the tests had little power, so they show only that no difference was found. The field-angle and voltage-imaging findings sit in other dandisets outside this attempt's scope.
