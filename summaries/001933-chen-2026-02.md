Most main findings held, but the Calb1 comparison and the phosphoproteomics did not. Evoked and spontaneous dopamine release were lower in Anxa1-region G2019S mice, RAB3A and RAB3C phosphorylation was higher, and RIM1 was lower in synaptosomes. The smaller Calb1 effect did not reproduce, most likely because the deposited Calb1 signal is not the one the paper analysed. The phosphoproteomics gave 1694 altered phosphopeptides against 289, with RAB3 T86 higher after MLi-2, so sample labels or the figure's direction may be swapped. Fewer bassoon-positive synaptosomes held in the paper's values but not in an image re-analysis that may be flawed.
