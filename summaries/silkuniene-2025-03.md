In this data, the one central Fig. 6 finding that could be tested did not hold, and most of the rest could not be tested. The paper says normalized YO-PRO-1 profiles decline similarly across 300, 600 and 900 ns pulses. In this data the profiles separate, with the 900 ns profile falling faster near the centre. Two findings held up: the 900 ns centre peak is highest but not significantly so, and the 900 ns spot is about 1.7 mm wide. The sharper peak with longer pulses was blocked, because the dandiset has 7 wells at 600 ns where the paper used 5 and does not say which five. The packet-rate, raised-electrode, burst-number and heating findings could not be tested because that data is not in the dandiset.
