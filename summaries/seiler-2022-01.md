The behavioral findings mostly held in this data, but the photometry findings did not and several could not be tested. The three punishment phenotypes and their differences in training and omission behavior held, as did the shift toward resistance with DMS stimulation and toward sensitivity with DMS inhibition. The DMS dopamine score did not separate phenotypes significantly, and the DLS score correlated with shocks where the paper found none, but only after a dropout step added in this analysis. The RI60 versus RR20 shock difference was not significant, with 5 RR20 mice against 9 in the paper. RI30 photometry, fear conditioning, open field and histology are not in the dataset.
