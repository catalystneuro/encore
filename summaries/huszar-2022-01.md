The paper's central findings, that same-birthdate CA1 neurons fire, cofire and share place maps more than other neurons, could not be tested, because no unit is labelled light-responsive in the dataset. Among the tests on all putative pyramidal cells, population vectors were most correlated on the common stem and firing rates carried over to novel mazes (rho 0.73). Left-right decorrelation after the stem was weaker than reported, and ratemap correlations were not higher in novel mazes. The decorrelation result depends on the firing-rate rule used to pick pyramidal cells, since the files hold no cell types. Mouse counts and ripple duration matched, but 6 of 17 mice have no units.
