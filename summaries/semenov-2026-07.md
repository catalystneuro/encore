Few of the paper's findings could be tested, and none held up cleanly. The dandiset holds only the Di-8-ANEPPS alternating-field and short-pulse experiments, so the FluoVolt, Gd3+, calcium and two-pulse recovery findings could not be tested. Stable responses before electroporation did not reproduce, and the lasting drop afterwards was larger than reported, both depending on an undescribed background subtraction and on which cells the paper used. The fall in polarization within a set at the electroporating field held only under one background choice made after seeing results. The maximum polarization with short pulses reached 34.9% against the reported 53%.
