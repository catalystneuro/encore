None of the paper's main findings could be tested in this data set, which holds only current-step recordings of 17 pyramidal cells and 6 basket cells. The larger basket cell inhibition of deep pyramidal cells, the running-evoked axon activity, the stronger superficial-to-basket excitation and the projection-specific results need paired recordings, imaging and anatomy that are not deposited. The network model result was too costly to run, at about 450 core-hours. Among the minor intrinsic-property claims, the lack of firing-curve and input-resistance differences and the larger superficial sag held, and rheobase matched only in part. Resting potential could not be tested because the holding current was not recorded.
