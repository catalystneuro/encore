Some of the paper's main findings held in this data, others did not, and two could not be tested. Unmodulated waves evoked more spikes than 20-Hz modulated waves, firing mostly locked to the beats, and tetrodotoxin abolished spikes. Excitation of every neuron with delayed spikes, the doubled 20-kHz threshold, higher thresholds for modulated waves and the membrane charging times did not reproduce; the amplitude calibration and the debris ROI are not deposited. The low-conductivity finding has no deposited data and the sine-versus-pulse finding was not attempted. The control holds 3 neurons, against 8 in the paper.
