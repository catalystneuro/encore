Most central findings for the J-array data held up, but several could not be tested and two failed. Preferred directions were captured best in the peri-movement space, and the weak link between preparatory and peri-movement tuning was only partly reproduced (mean r 0.172 against 0.13). The preparatory dimensionality count and the anterior versus posterior comparison did not reproduce, the first likely because of very few trials per condition.
