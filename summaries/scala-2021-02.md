Most main findings held up in this data, but those on variability within cell types were weaker and some could not be tested. Electrophysiology separated the transcriptomic families, with only ET and IT overlapping (49% of ET cells classed as IT), and gene expression predicted the features as reported. The Leiden entropy agreement with t-type variability did not reproduce, and the Vip gradients stayed undecided because the paper gives no measure.
