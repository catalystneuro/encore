Most of the paper's findings hold in this data. The crystal replicates have consistent remanence directions that turn random after zero-field heating to 590 °C, which gives the 0.2 to 0.5 µT baseline of fields that cannot be excluded. The glass findings (linear components in two specimens, changing-field directions in two others) held only if repeated measurements are averaged, a step the paper does not describe. The Table 2 fit statistics matched 23 of 29 values, just short of a full match, and non-random glass directions appeared in only 8 of 22 specimens. The impact-plasma and no-dynamo interpretation could not be tested.
