Most main findings hold in this data: stimulation raised fractional anisotropy in the stimulated cingulum bundle, lowered connectivity of the subcallosal seed with default mode, limbic and central executive hubs, and raised the share of myelinating oligodendrocytes in the mid-cingulate. The whole-brain connectivity matrices and the myelin g-ratio could not be tested, because the dataset has no fMRI time series and no hand axon measurements. The "no change" finding in the anterior cingulum did not reproduce, but it rests on the agent's own cell counting, since the paper does not describe its method. The white matter and connectivity results reuse the paper's own voxel selections, so they show the dataset holds the data, not that the regions would be found again. Among minor claims, the no-stimulation animal's foraging fell, unlike the reported null.
