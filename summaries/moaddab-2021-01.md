Most of the paper's main findings held up in this data: the eight cue-firing clusters at the stated sizes, their danger, uncertainty and safety patterns, and stronger signaling of exaggerated threat probability than of fear output. This rests on the paper's own code, whose normalization differs from its Methods text. The k1 shock response did not reproduce in the 0 to 8 s window drawn in Figure 6, though it did in 0 to 4 s, which fits the supplementary table. Some outcome-period results and the per-cluster regression table held only in part, the latter because the paper leaves out two unnamed units.
