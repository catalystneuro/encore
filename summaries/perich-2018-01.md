The dataset holds only the Baseline epoch, so the paper's central claims about curl field and rotation adaptation, including output-null activity failing to generalize in the curl field only, could not be tested. Two Baseline findings were partly supported. PMd was higher-dimensional than M1 in every session, but the medians were below the paper's, and this rests on an assumed noise scale. Output-null activity led output-potent, significantly only under one reading of the kernel width, with a smaller lag than the paper's. Minor checks on dimensionality across sample sizes and the null/potent index held, but pooled counts of units above 5 Hz fell short. I did not create `summaries/perich-2018-01.md`.
