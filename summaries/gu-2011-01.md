The main finding holds: MSTd noise correlations are lower in trained than in naive monkeys (0.024 against 0.119), and training lowers the relation between noise and signal correlation without changing its slope, though the intercept intervals overlap. The population simulations also hold, with correlated noise limiting sensitivity and the trained reduction changing it little, but narrowing preferences gives a smaller gain than reported, under tuning parameters the paper does not state. Smaller choice probabilities in fore-aft neurons did not reproduce, on 129 units from two trained monkeys that may not be the paper's sample. Time courses, Fano factors and naive against trained single-neuron tuning could not be tested, as the data set holds no spike times or naive single units.
