Only the voltage series of the paper's main findings could be tested here, and none of it reproduced. The 15- to 17-fold rise in dye uptake from 2.4 to 5.6 kV was not seen (ratios 5.7 to 7.3), possibly because the paper does not say whether background was subtracted. Focusing that does not depend on amplitude, in small and large arrays, also came out not reproduced, but this rests on an assumed ROI layout and, for the large array, two dishes against three. Single-cell bipolar cancellation could not be tested because those images are not on DANDI. The rotation, packet number, packet shape and electrode height findings appear to be in four sibling dandisets.
