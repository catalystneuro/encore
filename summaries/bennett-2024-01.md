Most Figure 4 findings held: units per session, drift, QC pass fraction, and stable unit yield, receptive fields and visual responses across days. The QC metric distributions were not comparable by the paper's test. Of the alpha-event findings, VISp events locking to stimulus offset held, but the claim of no hippocampal engagement and the replay correlation (r at most 0.63) did not. The cortical and striatal coupling networks could not be settled, since the paper does not fully define coherence and events and the readings disagree.
