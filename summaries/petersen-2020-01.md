Most of the paper's main findings on cooling the medial septum held in this data, but its claim that theta phase lags between place cells are preserved did not. Theta frequency fell with septal temperature (R = 0.79), place cell oscillation frequency fell with it, and time lags between place cell pairs lengthened. Choice errors rose about 2.3-fold, against the reported 3-fold. Phase lags were slightly larger during cooling, which may partly come from how each pair's lag is picked, and the size of the time compression could not be judged with this method.
