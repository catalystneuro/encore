The paper's main seizure-suppression findings held: after tFUS, seizure count and percent time in seizure fell and inter-seizure interval rose, matching the reported percentages. Differences from sham held only in part, and the sham group was not steady, with seizure duration falling from about 50 s to 35 s. The EEG band-power and asymmetry-index findings were partly supported, and the epileptic phase-lag decrease was not specific to S1. The wild-type evoked-potential and gamma phase-lag findings could not be tested, since resting sham EEG is missing and the evoked-potential analysis is underspecified. Several verdicts assume a repeated-measures ANOVA, which the paper does not specify.
