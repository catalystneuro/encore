Most of the paper's main findings could not be tested in this data. The dandiset holds no output from the six spike sorters, and Kilosort2, IronClust and HDSort need MATLAB, which the runner lacked, so the comparisons of sorters, the simulation results and the matches to the curations did not run. The recordings are present as described. The two experts' curations of the Kilosort2 output agree on 226 of 351 units (64.39%), as the paper reports. The 174 Kilosort2 units both experts discarded could not be checked, because the curated files do not link units to Kilosort2 clusters.
