The behavioural effects, the weaker hippocampal theta-gamma coupling under higher memory load, and persistent firing of category neurons largely held up, though some of these rest on choices the paper does not state. The reported absences of a load effect outside the hippocampus did not hold, because the pre-SMA differed, probably due to channels removed by the paper's undocumented visual artefact rejection. The findings on coupling neurons (that they are distinct from category neurons and enhance decoding) could not be tested. Only 8 hippocampal neurons qualified against the paper's 79, for a reason the records do not explain. The spike-field coherence analyses were not run.
