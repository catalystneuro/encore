Some of the paper's main findings hold in this data, but its three cell classes do not. The heading-selective fraction under controlled rotation (15.3%, paper 14.2%) and unimodal ADN versus bimodal visual axon tuning in light both reproduced, though the selection test is liberal and the shuffled control was not met. Coherent remapping and shared head and chamber rotation preferences held only partly, because matched cells did no better than random pairs. The three functional classes did not reproduce, and decoding from all tuned cells gave 0.71 against 0.82, with the decoder code missing from the linked repository. The free-chamber heading fraction could not be tested because those recordings are not in the dataset.
