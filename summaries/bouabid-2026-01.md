Most of the paper's main findings held up in this data. Dominant dopamine (DA) and acetylcholine (ACh) correlations are mostly negative with DA leading. They are strongest in an anterior dorsolateral hotspot, across spontaneous behavior, reward, cues, extinction, locomotion and dopamine stimulation. The slice drug results also matched the paper's source data. Two central findings did not reproduce. Control mice with non-functional sensors showed a hotspot and correlations as strong as the sensor mice's, possibly because their files lack artifact removal. The locomotion-initiation topography failed under site-level tests, where the paper shows maps. Equal cocaine effects on dopamine across regions could not be tested because the stated ANOVA does not fit the data. Some counts differed, such as 147 against 116 sites with ACh suppression.
