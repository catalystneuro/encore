In this data, the finding that dorsal raphe dopamine input evokes both excitatory and inhibitory responses in BNST, CeA and BLP cells held. The BLP split into mostly inhibited putative pyramidal cells and mostly excited putative interneurons held in direction only, with percentages outside tolerance. The CeA late-firing cluster being excited did not hold, because two or three of its cells were inhibited. Regional differences in response size held only under some of the readings of the paper's undescribed EPSP and IPSP detection rule. The in vivo imaging, behavioural, tracing and receptor findings could not be tested, because the dataset lacks those data and the behaviour annotations.
