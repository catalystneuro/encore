Most main findings hold up in this data: stimulation raised fractional anisotropy in the mid-cingulate bundle, increased myelinating oligodendrocytes with thicker myelin, lowered cortical and raised subcortical connectivity, and reduced subgenual cingulate connectivity with default mode, limbic and executive networks. In the implanted but unstimulated monkey, the opposite fractional anisotropy decrease also reproduced. The nonsurgical controls' lack of change, the local increase at the implant site and the network-sorted connectivity plot could not be tested, because whole-brain maps, control seed data and ROI names are missing. The connectivity results assume the deposited maps are those the paper analysed. One minor claim failed: that monkey's foraging fell, and the paper reports no test for it.
