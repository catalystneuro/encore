Most behavioural findings held up in this data, while the neural findings mostly did not or could not be tested. Context-dependent feature use, switching within about four trials, converging behavioural kernels and the behaviour-neural slope anticorrelation (r = -0.70, resting on two rats) all reproduced. Parallel choice axes did not, with the paper's own code giving 4.7 degrees, not 1.6. Frontal silencing impaired 2 of 3 rats; the network and choice-axis variance claims could not be tested, for lack of a defined method or runnable code. The electrophysiology is absent from the dataset, and its units linked to only about 112 of 199 sessions.
