Of the paper's main findings, the ones testable in this one-session dataset mostly held up, and the ones that need its two-workspace sessions could not be tested. Whole-arm kinematics fit area 2 neurons better than hand-only kinematics, with many neurons showing no significant difference, and active and passive movements could be told apart from arm kinematics (90%) and from single neurons (20 of 50 above chance). The finding that many neurons are inconsistent across active and passive movement only partly held: about as many neurons are negative when trained within one condition. Model consistency correlated negatively with separability on active trials (-0.38) but not on passive or all trials. The whole-arm advantage across workspaces, tuning curves and preferred-direction shifts could not be tested because the dandiset lacks those sessions.
