The paper's basic findings held in this data: GPi rests faster and has shorter spikes than VLa, and nearly all units change rate around movement. The timing and correlation findings mostly did not hold. VLa did not clearly lead GPi, and GPi–VLa spike correlations exceeded jittered controls, so they were not rare. VLa rate did not fall after GPi bursts, but the burst detector differed from the paper's, so that result says little. Several outcomes shift with the paper's own unit list, which the dandiset does not identify, and the VLa localization, EMG onset and model claims could not be tested.
