The paper's main findings that could be tested held up in this data: thousands of neurons recorded at once in a freely moving mouse (2948 footprints, before curation), and position decoded from activity above chance (octant median accuracy 0.313 against 0.125). Neurons with activity localized to parts of the maze were also found. Flat illumination of both microscopes only partly held. The other mice, the songbirds, the weights and the optical resolution could not be tested because the dandiset lacks that data, and the curated count could not be tested because nothing in the dandiset marks the curated footprints. This record is deprecated, as its claims were written from the abstract and figure legends only.
