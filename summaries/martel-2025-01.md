The data support most of the paper's main findings, with two exceptions and several parts that could not be tested. The paper reports no change in event rate or IEI CV between rest and task, higher M1 than SMA rates in monkey U, higher resting amplitude in monkey U's SMA, and synchronized cell pairs in SMA and M1. All of these held. Higher task amplitude in M1 than SMA did not (p = 0.099), probably because the deposited events differ from the paper's own per-cell table (p = 0.014 on that table). The Figure 2 and S1 cell-response findings could not be tested, because the trial timing and raw traces are not deposited. The sequence counts could not be tested, because the detection parameters are not given.
