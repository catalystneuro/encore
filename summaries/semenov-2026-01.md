Almost none of the paper's findings could be tested here, and none was shown to fail. The dandiset holds FluoVolt time courses of single 1-us pulses, not the paper's Di-8-ANEPPS and FluoVolt escalation sets, so the polarization pattern, the loss of pole charging at electroporating fields, and the Gd3+, calcium and two-pulse resealing results could not be tested. The one claim needing no recordings, the effect of membrane conductivity on charging, partly held: the 100-fold value matches, but a 10-fold rise gives 1.3% where the paper says about 2%.
