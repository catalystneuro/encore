Most main findings held up: MM and ALM carry the rule before the stimulus more strongly than S1 and S2, this state predicts the touch response and follows rule switches, and inhibiting MM or ALM before the stimulus lowers tactile sensitivity. The ALM inhibition result sits at the edge of significance and shifts with the bootstrap draw. Trajectory distance and subspace overlap held in direction but not in strength, and the paper does not state the normalization units. Block switches did not reproduce against the reported 88 and 91, probably because the paper counts every block including each session's first. Probe locations could not be tested because the dataset lacks electrode coordinates and histology.
