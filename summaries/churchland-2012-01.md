Most of the paper's central claim held in these two array datasets: in the first jPCA plane the neural state rotates the same direction across conditions (102 of 108 and 108 of 108), and a purely rotational dynamics matrix fits nearly as well as an unconstrained one. The angle peak near pi/2 and M1 rotating faster than PMd also held, while variance captured was low and the J-array rotation frequency was below range. The shuffle controls and the two kinematic models failed their criteria as fixed, but those outcomes depend on measures and model choices the paper does not specify. The dandiset has 192 per-electrode units, not the paper's sorted isolations, and none of the single-electrode, EMG, leech, walking or speed-task data, so six findings could not be tested.
