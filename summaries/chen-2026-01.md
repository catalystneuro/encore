The Anxa1 findings held in this data: evoked and spontaneous dopamine release were lower in LRRK2 G2019S mice than in wild type, and 7 of 8 mutant mice were individually reduced for evoked release. The Calb1 reduction also held in direction, but the dandiset's Calb1 dF/F is not the trace behind the paper's supplementary figure, so the claim that the Anxa1 effect is much larger than the Calb1 effect did not reproduce. The findings on LRRK2 enrichment, RAB3 phosphorylation, RIM binding and bassoon release sites could not be tested, because the dandiset holds only photometry. Of the minor claims, the per-mouse count for spontaneous release came out lower than reported. The published values use a measure that differs from the one the Methods state.
