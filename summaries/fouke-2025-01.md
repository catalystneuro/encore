Behavioral findings from freely swimming fish mostly held up, but the imaging and head-fixed findings could not be tested because that data is not in the dataset. Danionella swam faster (9.9 against 6.4 mm/s) and more steadily than zebrafish, zebrafish sped up for forward motion, and zebrafish followed the direction of optic flow while Danionella did not clearly. Danionella lacking speed modulation by direction did not reproduce, since the paper's own test finds three differing pairs, from one likely tracking artefact. Higher angular acceleration in zebrafish reversed, though noise in Danionella heading was not ruled out. A minor monocular result depends on how two unlabeled stimulus codes are assigned.
