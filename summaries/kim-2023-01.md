Most of the paper's Figure 2 findings held: paired pulses cancelled dye uptake at large field-direction angles, enhanced it at small angles, and cancelled more at 833 kHz than at 1 Hz. The two cancellation sizes the paper reports never matched together in one ROI layout, and the facilitation plateau appeared at 833 kHz but not at 1 Hz. The 60-fold co- versus counter-directional difference matched only at the single lowest-uptake region, so confidence is low. The stronger uptake near the apex electrode with paired pulses held. The square-array and voltage-imaging findings could not be tested because their data are not in the dataset.
