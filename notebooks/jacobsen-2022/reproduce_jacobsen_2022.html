Reproducing Jacobsen et al. (2022) from dandiset 000230¶

Paper. Jacobsen RI, Nair RR, Obenhaus HA, Donato F, Slettmoen T, Moser MB, Moser EI (2022). All-viral tracing of monosynaptic inputs to single birthdate-defined neurons in the intact brain. Cell Reports Methods 2, 100221. doi:10.1016/j.crmeth.2022.100221. The claims file (claims/jacobsen-2022.yaml) was written from the version of record and its supplement.

Dandiset. DANDI:000230, version 0.220506.1516 (doi:10.48324/dandi.000230/0.220506.1516). The paper's Data and code availability section cites this version, and it is also the most recent published one, so it is pinned. The earlier versions are listed in the data check: 0.220422.0713 holds the 9 sessions under hashed subject ids, 0.220426.0814 holds 18 assets (the 9 sessions under the real subject ids and again under the hashed ones), and 0.220426.1130 and the draft hold the same 9 paths as the pinned version.

What the dandiset holds. Nine NWB files of the optrode recordings in the MEC: three sessions of mouse 58313 (days 17, 18 and 19 after the rabies injection) and six of mouse 70375 (days 14 to 16, two per day). Each holds an open field part (position and head direction), laser trials, and only the curated units the figures show (12 units in all; the files say "Only a curated subset of tasks and/or units are exported"). The histology of the single-cell tracing (Figures 2 to 4, S1, S2, Tables S1 and S2) and the two-photon imaging (Figures 6 and 7) are not in it, and the paper names no other deposit for them.

Outside inputs. None as data. Methods details for the cell classes come from Rowland et al. (2018), which the STAR Methods cite for the analysis, and from the analysis code that paper links (see the docstring of mec_units.py).

fig5c-functional-cell-types¶

In mice with widespread RABV-ChR2-YFP expression, MEC recordings include grid, head direction and border cells, so the network as a whole is not disrupted by the rabies expression.

Criterion: grid, head direction and border cells classified with the scores and thresholds of Rowland et al. (2018); reproduced when at least one unit of each type is found. Scores and parameters are those of mec_units.py (docstring). The shuffle interval is run under both readings of Rowland et al.'s "random interval between +/- 20 s" (literal, within 20 s; standard, at least 20 s). Each unit has its own seeded generator. The passage of Rowland et al. (2018), Materials and methods, "Classification of cells into functional classes", reads: "For each permutation, the entire sequence of spikes fired by the cell was time-shifted along the animal's path by a random interval between ± 20 s, with the end of the session wrapped to the beginning. Time shifts varied randomly between permutations and between cells. From the shuffled distribution, we calculated the 95th percentile and used this value as a threshold". The analysis code it links (archdata, commit 9452263) has no spatial shuffle, so it does not settle the interval. A shift of under 20 s leaves the spike train close to the observed one, so its 95th percentile sits near the observed score (for the head direction cell T4 C16, 0.762 against an observed 0.912, printed below); the standard shuffle, shifts of at least 20 s, is adopted as an assumption, and both are shown.

How the analysis differs from the paper's. The paper's own analysis code is not published. Cell classes follow the Methods of Rowland et al. (2018), with the details the text leaves open taken from that paper's linked code (archdata, commit 9452263); the shuffle interval was run under two readings. The pulse time was run as TTL onset and offset; the onset reproduces 8 of the 9 printed latencies within 0.01 ms (day 17 taken per epoch). Pulses exclude the first TTL event of each epoch. The open field is taken as the whole position series, as the files have no open-field epoch; its first 1800 s, the other reading, moves no rate by more than 0.001 Hz except in day 14 session 2 of 70375, where it matches the printed value. For the day-17 file the laser epoch was taken per cell. The tuning-curve bin width and smoothing were varied for T4 C16.

What was found about the dataset. It holds only the optrode recordings, and of those only the 12 curated units the figures show; the histology and imaging behind 21 of the 28 claims are not in it and the paper names no other deposit. The open field has no epoch. The two laser epochs of the day-17 file carry no power label. The first TTL event of every epoch is spurious (onset at the epoch start, 45 ms to 1.9 s long). The pulses come in trains of 6 at 10 Hz every 2.6 s, where the Methods say 10 Hz continuously. The LFP timestamps run backwards within each file. waveform_mean is labelled volts but holds values with peak-to-trough 31 to 41 for the 70375 unit and 34 to 243 for the units of 58313. For day 14 session 2 of 70375, the file holds more open field and more pulses than the paper's printed values use. Version 0.220426.0814 holds every session twice, under real and hashed subject ids; the pinned version holds each once.
