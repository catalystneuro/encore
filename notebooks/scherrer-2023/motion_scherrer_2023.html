ed7-motion-small¶

Lateral shifts of the image due to the mouse's movement are small, with a root-mean-square displacement of 0.13 pixels (0.7 um).

Criterion: displacements NoRMCorre estimates on the raw imaging video; rigid registration with NoRMCorre's defaults is a standard choice. Readings of the root mean square: (a) of the 2D shift's length about the mean shift, (b) the same about zero shift, (c) over both axes' shifts about their means. Microns use the pixel sizes of abstract-kiloscope-fov (5.94 and 5.35 um). Reproduced when the value in pixels is within 0.13 +/- 0.013 under a reading.

The registration is written here after NoRMCorre's rigid algorithm (see the helper module); it is not MATLAB's NoRMCorre.

# The registration on the movie with a known jitter added: the frames of the first block moved by random shifts with
# a 2D RMS of 0.13 px about zero (the paper's value) and written back as integers, then registered as in the main run.
class Jittered:
    def __init__(self, reader, shifts):
        self.reader, self.shifts = reader, shifts
        self.blocks, self.chunks, self.shape = reader.blocks, reader.chunks, reader.shape

    def block(self, start):
        a = self.reader.block(start)
        return ks.fourier_shift_frames(a, self.shifts[start:start + len(a)])

rng_jit = np.random.default_rng(691_72)
jitter = rng_jit.normal(0, 0.13 / np.sqrt(2), (reader.shape[0], 2))
jr = ks.normcorre_rigid(Jittered(reader, jitter), blocks=reader.blocks[:1], log=None)
fr_, est = jr["frames"], jr["shifts"]
def rms_a(s_):
    d_ = s_ - s_.mean(0)
    return np.sqrt(np.mean((d_ ** 2).sum(1)))
change = est - sh[fr_]                 # what the jitter changed in the estimate
resid = change + jitter[fr_]           # zero (up to a constant) if the registration recovers the jitter exactly
print(f"frames {fr_[0]} to {fr_[-1]}, registered in {jr['seconds']:.0f} s")
print(f"RMS (a) of the added jitter {rms_a(jitter[fr_]):.4f} px; of the main run's shifts on these frames {rms_a(sh[fr_]):.4f} px; "
      f"of the shifts estimated on the jittered frames {rms_a(est):.4f} px")

Result: not reproduced. The RMS displacement is 0.0367 px under reading (a), 0.0381 px under (b) and 0.0260 px under (c), against 0.13 +/- 0.013 px; under (a) and (b) that is 0.20 to 0.23 um at the two pixel sizes, against the paper's 0.7 um. No frame moves more than 0.16 px along an axis, and the RMS in each 100 s window lies between 0.028 and 0.051 px. The registration finds motion of the paper's size when it is there: with a jitter of 0.132 px RMS added to the first block, the estimated shifts follow it (correlation 0.996 on each axis) and their RMS rises from 0.033 to 0.136 px. The movie is consistent with the raw video, not a motion-corrected one: the file holds no motion correction, the histogram of its counts keeps a roughness of 0.740 to 0.745 in every block, where moving frames by sub-pixel shifts of 0.13 px RMS and writing them back as integers lowers it from 0.745 to 0.052, and its zero values are at most 2 single pixels in 243 of 36596 frames, not strips at the edges. Registering high-pass filtered frames (a variant chosen here, on the first 2400 frames) gives a smaller value still, 0.0188 px under (a) against 0.0328 px for the main run on the same frames, so a static background pulling the estimate toward zero does not explain the difference. The registration is written here after NoRMCorre's rigid algorithm with its defaults, not MATLAB's NoRMCorre.
