Reproducing Bakalar et al. (2023) from dandiset 001362¶

Paper: Bakalar D, Gavrilova O, Jiang SZ, Zhang H-Y, Roy S, Williams SK, Liu N, Wisser S, Usdin TB, Eiden LE (2023). Constitutive and conditional deletion reveals distinct phenotypes driven by developmental versus neurotransmitter actions of the neuropeptide PACAP. J Neuroendocrinol 35(11), e13286. doi:10.1111/jne.13286. The claims file was written from the NIH author manuscript in PubMed Central (PMC10620107) with its Supplemental Methods, which describe the whole-brain c-Fos pipeline and its statistics.

Dataset: DANDI:001362, version 0.260923.2152. The paper cites no version (its data statement says data are available on request), so the most recent published version is pinned. The only other published version, 0.260701.1900, holds the same 765 assets with the same blobs (checked below); the later one adds ORCIDs and the paper's DOI to the metadata, none of which the analysis uses.

What the dataset holds: a BIDS derivative dataset (not NWB) of the whole-brain c-Fos light-sheet experiment of Figure 5 and Table 4 only: for 22 hemispheres of 13 mice (6 PACAP fl/fl, 7 PACAPko), the deconvolved and N4-corrected stacks, the registered atlas labels, the FRST image, binary FRST segmentations at several thresholds, atlas-space heatmaps, and, for 13 hemispheres (one per mouse), per-region cell count tables. None of the paper's behaviour, qPCR, immunohistochemistry, in situ hybridization, restraint or telemetry data is in it, and no other dandiset of this paper or lab was found on the archive.

What is done here: cells are recounted per atlas region from the binary segmentations of all 22 hemispheres, by the counting rule of the Supplemental Methods (26-connected components of 9 to 900 voxels, each assigned to the label holding most of its voxels), at the five FRST thresholds the segmentations of every hemisphere share, and at the finer series that 4 hemispheres hold. The notebook reproduce_bakalar_2023_thresholds_14000_26000.ipynb runs thresholds 14000 to 26000 on the 18 hemispheres that hold them. The recount is checked against the dataset's own count tables. The region-wise Mann-Whitney U tests with Benjamini-Krieger-Yekutieli adjustment are then run under every reading the claims file names. The recount streams the segmentations and label images of 22 hemispheres and takes most of the run time (cell 8 prints how long); nothing is written to disk. The synthetic check of the counting draws its volume from a generator with a fixed seed.

  • (2) Region size: (a) the region's voxel count in that hemisphere's label image (density, cells per cubic mm), (b) the atlas region volume, which is one constant per region, so that dividing by it changes neither U, p nor the percentage: (b) is run as the raw counts (raw).

  • (5) Percentage: ratio of means and ratio of medians after outlier removal.
  • (6) BKY alpha: 0.05, with 0.1 shown.

def matrix(hemis, t, rule="sized", size="density", labels=""):
    # Rows hemispheres, columns included regions; NaN where the region has no voxels in that hemisphere.
    # t is one threshold or a dict hemisphere -> threshold; labels "" (masked where the dataset has them) or "_u" (unmasked)
    rows = []
    for s, h in hemis:
        r = RC[(s, h)]
        c = r[rule + labels][t[(s, h)] if isinstance(t, dict) else t][inc.label_id.values].astype(float)
        v = r["volume" + labels][inc.label_id.values].astype(float)
        rows.append(np.where(v > 0, c / (v * VOX_MM3) if size == "density" else c, np.nan))
    m = pd.DataFrame(rows, columns=inc.acronym.values)
    return m.loc[:, m.notna().any()]
