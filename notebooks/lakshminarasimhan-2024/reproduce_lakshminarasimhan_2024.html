Lakshminarasimhan et al. (2024) on dandiset 000009¶

Paper. Lakshminarasimhan KJ, Xie M, Cohen JD, Sauerbrei BA, Hantman AW, Litwin-Kumar A, Escola S (2024). Specific connectivity optimizes learning in thalamocortical loops. Cell Reports 43, 114059. doi:10.1016/j.celrep.2024.114059. The claims file (claims/lakshminarasimhan-2024.yaml) was written from the NIH author manuscript in PubMed Central (PMC11104520) with the supplement (mmc2).

Dandiset. DANDI:000009, version 0.220126.1903, "Maintenance of persistent activity in a frontal thalamocortical loop" (Guo et al. 2017). The paper cites the dandiset without a version (key resources table). This is the only published version, and it is pinned for that reason. The draft holds the same 173 files with the same blobs (checked below); it differs only in metadata.

What the paper used from it. The working memory data of Figure 6 and Figure S6: "5 behavioral sessions from one mouse", 151 ALM and 72 thalamic (VM/VAL) units, correct trials, delay period. The motor control data of the same figures (Sauerbrei et al. 2020) are not in this dandiset, nor in any archive the paper names; a search of the DANDI API for "Sauerbrei" finds only dandiset 000221, a different study. The simulations of Figures 1 to 5 and S1 to S5 are generated by the paper's models and are not deposited.

Method. Each claim's criterion refers to the numbered method notes W1 to W10 at the head of the claims file; the helper tcloop.py says for each parameter where it comes from. Random splits and subsamples use each claim's own seed (tcloop.seed), and each result that depends on them is also computed under the seed plus 1000, 2000, 3000 and 4000.

Clock¶

The trial table and the spikes must share a clock. Choice (lick direction of correct trials without photostimulation) is decoded from the spike counts of each session's units in 200 ms windows around delay onset, with a cross-validated nearest-class-mean decoder, and compared with the same decoder on shuffled choices. ALM and thalamic activity in this task should carry little choice information before the sample epoch (before -1.3 s), more during the sample and delay epochs, and most after the go cue (1.3 s). A clock offset of more than a few hundred ms would move or remove this rise.

How the analysis differs from the paper's. The smoothing grid, the 10 ms bin and the padding are standard choices the paper does not give. Sigma is chosen per thalamic unit on the validation R2 of each regression: the 16-PC regression for the PC regressions, the regression on all units for that one. The noise ceilings, the larger test sets and the chance levels of the alignments are diagnostics added on the verifier's request; none decides an outcome. P3 does not apply, as no session holds both ALM and thalamic units. The thalamic units are regressed on ALM activity from other sessions, as the paper's design implies; their test sets are their own sessions' trials.

What was found about the dataset. The paper's mouse is anm00264943, and its unit totals match the paper's exactly; the thalamic units come from six sessions, not the five the paper states. The files give no interval over which each unit was recorded, though many units fire in only part of their session.
