The heading is the direction of travel measured with y reversed, theta = atan2(-vy, vx) (not atan2(vy, vx)), and 180 degrees is travel towards -x, upwind, as the code assumes; the files say only "standardized arena coordinates". The turn direction (+1 or -1) is the sign of the rotational velocity at the event's peak, so +1 turns are counterclockwise in the (x, -y) frame. Whether that is a left or a right turn depends on how the camera viewed the arena, which the files do not state, and the direction column is described only as "Turn direction label"; methods-intensity-right-bias needs that and is blocked. The event start is not the SI's onset as computed from the files' dtheta: it comes a median of 4 frames (67 ms) after the time the rotation in the turn's direction crosses 20% of its peak (from every 6th file above). The claims file asks for the SI's onset in that case, so it is the main reading below, and the code's use of the event start is the other. The rule follows the rotation in the turn's own direction; the first version of this attempt followed |dtheta|, whose run went on into a turn the other way in 0.2% of the turns (printed above).

Clock. The turn events share the trajectories' clock: every event's peak of |dtheta| has the sign of its direction. The odor signals were computed by the authors from the projected movie, and the files give no movie frame for a trajectory frame, so whether they share the movie's clock cannot be shown here.

fig5e-cue-betas¶

For upwind-facing flies in the bulk of the plume, the gradient cue 50 ms before a turn significantly predicts turn direction in the smooth plume while motion does not, and the motion cue does in the complex plume while the gradient does not.

Readings (claims file): heading window in the complex plume (160 to 200, SI; 150 to 210, code); the code's 15 mm margin (not applied; applied); z-scoring (none, code; pooled over the plume's frames; per trajectory); "not significant" (p >= 0.05; p >= 0.01). Onset: the SI's 20% rule, which the claims file asks for when the event start differs (main), and the event start (code, a variant).

Which complex files are the paper's complex plume is not stated in the paper. Assumed: the smoke-1a files, as the dandiset's description says the smoke_1a_orig plume "was analyzed throughout the paper" and the session descriptions of these files name that movie (Data check). The code selects "smoke-2a" (metadata/published_panel_params.json); those files and both sets together are shown. The code's published tables are compared below with every reading, to show which files and readings they come from. Expected values are compared, as the criterion says, under the code's readings (event start, margin, no z-scoring, complex heading 150 to 210, smoke-2a).

In the complex plume, a logistic model on the odor motion feature predicts the side of the centerline better (ROC AUC about 0.62) than one on the gradient feature (about 0.54), and the odor sum has no predictive power.

Fitted on all three features together in the complex plume, the logistic model gives motion a larger weight (about 0.40) than the gradient (about 0.10), and the sum a weight of 0; the combined model reaches a test AUC of 0.612.

How the analysis differs from the paper's. The Fig. 5 analysis is a port of the paper's code (its filters, smoothing, frame matching and models), run under the readings the claims file names, with the SI's turn onset as the main reading and the smoke-1a files as the complex plume (assumed). The port was not run beside the code here; its closest readings come within 0.1 SE of the code's published tables. The Fig. 2 samples are drawn by a procedure the paper does not give in full (mirror-balanced, assumed), with 10 repeats in place of 100. The motion cue of Fig. S1G is not defined in the paper; three forms are run, with and without smoothing. The Fig. S1 parts use the dandiset's deposited freestream maps and points, with masks from every 25th frame.

What was found about the dataset. The code's published Fig. 5 tables are matched most closely, within 0.1 SE and 0.001 in log-likelihood per turn, by the smoke-1a files with the dataset's turn start and the SI's heading window, and closely by the smoke-1a files with the SI onset (0.45 SE), not by the smoke-2a files and the 150 to 210 window the code names, which give a complex motion coefficient of -0.012 against +0.044. The two complex sets give coefficients of opposite sign; their intensity layouts are not mirrored relative to each other, and the mean gradient cue of neither follows its mean intensity profile, so a sign convention differing between them is not ruled out. The files do not say which complex set is the paper's plume, which turn direction is left or right, or how the heading's axes are oriented (theta is atan2(-vy, vx)).
