The example session (571619, the deep MOs subject of Figure 1D) has three probes; MOs (120 channels, 119 marked valid) on probe F and VISp (88, 87 valid) on probe C, each with channels in the ACSF above the brain ("none", 98 and 32), which the file marks invalid. The LFP is int16 at 2.5 kHz (0.4 ms intervals) with a conversion to volts. Channel locations are CCF areas without layers; layer 1 is approximated below by the top 100 um of each area, the channels nearest the ACSF, as an assumption, and the result is shown with and without it. The clock of the LFP is checked below with the stimulus artifact at the trial onsets.

fig3abc-decay-with-distance¶

In the example deep MOs session, evoked LFP, CSD and spiking magnitudes are large near the stimulation site (54.0, 51.4 and 30.9 SD.s in MO) and smaller in distant VIS (18.0, 4.8 and 1.8 SD.s).

Criterion: magnitudes as in fig3d-magnitudes for the session of fig1-supp1-example; reproduced when MO exceeds VIS for all three signals and all six values are within tolerance under one combination of readings; partially reproduced when the ordering holds but a value is out of tolerance.

LFP and CSD by claar.lfp_magnitudes (all awake trials, 1000 shuffles; the steps and where each comes from are in its docstring). The spiking magnitudes are those of claar.spike_site_summary, as in the spiking notebook.

Result: partially reproduced (agreed in the third verification round). Only the ordering is reproduced. MO exceeds VIS for all three signals in 112 of 128 combinations of readings; the LFP order reverses in 4 of 32 LFP readings, all at the high current with the 1 Hz cutoff and the -0.5 to 0 s baseline. No combination puts all six values within tolerance, and at most three are (medium current, 0.1 Hz, top 100 um left out, -2 to 0 s, epoch shuffle, 5 ms kernel, |z|: CSD 48.77 and 5.21, spiking MO 28.75; LFP 60.83 and 21.75 against 54.0 and 18.0, spiking VIS 2.76 against 1.8). The LFP magnitude in MO is never within tolerance (7.57 to 77.16; 60.83 and above with the 0.1 Hz cutoff). The CSD magnitude in MO is within tolerance in 60 of 128 combinations. The spiking magnitude as the Methods describe it (signed z) is 0.31 to 3.96 in MO; integrating |z| gives 3.57 to 31.37, and 28.31 to 31.37 with the 5 ms kernel and the -2 to 0 s baseline, near the paper's 30.9, so the paper's spiking magnitude probably counts falls below baseline as well as rises ("our magnitude metric accounts for both reductions as well as increases"). Leaving out the top 100 um changes no ordering and moves the CSD values by up to 2.84 (52.34 against 49.50, medium current, 0.1 Hz, -0.5 to 0 s, epoch shuffle). The layer-1 exclusion is an assumption (the files give no layers), and the onset shuffle over the whole recording is replaced by one over the span of the awake pulses.

fig3d-magnitudes: the size of the computation¶

Superficial stimulation modulated local LFP, CSD and spiking and non-local LFP and CSD, but evoked no spiking outside the stimulated area; deep stimulation evoked LFP, CSD and spiking both locally and in non-stimulated cortical regions.

Summary¶

Claim Outcome
fig3abc-decay-with-distance partially reproduced (assumption: layer 1 is the top 100 um; only the ordering holds)
fig3d-magnitudes blocked: too large

A failure to reproduce here may be a failure of this attempt, not of the paper: see the reasoning and the differences from the paper's method.

How the analysis differs from the paper's: the files give no cortical layers, so layer 1 is approximated by the top 100 um of each area (an assumption; the result is shown without the exclusion too); the Chebyshev channel rejection is applied per channel, once, where the Methods apply it per sample and epoch; the CSD is computed from the trial-averaged LFP (all its steps are linear, so this equals averaging single-trial CSDs) with rows of two channels, 13 then 2 rows of smoothing for the 26 then 4 channels of the Methods; the decimation and high-pass filters are zero phase; the shuffle "over the recording" draws onsets over the span of the awake pulses; the mask is the EEG's (k - 1 to k + 4), which covers the LFP artifact at k + 2 and k + 3. The current is not given; the high and the medium current are both run.

What was found about the dataset: the LFP of every probe is int16 at 2.5 kHz with timestamps that share the trial table's clock (the artifact peaks 2 samples after the onset sample); the channels in the ACSF above the brain are labelled "none" and marked invalid; channel locations carry no layers.
