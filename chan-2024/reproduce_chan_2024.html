Reproducing Chan et al. (2024) on Dryad doi:10.5061/dryad.vt4b8gtzk¶

Paper: Chan, Parasurama, Atlas, Xu, Jongebloed, Alexander, Langenhan, Thornton and Riffell (2024), "Olfaction in the Anthropocene: NO3 negatively affects floral scent and nocturnal pollination", Science 383, 607-611, doi:10.1126/science.adi0858.

Claims: claims/chan-2024.yaml, written from the author manuscript (NIHMS2120560) with the Supplementary Materials of the version of record, and checked by a second reader.

Dataset: Dryad doi:10.5061/dryad.vt4b8gtzk, version id 289757 (published 2024-04-22). Dryad's API lists one published version of this dataset, so it is the version the paper cites (the paper's ref. 40 names the DOI with no version) and the newest; it is pinned for both reasons. Dryad needs a token to download, so the files were staged before the run in /tmp/dryad/vt4b8gtzk/289757/ and are read there in place; the cell below checks every file's SHA-256 against Dryad's public file list and prints the digests.

The paper's other deposit: The paper's custom code release on Zenodo, record 8411927 (doi:10.5281/zenodo.8411927, ref. 41, the only version of the record), holds data the Dryad deposit lacks: the raw Vocus traces of Fig. 2D and Table S9, the Fig. 3B wind tunnel sheet, the fruit-set counts in the Fig. 1E R script, and the GEOS-Chem output of Fig. 4. It is the paper's own deposit, so claims resting on it are recorded as data: elsewhere and the record is listed under inputs. It is streamed from Zenodo (the zip is read by ranges); its MD5 is printed below. The outside inputs are the land mask of the Supplementary Methods (global-land-mask 1.0.0), the bottom-level MERRA-2 wind of July and January 2013 from the GEOS-Chem data bucket, and the city coordinates and populations of the UN World Urbanization Prospects 2018 (see the Fig. 4 section).

No analysis here draws random numbers, so no seeds are used.

Checks of the description against the values:

  • Ozone is in ppb (field values 10 to 48, one excursion to 137), with dates and times as the README says; the three logs cover the nights of 26-27 July, 2 August and 11 August 2018.
  • CAPS NO2 is in ppb; rows during zeroing (status 11004 and 12004) carry NaN, and status 4 rows include one value of -437 ppb. The status codes are not described.
  • The iButtons log every 3 min, not at the 0.5 Hz the Supplementary Methods state, over 11 to 16 July, 25 to 30 July and 1 to 5 August 2018. Their date column is an Excel serial date.
  • The anemometer file has one header (a PuTTY log started 2018-08-01 22:15:22) and 1,387,308 rows, 12.0 h at 32 Hz; no timestamps.
  • All field files are from 2018; Table S1 says 2018 and 2019.
  • GEOS-Chem mixing ratios are v/v (NO3 up to 46 ppt, O3 4 to 86 ppb), pressure in hPa, temperature in K, on the 2 x 2.5 degree grid with 14 monthly means. The file holds no wind.
  • The flowtube and field streams are separate experiments with no shared trial table; the only clock questions are the stage timing within each flowtube run (see the claims) and the field logs, whose dates overlap on 26-27 July and 1-2 August.

Fig. S6: oxidation products¶

figs6-oxidation-products: "NO3 oxidation of the scent yields organic nitrates (C10H15NO6, C10H17NO6, C10H17NO7) as well as C10H16O4 and C10H18O4; O3 yields only the latter two."

The workbook holds two segments without labels (finding above). The Supplementary Methods (Characterization of free-radical oxidation) run each flowtube experiment in three stages, clean air, then O3 ("stage II"), then NO3 ("Stage III was initiated by injecting NO3"), so the earlier segment (05:12 to 05:38) is taken as the O3 stage and the later (06:36 to 06:56) as the NO3 stage. In each segment the oxidant onset is the first row where C10H16O4 exceeds twice the median of the segment's first five rows (a standard choice); rows before it are the clean-air level. What counts as a rise above the clean-air level is not defined; three readings are run: a one-sided Welch test (r1, 5 against 9 and 4 against 7 points), every point after the onset above the clean-air mean plus 3 SD (r2), and a mean after the onset at least twice the clean-air mean (r3, a threshold chosen here, not the paper's).

Recognition distances: Fig. 4, Table S13 and Fig. S9¶

The recognition distance is the degradation time times "the mean horizontal wind speed of the bottom vertical grid" (Supplementary Methods, GEOS-Chem code availability). The deposited 12.1.0 output holds no wind. The model was driven by MERRA-2 2013 meteorology (Supplementary Methods, Global models), which GEOS-Chem reads from its processed MERRA-2 files; the bottom-level U and V of those files for July and January 2013 are read here from the public GEOS-Chem data bucket at 0.5 x 0.625 degrees and averaged to the 2 x 2.5 degree grid (chan.merra2_bottom_wind, chan.to_gc_grid). This is an assumption: the paper's own wind field is not deposited, and the 2 x 2.5 degree files for 2013 are not on the bucket. City coordinates and populations are from the UN World Urbanization Prospects 2018 (chan.table_s13_cities), since the source Table S13 cites (ref. 79, World Population Prospects 2019 Highlights) gives no coordinates; this too is an assumption.

Readings, as the criteria name them: density (1) at the cell's T and P or (2) Table S12's; threshold (a) either compound or (b) both; NO3 map (i) NO3 alone or (ii) NO3 and O3; wind (x) speed of the monthly mean vector or (y) monthly mean speed; city (containing cell, or bilinear interpolation of the map); factor map regridded by nearest cell or bilinearly. Summer months: July north of the equator, January south of it; land cells (any land) south of -56 degrees removed.

How the analysis differs from the paper's. The fruit-set counts, the Fig. 3B counts, the raw Vocus traces and the GEOS-Chem fields are not in the Dryad deposit and were read from the paper's Zenodo code release. The flowtube stage windows of Fig. 2D and Table S9 are those of the paper's own notebook in the Dryad deposit, as the claims file now says after a reader's answer, with the last half and the whole of each stage as variants; the blend run of Fig. S5 uses the last half and the whole of each stage; the blend CIMS signals are not divided by the reagent ion, which is not deposited. The Fig. 3B floral-odor group is the sheet's (11 of 12), with the 0.01x row of Fig. S7C as a second reading. The field tests use the pooled Table S10 counts, as the paper does, and the fruit-set tests the counts of the paper's R script: the deposit holds no flower-level or night-level data. The recognition distances use the bottom-level MERRA-2 wind of July and January 2013 from GEOS-Chem's processed 0.5 x 0.625 degree files, averaged to the 2 x 2.5 degree grid, as the model's own wind is not deposited, and the city coordinates and populations of the UN World Urbanization Prospects 2018, as the source Table S13 cites gives no coordinates; under these the Table S13 distances are not matched. The Fig. S9 regressions are run on the rows Table S13 prints, and on the recomputed values as a variant.

What was found about the dataset. The Dryad deposit holds the field logs, the flowtube CIMS and Fig. 2D traces, the EAG and dose-response sheets, the Fig. S6 products and Table S10, but none of the GEOS-Chem output its description is about, no pollinator or fruit-set data, and no Fig. 3B data. In the EAG file one Hyles dose series repeats eight values of another, and the file holds 159 values per species (about 18 series of 9 concentrations), not 159 moths. The Manduca floral-odor group of Fig. 3B has the counts of the 1x dose-response row, where the Methods give 1/100 floral equivalent, and the Fig. 3B sheet holds 113 Manduca where the Methods give 138. The deposited Fig. 2D b-pinene trace gives 29% and 74% losses, where the raw trace gives Table S9's 33% and 84%; its time column is in seconds, not the README's minutes. The anemometer file has no timestamps, and its wind speeds (1.7 to 2.1 m/s) are above Table S1's 1.14 m/s. The CAPS files carry an IgorTime column that is not the clock and undocumented status codes, and neither the ozone nor the CAPS files give a time zone. The Fig. S6 workbook does not say which segment is which oxidant.
