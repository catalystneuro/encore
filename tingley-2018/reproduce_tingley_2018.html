Reproducing Tingley & Buzsáki (2018) from dandiset 000213¶

Paper. Tingley D, Buzsáki G (2018). Transformation of a spatial map across the hippocampal-lateral septal circuit. Neuron 98, 1229-1242. doi:10.1016/j.neuron.2018.04.028. This attempt used the full text of the NIH author manuscript in PubMed Central (PMC6605060): Results, figure legends and STAR Methods. The supplementary figures were not read.

Dandiset. DANDI:000213, version 0.250624.0438. The paper cites no version (its data statement says the data were being prepared for CRCNS). There are two published versions, 0.220127.1738 and 0.250624.0438; they hold the same 67 files with the same asset identifiers and sizes (checked below) and differ only in dandiset-level metadata, so the most recent is pinned.

What the dandiset holds. 67 sessions from the five rats (DT2, DT5, DT7, DT8, DT9): sorted units labelled by region (ls, hpc, ca3 and a few others), LFP at 1250 Hz, head position, and trials. The paper's processed files (linearized behaviour, trial conditions, the CA1 channel of each session) are not in it; they are rebuilt here as described in lshpc.py.

Code read. The paper's figure scripts, https://github.com/DavidTingley/papers (LS_phasecoding, commit 9f4cd48a44f211956954558b66d776ccbb74d693); buzcode, https://github.com/buzsakilab/buzcode (commit e615663b2b100782d1f378580f6d7aaa35c12937); and the per-cell decoder in https://github.com/DavidTingley/scratch (commit 2f804532290774f52a442bf0f3ec23dd5398d58d). The functions that bin phases (bz_phaseMap2Bins), compute information (olypherInfo) and detect assemblies are not public.

Claims. The claims file is claims/000213/10.1016-j.neuron.2018.04.028.yaml.

Revision after the first verification round. Place fields now use the Gaussian rate map that the paper's code passes to bz_getPlaceFields1D (SD 4 bins), where the first version used the 5-bin box map; Figure 6 groups sessions by their unit labels, as its criterion states, and leaves out sessions without hippocampal units; the ensemble tests are one-sided; the theta reference of each session is described and the phase claims are repeated on sessions with a clear theta rhythm; fig5-assemblies is tested on a sample of pairs and ls-information-lower is tested, with criteria added to the claims file in this revision.

Second revision. The sentences the second verifier found unsupported are corrected, and three variants are added: place fields without the rule dropping peaks whose SD across trials exceeds their mean, the onset-phase test on sessions with one theta reference label per rat, and the peer prediction with the LS unit's spatial tuning as a covariate. No outcome changed.

The whole notebook streams the data and takes about twelve to fifteen minutes on four processes, most of it the per-cell decoding and the peer prediction.

Units carry a region label: ls (lateral septum), hpc, ca3, and a few cortical or other labels (acg, cc, ms, ppc, sFi). One file (DT7, 20170317) has units without any label and is left out of every count by region; one (DT7, 20170324) has no units. No two files share their spike totals, unit counts and LFP length, or their trial times, so no recording was deposited twice. Several sessions were recorded at the same depths on consecutive days (for example five DT8 sessions at 216 and 1944 um); each session's units are treated as separate units, as the paper's counts do, and no claim assumes the same unit across sessions.

The electrode labels of sub-DT2 are all unknown; its hippocampal and LS channels are taken from the electrode groups of its labelled units. The session names hold two depths that no metadata describes. In DT9 the units are labelled ca3 exactly in the sessions whose second depth is 828 um or more, which fits the second depth being the hippocampal probe's; the first depth is taken as the LS probe's. This is an assumption, used only by the depth claims. It is not settled by the other rats: in DT5 the files labelled hpc and ca3 alternate at second depths of 2232 to 2448 um, and the DT8 depths (0 to 612 and 0 to 2088 um) do not say which probe is which.

How the analysis differs from the paper's.

  • The paper's linearized behaviour, trial conditions and choice of CA1 channel are not in the dandiset. Positions are linearized here by the fraction of each trial's path (200 bins, about 1.1 cm), conditions are formed from trial type, direction and start and end sites, and the theta channel is the hippocampal channel with high theta and the most 100 to 250 Hz power. In the 11 sessions with ca3 channels it is a CA3 channel, and in DT5 its theta rhythm is weak.
  • The per-cell decoder follows the paper's scratch code; the function that bins spike phases is not public, and the circular mean per bin is used.
  • Place fields follow bz_getPlaceFields1D on the Gaussian rate map of bz_firingMap1D (SD 4 bins), with the Methods' limits converted to bins at 1.5 cm. This finds a third of the paper's share of hippocampal fields; without the code's rule dropping peaks whose SD across trials exceeds their mean it finds more than the paper in both regions (46.0% hippocampal, 20.0% LS).
  • The information measure of Figure 1 and of the information rates is built from the Methods' formula with choices fixed in the claims file; the paper's function is not public.
  • Coherence in Figure 6 is a phase locking value of the 4 to 12 Hz phases, not multitaper coherence.
  • Only the trials are read from the LFP, with 1 s of padding for filtering.
  • Assemblies (Figure 5) are computed on a random 1% of the pairs, with a bin size, kernel and window grid chosen here; the claims that need every pair (Figure 5G, 6E) were not tested.

What was found about the dandiset.

  • The paper's processed behaviour (linearized position and trial conditions), the CA1 channel per session, and the unit recording sites are not in the dandiset. No session has both CA1 and CA3 channels labelled, where the paper's Figure 6A has sessions with both.
  • Eight DT8 files state positions in mm but hold metres; in every file the z column of position is a copy of y; eight of eleven DT2 files repeat or reverse position timestamps; DT2 positions are in pixels with no scale; the head-orientation quaternion is stated in metres.
  • DT2 electrodes are all labelled unknown; DT7 20170317 has 43 units with no region label; DT7 20170324 has no units; 11 units hold spike times out of order.
  • The depths in the session names are not described; the first is taken as the LS depth.
  • DT9 20170501's trials do not match its positions; DT8 20170207 and 20170224 have trials without type or direction.
  • The units labelled hpc (2,593) are 12.9% more than the paper's 2,297 CA1 units.
  • In the script that writes the paper's place-field files (scratch_07_25_17_calc_firingMaps.m), the file the figures load is named for a 20% threshold, but no threshold is passed, so the function's default of 1% applies.
