Method details fixed from the paper, used by the Figure S2 criteria.
(F1) F is the mean emission over the entire cell soma per frame, F0 the mean over an interval
before the stimulus, dF = 100% (F - F0)/F0 (Methods, Recording of the optical APs). FluoVolt
emission rises with depolarization, so traces are not inverted.
(F2) Traces are not filtered or modified in any way (Methods); no detrending or smoothing.
(F3) The stimulus is a 250-ms, 2-kHz unmodulated sine wave (Figure S2 legend), starting 50 ms into
the session (Methods); the onset, the field and the solution of each session are taken from the
dataset, and the 50 ms is used when the onset is not given there.
(F4) Sessions after the change are those about 5 min after the solution was replaced by TTX or
vehicle (Figure S2 legend); the solution of each session is taken from the dataset.
(F5) The maximum firing rate of a session is the inverse of the peak-to-peak interval between its
first two APs (Methods).
(F6) The two-sided Student's t test, paired or unpaired as appropriate, at p < 0.05, is the paper's
test where a criterion needs one (Methods, Quantification and statistical analysis). The paper
reports no test for Figure S2.

Details the paper leaves open, with the readings named. Every Figure S2 criterion runs every
combination of the readings below; a claim is decided when all give its outcome, and blocked as
methods_underspecified when they give different outcomes.
(O1) The soma outline is not given. ROIs are taken from the dataset when it holds them. When it
does not, readings (a) the connected component nearest the image centre (the Methods centre the
neuron) of an Otsu threshold of the mean pre-stimulus frame, (b) that mask eroded by 2 pixels, to
leave out the neurite bases at its edge. Otsu and the 2 pixels are standard choices.
(O2) The length of the F0 interval is not given ("a time interval preceding the electrical
stimulation"); readings (a) all frames before the onset (about 50 ms), (b) the last 20 ms before
the onset. This is unlikely to change an outcome.
(O3) AP detection is given only as "brief emission upticks", in neurons selected for APs above 5%
dF lasting about 10 ms (typical 11.3% and 8 ms). An AP is a local maximum of dF with width at half
height between 2 and 20 ms ("about 10 ms"; the bounds are a standard choice) and at least 5 ms
from a larger maximum (standard choice; the paper's highest rates, 60 to 90 Hz, give intervals
over 11 ms). Readings of the height (a) above 5% dF (the Methods' selection level), (b) above 5 SD
of dF before the onset. After TTX, (a) and (b) are applied with the level of the same neuron's
sessions before the change, so that a drop of the baseline noise cannot create APs.
(O4) "Sustained depolarization" is not defined by a measure. It is no AP in the last 100 ms of the
stimulus while the mean dF over that window is above, reading (a) 5 SD of dF before the onset,
(b) half the median AP height of the neuron before the change. The 100-ms window is a standard
choice. "Sustained firing" is, as a standard choice, at least three APs with one in the last 50 ms
of the stimulus.
(O5) Which session pairs are compared before and after the change is not stated beyond the
legend's "recorded first ... and again"; readings (a) every field tested both before and after,
(b) only the highest field tested both before and after.

Readings and thresholds¶

Per session, APs are detected during the stimulus (50 to 300 ms, or to the end of a shorter recording) under every combination of O1 to O4 (sinewave.measure). The threshold of a neuron for a stimulus (kind and carrier) and an AP count N (1 or 5) is the lowest voltage at which a session has at least N APs during the stimulus (Figure 2D legend: "the minimum electric field strength that elicits 1 or 5 action potentials"); a voltage tested more than once counts when any session reaches N (a standard choice). Field strength: the files state 36 V/cm per V of a peak-to-peak voltage; dandiset 001271's voltages match the fields of Figure S2 at 18 V/cm per V (see that notebook), so both are shown, 18 V/cm per V first.

def thresholds(m, n_ap):
    out = []
    for (subj, kind, khz), g in m.groupby(["subject", "kind", "khz"]):
        if (kind, khz) not in STIMS:
            continue
        hit = g.loc[g["n_ap"] >= n_ap, "volt"]
        below = g.loc[g["n_ap"] < n_ap, "volt"]
        out.append(dict(subject=subj, kind=kind, khz=khz, n_ap=n_ap, thr_v=hit.min() if len(hit) else np.nan,
                        bracketed=bool(len(hit) and (below < hit.min()).any()), tested=len(g)))
    return pd.DataFrame(out)

def beat_flags(r):
    """Per session: share of APs within 12.5-37.5 ms of a beat, more than two APs in one beat, at least as

    tms = (r["ap_frames"] - r["onset"]) / r["rate"] * 1000
