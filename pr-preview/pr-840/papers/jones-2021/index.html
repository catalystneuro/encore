Summary: Few of the paper's main findings on suppressing PV+ and SST+ interneurons in mouse hippocampus held up in this data. The PV+ rise in CA1 sharp-wave ripple frequency reproduced, and the SST+ rise in fast gamma frequency held in CA3 and DG but not CA1. The opposing effects of the two on ripple rate, the PV+ rises in sharp-wave amplitude and slow gamma power, and the SST+ rise in fast gamma power did not reproduce. As deposited, one cohort has every CNO session after its vehicle sessions, and its empty-vector control mice also change with CNO. Histology, slice recordings and fast ripples are not in the dataset and could not be tested.
