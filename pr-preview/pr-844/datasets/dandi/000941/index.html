Dataset version 0.241029.1405 · tested 2026-10-31 · 8 open findings

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds no kinematic data. The paper's findings are about 22 joint angles computed from 36 markers tracked at 200 frames/s, and no file holds a marker position, a joint angle or any other kinematic series; the files hold trial tables, 16 channels of EMG at 50 Hz, an evaluation mask and multiunit spike times. 23 of the 26 claims are blocked for this reason.
    Found by the attempt on Rouse et al. 2015 (rouse-2015-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Data missingOnly monkey L is in the dandiset. The paper's monkeys X (session X20121219) and Y (Y20130516) are not; the description points to dandiset 001209 for another monkey, monkey X, as a draft with no published version; the claims file does not name it, so it is not used.
    Found by the attempt on Rouse et al. 2015 (rouse-2015-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Selection not documentedThe paper's session L20120928 is in the dandiset only in part. Its held-in-calib file holds 412 trials, all rewarded, numbered 201 to 673 and spanning 53 of the session's 86 minutes, where the paper reports 768 trials and 717 successes. Within 201 to 673, 61 numbers are absent in gaps of 1 to 15; the files do not say which trials were left out, or why (errors, or trials held back for the benchmark's evaluation). The result column, described as success, failure or abort, holds R in every trial of every file.
    Found by the attempt on Rouse et al. 2015 (rouse-2015-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Metadata errortgt_obj, described as the object to grasp at the target, does not name the grasped object. In session 20120928 it takes three values (Sphere, Button, Coaxial) in three runs of 118, 159 and 135 consecutive trials, each value occurring with all four obj_id codes and every location. obj_id has four codes whose locations within each block are offset by multiples of 45 degrees, as the paper's apparatus has them, and the grasped object is decoded from EMG around contact at 0.99 with obj_id against 0.42 with tgt_obj (largest class 0.39).
    Found by the attempt on Rouse et al. 2015 (rouse-2015-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Description missingobj_id, the actual object code, has no names. From the paper's Fig. 1 legend (clockwise order perpendicular cylinder, coaxial cylinder, button, sphere, with 157.5 degrees leftmost) and the locations at which each code occurs, codes 2, 1, 4 and 3 are the perpendicular cylinder, coaxial cylinder, button and sphere; the names fit every constraint the legend states, but the files do not confirm them.
    Found by the attempt on Rouse et al. 2015 (rouse-2015-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Metadata errorIn each of the four held-in-calib files one trial carries the number 127 between trials 254 and 256 or 257, in time order, where 255 (or 256) is expected; 127 is the largest signed 8-bit integer. In session 20120927 a second trial, the first of the file, is also numbered 127, so the number does not identify a trial there.
    Found by the attempt on Rouse et al. 2015 (rouse-2015-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Description missingThe event times move_onset_time, contact_time and reward_time are not defined, and all event times lie on a 20 ms grid. move_onset_time is very likely not the paper's movement onset. In each of the four monkey L sessions of the dandiset the median time from move_onset_time to contact_time (180 to 200 ms) is 52 to 58 ms shorter than the authors' own median for the session (238 to 255 ms, Rouse and Schieber 2016, J Neurophysiol 115, Table 2; 255 ms for 20120928 in the 2015 paper), and the median cue to onset (300 to 320 ms) is longer than the shortest median the authors give for monkey L (243 ms, J Neurosci 2016), while cue to contact agrees; the onset is about 55 ms later than the marker-speed onset, as the task software's center-release event marker would be. reward_time follows contact by a median of 0.10 s (0 to 0.92 s), where the paper's task requires a 1,000 ms hold before the reward. start_time and stop_time are exactly 1 s before the cue and 1 s after reward_time in every trial, a window, not the trial's start and stop.
    Found by the attempt on Rouse et al. 2015 (rouse-2015-01, 2026-10-31)
  • OtherEach held-in-minival file repeats the first two trials of the held-in-calib file of the same session (same trial numbers, event times, EMG timestamps and spike times on all 64 channels), as the description says. The EMG values of the two copies differ by a near-constant factor per file (0.955 to 1.016), so each file's EMG was scaled on its own, and the same recording has two different EMG amplitudes in the dandiset.
    Found by the attempt on Rouse et al. 2015 (rouse-2015-01, 2026-10-31)
