  • fig1g-ka-synchronized-bursting

    After kainic acid, slice S1 shows synchronized rhythmic bursting, with about 6 to 8 bursts grouped within larger burst epochs (superbursts).

    After administering kainic acid to slice S1, synchronized rhythmic bursting activity was observed ... approximately 6–8 bursts grouped within larger burst epochs

    Results, paragraph 1; Figure 1g and 1h

    How it will be judged

    The population rate as the Methods define it (spikes of all units in 1 ms bins, Gaussian smoothing with a standard deviation of 10 to 20 ms, divided by the number of units and multiplied by 1000). Neither the paper nor its code defines a burst or a burst epoch; the Figure 1g legend uses the first 150 s of seizure-like activity. Readings named here, not by the paper; a burst is a peak of the population rate (sigma 10 ms, and 20 ms) exceeding (a) twice its mean over the pre-KA part of the recording, or (b) its mean plus 3 standard deviations over the whole recording, with peaks less than 50 ms apart merged; epochs are runs of bursts separated by gaps under 5 s. The expected value is the range 6 to 8 widened by 10% of each bound (5.4 to 8.8). The sentence cites Figure 1h, the zoom on the initiation of bursting, so the count is read two ways; the median number of bursts per epoch in the first 150 s (Figure 1g), and the number of bursts in the first epoch (Figure 1h). Reproduced when, under one reading, that number lies in the range and the bursts are synchronous across units (a burst raises the rate of most units).
