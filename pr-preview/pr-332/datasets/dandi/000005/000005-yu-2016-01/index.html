    Method differences

    How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

    • The full text was not available; the touch window, the test and the threshold are choices made here, and no number of the paper is compared.vpm-touch
    • The whisking test of the claims file (a Rayleigh test of spike phases) was replaced after the first run, because its null control marked 0.88 of units as modulated with spikes in the wrong trial; the claims file records the amendment and what had been seen. Both versions are shown.vpm-whisking
    • The whisking band (6 to 30 Hz), threshold (2.5 degrees) and the measure are choices made here. The paper judges modulation by the r2 of a sinusoid fit (Supp. Fig. 2e), read after the criteria were written; it is shown as a variant.vpm-whisking
    • FS cells are identified here as cells spiking within 6 ms of isolated 5 ms light pulses in VGAT-ChR2 mice (23 cells, all tagged from the vS1 site or the site labelled VPM and vS1, at 1.6 to 4.6 ms latency). The paper also requires a spike waveform inside a boundary of peak-to-trough ratio and duration (Supp. Fig. 6), not given in numbers, so the tagged cells may include non-FS interneurons.fs-touch-and-whisking
    • The files do not mark ION-transected mice, so their cells are in the group; leaving out the mice flagged as lacking touch input is shown as a variant.fs-touch-and-whisking
    • The whisking test was amended as for vpm-whisking.fs-touch-and-whisking
    • Excitatory cells are identified here as untagged whole-cell cells with action potentials at least 0.6 ms wide at half maximum (22 cells). The paper's excitatory group also holds cell-attached cells (Supplementary Table 2) and leaves out ION-transected mice, which the files do not mark.exc-mainly-touch
    • The touch selectivity index is a choice made here; the paper's measure is not known.exc-mainly-touch
    • The supplement (read after the first run) states that this light drives eNpHR in PV cells; the files do not name the opsin.fs-suppress-movement-spiking
    • 7 whole-cell excitatory cells decide; the paper's PV-Cre excitatory cells are mostly cell-attached, and the juxtacellular cells are shown as a variant.fs-suppress-movement-spiking
    • As for fs-suppress-movement-spiking; the selectivity index is a choice made here, and the touch counts inside the light window are smaller than over whole trials.fs-enhance-touch-selectivity
    • The VPM-site light in VGAT-ChR2 mice is taken as the paper's thalamic photoinhibition, which the supplement describes as a 100 Hz train of 5 ms pulses; the files do not say what the light does, and its wavelength is given as 594 nm.thalamus-drives-fs
