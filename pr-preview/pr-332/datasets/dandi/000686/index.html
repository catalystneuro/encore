    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • The paper's ROI measurements are not deposited. Profiles are measured here from the images with automatically found electrode footprints and the ROI layout of Methods 2.4 (56 ROIs per half diagonal, from 224 along both diagonals, plus one on the centre, ending at 3.65/4.2 of the distance from the centre to the electrode's inner edge, from the +/- 3.65 mm of Methods 2.4 and the 8.4-mm diagonal of Results 3.5). Each ROI is one step (about 65 um) long along the diagonal, so that the ROIs tile it. The paper gives neither the ROI width across the diagonal nor how the inner edge was placed, so six geometries are run, widths of 1, 3 and 5 steps, and the inner edge at the footprint edge measured in the image or at 4.2/(4.2 + 0.75) of the centre-to-centre distance (1.5-mm needles, Methods 2.3). The main tables use width 1 and the measured footprint. The outcome is the same under all six.
    • e1 is not labelled in the files. It is taken as the top-right footprint of the image, which is both the electrode with the brightest outermost ROIs in all 8 non-rotated dishes and the mirror image of the top-left electrode energized in Fig. 3 (an inverted microscope images the dish from below). The two readings agree in every dish.
    • Background for O4 (b) is the median inside the electrode footprints (unexposed monolayer under the needle), an assumption; the paper names no background subtraction. No outcome changes with it.

Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds only the 18 monolayer images of Fig. 5 (40 Packets and 40 Trains, 4 dishes each; 1P x 40 and 1T x 40, 5 dishes each). The single-cell time-lapse data of Fig. 4 and the field simulations of Fig. 3 are not in it and were not found in any other dandiset.
  • OtherThe paper's monolayer data are split over five dandisets (000686 for Fig. 5, 000875, 001025, 001027 and 001028 for Figs. 6 to 9), each naming the paper, but none names the others and the paper names none of them, so a reader of one cannot find the rest.
  • OtherThe files hold the stitched images only, not the ROI measurements (positions and intensities) that the paper's profiles plot, so the figures can be rebuilt only by placing ROIs anew.
  • Metadata errorThe species is NCBITaxon_9913 (Bos taurus) in every file and in the dandiset summary, where the paper used CHO-K1 cells (Chinese hamster, Cricetulus griseus); the strain field does say "cellline CHO". The subject age is P1D, which has no meaning for a cell line.
  • Metadata errorThe dandiset's assets summary gives 36 subjects, but the 18 files have 18 distinct subject ids, one per dish.
  • Metadata errorThe image description says the 144 tiles were captured "from a six-well plate", where the paper (Methods 2.1) seeded the monolayers in 35-mm glass-bottom dishes, one dish per experiment.
  • Description missingThe image has no pixel size (resolution -1, unit n.a.). The tile geometry (22323 pixels from 12 tiles with about 10% overlap of 2048-pixel ORCA-Flash4 frames under a 10x objective) is consistent with 0.65 um per pixel; at that scale the footprint centres are 9.7 to 10.1 mm apart across the diagonal and their inner edges 8.4 to 8.8 mm, consistent with the paper's 8.4-mm diagonal if that is measured between inner edges. The footprints are 1.15 to 1.44 mm across, against the 1.5-mm needles of Methods 2.3.
  • Description missingThe electrode footprints are not labelled, so which one is e1 (needed for Fig. 5A) is not stated. In all 8 non-rotated dishes the top-right footprint of the image has the brightest outermost ROIs, consistent with e1 there.
  • Methods not availableThe paper does not say how the centre value of a profile is taken, the ROI at distance 0 or the LOESS fit there. With ROIs one step wide, the 1P x 40 centre exceeds its trough (Fig. 5B) with the LOESS value (p = 0.0049 to 0.033) but not with the single centre ROI (p = 0.064 to 0.114). Which was used, and with what span?
  • Methods not availableFor comparisons inside the same dishes (centre against periphery, Fig. 5B), the paper does not say whether its two-sided Student's t-test is paired or unpaired. For 1T x 40 the unpaired test gives p = 0.015 to 0.035 under every reading and the paired one up to p = 0.09. Which test was used?
  • Methods not availableFor normalized profiles averaged over both diagonals (Fig. 5C), the paper does not say whether "the most peripheral ROI" is the mean of the outermost ROIs at both ends or one end, nor whether each dish is normalized before averaging. Which ROI is taken as 100%, and is each dish normalized first?
  • Methods not availableThe paper gives neither the size of its ROIs across the diagonal, nor how close to the electrode the outermost ROI sits, nor any background subtraction for the monolayer images. Background changes no outcome here. ROI width decides fig5c-normalized-contrast, whose test holds under every reading with ROIs 3 steps (about 195 um) wide and fails in 5 of 32 readings (p = 0.051 to 0.082) with ROIs 1 step (about 65 um) wide; it also moves the Fig. 5A trains against packets comparison near e1 from p = 0.083 (1 step) to 0.015 to 0.024 (3 and 5 steps). What ROI size, placement and background were used?

Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • Profiles measured from the images as in fig5a-trains-stronger (six ROI geometries).fig5b-rotation-center-peak fig5c-normalized-contrast
  • The paper's ROI measurements are not deposited. Profiles are measured here from the images with automatically found electrode footprints and the ROI layout of Methods 2.4 (56 ROIs per half diagonal, from 224 along both diagonals, plus one on the centre, ending at 3.65/4.2 of the distance from the centre to the electrode's inner edge, from the +/- 3.65 mm of Methods 2.4 and the 8.4-mm diagonal of Results 3.5). Each ROI is one step (about 65 um) long along the diagonal, so that the ROIs tile it. The paper gives neither the ROI width across the diagonal nor how the inner edge was placed, so six geometries are run, widths of 1, 3 and 5 steps, and the inner edge at the footprint edge measured in the image or at 4.2/(4.2 + 0.75) of the centre-to-centre distance (1.5-mm needles, Methods 2.3). The main tables use width 1 and the measured footprint. The outcome is the same under all six.fig5a-trains-stronger
  • e1 is not labelled in the files. It is taken as the top-right footprint of the image, which is both the electrode with the brightest outermost ROIs in all 8 non-rotated dishes and the mirror image of the top-left electrode energized in Fig. 3 (an inverted microscope images the dish from below). The two readings agree in every dish.fig5a-trains-stronger
  • Background for O4 (b) is the median inside the electrode footprints (unexposed monolayer under the needle), an assumption; the paper names no background subtraction. No outcome changes with it.fig5a-trains-stronger
  • Profiles measured from the images as in fig5a-trains-stronger (ROI layout and the six geometries, e1, background).fig5a-packet-center-enhanced
  • The trough is the lowest point of the group's mean profile (ROI values, or the mean of the dishes' LOESS fits under O1 b) strictly between the centre and the e1 end; each dish's value at that ROI enters the test. The claims file says "the lowest value between the center and the e1 end" without saying whether per dish or on the mean; the mean profile avoids picking each dish's noise minimum. Choosing it on the dishes it is tested on favours centre > trough, so a variant finds each dish's trough on the mean of the other dishes (leave one out).fig5a-packet-center-enhanced
  • Reading of the criterion. Both the paired and the unpaired test of O6 must hold; see the notes for the outcome under the other reading.fig5b-rotation-center-peak
  • The positive end of the averaged profile joins the e1 end and the e2 end, e2 taken as the footprint next to e1 clockwise in the image; with rotation every electrode starts the same number of packets, so O2 (b) is read here as the outermost ROI at that joined end (e1 and e2), a labelling choice. It gives the same outcome as O2 (a).fig5b-rotation-center-peak
  • Trough as in fig5a-packet-center-enhanced (group mean profile, with a leave-one-out variant).fig5b-rotation-center-peak
  • Assumption. ROIs one step wide. Methods 2.4 says only that the measurements used "92 or 224 regions of interest (ROI), placed along one or both diagonals", which fixes the ROI length along the diagonal (2 x 3.65 mm / 112, about 65 um) but not its width.
