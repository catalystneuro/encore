  • Data missingThe imaging files hold traces, footprints, the raw movie and a link to the behaviour video, but no pose estimates, cup interactions, position, habituation and test epochs, or record of which cup held the juvenile, which every in vivo finding needs. The SLEAP model is not published. None of the 8 in vivo findings can be tested.
    Found by the attempt on Lee et al. 2025 (lee-2025-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Selection not documentedThe imaging files hold 869, 913 and 854 ROIs for the ChrimsonR mice in GH off, GH on and SI off, about twice the paper's 429, 441 and 484 cells, and 35, 138 and 89 for the TdTomato mice against 39, 61 and 76. Nothing marks which ROIs the paper kept (the Methods say non-neuronal objects were excluded by hand).
    Found by the attempt on Lee et al. 2025 (lee-2025-01, 2026-11-01)

Their 19,211 frames last 768 s at 25 Hz, but the behaviour videos last 961 s (median); at the Methods' 20 frames/s the imaging lasts 961 s, within 4 s of the video in 40 of 41 sessions (the video of C386, SI off, lasts 800 s). The data support 20 Hz.
    Found by the attempt on Lee et al. 2025 (lee-2025-01, 2026-11-01)

    Found by the attempt on Lee et al. 2025 (lee-2025-01, 2026-11-01)

The light time had to be read from the responses, and protocols with other sweep lengths put the light at other times.
