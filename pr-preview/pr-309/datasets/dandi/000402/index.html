Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Selection not documentedThe dandiset holds all 19 completed scans, while the paper describes 14 (Extended Data Table 1). Nothing in the files or the metadata marks which 14, so its counts (for example 125,413 masks) can be matched only after reading the table's image. The five other files are 4-9, 4-10, 5-3, 8-7 and 8-9.
  • Data missingThe files hold fluorescence traces only. The deconvolved traces that the paper's analyses use, and that the paper says can be read from the NWB files, are not in them; nor are the oracle scores.
  • Data missingThe structural stack, the registration of each field into it, and the stack coordinates of the masks are not in the dataset, so masks imaged in more than one scan cannot be grouped and the z drift cannot be measured.
  • Description missingNo mask or field has a visual area; every imaging plane's location is the same list of four areas. The area masks the Methods transform into the stack are not in the dataset.
  • Identifier missingThe EM columns (cave_ids, pt_root_id, pt_supervoxel_id and positions) exist only in one of the two fields at each depth (52 of the 104 fields of the paper's 14 scans) and in none of the fields of files 4-9, 4-10, 8-7 and 8-9.
  • Description missingThe Monet2 table gives num_directions but not the direction or onset of each period, which Fig. 3g needs. The directions can be measured from the stimulus video in the files, as this attempt did.
  • Metadata errorThe imaging_rate of every imaging plane is NaN (the rate is only in the field description), and the subject age is the range P75D/P81D in every file, though each scan was recorded on one day.
  • OtherIn scan 4-7, one of the paper's 14, the eye tracking ends 968 s before the last imaging frame; in files 4-10, 5-3 and 8-7 it ends 54 to 76 minutes early, and in 4-9 and 8-9 3.9 and 6.2 s early. Several files have missing (NaN) time stamps in the treadmill and eye series (up to 190).
  • OtherTrippy seed 6831 is shown in both scans 6-4 and 7-5, while the Methods say the 10 seeds that are not conserved are unique to each scan.
  • OtherAll fields of a scan share one array of frame time stamps, though the fields are imaged one after another within each frame; the per-field offset is not recorded.
  • OtherThe EM imagery of subvolume 65 on BossDB holds data up to section 27,882, while the paper gives the processed region as sections 7,931 to 27,904. The imagery of the two subvolumes overlaps from section 14,825 to 14,847, and the metadata give the bounds of each subvolume only in whole chunks of 64 sections (7,904 to 14,847 and 14,816 to 27,903).
  • File format errorpt_root_id and pt_supervoxel_id are stored as 64-bit floats, which hold integers exactly only up to 2**53; root ids near 8.65e17 are spaced by 128 in float64, so ids that differ in their last digits can collide. Of 538 pairs of masks in different scans that share a root id, 50 have EM cell body points more than 10 um apart.

Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • Which 14 of the 19 files are the paper's scans was taken from Extended Data Table 1 (Methods, 2P functional imaging, "Of 19 completed scans over 6 days of imaging, 14 are described here (Extended Data Table 1)"). The text the claims file was read from holds only the table's caption; its cells were read from the table's image in PMC11981939 (41586_2025_8790_Tab1_ESM.jpg). They are 4-7, 5-6, 5-7, 6-2, 6-4, 6-6, 6-7, 7-3, 7-4, 7-5, 8-5, 9-3, 9-4 and 9-6.scans-14-p75-p81
  • The table gives no direction or onset per period, so both were measured from the stimulus video in each file; a method of this attempt, not the paper's. Boundaries come from phase correlation between successive frames, and each period's direction from the global optical flow over the period (gradient method of Lucas and Kanade, frames smoothed by a 1 pixel Gaussian). The boundary detection, the 10 degree tolerance for distinct directions, the smoothing and the 0.1 s left out at each end of a period are standard choices, stated in the helper.monet2-directions
  • Fluorescence traces replace the deconvolved traces of the paper, which are not in the dataset.fig3f-oracle-reliability
  • Frames are taken from each field's time stamps from the onset of each repeat; the per-unit delay within a frame that the paper's code adds is not in the dataset and is not applied. All fields of a scan share one array of time stamps in the files.fig3f-oracle-reliability
  • Fluorescence replaces the deconvolved traces, which are not in the dataset.fig3g-direction-tuning
  • The direction of each period comes from the video (see monet2-directions), snapped to the 22.5 degree grid.fig3g-direction-tuning
  • The 80 units shown are ranked by the preferred response minus the mean over directions, divided by the standard deviation over directions, in place of the claims file's ratio, which is not defined for fluorescence with its arbitrary offset. This choice only picks the units shown.fig3g-direction-tuning
  • The first and last sections with data are read from the imagery at 64 nm per pixel on a grid of every 8th chunk in x and y (2,288 chunks a layer), a choice of this attempt; the release gives no list of processed sections.em-sections
  • The paper names no materialization version. Subvolume 65 is counted on the export of version 1181, the version the paper's code reads; subvolume 35 on its only public table, assigned.csv.gz (2021).synapse-detection
  • The tables are the 2021 releases on BossDB, not the CAVE tables the claims file names; the paper gives no version.cell-classification
  • The SVM table holds 27,698 rows that are not nuclei of nucleus_detection_v0 (27,692 called non-neurons); the count is restricted to subvolume 65's nuclei, as the paper's is.cell-classification
