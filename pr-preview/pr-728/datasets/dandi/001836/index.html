Dataset version 0.261030.0428 · tested 2026-11-05 · 8 open findings

  • Data missingThe decoder output behind Figures 4, 5 and S3 to S11 (the posterior per 2 ms bin over whole sessions, the continuous and fragmented state probabilities, the detected remote and local extended events) is not deposited, nor are the spike marks or MUA times. The triggered tables hold only the MAP position in windows around COM times and outer pokes. The rest would have to be recomputed from the raw voltage, 12.5 TB of raw files.
    Found by the attempt on Gu et al. 2026 (gu-2026-01, 2026-11-05)
  • Description missingNone of the 2415 Spyglass analysis files (`obj-*.nwb`) says what it holds; the file names carry only an object id, and their tables have empty descriptions. By content they are head position, velocity and orientation (750), linearized position at about 23 Hz and at 500 Hz (446), and decodes triggered on events (1219). The triggered tables could be read only with the paper's code and by aligning their trigger times with COM times and outer pokes: 720 are triggered at COM times, 353 at outer pokes, and 146 at times that match neither. The parameters that made each (trigger, window, restriction to the arm, MAP or mean, which decoder) are in the lab's database, not the files; the whole-track tables come in pairs with different decodes, and which decoder made each can be told only from which member agrees with the arm-restricted tables, not named. The track graph of the linearization is not deposited.
    Found by the attempt on Gu et al. 2026 (gu-2026-01, 2026-11-05)
  • Data missingThe dandiset holds fewer trials than the paper reports for three rats. Built from the poke and pump events, it holds 2083, 3519, 4578, 2959 and 1816 trials for rats M, E, J, K and L, against 2370, 3554, 4628, 3779 and 2087 in the Figure 1E inset (0.88, 0.99, 0.99, 0.78 and 0.87 of them). Rat M has 5 days against the 6 the paper says were collected; rat J has 12 days against about 11 in Figure 1C. Rat M's 5 days of about 400 trials would account for its shortfall; rats K and L have the days of Figure 1C, and no epoch is tagged other than Session or Sleep, so where their trials are missing is not known.
    Found by the attempt on Gu et al. 2026 (gu-2026-01, 2026-11-05)
  • OtherRat M's 500 Hz linearizations are each deposited twice, in 26 pairs of files with identical values, one pair per run epoch. They are counted once.
    Found by the attempt on Gu et al. 2026 (gu-2026-01, 2026-11-05)
  • Metadata errorRat M's Statescript logs are named "syayescript Seq2Session1" and so on, a misspelling of "statescript", and hold no trial or reward counts, unlike the other rats' logs. Rat M's days also have 4 LFP files for 5 days.
    Found by the attempt on Gu et al. 2026 (gu-2026-01, 2026-11-05)
  • Metadata errorThe head orientation series (`scratch/CompassDirection/head_orientation`) gives its unit as meters.
    Found by the attempt on Gu et al. 2026 (gu-2026-01, 2026-11-05)
  • Description missingThe electrode table of the LFP files has no locations, so the CA1 tetrodes and the corpus callosum reference used for ripple detection (Methods, SWR detection) cannot be identified; the reference named by `ref_elect_id` is in the LFP file on some days only (never for rat K).
    Found by the attempt on Gu et al. 2026 (gu-2026-01, 2026-11-05)
  • Metadata errorThe LFP series (`scratch/filtered data`) gives its unit as volts with a conversion of 1.0, while the data are int16 values with a standard deviation of about 240.
    Found by the attempt on Gu et al. 2026 (gu-2026-01, 2026-11-05)
