Dataset unversioned files (the archive keeps no versions) · tested 2026-11-07 · 10 open findings

  • Data missingThe data set holds three of the paper's four rats (EE, FF, GG; the paper's rat H is absent). Its trial tables list correct trials only; error trials can be found only from the nose-poke sensor channels in each session's raw .eeg file (read here for EE188). The layer 5 session of Figure 2a and Supplementary Figure 11 (142 cells) is not in it; the FF sessions hold 40 to 64 cells.
    Found by the attempt on Fujisawa et al. 2008 (fujisawa-2008-02, 2026-11-07)
  • Metadata errorThe data description names EE188_example.mat as a file of folder EE0708; it is a member of EE0708/EE.188.tar.gz, about 2 GB into the gzip stream, and the folder holds EE188_Behavior.mat, EE188_Behavior_PFC.mat and EE188_Behavior_CA1.mat instead.
    Found by the attempt on Fujisawa et al. 2008 (fujisawa-2008-02, 2026-11-07)
  • Description missingThe CellsInfo type column is described as pyramidal (1), interneuron (2) or unknown (3). In the 12 sessions of behaviors.zip and in EE188_Behavior_PFC.mat no cell is unknown (87 pyramidal and 18 interneurons in EE188), while EE188_example.mat of the same session has 33, 15 and 69. How either classification was made is not documented.
    Found by the attempt on Fujisawa et al. 2008 (fujisawa-2008-02, 2026-11-07)
  • Selection not documentedEach session's files keep a selection of the sorted clusters (627 cells of 1,495 clusters listed in spikeph); the description says only that Cells are neurons whose maximum rate in the trials exceeds 1 Hz. EE188_Behavior_PFC.mat holds 105 of the 117 cells of EE188_example.mat.
    Found by the attempt on Fujisawa et al. 2008 (fujisawa-2008-02, 2026-11-07)
  • Description missingThe layer of each rat's probe is not given in the data set; it can be taken only from the paper's Supplementary Figure 1, by assuming that rats EE, FF and GG are the paper's E, F and G.
    Found by the attempt on Fujisawa et al. 2008 (fujisawa-2008-02, 2026-11-07)
  • Metadata errorThe spikes' trial labels (spikeLtr, spikeRtr) and positions (spikeX) start at the first nose-poke of each trial, while the description defines the trial start as the end of the last nose-poke (SessionNP column 2). Spikes during the nose-poke, which lasts up to 315 s, are placed near position 0.
    Found by the attempt on Fujisawa et al. 2008 (fujisawa-2008-02, 2026-11-07)
  • Description missingThe pairs_all list of EE188_example.mat (43 connections) is described as the cell pairs with monosynaptic connections, but it holds fewer connections than the paper reports for that session (48 and 30 in Figure 3b) and its largest component is the 39-cell circuit of Figure 3d; which selection it is, is not said.
    Found by the attempt on Fujisawa et al. 2008 (fujisawa-2008-02, 2026-11-07)
  • OtherIn 10 of the 13 sessions the second LED is not tracked (zeros in the position file), so head direction can be computed only in EE152, EE165 and EE188.
    Found by the attempt on Fujisawa et al. 2008 (fujisawa-2008-02, 2026-11-07)
  • Selection not documentedThe trial table of EE188 (SessionNP) leaves out 3 trials that the nose-poke sensor channels and the position file show as correct (first pokes at 822.9, 2245.1 and 2370.7 s), and 1 error trial (2687.2 s). In two of the three (822.9 and 2370.7 s) the rat reaches the arm 193.4 and 176.9 s after its last poke, longer than any table trial, so they may be left out for that reason; why any are left out is not said.
    Found by the attempt on Fujisawa et al. 2008 (fujisawa-2008-02, 2026-11-07)
  • Description missingThe data description defines Cells as the units whose maximum rate in the trials exceeds 1 Hz, but the files hold no Cells variable. By the largest rate over the tenths of the maze, 0 to 10 cells per session of CellsInfo are below 1 Hz, so which selection CellsInfo is cannot be confirmed.
    Found by the attempt on Fujisawa et al. 2008 (fujisawa-2008-02, 2026-11-07)
