    The theta-repeated cross-correlograms of place cell pairs had a period of about 85 ms before cooling, which lengthened during MS cooling (P = 0.002), while the period of the phase cross-correlograms was unaffected (P = 0.32).

    Duration of the theta-repeated spike cross-correlograms (as in d), calculated for each session, significantly increased during MS cooling ... whereas the spike phase-lag correlations remained unaffected (n.s., P = 0.32).

    Figure 5D, 5E and 5F

    What the paper reports

    • period of the time cross-correlograms, cooling against control: two-sided signed rank test, p = 0.002
    • period of the phase cross-correlograms, cooling against control: p = 0.32

    Over 9 sessions the period lengthens from 109.2 to 114.0 ms (8 of 9, P = 0.0195) and the phase period does not change (1.740 and 1.760 pi rad, P = 0.59); the control period is not 85 +- 8.5 ms. The criterion's estimator takes the largest neighbouring peak 60 to 200 ms from the main peak, and in 47.7% of pre pairs that peak is more than 1.25 times as far as the nearest one, so it often picks the second neighbour and the 109.2 ms is biased upward. With the nearest neighbouring peak (a variant) the control period is 91.0 ms, within tolerance, and it lengthens to 95.5 ms (7 of 9, P = 0.031); pairs with |Ltr| below 10 cm give 91.0 ms. Under that variant the phase period, also from the nearest peak, does not change (1.285 pi rad in both, P = 0.367), so all three parts of the criterion hold; but that estimator is biased downward, as 211 of 3499 pre pairs give 60 to 70 ms, at the floor of the search window and below any plausible theta period, so its 91.0 ms is not a clean test of 85 ms either. The outcome follows the criterion's estimator. Both control periods are shorter than the LFP theta period (115.7 ms).

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • The period is measured per pair and summarised per session; the paper reads it from the sum over pairs. The criterion was changed to this before the analysis across sessions, after a pilot session showed that the summed correlogram's spectrum follows its envelope; the summed correlogram is shown as a variant (99 to 200 ms).
    • 9 sessions in 4 rats, against 18 in 5.
    • The criterion's period estimator often takes the second neighbouring peak; the nearest-peak estimator is shown as a variant.

Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Metadata errorThe temperature series of the nine MS10 sessions and of the two control sessions of 2018-07-19 (MS21 and MS22) is not a recording. The paper's code lists no temperature channel for these sessions. In the files ten of them run from 17.0 to 37.0 C (exactly in nine; MS10-170313 reaches 16.98 C) and sit at exactly 37.0 C for 16% to 35% of samples, and MS10-170307 is a constant 37.0 C, while no recorded trace repeats its maximum. Nothing in the files says the series is modelled.
  • Metadata errorThe two sessions of 2018-07-19, which the paper's code lists as controls without cooling (cooling_session 0, no onsets), have trials labelled Pre-Cooling, Cooling on, Cooling off and Post-Cooling and a temperature dip; their theta frequency during 'Cooling on' differs from pre by -0.20 and +0.44 Hz, against a median of -0.82 Hz in the cooled sessions.
  • Data missingSix of the paper's 53 behavioural sessions are not in the dandiset (MS12-170712, MS12-170715, MS13-171204, MS13-171205, MS13-171206, MS21-180806); four of them were in version 0.210812.1514 as files without behaviour.
  • Data missingMS21-180627 has trials without the cooling state and MS21-180712 has no trials, so two more of the paper's sessions cannot be used for the cooling comparisons.
  • Description missingThe files hold no cell types, no place field definitions and no electrode locations (every location is 'unknown'), so the putative pyramidal cells and interneurons and the place fields of the paper have to be made again.
  • Description missingThe trial state 'Cooling off' (a median 14 trials per session between cooling and recovery) is not described in the files or the paper.
  • OtherIn seven sessions (the six MS10 sessions with units and MS12-170723) every unit has quality 'unsorted', so no curated unit can be selected; MS10 has no usable units.
  • OtherIn MS21-180621 and MS22-180621 the units carry no spatial information (0.23 and 0.12 bits/spike, the same as with spikes shifted by 60 s), although they are locked to theta, so the clock of spikes and position could not be established there.
  • Selection not documentedThe dandiset holds three sessions that are not in the paper's behavioural analysis (the two 2018-07-19 controls and MS10-170307, whose temperature is constant) without saying so; they carry the same cooling labels as the analysed sessions.
  • OtherThe first published version (0.210812.1514) has no trials table in its files, so the cooling states and choice errors are only in the later versions.
  • OtherThe Methods give position tracking at 120 Hz; the MS10 sessions are sampled at 40 Hz (six), 30 Hz (one) and 120 Hz (two).
