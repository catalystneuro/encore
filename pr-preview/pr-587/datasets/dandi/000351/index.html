Dataset version 0.260828.1905 · tested 2026-10-30 · 6 open findings

  • Metadata errorEvent times (acquisition/eventlog/eventtime) are stated in milliseconds but are in seconds. The random reward sessions have a mean inter-reward interval of about 12 in the files' units (Methods, 12 s), and the Fig S8L sessions named 6, 12 and 30 have means near 6, 12 and 30.
    Found by the attempt on Jeong et al. 2022 (jeong-2022-01, 2026-10-30)
  • Data missingHJ-FP-M4 Day38, a start day listed in cumsum_pavlovian.m and cumsum_bgdrw_vs_extinction.m and that animal's first background reward session, is not in the dandiset. Six other integer days inside the day ranges of the paper's analysis code have no session either (HJ-FP-M2 Day23, M3 Day16 and Day31, M4 Day30, F2 Day16, M6 Day49). The paper's mean session counts for Experiments 2, 5 and 4 (13.3, 8.1, 2.6) equal the counts that include all seven days, while the dandiset gives 12.7, 7.9 and 2.4, which suggests that these are missing sessions too.
    Found by the attempt on Jeong et al. 2022 (jeong-2022-01, 2026-10-30)
  • Metadata errorIn 65 of the 101 Experiment 7 files the raw photometry (acquisition/photometry) has about 12 million samples per stated second and its timestamps end at 0.43 to 4.18, while the sessions last 600 to 4100 s; the timestamps are in thousands of seconds (12 kHz detection).
    Found by the attempt on Jeong et al. 2022 (jeong-2022-01, 2026-10-30)
  • Metadata errorSubject HJ-FP-datHT-stGtACR-M7, a DAT-Cre heterozygote by its name and in the paper's code, has genotype wt in its files.
    Found by the attempt on Jeong et al. 2022 (jeong-2022-01, 2026-10-30)
  • Description missingThe experimental condition of each session (Experiments 2 to 5) is not stated; only the first session of a condition carries a suffix in its name (8s, 8s-bgd6s, extinction), and the files of datHT-M6 and datHT-M7 do not name the task. The sessions used for each figure are given only in the analysis code's day lists and in a Google spreadsheet named in the description, which has no fixed version.
    Found by the attempt on Jeong et al. 2022 (jeong-2022-01, 2026-10-30)
  • OtherIn 13 sessions dF/F does not cover the whole event log by more than 1 s; HJ-FP-WT-stGtACR-M2 Day6 has no dF/F for its first 1117 s, and M6 Day22 and M7 Day43 lose their last 175 to 187 s. In Experiments 1 to 6 the first 20 or so dF/F timestamps of each session are NaN.
    Found by the attempt on Jeong et al. 2022 (jeong-2022-01, 2026-10-30)
