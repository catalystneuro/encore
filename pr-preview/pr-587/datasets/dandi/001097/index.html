Dataset version 0.260624.0330 · tested 2026-10-14 · 10 open findings

  • Data missingNo rSLDS fits or latent factors are deposited apart from the per-cell weights of two mice, though the dandiset's description says it contains low-dimensional latent factors from dynamical models. The per-mouse files hold under analysis only a table of two weight columns, and under processing one trace series and the annotations; there are no latent time series or dynamics matrices. The paper's central line-attractor results can only be checked by refitting.
    Found by the attempt on Liu et al. 2024 (liu-2024-01, 2026-10-14)
  • Data missingThe sessions with optogenetic inhibition of VMHvl during imaging (Figure 3h-k, Extended Data Figure 5) are not in the dandiset.
    Found by the attempt on Liu et al. 2024 (liu-2024-01, 2026-10-14)
  • Metadata errorThe per-mouse file sub-m542/sub-m542_behavior.nwb is labelled m542 but its neural data are those of mouse 543 (72 cells and 10,527 frames, matching 543's receptive day cell by cell with a median correlation of 0.80); m542's own sessions have 129 cells.
    Found by the attempt on Liu et al. 2024 (liu-2024-01, 2026-10-14)
  • Identifier missingOne mouse is labelled 543 in its natural-cycle files and m543 in its receptive session.
    Found by the attempt on Liu et al. 2024 (liu-2024-01, 2026-10-14)
  • Metadata errorThe same recording is filed under two experiments five times. m764's natural-cycle day 1 has the same traces and annotations as its primed ovariectomy session; m776's primed session has the same traces as its male-inhibition session, with different annotations; and day 3 of the ovariectomy series of m438, m541 and m551 has the same traces as the mouse's primed session, with annotations that differ in m541 and m551 and an interaction of 290.7 against 274.6 s in m438.
    Found by the attempt on Liu et al. 2024 (liu-2024-01, 2026-10-14)
  • OtherThe traces in the two per-mouse files, which carry the rSLDS weights, were processed differently from the session files of the same recordings (same-index median correlation 0.86 and 0.80, rising with smoothing of the session traces), and the files do not say how. The cell order matches the session files.
    Found by the attempt on Liu et al. 2024 (liu-2024-01, 2026-10-14)
  • OtherWhich natural-cycle days were receptive is not recorded, and the female's behaviour is not annotated on most of those days, so the paper's definition of a receptive day cannot be applied. The receptive session of each mouse is deposited separately, without a link to the day it repeats.
    Found by the attempt on Liu et al. 2024 (liu-2024-01, 2026-10-14)
  • Selection not documentedThe dandiset holds more animals or fewer trials than several analyses use, without saying which were used; 7 natural-cycle mice where Figure 5c has 4, 8 male-inhibition mice where Figure 4d has 4, 42 oestrogen-injected trials where Figure 5k has 50, and 69 annotated trials from 23 mice where Figure 1 has 74 from 28.
    Found by the attempt on Liu et al. 2024 (liu-2024-01, 2026-10-14)
  • OtherIn 17 sessions imaging continues for up to 7 minutes after the last annotation, which may be the pencil-cup period of the Methods, but nothing marks it. The labels other and eat, and the two names for the male-inhibition epoch (laser_on and mating_inhibition), are not described.
    Found by the attempt on Liu et al. 2024 (liu-2024-01, 2026-10-14)
  • Metadata errorEvery session file describes a two-photon microscope with 920-nm excitation, where the paper used a one-photon miniscope; the raw traces are labelled lumens but are arbitrary units with negative values; the traces described as z-scored are not z-scored per file (per-cell means -1.6 to 2.4); experimenter, lab, institution and session start time are placeholders; image masks are 1 by 1 pixel; and the file names say ecephys for imaging data. The per-mouse files describe their session as a resident-intruder assay.
    Found by the attempt on Liu et al. 2024 (liu-2024-01, 2026-10-14)
