Dataset unversioned files (the archive keeps no versions) · tested 2026-11-12 · 6 open findings

  • Data missingThe data set holds 53 neurons, not the 58 the paper analysed. The spreadsheet V1_Vasarely_data_SMC.xlsx lists 58 cells, five of them (050800J1, 062000J1, 072100_C, 062600A2, 09250A1) with no condition marked and no files. Per condition the files hold 22 Square and Edge, 27 Star and 26 Corners recordings, against the paper's 25, 30 and 28.
    Found by the attempt on Martinez Conde et al. 2019 (martinez-conde-2019-01, 2026-11-12)
  • Metadata errorTwo files in the Star folder, ABS042700J2 and ABS051000J3, are recordings with a Corners-type stimulus (their stimulus metadata gives the type CORNERS, triangles 45 DU wide and 60 DU deep), and the spreadsheet marks both cells as tested with the Star. They are not copies of the same cells' Corners files (pixel correlation 0.83 and 0.72).
    Found by the attempt on Martinez Conde et al. 2019 (martinez-conde-2019-01, 2026-11-12)
  • Metadata errorThe data descriptor says the spike rate map file holds image (raw spike counts), smoothed_image and smoothed_image_norm (normalised by eye time). In the files, image is the spike rate per 1 ms eye-time sample (times the eye time it is an integer count, and 98.5% of the pixels with eye time hold values strictly between 0 and 1, in the one file with an eye time map); smoothed_image_norm is absent from 39 of 77 files, and in the 38 that hold it it is smoothed_image minus the file's BaseActivity in 5 (among them the one file with an eye time map, so not normalised by eye time), a linear rescaling of smoothed_image in 21, not a linear function of it in 11 (26% to 77% of the pixels at exactly -1), and non-finite in 1; and most files hold a Standard struct and a norm flag that the descriptor does not mention.
    Found by the attempt on Martinez Conde et al. 2019 (martinez-conde-2019-01, 2026-11-12)
  • Description missingThe maps are in gaze coordinates. The receptive-field offset that places them on the stimulus is in no file; the authors' aligned maps (the undocumented Standard struct) are present for all Corners and Square and Edge files but only 12 of 27 Star files, so 15 Star recordings cannot be analysed. The offset differs between conditions of the same neuron by up to about 50 DU, so it cannot be carried across. Standard also holds BaseActivity in 38 of 60 files, with no description of how it was computed; it comes closest to squares centred on the corners of the circumscribing square for Square and Edge (median difference 0.46 spikes/s) and to squares inside them for the Star (0.39), matches neither exactly, and matches no Corners placement tried closely.
    Found by the attempt on Martinez Conde et al. 2019 (martinez-conde-2019-01, 2026-11-12)
  • Data missingNo file holds spike times, eye positions or spike count maps, and only one (ABC050900J1A) holds an eye time map, so the spikes per condition, the latency analysis of S3 Fig and any map rebuilt from the recordings cannot be computed.
    Found by the attempt on Martinez Conde et al. 2019 (martinez-conde-2019-01, 2026-11-12)
  • OtherIn four Square and Edge files (AEC052900J1, AEC070500J2, AEC09010J1C, AEC09040J3B) the 100% end of the Edge lies beyond the screen border, where the Methods say the rectangle was sometimes partly off the display "but never its edge".
    Found by the attempt on Martinez Conde et al. 2019 (martinez-conde-2019-01, 2026-11-12)
