  • zero-mg-isolated-bursts

    How it will be judged

    A count of slices, exact. The paper gives no burst detector. The detector is taken from the Methods' propagation section, population rate of all units in 100 ms bins smoothed with a Gaussian kernel (sigma 5 bins) and peaks found with scipy find_peaks; the peak threshold is open.
