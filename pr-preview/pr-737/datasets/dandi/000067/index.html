Dataset version 0.250624.0419 · tested 2026-10-16 · 9 open findings

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds three rats (EE, FF, GG); the paper recorded four. Of the 13 working memory sessions in the CRCNS session table, EE.152, EE.165 and EE.188 are not in the dandiset, and EE.210 has no trial table or position. EE.188 is the paper's example session (Figures 2a, 3b to 3d, 4 and 5).
    Found by the attempt on Fujisawa et al. 2008 (fujisawa-2008-01, 2026-10-16)
  • Description missingThe units table holds spike times only. It has no channel, shank, brain region, cell type or quality label, and mixes prefrontal units, CA1 units, multi-unit clusters (cluster 1 of each shank, kept by the conversion) and clusters of the sensor signals. Units can be placed only from their waveforms in the wideband signal, which EE.042 and the ten sessions of 2008-06-27 (EE.080 to EE.089, among them the working memory session EE.088) do not have.
    Found by the attempt on Fujisawa et al. 2008 (fujisawa-2008-01, 2026-10-16)
  • Description missingElectrode locations are "unknown", coordinates are missing and impedances are -1. The electrode groups "shank1" to "shank13" are cuts of eight consecutive channels, whatever the probe, so they do not identify the prefrontal shanks, the CA1 shanks or the sensor channels. The layer recorded in each rat is not stated.
    Found by the attempt on Fujisawa et al. 2008 (fujisawa-2008-01, 2026-10-16)
  • Metadata errorIn rat GG the channel order differs from the CRCNS description. The nose-poke sensors are on channels 65 and 66 (1-based) and the CA1 probe on 70 to 101, where the description gives 97 and 98 and 65 to 96. The sensor on 66 is active on trials labelled Right, the reverse of the order in rats EE and FF, so either the sensors or the trial side labels of rat GG are swapped.
    Found by the attempt on Fujisawa et al. 2008 (fujisawa-2008-01, 2026-10-16)
  • Metadata errorThe trial table's stop_time is the end of the last nose poke, the paper's trial start, and reward_time is the paper's trial end; the column left_or_right carries the description of reward_time ("Time when subject began consuming reward."). Only correct trials are listed, which the files do not say.
    Found by the attempt on Fujisawa et al. 2008 (fujisawa-2008-01, 2026-10-16)
  • Metadata errorThe two "(x,y) coordinates" series SpatialSeries1 and SpatialSeries2 are the x coordinates of the two head LEDs, in pixels although stated in metres, and the second is zero in every file; untracked frames are -1. The y coordinates are not in the dandiset. The linearized position is stated in metres but runs from 1 (trial start) to 2 (reward), with 0 outside trials.
    Found by the attempt on Fujisawa et al. 2008 (fujisawa-2008-01, 2026-10-16)
  • File format errorIn six files (EE.049, EE.088, EE.196, FF.103, FF.130, GG.069) the spike times of unit 1 are not in ascending order. In EE.049 and FF.103, examined in detail, they are two sorted runs one after the other; in the five analysed files the unit is a multi-unit cluster.
    Found by the attempt on Fujisawa et al. 2008 (fujisawa-2008-01, 2026-10-16)
  • OtherGG.063, a spontaneous alternation session in the CRCNS table, has a trial table with left and right trials like the working memory sessions; the files do not say which task a session is.
    Found by the attempt on Fujisawa et al. 2008 (fujisawa-2008-01, 2026-10-16)
  • Description missingThe session description of every file is the paper's abstract, and session start times are dates at midnight; the files do not say which sessions are sleep, test, spontaneous alternation or working memory sessions.
    Found by the attempt on Fujisawa et al. 2008 (fujisawa-2008-01, 2026-10-16)
