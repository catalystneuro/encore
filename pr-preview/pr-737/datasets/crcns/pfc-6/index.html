Dataset unversioned files (the archive keeps no versions) · tested 2026-11-05 · 9 open findings

  • Data missingThe data set holds no LFP, no SPWR, delta-wave or spindle times, and no tetrode of each cell. The paper's analyses of hippocampal sharp-wave ripples, cortical delta waves and spindles (Figures 5 to 7) and its same-tetrode control cannot be repeated, 14 of 29 claims.
    Found by the attempt on Peyrache et al. 2009 (peyrache-2009-01, 2026-11-05)
  • Data missingThe data set holds 14 sessions of rat 15, where the paper reports 16 analysed. With the paper's inclusion rule (at least ten cells and 4 min of SWS in each rest epoch) 56 sessions and 1499 cells remain, against 63 and 1,692 in the paper; rats 18 and 20 match the paper's counts, rat 19 has one fewer and rat 15 six fewer.
    Found by the attempt on Peyrache et al. 2009 (peyrache-2009-01, 2026-11-05)
  • Selection not documentedThe data set holds 86 sessions, including 23 of rat 20 that fail the 4 min SWS rule, and does not say which sessions the paper analysed.
    Found by the attempt on Peyrache et al. 2009 (peyrache-2009-01, 2026-11-05)
  • Description missingThe bounds of the pre and post rest epochs are not given; only the awake interval and the SWS intervals are. Position units and the maze's coordinates are not stated.
    Found by the attempt on Peyrache et al. 2009 (peyrache-2009-01, 2026-11-05)
  • OtherTwo of the ten learning sessions the description lists (201227 and 201229) have less than 4 min of SWS in one rest epoch by the data set's SWS intervals (2.2 min pre, and 3.3 min post), so they would fail the paper's inclusion rule, though the paper's Figure 7 uses ten learning sessions.
    Found by the attempt on Peyrache et al. 2009 (peyrache-2009-01, 2026-11-05)
  • OtherSessions without SWS in a rest epoch store a single [0, 0] interval (four sessions) or an empty file (150704 SwsPRE), not a documented empty value.
    Found by the attempt on Peyrache et al. 2009 (peyrache-2009-01, 2026-11-05)
  • OtherCell types are all 0 (unknown) in 200107 and all 1 (interneuron) in 200209, and mostly unknown in 200131, 200224 and 200301, which is unlikely for prefrontal recordings.
    Found by the attempt on Peyrache et al. 2009 (peyrache-2009-01, 2026-11-05)
  • OtherIn five sessions spikes continue 12 to 176 min after the position record ends (200106 by 176 min), and in 200213 the position record ends before the awake interval does.
    Found by the attempt on Peyrache et al. 2009 (peyrache-2009-01, 2026-11-05)
  • OtherIn the recorded trials rat 18 never reaches the rule-change criterion on the right-arm rule, yet its rule was changed, and three rats, not two, reach criterion on the left-arm rule.
    Found by the attempt on Peyrache et al. 2009 (peyrache-2009-01, 2026-11-05)
