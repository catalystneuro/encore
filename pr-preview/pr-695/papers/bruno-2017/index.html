Bruno et al. 2017 · eLife

A spiral attractor network drives rhythmic locomotion

doi:10.7554/elife.27342 · paper key bruno-2017

Datasets

  • CRCNS:apl-1

Most of the paper's main findings hold up in this data: Aplysia pedal ganglion activity forms a low-dimensional periodic orbit, sequential bouts fall on the same manifold, and single neurons vary widely in participation. Convergence within one oscillation period did not hold up, as only 12 of 30 responses reached high recurrence that fast. Motor output was decoded in 8 of the 9 responses with nerve recordings, not all nine. The stable spiral held for the grand mean but not in every preparation, and the caudo-lateral location of strongly participating neurons could not be tested, as the data do not give the array's orientation. Several outcomes depend on a kernel width and dimension count the Methods leave unclear.

2026-11-08 · on CRCNS:apl-1

  • ◐Partially reproduced Three bouts of fictive locomotion were evoked in each of 10 isolated preparations (30 population responses); each recorded population held 120 to 180 neurons, 1131 neurons in all.
  • ✓Reproduced After stimulation the spike-train power spectra of the populations are dominated by a slow oscillation at or below 0.1 Hz.
  • ✓Reproduced The population firing rate decays slowly after the stimulus, decreasing in most evoked responses.
  • ✓Reproduced Capturing 80% of the population variance needs 4 to 8 embedding dimensions in the evoked responses, a reduction by more than a factor of 10 from the number of neurons.
  • ✓Reproduced The evoked low-dimensional activity recurs with a regular period; strongly periodic recurrence on the scale of 10 to 15 s is found in many but not all of the 30 responses.
  • ✓Reproduced Almost all populations are dominated by a single periodic orbit; the majority of programs have more than 50% of their recurrent points in the dominant peak.
  • ✓Reproduced The periodic orbits are robust to the threshold for recurrence, with similar periodicity at thresholds of 10%, 5% and 2%.
  • ?Not reproduced Almost all evoked responses reach a state of high recurrence within one oscillation period of stimulation and are thereafter dominated by recurrence.
  • ✓Reproduced Sequentially evoked responses of a preparation fall on the same manifold; their distance is below that of random projections for all pairs except two, pairs (1,2) and (1,3) of preparation 4.
  • ✓Reproduced The convergence of sequential responses to the same manifold holds when the three programs are projected onto common axes from a PCA of their concatenated activity.
  • ✓Reproduced Stimulation drives responses of the same preparation far apart before coalescence, more than at stimulus onset or after both have coalesced, so the same manifold is reached from different initial conditions.
  • ✓Reproduced Pairwise similarity between neurons is preserved across responses of the same preparation, above a null model, except for pairs (1,3) and (2,3) of preparation 7.
  • ◐Partially reproduced Of 19 spontaneous divergences from the trajectory, 17 (90%) returned to a recurrent state; the two that did not were consistent with the end of the evoked locomotion.
  • ✓Reproduced Of the 17 divergences that returned to a recurrent state, all but three returned to the same manifold; the three appeared to move to a different periodic attractor.
  • ◐Partially reproduced The evoked responses have highly clustered maximum eigenvalues of the local linear dynamics, dominated by negative complex values, so the pedal ganglion implements a contracting periodic orbit, a stable spiral attractor, found in every preparation.
  • ✓Reproduced The linear model estimates a mean orbital period of about 10 s, consistent with the period from the recurrent points.
  • ?Not reproduced In most responses the trajectory contracts in all embedding dimensions; a few responses have a positive real eigenvalue in one low-variance dimension, a minor expansion.
  • ✓Reproduced Local trajectories were dominated by rotation throughout; in every preparation most local models have a complex eigenvalue among their top two.
  • ✓Reproduced Over a response the orbital period may slow, speed up or not change; all three occur among the 30 responses.
  • ✓Reproduced There is no relationship between the change in orbital period over a response and its rate of contraction, as a stable spiral predicts.
  • ✓Reproduced No recorded neuron is a P10 motorneuron; no neuron comes close to one-to-one locking of its spikes with P10 spikes at any delay, nor locks consistently across programs.
  • ?Not reproduced P10 nerve activity can be fitted and forecast from the low-dimensional trajectory in all nine responses with P10 recordings, so the trajectory encodes muscle commands.
  • ✓Reproduced The nine recordings with P10 activity need only 3 to 5 embedding dimensions for 80% variance, and at least double that for 90%; using the 90% dimensions does not improve the P10 forecasts.
  • ✓Reproduced Every response has a long-tailed distribution of single-neuron participation, a minority of neurons dominating its dynamics.
  • ✓Reproduced Neurons with high participation in one response often have low participation in another; every preparation contains neurons whose change in participation well exceeds that predicted by a noise model.
  • ✓Reproduced Even the most strongly participating neurons in a response could more than halve their participation in other evoked responses.
  • ✓Reproduced A neuron's participation reflects both its firing rate and its synchrony; firing rate explains on average 50% of the variation in participation within a response, and rate changes 23% of the change in participation between responses.
  • ◐Partially reproduced The distance between a pair of responses does not correlate with the total change in neuron participation between them nor with the distance between their participation distributions; the program is robust to the participation of single neurons.
  • ✓Reproduced No small invariant core of strongly participating, phasically active neurons, such as a classic central pattern generator, is found across preparations.
  • ⊘Blocked Neurons with strong participation across the three responses, and those with highly variable participation, lie mainly in the caudo-lateral quadrant of the ganglion.
