Hart et al. 2024 · Current Biology

Pheromone representation in the ant antennal lobe changes with age

doi:10.1016/j.cub.2024.05.031 · paper key hart-2024

Datasets

  • DANDI:000773

The behavioural findings and the whole antennal lobe findings held up, while most glomerulus-level findings did not or could not be tested. Old ants left the nest faster after alarm pheromone, and whole-lobe alarm responses showed no age effect. Responses to 3-hexanone and isopropanol declined with age, but the paper's fold declines were not reached. Fewer responding glomeruli in old ants could not be tested because the hand-drawn regions are not described. Stronger alarm responses in old PGb and PGa did not reproduce, but glomeruli had to be located by matching the paper's traces, which worked poorly in old ants (9 of 52 held out); the confocal control was unreachable.
