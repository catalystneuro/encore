Natraj et al. 2025 · Cell

Sampling representational plasticity of simple imagined movements across days enables long-term neuroprosthetic control

doi:10.1016/j.cell.2025.02.001 · paper key natraj-2025

Datasets

  • DANDI:001535

Almost none of the paper's main findings could be tested in this data, and none was contradicted. The dandiset holds only participant B1's decoder training trials, so the somatotopy of imagined movements, the closed-loop gains in accuracy and class separation, stable plug-and-play control over weeks and the robot arm results lack the second participant, band-power features or online decoder outputs. The one claim that held, the amount of training data (837.4 min stored), depends on counting the robot trials. Without them the total is 540.0 min. A spatial pooling control could not be tested either, as no electrodes table places the channels.
