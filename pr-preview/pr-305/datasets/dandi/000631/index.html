    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • The files give no pixel size. 0.65 um per pixel is assumed, from the ORCA-Flash4 camera and the 10x objective of Methods 4.4 (6.5 um camera pixels).     • The angle of each ROI is computed with the claims file's readings B (2D conduction on a 0.1 mm grid, equipotential disks of 1.5 mm, insulating wall of 17.5 mm radius centred on the image) and C (line sources at the electrode centres), as the paper does not say how the angles were obtained. Both give the same outcome.
    • Controls are the five unique 1 Hz single-pulse dishes (three of 7-31-21, two of 8-9-21) and the three 833 kHz ones (10-5-21). The paper does not give the number of control dishes or how they were matched with the paired dishes (Results 2.1, "The values measured in control samples at 1 Hz and 833 kHz were taken as 100%"); normalisation (i) averages the ratio over every pairing of a paired and a control dish. Control subsets are shown as variants.
    • Background reading (b) subtracts the mean YP within 0.5 mm of the three electrode centres of the same dish (Methods 4.4, footprint measurements made "to account for 'spontaneous' YP uptake"). The radius is a choice; the disk covers the needle lumen (0.25 mm radius, Methods 4.2) and the inner wall, where the YP profile is flat (to about 0.55 mm, apart from bright material in the lumen of the three 833 kHz controls).
