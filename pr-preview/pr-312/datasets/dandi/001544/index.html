    How it will be judged

    Time in each arm from tracking over the 10 min session (Methods, Odor preference behavior tests), expressed as percent change of time in the odor arm against the saline arm (Figure 1C legend). Reproduced when p < 0.05 with more time near the APP odor under every reading; blocked as methods_underspecified when they disagree.
