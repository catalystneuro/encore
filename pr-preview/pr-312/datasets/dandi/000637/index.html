Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingThe Units tables hold spike times only, with no waveforms, waveform features, electrode assignments or quality metrics, so the units cannot be classified as regular- or fast-spiking as the paper does, and every finding of the paper is about one or the other.
  • Data missingThe dandiset holds one S1 probe per recording. It has no POm or auditory cortex units, no recording identified as sham or as targeting CG1, and of the paper's duty-cycle series only the four conditions whose pulse duration is 100, 200 or 400 us, the three pulse durations the description lists (3000 Hz at 30 and 60%, 30 Hz at 0.6%, 1500 Hz at 60%).
  • OtherThe recordings cannot be linked to those the paper analysed. The paper's data statement names dandiset 000946, whose rats (BH494 to BH594, 20 rats) are not those of 000637 (BH401 to BH477), and 000637 has about 3.6 times as many S1 units over the paper's 2,000 spike threshold in the PRF series at 200 us as the paper reports S1 RSUs and FSUs (4,642 against 1,297). Which of these recordings, if any, the paper used is not stated.
  • Metadata errorThe description says each recording has 500 trials; 73 of the 292 files have fewer (230 in 45 files, 425 in 13, 375 in 11, and 252 to 400 in four).
  • Metadata errorThe description calls 100, 200 and 400 us the ultrasound durations (UDs) and says the pulse duration was kept at 67 ms; in the paper's terms and in the file identifiers these are the pulse durations and 67 ms is the ultrasound duration. A 600 us recording (BH446) and recordings at 3.5, 5.5 and 7 V are present and not described.
  • Description missingThe stimulation condition is coded only in each file's identifier (PRF, pulse duration, ultrasound duration, voltage, repeat); the trials tables have start and stop times only, the electrode locations are all 'unknown' (the one electrode group's location is 'brain area', every electrode has z = 0, and no file has experiment_description, notes, stimulus or protocol fields), and three recordings named BASELINE are not described (whether tFUS was off, how their trial times were made).

  • openasked 2026-10-31about fig4-s1-rsu-timelocked-3000

    Method S leaves the normalized rate open between N1 (summed window counts over summed pre-stimulus counts) and N2 (mean over trials of the per-trial ratio, trials without a pre-stimulus spike left out). On units with no response N2 is far from 100%, in a direction that depends on the firing rate. Is N2 a reading the paper allows, and if so should it be tested against 100% under R2? The question applies to every claim that uses Method S.
