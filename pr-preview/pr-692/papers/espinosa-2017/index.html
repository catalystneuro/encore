  • supp2-nphr-silences-som-cells

    light pulse (5 s) induces significant hyperpolarization in SOM+ (9.26 ± 3.01 mV, n = 4) compared to SOM- cells (-0.01 ± 0.478 mV, n = 5; p = 0.024, unpaired t-test) ... the effect of light (5 s pulses) on the firing rate of SOM+ (Pre: 6.21 ± 2.58 Hz vs Light: 0.73 ± 0.72 Hz, n = 4, p = 0.035, paired t-test) and SOM- (Pre: 6.61 ± 1.07 Hz vs Light 6.32 ± 1.11 Hz, n = 5, p = 0.349, paired t-test)

    Supplementary Figure 2G to 2K legend; Results, Synaptic output of the basal forebrain, paragraph 1

    How it will be judged

    In vitro whole-cell recordings (Methods, In vitro electrophysiological recordings). Reproduced when the unpaired t-test of hyperpolarization and the paired t-test on SOM+ rates give p < 0.05, the paired t-test on SOM- rates gives p >= 0.05, and the values are within tolerance (the stated spread, s.e.m. per the legend). The evoked-firing panels (2J, 2K) name no test, give no values and do not say at which steps the asterisks fall; the paired t-test named in the Methods (In vitro electrophysiological recordings) is used, and this part holds when SOM+ firing is lower with light at p < 0.05 at one or more current steps and SOM- firing is not at any.
