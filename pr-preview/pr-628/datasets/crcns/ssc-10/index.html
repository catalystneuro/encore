Dataset unversioned files (the archive keeps no versions) · tested 2026-11-08 · 9 open findings

  • Data missingThe cell-attached recordings with simultaneous GCaMP6s imaging (Figure 1F, 10 L2/3 cells in 5 C57BL/6 mice) are not in the data set.
    Found by the attempt on Lemessurier et al. 2019 (lemessurier-2019-01, 2026-11-08)
  • Description missingNo barrel areas or radii are stored. ROI_pos_barrel_norm divides each position by a radius along the ROI's direction (the ratio to ROI_pos_barrel varies by a median 37% within a barrel), and the radii used are not given.
    Found by the attempt on Lemessurier et al. 2019 (lemessurier-2019-01, 2026-11-08)
  • Selection not documentedprocessed_data.mat holds more ROIs than reduced_data.mat in 21 of 36 fields (1044 against 998 NH L2/3, 1276 against 1147 EN L2/3, 464 against 362 NH L4, 782 against 615 EN L4), with no list of the excluded ROIs or the reason. The paper reports 1257 EN L2/3, 322 NH L4 and 586 EN L4 cells, which match neither count.
    Found by the attempt on Lemessurier et al. 2019 (lemessurier-2019-01, 2026-11-08)
  • Selection not documentedsponTrace keeps fewer blank trials than Stimuli marks blank (for example 51 of 61 in DNH15b/20170502/f1), with no stated rule.
    Found by the attempt on Lemessurier et al. 2019 (lemessurier-2019-01, 2026-11-08)
  • Description missingThe code of Stimuli.Label is not described: label k is whisk{k+1} of settings.mat for k = 0 to 8 and 9 is a blank trial, which agrees with the per-whisker frame lists in every field.
    Found by the attempt on Lemessurier et al. 2019 (lemessurier-2019-01, 2026-11-08)
  • OtherField DNH13b/20161208/f2 has no trials for whiskers E1, E2 and E3 (absent from traceByStim, Stimuli frame lists and permTestResults), which the descriptor does not mention; it also lacks positions for three barrels.
    Found by the attempt on Lemessurier et al. 2019 (lemessurier-2019-01, 2026-11-08)
  • File format errorMovie names carry a leading m in Stimuli and not in deltaF in some fields, and the other way in others; three fields (DENR2b/20160408, DNH15a/20170505/f1, DENR15b/20170513/f1) have one dF/F movie with no Stimuli entry.
    Found by the attempt on Lemessurier et al. 2019 (lemessurier-2019-01, 2026-11-08)
  • Description missingThe stored L4 dF/F (npSub = 1) is reproduced (within 0.012) only by filtering the ROI and neuropil traces with the 5-frame median before subtracting 0.3 of the neuropil, as the data descriptor says; the Methods describe the subtraction from the raw ROI fluorescence and do not give the order.
    Found by the attempt on Lemessurier et al. 2019 (lemessurier-2019-01, 2026-11-08)
  • File format errorIn three L4 fields (ScENR10c/20180104/f1, ScENR10c/20180110, ScENR10b/20180105/f2) sponTrace holds 76-frame segments starting 0.5 s after each blank onset, where every other field, and traceByStim everywhere, hold 106 frames starting 0.5 s before onset. Read as the other fields, the blank-trial baseline and evoked windows fall 0.5 s late.
    Found by the attempt on Lemessurier et al. 2019 (lemessurier-2019-01, 2026-11-08)
