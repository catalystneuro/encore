The reversal thus shows that PTlower units are less often direction-selective in the response epoch, not that they lack movement-related activity.

    Findings about the data

    What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

    • Data missing11 sessions of the paper's processed data (Figshare doi:10.25378/janelia.7007846) that hold tagged units are not in the dandiset. They hold 17 of the 61 PTupper and 13 of the 69 PTlower units the paper analysed, so the dandiset holds 44 and 56. Every tagged unit the dandiset does hold is in the processed data with the same label and the same inclusion decision under the paper's code. The dandiset's description says some files were found to be broken and were left out, without naming them.
    • Data missingThe dandiset holds the electrophysiology only. The transcriptomic and anatomical data behind four of the abstract's findings (the two PT types and their projection targets) are not in it; they are at GEO (GSE119182) and on Figshare (doi:10.25378/janelia.7007846).
    • File format errorSpike times are not sorted in 1120 of 1149 units. They were written trial by trial as trial start + go cue time + the spike time from the go cue. Each trial's spikes cover 6.4 s from about 0.97 s before the pole comes in, and trial starts are a median 6.4 s apart (5.4 to 7.9 s from the 1st to the 99th percentile), so consecutive trials overlap on the file's clock and some spikes of one trial lie in the next trial's early epochs (11.7% of the spikes counted in the baseline epoch, under 2% in later epochs, are detectably out of order). Trial membership of a spike cannot be recovered in full from the files.
    • File format errorThe trial table's stop_time is NaN in every file, and pole_in_time, pole_out_time and cue_start_time are relative to the trial start without saying so. On the file's clock the go cue of a trial often falls after the next trial's start_time.
    • Description missingThe tagged unit labels PTupper and PTlower are not described in the files or the dandiset metadata, and the projection targets (thalamus and medulla) that the paper uses to name the types are stated only in the paper's code.
    • Description missingUnit quality mixes two vocabularies (single, and Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor) and has undescribed empty values ([], 0, blank). Sex is U and genotype empty for every mouse, the electrode positions are all zero, and every electrode group gives layer 5 whatever the depth.
    • OtherIn the paper's processed data, in every one of the 53 sessions the dandiset holds, the spike windows of the first ten trials start later relative to the pole than those of the other trials (by about 2.7 s in the session examined in detail); the paper's code leaves out the first ten trials of each unit, which hides this. The files carry the same shift, as their spike times equal the processed ones in every unit.
