Dataset version 0.250312.0036 · tested 2026-11-16 · 8 open findings

  • Description missingNo file records the condition of its cell (DCZ alone or DCZ plus mCherry) or the time DCZ was applied. No file has an intervals group or a pharmacology or notes field, every sweep's series has the same generic description and the comment no comments, and the session description is the same generic sentence in all 50 files; the conditions are named only in the dandiset's description. The paper's slice comparisons (Fig. 4i,j, Extended Data Fig. 12b) cannot be made from the files.#
    Found by the attempt on Kalogriopoulos et al. 2024 (kalogriopoulos-2024-01, 2026-11-16)
  • Data missingThe mEGFP intensity of each recorded cell (or the widefield images and soma ROIs it was measured from) and the mCherry preincubation time of each cell are not in the dandiset. Extended Data Fig. 12c-h and the preincubation figures of the Methods rest on them.#
    Found by the attempt on Kalogriopoulos et al. 2024 (kalogriopoulos-2024-01, 2026-11-16)
  • OtherThe files hold 50 cells from 8 mice; the paper analysed 41 cells (15 with DCZ, 26 with DCZ plus mCherry) from 7 and 6 mice. The paper's initial Rin exclusion (above 500 megaohms) removes one cell, leaving 49. Nothing in the files marks the cells the paper analysed or why the others were left out.#
    Found by the attempt on Kalogriopoulos et al. 2024 (kalogriopoulos-2024-01, 2026-11-16)
  • Metadata errorChannel 1 of every sweep (current_clamp-response-NN-ch-1) is a CurrentClampSeries with unit volts, but its values (-99.8 pA during the -100 pA step, -149.9 pA before the ramp, then the ramp) are the injected current in amperes.#
    Found by the attempt on Kalogriopoulos et al. 2024 (kalogriopoulos-2024-01, 2026-11-16)
  • Metadata errorThe stimulus series (stimulus-NN-ch-0) gives the ramp as a step to +500 pA from 1.1 to 3.1 s in every sweep of every file. The current actually delivered, in channel 1, is a ramp from -150 pA, and its duration differs between cells.#
    Found by the attempt on Kalogriopoulos et al. 2024 (kalogriopoulos-2024-01, 2026-11-16)
  • OtherIn 10 of 50 cells (eight of the nine recorded on 2024-08-07, and 2024_07_24_0011 and 2024_07_26_0003) the delivered ramp reaches +500 pA at 2.7 s (407.5 pA/s) or 2.5 s (465.7 pA/s); the Methods state -150 to +500 pA across 2 s, which the other 40 cells receive (326 pA/s). Rheobase depends on the ramp's slope, so these cells are not comparable with the others on absolute rheobase. Every sweep also holds -150 pA for 100 ms before the ramp, which the Methods do not describe.#
    Found by the attempt on Kalogriopoulos et al. 2024 (kalogriopoulos-2024-01, 2026-11-16)
  • OtherThe files are sampled at 20 kHz; the Methods state digitization at 10 kHz. Neighbouring samples are equal no more often within pairs than across them, so the samples are not repeated. When each half of the samples of a file's first sweep is fitted on the 16 nearest samples of the other half, both residuals are 0.87 to 1.58 ADC counts; the same sweeps' even samples upsampled back to 20 kHz by linear, cubic or band-limited (FFT) interpolation give 0.30 to 0.57 counts. This is consistent with acquisition at 20 kHz; an upsampling the test does not model, such as one that adds noise, is not excluded.#
    Found by the attempt on Kalogriopoulos et al. 2024 (kalogriopoulos-2024-01, 2026-11-16)
  • Description missingExperimenter, lab and institution are "_" in every file, the electrodes and the device have no description, and the series have no stimulus description ("N/A") or comments. The holding current the Methods describe (adjusted so each cell began at -65 mV) is not recorded: the current monitor reads about 0.5 pA before any injection in every sweep.#
    Found by the attempt on Kalogriopoulos et al. 2024 (kalogriopoulos-2024-01, 2026-11-16)
