Dataset unversioned files (the archive keeps no versions) · tested 2026-11-14 · 9 open findings

  • Data missingThe published data set (pvc-10/live/) holds the raw green and red two-photon movies of 58 planes in 6 mice, their stimulus tables and plane shifts, and 50 unlabelled example cell masks for one plane (mouse5, depth 260). It holds no masks for the cells the paper analysed (identified by hand, 338 PV+ and 3,982 PV-), no PV+/PV- labels and no per-cell responses or positions. Processed per-cell data for part of the animals exist only in an unpublished folder of the bucket (next findings).
    Found by the attempt on Scholl et al. 2015 (scholl-2015-01, 2026-11-14)
  • Metadata errorThe data descriptor says the data set "contains information about the fluorescence traces, peak responses per condition, the measured binocular characteristics for each cell like its disparity selectivity index, preferred relative disparity, the position of the cell in the imaging volume". None of these per-cell data are among the published files; they are in pvc-10/original/to_remove/, which the file list and checksums do not include, for mice 7 to 10 only.
    Found by the attempt on Scholl et al. 2015 (scholl-2015-01, 2026-11-14)
  • OtherThe bucket folder pvc-10/original/to_remove/, outside the data set's file list and checksums, holds MATLAB structs ce (PV-) and cePV (PV+), one entry per cell, with fields Ftrace, cyc, peaks, standard, dsi, disparitypref, peakfit, peakfitparams, masks, xy, xyshft, depth, sampleG, sampleR, neuropilcyc, neuropilpeaks, dsineuropil, date, filename and windowlength. They are made from the published movies: for 9 cells of mouse 8 at depth 205 um the stored Ftrace equals the mask mean of the published green movie in every frame, and for all 578 cells the trials per condition equal the stim file's repeats or one fewer. Readable structs cover mice 8, 9 and 10 (PV+ and PV-) and mouse 7 (PV+ only); the three mouse 7 PV- files (binoc7.mat, binoc7new.mat, 1452382802_binoc7.mat) are truncated and cannot be opened, and mice 5 and 6 have index lists only. Publishing complete structs for all six mice, with a description of their fields, would make the data set usable for the paper's analyses.
    Found by the attempt on Scholl et al. 2015 (scholl-2015-01, 2026-11-14)
  • Description missingNothing documents the structs or the binocmonocinds<mouse>[PV].mat index lists. The lists (109 PV+ and 769 PV- cells over 6 mice) look like the paper's visually responsive cells but cannot be confirmed. The field standard matches the responsiveness index of the Methods recomputed from peaks for 279 of 578 cells only; windowlength is 300 with no unit; neuropilpeaks is one region per plane; and the mouse 7 plane shift is stored in xyshft as (-70, 157) where stats.mat gives (157, -70) um.
    Found by the attempt on Scholl et al. 2015 (scholl-2015-01, 2026-11-14)
  • Selection not documentedThe structs hold a selection of the paper's cells, not all identified cells: every PV+ cell in them passes the binocular responsiveness index (157 of 157), and 400 of 421 PV- cells of mice 8 to 10 do. Mice 8 to 10 hold 58 PV+ and 421 PV- cells against the paper's 338 PV+ and 3,982 PV- in 6 mice. The selection rule is not stated, so the paper's counts of identified cells cannot be checked.
    Found by the attempt on Scholl et al. 2015 (scholl-2015-01, 2026-11-14)
  • Data missingThe two rAAV CaMKIIa-nls-tdTomato mice of Figure 5, the neuropil patches of Supplementary Figures 3 and 5, and the anti-PV histology of Figure 1E are not in the data set (the structs hold one neuropil region per plane), and the descriptor does not say they were left out.
    Found by the attempt on Scholl et al. 2015 (scholl-2015-01, 2026-11-14)
  • Metadata errorThe descriptor gives frames of 255 by 456 pixels and converts positions with 300/456 and 300/255 um per pixel. Every frame in the files is 256 by 455 pixels, as the paper states.
    Found by the attempt on Scholl et al. 2015 (scholl-2015-01, 2026-11-14)
  • Description missingThe data set gives no frame rate. At the paper's 30 Hz, the median stimulus onset intervals give cycles of 3.93 s in mouse 5 and 4.37 to 4.43 s in mice 7 to 10, against the 4.5 s and 5 s that the stimulus durations in the stats files plus the Methods' 3 s blank give; only mouse 6 fits. Either the frame rate or the blank differs from the Methods in five of six mice.
    Found by the attempt on Scholl et al. 2015 (scholl-2015-01, 2026-11-14)
  • OtherEight planes have fewer than the 6 to 7 repeats per condition the paper states (all six planes of mouse 10 have 4 or 5), and in four planes (mouse6 240 and 300, mouse8 250, mouse10 200) the last stimulus cycle runs past the last frame. Mouse 10 includes a plane at 125 um, above the 150 um the Methods give as the start. The structs hold the same numbers of trials per condition, so these planes were analysed with them.
    Found by the attempt on Scholl et al. 2015 (scholl-2015-01, 2026-11-14)
