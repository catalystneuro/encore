Dataset unversioned files (the archive keeps no versions) · tested 2026-11-14 · 7 open findings

  • Description missingThe data descriptor names only the session structs and the ablation logs. The files that say which neurons were ablated (manualCurationData.mat in the NYU mice, with per-attempt targets and success flags; an<id>_<date>.mat and ablation_ids_manually_curated.mat in the JRC mice, with attempted_ids and ablated_ids), and which directory is the pre-ablation and post-ablation data of each of the 24 ablations, can be found only by reading the paper's analysis code (get_data_path.m).#
    Found by the attempt on Peron et al. 2020 (peron-2020-01, 2026-11-14)
  • OtherThe session structs come in two MATLAB formats, 94 in version 5 and 107 in version 7.3 (HDF5), even within one mouse's directories, with no note of it; a reader needs both scipy.io and h5py. The archives are tar.gz files of 2.7 to 11.9 GB that cannot be read at an offset, so any use of one file means streaming the whole archive; the 16 archives hold 120.9 GB compressed, and the JRC ablation logs make up 77.1 GB of the 166.7 GB their members hold uncompressed.#
    Found by the attempt on Peron et al. 2020 (peron-2020-01, 2026-11-14)
  • Data missingThe six session files of an278939's session_neuropilone_postablate_silent_merged directory, the pre-ablation data of its whisking ablation, hold no trial-averaged dF/F (dffBased_HitLTrialAvg, dffBased_HitRTrialAvg), unlike the other 195 files; their 27 features include psthHitLeftTrialsMatrix and psthHitRightTrialsMatrix, which are not documented. The response-similarity analysis of Fig. 3g cannot include that ablation.#
    Found by the attempt on Peron et al. 2020 (peron-2020-01, 2026-11-14)
  • Otheran278937's pre-ablation directory holds six subvolumes (5,225 neurons) and its post-ablation directories five (4,230); the 995 neurons of the sixth subvolume have no post-ablation data, with no note of it.#
    Found by the attempt on Peron et al. 2020 (peron-2020-01, 2026-11-14)
  • Description missingan250220's curation file (an250220_all.mat) lists only the ablated ids, not the attempted ones, and the code looks for its logs under a folder that does not exist; the ablation logs of an257218 carry no target id (la.ablatedId). The success rate of these ablations cannot be computed from the logs.#
    Found by the attempt on Peron et al. 2020 (peron-2020-01, 2026-11-14)
  • Data missingThe histology behind Extended Data Fig. 3b-d (IBA1 and GFAP sections) is not in the data set.#
    Found by the attempt on Peron et al. 2020 (peron-2020-01, 2026-11-14)
  • OtherThe trial-averaged dF/F of the 8 NYU mice (80 session files) has 75 frames, those of the JRC mice 70, with no note of it; the analysis code keeps 70 rows.#
    Found by the attempt on Peron et al. 2020 (peron-2020-01, 2026-11-14)
