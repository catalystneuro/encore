What the attempts that used this dataset found about the data themselves, for its owner and the archive, under the version of the dataset each was found on.

Dataset version 0.210819.0345 · tested 2026-11-10 · 10 open findings

  • Data missingThe open field files hold no head pitch, roll or their angular velocities (the IMU data of Cohort 1), on which the paper's freely moving findings on head posture rest. The paper deposited them on figshare (Dataset 1, doi:10.6084/m9.figshare.13425392.v1).
    Found by the attempt on Mallory et al. 2021 (mallory-2021-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Identifier missingThe dandiset holds 20 head-fixed ecephys files from 9 mice (5120 units) and does not say which are the paper's 11 recordings (2861 units, 8 mice). They can be found only by matching subject, unit and frame counts to the paper's figshare deposit. Of the other 9, six hold gain and contrast trials and three only gain 1 and contrast 100%; their trial tables, like the paper's, have no field for the track or its landmarks, so the no-landmark blocks of Figure 8 (1621 cells in 6 sessions) cannot be identified, though six of the nine files from six mice sum to 1621 units.
    Found by the attempt on Mallory et al. 2021 (mallory-2021-01, 2026-11-10)
  • File format errorIn sub-npI3_ses-20190420_behavior+ecephys.nwb and sub-npI4_ses-20190421_obj-1h2xem6_behavior+ecephys.nwb the first entry of units/spike_times_index has wrapped at 2**16 (5852 for 71388, and 12175 for 77711), so unit 0 lacks its later spikes and unit 1 begins with them, out of time order. The paper's figshare deposit has the right trains.
    Found by the attempt on Mallory et al. 2021 (mallory-2021-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Metadata errorEye position is stated in metres and eye velocity in 'unknown'; the values are in % of the eye width (the unit of the paper's Results and Figure 4), the same as in the paper's deposit.
    Found by the attempt on Mallory et al. 2021 (mallory-2021-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Description missingThe hemisphere of each head-fixed recording is not recorded (every electrode location is 'unknown'), so the paper's left and right comparisons cannot be made. No subject has sex or age, and the open field cohorts are not identified (the six mice recorded in 50 and 70 cm arenas hold 179 units, consistent with Cohort 1). The paper's figshare Dataset 3 has no hemisphere field either (its cell_info fields are printed in the main notebook).
    Found by the attempt on Mallory et al. 2021 (mallory-2021-01, 2026-11-10)
  • OtherTen behaviour-only head-fixed files repeat the behaviour of ten ecephys files, and two probe insertions (npI4 2019-04-21, npJ5 2019-05-05) are each converted twice, with the same trial table and unit count over spans of different length.
    Found by the attempt on Mallory et al. 2021 (mallory-2021-01, 2026-11-10)
  • OtherTwo head-fixed sessions have gaps in the behaviour timestamps (npI1 0413, up to 82 s; npI3 0420, 217 and 308 s), and in npI3 0420 66% of the eye samples are missing.
    Found by the attempt on Mallory et al. 2021 (mallory-2021-01, 2026-11-10)
  • OtherThe open field files hold 1200 units from 25 subjects, against 26 mice in the paper, and include arenas of 75 and 91.4 cm that the Methods do not list.
    Found by the attempt on Mallory et al. 2021 (mallory-2021-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Metadata errorIn the paper's figshare Dataset 1 (doi:10.6084/m9.figshare.13425392.v1), head direction is in radians (1st to 99th percentile 0.05 to 6.23), though the deposit's description gives degrees; a few samples lie outside 0 to 2 pi (-1.67 to 8.42).
    Found by the attempt on Mallory et al. 2021 (mallory-2021-01, 2026-11-10)
  • OtherIn figshare Dataset 1, sessions Fuschia_052001 and Daisy_052001, of two different mice, have identical body position, speed and head direction over 400,050 samples, with different pitch and roll.
    Found by the attempt on Mallory et al. 2021 (mallory-2021-01, 2026-11-10)
