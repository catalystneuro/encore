Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Metadata errorThe running_speed series of every file is labelled cm/s, but its values (session medians around 0.07, 99th percentiles up to 0.67) are in m/s, the unit of the paper and of its code.
  • Selection not documentedThe dandiset holds 391 stage 5 and 6 sessions of the 13 included mice, where the paper analysed 381. The paper's inclusion criteria select 376 of them counted from the reward series and 375 counted from the hits, and nothing in the dandiset marks which sessions the paper used.
  • Data missingThe training sessions before stage 5, the other stage 5 and 6 sessions of the 500 the paper names, and the 18 mice that did not meet the inclusion criteria are not in the dandiset, so the training duration (21 +/- 8 sessions) cannot be tested.
  • OtherIn 38 of the 391 sessions the post hoc pupil diameter series is present but entirely missing values (14 of BW046's 34 sessions), so those sessions have no pupil measure.
  • Metadata errorThe rig names in the session metadata are spelled several ways for what appear to be the same rigs (Box 1, Box1, Boox1).
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the paper's authors. Figure2.py types each state by its nearest template with nansum, so a state with no trial of posterior 0.8 or more in the sessions where the optimal state occurs scores 0 against every template and displaces the optimal label. In this attempt's fits one state of BW059 is such a state; it was typed on all sessions. How were such states handled, and which 381 sessions were analysed?

Reproduction Arousal, uninstructed movements and decision-making states in mice (Hulsey et al. 2024)

Tests the findings of Hulsey, Zumwalt, Mazzucato, McCormick and Jaramillo (2024) on dandiset 000678, which holds the stage 5 and 6 behavioral sessions of 13 mice performing auditory or visual discrimination, with pupil diameter, face motion energy and locomotion speed. It fits the paper's GLM-HMM to each mouse's choices, labels the performance states, and relates the states to the arousal and movement measures, including the support vector machine decoding of the states. Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • As for figs2-n-states.fig2a-state-types fig2b-optimal-occupancy fig2c-dwell-times fig2d-prediction-accuracy fig3c-inverted-u-pupil figs4-r2-individual-movement fig3f-movement-index-inverted-u
  • As for fig5c-state-classification.figs7-pupil-best-raw-measure fig5c-movement-index-best
  • The sessions of every later analysis are the 376 that pass the criteria by the reward count; the paper analysed 381, and the dandiset does not say which.cohort-inclusion
  • The GLM-HMM of the paper's ssm fork is reimplemented (hulsey.py) with the same model, priors, initialisation scheme, EM updates and stopping rule; its random initial values differ from ssm's. The test log probability includes the log prior, as ssm's log_probability does.figs2-n-states
  • The visual stimulus values follow loadbehavior.get_stim_vals of the paper's code (uobrainflex at ef48985), which for a file with a visual_gabor_angle column maps the difficulty codes 0, 1 and 2 to the session's distinct values of abs(angle - 45) / 45, largest first, and otherwise to 1, 0.8 and 0.6. 118 of the 141 visual-task sessions get 1, 0.6 and 0.2 and the other 23 get 1, 0.8 and 0.6. The first version of this attempt used 1, 0.8 and 0.6 for every session.figs2-n-states
  • Fit on the 376 sessions that pass the inclusion criteria (see cohort-inclusion).figs2-n-states
  • As for figs2-n-states and fig2d-prediction-accuracy.fig1g-example-mouse-prediction
  • Assumption. A state with no discrete-state trial in the sessions where the optimal state occurs is typed on all of the mouse's sessions; Figure2.py has no rule for it (it would score the state as equal to every template). Affects one state of BW059.fig2a-state-types
  • flex_hmm.get_dwell_times adds a last epoch of dwell 0 when a session's last trial differs from the one before it, which also adds a zero indeterminate dwell. The code's output is used, as the claims file reads it; without the 3 such epochs the indeterminate dwell and the medians are unchanged (shown in the notebook).fig2c-dwell-times
  • The paper's code computes the filtered posterior of the preceding test trials by re-running expected_states on each prefix; here one forward pass gives the same filtered probabilities.fig2d-prediction-accuracy
  • As Figure3.py, a state absent from a bin with more than 5 trials leaves that bin missing, so a bin with no optimal trial is not a zero; this follows the code.fig3c-inverted-u-pupil
  • Stationary under reading (b) is taken as absolute speed below 0.02 m/s, so that backward movement is not counted as stationary.fig3e-u-disengaged-pupil
  • Rests on the optimal pupils of fig3c-inverted-u-pupil, from this attempt's state fits.fig3-distance-from-optimal-pupil
  • The pupil at onset is the filtered value at the stimulus sample; FigureS1.py takes it from the trial's pupil trace, 1 ms later. A standard choice that cannot change the result.figs1-iti-independence
  • 3 runs per shuffled condition and 20 null runs, in place of 1000, to fit the time limit. The unshuffled condition does not vary between runs. With 20 null runs the null SD and its 95th percentile are estimated less precisely.fig5c-state-classification
  • Rests on this attempt's state fits (figs2-n-states).fig5c-state-classification
