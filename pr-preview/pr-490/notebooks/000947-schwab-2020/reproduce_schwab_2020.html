Reproducing Schwab et al. (2020) from dandiset 000947¶

Paper. Schwab BC, Kase D, Zimnik A, Rosenbaum R, Codianni MG, Rubin JE, Turner RS (2020). Neural activity during a simple reaching task in macaques is counter to gating and rebound in basal ganglia-thalamic communication. PLOS Biology 18(10): e3000829. doi:10.1371/journal.pbio.3000829. The paper is open access and this attempt used its full text.

Dandiset. DANDI:000947, version 0.240510.2211. The paper predates the dandiset and cites no DANDI version (its data statement points to Dryad). 0.240510.2211 is the only published version, so it is pinned; the draft lists the same 378 files.

What the dandiset holds. Single-unit recordings from two monkeys (Gaia, "NHP G", and Isis, "NHP I") in GPi, VL thalamus, motor cortex and other areas, before and after MPTP, with trial tables and raw and filtered traces. The paper uses the pre-MPTP GPi and VLa recordings. EMG, the stimulation mapping of Fig 1 and the simulations of Fig 6 are not in the dandiset.

Outside inputs. The paper's Dryad deposit (doi:10.5061/dryad.0cfxpnvxm, read here from its Zenodo mirror, record 4000719) holds the analysis code read for the method and the per-unit result files Gaia_Analy.mat and Isis_Analy.mat. Those files name each analysed unit by file, channel and sort letter; they are used only by the claim unit-link and by variants that use the paper's own unit list. Every outcome on the paper's findings is decided on a unit selection made from the dandiset alone.

Clock check. With spikes and a trial table only, the check is whether the trial labels carry information in the spikes at the time of movement but not before the cue, when the animal does not know the target. For each unit with at least 10 valid trials per direction, left and right spike counts are compared (rank sum) in the movement window (-200 to +300 ms) and in a window before the cue (-1500 to -1000 ms from movement onset). Two controls: the labels permuted within the file, and the true labels with every trial time moved 1 s later (a clock offset).

Unit selection and peri-movement responses¶

The dandiset rule (fixed in the claims file): curated units labelled GPi or VLa (ignoring case) in pre-MPTP files; a unit-direction is analysed with at least 10 valid trials; a unit is kept when its mean rate is at least 30 spikes/s (GPi) or 1 spike/s (VLa) and at least one direction is analysed. Each file's unit is one unit. For every unit-direction the helper computes the SDF and ISI functions, detects increases and decreases, and records the rest rate, the integrated change and the control-period slope. The rate is taken over the span of the spike train; taken over the length of the raw recording, it would leave out 2 more GPi units (output below).

def gating(df, lo=0, hi=50):
    def changes(a):
        d = df[df.area == a]
        return np.r_[d.inc_onset.dropna(), d.dec_onset.dropna()], np.r_[np.ones(d.inc_onset.notna().sum()), -np.ones(d.dec_onset.notna().sum())]
    gt, gs = changes("GPi")
    vt, vs = changes("VLa")
    lead = vt[None, :] - gt[:, None]
    fits = (lead >= lo) & (lead <= hi) & (gs[:, None] != vs[None, :])
    return fits.size, int(fits.sum())

How the analysis differs from the paper's. The units are selected from the dandiset's own labels, and each file's unit is counted once, because the dandiset does not say which units the paper kept nor which units in consecutive files are one neuron; this gives about 40% more units than the paper. Two variants are shown for every claim: the paper's own unit list, linked through the Dryad files, and one record per presumed neuron (same day, channel and sort letter), which joins some units the paper kept apart. With the paper's list, hold-period-ramps, monophasic-more-in-vla and fig3a-vla-leads-gpi would meet their criteria and fig3b-gpi-decr-lag-vla-incr would be partially reproduced (its GPi median, -36 ms, leaves its tolerance), so these four outcomes depend on the selection; the fig3b GPi median also leaves its tolerance with one record per presumed neuron and with stricter detection. The pair claims keep their outcomes with the paper's list (329 pairs in the same file, against the paper's 427). The detection of responses follows the Methods and the paper's detection function, but the detrending and the ISI function are implemented here; on the paper's units they agree with its stored results for 91% (increases) and 81% (decreases) of unit-directions. With random alignment the detection makes 62% of GPi and 71% of VLa units responsive, and the control-slope test finds a slope in 98% to 99% of units, as in the data; chance decreases in VLa (34% of unit-directions, 31% at alpha 0.0001) may pull the VLa decrease onsets of fig3c late. Trial exclusion follows the Methods and matches the paper's trial counts for 63% of unit-directions. Widths are measured on the dandiset's 200 Hz filtered traces. The CCF jitters the VLa spikes; the Methods' exclusion of pairs with c0 > 0.3 is shown as a variant with an approximate c0, as the paper's simulation is not in its deposit. Burst and pause detection is a reimplementation of the surprise method and gives longer bursts than the paper's; its control shifts events within rest, and the per-pair test as the Methods describe it is at chance, and more than half of the pairs are left out as noisy. The surprise uses the natural log of the paper's MATLAB; log10, used in a first run, gives longer bursts still (shown as a variant). The behaviour is tested on the files that hold the units analysed; over all pre-MPTP files the reaction times differ between the animals, and no file set gives the paper's 452 degrees of freedom.

What was found about the dandiset. It holds the paper's pre-MPTP GPi and VLa recordings: 364 of the 394 units the paper analysed are among its curated units, and their spike times reproduce the paper's per-unit activity. It does not identify which units the paper analysed or link a neuron across consecutive files. Location labels vary in case, 54 Gaia thalamic units are labelled only "Thalamus" and 33 Isis units have no label or channel; 30 of the paper's units are missing from the curated tables, and 21 files have no curated units (13 of them have sorted clusters without channel or sort letter). Every session_start_time is two days after the date in the session_id. Spike times are at the sample resolution of the recording. Error trials are flagged only in files with an error_onset_time column (84 and 43 files in the column table), and movement ends are in only 32 of the 119 Gaia files. One pre-MPTP file has no trials table, and one M1 spike train is stored twice. The EMG and the stimulation mapping are not in the dandiset.
