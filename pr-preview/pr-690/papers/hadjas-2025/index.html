Hadjas et al. 2025 · Preprint

Parkinson’s Disease-vulnerable and -resilient dopamine neurons display opposite responses to excitatory input

doi:10.1101/2025.06.03.657460 · paper key hadjas-2025

Datasets

  • DANDI:001528

The main findings largely held: STN and PPN stimulation excited SNr GABA and Vglut2 dopamine neurons, and drove opposite dopamine release in tail (increase) and dorsolateral striatum (decrease), scaling with frequency. Inhibition of Anxa1 dopamine neurons, the contrast with Vglut2 neurons, held in direction but was only partly testable, because the dataset lacks dorsolateral recordings from several of the paper's mice (5 and 4 mice here, 8 and 10 in the paper). The PPN duration effect on pan-dopamine responses did not reproduce, and the records give no cause. The human tissue findings could not be tested because those counts are not deposited. Minor claims, such as the 5 Hz dopamine rise, group sizes and rebounds, were mixed or failed.
