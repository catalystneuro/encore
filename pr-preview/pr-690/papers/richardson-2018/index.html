Richardson et al. 2018 · Nature Communications

Development of the social brain from age three to twelve years

doi:10.1038/s41467-018-03399-2 · paper key richardson-2018

Datasets

  • OPENNEURO:ds000228

The behavioral results held in this data, but the brain findings held only in direction. The theory of mind and pain networks separated in adults and 3-year-olds, and within-network correlations rose and across-network correlations fell with age, but values and significance bounds missed, with adult across-network correlation at -.051 against -.17, an offset the code, available only on request, leaves unexplained. Reverse-correlation events were only partly found, age effects at event peaks did not reproduce, and the maturity finding failed on a within-Pain effect. The independent adult sample could not be tested because it is not deposited. The motion controls held.
