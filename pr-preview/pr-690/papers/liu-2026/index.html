DANDI:001746: Some main findings held in this data, two did not, and the central von Economo neuron (VEN) claims could not be tested. VENs mapped to the L5 ET and L5/6 CT clusters, and L2/3 IT neurons had the shortest membrane time constant. The lower firing rate of VENs did not reproduce, and neither did the correlation between tau and transcriptomic distance (r 0.053 against 0.53). The saddle-shaped phase plot could not be tested, because the hand-placed peaks are not described well enough to automate. The VEN versus L5 pyramidal action potential comparison and the VEN subtype comparisons need recordings that are not in this dataset.

DANDI:001750: Here is the paragraph, ready to save to that file: The paper's main findings mostly could not be tested here, and those that held rest on the authors' own tables. The lower Na+ current in von Economo neurons was not confirmed, since it was significant under only 2 of 8 readings and the paper does not say how the maximal current was measured. Larger eEPSPs, the lowest phase peak index and similar VEN subtypes held, checking statistics but not measurements. The metabolite gradient did not reproduce (175 differences against 228), and the glycolytic bias held for citrate and glutamate only. Model, sequencing, connectivity and morphology findings went untested for lack of runnable code or time.

DANDI:001751: The paper's local-circuit finding held in direction but not in its counts. Every VEN connection rate was below the matching pyramidal-cell rate, yet the data gave 26 connections against 31, and none of the four reported rates matched. The files carry no cell types or connection labels, so the counts rest on an annotation table from the paper's code that disagrees with the paper's totals. Lower sodium current, lower phase peak index, larger evoked responses and lower spontaneous event frequency in VENs reproduced on tables or sister dandisets. Soma distances could not be tested because no positions are stored, and the sequencing, morphology and modeling claims were not attempted. The file still needs to be created with this text.

DANDI:001752: Several main findings held in this data. VENs map to L5 ET and L5/6 CT, have the lowest phase peak index, smaller sodium currents and larger evoked EPSPs. Event amplitudes did not differ between VENs and PCs. Lower miniature EPSC frequency in VENs did not reproduce, because the deposited cell labels are the opposite of those in the paper's analysis sheet. The other event-frequency findings could not be settled, as the paper gives no detection setting beyond a 5 pA threshold. Broader, lower VEN action potentials and the metabolite gradient (179 metabolites against 228) did not reproduce, and the connectivity counts matched only in part. The transcriptomic atlas was too large to test, and the model code is missing.
