DANDI:000128: Of the findings that could be tested in this one array session, most hold up, but the paper's central claim of only weak agreement between preparatory and movement-period tuning did not reproduce. In the dataset the mean tuning correlation was 0.051 (Pearson) and 0.072 (Spearman), against 0.13 and 0.11 in the paper, with no test significant. Only 17 neurons passed the selection, and the paper's unit and trial selection is not recorded. The preparatory space capturing preparatory tuning best, the time course of the correlation, the controls and the model simulations all reproduced, but dimensionality results did not (best performance at 14 dimensions with no decline, and 15 to 18 components for preparatory activity against 7 to 10). Speed-task, single-electrode and EMG findings could not be tested because that data is not in the dandiset, and the anterior-posterior findings lacked unit locations.

DANDI:000138: Most main findings held in this data, but the preparatory and peri-movement tuning correlation only partly did, and its time course did not. Preferred directions captured preparatory tuning best in the peri-movement space, and the oscillator simulation behaved as reported. The tuning correlation was positive but larger than reported (mean r 0.195 against 0.13), and it was still 0.218 at movement onset, not near zero. The single-electrode datasets and the EMG are not in the dandiset, so two main findings could not be tested. Several results depend on analysis details the paper does not state, such as whether the PCA space is recomputed for each left-out condition.

DANDI:000139: Most central findings for the J-array data held up, but several could not be tested and two failed. Preferred directions were captured best in the peri-movement space, and the weak link between preparatory and peri-movement tuning was only partly reproduced (mean r 0.172 against 0.13). The preparatory dimensionality count and the anterior versus posterior comparison did not reproduce, the first likely because of very few trials per condition. The speed-task, single-electrode and EMG findings could not be tested because those data are not in the dandiset. Nothing in the files shows this session is the paper's own recording. The file still needs to be created with this text.

DANDI:000140: Few of the paper's main findings could be confirmed in this data, which holds one monkey's 100 trials over 27 of the 108 maze conditions. The weak positive correlation between preparatory and peri-movement tuning did not reproduce (mean r -0.079 against 0.13). The claim that a preferred direction in the peri-movement space best captures preparatory tuning only partly held, with no significant gaps between spaces. The linear-dynamics simulation reproduced. The findings for monkeys A and B, single-electrode recordings and EMG could not be tested because those data are missing.
