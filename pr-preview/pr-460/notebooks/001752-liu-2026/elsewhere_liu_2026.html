fig4d-sodium-current¶

Somatic Na+ currents in outside-out patches are lower in VENs (n = 14) than in L5 PCs (n = 21).

Per patch, the sweeps of each command step are averaged, the current is referred to the mean over 2 to 0.2 ms before the step and smoothed over 0.1 ms (10 samples at 100 kHz), and its most negative value in 0.1 to 3 ms after the step onset is the peak inward current (standard choices; the Methods give the steps, -80 to 0 mV in 5 mV for 25 ms, and leak subtraction online). The patch's Na+ current is the largest peak over the steps from -60 to 0 mV. Reading (b), current density, divides by the patch area estimated from capacitance; the files hold no capacitance, and the Methods give one area (~5 um2 from ~50 fF) and say similar areas were sampled, so (b) is (a) divided by a constant and gives the same test. Cell types come from the code's metadata_outSideOutSodiumCurrentRec.csv, which also gives a max_Na_current per cell, used as a check.
