Dataset unversioned files (the archive keeps no versions) · tested 2026-11-13 · 9 open findings

  • Data missingThe data set holds wild-type mice only. The optogenetic experiments of Figure 2 (29 POMC-Halorhodopsin Cre+ and Cre- mice, behaviour and cFos counts) are not in it, and the paper names no other deposit.
    Found by the attempt on Dijk et al. 2018 (dijk-2018-01, 2026-11-13)
  • Data missingSessions the paper describes are missing. WT15, WT16 and WT19 have one pretraining session where the paper gives two, and WT16 has no training 1 recording. The paper's df (H2,117 and H2,435) imply values that need these sessions; the data give 115 place cell values and 428 place cell-interneuron changes.
    Found by the attempt on Dijk et al. 2018 (dijk-2018-01, 2026-11-13)
  • OtherThe environment sessions 140731_WT15_testbox1 and 140731_WT15_testbox2 have the same position series, and three of the four listed units' spike trains begin with the same 20 spike times; two of the four listed units have identical trains, the other two differ in count (4371 against 4308 and 2615 against 2745 spikes), and the headers give different FIRST_TIMESTAMP values (11095 and 15235). They appear to be two sortings of one recording, and only one is counted. testbox1, the copy kept, fails the clock check (split-half map correlation no higher than with positions shifted by 60 s).
    Found by the attempt on Dijk et al. 2018 (dijk-2018-01, 2026-11-13)
  • Metadata errorThe Tracker files of the recordings of WT15 (after training 1 and 2) and WT16 (after training 2 and conflict) log a shock zone and 1 to 33 shocks with nonzero current, where the paper says recordings were made with the shock off. The WT15 training 2 recording is also deposited among the shock-on files (identical CRC), and WT16's conflict recording is named Conf1_1 in the TS files and Conf1_2 in its Tracker file. WT15's only training 2 Tracker file is 20 min long, where training 2 lasted 10 min, and holds one entrance.
    Found by the attempt on Dijk et al. 2018 (dijk-2018-01, 2026-11-13)
  • OtherFor WT15, WT16 and WT19 the conflict shock zone (centre 305 degrees) is 170 degrees from the training zone (135 degrees), where the paper says the zone was relocated 180 degrees; for WT20, WT26 and WT27 it is 180.
    Found by the attempt on Dijk et al. 2018 (dijk-2018-01, 2026-11-13)
  • Metadata errorThe data descriptor says the Spike and Time maps hold 256 x 256 pixels written as one column; most hold 1024 values, a 32 x 32 grid of 8 tracker pixels, and the place avoidance maps of WT15Hab1, WT16Hab1, 150720_WT19Ini1_2 and 150720_WT19Conf1_2 hold 1681 values, a 41 x 41 grid whose mapping to tracker pixels is not stated and could not be recovered exactly. On the 32 x 32 grid a bin is about 1.4 cm in the arena and 2 cm in the environment boxes, where the paper gives 1.5 cm/pixel. The plain spike-count maps are close to, but not the same as, maps made from the TS files (median r 0.988 and 0.985).
    Found by the attempt on Dijk et al. 2018 (dijk-2018-01, 2026-11-13)
  • Description missingThe data set holds smoothed maps (files ending RmSm and ArSm) without a description of the smoothing (a least-squares fit shows it acts like a sum over about 2 x 2 bins, shifted by half a bin; the smoothed dwell time totals 4.5 times the plain one), and the TS file variants (Rm, Ar, _4, .times, 0spks, test-fix, and a misnamed file 151215_WT26Ini2_2_spikes_cl-fix.P1C1Rm151215_WT26Ini2_2_spikes_cl-fix.P1C1Rm) are not described; the meaning of each was inferred from the headers and by comparing files.
    Found by the attempt on Dijk et al. 2018 (dijk-2018-01, 2026-11-13)
  • OtherThe WT19 pretraining session lasts 30 min, where the paper gives 20-min pretraining sessions. Environment sessions last 10, 20 or 30 min, which the paper does not state.
    Found by the attempt on Dijk et al. 2018 (dijk-2018-01, 2026-11-13)
  • Description missingThe files do not say how clusters were matched across the sessions of a day; the class lists give one entry per cell for the day, and this attempt assumes that a tetrode and cluster name is the same unit in every session.
    Found by the attempt on Dijk et al. 2018 (dijk-2018-01, 2026-11-13)
