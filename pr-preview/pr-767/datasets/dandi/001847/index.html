Dataset version 0.260610.1400 · tested 2026-11-03 · 8 open findings

  • Description missingThe files do not record the mouse lines. The subject genotype is "WT" for every mouse but one ("Ai148"), and strain is C57BL/6 for all, so no file says which mice carry VGAT-ChR2, jRGECO1a, GCaMP6f, tdTomato or GFP, or which received no opsin. The optogenetic, control and widefield groups can only be formed from the session lists of the paper's code.
    Found by the attempt on Bech et al. 2026 (bech-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Description missingSession groups are not recorded in a usable form. Muscimol and Ringer days, the injected area, baseline and injection days, and Context Off days have no field; the pharmacology, surgery and virus fields are "na" in every file, and most muscimol and Ringer sessions are typed behaviour_only_session. The Context Off trials still name pink and brown noise in context_background.
    Found by the attempt on Bech et al. 2026 (bech-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Metadata errorThe DeepLabCut series (processing/behavior/BehavioralTimeSeries) carry the unit "seconds" and a conversion of 1/28 (side camera) or 1/24 (top camera) for every series, likelihoods and angles included. The positions are in pixels, the whisker_angle series is in degrees (it reproduces the paper's 97.3 and 98.6 degrees unconverted), and pupil_area is in square pixels, for which the conversion would have to be 1/28 squared.
    Found by the attempt on Bech et al. 2026 (bech-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Description missingNo trial table column has a description (all "None"), and the perf codes 0 to 6 are not explained in the files; they were read from their cross-tabulation with trial type and lick (6 is an early lick).
    Found by the attempt on Bech et al. 2026 (bech-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Otherlick_time minus the stimulus time is about 100 ms longer than the paper's spout-contact times (470 against 370 ms for auditory W+), while the context differences and the tests match; lick_time minus response_window_start_time, which is 100 ms after the stimulus, gives the paper's 370, 400, 392 and 418 ms. What lick_time is measured from is not described.
    Found by the attempt on Bech et al. 2026 (bech-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • File format errorThree files (PB185_20240801_095038, PB186_20240801_110359, PB189_20240902_155436) have no BehavioralEpochs. In 76 of the 97 face-video sessions the DLC timestamps and data differ in length (printed in the DLC data cell), mostly by a few frames; in PB192_20241121_122848 the jaw series has 311,339 data points for 1,116,888 timestamps, and in PB177_20240529_121819 953,445 (jaw) and 948,779 (whisker) for 955,705.
    Found by the attempt on Bech et al. 2026 (bech-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Data missingThe light-sheet images of RSC axons (Figure 3, figure supplement 1C) are not in the dandiset and no other deposit is named.
    Found by the attempt on Bech et al. 2026 (bech-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • OtherIn 46 of the 46 widefield-with-optogenetics and photoactivation sessions, 910 of the trials (833 wf-opto, 77 photoactivation) have their first imaging frame after the stimulus more than one frame late, up to 81 s, so the imaging has gaps that the trial table does not mark.
    Found by the attempt on Bech et al. 2026 (bech-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
