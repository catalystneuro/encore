Summary: Most of the paper's cross-species comparisons of vagus nerve morphology hold up in this rat dataset with the authors' human and pig data: rat nerves are about ten times smaller, human and pig nerves have similar diameters, pigs have many more fascicles, and perineurium thickens with fascicle size, most in humans. Cervical fascicles are larger than subdiaphragmatic ones in all three species, but not significantly in pigs (p = 0.055) under the test matching the paper. The perineurium relationship differs between levels in human and pig, where the paper, naming no test, reports none. Of minor claims, the literature comparisons hold in part, the absence of age trends fails on uncorrected tests, and the carotid sheath bundles are untested.
