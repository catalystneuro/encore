Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds only the Figure 8 experiment, ten monolayer images (Packet 1, 3 dishes; Packet 2, 4; Packet 3, 3; first pulse 4 kV, one rotation, small array). The single-cell time-lapse of Figure 4 and the monolayer experiments of Figures 5, 6, 7 and 9 are not in it, and the paper names no other archive, so 15 of the 18 claims cannot be tested.
  • Metadata errorThe packet labels of the files labelled Protocol 2 and Protocol 3 appear exchanged with respect to the paper. The files labelled Protocol 3 (8 phases with 5-us delays) show the strongest central focusing (center 125 to 137% of the most peripheral ROIs per dish, raw) and the least uptake next to the electrodes, which the paper reports for Packet 2 (8 phases without delays), and the files labelled Protocol 2 show 63 to 106%, as the paper reports for Packet 3. With the two labels exchanged the order of Figure 8B appears. The dandiset alone cannot say whether its labels or the paper's figure are exchanged; each file's description defines the protocols as the paper defines the packets, so the data were tested as labelled and the question is unresolved.
  • Description missingThe images have no pixel size (ImageSeries resolution is 96 in nine files and 1.0 in one, unit "n.a."), so distances, which the paper's ROIs are defined by (+/-1.65 mm), rest on the camera's pixel and the objective. The files do not record where the electrodes are or which is e1, so they were found in the images and a reading that needs e1 could not be run. Exposure and gain are not recorded; the three Packet 1 dishes, imaged on 24 March 2021, have about twice the background of the seven others (mean 308 a.u., against 162 and 150 on 17 and 18 March), so a.u. values may not be comparable between imaging days.
  • Metadata errorThe dandiset's species summary is "Cricetulus griseus - Cricetulus aureus" for one cell line (CHO, Chinese hamster); each subject is a dish with age P1D, which does not apply to a cell line. The file of dish 10 names no microscope, camera or objective, where the other nine name the ORCA-Flash4.0 and a 10x objective, and its session starts on 18 March 2021 at 18:50, the evening after the other Packet 2 and 3 dishes.
