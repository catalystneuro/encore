Tests the findings of Li, Chen, Guo, Gerfen and Svoboda (2015) on dandiset 000010, which holds 99 extracellular sessions (19 mice) and 59 two-photon imaging planes (4 mice) from left ALM during the object location discrimination task. It tests behaviour, unit classes, trial-type selectivity and its time course, error trials, contralateral bias, PT and IT neurons and their antidromic latencies, and the imaging counts. The reaction time is run but blocked, as the paper does not fix how it is read from the signal. The photostimulation and anatomy findings are blocked, as the dandiset holds none of those data. Reproduction Anticipatory activity in mouse motor cortex (Chen et al. 2017)

Tests the findings of Chen, Li, Daie and Svoboda (2017) on dandiset 000015, which holds two-photon imaging of 105 image planes in left ALM and MM of six mice during a tactile delayed response task, with PT and IT labels in three of them. It tests the trial and neuron counts, the selectivity of single neurons for object location, lick direction and outcome, its map over ALM and MM, and the coding of PT and IT neurons. The plane depth, wide-field imaging, whisker tracking, photoinhibition and silicon probe data are not in the dandiset. Also uses DANDI:000015. All files
