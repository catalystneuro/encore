  • fig2c-brain-wide-increase

    TRAP-labeled (tdTomato+) cell density increases brain-wide when 4-OHT is given after male ejaculation, compared with the appetitive phase.

    As a result, we found the level of tdTomato expression increased in a brain-wide manner during the post-ejaculatory period when compared to the appetitive phase

    Results, Brain-wide mapping of neural response to male ejaculation, paragraph 1; Figure 2C and 2D

    How it will be judged

    Method from the Methods (Brain-wide analysis) and the Figure 2 legend, with the code where they are silent. Per brain, tdTomato+ cells per atlas region (Unified Anatomical Atlas, Kim lab, outside data; Figure 2.ipynb reads its label table atlas_info_KimRef_FPbasedLabel_v2.9) divided by region volume (cells per mm3), at hierarchy level 4. Per region a one-way ANOVA over the three groups (appetitive n=4, consummatory n=5, post-ejaculatory n=5), Benjamini-Hochberg correction at FDR 0.05 (Methods and legend; the code uses 0.1, shown as a variant), then Tukey's HSD (FWER 0.05) post-ejaculatory against appetitive for regions significant in the ANOVA. Open, and named here because it could change the outcome; (1) the level-4 family is the ten regions of the code or the twelve of the Figure 2C legend (adding VERM and HEM); (2) region volume is the atlas volume or the region's volume in each brain's own space; the paper gives neither. Supplementary file 2, which lists which regions were significant, is not in the text read, so "brain-wide" is read as follows, stated before analysis. Reproduced when the post-ejaculatory mean exceeds the appetitive mean in at least 80% of the level-4 regions of the family and at least half of the family is significant (ANOVA q < 0.05 and Tukey p < 0.05, post-ejaculatory higher). Partially reproduced when the direction holds in at least 80% but fewer than half are significant. Not reproduced otherwise. Every reading is run; when they give different outcomes the claim is blocked as methods_underspecified.
