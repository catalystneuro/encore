Hires et al. 2015 · eLife

Low-noise encoding of active touch by layer 4 in the somatosensory cortex

doi:10.7554/elife.06619 · paper key hires-2015

Datasets

  • DANDI:000013

Most of the paper's central findings on layer 4 held in this data, two did not, and two could not be settled. Cells in the C2 barrel fired about one spike per touch at 8 ms latency, with Fano factors near the binomial limit. Layer 4 cells outside C2 responded more weakly to touch, and layer 5 cells fired faster. Decoding of time since touch was less precise than reported (1.1 to 1.4 ms against 0.55 ms), and whisking-phase decoding did not reach the reported resolution. The share of spikes coupled to touch and the 15-neuron touch detection stayed undecided because the paper leaves out cutoff and decoder details, and some behavior and mouse-count claims differed (23 subjects, not 21).
