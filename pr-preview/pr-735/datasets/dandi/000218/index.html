Dataset version 0.250624.0439 · tested 2026-10-16 · 9 open findings

  • Data missingThe files hold no detected LS HFOs or hippocampal ripples, nor the detection channels and parameters, which the Methods say are stored with each event structure. Every analysis of events has to detect them anew, and the detection channels cannot be matched to the paper's.
    Found by the attempt on Tingley et al. 2019 (tingley-2019-01, 2026-10-16)
  • Description missingNine files (DT5 2016-10-07, DT7 2017-03-14, -16, -17, -19, 2017-04-07, -10, DT9 2017-04-28, 2017-05-26) store units without a location column, so their 268 units cannot be assigned to LS or hippocampus.
    Found by the attempt on Tingley et al. 2019 (tingley-2019-01, 2026-10-16)
  • Description missingHippocampal electrodes and units are labelled `hpc` or `ca3`; `hpc` does not say whether a site is in CA1, which the paper's analyses distinguish. Four DT7 files label all electrodes `unknown`.
    Found by the attempt on Tingley et al. 2019 (tingley-2019-01, 2026-10-16)
  • Data missingThe paper's CA3 recordings used a CA1 reference electrode to detect ripples. The 10 files with `ca3` electrodes hold 64 `ca3` and 32 `ls` channels and no CA1 channel, and the paper reports 25 CA3 and LS recordings against 10 here.
    Found by the attempt on Tingley et al. 2019 (tingley-2019-01, 2026-10-16)
  • Description missingElectrode x, y and z are NaN in every file. The probe geometry and its orientation in the brain, needed for the travelling-wave analysis of Figure 3, are not given, and the relative depth of each recording within LS (Figure 2) is not given.
    Found by the attempt on Tingley et al. 2019 (tingley-2019-01, 2026-10-16)
  • Data missingThe units carry no waveforms, no channel and no quality metric (the Methods compute isolation distance), so the CA1 depth of each neuron (Figure 7) cannot be estimated and single units cannot be told from multi-unit clusters; 11 units fire above 50 Hz on average. Wide-band data, from which waveforms could be re-extracted, are in 27 of the 98 files (DT1, DT2 and two DT9 files); 1579 of 2778 hippocampal units have none.
    Found by the attempt on Tingley et al. 2019 (tingley-2019-01, 2026-10-16)
  • Data missingThe two rats of the optogenetic experiments (Figure 6) are not in the dandiset.
    Found by the attempt on Tingley et al. 2019 (tingley-2019-01, 2026-10-16)
  • File format error25 units in 10 files have spike times out of order (1 to 2782 inversions each), against the NWB rule that spike times are sorted.
    Found by the attempt on Tingley et al. 2019 (tingley-2019-01, 2026-10-16)
  • OtherIn this attempt's detection, 35% of LS HFOs within 50 ms of a CA1 ripple fall on the same 0.8 ms sample as the ripple (71% in DT8, 62% in DT2, 7% in DT9), and the CA1-LS 120-200 Hz phase difference is consistent even at random times (median phase-locking value 0.134 against about 0.021 for random phases). Re-referencing the LS channel to another LS channel on its shank lowers the same-sample share to 4.1%. This points to a signal common to the LS and hippocampal channels, such as the reference, which the files do not document; some files list a device of "reference sites a few millimeters dorsal" without saying which channels use it.
    Found by the attempt on Tingley et al. 2019 (tingley-2019-01, 2026-10-16)
