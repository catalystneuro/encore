Summary: The paper's findings on fastigial photo-activation held up in this data: it disrupted the mice's choices, abolished ALM selectivity and collapsed ALM preparatory activity along the coding direction with a lasting effect. The contrast with dentate photo-activation held only in part, as the dentate effect was small but not absent. Photo-inhibition of the cerebellar nuclei reduced ALM selectivity by 73% to 82% without abolishing it, and the number and latency of modulated ALM neurons did not match, partly because the files hold more neurons than the paper reports and do not say which it used. The cerebellar nuclei recordings, lesions and anatomy are not deposited, so the findings that rest on them could not be tested.

Notes on the paper

What the attempts found about the paper itself: a method or rule it does not give, a place where it disagrees with itself, published code that does something else. What they found about the data is on each dataset’s page.

  • Selection not documentedStill presentThe datasets hold more ALM units than the paper reports for the same mice; 1186 units (1084 pyramidal) in the 9 photo-activation mice, where the paper reports 912 pyramidal neurons, and 737 units (682 pyramidal) in the 4 photo-inhibition mice, where it reports 490 units (454 pyramidal) over 19 sessions. The lab's own compiled analysis files deposited with CRCNS:alm-4 (analysis_compile_all_ALM_data.mat, analysis_compiled_all_ALM_DCNstim_FR.mat and analysis_compiled_all_ALM_DCNsilencing_selectivity.mat) hold exactly the deposited units, 1186 and 737 with the same cell types, and 20 photo-inhibition sessions, so they do not record the selection. Which units, and for the photo-inhibition mice which of the 20 sessions, entered the paper's analyses is not stated.
  • Published code disagreesNewThe analysis scripts deposited with CRCNS:alm-4 differ from the Methods in places. The photostimulation selectivity scripts fix each neuron's preferred trial type from 5 trials of each type, where the Methods say 10 for ALM; the coding direction script averages cd_t over windows starting from 3.0 s to 0.4 s before the go cue (pre-sample, sample and delay), where the Methods say the delay epoch; the selectivity test uses correct trials in the Fig. 2 script and correct and error trials in the photostimulation scripts. The coding direction compile (analysis_compiled_all_ALM_DCNstim_CD.mat) holds 23 sessions with 8 to 46 neurons, where the Methods give 28 of 39 sessions with that range; it keeps sessions with at least 3 trials per condition (the Methods say more than 5) and estimates the cd from all control trials but as many as the photostimulation trials of each type, not from a half. It declares the variables for the variance captured by the cd but never computes them.
  • Methods not availableStill presentFor the response latencies (4.5, 8.3 and 7.1 ms), how is a 10 ms bin placed in time (start, centre or end) and which window decides that a neuron is significantly modulated? Labelling by start gives 3.64 ms for fastigial and 3.30 ms for dentate photo-activation, by centre 8.64 and 8.30 ms. Only the start label matches both reported values, the dentate one at the lower bound of 8.3 +- 5.0; for CN photo-inhibition every choice gives 15 ms or more against 7.1.
  • Methods not availableStill presentFor the fractions captured by the coding direction (64.1% of selectivity, 27.0% of variance), are they ratios of RMS or of squared RMS, is selectivity measured on the trials that estimated the cd or on independent ones, and is activity variance over single trials or condition means? Only squared ratios on independent trials with single-trial variance give both values here (66.3% and 28.4%); other combinations give 73% to 85% and 36% to 59%.
  • Methods not availableNewWhich neurons were tested for photostimulation effects (the minimum numbers of control and photostimulation trials) and which sessions entered the coding direction analysis? With the deposited scripts' rules 177 to 190 ALM neurons are modulated by fastigial photo-activation (paper 227) and 20 of 34 sessions enter the coding direction analysis (paper 28 of 39).
  • Methods not availableStill presentfound on promptFor the population selectivity of Fig. 2c (E3), which epoch fixes each neuron's preferred trial type?
