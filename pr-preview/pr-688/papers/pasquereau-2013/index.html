Primary motor cortex of the parkinsonian monkey: altered neuronal responses to muscle stretch

Here is the paragraph to save there: In monkey V, the cell counts, the faster resting firing of pyramidal tract than corticostriatal neurons, and the fall in pyramidal tract rates after MPTP held up. The slowing of torque-induced movement did not: movements were faster and larger, but the torque command differed between states, so MPTP cannot be credited. The findings on torque responses (fractions of responsive cells, shorter latencies, lost directionality) could not be tested, because the described detection gave a false-positive rate of 0.23 against the 0.01 implied, which the Methods do not explain. Monkey L could not be tested, as its files hold no perturbation times. EMG and antidromic results were mixed.
