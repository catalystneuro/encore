    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • The paper's network runs in TensorFlow 1 (tf.contrib), which cannot be installed with Python 3.13. The network of network_construction.py is rebuilt with TensorFlow 2 operations from the released checkpoint; on the four patches of the first image its output differs from the model's own saved graph (model.ckpt.meta, run with training_phase False) by less than 1e-04 (the value printed in the notebook changes slightly between runs, at the level of floating-point round-off), and the classes agree on every pixel.
    • Resampling and histogram equalisation use the settings of scikit-image 0.13 (rescale and resize with order 1, mode constant, no anti-aliasing; an exact integer histogram), the release current when the code was published (it required scikit-image >= 0.12.3), as the defaults of the installed 0.26 differ.

Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Description missingThe dandiset does not say which mice are controls and which received cuprizone, though its description says it holds both groups; participants.tsv gives only the species. The groups, and the time points, are in the folder names of the NYU component of the White Matter Microscopy Database (OSF node adrcs), by mouse number; by these, the dandiset holds 8 control mice (7, 9, 25, 26, 27, 34, 35, 36) and 12 cuprizone mice.
  • Selection not documentedNeither the dandiset nor the paper's code records which 3 of the 20 mice formed the paper's test set (Table 1, 8 x 3 test and 8 x 17 training mice), so the mouse results of Table 3 cannot be repeated on the held-out mice alone. Every set of 3 mice gives values within the claims' tolerances here.
  • OtherThe masks in the dandiset are the 2025 revision (CHANGES, version 0.04, "Modify the masks of 155 images to separate all fibers with a 2px delineation"); the masks the paper scored against in 2018 are not in the dandiset.
  • Paper inconsistentThe paper gives the mouse pixel size as 0.002 um and the field of view as 6 x 9 um2; the sidecar files give 0.00236 um and the images are 2286 x 3762 pixels (5.39 x 8.88 um). The dandiset's README explains both. At 0.002 um the images would be too small for the released model's 512-pixel patches.
  • Metadata errorThe archive's assets summary for version 0.250512.1625 gives 11 samples, where the dandiset holds 158 images (samples.tsv has 158 rows); sample ids (sample-0001 to sample-0011) are reused across mice, and the summary counts distinct ids.
  • Other21 of the 158 axonmyelin masks are grey plus alpha pngs (alpha 255 everywhere), the others grey; their grey channel agrees with the axon and myelin masks in every image.
  • Data missingThe dandiset holds only the mouse TEM images of the paper. The SEM rat and human test images, the SEM training images, the macaque test image and the full rat spinal cord slice of Figures 6 and 7 are not in it. Of these, macaque TEM images of ref. 7 were found in the White Matter Microscopy Database, without a record of which the paper tested; the SEM rat test and training images in the AxonDeepSeg SEM dataset (GitHub, doi:10.5281/zenodo.5498378), which the paper does not name; and two candidate panoramas of a rat cervical spinal cord in the White Matter Microscopy Database, neither named as the paper's slice. The human SEM test image was not found.
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the paper's authors. Which macaque image formed the test sample of Table 3 (the White Matter Microscopy Database holds 8 samples of 3 images, with masks for one image per sample), which masks were its ground truth, and at what pixel size was it segmented? On the 7 image and mask pairs found, the accuracy is 0.573 to 0.763 against 0.8438 in Table 3.
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the paper's authors. Are sub-rat6 sample-data15 and sub-rat8 sample-V915 of the AxonDeepSeg SEM dataset (doi:10.5281/zenodo.5498378) the SEM test images Rat 1 and Rat 2 of Table 1, and are its masks at tag r20211130 the ground truth they were scored against?   • OtherIn the White Matter Microscopy Database macaque folder (outside the dandiset), the masks of sample 4 fit none of its three images, and the NIfTI headers give a voxel size of 2.0 with no pixel size.
  • OtherThe SEM test images of Rat 1 and Rat 2 and most of the SEM training images of Table 1 are public in the SEM dataset of AxonDeepSeg (https://github.com/axondeepseg/data_axondeepseg_sem, doi:10.5281/zenodo.5498378), identified by pixel size, field of view and fixation, though the paper's Data availability statement names only the White Matter Microscopy Database. Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • Tested on the mouse images only, as the criterion says.myelin-dice-below-axon-dice sensitivity-above-93
  • The correlation threshold of 0.5 for a counterpart was set on development runs on mice 25 and 26, before the full run, and written into the criterion then (see the claims file).osf-group-labels-link
  • The paper's network runs in TensorFlow 1 (tf.contrib), which cannot be installed with Python 3.13. The network of network_construction.py is rebuilt with TensorFlow 2 operations from the released checkpoint; on the four patches of the first image its output differs from the model's own saved graph (model.ckpt.meta, run with training_phase False) by less than 1e-04 (the value printed in the notebook changes slightly between runs, at the level of floating-point round-off), and the classes agree on every pixel.table3-tem-mice-accuracy
  • Resampling and histogram equalisation use the settings of scikit-image 0.13 (rescale and resize with order 1, mode constant, no anti-aliasing; an exact integer histogram), the release current when the code was published (it required scikit-image >= 0.12.3), as the defaults of the installed 0.26 differ.table3-tem-mice-accuracy
  • The pixel size is the dandiset's 0.00236 um (sidecar files), as the criterion says, not the 0.002 um of Table 1; at 0.002 um the images would be 457 pixels high after resampling, smaller than the model's 512-pixel patch, so the code could not run them.table3-tem-mice-accuracy
  • The ground truth is the dandiset's 2025 masks, in which every fibre is separated from its neighbours by a 2-pixel line (CHANGES, version 0.04), not the masks the paper scored against, which are not in the dandiset. The axon class is the axon mask and the myelin class the myelin mask.table3-tem-mice-accuracy
  • Neither test-set reading is the paper's set of 3 held-out mice, which is not recorded; the range over every set of 3 mice is shown. Mouse 25 has 6 images, the others 8; no mouse has more than 8, so the criterion's rule for more than 8 images does not apply.table3-tem-mice-accuracy
  • The control mice of reading (b) are labelled through the OSF deposit (osf-group-labels-link), as the dandiset names no group.table3-tem-mice-accuracy
  • Segmentation, ground truth and test-set readings as in table3-tem-mice-accuracy.table3-tem-mice-dice-detection
  • score_analysis of segmentation_scoring.py is reimplemented with the same rules (predicted objects of area above 2 pixels, their integer centroids kept as a set, a ground-truth object with at least one centroid is one true positive, centroids in no ground-truth object are false positives, values rounded to 3 decimals per image as the code does).table3-tem-mice-dice-detection
  • Data from the AxonDeepSeg SEM dataset on GitHub, not the dandiset (data elsewhere), identified as the paper's test images by the pixel size, field of view and fixation of Table 1.table3-sem-rat-accuracy
  • Assumed that sub-rat6 sample-data15 and sub-rat8 sample-V915 are the paper's Rat 1 and Rat 2 test images. The paper does not name its SEM test images or deposit them; these two match Table 1's test rows in pixel size, field of view and fixation, and six of the dataset's other eight images match its training rows, which supports but does not prove the identification.table3-sem-rat-accuracy
  • The masks are those of tag r20211130, unchanged since the first commit (2020-10-08); the masks the paper scored against in 2018 are not public. The 2025 masks of Rat 1 are a variant.table3-sem-rat-accuracy
  • Segmentation as segment.py does for default_SEM_model_v1 (working pixel size 0.1 um for a 512 patch, generate_resolution; overlap 25; no contrast inversion, which segment.py applies only for default_TEM_model_v1), with the network rebuilt in TensorFlow 2 as for the TEM model. The grey channel is used for Rat 1, whose png has an alpha channel (255 everywhere).table3-sem-rat-accuracy
  • Data from the White Matter Microscopy Database, not the dandiset (data elsewhere).table3-tem-macaque-accuracy
  • The pixel size is Table 1's 0.009 um; the NIfTI headers give a voxel size of 2.0, which is not a pixel size. 2300 x 3040 pixels at 0.009 um is 20.7 x 27.4 um, the 27 x 21 um2 of Table 1.table3-tem-macaque-accuracy
  • Each sample's masks are paired with the image they fit by grey-level contrast, as the deposit does not say which image they belong to.table3-tem-macaque-accuracy
  • Ground truth axon where avf.nii is above 0 (values 0 and 1/255), myelin where myelin.nii is 1.table3-tem-macaque-accuracy
  • Data, pixel size and the pairing of masks with images as in table3-tem-macaque-accuracy.macaque-myelin-underestimated
  • Assumed that the masks of ref. 7 in the White Matter Microscopy Database are the ground truth the paper scored its macaque sample against. The paper's macaque sample and its labels come from ref. 7 ("More details can be found about the generation of labels for the macaque 7", Methods, Ground truth labelling), and its Data availability statement names this database; no other macaque masks are public. The paper does not say which masks it used, and the other macaque measures, far below Table 3 (table3-tem-macaque-accuracy), leave open that they differ. The question to the paper's authors (methods_unavailable finding) asks which masks were used.macaque-myelin-underestimated
