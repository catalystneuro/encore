Ito et al. 2015 · The Journal of Neuroscience

Distinct Neural Representation in the Dorsolateral, Dorsomedial, and Ventral Parts of the Striatum during Fixed- and Free-Choice Tasks

doi:10.1523/jneurosci.1962-14.2015 · paper key ito-2015

Datasets

  • DANDI:001642

The behavioural findings held: the extinction tests (fixed tones stay biased while the choice tone moves toward chance) and choices that depend on the previous trial. The finding that the fixed-parameter-free reinforcement model with time-varying parameters predicts choices best did not hold, because Markov models scored higher, though the paper omits the model equations and the agent assumed them. The main neural findings for dorsolateral, dorsomedial and ventral striatum could not be tested. The dataset has no waveforms or recording depths, so the paper's unit selection cannot be rebuilt. Several of these analyses also depend on methods the paper leaves open, such as how activation or information is defined, and the readings disagree.
