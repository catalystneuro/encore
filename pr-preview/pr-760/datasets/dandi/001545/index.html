Dataset version 0.250809.1653 · tested 2026-11-03 · 7 open findings

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds only the current clamp recordings. The soma positions and the morphology from the biocytin fills (splay angle, branch points, dendritic length, 15 parameters), on which Fig 1F to I, Fig 2C to H, Fig S1 and the multiple regression rest, are not in it, and neither are the Golgi and RNAScope data; 23 of the paper's 56 claims cannot be tested for this reason.
    Found by the attempt on Rajebhosale et al. 2025 (rajebhosale-2025-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Selection not documentedThe files hold 13 WT GCs and 9 WT SGCs, where the paper's electrophysiology has 14 and 9, and 12 V321L GCs and 14 V321L SGCs, where the paper has 10 and 15. Nothing in the files says which WT GC is missing or why the V321L cell types differ, so the paper's cell sets cannot be rebuilt.
    Found by the attempt on Rajebhosale et al. 2025 (rajebhosale-2025-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Metadata errorEvery CurrentClampStimulusSeries stores the current in picoamperes (values -60 to 300) under the unit amperes with conversion 1.
    Found by the attempt on Rajebhosale et al. 2025 (rajebhosale-2025-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Metadata errorThe electrode cell_id is VP neuron in all 48 files, though the cells are dentate granule cells.
    Found by the attempt on Rajebhosale et al. 2025 (rajebhosale-2025-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Metadata errorThe general stimulus field says neurons were kept near -65 mV with a constant current and stepped from -60 to 200 pA. The voltage before the steps is -68 to -89 mV in every cell, 14 cells were stepped to 250 or 300 pA, and the bias_current attribute is 10 pA in every sweep, so whether a holding current was applied, and how much, cannot be told. Every sweep of every file also stores the same bridge balance (20 MOhm) and capacitance compensation (1 pF), which look like defaults.
    Found by the attempt on Rajebhosale et al. 2025 (rajebhosale-2025-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Description missingThe genotype is coded only as VV or LL in the subject ids; the surgery field says VV is wild type and LL the valine-to-leucine mutant, and GEO agrees, but the cell type is given only as the end of the session description (semilunar or not semilunar), with no column or field of its own.
    Found by the attempt on Rajebhosale et al. 2025 (rajebhosale-2025-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Data missingGEO GSE280167, where the paper deposited the snMultiome data, holds the raw feature-barcode matrix of every barcode per mouse (genes and peaks) and a smoothed ATAC cut-site track per sample. The nuclei kept after filtering and doublet removal, the cluster labels the paper's snRNA-Seq figures rest on, and the per-nucleus ATAC fragments are not deposited.
    Found by the attempt on Rajebhosale et al. 2025 (rajebhosale-2025-01, 2026-11-03)
