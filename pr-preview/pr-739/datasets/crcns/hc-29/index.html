What the attempts that used this dataset found about the data themselves, for its owner and the archive, under the version of the dataset each was found on.

Dataset unversioned files (the archive keeps no versions) · tested 2026-11-13 · 5 open findings

  • Data missingThe data set holds no position, speed or direction data, and no trial, lap or epoch markers. The paper's Methods describe position tracked at about 30 Hz by overhead cameras, and every finding from Figure 2 on (rate maps, spatial information, place fields, direction of travel, maze location, laps, and the matching of CA1 and LS fields for the cross-correlations) uses it, so none of the paper's findings beyond the counts of units recorded can be tested on the data set. The data description names only spikes and time files, and the paper's Data availability names only this data set.
    Found by the attempt on Wirtshafter et al. 2020 (wirtshafter-2020-01, 2026-11-13)
  • Metadata errorIn rat 7, two spike trains, of 1033 and 1441 spikes, are deposited byte for byte under 18 and 25 cluster names, across 16 of the 19 rat 7 recordings with clusters and three tetrodes (18, 27 and 32). The copies cannot all be the recordings they are filed under, as 27 of them hold spikes after the end of their recording's timestamps (5403 spikes in all, none before the start), and these are the only spikes in the data set outside their recording. The 452 LS clusters, the paper's count, include these 41 copies.
    Found by the attempt on Wirtshafter et al. 2020 (wirtshafter-2020-01, 2026-11-13)
  • OtherSeveral clusters hold the spikes of others. On rat 6's tetrode 26, in each of the 14 recordings with clusters there, the largest cluster holds at least 99.6% of the spikes of each other cluster on the tetrode (47 of 47 such clusters), as if a cluster of all spikes were deposited beside the units; two of these clusters (2017-05-01, cl_7 and cl_9) are identical. Over the data set 89 pairs of clusters on one recording share at least 99% of the smaller train's spikes within 0.5 ms. A further 24 pairs on neighbouring tetrodes (LS 29 and 30, 21 and 22; CA1 1 and 6, in rats 7, 9, 10 and 12) share 71.5 to 98.5% of their spikes, against a median of 0.5% (at most 1.3%) for the same pairs with one train shifted by 1 s, so the same unit or the same noise was probably sorted twice. The data set does not say which clusters are single units, and of its 630 clusters 588 are distinct trains.
    Found by the attempt on Wirtshafter et al. 2020 (wirtshafter-2020-01, 2026-11-13)
  • Description missingTimes.mat is described only as "time files for all sessions". It holds 57 continuous time bases of the six rats, most at 0.512 ms intervals, six of rat 6 at 3.2 ms and one of rat 7 at 0.0512 ms, lasting from 2.5 min to 31 h, while the paper analyses one 30 min maze session a day. Nothing marks which part of a recording is the maze session. Read whole, no recording changes its sampling interval or has a gap of 0.1 s, so the time bases do not mark the session. Between its first and last spike no recording has a stretch of 60 s without a spike from any of its clusters (45 of the 46 recordings with clusters checked; rat 7's 2017-08-02 holds only copies of the trains deposited many times). The spikes begin within 0.09 h of each recording's start; in rats 7, 8 and 9 they span 0.5 to 0.9 h, which may be the session, though nothing says so, while in rat 6 they span up to 4.9 h and in rats 10 and 12 the whole 3.8 to 6.4 h recording. Eleven recordings have no cluster. The sampling intervals are not stated. The six rat 6 recordings at 3.2 ms have 25 brief gaps of 4.9 to 93 ms, and two of them repeat timestamps (1121 in 3 s of 2017-04-21, 3280 at the end of 2017-04-26).
    Found by the attempt on Wirtshafter et al. 2020 (wirtshafter-2020-01, 2026-11-13)
  • Description missingThe cluster names follow the pattern the description gives (session date, tetrode, cluster) with the rat and a session suffix, but ten names of rat 7 (2017-07-21, tetrode 28) write cl1 for cl_1, and one LS cluster of rat 9 is labelled MUA9, a multi-unit label, with no description of what it holds. The CA1 clusters come from two of the six rats (10 and 12), which neither the description nor the paper states. Tetrodes 1 to 16 are CA1 and 17 to 32 LS, which the files show but the description does not say.
    Found by the attempt on Wirtshafter et al. 2020 (wirtshafter-2020-01, 2026-11-13)
