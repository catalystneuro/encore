Brief Stimuli Cast a Persistent Long-Term Trace in Visual Cortex.

Matthias Fritsche, Samuel G. Solomon, Floris P. de Lange

Summary: The paper's main findings held up in this data. In mouse visual cortex the response to a grating was reduced when a grating of the same orientation came up to 8 trials earlier, decaying with a half-life of about 14 s, while in the thalamus adaptation was limited to the last one or two trials, a result that rests on two reading choices. Adaptation by tuning, its accumulation over trials and the longer trace after static gratings also reproduced. The blank-baseline analysis did not: suppression after an orthogonal grating lasted to 2-back, not only 1-back. A few mice broke the across-mice consistency, and the simulation control failed a criterion that chance noise alone breaks.

  • openasked about fig8-no-spurious-long-term

    The criterion reads "no spurious adaptation effects" as every n-back ratio over 2 to 10 (or 2 to 50) having a 95% interval that contains 1, for both stimulus sets, with a partial rule of at most one excluding n within 0.01 of 1. Method differences

  • Units the files label `noise` are left out, read as part of the "default quality metrics of the AllenSDK" (Methods, Unit exclusion), whose units table omits them; the unit counts support this reading. The variant without it is shown.units-responsive-count
  • Channels labelled `grey` or `VIS` are not counted as regions; the paper names only areas.units-responsive-count
  • The invalid intervals listed in 13 sessions are not applied; the paper does not mention them. A variant leaves out, for all units of the session, the presentations that overlap them, as current trials and from the responsiveness test (a superset of a per-probe rule); the 1-back ratios move by at most 0.014 (LP) for drifting and 0.004 for static gratings.units-responsive-count
  • The ratio is pooled over current orientations within each unit (O1a); the Methods' "dividing the firing rate of each neuron for repeat by that for orthogonal" (Figure 1D legend) reads most directly this way. All readings are shown.fig1d-v1-1back
  • Tertiles are formed on all responsive units of the region before units with a zero-rate cell are left out, so the tertiles are not equal in the ANOVA (194, 192, 116 in V1); the paper does not say how such units were handled.
