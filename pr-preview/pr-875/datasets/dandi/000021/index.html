Dataset version 0.251116.2246 · tested 2026-11-21 · 6 open findings

  • Description missingThe units table holds every sorted unit (74658), with a `quality` column marking 20182 as `noise` and the AllenSDK quality metric columns (isi_violations, amplitude_cutoff, presence_ratio and others), none of which has a description in the files. The paper's unit counts are matched when the `noise` units are left out, as the AllenSDK's units table does; nothing in the files says that they should be, and keeping them changes the thalamic decay results.
    Found by the attempt on Fritsche et al. 2022 (fritsche-2022-01, 2026-11-21)
  • OtherThree sessions (715093703, 719161530, 721123822) hold 598 drifting gratings in place of the 600 the paper and the stimulus design give; one or two direction and frequency conditions there have 14 presentations.
    Found by the attempt on Fritsche et al. 2022 (fritsche-2022-01, 2026-11-21)
  • OtherThirteen sessions list invalid intervals tagged with a probe, totalling 12 to 2931 s, and several overlap the drifting or static grating epochs (375 drifting presentations in 5 sessions, 7001 static in 10). Their meaning is not described in the files; the paper does not mention them, and they are not applied in the main analysis. Leaving those presentations out changes the 1-back ratios by at most 0.014.
    Found by the attempt on Fritsche et al. 2022 (fritsche-2022-01, 2026-11-21)
  • Description missing3723 of the units that pass the quality metrics sit on channels whose area is `grey` and 686 on `VIS`, labels that name no structure; they reach 50 responsive units, the paper's rule for a region, and are left out here.
    Found by the attempt on Fritsche et al. 2022 (fritsche-2022-01, 2026-11-21)
  • OtherChecks that passed. 32 mice, one session each, with the genotypes the paper gives; stimulus parameters, durations and spacing as the Methods describe; no recording or unit deposited twice; the V1 orientation tuning is locked to the stimulus tables in every session (true labels against labels shifted by whole presentations).
    Found by the attempt on Fritsche et al. 2022 (fritsche-2022-01, 2026-11-21)
  • OtherStatic blank sweeps number 189 to 197 of 6000 presentations per session, about one in 31, where the Methods say roughly one in 25.
    Found by the attempt on Fritsche et al. 2022 (fritsche-2022-01, 2026-11-21)
