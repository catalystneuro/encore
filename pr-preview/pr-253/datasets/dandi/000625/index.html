Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Metadata errorThe imaging rate is stated as 5.7981 Hz for all three sessions. For sessions 21324 and 21322 that makes imaging 680 s long against 900 s and 879 s of behaviour, and the population activity does not follow running (r = 0.16 and 0.09). Rates of 4.395 and 4.385 Hz give r = 0.72 and 0.87, decode running at 0.94 and 0.89 balanced accuracy, and make all three sessions about 898 s long. Session 21333's stated rate fits.
  • Metadata errorThe unit of the dF/F series is given as lumens; the values are dF/F fractions.
  • Data missingThe Fluorescence series is the same HDF5 object as the DfOverF series, so the raw fluorescence the description lists is not in the files.
  • Metadata errorThe description of the subtype column reads "Recording location in Middle Earth", and the subtype table has 232 rows for 231 ROIs (id 231 has no ROI).
  • Description missingThe labels NOTHING and UNIDENTIFIED, the label "AAC (RIGHT CELL)", and the reward and water intervals have no description.
  • Identifier missingNothing states that a ROI index refers to the same cell in every session. The data are consistent with it (velocity correlations of the same index agree across sessions above a shuffled null) but tuning curves agree above the null for only one pair of sessions.
  • Data missingThe files hold no depth or position of the ROIs in the imaged volume, which several of the paper's analyses use, and no lick record.
  • Metadata errorAll three sessions are described as "session 1 of 4", and the experiment description names four random-foraging sessions; only three are in the dandiset. The session ids (21322, 21324, 21333) and the start times (21333 two days before the other two) give different orders, and nothing else in the files dates the sessions.
  • Description missingThe pixel masks lie on a strip of the 231 cropped frames side by side (x from 0 to 10625), the imaging plane's origin_coords (0, 0) and grid_spacing (50, 50 um) are placeholders, and there are no reference images, so a ROI has no position in the field of view or in depth.
  • Selection not documentedThe dandiset holds one of the paper's 16 mice and only its random-foraging sessions, without saying which part of the paper it covers.
  • Metadata errorThe emission wavelength is given as 920 nm, equal to the excitation, and the subject's age is P60D in sessions two days apart, where the paper gives 3 to 5 months.
  • Description missingThe description lists raw images of individual cropped ROIs, but each file holds a single cropped series (33 x 46 or 25 x 46 pixels) without saying which ROI it shows.
  • OtherWith the paper's place-field rule, which requires a peak above 100% dF/F, 1.0% of ROIs qualify, as only 1.1% of tuning curves reach 1.0 dF/F. The dF/F in the files may be scaled or smoothed differently from the paper's.

Reproduction Molecularly identified CA1 interneurons during random foraging (Geiller, Vancura et al. 2020)

Tests the findings of Geiller, Vancura et al. (2020) that can be tested on dandiset 000625, which holds three random-foraging sessions of one mouse with 231 interneurons, 74 of them assigned one of the paper's six subtypes. It checks the imaging clock against the behaviour, then measures each interneuron's correlation with running speed, run-stop responses, spatial selectivity, position decoding and the stability of tuning curves within and across sessions. The sharp-wave ripple, novel-context, goal-oriented learning and VIP experiments are not in the dandiset. All files
