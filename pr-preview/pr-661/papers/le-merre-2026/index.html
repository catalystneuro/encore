A prefrontal cortex map based on single-neuron activity

Here is the paragraph so it can be saved as that file: Most descriptive findings hold up in this data, but the hierarchy findings could not be tested and two cross-region claims did not reproduce. Wide and narrow waveform classes, higher rates in narrow-spiking units, regular firing in prefrontal cortex, stable firing categories over time, and prefrontal subregion maps all reproduced. Correlations of firing categories with the cortical hierarchy, and the ILA-specific activity module, stayed undecided because the paper gives no rule for numbering categories on a retrained map. The match between narrow and wide units across regions and the high-rate tone-responsive result did not reproduce. The replication on International Brain Laboratory data was too large to attempt.

2026-11-01 · on DANDI:001260

  • ✓Reproduced Dataset KI holds 24,248 single units from 99 Neuropixels recordings in awake head-fixed mice, 52% of them in the PFC and 48% in other brain regions.
  • ✓Reproduced Peak-to-trough durations are bimodal, splitting units into 19,186 ww units (81.1%) and 4,200 nw units (17.7%), with 862 intermediate units left unclassified.
  • ✓Reproduced Narrow-waveform units have significantly higher firing rates, burstiness and memory than wide-waveform units.
  • ◐Partially reproduced Firing metrics in sleep-like epochs differ from those in active spontaneous epochs, which is why sleep-like epochs are excluded.
  • ?Not reproduced Sleep-like episodes occupy a median 6% (IQR 0 to 18%) of recording time; sessions hold a median 227 (IQR 172 to 284) spontaneous active epochs and 19 (IQR 1 to 84) sleep-like epochs.
  • ?Not reproduced Across ww units, a linear correlation does not reveal a dependence between burstiness and memory.
  • ◐Partially reproduced On the ww SOM, burstiness and firing rate vary along perpendicular gradients, while high memory occurs only with medium to low burstiness, so high memory and high burstiness are mutually exclusive.
  • ✓Reproduced PFC ww units are widely spread on the SOM, with many matching regular-firing (low burstiness) nodes and few in anti-memory territory, while units from other regions populate different territories.
  • ✓Reproduced About 57% of ww units keep their category from one block of about 50 spontaneous epochs to the next, far above the 18% expected by chance from the marginal distributions.
  • ◐Partially reproduced Block-wise category assignment is less stable for low-rate categories (1 to 3, 7 and 8) than for high-rate categories, and low-rate regular categories 1 to 3 mostly transition among themselves, as do the low-rate bursty categories 7 and 8.
  • ◐Partially reproduced Superficial cortical layers (L2/3) are enriched in bursty ww units, while deep layers (L5 to 6) are enriched in regular-firing ww units.
  • ✓Reproduced Most PFC subregions (ILA, PL, ACAd, ACAv, ORBm and ORBvl) are enriched in low-rate, regular-firing ww units (categories 1 to 3).
  • ⊘Blocked ILA and PL are both strongly and significantly enriched in the most regular-firing category 1.
  • ⊘Blocked ORBl, MOs and AIv are significantly enriched in bursty category 8 units, setting them apart from the activity-defined prefrontal entity.
  • ⊘Blocked Thalamus and hippocampal formation are strongly and significantly enriched in bursty units (categories 6 to 8), unlike the PFC.
  • ⊘Blocked Regional enrichment profiles of dataset KI are highly similar to those of dataset IBL Passive; for category 1, enrichment across 14 cortical (sub)regions correlates between the two datasets.
  • ◐Partially reproduced Among nw units, ILA, PL and ACAd/v are significantly enriched in high-rate, regular-firing units with high memory, and ORBl in high-rate, bursty units.
  • ?Not reproduced Brain (sub)regions enriched in low-rate, regular-firing ww units are enriched in high-rate, regular-firing nw units, and regions enriched in low-rate bursty ww units are enriched in high-rate bursty nw units.
  • ⊘Blocked In dataset KI, enrichment of cortical (sub)regions in low-rate, regular-firing units (categories 1 to 3, category 2 shown) correlates positively with the Harris cortical hierarchy score, driven by the split between low-hierarchy sensory regions and high-hierarchy PFC subregions.
  • ⊘Blocked In dataset IBL Passive, across 32 cortical (sub)regions, enrichment in the PFC's signature categories 1 to 3 correlates positively with cortical hierarchy.
  • ⊘Blocked Cortical hierarchy correlates even more strongly, and negatively, with enrichment in bursty, low-memory units (categories 6 to 8), in both datasets.
  • ⊘Blocked Restricted to PFC subregions, enrichment in unit categories shows no relationship to cortical hierarchy.
  • ✓Reproduced The paper's per-unit metrics files on Zenodo (record 14205018) can be matched to the dandiset's units, and the matched records agree on what both describe, so that the flatmap position of each unit can be taken from them.
  • ✓Reproduced The PFC flatmap is parcellated into 42 dataROIs that respect subregion boundaries, each holding about 200 deep-layer ww units.
  • ✓Reproduced Low-rate, regular-firing categories 1 and 3 are significantly enriched in dataROIs in ILA and PL, while low-rate, bursty categories 7 and 8 are enriched in dataROIs in MOs and ORBl, the ORBl enrichment pinpointed to its most ventral dataROI.
  • ⊘Blocked Clustering dataROIs by enrichment profile gives activity modules; ILA forms a homogeneous ILA-specific module bordering a similar module in adjacent PL and ACAd, while other modules cross cytoarchitectural boundaries and occur in several places.
  • ⊘Blocked Across GaoROIs, enrichment in low-rate, regular-firing category 1 units correlates positively with the intra-PFC hierarchy score of Gao et al.
  • ⊘Blocked Across GaoROIs, enrichment in high-rate, bursty category 6 units correlates negatively with intra-PFC hierarchy.
  • ⊘Blocked GaoROIs 19, 20 and 41 in PL have exceptionally high hierarchy scores and the strongest category 1 enrichment, while GaoROI 23 in ventral ORBl has the lowest hierarchy score and the strongest category 1 depletion.
  • ✓Reproduced Deep-layer PFC ww tone responses cluster into eight categories ordered by peak delay; most units have flat or negative responses (category 8), categories 1 to 4 have sharp peaks with consistent onsets, and categories 5 to 7 have variable onsets and persistent responses.
  • ◐Partially reproduced Tone-response enrichment maps of the PFC do not follow cytoarchitectural boundaries; the earliest-responding units (category 1) are enriched in anterior ACAd and ORBvl and parts of PL, and tone response modules reveal heterogeneity within subregions.
  • ✓Reproduced Tone-responsive ww units (ZETA test) are enriched in spatial patches distributed across the PFC.
  • ⊘Blocked In dataset IBL, of 16,148 PFC ww units, only 105 are tuned to visual stimulus side, 1,109 to choice and 2,868 to feedback.
  • ⊘Blocked Across 73 IBL ROIs, choice-tuned units are enriched in a territory spanning central MOs, FRP and anterior ACAd, ORBvl and PL, and consistently depleted in ILA, ORBm, ORBl and ACAv; feedback-tuned units are enriched in AId, FRP and anterior MOs, ORBvl and PL, and depleted in ILA, ORBm and ACAd/ACAv; visual stimulus tuning is strongly enriched in a single IBL ROI in MOs.
  • ⊘Blocked Across GaoROIs, enrichment in choice-tuned units correlates with the intra-PFC hierarchy score (R = 0.48, P = 0.002), while correlations are weak for visual tuning and absent for feedback tuning.
  • ✓Reproduced Enrichment in tone-responsive units does not correlate with intra-PFC hierarchy across GaoROIs.
  • ⊘Blocked No pair of PFC maps is strongly congruent, but the choice-tuning map is most similar to the spontaneous category 1 map, the feedback-tuning map to the category 6 map, and the tone-responsiveness map to the category 8 map.
  • ?Not reproduced At the single-unit level, tone-responsive units (tone categories 1 to 7) are more often than expected high-rate spontaneous units (categories 4 to 6), and units without clear positive tone responses (category 8) tend to have low spontaneous rates (categories 1 to 3, 7 and 8).
  • ⊘Blocked Units with choice tuning and units with feedback tuning are associated with high-rate, Poissonian spontaneous firing (category 4).
  • ⊘Blocked Firing metric distributions do not differ significantly across behavioral settings.
