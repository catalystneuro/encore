What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Description missingThe paper's processed behaviour is not in the dandiset; the linearized position, the trial conditions used for every rate and phase map, and the CA1 pyramidal layer channel used as the theta reference in each session must be rebuilt, and several outcomes depend on how.

Reproduction Spatial phase coding in the lateral septum (Tingley and Buzsaki 2018)

Tests the findings of Tingley and Buzsaki (2018) on dandiset 000213, which holds 67 sessions of simultaneous hippocampal and lateral septum recordings from five rats running on alternation mazes and a linear track. It decodes position from the spike rate and the spike theta phase of each LS and hippocampal unit, finds place fields, measures spatial information, relates phase coding to recording depth, and measures hippocampal-LS theta coherence and theta-gamma coupling, and detects hippocampal-LS cell assemblies on a random sample of pairs. Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • The criterion was written after the counts had been seen in the data check.ls-neuron-count hpc-unit-count
  • As for fig2-ls-phase-vs-shuffle.fig2-ls-phase-beats-rate fig2e-ls-phase-lower-mse
  • The labels hpc and ca3 are read as CA1 and CA3; the dandiset does not define hpc.hpc-unit-count
  • Conditions are formed from trial type, direction and start and end site, as the paper's trial conditions are not in the dandiset; the count depends on them.ls-active-neurons
  • Linearization (path fraction, 200 bins), conditions and the theta channel are rebuilt; the paper's are not in the dandiset.fig2-ls-phase-vs-shuffle
  • The binning of spike phases (bz_phaseMap2Bins) is not public; the circular mean per bin is used.fig2-ls-phase-vs-shuffle
  • 10 active LS neurons of DT2 20160217, which has no hippocampal channel, are not decoded.fig2-ls-phase-vs-shuffle
  • 58 sessions are decoded, not 62; DT2 20160217 has no theta reference, and three sessions have no LS condition that passes.fig2-ls-phase-beats-rate
  • MSE is in bins squared with 200 bins of about 1.1 cm; the paper's bin count is not given.fig2fg-mse-difference
  • Only 531 hippocampal place fields are found against 1,820 (see ls-few-place-fields).fig2fg-mse-difference
  • 531 fields in 42 sessions, against 1,820 in 45; the field detector finds fewer fields than the paper's.fig1i-hpc-rate-phase-equivalent
  • Per-trial rate maps are smoothed with a Gaussian of SD 4 bins (FMAToolbox Smooth in bz_firingMap1D rateMaps, with tau = round(nBins/50) as in scratch_07_25_17_calc_firingMaps.m); the paper's bin count is not known.   • Per-trial rate maps come from a linearization by path fraction with about one position sample per bin and trial, which probably makes the code's rule dropping peaks whose SD across trials exceeds their mean strict.ls-few-place-fields

  • The paper does not define its coefficient of variance; two readings are shown.ls-place-fields-unstable

  • Bins are about 1.1 cm against the paper's 1.5 cm, which shrinks the medians in bins.fig1e-phase-info-smaller-scale
