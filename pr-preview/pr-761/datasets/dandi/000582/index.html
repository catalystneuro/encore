    Found by the attempt on Sargolini et al. 2006 (sargolini-2006-02, 2026-10-20)
  • Identifier missingStill present from sargolini-2006-01. No identifier links a cell across files. Of 584 cell-sessions with a depth, there are 425 distinct (rat, depth, hemisphere, name), 99 of which appear in 2 to 7 files.
    Found by the attempt on Sargolini et al. 2006 (sargolini-2006-02, 2026-10-20)
  • Selection not documentedStill present from sargolini-2006-01. The dandiset is described as a sample of the paper's data without saying how it was selected. It holds 620 cell-sessions from 15 rats against the paper's 627 cells from 17; layer II has 71 against 108, while layers III, V and VI hold slightly more than table S1A (227, 118 and 198 against 223, 110 and 186).
    Found by the attempt on Sargolini et al. 2006 (sargolini-2006-02, 2026-10-20)
  • Metadata errorNew. The positions are rescaled without saying so. In all 118 files they span exactly 100, 150 or 200 cm on the larger axis and the first trial is centred on zero; the 19 circular files spanning 200 cm are most likely the paper's 180 cm circle, about 11% too large. The distance between the two LEDs (median 5.2 cm per file, 2.6 to 36 cm, against 7 cm in the paper) is too variable to fix the scale.
    Found by the attempt on Sargolini et al. 2006 (sargolini-2006-02, 2026-10-20)
  • Description missingNew. The 23 twenty-minute files hold two 10-minute trials joined near 600 s (in all 23 the position jumps between 599.9 and 600.1 s by more than 99.9% of the file's 0.2-s steps, and only the first trial is centred). There is no trial table, and nothing says which 10-minute files, if any, are the two trials of one recording.
    Found by the attempt on Sargolini et al. 2006 (sargolini-2006-02, 2026-10-20)
  • Data missingNew. No file gives the recording sites' distance from the postrhinal border, which the paper's dorsoventral analyses (fig. S3) use; `depth` is the depth the electrodes were lowered to, and the electrodes table has one row per file, location "MEC", with no coordinates.
    Found by the attempt on Sargolini et al. 2006 (sargolini-2006-02, 2026-10-20)
  • Data missingNew. No file names its enclosure, condition or the position of the cue card, so the cue-rotation trials of five rats cannot be found.
    Found by the attempt on Sargolini et al. 2006 (sargolini-2006-02, 2026-10-20)
  • Description missingNew. Six cells have no layer (one in rat 11084, five in 11207), 36 no depth and 230 no hemisphere.
    Found by the attempt on Sargolini et al. 2006 (sargolini-2006-02, 2026-10-20)
  • Metadata errorNew. The descriptions disagree with the files. The experiment description says the sample holds conjunctive and head-direction cells from layers III and V, and the dandiset description that it holds spike times for recorded grid cells; the files hold cells of layers II, III, V and VI and of every class.
    Found by the attempt on Sargolini et al. 2006 (sargolini-2006-02, 2026-10-20)
  • OtherNew. Nothing in the files flags it. They hold 13 layer III and 11 layer II cells.
    Found by the attempt on Sargolini et al. 2006 (sargolini-2006-02, 2026-10-20)
