Brain-wide analysis reveals movement encoding structured across and within brain areas.

Summary: Most of the paper's main findings could not be tested in this data. How much movement explains activity across and within brain areas, whether neurons lead or follow movement, and decoding choice from video all need the video or its autoencoder embedding, which the cited dataset version lacks. Of the 106 sessions the paper used, 72 have no video in any version. The data do hold the stated scale of neurons and structures, and the mouse-specific pattern of choice decoding from jaw and nose markers appears, though weaker than reported. The paper does not describe its choice decoder, and the one used here was weaker, which may explain the gap.

    Deviations from the paper’s method
    • The marker decoder is not described in the paper and its code is not public; the claims file's readings (a) five-frame window, 10 features, and (b) single frame, 2 features, were run with L2 logistic regression, the penalty chosen by inner fivefold cross-validation from C in 0.01 to 100 (the grid of the paper's code, functions_for_r2.calculate_auc). Both give the same outcome.
    • Correct trials only with 20-fold stratified cross-validation (Methods, Trial selection and cross-validation). The paper does not say how the AUC at a time bin is formed from the folds. The main reading averages the test-fold AUCs, as the Methods do for the other cross-validated measures (Methods, 'Defining choice- and uninstructed movement-modulated neurons using video-based choice decoder', 'The final AUC for each neuron is the average of the test AUC across all test-folds'; 'Epoch-averaged explained variance', 'After this, the folds were averaged'); one AUC over the pooled out-of-fold decision values is a variant. Both give the same outcome.
    • The AUC is computed every 20th frame (68 ms) from -1.2 s to the go cue and averaged over the 17 bins, an assumption made to keep the run within time (the paper decodes each time bin); a mean over bins changes by noise only. The trimmed epoch (-1.05 to -0.15 s) is a variant.

No velocity is taken across the gap between two trial videos; a frame is flagged when the velocity into it is an outlier, so a single bad frame also flags the next one (median 8.7 frames per 10,000 imputed).
    • The main set is the 106 sessions the paper used, named in the authors' Fig. 6 and 7 file and matched by link-authors-deposit; the sessions that pass the clock check and all decoded sessions are variants and give the same outcome. Seven sessions fail the clock check (see findings), none of them among the 106. Since the main set comes from the authors' deposit, the claim's data field is set to with_labels in the claims file and it depends on link-authors-deposit.
    • Two sessions are not decoded, SC032_121719 (8 correct left trials) and SC066_041321 (4 correct trials with marker frames over the delay epoch, as markers exist for 7 trial videos only); neither is among the authors' 106.

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • The link claim was written into the claims file by this attempt (protocol, author step 1.4), with its criterion, before the full run; it is the registry's check, not a finding of the paper.link-authors-deposit
  • The 2 Hz filter uses the dataset's whole-session avg_firing_rate, not the rate over the included trials (Methods, Prediction of neural activity, 'Neurons with low firing rates (below 2 Hz) were excluded'); the count before the filter already decides the claim.data-scale
  • The paper names no quality label; both of the dataset's (unit_quality and classification) are reported, as the criterion allows. Units with unit_quality good but no CCF annotation count towards the neuron count and not towards any structure.data-scale
  • Structures are the twelve major divisions of the Allen CCF, read from the authors' structure tree in their deposit (mousebrainontology_2.csv and mousebrainontology_heirarchy_cortexMaskExclusions21_sc.xlsx), with isocortex split into ALM (units on probes targeted to ALM, as the code's get_single_area_inds) and other isocortex, the level of the areas the criterion lists; each unit is counted once.   • The marker decoder is not described in the paper and its code is not public; the claims file's readings (a) five-frame window, 10 features, and (b) single frame, 2 features, were run with L2 logistic regression, the penalty chosen by inner fivefold cross-validation from C in 0.01 to 100 (the grid of the paper's code, functions_for_r2.calculate_auc). Both give the same outcome.fig6e-marker-clustering
  • Correct trials only with 20-fold stratified cross-validation (Methods, Trial selection and cross-validation). The paper does not say how the AUC at a time bin is formed from the folds. The main reading averages the test-fold AUCs, as the Methods do for the other cross-validated measures (Methods, 'Defining choice- and uninstructed movement-modulated neurons using video-based choice decoder', 'The final AUC for each neuron is the average of the test AUC across all test-folds'; 'Epoch-averaged explained variance', 'After this, the folds were averaged'); one AUC over the pooled out-of-fold decision values is a variant.
