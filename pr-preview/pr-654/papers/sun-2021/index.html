The paper's main findings could mostly not be tested here, and the parts that could be tested were mixed. The 6 benchmark results on baseline models, task programming, style transfer, new behaviors and annotator similarity are blocked, because the linked baseline code is unreachable. The dandiset holds one CalMS21 sequence, and its pose and labels match the deposited dataset exactly. The counts and label fractions held except for a 10-frame gap in Table 5 (139,122 against 139,112 frames). Close contact during investigation and mount held, but the claim that tracking errors rise when the mice move fast did not: errors were higher when the mice were close and lower when they moved fast. The dandiset's confidence scores are all missing, and attack is absent from the session, so that part could not be tested.
