Dataset version 0.250624.0426 · tested 2026-10-20 · 11 open findings

  • Description missingNew in this attempt. The putative cell types the paper uses (pyramidal cell or interneuron for every unit, from monosynaptic connections and then k-means on the waveform) are not in the dandiset, and there are no spike waveforms. Five of the 33 files with units (Rat08-20130709 to 20130713) have a cell_type column with the values excitatory, inhibitory and unclassified, described only as "Type of cell this has been classified as."; it marks 152 excitatory and 36 inhibitory BLA units and 9 excitatory hippocampal units, consistent with the paper's monosynaptic identification alone. A short-latency cross-correlogram check supports this reading of the column. At p < 1e-6, 99.3% of the excitatory BLA units excite and 94.4% of the inhibitory ones inhibit another loaded unit at 1 to 4 ms, against 44% and 7.9% of the unclassified ones. Most of the paper's reactivation analyses cannot be repeated (17 claims blocked for this alone). The earlier attempt did not read this column.
    Found by the attempt on Girardeau et al. 2017 (girardeau-2017-02, 2026-10-20)
  • Data missingNew in this attempt. The dandiset holds 40 recording days with 4702 units, where the paper reports 61 sessions with 7,390 units; per rat it holds 2317, 515, 440 and 1430 units (Rat08 to Rat11) against 2,444, 1,294, 1,138 and 2,514. Which rat is which of the paper's rats 1 to 4 is not stated.
    Found by the attempt on Girardeau et al. 2017 (girardeau-2017-02, 2026-10-20)
  • Metadata errorNew in this attempt. The archive's asset metadata (variableMeasured) is wrong for all 40 files on two entries. It lists ElectricalSeries for the 24 files that hold no raw voltage and not for the 16 that hold it (acquisition/ElectricalSeries in Rat08-20130708 to 20130713 and all Rat09 files), and ProcessingModule for the 16 files without a behavior module and not for the 24 with one. Its Units entries agree with the files.
    Found by the attempt on Girardeau et al. 2017 (girardeau-2017-02, 2026-10-20)
  • Metadata errorStill present (found by girardeau-2017-01; the version and the files are unchanged, and the draft holds the same assets). The epochs table is stored in milliseconds although its columns say seconds; in all 40 files its last stop time is exactly 1000 times the LFP duration.
    Found by the attempt on Girardeau et al. 2017 (girardeau-2017-02, 2026-10-20)
  • Metadata errorStill present (found by girardeau-2017-01). Video timestamps are stated in seconds but are in milliseconds and are not on the session clock; the first timestamp matches the start of the video's epoch in 1 of 114 videos, while the spans match the epoch durations in 93 of 114.
    Found by the attempt on Girardeau et al. 2017 (girardeau-2017-02, 2026-10-20)
  • File format errorStill present (found by girardeau-2017-01). In ten files the spike_times_index is wrong at the first units; unit 1's spike times fall back once, after 65536 times k spikes, and unit 0 stops where unit 1 starts, so the first part of unit 1 is the end of unit 0. Units 0 and 1 of these files were left out of the analyses.
    Found by the attempt on Girardeau et al. 2017 (girardeau-2017-02, 2026-10-20)
  • Data missingStill present (found by girardeau-2017-01). Seven files have no units table, sixteen have no sleep states and fourteen have no ripple events; the Rat09 files have no hippocampal units and no ripples.
    Found by the attempt on Girardeau et al. 2017 (girardeau-2017-02, 2026-10-20)
  • Data missingStill present (found by girardeau-2017-01). There is no position tracking and no air puff location, only the puff times and the raw video frames, so behaviour on the track cannot be measured without tracking about 1.9 million frames; laps and the air puff direction can only be inferred from reward and puff times.
    Found by the attempt on Girardeau et al. 2017 (girardeau-2017-02, 2026-10-20)
  • Description missingStill present in the dandiset (found by girardeau-2017-01). Unit region labels are undescribed short codes (bla, blv, bmp, ladl, cen, den, ven, pir, plc01, PLCo1, cpu, hpc, HIP, no label), some in two cases, Rat09-20140328 has no location column, and the electrode table gives every channel as unknown. The paper's Supplementary Fig. 3 names the structures the codes match (BLA, BLV, LaDL, BMA, BMP, Pir, VEn, DEn), which lets this attempt read them; the dandiset still does not describe them.
    Found by the attempt on Girardeau et al. 2017 (girardeau-2017-02, 2026-10-20)
  • Description missingStill present (found by girardeau-2017-01). The trials table of the Rat08 and Rat09 files is described only as "experimental trials"; its intervals of 3 to 5 s fall partly outside the run and are not laps.
    Found by the attempt on Girardeau et al. 2017 (girardeau-2017-02, 2026-10-20)
  • Description missingStill present (found by girardeau-2017-01). The ripple annotations carry an undescribed number in each label (for example "Ripple peak 156"), one per file, presumably the detection channel; ripples, puffs and rewards are stored as annotation series under stimulus presentation.
    Found by the attempt on Girardeau et al. 2017 (girardeau-2017-02, 2026-10-20)
