    Over 25 Merkel and SA afferents (Table S1's fitted set), mean r 0.656 for the viscoelastic model against 0.686 for the spline model of M0 and M0' on the same frames, difference -0.030 (95% bootstrap CI -0.061 to -0.006); viscoelastic higher in 10 of 25; Merkel afferents alone -0.034.   • Metadata errorIn the 13 progressive cutting recordings every trial has cuttingGroup "intact" and the trial table's MOI has one value per file, although the whisking-in-air rate falls over most of these sessions (last fifth below half the first in 10 of 13). The cutting conditions (intact, sham, cut 1 to 3) and the moment of inertia after each cut are missing, which blocks Figure 6 and parts of Figure 7.
  • Data missingThe EMG files of the intrinsic protractor recordings (7A, 8A, 9A) hold the tracked whisker series but no EMG signal; the nasolabialis file (11A) holds spike times but not the EMG. The optogenetic tagging data, the histology, the whisker geometry and the manually curated contact frames are not in the dandiset.
  • Metadata errorVelocity, acceleration and jerk are stated in degree/s, degree/s^2 and degree/s^3, and dM0, dFaxial and dFlateral in N*m/s and N/s, but the values are per millisecond; each is close to half the change of its variable per 2 ms frame (median ratios 0.467 to 0.502 over files).
  • Description missingThe stored whisk phase is the negative of the Hilbert phase of the band-passed whisker position; its sine is anticorrelated (median -0.9999 over files) and its cosine correlated (median 0.9999) with those of the Hilbert phase, and position peaks at phase 0 in all 60 files. So phases from -pi to 0 are retraction, the reverse of the convention the paper cites. Neither the files nor the paper describe the convention.
  • Description missingEvery series has the description "no description". distanceToPoleCenter has unit "unknown"; its median in frames with contact = 1 is between -0.08 and 0.09 in every file but one (209A, a cut-whisker recording with almost no contact), which fits the Methods' distance from whisker to pole in mm, not a distance to the pole's centre. Series not used here (Mi, dMi, Fnorm, thetaAtContact, follicleCoordsX and Y) are not explained; Mi and dMi have units N*m and N*m/s and values below 3e-8 in absolute size, in every file including the EMG files.
  • Metadata errorTrial stop_time is 6.998 s after start_time in every trial of every file, while each trial's series hold 2,500 frames at 500 Hz (5 s), as the Methods describe.
  • File format errorThe spike times of file sub-KSm31 (KS0277A) are not sorted.
  • Metadata errorTwo identifiers, KS0125_ and KS0128_, lack the unit letter of their Table S1 recordings (125B and 128D). All session start times are 12:00, so only the dates carry information.
  • OtherSome series hold extreme values, likely tracking errors, such as whisker position from -985 to 596 degrees, whisk frequency from -250 to 250 Hz and M0 down to -0.52 N m. The Glossary's percentile outlier removal excludes them, but they are not flagged in the files.
  • Description missingTwo recordings made with the whisker cut before recording (199B and 209A) hold distance-to-pole series and frames with contact = 1 (5,014 and 32); in 209A the distance at those frames is 5.6 to 6.5. The files do not say whether a pole was presented in these sessions or what these frames are.
  • Methods not availableThe paper's MATLAB and R analysis code is available only on request; no public repository was found, so the GAM and viscoelastic fits had to be reimplemented.

Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • The criterion's rule of removing "KS0" was applied as removing "KS" and leading zeros, so that the EMG files KS0007A to KS0011A match 7A to 11A.table-s1-link
  • The Methods do not say what the bootstrap resamples; trials were resampled (1,000 times).fig2c-touch-rate
  • The Glossary's "at least 1,000 spikes" can be read as spikes in the recording (a) or during whisking in air (b); only (b) matches the paper's count and Table S1's classes.fig2c-wt-star
  • The paper shows direction selectivity in examples and tuning surfaces without a measure or threshold; the index and the 0.5 threshold were chosen before the analysis.fig3a-direction-selective
  • Penalized cubic B-splines (10 per variable, lambda 1) fitted as a binomial model with two trials per 2 ms frame replace mgcv GAMs on 1 ms bins with UBRE smoothing.fig3d-gam-full
  • At most 60,000 randomly chosen contact frames per recording.fig3d-gam-full
  • Spline models in place of mgcv GAMs, as for fig3d-gam-full.fig3d-gam-single
  • Spline models in place of mgcv GAMs; the ranking of close pairs may depend on the smoothing method.fig3d-gam-best-pair
  • Fitted with L-BFGS-B from 8 starts, with the rate of change of moment scaled by its 80th percentile inside the fit; the Methods used fmincon. During development an unscaled version found worse optima for the same model, so the result depends on the optimizer.fig4c-viscoelastic
  • Fitted and evaluated on the same at most 60,000 contact frames as the spline models, which also exclude frames that are outliers on the other nine variables.fig4c-viscoelastic
  • The paper gives no test for spanning the whisk cycle; the quarter-cycle and Rayleigh criterion was chosen before the analysis.fig5e-phase-span
  • The paper shows invariance with tuning surfaces only; the median split and the pi/4 threshold were chosen before the analysis.figs3-phase-invariance
  • Tested on 15 of the paper's 28 afferents; the intact condition of the 13 progressive cutting recordings cannot be separated.figs6-kinematic-gam
  • Spline models in place of mgcv GAMs, fitted on 60,000 frames and evaluated on up to 400,000 whisking-in-air frames per afferent.figs6-kinematic-gam
  • The file does not say that its spike times are motor units of m. nasolabialis; this is taken from Table S1.figs4-nasolabialis
  • The Methods give no rule for frames with a missing distance to the pole; these are treated as far from the pole.sigma-kappa
