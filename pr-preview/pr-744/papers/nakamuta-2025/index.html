  • Days in vitro are not in the dataset; DIV is taken as days since the dish's first recording plus 2 (assumed, as the Results put the onset of activity at 2 to 3 DIV, four dishes are active on their first recording and three become active one or two days later); d0 = 1, 3, 4 and 5, and per-dish readings that put each dish's first active recording at DIV 2 (O2) or 3 (O3), are shown.fig1b-async-to-bursts fig1d-power-law-emerges fig1d-shuffle fig2b-spatial-structure fig2c-locality-spectrum fig2d-shuffle-locality no-temporal-structure
