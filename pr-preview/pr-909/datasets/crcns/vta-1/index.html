Dataset unversioned files (the archive keeps no versions) · tested 2026-11-11 · 7 open findings

  • Data missingThe licks, recorded with an infrared beam (Methods), are not in vta-1; the three claims on behavior (Figure 3G, Figure S3) cannot be tested.
    Found by the attempt on Starkweather et al. 2018 (starkweather-2018-01, 2026-11-11)
  • Data missingFree water deliveries, and so the free water responses that normalize the responses of Figures S5B and S5C and enter two ANOVAs, are not in vta-1; every reward onset in the files belongs to an odor A to C trial.
    Found by the attempt on Starkweather et al. 2018 (starkweather-2018-01, 2026-11-11)
  • Data missingThe mPFC recordings (Figure 2, Figures S1 and S6A to S6E), the unidentified VTA neurons clustered in Figure S4, and the 26 neurons of the no-KORD SalB controls (Figure S6F to S6H) are not in vta-1, which holds only the 172 identified dopamine neurons of the four KORD groups. data.zip has 173 members, the 172 neuron files and the directory entry data/.
    Found by the attempt on Starkweather et al. 2018 (starkweather-2018-01, 2026-11-11)
  • Identifier missingNo file names its mouse or recording session. Sessions can be recovered as files with identical odor onset arrays (77 sessions), but mice cannot, so the per-mouse analysis of the Figure 5 inset cannot be repeated and results cannot be broken down by animal.
    Found by the attempt on Starkweather et al. 2018 (starkweather-2018-01, 2026-11-11)
  • OtherThree pairs of task 1 SalB files share most of their spikes within 0.5 ms (SalB_Task1_9 and SalB_Task1_10, 99.7% of the spikes of the first; SalB_Task1_17 and SalB_Task1_18, 90%; SalB_Task1_36 and SalB_Task1_39, 81%), against at most 1.5% at chance. Each pair is very likely one neuron sorted twice, so task 1 SalB holds 39 distinct neurons, not 42. The paper's statistics include all 42; counting each once changes no outcome.
    Found by the attempt on Starkweather et al. 2018 (starkweather-2018-01, 2026-11-11)
  • Description missing21 file trials (9 session trials in 8 sessions) are rewarded but have a missing (NaN) trial_type, at delays off the 0.2 s grid (1471 to 2421 ms). The data description does not mention them. They are 5, 1, 1 and 14 neuron trials in task 1 saline, task 1 SalB, task 2 saline and task 2 SalB; kept as odor A trials at the nearest grid ISI, they change no result.
    Found by the attempt on Starkweather et al. 2018 (starkweather-2018-01, 2026-11-11)
  • Description missingThe files give no recording onset or injection time, so the paper's window of the 40 minutes following recording onset cannot be applied exactly. The trials of each session span 23.8 to 38.3 min, consistent with files already cut to that window.
    Found by the attempt on Starkweather et al. 2018 (starkweather-2018-01, 2026-11-11)
