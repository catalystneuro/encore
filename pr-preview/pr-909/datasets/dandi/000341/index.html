Dataset version 0.241220.1312 · tested 2026-11-20 · 9 open findings

  • Metadata errorEvery membrane potential series gives a conversion of 1e-12 with unit volts, but the stored values are already in volts (medians near -0.065). Read with the stated conversion they are picovolts.
    Found by the attempt on Rozsa et al. 2023 (rozsa-2023-01, 2026-11-20)
  • Metadata errorThe stimulus series of the bleb recordings (731 sweeps) and some sweeps of other folders (52 active human, 11 ZD7288, 3 rat oscillation, and in 21 induction files) are stored in pA although their unit is amperes, giving values near 1e14 when read as stated.
    Found by the attempt on Rozsa et al. 2023 (rozsa-2023-01, 2026-11-20)
  • Description missingNo file carries AP labels (AIS-AP or RA-AP), RAF status, or the induction protocol's sweeps, so the classes and epochs the paper analyses must be rebuilt from the traces. The human and rat induction files carry no morphological cell class (NGFC, non-NGFC, rosehip), which only the bleb files record.
    Found by the attempt on Rozsa et al. 2023 (rozsa-2023-01, 2026-11-20)
  • Data missingThe local KCl application experiments (Figure 2I to 2L) are not identifiable in the dandiset, and only 2 of the 90 rat recordings without carbachol and SCH23390 (Figure S3) are in it. The in vivo folder records no anesthesia condition for any cell (Figure S5). The induction folders hold 313 human and 132 rat files against the 385 and 259 cells the paper tested.
    Found by the attempt on Rozsa et al. 2023 (rozsa-2023-01, 2026-11-20)
  • Selection not documentedThe induction folders are named Human_persistent_RAF and Rat_persistent_RAF, and nearly all their cells fire APs without current after induction, but the paper reports RAF in a quarter to a third of tested cells. 124 of 307 human and 33 of 118 rat cells already fire spontaneous APs before induction, and 58 human and 94 rat files list drugs (including carbachol, SCH23390 and calcium changes) that the induction experiments of the paper do not mention. The dandiset does not say how its cells were selected from those the paper tested.
    Found by the attempt on Rozsa et al. 2023 (rozsa-2023-01, 2026-11-20)
  • OtherSampling rates vary within and across files (5 to 200 kHz, mostly 16.7, 25, 50 and 100 kHz) against the 50 kHz of the Methods; the long gap-free sweeps of the induction files are often at 5 kHz. One bleb file (sub-20220317) has no bleb sweeps.
    Found by the attempt on Rozsa et al. 2023 (rozsa-2023-01, 2026-11-20)
  • Other18 session identifiers name both an induction file and an active-slice file (2 human, 16 rat, for example ses-1611161tm-3-1-4 in Rat_persistent_RAF and in In_vitro_oscillation_Rat); the active-slice files are larger and carry an LFP. Whether these are the same recordings deposited twice is not documented, and 16 of the 18 induction files list drugs.
    Found by the attempt on Rozsa et al. 2023 (rozsa-2023-01, 2026-11-20)
  • OtherIn the soma and bleb recordings the per-file median somatic AP thresholds lie at -30.4 to -11.2 mV with and without current, the voltage jump at the onset of depolarizing steps implies 26 to 57 MOhm of uncompensated resistance, and hyperpolarizing steps of -700 to -950 pA drive the recorded potential below -150 mV (sweep minima down to -434 mV). Bridge balance is not recorded.
    Found by the attempt on Rozsa et al. 2023 (rozsa-2023-01, 2026-11-20)
  • Description missingThe in vivo files give the genotype only inside subject/description (five values, including both 'mouse' and 'mouse-black6') and do not record the anesthesia condition; the folder holds 259 files from 149 mice against the paper's 242 cells, and some files also hold camera images ('Moovie').
    Found by the attempt on Rozsa et al. 2023 (rozsa-2023-01, 2026-11-20)
