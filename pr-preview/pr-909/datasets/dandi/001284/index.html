Dataset version 0.250701.1610 · tested 2026-11-20 · 8 open findings

  • Data missingPartly fixedDANDI:001047, which cites the paper, holds images from a 3.8-mm array with a 3.2-kV first pulse (per-file metadata and images), not the paper's 15.3-mm array with 7.2-kV pulses. Of the packet-rate series of Figures 3 and 4 (1 Hz to 0.2 MHz) only the 1-Hz, 0.2-MHz and control wells are deposited, in 001276; the 1-kHz and 10-kHz wells are in none of the four dandisets, and neither are the field simulation (Fig. 1), the thermochromic film images (Fig. 2) or the high-resolution images of Fig. 5C. Seven of the paper's claims cannot be tested.
    Found by the attempt on Silkuniene et al. 2025 (silkuniene-2025-04, 2026-11-20); that attempt also used DANDI:001047, DANDI:001276, DANDI:001277, and the finding does not say which dataset it is about
  • Description missingNewDANDI:001276 holds CT26 and CHO wells exposed at a 1-Hz packet rate and control wells, which its description (0.2 MHz, 1 to 6 bursts) does not mention and whose role in the paper is not stated; by the paper's protocol they fit the packet-rate series of Figures 3 and 4, and a reader's answer assigns four of its claims to them. The CT26 wells at 2 bursts (x4) hold CANCAN at 1 Hz (n = 2) and 0.2 MHz (n = 6) and controls (n = 4), with all three conditions on 13 August 2024.
    Found by the attempt on Silkuniene et al. 2025 (silkuniene-2025-04, 2026-11-20); that attempt also used DANDI:001047, DANDI:001276, DANDI:001277, and the finding does not say which dataset it is about
  • Selection not documentedStill presentSeveral groups hold more wells than the paper reports. 001284 has 12, 11 and 11 YP wells at 0, 1 and 2 mm (paper n = 6 to 7); 001276 has 5, 8, 7 and 8 CHO wells at 1, 2, 4 and 6 bursts (paper n = 4) and 6 CT26 wells at 2 bursts (paper n = 3 to 5); 001277 has 7 wells labelled 600 ns (paper n = 5). In 001284 the August 2024 YP wells (7, 6 and 7) match the paper's YP n and the October 2024 wells (5, 5 and 4) its Pr n. Which wells the paper used is not recorded.
    Found by the attempt on Silkuniene et al. 2025 (silkuniene-2025-04, 2026-11-20); that attempt also used DANDI:001047, DANDI:001276, DANDI:001277, and the finding does not say which dataset it is about
  • Metadata errorStill presentIn 001277, wells P1-20240926-B2 and B3 are labelled 600-ns 0.2-MHz CANCAN. In B2 the YP spot is 5.39 mm from the array centre, against at most 1.21 mm in every other 0.2-MHz CANCAN well with footprints; B3 has its YP maximum at the centre, but YP at 3 and 5 mm is 46% and 35% of the centre, against at most 13% and 9% in the other wells of 001277. The label or the exposure of these two wells may differ from the description.
    Found by the attempt on Silkuniene et al. 2025 (silkuniene-2025-04, 2026-11-20); that attempt also used DANDI:001047, DANDI:001276, DANDI:001277, and the finding does not say which dataset it is about

In 001284, the pre-exposure Hoechst files of wells P1-20241002-B2 and P2-20241004-B1 state 900-ns pulses, while the other files of these wells state 600 ns.
