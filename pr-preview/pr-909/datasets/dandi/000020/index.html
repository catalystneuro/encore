Dataset version 0.210913.1639 · tested 2026-11-22 · 4 open findings

Problems that change results 1

The data are wrong in a way the files do not show, so an analysis that takes them as given comes out wrong. Each was checked against the notebook’s outputs by someone other than the attempt’s author.

  • Metadata errorChanges resultsTwo files hold the same recording. sub-673135474_ses-680563483 and sub-673135516_ses-680897463 are from different mice, and the paper's join (Allen metadata) gives them different Cre lines (Vip-IRES-Cre and Ndnf-IRES2-dgCre), t-types (Vip Lmo1 Myl1 and Lamp5 Lsp1), layers and transcriptomic samples, yet the voltage traces of all their usable long squares are identical (SHA-1) and so are all 94 of their IPFX features. At most one of the two cells' recordings is in the dandiset, and one file carries another cell's data. No other two files share a trace or a feature vector.
    Checked: The outputs show it: in cell 9 the two files share an identical fingerprint, an identical long-square hash and identical IPFX feature vectors, and no other pair of files matches; in cell 11 the join gives them different Cre lines, t-types (Vip Lmo1 Myl1 / Lamp5 Lsp1), layers (VISp2/3 / VISp4) and transcriptomic samples (S010 / S012); the subjects differ by path (cell 7). Not printed: the "all 93 of their IPFX features" count for this pair. Critical is the right rating: one file carries another cell's recording under different metadata, and nothing in the files flags it; the effect on any statistic here is negligible, but the rating depends on the kind of error, not its effect.
    Found by the attempt on Yang et al. 2026 (yang-2026-flexible-01, 2026-11-22)

Other findings 3

  • File format errorThe four files of subject 636828314 (sessions 637823738, 637824890, 637833126, 637838825) list two response series for sweep number 1 in their sweep table, so IPFX cannot open them. The paper's join has no row for them either.
    Found by the attempt on Yang et al. 2026 (yang-2026-flexible-01, 2026-11-22)
  • Description missingThe files hold no transcriptomic label or sample identifier; the t-types, subclasses and RNA sample ids of these Patch-seq cells come only from the Allen metadata, joined by session or file name. The Cre line can be read from the subject genotype, which agrees with the Allen metadata for every cell.
    Found by the attempt on Yang et al. 2026 (yang-2026-flexible-01, 2026-11-22)
  • OtherEvery count the paper reports for the dataset is in the files; 4,435 files, 4,430 joined to the paper's metadata, and IPFX features that give the paper's 4,344, 4,139 and 4,422 cells per regime, its 42, 20 and 21 retained features and its k80. Sessions and subjects in the files equal the paths, and the voltage response of every cell's most hyperpolarizing long square begins at the stimulus onset (median change 0.01 mV in the 10 ms before onset, -15.3 mV 15 to 25 ms after), so stimulus and response share a clock.
    Found by the attempt on Yang et al. 2026 (yang-2026-flexible-01, 2026-11-22)
