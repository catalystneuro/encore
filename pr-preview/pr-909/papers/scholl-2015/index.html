Summary: In this data the paper's finding that PV+ interneurons in mouse V1 are more binocular and less disparity selective than PV- neurons mostly holds, with some values outside the paper's ranges, and so does the absence of a map for ocular dominance or disparity. Its central claim that PV+ cells match the ocular dominance and disparity preference of nearby neurons did not reproduce, on far fewer cells: 18 PV+ cells at the nearest radius against the paper's 86. The published files hold raw movies only, so the per-cell data came from an unpublished, undocumented folder covering part of the animals. The excitatory neuron comparison, histology and neuropil controls could not be tested, as those data are missing.
