Summary: In this data the paper's central ALM findings held up: fastigial photo-activation during the delay caused errors and collapsed ALM preparatory activity along its coding direction, and photo-inhibiting the cerebellar nuclei abolished late-delay ALM selectivity. The recordings from the cerebellar nuclei, the lesions, licking and the anatomy could not be tested, as the dataset holds only the ALM sessions. That ALM recovers after dentate photo-activation did not reproduce, as a few percent of neurons stayed altered more than 1 s later. Counts of modulated neurons and firing rate levels mostly missed, as the files hold more neurons than the paper reports and do not say which it excluded.
