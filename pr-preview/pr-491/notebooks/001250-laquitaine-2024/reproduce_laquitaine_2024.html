Spike sorting biases in a detailed cortical model (Laquitaine et al.), tested on DANDI:001250¶

Paper: Laquitaine, Imbeni, Tharayil, Isbister and Reimann, "Spike sorting biases and information loss in a detailed cortical model", bioRxiv doi:10.1101/2024.12.04.626805. The claims file (claims/001250/10.1101-2024.12.04.626805.yaml) was written from the bioRxiv full text (preprint, version 1 header; main text, Methods, Discussion and supplementary legends) and the paper's code, github.com/steevelaquitaine/spikebias at commit 6e9af03b81c3892aafa94b2d10b724ef59fdb247.

Dataset: DANDI:001250, version 0.251012.1648 (doi:10.48324/dandi.001250/0.251012.1648). The paper cites no version of the dandiset; this is its only published version (the other is the draft), so it is pinned. All files are streamed; nothing is downloaded to disk.

What the dandiset holds and lacks for the claims (data check below):

  • Fitted traces and ground truth spike times for NS (Neuropixels, spontaneous, 40 kHz, 34 min), E (evoked, 20 kHz, 1 h), the 10-minute evoked recording at 40 kHz, the dense probe at three depths (DS1 to DS3) and the in vivo recordings of Marques-Smith (M) and Horvath (H1 to H3); the ground truth tables of NS and the evoked recordings carry soma positions, layers, synapse classes and electrical types; those of the dense probe carry only spike times.
  • The paper's sortings (Kilosort, 2, 2.5, 3, 4, herdingspikes) of the first 10 minutes of NS, E (of the 1-hour recording), M, H and DS, and 1-hour sortings of E. The DS1 folder's sortings are a copy of the DS2 ones (so depth 1 has none). There is no sorting of Buccino's synthetic recording (S), of NS down-sampled by two (NS-2X), or of the 10-minute 40 kHz evoked recording.
  • No stimulus table: the evoked recordings carry no stimulus times or directions.
  • The isolated and summed traces of cell 3754013 on the Reyes probe (Figure 3d, e).

Recordings deposited more than once¶

The protocol asks for a check of duplicate deposits. Each sorting's spike trains are hashed and compared with every other; the dense probe sortings of depths 1 and 2 are compared explicitly. The ground truth spike trains of the raw and fitted files of one simulation are expected to be the same (one simulation, fitted traces), and the comparison checks that too.

Units, layers and clocks¶

Checks of the metadata against the values: the firing rates the sorting tables store against the spike counts, the sorted units' layers against the electrode tables, and the clock of the sortings against the ground truth. The spike times of a sorting and of its ground truth share a clock when the agreement scores are far above those obtained after shifting the sorted spike times (a shift of 50 ms breaks every true match). There is no trial table; the evoked stimulus schedule is not in the files (see the header).

# clock check: agreement with true and shifted sorted spike times
def best_scores(exp_gt, sort_key, shift=0.0, singles_only=False, gt=None):
    gdf, gtr = gt if gt is not None else GT[exp_gt]
    sdf, st = SORT[sort_key]
    if singles_only:
        keep = sdf.single.values
        sdf, st = sdf[keep], [t for t, k in zip(st, keep) if k]
    st = [t + shift for t in st]
    fs = sb.SAMPLING_HZ[exp_gt]
    return sb.agreement_scores(gtr, gdf.index, st, sdf.index, fs)

def coincident_fraction(gt_trains, s_trains, shift=0.0, window=0.00065):
    """Fraction of sorted spikes within +-window s of any ground truth spike."""
    allg = np.sort(np.concatenate(gt_trains))
    t = np.concatenate(s_trains) + shift
    i = np.clip(np.searchsorted(allg, t), 1, len(allg) - 1)
    d = np.minimum(np.abs(allg[i] - t), np.abs(allg[i - 1] - t))
    return (d < window).mean()

Traces: shared statistics¶

Six blocks of 10 s spread over the first 10 minutes of each recording (onsets printed below) are streamed, high-pass filtered at 300 Hz and re-referenced to the common median, and the cortical sites kept (Outside and WM dropped). Keeping the full 10 minutes of every recording in memory does not fit the run, so the medians, noise, spectra and templates below rest on 60 s per recording, a deviation from the claims' 10 minutes. The maxima and ANR extremes, which grow with duration, are also taken over the whole first 600 s, streamed in blocks (next section). The dense probe and Horvath depths are pooled by layer. Units: the simulated and Marques-Smith files store microvolts (conversion 1e-6 to volts); the Horvath files store int16 counts with conversion 0.195 (which NWB reads as volts per count) and an electrode column gain_to_uV of 195000, which cannot both be right and give implausible values either way. Two scales are therefore carried: H in counts (H), and H at 0.195 uV per count (H195, the Intan step). The dense probe model was fitted to Horvath's noise (Methods, Fitting amplitude and background noise), and the check below shows the fitted model's noise matches H in counts, not at 0.195 uV per count; the comparisons of DS with H use counts, as the fit implies (an assumption), and show H195 as a variant.

def wavemap_vectors(tem, n_sites=6, half_ms=3.0, n_points=50, nearest=True, min_spikes=1):
    """nearest: the largest-amplitude site and the sites nearest to it (n_sites in all), ordered by
    amplitude (Methods, "the six nearest sites"); otherwise the n_sites largest anywhere (variant)."""
    fs = tem["fs"]
    pos = tem["pos"]
    vecs, ids = [], []
    for uid, (w, n) in tem["templates"].items():
        if n < min_spikes:
            continue
        amp = np.max(np.abs(w), axis=0)
        if nearest:
            dist = np.sqrt(((pos - pos[np.argmax(amp)]) ** 2).sum(axis=1))
            near = np.argsort(dist, kind="stable")[:n_sites]
            sites = near[np.argsort(-amp[near])]
        else:
            sites = np.argsort(-amp)[:n_sites]
        tr = np.argmin(w[:, sites[0]])
        h = int(round(half_ms * fs / 1000))
        if tr - h < 0 or tr + h > w.shape[0]:
            continue
        seg = w[tr - h:tr + h, sites]
        seg = resample(seg, n_points, axis=0).T.ravel()
        seg = (seg - seg.min()) / (seg.max() - seg.min())
        vecs.append(seg); ids.append(uid)
    return np.array(vecs), ids

def wavemap(X, seed=42, resolution=None, n_neighbors=15):
    red = umap.UMAP(n_neighbors=n_neighbors, n_components=2, random_state=seed)
    red.fit(X)
    G = nx.from_scipy_sparse_array(red.graph_)
    res_grid = np.round(np.arange(0.1, 7.0, 0.1), 1)
    if resolution is None:
        mods = []
        for r in res_grid:
            part = community_louvain.best_partition(G, resolution=r, random_state=seed)
            mods.append(community_louvain.modularity(part, G))
        resolution = res_grid[int(np.argmax(mods))]
    part = community_louvain.best_partition(G, resolution=resolution, random_state=seed)
    return len(set(part.values())), resolution, collections.Counter(part.values())

def boot_var(x, rng, n=100):
    return np.array([np.var(rng.choice(x, size=len(x), replace=True)) for _ in range(n)])

def compare_to_m(sorter, exps, rng, singles=True):
    rows = []
    m = rates("M", sorter, singles)
    for l in sb.LAYERS:
        xm = np.log10(m.rate[m.layer == l].values)
        if len(xm) < 3:
            continue
        for e in exps:
            d = rates(e, sorter, singles)
            x = np.log10(d.rate[d.layer == l].values)
            if len(x) < 3:
                rows.append({"sorter": sorter, "exp": e, "layer": l, "n": len(x), "n M": len(xm)})
                continue
            pm = stats.mannwhitneyu(x, xm, alternative="two-sided").pvalue
            vx, vm = boot_var(x, rng), boot_var(xm, rng)
            pv = stats.mannwhitneyu(vx, vm, alternative="two-sided").pvalue
            rows.append({"sorter": sorter, "exp": e, "layer": l, "n": len(x), "n M": len(xm),

                         "p median": pm, "var log10": round(np.var(x), 3), "var log10 M": round(np.var(xm), 3),
                         "p var": pv})
    return pd.DataFrame(rows)

How the analysis differs from the paper's: the trace statistics use six 10 s blocks of the first 10 minutes, not the whole 10 minutes, except the maxima and ANR extremes, which use the whole first 600 s; WaveMap templates come from the same 60 s; E is the first 10 minutes of the 1-hour recording, as the 10-minute 40 kHz recording has no sorting; the sortings are the deposited ones, with SpikeInterface 0.105 for the agreement scores where the paper used 0.100.5; the Kilosort2 sorting of E covers 240 s, as the Methods say, and is scored over 240 s; the dense probe is summed over depths 2 and 3 where the deposited depth 1 sortings are copies of depth 2's.

What was found about the dataset: the DS1 folder's sortings are the DS2 sortings; no sorting of Buccino's synthetic recording, of NS-2X or of the 10-minute evoked recording; no stimulus table for the evoked recordings; no soma layers or positions for the dense probe ground truth; the Horvath files' scale (conversion 0.195 to volts, gain_to_uV 195000) is inconsistent; the Reyes traces are declared in microvolts but hold values about a thousand times smaller; 78 NS ground truth neurons have no spike in the first 10 minutes, though all fire later; the depth 3 dense probe sortings of every sorter match none of the three dense probe ground truths at any lag within 100 ms better than 0.011 above the median over lags.
