Welch et al. 2025 · Preprint

The impact of a psychedelic drug on olfactory search behavior by mice

doi:10.1101/2025.07.09.663970 · paper key welch-2025

Datasets

  • DANDI:001863

In this data, most of the paper's behavioral and sniffing findings held up, but its reward-port findings could not be tested and its speed findings did not reproduce. DOI lowered accuracy, reduced trials completed (mainly in females) and lengthened odor trials. It also raised sniff frequency in every trial period, and head twitches rose toward the odor end of the arena. Lower movement speed on DOI did not reach significance (p = 0.063 within trials, 0.079 in the intertrial interval). The reward-port durations of Figure 2 are blocked because the dataset has no port entries or exits. The tracking has no frame times, and the within-trial speed and head-twitch results depend on the paper's assumed clock.
