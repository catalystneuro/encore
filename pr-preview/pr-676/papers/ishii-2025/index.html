Post-ejaculatory inhibition of female sexual drive via heterogeneous neuronal ensembles in the medial preoptic area

The behavioral, in situ and calcium imaging findings held in the authors' Figshare data. The whole-brain mapping of Figure 2, including the medial preoptic increase, could not be tested. Fewer zone transitions and longer isolation after ejaculation, Fos rise in Vgat and Vglut2 cells, a late Vgat-only response cluster and lower receptivity under activation all reproduced. Figure 2 is blocked because the dandiset holds only 1.38 TB of raw volumes, with no cell counts and no segmentation classifier. Longer Vgat decay held only by giving 44 non-returning cells 120 s, and the "only post-ejaculatory" chemogenetic effect failed under the rank-sum test but held under the paired test.
