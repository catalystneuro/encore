Fedele et al. 2020 · Other journals

The relation between neuronal firing, local field potentials and hemodynamic activity in the human amygdala in response to aversive dynamic visual stimuli

doi:10.1016/j.neuroimage.2020.116705 · paper key fedele-2020

Datasets

  • DANDI:000576

Most main findings did not hold up in this data, and the BOLD findings could not be tested. The sustained delta decrease outside the seizure onset zone appeared under some z-scorings and not others, as the paper does not say how power was z-scored. The onset increase in high gamma power was not found, and the high gamma modulation index did not separate seizure onset zone amygdalae, though the paper does not state the window it used. Firing did not increase in all neurons during aversive videos (27 of 42 units did). The BOLD correlations need fMRI time courses that the dataset does not hold.
