Reproduced when, under every reading, the mean variation over the somatic mice is smaller than over the dendritic mice; blocked as methods_underspecified when the readings disagree. Not testable if the public package cannot be run on these masks.

  • fig5-stimulus-responses

    Somatic and dendritic ROIs respond to both the Gabor and the visual flow stimuli, many with increased fluorescence at stimulus onset, and their responses differ between consistent and inconsistent stimuli.

    Both the somatic and dendritic ROIs showed clear responses to both the Gabor and visual flow stimuli, with many showing increased fluorescence responses to the onset of the stimuli ... There were also clear differences in the responses to the consistent versus inconsistent stimuli as well

    How it will be judged

    The paper shows randomly chosen example ROIs and runs no test. Session 1 of each of the 11 analysed mice, per plane (line and plane). ROI selection, open, (a) all ROIs, or (b) the ROIs the Fig. 5 legends select (SNR above the session median, and the median pairwise correlation of single-sequence responses, smoothed with a four-point moving average, and the standard deviation and skew of the mean response for Gabor, or the inconsistent minus consistent mean response for flow, each above the session's 75th percentile). The test is open, as the paper names none; the readings are (i) a two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test across ROIs at p < 0.05, and (ii) the mean difference across ROIs exceeding twice its SEM (the legends plot mean and SEM). Reproduced when, under every reading pair, at least one stimulus in each of the four planes shows a consistent against inconsistent difference; blocked as methods_underspecified when the readings disagree.   • fig7c-correlations-similar

    The distributions of pairwise ROI correlations are very similar for apical dendrite ROIs and for somatic ROIs, so the dendrites are unlikely to be heavily over-segmented.

    The distributions of correlation coefficients were very similar for the apical dendrite ROIs and for the somatic ROIs ... suggesting that we were unlikely to be heavily over-segmenting the dendritic data

    Technical Validation, paragraph 3; Figure 7c

    How it will be judged

    Pearson correlations over all ROI pairs of each session, from whole-session dF/F traces smoothed with a 4-sample rolling mean (Fig. 7c legend; get_correlation, rolling_win 4), ROIs without NaN or Inf values, sessions 1 to 3 of the 11 analysed mice, pooled across mice within each line, plane and session. Reproduced when both readings hold in all six comparisons; not reproduced when neither holds in most; blocked as methods_underspecified otherwise. Quantiles and the fractions of pairs above 0.8 are reported for every group.
