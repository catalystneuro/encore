Shin et al. 2025 · Nature Neuroscience

Allen Institute Openscope - Illusion project

doi:10.1038/s41593-025-02055-5 · paper key shin-2025

Datasets

  • DANDI:000248

The Neuropixels findings mostly held, but the two-photon and holography findings could not be tested. IC-encoders were found in all six visual areas, and decoding of illusory-contour inference worked in V1 and most other areas. The exclusively center-responsive V1 neurons did not reproduce (44 units against 18), nor did their responses to illusory and real edges or their orientation match, possibly because the selection procedure differs. Stronger higher-area input to IC-encoders appeared in every analysis variant, but a tie of medians in one left it undecided. The findings on the causal role of IC-encoders in pattern completion could not be tested, as the imaging and holography recordings are not in the dataset.
