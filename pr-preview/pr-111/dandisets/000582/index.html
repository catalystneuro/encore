    Reproduction Grid, head-direction and conjunctive cells by entorhinal layer (Sargolini et al. 2006)

    Tests three conclusions of Sargolini et al. (2006) on dandiset 000582, which holds spike times and tracked positions for 620 cell-sessions from the medial entorhinal cortex of 15 rats. For every cell it computes a grid score, head-direction tuning and speed tuning, with chance levels from time-shifted spike trains, and compares the cell types found in each cortical layer.
