Zaki et al. 2024 · Nature

Offline ensemble co-reactivation links memories across days

doi:10.1038/s41586-024-08168-4 · paper key zaki-2024

Datasets

  • DANDI:000718

Most of the paper's main findings, including retrospective linking and offline ensemble recruitment, could not be tested here, because the dandiset holds only two of nine mice and one 10 min offline recording per period. Burst skew above shuffled data held, but burst frequency above shuffle was blocked, as the Methods do not say how bursts are counted in surrogates. Preferential co-bursting of the overlap and neutral ensembles during wake did not reproduce in the one usable mouse, and the slowing before bursts was not seen in a telemetry proxy for movement. Minor checks held: the mice match the source data and the high-shock mouse froze more (64% against 9%).
