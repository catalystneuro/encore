  • data-populations

    How it will be judged

    Counts are exact for sessions and populations. The selection is the Methods' one, "responses ≥ 1 s.d. above spontaneous rate", to the targets in isolation. The Methods do not say which response is compared (the mean over all isolated targets, or the best target) nor how the spontaneous rate and its SD are measured; the readings are (a) the mean count to the isolated targets against the mean plus 1 SD of the spontaneous count, (b) the largest of the per-orientation means against the same. Population sizes are tolerated by the reported SEM and neuron totals by 10%; the claim is reproduced when every value holds under one reading. The per-animal neuron counts of Figure S7 (V1 264 and 132, V4 288 and 403; which animal is which could not be read) are reported too.
