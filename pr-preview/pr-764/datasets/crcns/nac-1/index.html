What the attempts that used this dataset found about the data themselves, for its owner and the archive, under the version of the dataset each was found on.

Dataset unversioned files (the archive keeps no versions) · tested 2026-11-06 · 6 open findings

  • Metadata errorThe data set holds 368 units from 90 sessions (rat and date, one behavior program each), where the paper and the data description say 95 sessions.
    Found by the attempt on Ray et al. 2022 (ray-2022-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Data missingThe eight pre-surgery fear discrimination sessions of Figure 1C are not deposited; the authors' preRecordingBehavior.m reads them from a folder that is not in the data set.
    Found by the attempt on Ray et al. 2022 (ray-2022-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Data missingThe paper's population (k-means cluster) labels are not deposited. The authors' mainAnalysis.m computes them with one MATLAB k-means start after rng(1), which cannot be repeated outside MATLAB, and the reproduced clustering is unstable and does not give the paper's counts, so every population finding rests on a reproduced partition.
    Found by the attempt on Ray et al. 2022 (ray-2022-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Description missingThe data description does not say which EVT channel holds which event, nor that the cue channels change with the behavior program; only cubeScriptPR25.m gives the map (EVT01 shock, EVT02 pellet, EVT13 poke, cue channels per program a to f).
    Found by the attempt on Ray et al. 2022 (ray-2022-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Description missingThe recording is paused between trials, so the files hold spikes, pokes and pellets only from 20 s before each cue onset to about 33.5 s after it (inter-spike gaps of about 200 s). The data description does not say so, and the paper's inter-trial interval analyses can use only these windows.
    Found by the attempt on Ray et al. 2022 (ray-2022-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Metadata errorThe data description says NAcccube.poke.base is the nose poke rate 2 s before the cue; in cube.mat it is the rate over the 20 s before the cue (pokes per min), in all 368 units.
    Found by the attempt on Ray et al. 2022 (ray-2022-01, 2026-11-06)
