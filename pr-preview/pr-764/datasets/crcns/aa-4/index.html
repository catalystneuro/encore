What the attempts that used this dataset found about the data themselves, for its owner and the archive, under the version of the dataset each was found on.

Dataset unversioned files (the archive keeps no versions) · tested 2026-11-13 · 6 open findings

  • Description missingThe files do not mark the 914 single auditory units the data description and the paper report, nor the 404 units the paper analyses. 1281 h5 files are labelled single units (sortType), 1246 of them with stored I_AR of at least 2.3 bits/s, the 2015 auditory threshold. The spike-snippet SNR that defines a single unit is not stored; computed from the snippet files as the description defines it, the threshold of 5 leaves 461 or 274 of the 1401 auditory spike-sorted units (two readings of the definition), not 914. Without a list of units, none of the paper's population results can be repeated on its units.
    Found by the attempt on Elie et al. 2019 (elie-2019-01, 2026-11-13)
  • Metadata errorThe data description says the data set holds 688 multi-units and 914 single auditory units, one h5 file each. The files are 845 unsorted multi-unit files (sortType tdt) and 1453 spike-sorted files (named _ss): 1281 labelled single, 63 multi and 109 noise.
    Found by the attempt on Elie et al. 2019 (elie-2019-01, 2026-11-13)
  • Data missingThe anatomical area and the x, y, z coordinates of the recording sites, used in the paper's Table 1 and Figure 10, are not in the data set; only the electrode, site and array depths are stored.
    Found by the attempt on Elie et al. 2019 (elie-2019-01, 2026-11-13)
  • Metadata errorThe numbers of stimuli and trials differ from the stated ones. In the call protocol (groups Call, Call1, Call1c, Call2, Call3) single units have 115.3 stimuli on average (sd 34.2, 34 to 133) against the paper's 114 (sd 22, 34 to 123), and 90.7 call stimuli of the nine call types (34 to 110); over every group they have 121.9 stimuli (73 to 137) against the description's 144 (64 to 153). 1874 unit-stimulus pairs of the call protocol have 12 trials, against a stated maximum of 11. The other protocols in the files (Sel1 to Sel7, Mask1, STRF1), which also hold call stimuli, are not described.
    Found by the attempt on Elie et al. 2019 (elie-2019-01, 2026-11-13)
  • Metadata errorThree h5 files store sortType as 'singl'; and the description's section on file organisation names a bird WhiBlu5896M, where the files and the summary say WhiBlu5396M.
    Found by the attempt on Elie et al. 2019 (elie-2019-01, 2026-11-13)
  • Description missingThe per-site .f32 files of spike waveforms are described only as the waveform of the action potentials. Their layout (18 float32 samples per spike, indexed from 1 by the spike_ids of the h5 files) is documented only by the plotting function h5_plot_one_spike.m in the archive's CRCNSCode.tar.gz. There is one per site and protocol; bird BlaBro09xxF has two files for site 4 and Call1 (1,956,253 and 33,047 snippets, named with _ and -).
    Found by the attempt on Elie et al. 2019 (elie-2019-01, 2026-11-13)
