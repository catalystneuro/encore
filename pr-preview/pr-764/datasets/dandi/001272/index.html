  • Data missingThe dataset holds 3 neurons recorded with Di-8-ANEPPS (subjects 2, 3 and 4 of 2024-08-06), where the paper reports 8 for this experiment (Figure S1).
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2025 (semenov-2025-03, 2026-11-01)
  • Data missingNone of the paper's ten deposits holds the 250-ms sine wave sessions in sodium gluconate solution behind Figure 3B; 001030 holds only 2-s ramped waves in that solution.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2025 (semenov-2025-03, 2026-11-01)
  • OtherDANDI 001034 appears to duplicate DANDI 001032. All 80 of its 2-kHz unmodulated files have frame stacks identical to those of 001032, under subject names ending in _3 in place of _1; its other files were not compared.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2025 (semenov-2025-03, 2026-11-01)
  • Metadata errorEvery file of DANDI 001272 (65 of 65) describes the session as performed "without tetrodotoxin (vehicle control)", wording taken from the tetrodotoxin experiment (001271); the Di-8-ANEPPS experiment had no tetrodotoxin arm.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2025 (semenov-2025-03, 2026-11-01)
  • Metadata errorThe tetrodotoxin sessions of DANDI 001271 (71 of 71) describe tetrodotoxin as one "which abolished the action potentials and caused sustained depolarization during application of stimulating waves". The paper says it blocked the sustained depolarization, and in the data the late dF after tetrodotoxin is -0.38 to -0.70% in all 6 neurons.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2025 (semenov-2025-03, 2026-11-01)
  • Description missingThe stimulus onset is not stored in DANDI 001272 or 001271 (the Methods put it at 50 ms; the responses are consistent with that, peaking in an onset contrast at 50 ms), and no file stores the stimulus waveform or the field in V/cm other than as text in the session description.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2025 (semenov-2025-03, 2026-11-01)
  • Description missingAmplitudes are given in volts with inconsistent meaning. DANDI 001032 to 001035 call them peak-to-peak, 001030 and 001031 one polarity ("peak-to-peak would be two-fold larger"), and 001272 and 001271 say neither, yet all apply the same 36 V/cm per volt. The files also say the delivered amplitude is smaller by a frequency-dependent calibration "to be reported separately", which is not deposited. With the stated conversion the mean 2-kHz FluoVolt threshold is about 150 V/cm, against the paper's 57.7 V/cm.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2025 (semenov-2025-03, 2026-11-01)
  • Description missingThe frames are 16 x 16 pixels where the Methods give 64 x 64 after binning, and nothing says which part of the sensor they are or how the soma region was drawn, so the paper's soma ROI cannot be reproduced.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2025 (semenov-2025-03, 2026-11-01)
  • OtherFrame 0 of every DANDI 001272 file is 1.7 to 15.7 times brighter than the median frame, a camera artefact that must be dropped before computing F0. 33 of 62 intervals between consecutive sessions are under the 20 s the Methods give (range 4 to 168 s).
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2025 (semenov-2025-03, 2026-11-01)
  • Description missingDANDI 001273 does not hold the debris ROI the paper scales its strobe images to, nor the ROIs over the cell poles, nor the cell-shape groups of Figure 4. Without the scaling, laser-flash fluctuation of the order of the signal dominates the replicate-mean traces. The files form 22 experiments of 7 or 8 replicates, one pair and 4 single files, where the paper reports 19, with no mapping.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2025 (semenov-2025-03, 2026-11-01)
  • OtherIn DANDI 001273, 164 of the 175 replicates fitted have at least one dark frame (257 in all, 0.34 to 0.80 of the median frame mean), in 95 of them only the first acquired frame. They are left out of the fits.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2025 (semenov-2025-03, 2026-11-01)
