Reproduced when neither model finds a condition effect; not reproduced when either does; the effect sizes are reported either way. The F(1,28) the paper prints does not match a 3-level factor; the readings named are (a) each relation condition tested against identity in turn (two tests per measure, the claim holding when no test reaches p < 0.05), (b) a single 3-level condition term. Accuracy per subject and condition is taken under the two readings of behavior-proficient, (a) all 40 trials, (b) the trials kept in the preprocessed data (correct over correct plus incorrect); the kept-trial counts of correct-trial-counts (100 correct, 10 incorrect) give a mean near 0.91, which favours (b).   • power-theta-sustained

    Task-induced power showed sustained theta band (3 to 7 Hz) activity in MTL, PFC and OFC over encoding and delay, with transient activity above 16 Hz, and alpha (8 to 13 Hz) desynchronized in the MTL.

    Single-subject analyses revealed sustained theta band (centered at 3–7 Hz) and variable transient activities at higher frequencies (>16 Hz) in all regions (z > 1.96, p < 0.05; Fig 2 ). Activity in the alpha band (centered at 8–13 Hz) was desynchronized in the MTL (z < −1.96, p < 0.05)

    Results, Theta activity is sustained during encoding and delay, paragraph 2; Figure 2

    How it will be judged

    Power z as in M2 and M3 over the encoding and precue and the postcue segments. The paper shows representative electrodes and gives no count of subjects or electrodes for "sustained", so a measure is fixed here from its z threshold. For each subject and region, the theta z (filters centred in 3 to 7 Hz, averaged) averaged over the region's electrodes; theta is sustained when this z exceeds 1.96 over at least half of the encoding and delay time points. Readings for the unit, (a) the region average per subject, (b) per electrode, the fraction of electrodes meeting the rule. Reproduced when, under one reading, theta is sustained in each of MTL, PFC and OFC in the majority of the subjects with that region, and the MTL alpha z (filters centred in 8 to 13 Hz) is below -1.96 over at least half of the time points in the majority of subjects with MTL electrodes. The half and the majority are choices made here, as the paper gives none. The "variable, transient" activity above 16 Hz has no measure in the paper; the number of electrodes with any z > 1.96 above 16 Hz is reported but not judged.
