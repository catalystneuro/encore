Several main findings held in this data. VENs map to L5 ET and L5/6 CT, have the lowest phase peak index, smaller sodium currents and larger evoked EPSPs. Event amplitudes did not differ between VENs and PCs. Lower miniature EPSC frequency in VENs did not reproduce, because the deposited cell labels are the opposite of those in the paper's analysis sheet. The other event-frequency findings could not be settled, as the paper gives no detection setting beyond a 5 pA threshold. Broader, lower VEN action potentials and the metabolite gradient (179 metabolites against 228) did not reproduce, and the connectivity counts matched only in part. The transcriptomic atlas was too large to test, and the model code is missing.
