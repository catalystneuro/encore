Dataset version 0.260218.2052 · tested 2026-10-16 · 13 open findings

  • File format errorIn 24 files the end index of the first unit in the units table is short by a multiple of 65,536 spikes (k = 1 to 7). The missing spikes open the second unit's spike train, which then restarts at the beginning of the recording, so the second unit's times are not sorted. The first moved spike follows the first unit's last stored spike by 2 ms to 0.5 s. This looks like an integer overflow at conversion; the waveforms follow the same boundaries.
    Found by the attempt on Senzai et al. 2017 (senzai-2017-01, 2026-10-16)
  • Metadata errorThe genotypes in the files (6 SST-Cre, 5 Drd2-Cre::Ai35, 4 POMC-Cre::Arch, 1 POMC-Cre::Ai35) do not match the paper's 16 mice (7 SST-Cre, 5 DRD2-Cre::Ai35, 3 POMC-Cre::Ai35 and 1 wild type).
    Found by the attempt on Senzai et al. 2017 (senzai-2017-01, 2026-10-16)
  • Description missing83 of 101 files do not say which shank each unit was recorded on (`shank_id` is a cluster number, and there is no `electrode_group`), and no file gives site coordinates (x, y, z are NaN). The paper's depth measures can be computed for 17 files only, and distances between cells not at all.
    Found by the attempt on Senzai et al. 2017 (senzai-2017-01, 2026-10-16)
  • Description missingThe T-maze position series (`twhl_linearized`, `twhl_norm`) are not described. The values show column 0 of `twhl_linearized` runs along the central arm (0 to 2) and column 1 is an angle along the circular side arms, negative for the left arm, which fits the maze in the Methods but is not stated.
    Found by the attempt on Senzai et al. 2017 (senzai-2017-01, 2026-10-16)
  • Description missingThe states table holds `awake`, `quiet`, `nrem`, `rem` and `transit` without a description; the paper names wake, NREM and REM, and whether `quiet` counts as wake is not stated.
    Found by the attempt on Senzai et al. 2017 (senzai-2017-01, 2026-10-16)
  • Description missingThe interneuron labels (narrow-waveform, wide-waveform) do not say whether the unit is in the dentate gyrus or CA3, which the paper separates; only the excitatory label was split by region (granule cell or CA3 pyramidal cell).
    Found by the attempt on Senzai et al. 2017 (senzai-2017-01, 2026-10-16)
  • Data missingThe monosynaptic connections the authors detected and curated (`MonoSynConvClick.mat`) were not converted (the conversion code comments them out), so the pair-based results (Figures 3, 4 and 7) cannot be checked on the authors' pairs.
    Found by the attempt on Senzai et al. 2017 (senzai-2017-01, 2026-10-16)
  • OtherThe dandiset holds 568 mossy cells and 609 granule cells against the paper's 500 and 586, and 237 units labelled `missing` (units of the spike sorting with no row in the authors' unit summary).
    Found by the attempt on Senzai et al. 2017 (senzai-2017-01, 2026-10-16)
  • File format errorThe light-pulse series PulseStim_5V_20ms_LD12 of YutaMouse57-161006 holds 89,086 times of which 24,286 are duplicates, and its times decrease once.
    Found by the attempt on Senzai et al. 2017 (senzai-2017-01, 2026-10-16)
  • OtherThe units table has two layouts. 18 files (mice 33, 41, 42 and 54) carry an `electrode_group` column naming each unit's shank, one of them without waveforms (YutaMouse33-150218, which the conversion script lists as excluded); the other 83 files do not.
    Found by the attempt on Senzai et al. 2017 (senzai-2017-01, 2026-10-16)
  • Description missingSeven dates hold two files of one mouse (the second named with `b`). They share no spike trains, so they are different recordings, but the metadata do not say whether they come from a second probe or a second session.
    Found by the attempt on Senzai et al. 2017 (senzai-2017-01, 2026-10-16)
  • Metadata errorIn the 17 files with the wide-band signal and each unit's shank, the electrodes table's assignment of columns to shanks and sites disagrees with the data. The column where a unit's spike is largest in the wide-band signal lies on the shank the units table gives for 45% of units (11% to 79% by file) and is the table's own site for 9%, and the DS2 amplitude along the table's site numbers crosses zero more than once on 43 of 64 shanks where it crosses at all. The wide-band and LFP columns match each other. The units' shanks (`electrode_group`) and stored waveform channels fit the wide-band signal under one fixed map of columns, the same in all 4 mice (a map from three mice gives the column of a unit's largest spike for 78% to 89% of units in the fourth, against 5% to 13% for the table), so the error is in the table, whose blocks of 8 rows do not match the probe's columns.
    Found by the attempt on Senzai et al. 2017 (senzai-2017-01, 2026-10-16)
  • Description missingThe stored spike waveforms are about 5 times larger than the same spikes in the wide-band signal (median peak-to-peak 421 against 81 uV, 300 Hz high-pass), and their filtering and scaling are not described. They match the wide-band spike best after a 300 Hz high-pass (median correlation 0.987, against 0.981 unfiltered and 0.867 at 800 Hz), with a gain of 5.08, close to 5.13, the inverse of the wide-band conversion of 0.195 uV per count, which is consistent with waveforms stored in ADC counts and read as microvolts. Each holds 1.6 ms with the trough at 0.8 ms, which cuts off the peak of 43% of GC, 67% of MC and 78% of CA3 waveforms.
    Found by the attempt on Senzai et al. 2017 (senzai-2017-01, 2026-10-16)
