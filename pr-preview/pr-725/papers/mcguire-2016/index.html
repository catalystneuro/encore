    How it will be judged

The paper does not say which proportion test; the readings are (a) the chi-square test of two proportions with continuity correction, (b) the same without it (a two-proportion z test). Which trials count is also open; the readings are (i) all completed trials, (ii) completed trials excluding premature withdrawals and trials with no choice. Reproduced when, for every rat the data hold, FFF has a higher right-choice fraction than SSS, FMS and SMF both lie between them, and FMS against SMF is not significant at 0.00625 for every rat but 62SC, and for 62SC (if the data hold it) is significant with more right choices to FMS, under one reading. The paper tests eight rats; if the data hold fewer, the result is stated for those held and is at most partially reproduced.
