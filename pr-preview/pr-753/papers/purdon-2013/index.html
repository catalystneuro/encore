Outcomes of 22 claims

  • ⊘22 blocked

None of the paper's main findings could be tested in this data, because the dandiset holds the EEG of one of the ten volunteers and none of the task data. The loss of response to clicks before verbal stimuli, the timing of unconsciousness, the beta and gamma rise before it, the frontal alpha mode and the phase-amplitude shift all need the button-press responses, which are not deposited. The 64 channels also lack names and positions, so spatial results cannot be built. All 22 claims were blocked, and none reproduced or failed. The one subject's band-power course tests no claim.

Reproduction EEG signatures of loss and recovery of consciousness from propofol (Purdon et al. 2013)

Checks dandiset 001285 against the findings of Purdon et al. (2013) on behaviour and EEG during gradual propofol induction and emergence in 10 volunteers. The dandiset holds the 64-channel EEG of one subject, without channel names, behavioural responses, infusion levels or time markers, so none of the paper's 22 claims can be tested; the notebook describes the file and checks it against its description.
