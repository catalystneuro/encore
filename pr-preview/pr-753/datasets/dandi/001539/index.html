Dataset version 0.250815.1203 · tested 2026-11-03 · 9 open findings

  • File format errorThe units table's electrodes column, a reference to rows of the electrode table, holds tetrode numbers (the N of the electrode labels 'tetrodeN') in all 38 files. Read as rows, as NWB tools do, it places 131 units on OB tetrodes, which carried no units, and gives 540 CA1 and 752 PFC units; read as tetrode numbers it gives 915 CA1, 508 PFC and no OB units, against the paper's 934 and 508 active units.
    Found by the attempt on Symanski et al. 2022 (symanski-2022-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Metadata errorElectrode group names and electrode labels disagree in all 38 files (for example group Tetrode5 is labelled tetrode6), so the tetrode a channel belongs to is ambiguous without the units' values. Read as electrode-group numbers, the units' electrodes values put 24 units on OB tetrodes and match no group for 333 units; read as label numbers they put every unit on a CA1 or PFC tetrode.
    Found by the attempt on Symanski et al. 2022 (symanski-2022-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Description missingThe electrode table's referenced column, which separates the ground-referenced ('Ref OFF') from the locally referenced ('Ref ON') channels, has the placeholder description 'my description' in all 38 files, so which reference each channel uses is read from the labels and the paper's Methods.
    Found by the attempt on Symanski et al. 2022 (symanski-2022-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Description missingThe trial table has no odor identity and no chosen well; unrewarded trials have no reward times. The odor and the choice must be reconstructed from the rewarded well and the position, and which side is odor 1 (heptanol) cannot be known.
    Found by the attempt on Symanski et al. 2022 (symanski-2022-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Selection not documentedThe dandiset holds 915 CA1 and 508 PFC units, close to the paper's active units (934 and 508), not the 1,309 and 717 recorded; the selection is not described. 53 units have fewer than 100 spikes within the task epochs.
    Found by the attempt on Symanski et al. 2022 (symanski-2022-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Data missingThe files hold no spike waveforms or widths (needed for the paper's cell-type rule), no nasal thermocouple signal, no air-only sessions and no sleep epochs; an inventory of every object in the 38 files finds none of them. Three claims cannot be tested, and every claim that separates pyramidal cells from interneurons rests on a rate-only rule. The two rate-only rules (the Methods' 7 Hz alone and the code's 9.5 Hz) give different outcomes for three claims (fig5gh-phase-preference, fig5-rr-more-than-beta, fig4d-ccg-lags), which are blocked for this reason.
    Found by the attempt on Symanski et al. 2022 (symanski-2022-01, 2026-11-03)
  • OtherThe acquisition ElectricalSeries is a copy of the processed LFP in every file (equal on the stretch checked), which doubles the dandiset to 95 GB; the LFP is stored as uncompressed, unchunked float64.
    Found by the attempt on Symanski et al. 2022 (symanski-2022-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Metadata errorPosition in CS39 session 02 spans 59 by 25 units against about 105 by 45 cm on the other CS39 days, and its speeds are lower in proportion, so its stated unit of centimeters does not hold for that file.
    Found by the attempt on Symanski et al. 2022 (symanski-2022-01, 2026-11-03)
  • OtherFour CS42 trials (sessions 02, 04, 05) end after the last LFP sample; the odor port position also moves between epochs in CS31 session 02 and CS34 session 02 (camera frame changes).
    Found by the attempt on Symanski et al. 2022 (symanski-2022-01, 2026-11-03)
