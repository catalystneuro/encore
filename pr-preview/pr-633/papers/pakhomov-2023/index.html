DANDI:000686: None of the paper's main findings was confirmed in this data: four could be tested and did not reproduce, and sixteen could not be tested. The dataset holds only the Fig. 5 monolayer images, so the field simulation, the single-cell uptake results and Figs. 6 to 9 (packet number, amplitude, packet type, electrode gap) were blocked for missing data. Trains did not exceed packets at the far electrode, and the central peak with rotated packets was not significant under every reading. Every difference went the way the paper reports, and the contrast between rotated packets and trains narrowly failed in 5 of 32 readings, depending on an ROI width the paper does not give. Profiles were rebuilt from images, since no measurements are deposited.

DANDI:000875: Only the Fig. 6 experiment could be tested, and there the main findings held in part. More packets raised dye uptake across the array and narrowed the central peak, both reproduced. The central peak's near twofold excess over the periphery for one packet, and its twofold excess over the least affected area, held only partly (124% against about 200% for one packet under the main reading). Bipolar cancellation, rotation, amplitude, array size, packet type, electrode gap and the field simulation could not be tested, because the dataset holds only nine Fig. 6 images. I did not create `summaries/pakhomov-2023-02.md`.

DANDI:001025: Only the voltage series of the paper's main findings could be tested here, and none of it reproduced. The 15- to 17-fold rise in dye uptake from 2.4 to 5.6 kV was not seen (ratios 5.7 to 7.3), possibly because the paper does not say whether background was subtracted. Focusing that does not depend on amplitude, in small and large arrays, also came out not reproduced, but this rests on an assumed ROI layout and, for the large array, two dishes against three. Single-cell bipolar cancellation could not be tested because those images are not on DANDI. The rotation, packet number, packet shape and electrode height findings appear to be in four sibling dandisets.

DANDI:001027: The paper's main claims could not be tested for the most part, and the three that could be tested did not hold in this data. The dandiset holds only the Figure 8 experiment, so the single-cell uptake results (Figure 4), rotation and packet-number effects (Figures 5 and 6), amplitude and array-size independence (Figure 7) and the electrode-gap effect (Figure 9) are untested, because their data are not in the dataset. For Figure 8, Packet 2 did not show the best central focusing: its center was 83% of the periphery, against 183% in the paper, while Packet 3 reached 129%. The three packets also did not give equal central uptake, and Packet 3 was not stronger than Packet 2 near the electrodes. The Packet 2 and 3 labels in the files may be swapped relative to the paper, and the data cannot settle this.
