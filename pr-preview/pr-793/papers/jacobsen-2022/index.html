  • fig2a-four-or-fewer-starters

    With the single-cell protocol, starter cells could be identified conclusively in 15 of 31 animals (48%), and each of these had four or fewer starter cells.

    With this approach, we could unambiguously target four or fewer starter cells in 48% of animals (15 of 31) (Figure 2A)

    Results, A virus-based method for targeting a single cell and identifying its inputs, paragraph 4; Figure 2A

    How it will be judged

    Counted from histology, with the classification of the STAR Methods (Conclusive/inconclusive classification) over the 31 animals of Table S1. The denominator is 31, so the exclusions the Methods name (two missed injections, one lost section) are taken as already applied to Table S1. The 15 conclusive animals of Table S1 include the four with zero starter cells and no rabies-positive cells (61159, 59943, 60218, 60920), although the Methods call an animal inconclusive when no conclusive cell is found; the count follows Table S1. Reproduced when the count and fraction are within tolerance and no conclusive animal has more than four starter cells.
