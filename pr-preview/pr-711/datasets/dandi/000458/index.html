Findings about the data 17

What the attempts that used this dataset found about the data themselves, for its owner and the archive. The claims and their outcomes are on each paper’s page.

  • Data missingThe depth of the stimulating electrode in mm and its position are not in the files; the trial table gives only the target area and the label "superficial" or "deep". The paper's stimulation depths and the spatial analysis of the first ERP peak cannot be tested.
    Found by the attempt on Claar et al. 2023 (claar-2023-01, 2026-10-30)
  • Description missingProbe channels and units carry CCF area acronyms without cortical layers (MOs, SSp-ll, ...), so the layer-resolved CSD analyses of Figure 6 (a sink at the border of layers 2/3 and 5, CSD by layer) cannot be run from the files, and the exclusion of layer 1 in Figure 3 can only be approximated (here by the top 100 um of each area).
    Found by the attempt on Claar et al. 2023 (claar-2023-01, 2026-10-30)
  • Metadata errorThe units' waveform_duration is described as the mean duration in seconds, but its values (0.10 to 2.07, median 0.58) are in milliseconds.
    Found by the attempt on Claar et al. 2023 (claar-2023-01, 2026-10-30)
  • Description missingThe units are described as those that passed the authors' quality threshold, which is not stated. All meet ISI violations < 0.5 and amplitude cutoff < 0.1, but presence ratios go down to 0.02, so the threshold is not the three metrics of Siegle et al. (2021) that the paper cites.
    Found by the attempt on Claar et al. 2023 (claar-2023-01, 2026-10-30)
  • OtherThe RS unit counts in the Figure 4 legend (870, 4110, 2559 and 9363) are three times the counts of the corresponding units in the files (290, 1370, 853 and 3162; exactly three times for the first three). Every site with units has three currents, and counting each unit once per current gives 870, 4110, 2559 and 9486, so the legend most likely counts unit x current; the paper does not say.
    Found by the attempt on Claar et al. 2023 (claar-2023-01, 2026-10-30)
  • Selection not documentedis_valid marks all 720 pulses of the superficial SSp site of subject 586468, and 747 of 1440 pulses of subject 569070, as invalid, for "large electrical artifacts" or "off target" stimulation, without saying which. Leaving out the 586468 superficial site gives the paper's N of 12 superficial sites in Figure 1E and 7 in Figure 4.
    Found by the attempt on Claar et al. 2023 (claar-2023-01, 2026-10-30)
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the authors. Each stage delivered pulses at three currents; which current, or which pulses, do the ERP, PCIst and spiking analyses use? The middle current with all awake trials gives the example ERP of Figure 1, figure supplement 1 (1.011 s and 11.133 SD.s against 1.01 and 11.13) and the p-values of Figures 1E and 1F (0.081 and 0.035 against 0.0843 and 0.0354), which points to it; the highest current gives different outcomes, for example ERP durations and magnitudes above the paper's and an anesthetized PCIst of 23.5 against 16.5.
    Found by the attempt on Claar et al. 2023 (claar-2023-01, 2026-10-30)

The figure supplement's undivided area above z = 3, in SD.s, reproduces the example and Figure 1F at the middle current, which suggests no division.
    Found by the attempt on Claar et al. 2023 (claar-2023-01, 2026-10-30)

    Found by the attempt on Claar et al. 2023 (claar-2023-01, 2026-10-30)
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the authors. For the modulated-unit test, is the pre-stimulus window the one ending at onset or the mirror of the post window, and is the Benjamini-Hochberg correction applied per session or over all sessions?     Found by the attempt on Claar et al. 2023 (claar-2023-01, 2026-10-30)
  • OtherThe EEG stimulus artifact is largest at the sample before the first EEG sample at or after the trial table's start_time in 24 of 30 sites, and at that sample in the other 6, so the start times lag the artifact by up to one EEG sample (0.4 ms). A mask that starts at the onset sample leaves the artifact in.
    Found by the attempt on Claar et al. 2023 (claar-2023-01, 2026-10-30)
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the authors. The population spiking magnitude of Figure 3 is described as the area under the z-scored average spike density, in which a fall below baseline cancels a rise, while the Results say the metric "accounts for both reductions as well as increases". Was |z| integrated? In the example session the signed integral gives 2.95 in MO and the integral of |z| 28.75 (paper 30.9), and only |z| gives the deep medians and the significance pattern of Figure 3D.
    Found by the attempt on Claar et al. 2023 (claar-2023-01, 2026-10-30)
  • Published code disagreesThe paper's analysis code computes the evoked firing rate used for the exclusion (below 0.1 Hz or above 200 Hz) as the sum of the spikes' latencies in ms (np.sum of spike_times) over trials times 10 ms, where the Methods describe an average evoked firing rate. With this rate the dataset gives the paper's sample exactly (662 kept, 203 excluded, median 34 per recording, 1 to 93) and its Figure 4D means (synchrony 0.325 and 0.393, entropy 1.347 and 1.111); with the spike-count rate it gives 721 kept and 144 excluded. The 200 Hz bound thus acted on spike-count rates near 45 Hz.
    Found by the attempt on Burman et al. 2023 (burman-2023-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Selection not documentedVersion 0.230317.0039 holds 18 files with units and electrical stimulation awake and under isoflurane; the paper uses 17. sub-599017_ses-20220210 (943 units, SSp stimulation, 718 awake and 720 isoflurane valid trials) is not in the paper's code list, and the paper does not say why. Six further files hold EEG only, with no units.
    Found by the attempt on Burman et al. 2023 (burman-2023-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Data missingThe paper's gramicidin perforated patch recordings (Figures 1 to 3, S1 to S5), which carry most of its findings, are not in DANDI:000458 nor in any other dandiset found; the paper offers them on request. 24 of its claims cannot be tested.
    Found by the attempt on Burman et al. 2023 (burman-2023-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Published code disagreesThe Methods select regular spiking units with a waveform duration of 0.4 ms or more, the code more than 0.4 ms, and the Methods name the areas SSp-ll, SSp-tr and MOs where the code takes every unit whose location begins with the stimulated region. Neither difference changes the units selected in this dataset.
    Found by the attempt on Burman et al. 2023 (burman-2023-01, 2026-11-10)
