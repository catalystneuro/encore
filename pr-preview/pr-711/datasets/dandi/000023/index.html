Findings about the data 10

What the attempts that used this dataset found about the data themselves, for its owner and the archive. The claims and their outcomes are on each paper’s page.

  • Identifier missingThe files carry no SpecimenID, the identifier by which the paper's code and tables name cells. Each file can be linked only by its donor and its ephys session id, which lies 13 to 685 below the cell's SpecimenID. The code's per-cell features confirm the link for 261 of the 283 cells that have them under the Methods' sweep QC and for 282 under IPFX's sweep QC alone (one gives no features under either); for the 35 cells without features in the code, the sample dates and tube order in the code's sample_id are consistent with it.
    Found by the attempt on Berg et al. 2020 (berg-2020-01, 2026-10-23)
  • Description missingThe files carry no t-type, soma depth, brain region or medical condition, on which every finding of the paper rests; they must be taken from the paper's code. session_description and the filtering field read PLACEHOLDER, and the archive summary gives the variable measured as ProcessingModule and the technique as "analytical technique".
    Found by the attempt on Berg et al. 2020 (berg-2020-01, 2026-10-23)
  • Selection not documentedThe dandiset holds 318 of the 385 cells of the paper's table, including all 283 cells with electrophysiological features in the paper's code; the other 67 are absent. Of the 35 cells without features in the code, 19 give features here under the Methods' sweep QC (2 of them fail cell QC) and 25 under IPFX's sweep QC alone, and which recordings were judged too incomplete, or why, is not said.
    Found by the attempt on Berg et al. 2020 (berg-2020-01, 2026-10-23)
  • Metadata errorDonor H18.03.318 is female in the files and male in the paper's donor table and cell table; donor H18.06.005 is 56 years old in the files (P20440.0D) and 57 in both tables.
    Found by the attempt on Berg et al. 2020 (berg-2020-01, 2026-10-23)
  • Published code disagreesThe code's feature table measures adaptation as the mean over all spiking long squares, initial firing rate on the hero sweep (about 50 pA above rheobase) and tau from long squares, where the Methods describe adaptation at about 50 pA above rheobase, initial rate at rheobase and tau from short pulses. The features recomputed here match the code's table, not the Methods.
    Found by the attempt on Berg et al. 2020 (berg-2020-01, 2026-10-23)
  • Published code disagreesThe code's feature table is consistent with sweep QC on noise alone, as IPFX applies it, not with the Methods' sweep QC. With the Methods' bias current and bridge balance limits (input resistance from IPFX's cell QC for the 15% limit), 22 of its 283 cells have no features here, against 1 with IPFX's sweep QC alone, and fewer features equal the table's. 29 of its 283 cells fail the cell QC as IPFX computes it (seal, access resistance, electrode offset).
    Found by the attempt on Berg et al. 2020 (berg-2020-01, 2026-10-23)
  • Published code disagreesCode7_cortical_depth_code_mouse.r as committed does not give the values its comments print. It names the four mouse boundary lines L2, L4, L5 and L6 and sets 100 um = 330 pixels for all three mouse sections, which gives mouse L2/3 thicknesses of 32, 133 and 90 um (printed 281, 223 and 257), a density of 350 thousand per mm3 (printed 165) and puts every mouse soma below the lower L2/3 line. Reading the lines as L1 to L4, as Code6 reads the human ones, with case_set_1 at 100 um = 165 pixels, the scale its own comment gives, reproduces its printed thicknesses, L2 fractions, densities (165 +/- 24.9 and 27.7 +/- 4.46), pooled area quantiles and six largest areas, and its two ratios of mean soma area; its two ratios of SD need the repeated rows of the next finding.
    Found by the attempt on Berg et al. 2020 (berg-2020-01, 2026-10-23)
  • Published code disagreesCode6_cortical_depth_code_human.r never sets q9 to NULL (Code7 does, for each section), and run on its own stops at q9. When q9 is present, it grows from section to section, and cbind repeats each human section's 20 bins once per 20 values of q9, so the human bin table Code7 averages counts the five sections unequally. Counting each section once gives SD ratios of 2.33 (L2) and 3.94 (deep L3), against the 2.4 and 4.0 Code7 prints; with the repeated rows and one or two sections' worth of q9 at the start, the port gives all four printed ratios.
    Found by the attempt on Berg et al. 2020 (berg-2020-01, 2026-10-23)
  • Published code disagreesThe code's gene-depth table (frem_genes_depth.csv) is from the revision (4417 genes at FDR below 0.05), not the preprint (790), and the Patch-seq transcriptomes behind either are not in the repository.
    Found by the attempt on Berg et al. 2020 (berg-2020-01, 2026-10-23)
  • OtherSpike times computed here with IPFX equal the spike times stored in each file's processing/spikes for the rheobase sweep of all 280 cells with features, and no spike occurs in the second before any long-square step, so the stored spikes, responses and stimuli share one clock.
    Found by the attempt on Berg et al. 2020 (berg-2020-01, 2026-10-23)
