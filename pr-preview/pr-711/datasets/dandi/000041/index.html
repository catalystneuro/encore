Findings about the data 11

What the attempts that used this dataset found about the data themselves, for its owner and the archive. The claims and their outcomes are on each paper’s page.

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds 22 sessions from 10 rats, where the paper and the dandiset's own description report 27 sessions from 11 rats; the files hold 945 units (826 excitatory, 96 inhibitory, 23 unknown) against the 995 putative pyramidal cells and 126 interneurons analysed. Which sessions and units were left out, and why, is not stated.
    Found by the attempt on Watson et al. 2016 (watson-2016-01, 2026-10-16)
  • File format errorIn sub-Dino_ses-Dino-072114 the spike times are whole seconds divided by 20000 (8451 distinct values from 0 to 0.4252 s, in a recording of 7959 s). The conversion read this session's spikes from a CellExplorer file with a hard-coded 20 kHz rate. The session's spikes cannot be used.
    Found by the attempt on Watson et al. 2016 (watson-2016-01, 2026-10-16)
  • File format errorIn sub-Dino_ses-Dino-061914 the LFP has 125 columns for 128 electrodes, lasts 331.7 min against 291.9 min of states and spikes, and its samples are scrambled (lag-1 autocorrelation 0.28 on the reference channel, against 0.99 or more in every other session), consistent with a 128-channel file read as 125 channels.
    Found by the attempt on Watson et al. 2016 (watson-2016-01, 2026-10-16)
  • Data missingThe state table holds only Awake, Non-REM and REM. The paper scores microarousals as a state of their own and builds WAKE-SLEEP episodes, nonREM packets and episodes, UP/DOWN states and spindles, none of which is in the dandiset. MAs derived from short Awake epochs are fewer than the paper reports (391 in 21 sessions, against 790 in 27).
    Found by the attempt on Watson et al. 2016 (watson-2016-01, 2026-10-16)
  • Selection not documentedThe dandiset does not say how the state table was scored. The conversion code takes it from each session's SleepState.states.mat; it yields 30 WAKE-SLEEP cycles in 21 sessions (3 sessions with none, though the paper kept only sessions with a full cycle), and only 49 of 534 REM epochs are followed directly by Non-REM, so it may not be the scoring the paper used.
    Found by the attempt on Watson et al. 2016 (watson-2016-01, 2026-10-16)
  • Metadata errorThe dandiset description gives 11 animals, 27 sessions and 1360 units, the paper's numbers, while the files hold 10 animals, 22 sessions and 945 units (the archive's summary counts 10 subjects). The description gives an age of 4-7 months, the files 3-7 months.
    Found by the attempt on Watson et al. 2016 (watson-2016-01, 2026-10-16)
  • File format errorThe LFP is stored as int16 with unit volts and conversion NaN, so it cannot be converted to volts.
    Found by the attempt on Watson et al. 2016 (watson-2016-01, 2026-10-16)
  • Description missingEvery electrode's location is "unknown" and every unit's region is "CTX" or "unknown", although the paper records from medial prefrontal, orbitofrontal, anterior cingulate and secondary motor cortex; only the electrode groups carry a region.
    Found by the attempt on Watson et al. 2016 (watson-2016-01, 2026-10-16)
  • Description missing23 units of sub-BWRat21_ses-BWRat21-121613 have cell type and region "unknown", with no description of what the label means.
    Found by the attempt on Watson et al. 2016 (watson-2016-01, 2026-10-16)
  • Data missingNo movement, accelerometer or EMG data are in the dandiset, though the paper uses EMG to score states and movement to define WAKE and to relate WAKE to rate changes.
    Found by the attempt on Watson et al. 2016 (watson-2016-01, 2026-10-16)
  • OtherThe state labels are consistent with the spikes in 20 of 21 sessions (not established for BWRat21-121813), but in 14 sessions the best agreement is with the table shifted 5 to 60 s earlier, so state boundaries may lag the activity. The LFP agrees with the spikes to within 50 ms in 14 of 21 sessions. Of the other seven, three (BWRat21-121113, Bogey-012615, Dino-072414) have a clear cross-correlation extreme 56 to 64 ms from zero lag; in three (BWRat17-121912, BWRat21-121613, BWRat21-121813) the extreme is less than three times the value at plus or minus 1 s, so agreement is not established; and the LFP of Dino-061914 is scrambled.
    Found by the attempt on Watson et al. 2016 (watson-2016-01, 2026-10-16)
