Findings about the data 9

What the attempts that used this dataset found about the data themselves, for its owner and the archive. The claims and their outcomes are on each paper’s page.

  • Paper inconsistentThe Methods say the keywords of SYN-P and SEM are the same as those of CORR, but in the supplement's lists sentences 3 and 11 of SYN-P and SEM have 孩子 and 衣服 where CORR has 小孩 and 上衣. The frequencies the Methods report (CORR mean 31.88 per million) match the CORR keywords; with the SYN-P keywords the mean is 47.35 per million.
    Found by the attempt on Zhu et al. 2022 (zhu-2022-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Data missingNo file holds trial, word or keyword event times, condition labels (CORR, SYN-P, SYN-C, SEM, COM, NL-CORR, NL-VIO), button responses or the photodiode signal the Methods describe. The stimulus, analysis and processing groups are empty and there is no trials table or intervals, so no analysis of the paper can be repeated, although the description says the dataset supports the paper's findings.
    Found by the attempt on Zhu et al. 2022 (zhu-2022-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Data missingThe electrode table has no coordinates (MNI or native) and no anatomical labels; every electrode's location is "unknown", and the two electrode groups (Grid_1 and Grid_2, location "unknown") do not say which covers the IFG and which the MFG or STG. The paper's selection of IFG electrodes and its spatial analyses need these.
    Found by the attempt on Zhu et al. 2022 (zhu-2022-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Data missingOnly the high-gamma activity is deposited. The broadband signal, or any other band, which the frequency analyses of Figure 1b and Supplementary Figures 3, 4 and 7c use, is not in the dataset.
    Found by the attempt on Zhu et al. 2022 (zhu-2022-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Description missingEvery subject has sex U and age P19Y/P50Y (the range of the whole group), with the sentence "chinese, 4 males and 6 females" as the subject description in every file. Supplementary Table 1 gives each participant's sex, age and grid coverage, but nothing maps the subject ids TC5 to TC20 to its participants 1 to 10, and nothing says which participants did the COM task and which the NL task.
    Found by the attempt on Zhu et al. 2022 (zhu-2022-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Metadata errorThe ECoG series is stated in volts, but every channel of every file has mean 0 (within 3e-6) and standard deviation 1 (within 1e-4); the values are z scores per channel and per block, as the Methods describe for the HGA.
    Found by the attempt on Zhu et al. 2022 (zhu-2022-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Metadata errorThe series' rate is stated as 400 Hz. At 400 Hz every block lasts 70 to 95 s, less than 3 s per trial for the at least 32 linguistic trials a block holds, while each trial lasts at least 4.3 s before the question; at 100 Hz, the rate the Methods give for the HGA, blocks last 282 to 381 s. The data are consistent with 100 Hz, not 400 Hz (400 Hz is the Methods' rate before the HGA was taken), unless each file holds under half of its block. A weak spectral check leans the same way (power at the 2 Hz word rate above its flanking band in 34 of 40 files under the 100 Hz reading, 19 of 40 under the 400 Hz reading). Without event times this cannot be shown directly.
    Found by the attempt on Zhu et al. 2022 (zhu-2022-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Methods not availableThe Methods give the keyword frequencies with the address of Jun Da's Chinese text computing site only. The site has no word frequency list, and its bigram counts come from two sub-corpora (news and general fiction). Which sub-corpus, or both, and which total (bigrams or characters) did the frequencies per million use? The attempt assumes both sub-corpora pooled, per million bigrams, the one reading whose t statistic (0.113) matches the paper's 0.11.
    Found by the attempt on Zhu et al. 2022 (zhu-2022-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Description missingThe Methods exclude bad channels and bad time intervals, but all 256 channels are present in every file and nothing marks which channels or intervals were excluded (filtering is "none" for every electrode); as each file is z scored on its own, bad channels cannot be told apart by scale.
    Found by the attempt on Zhu et al. 2022 (zhu-2022-01, 2026-11-01)
