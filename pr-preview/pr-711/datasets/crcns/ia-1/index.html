Findings about the data 7

What the attempts that used this dataset found about the data themselves, for its owner and the archive. The claims and their outcomes are on each paper’s page.

  • Selection not documentedThe paper keeps 86 of 132 sessions by a trial-to-trial rate SD below 35 Hz and a rate near 100 Hz. In Data1 the SD rule keeps 115 of the 116 sessions of the paper's seven protocols (and all 116 with whole-trial rates), and nothing marks which sessions the paper kept. Leaving out also the 9 sessions with fewer than 98 trials and the off-line sorted sessions keeps 89, with 47, 6, 7, 7, 8, 7 and 7 per protocol against Table 1's 43, 6, 7, 7, 8, 7 and 8, and bursting sessions per protocol equal to Table 1's in six of seven, which suggests the paper used only on-line sorted sessions; the paper does not say so.
    Found by the attempt on Eyherabide et al. 2008 (eyherabide-2008-01, 2026-11-04)
  • Data missingData1 holds no threshold, best frequency or f-I measurement for its cells, though the paper's model (Eq. 2) uses the measured threshold s_TH and the descriptor says the best frequency was determined from f-I curves.
    Found by the attempt on Eyherabide et al. 2008 (eyherabide-2008-01, 2026-11-04)
  • Identifier missingData1 does not record the animal; the paper's 23 animals can only be matched to the 23 recording dates of the seven-protocol sessions by assuming one animal per date. Two experiments (03-01-15-aa, 03-06-27-ae) are missing from singlecells.txt.
    Found by the attempt on Eyherabide et al. 2008 (eyherabide-2008-01, 2026-11-04)
  • Description missing28 sessions use the stimuli gaussModality_co200 and lmd_co200 and 6 use cutoffs of 50 and 1600 Hz; the descriptor does not describe these stimuli and the paper does not use them. 28 sessions sorted off-line have no header, so their target rate and pause are not recorded, and in 25 of them each trial block runs on into the pause (spikes up to 2.76 s), which the descriptor does not mention. The file Data1/firing_rates is not described; it gives each spike file's trial count and a rate with a +/- value of 57 to 189 Hz that is not the trial-to-trial SD of the rate.
    Found by the attempt on Eyherabide et al. 2008 (eyherabide-2008-01, 2026-11-04)
  • Metadata errorThe descriptor says the 1 s trials number 98 to 533; the files hold 69 to 503. The intensity in three trial headers differs from cell.dat by more than 1 dB (03-04-11-aa sig2, 03-04-16-ae sig2, 03-04-23-ae sig1, up to 6.6 dB), and six sessions of 03-01-30-ad, 03-02-25-ac and 03-02-25-aj had 450 ms pauses where the paper states 700 ms.
    Found by the attempt on Eyherabide et al. 2008 (eyherabide-2008-01, 2026-11-04)
  • Methods not availableQuestions for the paper's authors. (1) Burst identification; which maximum is the first peak of C_s (the first local maximum falls on edge effects of the refractory plateau and leaves 11 of 89 sessions bursting, 61 when the search starts after the plateau, the largest value below 5 ms 64), and what sigma and N are in Delta = sigma / sqrt(N) (the product terms and their number give 2.6% of the span when tau = 0 is left out of the span; N the number of trials gives no bursting session). (2) Which rule, besides the rate SD, removed the sessions not among the 86; whether the sessions with spikes detected off-line and those with fewer than 98 trials were left out. (3) How events were formed for the jitter (Rokem et al., 2006). (4) Whether the mean stimulus was subtracted before summing 1BTAs in Eq. 15. (5) What separates a peak of C_s for the multi-modal and single-peaked shapes of Figure 3. (6) Whether the maximum of the nBTA that gives tau_n was searched before burst onset, and how many bursts of each n a session needed. (7) Whether I_n(1) was corrected for the bias of the plug-in estimate, whose bias here is of the order of its rise with n.
    Found by the attempt on Eyherabide et al. 2008 (eyherabide-2008-01, 2026-11-04)
  • Paper inconsistentThe paper counts bursting sessions three ways, 63 with n > 1 in Table 1, 59 bursting cells for Figure 7B and 58 bursting sessions for Figure 8, without saying why.
    Found by the attempt on Eyherabide et al. 2008 (eyherabide-2008-01, 2026-11-04)
