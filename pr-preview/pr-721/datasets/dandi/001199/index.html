Dataset version 0.250829.1250 · tested 2026-11-01 · 12 open findings

  • Data missingThe files hold spike times only, with no spike waveforms and no RSU or FSU labels, so the paper's typing of units (IP and AHP of the waveform, k-means) cannot be repeated. Of the 20 claims on RSUs and FSUs, 18 cannot be tested and the unit counts of Fig. 4 and 5 only through upper bounds from untyped units.
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2025 (gao-2025-03, 2026-11-01)
  • Metadata errorThe units column celltype_label is described as "label 1 = RSU, label 2 = FSU", but no file holds a 1 or a 2 (the per-file value counts give 1,272 units labelled 0, 414 POm and 773 S1). It holds 0 in the 49 files of BH590 and BH594 and the area of the unit (S1 or POm) in the 47 files of BH596 and BH615; the 133 files of the 2025 cohort have no such column.
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2025 (gao-2025-03, 2026-11-01)
  • Data missingEvery stimulated file has a pulse duration of 200 us. The duty-cycle series of the paper (Fig. 7 to 9 and 10D to 10F) are not in the dandiset, nor the target-determination trials and raw voltage of Fig. 2, nor a CG1 control session at 3,000 Hz (CG1 sessions are at 300 and 4,500 Hz).
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2025 (gao-2025-03, 2026-11-01)
  • OtherThe dandiset names the paper as the description of its data, but its 13 rats were recorded from August 2024 to July 2025, after the paper's preprint of June 2024, and it holds conditions the paper does not describe (10 and 15 V, stimulation at POm, 64-channel probes with and without z-coating, 2 mm offsets). Of its rats only BH594 has a file in dandiset 000946, which the paper's Methods name as the repository of all its data and which lists 20 rats recorded from August 2023 to April 2024. Simultaneous S1 and POm recordings exist for 6 rats and S1 and AC recordings for 6, against the paper's 16 and 12.
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2025 (gao-2025-03, 2026-11-01)
  • Metadata errorThe data_collection field gives "PRESSURE:67V" in most 2024 files whatever voltage the identifier gives (the 67 is the ultrasound duration), and the 2025 files state no pressure or voltage anywhere. The description says pressure is in the identifier, which holds for the 2024 files only.
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2025 (gao-2025-03, 2026-11-01)
  • Description missingStimulation targets "S1-6", "NEW", "y-1" and "SEP" in the notes of 2025 files, and the identifier tags of 2024 files (coating, nocoating, NZ, C, P, 2mm, bad, break, x0y35z165), are not explained anywhere in the dandiset. Electrode locations are "unknown" in every file.
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2025 (gao-2025-03, 2026-11-01)
  • Metadata errorIn the 2025 cohort session_start_time does not date the session. Files of two probes that share a trial table, and so are one session, have start times minutes apart, and the BH608 sham pair has start times five months apart (2025-02-06 and 2025-07-11). One file is named ses-None.
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2025 (gao-2025-03, 2026-11-01)
  • Metadata errorIn the BH608 30 Hz session the S1 file gives stimulation at S1 and the POm file, which shares its trial table, stimulation at "SEP".
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2025 (gao-2025-03, 2026-11-01)
  • File format errorIn 64 units of 16 files of the 2025 cohort the spike times are not in increasing order (one to seven places per unit). They are sorted on reading.
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2025 (gao-2025-03, 2026-11-01)
  • OtherThe files sub-BH646_ses-None and sub-BH646_ses-20250308T003900 hold the same spike trains and trial table (AC at 30 Hz); the recording is deposited twice.
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2025 (gao-2025-03, 2026-11-01)
  • OtherThe paper used two 32-channel A1x32-Poly3 probes; the S1 files here list 64-channel A1x64-Edge probes with or without z-coating, as the dandiset's description says, and four BH590 files name a device "H9". The description gives 500 trials per recording; most files hold 499 and some 113 to 480.
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2025 (gao-2025-03, 2026-11-01)
  • OtherIn the 2025 cohort, which holds every AC recording, the spikes show no more locking to the trial starts than to starts shifted by 1.25 s (true above shifted in 7 of 108 stimulated files), and the population correlogram peaks within 0 to 400 ms of the trial start in 4 of 108 stimulated files (among them BH648 S1 at 4,500 Hz), with no other lag within 60 s at which the files lock, though trial tables and spikes span the same times. In the 2024 cohort the locking is clear (0.39 against 0.13 of units modulated, 50 of 92 files; correlogram peak within 0 to 400 ms in 39 of 92). Untyped S1 units of the 2025 cohort do show a weak rise after trial starts at 30 and 1,500 Hz (means 107 to 109%), so some 2025 sessions lock. For the others, either the sessions evoked no response or their trial times are not the sonication onsets; the files cannot tell which. The data owners could state the voltage and the trial timing of the 2025 sessions.
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2025 (gao-2025-03, 2026-11-01)
