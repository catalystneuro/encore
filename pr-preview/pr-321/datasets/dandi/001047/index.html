Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds CT26 monolayers at two packet rates (1 Hz and 0.2 MHz) and CHO monolayers with one burst label. It has no images at 1 kHz or 10 kHz, no 300- or 900-ns pulses, no thermochromic film images and no CHO burst series, so 9 of the paper's 20 findings cannot be tested for missing data. Two more are blocked for missing metadata: no file gives the electrode elevation, so wells with raised electrodes, if any are in the dandiset, cannot be identified, and the burst labels cannot be matched to 1 burst with certainty.
  • Metadata errorThe dandiset description and every file's protocol text give a 3.8-mm electrode spacing, a 3.2-kV first pulse and 75, 50 and 25% steps. The paper used a 15.3-mm array, a 7.2-kV first pulse and 16-pulse packets falling by 12.5% per phase, and the CT26 images show electrode footprints at the paper's spacing (best match 6.1 mm from the centre along each axis, against 6.4 mm from the paper's geometry, over 52 two-well areas). The voltage cannot be checked from the images; if the CT26 wells were exposed at 3.2 kV, they are not the paper's 7.2-kV experiments.
  • Description missingThe number of bursts is given only in the subject label (x1, x2, x3, x5); all but one CT26 subject say "Unknown repetitions", and the protocol text says only that the protocol "was either repeated 2, 3, or 5 times", without saying whether the first application counts, so the labels cannot be matched to the paper's "1 burst (x4)" to "6 bursts (x4)" with certainty. The CT26 files state no pixel size (the `resolution` attribute of the image data is 96 in CT26 files and 0.1 in CHO files, neither a pixel size), and no file gives the electrode elevation.
  • OtherThe CT26 images are on two scales without saying so. Whole-well images (21870 x 21289 pixels) fit the 35-mm well at 1.625 um per pixel, and two-well images fit the electrode spacing at 3.25 um per pixel (best match 4% below the paper's geometry). At 1.625 um the whole-well images match the electrode spacing 9% above the paper's geometry (3 areas), which was not resolved. Whole-well images also have higher backgrounds (mean of the four footprints 1061 to 4560 a.u., mean 2628, 7 YP areas, against 180 to 1121, mean 332, 45 YP areas, in two-well images), so their arbitrary units differ.
  • OtherThe paper's 0.2 MHz CANCAN result, a sharp central spot with 40-fold suppression near the electrodes, is not seen in the dandiset. At x1, read here as the paper's 2 bursts (x4), the 0.2 MHz CANCAN wells are unstained everywhere (group-mean profile within 32 a.u. of background); at x3 and x5 they are broadly stained, with a centre to near-electrode ratio of 1.4 to 4.0. The dandiset's images may be other experiments than those of the paper's figures.
  • Selection not documentedThe dandiset holds more wells per condition than the paper reports (for example 6 CT26 1 Hz CANCAN wells at label x1 where the paper's S1 has n = 3 per rate), and a CHO set at 1 Hz that the paper does not report. Which wells the paper used is not stated. No well was found deposited twice (peak normalised cross-correlation at most 0.039 over all 1654 pairs of areas in the same channel, whatever their condition and label, against 0.70 for a shifted, noisy copy of one area).
  • Metadata errorThe CHO files use session_start_time 2024-12-11, the conversion date, while their OME metadata give acquisition on 2024-08-14.
  • Methods not availableQuestions for the paper's authors. Does the subject label (x1, x2, x3, x5) count the repeats after the first burst or all bursts, and which wells make up the 2 bursts (x4) sets of Figures 3 to 5? Were the CT26 wells exposed at 7.2 kV, as in the paper, or 3.2 kV, as in the files? Were the wells imaged for propidium on May 24 2024 exposed with the electrodes touching the monolayer? In which electrode footprint were the 3 background ROIs placed, and how was the array centre located for the ROIs? When two error bars differ, which length must the gap exceed (Methods 2.5)? This attempt read the labels as repeats after the first burst, assumed the propidium wells had the electrodes touching, used the mean of the four footprints for background, located the centre from the footprints in the YP image, and required the gap to exceed the longer error bar.
