Hristova et al. 2025 · Other journals

Absence seizures and sleep-wake abnormalities in a rat model of GRIN2B neurodevelopmental disorder

doi:10.1111/epi.18606 · paper key hristova-2025

Datasets

  • DANDI:001888

The excess of spike-wave discharges in Grin2b+/- rats held up, as did their reduced REM sleep in fewer, though the REM result is fragile and fails if one Het rat is left out. Higher NREM beta power and lower wake power did not reproduce, because the effects went the other way. The ethosuximide effects, western blots and development findings could not be tested, since those data are not in the dandiset. Every genotype comparison rests on two wild type rats, where the paper had nine. The wake-time results depend on how spike-wave epochs are removed from wake.
