Recurrent interactions in local cortical circuits

Summary: Most of the paper's experimental findings hold up in this data: ablating a few touch neurons lowered touch encoding in the remaining neurons, most near the ablated cells, while ablating whisking or silent neurons did not. The finding that neurons responding most like the ablated ones lost the most did not reproduce by the paper's described method; its values come from code using only right-trial averages. The network model, rebuilt from the Methods with unstated parameters chosen by the agent, gave the claimed directions of change at scores about 2.3 times the paper's, but not its response-similarity result. Among minor claims, behaviour changed after some ablations under the legend's paired test, and the histology is not deposited.

    • Assumption, revised after verification round 2. "All neurons that met the 0.1 encoding score criteria" (Methods) is taken as the excitatory neurons.
