Summary: Most of the paper's main findings held up in this data: CA1 population activity drifts within a session, faster in a novel environment, place fields switch on and off abruptly, switching is more common in novel sessions and drives the drift, and a neuron's tendency to switch does not carry across environments. Fewer fields switched than reported, for reasons the attempt did not find. Switching was not most common in the delay zone, it was predicted by the previous trial's outcome, its decrease over trials held only under one reading of which trials count, and co-switching was more common than reported. The cross-day imaging result could not be tested, as the imaging data are not deposited.
