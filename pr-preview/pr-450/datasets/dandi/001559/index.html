  • Data missingThe dandiset holds one mouse (sub-582364L); the Methods report six mice for the cortical recordings ("C57BL/6J mice (n = 6)"). The one mouse is taken to be the representative mouse of Figure 4D.
  • Data missingThe description says the dandiset includes "the python scripts used for analysis and for figure generation" of Figure 4; the published version holds only the 35 NWB files.
  • Description missingThe PMT recordings of 2025-05-31 have no stimulus table or events.   • Description missingThe channels are not described. No file says which detector or filter each of the four ScanImage channels is; every imaging plane has indicator and location "unknown" and no emission wavelength, and every session description is empty. From the images, channel 2 is green (FITC-dextran), channels 3 and 4 red, and channel 4 the one without green, consistent with the 645/65 filter of the Methods.
  • Metadata errorThe three 2025-04-07 movies are stored as TwoPhotonSeries described as "Imaging data from two-photon excitation microscopy", but they are 40 Hz camera movies whose frames cycle through four exposure levels and whose contrast maps show the vessel tree, consistent with multi-exposure laser speckle imaging (Figure 4C). They carry no stimulus times.
  • Metadata errorThe stated pixel size of the ScanImage series (grid_spacing 0.028125 um for 512 pixels and 0.0140625 um for 1024 pixels) gives a field of 14.4 um, about one neuron, while the stack of cells shows on the order of a hundred cell bodies and the wide field a whole vessel tree; the stated size is likely a unit or scale error.
  • Description missingThe dates of surgery and virus injection are not recorded (only the date of birth, 2024-10-29), so the paper's "three months after surgery" for the expression images cannot be checked; the stacks were taken 63 days after the first recording through the window.
  • OtherTwo of the six PMT recordings of 2025-05-31 (14:42 and 14:46) have no stimulus pulses; the paper does not mention unstimulated recordings and the files do not say what they are. The bioluminescence level rises within them and over the day (channel 4 from about 352 to about 402).

  • Methods not availableQuestion for the paper's authors.   • Methods not availableQuestion for the paper's authors. When was the whisker pad stimulated during the three speckle movies of 2025-04-07, and how was the speckle response of Figure 4C measured? The files record no stimulus times.

Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • The criterion is qualitative; the count of compact bright objects (a Laplacian of Gaussian with a threshold of 5 robust s.d., at two scales) is a standard choice made here to put numbers to the image, not the paper's.fig4b-expression-healthy
  • The channel identities (channel 2 green, channels 3 and 4 red, channel 4 the 645/65 channel) are inferred from the images; the files do not state them.fig4b-expression-healthy
  • Stacks are subsampled by 2 in each spatial dimension and averaged over all frames; the paper's inset is an average projection over 30 um in z, and the z step is not in the files.fig4b-expression-healthy
  • The criterion compares the drift with the 4 mA response in the same recording, but each recording here holds one condition. The comparison is with the mean 4 mA response of the same channel over the two assumed 4 mA recordings; the recording's own response is shown as a variant, and with it the same 14:50 recording fails.fig4d-steady-baseline
  • Assumed, as the dataset does not record the current of each session, that the four stimulated sessions are the four conditions of Figure 4D and that the two with the larger mean responses are the 4 mA ones. The paper says the 4 mA condition gives the response strong enough to detect single events (Results, In Vivo BLI, paragraph 1), which supports the larger responses being 4 mA. Every pairing is shown. The per-trial responses in trial order show no alternation or blocks within a session (odd against even trials and first against second half, Mann-Whitney p 0.31 to 0.89); every trial of the two strong sessions is 7.64 or more, and one of the 18 trials of the weak sessions (7.77) is above that. This is consistent with one current per session but does not show it.fig4d-steady-baseline
  • Assumed that channel 4 is the PMT with the 645/65 filter (Methods, Surgery Preparation and Bioluminescence Recording), from the 2025-06-09 images where channel 4 shows the red expression without the FITC-dextran vessels and channel 3 shows both. Channel 3 is shown and gives the same outcome.fig4d-steady-baseline
  • The PMT signal is the mean of the pixels of each scan line, in bins of 128 lines (59 ms), a standard choice; the paper does not give the time base of its traces.fig4d-steady-baseline
  • Stimulus times come from image channel 1, read as a marker of the 5 s trains (Methods, "Electrical stimulation (5 Hz, 5 s, 2 mA and 4 mA)"); its pulses last 4.985 to 4.992 s.fig4d-steady-baseline
  • Assumed that the four stimulated sessions are the four conditions (two electrodes by two currents), one condition per session; the dataset does not label them. The trial order shows no alternation or blocks within a session (Mann-Whitney p 0.31 to 0.89), which is consistent with this but does not show it.fig4d-evoked-increase
  • Channel 4 is taken as the 645/65 PMT and channel 3 shown; both give the same disagreement among readings.fig4d-evoked-increase
  • One mouse of the paper's six is in the dandiset; it is taken to be the representative mouse of Figure 4D.fig4d-evoked-increase
  • Rests on the same assumed current assignment and channel as fig4d-steady-baseline.fig4d-4ma-single-trials
  • The single-pulse variant uses the line signal (0.46 ms per line), with each stimulation period detrended and folded in 20 phase bins of the 0.2 s period, with the first pulse assumed at the marker onset; the paper gives no pulse times.fig4d-4ma-single-trials
  • Rests on the same assumed current assignment and channel as fig4d-steady-baseline; under the other pairings of sessions into currents, some session also has both later times at one current (printed in the notebook).fig4d-electrode-timing
  • The electrodes are not labelled, so the claim is tested as one electrode having both later times, as the criterion says for that case.fig4d-electrode-timing
