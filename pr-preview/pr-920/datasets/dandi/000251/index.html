Problems that change results 1

The data are wrong in a way the files do not show, so an analysis that takes them as given comes out wrong. Each was checked against the notebook’s outputs by someone other than the attempt’s author.

  • OtherChanges results78 open-loop units are deposited twice. Each SU-VR-ALL-OL file holds the same spike train as an SU-VR-OL file of the same subject, under another session number (for example sub-3014 SU-VR-ALL-OL-1 and SU-VR-OL-2). The two files of each pair have different identifiers and session IDs and the same generic session description ("Data for Kim et al. 2020."); they share the subject and session start time, as do files that hold different units of one session (59 of 165 subject and start time pairs hold more than one distinct spike train), and the dandiset's description does not mention the copies. An analysis of all single-unit files counts these units twice.#
    Checked: The outputs show it, in vr_population cell 4: "257 distinct spike trains in 336 files; 78 deposited more than once". All 78 SU-VR-ALL-OL files share a spike train with an SU-VR-OL file under another session number, 77 pairs plus one group that also includes SU-VR-ALL-TP-3; the match is on an exact hash of the spike times. Not shown is that nothing in the files says so; session IDs, start times and descriptions of the pairs are not compared. critical is right; a unit deposited as two with nothing linking the copies is the defining case, and counting all single-unit files double-counts 78 units.
    Found by the attempt on Masset et al. 2025 (masset-2025-01, 2026-11-20)

Other findings 5

  • Metadata errorsub-3015/sub-3015_ses-SU-VR-OL-4.nwb, named as an open-loop session, holds a teleport session; its trial table has the four teleport trial types, and its spike train is the same as that of SU-VR-ALL-TP-3 and SU-VR-ALL-OL-3.#
    Found by the attempt on Masset et al. 2025 (masset-2025-01, 2026-11-20)
  • Data missingThe dandiset holds no cued delay (odor) task recordings, although the paper's deposit (doi:10.17632/tc43t3s7c5.1) says its raw electrophysiological data are in DANDI:000251. Only the VR task single units are there; the cued delay units are in the Mendeley deposit.#
    Found by the attempt on Masset et al. 2025 (masset-2025-01, 2026-11-20)
  • Description missingThe single-unit files have no description of their units and no flag for optogenetic identification, and the dandiset's description does not say which units are identified dopamine neurons. The paper's own flags (Valid_S.mat in its Mendeley deposit) mark all 101 open-loop units as analysed, so the units can be taken as identified only through that deposit.#
    Found by the attempt on Masset et al. 2025 (masset-2025-01, 2026-11-20)
  • OtherThe dandiset has no published version; it exists only as a draft (last modified 2023-06-20), which can change and has no DOI.#
    Found by the attempt on Masset et al. 2025 (masset-2025-01, 2026-11-20)
  • OtherChecks that passed. All 101 open-loop units match the paper's per-neuron table trial by trial, and the spikes, licks and trial events share one clock (reward responses at the trial table's reward times, none at times shifted by 5 s). The reward comes a median 7.31 s after scene motion onset in the files, where the paper gives 7.35 s.#
    Found by the attempt on Masset et al. 2025 (masset-2025-01, 2026-11-20)
