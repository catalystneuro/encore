Spatiotemporal distribution of location and object effects in reach-to-grasp kinematics

Adam G. Rouse, Marc H. Schieber

Outcomes of 26 claims

  • ⊘26 blocked

Summary: None of the paper's main findings could be tested in this data. The findings that location effects dominate the shoulder and wrist and object effects the digits, that both emerge early in the reach, and that object effects grow to dominate before contact all rest on joint angles from motion capture, and the dataset holds no kinematics, only EMG and spikes. It also holds only one of the three monkeys and part of one session. The movement time could not be checked either, since the dataset's undefined onset time is very likely a later event than the paper's onset.

Reproduction Location and object effects in reach-to-grasp kinematics (Rouse and Schieber 2015)

Tests the claims of Rouse and Schieber (2015) on dandiset 000941, the FALCON M1-A benchmark, which holds trial tables, EMG and multiunit spikes of monkey L, including part of the paper's session L20120928, and no marker positions or joint angles. Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • Only the held-in-calib file of the session is counted; the minival file repeats its first two trials.table2-trial-counts
  • The dataset's move_onset_time and contact_time are used, as the claims file directs when the dataset gives onset times; the paper's onset (average speed of 36 markers above 5 SD of a baseline, Methods, Temporal Normalization) cannot be recomputed without markers. The notebook indicates the dataset's onset is very likely a later event, so the result is shown but not compared.table2-movement-time
  • The dataset's onset is checked against reference values from two later papers of the same authors on the same monkey and sessions, in paper/ (J Neurophysiol 115:3238, Table 2, median movement time per session; J Neurosci 36:10640, Methods, which gives 243 ms as the minimum over monkey L's sessions of the median time from instruction to movement onset). They are used only to check the event, not to judge the claim.table2-movement-time
  • Event times are at 20 ms resolution, where the paper's onset is at 5 ms and contact at 1 ms.table2-movement-time
  • Only 412 of the session's 717 successful trials are in the dandiset; reading (a), the analyzed trials, is approximated by the trials whose preceding trial number is present, as the error trials are absent. Many absent numbers are more likely successful trials held back in FALCON splits not on DANDI, so this drops more trials than the paper's rule would; both readings give 200 ms.table2-movement-time
  • Monkey L only; X and Y are not in the dandiset.table2-movement-time
  • The name of obj_id 1 (coaxial cylinder) is inferred from the order of the objects in the paper's Fig. 1 legend and the locations at which each code occurs; the files do not name the codes.fig2-trial-count-coax-45
