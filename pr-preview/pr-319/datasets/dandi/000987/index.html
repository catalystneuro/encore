  • fig5f-rsc-pmr-onset

    In retrosplenial cortex, as in PoSub, units with a larger firing-rate overshoot at UP state onset (peak-to-mean ratio, PMR) enter the UP state earlier; UP onset delay and PMR are negatively correlated (Kendall tau = -0.28, n = 570 units from 14 sessions in 3 mice, p = 6.76e-21).

    We found a similar relationship in the frontal cortex ... and RSC (data available at DANDI:000987) (Figure 5F).

    Figure 5F legend; Results, Post-DOWN rebound suggests a widespread role for Ih in neuronal activation at UP onset, paragraph 4

    How it will be judged

    Method from Figure5F.py and detectDOWNs_RSC.py (commit 4ee34f3), which agree with the STAR Methods (UP and DOWN state detection; Peri-Event Time Histograms; Sequential Activation at UP onset) and the Results definition of PMR. NREM epochs are those of the sleep scoring (SleepScoreMaster). DOWN states are the ones the dandiset carries as the study's, if it carries them; they are also re-detected as the code does it, and the re-detection is shown as a check. Re-detection, per session, from the spikes of all units in NREM, counted in 10 ms bins, smoothed with a Gaussian of 20 ms s.d. (pandas rolling window of 100 bins, std 2 bins) and summed; DOWN states are the intervals below the 20th percentile of that rate; intervals shorter than 10 ms are dropped, those closer than 20 ms merged, and then those shorter than 30 ms or longer than 500 ms dropped; UP states are the intervals between consecutive DOWN states, and the DOWN states are then restricted to NREM. Per unit, the PETH of its spikes around each UP state start (the DU transition) is pynapple's compute_eventcorrelogram with 5 ms bins, a 0.255 s window, ep = the UP states and norm = True (spikes and events restricted to UP states, the PETH divided by the unit's mean rate in UP states), smoothed with a Gaussian of 2 bins s.d. (10 ms, rolling window of 8 bins) and cut to 0 to 0.155 s after DU. UP onset delay is the first time in that interval at which the normalized PETH exceeds 0.5; PMR is the maximum of the normalized PETH in that interval; a unit that never exceeds 0.5 is left out. Units of all sessions are pooled and scipy's kendalltau is computed between UP onset delay and PMR. Which units enter is open. The text allows two readings. (a) All sorted units, as Figure5F.py does (no cell-type selection) and as the legend's n of 570 units, the total recorded per the Methods, implies. (b) Putative EX units only, per the Methods sentence "only EX units were used for analyses", classified by trough-to-peak duration above 0.35 ms and mean rate below 10 Hz in open-field exploration (Spike sorting and unit classification). Both are run. Reproduced when tau is negative with p < 0.05 under both readings and within tolerance of -0.28 under at least one; partially reproduced when tau is negative with p < 0.05 under both but outside tolerance under both; not reproduced when tau is not negative with p < 0.05 under either; blocked as methods_underspecified when the readings disagree on sign or significance. The number of units entering the test is reported under each reading. The pynapple version is not pinned by the code; any version whose compute_eventcorrelogram takes these arguments is a standard choice and is reported. The Figure 5F legend reads "Same as (D)", an Isomap plot, but Figure5F.py draws the 2D histogram of UP onset delay against PMR with its fit line, as Figure5C.py does; only the correlation is tested.
