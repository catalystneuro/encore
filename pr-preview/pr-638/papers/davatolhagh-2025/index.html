Altered Use of Prior Expectations and Modified Neural Dynamics in a Mouse Model of Autism

On this one session of one mouse, the paper's task behavior held up, but its central knockout versus wild-type findings could not be tested. Choices followed contrast, shifted with the block prior and adapted after block switches. Every genotype comparison was blocked because the dandiset has one mouse with no stated genotype. Reaction time did not fall with contrast, and the paper does not say how first movement is detected. The contralateral visual response failed the preset test because its peaks fell at frame corners outside the brain. In the encoding model movement outweighed task variables, but the explained variance fell below the paper's range.
