Padilla Coreano et al. 2022 · Nature

Padilla-Coreano, N., Batra, K., Patarino, M. et al. (2022). Cortical ensembles orchestrate social competition through hypothalamic outputs. Nature 603, 667-671.

doi:10.1038/s41586-022-04507-5 · paper key padilla-coreano-2022

Datasets

  • DANDI:001749

Most of the behavioural findings that could be tested held up in this data, but nearly all of the neural findings could not be tested. Dominant mice won the most competitions and displaced competitors more, rewards, port occupancy and time displaced did not differ by rank, and the HMM-GLM decoded behaviour from neural activity, including across rank. The six-state model did not reproduce, because held-out likelihood here favoured two states, while the paper's own processed input gave six. Subordinates' slower latency held only when trials that could not be aligned were kept. The single-unit, phototagging and optogenetic findings could not be tested, mainly because all 5,879 clusters are uncurated, against 998 cells in the paper, and the behaviour clock offset is missing.
