Girardeau et al. 2017 · Nature Neuroscience

Reactivations of emotional memory in the hippocampus–amygdala system during sleep

doi:10.1038/nn.4637 · paper key girardeau-2017

Datasets

  • DANDI:000061
  • CRCNS:hc-14

DANDI:000061: Several main findings held up in this data, one did not, and most could not be tested. Hippocampus-amygdala reactivation in post-run NREM held when all cells were used (13 of 15 sessions). So did the lack of central nuclei reactivation and the higher BLA pyramidal firing in REM and NREM sleep, which rests on one rat. Ripple-peak reactivation was only partly tested, and unchanged REM firing in BLA interneurons did not hold (p = 0.020). The pyramidal-pair reactivation and ripple-subgroup findings could not be tested because the dataset lacks putative cell types. The air puff learning findings could not be tested because it lacks position tracking.

CRCNS:hc-14: Most of the paper's main findings held up in this data. Hippocampus-BLA pairs reactivated in NREM but not REM sleep after training, less so for piriform cortex and not for the central nuclei, ripple-modulated BLA cells contributed most, and the air puff trajectory was reactivated around ripples. The sleep states are not deposited, so these results rest on states decoded from the authors' firing-rate tables, and several change under other decoder settings. The rats' learning of the daily danger zone held up only in part, as the air puff location is deposited for 6 of 55 sessions and had to be estimated. A control finding no bias from that location did not reproduce.
