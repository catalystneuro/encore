Dehaqani et al. 2024 · Cell Reports

A mechanosensory feedback that uncouples external and self-generated sensory responses in the olfactory cortex

doi:10.1016/j.celrep.2024.114013 · paper key dehaqani-2024

None of the paper's main findings could be tested, because the dandiset holds one mouse out of 22 and none of the breathing, odor-trial, brain-area or waveform data the analyses need. That covers the sorting of inhalations into slow and fast, the sniff-driven changes in piriform odor responses, the model of speed and concentration inputs, the airflow tuning, the bulbectomy effects and the sniff-invariant concentration code. All 37 claims are blocked, and the paper says its processed data are available on request. The file holds only spike times for 1013 units, with no brain area to select piriform neurons. The checks that could be made agree with the paper: unit quality measures match the reported ranges, and units near the probe tip show a 2.78 Hz rhythm within the mouse breathing range.
