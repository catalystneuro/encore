Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Description missingThe files carry no cell type, so VENs and PCs cannot be told apart from the dandiset. Types come only from the paper's code table metadata_outSideOutSodiumCurrentRec.csv, which names each NWB file; the file names and electrode cell IDs agree with it for all 35 patches.
  • Data missingNo file holds the patch capacitance, series resistance or compensation values, so the patch size the Methods report (about 50 fF, 5 um2) and current densities cannot be computed.
  • Methods not availableHow the maximal Na+ current was measured from the traces is not stated in the paper or its code. Plain peaks of the repetition-averaged traces, with or without a pre-step baseline, do not match the code table (at most 2 of 35 within 10%), and the VEN against PC difference is significant only under two readings without baseline subtraction. With a 2.9 kHz Bessel low-pass, or a 0 to 3 ms window, it is significant under every baseline-subtracted reading. A peak after 0.75 ms smoothing comes closest to the table (26 of 35 within 10%, 19 of 35 at 0.5 ms), at a width fitted to it. Question for the authors, which baseline, filter or fit, steps and window gave the maximal currents of Fig. 4d?
  • Metadata errorEvery file gives the session start time 2018-01-01 01:00, while the session ids and the code table's cell IDs date the recordings to 6 December 2022. The series starting_time values (0 to 3 s) carry no order.
  • OtherThe traces keep holding currents of -131 to +92 pA before the step although the Methods say they were leak subtracted online, so a baseline must be removed before a peak is taken. One file (cell HS018, a VEN) has a positive holding current and noise near 57 pA SD. Files hold 28 or 34 steps (to +55 or +85 mV) and 1 to 10 repetitions, where the Methods describe steps to 0 mV.
  • OtherThe MetaboLights metabolite table of MTBLS13927 holds 558 identified metabolites, where the Results report 581, and one AIC sample is named A1C-1-Q. The Welch and Games-Howell counts on it fall short of the reported 228 and 108.
  • OtherIn the paper's code repository, AIScmp_PCvsVEN_Fig5c.xlsx has 500 PC and 120 VEN values where the Fig. 3c legend gives 430 and 108, and no slice or animal column for its two-way ANOVA. sumData_StimWM.xlsx gives stimulation values 0.5, 1 and 2 labelled mA where the Methods give 0.05 to 0.2 mA, and no unit for the amplitudes.
  • OtherIn at least 10 of the 35 files the repetition-averaged trace holds brief biphasic transients about 0.1 ms wide (0.07 to 0.16 ms at half height), larger than the Na+ current in some patches (for example AS3c11, AS4c14, AS5c20). They set the maximum of the unfiltered trace in 6 of the 14 VENs and 4 of the 21 PCs under the baseline-subtracted reading a2 b1 c1, 9 of the 10 in session 221206AS, so a peak taken without a filter measures them, not the Na+ current.
  • Published code disagreesIn the paper's code repository, the two blocks of sumData_StimWM.xlsx that hold the eEPSP amplitudes of Fig. 4j ("copied data" and "re-orgnized data") agree in 69 of 78 rows and differ in 9; the re-organized block reproduces the paper's P = 0.0034 (0.0033). The script extFig9ad_plotVENsubtypeAP_Cmp.m loads VENsubtype_APs.mat from a folder VENsubtypeCmp_extFig9ab that is not in the repository, which holds it under VENsubtypeCmp_extFig8ab.
