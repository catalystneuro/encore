Dataset version 0.260318.1448 · tested 2026-11-03 · 14 open findings

  • Description missingThe files carry no figure, cohort, genotype or virus-construct labels; subject metadata is a placeholder in every file (age P0D, sex U, no genotype), where the paper used male mice 6 to 8 weeks old. Cohorts can be formed only from manuscript_sessions.csv of the export repository the dandiset links.
    Found by the attempt on Chantranupong et al. 2023 (chantranupong-2023-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Metadata errorThe trials tables' times (start_time, side_in_time and the others) are not on the clock of the photometry series. They are offset from the trials' own photometry sample indices by a median of -4.6 s (range -125 to +1.2 s) and drift within one session. The sample indices land on the port onsets of the event channels in every session.
    Found by the attempt on Chantranupong et al. 2023 (chantranupong-2023-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Metadata errorIn a median of 17% of trials per session (up to 43%, above 10% in 282 sessions) the trials table's photometry_center_out_index is after photometry_side_in_index, which cannot happen in the task.
    Found by the attempt on Chantranupong et al. 2023 (chantranupong-2023-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Metadata errorThe trials tables' center_laser and side_laser columns are False in every session, while the laser channel of the seven CIN-stimulation sessions has bursts starting at side entry in about a quarter of trials.
    Found by the attempt on Chantranupong et al. 2023 (chantranupong-2023-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Data missingChannels whose notes name a sensor have no raw trace in 193 channel-sessions (30 mice) and no z-scored trace in 30 (14 mice). This removes the right-hemisphere dLight1.1 of all CIN-stimulation mice, the raw traces of the thalamic mCherry mice and of several Drd2 and Fig. 5 mice, and some Ach channels.
    Found by the attempt on Chantranupong et al. 2023 (chantranupong-2023-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Metadata errorThe stored baseline_f0 series is about 0.07 times the raw trace it is described as the dF/F baseline of (median ratio over channels), so dF/F0 computed with it is wrong.
    Found by the attempt on Chantranupong et al. 2023 (chantranupong-2023-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Metadata errorThe sensor field of the channel metadata holds virus codes in S950, S952, S1142 and S1143 ('279B', '333 + 304'), and three viruses are named only by number ('unknown (virus 216)', 'unknown (virus 390)', and 222 in a compound code); 216 is the Chrimson of the CIN-stimulation cohort and 222 appears to be the TelC of S1142 and S1143, neither stated.
    Found by the attempt on Chantranupong et al. 2023 (chantranupong-2023-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Description missingNothing marks the cue-omission sessions of Fig. 1f. Their LED channels have cue onsets at the trial events, as in the cued sessions, so the files do not show that the cues were omitted. They are known only from the paper's analysis script.
    Found by the attempt on Chantranupong et al. 2023 (chantranupong-2023-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Data missingThe slice amperometry and cell-attached recordings, the histology, the head-fixed optogenetic sessions, the stGtACR2 DAN-inhibition sessions and the dLight1.1 ligand-binding mutant are not in the dataset. The export table lists 21 mice and 64 sessions that are not in it (for example the six Fig. 4 'Chat Dat' mice and two cortical TelC mice).
    Found by the attempt on Chantranupong et al. 2023 (chantranupong-2023-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Selection not documentedCohorts formed from the export table differ in size from the paper's in several places (Fig. 1, 14 DA and 16 Ach mice against 13 and 14; jRCaMP1b, 9 against 6; Extended Data Fig. 3, 14 DA mice against 7), and the mice of Extended Data Fig. 1 (n = 8) cannot be identified. The table's figure labels do not follow the published numbering (EDFig10, EDFig12, EDFig16).
    Found by the attempt on Chantranupong et al. 2023 (chantranupong-2023-01, 2026-11-03)
  • OtherThe high-port reward probability is 0.95 in 180 of 379 sessions and 0.8, 0.85, 0.9 or 1.0 in the others; the Methods state P(high) = 95%.
    Found by the attempt on Chantranupong et al. 2023 (chantranupong-2023-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Metadata errorThe seven CIN-stimulation sessions (EDFig12) carry the laser artefact in the right fibre's red channel, labelled DIO mCherry, and not in the left fibre with the Chrimson virus (216), so the illuminated hemisphere is the control one, whose dLight1.1 trace is not stored. The laser bursts last about 1.0 s, where the Methods give 1.5 s, and the Methods' statement that the artefact is removed does not apply to the stored traces.
    Found by the attempt on Chantranupong et al. 2023 (chantranupong-2023-01, 2026-11-03)
  • OtherThe VLS-TelC behaviour cohort (2020) was run with a high-port probability of 0.9 or 1.0 and blocks of about 46 trials, the striatum-wide mCherry controls (2021) with 0.95 and about 36 trials; no control group in the dataset matches the VLS-TelC task parameters.
    Found by the attempt on Chantranupong et al. 2023 (chantranupong-2023-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Selection not documentedThe thalamic mCherry controls (S1351, S1389, S1393, Vglut2Cre) carry virus 349, named retro mCherry, the virus of the cortical controls, at VLS fibres in both hemispheres; the Fig. 5f legend describes mCh expression in the thalamus, and the files record no injection into the thalamus.
    Found by the attempt on Chantranupong et al. 2023 (chantranupong-2023-01, 2026-11-03)
