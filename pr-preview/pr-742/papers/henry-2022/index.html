Most of the paper's main findings held up in direction in this data: distractors impair orientation discriminability more in V4 than in V1 for single neurons and populations, modulate V4 responses more strongly, and reduce noise correlations and Fano factors. The size of the crowding effect did not hold: the V4 threshold elevation was 7.30 against the paper's 2.52 at the closest spacing, and V1 values were also higher, with decoder settings the paper does not give. The tuning model's terms, the lower tuning correlation in V4 and the fixed decoder's failure to generalize in V4 held in direction with different values. Whether V4 receptive fields are larger could not be tested, as the mapping data are not deposited.

Notes on the paper

What the attempts found about the paper itself: a method or rule it does not give, a place where it disagrees with itself, published code that does something else. What they found about the data is on each dataset’s page.

  • Selection not documentedThe data set holds exactly the paper's 396 V1 and 691 V4 units, but the Methods' rule (responses at least 1 SD above spontaneous) applied to them keeps only 115 to 133 V1 and 147 to 174 V4 units, using blank epochs as the spontaneous condition. Figure S6A, which uses the same definition, shows only 0.3 to 0.5 of each population driven. How the deposited units were selected is not documented.
  • Paper inconsistentThe Results state that the additive term was significantly larger in V4 than in V1 as "t test, p > 0.001", in a sentence that calls the difference significant.
  • Methods not availableFor the paper's authors. (1) How was the spontaneous rate of the selection ("responses at least 1 SD above spontaneous rate") measured, and against which response; the deposited units cannot be regenerated from the blank epochs. (2) The decoders' feature scaling, penalty grid and folds, and the binomial trial count of the threshold-elevation fit, are not given; the threshold elevations here (V1 1.69, V4 7.30 at 1.3 degrees) differ from the reported ones. (3) How the tuning model's means and fit quality were averaged; V1 gives the paper's 146 fitted neurons but m = 0.41 against 0.59. (4) Whether the even/odd reliability used one split in time order; with reliability averaged over random splits V4 keeps 160 neurons, its m and a correlate at r = -0.46 and its simulated V4 slopes are at or below the Figure 2C slopes at only 1 of 4 spacings, so fig5-terms-covary and fig5de-simulated-discriminability rest on the single split; single random splits all give V4 r of -0.64 to -0.89. (5) Under the stated penalty rule, with the grid used here, the chosen decoder has no nonzero coefficient in 5% to 18% of outer folds; whether the paper's did is not said. The analysis code, which the paper offers on request, would settle these.
