      • The code's variant is run on a 60 pixel grid where the criterion says 40; on the 40 pixel grid the notebook took 32 minutes on four cores, over the 30 minute limit.

    Findings about the data

    What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

    • Data missingThe dandiset holds one of the paper's four mice and none of the songbird recordings or optical test data. The Academic Torrents deposit the paper names for its imaging data holds files of the same mouse session only, so the other mice and the birds are not public.
    • Metadata errorThe device description reads "Head-mounted microscope (Kiloscope) optimized for weight (1.0 mm FOV; 1.0 g)", and the dandiset description says the data come from the microscope optimized for weight. The data are from the large-FOV microscope (1.4 g, about 4 mm FOV) that the paper calls the Kiloscope; a 4 mm window, 2948 footprints and 808 x 608 pixel frames.
    • Metadata errorThe imaging is stored as a TwoPhotonSeries and the dandiset lists two-photon microscopy among its techniques, but the microscope is a one-photon head-mounted epifluorescence microscope.
    • Metadata errorThe behaviour video is described as about 30 fps and its AVI header says 30 frames/s, but its 15521 timestamps run at a median 12.5 frames/s over 1234 s; at 30 frames/s it would cover only 517 s of the 1234 s of imaging.
    • Description missingThe imaging plane gives no pixel size, and its indicator and location are "unknown" with no excitation wavelength, although general/virus says GCaMP7f. Distances in the paper's figures cannot be computed from the dandiset alone.
    • Selection not documentedThe 2948 footprints are the EXTRACT output before curation; the paper analyses the 2382 it kept (Extended Data Fig. 6a). Nothing in the dandiset marks the curated set or says that the footprints are uncurated.
    • Data missingThe mouse's position, which the paper's decoding and place analyses use, is not in the dandiset; it must be tracked from the behaviour video. The first 64 s of the video (by its nominal rate), which the paper's code drops because the experimenter is in view, are not marked.
    • Published code disagreesThe Extended Data Fig. 9c legend describes decoders on the 32 closest footprints within 270 um; the code (subregion_map_octant.m) uses 64 footprints within 50 pixels, which at the code's own 5.94 um per pixel (sf3/widefield/analysis.m) is 297 um.
    • Paper inconsistentThe title of Extended Data Fig. 8 refers to the Kiloscope data "shown in Figure 3", and its legend takes the ridgeline plot from Fig. 2d, while the version of record has two figures, and Supplementary Video 1 and Extended Data Fig. 10 place the bird data in Fig. 2a-d and the mouse data in Fig. 2e-h.

    Reproduction Imaging thousands of neurons in a freely moving mouse with a large-FOV head-mounted microscope (Scherrer et al. 2023)

    Tests the findings of Scherrer, Lynch, Zhang and Fee (2023) on dandiset 000691, which holds one session of one mouse imaged with the large-FOV head-mounted microscope while it explores a looped maze, with 2948 EXTRACT footprints, the imaging video and a behaviour video. It counts the footprints, computes the field of view, registers the whole movie for its motion, tracks the mouse from the video, decodes its position and direction against shuffled activity, looks at the spread of coactive footprints and at place maps, and tests the bead resolution, field curvature and illumination profiles on measurements in the paper's code repository. The other mice, the birds and the other optical tests are not in the dandiset. All files
