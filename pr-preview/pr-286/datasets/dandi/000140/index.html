What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Metadata errorEvery unit's `electrodes` entry in the train and test files points to an electrode of the PMd group (indices 0 to 94), though the unit ids carry two array prefixes (72 units with 1, 70 with 2) and the dandiset describes recordings from M1 and PMd. The electrode index equals the channel number in the id minus one for both prefixes, so the units of one array point to the wrong group and no unit points to an M1 electrode. Which prefix is PMd is not stated.
  • Description missingThe electrodes table has no positions or impedances (x, y, z and imp are NaN for all 192 electrodes), so no unit has an anterior-posterior location, which the paper's comparison of anterior and posterior sites uses.
  • Data missingThe dandiset holds 100 trials of 27 of the 108 maze conditions from the arrays of monkey J (1 to 4 trials per condition after the paper's delay rule), where the paper's J-array dataset has about 2166 trials per neuron. The single-electrode datasets of monkeys A, B and J and all EMG are not in it. Four claims cannot be tested, and the claims that were tested rest on condition means whose split-half reliability has a median of 0.20. With condition labels shuffled across trials, 82 to 96 of the 142 units pass the paper's > 5 spikes/s inclusion rule (read as the range), against 93 with the true labels; the paper justified that threshold by its high trial count.

Reproduction Preparatory and peri-movement tuning in the maze-task array data (Churchland et al. 2010)

Tests the findings of Churchland, Cunningham, Kaufman, Ryu and Shenoy (2010) on dandiset 000140 (MC_Maze_Small), which holds 100 trials of 27 maze conditions from the two Utah arrays of monkey J. It correlates preparatory and peri-movement tuning, compares how well a preferred direction in the peri-movement, kinematic and task spaces captures preparatory tuning, runs the paper's controls, compares the two arrays, and repeats the paper's simulation of a rotational linear system. The single-electrode datasets and EMG of the paper are not in the dandiset. All files

Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • Assumed O1 is the range across conditions (see fig4-broad-near-zero).fig4e-correlation-time-course fig6-dimensionality-trend
  • 27 conditions with 1 to 4 trials each after the delay rule, against 108 conditions and about 20 trials each in the paper's J-array dataset.fig4d-mean-correlation-j-array
  • Spike trains smoothed with a Gaussian of SD 20 ms before averaging (Supplemental Experimental Procedures, Computing the mean firing rate versus time; the width is read as the SD, a standard choice).fig4d-mean-correlation-j-array
  • Assumed O1 is the range of the preparatory rate across conditions, as the Methods describe the inclusion rule as a rate difference ("Firing rate modulation was typically lower ... >5 spikes/s of preparatory tuning"); under the SD reading the claim is reproduced, on 3 to 5 units.fig4-broad-near-zero
  • The time axis uses go-locked means from -200 to +200 ms and movement-locked means from -400 to +400 ms, in 10 ms steps (Supplemental Experimental Procedures, the spliced trace); the judgement uses the movement-locked part.fig4e-correlation-time-course
  • Each unit is normalised by its maximum condition-mean rate over the go-locked and movement-locked spans stored (-300 to +200 ms and -400 to +900 ms); the legend says "normalizing each neuron's activity by its maximum rate".suppfig1-population-averages-lose-tuning
  • Assumed O1 range (see fig4-broad-near-zero). Under O1 SD with the T matrix of the included units the claim is not reproduced; under O1 range every reading gives reproduced or partial.fig6d-perimovement-best-j-array
  • The units of the peri-movement T matrix are read two ways, all recorded units (main) and the units passing the inclusion rule. The Methods say each row "contained data from all neurons except the neuron whose preparatory activity we were trying to capture" and that "Analysis of preparatory responses was restricted to neurons with at least 10 spikes/s of preparatory tuning".fig6d-perimovement-best-j-array
  • The upper limit uses the preparatory matrix of all units except the fitted one, as the peri-movement space does (Methods, PCA-based spaces); the supplement says "all neurons".fig6d-perimovement-best-j-array
  • Task spaces follow the Methods' list (Task spaces). Details they leave open, set here and stated in maze_prep.py, are the end of the reach (last time within 800 ms of onset with speed at least 10% of peak), the peak velocity components (the signed value of largest magnitude), and the segments (equal path length, with equal duration as a variant, which gives the same outcome).fig6d-perimovement-best-j-array
  • Acceleration for the kinematic space is the derivative of the dataset's hand velocity after a 20 ms Gaussian, a standard choice.fig6d-perimovement-best-j-array
  • Assumed O1 is the range across conditions (see fig4-broad-near-zero); under the SD reading with the T matrix of all recorded units the claim is reproduced.fig6-maze-endpoint-speed-space-poor
  • Assumed O1 SD (O1b). Under the O1 range reading adopted elsewhere (see fig4-broad-near-zero) performance at -150 ms is below zero and the ratio is undefined, so the outcome rests on the O1b readings (7 or 8 units) alone.fig7b-window-start-slide
  • Start times -150 to +100 ms in 50 ms steps, end fixed at +800 ms, best k up to 14 for each start (Figure 7B legend, "the best dimensionality for that dataset/start-time").fig7b-window-start-slide
  • The electrodes have no positions, so AP location is the array, PMd anterior and M1 posterior (Supplemental Figure 3 legend). Regression on a 0/1 location, the two-group comparison the criterion names as the variant for array identity.ap-location-no-effect-j-array
  • Assumed the array of a unit is the first digit of its id, 1 for PMd and 2 for M1, as units with prefix 1 map onto the PMd electrodes of the table; every unit's electrodes entry points to the PMd group. Swapping the arrays changes only the sign of r.ap-location-no-effect-j-array
  • AP halves are the two arrays (see ap-location-no-effect-j-array), not a median split of positions.fig7c-anterior-posterior-performance
  • O1 range, O3a and O4a (see fig6d-perimovement-best-j-array); 20 random halves of the size of the prefix-1 array.fig7c-anterior-posterior-performance
  • AP location is the array, PMd anterior and M1 posterior (Supplemental Figure 3 legend); both mappings of the unit-id prefixes to the arrays are run.prep-activity-more-anterior
  • The paper does not give the scale of task-space axes, which sets a PD's direction when units are mixed (mm and mm/s); raw and z-scored axes are both run and agree on the segment-directions comparison in 11 of 12 pairs (they differ under O1 SD with O3a and O4b).fig7d-pd-consistency
  • Draws in which the unit's rate is the same in all drawn conditions (a zero PD) are dropped; 100 repeats per unit, the claim's standard choice.fig7d-pd-consistency
