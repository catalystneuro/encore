Dataset unversioned files (the archive keeps no versions) · tested 2026-11-06 · 6 open findings

  • File format errorIn the cortical files the whisker timestamps (processing/whisker/BehavioralTimeSeries/*/timestamps, labelled Seconds) are the trial start in milliseconds plus the time within the trial in seconds, so trial starts are 1,000 times those of the ephys timestamps. Read as milliseconds, each ephys trial starts 10 ms before the first whisker frame (10 kHz files) or with it (20 kHz files).
    Found by the attempt on Yu et al. 2016 (yu-2016-03, 2026-11-06)
  • Description missingSeventy cortical files hold two tracked whisker series (whisker_angle_1, whisker_angle_2, with their touch annotations), and nothing says which whisker each is. Mapping them in the order of general/whisker_configuration to analysis/principal_whisker gives FS neurons almost no touch response (0.45 against 1.78 spikes per touch with series 1), while for several L4 E neurons it picks the series with the larger touch response (JY1345 0.857 against 0.235). The example code takes the first whisker as the main one.
    Found by the attempt on Yu et al. 2016 (yu-2016-03, 2026-11-06)
  • Data missingNot in the data set, though the paper reports them. The 17 thalamic multiunit recordings during TRN-axon photostimulation (Fig. 6b,c), the rabies tracing (Supplementary Fig. 10), the L5 FS neurons after ION cut, and the whole-cell recordings with depolarizing current injection (Fig. 3, n = 6). No whole-cell L4 E trial has a median Vm above -42.8 mV.
    Found by the attempt on Yu et al. 2016 (yu-2016-03, 2026-11-06)
  • Metadata errorThe neuron JY1139 in the L4FS_whole_cell folder has the unit description "Excitatory", while the folder and the cell list's FS sheet give it as an FS neuron. Its first 18 trials have median Vm between -2.3 and -26.8 mV while it fires 8 to 85 spikes per trial with no injected current above 0.1 nA; from trial 20 it sits near -41 mV. Nothing in the file marks these trials.
    Found by the attempt on Yu et al. 2016 (yu-2016-03, 2026-11-06)
  • Description missingEight whole-cell L4 E files (JY0865, JY0861, JY1002, JY0989, JY0913, JY1009, JY0781, JY0986) flag 132 stimulus trials in analysis/trial_type_mat but hold no photostimulus series, and Exp_2015-09-24_004 and JY1633 have 23 flagged trials without light in their series. What stimulus these trials had cannot be read.
    Found by the attempt on Yu et al. 2016 (yu-2016-03, 2026-11-06)
  • Metadata errorThe cell list (docs/yu_gutnisky_CellList.xlsx, sheet AllCellsReported) does not name JY1268, which the cell-attached L4 E folder holds with eNpHR light trials; its "Extracellular units" section has 47 rows against 48 files. It lists JY0781 among the PV-IRES-Cre L4 E neurons, though that file holds no photostimulus series.
    Found by the attempt on Yu et al. 2016 (yu-2016-03, 2026-11-06)
