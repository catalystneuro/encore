Dataset unversioned files (the archive keeps no versions) · tested 2026-11-06 · 10 open findings

  • OtherThe units come from 182 recording sessions (74 Control, 108 EAA), each with its own trial table, where the paper and the data description state 72 Control and 107 EAA sessions. The unit counts (151 and 203) and rat counts (8 and 12) match. No session holds more than one behaviour program and no two sessions share a trial table, so the extra sessions are not duplicates.
    Found by the attempt on Moaddab et al. 2020 (moaddab-2020-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Description missingThe group of each rat (Control or EAA) is given only by lists in the authors' script vlpagAnalysis.m (lines 99 to 122), not by the data description or the files.
    Found by the attempt on Moaddab et al. 2020 (moaddab-2020-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Description missingThe event codes EVT01 to EVT14 of the unit files are not described. EVT09, EVT11 and EVT12 are the cue onsets, but which is danger, uncertainty or safety depends on the behaviour program in the file name (epr38a, b, c); EVT08 holds the shocks, EVT13 the nose pokes, EVT14 a marker about 31.5 s after each onset, and EVT02 could not be identified.
    Found by the attempt on Moaddab et al. 2020 (moaddab-2020-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Metadata errorThe data description says poke.base is the baseline nose poke rate 2 s prior to cue presentation. It is the rate in the 20 s before cue onset (99.8% of trials; 4.2% match a 2 s window), in pokes per minute, a unit the description does not give.
    Found by the attempt on Moaddab et al. 2020 (moaddab-2020-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Metadata errorThe data description gives the uncertainty shock rows a shock probability of 0.25; the paper and the description's own summary give 0.375.
    Found by the attempt on Moaddab et al. 2020 (moaddab-2020-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Description missingNo bregma recording level per rat is given; the description gives only how many rats were at each of four levels, so the paper's ANOVAs with bregma level cannot be repeated.
    Found by the attempt on Moaddab et al. 2020 (moaddab-2020-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Data missingThe body weights (Fig. 1B, C) and the eight pre-surgery discrimination sessions (Fig. 1E, F) are not in the data set.
    Found by the attempt on Moaddab et al. 2020 (moaddab-2020-01, 2026-11-06)
  • OtherThe cube stores raw firing rates as unsigned integers truncated towards zero, so units firing below 1 Hz have stored rates of 0 (775 trials with spikes have a stored 10 s baseline of 0). The paper's counts of cue-excited and cue-inhibited neurons follow exactly from these truncated rates; with exact rates from the spike times the Control cue-inhibited count is 54, not 48.
    Found by the attempt on Moaddab et al. 2020 (moaddab-2020-01, 2026-11-06)
  • OtherSpikes and nose pokes are stored only around the trials, from about 20 s before to about 33.5 s after each cue onset (99.98% of spikes and 99.99% of nose pokes fall within 20.5 s before to 33.5 s after an onset), not over the whole session, which the data description does not say.
    Found by the attempt on Moaddab et al. 2020 (moaddab-2020-01, 2026-11-06)
  • OtherThe cube stores a suppression ratio of 1 for the 255 unit-trials (143 session-trials) with no nose poke in the baseline or the cue, where the ratio is 0/0; the paper does not say how such trials are treated.
    Found by the attempt on Moaddab et al. 2020 (moaddab-2020-01, 2026-11-06)
