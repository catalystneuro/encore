Dataset version 0.261003.2342 · tested 2026-10-31 · 10 open findings

  • Metadata errorThe dandiset description says the 14 recordings hold 570 isolated units, as the paper's Methods do, but the files hold 638 units (31 to 71 per session). 570 is the number of units whose DOWN-to-UP PETH crosses half the mean rate within 155 ms, the units entering the paper's Figure 5F.
    Found by the attempt on Mehrotra et al. 2024 (mehrotra-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Data missingThe dandiset description lists LFP in each file, but no file holds LFP or any other electrical series; only spike times, epochs, sleep scoring, DOWN and UP states and tracking.
    Found by the attempt on Mehrotra et al. 2024 (mehrotra-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Description missingUnits carry no waveform, cell type, channel or depth, and the electrode table gives x, y and z as 0 for every channel, so the putative excitatory rule of the Methods (trough-to-peak and rate) cannot be applied and units cannot be placed on the probe.
    Found by the attempt on Mehrotra et al. 2024 (mehrotra-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Metadata errorsession_start_time is 2024-05-09 (the upload day) in every file, while the identifiers carry the recording dates (A7801-210225 and so on, 2021).
    Found by the attempt on Mehrotra et al. 2024 (mehrotra-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Metadata errorIn 9 of 14 sessions part of the SWS (NREM) epochs lies outside the sleep epochs; in A7803-210730 one SWS interval, 7,680 to 9,565 s, covers two whole wake (arena) epochs.
    Found by the attempt on Mehrotra et al. 2024 (mehrotra-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Metadata errorIn DANDI:000939 (version 0.261016.2310) the NREM epochs of sleep_states lie partly outside the home cage epochs in the paper's sessions, and in A3711-200810b, A3717-201021 and A3723-201115 they run 3,508 to 5,092 s past the last spike of the recording.
    Found by the attempt on Mehrotra et al. 2024 (mehrotra-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • OtherIn DANDI:000939 the LFP of A3705-200306 covers 11,168 s and that of A3711-200810b 9,499 s, while their spikes run to 16,199 s and 12,177 s; both LFPs have starting_time 0 and rate 1,250 Hz. In these two sessions and A3708-200317b high gamma power does not rise after the DOWN-to-UP transitions of the spikes (by 0.02 or less over shifted times, against 0.053 to 0.357 elsewhere), which suggests that their LFP and spikes do not share a clock. In A3708-200317b the LFP fits neither the spikes nor the session's event file in the paper's code (power after / before the event file's UP starts 0.997, 0.998 when shifted by 0.5 s); only 1,488 of the event file's 6,187 UP starts lie in the file's NREM within the home cage.
    Found by the attempt on Mehrotra et al. 2024 (mehrotra-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Description missingIn DANDI:000939 the electrode table gives x, y and z as NaN, and its rel_y column is described only as "xy coordinate in electrode group"; which way it runs is said only in the probe description ("Channels sorted top to bottom of shank", electrode 0 with the largest rel_y).
    Found by the attempt on Mehrotra et al. 2024 (mehrotra-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Description missingDANDI:000056, which holds 8 of the 9 ADN sessions the paper's code names, gives no location, shank or electrode for its units, so ADN units cannot be selected.
    Found by the attempt on Mehrotra et al. 2024 (mehrotra-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • OtherIn DANDI:000939 the Isomap ring of the HD cells (makeRing.py) does not follow head direction in wake in A3706-200313 and A3730-210323b (mean resultant length of ring angle against head direction 0.114 and 0.328, against 0.627 to 0.936 in the other 14 sessions).
    Found by the attempt on Mehrotra et al. 2024 (mehrotra-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
