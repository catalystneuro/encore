Dataset version 0.260129.0829 · tested 2026-10-18 · 11 open findings

  • Metadata errorThe ΔF/F series (DFFOnePhotonSeriesBlue, Green and Violet) are given the starting time of the first LED pulse, the time of raw frame 0, but their frame 0 is raw frame 499, as the detrending drops the first frames. Their times are 49.9 s early in every session; both the raw movies and the alignment of calcium with wheel speed show it.
    Found by the attempt on Lohani et al. 2022 (lohani-2022-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Metadata errorThe FaceMap, pupil, eye and video timestamps are spaced 111 ms (9 Hz) where the camera pulses are 100 ms apart, span about 10% longer than the session, and end with two values equal to the first camera pulse. With these timestamps face movement and wheel speed are misaligned by 23 to 60 s; with frame i at camera pulse i, as the paper's code does, they align.
    Found by the attempt on Lohani et al. 2022 (lohani-2022-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Metadata errorRoiResponseSeriesParcellated holds the calcium (green excitation) ΔF/F averaged over its ROIs, but its segmentation is linked to the ACh imaging plane. Its image masks are transposed relative to the ΔF/F images, and its starting time is that of the first green pulse, 64.9 s before its first frame (150 frames after the ΔF/F's first frame).
    Found by the attempt on Lohani et al. 2022 (lohani-2022-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Description missingThe ΔF/F descriptions mention detrending only; the blue and green ΔF/F are also hemodynamically corrected and registered to the Allen atlas, rotated by 180 degrees, as the paper's code shows (the data are consistent with the correction, in one session checked). The Allen parcels the paper analyses are not in the files; they come from the paper's code. The parcellation in the files is a different, functional one with 89 to 107 ROIs per session and no area names.
    Found by the attempt on Lohani et al. 2022 (lohani-2022-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Metadata errorIn the VisualStimulus tables the columns from stimulus_size on are shifted by one; stimulus_size holds the duration (2) and the first screen coordinate holds the size (20 degrees).
    Found by the attempt on Lohani et al. 2022 (lohani-2022-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Data missingThe air-puff session grabAM07 11262019 has no Airpuff table, so its puff times are unknown.
    Found by the attempt on Lohani et al. 2022 (lohani-2022-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Selection not documentedNo air puff in the Airpuff tables is followed by a locomotion onset within 5 s (29 puffs), which fits the paper's code keeping only puffs without running. The tables then hold a selection of the puffs given, which the files do not say.
    Found by the attempt on Lohani et al. 2022 (lohani-2022-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Description missingWheelSpeed and pupil_area have the unit "unknown"; the paper's code treats wheel speed as m/s, and its range fits that. WheelSpeed is signed (minimum -0.07 to -0.14 per session), which its description does not say.
    Found by the attempt on Lohani et al. 2022 (lohani-2022-01, 2026-10-18)
  • OtherIn the calcium ΔF/F of grabAM08 11232019 spont, 263 pixels inside the brain mask, all in the right AUD parcel, are zero in every frame; in the other 25 ΔF/F movies every pixel of the mask is non-zero.
    Found by the attempt on Lohani et al. 2022 (lohani-2022-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Description missingThe VisualStimulus tables give screen coordinates but not where the monitor stood relative to the mouse, so the hemisphere expected to respond cannot be read from the files.
    Found by the attempt on Lohani et al. 2022 (lohani-2022-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Data missingThe dandiset holds 4 of the 6 ACh3.0 and jRCaMP1b mice of the paper's code (grabAM05 to grabAM08, not grabAM09 and grabAM10), and no ECoG, drug sessions, control mice or ChAT-GCaMP6s mice. Five of the paper's findings cannot be tested.
    Found by the attempt on Lohani et al. 2022 (lohani-2022-01, 2026-10-18)
