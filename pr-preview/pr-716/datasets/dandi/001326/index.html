Dataset version 0.250528.1957 · tested 2026-11-02 · 11 open findings

  • File format errorThe spike times of every one of the 9554 units are stored in no particular order (about half of successive differences are negative); NWB expects them ascending. Sorted, they agree with the stimulus table: the population response 0 to 200 ms after each puff is 0.03 to 0.19 z in every session and -0.03 to 0.003 at onsets shifted by 1.5 s.
    Found by the attempt on Kauvar et al. 2025 (kauvar-2025-01, 2026-11-02)
  • Description missingThe animal excluded from the paper's Figure 5 and 6 analyses (Harderian gland secretion) is not identified in the dandiset or the paper. Removing sub-1, sub-3 or sub-5 each leaves 11 sessions from 9 mice; only removing sub-5 also leaves 7 tone sessions from 7 mice as table S3 reports, and several outcomes depend on the choice.
    Found by the attempt on Kauvar et al. 2025 (kauvar-2025-01, 2026-11-02)
  • OtherTone series are in 9 sessions from 8 mice, where table S3 reports 7 sessions from 7 mice, and 9 sessions also hold light stimuli (240 to 248 per session) that the paper does not describe.
    Found by the attempt on Kauvar et al. 2025 (kauvar-2025-01, 2026-11-02)
  • Description missingThe ketamine infusion time in each file is described as approximate and falls 94 to 420 s after the code's ketamine onset (30 s after the last puff of series 20); with the code's onset, the tenth pre-infusion tone series counts as post-infusion.
    Found by the attempt on Kauvar et al. 2025 (kauvar-2025-01, 2026-11-02)
  • Description missing867 of 9554 units carry the region label NA, and the files give no description of the region labels beyond the acronyms.
    Found by the attempt on Kauvar et al. 2025 (kauvar-2025-01, 2026-11-02)
  • Data missingDANDI 001326 holds no eye closure; the recorded mice's eye closure is in DANDI 001347, with no file for sub-3_ses-20230225, sub-5_ses-20230706 or sub-5_ses-20230822, and with no tone events.
    Found by the attempt on Kauvar et al. 2025 (kauvar-2025-01, 2026-11-02)
  • Description missingIn DANDI 001347 the behavior-rig files hold only an eye-closure trace and an experiment id, with no drug, dose, pressure or injection label, and the human P50 files label trials only as pre and post. The conditions come only from the session lists of the paper's code (Zenodo doi:10.5281/zenodo.14920570).
    Found by the attempt on Kauvar et al. 2025 (kauvar-2025-01, 2026-11-02)
  • Identifier missingThe recorded-mouse eye-closure files of DANDI 001347 (sub-m<mouse>-<recording>) carry no identifier of the 001326 sessions; the link comes from the paper's conversion script and a clock fit to the series onsets.
    Found by the attempt on Kauvar et al. 2025 (kauvar-2025-01, 2026-11-02)
  • Data missingLick data (fig. S4), participant reports (Fig. 1F) and CADSS scores (Fig. 2D, fig. S14G) are not in DANDI 001347 or the code archive.
    Found by the attempt on Kauvar et al. 2025 (kauvar-2025-01, 2026-11-02)
  • Description missingThe "Ketamine infusion start" of every file falls exactly 5.0 s before the first puff of series 21 (series 23 starts 235 to 266 s after it), so the stored infusion time appears to be placed from the puff schedule; its description says only that it is approximate.
    Found by the attempt on Kauvar et al. 2025 (kauvar-2025-01, 2026-11-02)
  • OtherThe derived human files (sub-H) hold the inputs of the paper's factor and cluster analyses, and an NMF recomputed from sub-H_ses-sgramPerChannel with the paper's code settings gives back the deposited loadings exactly. The factor order of sub-H_ses-sgramNMFFactors is not the code's order by peak time (its factors 2 and 3 are swapped), and the files do not say which order the figures use.
    Found by the attempt on Kauvar et al. 2025 (kauvar-2025-01, 2026-11-02)
