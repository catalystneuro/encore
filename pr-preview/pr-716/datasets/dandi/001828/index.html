  • Data missingThe dandiset holds only behaviour and fiber photometry. The histology, RNA-scope and tracing images or counts, the qPCR of iPSC-derived neurons and the nosepoke counts of the self-stimulation and ratio tasks are not in it, so 16 claims of Figures 1 to 3 cannot be tested. GEO GSE282753 holds the MitoPark RNA-seq counts but not the cMfn2KO ones. No file holds a nosepoke, port, reward, zone, entry or trial object.
    Found by the attempt on Mantas et al. 2024 (mantas-2024-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Data missingThe arrow maze files hold DeepLabCut pose only. The Bonsai end-zone entries, reward deliveries and arena geometry behind Figure 6D are not in them.
    Found by the attempt on Mantas et al. 2024 (mantas-2024-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Metadata error86 open field sessions have session_id tmaze-..., 84 of them in files named ses-tmaze-..., although their descriptions, contents (VAME labels, kinematics, the open field DeepLabCut model) and frame counts are those of open field sessions.
    Found by the attempt on Mantas et al. 2024 (mantas-2024-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Description missingExperimental groups, cohorts, days, test phases and light conditions are given only in free text inside session_description, with names that differ between experiments (ctrl, asc_acid, Ctrl 10-11 wks, Littermate control); the subject metadata carry no group.
    Found by the attempt on Mantas et al. 2024 (mantas-2024-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Metadata errorSubject genotypes conflict across files. Mice with dLight (776769, 776770, 796284, 796287, 802369, 802372, 821709, 821710, 829248, 842388, 842395) are WT in their open field files and Anxa1-flp or Dat_cre in their other files, and 687662 is anxa1-flp in the open field and Dat_cre in reaching. Mouse 779575 is a control in the open field TeTx experiment and a TeTx mouse in the reaching cohort.
    Found by the attempt on Mantas et al. 2024 (mantas-2024-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Data missingThe paper's 37-motif VAME labelling is not in the dandiset. Its only labelling, VAME_42, has 42 labels (0 to 41) with no names or action categories, while the paper reports 37 motifs numbered up to 42 and grouped into categories; motif 42 is not a label and motif 28 is unused by the Figure 5 mice. Ten claims rest on the paper's motifs.
    Found by the attempt on Mantas et al. 2024 (mantas-2024-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Description missingThe VAME motif labels are stored with starting_time 0 on the frame grid of the pose and kinematics, but they explain linear speed best when placed 15 to 21 frames later (median 18, eta squared 0.69 against 0.49 as stored), about half the 30-frame VAME window, so a stored label is consistent with describing the window that starts at its frame. The files do not say how labels are placed in time, and motif onsets as stored come about 0.6 s early.
    Found by the attempt on Mantas et al. 2024 (mantas-2024-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Description missingThe 30 Hz z-scored open field photometry (FiberPhotometrySeriesZScored) is smoothed (its first and last 7 or 8 samples are held constant) and best matches the dF/F 0.1 s earlier; neither the smoothing nor the lag is documented.
    Found by the attempt on Mantas et al. 2024 (mantas-2024-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Selection not documentedThe dandiset holds more photometry recordings and mice than the legends count (water drop, Anxa1 15 against 10, DAT 8 against 4, dLight-dCP 21 against 13, dLight-cCP 15 against 11; open field Anxa1 10 against 9) and fewer in some (open field DAT 6 against 7, arrow maze controls 17 against 18), without saying which recordings the paper used.
    Found by the attempt on Mantas et al. 2024 (mantas-2024-01, 2026-10-21)
  • OtherThe dandiset holds experiments that are not in the preprint version read (6-OHDA in the tail striatum in open field, reaching and arrow maze; anti-Anxa1 open field; a 2025 Arch reaching cohort), labelled only in free text.
    Found by the attempt on Mantas et al. 2024 (mantas-2024-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Metadata errorAll 17 Arch-cohort reaching files of the first training day, the 2024 mice included, say the laser was switched on after the mouse's first reach, while the Methods say it was off during the first day of reaching and on during the last three; either the label or the protocol differs from the paper.
    Found by the attempt on Mantas et al. 2024 (mantas-2024-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Metadata errorThe 20 evoked release sessions are described as nosepoke self-stimulation, but their 1 s trains come at fixed 4 s intervals in every session; the reaching photometry sessions' descriptions call the GCaMP signals SN cell-body recordings, while the fiber table and the indicator descriptions place the fiber in dCP.
    Found by the attempt on Mantas et al. 2024 (mantas-2024-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Metadata errorThe archive's variableMeasured lists only ProcessingModule and BehavioralTimeSeries, though 110 files hold fiber photometry; the photometry sessions of the water drop task are stored as reaching sessions.
    Found by the attempt on Mantas et al. 2024 (mantas-2024-01, 2026-10-21)
  • OtherSeven open field photometry recordings hold under 13 minutes of photometry (2.9 to 11.9 min) where the Methods describe 20 minute sessions.
