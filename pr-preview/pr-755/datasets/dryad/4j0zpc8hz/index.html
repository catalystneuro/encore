Dataset version 260590 · tested 2026-10-27 · 9 open findings

  • Metadata errorMetadata.csv and the README sheet give the treatment codes as C, W, MR and WMR; the Treatment column holds C, D, H and HD. The Warming and Soil moisture reduction columns map them one to one (H is W, D is MR, HD is WMR).
    Found by the attempt on Didion Gency et al. 2024 (didion-gency-2024-01, 2026-10-27)
  • OtherThe 'Total Data' sheet has 35 rows that repeat another row exactly (trees 17, 35, 45 and 144; tree 35 has 24 rows in place of 9), and tree 144 has two rows for campaign 5 of 2020 with different sugar (7.71 and 6.69 %) and starch (4.99 and 9.99 %) values.
    Found by the attempt on Didion Gency et al. 2024 (didion-gency-2024-01, 2026-10-27)
  • OtherStarch (77 tree-campaign rows, down to -6.28 %), sugar (3, down to -4.06 %) and Asat (5, down to -0.29) have negative values, which a concentration or a light-saturated assimilation rate cannot have; the paper takes their square root.
    Found by the attempt on Didion Gency et al. 2024 (didion-gency-2024-01, 2026-10-27)
  • Metadata errorGrowth_length ('growing season length') never equals onset of senescence minus bud swelling (Senes_10 - Flush_S1), the Methods' definition: it is a median 55 days longer, and 2 to 33 days longer than the end of senescence (Senes_10 + Senes_time) minus bud swelling. Its definition is not given.
    Found by the attempt on Didion Gency et al. 2024 (didion-gency-2024-01, 2026-10-27)
  • Metadata errorDiameter_growth is labelled cm, but its values (median 0.42, up to 4.46 a year) on stems of 5.2 to 6.1 mm at planting (Table S4) are consistent with mm.
    Found by the attempt on Didion Gency et al. 2024 (didion-gency-2024-01, 2026-10-27)
  • Data missingThe soil moisture (Mspring, Mfall, Mannual), the chamber climate records (air temperature, relative humidity, VPD, soil temperature and water content at 10 min) and the initial heights and diameters (Table S4) are not deposited; five claims rest on them.
    Found by the attempt on Didion Gency et al. 2024 (didion-gency-2024-01, 2026-10-27)
  • Description missingTspring, Tfall and Tannual are one value per treatment and year (the same in the four chambers of a treatment), and the sensor height (50 cm or 2 m) they come from is not stated; GDD and Chill_days are given per tree without the records they were computed from, and the GDD description does not say whether the excess over 5 C or the daily mean is summed.
    Found by the attempt on Didion Gency et al. 2024 (didion-gency-2024-01, 2026-10-27)
  • OtherFour compartments (6 in chambers A3, A4, B3 and B4) hold a beech monoculture tree and an oak mixture tree, which the design (a compartment holds a monoculture or a mixture) does not allow.
    Found by the attempt on Didion Gency et al. 2024 (didion-gency-2024-01, 2026-10-27)
  • OtherAGB_growth (estimated AGWB increment) is exactly 0 in 57 of 191 tree-years of 2020 and 2021, whose median height increment is 5 cm, and is never negative; how it was bounded is not described.
    Found by the attempt on Didion Gency et al. 2024 (didion-gency-2024-01, 2026-10-27)
