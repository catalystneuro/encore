Dataset unversioned files (the archive keeps no versions) · tested 2026-11-12 · 8 open findings

  • Data missingThe L-ratio and isolation distance fields of every unit's cluster metadata are NaN, in the unit's overall cluster info and in each epoch's own (PRE, RUN1, RUN2 and RUN3), and the data set holds only the spikes of the sorted units (no unclustered threshold crossings and no waveforms; the width and wave fields are empty), so the cluster quality analysis of Fig. S3 cannot be repeated and the waveform criterion for excluding interneurons cannot be checked.
    Found by the attempt on Rich et al. 2014 (rich-2014-01, 2026-11-12)
  • Data missingThe scale model of the track (its vertices), which the paper uses for the track angle and corners of Fig. S10, is not in the data set; only the animal's room position is, from which the track can be reconstructed approximately.
    Found by the attempt on Rich et al. 2014 (rich-2014-01, 2026-11-12)
  • Description missingThe readme gives no units. Linearized and room positions are in inches, open-field positions in camera pixels with a per-animal unitsPerCm, the speed column in inches per microsecond, and spike times in microseconds; the direction column is 180 for outbound travel. The direction code of the fields in fieldInfoC (1 inbound, 2 outbound, 3 both) is not described.
    Found by the attempt on Rich et al. 2014 (rich-2014-01, 2026-11-12)
  • Description missingThe region of every unit of ANM228899 is N/A, so the localization of its tetrodes to CA1 cannot be checked from the file.
    Found by the attempt on Rich et al. 2014 (rich-2014-01, 2026-11-12)
  • OtherThe data set's rate maps and fields (rateMapInfoC, fieldInfoC) use 1 inch bins, where the Methods give 1 cm bins; its field edges and sizes are whole inches (sizes from 15.24 cm).
    Found by the attempt on Rich et al. 2014 (rich-2014-01, 2026-11-12)
  • OtherThe section extents in sectExt give sections of about 3.7, 6.1, 11.5 and 26.9 m (track ends 3.5, 9.6, 21.2 and 48.0 m), where the Methods give sections of 3, 7, 12 and 26 m (3, 10, 22 and 48 m).
    Found by the attempt on Rich et al. 2014 (rich-2014-01, 2026-11-12)
  • OtherWith the file's unitsPerCm the open-field positions span about 100 to 119 cm by 72 to 97 cm (1st to 99th percentile), where the Methods describe an oval of about 40 x 100 cm; either the scale or the description is off. One open-field session lasts 9.2 min, against 10 to 25 min in the Methods.
    Found by the attempt on Rich et al. 2014 (rich-2014-01, 2026-11-12)
  • Description missingThe data set's in-field rate (fieldInfoC inFieldRate) is not described. On the same field extents it averages 5.45 Hz, against 4.94 Hz for the mean of a recomputed smoothed rate map and 5.39 Hz for spikes over occupancy in the field; for fields of both directions it is 6.45 Hz against 5.18 and 5.25 Hz, so it is computed some other way there.
    Found by the attempt on Rich et al. 2014 (rich-2014-01, 2026-11-12)
