Dataset unversioned files (the archive keeps no versions) · tested 2026-10-20 · 6 open findings

  • Data missingThe data set holds only the white-noise recordings behind Figure 9D to 9H. The histology of Figures 1 to 8 and Supplementary Figure 1, and the flashing-spot, flash, moving-bar and grating recordings of Figure 9A to 9C, are not in it, and the paper names no other deposit. Of the 26 claims, 19 cannot be tested.
    Found by the attempt on Lefebvre et al. 2008 (lefebvre-2008-01, 2026-10-20)
  • Selection not documentedThe data set holds 200 mutant and 122 control units (and 3 from a Bax -/- mutant), where the paper's Figure 9 has 115 mutant and 143 control cells. The data set does not say how its units relate to the paper's, which were kept, or why the counts differ.
    Found by the attempt on Lefebvre et al. 2008 (lefebvre-2008-01, 2026-10-20)
  • File format errorIn the 8 recordings of 20080628_R4, 20080628_R6, 20080702_R1 and 20080702_R4, 82 of 86 spike trains are not in time order. Each runs to 1440 s and then holds a second segment between 720 and 780 s, though the stimulus runs to 1500 s. The 4 trains in order have spikes between 1440 and 1500 s, about as many as between 720 and 780 s, and moving the segments by 720 s raises their STA peaks over the null in most units. This is consistent with the last minute stored with a 720 s offset in place of 1440 s. One checkerboard recording (20080628_R5) has 1 or 2 spikes near 2 s out of order in 15 units.
    Found by the attempt on Lefebvre et al. 2008 (lefebvre-2008-01, 2026-10-20)
  • Metadata errorThe frame lengths stated for the vertical-bar recordings of 20080702_R1 and 20080702_R4 (16.6691 and 16.6653 ms) are not those of the stimulus. Rebuilt with the stated frame, the bars of 20080702_R4 give no STA above the null in any of its 12 units (median ratio 0.96), though all 12 respond to the checkerboard of the same retina. A scan of the frame peaks at 16.671 to 16.672 ms in both recordings; with 16.6718 ms, the frame of every other bar recording and half the checkerboard frame of the same two retinas, the median ratio is 2.57 and 10 of the 12 units have peaks above twice the null's.
    Found by the attempt on Lefebvre et al. 2008 (lefebvre-2008-01, 2026-10-20)
  • Other16 pairs of units share 70 to 100% of their spikes within 0.5 ms in every recording of their retina (12 pairs in mutant retinas, 4 in a control retina), most likely single neurons sorted twice. The data set does not mark them.
    Found by the attempt on Lefebvre et al. 2008 (lefebvre-2008-01, 2026-10-20)
  • Description missingThe files do not give the age of the mice, the Pcdh-gamma allele (the paper's Results say fcon3 and its Figure 9 legend f-del), the region recorded in the Pax6-Cre retinas (the paper used their periphery only), or the cell type of the units.
    Found by the attempt on Lefebvre et al. 2008 (lefebvre-2008-01, 2026-10-20)
