Dataset unversioned files (the archive keeps no versions) · tested 2026-11-13 · 9 open findings

  • Description missingThe horizontal and vertical eye signals have no units, calibration or sign convention. They are on a +-5000 scale in 36 hc-12 sessions and a +-5 scale in 26, with saturated samples at the bound (up to 22% of a session). The paper's point-of-gaze maps, foveation and landmark eccentricity cannot be computed, which blocks 11 of the 28 claims and part of a twelfth. The sign used here is inferred from the slow phases during turns.
    Found by the attempt on Wirth et al. 2017 (wirth-2017-01, 2026-11-13); also about CRCNS:hc-21
  • Metadata errorThe descriptor puts the probability of a correct response in the middle column of learning_curves and the bounds in columns 1 and 3. In all 187 files column 2 is the lowest and column 3 the highest, so the columns are probability, lower bound and upper bound. The field learningtrial, not described, is the first trial whose lower bound exceeds 1/4 (-2 when none).
    Found by the attempt on Wirth et al. 2017 (wirth-2017-01, 2026-11-13)
  • Metadata errorThe descriptor gives the eye positions at 250 Hz; eye_times are 5 ms apart (200 Hz) in every file.
    Found by the attempt on Wirth et al. 2017 (wirth-2017-01, 2026-11-13)
  • Metadata errorThe descriptor says code 43 is the rewarded start arm and code 53 the rewarded chosen arm. The arm chosen on rewarded trials is 53 in 32 of 66 sessions and another arm in the rest, as the reward moved each day.
    Found by the attempt on Wirth et al. 2017 (wirth-2017-01, 2026-11-13)
  • Data missingTwo hc-12 files (sn20121015_HPC_e2_c1a and c1c) have no new-maze (newallo) data, the only maze the paper analyses, so 187 of the 189 cells can be analysed.
    Found by the attempt on Wirth et al. 2017 (wirth-2017-01, 2026-11-13)
  • Data missinghc-12 holds 12 probe sessions of monkey K and none of monkey S, where the paper reports 15 and 9; hc-21 holds 8 probe-like sessions, all among K's 12.
    Found by the attempt on Wirth et al. 2017 (wirth-2017-01, 2026-11-13); also about CRCNS:hc-21
  • Other87 of the 101 hc-21 files hold the same spike trains (same spike count and first five spike times) as hc-12 files, under other file names. Neither descriptor says that the two data sets overlap.
    Found by the attempt on Wirth et al. 2017 (wirth-2017-01, 2026-11-13); also about CRCNS:hc-21
  • OtherThe files come in seven layouts. The spike times are under file.elec in 175 files and only under newallo.elec in 12; camera data are at the top level or under newallo; the camera orientation runs from 0 to 360 in some files and -180 to 180 in others. The descriptor gives one layout.
    Found by the attempt on Wirth et al. 2017 (wirth-2017-01, 2026-11-13)
  • OtherIn 3 of the 12 probe sessions of monkey K the first entries lie on both sides of the rewarded arm (for example ke20140306 starts from arms at -72 and +144 degrees from it), and in the other 9 they lie on one side, clockwise in 2 and counterclockwise in 7. The paper describes probe sessions as starting from the northeastern and southeastern entries.
    Found by the attempt on Wirth et al. 2017 (wirth-2017-01, 2026-11-13)
