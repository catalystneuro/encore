None of the paper's main findings could be tested in this data, because the dataset holds sorted spike times but no position tracking, which every spatial analysis needs. Untested for that reason are the lower spatial information and scarcer place fields of lateral septum than CA1, the skew of fields by direction of travel toward reward, the excess of lateral septum fields on the rewarded choice side, and the stronger CA1 to lateral septum cross-correlations there. The unit counts hold in part: the files hold exactly the paper's 452 lateral septum and 178 CA1 clusters, though some septal clusters are exact copies of others. The counts of cells meeting the paper's inclusion criteria also need position.

    Results, opening paragraph; Results, Place fields are less abundant, paragraph 5

    Two of the four counts are tested and both match exactly. The data set holds 452 LS and 178 CA1 clusters, the paper's two counts of units recorded, from six rats (LS from rats 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 and 12; CA1 from rats 10 and 12 only). Of the 452 LS clusters 42 are byte for byte copies of other clusters (two rat 7 trains deposited 18 and 25 times, one rat 6 pair), leaving 410 distinct trains, within the tolerance of 45 when only byte for byte copies are discounted; dropping as well the 14 largest clusters of rat 6's tetrode 26, which hold the spikes of the others, and the multi-unit cluster MUA9 leaves 395, outside it. The outcome rests on the clusters deposited, which the paper's 452 matches exactly and so evidently counts. All 178 CA1 trains are distinct. The other two counts, 165 CA1 and 378 LS cells meeting the rate and track coverage criteria, are untested (data_missing). They need the rate above 12 cm/s and the coverage of the track under either reading the criterion names, and so the animal's position and speed, which the data set does not hold. The criterion needs all four counts, so the claim holds in part. Over whole recordings 423 LS and 166 CA1 clusters reach 0.05 Hz, a variant that decides nothing, as the paper's rule counts only time above 12 cm/s. The first version of this record gave the claim as blocked; it was changed after verification round 1 (see verifications).

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • Units are counted as the cluster fields deposited, copies included; the count of distinct spike trains is shown beside it.
    • The two counts of cells meeting the rate and coverage criteria are not computed, for lack of position and speed.

Reproduction Reward biased spatial representations in lateral septum and CA1 (Wirtshafter and Wilson 2020)

Tests the findings of Wirtshafter and Wilson (2020) on CRCNS hc-29, which holds the sorted spike times of 452 lateral septum and 178 CA1 clusters and the time bases of 57 recordings of six rats. The data set holds no position data, so every finding of the paper beyond the count of units recorded, which matches, is blocked; the notebook counts the units and checks the files, finding spike trains deposited many times and clusters that hold the spikes of others.
