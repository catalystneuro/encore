Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • The SVC is fitted on 4 of the 500 subsets per session (every 125th), as one per task on all 500 would take about 25 hours on one core; the theoretical error is the mean over all 500. On the same 4 subsets the theoretical error gives r2 = 0.931 (PFC) and 0.829 (CA1).svc-hebbian-correlation
  • CA1 uses 24 units per subset (Methods); the paper's SVC figure used 19.svc-hebbian-correlation
  • The random subsets use this attempt's seeds; the per-session means agree with the paper's file to a median relative difference of 0.14% (PFC theory).fig7e-readout-error-learning
  • The GLM basis is the one the code chose (position bins 10 cm, velocity bins 3 cm/s, variances 100 and 40); its grid search over 108 basis settings was not repeated, as each setting costs about 27 minutes here.fig7h-task-dimension-increases
  • The GLM basis is the code's chosen one; its grid search was not repeated (see fig7h-task-dimension-increases). The cross-validation folds are scikit-learn's unshuffled KFold, as GridSearchCV uses for a regressor, so they are fixed, not random as the criterion supposes.glm-cross-validated-d2
