The paper's membrane recovery findings mostly hold in this data, which covers only its two-pulse experiment, so most other findings could not be tested. Test-pulse charging stays cosine-shaped and unchanged after mild conditioning pulses and falls after the strongest ones, as reported. The fall differs in size from the paper's at the cathodic pole, and at the anodic pole it is not significant after the weaker of the two strongest pulses. Full resealing after mild pulses and residual leak after strong ones hold, but the test pulse also finds a pole difference after the strongest pulse. These results depend on an automatic membrane trace, and the dye calibration, escalation, Gd3+ and calcium findings are absent from the dataset.

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • The perimeter trace is found automatically, where the paper draws "the 6-pixel-wide trace ... along the cell perimeter" by hand in MetaMorph (Methods 2.3). The membrane at each angle is the intensity peak within 8 px inside the cell's outer edge (the steepest outward fall of intensity), an assumption adopted after the first round's plain intensity peak was seen to fall inside two cells; that trace is shown as a variant, and so are windows of 4, 6, 10 and 12 px.
    • The analysed cell is the largest object in each subject's field (an assumption; the paper says one cell per experiment, Fig. 2 legend, and does not say how it was chosen).
    • The field direction in image coordinates is not given by the dataset; it is the A-weighted circular mean of cosine-fit phases over all 5-us files (-75.6 degrees, per-file sd 6.8), and from the control files alone as a variant (-73.9 degrees). 0 degrees is the depolarized pole, as Fig. 7A plots it.
    • Conditioning strengths are mapped by rank from the files' generator volts (100 to 350 V) to the Fig. 7A labels 360 to 1400 V/cm, and the single 50 V pulse, recorded first in every cell, is taken as the 150/150 benchmark; an assumption, as the files give no field strengths and no 150/150 pair. Read as steps of 100 V from 0.15 kV/cm at 50 V, the strengths in the files' general description would put the levels 0 to 11% lower, with the same order.
    • Six 5-us cells are in the dataset where the paper reports n = 5; all six are used, and each five-cell subset is shown.
    • Background and dark frames are open in the paper. Main reading adopted as an assumption, background removed (median of an annulus 1.6 to 2.2 radii) and frames without a laser flash dropped; the paper's own scale (Fig. 7A about 13% dF at 150/150; 9.1% dF per 100 mV in Results 3.3, about 16% dF at 150 V/cm) is met only with a background removed (12.6 to 12.9% against 5.6%), and the files' camera floor is about 209 counts under a membrane trace of 451 to 692. All four combinations are shown.
    • The criterion's pole dF is tested under the pole readings of fig7b-test-pulse-unchanged-to-760 (cathodic, anodic, mean of both), with its test, the unpaired two-sample t-test against the control group (the paired test shown as a variant); reading (a) compares the fit at each pole with the s.e. of the pole mean over cells, for the legend's gray outline.

Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • Same trace, cell, field direction, strength mapping, benchmark, cell count and main reading as fig7a-test-pulse-cosine-up-to-1000.fig7b-test-pulse-unchanged-to-760 fig7b-test-pulse-reduced-1200-1400 fig7c-full-reseal-mild-residual-strong
  • The perimeter trace is found automatically, where the paper draws "the 6-pixel-wide trace ... along the cell perimeter" by hand in MetaMorph (Methods 2.3). The membrane at each angle is the intensity peak within 8 px inside the cell's outer edge (the steepest outward fall of intensity), an assumption adopted after the first round's plain intensity peak was seen to fall inside two cells; that trace is shown as a variant, and so are windows of 4, 6, 10 and 12 px.fig7a-test-pulse-cosine-up-to-1000
  • The analysed cell is the largest object in each subject's field (an assumption; the paper says one cell per experiment, Fig. 2 legend, and does not say how it was chosen).fig7a-test-pulse-cosine-up-to-1000
  • The field direction in image coordinates is not given by the dataset; it is the A-weighted circular mean of cosine-fit phases over all 5-us files (-75.6 degrees, per-file sd 6.8), and from the control files alone as a variant (-73.9 degrees). 0 degrees is the depolarized pole, as Fig. 7A plots it.fig7a-test-pulse-cosine-up-to-1000
  • Conditioning strengths are mapped by rank from the files' generator volts (100 to 350 V) to the Fig. 7A labels 360 to 1400 V/cm, and the single 50 V pulse, recorded first in every cell, is taken as the 150/150 benchmark; an assumption, as the files give no field strengths and no 150/150 pair. Read as steps of 100 V from 0.15 kV/cm at 50 V, the strengths in the files' general description would put the levels 0 to 11% lower, with the same order.fig7a-test-pulse-cosine-up-to-1000
  • Six 5-us cells are in the dataset where the paper reports n = 5; all six are used, and each five-cell subset is shown.fig7a-test-pulse-cosine-up-to-1000
  • Background and dark frames are open in the paper. Main reading adopted as an assumption, background removed (median of an annulus 1.6 to 2.2 radii) and frames without a laser flash dropped; the paper's own scale (Fig. 7A about 13% dF at 150/150; 9.1% dF per 100 mV in Results 3.3, about 16% dF at 150 V/cm) is met only with a background removed (12.6 to 12.9% against 5.6%), and the files' camera floor is about 209 counts under a membrane trace of 451 to 692. All four combinations are shown.fig7a-test-pulse-cosine-up-to-1000
  • The criterion's pole dF is tested under the pole readings of fig7b-test-pulse-unchanged-to-760 (cathodic, anodic, mean of both), with its test, the unpaired two-sample t-test against the control group (the paired test shown as a variant); reading (a) compares the fit at each pole with the s.e. of the pole mean over cells, for the legend's gray outline.fig7a-test-pulse-cosine-up-to-1000
  • Normalized test dF for the pole comparison is per-cell dF over the group control of each pole; normalization by each cell's own control is shown as a variant. A standard choice the criterion leaves open; it does not change the result at 1400 V/cm.fig7c-full-reseal-mild-residual-strong
