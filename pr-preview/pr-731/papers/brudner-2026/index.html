None of the paper's main findings was fully confirmed in this data, and most could not be tested. The plume-specific organization of gradient and motion cues, the networks and the navigating agents could not be tested, because the smooth plume movie is not in the dataset. In the complex plume, motion predicted the centerline side better than the gradient, but the model values missed the paper's. Fly turns followed the gradient in the smooth plume, but the complex-plume motion effect did not hold in every reading and its dominance held only in part, as the dataset holds two undocumented complex-plume file sets. Minor claims on cue robustness and turn size (about 21 against 34 degrees) did not reproduce.

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • Assumed that the samples are balanced by mirroring each sample (antennae swapped, label flipped). The paper does not say how samples are drawn or balanced. Its Fig. 2B-C and the code's published table give a sum AUC of exactly 0.5 with SD 0 over 100 repeats and a sum weight of 0.000 'as expected' (Results, Plume-Specific Features Predict Centerline Direction, paragraph 3), which only a sample symmetric under the mirror gives; on the deposited plume the samplings the claims file names give a sum AUC of 0.62 to 0.66. The two named samplings are shown, and the mirror-balanced one is run with the windows split at random and by location.
    • 10 repeats per reading, not the paper's 100; each repeat draws 1000 new locations and 2,400,000 training and 600,000 test samples, as the SI (Plume movies, Logistic regression models).
    • The two antennae are at rows r and r + 3 of column c, the label is whether r + 1.5 is above row 590 (SI, centerline of the complex plume), and frames are filtered by a Gaussian of SD 1.5 pixels before sampling (SI). Which antenna is left is a convention that changes no AUC. Row 571 is run as a variant.
    • The Otsu region is the mask of figs1-antisymmetry-index with the bias of 48 subtracted from the Otsu threshold (0.841 of pixels); the region of mean intensity above 5 covers 0.890.
    • The complex window is used as stored (0 to 255). Its provenance (printed in the notebook) names a preprocessing run 'bgsub_gauss3_fullslice_p001_p99', which may differ from the movie the paper analysed; this cannot be undone here.

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • The motion cue for a sensor spacing and a delay is not defined in the paper or its code. Three forms were run, the Fig. 1 cue -(dI/dy)(dI/dt) with centered differences and the spacing and the delay widened (Fig. S1 column iv is that cue), the same with a backward difference in time, and the correlator A(t - tau) B(t) - A(t) B(t - tau) of the SI's motion feature; all fail the criterion.
    • Whether the Fig. S1F-G maps are of smoothed frames is not stated; each map is computed from the frames as stored and from frames smoothed by the antennae's Gaussian (SD 1.5 pixels), as the code smooths its Fig. 1 cue panels. The motion part fails under both.

    • Sensor pairs are aligned on the row between them (rows y - floor(d/2) and y + ceil(d/2)), and 6 rows are cropped at each edge so maps of every spacing share their pixels.

Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • The motion cue for a sensor spacing and a delay is not defined in the paper or its code. Three forms were run, the Fig. 1 cue -(dI/dy)(dI/dt) with centered differences and the spacing and the delay widened (Fig. S1 column iv is that cue), the same with a backward difference in time, and the correlator A(t - tau) B(t) - A(t) B(t - tau) of the SI's motion feature; all fail the criterion.figs1fg-resolution-robustness
  • Whether the Fig. S1F-G maps are of smoothed frames is not stated; each map is computed from the frames as stored and from frames smoothed by the antennae's Gaussian (SD 1.5 pixels), as the code smooths its Fig. 1 cue panels. The motion part fails under both.figs1fg-resolution-robustness
  • Maps use every 4th column (432 of 1728), a standard choice that cannot change the result, since the cues are crosswind and columns are computed independently; Pearson r is over all those pixels, and over the Otsu mask as a variant.figs1fg-resolution-robustness
  • Sensor pairs are aligned on the row between them (rows y - floor(d/2) and y + ceil(d/2)), and 6 rows are cropped at each edge so maps of every spacing share their pixels.figs1fg-resolution-robustness
  • Assumed that the samples are balanced by mirroring each sample (antennae swapped, label flipped). The paper does not say how samples are drawn or balanced. Its Fig. 2B-C and the code's published table give a sum AUC of exactly 0.5 with SD 0 over 100 repeats and a sum weight of 0.000 'as expected' (Results, Plume-Specific Features Predict Centerline Direction, paragraph 3), which only a sample symmetric under the mirror gives; on the deposited plume the samplings the claims file names give a sum AUC of 0.62 to 0.66. The two named samplings are shown, and the mirror-balanced one is run with the windows split at random and by location.fig2c-complex-auc
  • 10 repeats per reading, not the paper's 100; each repeat draws 1000 new locations and 2,400,000 training and 600,000 test samples, as the SI (Plume movies, Logistic regression models).fig2c-complex-auc
  • The two antennae are at rows r and r + 3 of column c, the label is whether r + 1.5 is above row 590 (SI, centerline of the complex plume), and frames are filtered by a Gaussian of SD 1.5 pixels before sampling (SI). Which antenna is left is a convention that changes no AUC. Row 571 is run as a variant.fig2c-complex-auc
  • The Otsu region is the mask of figs1-antisymmetry-index with the bias of 48 subtracted from the Otsu threshold (0.841 of pixels); the region of mean intensity above 5 covers 0.890.fig2c-complex-auc
  • The complex window is used as stored (0 to 255). Its provenance (printed in the notebook) names a preprocessing run 'bgsub_gauss3_fullslice_p001_p99', which may differ from the movie the paper analysed; this cannot be undone here.fig2c-complex-auc
  • As fig2c-complex-auc (10 repeats, sampling readings, antenna geometry, regions, centerline variant).fig2e-complex-weights
  • Weights are those of the standardized features of one unpenalized logistic model on the three features (code, centerline_models.py), compared in absolute value as the claims file says.fig2e-complex-weights
  • Assumed that the smoke-1a files are the paper's complex plume. The paper does not say which complex-plume behavior files it analysed; the dandiset's description says the smoke_1a_orig plume 'was analyzed throughout the paper', and the session descriptions of the smoke-1a files name that movie (smoke_1a), those of the smoke-2a files another (smoke_2a). The code selects smoke-2a (metadata/published_panel_params.json); its published tables are matched most closely by smoke-1a, not smoke-2a. Whether smoke-1a should stand for the paper's complex plume is also asked of a reader. Both alternatives and both sets together are shown.fig5e-cue-betas
  • Onset is the SI's (the time the rotation in the turn's direction crosses 20% of its peak on the rising edge; SI, Behavioral Analysis), as the criterion asks when the dataset's turn start is defined otherwise. The files' events start a median of 4 frames after that onset (every 6th file, 15841 turns). The peak is searched between the event's start and stop, a standard choice. The first version of this attempt followed |dtheta|, whose run went on into rotation the other way in 0.2% of turns; the rule now follows the signed rotation. The files' turn start, which the code uses, is shown as a variant.fig5e-cue-betas
  • Z-scoring (SI, Behavioral Analysis, which the code does not do) run as none, over all frames of the plume group (same p-values) and per trajectory, the claims file's three readings.fig5e-cue-betas
  • The predictor is read at the last frame at or before onset minus 50 ms within one frame, after rounding the target time to 10 decimals, exactly as the code's merge_asof does. Heading is read at that frame and position at the event start (turn_x, turn_y), as the code does.fig5e-cue-betas
  • The criterion's other readings (heading window, margin, alpha of 0.05 or 0.01 for not significant) are all run; the margin removes no kept turn, as the position filter lies inside it.fig5e-cue-betas
  • The assumption of fig5e-cue-betas (smoke-1a files) and the SI onset the criterion asks for; the outcome rests on both.fig5f-cue-dominance
  • The code's four models (VARIANT_FORMULAS) with trajectory-clustered SE; LL is the mean per turn. Smooth and complex readings are paired by onset, margin and z-scoring.fig5f-cue-dominance
  • A variant, which does not decide the outcome, bounds the turn from the SI's onset (20% of the peak on the rising edge; SI, Behavioral Analysis) to the 20% crossing on the falling edge of the rotation in the turn's direction, the end the paper does not state. This bound was adopted after the event bounds failed.figs5c-turn-angles
  • The complex files are smoke-1a, as assumed for fig5e-cue-betas; smoke-2a and both sets are shown and give the same outcome.figs5c-turn-angles
