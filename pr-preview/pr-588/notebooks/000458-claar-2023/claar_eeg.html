Claar, Rembado et al. (2023) on dandiset 000458: EEG claims¶

Paper. Claar LD, Rembado I, Kuyat JR, Russo S, Marks LC, Olsen SR, Koch C (2023). Cortico-thalamo-cortical interactions modulate electrically evoked EEG responses in mice. eLife 12:RP84630. doi:10.7554/eLife.84630. The claims file was written from the version of record (full text from Europe PMC), with its figure supplement legends.

Dandiset. DANDI:000458, version 0.230317.0039. The paper's Data availability cites this version, and it is the only published version, so it is pinned. The draft lists the same 24 assets with the same total size.

What the dandiset holds. 24 sessions of 23 mice: skull EEG (30 electrodes), Neuropixels LFP and sorted units in 18 sessions, wheel speed, isoflurane epochs, and every stimulus pulse with its current, target and state. This notebook tests the EEG claims (Figures 1, 7C, 7D and 8); claar_spikes.ipynb tests the spiking claims.
