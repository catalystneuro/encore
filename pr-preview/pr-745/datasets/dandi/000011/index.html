Dataset version 0.220126.1907 · tested 2026-10-21 · 11 open findings

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds only the recording sessions. The behavioural photoinhibition sessions (Figs 1d, 2b, 5b and Extended Data Fig. 3), the photoactivation mice (Extended Data Fig. 5), the untrained mice used to map photoinhibition (Extended Data Fig. 2d) and any session before callosotomy are not in it. Nine of the paper's findings cannot be tested for this reason.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2016 (li-2016-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Data missing33 sessions of 7 mice (the callosotomy group by genotype and delay) and 3 experiment-1 sessions of mouse 255200 have no photostimulus record (no laser traces, no photostimulus events, no trial column), though most of them contain trials whose activity is silenced in the light window of the other sessions. Their photoinhibition trials cannot be identified.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2016 (li-2016-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Description missingNo file says which mice were callosotomised, on which side, or when; the subject description holds only the JAX strain numbers.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2016 (li-2016-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Description missingThe trial table has no photostimulus column. The condition of a trial (site, number of laser spots, timing) can be recovered only from the per-site laser power traces (one 5 s chunk per photostimulus trial, matched to trials by time) and the photostim_start_time events, whose value is the peak power. The number of spots is not stated anywhere and is inferred from the waveform and power.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2016 (li-2016-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Metadata errorIn lick-early trials the photostim_start_time and photostim_stop_time events are logged at the restarted delay, after the light seen in the laser trace (20 of 41 lick-early photostimulus trials in two sessions read in full); in other trials they agree within 5 ms. Over all labelled sessions, 339 lick-early photostimulus trials have the event more than 0.1 s after the light, with the light up to 10 s before the logged go cue.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2016 (li-2016-01, 2026-10-21)
  • File format errorSession 258250_20141202 holds two interleaved sequences of trial ids (1 to 24 and 105 to 160) whose times overlap (98 overlapping pairs), so its events cannot be assigned to trials. In 28 units of 3 sessions the spike times are not in time order, and two sessions have trials out of time order.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2016 (li-2016-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Data missingThe control mice hold 979 units against the paper's 1012; 15 have intermediate spike widths against 50, so about 33 intermediate units were not deposited.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2016 (li-2016-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Metadata errorThe units' cell_type labels intermediate-width units (0.35 to 0.45 ms trough to peak) as FS, where the Methods exclude them (45 units in all).
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2016 (li-2016-01, 2026-10-21)
  • OtherThe asset sub-255200_ses-20140910_behavior.nwb holds only metadata (no trials, events or time series).
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2016 (li-2016-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Selection not documentedThe paper's neuron and session counts for its selectivity and population analyses (55, 143 and 40 selective neurons; 16 sessions with 6 to 20 neurons) cannot be reached with the criteria the paper states; the dataset gives 138 to 173, 221 to 293, 58 to 62 and 24 sessions with 6 to 37 neurons. Experiment 1 holds 4 mice where the paper reports 6.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2016 (li-2016-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Data missingNo file holds lick times; licks exist only as the analogue lick_trace (5 s chunks at 10 kHz, unit a.u.). In the 24 experiment-2 sessions used for the activity space the trace stays within about 0.002 of its baseline after the go cue on correct trials, and lick-detection rules fire no more often on correct than on no-response trials, so it holds no licks; an experiment-1 session read for reference is flat as well, while an experiment-3 session and a session of the mice without a photostimulus record carry licks. Reaction times cannot be computed for these sessions.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2016 (li-2016-01, 2026-10-21)
