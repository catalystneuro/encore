Dataset version 0.230307.2132 · tested 2026-10-30 · 12 open findings

  • Selection not documentedThe dandiset holds all 19 completed scans, while the paper describes 14 (Extended Data Table 1). Nothing in the files or the metadata marks which 14, so its counts (for example 125,413 masks) can be matched only after reading the table's image. The five other files are 4-9, 4-10, 5-3, 8-7 and 8-9.
    Found by the attempt on Microns et al. 2025 (microns-2025-01, 2026-10-30)
  • Data missingThe files hold fluorescence traces only. The deconvolved traces that the paper's analyses use, and that the paper says can be read from the NWB files, are not in them; nor are the oracle scores.
    Found by the attempt on Microns et al. 2025 (microns-2025-01, 2026-10-30)
  • Data missingThe structural stack, the registration of each field into it, and the stack coordinates of the masks are not in the dataset, so masks imaged in more than one scan cannot be grouped and the z drift cannot be measured.
    Found by the attempt on Microns et al. 2025 (microns-2025-01, 2026-10-30)
  • Description missingNo mask or field has a visual area; every imaging plane's location is the same list of four areas. The area masks the Methods transform into the stack are not in the dataset.
    Found by the attempt on Microns et al. 2025 (microns-2025-01, 2026-10-30)
  • Identifier missingThe EM columns (cave_ids, pt_root_id, pt_supervoxel_id and positions) exist only in one of the two fields at each depth (52 of the 104 fields of the paper's 14 scans) and in none of the fields of files 4-9, 4-10, 8-7 and 8-9.
    Found by the attempt on Microns et al. 2025 (microns-2025-01, 2026-10-30)
  • Description missingThe Monet2 table gives num_directions but not the direction or onset of each period, which Fig. 3g needs. The directions can be measured from the stimulus video in the files, as this attempt did.
    Found by the attempt on Microns et al. 2025 (microns-2025-01, 2026-10-30)
  • Metadata errorThe imaging_rate of every imaging plane is NaN (the rate is only in the field description), and the subject age is the range P75D/P81D in every file, though each scan was recorded on one day.
    Found by the attempt on Microns et al. 2025 (microns-2025-01, 2026-10-30)
  • OtherIn scan 4-7, one of the paper's 14, the eye tracking ends 968 s before the last imaging frame; in files 4-10, 5-3 and 8-7 it ends 54 to 76 minutes early, and in 4-9 and 8-9 3.9 and 6.2 s early. Several files have missing (NaN) time stamps in the treadmill and eye series (up to 190).
    Found by the attempt on Microns et al. 2025 (microns-2025-01, 2026-10-30)
  • OtherTrippy seed 6831 is shown in both scans 6-4 and 7-5, while the Methods say the 10 seeds that are not conserved are unique to each scan.
    Found by the attempt on Microns et al. 2025 (microns-2025-01, 2026-10-30)
  • OtherAll fields of a scan share one array of frame time stamps, though the fields are imaged one after another within each frame; the per-field offset is not recorded.
    Found by the attempt on Microns et al. 2025 (microns-2025-01, 2026-10-30)
  • OtherThe EM imagery of subvolume 65 on BossDB holds data up to section 27,882, while the paper gives the processed region as sections 7,931 to 27,904. The imagery of the two subvolumes overlaps from section 14,825 to 14,847, and the metadata give the bounds of each subvolume only in whole chunks of 64 sections (7,904 to 14,847 and 14,816 to 27,903).
    Found by the attempt on Microns et al. 2025 (microns-2025-01, 2026-10-30)
  • File format errorpt_root_id and pt_supervoxel_id are stored as 64-bit floats, which hold integers exactly only up to 2**53; root ids near 8.65e17 are spaced by 128 in float64, so ids that differ in their last digits can collide. Of 538 pairs of masks in different scans that share a root id, 50 have EM cell body points more than 10 um apart.
    Found by the attempt on Microns et al. 2025 (microns-2025-01, 2026-10-30)
