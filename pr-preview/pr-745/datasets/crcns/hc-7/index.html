Dataset unversioned files (the archive keeps no versions) · tested 2026-11-06 · 8 open findings

  • Metadata errorThe stimulation timings in the data differ from the data descriptor and the paper. RIP bursts are four APs 4.5 ms apart (222 Hz), not 160 Hz; the EPI burst is 30 APs 6.7 ms apart (147 Hz) and four 3.3 ms apart (303 Hz), not 160 and 320 Hz; the single AP precedes the RIP and EPI bursts by 1.3 s, not 1 s. The 10-AP trains are at their nominal rates.
    Found by the attempt on Kohus et al. 2016 (kohus-2016-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Metadata errorExperiments_description.txt mislabels two recordings (PV+BC to PV+BC pair 10 s130807-0205, listed as 5 Hz, and CCK+DTI to PC pair 5 s150222-0305, listed as 40 Hz, are 10 Hz trains), names eight files that are not in the zip (three misdated s1508.. for s1308..), lists one file for two protocols in two pairs, leaves 20 recordings of the zip unnamed, lists no PV+BC to AAC pairs, and numbers the IN to PC pairs differently from the PairNN folders.
    Found by the attempt on Kohus et al. 2016 (kohus-2016-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Data missingThree recordings have a header but no sweeps (CCK+BC to PC Pair07 s140616-0105 and -0106, CCK+DTI to PC Pair01 s150119-0104), three have sweeps but no _EPA table, and the RIP recording listed for PV+BC to PC pair 4 is absent. The data set holds 7 PV+BC, 8 AAC, 12 CCK+BC and 7 CCK+DTI to PC and 14 PV+BC to PV+BC pairs, fewer than the paper's 8, 9, 14, 8 and 16. The CCK+ to CCK+ pairs were recorded only with 10, 40 and 160 Hz trains, with no RIP recording, 1 Hz train or 320 Hz train, so their single-AP uIPSCs (Fig. 8) and 320 Hz charges (Fig. 10B) cannot be measured.
    Found by the attempt on Kohus et al. 2016 (kohus-2016-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Data missingThe current-step recordings (Fig. 2, Tables 1 and 2), the connectivity tests with unconnected pairs (Fig. 3, Table 3), the spontaneous IPSCs (Fig. 12) and the field potential and optogenetic recordings (Fig. 13A, C) are not in the data set, which is described as holding the paired recordings only.
    Found by the attempt on Kohus et al. 2016 (kohus-2016-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Description missingThe _EPA tables use a code AR, not described in the descriptor, that cuts off the mean row of every CCK+BC and CCK+DTI to PC EPI table and four CCK+DTI to PC 160 Hz tables from some AP on; its position matches the onset of asynchronous release the paper reports. The block the descriptor calls Relative ampl. holds amplitudes in pA.
    Found by the attempt on Kohus et al. 2016 (kohus-2016-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Description missingThe pairs are not labelled by pipette solution (high KCl, or low KCl with CsCl), which the paper's comparisons of Figure 7 need, and the order of the recovery intervals across sweeps, and which _EPA block holds which interval, are not documented (the AP times show 1200 down to 50 ms).
    Found by the attempt on Kohus et al. 2016 (kohus-2016-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Identifier missingThe files carry no animal or slice identifiers; the recording day in the file name is the only grouping above the pair, so results cannot be broken down by animal.
    Found by the attempt on Kohus et al. 2016 (kohus-2016-01, 2026-11-06)
  • File format errorTwo _EPA tables (CCK+BC to PC s140619-0204 and s140619-0105) hold column labels in place of the mean values; several tables list fewer sweeps than the raw data without a note (for example PV+BC to PC s140603-0406, 14 of 20); some have blank amplitude cells; four AAC to PC tables have successes with amplitude 0 or failures with an amplitude; a few mean rows differ from the mean of their own per-sweep values (up to 38 pA).
    Found by the attempt on Kohus et al. 2016 (kohus-2016-01, 2026-11-06)
