Reproduction Robust preparatory activity in mouse premotor cortex (Li, Daie et al. 2016)

Tests the findings of Li, Daie, Svoboda and Druckmann (2016) on dandiset 000011, which holds extracellular recordings from left ALM of 12 control mice during unilateral and bilateral photoinhibition (58 sessions) and 33 sessions of 7 further mice with no photostimulus record. It reads the photostimulus conditions from the laser traces, and tests unit classification, selectivity, recovery of spike rates and selectivity after photoinhibition, the coding direction analyses and the network models of the paper. The behavioural photoinhibition sessions, the photoactivation and untrained mice, and the callosotomy conditions are not in the dandiset. All files

Reproduction Robust preparatory activity in mouse premotor cortex (Li, Daie, Svoboda and Druckmann 2016)

Tests the findings of Li, Daie, Svoboda and Druckmann (2016) on CRCNS data set alm-3, which holds the sorted single units, trial tables, photostimulus types and lick and laser traces of 91 recording sessions from 19 mice performing a delayed response task with transient unilateral or bilateral photoinhibition of the anterior lateral motor cortex, with and without callosotomy. It covers unit counts and selectivity, silencing and recovery of rates and selectivity, behaviour in the recording sessions, and the population analyses along the coding direction. All files
