  • uni-and-landscape-placements

    The platform placed each party on five uni-dimensions, using 18 of the 39 statements, and on a two-dimensional socio-economic and socio-cultural landscape from 0 to 100, using 16 statements; the placements (Figs. 2 and 3) conform to the anticipated placements of Slovak parties.

    Out of the 39 statements, 18 were assigned to the five uni-dimensions ... Of the 39 statements, 16 influenced this two-dimensional political landscape

    Methods, Volebný Kompas implementation and usage, paragraphs 3 and 4; Figs. 2 and 3; Technical Validation, Content validation, last paragraph

    How it will be judged

    The dimensions and polarities of each statement come from Supplementary Table 1, which was not in the text read; the author takes them from it or from the data dictionary. Party positions on 0 to 100 as in the Methods (Completely disagree 0 to Completely agree 100). The scoring is said to follow the euandi method (ref. 30) and is not given in full. Readings for a dimension score (a) the mean over its statements of the position for polarity +1 and 100 minus the position for polarity -1; (b) for the landscape, a start at 50 moved by each statement's polarity-adjusted deviation from 50, averaged over the axis's statements. How "No Opinion" party positions enter is not stated, readings (i) left out and (ii) scored as 50, as the cleaning does for users. Reproduced when the counts of assigned statements are 18 and 16 and, under one reading, the order of the parties on each uni-dimension and each landscape axis matches Figs. 2 and 3 of the published paper. The figures were not readable here, so the expected placements are read from the published figures by the author.
