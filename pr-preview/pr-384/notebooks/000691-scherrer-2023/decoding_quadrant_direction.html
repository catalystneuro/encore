fig2-quadrant-direction-decoding¶

Linear SVM decoders trained on half of the data decode the mouse's combined quadrant and movement direction on the other half at an accuracy at least three times chance, above that of decoders trained on shuffled data.

Criterion: eight classes (four quadrants by two directions), lambda 3.2e-7, the average matrix of 200 fits. The null is 1,000 decoders on their own shuffles (each footprint's activity circularly shifted by its own offset); p is the fraction of null median accuracies at or above the real one. Null readings: (n1) one fit per shuffle, (n2) the average matrix of 200 fits per shuffle; when they disagree on p < 0.001 the claim is methods_underspecified. Accuracy readings: (a) the median of the diagonal at least 0.375, (b) every diagonal element at least 0.375. Reproduced when p < 0.001 and the accuracy holds under a reading; partially reproduced when p < 0.001 and the median exceeds 1/8 but neither reading holds.

fig2k-decoder-size¶

Decoding accuracy depends on the number of footprints used, with P at or below 2.7 x 10^-4 (Mann-Whitney U) for every decoder size from 2 to 2,048 footprints.

Criterion: for each size 2^1 to 2^11, 64 random subsets; for each, the average matrix of 100 fits and the same subset shuffled, again 100 fits; Mann-Whitney U under two readings. It depends on fig2-quadrant-direction-decoding. Its cost is measured here: one fit at each size, timed.

fig2l-local-decoding-map¶

Decoders built from the 32 footprints closest to a point decode quadrant and direction with accuracies that differ substantially across the field of view.

Criterion: map points every 5 image pixels over rows 1 to 608 and columns 140 to 700; a point is used when at least 32 footprints lie within 270 um (45 pixels at 5.94 um, or 50 at 5.35); at each, the average matrix of 10 fits on its 32 nearest footprints; the null is 1,000 decoders on shuffled data from 32 random footprints (one fit or 10 each); Benjamini-Hochberg 5%. It depends on fig2-quadrant-direction-decoding. Its cost is measured here.

How the analysis differs from the paper's. All 2948 footprints are used where the claims name the 2,382 curated ones, which the dandiset does not mark; position is tracked from the behaviour video with the code's rule; MATLAB's linear SVMs are replaced by scikit-learn's SGD (10 passes, for more than 100 predictors) and LinearSVC (exact, for 100 or fewer), as MATLAB's defaults choose. Only null reading (n1) is run in full; (n2) and the analyses that depend on these claims are costed, not run (more n2 decoders and reduced runs are in the added notebooks). The first attempt (-01) did not test fig2-quadrant-direction-decoding: its claims file had dropped the claim, which the full text states.
