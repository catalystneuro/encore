Iram et al. 2024 · Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences

SRF transcriptionally regulates the oligodendrocyte cytoskeleton during CNS myelination

doi:10.1073/pnas.2307250121 · paper key iram-2024

Datasets

  • DANDI:001350

Mostly untested: the hypomyelination of SRF knockout mice could not be checked, because the paper's hand scoring is not deposited and the masks cover one of five control mice. The sequencing findings largely held. SRF binds CArG-rich sites near promoters, Srf and Actb fall in knockout cells, myelin genes are not SRF targets, and the knockout changes 156 and 263 genes in progenitors and oligodendrocytes, matching the paper's counts. The number of high-confidence peaks and of shared targets held only in part, as the paper does not say how peaks and motif matches were chosen. Optic nerve, staining, culture and single-nucleus findings could not be tested, because the data are missing or need an R pipeline that was unavailable.
