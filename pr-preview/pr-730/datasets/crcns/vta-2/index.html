Dataset unversioned files (the archive keeps no versions) · tested 2026-11-11 · 6 open findings

  • Data missingThe data set holds only the spike times and behaviour of the 29 tagged dopamine cells. The paper's microdialysis (26 sessions, 12 rats), dLight photometry (10 sessions), in vitro dLight imaging, CTb histology and the untagged VTA-l units recorded with the tagged cells (including the 38 non-dopamine cells) are not in it, though the paper says all data will be available through CRCNS. 20 of the 37 claims cannot be tested.
    Found by the attempt on Mohebi et al. 2019 (mohebi-2019-01, 2026-11-11)
  • Metadata errorThe data description says the 29 cells were recorded from 4 rats; they come from 5 (IM-657 1, IM-1002 3, IM-1003 15, IM-1037 8, IM-1078 2), the last being the rat with NAc core virus.
    Found by the attempt on Mohebi et al. 2019 (mohebi-2019-01, 2026-11-11)
  • File format errorThe CNIlatency field ("latency to center-nose-in", ms) is stored as 65535 for every latency above 65.535 s (120 trials in 19 of 21 sessions), the largest 16-bit integer; and in 4 sessions one value differs from Center-In minus Light-On by more than 5 ms (in IM-657_160613 the last trial repeats the previous trial's value). ReactionTime also differs from Center-Out minus Go cue by up to 273 ms in 3 sessions.
    Found by the attempt on Mohebi et al. 2019 (mohebi-2019-01, 2026-11-11)
  • File format errorSpike times are not in ascending order in at least one segment of 11 of the 29 units (inversions of at most 0.7 ms, for example 2399.4923 s after 2399.4926 s in the RL segment of IM657160613t11a).
    Found by the attempt on Mohebi et al. 2019 (mohebi-2019-01, 2026-11-11)
  • Description missingSeveral fields are not described. The FR field ("session-wide average firing rate") matches none of the rates computable from the segments (it differs from the bandit-task rate by up to 1.95 Hz). The seven rows of the bandit event table are named only in Fig5_replicate.m, and the two rows of the Pavlovian one not at all. The Pavlovian portStatus window (Win, -2 to 10 s) does not say what it is aligned to, nor its 30 kHz sampling. meanWave and meanLightEvoked have no units or sampling rate.
    Found by the attempt on Mohebi et al. 2019 (mohebi-2019-01, 2026-11-11)
  • OtherAgainst the Methods' identification criteria: the deposited light-evoked and session-wide mean waveforms correlate below 0.9 for 4 of the 27 VTA-l cells on the largest wire (9 with the four wires), where the Methods say all light-evoked units exceeded 0.9; units IM657160613t11a and IM1002170114t05b received no 5 or 10 ms pulses, where the Methods require p < 0.001 for 5 and 10 ms trains; and IM1003170321t01c is not significant by SALT with its 5 and 10 ms trains (p = 0.087 and 0.05), though it is with 1 ms trains.
    Found by the attempt on Mohebi et al. 2019 (mohebi-2019-01, 2026-11-11)
