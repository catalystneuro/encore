Dataset version 0.230602.1307 · tested 2026-11-04 · 9 open findings

  • Data missingThe single-plane time-averaged 2D images and their segmentations, which the Methods use to validate the 3D segmentation (at least 70% overlap for PVS and vessel), are not in the dandiset, so the validation cannot be checked.
    Found by the attempt on Raicevic et al. 2023 (raicevic-2023-01, 2026-11-04)
  • OtherThe deposited vessel and PVS masks overlap in 11 of the 14 files (from 24 voxels at C to 344565 at E and 177417 at J), though the Methods say overlapping pixels were removed from one segmentation. The dandiset does not say which mask should take them.
    Found by the attempt on Raicevic et al. 2023 (raicevic-2023-01, 2026-11-04)
  • OtherAt E and F the PVS mask fills every plane below a depth (87 and 58 planes, 98 to 99% of the field) and 50 to 60% of the whole volume, so it holds far more than the space beside the vessel. The dandiset does not describe this; this attempt clears those planes.
    Found by the attempt on Raicevic et al. 2023 (raicevic-2023-01, 2026-11-04)
  • OtherThe vessel mask of E holds a network of branching vessels in one connected region, though the Methods say the vessels branching off the main vessel were removed by hand. The longest path through its skeleton runs through branches; after the cleaning of this attempt 361 of its 939 points (0.648 um) are kept, along a side branch, while the points removed run along the vessel with PVS beside it.
    Found by the attempt on Raicevic et al. 2023 (raicevic-2023-01, 2026-11-04)
  • Metadata errorFor L, M and N the reference_frame gives a depth step of 0.648 um, while the session descriptions say the planes are 1 micron apart. Vessel cross sections at the stated step are close to round (median height over width 0.91, 1.08 and 1.13), which is consistent with the reference_frame; the session descriptions appear to be wrong for these files.
    Found by the attempt on Raicevic et al. 2023 (raicevic-2023-01, 2026-11-04)
  • Metadata errorThe vessel channel (ChanA, described as FITC-dextran) has an emission wavelength of 698 nm and the PVS channel (ChanC) 525 nm, while the Methods say the green 525 nm channel holds the FITC-dextran. Every imaging plane names GFP as its indicator.
    Found by the attempt on Raicevic et al. 2023 (raicevic-2023-01, 2026-11-04)
  • Description missingChanB is described as injected fluorescent microspheres and its mask only as 'Segmentation masks for ChanB'; the paper mentions no microspheres, and L, M and N have no ChanB.
    Found by the attempt on Raicevic et al. 2023 (raicevic-2023-01, 2026-11-04)
  • Metadata errorThe subjects of locations A to D (BPN-OLD-M2 and BPN-OLD-M3) are 573 to 603 days old in the files, and most other subjects 76 to 113 days, while the Methods say all mice were 8 to 12 weeks old.
    Found by the attempt on Raicevic et al. 2023 (raicevic-2023-01, 2026-11-04)
  • OtherThe first published version, 0.230427.1809, holds no PVS mask for L, M and N; version 0.230602.1307 adds them. The dandiset's description does not record the change.
    Found by the attempt on Raicevic et al. 2023 (raicevic-2023-01, 2026-11-04)
