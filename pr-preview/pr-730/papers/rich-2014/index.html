Most of the paper's main findings held in this data. Field counts per cell were overdispersed and fit the gamma-Poisson model of cell-specific propensities, only 64.0% of cells were recruited on the 48 m track, field density was uniform, recruitment was memoryless, and propensity tracked firing rates in prior sleep. The simulations of single-field cells and active subsets also held, the latter on an assumed spacing of environment sizes. The match of the recruitment curve to the model's prediction held only in part, under an assumed definition of a pass through a field. Several supplementary controls did not reproduce, among them no effect of track angle, no remapping at section boundaries and uncorrelated field locations across cells.

Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • Fields are recomputed from spikes and position by the Methods (field set F-a); the paper's own detection code is not public. They number 852 (P-a) and 793 (P-b), against 887 in the data set's fieldInfoC, and a recomputed field overlaps 842 (P-a) and 787 (P-b) of the data set's fields. The 45 data set fields that P-a misses fail its pass rule (29) or its 15 cm width (16); 1 inch bins, as the data set's maps use, give the same count (852 fields, 840 overlapped).fig1-recruitment-by-section
  • Under P-a an edge of a field within 20 cm of either end of the positions visited in its novel section counts as reached when the run gets within 20 cm of that end, a standard choice (two smoothing SDs). A run cannot cross beyond the end of a section, so without it P-a dropped every field at a section end (791 fields, none at an end, against 852 with 72 at an end).fig1-recruitment-by-section
  • Anderson-Darling p-values come from Monte Carlo null distributions (20000 samples per test) of the A2 statistic, a standard choice; field locations are clipped to 0 to 48 m (the track starts at -0.25 to -0.10 m in the data).fig2-individual-poisson
  • Each surrogate's interval deviation is normalized by its own mean interval, as the observed one is, the computation the Methods describe for each cell.fig2bc-population-bias
  • Assumed reading P-a of a pass (a run in one direction that crosses the whole field), as the readings P-a and P-b give different outcomes. The Methods say "passes through the field" (Materials and Methods, Place field determination).
