Pakhomov et al. 2023 · Bioelectrochemistry

Next generation CANCAN focusing for remote stimulation by nanosecond electric pulses

Andrei G. Pakhomov, Emily Gudvangen, Uma M. Mangalanathan, Oleg Kondratiev, Luis M. S. Redondo, Iurii Semenov

Summary: Few of the paper's findings on CANCAN focusing held up fully in this data. Lifting the electrodes 2 mm above the cells raised targeting of the array's centre as reported, and more packets or a higher amplitude raised uptake, though amplitude by about 6 to 7 times where the paper gives about 16, and uptake was not restricted to the centre. Packet 2 did not give the best targeting; the dishes labelled Packet 3 did. Whether rotation creates a central peak and whether targeting is independent of amplitude and array size could not be settled, as the result depends on an ROI layout the paper does not describe. The single-cell cancellation experiments and field simulations are in no dataset.

Summary written by the agent that recorded the attempt, from its claims and the agents’ reports. A failure to reproduce may be a failure of the agent.
