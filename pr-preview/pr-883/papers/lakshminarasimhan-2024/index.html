Summary: None of the paper's working memory findings held up in this data: ALM activity did not predict the mouse's choice on held-out trials (test R2 at most -0.247 against 0.97) or explain thalamic activity, and the top five cortical PCs did not capture most of the thalamic variance. The same fits come close to the paper's values on the training trials, so the paper's numbers may be in-sample, a question put to its authors. The leading cortical PCs drove the choice readout under only one of two readouts. The motor control findings could not be tested, as those recordings are not deposited. The model simulations of Figures 1 to 5 were too large to run here.

Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • The smoothing grid (10, 25, 50, 100 and 200 ms), the 10 ms bin and the padding before smoothing are standard choices; the paper gives none of them (claims W3, W4).fig6-wm-choice-r2
  • The delay window is a fixed 1.3 s from delay onset (pole_out_time); the go cue follows delay onset by 1.26 to 1.30 s in nearly every trial and by up to 4.7 s in a few.fig6-wm-choice-r2
  • Each unit is averaged over all selected trials (U1), as the files give no interval over which each unit was recorded; U2 is shown as a variant and gives the same outcome.fig6-wm-choice-r2
  • The criterion's readouts R1 and R2 give different results. Both are required, as the criterion lists them together and lets only the trial reading vary; (a) and (b) hold under R2, only (b) under R1 for T1. The chance levels of the alignments are diagnostics, not part of the criterion.fig6c-wm-leading-pcs-drive-choice
  • Thalamic units and ALM units come from different sessions, so each thalamic unit's trial averages (split 80/10/10 within its own session) are paired with the ALM averages of the corresponding sets of the ALM sessions. The paper does not say how its training and test sets were paired across sessions.figs6d-wm-thalamic-r2
  • Sigma is chosen per thalamic unit on the validation R2 of the regression itself (Methods, "optimized the smoothing parameter by cross-validation"); the grid is a standard choice.figs6d-wm-thalamic-r2
  • Each normalization is applied to the unit's mean R2(k) curve over splits and subsamples, a choice the paper does not state.fig6d-wm-top5-pcs
  • The main reading uses P1, as the Figure S6 legend says the ALM population was subsampled "in all analysis", and T1, which claims W2 lists first; all 16-PC readings give the same outcome under the claim seed.figs6f-ct-alignment-not-significant
