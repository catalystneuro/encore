Dataset version 0.251007.0553 · tested 2026-11-03 · 5 open findings

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds only the 94 imaging sessions. The muscimol experiment (three further mice, muscimol and PBS sessions, Fig. 1d-g and Supplementary Fig. 2) and the sound-mapping trials recorded before each imaging session (Supplementary Fig. 4) are not in it, and the paper's data page names no other deposit. Six claims cannot be tested.
    Found by the attempt on Funamizu et al. 2016 (funamizu-2016-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Metadata errorThe description says the files hold "sound on/off indicators", and sound_on_off is described as "any speaker on". In every file sound_on_off and speaker_on_matrix are on only in the first frame of each trial (150 frames), and every speaker_presentations row lasts one frame at a trial start (6 to 26 rows per trial start, one per speaker listed). The task sound of the continuous and intermittent conditions is not recorded, so sound and no-sound zones must be computed from the condition and distance.
    Found by the attempt on Funamizu et al. 2016 (funamizu-2016-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Description missingLicking is stored as lick_rate, "Lick frequency (0-10 Hz)", a held 1/inter-lick-interval value capped at 10 Hz, without lick times or a description of how it was derived. Lick counts and first licks can only be approximated from it.
    Found by the attempt on Funamizu et al. 2016 (funamizu-2016-01, 2026-11-03)
  • OtherEach trial record runs from one start tone to the next, so it holds the reward and the inter-trial interval; the goal time and the first reward edge are not stored per trial and must be derived from the position and reward series. The mean time from the start tone to the reward is 9.30 s (s.e.m. 0.11 s over sessions), against 7.4 +- 0.1 s in the Methods, and 0.48% of trials take over 20 s, against 2.71%.
    Found by the attempt on Funamizu et al. 2016 (funamizu-2016-01, 2026-11-03)
  • OtherThe files hold exactly the paper's counts (94 sessions, 8 mice, sessions per area and layer, 41,031 ROIs per area and layer equal to the active-neuron counts) and dF_over_F equals (R - mean R)/mean R of RawFluorescence to 3e-5, as the Methods define dR/R. No recording is deposited twice (94 distinct trial sequences).
    Found by the attempt on Funamizu et al. 2016 (funamizu-2016-01, 2026-11-03)
