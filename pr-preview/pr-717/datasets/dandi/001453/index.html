Dataset version 0.250518.1950 · tested 2026-10-14 · 9 open findings

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds only the freely swimming tracking of Figure 1 and Figure S1. The two-photon imaging and the head-fixed tail tracking behind Figures 2 to 4 are not in it, and the GitLab repository the paper names for all behavioural and imaging data holds analysis notebooks only. Nine of the paper's main findings cannot be tested.
    Found by the attempt on Fouke et al. 2025 (fouke-2025-01, 2026-10-14)
  • Description missingThe stimulus codes 0 to 20 have no names or description in the files or the dandiset metadata. They can be named only from the paper's analysis notebook and the protocol file of its stimulus software.
    Found by the attempt on Fouke et al. 2025 (fouke-2025-01, 2026-10-14)
  • Metadata errorSubject ids 0 to 11 are each used for one zebrafish and one Danionella cerebrum, so subject_id does not identify an animal. The archive's summary counts 25 subjects and lists only Danio rerio; the files hold 37 fish of two species.
    Found by the attempt on Fouke et al. 2025 (fouke-2025-01, 2026-10-14)
  • Metadata errorf0_vtheta is stated in radians/s but holds the heading change per frame in radians, and f0_vx and f0_vy are stated in pixels/s but are per frame. In most Danionella files the sum of f0_vtheta over a trial does not match the change of f0_theta (median ratios 0.09 to 0.89).
    Found by the attempt on Fouke et al. 2025 (fouke-2025-01, 2026-10-14)
  • Other18 of 25 zebrafish files and 1 of 12 Danionella files are sampled at 32 to 35 Hz, where the Methods state tracking at more than 150 Hz. Their timestamp intervals are regular (28.8 to 30.8 ms) and their trials last the protocol's 35 s, so these are the acquisition rates. Some measures differ with frame rate, such as the zebrafish static speed (8.31 against 4.50 mm/s).
    Found by the attempt on Fouke et al. 2025 (fouke-2025-01, 2026-10-14)
  • Description missingNo spatial scale is given; positions are in pixels. They are consistent with the 10 pixels per mm in the paper's code, except one Danionella file (sub-5) whose positions reach 1490 pixels against about 1120 in the others.
    Found by the attempt on Fouke et al. 2025 (fouke-2025-01, 2026-10-14)
  • OtherZebrafish and Danionella files were exported differently. Zebrafish files keep untracked frames and frames between trials; Danionella files hold only tracked trial frames and add a `dist` series. The paper's code filters Danionella frames on a `vel` column that is not in the files.
    Found by the attempt on Fouke et al. 2025 (fouke-2025-01, 2026-10-14)
  • OtherThe `dist` series of the Danionella files does not agree with the positions; over the first 35 s of a trial its sum is a median 1.11 times the path length of the smoothed positions, with a 5th to 95th percentile of 0.31 to 3.79. The paper's speed check uses it, though computing the check from positions passes the same trials.
    Found by the attempt on Fouke et al. 2025 (fouke-2025-01, 2026-10-14)
  • Selection not documentedThe dandiset holds the 25 zebrafish and 12 Danionella the code keeps after exclusion (its comments give 28 and 40 recorded). The dandiset does not say that the excluded fish were left out or why.
    Found by the attempt on Fouke et al. 2025 (fouke-2025-01, 2026-10-14)
