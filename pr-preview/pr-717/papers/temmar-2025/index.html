  • figs2-throughput-improves

    The monkey's real-time task performance, measured as average Fitts' law throughput per day, appears to improve gradually over the dataset.

    In S2, we show the average throughput across all trials for each day, and noted that Monkey N’s performance appears to gradually improve throughout the course of the dataset.

    Appendix A.1, paragraph 2; Figure S2

    How it will be judged

    Throughput per trial is computed "as described in [1]" (Nason et al. 2021), which is not part of this paper, and the code holds no throughput function, so its form is open. The readings named are (a) index of difficulty log2(1 + D/W) over acquisition time, with D the Euclidean distance in the 2-DOF position space from the previous targets to the current targets and W the target width of 0.15 (Appendix A.1), acquisition time being the trial length minus the 750 ms hold; (b) the same with the trial length including the hold; (c) the sum over the two finger groups of each group's log2(1 + D/W), over the time of (a). Per day, the mean over all trials of both sessions. Reproduced when, under one reading, a linear fit of daily mean throughput on date has a positive slope with p < 0.05 (two-sided linregress; the paper gives no test, so the threshold is a standard choice and not the paper's).
