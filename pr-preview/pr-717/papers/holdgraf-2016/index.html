    Results, Behavioural control study, paragraph 1; Methods, Behavioural controls for filtered speech (group 1, n = 5)

    How it will be judged

    As the Methods state, the per cent correct of sentence words for each sentence, averaged per subject, then the mean over the five group 1 subjects. Reproduced when the mean is within 0.4 of 3.5.
