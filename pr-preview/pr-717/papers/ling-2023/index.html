  • ✓1 reproduced

Most central mouse findings did not hold up in this data. ON-OFF angles exceed 90 degrees, but the paper's centring produces that by itself, and time-averaged ON and OFF responses were positively correlated (median r 0.48), as were later post-odor responses. Persistent post-odor anticorrelation and contrast enhancement appeared only with whole-trial z-scoring, which the dandiset does not describe and which can create them alone, and ON/OFF clustering failed under both normalisations. Odor decoding from the intervals held, but each odor has one block, so slow drift cannot be excluded, and the locust findings largely held. The acetophenone contrast and the PID recordings could not be tested because the data are missing.

What the attempts found about the paper itself: a method or rule it does not give, a place where it disagrees with itself, published code that does something else. What they found about the data is on each dataset’s page.

Tests the findings of Ling, Moss, Smith, Deng, Kroeger, Reimer, Raman and Arenkiel (bioRxiv 2023, v2 2024) on dandiset 001170, which holds two-photon imaging of olfactory bulb glomeruli in awake mice, seven sessions from four mice under the paper's block protocol and five random-order sessions. It compares glomerular ON and OFF responses, contrast enhancement between sequential odors, and odor classification after offset, and tests the locust findings on the recordings the paper deposited on Figshare. Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • dF/F with F0 the session median of each ROI.fig7h-iti-knn-mouse fig7j-iti-timebin-mouse
  • Sessions are matched to the paper's figures by ROI count; no identifier links them.mouse-sessions
  • dF/F with F0 from the 2 s before onset; the paper's deposited arrays are close to per-trial z-scores. The z-scored result is shown as a variant.fig2h-onoff-angle-mouse
  • Windows placed in seconds on a 16 Hz grid; the code counts frames at a nominal 16 per second.fig2h-onoff-angle-mouse
  • dF/F with F0 from the 2 s before onset, not the paper's (undocumented) normalisation.fig2f-onoff-anticorrelated-mouse
  • The mouse code for this figure is not in the Figshare record (it loads precomputed angles); the locust code's procedure (top three PCs, mean vectors) was used.fig4b-reference-odor-mouse
  • The paper shows one representative session without naming it; the 198-glomerulus session is taken as it, and all seven are reported.fig4d-clustering-mouse
  • All seven block sessions are used, as the preprint does not identify its three sessions from two mice; the three the published version names are shown as a variant.fig6e-contrast-msc
  • dF/F with F0 from the 2 s before the pair's first onset.fig6e-contrast-msc
  • Correlation of the ON vector with 5-s bins, a summary of the paper's frame-by-frame matrix.fig7d-persistent-anticorrelation
  • dF/F with F0 from the 2 s before onset.fig7d-persistent-anticorrelation
  • k = 20 from the legend; the Methods' k = 50 exceeds the 39 other vectors.fig7h-iti-knn-mouse
  • The shuffled-label control is a permutation of whole-trial labels with the neighbours kept.fig7j-iti-timebin-mouse
  • The 318-glomerulus session has only three complete IBIs (54 bins), so k = 50 was run on the four sessions that have four.figs7e-ibi-chance
  • The paper's random-order session (286 glomeruli, mouse 129 in the Figshare code) is not in the dandiset; five sessions with the same paradigm, four of them from mice not in the block sessions, were used.figs8e-random-order-decline
  • In three of these sessions odors have different concentration values; the first 10 presentations of each odor were used regardless.figs8e-random-order-decline
  • Data from the Figshare record of the published version; the preprint's data may differ.fig1g-onoff-angle-locust
  • The paper's ~40% is read from its text; the last 5 s of the ITI was taken as the end.fig7i-iti-timebin-locust
