Most central findings held in this data: head-direction tuning stayed stable across weeks, offsets between cell pairs were preserved, and drift rates matched the paper. Remapping between environments held in part: the offsets and an environment classifier reproduced, but coherent remapping did not (10 of 15 mouse and environment pairs against the 80% sought). That result depends on how head-direction cells are defined, which the paper does not fully state. The probe-week memory findings could not be tested because no table links cells across the two protocols, and decoding across days exceeded the runner's time limit. Single-visit memory held in part, and the per-session proportions of head-direction cells did not reproduce.

Notes on the paper

What the attempts found about the paper itself: a method or rule it does not give, a place where it disagrees with itself, published code that does something else. What they found about the data is on each dataset’s page.

  • Methods not availableQuestions for the authors on the HD cell rule. Is the HDI computed on the raw or the smoothed tuning curve, and is the kernel 12 degrees or 12 bins? Smoothed HDI with a 12 degree kernel reproduces the paper's session and pair counts here, but raw HDI gives about twice as many HD cells, the outcomes of edf2ab-jaccard, fig2h-coherent-remapping and edf4c-previous-session change with it, and part of fig1f-example-stable (no mouse with 18 sessions matches) and of edf4ij-pv-decay (mouse 5's mean at the longest interval is 0 or below) fails with it. Is "detected in at least six sessions" registered by CellReg or HD in those sessions, and which sessions enter the average HDI? With either HDI the proportions of Extended Data Fig. 1e are not reproduced (main reading 0.12, 0.40, 0.04, 0.29, 0.07).
  • Methods not availableQuestions for the authors on pairwise offsets and drift rates. Are angular offsets unsigned (0 to 180 degrees)? The paper's shuffle medians (51 to 57) match unsigned offsets (expected 52.7), while signed offsets give 90; unsigned was assumed. How is the drift rate per week computed? A least-squares slope against day difference was assumed; a line through the origin gives two to three times larger rates. What is the sign of a change in preferred direction? Taken as the earlier session minus the later one, the paper's signs of Fig. 1g and of Extended Data Fig. 10b are both matched (5 of 5 each), and Extended Data Fig. 10b,c are reproduced; with the opposite sign they are not.
  • Methods not availableQuestions for the authors on the decoders. What are the XGBoost hyperparameters, how is dF/F normalized, how is population activity summed for the top 50% bins, do cells have to be HD cells in the test session, and is the error variance of Extended Data Fig. 8c circular or linear? Library defaults, division by the maximum, the sum, HD in both sessions and both variances were used; the circular variance matches the paper's values and the linear one does not. Requiring test cells only to be registered in the test session gives the same outcomes.
