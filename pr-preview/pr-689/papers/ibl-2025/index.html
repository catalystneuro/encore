Ibl et al. 2025 · Nature

A brain-wide map of neural activity during complex behaviour

doi:10.1038/s41586-025-09235-0 · paper key ibl-2025

Datasets

  • DANDI:000409

Dataset counts, task behaviour and the pattern of stimulus, choice and feedback decoding held up, with decoding tested in a subset of regions and feedback only partly. The visual latency ordering did not reproduce, and 46 regions showed significant stimulus trajectories against the paper's 104, with the cause not found. The licking-related feedback oscillation peaked at a lower frequency than reported. Single-cell selectivity could not be judged because the two tests are not independent, so the paper's stated false-positive rate was far below the null rate measured here. Receptive-field mapping is missing from the dataset, and four whole-brain analyses were not attempted.
