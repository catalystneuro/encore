Dataset unversioned files (the archive keeps no versions) · tested 2026-11-07 · 9 open findings

  • Data missingThe data set holds only the electrophysiology of Figures 2 and 3 and Extended Data Figures 1, 2 and 5. The photoinhibition and photoactivation behaviour (Fig. 1, Fig. 5, ED Figs 3 and 6), the calcium imaging (Fig. 4), the anatomy (ED Fig. 3) and the recordings with somatic PT photoactivation (ED Fig. 7) are not in it, and the paper names no other deposit. Eleven of the paper's findings cannot be tested.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2015 (li-2015-02, 2026-11-07)
  • Description missingPhotostimulationType takes the values 3, 4 and 5 in 495 trials, all in the two Tlx_PL56-Cre x Ai32 mice ANM218453 and ANM218457 (eight 1 ms pulses at about 23 mW starting 1.8, 2.3 or 2.8 s into the trial), but the data description lists only 0, 1, 2 and NaN. The data set's own compile script treats 1 to 5 as tagging trials.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2015 (li-2015-02, 2026-11-07)
  • Description missingThe NaN-type trials ("photostimulation configurations for testing purposes") carry light in 3,756 trials of all 19 mice, in configurations the description does not give. In three Sim1_KJ18-Cre mice (ANM210861 to ANM210863) they are 50 ms pulses at about 47 mW starting 0.70 to 4.98 s into the trial (session medians 1.9 to 3.2 s). In the other mice without Ai32 they fall mostly outside performing trials, as 50 ms pulses mostly at about 23 mW (session median onset 0.70 s) or 1 ms pulses at about 47 mW (session median onset 0.97 s). In the three Ai32 mice they are eight 1 ms pulses at about 23 mW starting 0.49 to 2.8 s into the trial, 135 of them in the sample epoch of the Sim1 x Ai32 mouse. Which of these, if any, are the ED Fig. 7 PT photostimulation sessions cannot be told.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2015 (li-2015-02, 2026-11-07)
  • Metadata errorIn session ANM210862_20130627, 43 trials have PhotostimulationType 0 ("non-stimulation trials") but their AOM and laser power traces carry 8 to 22 light pulses of 50 ms at 47.1 mW, starting 1.9 to 4.35 s into the trial (median 3.2 s).
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2015 (li-2015-02, 2026-11-07)
  • Description missingOnly neurons that passed the collision test are labelled (45 PT, 27 IT). The 22 pyramidal-tract-activated and 106 intratelencephalic-activated neurons and the 7 untestable ones are not identified, so their counts, latencies and jitter cannot be checked.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2015 (li-2015-02, 2026-11-07)
  • OtherThe trough-to-peak widths of the deposited mean waveforms put 34 of the 39 unlabelled units below 0.35 ms (158 in all), where the paper and the data description call all 39 intermediate (0.35 to 0.45 ms). The labels split the deposited widths cleanly (FS 0.154 to 0.256 ms, unlabelled 0.307 or 0.410 ms, pyramidal 0.461 to 0.666 ms), so the FS boundary the labels use lies between 0.256 and 0.307 ms, not at the stated 0.35 ms. The cellType labels match the paper's counts; the stated thresholds do not reproduce them from the deposited waveforms.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2015 (li-2015-02, 2026-11-07)
  • File format error10 units have spike times out of order (7 in session ANM214429_20130806, and ANM219030_20130829 unit 12, ANM219030_20130831 unit 5, ANM219247_20130920 unit 5).
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2015 (li-2015-02, 2026-11-07)
  • Description missingThe lick trace is described only as "licking movement from photodiode"; its baseline (0.07 to 4.83 V per session), scale and sign of excursion differ between sessions, and no detection rule or calibration is given, so the paper's reaction time cannot be recomputed reliably.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2015 (li-2015-02, 2026-11-07)
  • OtherIn 3,707 of 28,465 performing trials the sample or delay epoch differs from 1.3 s by more than 50 ms (up to 14 s), which the data description does not mention. The NWB copies of the sessions omit the photostimulation type, the AOM and laser power traces and GoodTrials, which the analysis needs, so only the MATLAB files support it.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2015 (li-2015-02, 2026-11-07)
