Dataset version 0.261004.1511 · tested 2026-10-31 · 6 open findings

  • Description missingThe dandiset holds no ROI positions, glomerulus identities or responding-ROI counts. The six focal glomeruli (PGb, PGa, G1 to G4) and the responding ROIs of Figures 2E and S3C were drawn by hand and cannot be located from the dandiset alone; twelve claims depend on them (eight list the link claim as a dependency, pg-opposite-modulation depends on it through others, and three rest on the responding ROIs). Data S2 of the paper holds the traces of the focal glomeruli but not their positions.
    Found by the attempt on Hart et al. 2024 (hart-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Description missingStimulus timing is not stored as times or intervals, only in the series description ("odor stimulus triggered at 3s with duration 5s"). The traces are consistent with it; the response first exceeds baseline at frame 6 and peaks at frame 12, and paraffin oil trials show no response.
    Found by the attempt on Hart et al. 2024 (hart-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Metadata errorThe timing metadata disagree. The series description gives 40 cycles in 48.0 s (1.2 s per volume, as 33 planes at 27.5 frames per second take), the series rate is 1.2097 Hz (0.83 s per volume, the Methods' value), and the imaging plane's imaging_rate is 0.83, a period stored as a rate.
    Found by the attempt on Hart et al. 2024 (hart-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Metadata errorOne trial (ant1-m2-d24-y2023, trial 135) spells ethylpyrazine "ethylyprazine" in its 33 file names; its place in the odor sequence shows it is ethylpyrazine.
    Found by the attempt on Hart et al. 2024 (hart-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • OtherThe behaviour data of Figure 1 and S1 are only in the paper's supplement (Data S1), the confocal stacks of Figure 2A and 2B only in the Brain Image Library (doi:10.35077/g.1153), and the 90-day ants of Figure 3C and 3D in DANDI 000467. The Brain Image Library download server could not be reached during this attempt. In Data S2 the trailing number of a trial name is the repeat for most ants but the recording's trial number for the 90-day PGb traces; one of those 96 numbers does not match the 000467 trial of that odor and repeat.
    Found by the attempt on Hart et al. 2024 (hart-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • OtherRaw fluorescence is stored as uncompressed float64 (8 bytes per value), 1.28 TB for 21 ants; each file is one plane of one trial (15,246 files). Reading needs only range requests, but the dataset is large for what it holds.
    Found by the attempt on Hart et al. 2024 (hart-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
