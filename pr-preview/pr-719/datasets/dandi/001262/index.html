Dataset version 0.241205.0959 · tested 2026-11-01 · 7 open findings

  • File format errorThe ABR threshold in general/notes ('threshold (db SPL) ...') is written as a single character whose code point is the threshold, for example '#' for 35 and 'K' for 75, and control characters such as a line feed for 10; 11 animals have 'NaN'. Read as code points, the values give Table 1's means and SDs exactly, so the number appears to have been converted to a character in the conversion to NWB.
    Found by the attempt on Heeringa et al. 2024 (heeringa-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Data missingThe analysed outcomes the paper describes as stored with each recording (exp.data.[type].analysis) are in the NWB files only as best frequency, spontaneous rate and threshold per recording type. The characteristic frequency, Q10, vector strength with its p-value and levels, the click latencies and first-spike statistics, and the rate-level and frequency-response rates are not there. Most can be recomputed from the spike times with the paper's code, but the manual corrections the code allows (CF and response criterion, RLF threshold) cannot.
    Found by the attempt on Heeringa et al. 2024 (heeringa-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Description missingThe CLICK trials carry no delay (NaN in the trial table), so the click's onset within the trial, needed for click latencies, is not in the files. The SR, NOISE, SPS and CVC trials also have no delay. The click_polarity column holds 'COND' in every CLICK trial.
    Found by the attempt on Heeringa et al. 2024 (heeringa-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Other370 of 1160 units have two or more within-trial inter-spike intervals below 0.6 ms in one recording (391 pooled over recordings, 148 in the BF recording alone), although the paper says units with multiple such intervals were excluded. Most short intervals are 0.3 to 0.6 ms. About half of the 13,904 short intervals lie on one spike waveform in the voltage trace (all 1171 below 0.1 ms, 91% of those from 0.1 to 0.3 ms, 39% of those from 0.3 to 0.6 ms) by a dip-ratio rule. The intervals below 0.1 ms are double detections of single spikes; for those from 0.3 to 0.6 ms this is a plausible reading, as the rule was checked only against intervals of 0.8 to 1.5 ms. Counting only intervals between separate waveforms, 176 units still have two or more in one recording.
    Found by the attempt on Heeringa et al. 2024 (heeringa-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Other275 units have a CLICK recording, one each, and 271 give a 2-bin latency with the paper's function, where the Fig. 7 legend counts 261 units with a CLICK recording. Without the stored latencies the difference cannot be traced.
    Found by the attempt on Heeringa et al. 2024 (heeringa-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Metadata errorThe recordings are extracellular single-unit recordings with a glass electrode (as the file's own device description says), but they are stored as intracellular (icephys) VoltageClampStimulusSeries, and the archive's summary lists the measurement technique as 'voltage clamp technique'.
    Found by the attempt on Heeringa et al. 2024 (heeringa-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Data missingThe dataset holds only the 104 animals with recorded units. The animals operated on without a successful recording, which the paper's success rates (43% of old and 88% of young-adult gerbils) count, are not listed in the files or the metadata.
    Found by the attempt on Heeringa et al. 2024 (heeringa-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
