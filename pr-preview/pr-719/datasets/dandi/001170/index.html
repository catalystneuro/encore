Dataset version 0.260528.1016 · tested 2026-10-18 · 9 open findings

  • Description missingThe fluorescence traces are raw F (unit "n.a.") with no description of how they were extracted or of the normalisation the paper applied. The paper's deposited arrays are close to F z-scored within each 57-s trial window. With that normalisation the contrast enhancement and negative correlations between the ON response and activity from 5 s after offset appear in these data, and they do not with dF/F relative to the 2 s before each odor or with F z-scored on those 2 s. The time-averaged ON and OFF vectors are not anticorrelated with any of these (median r 0.068 with the whole-window z-score, 12 of 28 below 0). On synthetic glomeruli with excitation only, the whole-window z-score gives such anticorrelations by itself.
    Found by the attempt on Ling et al. 2023 (ling-2023-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Description missingThe trials table has one row per second of valve opening, without a description; odor presentations, blocks and sequential pairs have to be rebuilt from timing. Five block sessions fill the gaps with "Empty" rows and two do not. The concentration column has no unit, gives 0.0 to two odorants (2-ethylphenol, anisaldehyde) in three random-order sessions, and 0.1 to the Empty rows of sub-141 ses-5.
    Found by the attempt on Ling et al. 2023 (ling-2023-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Data missingIn sub-134 ses-22, sub-141 ses-5 and sub-141 ses-8 the raw TwoPhotonSeries holds half the frames of the fluorescence traces (91000 against 182000 in sub-134 ses-22) at twice the frame interval; the conversion notes give 182000 frames for sub-134 ses-22.
    Found by the attempt on Ling et al. 2023 (ling-2023-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Data missingNo sequential pair ends in acetophenone (Figure 6E right, S6) and no PID recordings are included (Figure S2). The published version's Table 1 assigns the acetophenone panel to the 73-, 210-, 153- and 198-glomerulus sessions, which in the dandiset hold only pairs ending in methyl salicylate. The random-order session of Figure S8D and S8E (286 glomeruli) is not in the dandiset, and of the eight random-order sessions that Table 1 lists (175, 340, 323, 306, 286, 204, 250 and 280 glomeruli) only two (323 and 306) match a dandiset session by ROI count.
    Found by the attempt on Ling et al. 2023 (ling-2023-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Selection not documentedThe dandiset holds the paper's seven block-protocol sessions from four mice and five random-order sessions from five mice (four not in the block sessions) with odor-specific concentrations, apparently from a second study (the conference paper it also names, whose authors are the dandiset's contributors). Neither the metadata nor the description says which sessions belong to which paper, and the description is that of the second study.
    Found by the attempt on Ling et al. 2023 (ling-2023-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Identifier missingNo identifier links the paper's datasets to the dandiset's sessions. They match by ROI count (198, 219, 318), but the session names in the paper's Figshare code (for example m149_3_1, m129_1_2) do not all exist in the dandiset (it has no mouse 129 and only session 1 of mouse 149).
    Found by the attempt on Ling et al. 2023 (ling-2023-01, 2026-10-18)
  • OtherThe dandiset and the published version's Table 1 disagree on which mouse one session belongs to. The dandiset puts the 318-ROI session under sub-148 with the 286-ROI session; Table 1 puts the 318-glomerulus session under mouse 4 with the 219-glomerulus session (sub-149 here). Which is right cannot be told from the data.
    Found by the attempt on Ling et al. 2023 (ling-2023-01, 2026-10-18)
  • OtherThe random-order odorant names differ from the paper's; the files have 2-ethylphenol and anisaldehyde where the Methods list 2-ethylpyrizine (2ep) and isoamyl acetate (asa). Names are spelled differently across sessions ("Anisole" and "anisole"), and "2-methypyrazine" is misspelled.
    Found by the attempt on Ling et al. 2023 (ling-2023-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Description missingThe genotype (Thy1-GCaMP6f) and the ages are not stated, and every session starts at 00:00, so the time of day is missing. In sub-124 ses-3 the last trial ends 8.9 s after the last imaging frame.
    Found by the attempt on Ling et al. 2023 (ling-2023-01, 2026-10-18)
