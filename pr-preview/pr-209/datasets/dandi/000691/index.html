Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingStill present (finding of -01). The dandiset holds one of the paper's four mice and none of the songbird recordings. The bead and dye images of the optical tests are in the paper's code repository, but the USAF target images and ADC recordings are not public. The Academic Torrents deposit lists files of the same mouse session only.
  • Metadata errorStill present (finding of -01). The device description reads "Head-mounted microscope (Kiloscope) optimized for weight (1.0 mm FOV; 1.0 g)", and the dandiset description says the data come from the microscope optimized for weight; the data are from the large-FOV Kiloscope (4 mm window, 2948 footprints, 808 x 608 pixel frames).
  • Metadata errorStill present (finding of -01). The imaging is stored as a TwoPhotonSeries and the dandiset lists two-photon microscopy among its techniques, but the microscope is a one-photon head-mounted epifluorescence microscope.
  • Metadata errorStill present (finding of -01). The behaviour video is described as about 30 fps and its AVI header says 30 frames/s, but its 15521 timestamps run at a median 12.5 frames/s over 1234 s.
  • Description missingStill present (finding of -01). The imaging plane gives no pixel size (no grid spacing), its indicator and location are "unknown" and its excitation wavelength is NaN, although general/virus says GCaMP7f.
  • Selection not documentedStill present (finding of -01). The 2948 footprints are the EXTRACT output before curation; the paper analyses the 2,382 it kept, and nothing in the dandiset marks them or says the footprints are uncurated.
  • Data missingStill present (finding of -01). The mouse's position, which the paper's decoding and place analyses use, is not in the dandiset (its only processing module is ophys), and the opening of the behaviour video that the paper's code skips is not marked.
  • Published code disagreesStill present for the number of footprints (finding of -01). The local decoding maps are described with the 32 closest footprints within 270 um (Fig. 2l and Extended Data Fig. 9c legends, Methods); both map scripts (subregion_map_quaddir.m, subregion_map_octant.m) use 64 footprints within 50 pixels. The radius differs only at the 5.94 um per pixel of the illumination script (297 um); at the bead script's 5.35 um per pixel it is 267.5 um.
  • Paper inconsistentStill present (finding of -01), now confirmed in the version of record's full text. The Extended Data Fig. 8 legend refers to "the Kiloscope dataset shown in Figure 3" and a "Ridgeline plot from Fig. 2d"; the paper has two figures and the ridgeline plot is Fig. 2h.
  • Description missingNew. The imaging movie is described only as "Calcium imaging data at ~30 fps in grayscale format", without saying whether it is the raw video or the motion-corrected one the paper extracted footprints from, and the file holds no motion-correction module. Its values are consistent with the raw video (the histogram of its counts keeps the converter's uneven pattern, which sub-pixel resampling smooths), and its rigid shifts against a template have an RMS of 0.037 pixels, about a quarter of the 0.13 pixels the paper reports for the raw video.
  • Description missingNew. ROICentroids are described as "The x, y, (z) centroids of each ROI", but they equal the mask's peak pixel (its first pixel of weight 1) for 84% of footprints and the rounded weighted centroid for only 43%; how they were computed is not said.
  • OtherNew. The paper's tracking code unwraps linear position with MATLAB's unwrap on centimetres with a tolerance of half the loop, which corrects each lap's jump by a multiple of 2 pi cm; as the loop (147.1 cm) is not such a multiple, the position gains an error of up to pi cm at every lap. With it, about 1% of the running and direction labels differ from those of a position unwrapped by the loop length.
  • OtherNew. The paper's bead scripts (feather_analysis_secondorder.m, wf_analysis_secondorder.m) order the images of each stack by file date, which the repository does not keep, so the order that the region lists' slice numbers refer to is not recorded. The Featherscope's region labels name their image and confirm the file-number order; the Kiloscope's labels do not.

Reproduction Imaging thousands of neurons in a freely moving mouse with a large-FOV head-mounted microscope (Scherrer et al. 2023)

Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • The count is of the EXTRACT output before curation, as the claim states; the curated 2,382 are not marked in the dandiset.abstract-thousands-of-neurons
  • The pixel size comes from the paper's code, not from the dandiset, as the criterion allows.abstract-kiloscope-fov
  • MATLAB's lsqcurvefit is replaced by scipy's least_squares (trust-region-reflective, the code's start, bounds and tolerances).fig1e-featherscope-psf
  • MATLAB's lsqcurvefit is replaced by scipy's least_squares (trust-region-reflective, the code's start, bounds and tolerances); the fits of sub-pixel beads depend on the optimizer and differ between runs on different machines, by up to 0.4 um in a group's median (4.467 against 4.084 um for the centre's lower-cost fits).fig1f-kiloscope-psf
  • The code reads the 91 images in order of file date, which the repository does not keep; they are read in file-number order, which the Featherscope's region labels confirm for that stack. For the Kiloscope stack the order is not confirmed; each region's listed slice lies at the median of its brightest slice, but within 5 slices of it for only 72% of regions.fig1f-kiloscope-psf
  • The 0.5 mm reading lies one bin beyond the code's profile (0 to 499 um) and is read at 499 um; the bin at 500 um of an extended profile differs by at most 0.005.ed2b-featherscope-flat-illumination
  • The 2.0 mm reading is read at the code's last bin, 1999 um; the bin at 2000 um of an extended profile differs by 0.001.ed2c-kiloscope-flat-illumination
  • NoRMCorre (MATLAB) is replaced by a rigid registration written after its algorithm and defaults (dftregistration with 1/50 pixel upsampling, a template of the 100 middle frames, updated every 200 frames); the paper's settings are not given.ed7-motion-small
  • All 2948 footprints in place of the 2,382 curated ones (the claims file's variant); the curated set is not marked.fig2-quadrant-direction-decoding
  • Position tracked from the behaviour video with the code's rule; linear position unwrapped by the loop length.fig2-quadrant-direction-decoding
  • MATLAB's fitcecoc with templateLinear replaced by scikit-learn's SGDClassifier (hinge loss, alpha equal to lambda, 10 passes), as MATLAB's default for more than 100 predictors is 10 passes of dual SGD.fig2-quadrant-direction-decoding
  • All 2948 footprints in place of the 2,382 curated ones (the claims file's variant).ed8-coactive-distributed
  • MATLAB's nnmf replaced by scikit-learn's multiplicative-update NMF (100 iterations, 4 random starts).ed8-coactive-distributed
  • All 2948 footprints in place of the 2,382 curated ones; position tracked from the video; SGD in place of MATLAB's solver, as for fig2-quadrant-direction-decoding.ed9b-octant-decoding
  • Entropy over the 99 occupied position bins; the code's formula would give 0 for every footprint when a bin is empty.ed10-localized-activity
