Dataset version 0.250512.1625 · tested 2026-11-02 · 6 open findings

  • Description missingThe dandiset does not say which mice are controls and which received cuprizone, though its description says it holds both groups; participants.tsv gives only the species. The groups, and the time points, are in the folder names of the NYU component of the White Matter Microscopy Database (OSF node adrcs), by mouse number; by these, the dandiset holds 8 control mice (7, 9, 25, 26, 27, 34, 35, 36) and 12 cuprizone mice.
    Found by the attempt on Zaimi et al. 2018 (zaimi-2018-01, 2026-11-02)
  • OtherThe masks in the dandiset are the 2025 revision (CHANGES, version 0.04, "Modify the masks of 155 images to separate all fibers with a 2px delineation"); the masks the paper scored against in 2018 are not in the dandiset.
    Found by the attempt on Zaimi et al. 2018 (zaimi-2018-01, 2026-11-02)
  • Metadata errorThe archive's assets summary for version 0.250512.1625 gives 11 samples, where the dandiset holds 158 images (samples.tsv has 158 rows); sample ids (sample-0001 to sample-0011) are reused across mice, and the summary counts distinct ids.
    Found by the attempt on Zaimi et al. 2018 (zaimi-2018-01, 2026-11-02)
  • Other21 of the 158 axonmyelin masks are grey plus alpha pngs (alpha 255 everywhere), the others grey; their grey channel agrees with the axon and myelin masks in every image.
    Found by the attempt on Zaimi et al. 2018 (zaimi-2018-01, 2026-11-02)
  • Data missingThe dandiset holds only the mouse TEM images of the paper. The SEM rat and human test images, the SEM training images, the macaque test image and the full rat spinal cord slice of Figures 6 and 7 are not in it. Of these, macaque TEM images of ref. 7 were found in the White Matter Microscopy Database, without a record of which the paper tested; the SEM rat test and training images in the AxonDeepSeg SEM dataset (GitHub, doi:10.5281/zenodo.5498378), which the paper does not name; and two candidate panoramas of a rat cervical spinal cord in the White Matter Microscopy Database, neither named as the paper's slice. The human SEM test image was not found.
    Found by the attempt on Zaimi et al. 2018 (zaimi-2018-01, 2026-11-02)
  • OtherIn the White Matter Microscopy Database macaque folder (outside the dandiset), the masks of sample 4 fit none of its three images, and the NIfTI headers give a voxel size of 2.0 with no pixel size.
    Found by the attempt on Zaimi et al. 2018 (zaimi-2018-01, 2026-11-02)
