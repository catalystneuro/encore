Dataset version 0.240702.1725 · tested 2026-11-21 · 7 open findings

  • Description missing1,508 of the 54,045 analysed units (quality good, presence ratio at least 0.95, in the six visual areas) have a CCF coordinate (electrodes table x, y or z) of 0 or below, a value that falls outside the brain; the files do not say that 0 marks a missing registration, while these units still carry an area label in the location column.
    Found by the attempt on Souza et al. 2026 (souza-2026-01, 2026-11-21)
  • OtherThe units table's waveform_duration and the trough-to-peak duration measured on the stored mean waveform of the peak channel agree only at r = 0.66 (median difference -0.004 ms). The stored mean waveforms are sampled at 30 kHz (82 samples), so their durations take 60 distinct values and a two-component mixture on them does not separate narrow from broad units, while the stored waveform_duration (151 values) does; the files do not say how waveform_duration was computed. Upsampled to 200 points, the measured durations agree with the stored ones within 0.02 ms for 97.5% of units, but 1,263 units differ by more than 0.1 ms (median 1.421 against 0.371 ms).
    Found by the attempt on Souza et al. 2026 (souza-2026-01, 2026-11-21)
  • OtherChecks that passed. In all 104 sessions analysed, block 0 is the active natural-image block and block 5 its passive replay with the same image sequence and no licks or rewards; water follows the nearest lick by 1.3 to 53.5 ms (median 9.78 ms), so rewards and licks share a clock; and the summed spiking of each session's units rises after the image changes of the stimulus table (post to pre ratio 1.19 to 4.12, against 0.52 to 1.29 with the times shifted 0.25 s), so spikes and stimuli share a clock.
    Found by the attempt on Souza et al. 2026 (souza-2026-01, 2026-11-21)
  • Metadata errorIn 7 sessions, 351 VISp units that pass the quality criteria have peak-channel CCF coordinates x, y and z all 0, while the electrodes table names their location VISp. The zeros stand for missing coordinates, so these units cannot be given a layer.
    Found by the attempt on Jarzebowski et al. 2026 (jarzebowski-2026-01, 2026-11-21)
  • Description missingThe units and electrodes tables have no column descriptions (firing_rate, waveform_duration, snr, isi_violations, presence_ratio, amplitude_cutoff, x, y, z among them). Units (Hz, ms, um in the CCF) can be read from the values and the Allen documentation, but not from the files.
    Found by the attempt on Jarzebowski et al. 2026 (jarzebowski-2026-01, 2026-11-21)
  • OtherIn session 20211111T220915 the 10 s spontaneous block that in the other 152 sessions follows the active task by 18 to 29 s starts 3277 s after the last task presentation, and the later blocks follow correspondingly later. The files do not say what happened in the gap.
    Found by the attempt on Jarzebowski et al. 2026 (jarzebowski-2026-01, 2026-11-21)
  • OtherChecks that passed. 153 sessions from 81 mice, no two sharing a spike-train signature; 4795 to 4811 active-task presentation rows (with omissions) per session at 750 ms onset intervals; spontaneous blocks of 10.0 and 289.0 s; running speed in cm/s. The VISp population count separates images from omissions 30 to 100 ms after onset (median AUC 0.95) and not before onset (0.44 to 0.56), consistent with spikes and stimulus tables sharing a clock. In 10 sessions the AUC is below 0.8 (lowest 0.55, 20201203T231053), and 2 sessions have no VISp unit passing the criteria (20210616T205318, 20210819T204407); there the shared clock is not established here. 7996 of 7997 VISp units with coordinates fall in VISp in the CCFv3 annotation.
    Found by the attempt on Jarzebowski et al. 2026 (jarzebowski-2026-01, 2026-11-21)
