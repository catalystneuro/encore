  • fig3c-success-rate-constant

    Technical Validation, paragraph 2; Figure 3c, middle panel

    How it will be judged

    The success rate is the number of successful (rewarded) pulls over the number of tones (Figure 3 legend), per session, and the population value is the median over mice. The paper gives no test and no bound for "constant", so the readings are (a) the session-15 median within 10% (relative) of the session-1 median, the protocol's default tolerance; (b) no monotonic trend of the 15 session medians, Spearman correlation with session index at p >= 0.05. The claim holds when both readings hold, fails when both fail, and is blocked as methods_underspecified when they differ. The change in the response rate over the same sessions is shown alongside for contrast.
