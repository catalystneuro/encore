  • fig2-target-artifact

    High-pressure tFUS (about 170 kPa) aimed at the electrode site produces an artifact aligned with sonication that vanishes off target or at lower pressure, and extracted waveforms show tail oscillations at the 1,500 Hz PRF.

    Once the ultrasound was delivered at the desired target, high “artifact” would appear and align with the stimulation duration (Fig. 2A). To lower the pressure or move off the target could eliminate the “artifact” (Fig. 2B). The extracted spike waveform also showed oscillations at the tail due to the displacement of electrode (Fig. 2C). The frequency of the oscillations was 1,500 Hz which aligned with PRF.

    Materials and Methods, Transcranial focused ultrasound stimulation, paragraph 4; Figure 2

    How it will be judged

    Needs the target-determination trials (10 to 20 trials at about 170 kPa, 1,500 Hz PRF, Methods) and a lower-pressure recording at the same site. The paper gives no measure of the artifact; it shows traces. Reproduced when the voltage aligned to sonication onset shows a deflection confined to 0 to 67 ms at high pressure and not at lower pressure, and the dominant frequency of the mean waveform tail (after the AHP) is within tolerance of 1,500 Hz. The spectral method is a standard choice (FFT peak), named as one.
