None of the paper's main findings could be tested in this data. The map of touch and whisking coding across layers, cell types and barrel columns, the concentration of touch neurons in the principal column, the stability of representations during learning and the map of directional tuning all need layer, cell-type or column labels that the data set does not carry.     How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • Three of the paper's eight mice, five sessions.
    • The sample epoch is the pole-in-reach interval of the data set's poleInReach series (the paper's "1 s sample epoch (pole in reach)", Results, Imaging Large Neuronal Populations, paragraph 1). The Figure 1C legend gives the epoch as 1 to 2 s and the claims file's opening comment as 1 s; a reader is asked about the comment (questions/peron-2015.yaml). The data's intervals are used as they are, so the difference does not enter the result.
    • The band used to isolate whisking before the Hilbert transform (reading c) is not given in the paper or in the Supplemental Experimental Procedures, which cite Huber et al. (2012); 6 to 30 Hz is used, with 4 to 30 and 8 to 25 Hz shown. Every band gives the same outcome, so the outcome does not rest on it.
    • Only trials the data set lists as valid whisker trials are used. Rare angle samples at +/-90 degrees (at most 0.06% of samples), which look like tracking errors, are kept in the main readings, as the criterion's maximum minus minimum includes them. Readings (a) and (b) are also shown without samples above 85 degrees and without spikes over 20 degrees from a 5-sample running median, with other thresholds, and after unwrapping; reading (a) also without jumps of several samples (a step over 30 degrees in one sample that returns within 25 samples, with other values, and running medians of 11 and 21 samples); reading (c) without 0.2 s at each end. These are standard choices, not from the paper, which says nothing on tracking errors. Every one that removes only errors gives the same outcome; the 21-sample median, which also cuts real whisk peaks, does not (68.6, or 68.3 with every mask). The 1st to 99th percentile range is shown as a further variant.

Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingThe data set holds 5 imaging sessions from 3 of the paper's 8 volume-imaging mice (an197522, an229717, an229719), with 20% to 72% of their Table S1 ROIs (26,761 of 108,374 for all eight mice).   • Metadata errorOne subvolume (an197522, 2013-03-08, dffTSA 10, raw folder fov_04001, 202 ROIs) is a single plane imaged at 29 Hz, not three planes at 7 Hz as the description says of every subvolume; and many 7 Hz subvolumes have ROIs on only one or two of their three planes.
