Nested sequences of hippocampal assemblies during behavior support subsequent sleep replay

Céline Drieu, Ralitsa Todorova, Michaël Zugaro

Summary: In this data the paper's contrast between active and passive travel holds for most waking measures and only in part for sleep. Place fields stay stable, and passive travel weakens theta power, theta asymmetry and theta sequences, though the sequence effects depend on keeping the recorded sign of two rats' LFP, which may be inverted. Replay improves after active travel but not after the second passive travel, and steeper replay slopes after active travel, the self-consistency of theta sequences and their correlation with later replay do not reproduce. Faster-than-theta spike bursts in active travel were not found; the paper does not give its spectrogram windows. Three supplementary controls of the theta-sequence result did not hold.
