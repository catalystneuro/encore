Reproducing Girardeau, Inema & Buzsaki (2017) from dandiset 000061¶

Paper. Girardeau G, Inema I, Buzsaki G (2017). Reactivations of emotional memory in the hippocampus-amygdala system during sleep. Nature Neuroscience 20, 1634-1642. doi:10.1038/nn.4637.

Claims. claims/000061/10.1038-nn.4637.yaml, written by a reader from the version of record (main text, figure legends, Online Methods, reporting summary) and the Supplementary Information, with the paper's code (github.com/buzsakilab/papers, GGirardeau-BLAHpcInteractions-Package, at commit cdcec89f), and checked by a second reader. Its criteria fix every method; the shared methods M1 to M8 and the open details O1 to O7 are in its opening comment. This attempt (000061-girardeau-2017-02) replaces 000061-girardeau-2017-01, whose claims were written from the abstract alone. Three questions this attempt asked (questions/000061.yaml) were answered by a reader and the claims file updated: laps are judged on O1 reading c alone when the dataset has neither lap intervals nor position; the sleep firing claims are judged on the identified cells (O4 reading a) alone; and the all-cell tests of reactivation-peaks-at-ripples, direction-all-cell-pairs and ripple-modulated-cells-larger-reactivation no longer depend on claims that need putative cell types, so they are judged here.

Dandiset. DANDI:000061, version 0.250624.0426. The paper cites no DANDI version (it says the data will be on CRCNS). There are two published versions, 0.210812.1517 and 0.250624.0426, with the same 40 files, and the draft (modified 2026-10-05) holds the same 40 assets as 0.250624.0426; the data check prints this. The most recent published version is pinned, as the protocol asks when the paper cites none: it is the version the earlier attempt tested, no later published version exists, and the draft differs from it only in metadata the analysis does not use (keywords, anatomy terms, ethics approval, affiliations).

What the dandiset holds. 40 NWB files, one per recording day, from four rats (Rat08 to Rat11): sorted units with a region label, sleep states, epochs, and event times of hippocampal ripples, air puffs and rewards; some files also hold raw voltage, LFP and video frames. There is no position tracking, and the putative cell types the paper uses (pyramidal cell or interneuron, from monosynaptic connections and then k-means on the waveform) are not in it: five Rat08 files have a cell_type column that marks only cells identified as excitatory or inhibitory. Every claim that needs putative pyramidal cells is therefore blocked; where a claim's criterion judges its all-cell test alone when the pyramidal test cannot be run, that test decides it.

Random numbers are drawn only where power and bounds are given for the claims of absence (bla-int-sleep-firing, cen-no-reactivation, ripple-rate-unchanged); each of those sections has its own generator with a fixed seed, and none of these numbers decides an outcome.

The unit labels are short codes with no description in the files or the dandiset metadata. They match the structure names of the paper's Supplementary Fig. 3 (BLA, BLV, LaDL, BMP; Pir, VEn, DEn; and CeN for the central nuclei), so bla, cen, pir and hpc/HIP are read as the paper's BLA, central nuclei, piriform cortex and hippocampus (M1); this is consistent with the paper's names, not shown by the dandiset. Some labels appear in two cases (bla and BLA, pir and PIR, plc01 and PLCo1); others (cpu, no label) are not defined anywhere. The electrode table gives every channel the location unknown, and Rat09-20140328 has no location column.

Cell types. Only five Rat08 files (20130709 to 20130713) have a cell_type column, described only as "Type of cell this has been classified as", with the values excitatory, inhibitory and unclassified. Most units are unclassified, and almost no hippocampal unit is typed (no file has six typed excitatory hippocampal units), while the paper's k-means step types every remaining cell as a putative pyramidal cell or interneuron. The column therefore holds at most the paper's monosynaptic identification, and the putative types are missing: excitatory units are read as monosynaptically identified pyramidal cells and inhibitory ones as identified interneurons (O4 reading a). This reading is consistent with the data (inhibitory units fire faster than excitatory ones, the BLA ratio of excitatory to inhibitory units is close to the paper's 675 to 175, and the short-latency check below finds connections of the marked kind for nearly every marked unit), not shown by it. The files hold no spike waveforms; the raw voltage of 16 files would be needed to classify cells, which the claims file does not provide for. The earlier attempt did not read this column. Every claim whose criterion uses putative pyramidal cells or interneurons is blocked as metadata_insufficient, except bla-rate-same-across-directions, which position blocks first (too large).

# (3) Lap direction decoded from hippocampal rates in each lap (leave one lap out, nearest
# centroid), with the true laps and with laps shifted by half their own duration.
def decode_direction(s, half_shift=False):
    u = s["units"]
    ids = hpcbla.units_of(s, hpcbla.HPC_LABELS)
    laps = hpcbla.run_laps(s)
    if len(ids) < 5 or len(laps) < 6 or len({d for _, _, d in laps}) < 2:
        return None
    if half_shift:
        laps = [(a + (b - a) / 2, b + (b - a) / 2, d) for a, b, d in laps]
    x = np.array([[np.count_nonzero((u["trains"][i] >= a) & (u["trains"][i] < b)) / (b - a) for i in ids]
                  for a, b, _ in laps])
    y = np.array([d == "LR" for _, _, d in laps])
    x = np.log1p(x)
    x = (x - x.mean(0)) / (x.std(0) + 1e-9)
    hits = 0
    for k in range(len(y)):
        keep = np.arange(len(y)) != k
        c1, c0 = x[keep & y].mean(0), x[keep & ~y].mean(0)
        hits += (np.sum((x[k] - c1) ** 2) < np.sum((x[k] - c0) ** 2)) == y[k]
    return hits / len(y), len(y)

habituation-over-days¶

The aversive valence of the air puff diminishes over training days; rats run more laps per unit time as they habituate.

Habituation index per session = laps / run epoch duration x 100 (Performance.m). Laps are O1 reading c (between consecutive rewards at opposite ends, both inside the run): readings a (lap intervals the dataset holds) and b (position) cannot be run, as the dandiset has neither. As answered (questions/000061.yaml), the criterion now judges the claim on reading c alone in that case, the back-and-forth runs between the two reward ends that the Methods count. The three lap counts of the criterion: all laps (back-and-forth), air puff laps, and left-to-right laps. A reading c lap includes the time on the starting platform after its reward, so lap counts are those of back-and-forth runs between rewards. Readings of "gradually diminished": (a) pooled Spearman correlation of day rank within the rat and index, positive with p < 0.05; (b) positive Spearman correlation in at least 3 of the 4 rats (shown as the sign alone and with p < 0.05).

How the analysis differs from the paper's. Every method is the claims file's, from the paper's Methods and code. The differences come from the data: the dandiset holds 40 of the paper's 61 sessions; it has no putative cell types, so the cells of bla-pyr-sleep-firing-increase and bla-int-sleep-firing are the identified excitatory and inhibitory units of five Rat08 files (O4 reading a, which decides alone as answered), and every claim that needs putative pyramidal cells is blocked, except that reactivation-peaks-at-ripples and ripple-modulated-cells-larger-reactivation are judged on their all-cell tests alone, as their criteria now allow; it has no position, so laps are the intervals between rewards at opposite ends (O1 reading c, which decides alone as answered), which include the time on the starting platform and the stops that the M8 laps leave out, and the behaviour claims and bla-rate-same-across-directions (O7 reading b) are blocked; brain regions are the dandiset's labels read as the paper's structures; units 0 and 1 of ten files are left out of the analyses because their spike times are mixed. What was found about the dandiset.

  • The putative cell types the paper uses are missing; a cell_type column in five of 33 files with units marks only identified excitatory and inhibitory cells and is described in one line. There are no waveforms. A short-latency cross-correlogram check finds connections of the marked sign for nearly every marked BLA unit.
  • It holds 40 recording days against the paper's 61, and 4702 units against 7,390.
  • Still present from the earlier attempt (the files are unchanged): the epochs table is in milliseconds although it says seconds; video timestamps are in milliseconds, stated as seconds, and are not on the session clock; in ten files the units index is wrong at units 0 and 1; seven files have no units table, sixteen no sleep states and fourteen no ripple events; there is no position tracking; the unit region labels, the trials table and the ripple labels are undescribed.
