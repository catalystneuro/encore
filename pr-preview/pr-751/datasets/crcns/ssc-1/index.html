Dataset unversioned files (the archive keeps no versions) · tested 2026-11-09 · 7 open findings

  • Data missingThe data set holds 5 imaging sessions from 3 of the paper's 8 volume-imaging mice (an197522, an229717, an229719), with 20% to 72% of their Table S1 ROIs (26,761 of 108,374 for all eight mice). The other five mice, including the Gad2 mouse with labelled GABAergic neurons, the imaging during learning, the L4 axon mice, the anesthetized mice, the L5 soma and dendrite pairs, the training sessions and the high-resolution reference stacks are not in it, although the paper says the data set includes the imaging data of Table S1.
    Found by the attempt on Peron et al. 2015 (peron-2015-01, 2026-11-09)
  • Metadata errorThe data description lists raw fluorescence and algorithmically extracted calcium events for each ROI, and files named vol_xxx holding multi-session data. The session files hold only dF/F (dffTSA) and whisker variables as time series, and no vol_xxx file exists; the imaging archives hold registered TIFFs.
    Found by the attempt on Peron et al. 2015 (peron-2015-01, 2026-11-09)
  • Description missingNo ROI carries a layer (L1, L2/3, L5 apical dendrite), cell-class or barrel-column label, and no imaging plane carries a depth or position relative to the others. The description says laminar boundaries were drawn in the reference stack, but neither the boundaries nor the stack are deposited, so the paper's comparisons between layers, classes and principal and surround columns cannot be made from the data set.
    Found by the attempt on Peron et al. 2015 (peron-2015-01, 2026-11-09)
  • Metadata errorOne subvolume (an197522, 2013-03-08, dffTSA 10, raw folder fov_04001, 202 ROIs) is a single plane imaged at 29 Hz, not three planes at 7 Hz as the description says of every subvolume; and many 7 Hz subvolumes have ROIs on only one or two of their three planes. Neither is described.
    Found by the attempt on Peron et al. 2015 (peron-2015-01, 2026-11-09)
  • Metadata errorThe event series structure is named eventSeriesArrayHash in the files, not eventSeriesHash as described; the file an229717_2013_12_01/an229717_2013_12_01_sesssion.mat has a misspelt name; the description's citation of the paper is a placeholder ("Article title. Journal Name xxx").
    Found by the attempt on Peron et al. 2015 (peron-2015-01, 2026-11-09)
  • OtherIn an197522 on 2013-03-10 the dF/F response to the first touch is not larger with the true touch times than with the times shifted by 3 s (3 of 9 subvolumes; median 0.0009 against 0.0083 dF/F), so that session's imaging could not be shown to share the trial clock. In the other four sessions the true alignment gives the largest response in 21 of 25 subvolumes.
    Found by the attempt on Peron et al. 2015 (peron-2015-01, 2026-11-09)
  • OtherThe whisker angle holds tracking errors that the description does not mention. Samples jump to the tracker's +/-90 degree limit for one or two samples (for example 87.3, 37.8, -89.2, 41.7 degrees on successive 2 ms samples in an229717), jump to a distant value for three samples or more (-24.1, 72.8, 72.8, 72.1, -23.1 in an229719; in an229717 a step over 30 degrees that returns within 25 samples occurs in 116 of 183 trials, and its 99.9th percentile step is 34.5 degrees against 4.5 to 7.0 in the other sessions), and protractions past 90 degrees wrap to the other sign (84.6, 89.2, -87.5, -88.7, 89.3 in an197522). Samples above 85 degrees occur in 26 of 764, 52 of 183 and 2 of 496 valid whisker trials of an197522, an229717 and an229719. The largest per-trial angle range, 179.8 degrees, spans these limits.
    Found by the attempt on Peron et al. 2015 (peron-2015-01, 2026-11-09)
