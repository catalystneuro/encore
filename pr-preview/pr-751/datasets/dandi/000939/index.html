Dataset version 0.261016.2310 · tested 2026-10-31 · 6 open findings

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds 31 of the paper's 32 PoSub sessions, all 14 vertical ones (931 units, the paper's number) and 17 of the 18 parallel ones (1,760 units against 1,999). The missing one is A8603-210602, the Neuropixel recording, which the code's session list (dataset_All.list) and the paper's parallel count include (239 units, 1,760 + 239 = 1,999); it is only in figshare Dataset_4.
    Found by the attempt on Duszkiewicz et al. 2024 (duszkiewicz-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Data missingOf the five ArchT mice of the PoSub optogenetic experiment the dandiset holds two (A1813, A1815); the other three (A1801, A1804, A1806, six sessions) are only in figshare Dataset_6. The paper's 83 HD and 47 FS ArchT cells cannot be reached from the dandiset (62 and 33 here; 102 and 50 with the figshare mice added).
    Found by the attempt on Duszkiewicz et al. 2024 (duszkiewicz-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • File format errorIn eight files processing/ogen/optogenetic_stim holds four channels interleaved in one dimension, so its length at the stated 20 kHz is four times the session's (in A1813, 933,367,680 samples against 233,341,920 of the ElectricalSeries, an 11,667 s session); only every fourth sample carries the light, with values 0 and 16368, and the other three channels are 0 throughout. In A1808 it holds one channel of 0 and 1 at the session's length. The light pulses are recoverable, but the series' shape and units (watts) do not describe it. The decoded pulses match the figshare Opto_TS.csv tables within 0.1 ms.
    Found by the attempt on Duszkiewicz et al. 2024 (duszkiewicz-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Description missingThe files hold no speed series, though every analysis restricts tuning curves by speed, and no table of light pulse times; both must be derived. The probe orientation (vertical or parallel) is stated only in the free-text device description.
    Found by the attempt on Duszkiewicz et al. 2024 (duszkiewicz-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Metadata errorThe sleep states (intervals/sleep_states) of A3711, A3717 and A3723 run to about twice the session's length (to 24,351 s in a 12,177 s session), so that 298, 518 and 223 s of their REM lie after the recording ends; in A1821 (16 s in the square epoch and 41 s in the optogenetic epoch), A3712 (12 s in the square epoch) and A3701 (0.3 s in the triangle epoch) REM intervals lie inside exploration epochs. The REM intervals are the same in version 0.250207.0025, whose 2026 successor says it fixed the indexing of the sleep scoring epochs.
    Found by the attempt on Duszkiewicz et al. 2024 (duszkiewicz-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Metadata errorThe units' is_excitatory and is_fast_spiking labels follow the code's 0.35 ms trough-to-peak cut (agreement 99.9% with it), not the Methods' 0.4 ms; the column descriptions give no criterion.
    Found by the attempt on Duszkiewicz et al. 2024 (duszkiewicz-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
