Summary: Every claim could be tested, and the central result holds in this data: deviance detection, global oddballs and stimulus-specific adaptation each raise V1 gamma power over their controls, the gamma peak frequency rises for deviants in V1 and LM, and the aperiodic component is unchanged. The feedforward gamma pattern in the current source density holds, but the feedback alpha/beta and theta patterns do not, under an onset rule chosen here because the paper judges them by eye. The Figure 5 finding that two of the effects stay in early visual areas did not reproduce, with at most 3 of 13 area values within tolerance. The laminar profiles and single-condition spectra hold only in part.
