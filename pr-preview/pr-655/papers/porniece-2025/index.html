Porniece et al. 2025 · Scientific Reports

Obesity dysregulates feeding-evoked response dynamics in hypothalamic satiety neurons

doi:10.1038/s41598-025-19800-2 · paper key porniece-2025

Datasets

  • DANDI:001474

Most main findings held in this data, though the neural ones only in direction. HFD mice ate smaller meals and moved less, and NCD mice raised their PVH MC4R response across a meal while HFD mice did not, but exact response sizes matched only under some Z-score readings. The test that HFD early responses exceed NCD did not reproduce (p 0.09 to 0.35), because the paper does not say how the Z-score was computed; the depositors' own Z-score supports it. The diet-switch change in response was not reproduced. The claim that HFD mice needed more training could not be tested, since training sessions are not in the dataset.
