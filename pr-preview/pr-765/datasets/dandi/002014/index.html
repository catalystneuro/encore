Dataset version 0.261012.0921 · tested 2026-10-15 · 12 open findings

  • File format errorThe raw ephys and the spike times are not on the clock of the trials, events, LFP and FSCV table. The raw ephys is stored with a starting time of 0 s and a rate, and the spike times count from its first sample. Locating the raw c7b minus c5c signal in the stored LFP puts raw time 0 at 11812.11 s on the trial clock (correlation 0.98 to 1.00 at eight points, drift about 4 ms per hour). Without this correction spikes cannot be aligned to the task. The dandiset description says all data are expressed relative to the start of the Neuralynx recording, which the data contradict.
    Found by the attempt on Choi et al. 2025 (choi-2025-01, 2026-10-15)
  • Metadata errorThe absolute times disagree. On the trial clock the raw samples run from 12:06:44 to 17:12:20, while the Neuralynx header gives the recording as opened at 08:49:52 (the session start) and closed at 15:12:24, two hours before the raw samples end. The header span (22952 s) is also longer than the raw samples (18336 s).
    Found by the attempt on Choi et al. 2025 (choi-2025-01, 2026-10-15)
  • OtherThe stored spike times of the thresholded units are about 7.1 ms after the spike troughs in raw c5d (in one 300 s segment of 1633 spikes, per-spike trough lags of -7.4 to -6.9 ms from the 10th to the 90th percentile, 98.8% within 0.5 ms of -7.1 ms).
    Found by the attempt on Choi et al. 2025 (choi-2025-01, 2026-10-15)
  • Metadata errorThe series "bipolar_lfp_series" is described as beta-band LFP but is broadband with line noise removed. Its values are already in volts, while it carries the raw int16 conversion factor (3.05e-8), which would scale them about 3e7 times too small.
    Found by the attempt on Choi et al. 2025 (choi-2025-01, 2026-10-15)
  • Metadata errorThe thresholded spike waveforms are stated in volts but hold microvolts (mean trough about -56), and the raw FSCV current is stated in amperes but holds nanoamperes (values around 1000).
    Found by the attempt on Choi et al. 2025 (choi-2025-01, 2026-10-15)
  • Description missingThe columns of the trial-aligned FSCV table (da, ph, m, iox) state no units, and the `good` column is true for every trial.
    Found by the attempt on Choi et al. 2025 (choi-2025-01, 2026-10-15)
  • Metadata errorThe subject description ("Two adult female rhesus monkeys were used for in vivo validation of the s-µIP device"), the session and experiment descriptions and the keyword "micro-invasive neural implant" describe the lab's micro-invasive probe study, not this paper's chamber.
    Found by the attempt on Choi et al. 2025 (choi-2025-01, 2026-10-15)
  • File format errorThe event table stores each event once per overlapping 60 s trial window, about five copies of each (7221 stored trial-start events for 1412 distinct ones), and its event times are not sorted. The Plexon units table's spike times are not sorted either.
    Found by the attempt on Choi et al. 2025 (choi-2025-01, 2026-10-15)
  • Description missingThe trial tags are all "initial cue display" and carry no trial type. Reward size and outcome can be rebuilt from the events, and they then match the paper's Zenodo trial lists except trial 1411, which the events mark as a successful large reward trial and the list leaves out.
    Found by the attempt on Choi et al. 2025 (choi-2025-01, 2026-10-15)
  • Description missingThe paper says the reward size depends on whether the target is at the top or bottom of the screen. The events code left and right conditions, and both reward sizes occur on both sides, so the target position that sets the reward is not in the dandiset.
    Found by the attempt on Choi et al. 2025 (choi-2025-01, 2026-10-15)
  • OtherThe raw FSCV minute files are joined without their gaps (328 min of scans against 302 min of trials, placed from a single starting time), and the trial-aligned oxidation current cannot be located in them, so the raw FSCV's clock could not be established.
    Found by the attempt on Choi et al. 2025 (choi-2025-01, 2026-10-15)
  • Data missingOnly one session of monkey T is deposited. The data behind the electrode yields, cultures, drainage volumes and bone growth, and all of monkey J, are not, so eight of the thirteen claims cannot be tested.
    Found by the attempt on Choi et al. 2025 (choi-2025-01, 2026-10-15)
