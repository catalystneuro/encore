Dataset version 0.231010.1811 · tested 2026-10-30 · 10 open findings

  • Data missingThe BOLD time courses of the clinical fMRI, on which six of the paper's claims rest, are not public. The dandiset holds none, the OpenNeuro version (ds003374, 1.1.1) holds only iEEG, and the paper names no deposit.
    Found by the attempt on Fedele et al. 2020 (fedele-2020-01, 2026-10-30)
  • Data missingThe continuous microwire signals behind the microwire high gamma power (muHGP) are not in the dandiset, which holds only spike times and mean waveforms of the microwire data.
    Found by the attempt on Fedele et al. 2020 (fedele-2020-01, 2026-10-30)
  • OtherEach session is deposited twice, as `_ecephys.nwb` and `_ecephys+image.nwb`; the second adds a link to the video and holds identical iEEG, spikes and trials. The 18 NWB files hold 9 recordings.
    Found by the attempt on Fedele et al. 2020 (fedele-2020-01, 2026-10-30)
  • Metadata errorThe iEEG is stated in volts, but its values (standard deviation 15 to 101, up to 1546) are microvolts.
    Found by the attempt on Fedele et al. 2020 (fedele-2020-01, 2026-10-30)
  • Metadata errorThe trial table gives trial 1 as 0 to 26 s, including its 2 s baseline clip, and trials 2 to 17 as their 24 s blocks only (starting 2 s into each 26 s epoch). The iEEG timestamps run without a gap from 0 to 442 s, but the 17 epochs are not contiguous; the signal jumps at each boundary (median step 12 to 36 against 1.4 to 1.9 within epochs).
    Found by the attempt on Fedele et al. 2020 (fedele-2020-01, 2026-10-30)
  • Metadata errorEvery unit's obs_intervals cover all 17 epochs, but the 16 units of subject 7 have no spike after 286 s (epochs 12 to 17) and four units of subject 2 none in epoch 1, at rates of 1 to 16 Hz elsewhere.
    Found by the attempt on Fedele et al. 2020 (fedele-2020-01, 2026-10-30)
  • Metadata errorThe ages of subjects 2 and 8 (39 and 24) differ from those in the paper and the data descriptor (48 and 27). Subject 2's file holds the same subject metadata as subject 7's (age 39, gliosis, AL and AR implanted, AR in the SOZ), the age being that of the paper's patient 7.
    Found by the attempt on Fedele et al. 2020 (fedele-2020-01, 2026-10-30)
  • Metadata errorThe electrode location of the right channel of subjects 1, 5 and 9 is "Right Hippocampus, rostral hippocampus", and both channels of subject 6 are "unknown", while their labels (AR, AL), the paper and the data descriptor place them in the amygdala.
    Found by the attempt on Fedele et al. 2020 (fedele-2020-01, 2026-10-30)
  • Description missingUnits are assigned only to the bipolar macro channel of their amygdala; the microwire each was recorded on is not given, so the paper's spatial synchronisation index (neurons on different microwires) cannot be computed as defined. The units table has no column for it, and a spike train test of coincident firing flags only 4 of 116 testable pairs as likely sharing a wire.
    Found by the attempt on Fedele et al. 2020 (fedele-2020-01, 2026-10-30)
  • OtherThe units are in 8 amygdalae of 5 patients (1 L and R, 2 L and R, 3 L, 4 L, 7 L and R). The paper's Results give 9 amygdalae, and its Table 1 lists multi-unit activity for patients 5 and 8 and none for 7. Three of the 8 (1 R, 2 R, 7 R, with 17 units) are SOZ amygdalae, while the paper's Discussion 3.4 says there were no microwire recordings from SOZ amygdalae. Their mean rate is 4.65 +- 3.91 Hz against the data descriptor's 1.66 +- 2.65 Hz, and two units of subject 7 are below 1 Hz over the whole session.
    Found by the attempt on Fedele et al. 2020 (fedele-2020-01, 2026-10-30)
