Open findings about the data 13

What the attempts that used this dataset found about the data themselves, for its owner and the archive, under the version of the dataset each was found on.

Dataset version 0.240930.1859 · tested 2026-10-18 · 13 open findings

  • Data missingThree of the 113 subjects (20, 23, 33) have only the raw imaging file, without the segmentation file that holds the traces and the stimulation table.
    Found by the attempt on Randi et al. 2023 (randi-2023-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Data missingThe unc-31 recordings, the spontaneous recordings of Fig. 6 and the unstimulated control recordings are not in the dandiset. The control recordings, which every q value in the paper needs, are not public on OSF either, so the paper's central statistics cannot be recomputed.
    Found by the attempt on Randi et al. 2023 (randi-2023-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Identifier missingFor about half of the stimulations the files do not say which ROI was stimulated; they give only a target position. The paper's assignment uses tracking matches that are not in the dandiset; from the position, 66% of these stimulations get the paper's ROI. RID stimulations can be recognised only where RID was segmented and labelled (8 stimulations in 5 animals).
    Found by the attempt on Randi et al. 2023 (randi-2023-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Selection not documentedThe paper's export marks 950 stimulations as excluded (-2); the dandiset keeps all stimulations and does not say which were excluded or why.
    Found by the attempt on Randi et al. 2023 (randi-2023-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Metadata errorIn five recordings the labels reached through `neuropal_ids` and the NeuroPAL table differ from the paper's export for every named ROI. In subjects 64, 76 and 77 the dandiset has almost no labels (4, 0 and 15 distinct names against 70, 102 and 87 exported). In subjects 86 and 91 most names are shared with the export but sit on other ROIs. In subject 11 the number of ROIs (161) differs from the export (200), and the export has no labels for it.
    Found by the attempt on Randi et al. 2023 (randi-2023-01, 2026-10-18)
  • File format errorThe column `neuropal_ids`, documented as a NeuroPAL ROI id or blank, holds the text "merge" in 63 recordings and "check", "I4" or "RIAR" in others.
    Found by the attempt on Randi et al. 2023 (randi-2023-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Metadata errorThe target column `depth_in_um` holds values from -0.875 to 0.875, which cannot be micrometres in a volume imaged from 2.5 to 37.5 um; the unit is not stated. 122 stimulations have the target position (0, 0), meaning no stored position.
    Found by the attempt on Randi et al. 2023 (randi-2023-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Metadata errorEvery stimulation is stored as 0.5 s at 1.2 mW, constants from the paper written by the conversion; the 0.1 to 0.5 s pulses the Methods describe for AFD stimulations are not represented.
    Found by the attempt on Randi et al. 2023 (randi-2023-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Metadata errorThe red optical channel is described with the indicator GCaMP6s, but it images tagRFP-T. Two subjects (24, 25) have strain "none". The age is P1D for every animal, L4 or young adult. One label confidence is 11, outside the 0 to 1 range.
    Found by the attempt on Randi et al. 2023 (randi-2023-01, 2026-10-18)
  • OtherOf the 14,039 traces, 897 cannot be corrected for photobleaching with the paper's fit, 729 of them because values are missing even in the interpolated copy. 3,173 of 433,216 observation rows have a baseline F0 at or below zero, so their dF/F0 has no meaning; the variants without them change no outcome.
    Found by the attempt on Randi et al. 2023 (randi-2023-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Description missingThe traces have unit "n.a." and are described only as average baseline fluorescence, without saying what processing they have had; while preparing (not run in the notebook) they were seen to differ from the paper's exported traces. Nine recordings (subjects 2, 3, 4, 34, 35, 36, 38, 39, 40) hold values far below zero, down to -196,646.
    Found by the attempt on Randi et al. 2023 (randi-2023-01, 2026-10-18)
  • OtherSome ROIs carry the NeuroPAL label "target" (49 ROIs in 21 recordings), and labels such as RIG or SAAD name no single neuron.
    Found by the attempt on Randi et al. 2023 (randi-2023-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Metadata errorThirteen recordings last longer than the "up to 40 min" of the paper (up to 48.7 min), and three have gaps in their timestamps of up to 8.4 s.
    Found by the attempt on Randi et al. 2023 (randi-2023-01, 2026-10-18)
