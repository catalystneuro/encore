Summary: Much of this Patch-seq study of human interneurons holds: subclasses differ in sag and spike shape, SST FRZB neurons resemble PVALB neurons, and human and mouse homologues differ in sag, rheobase and spike width. Not reproduced were the electrophysiological overlap of cultured and acute PVALB WFDC2 neurons, the intermingling of double bouquet cells with other SST cells, and the species separation of Sst 5 cells. Gene differences between SST CALB1 morphology types came to 2, not 253, suggesting the Methods do not describe the procedure run. The culture effect on sag and spike width could not be decided, as the paper does not name its sweeps, and the transcriptomic comparisons need a Seurat integration the agent could not run.
