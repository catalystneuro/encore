Dataset version 0.230417.1502 · tested 2026-11-08 · 13 open findings

  • Metadata errorSessions 216300 and 216301 are labelled Vglut2-cre in the files' subject genotype, while the paper's code lists them as C57BL-6J controls. With the files' labels the dandiset has 3 controls and 7 Vglut2-cre mice, where the paper has 5 and 5.
    Found by the attempt on Calvigioni et al. 2023 (calvigioni-2023-01, 2026-11-08)
  • Metadata errorThe files give sex F for 10 of the 25 mice; the Fig. 4 legend states that all data were acquired in male mice.
    Found by the attempt on Calvigioni et al. 2023 (calvigioni-2023-01, 2026-11-08)
  • OtherFour sessions (128515, 258412, 259112, 273853) have no unit whose main channel is labelled ACAd, PL, ILA or ORBm (their probes are labelled in ORBl, ORBvl, AON, MOs and other areas), so only 21 of the 25 mice contribute units to the paper's analyses, 7 of the 10 Esr1-cre mice and 4 of the 5 Npy-cre mice. The paper reports 25 mice and n = 10 Esr1-cre mice for its tests.
    Found by the attempt on Calvigioni et al. 2023 (calvigioni-2023-01, 2026-11-08)
  • Metadata errorThe trials table's opto_power column is 0 in every trial, and the OptogeneticSeries (unit 'mW') peaks at 0.9 to 3.3 per session, where the paper states 6 mW in block 2.
    Found by the attempt on Calvigioni et al. 2023 (calvigioni-2023-01, 2026-11-08)
  • Description missingThe trials table holds blocks 9 to 12 (light at 5, 10 and 20 Hz and a block without frequency) and, in two sessions, a block 0, none described in the paper or the files; 259112 has 100 trials in block 1 and some blocks have 51 to 61 trials where the paper states 50.
    Found by the attempt on Calvigioni et al. 2023 (calvigioni-2023-01, 2026-11-08)
  • Description missingThe trials table's opto_pulse_ts column has no unit or description; its values are the light-pulse times multiplied by about 11,574.
    Found by the attempt on Calvigioni et al. 2023 (calvigioni-2023-01, 2026-11-08)
  • OtherSessions 268951, 273853 and 273858 hold 629, 520 and 570 tone events in AudStim_10kHz, where the trials table has 200 tone trials; the extra events have no trials.
    Found by the attempt on Calvigioni et al. 2023 (calvigioni-2023-01, 2026-11-08)
  • Metadata errorThe airpuff_duration column gives 20 ms, where the paper states a 50 ms air puff.
    Found by the attempt on Calvigioni et al. 2023 (calvigioni-2023-01, 2026-11-08)
  • Description missingThe optogenetic stimulus site is 'LHA' in all 25 files, while the paper stimulates LHA somata in retrograde and LHb terminals in anterograde Esr1-cre mice; the files do not say which Esr1-cre mice are which (the code's table does).
    Found by the attempt on Calvigioni et al. 2023 (calvigioni-2023-01, 2026-11-08)
  • OtherThe files hold two saturation series. The 'LFP saturation' series of session 147463 spans 1500 to 2671 s, all of block 3; removing spikes in it, as the Methods' words say, silences block 3 for all 209 units of that session. The paper's code uses the 'spike saturation' series (274 s at most).
    Found by the attempt on Calvigioni et al. 2023 (calvigioni-2023-01, 2026-11-08)
  • Description missingThe Patch-seq deposit (BioStudies S-BSST1069) labels cells with Ephys_Category codes 1 to 6 and gives no mapping of the codes to the paper's six neuron types.
    Found by the attempt on Calvigioni et al. 2023 (calvigioni-2023-01, 2026-11-08)
  • Data missingThe pupil and eye tracking of Fig. 4f and Extended Data Fig. 7g-j are not in the dandiset; one file (128514) holds an eye-area trace. The LHA optotagging recordings of Extended Data Fig. 1e-j are not in it either.
    Found by the attempt on Calvigioni et al. 2023 (calvigioni-2023-01, 2026-11-08)
  • Description missingThe units table has no curation label column, so the Methods' selection of 'good' units cannot be checked on the files. In session 128514 the series named eye_area is described as a smoothed pupil trace, so it is not clear whether it measures the eye or the pupil.
    Found by the attempt on Calvigioni et al. 2023 (calvigioni-2023-01, 2026-11-08)
