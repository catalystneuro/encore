  • fig1i-hmmglm-decodes-behaviour

    A 6-state HMM-GLM decodes the 9 behaviour labels from mPFC multi-unit activity above chance and better than a GLM or an ensemble of 9 GLMs, while the GLM and the 9 GLMs do not differ.

    An HMM-GLM model with 6 hidden states decoded behavioral labels from neural activity with superior cross-validated performance to static models

    Figure 1i; Extended Data Fig. 5j; Methods 17

    How it will be judged

    Input, from Methods 17 (Dataset), multi-unit rates on the 32 channels after common average reference, spikes at 4 SD from the mean, in 70 bins of 200 ms per trial (aligned at -2 to +12 s from tone onset, from the dandiset description; the paper gives no alignment), and one of the 9 labels per bin. HMM-GLM as fixed by run_model.m and model_HMMGLM.m (see the opening comment). FT, GLM (multinomial logit, 9-output softmax), SVM (Gaussian kernel, one against all) and 9 GLMs (one logit GLM per label) as Methods 17 describe. Per behaviour, the AUC is the mean over the 10 folds. Tests as the legend gives them; the printed p-values match a two-sided sign test of 9 of 9 AUCs above 0.5 (0.0039) and an exact rank-sum with no overlap between 9 and 9 (4.1e-5). Reproduced when HMM-GLM AUCs exceed 0.5 by the sign test (p < 0.05), exceed the GLM and 9-GLM AUCs by rank-sum (p < 0.05), and the GLM against 9 GLMs rank-sum gives p >= 0.05 (a claim of absence, judged on that test). Open, with readings; spike threshold (a) negative crossings below mean minus 4 SD, (b) crossings in either direction; rates (a) counts per bin, (b) z-scored per channel; AUC (a) with the one-bin backward shift the Methods describe (auc_ROC_delay), (b) without (auc_ROC); folds (a) contiguous in session order, as the code forms them, (b) random. The processed input in the code repository is a variant input, not the basis of the outcome.
