Summary: Most of the paper's main findings held up in this data: dopamine neurons discount reward exponentially with a spread of discount factors, the population decodes reward timing, one value function fits the ramps in virtual reality, and each neuron's discount factor correlates across the two tasks. The advantage over a single-discount decoder held at some delays only, and the mean ramp slope came out below the paper's. The Figure 1 simulations were not run, for lack of time. The cued delay recordings are not on DANDI and were tested on the paper's own deposit, which lacks 11 of its 78 recorded neurons. Several supplementary results were weaker than reported, and one did not reproduce.
