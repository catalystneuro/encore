Dataset version 0.211118.1526 · tested 2026-11-15 · 10 open findings

  • File format errorThe MUA spike times are not on the clock of the LFP. CA1 MUA peaks around the SWRs detected on the LFP in 261 of 311 analysed home cage sessions, but displaced from the SWR midpoint by a session-specific lag (median -0.145 s, quartiles -0.395 to -0.055 s, range about -2 to +1 s, the MUA earlier in most sessions); only 26 sessions have the peak within 20 ms. With the deposited times the mean normalised CA1 MUA during SWRs is 0.39 SD (median -0.2); with each session shifted by its lag, 1.73 SD.
    Found by the attempt on Jones et al. 2021 (jones-2021-01, 2026-11-15)
  • Metadata errorFour 4-shank mice (Beverly and Worf, PV-Cre; Picard and Riker, SST-Cre) have only home cage sessions labelled CNO, numbered 8 to 17, so they have no vehicle sessions as deposited; the paper analyses 16 PV-Cre and 16 SST-Cre mice with vehicle and CNO.
    Found by the attempt on Jones et al. 2021 (jones-2021-01, 2026-11-15)
  • Metadata errorIn cohort 3 every mouse has its vehicle sessions before its CNO sessions, on the track and in the home cage, where the Methods say the treatments alternated; cohort 2 alternates. Either the labels or the description is wrong. As deposited, treatment is confounded with session order, and the empty vector mice change with CNO in 4 rest and 9 run features, and run faster on CNO days.
    Found by the attempt on Jones et al. 2021 (jones-2021-01, 2026-11-15)
  • OtherThe scale of the LFP values is unclear. The unit attribute says microvolts, and the ripple-band envelope over rest (mean 68.8 file units over 311 home cage sessions) is close to Table S2's 72.39 uV. But the SW amplitude in vehicle sessions is about 3000 file units against Table S4's 375 and 385 uV, the rest SG power matches Table S2 neither in file units nor scaled by 0.195, and values reach the int16 limits in 123 of 311 home cage sessions.
    Found by the attempt on Jones et al. 2021 (jones-2021-01, 2026-11-15)
  • Metadata errorThe session start times of the linear track files put all sessions of a mouse within minutes of each other (shortest gap 0.9 to 4.6 min in all 30 mice), which cannot be right for 25 min sessions.
    Found by the attempt on Jones et al. 2021 (jones-2021-01, 2026-11-15)
  • OtherSession Chakotay01 (home cage) is deposited twice, as sub-Chakotay_ses-Chakotay01-HomeCage and sub-Chakotay_ses-Chakotay01_HomeCage, with the same LFP length, spike count and start time.
    Found by the attempt on Jones et al. 2021 (jones-2021-01, 2026-11-15)
  • OtherHome cage sessions of cohort 3 last about 25 min and track sessions about 25 min, where the Methods give 60 and 30 min; many cohort 2 mice have 7 home cage sessions where the Methods give 6.
    Found by the attempt on Jones et al. 2021 (jones-2021-01, 2026-11-15)
  • File format errorEvery unit's spike train begins with a spike at exactly 0.0 s (about 31 per session, one per site), which is not a recorded spike.
    Found by the attempt on Jones et al. 2021 (jones-2021-01, 2026-11-15)
  • Description missingThe electrode location labels carry undocumented tokens ("ca1-pyr 1", "mua", "dg-val gc"). They were read as the SWR detection site, the highest-MUA site of a region, and DG sites with confirmed dentate spikes, from the Methods' wording; the dandiset does not say so. The session description says "1 hour after CNO injection" for vehicle sessions too.
    Found by the attempt on Jones et al. 2021 (jones-2021-01, 2026-11-15)
  • OtherOn the linear track, position and LFP agree to within about 2 s: the log theta/delta power ratio correlates with speed at zero lag (mean r 0.134, positive in 141 of 170 sessions) and not with speed shifted by 30 s or more (mean 0.009). The sub-second spike offset found in the home cage could be checked on the track in only 24 of 170 sessions, those with a clear CA1 MUA peak around SWRs in still periods (median lag -0.245 s).
    Found by the attempt on Jones et al. 2021 (jones-2021-01, 2026-11-15)
