    The tract and CB subregion voxels of the paper's FA source data can be located in the dandiset's FA maps.

    6332 of 6332 FA values of the four sheets are found in the map of their monkey and session, within 5e-7, 199 by exact float32 equality and 6133 as the nearest value within 1e-6. 641 values have more than one candidate voxel (17 exact, 624 within 1e-6), so for about one value in ten the voxel itself is not identified; the candidates' values differ by at most 1.6e-6, and the tests use values only. The lists repeat voxels in two ways. Within one monkey, session, side and tract there are 99 repeats in the whole-tract list of N and T, 44 in the subregion list, and 40 and 18 in the lists of D, each the same value listed twice in the paper's sheet. Across the tracts of one session, 233 voxels of the whole-tract list of N and T are listed in two or more tracts (2 across sides), most often UF with FM or CB with UF on one side, as are 71 voxels of the whole-tract list of D, 1 of the N and T subregion list and 18 of the D subregion list (dACC-CB with MCC-CB). Each carries the same value in every tract it is listed in; for 23, 12, 1 and 3 of them the value has more than one candidate voxel, so they may be two voxels of equal value, and the others are one voxel in two tract lists, where the paper's tracts overlap. No voxel is matched by two different values. So the paper's voxel n counts some voxels twice within a tract, and some voxels enter the tests in more than one tract. Pre and post values are both found in the deposited maps, although these lie each in their own space. Since the values are the paper's and every tract and subregion selection is the paper's, the FA claims re-run the paper's statistics on its own data.

Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • File format errordataset_description.json is not valid JSON, so BIDS tools cannot read it; the second author name is in typographic quotes and a comma is missing after the Description value.
  • Description missingNothing in the dandiset says what the MRI files hold. The files in MRI/FC are single volumes, the paper's normalized seed maps (Fisher z with the seed in area 25 of the stimulated or implanted hemisphere, divided by a session maximum), on a 1.5 mm grid in NMT space; the files in MRI/FA are FA maps, each in its own session's space. This was found only by matching the values to the paper's source data in DANDI:001773. The normalizing maximum is not in the deposited maps (their maxima are 0.55 to 1.38), and the seed of the control animals is not stated in the files.
  • Data missingNo tract masks, CB subregion masks or target ROI masks are deposited. Which voxels the paper analysed can be recovered only from the source data of DANDI:001773, by position for the seed maps and by matching FA values for the FA maps.
  • Data missingNo EPI data are deposited, only one seed map per animal and session, although the archive's description lists "Functional MRI (EPI; FC) data". The whole-brain connectivity findings (Fig. 4, Extended Data Fig. 10c) and the voxel-wise test of the control group cannot be tested.
  • Data missingThe cell counts behind the immunofluorescence results and the per-axon measurements behind the g-ratio are not in the dandiset, which holds the images only. Counts made here automatically agree with the paper's in level for the MCC-CB of N and T and the dACC-CB, less well in the UF-FM overlap and for monkey D, and give a significant dACC-CB side difference the paper's counts do not.
  • Methods not availableFor the paper's authors, how were cells counted in FIJI? The Methods give "section-specific intensity thresholds for each channel" but not the threshold method, any background subtraction, size filter or watershed split. Here an automatic threshold per section and channel was used with objects of at least 10 or 50 pixels; no single reading matches the paper's counts in all four regions (10 pixels in the MCC-CB and dACC-CB of N and T, 50 pixels in the MCC-CB of D, neither in the UF-FM overlap). fig6c-sham-cc1-no-change depends on the size (p = 0.31 at 10 pixels, 0.044 at 50) and is blocked; ed6a-dacc-cb-no-change is not reproduced under every reading, though the paper's counts give p = 0.09.
  • OtherEM/TR-1-8r.tif is a byte-identical copy of EM/TR-1-8.tif (same blob); the paper's per-axon sheet names only TR-1-8.
  • OtherIF/IF_CC1_UF_FM_overlap_mkNT holds six fields for section N122, right hemisphere, where the design has five per section; the paper's sheet has no row for N122 RW6. Two other file names carry a suffix (N112 LW1B, N453-RW-6r) that the paper's sheets do not explain.
  • Description missingThe immunofluorescence TIFFs carry no pixel size (the resolution tag is the default 72 per inch) and no channel names; red holding CC1 and blue holding DAPI is inferred from the paper's labels. The sections differ in bit depth (8-bit for N603, N613, N623; 16-bit elsewhere).
  • OtherThe paper's per-voxel FA lists repeat voxels. Within one tract, side and session the same value is listed twice (99 times in the whole-tract list of N and T, 44 in its subregion list, 40 and 18 in the lists of D), so the voxel n of the FA tests counts some voxels twice. Other voxels are listed, with the same value, in two or more tracts of one session (233 in the whole-tract list of N and T, mostly UF with FM or CB with UF on one side; 71, 1 and 18 in the other lists). The paper does not explain either kind of repeat.
  • Paper inconsistentExtended Data Fig. 6b legend gives the UF-FM DAPI side effect as F 1,76 = 0, P = 0.9611, partial eta squared 4.97e-05. The paper's own per-field counts (CC1_UF-FMoverlap_data_a.xlsx, DANDI:001773) give F(1,76) = 0.0038, p = 0.9511, and the stated partial eta squared fits F = 0.0038, so 0.9611 is a misprint for 0.9511.
  • OtherAll six FA maps reach 1.21 to 1.22, above the bound of 1 that FA has by definition; these voxels lie outside the paper's tract lists.
