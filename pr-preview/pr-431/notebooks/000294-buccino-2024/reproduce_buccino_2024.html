ecode-protocol-structure¶

Each run applies the eCode protocols with the sweep numbers, step durations and amplitudes (in percent of the estimated rheobase) that the Methods list.

Criterion: in every run, all eight protocols with the Methods' sweep counts and durations (within 10%) and every step amplitude within 10% of its stated percentage of the IDthres scale (sweep k of IDthres at 50 + 4k %); IV under reading (a) 11 steps from -140 to 60% or (b) 9 steps from -140 to 20%. Amplitudes are measured above the holding level. For a stated 0% (IV, reading a) a relative tolerance is not defined; 10% of the IV increment (2% of the scale) is used (deviation).

def scale_of(s, r):
    ids = [d for d in patch[s] if d["run"] == r and d["protocol"] == "idthres"]
    amps = [mm.segments(d)[1][2] for d in ids]
    return float(np.median([a / ((50 + 4 * k) / 100) for k, a in enumerate(amps)])), amps

def ok_amp(meas, exp):
    return abs(meas - exp) <= (0.1 * abs(exp) if exp != 0 else 2.0)

How the analysis differs from the paper's. The template follows Methods 5.7.1 with the code's parameters, on the filtered MEA series of the dandiset, after the code's removal of blank channels; the filter is applied forward and backward. The holding potential is read before the stimulus onset found in the stimulus, 50 ms before the code's ton. The ground-truth model is simulated with BluePyOpt 1.14.26 in place of 1.13.88: the AIS timing, the template check and the evaluators use its LFPy simulator as the paper's notebooks do, and the rheobase and the fixed-step AIS check its NEURON simulator. The AIS order is read between the samples of the code's 0.05 ms cut-outs (an assumption), where at the sample the distal and middle AIS tie. Table 1 is counted on the code's evaluators.

What was found about the dataset. The patch voltages are in mV and the currents in pA, though the files say volts and amperes. Most protocols are sampled at 20.41 kHz and APWaveform at 125 kHz, where the paper says 20 kHz and about 200 kHz. The icephys grouping tables leave out the first sweep of most protocols and every PosCheops sweep. Many MEA channels are constant for part of a run (117 to 132 per run). In the files, the stimulus starts 50 ms before the code's ton timings. No culture age is given. The dandiset's metadata cite the paper as doi:10.1162/neco_a_01676, not 10.1162/neco_a_01672.
