  • fig1gh-aging-reduction-smaller-than-5xfad

    Normal aging also reduces CA2 PNN coverage in control mice, but the loss is significantly more pronounced in 5XFAD mice.

    Note that normal aging also presented with a reduction in PNN coverage, yet the loss of PNNs was significantly more pronounced in 5XFAD mice

    Results 3.1, paragraph 2; Figure 1G and 1H

    How it will be judged

    PNN-AREA per slice, two-way ANOVA on genotype and age with Tukey's multiple comparisons over all eight cells (Figure 1 legend). The paper does not say which comparisons show the aging effect, so the reading of the reported finding is any older control group (6, 9 or 12 M) below control at 3 M with Tukey p < 0.05. Nor does it say which result makes the 5XFAD loss "significantly more pronounced"; the two-way ANOVA named in the legend tests this as the genotype by age interaction, which is used. Reproduced when the aging decline holds, the interaction has p < 0.05, and at each of 6, 9 and 12 M the 5XFAD loss relative to 5XFAD at 3 M is larger than the control's loss relative to control at 3 M. Partially reproduced when the aging decline holds and the 5XFAD loss is larger but the interaction is not significant. The same is reported for PNN-INTENSITY.
