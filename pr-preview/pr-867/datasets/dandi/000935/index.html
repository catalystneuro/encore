Dataset version 0.240319.2026 · tested 2026-11-16 · 8 open findings

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds only the in vivo experiment of Fig. 7. The culture MEA recordings behind Figs. 2 to 6 and Supplementary Figs. 2 to 4, and the IVIS and confocal images of Supplementary Figs. 5 and 6, are not in it; the article publishes only the per-electrode counts of the ladder plots, and the paper offers raw data on request.
    Found by the attempt on Prakash et al. 2022 (prakash-2022-01, 2026-11-16)
  • Description missingThe electrode table's Position column (x 0 or 50, z 0 to 775 um) has an autogenerated description and does not say whether z is depth below the surface or height above the deepest contact, so the laminar depth of each channel cannot be established.
    Found by the attempt on Prakash et al. 2022 (prakash-2022-01, 2026-11-16)
  • Description missingThe position of the 50-pixel ROI the paper averages next to the electrode shank is not recorded, so the paper's bioluminescence series and onsets cannot be recomputed; the whole-frame mean was used.
    Found by the attempt on Prakash et al. 2022 (prakash-2022-01, 2026-11-16)
  • OtherThe ElectricalSeries description says the data were acquired at 20 kHz and downsampled to 10 kHz; the Methods say the data were acquired at 30 kHz. The images of interlum_4 are 1024 x 1024 pixels and those of interlum_ctrl_2 684 x 697, where the Methods give 512 x 512.
    Found by the attempt on Prakash et al. 2022 (prakash-2022-01, 2026-11-16)
  • OtherThe image series of interlum_4 has 1111 frames, 58 of them entirely zero (between 810 and 891 s), and ends 866 s after the bioluminescence onset, where the paper's Fig. 7b series cover 1200 s after onset for all five imaged mice.
    Found by the attempt on Prakash et al. 2022 (prakash-2022-01, 2026-11-16)
  • OtherIn interlum_ctrl_4 the bioluminescence onset (206 s) comes 8 s before the recorded start of CTZ infusion (214 s); in the other four imaged mice the onset follows the infusion start by 16 to 103 s. The infusion interval or the image timing of this file may be offset.
    Found by the attempt on Prakash et al. 2022 (prakash-2022-01, 2026-11-16)
  • OtherThe whole-frame bioluminescence series of the three Opsin (-) mice match the paper's Supplementary Data 7 series cntrl 1 to 3 (r = 0.975 to 0.990, as interlum_ctrl_2, ctrl_4 and ctrl_5). Those of the two imaged Opsin (+) mice match exp 3 at most at r = 0.906, and no file matches exp 1 (r at most 0.68), so the dandiset's imaged Opsin (+) recordings may not be the ones shown in Fig. 7b. On mean gamma power over contacts, interlum_8 (0.349 dB) matches the paper's exp 2 (0.351 dB, Supplementary Data 7, Fig. 7e), a mouse absent from the paper's imaged series, while interlum_8 has images; interlum_ctrl_4 (-0.234) matches cntrl 2 (-0.230), as its bioluminescence does. Which dandiset mouse is which animal of the paper is not documented.
    Found by the attempt on Prakash et al. 2022 (prakash-2022-01, 2026-11-16)
  • OtherThe recordings are small for unfiltered cortical signals stored in volts, a median contact RMS of 1.9 to 6.7 uV per file, and the MUA band crosses -3 SD at baseline at 11 to 14 per s on every contact, no more than band-limited Gaussian noise. This is consistent with a missing scale factor or with recordings dominated by noise in the spike band; relative measures are unaffected by scale.
    Found by the attempt on Prakash et al. 2022 (prakash-2022-01, 2026-11-16)
