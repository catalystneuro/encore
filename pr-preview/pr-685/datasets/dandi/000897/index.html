Dataset version 0.240605.1710 · tested 2026-10-31 · 7 open findings

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds 15 of the paper's 32 recording sessions (monkey A 6 of 17, monkey M 9 of 15), with 364 and 729 units against the paper's 614 and 864. The behavioural training and generalization sessions of Fig. 1c,e and the behavioural sessions behind Fig. 4g are not in it.
    Found by the attempt on Neupane et al. 2024 (neupane-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Description missingAbout half of monkey M's MNAV trials have seqq 3, whose vectors come in steps of 0.975 s (0.975 to 4.875 s) with ta/0.65 not equal to target minus curr. The paper describes a landmark spacing of 0.65 s for both monkeys, and the seqq description ("which sequence, 1, 2, 3 or 4") does not explain it.
    Found by the attempt on Neupane et al. 2024 (neupane-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • File format errorIn 10 of the 15 sessions (A 08152019 and all monkey M sessions) the eye and joystick timestamps are on another clock than the trial table and the spikes; they start between 3,209 and 1,559,866 s while trials start near 4 s. The offset is not documented. Estimated here from the joystick deflection, it is 3,326 to 1,561,450 s, and once it is removed the joystick moves at the recorded joystick onsets.
    Found by the attempt on Neupane et al. 2024 (neupane-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • OtherIn three sessions (A 09172019, M 04132021, M 04142021) no unit changes its rate between 1.0 to 0.6 s and 0.4 to 0.1 s before joystick offset (paired t-test, p < 0.01), while 2 to 89% of units do in the others, and while preparing the notebook their population rate was flat around every task event. In M 04132021 the spikes end at 9,306 s while the trials run to 18,599 s. That spikes and trials share a clock could not be established in these sessions.
    Found by the attempt on Neupane et al. 2024 (neupane-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Description missingThe inter-trial interval is not marked. Trial rows are contiguous, from stimulus onset (start_time equals stim1onttl) to the next stimulus onset, so the ITI segments longer than 4 s that the paper compares with the navigation epoch cannot be located.
    Found by the attempt on Neupane et al. 2024 (neupane-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Description missingRows with attempt 2 are trials that took two attempts. Their joystick times and tp are consistent with the first attempt (the joystick is pressed about 0.5 s after the go cue, as on single-attempt trials, and the row runs 2.4 to 5.4 s past the joystick offset, against 0.9 to 1.3 s), and the second attempt is not timed. The files do not say which attempt the row holds, nor what succ and numrepeat mean on these rows.
    Found by the attempt on Neupane et al. 2024 (neupane-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Selection not documentedMonkey A units carry phy's quality label, and 103 of the 364 are labelled noise; monkey M units carry only Kilosort's KSLabel. The paper analysed all single and multi-units, and the files do not say which units are those. The unit columns amp, ch, depth, fr, n_spikes and sh have no description.
    Found by the attempt on Neupane et al. 2024 (neupane-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
