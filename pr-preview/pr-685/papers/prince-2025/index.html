Prince et al. 2025 · Nature Communications

New information triggers prospective codes to adapt for flexible navigation

doi:10.1038/s41467-025-60122-8 · paper key prince-2025

Datasets

  • DANDI:001371

The paragraph is below for you to save: Most behavioral and CA1 findings held up in this data, but the prefrontal choice findings could not be tested and some claims of no difference did not hold. CA1 decoding of both goal arms rose on switch trials after the update cue, and goal coding was stronger in CA1 than in prefrontal cortex. The prefrontal switch from old to new choice could not be tested, because the movement-based choice estimate would take about 10 hours to compute. Prefrontal goal-arm decoding rose slightly on switch trials, which the paper reports as absent, and in the first theta half cycle central-arm decoding exceeded goal arms, where the paper reports the opposite.
