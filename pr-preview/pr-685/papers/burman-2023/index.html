  • ✓1 reproduced

The paper's central patch-clamp findings, that synaptic GABAA receptor reversal is more depolarized and inhibition shunting in awake cortex and that blocking local network activity undoes this, could not be tested, as those recordings are not deposited. Its Neuropixels finding that neurons are less synchronous and more variable in spike timing awake than under isoflurane held up in direction and test, with means slightly off. Exact numbers come out only with the firing rate the paper's code computes, not the Methods' rate (144 units excluded against 203). The network model's shunting inhibition gave lower synchrony, higher entropy and better decoding in every run, two means just outside tight tolerances. Higher awake firing and the rate-regressed control also held.

Summary written by the agent that recorded the attempt, from its claims and the agents’ reports. A failure to reproduce may be a failure of the agent.

    Results, Shunting inhibition promotes local network desynchronization and response flexibility, paragraph 2; Figure S7 (not read); Methods, Characterization of stimulus-evoked spiking responses, paragraph 2

    Mean decoding accuracy 0.9848 shunting against 0.9315 hyperpolarizing (chance 0.01). D1, paired t test of the 20 per-run accuracies, t = 16.42, p = 1.11e-12 in this execution; D2, shunting higher in 20 of 20 runs. The spike counts the classifier uses are small integers, and in 0.80 to 0.81 of the test trials under shunting and 0.90 to 0.92 under hyperpolarizing the fifth-nearest training trial is at the same distance as the sixth, so the accuracy depends on how ties are broken, which differs between runners. Executions on different runners, with the same per-run rate, entropy and synchrony, printed t = 16.92, p = 6.52e-13 (the first execution and the one verifier round 2 read) and t = 16.42, p = 1.11e-12 (the round-1 revision and this one). With tied neighbours chosen by five other random orders of the training trials, a run's accuracy moves by at most 0.0020 (shunting) and 0.0045 (hyperpolarizing), D1 gives t = 16.35 to 17.22 (p at most 1.20e-12) and D2 holds in 20 of 20 runs each time, so the outcome does not depend on the tie-breaking.

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • Seeds are fixed (1 to 20, one per run) where the gist sets none, so the runs are other draws of the paper's model; this cannot change the model, only the sample.
    • The stimulus is held as one periodic 2,500 ms set instead of the gist's 4 GB whole-run array, to fit in memory; a small run with both gives identical spike trains, and the gist itself run unmodified on that small run gives the same spikes and the same per-neuron rate, entropy and synchrony (data check).
    • The cross-validation shuffle is seeded with the run's seed (the gist sets none). The seeds fix the spikes and the folds but not the accuracy exactly, as the classifier's ties between equally distant trials are broken differently on different runners; the notebook shows D1 and D2 under five other tie-breaking orders, and all give the same outcome.

    Results, Awake cortex exhibits more depolarized synaptic EGABAAR and shunting fast synaptic inhibition, paragraph 3; Results, Network activity in awake cortex raises synaptic EGABAAR, paragraph 2; Figure S4 (not read); Methods, Simulating measurements of synaptic EGABAAR

    The claim rests on the paper's NEURON model of an L2/3 pyramidal neuron and a Matlab analysis of its simulated ramps (github.com/richardjburman/burman-et-al-2022---Awake-EGABAAR at commit a404c3e, hoc files and NEURON_Egaba_Analysis.m). The model is public and the Methods ("Simulating measurements of synaptic EGABAAR", Table 2) give its membrane properties per state, the synapses, the E_GABA values (-85 to -35 mV in 5 mV steps) and the ramp protocol, so the ramp estimates could be simulated under the anesthetized and awake properties. What is open is what they would be compared with. The measure Figure S4 shows and the comparison behind "could not be explained" are in the Figure S4 legend, which was not in the text read, and the claims file gives no criterion; a choice such as the estimation error under each state's properties against the measured difference of Figure 2H would be arbitrary. So the claim is blocked as methods_underspecified and was not simulated (a further obstacle, the analysis is Matlab, and NEURON is not in the entry's environment). The question is for the paper's authors (findings, methods_unavailable). Neither part uses the dandiset.

Notes on the paper

What the attempts found about the paper itself: a method or rule it does not give, a place where it disagrees with itself, published code that does something else. What they found about the data is on each dataset’s page.

  • Published code disagreesThe paper's analysis code computes the evoked firing rate used for the exclusion (below 0.1 Hz or above 200 Hz) as the sum of the spikes' latencies in ms (np.sum of spike_times) over trials times 10 ms, where the Methods describe an average evoked firing rate. With this rate the dataset gives the paper's sample exactly (662 kept, 203 excluded, median 34 per recording, 1 to 93) and its Figure 4D means (synchrony 0.325 and 0.393, entropy 1.347 and 1.111); with the spike-count rate it gives 721 kept and 144 excluded. The 200 Hz bound thus acted on spike-count rates near 45 Hz.
  • Selection not documentedVersion 0.230317.0039 holds 18 files with units and electrical stimulation awake and under isoflurane; the paper uses 17. sub-599017_ses-20220210 (943 units, SSp stimulation, 718 awake and 720 isoflurane valid trials) is not in the paper's code list, and the paper does not say why. Six further files hold EEG only, with no units.
  • Published code disagreesThe Methods select regular spiking units with a waveform duration of 0.4 ms or more, the code more than 0.4 ms, and the Methods name the areas SSp-ll, SSp-tr and MOs where the code takes every unit whose location begins with the stimulated region. Neither difference changes the units selected in this dataset.
  • Methods not availableFor the multi-compartment model of Figure S4 (figs4-intrinsic-properties), which measure of the simulated ramp estimates of synaptic E_GABAAR, under the anesthetized and awake membrane properties of Table 2, is compared with what, and what result shows that the membrane properties "could not explain" the measured difference? The Methods give the model and protocol; the comparison is only in the Figure S4 legend, not in the text this attempt read.
