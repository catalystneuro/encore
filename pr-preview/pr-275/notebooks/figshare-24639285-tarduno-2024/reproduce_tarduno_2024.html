def factors(df, step):
    g = df[df.step == step]
    out = {}
    for c in ("moment", "dec", "inc"):
        v = g[c].abs().to_numpy()
        for v0 in v:
            for f0 in np.arange(90, 111) / 100:
                r = v / (v0 / f0)
                if np.all(np.abs(r * 100 - np.round(r * 100)) < 1e-6 * r * 100):
                    out[c] = np.round(r, 2).tolist()
                    break
            if c in out:
                break
    return out

Checks of the description against the data:

  • Columns: every sheet has sample, step, moment (emu), declination and inclination, as the Data Records say, except ss48, which has an extra column CSD (empty or 0) before the moment. The moment column is found by name.
  • Specimen names: the glass sheet ss11 is named s11 in Table 2; the sheet g2 labels its rows 64455g2. Twelve crystal workbooks match the twelve names of Table 1.
  • Step labels follow the description (#.0, #.1, #.2 for the glass; #.3, #.4 for the crystals); NRM appears as both 0 and 0.0 (ss27, ss38).
  • Laboratory field: the glass sheets give 18 uT (10 sheets), 15 uT (11) or 14.4 uT (g2); the paper gives 18 uT only for Figure 1, whose specimens (ss19, ss43) are 18 uT sheets. The crystal sheets give 20 and 40 uT for #.1 and #.4, as the Methods say.
  • Replicates: each glass step was measured 2 to 19 times, each crystal NRM 1 to 3 times, each 590 zero-field step 3 or 4 times, and each crystal field-on step once. The paper does not say how repeated measurements enter Table 2; both the mean vector and the first measurement are run below.
  • Four glass sheets (g2, ss8, ss39, ss48) do not hold repeated measurements: every step's rows are one row times factors near 1. The analysis keeps them, as the deposit presents them, and the results that use them say so; their mean vector is close to the base row.
  • Values out of range: one inclination of -136 deg (ss19, 10 mT, almost certainly -13.6), one declination of 361.7 deg (ss8 NRM, a scaled copy), and an ss18 row at 250 C with no values. The first two are used as they are (converted to vectors, a declination of 361.7 is 1.7 deg); the ss19 value is in an AF step no analysis below uses; the empty row is dropped.

table2-fit-statistics¶

For the nine failed glass specimens with a line fit (ss19, ss43, ss22, ss41, ss6, ss9, ss10, ss30, ss34), the number of points, temperature range, MAD, R2 and pTRM check differences are as in Table 2, which lists the criteria each failed.

Criterion: per specimen, the paired steps in the Table 2 range, NRM against TRM gained, R2 of the least-squares line, MAD under readings (a) free, (b) anchored, (c) free on the three-point running mean, and pTRM check percentages under readings (a) over the original pTRM, (b) over the length of the fitted segment, with the check's pTRM referred to (i) the field-off step just before it or (ii) the field-off step at its temperature. A value matches when one reading puts it within tolerance. Reproduced when n matches for all nine and at least 80% of the MAD, R2 and pTRM values match; partially for 50 to 80%.

The paper does not say how the repeated measurements of a step are combined. Both the mean vector and the first measurement are run; the mean is adopted as an assumption (see the end of this section).

def step_scatter(df):
    rows = []

# Steps of the glass sheets whose repeated measurements fall in two groups of directions, like ss6
# 250.1. Each step with 4 or more rows is split in two by the angle to the two most distant rows
# (then 10 rounds of reassignment to the group means); listed when both groups have 2 or more rows,
# their means are more than 45 deg apart and every row is within a third of that angle of its group's mean.
def two_groups(X):
    u = X / np.linalg.norm(X, axis=1, keepdims=True)
    i, j = np.unravel_index(np.argmin(u @ u.T), (len(u), len(u)))
    lab = (u @ u[j] > u @ u[i]).astype(int)
    for _ in range(10):
        cen = [u[lab == k].sum(axis=0) for k in (0, 1)]
        lab = (u @ cen[1] / np.linalg.norm(cen[1]) > u @ cen[0] / np.linalg.norm(cen[0])).astype(int)
    sep = A.angle(cen[0], cen[1])
    within = max(A.angle(x, cen[k]) for k in (0, 1) for x in u[lab == k])
    return int((lab == 0).sum()), int((lab == 1).sum()), sep, within

# Curvature of the NRM lost against TRM gained plots, which the criterion does not test. A circle is
# fit by least squares (Kasa's algebraic fit) to the points of each Table 2 range, with NRM and TRM
# gained divided by the NRM of the first point; curvature is 1 / radius, positive when the points
# sag below the line through the first and last point. With three points (ss41, ss43) the circle is
# exact. ss19 and ss43 are given for comparison.
def curvature(x, y):
    M = np.column_stack([x, y, np.ones_like(x)])
    a, b, c = np.linalg.lstsq(M, x ** 2 + y ** 2, rcond=None)[0]
    cx, cy = a / 2, b / 2
    r = np.sqrt(c + cx ** 2 + cy ** 2)
    mid = len(x) // 2
    chord_y = y[0] + (y[-1] - y[0]) * (x[mid] - x[0]) / (x[-1] - x[0])
    return np.sign(chord_y - y[mid]) / r

How the analysis differs from the paper's: the paper used PmagPy and gives no formulas; MAD, R2 and pTRM checks are computed here by the claims file's readings, and repeated measurements are averaged, an assumption that reproduces most of Table 2 and that the deposit's Supplementary Figure 1 supports. No test of randomness is named by the paper; Watson's test is the claims file's choice.

What was found about the dataset:

  • The "replicates" of four glass sheets (g2, ss8, ss39, ss48) are one measurement multiplied by factors near 1 (0.95 to 1.03), applied to moment, declination and inclination; they are not repeated measurements.
  • One inclination of -136 deg (ss19, 10 mT), one declination of 361.7 deg (ss8), an empty row (ss18), and an extra column in ss48.
  • The number of repeated measurements varies from 2 to 19 per step, eleven steps of seven sheets (ss6's 250.1 among them) hold two groups of directions more than 45 deg apart, and the deposit does not say how repeated measurements were combined.
  • The glass laboratory field varies between sheets (14.4, 15 and 18 uT); the paper states only 18 uT.
  • The Technical Validation example for 14053g1 uses the numbers of 12040g1.
  • Table 1's M590 is the moment of the 590.1 measurement alone for all 12 crystals, while the Methods define the TRM as the vector difference of the field-on and field-off steps.
  • The deposit's Supplementary Figure 1 plots TRM gained from mean vectors and NRM as the mean of the rows' moments for 12 of its 14 non-synthetic specimens, and the pTRM checks against the field-off step before them. Its points for the four synthetic sheets differ from every combination of their deposited rows by 0.5% or more.
  • The crystals' 590 C zero-field moments are all above the 8e-11 emu threshold the paper uses, so the Table 1 maximum intensities rest on a threshold below the level the null state reaches.
