  • units-recorded

    452 caudodorsal LS units and 178 CA1 units were recorded in six rats, of which 165 CA1 and 378 LS cells met the spike rate and track coverage criteria.

    we recorded the activity of 452 caudodorsal LS units and 178 CA1 hippocampus units in six male Long Evans rats

    Results, opening paragraph; Results, Place fields are less abundant, paragraph 5

    How it will be judged

    Units are counted from the data set's sorted clusters by region. The criteria are a mean rate of at least 0.05 Hz above 12 cm/s and full track coverage (Results paragraph 1 of the first section; Methods, Bits/spike). What full coverage means is open; the readings are (a) the code's rule (a session's 5 cm occupancy chart has at least 1950 bins, as written in bitsperspike.m) and (b) every arm, the stem and both T junctions visited at 12 cm/s or more in the session. Reproduced when the four counts are within tolerance under one reading.

  • fig2b-poisson-control

    Artificial LS units firing as Poisson processes at the recorded LS units' mean rates carry less spatial information than the recorded units (mean 0.43 against 0.73 bits/spike; 7.96% above 0.8), and their bits/spike distribution differs from the recorded one.

    The distribution of the bits/spike for the artificial units was highly significantly different than the distribution of bits/spike for actual units (KS test, p<10 −15 ), and only 7.96% of artificial units had bits/spike measurements of greater than 0.8. The average bits/spike for the artificial units was 0.43

    Results, Place fields are less abundant in the LS, paragraph 1; Figure 2B inset and legend

    How it will be judged

    The paper says only that 454 artificial units were made "using Poisson firing and the mean firing rates of the recorded LS units"; no script for it was found in the code. Open, and could change the outcome. Readings (a) a homogeneous Poisson train at each LS unit's mean rate over the whole session, bits/spike then computed exactly as for recorded units; (b) the same at the unit's mean rate above 12 cm/s, laid over the fast periods only. Which recorded units the 454 are built from is also open (454 is near the 452 LS units recorded, not the 378 included); the readings are (i) one artificial unit per recorded LS unit, 452 in all, and (ii) the 378 included LS cells, cycled to 454. The t-test's df (818) matches neither 454 + 378 - 2 = 830 nor 454 + 452 - 2 = 904; it is noted, not used. Reproduced when, under one reading, both values are within tolerance, the KS test gives p < 1e-15 and the t-test p < 1e-10 with recorded units higher.
