Dataset version 0.220126.1905 · tested 2026-10-21 · 13 open findings

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds none of the photoinhibition (Figure 1) or photoactivation (Figure 5, Extended Data Figures 3, 6 and 7) data, and no histology. Nine of the paper's findings cannot be tested for this reason. The Ai32 mice that are in it have no record of light.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2015 (li-2015-01, 2026-10-21)
  • OtherEvery unit labelled PT (45) or IT (27) is a second copy of a unit labelled Pyr in the same file, with an identical spike train, so the 1,441 units are 1,369 distinct units. Counting units by label double-counts the tagged neurons.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2015 (li-2015-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Selection not documentedThe 39 units of intermediate spike width (0.35 to 0.45 ms) that the paper recorded and excluded are not in the dandiset, which does not say so; no deposited unit has an intermediate width.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2015 (li-2015-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Data missingThe activated neurons that failed the collision test (22 from the pons, 106 from ALM) and the 7 that could not be tested carry no label, so the tagging outcome can be read only for the 72 neurons that passed.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2015 (li-2015-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Description missingIn 75 sessions the antidromic tagging light was delivered during behavioural trials (4,332 of 14,388 correct trials in those sessions). The trial tables do not flag these trials; they can be found only from the laser traces under stimulus/presentation, whose series have the description 'no description'.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2015 (li-2015-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Metadata errorThe laser power series are labelled 'watts' but hold values of 47.1 and 82 at the pulses, the milliwatts of the Methods (47 to 82 mW); the AOM input traces, also labelled 'watts', run from 0 to 4.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2015 (li-2015-01, 2026-10-21)
  • OtherThe extracellular trial tables hold 1,540 trials with a lick instruction that have no behavioural events; 1,447 of them are marked 'right', 'hit' and 'no early', last exactly 5.5 s, and 1,345 hold no spike from any unit. They look like placeholders for removed trials filled with default values. Taken as given they raise performance and bias trial counts towards lick right. 1,502 of them are in the 75 sessions with tagging light, 754 (49%) in the first or last tenth of a session, and 7 in the Ai32 mouse 218693, so they are not the photoactivation trials.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2015 (li-2015-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Description missingThe imaging files flag no trial as an early lick (all False, stored as booleans, where the extracellular files use 'early'/'no early'), so early-lick trials cannot be excluded from the imaging analyses.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2015 (li-2015-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Description missingNo imaging plane gives its depth, so the layer 5 PT and IT populations of Figure 4g, h cannot be selected; the files label 843 included cells IT against the paper's 542 in layer 5.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2015 (li-2015-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Metadata errorThe imaging mice are given the genotype 'Rosa-lsl-tdtomato' (one) or none (three); the Methods use C57Bl/6Crl mice. The ROI labels include 'Pyr' (23) and 'interneuron' (9), which the paper does not describe, and the `included` flag is described only as 'whether to include this roi into later analyses', without its rule; 424 included ROIs are not 5% brighter than their neuropil by the median ratio.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2015 (li-2015-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Description missingThe `lick_trace` series (10 kHz) is described only as the tongue movement when licking; nothing says it is the photodiode signal used for reaction times or how a reaction time is read from it. Its level and direction differ between sessions (median baselines 0.069 to 4.83 a.u.; it falls after the go cue in 15 of 99 sessions), and nothing describes the gain or polarity.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2015 (li-2015-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Other66 extracellular trials without an early lick have a go cue that is not 2.6 s after the sample event (65 sooner, down to 0.03 s, and one later, 2.68 s), against 2.6 s in all others, and 75 have no go cue; they are dropped here.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2015 (li-2015-01, 2026-10-21)
  • OtherSpikes are stored almost only within 5.5 s trial windows (from 0.57 s before the sample event); in 97 of 99 sessions at least 99% of the spikes fall in a window. In two sessions (mice 214429 and 219031) 24% and 30% fall between trials, in gaps between the windows. So baselines before the trial and inter-trial activity cannot be measured in most sessions, and in early-lick trials, where the go cue comes later, part of the response epoch is not stored.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2015 (li-2015-01, 2026-10-21)
