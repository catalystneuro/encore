Dataset version 0.261106.2004 · tested 2026-11-15 · 12 open findings

  • Data missingNewThe NWB file of session EE.088 in DANDI:000067 has no acquisition data (no wideband ElectricalSeries), though CRCNS pfc-2 holds EE.088.dat.gz; every other working memory session has its 104 or 101 channel signal.
    Found by the attempt on Fujisawa et al. 2008 (fujisawa-2008-03, 2026-11-15)
  • Data missingNewSessions EE.188 and EE.210 in DANDI:000067 have no trials table and no linearised position, though the CRCNS behavior files of both sessions hold them (SessionNP and the extended whl file); the other 11 working memory sessions have both.
    Found by the attempt on Fujisawa et al. 2008 (fujisawa-2008-03, 2026-11-15)
  • Description missingNewIn 11 of 13 working memory sessions of DANDI:000067 every unit has cell_type 'not classified in the source' and lab_cell_number -1, although CRCNS pfc-2 behaviors.zip holds the lab's cell list and cell type codes (CellsInfo) for all of them. The NWB units can be linked to the lab's cells only through shank and cluster_id (spikeph), and then their spike times are identical to the lab's for every cell the lab gives spike times for (615 of the 627 used; the other 12 are EE.188 cells of the example file that EE188_Behavior_PFC.mat does not list).
    Found by the attempt on Fujisawa et al. 2008 (fujisawa-2008-03, 2026-11-15)
  • Metadata errorNewIn the three GG sessions channels 97 and 98, which the CRCNS description gives as the right and left nose-poke sensors for rats FF and GG, carry no poke signal (equal activity on left and right pokes), and no channel from 97 to 101 does; the NWB files label channels 97 to 101 'neural'. In EE and FF the sensors are on 97 and 98 as described, and the trial's side sensor is the more active in every trial.
    Found by the attempt on Fujisawa et al. 2008 (fujisawa-2008-03, 2026-11-15); also about CRCNS:pfc-2
  • Description missingNewDANDI:000067 does not say which channels of the GG sessions hold the nose-poke sensors; channels 97 to 101 are labelled 'neural', the CRCNS map (97 right, 98 left) does not fit the GG signals, and no channel from 97 on carries a poke signal, so nose pokes of rat GG can be timed only from the lab's SessionNP.
    Found by the attempt on Fujisawa et al. 2008 (fujisawa-2008-03, 2026-11-15)
  • Metadata errorNewThe FF sessions in DANDI:000067 have 104 channels, labelled as rat EE's layout (99 and 100 not in use, 101 and 102 encoders, 104 sync), while the CRCNS description gives FF 101 channels with encoders on 99 and 100 and sync on 101.
    Found by the attempt on Fujisawa et al. 2008 (fujisawa-2008-03, 2026-11-15)
  • Metadata errorStill presentThe two CRCNS lists of EE.188's cells disagree. EE188_Behavior_PFC.mat lists 105 cells (87 pyramidal, 18 interneurons); EE188_example.mat, which the description names as the paper's Figure 2a and 3d session, lists 117 (33 pyramidal, 15 interneurons, 69 unknown), and the types agree for 42 of the 105 common cells. DANDI:000067 uses the 105 cell list, so 12 of the paper's 117 cells are 'not used in the paper' there.
    Found by the attempt on Fujisawa et al. 2008 (fujisawa-2008-03, 2026-11-15); also about CRCNS:pfc-2
  • Data missingNewOnly correct trials are listed (SessionNP, the whl trial numbers and the NWB trials tables), so the task performance and any analysis of error trials cannot be made from either dataset.
    Found by the attempt on Fujisawa et al. 2008 (fujisawa-2008-03, 2026-11-15); also about CRCNS:pfc-2
  • Description missingStill presentNeither dataset gives the layer of each rat's probe (the paper has three rats in layer 2/3 and one in layer 5, named E to H in Supplementary Figure 1), and they hold three rats (EE, FF, GG) whose names are not linked to the paper's; no rat holds the paper's 222 layer 5 units and no session the 142 cells of the layer 5 session of Figure 2a and Supplementary Figure 11.
    Found by the attempt on Fujisawa et al. 2008 (fujisawa-2008-03, 2026-11-15); also about CRCNS:pfc-2
  • Data missingStill presentTen of the 13 working memory sessions have a single tracked LED (the whl file's second LED is zero, and the NWB files have led_1 only), so head direction, which the paper measures from two LEDs, exists only for EE.152, EE.165 and EE.188.
    Found by the attempt on Fujisawa et al. 2008 (fujisawa-2008-03, 2026-11-15); also about CRCNS:pfc-2
  • Description missingNewThe linearised position d is described as 0 when the rat is not in a working memory trial and 1 at the end of the last nose poke, but it is nonzero (positions 0.02 to 0.05 above 1) through each trial's nose poke: 94% to 100% of the frames with d > 0 outside the listed trials (18% to 67% of all frames with d > 0) fall between the first poke start and the trial start, and all carry the trial's number. The description does not say so.
    Found by the attempt on Fujisawa et al. 2008 (fujisawa-2008-03, 2026-11-15); also about CRCNS:pfc-2
  • Description missingStill presentThe CRCNS description documents a variable 'Cells' (units whose maximum rate in the trials exceeds 1 Hz) that the Behavior_PFC files do not hold, and does not describe their CellsInfo, the per-session cell list (627 units with EE.188's example file, against 1,373 sorted mPFC units in spikeph and the NWB files). CellsInfo is consistent with the 1 Hz rule (98.9% of listed units reach 1 Hz in the trials, 28.7% of those left out), but the criterion is not stated, and neither dataset marks which units are the paper's 'well-isolated' units.
    Found by the attempt on Fujisawa et al. 2008 (fujisawa-2008-03, 2026-11-15); also about CRCNS:pfc-2
