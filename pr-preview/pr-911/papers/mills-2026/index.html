Summary: Most of the paper's main findings could not be tested in this data, and only one held up fully. Population trajectories of amygdalostriatal neurons to shock and reward cues diverged as reported, but comparisons with other regions and unpaired mice could not be run, as the Neuropixels files hold no trial events or unit regions. In imaging, Drd2+ neurons of paired mice responded more to the shock cue across neurons but not across mice. The reported lack of a Drd1a+ difference, the Drd2-rich clusters and cue decoding did not reproduce, the imaging tests using uncurated ROIs 2.4 to 4 times the paper's counts. The transcriptomic, RNAscope and optogenetic data are not in the dandiset.

Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • The Open Ephys files do not state the group; their 12 mice are taken as paired because the paper's 4 unpaired ASt mice match the 4 Neuropixels files marked unpaired.fig2-recorded-neurons
  • Open Ephys rates are spike counts over the recording span; Neuropixels rates are the files' firing_rate column. The paper's code (discall.py) computes rate as count over duration.fig2-recorded-neurons
  • 10 of the paper's 15 paired ASt mice (Open Ephys only); df 333 against the paper's 479.figs9-sustained-only-cs-shock
  • Port latency is the tone duration (STAR Methods, Two-tone discrimination tasks: 'Collection of the reward prior to the end of the tone resulted in termination of the tone'); the first port entry at or after onset is run as the other reading and gives the same outcome. A trial is slow when it is over 2 s.figs9-sustained-only-cs-shock
  • Units are the files' curated units below 50 Hz (code, discall.py).figs9-sustained-only-cs-shock
  • The PCA is fitted on paired animals only; the Methods' single PCA over paired and unpaired animals cannot be run, as no unpaired animal has trial events. With all components kept the PCA is a rotation of the centred neuron space, so the complete-space distance is the same whatever rows the PCA is fitted on and is the Methods' reading; the 90% variant depends on the fit.fig2q-trajectories-diverge
  • 112 neurons from 10 of the paper's 15 paired ASt mice.fig2q-trajectories-diverge
  • Paired part only; 5 Open Ephys animals against the 8 paired animals of the code's printout.figs8-single-trial-divergence
  • 100 ms bins, as the code that plots the example pairs (standard choice; the Methods give none).figs8-single-trial-divergence
  • Paired part only; 6 Open Ephys mice against the code's 9 paired mice.fig3h-cs-decoded-paired-only
  • The two-sided Mann-Whitney U test is used as the main reading as the legend does not say one-sided; the one-sided p (0.0076 to 0.033) is below 0.01 under one of five seeds only.fig3h-cs-decoded-paired-only
  • Assumed the pixel scale from the extent of the body points (0.5th to 99.5th percentile) against the 30 x 40 cm chamber the Methods give, as the poses are in pixels with no scale; 18.8 to 20.7 px/cm.figs13-imaging-mice-discriminate

  • The freezing rule ('total displacement of ALL mouse body points less than 0.08 cm per frame') is run as every point below 0.08 cm and as the sum below 0.08 cm; both give the outcome.figs13-imaging-mice-discriminate
  • Freezing measured by the paper's rule counts only frames whose pose repeats the previous one within 0.5 px (no frame falls between 0.5 and 1.6 px); a median-displacement rule is shown as a variant.figs13-imaging-mice-discriminate
