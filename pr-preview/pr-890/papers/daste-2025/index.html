Summary: The paper's results on response timing and odor identity coding in piriform neurons projecting to the olfactory bulb (OB-p) or mPFC (mPFC-p) mostly held up in this data, while most valence and concentration decoding results did not, and the projection classifiers could not be tested. OB-p neurons responded more often and earlier and decoded identity earlier, though in the fast imaging data projection is confounded with mouse. mPFC-p populations decoded concentration further above chance than reported, OB-p populations still encoded valence late in the task, and performance gains across days were not significant. The transformer and MLP classifiers need hyperparameter tables missing from the preprint. Correct-trial valence decoding was blocked because the paper's trial construction cannot be identified.
