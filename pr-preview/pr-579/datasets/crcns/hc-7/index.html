  • fig2b-pv-cck-clusters

    Hierarchical clustering of 67 CA3 interneurons on principal components of 40 electrophysiological features gives two clusters that match PV+ and CCK+ cells with more than 98% overlap; a single PV+ cell falls in the CCK+ cluster.

    We found that the cells clearly formed two separate clusters based on their physiological features ... the two main clusters corresponded almost perfectly to PV+ and CCK+ cells, respectively, with only a single PV+ cell misplaced into the group of CCK+ cells. This resulted in a more than 98% overlap between the anatomical and physiological classification

    Results, Hierarchical clustering based on electrophysiological properties identifies interneuron types, paragraphs 1 and 2; Figure 2B, left

    How it will be judged

    Methods, Electrophysiological characterization and clustering of cell types. The 40 features of Tables 1 and 2 from the 800 ms current steps; PCA on the features, keeping the components that carry most of the variance; hierarchical clustering with Euclidean distance and Ward linkage, cut into two clusters. The paper does not say whether features were standardized before PCA or how many components were kept ("solutions involving different numbers of principal components were tested"). Readings, (a) z-scored features, components explaining 80% of the variance; (b) z-scored features, components with eigenvalue above 1; (c) raw features, same two rules. Reproduced when under one reading the two-cluster cut agrees with the PV+/CCK+ labels for at least 98% of cells (at most one cell of 67 misplaced). The tolerance of 2 points is the step between "more than 98%" and the next lower whole percent.
