Reproducing the behavior of Kauvar, Richman, Liu et al. (2025) from dandiset 001347¶

Paper. Kauvar IV, Richman EB, Liu TX, et al. (2025). Conserved brain-wide emergence of emotional response from sensory experience in humans and mice. Science 388, eadt3971. doi:10.1126/science.adt3971. The claims file (claims/001326/10.1126-science.adt3971.yaml) was written from the full text with its supplement (table S3).

Dataset. DANDI:001347, version 0.250528.0702. The paper's data statement points to this dandiset for the human and mouse behavior without naming a version; 0.250528.0702 is its only published version, so it is pinned. The mouse Neuropixels sessions are in dandiset 001326 and are tested in the entry's other notebook; this notebook reads 001326 (version 0.250528.1957) only for the stimulus tables, to link the recorded mice's eye closure files to those sessions.

What 001347 holds that is used here. The eye closure traces of the behavior-rig mice (sub-mouse, one file per experiment id), the eye closure of the ten Neuropixels-recorded mice (sub-m<mouse>-<rec>), and the per-trial eye closure of the four non-implanted participants (sub-h009 to sub-h013, P50 study). The iEEG files (sub-SD*, sub-H) are not analysed here; the data check lists the datasets every one of them holds, to look for the data of the blocked claims, and the derived sub-H files are analysed in reproduce_kauvar_2025_ieeg.ipynb.

Outside input. The sub-mouse files carry an experiment id and nothing else: no drug, dose, pressure or injection label. The session-to-condition lists (and the trial selections) come from the paper's behavior code, Zenodo doi:10.5281/zenodo.14920570 (mouse_eyepuff/scripts/fig1_*.py). These only name the experiments the dataset already holds (a with_labels input). The recorded-mouse files are matched to the 001326 sessions by the date and mouse in convert_npx_mouse_airpuff_to_nwb.py of the same deposit, and the match is tested below on the puff series timing. The human files hold pre_trials and post_trials; the same code (human_ieeg/scripts/analyze_airpuff_p50.py) labels them saline and ketamine.

No step draws random numbers; the curve fits are deterministic.

Recorded mice¶

The files sub-m<mouse>-<rec> hold the eye closure of the Neuropixels-recorded mice (100 Hz). The code names each as <date>_m<mouse>_<rec>, and the date and mouse give one 001326 session. The data check shows the link holds for the ten files: the onset of every puff series in the video falls on the matching 001326 series onset within 14 ms under one linear clock map. 001326 has two more series at the end than the video in nine sessions (the video stops first), and m94-549 has 79 series events against 40 series in 001326 (the first 40 match). Three 001326 sessions have no file: sub-3_ses-20230225 and both sub-5 sessions, so the paper's 13 sessions are 10 here, and its 11 (Fig. 5) are at most 10.

Method (analysis.py make_puffseries_behavior_plot, lines 38-90, Zenodo doi:10.5281/zenodo.14876694): puff 1 at events-on + 5 s, puffs 3 s apart, each puff cut from 1 s before to 2 s after (5 s more after puff 8), each series minus the mean of the 1 s before its first puff, Pre series 1 to 20, Ketamine series 23 to 32, mean over series per session, each condition's mean trace divided by its maximum (the code). Late closure is the mean over 0.3 to 0.8 s after a puff.

Early (reflexive) closure is the mean over 0.1 to 0.2 s after a puff, late (affective) closure over 0.3 to 0.8 s (Fig. 1 legend). Normalized late closure is the per-trial late closure divided by the mean early closure over the trials of that condition, averaged over trials (mean_ref=True). Drug and dose experiments use Pre trials 6 to 20 and infusion trials 23 to 37 (15 each; which_pre = np.arange(5, 20), which_post = np.arange(22, 37)); PSI and muscimol experiments use trials 3 to 17.
