Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingLight pulse times are deposited for 2 of 28 sessions (fCamk1 sess9 and sess12). Twenty other sessions name uLED stimulation in their epochs but hold no pulse series, so their light responses cannot be computed. Laps are also in these two sessions only.
  • Metadata errorThe OptogeneticSeries are stated in watts but hold one value per uLED, 19 in sess9 and 62623 to 63599 in sess12, not a light power (the paper gives 0.02 to 0.1 uW). No light intensity per pulse is stored, so the paper's three intensities cannot be recovered.
  • Data missingThe files hold pulse series for 6 of the probe's 12 uLEDs (Site3 to Site8), two of them with only 120 to 221 pulses, and most pulses of the protocol are missing. In PreStim the deposited pulses come at 5.76 and 7.50 per s, where the stated protocol implies about 17 to 25; in fCamk1 sess9 the offsets between deposited pulses fall only near multiples of 20 ms, the gaps left by missing pulses. No putative pyramidal cell on shank Group3 responds to any deposited uLED. Time without a deposited pulse therefore cannot be taken as dark.
  • OtherEvery session is deposited twice, as a full file and a no-raw-data file; in all 28 sessions the two hold the same units, spike times, ripples and position. The full file of fCamk3 sess20 is named sub-fCamk3_behavior+ecephys+image.nwb, without its session.
  • Data missingThe full file of fCamk3 sess17 is 28 MB and holds 378 s of raw signal and 6850 s of LFP of a 34621 s session. The six full files of fCamk1 sess13 to sess19 hold the LFP but no raw signal.
  • Description missingUnits carry no cell type and the no-raw-data files no waveforms, so pyramidal cells and interneurons cannot be told apart as in the paper.
  • Metadata errorsession_start_time is 2016-03-15 in every file, while the session ids date the recordings to 2020 and 2021.
  • Metadata errorThe linearized position holds values from 0 to 110 cm (the track length) but has a conversion factor of 100, which would put it at 0 to 110 m.
  • Description missingSix sessions (fCamk2 sess1 to sess4, fCamk3 sess13, fCamk5 sess10) name their epochs only by a time stamp, without behavioral paradigm or manipulation. fCamk2 sessions have no position and 0 to 1110 ripples.
  • Selection not documentedThe dandiset holds 28 sessions and 1351 units with a baseline rate below 5 Hz, where the paper reports 822 pyramidal neurons at 43.3 per session (about 19 sessions). It does not say which sessions the paper used. 14 sessions include CNO or DMSO epochs, which the paper does not describe.
  • OtherIn fCamk3 sess13, fCamk3 sess17 and fCamk5 sess10 the deposited ripple intervals mark population bursts weakly (population rate 1.2 to 1.98 times that 1 s later, against at least twice in the other 22 sessions with ripples). fCamk5 sess10 has 217 events with a median length of 109 ms.
  • Paper inconsistentThe paper gives randomly variable 20 to 40 ms offsets between pulses, the files' description 40 to 60 ms. Measured onset to onset, 30% to 32% of the deposited intervals fall in 40 to 60 ms; measured end to start, 30% to 32% fall in 20 to 40 ms, and most others span missing pulses.
  • Methods not availableThe paper's Materials and Methods are in its supplement. This attempt could not obtain the supplement from PMC or the publisher, and the analysis code the paper names (buzcode) is a general toolbox without scripts for its figures.
  • Paper inconsistentThe paper reports 822 pyramidal neurons of which 73% of light-responsive and 71% of non-responsive neurons were place cells, which leaves about 230 non-place cells, but gives the unmasked fraction as 289 of 417 non-place cells.
  • Description missingThe units table of some files lacks the shank column (group_id); columns are spike_times, unit_name, clu_id and, in the others, group_id.
