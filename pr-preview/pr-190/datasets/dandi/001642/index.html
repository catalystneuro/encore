  • trial-counts

    The analysis covers 78,107 trials in 86 recording sessions from seven rats, of which 12,185 are single fixed-choice, 22,234 double fixed-choice, 36,292 free-choice and 3505 extinction-test trials.

    Here, we report the results of all 78,107 trials in 86 recording sessions, performed by seven rats, consisting of 12,185 single fixed-choice trials (16.4%), 22,234 double fixed-choice trials (30.0%), 36,292 free-choice trials (48.9%), and 3505 extinction test trials (4.7%)

    Results, opening section, paragraph 2

    How it will be judged

    The rat count must match exactly. The paper does not say which trials it counts (the four categories sum to 74,216, not 78,107). Readings are (a) all trials started, including wait-error and error trials; (b) trials with an L/R choice only. Reproduced when, under one reading, every count is within tolerance.

  • fig2d-f-decision-trees
