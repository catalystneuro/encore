Summary: The dandiset holds no calcium imaging, so the findings were tested on the authors' analysed data on Zenodo. There, avoidance learning held, as did better decoding of trial outcome in shocked mice and the best fit of a hybrid Rescorla-Wagner model, the last two under assumed readings of the methods. The day 2 rise in mixed-selective prefrontal neurons did not reproduce: shocked mice had fewer than controls, where the paper reports 51% against 25%, and the paper does not describe how it counts a mixed cell. Decoding did not peak just before the shock. Cells tracked across days and the latent-trajectory analyses could not be tested, as no registration is deposited and CILDS runs only in MATLAB.
