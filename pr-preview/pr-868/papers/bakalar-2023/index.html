Constitutive and conditional deletion reveals distinct phenotypes driven by developmental versus neurotransmitter actions of the neuropeptide PACAP

Dana Bakalar, Oksana Gavrilova, Sunny Zhihong Jiang, Hai‐ying Zhang, Snehashis Roy, Sarah K. Williams, Naili Liu, Stephen Wisser, Ted Björn Usdin, Lee E. Eiden

Outcomes of 27 claims

  • ⊘27 blocked

Summary: None of the paper's main findings could be confirmed or ruled out in this data. The dandiset holds only the whole-brain c-Fos experiment, so the homecage hyperactivity of PACAP knockout mice, its development, the conditional knockouts and the body temperature results could not be tested. In the c-Fos data, knockout hemispheres had more c-Fos cells in the dentate gyrus under every reading, but both dentate regions were significant after correction in 12 of 20 readings. That outcome depends on the segmentation threshold and the hemispheres analysed, which neither the paper nor the dataset records. The further regions of Table 4 differed in the paper's direction, but few matched its values.

Notes on the paper

What the attempts found about the paper itself: a method or rule it does not give, a place where it disagrees with itself, published code that does something else. What they found about the data is on each dataset’s page.

  • Published code disagreesThe count tables count every connected component of the segmentation. The Supplemental Methods count only objects of 9 to 900 voxels; such objects are 43% to 65% of the table totals. A recount of every 26-connected component matches the tables to a median 1.25% per-region difference.
  • Paper inconsistentThe Supplemental Methods give 462 regions after excluding tracts, nerves and ventricles from the 561, and 431 in the end. The dataset's label table has 64 labels with the Allen colours of fibre tracts and ventricles, leaving 497, and the stated exclusions leave 438, so the list of 431 regions cannot be rebuilt.
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the authors. Which FRST threshold was used for each hemisphere; which 19 of the 22 deposited hemispheres were analysed and why the others were left out; whether the 1.5 IQR outlier rule was applied within each genotype or to both pooled; what region size the counts were divided by (the region's volume in each hemisphere's label image or the atlas volume); the connectivity used for connected components; which label image was used for the 6 hemispheres without a masked one; which regions the artifact mask removed in which hemispheres; and the list of 431 regions. Under the readings these leave open, DG-sg and DG-po are both significant after correction in 12 of 20 readings on the deposited hemispheres.

Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • The dataset's count tables cover 13 of the 22 hemispheres and count every connected component, so cells were recounted from the deposited binary FRST segmentations by the Supplemental Methods' rule (Cell counting, 'its volume was chosen to be within 9 and 900 voxels', each cell assigned to the label 'which contained the highest proportion of the cell volume'). Counting every component reproduces the tables to a median summed per-region difference of 1.25% (at most 2.26%); the tables' rule is shown as a variant.fig5-dg-fos table4-lenient-regions table4-region-values
  • Connectivity of the components is not stated; 26-connectivity was assumed (the 3-D default of MATLAB's bwconncomp, the CATNIP binaries being compiled MATLAB, Supplemental Methods, Atlas alignment), and it is the reading that matches the dataset's count tables.fig5-dg-fos table4-lenient-regions table4-region-values
  • Components that reach the edge of a slab (which takes more than 48 z-planes) cannot be measured by the slab-wise count and are dropped; the dropped pieces are 1.6e-04 of all components and count pieces, an upper bound on the components. Every component of at most 48 planes is counted exactly, as a check against scipy.ndimage.label on a synthetic volume shows.fig5-dg-fos table4-lenient-regions table4-region-values
  • Cells are counted on AtlasLabelMasked where the dataset has it and AtlasLabel for the 6 hemispheres without it (CATNIP.sh at commit 4fb92da counts on the hemisphere-masked labels); the region volumes so obtained equal the label_volume_pixels of every count table. Counting on the unmasked AtlasLabel for all 22 hemispheres, the one label image every hemisphere has, is shown as a variant; the DG cell counts are the same on both, and the unmasked DG regions are larger, so DG densities are 0% to 17% lower.fig5-dg-fos table4-lenient-regions table4-region-values
  • The FRST threshold is not stated (Supplemental Methods, Segmentation, 'An expert user determined which threshold(s) to use') and the dataset does not mark one.
