  • features-and-labels-link

    The dandiset's recordings can be matched to the paper's per-cell table (t-type and depth) by specimen ID, and the 18 features computed from them agree with the paper's feature table.

    How it will be judged

    Cells are matched by the specimen ID (SpecimenID in the code's cell table). The 18 features are computed from each matched recording with IPFX, after the sweep QC of the Methods. For each feature, the median over matched cells of the absolute difference from the code's table relative to the table's absolute value is taken (10%, the protocol's default tolerance, as the bound). Reproduced when at least 283 of the 385 cells match and all 18 features are within 10%; partially reproduced when cells match and 9 to 17 features are within 10%; not reproduced otherwise. The number of matched cells, and how many of them carry a t-type or depth in the files themselves, is reported. The code's table is from the revision (316 human cells), so cells beyond the preprint's 283 are expected.

  • fig2ef-pathology-ephys

    Of 18 electrophysiological features compared between neurons from donors with low (0 to 1) and high (1 to 3) GFAP or IBA1 scores, only resting membrane potential under IBA1 differs, neurons from high-IBA1 donors being about 5 mV more hyperpolarized; the other 17 IBA1 and all 18 GFAP comparisons do not differ.

    Among the 18 electrophysiological features analyzed for GFAP and IBA1, only one feature (resting membrane potential, RMP) related to IBA1 was significantly different between low versus high groups. Neurons in the high IBA1 group were approximately 5 mV more hyperpolarized at rest than neurons in the low IBA1 group ( Fig. 2f ). The remaining 17/18 features for IBA1 and all 18 features for GFAP were not statistically different between low and high groups

    Results, Detailed histological assessment of human surgical tissue, paragraph 3; Figure 2e and 2f

    How it will be judged

    Partly a claim of absence, judged on the paper's test. Cells are the supragranular glutamatergic neurons (the code's 385-cell table) with all 18 features defined (the code drops cells with any missing feature). The paper's text says "all supragranular glutamatergic neurons"; the original pathology notebook also keeps only temporal cortex cells of the hIVSCC-MET project, from a table not in the repository. So the readings are (a) temporal cells only (region temporal in the code's cell table), (b) all regions; the group counts above say which the paper used. Each donor's GFAP and IBA1 scores are the columns GFAP Cortex and Iba1 Cortex of the code's data/pathology_scoring.csv (the mean of three neuropathologists), low being at most 1 and high above 1 (the code; the text gives 0-1 and 1-3). The test is the code's, scipy's Mann-Whitney U with its default of scipy 1.4 (the pinned version), whose p is half the two-sided p, at the Bonferroni level 0.05/18 per marker (Figure 2f legend and the code). The paper does not say whether the 5 mV is a difference of group medians or of group means; both are computed, and the size part of the outcome is the one both give (methods_underspecified for the size when they differ). Reproduced when, under one reading of the cells, RMP under IBA1 is the only significant comparison of the 36, with the high group more hyperpolarized and the difference within tolerance; partially reproduced when the significance pattern holds but the size is outside tolerance; not reproduced when any other comparison is significant or RMP under IBA1 is not. The two-sided Mann-Whitney test is shown as a variant, as is the updated notebook's result.

  • fig3d-depth-by-type

    LTK neurons lie mainly in L2 and at the L2/L3 border, GLP2R mainly in upper L3, FREM3 span L2 and L3 and continue into L4, and CARM1P1 and COL22A1 lie almost entirely in deep L3 and along the L3/L4 border.
