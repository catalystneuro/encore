    This study included ten participants (age range, 19–50 years; four males, six females) ... All the ten participants performed under CORR, SYN-P, SYN-C and SEM conditions. Five participants performed under COM and the other five performed under NL conditions.

    Methods, Participants, paragraph 1, and The Chinese language and stimuli design, paragraph 3; Supplementary Table 1

  • task-structure

    Each of the CORR, SYN-P, SYN-C, SEM and COM conditions has 32 sentences, with six words per sentence in CORR, SYN-C and SEM and seven in SYN-P and COM, each word shown for 400 ms with a 100 ms interval after an 800 ms fixation and a 500 ms blank.

    Each trial started with an 800 ms fixation followed by a 500 ms blank screen, and then each word of the sentence (or geometric shape) was displayed for 400 ms, with a 100 ms interstimulus interval. There were six words per sentence in the CORR, SYN-C and SEM conditions and seven words in the SYN-P and COM conditions.

    Methods, The Chinese language and stimuli design, paragraph 4; Supplementary Information, stimulus sentence lists

    How it will be judged

    The 32 sentences per condition are read from the numbered lists in the supplement (no sentence of the text gives the count). Per participant, the number of trials of each condition (before bad-interval exclusion, where the dataset says which were excluded) and the word timing from the event times. The timing tolerance of 10 ms is one sample of the 100 Hz HGA. Reproduced when every count matches and the timings are within tolerance in every participant; partially reproduced when they do in some.

  • fig1b-high-gamma-best-band

    Results, paragraph 3; Figure 1b; Supplementary Figure 3a to 3e

    How it will be judged

    Needs the broadband signal, from which the analytic amplitude of bands from 0 to 150 Hz in 10 Hz steps is taken as the first principal component across band-passes, and classified with the SVM, logistic regression and k-nearest-neighbour settings of the Methods (Frequency component extraction) into correct, syntactic violation, semantic violation and resting state. If the dataset holds only high-gamma activity, as its description says, the claim cannot be tested. Otherwise, open, the cross-validation and the features (time samples of the band amplitude in the keyword window). The paper reports the band accuracies for the SVM (Fig. 1b legend) and for logistic regression (Supplementary Fig. 3a legend) only; reproduced when, under both, a band with lower bound at or above 70 Hz gives the highest accuracy and, for the SVM, it exceeds 0.72 (80% less the tolerance). The kNN result is shown as a variant. The ERP part is judged from the measure of Supplementary Fig. 3d (the peak power during violation against the peak power during CORR, per responding electrode), and holds when the mean difference is largest in the 70 to 150 Hz band.
