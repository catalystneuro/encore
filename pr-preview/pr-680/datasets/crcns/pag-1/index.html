  • fig1c-total-suppression

    Over the 88 recording sessions, suppression ratio over the whole 10 s cue differs by cue, high to danger (0.80), intermediate to uncertainty (0.53) and low to safety (0.03), and each cue pair differs.

    Across all 88 recording sessions, these six rats showed excellent discriminative fear ... high to danger, intermediate to uncertainty, and low to safety

    Results, first section, paragraph 2 (after Figure 1 supplements); Figure 1C

    How it will be judged

    Per session (88, each counted once however many units it holds) and cue, the total cue suppression ratio (Methods, Calculating suppression ratios), uncertainty trials collapsed. Reproduced when, under one reading of details 1 to 3 of the opening comment, all three means are within tolerance, the repeated-measures ANOVA of cue has p < 0.05 and both paired t-tests have p < 0.05 in the reported direction; partially reproduced when the tests hold and a mean is outside tolerance. The safety tolerance is 0.05, not 10%, because 10% of 0.03 is below the printed precision; 0.05 is about the tolerance of the uncertainty value (0.053). The 0.83 for danger in the Figure 2, supplement 2 legend is reported against the same values.
