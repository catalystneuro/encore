  • dataset-size

    The dataset holds 15 mice, 115 sessions, 18,514 trials and 63,979,800 video frames, which is 88.9 hours of video.

    The size of the dataset ... 15 mice, 88.9 hours, 115 sessions, 18,514 trials, 63,979,800 frames

    Methods, High-speed videography and whisker tracking, paragraph 2; Background & Summary, paragraph 5

    How it will be judged

    Mice are subject folders and sessions are NWB files. Tolerance 0 for the counts, as they are counts of deposited records, and 0.05 h for the hours, the rounding of the printed value. The paper does not define which trials and frames are counted. Readings for trials are (a) all rows of all trials tables, (b) rows with ignore_trial False. Readings for frames are (a) the frame counts of the raw video files, (b) the number of frames of the raw_whisker_position series (N_frames, Data Records), summed over sessions; hours are frames divided by 200 and by 3600 under each. Reproduced when mice and sessions match and trials and frames match exactly under one reading each; partially reproduced when mice and sessions match and trials and frames are within 10% under some reading; not reproduced otherwise.

  • response-window-timing

  • choice-time-first-lick

    The choice time is the first lick after the response window opens, and on spoiled trials with no lick it is set 45 s after the window opens.

    choice_time (float) The time of the choice lick, which is the first lick after the response window opened. On spoiled trials with no lick, this value is 45 seconds after the response window opened

    Data Records, NWB file, trials table, Table 6 (choice_time)

    How it will be judged

    Lick times from the left, right and (where present) both lick series. For each non-spoiled trial, the first lick at or after response_window_open_time, compared with choice_time; its side compared with choice. For spoiled trials, choice_time minus response_window_open_time compared with 45 s. Reproduced when all of these hold on at least 99% of trials of each kind; partially reproduced at 95%. All trials are used, as the definition does not depend on ignore_trial.

  • reward-and-timeout

  • neural-video-alignment

    The neural and video timebases are related by a linear fit whose residuals at the trial synchronization pulses are never more than one frame (5 ms).

    How it will be judged

    Needs the pulse times in both streams, the neural from the analog input of the raw Open Ephys files under sourcedata/ (the sample at which it goes low), the video from the frame on which the raw video goes dark (Methods, Alignment).
