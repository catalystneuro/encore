Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Metadata errorEvery voltage series states the unit volts and every stimulus series amperes, with a conversion of 1, but the values are in millivolts (-150 to 67) and picoamperes (-200 to 1,580).
  • Description missingEvery series has an undefined (NaN) starting time, so the timing of sweeps relative to each other is not recorded. Within a sweep the response and the stimulus share a clock (onset at 0.1 s, offset at 0.7 s).
  • Description missingThe t-type of the 12 ET cells is stored under the older names L5 PT_1 to L5 PT_4, which differ from the paper's and its tables' L5 ET_1 to L5 ET_4; no mapping is given. All other t-type names match the paper's table.
  • Metadata errorThe dandiset's description repeats the main data set's "over 1300 neurons"; the dandiset holds 185 neurons of the follow-up at 34 degrees.
  • Metadata errorIn the published version the contributor list has a placeholder "Last, First" with Zizhen Yao's ORCID, the brain region is typed as a disorder, and the assets summary gives no approach or measurement technique. The unpublished draft corrects these.
  • OtherEleven cells were stimulated beyond the 1,380 pA the Methods give (up to 1,580 pA), and two of them started at -180 pA and -100 pA, not -200 pA. Nine of the eleven are from one mouse (mouse_UEQOU).
  • Published code disagreesThe paper's code takes the maximum number of APs before excluding sweeps with spikes outside the step, and looks for those spikes over the whole trace before and after the step; the Methods exclude such sweeps first and look 100 ms before and 200 ms after. The two definitions differ for 3 of the 185 cells.
  • Paper inconsistentThe Methods build the electrophysiological representation from 17 principal components of 17 features and later call it "the 16-dimensional electrophysiological representation"; the paper's code uses all 17.
  • Paper inconsistentThe legend of Extended Data Fig. 6j describes it as "the same analysis as in (c)", but in the paper's code panel j is the 34 degree version of panel i (the Sst overlay and the transcriptomic-distance correlation), not of panel c.

Reproduction Patch-seq neurons of mouse motor cortex at physiological temperature (Scala, Kobak et al. 2021)

Tests the findings of Scala, Kobak et al. (2021) that can be tested on dandiset 000035, which holds the paper's follow-up recordings at 34 degrees C (185 neurons, mostly layer 5 Sst and excitatory cells). It extracts the paper's 17 electrophysiological features from the traces, checks the cohort and the stimulus against the Methods, tests whether IT and ET neurons overlap electrophysiologically, and compares Sst t-types between temperatures using the paper's published room-temperature features. The findings on the main room-temperature data set are in dandiset 000008 and are not tested here. All files
