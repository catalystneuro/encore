Most of the paper's tested findings on burst coding held up in this data, but its session counts and the share of information carried by bursts did not. Its threshold model could not be tested because Data1 holds no thresholds. Held: bursts are less correlated than single spikes, burst probability follows stimulus statistics, burst-triggered stimulus averages differ by burst length, and their latency falls with length. Table 1's counts did not reproduce, probably because Data1 gives 115 sessions against the paper's 86 and does not mark which were kept. Information per burst rising with length held only partly, since estimator bias is of the order of the rise.
