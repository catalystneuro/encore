  • responsive-fraction

    About half (50.2%) of all neurons in the data set responded to whisker stimulation.

    Overall, 50.2% of neurons in the data set responded to whisker stimulation

    Results, paragraph 3; Discussion, paragraph 3

    How it will be judged

    The paper does not define a responsive neuron (open detail 6). Readings the text allows, each run on all segmented neurons, pooled across fields, are (a) the mean response amplitude over all 360 presentations is greater than zero, one-sided signed-rank test p < 0.05; (b) at least one of the nine stimuli evokes a response greater than zero (signed-rank test over its 40 presentations, p < 0.05), with the Benjamini-Hochberg-Yekutieli correction over the neurons and stimuli of a field as the paper uses for tuning. Reproduced when a reading gives 50.2% within the tolerance; the outcome rests on that reading as an assumption.
