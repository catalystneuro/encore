Duszkiewicz et al. 2024 · Nature Neuroscience

Local origin of excitatory–inhibitory tuning equivalence in a cortical network

doi:10.1038/s41593-024-01588-5 · paper key duszkiewicz-2024

Datasets

  • DANDI:000939

Most main findings hold in this data. PoSub fast-spiking cells show stable head-direction tuning anchored to landmarks, thalamic disinhibition acts multiplicatively on HD cells and additively on FS cells, and coupling and the ring manifold persist in REM sleep. The Fourier-equivalence findings hold in part: ADN-HD spectra differ in the paper's direction, but the Fig. 3e interaction is not significant under the code's reading, and 47 FS cells peak in component 3 against the paper's 55. The simulation, the model fit and the thalamic tracing could not be tested, because their code or images are not deposited.
