The imaging findings, such as broader tuning in experienced females, could not be tested because the dandiset holds only raw movies, with no cell regions or record of which call played on each trial. The retrieval behaviour findings are in neither the dandiset nor its linked collection. In the whole-cell recordings the current almost never follows the calls (2 of 39 cells), so the excitation and inhibition findings could not be tested.
