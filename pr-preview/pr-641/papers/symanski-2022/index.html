Symanski et al. 2022 · eLife

Rhythmic coordination and ensemble dynamics in the hippocampal-prefrontal network during odor-place associative memory and decision making

doi:10.7554/elife.79545 · paper key symanski-2022

Datasets

  • DANDI:001539

The paper's core rhythm findings held in this data, but most single-cell and ensemble findings held only in part or not at all. Beta power and coherence rose during odor sampling in CA1, PFC and olfactory bulb, and ripples were almost absent at the odor port. Choice selectivity matched in CA1 but not PFC, the correct versus incorrect anticorrelation was weaker than reported, and ensemble discrimination times did not match. The sniff signal, the air-only sessions and interneuron-dependent claims such as the cross-correlogram lags could not be tested, because the dataset lacks thermocouple data, air sessions and spike widths. Several minor spatial-field comparisons also failed.
