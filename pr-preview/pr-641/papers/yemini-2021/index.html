NeuroPAL: A Multicolor Atlas for Whole-Brain Neuronal Identification in C. elegans

doi:10.1016/j.cell.2020.12.012 · paper key yemini-2021

DANDI:000541: In these head recordings, most Figure 6 responses held: ASER excitation by NaCl, NaCl responses in six interneurons, more inhibition by the odors than by salt, and pharyngeal responses. These results rest on assumed trace normalisation. The weak match between functional correlation and connectome held in heads, where every R2 was below 5%. The 0.3 Hz AVF cycle (0.12 Hz here), distinguishable stimulus trajectories in PCA, and all 21 heads showing the expected responses (19 did, by a review the paper does not describe) did not reproduce. AWC asymmetry, tail responses and Figures 1 to 5 and 7 could not be tested, because the dataset lacks AWC ON/OFF labels, tails and that imaging.

DANDI:000715: In this head-only data, the atlas findings held but the identification results did not. Positional variability differs across neuron types, OLLR is more variable than OLLL, and NeuroPAL's color-violation figure matched. Head identification accuracy was 39 to 63% against the paper's 86%, and the eight-annotation and position-only results also failed, most likely because the five dorsal-ventral heads are stored rotated and the paper does not say how worms were oriented. Every tail result could not be tested because the dataset has no tails. The 3-reporter optimal coloring was blocked because the paper gives no sparsity. I did not create `summaries/yemini-2021-02.md`.
