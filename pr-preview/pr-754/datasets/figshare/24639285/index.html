  • OtherIn four glass sheets of 64455lunar_cryodata.xlsx (g2, ss8, ss39, ss48) the rows given as repeated measurements of each step are one row multiplied by factors between 0.95 and 1.03. In g2, ss8 and ss39 the same factor multiplies the moment, the declination and the inclination of a row; in ss48 each column has its own factors. Every step of these sheets is built this way and no step of the other 18 sheets or the 12 crystal files is. These rows are not measurements, and the scaling of a declination has no physical meaning (one ss8 declination becomes 361.7 deg). The deposit does not say how they were made.

    Found by the attempt on Tarduno et al. 2024 (tarduno-2024-01, 2026-10-27)
  • Description missingThe glass sheets hold 2 to 19 repeated measurements per step, and neither the deposit nor the paper says in words how they were combined for Table 2 and Figures 1 and 2. The mean vector reproduces most of Table 2; the first measurement does not. The deposit's Supplementary Figure 1 plots means (see the finding on it). Eleven steps of seven glass sheets hold two groups of directions more than 45 deg apart, nine of them inside the specimen's Table 2 range; sheet ss6's 250.1 step holds 19 rows in two groups (inclination near 71 deg and near -13 deg). NRM is labelled both 0 and 0.0 in ss27 and ss38.

  • OtherThe deposit's "SupplFig1 - other Thelliers March 2024.pdf" plots, for 12 of its 14 specimens that are not synthetic sheets, TRM gained from the mean vectors of the repeated rows and NRM as the mean of their moments, to 0.1% or better. For the four sheets whose rows are scaled copies (g2, ss8, ss39, ss48) its points differ from every combination of the deposited rows by 0.5% or more (median), so these sheets' deposited rows are not the values plotted.
