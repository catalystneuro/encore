Reproduction PAGER-Gi in hippocampal slices (Kalogriopoulos, Tei et al. 2024)

Tests the acute slice findings of Kalogriopoulos, Tei et al. (2024) on dandiset 001354, which holds whole-cell current clamp recordings of 50 CA1 neurons from 8 mice expressing anti-mCherry PAGER-Gi. The files do not record which cells received DCZ alone and which DCZ plus mCherry, when DCZ was applied, the mEGFP intensity of each cell, or the mCherry preincubation, so every claim is blocked. The notebook checks the files against the paper and computes the paper's per-sweep measures and exclusions for all cells. All files
