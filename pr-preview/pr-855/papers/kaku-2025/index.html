Summary: The paper's findings on licking and breathing held in part in this data, and its photostimulation, cued-task and paired-recording findings could not be tested, as the dandiset holds only the licking-task recordings. Breaths were delayed before slower licks, both rhythms resumed at fixed latencies after swallows, and licking units clustered in the hypoglossal and trigeminal nuclei and the IRN. Inspirations did not fall only between licks (30.4% came during a tongue protrusion), and the lick interval was not preserved across swallows. Licks were delayed before slower breaths over sessions but not over mice, and whether breathing units cluster near the preBötzinger complex could not be decided, as the paper does not define that region.

Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • The files are read directly; the code's nwb2mat converter (a Windows MATLAB executable) was not used.code-dataset-link
  • Regions are taken from the Allen structure tree (iblatlas copy, pinned) by the unit's region name; whether the paper counts the arbor vitae as cerebellum is not stated, and both counts are shown.units-count
  • Reading (b) uses spikes in the code's analysis window (the first 5.0014 s of each good trial) over that time, since the trials carry no session times.region-spike-rates
  • measured gives reading (b), the reading under which both means are within tolerance.fig1d-lick-breath-frequencies
  • A protrusion is taken from each tongue onset to the first tongue offset after it, both as the code detects them (likelihood > 0.95 in both views); the paper does not define the interval.fig1f-inspiration-between-licks
  • Simulated sessions match the data sessions in the number of in-bout inspirations (breathing model) or licks (licking model); sessions with fewer than 50 in-bout inspirations are skipped.ed1-unidirectional-models-fail
  • Licks are added evenly inside inter-lick intervals longer than the 97.5th percentile (breathing model), and an inspiration is omitted when it would follow the previous one by less than the 2.5th percentile inter-breath interval (licking model); the Methods describe both steps without numbers.ed1-unidirectional-models-fail
  • Expiration onset is the Hilbert phase (code filter, plus pi) wrapping through 2 pi, the claims file's choice.ed1bc-licks-pause-at-swallows
  • The 1000 resampled tuning curves per unit are fitted by a grid search over width and preferred phase with amplitude and baseline in closed form, not curve_fit; the measured MI uses curve_fit as the code. On 300 random units the two fits differ by a median of 0.0015 in MI and fall on the same side of 0.6 in 99%; on 24 units with MI > 0.6 the code's 100-resample test gives the same decision with either fit.fig2-licking-breathing-tuned-counts
  • Resampled histograms are drawn as multinomials on the measured histogram's bins, which has the distribution of the code's resampling; the code's bin edges follow each resample's range.fig2-licking-breathing-tuned-counts
  • Significance resamples are computed only for units whose measured MI exceeds 0.6, the only units that can count.fig2-licking-breathing-tuned-counts
  • AMB's "anterior-posterior and medial-lateral extent" in O3 is read both as the extent of the whole nucleus in the CCF annotation and as its extent in each 100 um AP step. Regions come from the Allen CCFv3 2017 annotation at 25 um (inputs), whose label at the unit's position equals the file's region name for 96.3% of good units.fig2f-breathing-units-location
  • "Lateral of NTS" is units beyond the lateral edge of NTS in the CCF annotation in the same 100 um AP step, within the distance; "above AMB" is units within the distance above AMB's dorsal edge inside its AP and ML extent.fig2h-swallow-units-location
  • Inspiration onset is phase pi in the tuning phase (Hilbert angle plus pi, as the code), so the inspiration half is preferred phase minus pi in [0, pi).ed8bc-prebotc-inspiration-phase
  • The region is built as in fig2f, under both readings.ed8bc-prebotc-inspiration-phase
  • The 40 fastest and 40 slowest two-lick cycles, as the claims file's header says; the code takes the 40 on each side nearest the median (shown as a variant; questions/kaku-2025.yaml).fig4e-licking-units-anticipate-breath
  • Assumed: reading O2(a) decides where O2(b) cannot be tested (2 units).fig4f-breathing-units-anticipate-lick
