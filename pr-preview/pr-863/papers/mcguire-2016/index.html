Leah M. McGuire, Gregory I. Telian, Keven J. Laboy-Juárez, Toshio Miyashita, Daniel J. Lee, Katherine A. Smith, Daniel E. Feldman

Summary: Most main findings held up: rats judged whisker sequences by mean speed and treated FMS and SMF alike, S1 units split into Fast, Medium and Slow classes with the reported laminar pattern, Fast units tracked impulse velocity, L5 Slow Positive units signalled choice, and Fast and Medium units decoded the sequence. The choice decoder did not reproduce: Slow units predicted choice far above the paper's 65%, Fast and Medium units also well above chance, and the Methods leave open how it was trained. The one rat that told FMS from SMF is not deposited. Several class outcomes assume the dataset's stored modulated units are the paper's. Minor claims on baseline rates and latencies reproduced in part.
