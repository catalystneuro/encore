Dataset version 0.240822.1759 · tested 2026-10-17 · 11 open findings

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds the 12 light-sheet sessions behind Figure 2 and its supplement, with behaviour, but none of the perturbation or noradrenergic imaging experiments of Figures 4 to 6, nor the behavioural recordings of the other 62 of the 74 fish of Figure 1. Some sessions hold undescribed protocols (randomised gain, brief grating speed-ups) that may belong to Figures 1K and 7, but they cannot be identified. No other dandiset of the paper was found.
    Found by the attempt on Mu et al. 2019 (mu-2019-01, 2026-10-17)
  • Data missingFour files have raw images only and no cell traces, among them the only three glia-only GCaMP6f recordings (the paper's glial NMF used 6). Raw images cover only part of 20170113-4 (3065 of 12050 volumes present) and 20170228-4 (5776 of 7200 present), and 20170113-2 holds 7616 imaging volumes against 9751 camera triggers.
    Found by the attempt on Mu et al. 2019 (mu-2019-01, 2026-10-17)
  • Metadata errorThe glial imaging plane of the four two-colour files is labelled GCaMP6f but excited at 561 nm and detected at 660 nm, the jRGECO1b channel of the Methods (Tg(gfap:jRGECO1b)). The raw images are typed TwoPhotonSeries and the archive lists two-photon microscopy, though the microscope is a one-photon light sheet; the device is described as Zeiss confocal microscopes.
    Found by the attempt on Mu et al. 2019 (mu-2019-01, 2026-10-17)
  • Metadata errorThe DfOverF series are not ΔF/F. In the four single-colour files each ROI trace is divided by its mean (float64, mean 1); in the four two-colour files traces are centred near 0 (float32) with the first 60 s held constant. The Fluorescence series vary by about 1e-6 per frame or not at all, consistent with baselines, and carry the same description as DfOverF.
    Found by the attempt on Mu et al. 2019 (mu-2019-01, 2026-10-17)
  • Description missingThe ActivityStates table is described only as classified periods of activity. It does not follow the Methods' definition (many active rows of 5 s or less), its transient rows are undefined and duplicate active rows, it holds 3 rows in the glia-only files, and it does not say which condition each row belongs to.
    Found by the attempt on Mu et al. 2019 (mu-2019-01, 2026-10-17)
  • Description missingThe stimulus signals have no units or descriptions beyond their names, and the protocol codes in StimulusParameterSignals (12, 22, 23, 32, 33, 51, 52, 53) are not described. Closed and open loop can be read only from the gain signal. From the signals, code 33 is a 0.3 to 0.5 s speed-up of the grating in open loop, code 52 a randomised gain, and codes 51 and 53 alternate closed and open loop in six files but both have the gain at 0 in 20170228-4.
    Found by the attempt on Mu et al. 2019 (mu-2019-01, 2026-10-17)
  • Metadata errorTotalStimulusVelocitySignal is described as the drift speed minus the motosensory gain times the swim power, but its values lie between -0.002 and 0.008 in every file, against baseline velocities of 0.5 to 2. The visual feedback each bout produced is not recorded.
    Found by the attempt on Mu et al. 2019 (mu-2019-01, 2026-10-17)
  • OtherIn two of the four two-colour files (20161109-2 and 20170203-1) the whole-brain glial trace has an SD of 0.0016 and 0.0011, against 0.0097 and 0.0072, correlates with the neuronal trace at 0.58 and 0.59 (0.06 and 0.15 in the others), and falls after passivity onset. In 20170203-1 the neuronal trace also has a low SD (0.0016, against 0.0069 to 0.0180 in the other fish), so there the low SD affects both channels. The files do not allow the cause to be checked.
    Found by the attempt on Mu et al. 2019 (mu-2019-01, 2026-10-17)
  • Description missingThe ROIs are in each fish's own image space, with no registration to a reference brain and no region labels, so the paper's region-level results (L-MO, NE-MO) cannot be located.
    Found by the attempt on Mu et al. 2019 (mu-2019-01, 2026-10-17)
  • OtherThe two groups of files were processed differently (trace scaling, data type, censoring, chunking), and the volume rate is 1.6 to 2.9 Hz against the 3 volumes per second of the Methods.
    Found by the attempt on Mu et al. 2019 (mu-2019-01, 2026-10-17)
  • Metadata errorThe SwimIntervals width column is described as the estimated width spanned by the swim event but equals the bout duration in seconds.
    Found by the attempt on Mu et al. 2019 (mu-2019-01, 2026-10-17)
