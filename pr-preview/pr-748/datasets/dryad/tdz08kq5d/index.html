Dataset version 263142 · tested 2026-10-27 · 7 open findings

  • Metadata errorThe twitching sheet (Figure 2D) does not identify its clones. It has no morphotype column; its four 35_1 rows carry the names of four 35_0 SCV clones (6b6s1, 6b6s2, 6b8s1, 6b8s2) with different values, where the biofilm and disease sheets name the 35_1 clones 6b1s1, 6b1s2, 6b2s3 and 6b2s4; and in 25_0 the four mucoid names 6d5a2 to 6d8a2 appear twice with different values, where the other sheets have four SCVs.
    Found by the attempt on Wang et al. 2024 (wang-2024-01, 2026-10-27)
  • OtherThe deposited twitching values do not reproduce Table S3d: with the colony types of the design, the residual sum of squares is 28.32 against 35.78 and the temperature sum of squares 2.48 against 3.857; only colony type x phage (27.04) and temperature x colony type (0.081) agree. The total sum of squares of the data is 72.28 against 82.08 summed from Table S3d, so no assignment of colony types reproduces it if its sums of squares are sequential, which the table does not state. Every other ANOVA the paper prints (Tables S1a, S1b, S2, S3a to S3c, S3e, S3f) is reproduced from the deposited data.
    Found by the attempt on Wang et al. 2024 (wang-2024-01, 2026-10-27)
  • Description missingThe variant table (snp_indel) gives, per variant and group of four sequenced clones, the number of clones that carry it, not which clones. Per-clone mutation counts and co-occurrence (for example whether the gspE and gspF mutations of 35 C +P SCVs are in different clones) cannot be read, and the genome reads are not public.
    Found by the attempt on Wang et al. 2024 (wang-2024-01, 2026-10-27)
  • Data missingBioProject PRJNA1028400, named in the paper for the genomes of the evolved clones, has 28 BioSamples and 14 assemblies and no sequencing runs, so the reads the variant calls come from are not public. The RNA-seq reads are public in PRJNA1032726 (ten runs), though the Methods say they are shared on request.
    Found by the attempt on Wang et al. 2024 (wang-2024-01, 2026-10-27)
  • Data missingThe growth curves the maximum growth rates and carrying capacities were fitted to are not deposited, only one fitted value per clone, so the fits cannot be repeated and the model behind them (logistic, as the Methods say, or the code's best of four by AIC) cannot be checked. The pilB and pilM data of Table S6 are not deposited either.
    Found by the attempt on Wang et al. 2024 (wang-2024-01, 2026-10-27)
  • Description missingThe OD600 values of Figure 1B include day 0 and negative values (eight, down to -0.029, at 25 C with phage), so they are blank-corrected and include the inoculation, neither of which the README or Methods says; the paper's ANOVA uses days 2 to 12. The README does not give units for the growth rate (headed maxgrowthrate(OD600)) or say that adsorption is a proportion.
    Found by the attempt on Wang et al. 2024 (wang-2024-01, 2026-10-27)
  • Metadata errorIn the carrying capacity sheet the ancestor's value (1.4355) is headed maxgrowthrate(OD600). The variant table's GENE column holds PROKKA names that differ from the paper's (phcS is sasA_8, pilD outO, gspE hxcR, gspF epsF_1); the gene_blast sheet gives the paper's names by locus tag. The Figure 4B list holds 11 genes where the paper speaks of 13.
    Found by the attempt on Wang et al. 2024 (wang-2024-01, 2026-10-27)
