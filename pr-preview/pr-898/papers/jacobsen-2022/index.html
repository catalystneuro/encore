Jacobsen et al. 2022 · Cell Reports Methods

All-viral tracing of monosynaptic inputs to single birthdate-defined neurons in the intact brain

R. Irene Jacobsen, Rajeevkumar Raveendran Nair, Horst Andreas Obenhaus, Flavio Donato, Torstein Slettmoen, May‐Britt Moser, Edvard Ingjald Moser

Outcomes of 28 claims

Summary: The paper's optogenetic tagging results held up in this data, but its single-cell tracing and imaging results could not be tested. Entorhinal input cells to the hippocampus responded to light within 2 to 4 ms, including a grid cell, and grid, head direction and border cells were present. The border cell and the example cells' values rest on assumptions about the shuffle and about which unlabelled laser epoch the figure uses. How reliably single starter cells are labelled, which inputs they receive and the two-photon imaging could not be tested, because the dandiset holds only the electrophysiology of two mice. Of the minor claims, a cell followed across sessions and the non-responsive cells matched in part.

    We found several functional cell types, including grid, head direction, and border cells (Figures 5C and S4), indicating that the network as a whole was not affected by the widespread rabies expression

    Results, The functional properties of input cells to birthdate-defined cell populations can be determined with optogenetics in vivo, paragraph 3; Figures 5C and S4

    Under the standard shuffle the 12 units include 2 grid cells (T1 C89 and T1 C50 of 58313, day 19, gridness 0.854 and 0.996), 2 head direction cells (T4 C16, day 17, mean vector length 0.912 and half-session stability 0.97; and the 70375 unit of day 15 session 2, 0.123 and 0.675) and 1 border cell (T4 C50, day 18, border score 0.656 against a shuffle threshold of 0.555). Under the literal shuffle T4 C50 falls short (0.656 against 0.663), giving 2 grid, 1 head direction and 0 border cells, so the outcome rests on the shuffle reading. Under the standard shuffle each unit the figures name is classed as the paper names it. For T4 C16 the literal shuffle's 95th percentile of the mean vector length is 0.762 and its largest value 0.912, the observed score, against 0.225 and 0.272 under the standard shuffle. The standard shuffle is lenient for a weakly tuned cell, as the day 15 session 2 unit of 70375 passes at 0.123 against 0.082. Taking the open field as its first 1800 s changes no class. The part of the statement about the network as a whole cannot be tested, as the dandiset holds only the 12 curated units shown in the figures, so finding one unit of each type partly reflects what was deposited.

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • Rowland et al. (2018), Materials and methods, classification into functional classes, shift spikes "by a random interval between ± 20 s, with the end of the session wrapped to the beginning"; the archdata code has no spatial shuffle. Read literally (uniform within 20 s) and as the standard shuffle of the papers Rowland et al. cite for the scores (Langston et al. 2010; Solstad et al. 2008, a shift of at least 20 s). The readings differ for the border cell; the standard shuffle is adopted as an assumption, since a shift of under 20 s leaves the spike train nearly in place and gives a null distribution close to the observed map.
    • Rate map bin of 2.5 cm from Rowland et al. (2018), Methods, gridness score ("steps of 1 bin (2.5 cm)"); the linked code's parameter file gives 2 cm. The text was followed.
    • Smoothing, speed cutoff and tuning bins from the archdata code (see inputs), as neither paper's text gives them.
    • The open field is taken as the whole position series, an assumption, as the files have no open-field epoch and the Methods give its length only as "approximately 30 min". The other reading, the first 1800 s, was run for all 12 units and changes no class.

Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • Rowland et al. (2018), Materials and methods, classification into functional classes, shift spikes "by a random interval between ± 20 s, with the end of the session wrapped to the beginning"; the archdata code has no spatial shuffle. Read literally (uniform within 20 s) and as the standard shuffle of the papers Rowland et al. cite for the scores (Langston et al. 2010; Solstad et al. 2008, a shift of at least 20 s). The readings differ for the border cell; the standard shuffle is adopted as an assumption, since a shift of under 20 s leaves the spike train nearly in place and gives a null distribution close to the observed map.fig5c-functional-cell-types
  • Rate map bin of 2.5 cm from Rowland et al. (2018), Methods, gridness score ("steps of 1 bin (2.5 cm)"); the linked code's parameter file gives 2 cm. The text was followed.fig5c-functional-cell-types
  • Smoothing, speed cutoff and tuning bins from the archdata code (see inputs), as neither paper's text gives them.fig5c-functional-cell-types
  • The open field is taken as the whole position series, an assumption, as the files have no open-field epoch and the Methods give its length only as "approximately 30 min". The other reading, the first 1800 s, was run for all 12 units and changes no class.fig5c-functional-cell-types
  • The first TTL event of each laser epoch starts at the epoch start and lasts 45 ms to 1.9 s; it is not a pulse onset and is left out, leaving 275 pulses in a 120 s epoch.fig5c-responsive-grid-cell
  • The day-17 file holds two laser epochs with no power label, and the paper does not say which Figure 5C uses. Each epoch and both together were run; the outcome takes the epoch per cell, an assumption. This is asked of the paper's authors (findings).fig5c-example-cells
  • No cluster label is shared across sessions. The unit is taken as the same cell because each file holds this single curated unit on tetrode 3 and the Methods say the tetrodes were not turned down between these sessions ("except when trying to record from the same cell across multiple sessions, Figure S3"); the stored waveform (one channel) is consistent with this but does not separate it from the units of the other mouse.figs3-stable-input-cell
  • The open field is taken as the whole position series, an assumption (see fig5c-functional-cell-types). The first 1800 s, the reading of the Methods' "approximately 30 min", was run for every session and changes the outcome to a match on all 18 values; it is not adopted, because the cut is stated nowhere and was identified from the expected values.figs3-stable-input-cell
  • Rowland et al. (2018) define one quantity, "Mean vector length (head-direction score)"; the paper reported a head direction score and a mean vector length with different values. Both are taken as the mean vector length of the tuning map, as Rowland et al. define it, with the archdata tuning curve (2 degree bins, sigma 2 bins, no speed filter), an assumption; other bin widths, smoothing and speed filtering are shown. Asked of the paper's authors (findings).figs4-nonresponsive-cells
