  • Data missingThe widefield imaging of Figure 5 (three mice with a cannula in the left auditory cortex, before and after contralateral and ipsilateral ZX1, with red-channel images) is not in the dandiset, so the three Figure 5 claims cannot be tested.
    Found by the attempt on Cody et al. 2024 (cody-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • OtherThe pupil radius series hold tracking failures, single frames and runs at 3 to 1e6 times the pupil size, often next to dropped frames (68 trials have a maximum above three times their median; 118 trials step by more than a factor 2 between consecutive tracked frames). In AA0319 and AA0334 the failures also come as runs at a second level (130 to 160 against a pupil of about 25) that a spike filter does not remove. The DeepLabCut points and likelihoods that the Methods use to drop frames are not deposited, and the Methods' single derivative pass does not remove these failures, so each animal's maximum PD, and every baseline in % of maximum, depends on how they are cleaned.
    Found by the attempt on Cody et al. 2024 (cody-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Metadata errorEvery pupil_radius series's description names one TwoPhotonSeries (the file's last, for example TwoPhotonSeries_036), not its own; the series can be matched to its trial only through the .tif file named in its comments.
    Found by the attempt on Cody et al. 2024 (cody-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Metadata error25 pupil series attached to DRC trials have the length of a mapping trial (1430 samples) or an LED trial (300 samples) in place of twice the trial's 170 frames, in seven files.
    Found by the attempt on Cody et al. 2024 (cody-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • File format errorIn the stimulus table, starting_time is wrong by 24 h for 84 trials in seven files (AA0318, AA0319, AA0320, AA0322, AA0327, AA0330, AA0333). Trials recorded before the reference .tif have starting times near 86400 s, where the .tif creation times show they came minutes earlier. The pupil and fluorescence series take the same starting times.
    Found by the attempt on Cody et al. 2024 (cody-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • OtherThe DRCs without a tone in the files (pulseSets PC_contrastChange) are 10 s of one contrast followed by 10 s of the other. The paper describes the sound-evoked pupil stimuli as low- or high-contrast DRCs and does not mention the switch.
    Found by the attempt on Cody et al. 2024 (cody-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Selection not documentedGroup sizes in the files differ from the paper. The files label ten mice pupilPTinContrast (four F, six M, one ZnT3-WT), where the paper gives eight C57BL6/J mice (four F, four M) for Figures 1 to 3. The pupil_contrast_ZnT3WT label holds five F and five M, where the paper gives six F and four M. Twelve mice have CGC-assay trials before and after ZX1 (two of them ZnT3-KO), where the paper gives fourteen C57BL6/J mice for Fig. 2d, e.
    Found by the attempt on Cody et al. 2024 (cody-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Description missingNo label identifies the mice of Fig. 2d, e, and the FRA mapping trials used to select cells are in only 7 of 30 files. Whether the RoiResponseSeries are neuropil-corrected is not stated (unit lumens, description 'motion corrected ROIs'). The pupil radius has unit 'na' and no spatial scale.
