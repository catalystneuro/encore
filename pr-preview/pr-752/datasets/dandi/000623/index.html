Dataset version 0.240130.2108 · tested 2026-10-31 · 7 open findings

  • Description missingThe files hold the movie frame times but no scene cuts or cut types; the 93 cuts (80 continuity cuts, 13 scene changes) used by the event analyses come from the annotation file of the paper code repository (also on Figshare), linked by frame number.
    Found by the attempt on Keles et al. 2024 (keles-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • File format errorEvery LFP_micro and LFP_macro ElectricalSeries stores a starting_time at the movie start (near 0 s), while its first sample is 10 s before the movie. The paper's Usage Notes say so ("start at 10 seconds before the starting time") and the paper code subtracts 10 s, but NWB defines starting_time as the time of the first sample, so a reader of the file alone reads the LFP 10 s late. Without the offset 18 of 2192 micro and 20 of 2240 macro channels respond to recognition images; with it 650 and 683 do.
    Found by the attempt on Keles et al. 2024 (keles-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Metadata errorThe units column isibelow is described as the proportion of inter-spike intervals below 3 ms, but its values are percentages (median 0.24, up to 10.35), as the paper reports them.
    Found by the attempt on Keles et al. 2024 (keles-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Other448 of the 1450 units of the paper (455 of the 1457 in the files) have no isolation distance (NaN), and one has 3.4e27; the paper code removes both before the median.
    Found by the attempt on Keles et al. 2024 (keles-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • OtherThe electrodes column pairwise_distances holds 1242 distances in all, where the units on each wire imply 1266 pairs. The pairs of the 7 units without waveform metrics are left out (26), and two wires of P58CS_R1 (ROFC4, ROFC8) with no unit in the file have one distance each (2).
    Found by the attempt on Keles et al. 2024 (keles-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • OtherP53CS run 2 has 34 recognition trials (17 novel, 17 familiar), where the Methods describe 40.
    Found by the attempt on Keles et al. 2024 (keles-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • OtherThe dandiset holds the EMU data only. The fMRI data are on OpenNeuro ds004798 as raw BIDS, without the fMRIPrep derivatives the paper fMRI checks use, and the demographics of the four fMRI-only participants are only in the paper Table 1 and its code.
    Found by the attempt on Keles et al. 2024 (keles-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
