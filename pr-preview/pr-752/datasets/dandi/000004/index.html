Dataset version 0.220126.1852 · tested 2026-10-15 · 13 open findings

  • Metadata errorThe new_old_labels_recog column is described in every file, and in the paper, as 0 for old and 1 for new stimuli. The data have 1 for old; label-1 images are those shown during encoding and draw most "old" responses. The paper's code reads it correctly, but a user following the description gets an AUC of 0.25.
    Found by the attempt on Chandravadia et al. 2020 (chandravadia-2020-01, 2026-10-15)
  • OtherSessions 43 and 44 (subject H23) and sessions 54 and 55 (subject H42) hold identical spike trains for every unit, 23 and 32 units. In each pair the units are the same neurons, so 55 of the 1864 units are repeats and the paper's neuron count includes them twice. Session 55 also repeats session 54's learning block trial for trial and differs only in its recognition block.
    Found by the attempt on Chandravadia et al. 2020 (chandravadia-2020-01, 2026-10-15)
  • Data missingSeven sessions (18, 20, 21, 23, 26, 27, 44) have 50 encoding trials and session 123 has 97, where the paper describes 100. Only 52% to 98% of the images later shown as old are in the stored encoding blocks of these sessions.
    Found by the attempt on Chandravadia et al. 2020 (chandravadia-2020-01, 2026-10-15)
  • Description missingElectrode impedance is empty for all 887 electrodes, though the paper lists it in the electrodes table. MNI coordinates are missing for 99 electrodes in 8 sessions.
    Found by the attempt on Chandravadia et al. 2020 (chandravadia-2020-01, 2026-10-15)
  • Description missingThe isolation distance is NaN for 493 of 1864 units and implausibly large for 4 (up to 8.08e+32), and SNR is NaN for 75 units and infinite for 11.
    Found by the attempt on Chandravadia et al. 2020 (chandravadia-2020-01, 2026-10-15)
  • File format errorThe spike times of 13 units in 7 sessions (21, 58, 68, 70, 73, 77, 128) are not in increasing order, and units 35 and 37 of session 58 hold every spike time twice, which doubles their firing rates and puts half their interspike intervals at zero.
    Found by the attempt on Chandravadia et al. 2020 (chandravadia-2020-01, 2026-10-15)
  • Metadata errorAge or sex differ from the paper's Online-only Table 1 for three subjects (C24 57 against 47, H33 30 against 29, H48 female 32 against male 54). The files follow the code's session list; Table 1 of Faraut et al. 2018 gives the same values as Online-only Table 1.
    Found by the attempt on Chandravadia et al. 2020 (chandravadia-2020-01, 2026-10-15)
  • Metadata errorSession 48 keeps the day of the experiment (2010-03-13) in session_start_time, where the paper says every day was set to the first of the month to protect patient information. Session 68 is dated 2000-01-01.
    Found by the attempt on Chandravadia et al. 2020 (chandravadia-2020-01, 2026-10-15)
  • Metadata errorExperiment ids outside Table 2 are used in four files (sessions 55, 77, 100, 105), and event codes outside Table 1 (0, 4, 22, 23, 98, 99) occur in nine files. In six files 1 to 18 trials have a response event that differs from the trials table's response_value.
    Found by the attempt on Chandravadia et al. 2020 (chandravadia-2020-01, 2026-10-15)
  • Identifier missingThe example neuron of Figure 3 is labelled as from session 144, which is not in the dandiset or in the paper's session list. The paper's code names NOID 144 as a recording of subject T103, whose one session in the dandiset is 2009, but nothing maps 144 to 2009.
    Found by the attempt on Chandravadia et al. 2020 (chandravadia-2020-01, 2026-10-15)
  • Data missingSession 98 of subject H48, listed in the earlier release (Faraut et al. 2018, Table 1), is not in the dandiset or in Online-only Table 1; its section in the code's session list is commented out.
    Found by the attempt on Chandravadia et al. 2020 (chandravadia-2020-01, 2026-10-15)
  • OtherThe 23 sessions that are new relative to the earlier release hold 325 units, where the paper says 288 neurons were added; 288 is the dandiset's 1864 units minus the earlier release's 1576.
    Found by the attempt on Chandravadia et al. 2020 (chandravadia-2020-01, 2026-10-15)
  • OtherThe institution is written "Hunigton Memorial Hospital" in 28 files and "Hunington Memorial Hospital" in 6, and the device as "Neuralynx-cheetah" and "Neuralynx-Cheetah".
    Found by the attempt on Chandravadia et al. 2020 (chandravadia-2020-01, 2026-10-15)
