Gu et al. 2026 · Preprint

Naturalistic behavior and self-generated neural activity predictive of self-correction

doi:10.64898/2026.05.26.727951 · paper key gu-2026

Datasets

  • DANDI:001836

Most behavioral findings held: rats learned the sequence, started biased toward side switching, corrected course on change-of-mind trials to the level of run-through trials, and showed fewer ripples during change of mind. The fall in switch bias held only in part (final ratio 1.85, not about 1.5), and the drop in switching on final choices was significant in fewer rats than reported. Distant decoded content was stronger after stopping than before in most rats, but its excess over inbound runs did not hold, and the files do not say which decoder made those tables. Links of remote and local extended content to correct next choices could not be tested because the decoder output is not deposited. Trial counts fell short because the dandiset holds fewer trials.
