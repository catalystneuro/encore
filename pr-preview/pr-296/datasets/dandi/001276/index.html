  • fig7be-center-linear-with-bursts

    In both CT26 and CHO cells, YP uptake at the array center increases linearly with the number of bursts (slopes different from zero at p < 0.001), except for saturation at 6 bursts (x4) in CT26 cells.

    The increase was linear in both cell lines, except when it reached saturation after 6 bursts (x4) in CT26 cells

    Results 3.4, paragraph 1; Figure 7B and 7E and legend

    How it will be judged

    Center YP is the background-subtracted intensity of the ROI at 0 mm, by the profile method, in a.u. A linear fit of center YP on burst number (1, 2, 4, 6 bursts (x4)), with the 6-burst point excluded in CT26 as the Fig. 7 legend says, and its slope tested against zero by a 2-sided one-sample t test (Fig. 7 legend). The paper does not say whether the fit is to individual experiments or to group means; the readings are (a) individual experiments and (b) group means. Burst number counted as 1, 2, 4, 6 or as 4, 8, 16, 24 total bursts gives the same p. Reproduced when, under one reading, both slopes are positive with p < 0.001; partially reproduced when that holds in one cell line only. A cell line absent from the dataset is reported as untested and the claim judged on the other.

  • fig7b-ct26-saturation

    In CT26 cells the central YP uptake saturates at 6 bursts (x4), falling below the linear increase seen from 1 to 4 bursts, while CHO cells show no saturation.

    Considering that even 2 bursts (x4) caused strong cell death in the center ( Fig. 5 ), saturation was expected and indicated that all cells were already killed and YP staining reached its maximum

    Results 3.4, paragraphs 1 and 2; Figure 7B and 7E legend

    How it will be judged

    The paper gives no test of saturation; it shows it by excluding the 6-burst point from the CT26 fit and by the 95% confidence band of that fit (Fig. 7 legend). Reproduced when the CT26 mean center YP at 6 bursts (x4) lies below the lower edge of the 95% confidence band of the linear fit through 1, 2 and 4 bursts, extrapolated to 6, and the CHO 6-burst mean lies within the band of the CHO fit through 1, 2 and 4 bursts. This band test is the file's reading; the paper's own CHO fit (Fig. 7E) uses all four burst numbers, and its significant slope is tested in fig7be-center-linear-with-bursts. Partially reproduced when the CT26 point lies below the fitted line but within the band. Readings (a) and (b) of fig7be-center-linear-with-bursts apply.

Reproduced when the FWHM and the gap percentage are within tolerance and the area percentage is within tolerance under one reading; partially reproduced when the FWHM alone is.
