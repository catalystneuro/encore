Dataset version 0.241023.2011 · tested 2026-10-18 · 9 open findings

  • Data missingThe optogenetic sessions (Opto2, Opto4, Opto9, UG21) hold no stimulation times and no record of which fiber was stimulated; the trigger line the paper's code uses is not in the files. Both can be recovered only from the laser artefact in the DF/F, which is unambiguous in Opto2 and UG21 but spreads over many fibers in the tdTomato mice.
    Found by the attempt on Vu et al. 2024 (vu-2024-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Description missingThe event table gives tone presentations without their frequency. DL18 has 44 tones, of which the paper analysed the 14 at 12 kHz; the frequency is stored only in a MATLAB object of the behaviour file on Zenodo. The tone start times are the trigger times, 11 frames before the sound according to the paper's code, which the dandiset does not say.
    Found by the attempt on Vu et al. 2024 (vu-2024-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Data missingDL15's sessions from 2021-09-10 (44 to 70 rewards) were run in a ViRMEn task; the paper keeps only rewards of one task phase, but the task variables are not in the files, so unpredicted rewards cannot be separated and Figure 1D can be tested only to week 8.
    Found by the attempt on Vu et al. 2024 (vu-2024-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Metadata errorThe FiberPhotometryTable column `included` is described as "whether this fiber has been successfully implanted", but it marks fibers within the striatum (the paper's in_striatum); in Opto2 two fibers labelled Caudoputamen are not included, and with them the counts match the paper's "within or on the border of the striatum" (24 locations).
    Found by the attempt on Vu et al. 2024 (vu-2024-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Metadata errorUG21's indicator is given as tdTomato. Its 3 sessions, 22 included fibers and stimulated fibers 12, 18 and 24 match the paper's rDAm3.0 mouse without opsin (Figure S7A), so the indicator is consistent with being a mislabel; the files cannot show which indicator was expressed.
    Found by the attempt on Vu et al. 2024 (vu-2024-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Metadata errorIn DL31 and DL32 (18 Hz, interleaved 470 and 415 nm) the fiber photometry series store a regular rate, but the frame timestamps of the behaviour drift from it by up to 0.43 and 0.54 s over the session, so times computed from the stored rate are wrong by up to half a second.
    Found by the attempt on Vu et al. 2024 (vu-2024-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Data missingThe data of Figures 2, 3, 7, S1 and S5A-D are not in the dandiset (the CT scans, the freely moving recordings, the D1-cre jaw experiments, histology and the 470 and 405 nm sessions of the salient-stimulus mice); they are on Zenodo. Opto9 has 12 sessions, so one of the paper's 26 tdTomato stimulations is missing.
    Found by the attempt on Vu et al. 2024 (vu-2024-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Data missingThe planned fiber targets behind Figure S3 are neither in the dandiset, whose fiber table gives only the localized tips, nor on Zenodo, where the code reads them from calibration files that were not deposited.
    Found by the attempt on Vu et al. 2024 (vu-2024-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Description missingThe archive's summary gives the approach as "cell population imaging" and lists only NWB container types as variables measured; the files hold multi-fiber photometry, treadmill velocity and behavioural events.
    Found by the attempt on Vu et al. 2024 (vu-2024-01, 2026-10-18)
