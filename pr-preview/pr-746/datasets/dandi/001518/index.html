Dataset version 0.261006.1957 · tested 2026-11-02 · 6 open findings

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds ten escalations of 1-us pulses, 19 sets each from 25 to 250 V, five with FluoVolt and five with Di-8-ANEPPS, all from 2025-01-24. It holds none of the alternating sets (Fig. 2C, D), 0.3-us pulses (Fig. 4D), Gd3+ experiments (Fig. 5), Ca2+ imaging (Fig. 6) or two-pulse experiments (Fig. 7). Its voltages span a factor of 10, so it cannot hold the paper's 43 to 1400 V/cm escalation (a factor of 33, in 9 sets). The paper's FluoVolt series has n = 4; the dandiset has 5.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2026 (semenov-2026-04, 2026-11-02)
  • Description missingThe files give the pulse amplitude in volts, and the paper reports field strengths in V/cm from Sim4Life modelling; neither the files, the paper nor its reference [20] give the factor between them, so the sets cannot be matched to the paper's field strengths without assuming one.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2026 (semenov-2026-04, 2026-11-02)
  • Metadata errorEvery file states an electrode distance of 1.00 mm, while the paper's Methods 2.2 state 1.6 mm. A two-dimensional model of two 0.5-mm rods gives 8.0 to 17.5 V/cm per volt at the midpoint for 1.00 mm and 4.6 to 7.2 for 1.6 mm (gap or centre distance). The paper's dye calibration, applied to the low-field response, would imply about 1.5 V/cm per volt, so either the stated geometry and voltage do not describe the field, or the dyes respond less here than in the paper.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2026 (semenov-2026-04, 2026-11-02)
  • Identifier missingEach field of view holds 3 to 6 cells, while the paper analysed one cell per experiment. Nothing in the files identifies the cell analysed, or gives cell outlines.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2026 (semenov-2026-04, 2026-11-02)
  • Description missingThe files give no pixel size, no field direction and no description of which images carry the pulse. The field direction (cathode-facing side towards the bottom of the image, 94 to 106 degrees) and the pulse onset at image 41 are clear from the data.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2026 (semenov-2026-04, 2026-11-02)
  • OtherFrame 0 is dark (laser not fired) in 175 of 190 sets, and 51 other frames in 45 sets are dark; the files do not mention it. With these frames left out, most baselines have 39 lit images, not 40.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2026 (semenov-2026-04, 2026-11-02)
