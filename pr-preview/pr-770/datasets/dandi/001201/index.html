Dataset version 0.261026.1912 · tested 2026-11-12 · 5 open findings

  • Metadata errorThresholdCrossings is stored with unit volts and conversion 1.0, but it holds integer counts of threshold crossings per 20 ms bin.
    Found by the attempt on Temmar et al. 2025 (temmar-2025-01, 2026-11-12)
  • Data missingThe files and the dandiset metadata give the subject's date of birth but not the implant date, so the paper's days after implant (349 to 1591) cannot be recovered from the dataset.
    Found by the attempt on Temmar et al. 2025 (temmar-2025-01, 2026-11-12)
  • File format errorIn 81 of 312 files the electrodes table has no imp column at all, in place of empty values; Appendix A.2 says such sessions have no impedance within three weeks, but a column that comes and goes breaks code that reads every file the same way.
    Found by the attempt on Temmar et al. 2025 (temmar-2025-01, 2026-11-12)
  • Description missingThe velocity series are described only as velocity of flexion. In the data, velocity at bin t is position at t+1 minus position at t (a forward difference), except at the bin before a pause, so velocity leads position by one bin; the direction of the difference is not stated in the files or the paper.
    Found by the attempt on Temmar et al. 2025 (temmar-2025-01, 2026-11-12)
  • Description missing29 sessions have 72 pauses of 1.05 to 472.9 s between runs of 20 ms bins, which the paper's '(mostly) contiguous' allows but the files do not mark; 998 trials have a length (stop minus start) of at most the 750 ms hold.
    Found by the attempt on Temmar et al. 2025 (temmar-2025-01, 2026-11-12)
