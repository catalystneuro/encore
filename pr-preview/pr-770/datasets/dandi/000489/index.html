  • Data missingStill present000489 holds only the 17 dishes of Fig. 2 and one sample image (Fig. 1F); 000448 holds the strobe images of Fig. 7. The dishes of Figs. 3 to 6 and the thermometry of Fig. 1E are in neither. Three more dandisets of the same lab name the paper in their metadata and match Figs. 3, 4 and 6 by title (000548, 000549, 000550); the registry's list of the paper's datasets and the 000489 title, which the claims file follows, do not point to them, so their 12 claims are blocked here and the thermometry claim is blocked as missing.
    Found by the attempt on Kim et al. 2022 (kim-2022-02, 2026-11-10); also about DANDI:000448
  • Metadata errorStill presentThe YO-PRO-1 channel (index 1) of sub-Fig2-0-03 (unipolar) and sub-Fig2-25-04 (25% bipolar) is the same image, value for value, while their MemBrite channels differ. One recording was deposited under two dishes of different conditions, and nothing says which dish it belongs to; the two files also give different session dates (2021-07-29 and 2021-07-26). With it left out the unipolar and 25% groups have 2 dishes each.
    Found by the attempt on Kim et al. 2022 (kim-2022-02, 2026-11-10)
  • OtherStill presentThe two channels of the 000489 sample image sub-Fig1F (MemBrite and YO-PRO-1 by its session description) are the same image, value for value.
    Found by the attempt on Kim et al. 2022 (kim-2022-02, 2026-11-10)
  • Description missingStill presentThe 000489 images give no pixel size (resolution -1), no electrode positions or polarity, and no ROI positions or values; the analysis has to locate the footprints and assume 0.65 um per pixel, which the electrode spacing supports. The field of each ROI, from the paper's finite element model, is not deposited.
    Found by the attempt on Kim et al. 2022 (kim-2022-02, 2026-11-10)
  • Metadata errorStill presentThe series description of the 17 Fig. 2 files of 000489 names "Channel1" for both filter sets and for MemBrite; the data show index 0 is MemBrite and index 1 YO-PRO-1. It also describes 144 images (12 x 12) of a well of a six-well plate, where Methods 2.5 describes 216 images of 35 mm dishes. Three files name their series "Fig2_25" without the dish number.
    Found by the attempt on Kim et al. 2022 (kim-2022-02, 2026-11-10)
  • File format errorStill presentfound on promptEach of the 18 files of 000489 stores its stitched two-channel image as one ImageSeries with data of shape (22323, 22323, 2), a starting_time rate of 2.0 Hz and unit n.a. An ImageSeries takes the first axis as time, so each file reads as 22323 frames of 22323 x 2 pixels; two GrayscaleImage objects in an Images container, or a series of shape (1, 22323, 22323) per channel, would describe the data. The three 25% files share the acquisition name Fig2_25.
    Found by the attempt on Kim et al. 2022 (kim-2022-02, 2026-11-10)
  • Selection not documentedStill presentfound on promptThe dish numbers in the subject ids of the 000489 Fig. 2 files run up to 18 (Fig2_50_18), while 17 dishes are deposited (3 per amplitude, 5 at 50%), and neither the dandiset description nor the files say which dishes were exposed, which were left out, or why. All unipolar dishes have the session date 2021-07-29 and all 75% and 100% dishes 2021-08-24, a date with no unipolar dish. Measured along bb', the 100% dishes take up more YP than the unipolar ones (largest mean 750.5 against 650.8 a.u.; higher at 9 of 11 ROI within 2 mm of the centre), where Fig. 2C shows every second phase inhibiting uptake.
    Found by the attempt on Kim et al. 2022 (kim-2022-02, 2026-11-10)
