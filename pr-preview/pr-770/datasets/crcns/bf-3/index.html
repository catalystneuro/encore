Dataset unversioned files (the archive keeps no versions) · tested 2026-11-06 · 5 open findings

  • Description missingSpikes, nose pokes and pellet deliveries were recorded only from 20 s before each cue onset to 31.5 s after it, where an EVT14 event (pause recording, in the authors' script) ends each window; the rest of each 3.5 min inter-trial interval is not in the files (177497 of 177598 pokes and 5871 of 5877 pellets fall in these windows). The data descriptor does not say so, and analyses of the inter-trial interval can only use these windows.
    Found by the attempt on Moaddab et al. 2021 (moaddab-2021-ventral-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Description missingThe data descriptor does not say what the event channels EVT01 to EVT14 hold, or which channel carries which cue in each behavior program (the letter after epr25 in the file name). Only the authors' script cubeScriptPR25.m gives them; the mapping it gives agrees with the shocks (all 780 danger trials and 390 of 1560 uncertainty trials shocked 12 s after onset, no safety trial). EVT08 repeats EVT01 in every file and is not described.
    Found by the attempt on Moaddab et al. 2021 (moaddab-2021-ventral-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Metadata errorThe data descriptor says VPcube.poke.base is the nose-poke rate over the 2 s before the cue; the cube holds the rate over the 20 s before it (equal in all 435 units to the rate computed from the poke times). It lists half duration and amplitude ratio among the cube's fields; the cube has neither (vpAnalysis.m computes them). It describes mean waveforms for each trial type; each unit file has one 32-sample waveform.
    Found by the attempt on Moaddab et al. 2021 (moaddab-2021-ventral-01, 2026-11-06)
  • OtherThe files hold 195 sessions (distinct session file names, each with its own trial table), one more than the 194 the paper reports for the 435 neurons.
    Found by the attempt on Moaddab et al. 2021 (moaddab-2021-ventral-01, 2026-11-06)
  • OtherFour pairs of units recorded in one session share 70 to 88% of their spikes within 0.1 ms (pa02082418 SPK01a and SPK15a; pa21040319, pa21040419 SPK06a and SPK15a; pa21040519 SPK07a and SPK15a), and a fifth pair 49% (pa21031519 SPK06a and SPK07a). They may be one neuron recorded on two wires and counted twice.
    Found by the attempt on Moaddab et al. 2021 (moaddab-2021-ventral-01, 2026-11-06)
