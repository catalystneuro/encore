A Three-Dimensional Spatiotemporal Receptive Field Model Explains Responses of Area MT Neurons to Naturalistic Movies

Shinji Nishimoto, Jack L. Gallant

Most of the paper's main findings held up in this data, on a reduced version of its model fitted to 45 of its 52 MT neurons. The compressive and divisive V1 filter model predicted responses as well as reported (mean correlation 0.518 against 0.52) and beat the other models. Excitatory spectral receptive fields lay on a plane, suppressive ones off it, and most formed a partial ring. The link between the horizontal-vertical ratio and simulated pattern selectivity did not reproduce, in a simulation whose settings the paper does not give. Divisive normalization alone gave no significant gain, and the explainable variance could not be tested, as the files hold only repeat-averaged responses.

    The pattern index for most neurons ranges from ... 6 to 2, and highly pattern-selective neurons are relatively rare

    Results, The horizontal-vertical ratio is correlated with simulated pattern selectivity, paragraphs 2 and 3; Figure 7B; Discussion, paragraph 4

    On the rectified model responses (firing rates, which cannot be negative), 64% of neurons have pattern indices within -6.6 to 2.2 with the per-neuron peak frequency and 120 degree plaids (median -5.10) and none exceed 2.2, which meets the criterion; with 90 degree plaids 49%, with the population frequency 58% (120 degrees) and 53% (90 degrees), with 2 and 3 neurons whose rectified responses are zero in every direction. With Poisson noise on the rectified rates (1 s presentations, 5, 10 or 20 repeats) the criterion is met in 17 to 20 of 20 draws under every combination. The unrectified model outputs give strongly component-like indices (medians -8.6 to -10.7, 0% to 18% within the range) and meet it under no combination, so the outcome rests on the rectification. The rectification sets a median of 25% of the grating and plaid directions to zero (28% with 90 degree plaids, range 0% to 58%), and the change of the index grows with that share (Spearman rho 0.568, p = 4.7e-5; 0.642 with 90 degree plaids). The 8 neurons with no direction clipped keep their component-like indices (median -8.33, 2 of 8 within the range), while 27 of the 37 clipped neurons fall within it. The indices thus reach the paper's range through the clipping of the tuning curves.

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • Assumed, the simulated responses are the model's predictions half-wave rectified, as firing rates. The paper does not say whether its simulated grating and plaid responses were rectified or carried noise; the pattern index of Smith et al. (2005), which it uses, is computed from measured firing rates, and the paper describes the simulation as "a prediction about the plaid selectivity that we would expect to obtain ... had we probed them with plaids" (Results, The horizontal-vertical ratio is correlated with simulated pattern selectivity). Where the paper adds noise to simulated responses it says so (Poisson noise for the null model), so no noise is added in the adopted reading; noisy readings are shown.
    • Gratings at 24 directions and plaids at 12, each component at the RMS contrast of the training movie (amplitude sqrt(2) times its SD), 30 frames each with the steady-state response read from frames 10 to 23; the response is the 2 x 2 model's prediction with the delay weights summed. The paper gives no stimulus parameters.
    • The pattern index uses 12 - 3 degrees of freedom in the Fisher transform, as in Smith et al. (2005) for 12 directions; correlations are clipped at +-0.999999 so that a pure pattern or component cell gives a finite index.

Reproduction Spatiotemporal receptive fields of MT neurons in naturalistic movies (Nishimoto and Gallant 2011)

Tests the findings of Nishimoto and Gallant (2011) on CRCNS mt-2, which holds the movie patches and trial-averaged responses of 45 MT neurons from two macaques. It fits the paper's V1 filter bank model with its 12 switched nonlinear variants by boosting, compares their prediction accuracy, and derives the on-plane and horizontal-vertical ratios, simulated pattern indices, the null model and the natural-movie control from the fits.
