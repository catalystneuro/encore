The paper's core result holds in this data: a voxel-based state space separates the task states of both the movie attention and the video game experiments, and classifies held-out time points well above chance. With public atlas regions standing in for the paper's unreleased localizer regions, the attention dimensions map onto face, body and scene areas in 4 of 5 subjects. The finding that attention sharpens state separation over passive viewing did not reproduce (2 of 5 subjects), and the paper does not say how that test was run. The grouping of game dimensions into two sets of cortical regions failed in the one player whose data could be mapped; the other player's data match no cortical mapper.

Notes on the paper

What the attempts found about the paper itself: a method or rule it does not give, a place where it disagrees with itself, published code that does something else. What they found about the data is on each dataset’s page.

  • Methods not availableQuestion for the paper's authors. The attention variable has zero weight in the deposited deconvolved data (attentive conditions z-scored per condition), so the third axis of the attention state space, and with it the CSIs, depends on rounding. Was the paper's analysis run on these files, and how was the attention dimension defined? This attempt adopted the axis the published code gives and showed the plane and random third axes as readings.
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the paper's authors. For the video game state space, in what order were the nine variables entered into the QR decomposition?   • Methods not availableQuestion for the paper's authors. In S1 and S6 the attend-humans CSI is below the passive one, in the paper's figures and here.
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the paper's authors. Were the video game state labels shifted in time against the BOLD, which is not deconvolved? The CSI peaks with the labels one TR later in both subjects (S1 0.729 against 0.691 at lag 0). This attempt used the labels as stored.
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the paper's authors. Reproduction Voxel-based state space modeling in a visual attention task and a video game (Zhang et al. 2021)

Tests the findings of Zhang, Gao, Cukur and Gallant (2021) on CRCNS data set vim-4, which holds deconvolved whole-cortex fMRI of five of the six subjects of a visual attention task and the BOLD data and behavioural state labels of the two subjects of a video game task. It recovers the supervised state space of each task, its cluster separation index, cross-validated classification and the projections between video game states. The functional ROIs behind the cortical maps are not in the data set; the cortical claims use public fsaverage atlases through the data set's mappers. All files

In the deposited deconvolved data the attentive conditions are z-scored per voxel within each condition, so the attention variable (-1, 0, +1) has an OLS weight of 1e-14, and the third QR direction is set by rounding. The code is the paper's method and the paper's values were presumably computed the same way; three readings are shown (code, random third axes, plane), and the outcome holds under the first two.fig1-csi-s2
  • Permutations 1000 per reading of the claims file, so p reaches 0.001 and not 1e-5.fig1-csi-s2
  • The state space is computed exactly in the space of the 24 PCs (helper docstring); the code itself is run for the observed values, and the human and vehicle axes of both agree to |r| = 1.0000.fig1-csi-s2
  • How condition labels are permuted is not fixed by the paper; two schemes were run (matched by movie frame, and global), and the outcome is the same under both.att-passive-lower
  • Permutations 1000, so the bound reached is 0.001 and not the paper's 1e-5.att-passive-lower
  • Held-out positions are z-scored with the training statistics, a standard choice the claims file names.att-classification
  • Assumed that group-atlas regions on fsaverage can stand in for the paper's per-subject localizer ROIs, which are not public; the claims file leaves this to the author and names an fsaverage atlas through the data set's mappers. FFA (mFus and pFus faces), OFA (IOG-faces), EBA (LOS, ITG and MTG bodies), PPA (CoS-places), OPA (TOS-places, right hemisphere only) and MT+ (hMT) from visfAtlas (Rosenke et al. 2021), whose authors relate the fusiform face areas to the FFA, the lateral body areas to the EBA and CoS-places to the PPA; RSC, LO, FO, IFS, IPS, FEF and SEF from HCP-MMP1.0 areas of the same names. Other definitions of the body, face and place areas are shown as variants.att-weights-cortex
  • The WordNet model of threshold (ii) follows the paper's deconvolution model (Methods, Deconvolution of the Hemodynamic Response in the Attention Task: WordNet labels, FIR delays 1 to 4 TRs, ridge with 10-fold cross-validation), fitted on the non-deconvolved BOLD of the three conditions for the voxels the regions reach. The ridge parameter is one per subject, the one with the highest mean prediction correlation (an assumption; the paper does not say), and a voxel passes when the correlation of its held-out prediction has one-sided p < 0.05 by the t distribution (the claims file leaves the test open).att-weights-cortex
  • S7 has no flash TRs, so its state space has 8 dimensions.game-csi
  • Each voxel of the video game BOLD is z-scored over the session, as the paper's Methods say ("The detrended voxel responses were normalized to zero mean and unit variance", Preprocessing and Visualization); S1's deposited data are already z-scored, S7's are not. Rounds 1 and 2 used S7 as stored; that analysis is shown as a variant.game-csi
  • The S1 video game data hold 65 min, where the paper reports 90 min for one subject.game-csi
  • Assumed the order of the variables in the QR is the order of the data set's state columns, since the paper's code takes the state matrix as it is stored and has no video game analysis; under Table 1's order the outcome would be partially reproduced.game-mutual-negative-projection

  • Under single-state labels a class with too few TRs in a training fold is left out of that fold and the fold's balanced accuracy is taken over the classes it could predict; in the first run such a class was scored with a recall of 0.game-classification
  • Assumed HCP-MMP1.0 areas on fsaverage for the regions the paper names (TPJ as TPOJ1-3, PGi and PGs; precuneus as 7m, PCV, POS2, 31pd and 31pv; prefrontal as the dorsolateral, inferior frontal, orbital and polar and medial prefrontal areas of Glasser et al. 2016; motor 4; premotor 6a, 6d, 6v, 6r, 55b and PEF; supplementary motor 6ma and 6mp; IPS as IPS1, IP0-2, LIPv, LIPd, VIP, MIP and AIP; FEF; SEF as SCEF), in place of the paper's ROIs, which are not public.game-weights-two-groups
  • Threshold (ii) uses the nine-state OLS model, 10 contiguous folds, one-sided t test of the prediction correlation, Bonferroni over all of the subject's voxels, as the claims file names it. A dimension counts when its mean weight is positive in each of its group's regions and the mean over them is above the mean over the other group's regions; a stricter variant is shown.game-weights-two-groups
