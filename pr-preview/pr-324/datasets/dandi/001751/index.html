  • fig5d-connectivity-totals

    Simultaneous recordings of up to eight layer 5b neurons tested 1,292 potential connections among 377 neurons and found 31 monosynaptic connections.

    In total, we tested 1,292 potential connections from an additional set of 377 neurons in layer 5b and confirmed 31 monosynaptic connections.

    Results, Altered connectivity profile of deep-layer AIC neurons, paragraph 2; Figure 5d

    How it will be judged

    A neuron is one recorded cell (one channel of one slice recording, counted once across the files of that slice). A potential connection is an ordered pair, a presynaptic cell stimulated while another cell of the same slice is recorded; the paper counts direction apart (VEN-to-PC and PC-to-VEN each 0 or 1 of 200, Results), so ordered pairs are the reading its text gives. A connection is a uEPSP or uIPSP evoked by single presynaptic spikes in the response averaged over more than 20 sweeps (Fig. 5f legend), uEPSPs at -70 mV and uIPSPs at -60 mV (Methods). The paper and its code give no amplitude, latency or noise rule for calling a connection, so the connections counted are those annotated, with two readings of the annotation, (a) the dataset's own connection labels and (b) the ConnType annotations of the code's metadata_multiPatch.xlsx, matched through file name and channels; each is run where it exists, and when neither exists the claim is blocked as methods_underspecified. Whether the code's "OK" quality connections count is not said; counted, with the count without them shown. A detection from the traces themselves would be the author's rule and is shown only as a variant that does not decide. Reproduced when all three counts are within tolerance under every reading run; blocked as methods_underspecified when the readings disagree.
