Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds about 200 reward trials per dual-array center-out session of Monkeys C and M, the size of the paper's Baseline epoch, with steady reach errors. The Force, Rotation and Washout epochs that 23 of the paper's 28 findings rest on are not in it.
  • Description missingNo file or metadata field says which sessions were curl field or visuomotor rotation sessions, which epoch the trials belong to, or which 16 sessions the paper analysed. Monkey C has 14 candidate sessions for the paper's 9.
  • Metadata errorIn seven Monkey C sessions (2016-09-09 to 2016-09-23) the cursor ends rewarded reaches about 4.2 cm from the center, with peak speeds near 14 cm/s, while target_corners places every target at 8 cm and the later sessions end at about 7.9 cm with peak speeds near 25 cm/s. The paper says Monkey C's first three sessions used 6 cm targets, which fits neither.
  • Metadata errorThe session description of the seven Monkey C sessions from 2016-09-09 to 2016-09-23 ends in a cut-off warning ("Warning: This session with Monkey"), in the files and in the archive's metadata, so what it warns of is lost. These are the sessions whose cursor positions do not match the stored targets.
  • Metadata errorIn all seven early Monkey C sessions the cursor never enters the square given by target_corners on rewarded trials, and in Monkey M 2014-02-03 and 2014-02-18 it enters it on 1% of them.
  • File format errorThe cursor timestamps repeat 8531 times in C 2016-09-23 and 5014 times in C 2016-10-11, so velocities computed from them are undefined on many samples.
  • OtherUnits above 5 Hz are far fewer than the paper reports, at most 634 M1 and 1224 PMd for any choice of 16 candidate sessions, against 918 and 2221. Monkey M has 6 to 21 PMd units above 5 Hz per session.
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the authors. How was the noise scaled in the dimensionality estimate adapted from Machens et al. (2010)? The Methods subtract random pairs of trials; taken as it is, that noise exceeds every component of the trial-averaged rates and gives 0 dimensions. This attempt assumed the noise of the trial average (difference over the square root of 2K) and the 95% rule, which gives medians of 7 and 10 with a kernel of 100 ms standard deviation (9 and 13 with a full width at half maximum of 100 ms).
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the authors. Is the 100 ms width of the Gaussian kernel its standard deviation or its full width at half maximum, and how were trials of unequal length averaged? With the full width the dimensionality medians are 9 and 13 (paper 8.5 and 14) and the Baseline lag is not significant; with the standard deviation the medians are 7 and 10 and the lag is significantly negative over 21 sessions. This attempt assumed the standard deviation for every claim.
  • Selection not documentedThe dandiset holds 14 dual-array Monkey C sessions where the paper used 9, and does not say how they relate to the paper's sessions or why the perturbation epochs were left out.
