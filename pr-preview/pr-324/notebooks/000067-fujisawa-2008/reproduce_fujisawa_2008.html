Reproducing Fujisawa et al. (2008) from dandiset 000067¶

Paper. Fujisawa S, Amarasingham A, Harrison MT, Buzsáki G (2008). Behavior-dependent short-term assembly dynamics in the medial prefrontal cortex. Nature Neuroscience 11, 823-833. doi:10.1038/nn.2134. This attempt used the full text of the author manuscript in PubMed Central (PMC2562676): Results, figure legends and Methods. The supplementary information could not be retrieved (PubMed Central and the publisher returned a reCAPTCHA page), so measures it defines are fixed in the claims file instead. This is the first revision, after the verifier's round-1 report: the variants it asked for are added in each section, and traj-diverge was compared on the x coordinate under a criterion added before that comparison was made; after the third round of verification it is blocked, as the x coordinate cannot show a lateral separation.

Dandiset. DANDI:000067, version 0.250624.0419. The paper (2008) cites no DANDI version. There are two published versions, 0.210812.1457 and 0.250624.0419; the data check below shows that both, and the draft, hold the same 28 assets (same asset and blob ids). The later one differs only in metadata (contributor identifiers and affiliations, a fuller link to the paper). The most recent published version is pinned.

What the dandiset holds. 28 sessions of three rats (EE, FF, GG): spike times of every sorted cluster, the LFP, in most files the wideband signal, and in ten files a trial table and position. The units table has no channel, shank, brain region or cell type. The recordings combine a 64-site prefrontal probe and a 32-site CA1 probe. The paper's four rats and its example session (EE.188) are not all here.

Outside inputs. The channel map (which channels are the prefrontal probe, the CA1 probe and the nose-poke sensors) comes from the CRCNS pfc-2 data description (version 0.7, doi:10.6080/K01V5BWK), which documents the same recordings; the link claim tests it against the data. Claims marked "with labels" use it. The analysis itself runs on the dandiset's data only.

Randomness. Each claim's section draws from its own generator, seeded by a fixed number and the session's index.

The trial table's start_time, stop_time and reward_time are the three columns of the CRCNS SessionNP matrix: the start of the first nose poke, the end of the last nose poke (the paper's trial start, position 0) and the start of reward consumption (the paper's trial end, position 1). The data agree: the first tracked linearized position after stop_time is 1.02 to 1.05 and the last before reward_time is 1.89 to 1.99 (medians over trials), the position is above 0 in 85% (EE.049) to 99.6% of run frames, and in at most 0.5% of frames outside the trials (from the first nose poke to the reward). stop_time is therefore not the end of the trial, and the description of left_or_right ("Time when subject began consuming reward") is wrong. The linearized position starts at time lin_t0 (0 s in every file, so frame k is at k / 39.0625 s) and is stated in metres but runs from 0 to 2 (0 outside trials), and the two "(x,y) coordinates" series are both x coordinates, of the two head LEDs (the conversion code takes columns 1 and 3 of the position file); they are stated in metres but are in pixels, and the second LED's is zero throughout. There is no y coordinate; x1 runs along the maze (see traj-diverge). The session durations match the CRCNS table (for example EE.049, 29.81 min). The trial table holds correct trials only (CRCNS description), so the task performance cannot be computed.

How the analysis differs from the paper's. Units are placed on the prefrontal probe by their mean waveform in the wideband signal, with the CRCNS channel map corrected for rat GG, and multi-unit and sensor clusters are left out by rules chosen here; the paper's own selection of well-isolated units is not known. Eight sessions of the dandiset are used (the paper does not list its sessions); EE.088 is left out because its units cannot be placed by waveform. With EE.088 placed by the conversion's block order the unit count is 631, within the paper's 633 plus or minus 63; the count of rats fails because the fourth rat is not in the dandiset. Units are not matched across sessions. The layer of each rat is not known, so values by layer are matched rat by rat. The cross-correlogram bin (1 ms), the number of permutations, the Poisson screen before the jitter test, the measure of transmission for first and later spikes, the definition of satellites and the 1 Hz rule for position analyses are choices made here, as the supplementary information was not available. The test of position dependence uses a band of mean plus c times s.d. because the rank band reaches 0.01 in only 102 of 285 pairs, and without the paper's thinning step it flags pairs of constant efficacy, so that claim is blocked. Inhibitory connections include troughs that mirror a peak in the other direction, as the criterion was written; the rates and classes without them are shown. The trajectories are compared on the x coordinate only, which marks the turn of the path in x and not a lateral separation, so traj-diverge is blocked.

What was found about the dandiset. It holds three of the paper's four rats, and not the example session EE.188 (nor EE.152 and EE.165). The units table has no channel, shank, region, cell type or multi-unit flag, and mixes prefrontal units, CA1 units, multi-unit clusters and clusters of the sensor signals; channel locations are "unknown" and the electrode groups are cut every eight channels whatever the probe. In rat GG the channel order differs from the CRCNS map, and the nose-poke sensors are in reverse order relative to the side labels. In six files one unit's spike times are out of order. The trial table's stop_time is the end of the nose poke (the paper's trial start), left_or_right carries the description of reward_time, and only correct trials are listed. The two position series are the x coordinates of the two LEDs, in pixels though stated in metres, the second all zero; there is no y coordinate. 100 laps outside the listed runs cannot be classed. The linearized position is stated in metres but runs from 0 to 2. EE.088 and the other sessions of its day have no wideband signal.
