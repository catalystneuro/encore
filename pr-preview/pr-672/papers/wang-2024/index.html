Wang et al. 2024 · Other journals

Phage selection drives resistance–virulence trade-offs in <i>Ralstonia solanacearum</i> plant-pathogenic bacterium irrespective of the growth temperature

doi:10.1093/evlett/qrad056 · paper key wang-2024

Datasets

  • DRYAD:tdz08kq5d

The main findings largely hold up in this data: phage lowered bacterial density at both temperatures, SCVs were far more phage-resistant, grew faster, reached lower carrying capacity and lost virulence, and mucoid clones from the 25 C phage treatment lost twitching motility and virulence. The "SCVs retain ancestral-level biofilm" claim failed (all 16 SCVs formed more biofilm than the ancestor), as did the mutation count (10.07 per clone against 8.5). The RNA-seq counts (1477 genes) and defense-system changes held, but phcS and gspG did not change as the text states. Two items could not be tested: the pilB and pilM mutants (data missing) and whether the gspE and gspF mutations fall in 2 or 3 clones (the variant table does not say which clones carry which variants). The genome reads are not public, so sequencing was checked on processed tables only.
