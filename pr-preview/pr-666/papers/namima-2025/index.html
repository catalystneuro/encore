The sparse, non-columnar shape clusters and the widespread contrast-invariant tuning held up in this data. The exact counts of clustered neurons and the texture findings did not. Clustered neurons were a minority and levelled off in well-sampled penetrations (21.5% here), but no analysis choice matched the paper's counts, probably because its code is unpublished and its reliability rule is ambiguous. Texture clustering did not reproduce, with a higher clustered fraction than the paper reports (26% to 52%), and the records give no clear cause. The current-source, orientation, curvature and gyral-penetration findings could not be tested, because the files hold only spike times, with no shape images or grid coordinates.

    Results, Position invariance for shape tuning similarity, paragraph 1; Figure 12B

    What the paper reports

    • Spearman's correlation, r = 0.325, p < 10⁻⁴ (n = 723 neurons, 33 penetrations)

    721 shape-responsive neurons in 33 M2 penetrations (723 in the paper) enter the position test (penetrations with 4 or more). Isimilarity at the optimal position against the main shape test with dark shapes (reading b) gives Spearman's r = 0.297 (reliability a, n = 378) and 0.325 (b, n = 676), p below 1e-4. Against the position test's center (reading a), r is 0.447 to 0.474 with the inferred center, p below 1e-4, so with the inferred center every reading gives a positive r with p below 0.0001 and reading (b) is within tolerance. Reading (b) does not use the position labels. Under reliability (b) the five choices of center give r = 0.274 to 0.406, all p below 1e-4, within tolerance only with position 3 (0.329); under (a) 0.279 to 0.567, p below 1e-4 except position 4 (r = 0.279, p = 2.1e-4), which fails the criterion's p below 0.0001. The outcome therefore assumes that the center is not position 4.

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • The files code positions 0 to 4 without saying which is the center; the position with the largest mean response of the penetration's responsive neurons is taken as the center (position 1 in 12 files, 2 in 11, 4 in 5, 0 in 3, 3 in 3), and all five are shown. The aggregate RF center is where the stimuli were placed on the neurons' RFs, so the population response should peak there; this is assumed, since the files do not label it. With position 4 as the center under reliability (a), p = 2.1e-4 and the claim would not be reproduced.
    • Neurons are those responsive in the main shape test; neurons responsive in the position test are a variant (603 neurons, r = 0.298 and 0.348).
    • Penetrations enter with 4 or more neurons, since a regression line needs 3 other neurons with reliable similarities (Materials and Methods, Clusters of similarly tuned neurons); this follows from the line rule.
