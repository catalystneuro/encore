Hulsey et al. 2024 · Cell Reports

Hulsey, D., Zumwalt, K., Mazzucato, L., McCormick, D. A., & Jaramillo, S. (2024). Decision-making dynamics are predicted by arousal and uninstructed movements. Cell Reports, 43(1), 113709.

doi:10.1016/j.celrep.2024.113709 · paper key hulsey-2024

Datasets

  • DANDI:000678

Most main findings held up in this data, one did not, and one could not be tested. Mice moved between optimal, disengaged and sub-optimal states, the optimal state was the longest lasting, and its probability followed an inverted U of pupil diameter. Variability was lower in the optimal state, and arousal measures classified states above chance. The claim that a movement index predicts state better than pupil did not reproduce (68.6% against 71.5%), and the variants that favour it keep pupil information. Training duration could not be tested because sessions before stage 5 are missing, and some dwell time values only partly matched.
