Zheng et al. 2022 · Nature Neuroscience

Neurons detect cognitive boundaries to structure episodic memories in humans

doi:10.1038/s41593-022-01020-w · paper key zheng-2022

Datasets

  • DANDI:000207

The behavioural findings held up in this data, the population state shift held for hard boundaries only, and the neuron-level findings could not be tested. Hard boundaries lowered temporal order accuracy, slowed responses and lowered confidence, and scene recognition did not differ by boundary type. The population state moved further after hard boundaries than before them, but after soft boundaries it was no larger than before. Boundary and event cells could not be tested because the Methods do not give a procedure that reproduces the paper's counts (1 unit found here against 42). Links to memory and reinstatement could not be tested because test trials cannot be matched to their clips, and theta phase needs field potentials the dataset lacks.
