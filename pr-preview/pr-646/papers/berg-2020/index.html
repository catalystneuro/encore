  • ✓Reproduced The dandiset's recordings can be matched to the paper's per-cell table (t-type and depth) by specimen ID, and the 18 features computed from them agree with the paper's feature table.
  • ?Not reproduced 385 Patch-seq neurons passing transcriptomic QC mapped to the five supragranular glutamatergic t-types, and 283 of them had recordings complete enough to compute electrophysiological features.
  • ?Not reproduced The neurons came from neurosurgical specimens of 90 tissue donors.
  • ?Not reproduced The electrophysiology figures show 66 LTK, 25 GLP2R and 136 FREM3 neurons, and 21 deep FREM3, 17 CARM1P1 and 37 COL22A1 neurons.
  • ◐Partially reproduced LTK neurons lie mainly in L2 and at the L2/L3 border, GLP2R mainly in upper L3, FREM3 span L2 and L3 and continue into L4, and CARM1P1 and COL22A1 lie almost entirely in deep L3 and along the L3/L4 border.
  • ◐Partially reproduced Among the three human homologous t-types (LTK, GLP2R, FREM3), 3 of 18 electrophysiological features explain more than 10% of variance by t-type (input resistance, f-I slope and rheobase), at FDR below 1e-5, with a maximum R2 of 0.16.
  • ✓Reproduced Among the three mouse L2/3 t-types, 3 of 18 electrophysiological features explain more than 10% of variance (input resistance, AP width and AP downstroke rate), at FDR below 0.05, with a maximum R2 of 0.12, below the human maximum.
  • ✓Reproduced GLP2R neurons, like LTK neurons, lack firing rate adaptation and have higher input resistance, but unlike LTK neurons (which have no sag) they have pronounced sag.
  • ✓Reproduced Between human and mouse homologous t-types, 17 of 18 electrophysiological features differ at FDR below 0.05.
  • ✓Reproduced A random forest predicts the homologous human t-type from electrophysiological features with 67% accuracy.
  • ✓Reproduced The same classifier predicts mouse L2/3 t-types from electrophysiological features with 54% accuracy, lower than for human.
  • ✓Reproduced Combining morphological and electrophysiological features raises accuracy slightly, to 69% for human and 60% for mouse neurons.
  • ◐Partially reproduced In FREM3 neurons, 9 of 18 electrophysiological features correlate with normalized depth at FDR below 0.05 (7 at FDR below 0.01); the strongest are increasing sag, increasing AP upstroke/downstroke ratio and decreasing latency at rheobase.
  • ✓Reproduced The three deep L3 types (deep FREM3, CARM1P1, COL22A1) differ in 15 of 18 electrophysiological features (FDR below 0.05), with R2 above 0.4 for 4 of them, while no feature reaches R2 0.4 among the three homologous types.
  • ⊘Blocked CARM1P1 neurons have a faster AP upstroke than deep FREM3 and COL22A1 neurons, and a higher input resistance than deep FREM3 neurons.
  • ✓Reproduced COL22A1 neurons have very high input resistance and a steeper f-I gain than the other deep L3 types, and less sag than CARM1P1 neurons.
  • ◐Partially reproduced Of 18 electrophysiological features compared between neurons from donors with low (0 to 1) and high (1 to 3) GFAP or IBA1 scores, only resting membrane potential under IBA1 differs, neurons from high-IBA1 donors being about 5 mV more hyperpolarized; the other 17 IBA1 and all 18 GFAP comparisons do not differ.
  • ✓Reproduced Average pathology scores over six markers ranged from 0.06 to 1.83 across donors, and most cases had averages below 1.0, a range not considered overtly pathological.
  • ✓Reproduced The six pathology markers are very weakly correlated with one another across donors, with only Ki-67 and Nissl (cellularity) modestly correlated.
  • ✓Reproduced 16 of 60 morphological features have R2 above 0.15 between the human homologous t-types (FDR below 1e-3), against 12 of 60 for mouse (10 of them at FDR below 0.05).
  • ✓Reproduced In FREM3 neurons, 37 of 58 morphological features correlate with depth at FDR below 0.05 (28 at FDR below 0.01), including apical height, maximum basal dendrite length and soma radius.
  • ✓Reproduced The deep L3 types differ in 24 of 60 morphological features (FDR below 0.05), with R2 above 0.4 for 20 of them.
  • ⊘Blocked CARM1P1 neurons have the largest total dendritic length of the L2 and L3 t-types, with extensive proximal oblique and basal branching, while COL22A1 neurons have very sparse branching.
  • ⊘Blocked FREM3 gene expression varies with soma depth; 790 genes correlate with depth at FDR below 0.05, enriched for synaptic transmission, cell migration and neuron projection morphogenesis.
  • ✓Reproduced Human L2 and L3 together (1.23 +/- 0.15 mm) are on average 1.16 times thicker than the whole mouse cortex (1.06 +/- 0.01 mm).
  • ✓Reproduced Human supragranular neuron density is 27.7 +/- 4.5 thousand cells/mm3, highest in L2 and halving to a minimum in mid L3, while mouse VISp L2/3 has about 6 times that density (165 +/- 24.9 thousand cells/mm3), uniform with depth.
  • ✓Reproduced Mean soma area doubles from L2 to deep L3 in human but is uniform across mouse L2/3; the interquartile ranges are 44 to 96 um2 in mouse and 107 to 253 um2 in human, and the largest human somata exceed 850 um2.
  • ✓Reproduced The variation of soma size in deep L3 is four times higher in human than in mouse.
  • ⊘Blocked The human FREM3 t-type has more within-type transcriptomic heterogeneity (variance explained by PC1 above permuted data) than any other human or mouse supragranular t-type, which are similar to one another.
  • ⊘Blocked The homologous types are similarly discrete from one another in mouse and in human, while CARM1P1 and COL22A1 have many more differentially expressed genes against the homologous types than the homologous types have against one another, in either species.
  • ⊘Blocked Human Patch-seq cells and dissociated nuclei of matched t-types are highly correlated in gene expression (R = 0.85), more than mouse cells and human nuclei (R = 0.57); 177 genes are enriched in nuclei and 2,670 at least four-fold in Patch-seq.
  • ⊘Blocked 219 marker genes distinguish deep FREM3, CARM1P1 and COL22A1, 77 of them also found in Patch-seq cells, enriched for axon, synapse, calcium ion binding and extracellular matrix terms.
