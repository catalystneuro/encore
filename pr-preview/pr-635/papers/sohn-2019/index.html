Bayesian computation through cortical latent dynamics

Here is the paragraph so you can save it yourself: The main findings held in part in this single session of one monkey. Sigmoidal increments of produced intervals, more bias for the Long prior, and the sigmoidal mean neural projection held; opposite biases at the overlapping interval, slopes below one in every condition, and the inverted-U variance across intervals did not. The neural projection matched the Bayesian estimate, though random axes often fit nearly as well. The recurrent network results and monkey G could not be tested, because the dandiset holds one session of monkey H and no trained networks.
