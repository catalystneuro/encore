Li et al. 2016 · Nature

Robust neuronal dynamics in premotor cortex during motor planning

doi:10.1038/nature17643 · paper key li-2016

DANDI:000011: Here is the paragraph for you to save: Spike-rate recovery after unilateral silencing and the coding direction's prediction of licks, including after bilateral silencing, held up in this data. Selectivity recovery times after ipsilateral silencing did not reproduce, and the persistent and ramping modes did predict licks, against the paper. Recovery times in the unilateral experiment could not be settled because the paper leaves analysis details open. The behavioural effects of silencing and the callosotomy findings could not be tested, since the behaviour sessions are missing and no file marks callosotomised mice or photoinhibited trials. The network models reproduced in part.

CRCNS:alm-3: Several central findings held up in this data: selectivity along the coding direction recovered after silencing ALM on one side but not both, predicted the upcoming lick, and after callosotomy no longer recovered from ipsilateral silencing. Single neurons regained selectivity on the paper's time course after unilateral silencing, but in a different neuron set, and in the bilateral experiment only under a few analysis readings. Two central claims did not reproduce: the persistent and ramping modes predicted the lick, and the modular integrator built from the matrix printed in the Methods recovered on one trial type only. The behavioural effect of silencing at the go cue could not be tested, as the behaviour-only sessions are not in the dataset.
