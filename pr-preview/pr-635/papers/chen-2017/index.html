Chen et al. 2017 · Neuron

A Map of Anticipatory Activity in Mouse Motor Cortex

doi:10.1016/j.neuron.2017.05.005 · paper key chen-2017

Datasets

  • DANDI:000015

Most findings that could be tested held. Object location was represented mainly in MM, preparatory lick direction activity mainly in ALM, and PT neurons showed earlier lick direction selectivity than IT neurons, though only under one onset rule, bin size and bootstrap. The fraction of trial-type-selective neurons in MM did not match (26.3% against 23%). The shift of peak activity from MM to ALM did not appear when the peak was taken over the whole window, which the paper does not define. Findings that depend on layer could not be tested because the files hold no plane depth, and the wide-field, photoinhibition and silicon probe findings could not be tested because those data are not in the dataset.
