  • fig1c-clicks-lost-before-verbal

    In all 10 subjects, during induction, the probability of response to clicks was below that to verbal stimuli in the transition between EON and LOC.

    During induction, in 10 of 10 subjects, we observed that Pclicks < Pverbal during the transition between EON and LOC

    Results, Continuous Transitions Between Consciousness and Unconsciousness, paragraph 2; Figure 1C

    How it will be judged

    Common method, with Open A and B. Per subject, Pclicks < Pverbal holds where the posterior Pr{Pclicks < Pverbal} exceeds 0.95, which is where the 95% credibility interval of Pverbal - Pclicks excludes 0 (Results, paragraph 2). The text does not say over how much of the EON to LOC interval this must hold. Readings, (i) at some time in the interval; (ii) over more than half of it. The subject count is compared with the expected value.
