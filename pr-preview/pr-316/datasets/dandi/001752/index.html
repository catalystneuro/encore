  • fig5b-sepsc-frequency

    The frequency of spontaneous EPSCs is lower in VENs than in neighbouring PCs.

    The frequencies of both excitatory and inhibitory events were significantly lower in VENs than in PCs (Fig. 5b )

    Results, Altered connectivity profile of deep-layer AIC neurons, paragraph 1; Figure 5b

    How it will be judged

    Per cell, sEPSCs are detected in the recording held at -70 mV (inward events), and the cell's frequency is the number of events over the duration analysed, in Hz (Methods, "Recordings of synaptic events were assessed for frequency (Hz)"; the code's tables give one frequency per cell as events over duration). The Methods fix the event threshold at "the maximus root mean squared noise level (5 pA)" and give no other detection parameter (miniAnalysis settings such as baseline window or area threshold are not given, and the code holds no detection step); a standard peak detector on the baseline-subtracted trace is used, and the threshold has two readings, (a) a fixed 5 pA amplitude and (b) the recording's own RMS noise level. The peak detector itself is a standard choice, not the paper's (miniAnalysis is not reproduced). The Methods say each trace was recorded for at least 5 min after the signal had stabilized (no shift beyond 4 s.d. of the baseline) and that at least 200 events were included for analysis, but not how long a stretch was analysed; the analysis uses the whole stable recording, and the code's 30 s per cell (its mIPSC table) is shown as a variant. Cell types are the dataset's labels; the paper's n is 8 VENs and 11 PCs (Fig. 5b legend), of which the code's 11 PCs are 10 distinct cells (one cell's two traces counted twice), so the PC group is read (a) as the paper's 11 entries and (b) as 10 distinct cells. Test, as in the legend and in Fig5ab_ana_sPSC.m, a two-tailed Wilcoxon rank-sum test of VEN against PC per-cell frequencies, uncorrected, at p < 0.05 (conventional; the panel's p-value was not readable). Reproduced when, under every combination of the readings, the VEN median is below the PC median with p < 0.05; blocked as methods_underspecified when the readings disagree, with every result shown. Group medians and ranges are reported.
