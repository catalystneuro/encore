Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Metadata errorEvery unit's electrode reference points at a row of the PMd electrode group, although the unit ids (array x 1000 + channel x 10 + sort) put 77 of the 152 units on a second array. The references of those units (rows 14 to 94) match their channel numbers without the offset of 96 that would place them on the M1 rows, so the M1 units are labelled as PMd.
  • Description missingThe electrode table's x, y, z and impedance columns are empty, so the anterior-posterior location of a unit cannot be read; the array is known only through the unit id convention, which the files do not describe, and nothing says whether id prefix 1 or 2 is the PMd array. This blocks prep-activity-more-anterior.
  • Data missingThe dandiset holds one array session of monkey J on the maze task with 27 conditions and 250 labelled trials. The paper's J-array dataset had 108 conditions and about 2166 trials per neuron, and the speed-task datasets of monkeys A and B, the single-electrode monkey J dataset and all EMG are not deposited, so 4 of the paper's main findings cannot be tested and the others rest on 6 to 10 trials per condition.
  • Identifier missingNothing in the files or the dandiset metadata says whether this session (2009-09-29) is the paper's J-array recording or a different one, or how its 27 conditions relate to the paper's 108.
  • Selection not documentedThe files keep 250 successful trials split into train and val, and 100 test trials as 700 ms segments without condition labels, go cue or behaviour. How the trials and the 9 mazes were chosen from the session is not described.
  • Methods not availableFor the paper's authors. The supplement keeps trials whose velocity profiles correlate with a reference proto-trial at r > 0.9. Is the proto-trial the condition mean or one trial, and are the horizontal and vertical profiles correlated separately or together? Here separate comparison keeps 70 to 82% of trials and concatenation 93 to 96%, against the stated ~90%; the mean with concatenation was assumed, and the mean correlation of Figure 4D passes p < 0.05 under 5 of 8 readings.
  • Methods not availableFor the paper's authors. How is "spikes/s of preparatory tuning" measured (range or standard deviation of condition means)? The range was assumed; with the standard deviation almost no array unit passes 5 spikes/s.
  • Methods not availableFor the paper's authors. In the leave-one-condition-out performance, is the PCA-based space S recomputed without the left-out condition? S fixed was assumed (one S per space, only the PD refitted). With S recomputed, peri-movement performance here does not decline at high dimensionality (best k = 13).
  • Methods not availableFor the paper's authors. For the segment-directions and kitchen-sink spaces, is the reach divided into three segments of equal time or equal path length? Here segment directions perform above zero with time segments (0.008) and below with path segments (-0.027), which decides fig6-maze-task-spaces, reproduced under time segments.
  • Methods not availableFor the paper's authors. How was movement onset detected, where is the go-locked trace spliced to the movement-locked one in Figure 4E, and what is the gray interval of Figure 8? Here the dataset's onsets, a splice at the median RT and a full cycle were used, and the other readings shown.

  • The 100 test trials of the Neural Latents Benchmark carry no condition labels or go cue times, so only the 250 training and validation trials are used in every analysis.j-array-recordings
  • Assumed the tuning strength is the range across conditions. The Methods give the thresholds in spikes/s of tuning ("at least 10 spikes/s of preparatory tuning", Methods, Neural recordings and datasets) without the measure; the SD reading leaves 5 to 8 of 152 units above 5 spikes/s and none passing both thresholds, which cannot be the paper's array analysis, so the range is the reading the paper supports.fig4d-mean-correlation-j-array
  • Assumed the proto-trial is the condition-mean velocity profile and that the horizontal and vertical profiles are concatenated.   • 6 to 10 trials per condition, against about 20 in the paper's J-array dataset.fig4d-mean-correlation-j-array
  • Assumed the neurons of Figure 4A to 4D (both thresholds) and the spliced trace. The legend of Figure 4 places E with A to D, and the Supplemental Experimental Procedures (Computing the mean firing rate) say the spliced trace "was also useful for the analysis in Figure 4E". The splice maps the go-locked trace (-200 to +100 ms) to movement time with the median RT (354 ms) and continues with the movement-locked trace; where the paper splices is not given, and the median RT is an assumption. The decline under the spliced trace (-263 ms) falls near the splice point (-254 ms) and is set by it; the movement-locked trace alone gives -307 ms.fig4e-correlation-time-course
  • The two intermediate conditions are those at one third and two thirds of the rank order of preparatory rate, a standard choice (the legend says only "two intermediate conditions").suppfig1-population-psth-weak-movement-tuning
  • Reach end, used for endpoints and the trajectory-based task spaces, is where the condition-mean speed falls below 10% of its peak, a standard choice; acceleration is the derivative of the smoothed velocity; the T-matrices are sampled every 10 ms, a standard choice.fig6d-perimovement-best-j-array

  • Assumed S is computed once from all conditions and only the PD regression leaves the condition out. The Methods (Using a PD, PD performance) define S as "a c x k matrix containing the location of every condition" and say "One condition was left out and we found the PD that best captured tuning for the remaining conditions", which refits only the PD.
