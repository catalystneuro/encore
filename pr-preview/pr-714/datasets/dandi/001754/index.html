Findings about the data 14

What the attempts that used this dataset found about the data themselves, for its owner and the archive. The claims and their outcomes are on each paper’s page.

  • Metadata errorStill present (partly fixed in the unpublished draft). The Rat1 and Rat2 files of each flight day hold one tracker's trace over the whole session (98.6 to 98.8% of positions at shared timestamps within 2 pixels), each presented as that rat's position, and nothing names the animal tracked. With real against time-shifted spikes, the trace behaves as Rat2's position in Rat2's flight day 9 ES window (12 of 28 Rat2 units, 0 of 36 Rat1 units), and in Rat2's flight day 4 ES window neither rat's units are selective (0 of 17 and 1 of 17), so whose position it is there is not established; there the two files hold the same trace (4 of 707 shared valid positions more than 2 pixels apart), so neither file's copy settles it.
    Found by the attempt on Knierim et al. 2000 (knierim-2000-03, 2026-10-20)
  • Data missingStill present. Position is given only at spike times, so occupancy time cannot be computed from it, and the preflight files have no position at all.
    Found by the attempt on Knierim et al. 2000 (knierim-2000-03, 2026-10-20)
  • Metadata errorStill present in this version (the draft's description drops it). The dandiset description says the preflight recordings were made on a flat rectangular track, but the preflight files label their epochs ES (Escher staircase) and MC (Magic Carpet) and hold ES and MC rate maps. The paper states the rectangular track for rat 2 only.
    Found by the attempt on Knierim et al. 2000 (knierim-2000-03, 2026-10-20)
  • Metadata errorNew. The files' experiment description says baseline sessions were on a rectangular track, interleaved with task sessions; the paper says the baseline sessions were in the sleeping pouch, and the rectangular track was rat 2's preflight task. The preflight files have no baseline epoch.
    Found by the attempt on Knierim et al. 2000 (knierim-2000-03, 2026-10-20)
  • Identifier missingStill present (fixed in the draft). The rate-map table says cell_number matches the cluster id on the same tetrode, but the stated link exists for 98 of 211 flight unit-epochs and names the best-matching map for 84. Every one of Rat3's 42 flight day 4 ES maps has cell_number -1 ("not a per-cell map") though their names are per-cell short names, so Rat3's session 2 and session 3 maps cannot be paired by cell; tying each to the unit it best matches is not one to one in session 3. Elsewhere 2 to 10 active maps per file and epoch have cell_number -1, and in flight these match one unit about as well as the numbered maps (median correlation 0.555 against 0.595), so they appear to be per-cell maps without their cell number.
    Found by the attempt on Knierim et al. 2000 (knierim-2000-03, 2026-10-20)
  • Description missingStill present (partly fixed in the draft). The rate maps are smoothed (91% of the positive-rate bins of a well-matched map hold no spike of the unit), and neither that nor the kernel is stated.
    Found by the attempt on Knierim et al. 2000 (knierim-2000-03, 2026-10-20)
  • Description missingStill present (fixed in the draft). Rate maps use -999 for unvisited bins, which the column description (rate in Hz) does not state, and the occupancy counts the lost position (0, 255) in bin [63, 0], up to 19% of a flight occupancy map.
    Found by the attempt on Knierim et al. 2000 (knierim-2000-03, 2026-10-20)
  • OtherStill present (fixed in the draft). Six groups of identical rate maps are stored in three files, some under different tetrodes (Rat1 FD4 TT1 and TT11; Rat2 FD4 TT13, TT19 and TT22; Rat2 FD9 TT13 and TT14).
    Found by the attempt on Knierim et al. 2000 (knierim-2000-03, 2026-10-20)
  • Data missingStill present (partly fixed in the draft's description). The dandiset description reports normal theta and sharp waves and ripples in the hippocampal EEG, but no file holds EEG, and no file name in the NASA record OSD-968 suggests it.
    Found by the attempt on Knierim et al. 2000 (knierim-2000-03, 2026-10-20)
  • Description missingStill present (fixed in the draft). Spike times are resolved to 1 ms or finer below 1000 s, 10 ms to 10000 s and 0.1 s beyond, with zero intervals that follow, and this is not stated; Rat1's flight day 4 file has two overlapping baseline epochs (2525 to 3390 s and 2720 to 3390 s), and 1 to 14 spikes per file lie outside every epoch.
    Found by the attempt on Knierim et al. 2000 (knierim-2000-03, 2026-10-20)
  • Description missingStill present (partly fixed in the draft). The age is "/" for every subject, and the position's pixel scale and camera view are not described.

    Found by the attempt on Knierim et al. 2000 (knierim-2000-03, 2026-10-20)
