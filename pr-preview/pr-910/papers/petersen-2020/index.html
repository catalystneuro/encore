Cooling of Medial Septum Reveals Theta Phase Lag Coordination of Hippocampal Cell Assemblies

Peter Petersen, György Buzsáki

Summary: Most of the paper's main findings hold in this data: cooling the medial septum slows hippocampal theta, raises choice errors (about twofold, where the paper reports threefold) and slows place cell oscillations along with LFP theta. Theta time lags between place cell pairs lengthen, and phase lags stay unchanged only under one reading of the lag. The preserved gap between place cell and LFP theta frequency, and the stable place field size and information, did not hold; the paper drew its fields by hand and they are not shared. Distance-time compression could not be tested, as the paper's surface fit fails on this data and its procedure is not described.
