Dataset version 0.240329.1926 · tested 2026-11-10 · 9 open findings

  • Metadata errorThe `rate` attribute of the `starting_time` of `wheel_position`, `lickPiezo` and `face_motion_energy` holds the sampling interval in seconds (0.0004, 0.002 and 0.0253), not a rate in Hz. Sample counts times these values give the recording lengths (2,609 to 4,103 s), so a reader that takes the attribute as a rate in Hz gets every sample time wrong.
    Found by the attempt on Steinmetz et al. 2019 (steinmetz-2019-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Description missing`units/peak_channel` is 1-based: the electrodes row peak_channel - 1 matches the unit's cluster depth (median difference 8.0 um, against 13.4 um for row peak_channel). The description does not say so, and a unit's region depends on it.
    Found by the attempt on Steinmetz et al. 2019 (steinmetz-2019-01, 2026-11-10)
  • File format error`wheel_moves` is an IntervalSeries whose `data` (the movement type) has half the length of its `timestamps`, which hold each movement's onset and offset in turn; an IntervalSeries should have one data value per timestamp.
    Found by the attempt on Steinmetz et al. 2019 (steinmetz-2019-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Description missingChannel locations mix levels of the Allen hierarchy. 2,262 curated units carry finer labels (SCig, LSr, COA, TT, SCsg, ORBm, LSc, PIR, DP) whose parents are among the paper's 42 regions, and others carry broad labels (TH, MB, CA). The paper's counts per region are recovered only after mapping the finer labels to their parents, which the dandiset does not document.
    Found by the attempt on Steinmetz et al. 2019 (steinmetz-2019-01, 2026-11-10)
  • OtherGenotypes are written differently from Supplementary Table 1 (Ai95 x VGlut1-Cre for RCL-GCaMP6f x VGlut-Cre, Vglut1-Cre for Vglut1-IRES2-Cre-D); sexes, ages and the 39 sessions match the paper.
    Found by the attempt on Steinmetz et al. 2019 (steinmetz-2019-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Selection not documentedThe Methods exclude 5 of 39 sessions from the pupil analyses for unusable video, but the dandiset does not say which; eye_area is present in all 39 files, with missing values in two.
    Found by the attempt on Steinmetz et al. 2019 (steinmetz-2019-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Description missing`spike_amps` and `waveform_mean` give no unit, and the trial table's `included` flag (engaged trials) differs from the Methods' disengagement rule on 1.7% of trials, although it gives the paper's 9,538 trials exactly.
    Found by the attempt on Steinmetz et al. 2019 (steinmetz-2019-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Data missingThe dandiset holds no histology (DiI spot coordinates, slice images) or electrophysiological landmarks, so the probe localization of the Methods cannot be checked.
    Found by the attempt on Steinmetz et al. 2019 (steinmetz-2019-01, 2026-11-10)
  • OtherThe kernel model of the paper's code runs on the dandiset: with the basis of CanonCor2all.m and the elastic net of the Methods, 2,886 of 13,852 task-responsive neurons pass the 2% cross-validated VE of the nested tests, against about 4,240 in the paper, so counts of kernel-requiring neurons come out lower than the paper's. Following four details of the code's fits (one glmnet fit on all basis columns, truncated; no intercept; VE from the variance of the residuals; glmnet's scaling of the ridge term) raises the count to 3,199; the rest of the gap is unexplained.
    Found by the attempt on Steinmetz et al. 2019 (steinmetz-2019-01, 2026-11-10)
