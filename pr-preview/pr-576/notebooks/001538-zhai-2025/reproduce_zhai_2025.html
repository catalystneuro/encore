def mw(a, b):
    """Two-sided Mann-Whitney test: n, medians, U, P and the rank-biserial effect (positive when b > a).

    P is scipy's default (exact for small samples without ties, otherwise asymptotic); P_exact is
    the exact P where there are no ties (NaN otherwise), as Prism reports for small samples."""
    a, b = np.asarray(a, float), np.asarray(b, float)
    a, b = a[~np.isnan(a)], b[~np.isnan(b)]
    u, p = stats.mannwhitneyu(a, b, alternative="two-sided")
    ties = len(np.unique(np.concatenate([a, b]))) < len(a) + len(b)
    p_exact = np.nan if ties else stats.mannwhitneyu(a, b, alternative="two-sided", method="exact").pvalue
    return {"n_a": len(a), "n_b": len(b), "median_a": np.median(a), "median_b": np.median(b),
            "U": u, "P": p, "P_exact": p_exact, "effect_r": 1 - 2 * u / (len(a) * len(b))}


def show(d):
    """Rounded for printing; P to three significant digits."""
    out = {}
    for k, v in d.items():
        if isinstance(v, (float, np.floating)):
            out[k] = float(f"{v:.3g}") if k in ("P", "P_exact") else round(float(v), 4)
        else:
            out[k] = int(v) if isinstance(v, np.integer) else v
    return out

The dendritic assay evokes three spikes, the first at 1.00 s, and the Ca2+ signal rises after it: the area in the analysis window is larger than in the same window 1.5 s later at nearly every site, so the line scans and the voltage share the sweep clock. The stored dendritic stimulus is zero in every sweep, so the injected current is not in the files; the three spikes show it was delivered. The line-scan rate (678 to 693 Hz, 2500 lines per sweep) is the rate of the region series used here. Some sweeps have no kymograph slice in the index table (count above; the kymographs hold fewer trials than the region traces); the analysis uses the region traces (mean along the scan line), which have a slice for every sweep.

Six site series do not hold the usual three trials. Three hold six trials under one name while the cell has no series of the other compartment with that number (iSPN 20160526 cell 3 Distal1, iSPN 20160608 cell 1 Proximal1, dSPN 20170204 cell 1 Proximal1). def cell_index(sites, basal_range=lid.BASAL_RATIO_RANGE):
    out = []

How the analysis differs from the paper's. Spike counts, rheobase, the dendritic index and the GRAB-ACh area follow the paper's scripts, but the dendritic background is the script's constant for every session (the files carry none), sites are paired by number, and the basal-ratio rule for keeping dendritic pairs is a choice made here (0.5 to 2; it drops no pair). The stricter 0.8 to 1.25 would change two outcomes in opposite directions: fig3f-ispn-dendritic-on-lower would fail (P = 0.077) and fig3f-ispn-dendritic-sul-no-change would pass (P = 0.066). Three site series that hold six trials are split into two sites, the half with the smaller area taken as distal. The GRAB-ACh stimulus time comes from the trial table (3 s in six files where the script assumes 10 s). Sr2+-oEPSC events were found with a threshold detector written here, which finds fewer, larger events than the paper's TaroTools detection with visual verification. Mann-Whitney P values are computed with scipy (exact for small samples without ties, otherwise asymptotic), where Prism was used; the exact P is printed beside it where there are no ties.

What was found about the dandiset.

  • The somatic and dendritic patch-clamp data of Figures 1, 3, 6, 7 and 9 are there for every group except the unlesioned control and 6-OHDA groups, with the paper's cell counts; most cells' spike counts match the paper's table exactly. The groups must be read from condition_subfolder: files named for SCH 23390 or sulpiride hold baseline cells and cells recorded in both conditions.
  • The Sr2+-oEPSC data hold one cell per mouse (14 of 33), the GRAB-ACh data one region per mouse (11 of 34), and the AIM data 9 mice, two of which are not in the paper; too few for the paper's tests. The AIM totals of the Figure 8 mice differ from the paper's.
  • The AIM genotype labels contradict each other between the subject metadata, the session names, the table's genotype column and the video files.
  • Spine density, rotation counts and open-field data are not in the dandiset as measurements; the spine and rotation raw data are (image stacks and videos), but the counts need manual software.
  • Three dendritic site series hold two sites under one name (six trials), one site has a single trial, and two cells lack sites the paper's values need: dSPN on+SCH 20190411 cell 1 has only its second pair (0.584 here against 0.228 in the paper, which decides fig1i-dspn-dendritic-sch-lower) and iSPN on+sul 20190508 cell 3 only a distal site (with the paper's 0.217 for it, the sulpiride test gives the paper's P = 0.0545, which decides fig3f-ispn-dendritic-sul-no-change under the main rule). That these missing sites explain the differences is an inference from the recovered P values; the paper does not list each cell's sites.
  • The spine files hold image stacks only, with no processing or analysis entries.
  • The dendritic stimulus series are all zero, the Sr2+-oEPSC light-pulse series are zero in many sweeps and are not referenced by the recordings table, and the GRAB-ACh "zero-laser" background images are brighter than the signal.
