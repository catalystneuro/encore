  • Data missingStill presentThe two rats of the optogenetic experiments (Figure 6, Figure S4), with their light stimulation times and waveforms, are not in the dandiset; its 98 files hold six rats (DT1, DT2, DT5, DT7, DT8, DT9) and no stimulus or optogenetic series.
    Found by the attempt on Tingley et al. 2019 (tingley-2019-02, 2026-11-18)
  • Description missingStill presentElectrode positions are not stored, nor the probe model of each recording, so distances between sites, travelling-wave speeds (Figure 3) and CA1 neuron depths (Figure 7D, E) cannot be computed.
    Found by the attempt on Tingley et al. 2019 (tingley-2019-02, 2026-11-18)
  • Description missingNewThe estimated relative depth of each recording within LS (STAR Methods), on which Figure 2E, F and Figure S1C, D rest, is not stored; session names carry drive depths only.
    Found by the attempt on Tingley et al. 2019 (tingley-2019-02, 2026-11-18)
  • Description missingNewThe pre-behavior home cage, maze and post-behavior home cage periods of each recording are not marked; they can be inferred only from the first and last trials.
    Found by the attempt on Tingley et al. 2019 (tingley-2019-02, 2026-11-18)
  • Description missingStill presentMost hippocampal units are labelled hpc, not CA1 or CA3, so CA1 and CA3 populations cannot be told apart reliably: 10 recordings have units labelled ca3 and 19 have a ca3_channel flag, against the paper's 25 CA3 recordings.
    Found by the attempt on Tingley et al. 2019 (tingley-2019-02, 2026-11-18)
  • Metadata errorPartly fixedThe 36 units of sub-DT7 session 20170407-936um-1152um-merge have an empty region label.
    Found by the attempt on Tingley et al. 2019 (tingley-2019-02, 2026-11-18)
  • OtherNewIn three files less than half of the trial time is scored WAKE (DT2 3540um-1288um 20160227, 12.5%; DT5 20161014-1296um-1476um, 20.3%; DT8 20170201-72um-432um, 31.6%), against 100% in the median file; either the sleep scoring or the trial times of these files are doubtful.
    Found by the attempt on Tingley et al. 2019 (tingley-2019-02, 2026-11-18)
  • Data missingPartly fixedThe DT9 CA3 recordings (20170518, 20170519, 20170521, 20170525, 20170526) have no CA1 ripple table, although the paper detected SPW-Rs on a CA1 reference electrode for CA3 recordings; DT7 20170410 has 61 LS units but no LS HFO table, and DT2 20160215-122710 has 37 LS units and no event table at all.
    Found by the attempt on Tingley et al. 2019 (tingley-2019-02, 2026-11-18)
  • OtherNewThe deposited LS HFO detections (LSRipples) were made with parameters that differ from the paper's Methods (120-180 Hz, durations up to 100 ms, peak thresholds of 4 to 6 SD in some files, against 120-200 Hz, 15-250 ms and 5 SD), and CA1Ripples with 130-200 Hz; the files' descriptions say so. Their event counts, durations and frequencies match the paper's (27014 LS HFOs, 27.8 ms, 147.6 Hz).
    Found by the attempt on Tingley et al. 2019 (tingley-2019-02, 2026-11-18)
  • Description missingPartly fixedThe electrode table holds no site positions, and the units table at most one waveform per unit (raw_waveform, 32 samples, with max_waveform_channel, in the DT1, DT2 and DT8 files listed; no waveform in the DT5, DT7 and DT9 files). On the septal shank of the DT7 recordings the sites' region labels (ls, ms, acg, cc) alternate along the LFP's channel order, so the labels cannot be checked against the sites' geometry.
    Found by the attempt on Tingley et al. 2019 (tingley-2019-02, 2026-11-18)
  • Description missingNewfound on promptFour DT7 files label all 128 electrodes "unknown" (20170327-864um-360um, 20170406-864um-1152um-merge, 20170407-936um-1152um-merge, 20170408-936um-1152um-merge), so their channels cannot be assigned to LS, hippocampus or the other recorded regions.
    Found by the attempt on Tingley et al. 2019 (tingley-2019-02, 2026-11-18)
  • OtherStill presentfound on promptThe lab's LS and CA1 detection channels share a 120-200 Hz signal outside any event: at 2000 random times in 45 recordings the phase-locking value of their phase difference has a median of 0.177 (0.060 to 0.311), against 0.024 with the CA1 phase taken 1 s later and about 0.020 for random phases, and their band-passed signals correlate with a median r of 0.245. The files do not say how the channels are referenced; ref_channel marks the detector's noise channel, not a re-reference.
    Found by the attempt on Tingley et al. 2019 (tingley-2019-02, 2026-11-18)
