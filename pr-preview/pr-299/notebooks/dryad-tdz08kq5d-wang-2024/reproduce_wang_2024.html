Reproducing Wang et al. (2024) from Dryad dataset tdz08kq5d¶

Paper. Wang J, Wang X, Yang K, Lu C, Fields B, Xu Y, Shen Q, Wei Z, Friman V-P (2024). Phage selection drives resistance-virulence trade-offs in Ralstonia solanacearum plant-pathogenic bacterium irrespective of the growth temperature. Evolution Letters 8(2), 253-266. doi:10.1093/evlett/qrad056. The claims file was written from the version of record (Europe PMC full text, PMC10959482) with its supplement (Figures S1 to S5, Tables S1 to S6).

Dataset. doi:10.5061/dryad.tdz08kq5d, Dryad version id 263142 (dryad:tdz08kq5d/263142). The paper cites the dataset's DOI and no version. Dryad's API lists one version, 263142 (version number 5, published 2023-11-03), so it is the most recent published version and the one pinned here. It holds two files: README.md (SHA-256 79a23ead0ade6ec77d36e7d1dc34ed51508417d827175829093609d8740c051a) and temperature_phage_data_allfigures2.xlsx (SHA-256 6bc2c64abc9e1c65bcdc4080fcaa81c669c39f56a6f63708483e47c563c5c0dd), both read here. Dryad needs a token to download a file, so the registry's workflow stages the files before the run and the helper reads them from there, checking each SHA-256 against Dryad's file list (docs/archives.md, "Dryad").

What the dataset holds. One sheet per figure: bacterial OD600 of the 40 selection lines (Figure 1B), phage densities (1C), colony counts of 16 lines at day 12 (1D), the per-clone phage resistance, maximum growth rate and carrying capacity of 128 clones (2A to 2C), twitching, biofilm and disease incidence of 28 clones and the ancestor (2D to 2F), the variant calls of the 28 sequenced clones aggregated by group (Table 1, Table S4), the DESeq2 table and gene lists of Figure 4, the KEGG enrichment table of Figure S5, the adsorption assay of Figure S3, and the curves of Figures S2 and S4. The growth curves the growth parameters were fitted to, the per-clone variant calls, the raw reads and the pilB/pilM data of Table S6 are not in it.

Outside data. The paper names NCBI BioProjects PRJNA1028400 and PRJNA1032726. Their public metadata are read in the data check to say what they hold; no data from them enter a result.

Answered questions. The first run of this attempt asked four questions (questions/dryad-tdz08kq5d.yaml), and a reader answered them in the claims file: the OD600 time points of Figure 1B are days 2 to 12; the 0.24% of Figure 1D is read both as a mean of replicate frequencies and from pooled counts; the unit of the Figure 2A to 2C ANOVAs is the mean of the clones of each colony type within each replicate population, with each clone as a variant; and the sequencing and RNA-seq claims are data: dataset, tested on the deposited tables. The claims' sections below follow the criteria as they now stand.

# Phage selection drives resistance-virulence trade-offs in Ralstonia solanacearum plant pathogenic bacterium irrespective of the growth temperature

[https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.tdz08kq5d](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.tdz08kq5d)
This is an experiment focused on the coevolution of Ralstonia solanacearum and phages. We gathered information concerning the growth of R. solanacearum and the presence of phages at various stages of the experiment. Subsequent to isolating the evolved strains, we conducted assessments of their fundamental characteristics, encompassing growth parameters, motility, biofilm formation capability, and pathogenicity. Furthermore, we performed genomic analysis on these evolved strains, identified pivotal genes through SNP analysis, and corroborated the functionality of these genes via experimental assays.

## Description of the data and file structure

Datasets included:

1. transfer OD
    •	every two days’ OD600 value of each replicate in R. solanacearum -phage selection experiment
   * in the row of “phage”, “1” represents in the presence of phage, and “0” represents in the absence of phage
2. phage density
   * every four days’ phage density of each replicate of treatments with the presence of phage in R. solanacearum -phage selection experiment
3. scv frequency
   * the densities and frequencies of SCV and mucoid ones in each replicate of the end of R. solanacearum -phage selection experiment. 4 out of 10 replicates of selection experiment were randomly chosen here.
   * in the row of “phage”, “1” represents in the presence of phage, and “0” represents in the absence of phage
4. resistance to phage
   * the resistance to ancestral phage of all isolates (N=128) from all treatments, and together with the phage resistance of ancestral RS-N
   * the resistance to ancestral phage of each tested clone were replicated three times, and only average resistance to phage were shown here
   * the “treatment” row refers to the evolution trajectory of isolates, in detail, “35\_1” represents evolved with phage at 35°C, “35\_0” represents evolved without phage at 35°C, “25\_1” represents evolved with phage at 25°C, and “25\_0” represents evolved without phage at 25°C
5. maximum growth rate and
6. carrying capacity have the same dataset structure with dataset 4)
7. twitching,
8. biofilm and
9. disease incidence were the twitching ability, biofilm formation ability and ability of causing tomato wilt disease of a subset of all isolates (N=28)
10. snp\_indel
   * variant calling of a subset isolates from all treatments comparing with RS-N using Snippy
   * Missing values are denoted by “n/a”
11. gene blast in uniprot
   * blast variant gene sequence in UniProt (https://www.uniprot.org) to ensure the gene name and related protein
12. transcriptome
   * changes in RS-N gene expression in response to phage infection
13. anc RS growth at 30°C
   * RS-N density dynamics in the presence of phage and absence of phage at 30°C for 24 hours
14. adsorption assay
   * adsorption rate of NNP42 phage to RS-N (anc) and phcS, gspE, gspF and pilD mutants isolated at the end of the selection experiment
15. one-step growth cuve
   * One-step growth curve of NNP42 phage infecting RS-N.
16. kegg enrichment
   *  KEGG enrichment analysis for differentially expressed genes in the presence of phage

The 128 clones of Figures 2A to 2C are the same strains, in the same order and with the same colony type, in all three sheets: 32 per treatment, with mucoid and SCV clones as Figure S1 describes (four of each from a replicate holding both types, eight of one type otherwise). The colony types per tube agree with the colony counts of Figure 1D taken in order: at 35 degrees C without phage, replicates 1 and 3 are all mucoid, 2 all SCV and 4 mixed, as tubes 5 and 7 (mucoid), 6 (SCV) and 8 (both); at 25 degrees C with phage, only replicate 2 has mucoid colonies, as only tube 2 has mucoid clones. This link of tubes to the Figure 1D replicates is consistent with the data, not shown by an identifier. The README says resistance is the mean of three technical replicates; R runs from 0.18 to 1.12. The growth rate column is headed maxgrowthrate(OD600); its values (0.04 to 0.07) are consistent with OD600 per hour. How the analysis differs from the paper's. The growth parameters, the variant calls, the differential expression and the KEGG enrichment were not recomputed: the dataset holds the authors' fitted growth rates and carrying capacities (no growth curves), their variant calls aggregated by group, their DESeq2 table and their KOBAS table, and the claims are tested on these. The ANOVAs are computed in Python (statsmodels) where the paper used R 3.6.3. The colony types of the twitching sheet are a choice this attempt had to make; it is shown against the reading by strain name. The OD time points of Figure 1B, the readings of the 0.24% and of the area under the curve, and the unit of the Figure 2A to 2C ANOVAs were questions of the first run; the claims file now settles them (days 2 to 12; the mean of replicate frequencies or pooled counts; the readings by day; unit means, with clones as a variant), and the alternatives are kept as variants. The sequencing and RNA-seq claims are data: dataset after the same answers, tested on the deposited tables.

What was found about the dataset. Where the paper prints a test, the deposited data reproduce it: Tables S1a, S1b, S2, S3a to S3c, S3e and S3f, the F values of the area under the curve, the two one-way ANOVAs of Figure 2A and 2D, and the counts of Table S4. The matches to Tables S1a and S3a to S3c identify the paper's choices (days 2 to 12; replicate means by colony type with sequential sums of squares). The exceptions are the twitching sheet, whose values do not reproduce Table S3d (read as sequential sums of squares) and whose strain names do not identify the clones (four 35_1 rows carry 35_0 names, and four 25_0 names appear twice), and the paper's 20% mucoid at 25 degrees C without phage, which the data put at 15.3%. The variant table is aggregated by group, so per-clone counts cannot be formed, and the genome reads of PRJNA1028400 are not public (28 BioSamples, 14 assemblies, no runs).
