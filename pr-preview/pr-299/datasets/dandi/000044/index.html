Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Metadata errorIn versions 0.210812.1516 and 0.250624.0426 every position series stores its sampling period (0.0256 s) as its rate, so the positions span about 880 to 2360 hours in place of the MAZE epoch. Version 0.261005.2101 stores 39.06 Hz, which matches the epoch lengths.
  • Metadata errorIn Gatsby_08022013 the POST epoch ends at 30413.6 s, while the LFP, the spikes and the sleep states run to about 32000 s.
  • Data missingThe dandiset description lists neck EMG and head-mounted accelerometer signals, and the files hold neither; they also hold no wide-band data (acquisition is empty), only the LFP at 1250 Hz.
  • Data missingThe paper's derived data are not deposited. There are no candidate or ripple events, no place-cell list, no sequence scores and no PCC values or rigid and plastic labels, so the paper's selections cannot be checked against the dandiset and every one must be rebuilt.
  • Methods not availableNeither the paper nor the dandiset links analysis code, and the supplementary Materials and Methods, which define the candidate events, place cells, the PCC and its test, are not openly available (PMC and Science serve them only behind a browser check).
  • Description missingEvery electrode's location is "unknown" and no depth or layer is given, so the channel for ripple detection has to be chosen from the signals. Units carry only the hemisphere (lCA1 or rCA1). Subject age is "/".
  • Description missingOn the circular track the animals ran toward decreasing linearized position (47 to 63% of samples against 7 to 16% the other way). The series description says the position increases clockwise but not which way the animals ran.

Reproduction Rigid and plastic hippocampal sequences (Grosmark and Buzsaki 2016)

Tests the findings of Grosmark and Buzsaki (2016) on dandiset 000044, which holds eight sessions of CA1 units, LFP, sleep states and track positions from four rats, each with sleep before and after exploring a novel track. It decodes candidate ripple events with a Bayesian decoder, compares sequence scores across PRE, MAZE and POST, estimates each place cell's contribution to sequences, and relates firing rate and ripple gain to coactivation and replay.
