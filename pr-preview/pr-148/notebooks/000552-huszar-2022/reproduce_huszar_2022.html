Reproducing Huszar et al. (2022) from dandiset 000552¶

Paper. Huszar R, Zhang Y, Blockus H, Buzsaki G (2022). Preconfigured dynamics in the hippocampus are guided by embryonic birthdate and rate of neurogenesis. Nature Neuroscience 25, 1201-1212. doi:10.1038/s41593-022-01138-x. This attempt used the full text of the author manuscript in PubMed Central (PMC10807234); the version of record is not open access. The Supplementary Tables were not available to it.

Dandiset. DANDI:000552, version 0.230630.2304. The paper cites no DANDI version (its data statement points to the Buzsaki lab repository). This is the only published version and the most recent one, so it is pinned. The draft differs from it only by three small BIDS files (README, dataset_description.json, bids-validator-config.json) that the analysis does not use; the data check below shows this.

What the dandiset holds. 104 processed files (one per session, 17 mice) with sleep states and sharp-wave ripples, of which 65 (11 mice) also hold spike-sorted units, position and maze trials; and 13 raw files. The units carry no label of light-responsiveness (birthdate), no cell type and no waveforms, and no optogenetic pulse times are stored. The paper's central comparisons, same- against different-birthdate neurons and light-responsive neurons across birthdates, therefore cannot be made. What can be tested is the description of the dataset and the findings that use all pyramidal neurons on the familiar and novel mazes.

Code read. The paper's analysis code, https://github.com/rhuszar/optotagCA1_final at commit 51320d45c5ee41147048128df333f251379bf755 (MATLAB, with some result files). It sets the figure-eight segments and the stem exclusion used here (see huszar.py). No data are taken from it.

No random numbers are drawn in the claim sections; the only random draw is the time shuffle of the peri-ripple control in the data check, seeded in huszar.py.

def pv_correlations(s, max_rate=h.PYR_MAX_RATE_HZ, min_speed=h.MIN_SPEED_CM_S):
    fam = familiar(s)
    keep = h.mean_rates(s) <= max_rate
    sp = [x for x, k in zip(s["maze_spikes"], keep) if k]
    m = h.rate_maps(sp, fam, min_speed=min_speed)["maps"]
    cc = np.full(h.FIG8_BINS, np.nan)
    for b in range(h.FIG8_BINS):
        l, r = m[:, 0, b], m[:, 1, b]
        ok = np.isfinite(l) & np.isfinite(r)
        if ok.sum() > 2 and l[ok].std() > 0 and r[ok].std() > 0:
            cc[b] = np.corrcoef(l[ok], r[ok])[0, 1]
    return cc, int(keep.sum())

FAMNOV = ("e13_26m1", "e15_13f1", "e16_3m2")

def famnov_measures(max_rate=h.PYR_MAX_RATE_HZ, run_speed=h.RUN_SPEED_CM_S, include_stem=False):
    cells, pairs, infos = [], [], []
    for s in sessions:
        if s["subject"] not in FAMNOV:
            continue
        keep = h.mean_rates(s) <= max_rate
        sp = [x for x, k in zip(s["maze_spikes"], keep) if k]
        rates = {}
        for r in h.recordings(s):
            m = h.rate_maps(sp, r)
            bins = np.arange(1, r["n_bins"] + 1) if include_stem else h.correlation_bins(r)
            c = h.pairwise_map_correlations(h.concatenated(m["maps"], bins))
            pairs.append(pd.DataFrame({"session": s["session_id"], "subject": s["subject"], "familiar": r["familiar"],
                                       "kind": r["kind"], "r": c}))
            flat = m["maps"].reshape(len(sp), -1)
            infos.append(pd.DataFrame({"session": s["session_id"], "subject": s["subject"], "familiar": r["familiar"],
                                       "kind": r["kind"], "bits": h.skaggs_information(flat, m["occupancy"].ravel()),
                                       "info_unweighted": h.uniform_information(flat)}))
            rates["fam" if r["familiar"] else "nov"] = h.running_rate(sp, r, min_speed=run_speed)
        cells.append(pd.DataFrame({"session": s["session_id"], "subject": s["subject"], "fam": rates["fam"],
                                   "nov": rates.get("nov", np.full(len(sp), np.nan))}))
    return pd.concat(cells, ignore_index=True), pd.concat(pairs, ignore_index=True), pd.concat(infos, ignore_index=True)

Blocked claims¶

The data check shows what the files lack. Every claim that compares same-birthdate (SBD) with different-birthdate (DBD) neurons, or light-responsive neurons across birthdates, needs to know which units responded to light. How the analysis differs from the paper's. Putative pyramidal cells are units with a session mean rate of at most 5 Hz, as the files hold no cell types or waveforms; the Figure 5e values move a long way with this choice, and a 2.59 Hz rule, which classes the paper's fraction as pyramidal, puts all four within tolerance. The "theta-related" rate is the rate while running at 5 cm/s or faster, not the rate in LFP theta cycles; its Figure 6d difference is weak (p = 0.041, paired p = 0.19). Speed comes from smoothed linearized position, not a Kalman filter. The Figure 6c pair correlations use all pairs of putative pyramidal cells, where the paper's pairs each include a light-responsive neuron, and the novel mazes are only those with position (no open fields). The familiar maze is taken as the first maze recording of each session.

What was found about the dandiset.

  • No unit is labelled light-responsive, no cell type is given, no waveforms are stored and no optogenetic pulse times are stored, in the processed files or any of the 13 raw files. The paper's main findings, which compare same- and different-birthdate neurons, cannot be tested.
  • Six of the 17 mice (39 files) have sleep states and ripples but no units and no position; the dandiset holds 8,201 units where the paper reports 9,471.
  • No open field has position: only 9 of the 16 novel explorations have trials and position, and the novel-cohort sessions hold 7 further epochs without position that may be the others. Several novel recordings give x and y between 0 and 1 though stated in cm.
  • is_familiar_maze is wrong in sessions with a single maze recording (True only for the first two trials). The sex field differs between files of e13_1f1 (Female 3, Male 2) and e13_1m1 (Male 9, Female 3). Two files of e13_1f1 have no scored sleep.
  • Twelve of 13 raw files give a session start of 2011-08-18. The processed file of e16_3m1_210201 holds a copy of the 1250 Hz LFP labelled 30,000 Hz with a wrong conversion factor.
  • Units have no link to electrodes, and their group_id values do not match the electrode group names in 64 of 65 files. The birthdate of each mouse is stored only in its subject id.
  • The 96 files of the 15 homecage mice match the paper's per-birthdate session counts and duration bounds exactly; the Methods' count of 92 sessions disagrees with the paper's own 96.
