  • spectral-classes

    Of 6182 FRandR events on the highest-rate FRandR channel in each patient's HFO area, 45% had two spectral peaks (second peak median 240 Hz), 28% one peak in the HFO range (median 184 Hz) and 27% no prominent peak above 100 Hz.

    Out of 6182 detected events, 45% exhibited two distinct peaks in ripple and FR band with the second peak at median frequency 240 Hz (Fig. 4A ), 28% had only one peak in the HFO range (median frequency 184 Hz, Fig. 4B ), and 27% did not show a prominent peak >100 Hz in the HFO range.

    Results, Spectral analysis of ripples co-occurring with FR, paragraph 1; Figure 4

    How it will be judged

    From the Methods, Spectral analysis. Per patient, the bipolar channel of the FRandR area with the highest FRandR rate; every FRandR event on it; Stockwell transform of 0.6 s of signal centred on the event (st.m of the linked repository); the instantaneous power spectrum averaged over the duration of the FR part of the event (the deposit's FR marking overlapping the FRandR); a fit of four gaussians to the spectrum over 80-500 Hz by least squares (lsqcurvefit); peaks and troughs found on the fitted curve. Bimodal, a trough between 150 and 250 Hz and at least one peak above 200 Hz; unimodal, a single peak above 100 Hz detectable in the ripple band; otherwise no peak. Open, with readings; the centre of the 0.6 s window, (a) the FRandR midpoint, (b) the FR midpoint; the signal transformed, (a) the unfiltered bipolar signal, (b) the signal high-passed at 80 Hz; the gaussian starting values and bounds, not given, (a) four gaussians at 100, 200, 300 and 400 Hz with widths of 50 Hz, (b) starting peaks at the four largest local maxima of the spectrum; a peak, (a) a local maximum of the fitted curve, (b) a fitted gaussian centre with positive amplitude inside the band; the "second peak" of bimodal events, (a) the higher-frequency peak (the Results say it is the second peak "in ripple and FR band"), (b) the more prominent peak (the Discussion calls 240 Hz the median of a prominent peak "around 200 Hz"). Patients whose FRandR area is empty contribute no events. The event count tolerance is 10%. Reproduced when the three shares are within tolerance under one reading and the two median frequencies are; partially reproduced when the shares are in the same order (bimodal largest) under one reading.
