  • ✓Reproduced In ALM scRNA-seq, glutamatergic neurons form 27 clusters and PT neurons map to three of them (Slco2a1, and the closely related Npsr1 and Hpgd), and with axonal reconstructions this suggests that PT neurons can be divided into two cell types.
  • ✓Reproduced Of 12 reconstructed PT neurons, 8 innervate the thalamus.
  • ✓Reproduced The other 4 of 12 reconstructed PT neurons bypass the thalamus and branch in the medulla, including the reticular nuclei with premotor neurons for orofacial movements.
  • ⊘Blocked Both PT types project to several shared targets; all reconstructed cells innervate the superior colliculus, and both types send collaterals to the subthalamic nucleus, zona incerta and midbrain reticular nucleus.
  • ⊘Blocked Thalamus-projecting PT neurons lie in upper L5b and medulla-projecting PT neurons in lower L5b; few neurons are co-labelled from thalamus and medulla (2.2%, 22 of 984) while many are co-labelled from thalamus and superior colliculus (77.1%, 687 of 890).
  • ✓Reproduced All 62 medulla-projecting PT neurons map to the Slco2a1 cluster, and 91 of 94 thalamus-projecting PT neurons map to the Npsr1 and Hpgd clusters.
  • ◐Partially reproduced All 18 partially and fully reconstructed PT neurons project to the superior colliculus and none lacks projections to both thalamus and medulla, suggesting that most if not all PT neurons belong to the two types.
  • ⊘Blocked smFISH confirms Slco2a1 as a PTlower marker and Npnt as a PTupper marker (Slco2a1 puncta IQR 1-2.5 in PTupper against 6-22 in PTlower; Npnt IQR 16-40 in PTupper against 1-9 in PTlower).
  • ✓Reproduced Fold changes between PTupper and PTlower of the 200 most discriminative genes correlate between scRNA-seq and bulk RNA-seq (Pearson's r = 0.87).
  • ?Not reproduced The recordings yield 61 identified PTupper cells from 8 mice and 69 identified PTlower neurons from 4 mice after exclusions (130 of 143 tagged units kept).
  • ✓Reproduced Identified PTupper cells are at more superficial recording depths than PTlower neurons.

  • ◐Partially reproduced A substantial proportion of PTlower neurons fire bursts (18.8%), which are rare among PTupper cells (3.3%).
  • ?Not reproduced Most recorded PT neurons (122 of 130, 94%) have significant trial-type selectivity in at least one task epoch.
  • ✓Reproduced Trial-averaged activity patterns are nearly as diverse within each PT population as across all neurons recorded in ALM; cell type explains relatively little of the variance.
  • ◐Partially reproduced Along the late delay coding direction (CDlate) selectivity is substantial in both PT types, with 40 of 61 PTupper and 44 of 69 PTlower neurons selective.
  • ⊘Blocked Along the early coding direction (CDearly, first 400 ms of the sample epoch), selectivity is larger and more consistent across trials in the PTupper population than in the PTlower population.
  • ⊘Blocked In the PTupper population, selectivity along CDearly remains constant through the sample and delay epochs until the go cue.
  • ✓Reproduced In the PTlower population, selectivity along CDearly decays rapidly, with no significant selectivity in the 400 ms before the go cue (P = 0.34, bootstrap).
  • ✓Reproduced The coding direction stays more similar across the sample and delay epochs in the PTupper population than in the PTlower population, whose delay-epoch coding directions are largely orthogonal to those of the sample epoch.
