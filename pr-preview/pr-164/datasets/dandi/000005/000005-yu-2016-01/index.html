    Findings about the data

    What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

    • Description missingNo L4 file names the cell type of its neuron (excitatory or FS). The cell types the paper compares can only be inferred from the data, here from light tagging in VGAT-ChR2 mice and from action potential width.
    • Description missingNo file marks the mice whose infraorbital nerve was transected (9 VGAT-ChR2 mice in Supplementary Table 2 of the paper), and the files hold no surgery or notes field. Their cells cannot be told from control cells except by inference; 4 mice whose cells have no touch input are probably among them. This blocks the test of Figure 7, and these cells may be mixed into any analysis of control cells.
    • Metadata errorThe genotype is missing for the 6 VPM mice (VGAT-ChR2-EYFP in Supplementary Table 1) and for 3 L4 mice (anm260074 VGAT-ChR2-EYFP, anm223517 PV-IRES-Cre x Ai32, anm250509 PV-ChR2), and the 5 SOM-IRES-Cre x Ai32 mice are given as Ai32.
    • Description missingEvery L4 file gives cortical layer 4 (depth 0.42 to 0.62 mm), though the paper's waveform analysis (Supp. Fig. 6) includes L5 cells; the layer of each cell cannot be checked.
    • Description missingThe optogenetic conditions are not described. `photo_stim_mode` has an empty description and its labels do not map to one kind of light (VPM-site pulse trains are labelled `5ms_pulse` in early files and `shift` in later ones); `photo_stim_power` is NaN in every L4 file; no file names the opsin. The light epochs can be read only from the photostimulation series.
    • Metadata errorThe session description gives the wrong recording mode for 24 of 130 L4 files, all from PV-Cre mice. 23 described as whole-cell hold traces near 0 mV with small extracellular spikes, as juxtacellular recordings do, and one described as juxtacellular sits near -65 mV. By the trace, PV-Cre has 9 whole-cell and 39 juxtacellular cells where Supplementary Table 2 has 5 and 42.
    • Metadata errorIn the L4 files the trial table's `first_touch_time` is the trial start plus the true first-touch latency divided by 1000 (it matches the touch onset series within 2 ms in 0.956 of trials once multiplied by 1000). In the VPM files it is correct.
    • Description missingThe L4 trace is sampled at 10 kHz in files up to March 2015 and at 20 kHz from late August 2015 on, with trials of 5.2 or 5.5 s at 10 kHz and 5.9 s at 20 kHz; nothing but the timestamps states the rate.
    • Description missing8 files name no principal whisker, and the VPM files give one principal whisker per file though the session description says each unit has its own.
    • OtherThe light at the site labelled VPM in VGAT-ChR2 mice is given as 594 nm, and isolated pulses at the site labelled vS1 (594 nm) evoke short-latency spikes in VGAT-ChR2 cells; these wavelengths may be worth checking against the experiment.
    • Methods not availableThe paper's full text is not open access and neither it nor the dandiset links analysis code. The public supplement names the measures (a sinusoid fit of rate against phase, a waveform boundary for FS cells) but not their windows, bins or boundaries.

    Reproduction VPM and layer 4 responses to touch and whisking, and their control by thalamus and FS interneurons (Yu et al. 2016)

    Tests the claims written from the full text of Yu, Gutnisky, Hires and Svoboda (2016) on dandiset 000005, which holds silicon probe recordings in VPM and single-cell recordings in layer 4 of barrel cortex of mice locating a pole with their whiskers. It measures touch responses, whisking rates and phase coupling of VPM units, FS interneurons and excitatory cells, their membrane potential, and the effects of thalamic photoinhibition and of PV interneuron suppression, by the paper's methods as the claims file gives them.
