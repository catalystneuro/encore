What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingThe data set holds only the white-noise recordings behind Figure 9D to 9H. The histology of Figures 1 to 8 and Supplementary Figure 1, and the flashing-spot, flash, moving-bar and grating recordings of Figure 9A to 9C, are not in it, and the paper names no other deposit. Of the 26 claims, 19 cannot be tested.
  • Selection not documentedThe data set holds 200 mutant and 122 control units (and 3 from a Bax -/- mutant), where the paper's Figure 9 has 115 mutant and 143 control cells. The data set does not say how its units relate to the paper's, which were kept, or why the counts differ.
  • Paper inconsistentThe data set labels 95 of its 122 control units "WT (chx10 het)", while the paper's Figure 9 legend gives the controls as Pax6-Cre;Pcdh-gamma +/f-del or Pcdh-gamma f-del/f-del without Cre. The labels and the paper disagree on what the controls were.
  • File format errorIn the 8 recordings of 20080628_R4, 20080628_R6, 20080702_R1 and 20080702_R4, 82 of 86 spike trains are not in time order. Each runs to 1440 s and then holds a second segment between 720 and 780 s, though the stimulus runs to 1500 s. The 4 trains in order have spikes between 1440 and 1500 s, about as many as between 720 and 780 s, and moving the segments by 720 s raises their STA peaks over the null in most units. This is consistent with the last minute stored with a 720 s offset in place of 1440 s. One checkerboard recording (20080628_R5) has 1 or 2 spikes near 2 s out of order in 15 units.
  • Metadata errorThe frame lengths stated for the vertical-bar recordings of 20080702_R1 and 20080702_R4 (16.6691 and 16.6653 ms) are not those of the stimulus. Rebuilt with the stated frame, the bars of 20080702_R4 give no STA above the null in any of its 12 units (median ratio 0.96), though all 12 respond to the checkerboard of the same retina. A scan of the frame peaks at 16.671 to 16.672 ms in both recordings; with 16.6718 ms, the frame of every other bar recording and half the checkerboard frame of the same two retinas, the median ratio is 2.57 and 10 of the 12 units have peaks above twice the null's.
  • Other16 pairs of units share 70 to 100% of their spikes within 0.5 ms in every recording of their retina (12 pairs in mutant retinas, 4 in a control retina), most likely single neurons sorted twice. The data set does not mark them.
  • Description missingThe files do not give the age of the mice, the Pcdh-gamma allele (the paper's Results say fcon3 and its Figure 9 legend f-del), the region recorded in the Pax6-Cre retinas (the paper used their periphery only), or the cell type of the units.

  • answered 2026-10-20asked about fig9d-rf-size-similar

    Which flickering-bar recording of a retina gives a cell's spike-triggered average, its firing rate and its LN fit? The data set holds, per retina, one or two recordings of vertical bars (full height) at 8.3 or 16.6 um, and for six retinas also one of horizontal bars (full width) at the same width; retina 20080624_R2 has two vertical-bar recordings at 16.6 um and 20080624_R3 one more at 41.5 um. The claims file names bars of 8.3 or 16.6 um but not their orientation, nor what is done when a retina has more than one such recording. The same question applies to fig9e-slower-time-course, fig9f-lower-firing-rate, fig9g-threshold-unaltered, fig9h-gain-reduced and s3-receptive-field-surrounds. Tested here under three readings, (A) the first vertical-bar recording at 8.3 or 16.6 um, (B) all vertical-bar recordings at 8.3 or 16.6 um pooled (spikes and frames together), (C) the horizontal-bar recording where the retina has one, otherwise as A.

    Passage. Methods, Electrophysiology, "To map spatio-temporal receptive fields, we projected gratings of adjacent thin bars (8.3 or 16.6 μm width). Each bar flickered black or white according to a pseudo-random binary sequence (16.6 ms frame duration)." Figure 9F legend, "Average firing rate observed during stimulation with flickering gratings."

    Why it matters. The measures differ between recordings of the same cells (the receptive field profile along a different axis, a different number of spikes and so a different noise level, a different firing rate), and the readings change the six mutant and one control retina with horizontal bars and the mutant retina with two vertical recordings, so a band comparison near its limit could change.

    The reader’s answer. The paper does not give it. The Methods (Electrophysiology) say only "we projected gratings of adjacent thin bars (8.3 or 16.6 μm width)", with no orientation and nothing on retinas with more than one such recording; the Results ("randomly flickering bars") and the Figure 9 legends add nothing, the supplement is not in the text, and the paper links no code. The claims file now names readings A, B and C in the fig9d-rf-size-similar criterion, and fig9e, fig9f, fig9g, fig9h and s3 require their outcome under all three.

Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • The vertical bars of 20080702_R1 and 20080702_R4 are rebuilt with a frame of 16.6718 ms, as for fig9d-rf-size-similar.s3-receptive-field-surrounds fig9e-slower-time-course fig9g-threshold-unaltered fig9h-gain-reduced
  • The claims file asks also for the count of units with a flicker response without defining one; the counts above two thresholds of the STA peak over a null STA are shown as a standard choice, and none can change the outcome.fig9-cell-counts
  • The control retinas are as the data set labels them; 95 of the 122 control units carry a label ("WT (chx10 het)") that the paper's description of its controls does not include.fig9-cell-counts
  • All 5 retinas recorded with 8.3 um bars are mutant; every control retina was recorded with 16.6 um bars.fig9d-rf-size-similar
  • The mutant group cannot be restricted to the peripheral region of the Pax6-Cre retinas, as the data set does not say where they were recorded.fig9d-rf-size-similar
  • The vertical bars of 20080702_R1 and 20080702_R4 are rebuilt with a frame of 16.6718 ms, the frame of the other bar recordings, not the stated 16.6691 and 16.6653 ms (see findings).fig9d-rf-size-similar
  • Lag j is placed at (j + 0.5) frames, the data set's example script's convention; the offset is the same for every unit.fig9e-slower-time-course
  • Theta is searched on 99 quantiles of y for the frame fit and at every split between the 20 bin centres for the binned fit, a standard choice of resolution; the binned grid cannot place theta above the last split, which many printed-form fits reach.fig9g-threshold-unaltered
