Information flows from hippocampus to auditory cortex during replay of verbal working memory items

The behavioural results held, but the central finding, a reversal of theta flow between auditory cortex and hippocampus from encoding to maintenance, did not reproduce in the ECoG participants or the group. It appeared only when ΔGranger was taken as the minimum in encoding and maximum in maintenance, as in the authors' code, a statistic that gives that sign pattern without a directed effect; the band average the Results describe showed none. Hippocampal beta increases and theta phase locking at the auditory contact also failed, while the gamma and cortical beta results in Participant 1 held in part. The left-hemisphere source result could not be tested because the source grid is not deposited.
