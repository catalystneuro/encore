DANDI:000637: None of the paper's main findings could be tested in this data. Its claims on PRF and duty-cycle dependent responses of regular-spiking and fast-spiking units in S1 and POm, and on S1 to POm correlations, all need the cell-type split, but the Units tables hold spike times only, with no waveforms. Most also need POm or auditory cortex units, sham or CG1 sessions, or duty cycles the dandiset lacks. The recordings could not be linked to the paper's, which names another dandiset, and S1 units here number 4,642 against the paper's 1,297. Pooled across cell types, every PRF raised S1 firing during sonication, which decides nothing.

DANDI:000946: Most of the paper's main findings could not be tested in this data, and none reproduced. The responses of regular and fast spiking units in S1 and POm to ultrasound, and the S1 to POm correlations, need spike waveforms and cell types, which the dataset lacks. The unit counts did not reproduce: POm has 352 units at 3000 Hz against 250 reported, perhaps because the paper's unit rule differs. The auditory cortex responses stayed undecided because the paper does not say how rates were normalized, and the readings gave different outcomes. Sham and cingulate controls could not be found.

DANDI:001199: No main finding of the paper could be confirmed in this data. The cell-type results for S1 and POm, including responses to repetition rate and duty cycle and the cross-area correlations, could not be tested, because the files hold spike times only, with no waveforms or cell-type labels, and only one pulse duration. The auditory cortex responses did not reproduce: 194 units against the paper's 453, with no significant dependence on repetition rate, resting on assumed voltage and trial timing for the newer sessions. The rats are fewer than the paper reports and were recorded after its preprint, so this dandiset probably holds different recordings.
