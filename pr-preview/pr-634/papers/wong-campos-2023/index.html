Here is the paragraph for that file: The paper's findings on backpropagating spikes hold in part. Conduction speed (0.088 m/s) and the distance at which amplitude halves (239 μm) reproduced, but the range of conduction delays did not, as the deposited delays run about 1.9 ms below the paper's. Doublet intervals and the sensory plateau duration did not reproduce, and the paper does not describe its spike detection or doublet definition. First-spike failure in doublets, bursts and accelerating trios held only for some detection thresholds. The distal-dendrite optogenetic results and the dendrite-targeted stimulation findings could not be tested, because no dendrite met the paper's delay cutoff and those recordings are missing.

  • ✓Reproduced In anesthetized mice, the bAP peak amplitude decays to 50% at a contour distance from the soma of 242 ± 30 μm (95% C.I., 30 sites, 5 neurons, 5 mice), with R2 = 0.49 for amplitude against distance.
  • ?Not reproduced Back-propagation delays range from 1.5 to 4 ms over contour distances from the soma of 50 to 280 μm.
  • ✓Reproduced At distances over 50 μm from the soma, the bAP conduction speed is 0.09 ± 0.02 m/s (95% C.I., 30 sites, 5 neurons, 5 mice), with R2 = 0.43 for delay against distance.
  • ◐Partially reproduced Spontaneous voltage in pairs of dendritic branches of the same neuron is highly cross-correlated, 91 ± 3% (mean ± s.d.) in the anesthetized state and 73 ± 4% in the awake state (23 branch pairs, 3 neurons, 3 mice).
  • ⊘Blocked Accounting for photon shot noise, the upper bounds on dendrite-to-dendrite cross-correlation are 94 ± 2% (anesthetized) and 85 ± 4% (awake), so the electrical dynamics are largely shared throughout the L2/3 apical dendritic tree.

  • ◐Partially reproduced In spontaneous spike trios, the third bAP is amplified relative to the second when the spike rate accelerates (ISI n-1 > 75 ms, ISI n < 75 ms) and suppressed when it decelerates (186 spike triplets, 10 branches, 3 neurons, 3 mice), so spontaneous bAP propagation is favored by an accelerating spike rate.
