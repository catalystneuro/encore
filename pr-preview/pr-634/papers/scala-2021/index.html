Scala et al. 2021 · Nature

Phenotypic variation of transcriptomic cell types in mouse motor cortex

doi:10.1038/s41586-020-2907-3 · paper key scala-2021

DANDI:000035: None of the paper's main findings could be tested, because this dandiset holds only the physiological-temperature follow-up and lacks the room-temperature recordings, sequencing and morphologies. Untested are t-type coverage, gene-expression prediction of electrophysiology, family separability, transcriptomic versus electrophysiological distance, and t-type variability. The follow-up claims held: IT and ET neurons overlapped, IT cells had a higher spike threshold, and Sst types shifted with temperature mainly in spike width and amplitude. The follow-up link between Sst electrophysiological and transcriptomic distance was not tested, since the agent did not fetch the public reference data in time. Cohort counts matched.

DANDI:000008: Most main findings held up in this data, but those on variability within cell types were weaker and some could not be tested. Electrophysiology separated the transcriptomic families, with only ET and IT overlapping (49% of ET cells classed as IT), and gene expression predicted the features as reported. The Leiden entropy agreement with t-type variability did not reproduce, and the Vip gradients stayed undecided because the paper gives no measure. Axon shape and the 34 degree follow-up could not be tested, as the paper gives no measure for them. Minor cohort claims mostly matched. You'll need to save the paragraph to that file yourself.

Attempt scala-2021-01

2026-10-14 · on DANDI:000035

  • ⊘Blocked After quality control, 1,227 Patch-seq neurons from mouse primary motor cortex were assigned to transcriptomic types, covering 77 of the 90 neuronal t-types of the reference taxonomy.
  • ⊘Blocked A sparse reduced-rank regression of 17 electrophysiological features on gene expression, using 25 genes and a rank-5 latent space, reached a cross-validated R2 of 0.38, with cross-validated correlations of 0.90, 0.74 and 0.67 between the first three pairs of projections.
  • ⊘Blocked A reduced-rank regression restricted to ion channel genes did not perform much worse than the full model, with a cross-validated R2 of 0.33 and correlations of 0.86, 0.71 and 0.56.
  • ⊘Blocked In a k-nearest-neighbour classification of cells into seven transcriptomic families from electrophysiological features, the confusion matrix was mostly diagonal, with only the ET and IT subclasses strongly overlapping.
  • ⊘Blocked Families were less separable by morphology than by electrophysiology among inhibitory neurons, and most separable when electrophysiological and morphological features were combined.
  • ⊘Blocked Across 200 pairs of t-types from the same family, the transcriptomic distance and the electrophysiological distance between t-types were correlated with r = 0.60.
  • ⊘Blocked Among IT t-types, transcriptomic distances were strongly correlated with differences in average soma depth, r = 0.70.
  • ⊘Blocked Most t-types were classified into their own family from electrophysiology, but some, such as Sst Pvalb Calb2, Lamp5 Egln3_1 and Vip Mybpc1, were often classified into another family.
  • ⊘Blocked Many t-types had a normalized total variance of electrophysiological features well above that of k-means clusters, while some, such as Pvalb Vipr2_2, appeared homogeneous.
  • ✓Reproduced At physiological temperature, 185 neurons were sequenced from 8 adult mice (7 Sst-Cre/Ai9 and 1 Pvalb-Cre/Ai9); 177 got a t-type (110 Sst, 43 IT, 12 ET, 10 Pvalb, 2 NP); 175 were in layer 5; electrophysiological features were extracted for 184.
  • ◐Partially reproduced Cells at physiological temperature were stimulated with the same paradigm as the main data set, 600 ms current pulses from -200 pA upwards in 20 pA steps up to 1,380 pA, sometimes stopped earlier.
  • ✓Reproduced Follow-up experiments at 34 degrees C confirmed the electrophysiological overlap between IT and ET neurons seen at room temperature.
  • ✓Reproduced At 34 degrees C, the AP threshold showed a prominent difference between layer 5 IT and ET neurons.
  • ◐Partially reproduced The dandiset's recordings are the cells of the paper's published table of the 34 degree cohort, with the same t-type, layer and soma depth.
  • ✓Reproduced The electrophysiological features computed here from the dandiset's traces agree with the paper's published features for the same cells.
  • ✓Reproduced For layer 5 Sst t-types, AP amplitude and AP width changed the most between room temperature and 34 degrees C, but the relative differences between t-types stayed roughly the same; AHP, input resistance, rebound and sag ratio changed little, and the relative differences between t-types stayed the same.
  • ⊘Blocked At physiological temperature, as at room temperature, morpho-electric properties of layer 5 Sst neurons varied continuously across the transcriptomic landscape, and the electrophysiological distance between Sst t-types correlated with their transcriptomic distance.
