Knierim et al. 2000 · Nature Neuroscience

Three-dimensional spatial selectivity of hippocampal neurons during space flight

doi:10.1038/72910 · paper key knierim-2000

Datasets

  • DANDI:001754

Most of the paper's testable main findings hold up in this data. The dandiset's rate maps show rat 2's and rat 3's place cells in flight carrying spatial information per spike not different from preflight, though rat 2's means run above the paper's (1.38 against 1.13). Rat 1's comparison could not be settled, since its two preflight epochs disagree and the paper does not say which was used. Abnormal selectivity on flight day 4 held for rat 2 but not rat 1, and rat 2's position trace there may not be its own. Stability of rat 3's fields across sessions did not reproduce (6 of 16 cells). Direction selectivity could not be tested (paper gives no method), nor could the normal theta and ripples (no EEG in the files).
