This is the paragraph to save in that file: Most of the paper's main findings held in this data, with a few central exceptions. Behaviour, neuron counts, persistent hippocampal category-neuron activity, hippocampal but not amygdala prediction of later memory, and the confidence effect in memory-selective neurons reproduced. The higher hippocampal firing for remembered pictures held with high confidence did not reach significance, likely because the paper's trial rows differ (80 neurons had both conditions, against 83). The early-delay difference appeared only late, and strong maintenance activity did not boost memory-neuron responses. The load and accuracy effects could not be settled because the paper does not say which model was fitted.
