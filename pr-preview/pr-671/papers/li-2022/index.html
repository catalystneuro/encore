Li et al. 2022 · Nature

Li, H., Namburi, P., Olson, J.M. et al. Neurotensin orchestrates valence assignment in the amygdala. Nature 608, 586-592 (2022).

doi:10.1038/s41586-022-04964-y · paper key li-2022

Datasets

  • DANDI:000689

Almost none of the paper's main findings could be tested in this data, and none was confirmed. The dandiset holds only amygdala recordings and neurotensin sensor photometry, and no file says which mice are CRISPR or control, so the knockout behavior, anatomy, terminal photometry and group comparisons cannot be tested. The sensor's rise to sucrose after learning was significant only with all trials (p = 0.0046, against 0.093 with the first 16), and its fall to airpuff and its early dip were not reproduced. The seven functional clusters depend on an unreported smoothing window, and the units are not sorted single neurons. Behavior clustering matched only one of its two cluster counts.
