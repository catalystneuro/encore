    How it will be judged

    A cell is one recorded neuron (one ephys_roi_id, one NWB file, as the paper's code LCNE-patchseq-figures at 79fa20a reads them), a mouse is one donor, and the group is the injection target. The tolerance is zero because these are counts of the deposited cells themselves, and the paper's code reads exactly 96.   • patchseq-ap-waveform-spinal-vs-cortex

    The intracellular action potential waveforms of LC-NE neurons projecting to the spinal cord differ significantly from those of neurons projecting to isocortex (two-sided Welch's t-test, p < 0.01).

    Neurons projecting to spinal cord had significantly different action potential waveforms than those projecting to isocortex or cerebellum.

    Extended Data Fig. 11k, left; Results, LC-NE activity during action-outcome learning, paragraph 5

    How it will be judged

    Method from the paper's code (LCNE-patchseq-figures at 79fa20a, code/spikes.py and code/generate_S14jk.py), as the legend names no waveform measure. For each cell, take the long-square rheobase sweeps (protocols X3LP_Rheo_DA_0 and X5LP_Rheo_DA_0) whose main pulse lasts at least 0.8 s, and choose the one of lowest absolute amplitude that has at least one spike during the pulse. Detect spike peaks as the voltage maximum between paired upward and downward crossings of -10 mV (after eFEL-style resampling at the sampling step), and average the raw voltage from -5 to 10 ms around every peak of that sweep. Min-max normalize each average over -2 to 4 ms, keep -3 to 6 ms, mean-centre across all cells, and take PC1 by SVD (its sign is arbitrary and does not affect the test). Compare PC1 between spinal cord and cortex cells with a two-sided Welch t-test at the cell level (scipy ttest_ind, equal_var=False). Reproduced when p < 0.01; partially reproduced when 0.01 <= p < 0.05. The group means, t, degrees of freedom and p are reported. The Methods and the code disagree on sweep inclusion. The Methods give a sweep inclusion rule (bridge balance below 20 MOhm and 15% of input resistance, bias current within 100 pA, RMS noise below 0.07 mV over 1.5 ms and 0.5 mV over 500 ms; Methods, Patch-seq recordings) that the code does not apply. Following protocol step 2, the main reading applies the Methods' sweep rule before choosing the rheobase sweep, where the files hold the values it needs; the variant is the code's, with no further sweep rule. Where the files lack those values the two coincide, and this is recorded. Results under both are shown.

  • patchseq-tau-cortex-vs-spinal

    LC-NE neurons projecting to isocortex have significantly longer membrane time constants than neurons projecting to the spinal cord (two-sided Welch's t-test, p < 0.001).

    Neurons projecting to isocortex had significantly longer membrane time constants than those projecting to cerebellum or spinal cord

    Extended Data Fig. 11k, right; Results, LC-NE activity during action-outcome learning, paragraph 5

    How it will be judged

    The Methods say the features were computed with the IPFX package (Methods, Patch-seq recordings). The paper's code (LCNE-patchseq-figures at 79fa20a) does not compute the time constant; it reads IPFX's tau from a frozen table, in ms, missing for 14 of 96 cells. Main reading, from the Methods and the dataset - compute tau per cell with IPFX's long-square subthreshold analysis at its default settings (exponential fits to the hyperpolarizing long-square responses, averaged over the qualifying sweeps), using the Methods' sweep inclusion rule where the files hold its values. Variant, from the code - the frozen values in code/data/LCNE_patchseq_S14_cell_table.csv at 79fa20a, keyed by ephys_roi_id (an outside input). Compare cortex and spinal cord cells with a two-sided Welch t-test at the cell level (code/generate_S14jk.py, scipy ttest_ind, equal_var=False). Reproduced when p < 0.001 and the cortex mean exceeds the spinal cord mean; partially reproduced when p < 0.05 with cortex longer. The number of cells with a time constant per group, the group means, t and p are reported for each reading.
