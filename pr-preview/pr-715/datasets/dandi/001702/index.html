  • Data missingNone of the 481 NWB files holds data. Each is 185,480 bytes and holds only file-level metadata (identifier, session description, keywords, subject); acquisition, processing, analysis and stimulus are empty. The identifiers name the original image files of Figures 1, 3, 5 and S1 to S6 and S9, but no image, ROI, table or measurement is deposited, although the description says the dandiset includes immunohistochemical imaging data quantifying PNN integrity. All 22 IHC claims of the paper are blocked.
    Found by the attempt on Chaunsali et al. 2025 (chaunsali-2025-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Description missingThe 175 behaviour videos are MP4 files outside NWB, named only by figure panel and trial number. They carry no subject, genotype, treatment, DPI or trial-type (Novel 1, Familiar, Novel 2; familiarization or test) label, several arenas share each video, and the counts do not match the paper's mice (for example 15 videos for the 16 mice of Figure 2B, 33 for the 26 of Figure 5E). The scored interaction and exploration times are not deposited. None of the 19 behaviour claims can be tested.
    Found by the attempt on Chaunsali et al. 2025 (chaunsali-2025-01, 2026-11-03)

  • Metadata errorThe description says the dandiset includes bulk RNA-sequencing data with associated metadata; it holds none, and the counts are in GEO GSE317499 only. The description also keeps a placeholder sentence naming GEO accession "GSEXXXXX" next to the one naming GSE317499.
    Found by the attempt on Chaunsali et al. 2025 (chaunsali-2025-01, 2026-11-03)

Sex is U in 476 of 481 files, and session descriptions give the region as Unknown.
