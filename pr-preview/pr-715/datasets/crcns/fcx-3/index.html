Dataset unversioned files (the archive keeps no versions) · tested 2026-11-08 · 5 open findings

  • Metadata errorThe data set's list of analysed channels (docs/summary_channel_analysis.csv) holds 187 channels where the paper reports 192 (Table 1). S4 has 5 right parietal channels (RLD6 to RLD8, RC10, RC11) where Table 1 gives 10. The list yields 1135 same-hemisphere PFC-parietal pairs, where the paper reports 1129; Table 1's counts would give 1165.
    Found by the attempt on Johnson et al. 2019 (johnson-2019-01, 2026-11-08)
  • Metadata errorThe description says summary_file_trial.csv holds information about each trial, with 120 trials per subject. In S6 and S7 it holds only the trials kept after artifact rejection (101 and 90 rows, numbered up to 120), matching their derived epochs; in S1 to S5 it holds all 120. Single-trial accuracy over all 120 trials cannot be rebuilt for S6 and S7 (summary_file.csv gives it per condition).
    Found by the attempt on Johnson et al. 2019 (johnson-2019-01, 2026-11-08)
  • OtherA timing contrast between trials with 900-ms and 1150-ms delays (70-150 Hz amplitude) shows, in S2, S3 and S4, responses in the processing epoch of the 1150-ms trials 0.21 to 0.26 s later relative to the trial table's processing column than in the 900-ms trials, and in S4 the same shift in the encoding epoch, while the pretrial epochs show shifts of 0.03 s or less. This is consistent with the encoding and processing columns (computed at fixed offsets from the pretrial start) lying about 0.25 s before the events in 1150-ms trials, which would put the end of the cue in their processing window. The test is weak in the other subjects (S1, S5 and S6 show shifts of 0.28 to 0.35 s in the processing epoch that do not pass the shuffle test) and does not prove it. The ERP and power tests and the task-induced PSI on the 900-ms-delay trials alone are shown as variants. Power on those trials loses most effects, and so does power on a random draw of as many trials from both delays, so these tests cannot tell whether the timing affects the results.
    Found by the attempt on Johnson et al. 2019 (johnson-2019-01, 2026-11-08)
  • OtherS7's derived epochs record a continuous recording of 431506 samples (hdr.nSamples) while the deposited data_primary.mat of S7 has 445952; the derived traces still match the deposited continuous data at the trial table's samples (r >= 0.9989). S3's elec_labels.mat is a MATLAB 5 file, the others MATLAB 7.3.
    Found by the attempt on Johnson et al. 2019 (johnson-2019-01, 2026-11-08)
  • Description missingThe electrode position files (s<n>_MNI_grid.csv) name grids and list positions in order, but do not say how the contact numbers of the channel labels map to grid positions, and S3's labels (RH1 to RH256) do not match the name of its grid (HD_Grid). The derived channels are bipolar, labelled by one contact, with the reference contact found here only from the data (the next contact on strips and depth leads, the next grid row on S3's and S6's grids).
    Found by the attempt on Johnson et al. 2019 (johnson-2019-01, 2026-11-08)
