Dataset version 0.250528.0702 · tested 2026-11-02 · 16 open findings

  • Description missingThe non-iEEG files (sub-h009, h011, h012, h013, ses-P50) call their two series pre_trials_data and post_trials_data and do not say which is the saline and which the ketamine visit; the visits were a blinded crossover, so the names are not a visit order either. Only the paper's code (analyze_airpuff_p50.py) reads pre as saline.
    Found by the attempt on Kauvar et al. 2025 (kauvar-2025-02, 2026-11-02)
  • Data missingThe CADSS dissociation scores of both human cohorts (Figs. 1, 2D, figs. S1D, S14G, S27) and the non-iEEG participants' verbal reports (Fig. 1F, fig. S1F) are not in the dataset; the scores are in the paper's code record (data/cadss_across_pts.csv and constants in analyze_airpuff_p50.py).
    Found by the attempt on Kauvar et al. 2025 (kauvar-2025-02, 2026-11-02)
  • Description missingThe iEEG behaviour and LFP files name sessions baseline, preinfusion, infusion, postinfusion, postinfusionfast and control without saying when each was recorded; the code's DATASETS.py says baseline is the day before infusion. For SD168, SD172, SD242 and SD245 the behaviour file holds a baseline session and no preinfusion session, and the paper treats that baseline as preinfusion.
    Found by the attempt on Kauvar et al. 2025 (kauvar-2025-02, 2026-11-02)
  • Description missingThe 142 head-fixed mouse behaviour files (sub-mouse/sub-mouse_ses-<id>_behavior.nwb) carry no label of drug, dose, mouse, sex, day, puff pressure or skipped puff; subject_id equals the session id, sex is "O" and age "P8W/" in every file, and the trials have no timestamps. The labels exist only as experiment-id lists in the paper's scripts (Zenodo 14920570, mouse_eyepuff/scripts/fig1_*.py), so no analysis can be run from the dataset alone, sessions of the same mouse cannot be paired from the data, and the Methods' rule for post-infusion trials (at least 3 min after injection) cannot be applied.
    Found by the attempt on Kauvar et al. 2025 (kauvar-2025-02, 2026-11-02)
  • Data missingThe Neuropixels-rig behaviour (eye closure) of 3 of the 13 recorded sessions of Fig. 4B (Table S3) is not in 001347: the 001326 sessions of 2023-02-25 (sub-3) and 2023-07-06 and 2023-08-22 (sub-5) have no sub-m* behaviour file; the paper's converter lists only the 10 deposited sessions (its commented "extra" list, printed in 02, names m95-544 of 2022-02-23, m11210-961, m11217-1104 and m11217-1107).
    Found by the attempt on Kauvar et al. 2025 (kauvar-2025-02, 2026-11-02)
  • Data missingThe lick data of the liquid-reward eyepuff assay (fig. S4, 6 mice) are not in the dataset or in 001326, and the code record has no script for them.
    Found by the attempt on Kauvar et al. 2025 (kauvar-2025-02, 2026-11-02)
  • Description missingThe per-participant LFP files are 20-min windows around each eyepuff session with no infusion start time, no epochs and no sleep or seizure annotations, so the 25-min pre-infusion baseline of fig. S15 and the phase-locking analysis is not in the dataset, and the peak-ketamine window of figs. S15 and S27 can be placed only from the Methods' "approximately 30 minutes after the infusion start" for the infusion eyepuff session. No CADSS scores are in the behaviour files.
    Found by the attempt on Kauvar et al. 2025 (kauvar-2025-02, 2026-11-02)
  • Data missingSpontaneous blink onsets for fig. S12 are not in the dataset; they are a hand-made list in the code record (for_reference/analyze_airpuff_ieeg_natural_blinks.py lines 42 to 79).
    Found by the attempt on Kauvar et al. 2025 (kauvar-2025-02, 2026-11-02)
  • OtherThe time bins of the per-network and per-region files (-0.531 to 1.444 s) are offset by 0.6 ms from the corresponding bins of the per-channel per-trial file (-0.532 to 1.443 s); immaterial for the analyses here.
    Found by the attempt on Kauvar et al. 2025 (kauvar-2025-02, 2026-11-02)
  • Description missingThe iEEG files do not mark bad channels, although the paper removes them; the list exists only in the paper's code (ketman/analyze/utils.py, bad_ch, 10 to 33 contacts per participant). With and without it the CD results differ in the second decimal and decide two readings of fig6e.
    Found by the attempt on Kauvar et al. 2025 (kauvar-2025-02, 2026-11-02)
  • Description missingThe electrode tables hold a region label per contact but no Yeo7 or Yeo17 network label, and x, y, z and impedance are NaN for every contact; the network labels used by figs. S19 to S25 and S35 are only in the code record (anat/pts_ch_labels.pkl).
    Found by the attempt on Kauvar et al. 2025 (kauvar-2025-02, 2026-11-02)
  • Metadata errorThe LFP ElectricalSeries (processing/ecephys/LFP/v_trace) declares unit 'volts' with conversion 1.0, but the values are digitiser units (1st to 99th percentiles of order 300 to 3000); the scale (0.390625 or 0.09765625 microvolts per unit) is only in the series comment and the device description.
    Found by the attempt on Kauvar et al. 2025 (kauvar-2025-02, 2026-11-02)
  • Description missingThe puff TTL is DC channel index 2, named 'CCEP' in four participants' files and 'DC03' in three (SD168, SD172, SD245); no description identifies it as the puff sync channel.
    Found by the attempt on Kauvar et al. 2025 (kauvar-2025-02, 2026-11-02)
  • File format errorIn all 13 NWB files of DANDI:001326 (0.250528.1957) the spike times of every unit (9554 of 9554) are stored out of order; they must be sorted before use.
    Found by the attempt on Kauvar et al. 2025 (kauvar-2025-02, 2026-11-02)
  • Identifier missingDANDI:001326 numbers its subjects 0 to 9 and gives recording dates, with no link to the paper's mouse or experiment identifiers or to the Neuropixels-rig behaviour files of DANDI:001347 (sub-m<mouse>-<experiment>). Matching inter-series intervals links 10 of the 13 sessions to a behaviour file (all intervals equal within 0.02 s); sub-3 ses-20230225 and both sub-5 sessions have no behaviour file in DANDI:001347.
    Found by the attempt on Kauvar et al. 2025 (kauvar-2025-02, 2026-11-02)
