Intrinsic geometry of trial-to-trial variability in primary visual cortex is optimal for robust representation of visual similarity

Jehyun Kim, Hyeyoung Shin

Summary: The paper's main findings held up, tested mostly on the Allen Neuropixels sessions it analysed, since dandiset 000248 (OpenScope Illusion) holds only its example Isomap pictures, which reproduced too. Across V1 neurons, log variance rose with log mean at a slope near 1 (1.0489 against 1.048), and when that slope was rescaled, manifold overlap rose while alignment and the consistency of representations within and across mice peaked near 1. These results assume the paper used AllenSDK's default units labelled good, which the Methods do not state. The fall of decoding accuracy with slope was too slow to run in full, and the tangent space angle was not implemented in time, so neither was tested.
