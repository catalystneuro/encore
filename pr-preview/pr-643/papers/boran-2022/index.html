Boran et al. 2022 · Other journals

Persistent neuronal firing in the medial temporal lobe supports performance and workload of visual working memory in humans

doi:10.1016/j.neuroimage.2022.119123 · paper key boran-2022

Datasets

Behavior, the period-based firing results (maintenance neurons in all three regions) and the dPCA speed result held in this data. Findings that compare trials by workload did not: workload-modulated maintenance neurons, dPCA separation of set sizes, and decoding of workload (about 45% against the paper's 87.5%). The records suggest the deposited spikes may be misaligned with the trial labels, though no single offset was established. Region counts depend on an assumed placement of units, because the files lack the paper's region assignment. Spike-sorting quality and the response-time slope also differed from the paper.

  • ✓Reproduced Performance was almost perfect at set sizes 1 (98% correct) and 2 (99%) and fell at 4 (88%) and 6 (73%), a significant effect of set size in a permuted repeated-measures ANOVA over 16 sessions; average performance was 89%.
  • ✓Reproduced Across sessions the median working memory capacity was 3.2 by Pashler's K (range 2.2 to 5) and 3 by Cowan's K (range 1.9 to 4.5), so participants held at least 3 squares and set sizes 4 and 6 exceeded capacity.
  • ✓Reproduced Table 1 gives, per participant, the number of sessions, the memory capacity Kmax (2.41, 2.25, 3.04, 3.00, 2.87, 3.88, 3.00, 3.23, 3.58, 3.81, 5.00, 3.83, 4.50 for participants 1 to 13) and the number of neurons (33, 36, 106, 85, 29, 96, 48, 150, 23, 62, 11, 57, 73).
  • ◐Partially reproduced Mean response time on correct trials (2678 trials) increased with set size, by 118 ms per item, a significant effect in a permuted repeated-measures ANOVA over 16 sessions.
  • ✓Reproduced 809 neurons were recorded, 454 in hippocampus, 209 in entorhinal cortex and 146 in amygdala, 50 plus or minus 25 (range 11 to 96) per session.
  • ?Not reproduced Over all neurons, 0.79 plus or minus 1.54% of inter-spike intervals were shorter than 3 ms, most neurons were below 0.5%, and the peak signal-to-noise ratio of the mean waveform was 3.81 plus or minus 0.82.
  • ✓Reproduced The median (IQR) firing rate over all neurons was 1.09 (0.52 to 2.43) Hz over the trial, 1.12 (0.54 to 2.56) Hz during fixation, 1.10 (0.53 to 2.50) Hz during encoding, 1.09 (0.54 to 2.51) Hz during maintenance and 1.02 (0.49 to 2.45) Hz during test.
  • ✓Reproduced Maintenance neurons, firing more during maintenance than during fixation, were found in all three regions, 55 of 454 in hippocampus, 21 of 209 in entorhinal cortex and 16 of 146 in amygdala, each a significant proportion.
  • ?Not reproduced Among maintenance neurons, a significant number increased firing with workload in hippocampus (10 of 55, 18%) and entorhinal cortex (5 of 21, 24%), and a significant number decreased firing with workload in hippocampus (6 of 55, 11%); in entorhinal cortex 2 decreased and in amygdala 1 increased, not significant.
  • ?Not reproduced More hippocampal neurons were classified as maintenance neurons on correct trials than on incorrect trials (55 of 454, 12%, against 30 of 454, 7%).
  • ◐Partially reproduced Test neurons, firing more after the test array than during encoding and maintenance, made up 46 of 809 neurons (6%), half the number of maintenance neurons, and were found in all regions (17 of 454 in hippocampus, 19 of 209 in entorhinal cortex, 10 of 146 in amygdala).
  • ?Not reproduced Demixed PCA of the population separated set sizes 1, 2, 4 and 6; dPCs 2, 3 and 4 explained 36% of the variance (significant against scrambled labels), the separation appeared early in the trial, and the first 15 dPCs explained 81.4% of the variance.
  • ✓Reproduced Multidimensional speed in the 15-dPC space was highest during encoding and lowest during maintenance, lower than in encoding and test.
  • ◐Partially reproduced The pairwise distance between set-size trajectories was larger during maintenance than during encoding, and the largest of all periods, consistent with attractors during maintenance.
  • ?Not reproduced During maintenance, workload (set sizes 1 and 2 against 4 and 6) was decoded from all neurons pooled with median accuracy 87.5%, above chance; leaving out any one participant gave accuracies with IQR 85.7 to 87.6%.
  • ?Not reproduced Workload was decoded during maintenance from hippocampal neurons (75%) and entorhinal neurons (80%) but not from amygdala neurons.
  • ✓Reproduced Trial-wise performance (correct against incorrect) could not be decoded from MTL firing in any group (all 55%, hippocampus 55%, entorhinal 55%, amygdala 50%; p from .40 to .56).
