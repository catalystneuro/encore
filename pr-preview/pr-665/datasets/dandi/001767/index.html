The paper's core photometry findings held in this data: dopamine fell and acetylcholine rose after unrewarded side entry, trial history shaped both, and the two were anticorrelated at a lag near 0.1 s. Striatum-wide TelC changed behaviour while dopamine kept its reward polarity. The Drd2 knockout's larger acetylcholine signals did not reproduce with delta-Ach, a measure the figure legend does not name. Unchanged dopamine after VLS-selective TelC did not hold, and the cortical and thalamic input results were only partly testable. The slice, head-fixed optogenetic and dopamine-neuron inhibition findings could not be tested because that data is missing from the dataset.
