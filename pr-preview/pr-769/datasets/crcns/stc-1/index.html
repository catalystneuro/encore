Dataset unversioned files (the archive keeps no versions) · tested 2026-11-06 · 6 open findings

  • Data missingThe data set holds derived values only. For the paper's pairs there are no spike times or trial responses, no interneuronal distances, none of the 52 pairs over 1 mm, no discrimination-task correlations and no eye records; the single-electrode database of Figures 4, S4 and 8A is represented only by 129 units of two trained monkeys, and the training histories of Figure 2A by one threshold for each of two monkeys. Eighteen of the 35 claims cannot be tested.
    Found by the attempt on Gu et al. 2011 (gu-2011-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Description missingNo pair or monkey carries a naive or trained label, which every comparison in the paper needs. The groups can be recovered here only because the paper's per-animal pair counts are all distinct and match the counts per monkey in the file_ids.
    Found by the attempt on Gu et al. 2011 (gu-2011-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Description missingNo pair carries an electrode or distance label, and ten pairs of monkey 17, in six recordings (m17c36r1s, m17c40r1s, m17c41r1s, m17c42r1s, m17c44r1s, m17c48r1s), have file_ids ending in "s" without explanation, so the single-electrode and multi-electrode pairs cannot be told apart.
    Found by the attempt on Gu et al. 2011 (gu-2011-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Description missingThe data descriptor does not say how heading_pref was computed, how cp is signed (relative to the unit's preferred direction or to a fixed choice), or the units of resp_global (demo.m labels them spikes/s).
    Found by the attempt on Gu et al. 2011 (gu-2011-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Metadata errorThe data descriptor lists monkeys 1, 2, 3, 5, 17, 18 and 19 for the 127 MSTd pairs; the file_ids name an eighth, monkey 24, with two pairs, the trained animal with two pairs of the paper's Figure S2B.
    Found by the attempt on Gu et al. 2011 (gu-2011-01, 2026-11-06)
  • OtherRecording m19c21r1 holds six pairs from four units, where the paper states a maximum of five pairs in an experiment (Experimental Procedures, Electrophysiological Recordings).
