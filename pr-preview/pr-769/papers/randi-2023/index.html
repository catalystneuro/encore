Reproduction Neural signal propagation atlas of C. elegans (Randi et al. 2023)

Tests the findings of Randi, Sharma, Dvali and Leifer (2023) on dandiset 001075, which holds the 113 wild-type-background recordings of whole-brain calcium imaging during single-neuron optogenetic stimulation, 110 of them with extracted traces. It checks the recordings, the number of measured neuron pairs, three example connections of Figure 1, the spatial specificity of the stimulation, the responses to RID stimulation in WT and, from the paper's OSF export, in unc-31 animals, and how the per-pair measurements and recordings link to the paper's Source Data and OSF export. The q values, the spontaneous recordings and the kernel analyses are not testable. All files

Environment: Python 3.13, 103 packages pinned in requirements.lock.txt; CI runs each notebook from a new environment built from it.
