Two main findings held up, several did not, and the tetrodotoxin results could not be tested. Pure sine waves evoked more spikes than 20-Hz modulated waves, and modulated firing locked to the beats, both on an assumed reading of the criterion. Firing rates stayed well below the reported 60 to 90 Hz, and 20-kHz thresholds were not about twice the 2-kHz ones. Sustained depolarization was not detected, though the measure may miss plateaus this small. The tetrodotoxin effects stayed untested because the paper does not say how the soma was outlined on the deposited crops, the low-conductivity recordings are absent, and the pulse-kinetics findings were not attempted.
