  • baseline-rate

    Before torque onset, the firing rate of M1 neurons was lower after MPTP, because PTN rates fell (Mann-Whitney p < 0.001) while CSN rates did not change (p = 0.5). In monkey V the rates were 12.6 and 8.9 spikes/s for all cells, 20.3 and 14.8 for PTNs, and 3.5 and 3.4 for CSNs, before and after MPTP.

    The pre-torque firing rate of PTNs was reduced by 27% following MPTP treatment (Mann–Whitney U-test, p < 0.001; Table 1) whereas the mean activity of CSNs remained unchanged (Mann–Whitney U-test, p = 0.5).

    Supplementary table; Table 1; Results, Database, paragraph 3

    How it will be judged

    Mean baseline rate per group of cells studied. Reproduced when all six means are within tolerance, the PTN rate is lower after MPTP (Mann-Whitney p < 0.05) and the CSN rate does not differ (p >= 0.05). Partially reproduced when the two tests hold and some means are outside tolerance, or the means hold and a test does not. The M1 test (p < 0.05/2 in the paper) is reported.
