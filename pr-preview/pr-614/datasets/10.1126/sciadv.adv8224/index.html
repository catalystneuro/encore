From the paper State-dependent modulation of spiny projection neurons controls levodopa-induced dyskinesia in a mouse model of Parkinson’s disease · claims file · not yet checked

  • link-somatic-cells

    The dandiset's somatic current-step recordings are those behind the paper's somatic excitability figures, and the spike counts computed from them agree with the paper's per-cell tables.

    How it will be judged

    Groups are matched by name. Each dandiset cell is compared with every row of the paper's table for its group over the steps from 20 to 300 pA present in both, and its best match is the row with the smallest summed absolute difference in spike counts. The link holds (reproduced) when, over all groups the dandiset holds, the cell counts per group equal the paper's n, and at least 80% of cells have an exact match and at least 95% a best match within 3 spikes in total. Partially reproduced when at least 50% match exactly; not reproduced otherwise. Rheobase agreement with the best match is reported.

  • link-dendritic-cells

    The dandiset's dendritic line scans are those behind the paper's dendritic excitability index figures, and the index computed from them agrees with the paper's per-cell values.

    How it will be judged

    Where the paper's table names cells by date and letter (Figure 6D), cells are matched by that identifier (letter a, b, c for the first, second and third cell of the day). Elsewhere the paper's rows are taken to follow the dandiset's order by date and cell number, an assumption checked by the somatic link, where rows follow that order. Reproduced when the counts of cells with an index equal the paper's n in at least 7 of the 10 groups and the matched values differ from the paper's by a median of at most 10% of the paper's value. Partially reproduced when only one of these holds.

  • link-sr-cells

    The dandiset's Sr2+-oEPSC recordings are cells of the paper's Figures 2 and 4, and their events agree with the paper's per-cell values.

    How it will be judged

    Cells are matched by date and session letter (the paper's identifiers are the date followed by a letter). Reports the fraction of the paper's cells that are in the dandiset and, for those, the events found by lid.detect_events against the paper's event counts and mean amplitudes. Reproduced when every dandiset cell matches a paper identifier and the per-cell mean amplitude and event count are within 25% of the paper's for at least 75% of cells; partially reproduced when every cell matches but the values do not; not reproduced when cells do not match.

  • link-grab-rois

    The dandiset's GRAB-ACh recordings are regions of the paper's Figure 5F, and the normalised areas computed from them agree with the paper's per-region values.

    How it will be judged

    The paper's regions carry no identifier that links them to the dandiset's mice, so each mouse's vector of single-pulse areas (no drug, quinpirole, sulpiride, and dopamine where present) is matched to the paper's region in the same group with the smallest summed absolute difference. Reproduced when the counts per group are those of the paper and every mouse has a match within 0.05 per treatment; partially reproduced when the dandiset holds part of the regions and at least half of its mice match within 0.05 per treatment; not reproduced otherwise.

  • link-aim-mice

    The dandiset's AIM score tables are mice of the paper's Figures 8 and 10, with genotypes and scores that agree with the paper's tables.

    How it will be judged

    Mice are matched by their numeric identifier. The paper's total AIM score per session is taken to be the sum over the session's time points of the three scores. Reproduced when every dandiset mouse matches a paper mouse and every matched session total is equal; partially reproduced when the matched mice agree but some dandiset mice are not in the paper or the dandiset's genotype labels disagree with the paper's; not reproduced when matched values differ.
