  • pose-errors-close-fast

    Errors in pose estimation are most common on frames when the two animals are in close contact or moving very quickly.

    some errors in pose estimation and noise in keypoint placement still occur. These are most common on frames when the two animals are in close contact or moving very quickly.

    Appendix B (Composition, errors and noise)

    How it will be judged

    On the dandiset's pose alone, as it holds no usable confidence values. A frame is counted as a pose error when any of four body lengths of either mouse (nose to neck, neck to tail base, left to right ear, left to right hip) lies outside the session's quartiles widened by 3 times the interquartile range. Close frames are the lowest 10% of the distance between the two mice's centroids; fast frames are the highest 10% of the faster mouse's centroid speed after a 15-frame running median. Reproduced when the error fraction is higher in close frames than in the others and higher in fast frames than in the others, each with a 95% moving-block bootstrap interval (blocks of 300 frames, 2000 resamples) of the difference that excludes 0. Partially reproduced when this holds for one of the two. Thresholds of 5% and 20%, and a factor of 1.5, are shown as variants.
