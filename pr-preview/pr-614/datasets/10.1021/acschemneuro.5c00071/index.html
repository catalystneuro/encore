  • insertion-yield

    Insertion yields were low, 21.4% (3 of 14 sensors) in monkey T and 33% (3 of 9) in monkey P, counted as recording sites that stayed functional after insertion until the end of the study.

    insertion yields remained low ranging from 21.4% (3/14 individual sensors) in monkey T to 33% (3/9) in monkey P. These values indicate the number of recording sites that retained functionality after insertion, until the end of this study.

    Results and Discussion, paragraph 3

  • chronic-duration

    The s-µIPs kept recording for more than a year after implantation; the example sites were recorded 483 days (c8dg), 496 days (c3bs-c3a) and 390 days (monkey P) after implantation.

    sites c8dg and c8ds: 483 days postimplant ... site c3bs-c3a: 496 days postimplant ... site c33:390 days postimplant ... Measurements were made from chronically implanted s-μIPs that maintained functionality >1 year postimplant.

    Figure 3A, 3B and Figure 5 legends; Results and Discussion, paragraph 6

    How it will be judged

    Needs the implantation date of each site. The tolerance is one day, since a count of days can only be off by the convention for the first day. Reproduced when all three are within tolerance.

  • fscv-waveform

    FSCV applied a triangular waveform from -0.4 to 1.3 V at 400 V/s, every 100 ms (10 Hz).

    FSCV operates by applying a triangle waveform from −0.4 to 1.3 V at a rate of 400 V/s ... The triangular waveform is applied every 100 ms for an effective sampling frequency of 10 Hz.

    Methods, FSCV and EPhys Recordings, paragraph 2

    How it will be judged

    Measured from the applied voltage series of session 09/13/2024. The sweep rate is the median absolute step between samples of the rising ramp times the sampling rate, and the scan frequency is one over the median interval between scan starts. Reproduced when all four values are within tolerance, partially reproduced when three are.

  • ephys-acquisition

    EPhys was sampled at 30 kHz in monkey P and 32 kHz in monkey T, and band-pass filtered from 0.1 to 7500 Hz.

    EPhys recordings were made using a unity-gain analog amplifier headstage ... at a sampling frequency of 30 (monkey P) or 32 kHz (monkey T), and bandpass filtered from 0.1 to 7500 Hz.

    Methods, FSCV and EPhys Recordings, paragraph 3

    How it will be judged

    Sampling rates from the timestamps or rate of the raw series; the filter from the Neuralynx settings stored in the electrodes table (monkey P's file has none). Reproduced when all four values are within tolerance.

  • shape-task-structure

    In the shape task, forced trials made up 25% of all trials, and the cue-reward association was reversed after blocks of 50 to 300 trials.

    Forced choice trials were also interleaved (25% of all trials) where only one value cue was displayed ... The cue-reward association was reversed after a block of 50–300 trials depending on the session.

    Methods, Behavioral Tasks, paragraph 2

    How it will be judged

    Forced trials are those with code 119, of all trials in the trials table. A block is a run of trials in which the same cue image (code 81 or 88) signals the large reward in the successful forced trials; a reversal is placed at the first trial after the last forced trial of the earlier association. Block lengths count all trials, and the first and last blocks, cut by the start and end of the file, are reported but not judged. Reproduced when the forced share is within tolerance and every complete block is 45 to 330 trials long (the reported range widened by 10% of each bound); partially reproduced when only one of the two holds.

  • zenodo-link

    The dandiset reproduces the processed data the paper deposited on Zenodo; the trial selections and the beta power of the paper's example trial computed from the dandiset agree with the paper's own files.

    Data used in this study are available at zenodo (https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.14826579).

    Data Availability Statement (doi:10.5281/zenodo.14826579)

    How it will be judged

    Trials are matched by their number (1 to 1501 in the trials table of session 09/26/2024). The four Zenodo lists of successful trials (large reward, small reward, left, right) are compared with the selections decoded here from the event codes, and the beta power computed here for the 35th trial of the large-reward list is compared with the Zenodo example trace (-3 to 12 s from the value cue). Reproduced when each list agrees for at least 99% of the trials in either and the beta traces correlate at r >= 0.9 at a lag of at most 10 ms; partially reproduced when only one of the two holds.

  • fig3a-dual-channel

    Dopamine was measured from two neighbouring channels of one dual-channel s-µIP (sites c8dg and c8ds, 0.1 to 0.2 mm apart), and both showed dopamine increases after the value cue in a single forced large-reward trial.

    These measurements also demonstrate the ability to measure dopamine from two neighboring channels on a single s-μIP with 0.1–0.2 mm separation between the recording tips. ... (e.g., increases in both channels after the value cue, V).

    Figure 3A; Results and Discussion, paragraph 4

    How it will be judged

    Needs the trial-aligned dopamine of both c8dg and c8ds. Reproduced when, in forced large-reward trials, the mean change in dopamine from the value cue over 0 to 3 s after it is above zero at both sites in more than half of the trials.

  • fig3a-redox-peaks

    The single-trial FSCV colour plot shows dopamine redox current, with changes near 0.6 V (oxidation) and -0.2 V (reduction) after the value cue.

    Color plot shows clear dopamine redox current (i.e., color changes ∼0.6 and −0.2 V).

    Figure 3A

    How it will be judged

    Needs the raw FSCV current on the trial clock. The background-subtracted voltammogram (mean of 0.5 to 2 s after the value cue minus the scan at the value cue), averaged over forced large-reward trials, should have its largest positive value on the rising sweep within 0.45 to 0.75 V and its most negative value on the falling sweep within -0.35 to -0.05 V. Reproduced when both hold.

  • fig3b-beta-bursts

    Single-trial bipolar LFP from site c3bs-c3a shows beta bursts (one to a few cycles of high beta power) whose frequency changes around the cue and other task events.

    Single-trial LFPs measured from another s-μIP are shown to display fluctuations in the frequency of β-band “bursts” (i.e., 1–few cycles of high β-band power) around the cue and other task events

    Figure 3B; Results and Discussion, paragraph 4

    How it will be judged

    Successful large-reward trials of session 09/26/2024. A burst is a run of beta power above the 75th percentile of the beta power over all trial windows of the session that lasts at least 50 ms (one cycle at 20 Hz). For each trial the burst rate (burst onsets per second) is counted in -1 to 0 s and 0 to 1 s from the value cue. Reproduced when the burst rates in the two windows differ by a two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test at p < 0.05. Thresholds at the 50th and 90th percentiles are shown as variants. The paper does not define a burst; the threshold and minimum length here are common choices.

  • fig4a-dopamine-reward

    Trial-averaged dopamine at site c8dg is higher after large-reward than after small-reward value cues, in successful forced trials of the shape task.

    Higher dopamine levels are observed following the display of large reward associated value cues as compared to small reward cues in monkey T

    Figure 4A

    How it will be judged

    Successful forced trials of session 09/13/2024 that have a row in the trial-aligned FSCV table. Per trial, the mean of the re-referenced dopamine over 0 to 3 s after the value cue (inside the value cue fixation, which ends at feedback). Reproduced when the large-reward median exceeds the small-reward median and the Mann-Whitney test gives p < 0.05. Variants (not deciding) are the stored `da` without re-referencing, the oxidation current `iox` re-referenced the same way, and windows of 0 to 1.5 s and 1.5 to 3 s.

  • fig4b-beta-reward

    Beta-band LFP power at c3bs-c3a in the direction task is more suppressed (stronger event-related desynchronization) after large-reward than after small-reward value cues, in a brief window after the cue.

    Trial-averaged β-band LFP power for large and small reward conditions in the direction task, showing increased suppression (i.e., ERD, highlighted in yellow) for large as compared to small reward cues during the brief time window following the value cue at 0 s

    Figure 4B

    How it will be judged

    Successful trials of session 09/26/2024, large against small reward. Per trial, the mean beta power over 0 to 0.5 s after the value cue. Reproduced when the large-reward median is below the small-reward median and the Mann-Whitney test gives p < 0.05. Variants (not deciding) are windows of 0 to 1 s and 0.25 to 0.75 s, and power divided by each trial's mean over -0.5 to 0 s.

  • fig4c-beta-side

    Increased suppression for contralateral is observed as compared to ipsilateral movement conditions.

    Figure 4C

    Successful large-reward trials of session 09/26/2024, left against right condition. Per trial, the mean beta power over 0 to 0.5 s after the value cue. Reproduced when the left median is below the right median and the Mann-Whitney test gives p < 0.05. Variants as for fig4b-beta-reward.

  • fig5a-spike-reward

    Spikes recorded from an s-µIP in the caudate of monkey P (site c33, 390 days post implant) fire at a different rate for large and small reward during the value cue fixation of the direction task.

    Spike measurements from an s-μIP in the CN of monkey P also displayed reward-modulated differences in firing rate during the value cue fixation window

    Figure 5A; Results and Discussion, paragraph 5

  • fig5bc-unit-isolation

    The s-µIP recorded a spike-sorted unit in monkey P, shown by its average waveform and its interspike interval histogram.

    Average spike waveform ± standard deviation. ... Interspike interval (ISI) histogram showing the distribution of the timing between spikes detected.

    Figure 5B and 5C

    How it will be judged

    The sorted unit is Plexon unit 1, as in the paper's spikes_PCA.m (Plexon unit 0 is the unsorted cluster). Partially reproduced when only one holds.
