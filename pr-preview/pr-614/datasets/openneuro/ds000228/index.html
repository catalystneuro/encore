The behavioral results held in this data, but the brain findings held only in direction. The theory of mind and pain networks separated in adults and 3-year-olds, and within-network correlations rose and across-network correlations fell with age, but values and significance bounds missed, with adult across-network correlation at -.051 against -.17, an offset the code, available only on request, leaves unexplained. Reverse-correlation events were only partly found, age effects at event peaks did not reproduce, and the maturity finding failed on a within-Pain effect. The independent adult sample could not be tested because it is not deposited. The motion controls held.

    replication-sample

    The independent sample of 20 adults is not in the snapshot, and neither the paper nor the dataset's README names an archive that holds it. The claims file marks the claim `elsewhere` with no archive; as the data are public nowhere, data_missing would apply, which the checker does not allow for an `elsewhere` claim. This restates the authors' result and is not the second sample's data, so the claim stays blocked.

    The hand-coded events of Jacoby et al. 2016 are not in the snapshot (its README lists only reverse-correlation events) or the paper. The Saxe lab web page (https://saxelab.mit.edu/theory-mind-and-pain-matrix-localizer-movie-viewing-experiment) offers the experiment script movloc.zip (version of 29 March 2019) whose mov_localizer.m holds them, but with no DOI or repository commit, so it cannot be pinned as the protocol requires for an outside input.

Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • OtherWith the paper's own deposited preprocessed images, artifact and CompCor regressors and ROI masks, and the pipeline of the Methods, the inter-region correlations differ from the paper's, higher within networks and less negative across them (adults .604, .398 and -.051 against .48, .35 and -.17). In the 3-year-olds within-ToM is higher (.326 against .21) but within-Pain is lower (.179 against .23; non-bilateral Pain .146 against .17), so the offset is not the same in every group. Several downstream results (within-ToM difference between false-belief passers and failers, age effects at event peaks, the T04 effect of ToM score) do not come out as reported. Either the deposited data or the described pipeline differs from what produced the paper's numbers.
  • Methods not availableFor the authors, which timepoints entered the inter-region correlations (all 168 TRs, or TRs 11 to 168 as in the reverse correlation), and whether the artifact timepoints were regressed with one indicator each? irc-tom-behavior-age, irc-fb-across and maturity-within change outcome with this choice; this attempt assumed all 168 TRs and one indicator each, as in the deposited regressor files.
  • Methods not availableFor the authors, over which timepoints was each ROI timecourse z-normalized before the reverse correlation, and were child event magnitudes taken from the z-normalized timecourse? rc-3yo-events depends on the first (all 168 TRs assumed) and rc-fb-no-difference on the second (z-normalized assumed). The alpha of the reverse-correlation t-tests is also not stated; p < .05 uncorrected matches Supplementary Table 3 best.
  • Methods not availableThe analysis code is available only on request, and the high-pass filter implementation (SPM discrete cosine assumed) is not stated.
  • Metadata errorparticipants.tsv lists 2 seven-year-olds and 21 8-12-year-olds with 3.13 mm voxels and no slice gap, where the Methods say 3 and 20. The voxel-size column name is misspelled ("Voxel slize"), and the no-gap level is stored as an empty value where participants.json defines the level "None".
  • Other19 participants (14 aged 8-12, 2 adults, one each 4, 5 and 7 years) have face-ROI voxels without data at some timepoint, where Supplementary Fig. 4 counts 17 with incomplete ventral coverage (12 aged 8-12, 2 adults, one each 3, 4 and 7 years); the minimum of 101 voxels with data agrees with its "at least 100 voxels". The coverage criterion may differ.
  • OtherThe independent sample of 20 adults that defined the ToM and pain ROIs and replicated the events is not deposited anywhere; the ROIs it produced are in the dataset.

Reproduction Development of the social brain from age three to twelve (Richardson et al. 2018)

Tests the findings of Richardson, Lisandrelli, Riobueno-Naylor and Saxe (2018) on OpenNeuro ds000228, which holds movie-viewing fMRI of 122 children aged 3 to 12 and 33 adults, with the paper's preprocessed images, artifact and CompCor regressors, group ROIs and behavioural scores. It covers the behavioural results, motion, inter-region correlations within and across the ToM and pain networks, reverse correlation of network timecourses, functional maturity, and the face-region analyses. Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • The paper does not define "remaining ToM items"; the criterion's two readings were run and reading (a) matches. participants.json describes ToM Booklet-Matched-NOFB as the 18 items without the six false-belief questions.beh-fb-tom
  • The 100 s high-pass filter is SPM's discrete cosine basis (spm_filter); the Methods say SPM8 and "a cutoff of 100 s" without naming the filter. A standard choice.irc-adult
  • The cleaning is applied to the ROI mean timecourse, which equals cleaning each voxel and averaging, as every step of Methods paragraph 4 is linear and the same for each voxel.irc-adult
  • The face and scene parcels are the ones deposited in derivatives/ROIs of the snapshot (rSTS_masked, lSTS, rOFA, lOFA, rFFA, lFFA; rRSC, lRSC, rTOS, lTOS, rPPA, lPPA), which the dataset's README names as the Julian et al. 2012 parcels; the claims file treats them as an outside input, but no outside data were used. The M1 and V1 ROIs are also deposited.irc-control-networks
  • The paper reports z for Williams' test; Williams' T2 is a t on n - 3 df. Both T2 and Steiger's Z are shown; they give the same conclusions.irc-williams
  • Assumed O2a (one regressor per artifact timepoint), as deposited in the paper's own nuisance files, and O3a (all 168 TRs), since the Methods restrict the timepoints to TRs 11 to 168 only for the reverse correlation and maturity analyses (paragraphs 9 and 13), not for the inter-region correlation (paragraph 7). Under O3b the claim would be reproduced.irc-tom-behavior-age
  • Assumed O2a and O3a, as for irc-tom-behavior-age. Under O3b the claim would be reproduced (p = .051).irc-fb-across
  • Seed fixed (a standard choice); the random gaps split the remaining zeros by a multinomial draw after one zero between events, a reading of Methods paragraph 10, which says only "a random proportion of zeros between events".rc-overlap
  • Reading (b) of the criterion is run as 159-TR timecourses (159 minus each network's event TRs as zeros), the length implied by the paper's zeros (125, 116) and event totals (34, 43 TRs); with the Supplementary Table 3 events it gives 125 and 116 zeros.rc-overlap
  • Face, scene, M1 and V1 ROIs are the ones deposited in the snapshot, not an outside input.supp-table4-other-networks
  • Assumed O7a, magnitudes from the z-normalized network timecourse, because the events and their peak timepoints are defined on that timecourse (Methods paragraphs 9 and 11, "the timepoint with the highest average signal value in adults"). Under O7b the claim would not be reproduced.rc-fb-no-difference
  • The T03 permutation test permutes the group labels and refits the regression (5000 permutations, fixed seed); the paper cites R's lmPerm without its settings.rc-fb-no-difference
  • The rc-events reading ties between O6a and O6b. Assumed O6b, z-normalization over all 168 TRs, because Methods paragraph 9 z-normalizes each ROI timecourse first and leaves out the first 10 timepoints only from the t-tests. Under O6a the claim would not be reproduced.rc-3yo-events
  • The criterion asks for the reading that reproduced rc-events, and no reading did. The reading that matched rc-events best (O5a) is used, an extension of the criterion.rc-3yo-events
  • Assumed O2a and O3a, as for irc-tom-behavior-age. Under O2b with O3b the Pain maturity effect is significant and the claim would not be reproduced.maturity-within
  • The fusiform ROIs are the deposited rFFA (1019 voxels) and lFFA (531), the sizes of Supplementary Table 2.face-rc
  • Face-event peaks are the found adult events' peaks, as the paper gives no face peak TRs.face-rc
