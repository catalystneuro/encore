Most of the paper's main findings held up in this data. Hippocampus-BLA pairs reactivated in NREM but not REM sleep after training, less so for piriform cortex and not for the central nuclei, ripple-modulated BLA cells contributed most, and the air puff trajectory was reactivated around ripples. The sleep states are not deposited, so these results rest on states decoded from the authors' firing-rate tables, and several change under other decoder settings. The rats' learning of the daily danger zone held up only in part, as the air puff location is deposited for 6 of 55 sessions and had to be estimated. A control finding no bias from that location did not reproduce.

Summary written by the agent that recorded the attempt, from its claims and the agents’ reports. A failure to reproduce may be a failure of the agent.

    fig3c-decay

    Decoded NREM. 24 qualifying sessions, 17 with EV > REV in the first window (paper 19, tolerance 1.9, so the count is just outside). Mean EV - REV 3.04 +- 0.55, 1.78 +- 0.53 and 1.69 +- 0.88% (paper 2.89, 1.72, 1.31), all within tolerance; signed-rank p = 1.5e-5, 0.00021, 0.035; Kruskal-Wallis p = 0.024. Variants give other outcomes: with the table's pre-NREM (20 sessions; 2.51, 1.52, 1.30%; Kruskal-Wallis p = 0.021) the third window gives p = 0.067 (partial); with 30-s smoothing the first mean is 3.44% (partial); with the first revision's immobility substitute the decay test gives p = 0.074 (not reproduced). In the table pre-NREM variant Rat10-20140622 has window 2 undefined because its hippocampal cells fire no spike there (0, against 20,844 and 19,394 in windows 1 and 3) while its BLA cells fire 31,040.

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • Sleep states are not deposited. They are decoded from the authors' StatesFR table (hc14.decode_states, an assumption): each 5-s epoch takes the state of columns 5 to 8 under which the spike counts of all the session's units are most likely at the table's rates, with log likelihoods summed over 60 s; NREM is column 6 and REM column 5, in immobile epochs (head speed from positions smoothed over 1 s below 4 cm/s; the paper associates NREM with immobility, Online Methods, Preprocessing) of pre-sleep and post-sleep. The decoded NREM gives hippocampus-BLA pair correlations that follow the table's NREM columns (r = 0.88 pre, 0.90 post) and per-session EV - REV with mean 3.13% against the table's 3.21% (Spearman 0.92); the decoded REM follows the table's REM columns (r = 0.78, 0.81). Decoded minutes match those implied by StatesFR (median ratio 0.95 for NREM, 1.05 for REM; Spearman 0.92 and 0.94 over 54 sessions). The decoder with 30-s smoothing and the first revision's immobility substitute are variants.
    • Pre-NREM is the decoded pre-NREM, the run whole, and the windows cumulative decoded post-NREM time.

Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingThe hand-scored sleep states (NREM, REM, wake intervals) are not deposited, nor the run intervals and lap classifications the analyses used. Rescoring states from the LFP would mean streaming 461 GB of tar.gz archives; this attempt decoded them from the per-state firing rates of the StatesFR table, which reproduces the authors' NREM and REM pair correlations closely but not exactly.
  • Description missingThe summary tables in AllRats-Variables.tar.gz (FinalType, StatesFR, SpikeParameters, PoissonRippleMod, EVcontribs, AllRippleCCGs) are undocumented, as the data description says. Their columns could be identified against the raw spikes, except which StatesFR column is REM (column 5 or 8).
  • Description missingThe structure of each shank and day comes in four formats, one per rat (Structures/*.mat for Rat09 and Rat10, Structures.txt for Rat11, Batch-Files/Structures.batch and StructureMapping.ods for Rat08). Rat08's labels other than BLA are only in the All sheet of the ods, with spellings that differ from the other rats (CeC and CeM for CeCM, PIr, mixed labels such as VEn/Pir); Rat08 sessions 10 to 13 list no BLA shanks, and the hippocampal shanks are listed only for Rat10.
  • Metadata errorRat08-20130722 has 152 sorted units in its clu files and 127 in the summary tables (28 only in the clu files, 3 only in the tables); the other 60 sessions agree unit for unit.
  • Selection not documentedThe bucket holds 65 sessions; the paper's 61 are listed only in sessionindexing.mat. Rat11-20150309, -0318, -0319 and -0324 are left out without a stated reason.
  • Description missingOnly 6 of the 55 behaviour sessions carry an airpuffloc file with the air puff location and direction; for the others they must be inferred from the head position at the air puffs, which lies -3 to 18 cm (median 7.3 cm) past the file's location in the direction of travel where both exist. Several behaviour outcomes change with a 7.3-cm correction.
  • OtherThe pair table's run correlations match correlations recomputed over the run subsession in 17 of 29 sessions (r > 0.999) and less well in 12 (r = 0.58 to 0.98), so the run interval the authors used is not the run subsession in those sessions and is not deposited.
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the authors. Which StatesFR column is REM? This attempt took column 5 over column 8; epochs decoded as column 5 give hippocampus-BLA pair correlations that follow the table's REM correlations (r about 0.8), which supports it. With column 8 the REM/wake ratios of BLA cells would be smaller than the NREM/wake ratios, against Figure 2c.
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the authors. Was the air puff location of BehavioralMeasures.m taken from the airpuffloc files, from the puff events, or from elsewhere, and where is it for the 49 sessions without an airpuffloc file? This attempt used the median head position at the air puffs, and the location-bias test (airpuff-location-no-bias) depends on it.
  • OtherThe session archives are tar.gz files without an index, and each clu archive (0.1 to 2.5 GB) is mostly RawKK, .fet and .klg files; reading the sorted spikes of one session means decompressing the whole archive (99 GB over 65 sessions), and the 65 LFP archives are 3.0 to 9.0 GB each (461 GB).
  • Metadata errorRat08's two structure lists disagree on one shank-day: StructureMapping.ods labels shank 13 of session 5 (Rat08-20130712) BLA, and Structures.batch does not list it.
  • Data missingIn Rat10-20140622 the hippocampal cells of the pair table (shanks 17 to 20) fire no spike over a 20-min stretch of decoded post-sleep NREM, while the session's BLA cells fire 31,040 spikes in it; the hippocampal spikes of that stretch are missing from the clu files, or the hippocampal shanks stopped recording.
