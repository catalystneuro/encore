Multi-unit spiking activity recorded from rat frontal cortex (brain regions mPFC, OFC, ACC, and M2) during wake-sleep episode wherein at least 7 minutes of wake are followed by 20 minutes of sleep.

The paper's central finding, that sleep evens out cortical firing as fast-firing pyramidal cells slow down and slow-firing cells speed up, held up in this data across sleep and within nonREM packets (first-to-last packet slope 0.872), as did the overall decline in population rate. The reported drop in rate variability over sleep and the firing changes within single UP states did not reproduce. Most of the lasting effects of individual sub-states (REM, microarousals, waking between packets) did not reproduce, likely because the paper does not say how sequences of states were formed and no rule gave its cell counts. Lower firing in waking after sleep held only weakly, as most cycles lack scored waking right after sleep.

Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Metadata errorThe files hold 51 WAKE-SLEEP cycles (WakeSleepTimePairFormat over the 27 sessions), where the paper reports 54; the Wake and Sleep intervals are the same 51 pairs, with none unpaired. All other counts (995 pE, 126 pI, 27 sessions, 11 rats, 43230 UP and 86839 DOWN states, 5203 spindles) match the paper exactly.
  • Metadata errorThe paper reports 790 microarousals; 434 MA intervals lie inside SLEEP (2130 MA intervals in the files, most outside SLEEP). 345 WakeInterruption intervals inside SLEEP are shorter than the 40 s minimum the data description gives for them; none lies between two packets (123 between two REM intervals, 80 between REM and a packet), so they are not MAs by the paper's definition either, though counted with the MAs they give 779 events. One nonREM packet (14 s) is shorter than the 20 s minimum of Table S1.
  • Metadata errorIn Rizzo_022715 and Splinter_020515 a WAKE-SLEEP cycle's WAKE starts before the previous cycle's SLEEP ends (WAKE 9919-14394 s against SLEEP 5655-10010 s; WAKE 5335-7210 s against SLEEP 3543-5455 s), so the cycles overlap.
  • OtherDino_061914 and Dino_062014 each appear as two sessions, ACC and mPFC probes recorded at the same time (the data set's WatsonSleepHomestasis2016Table.xlsx lists the same days), each with its own state files, and the two scorings of Dino_062014 differ (mean SLEEP per cycle 59.5 min in ACC and 27.1 min in mPFC; 41 and 17 packets). State- and LFP-level analyses over the 27 sessions count these days twice.
  • Metadata errorThe data description says the motion signal in _Motion.mat is z-scored; it is non-negative in every session, with per-session means from 0.55 to 4076, and BWRat19_032513 holds NaN values.
  • Metadata errorRecordingFileIntervals in _BasicMetaData.mat ends at infinity in the three sessions of rat JennBuzsaki22, and the UP/DOWN file is named _UPDOWNINtervals.mat, where the description says _UPDOWNInts.mat and _UPDOWNIntervals.mat.
  • Paper inconsistentThe Results give spindle incidence as 1.94 +- 0.39 per minute; Table S1 gives 0.049 +- 0.008 Hz (2.94 per minute). The deposited spindles give 2.85 per minute, matching Table S1.
  • Description missingScored WAKE exists only as the WAKE of a WAKE-SLEEP cycle, so the WAKE that follows a SLEEP is in most cycles absent or hours later (23 of 51 cycles have a later scored WAKE, 6 within 1 s). The 5 min post-sleep WAKE windows of the paper cannot be located for most cycles.
  • OtherEach session is one tar.gz of 0.9 to 6 GB, and the order of members differs between files (the LFP .eeg comes before the spike and state files in some), so reading any one small file needs the whole archive streamed. Reading all 27 takes 7 to 9 minutes from the bucket.
  • Methods not availableQuestions for the paper's authors. (1) The sampling unit of the correlations against normalized time (per packet, 10 s bins, or bins of normalized time averaged over periods) and whether SLEEP is normalized by its clock time or by its concatenated nonREM time; the readings give different outcomes for the CV, spindle and UP/DOWN trends over SLEEP. (2) Whether the burst index is the proportion of ISIs <= 15 ms (Supplemental Experimental Procedures) or the fraction of spikes with such an ISI (Results); the latter gives the reported nonREM median. (3) The model and fit range of the autocorrelogram decay exponent. (4) How triplets are formed (adjacency of scored intervals, which WAKE counts between packets, giving the 24 nonREM-WAKE-nonREM triplets) and whether REM-packet-REM triplets allow an arousal after the first REM; the attempt assumes they do, which gives 80 triplets and 660 cells against the about 136 cells of Figure S5D. (5) Whether the motion signal was z-scored before the movement correlation. (6) How the WAKE sextiles are computed for cells recorded over several cycles (session WAKE or each cycle's WAKE); fig3e-wake-before-after-sextiles and fig3f-within-up-changes differ between the two.
