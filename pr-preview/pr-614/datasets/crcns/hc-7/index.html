The paper's main findings on short-term plasticity largely held up here: uIPSC kinetics and amplitude differ by presynaptic cell type, PV+ interneuron synapses depress more at higher frequencies, and CCK+ synapses facilitate during short bursts. The late onset of asynchronous release at CCK+ synapses did not reproduce, likely because the paper does not describe its onset rule and the rules tried also flag PV+ pairs the paper calls synchronous. CCK+ basket cell to pyramidal cell trains facilitated in only 1 of 6 pairs, and the plasticity models reproduced in part. The interneuron firing clusters, connection probabilities, spontaneous IPSCs and sharp-wave ripple results could not be tested, as those recordings are not in the dataset.

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Metadata errorThe stimulation timings in the data differ from the data descriptor and the paper. RIP bursts are four APs 4.5 ms apart (222 Hz), not 160 Hz; the EPI burst is 30 APs 6.7 ms apart (147 Hz) and four 3.3 ms apart (303 Hz), not 160 and 320 Hz; the single AP precedes the RIP and EPI bursts by 1.3 s, not 1 s. The 10-AP trains are at their nominal rates.
  • Metadata errorExperiments_description.txt mislabels two recordings (PV+BC to PV+BC pair 10 s130807-0205, listed as 5 Hz, and CCK+DTI to PC pair 5 s150222-0305, listed as 40 Hz, are 10 Hz trains), names eight files that are not in the zip (three misdated s1508.. for s1308..), lists one file for two protocols in two pairs, leaves 20 recordings of the zip unnamed, lists no PV+BC to AAC pairs, and numbers the IN to PC pairs differently from the PairNN folders.
  • Data missingThree recordings have a header but no sweeps (CCK+BC to PC Pair07 s140616-0105 and -0106, CCK+DTI to PC Pair01 s150119-0104), three have sweeps but no _EPA table, and the RIP recording listed for PV+BC to PC pair 4 is absent.
