Most main findings held only in part here, and the maze findings could not be tested. SWR locking to mPFC delta and spindle phase reproduced, and SWR rate rose around K-complexes, but the thalamic rebound after them was only partly seen. Thalamic firing did not fall at SWRs or track SWR rate (1 of 3 units against 61.7%), and units were not less rhythmic than multi-unit activity. These results rest on 3 of the paper's 32 sessions and 5 thalamic units. Every comparison of sleep before and after the maze is untestable because the dataset holds no maze or pre-maze sleep.

Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingThe data set holds 3 recording sessions (about 3.5 h, 5 thalamic units) of the paper's 32 sessions with thalamic units (63 thalamic units). It holds no maze epoch, no sleep before the maze, none of the four long control sessions and no position (LED) data, so the paper's comparisons of sleep before and after the maze (six claims) and the waking definition by velocity cannot be tested, and most unit-level claims rest on 2 to 13 units.
  • Description missingThe thalamic nucleus of each unit is not given; only the Session 1 unit is named (MD), in the title of a deposited script. Session 2's two units sit in a folder with no region in its name and are thalamic only by the deposited script's title. The LFP files state no unit, and the descriptor does not say which tetrode and wire each LFP comes from.
  • Methods not availableThe deposited spindle troughs do not follow the detection rule of the Methods (local minima three sd below the mean of the spindle-filtered LFP): they are 3.7 to 6.2 times as many as that rule gives (3817 against 1019 in Session 1) and their filtered values reach -0.7 to -1.1 sd. The deposited Session 1 SWRs (1263) are also more than the Methods' k = 3 rule gives (840). Question for the authors: what threshold and reference period produced the deposited troughs and SWRs, and were these the events behind the paper's figures?
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the authors: how was the spindle-trough-triggered scalogram of Figure 1, figure supplement 3 scaled (raw wavelet power, or power relative to a baseline)? This attempt shows both raw power and power relative to the mean power of the same state; in raw power the Session 3 mPFC peak falls at 4 to 8 Hz, not 10 to 15 Hz.
  • File format errorSpike times are stored at single precision. In Session 3 every spike time equals its float32 rounding, a resolution of 2^-10 s (0.98 ms) at these values, which produces repeated timestamps (6230 in the thalamic MUA, 657 in the mPFC MUA, 17 and 8 in two thalamic units); in Sessions 1 and 2, 73 to 87 % of times do. LFP and event times are in double precision.
  • Description missingIn Session 3 the mPFC unit and MUA start at 11750 s, 24 min before the LFP, the sleep periods and every other stream (13200 s); the descriptor does not say what this epoch is. The CA1 files labelled "InRun" hold only spikes of the sleep after the maze; the maze spikes behind the label are not included.
  • Paper inconsistentThe paper's Data availability statement names the CRCNS data set "HC-24" (doi:10.6080/K0K35RVG) and then says that the data set and the documentation that describes it are available as "HC-19" (crcns.org/data-sets/hc/hc-19). The hc-24 data descriptor (version 0.6) cites the 2019 bioRxiv preprint (doi:10.1101/653436), not the eLife paper.
  • Methods not availableThe Methods' sleep rule, mPFC delta power and CA1 ripple power both above the session mean for at least 2 min, finds no sleep in Sessions 1 and 3 when power is a 1 s or 10 s moving average of the squared filtered LFP, against 42 and 78 min of deposited sleep. Filling dips shorter than 30 s finds 19 and 44 min inside the deposited periods. Question for the authors: what smoothing, and what tolerance for short dips, produced the deposited sleep periods?
