Most of the central findings held in this data. The Fig. 1f suppression effect, the cue-excited versus cue-inhibited split, the baseline-firing dependence of cue-inhibited neurons, the three-cluster structure of cue-inhibited neurons (Low 74, Intermediate 34, High 18) and the relative-threat regressions reproduced. Several of these rest on the authors' own scripts: their screen on integer-rounded rates, their single-start clustering, and their pause definition. Differential firing to danger, uncertainty and safety held, but the Methods' normalization gave means about twice the printed ones, so exact values matched only with the scripts' version. The Intermediate-neuron beta pattern (Fig. 4g) and a supplementary cue-correlation claim did not reproduce, the latter because the window it used is unstated. The 194-session count came out as 195, and reward-firing correlations kept their signs but missed a tight R² tolerance.
