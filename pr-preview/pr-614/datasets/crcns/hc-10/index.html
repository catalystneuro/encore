None of the paper's main findings could be tested in this data set, which holds only current-step recordings of 17 pyramidal cells and 6 basket cells. The larger basket cell inhibition of deep pyramidal cells, the running-evoked axon activity, the stronger superficial-to-basket excitation and the projection-specific results need paired recordings, imaging and anatomy that are not deposited. The network model result was too costly to run, at about 450 core-hours. Among the minor intrinsic-property claims, the lack of firing-curve and input-resistance differences and the larger superficial sag held, and rheobase matched only in part. Resting potential could not be tested because the holding current was not recorded.

✓Reproduced4

  • ✓Reproduced

    figs1e-if-curves

    Two-way ANOVA of firing frequency on group and step (0 to +550 pA), reading (a) of the rate (spikes per second of the 1 s step), group F = 0.68, p = 0.411, eta squared 0.0005, interaction p = 0.997; reading (b) p = 0.866. The mixed ANOVA with step within cell gives p = 0.759 and 0.951. At +550 pA, sPC 31.62 and dPC 30.56 Hz under reading (a), 33.63 and 31.24 Hz under reading (b), all within tolerance. Reading (a) also gives the paper's p > 0.4. Spike counts change by under 1% between thresholds of -20, 0 and +10 mV. Reading (b) gives 0 Hz to a sweep with one spike; 2 PC sweeps have one spike and none has two.

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • The files record no injected current; the step levels are those of the data descriptor's table (-400 to +550 pA in 50 pA steps), and the sweep order is checked against the voltage responses in the data check.
    • The paper says only "two-way ANOVA"; the model with the group by step interaction is the main test, and the additive model (group p = 0.400) and the mixed ANOVA are shown. All agree.
    • Under rate reading (b), a sweep with fewer than two spikes has no interval and is given 0 Hz, a standard choice that affects only sweeps at the rheobase.
    • The data set has no animal or slice identifiers, so the result is broken down by cell, not by animal.
  • ✓Reproduced

    figs1f-input-resistance

    Reading (a), slope over the -100 to +100 pA steps at steady state 0.8 to 1.0 s after onset, gives sPC 118.7 and dPC 124.9 MOhm, both within tolerance of 117 and 124. Shapiro-Wilk fails, and Mann-Whitney gives p = 1.000 (Cohen's d -0.13). Reading (b) gives 118.9 and 123.7 MOhm, p = 1.000. Three cells (D1_25abf, D4_11abf, S1_47abf) fire on the +100 pA step; with the -100 to 0 pA steps only, sPC 107.2 and dPC 101.8 MOhm (p = 0.673), the dPC value then outside tolerance.

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • Step levels from the data descriptor, as for figs1e-if-curves. The pre-step baseline for reading (b) is the 0.2 s before onset, a standard choice.
  • ✓Reproduced

    figs1h-sag

    Reading (a), the -400 pA step, gives sPC 8.71 and dPC 5.69 mV, both within tolerance of 8.7 and 5.6, and sPC exceeds dPC (t test p = 0.0020, Cohen's d 1.81). Readings (b) -300 pA and (c) -200 pA give the same difference (p = 0.0011 and 0.0050) with smaller sags (7.04 and 4.48; 5.25 and 3.56 mV), outside tolerance. Taking the peak of the voltage smoothed over 1 ms changes the means by at most 0.05 mV.

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • Step levels from the data descriptor, as for figs1e-if-curves.
  • ✓Reproduced

    figs3f-pvbc-firing

    Under reading (a), one PVBC, with the rate as spikes per second, PV34 is silent from 0 to +300 pA and fires at 2, 17, 36 and 52 Hz at +350, +400, +450 and +500 pA, the four expected values. With the rate's reading (b) PV34 gives 14.9 Hz at +350 pA (two spikes close together), outside tolerance. No other PVBC matches; PV37, PV38, PV72 and PV80 fire at or below +300 pA, and PV36 starts at +500 pA. The mean over all PVBCs (reading b of the cell) cannot be formed above +300 pA, since PV38 was recorded only to +300 pA. The paper does not say which cell Figure S3F shows, so taking the best match of six cells is permissive; PV34 is the only one that matches, at all four steps and in its silence up to +300 pA. The model curve of the statement is not tested.

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • The 1 s step of 50 pA increments is assumed by the criterion; the data show a 1.000 s step and the descriptor gives 50 pA increments for the six PVBCs.
    • PV34 is deposited twice with identical bytes; it is counted once.

  • ◐Partially reproduced

    tables1-rheobase

    Per-cell rheobases are 100 to 250 pA for sPCs and 100 to 300 pA for dPCs. Reading (a), the median, gives sPC 200 and dPC 150 pA, so only dPC matches Table S1. Reading (b), the mean rounded to 50 pA, gives 200 pA for both (means 187.5 and 177.8 pA). By the criterion the groups share a step under (b) but it is not +150 pA, which is partial. No cell's rheobase changes with a spike threshold of -20 or +10 mV, or under the definition of SimTracker's CellData tool (least positive step with an upward crossing of -20 mV anywhere in the sweep). The single spikes that set the rheobase of D5_15abf and D6_19abf peak at 46.1 and 42.0 mV. The per-cell rheobases do not differ between groups (t test p = 0.732).

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • Step levels from the data descriptor; steps are added to the holding current, so the tonic current is excluded as the Table S1 footnote says.
    • The paper does not define rheobase or its spike threshold. The data descriptor names the CellData tool of SimTracker for the cells' intrinsic properties; its rheobase, "Least depolarized step with spikes" with spikes as crossings of -20 mV (tools/CellData.m), is run as a variant and gives the same per-cell values.

⊘Blocked3

  • ⊘Blocked· Data missing from the dataset

    figs1g-resting-potential

    By the criterion, sweeps with no current channel and a baseline at the holding level do not hold the resting potential. No PC file has a current channel, and every PC's pre-step baseline on the 0 pA sweep is within 2.6 mV of the -70 mV holding potential of the Methods (-72.2 to -67.4 mV; sPC -70.03, dPC -69.76 mV, t test p = 0.659). The Table S1 footnote says the tonic current that set this level was not recorded. The resting potential without that current is not in the data set, and no other public copy of these recordings was found.

  • ⊘Blocked· Too large to analyse

    fig4d-model-feedforward

    The claim needs 39 simulations of the network model (13 conditions in three networks, 5,000 ms each at 0.05 ms steps). The model's code is public (ModelDB 153280, mirrored on GitHub at the commit listed under inputs) and compiles and runs with NEURON 9.0.2. In the notebook a 20 ms run of one network on one core took 694 s, 509.9 s to build the cells and then 8.20 s per simulated millisecond; a 5 s run is so about 11.5 hours, and the 39 runs about 450 core-hours, far over the notebook's limit of 60 minutes and the run's time. The model's 75 job scripts each run a simulation on 64 cores (mpiexec -np 64), as the notebook prints. The simulation results, which the README says are available on request, are not public.

  • ⊘Blocked· Too large to analyse

    fig4ef-model-pvbc-rates

    Needs the same simulations as fig4d-model-feedforward, which are too large to run here (about 450 core-hours, estimated from the 20 ms run timed in the notebook).

Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingFor Lee et al. (2014) the data set holds only the current-step sweeps of 17 PCs and 6 PVBCs behind Figures S1E to S1H, S3F and Table S1. The paired recordings, optogenetics, imaging, anatomy and retrograde labelling behind the paper's main figures are not in it, and the paper names no other deposit; 26 of its 34 claims cannot be tested.
  • OtherPV34 is deposited twice, as PVBC_PV34.atf and PV34_PVBC_mouse.atf, with identical bytes. The descriptor lists 36 cells; the zip holds 37 files.
  • Description missingThe ATF files record only the membrane voltage, with no injected current and no holding current. The step levels can be taken only from the descriptor's table, and the voltage responses are consistent with them in sweep order. Because the PCs were held at -70 mV with an unrecorded current, their resting potential (Figure S1G, Table S1) cannot be measured.
  • Description missingNo file or the descriptor gives the animal, slice, sex, hippocampal region (septal or temporal) or soma distance from the Pyr/Rad border of a cell, so the sublayer assignment rests on the file names alone and results cannot be grouped by animal.
  • OtherIn PVBC PV37 the sweep order breaks between the sweeps the descriptor gives as -250 and -200 pA (steady-state deflection -8.7 then -9.1 mV; -13.1 mV at -300 pA); its other sweeps rise with the step as in every other cell.

Reproduction Intrinsic properties of superficial and deep CA1 pyramidal cells and PV+ basket cells (Lee et al. 2014)

Tests the findings of Lee, Marchionni, Bezaire, Varga, Danielson, Lovett-Barron, Losonczy and Soltesz (2014) that CRCNS hc-10 can test. The data set holds the current-step sweeps of 8 superficial and 9 deep CA1 pyramidal cells and 6 PV+ basket cells, behind Figures S1E to S1H, S3F and Table S1. It compares firing, input resistance, sag and rheobase between the sublayers and the firing of a basket cell. The paired recordings, anatomy, imaging and model behind the paper's main figures are not in it. All files
