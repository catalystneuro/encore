Two photon calcium imaging recordings of spontaneous activity from mouse somatosensory cortex in wild-type and Fmr1 knock-out mice from three developmental age groups.

The paper's calcium imaging findings held up in part in this data; its whole-cell, EEG and anesthesia findings could not be tested, as those recordings are not deposited. Correlation between nearby neurons fell with age in both genotypes and, pooled over ages, was higher in Fmr1 knockout mice, though the pooled means missed the paper's values. The genotype difference at P14-16, which the paper calls largest, missed significance (p = 0.057), and the larger share of knockout neurons joining peaks of synchrony was not found. The paper does not fully define these measures, and the deposited groups differ in size from its sample. Firing rates did not differ between genotypes, as reported.

Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Selection not documentedThe data set holds 13, 8 and 7 WT mice and 9, 10 and 6 Fmr1 -/- mice at P9-11, P14-16 and P30-40 (53), while the paper analysed 12, 9 and 8 and 9, 10 and 8 (56). Neither the descriptor nor the files say which mice differ from the paper's sample or why.
  • Metadata errorThe descriptor gives 40 to 149 neurons per animal; two WT P9-11 recordings have 164 and 198. It gives about 7 to 8 min per recording; at the stated 3.9 Hz the recordings last 3.8 to 15.4 min, and 27 of 53 are 2160 frames (9.2 min).
  • Description missingThe descriptor gives no unit for the estimated firing rates; their means fit Hz, and the values, which are continuous, do not show the unit. It does not say that the k-th matrix of each group belongs to Movie_k.tif, which the frame counts and the correlation of the population rate with each movie's brightness show.
  • OtherThe movie folders are named "4Hz" where the paper and the descriptor give 3.9 Hz, and most recordings are 2160 frames, three 3-min movies at 4 Hz. Frames are 128 x 256 pixels in 50 movies (and 357, 404 and 512 wide in one each), where the Methods give 512 x 128. The TIFFs carry no time stamps, so the frame rate cannot be checked.
  • Data missingNo cell contours or dF/F traces are deposited, so the paper's correlations cannot be computed from the filtered dF/F if that is what the paper used; only the estimated firing rates are given. The whole-cell recordings, the EEG, the isoflurane imaging and the 15.6 Hz imaging are not deposited, and 17 of the paper's 28 claims cannot be tested.
  • File format errorThe TIFF archive holds five macOS resource-fork files (._.DS_Store and ._Movie_1.tif) beside the 53 movies.
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the authors. Which trace and which measure give the pairwise correlation of Fig. 1 (the Methods refer to ref. 15 for "continuous-trace cross-correlations")? Was it the deconvolved trace deposited here or the filtered dF/F, was the mean subtracted, and at which lag? The zero-lag normalised cross-correlation of the deposited rates gives the paper's P14-16 values (WT 0.169, KO 0.204) but pooled means that differ from the paper's.
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the authors. For peaks of synchrony, when is a cell active in a frame, how was each trace shuffled, and how is the proportion of neurons participating in an event formed? With an active cell as any non-zero rate the frequencies match the paper's, but no reading gives proportions within the paper's s.e.m. (0.300 and 0.380). Q-a with R-b and the rates in Hz gives proportions below them (0.13 to 0.17), and every other reading gives proportions above them.
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the authors. How was the pooled average correlation (0.230 and 0.265) formed, and how was the unequal-variance 2 x 3 ANOVA fitted? The paper's s.e.m. fit an average of the three age means, not the mean over mice.

Reproduction Network synchrony in the developing barrel cortex of Fmr1 knockout mice (Goncalves et al. 2013)

Tests the calcium imaging findings of Goncalves, Anstey, Golshani and Portera-Cailliau (2013) on CRCNS data set ssc-8, which holds the estimated firing rates and cell-to-cell distances of L2/3 barrel cortex neurons in unanesthetized WT and Fmr1 knockout mice at three ages. It compares pairwise correlation between genotypes and ages, peaks of synchrony and firing rates. The whole-cell, EEG, isoflurane and 15.6 Hz data of the paper are not in the data set. All files
