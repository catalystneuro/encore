  • behavior-error-rate

    Results, paragraph 4 (after Figure 1); Supplemental Results 1.1

    How it will be judged

    Error rate per participant over the trials of the five runs, then the mean over participants. Whether time-outs count as errors is open (both led to feedback, Methods, Task; time-outs were 0.3% of trials, Supplemental Results 1.1, more than the tolerance); readings are errors only, and errors plus time-outs.
