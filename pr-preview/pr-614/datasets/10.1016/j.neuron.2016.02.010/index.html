  • wtrack-learning

    On the W-track all animals performed above chance by the third day of training and above 80% correct by the eighth and final day.

    All animals performed above chance by the third day of training, and performed >80% by the eighth and final day of training

    Results, Excitation and Inhibition of PFC Neurons during Awake Hippocampal SWRs, paragraph 1

    How it will be judged

    The paper gives no scoring procedure beyond the task rule (SEP, Behavioral Tasks) and the cited Jadhav et al. (2012). Trials are well-to-well visits read from position; an inbound trial (to the center after a side well) is correct when the animal returns to the center; an outbound trial (from the center) is correct when it goes to the side opposite the previous side visit. Chance is not defined in the paper; readings are (a) 50% correct over all trials of the day, (b) a binomial test of the day's correct count against 0.5 at p < 0.05. Reproduced when every animal is above chance under one reading on day 3 and above 0.8 on day 8. A well visit is a stop within the reward-well zone; its radius is not given, and the author states the one used.
