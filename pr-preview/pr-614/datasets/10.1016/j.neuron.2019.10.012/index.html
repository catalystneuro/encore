    How it will be judged

  • fig4b-pre-post-unchanged

    The co-activation of LS neuron pairs during HFOs (excess correlation) is not modulated by waking experience on a familiar maze (PRE against POST, rank-sum P = 0.36).

    It also suggested that the degree of co-activation observed is not modulated by waking experience in a familiar environment (Figure 4B; rank-sum test; P = 0.36; PRE vs POST)

    Results, third paragraph; Figure 4B

    How it will be judged

    A claim of absence, judged on the paper's test, a rank-sum test of PRE against POST excess correlations over all pairs, at p < 0.05. Pairs are LS neurons on different shanks in recordings with all three epochs. CCGs use 1 ms bins of the spikes inside LS HFOs (PRE, POST) or inside trials (BEHAV), normalized by the square root of the product of the spike counts, over +-400 ms, smoothed with Gaussian kernels of SD 5 ms and 200 ms (each divided by the kernel mass inside the window); the excess correlation is the difference of the two at zero lag. The jittered value moves every spike by a uniform +-200 ms before the CCG. A pair is kept when its CCG holds more than 5 spike pairs within +-200 ms in each epoch. Reproduced when the test finds no difference, not reproduced when it does; the effect size is reported either way.

    How it will be judged

    The paper does not state its model. Fixed here, for LS neurons in recordings with hippocampal units and at least 100 CA1 ripples, and with at least 20 spikes inside ripples in total.
