  • fig4d-decoding

    Position decoded from the small subset of spatially selective interneurons was about as accurate as from all interneurons, and more accurate than from as many randomly chosen interneurons or with position shuffled.

    Decoding accuracy originates mainly from spatially modulated cells, although they represent only a small fraction of all recorded interneurons

    Figure 4D

    How it will be judged

    Per session, a linear SVM decodes the position bin from lap-wise tuning curves, leaving one lap out. The selected subset is the cells with a positive field under the peak rule where a session has at least 5 of them, and otherwise without the peak rule. The claim holds in a session if the error from the subset is at most 1.25 times the error from all ROIs, below the mean error of 10 random subsets of the same size, and below the error with dF/F shifted circularly against position. It holds overall if it holds in at least two of the three sessions.

  • s6-later-lower-above-chance

    Tuning curve correlations of the same cells after 1 hour and after 24 hours were lower than within-session correlations, and still higher than chance.

    Tuning curve correlations after both 1 hour and 24 hours were significantly lower than the within-session correlations across all IN subtypes ... correlations at later time points (1h and 24h) were still significantly higher than chance levels

    Results, Spatially selective activity dynamics of CA1 INs during head-fixed spatial navigation, paragraph 2 (Figure S6I)

    How it will be judged

    The same-day pair of sessions stands in for 1 hour and the pairs two days apart for 24 hours. The ROI with the same index is taken as the same cell, as the dandiset describes. Lower is a paired Wilcoxon test against the within-session correlation of the earlier session at p < 0.05. Chance is the mean correlation with cell identities shuffled; two readings are allowed, shuffling among all ROIs and shuffling within subtype. Above chance is p < 0.05 over 1000 shuffles. Both are tested on the ROIs in the six subtypes, and must hold for the same-day pair and for both two-day pairs.
