  • fig5-assemblies

    Spike trains of hippocampal neurons predicted LS firing in 18.5% (2,087 of 11,264) of hippocampal-LS pairs, with a median optimal timescale of 25 ms.

    Across 18.5% (2,087/11,264) of hippocampal-LS cell pairs examined, spike trains of hippocampal neurons reliably predicted the firing of action potentials in LS neurons ... the median optimal timescale for peer prediction was 25 ms (Figure 5F)

    Results, paragraph 7; Figure 5D to 5F

    How it will be judged

    Tested on a random sample of pairs, as all pairs do not fit the time limit. A pair is a hippocampal and an LS unit both active (1.5 spikes per trial) in the same condition, taken in that condition; each is kept with probability 0.01 (seeded). Spikes are counted in 1 ms bins inside the condition's trials. A Poisson GLM predicts the LS unit's counts from theta phase (sine, cosine and angle), position bin, speed and the hippocampal unit's spike train smoothed by a Gaussian of SD w, for w in 1, 2, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 40, 50, 75, 100 and 150 ms. The best w has the least deviance. Assembly strength is the reduction in deviance at the best w from the model without the hippocampal train, divided by the largest reduction over 10 shuffles of the hippocampal train's trial order (no trial kept with its own) at that w; an assembly has strength of at least 4. Reproduced when the 95% binomial interval of the fraction of assemblies overlaps 0.185 plus or minus 0.0185 and the median best w of the assemblies is within tolerance; partially reproduced when one of the two holds.
