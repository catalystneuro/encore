  • beh-slopes

    Regression slopes of tp on ts are positive and less than one in every condition, so the animals estimated ts and their responses were biased toward the mean of the prior.

    The regression slopes were positive, indicating that animals were able to estimate ts, and were less than unity, indicating that responses were biased toward to the mean of the prior (Figure 1E and S1, Table S1).

    Results, Task and behavior, paragraph 3; Figure 1E and S1; Table S1

    How it will be judged

    For each prior, effector and direction (8 conditions), a weighted linear regression of tp on ts with each trial weighted by 1 over the variance of tp at its ts. Reproduced when all 8 slopes lie between 0 and 1 with 95% confidence intervals that exclude 0 and 1; partially reproduced when all 8 slopes lie between 0 and 1 but some interval includes 0 or 1; not reproduced otherwise.

  • beh-overlap-prior

    The effect of the prior was most conspicuous at the overlapping ts for which biases were in opposite directions for the two prior conditions

    Results, Task and behavior, paragraph 3; Figure 1E; Table S2

The paper's p value pools 17 sessions and is not expected here; the p value of this session is reported, and the test is shown per effector and direction.
