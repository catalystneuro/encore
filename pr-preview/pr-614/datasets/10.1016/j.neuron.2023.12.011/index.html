  • fig1c-isosbestic-control

    In a D1-cre mouse expressing GCaMP7f in D1 spiny projection neurons, fluorescence under 470 nm excitation shows Ca2+ transients across fibers that are absent under 405 nm (isosbestic) excitation.

    470 nm excitation light (green trace) was alternated with 405nm light (black) at 18hz to provide a quasi-simultaneous isosbestic control. Significant transients are absent with 405 illumination

    Figure 1C; Results, Chronic measurements of large-scale calcium and neuromodulator signals in head-fixed mice, paragraph 1

    How it will be judged

    Mouse 842, session 2022-11-23 (the session the paper's Fig01/C code reads), the 61 fibers flagged included. For each fiber, the standard deviation of DF/F under 470 nm divided by that under 405 nm. Reproduced when the ratio exceeds 1 in at least 90% of fibers and its median is at least 2; partially reproduced when the median is above 1 but either condition fails; not reproduced otherwise. The other session of the mouse (2022-11-18) is a variant.
