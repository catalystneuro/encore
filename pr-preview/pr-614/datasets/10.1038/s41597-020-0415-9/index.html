  • counts-neurons-subjects

    The dataset holds 1863 single neurons recorded from the medial temporal lobes of 59 subjects.

    we present a NWB-formatted dataset of 1863 single neurons recorded from the medial temporal lobes of 59 human subjects

    Abstract; Technical Validation, proportion of selective cells, paragraph 3

    How it will be judged

    Neurons are the rows of the units tables over all files, subjects the distinct subject_id values. The readings are (a) every unit row of every file, as the paper counts neurons per session, and (b) units whose spike trains are deposited in two files counted once. Reproduced when both values are within tolerance under one reading. The exact differences are reported.

  • task-structure

    In the encoding phase, subjects were presented with 100 novel images ... in the recognition phase, subjects were presented with 50 “novel” images and 50 “old” images ... (there are 5 different categories in each experiment)

    Methods, Task, paragraph 1; Data Records, stimuli group, paragraph 1; Technical Validation, proportion of selective cells, paragraph 2

    How it will be judged

    Per session, counted from the trials table and the stimulus images. Reproduced when every session has 100 encoding trials with 100 distinct image files, 100 recognition trials with 50 of each label, and 5 categories; partially reproduced when each part holds in more than half of the sessions; not reproduced otherwise.

  • new-old-label-coding

    In the trials table, new_old_labels_recog is 0 for old stimuli (seen during encoding) and 1 for new stimuli.

    New_old_labels_recog provides the ground truth label of whether the trial showed a new or old stimulus during the recognition phase (0 is old, 1 is new).

    Data Records, interval group, paragraph 1

    How it will be judged

    Two tests per session. (1) Under the stated coding, old trials (label 0) should show image files from the session's encoding block and new trials (label 1) should not. (2) Old trials should receive "old" responses (4 to 6) more often than new trials. Reproduced when both hold in every session; not reproduced when, in most sessions, both hold with the labels reversed.

  • isi-criterion

  • fig5b-roc-asymmetric

    The ROC curve is asymmetric (p < 0.05), as expected for declarative memory.

    Also, the shape of the ROC curve was asymmetric (Fig. 5b, p < 0.05), as expected for declarative memories

    Technical Validation, behaviour, paragraph 1; Figure 5b

    How it will be judged

    The paper does not name the measure or the test. Asymmetry is measured, as in the paper's code (calcZROCSlope), by the slope of the z-ROC, the regression of z(hit rate) on z(false alarm rate) over the five interior points of each session. Reproduced when the mean slope over sessions is below 1 with a two-sided one-sample t-test against 1 at p < 0.05. The Wilcoxon signed-rank test and the slope of the pooled ROC are variants.

  • fig5d-confidence-accuracy

    Accuracy is significantly higher in high than in low confidence trials (P < 0.001, paired t test) across all sessions.

    Accuracy was significantly larger in high compared to low confidence trials (P < 0.001) (Fig. 5d) across all sessions.

    Technical Validation, behaviour, paragraph 1; Figure 5d

    How it will be judged

    Per session, the percentage of correct responses among high and among low confidence responses. The readings of the split are (a) the fixed split of the paper's MATLAB code (high is 1 or 6, low is 2 to 5) and (b) the dynamic split of its Python code. Sessions without responses in one group are left out and counted. Reproduced when, under one reading, high exceeds low with a paired t-test p < 0.001. The unpaired test the code runs is a variant.
