  • fig2a-order-accuracy

    In the time discrimination task, subjects chose the frame that was shown first in 73% of trials when the two frames were separated by no boundary or by a soft boundary, and in 53% of trials, significantly fewer, when they were separated by a hard boundary.

    subjects correctly identified which frame was shown first in 73 ± 7% and 73 ± 8% of trials when the two frames were separated by no boundary (NB) or a soft boundary (SB) ... subjects performed significantly worse when discriminating the order of frames separated by a hard boundary (HB) (Fig. 2a, red, HB: 53 ± 5%

    How it will be judged

    Besides the expected value, accuracy with no boundary and with a soft boundary must each be within 0.03 of 0.73, and accuracy across a hard boundary must be lower than both in paired tests over subjects (p < 0.05).
