  • recordings-protocol

    Activity was recorded from 113 WT-background animals, each for up to 40 min, while a neuron was stimulated once every 30 s with a 0.5 s train of light pulses.

    We recorded activity from 113 wild-type (WT)-background animals, each for up to 40 min, while stimulating a mostly randomly selected sequence of neurons one by one every 30 s ... a 500-ms (300-ms for the unc-31-mutant strain) train of light pulses was used to optogenetically stimulate that neuron

    Results, "Population imaging and single-cell activation", paragraph 1; Methods, "Stimulus delivery and pulsed laser", paragraph 3

    How it will be judged

    Reproduced when 113 recordings hold both calcium traces and a stimulation table, the median interval between stimulations is within 10% of 30 s, and at least 90% of recordings last no more than 44 min (40 min plus 10%). Partially reproduced when the interval and duration conditions hold and between 100 and 112 recordings hold traces and stimulations. The stated 0.5 s duration is reported, with whether the files could show any other.

  • pairs-measured

    Responses to stimulation were imaged for 23,433 pairs of neurons (66% of all possible pairs in the head), from 186 of the 188 head neurons, and some pairs were observed as many as 59 times.

    We imaged activity in response to stimulation for 23,433 pairs of neurons (66% of all possible pairs in the head). Measured pairs were imaged at least once, and some as many as 59 times ... This includes activity from 186 of 188 neurons in the head

    Results, "Signal propagation map", paragraph 1; Extended Data Fig. 4a

    How it will be judged

    An observation of pair (i, j) is a stimulation of the neuron labelled j that evoked an autoresponse, while an ROI labelled i had a usable trace (5% NaN rule and a nonzero local standard deviation); both labels must be among the 188 head neurons. The paper's text allows these readings, (a) pairs including i = j (the Source Data count of 23,433 includes the diagonal), (b) pairs with i different from j, (c) as (a) without requiring the autoresponse. Reproduced when under one reading all three values are within tolerance; partially reproduced when one or two are. Counts per animal are reported.
