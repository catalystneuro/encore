  • source-data-link

    The dandiset's recordings are those behind the paper's Figure 6 and Supplementary Figures 7 and 8, and the paper's published per-stimulation traces, evoked integrals and spontaneous transient integrals can be recomputed from them.

    Raw tubular Data generated in this study from all experiments, and uncropped western blot images with metadata files in this study are available in source data file via the open-access option on the Zenodo repository (https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19930567).

    Figure 6C to 6F, Supplementary Figures 7B, 7C, 8C to 8E; Source Data (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.19930567); Data availability

    How it will be judged

    Records are matched by mouse ID (the number in the dandiset's subject_id against id_animal), group (WT or GS), labelled power and the rank of the stimulation within its power. Four parts are scored. (1) Every mouse in the source data is in the dandiset with the same genotype and Cre line. (2) Each triggered trace (Figure 6C, Supplementary 7B and 8D) equals the dandiset's dF/F from 1 s before the matched onset to 1.5 s after it (largest absolute difference below 1e-6). (3) Each published evoked integral (Figure 6E, Supplementary 7C and 8E) equals the code's measure (samples 91 to 200 of that trace minus the mean of samples 1 to 90, trapezoid rule in samples) up to one scale factor common to all, reported, within a relative difference of 1e-4. (4) Per mouse, the number of spontaneous transients and their sorted integrals (Figure 6F, Supplementary 8C) equal those computed here, up to the same kind of common factor, under either the Methods' exclusion window or the window the code computes as written; which one matches is reported. Reproduced when each of the four parts matches for at least 95% of its records; partially reproduced when every part matches for at least 50%; not reproduced otherwise. Whether the figures use the power their legends state is reported.

    Each G2019S mouse's eight 0.5 mW integrals against all WT integrals, significant when the mouse's mean is lower and p < 0.05/8. The test is stated as Welch's in the Methods and the legend's asterisks, the legend's p-values are labelled one-tailed two-sample t-tests, and the code runs MATLAB's ttest2 (Student, two-tailed). Reproduced when, under one reading, 7 of 8 are significant; partially reproduced when the best reading gives 5, 6 or 8; not reproduced when it gives 4 or fewer. Whether 3743 is the exception is reported.
