From the paper Individual variability of neural computations underlying flexible decisions · claims file · not yet checked

  • session-link

    The dandiset's sessions can be linked to the paper's published per-session behavioural tables, and the linked records agree on trial counts, outcomes, evidence and pulse timing.

    Rat behavioural and electrophysiological data are available at https://github.com/Brody-Lab/flexible_decision_making_rats.

    Data availability; the paper's data/Sessions_trials at GitHub commit 0ea8fdb

    How it will be judged

    Records are matched by rat and session name (protocol, date and letter, taken from the source file names in the paper's tables). The link holds (reproduced) when at least 95% of the paper's sessions that have valid trials are found in the dandiset; in at least 95% of matched sessions the number of valid trials and the fraction correct are equal; and in a seeded sample of 20 sessions that pass the selection, the per-trial context, correct side, evidence strengths, choice and position in block agree on at least 99% of valid trials and the pulse counts in the code's 10 ms bins agree on at least 99% of trial bins. It is partially reproduced when the lowest of these fractions is between 80% and the threshold, and not reproduced below 80%. The per-rat numbers of trials passing the selection are compared with the paper's table numsessions_all_rats.mat, and the sessions in one source only are listed.

  • ed1e-switch-four-trials

    Rats switch the feature they attend to within about four trials of a new context block.

    were able to switch between selected stimulus features within four trials of a new context block

    Results, Flexible evidence accumulation in rats, paragraph 1; Extended Data Figure 1e (legend panel g)

    How it will be judged

    As in compute_taskswitch.m, per rat, a logistic regression of choice on the two generative strengths for the valid trials at each position k (counting all trials since the switch) of the new block, k from 1 to 30, pooled over sessions that pass the selection (main) or the code's looser selection (fraction correct and index at least 0.65; variant); relative weights are averaged over rats with a fit. The plateau is the mean relative weight of the new block's relevant feature over positions 10 to 30. Readings are "within four trials" (position 4) and "within 4 to 5 trials" (position 5). Reproduced when, under either reading, the across-rat mean relative weight of the relevant feature at that position is at least 90% of its plateau for both switch directions; partially reproduced when for one. The weight at position 1 is reported.

  • ed4-bilateral-fof-impairs

    Bilateral optogenetic silencing of the frontal orienting fields during the stimulus impairs performance of the task (three rats).

    bilateral optogenetic silencing of rat FOF demonstrated that they are required for accurate performance of the task

    Results, Flexible evidence accumulation in rats, paragraph 2; Extended Data Figure 4g

    How it will be judged

    Opto sessions are those with an opto_epochs table and at least 100 valid trials. An epoch belongs to the trial whose start and stop contain its start. Per rat, the fraction correct on valid trials with a bilateral full-trial epoch is compared with valid trials with no epoch in the same sessions, two-sided Fisher exact test. Readings of which rats are meant, as the paper does not name them, are (a) the three with the most opto sessions and (b) all rats with opto sessions; the epoch type reading is (i) full trial, as the legend says, or (ii) any bilateral epoch. Reproduced when, under a reading, accuracy is lower with p < 0.05 in every rat of that reading; partially reproduced when it is in at least one.

  • choice-axes-parallel

    Choice axes estimated separately in the two contexts are essentially parallel (average angle 1.6 degrees, not different from 0 in 6 of 7 rats).

    we found that the choice axes, estimated separately for each of the two contexts, were essentially parallel (average angle between contexts = 1.6°; not significantly different from 0 (P > 0.1) for 6 out of 7 rats

    Results, Flexible evidence accumulation in rats, paragraph 3; Extended Data Figure 5

    How it will be judged

    As ExtDataFigure5ag.m, per rat the first principal component of the choice kernels (from 0 s, 66 time points) of the paper's per-neuron fits in each context; the two are projected on the first 3 principal components of both contexts' choice kernels from 0 s (each divided by its matrix norm, stacked), and the angle between the projections is folded to at most 90 degrees, the value the code plots. The average over the 7 rats decides the outcome. The paper's test of each angle against 0, a permutation test over shuffled context labels described in the Methods, is not in its code and needs the per-neuron kernels refitted for every shuffle, so it is not run; the angle in the full space, which the code computes first and overwrites, is shown as a variant. (Corrected after the first verification round. The criterion first described only that first angle, unfolded, as the code's; both readings are outside the tolerance. After the second round the sentence on the test against 0 was corrected; it first said the Methods do not describe the test.)
