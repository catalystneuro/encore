    How it will be judged

    A session is a processed (behavior+ecephys) file; its duration is the end of its last sleep-state or ripple interval (the files' clocks start at 0). The paper gives two session counts, 92 (Methods) and 96 (the per-birthdate session counts of Figure 1e, and the Figure 7f legend); the count is judged under either reading, within 10%. The range holds when every duration lies within 113.7 to 475.6 min widened by 10% of each bound (102.3 to 523.2 min). Reproduced when the count (either reading), the median and the range all hold. Partially reproduced when the durations hold and the count does not. Not reproduced when the durations do not hold. Each session must also hold sleep states including non-REM sleep.
