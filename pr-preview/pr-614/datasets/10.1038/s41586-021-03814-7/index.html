  • label-link

    The dandiset's behaviour labels can be named through the paper's code; "Mom agressing" is shepherding, "Virgin in rest" is nest entry (a nest dwell) and "Mom retrieval" is spontaneous pup retrieval by the dam.

    Distribution of durations of time in nest for virgins after entry events. ~70% of all nest dwell times are ≤40 s

    Methods (Spike train analysis, paragraph 1); Extended Data Fig. 7b; shepNestPup_xCorr_ForNature.m at GitHub commit 2db2a75

    How it will be judged

    Records are matched by label text; a label that differs only in spelling ("Mom aggressing") is the same label. The link holds (reproduced) when (1) in the one file that carries both the code's labels and the paper's names (V116 03122018), every "Mom agressing" interval coincides with a "Shepherding" interval and every "Mom retrieval" interval with a "Self gen retrieval" interval (start and stop within 0.5 s), and (2) the fraction of "Virgin in rest" intervals lasting 40 s or less, pooled over all files that have the label, is within the tolerance of 70.5%. Partially reproduced when only one of (1) and (2) holds; not reproduced when neither does.

  • fig2j-shepherded-entry

    PVN units fired more when the virgin entered the nest after being shepherded than when she entered it voluntarily (18 units, Wilcoxon matched-pairs signed rank test, p = 0.0056).

    Spiking rate when virgin entered nest after shepherding (red) or voluntarily (grey) ... shepherding vs voluntary entry, p=0.005, Wilcoxon signed-rank test

    Fig. 2j; Extended Data Fig. 7a; Source Data Fig. 2j

    How it will be judged

    A nest entry ("Virgin in rest" start) is shepherded when it starts between the start of a "Mom agressing" episode and 10 s after its end, and voluntary otherwise; the paper does not give a rule. Each unit's change during nest entry (method of the file header, 40 s cap) is averaged separately over shepherded and voluntary entries. Units are all units of the sessions with at least one entry of each kind and a defined value for both. Reproduced when the shepherded value exceeds the voluntary one in the median over units and a two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test gives p < 0.05; not reproduced otherwise. Variants, which do not change the outcome; windows of 5 and 30 s, and the units activated in ed7a-nest-entry-fraction.
