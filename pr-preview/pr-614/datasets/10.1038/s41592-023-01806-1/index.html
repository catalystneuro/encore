  • fig2-quadrant-direction-decoding

    Linear SVM decoders trained on half of the data decode the mouse's combined quadrant and movement direction on the other half at an accuracy at least three times chance, above that of decoders trained on shuffled data.

    We trained a support vector machine (SVM) model on half of the data and used the other half to decode the mouse's combined position and movement direction, with the resulting accuracy at least three times chance level (Fig. 2j, P < 0.001, Monte Carlo sampling of n = 1,000 shuffled datasets).

    Main text, page 549, paragraph 1; Fig. 2i and 2j; Methods, Data analysis, paragraphs 4 and 5

    How it will be judged

    Eight classes, the four quadrants by two directions (Fig. 2i), and the common method above with lambda 3.2e-7. The decoding matrix is the average of 200 fits (quadrant_direction_decoding.m). The null is 1,000 decoders trained on their own shuffles (Methods, paragraph 5), each giving a median accuracy; p is the fraction of null values at or above the real median accuracy. The code holds no null for this panel, so how many fits make one null decoder is open. The readings are (n1) one fit per shuffle, (n2) the average matrix of 200 fits per shuffle, as for the real matrix (subset_sweep.m averages its shuffled decoders over the same number of fits as its real ones). The p-value is judged under both; when they disagree on p < 0.001, the claim is methods_underspecified. Chance is 1/8 (the code sets white at 1/Nclass). Which accuracy must reach three times chance is open. The readings are (a) the median of the diagonal at least 0.375, (b) every diagonal element at least 0.375. Reproduced when p < 0.001 and the accuracy holds under a reading; partially reproduced when p < 0.001 and the median of the diagonal exceeds 1/8 but neither reading holds.

  • ed8-coactive-distributed

    The correlated activity in each NMF component is driven by footprints spread across the field of view.

    Main text, page 549, paragraph 1; Extended Data Fig. 8; Methods, Data analysis, paragraph 1

    How it will be judged

    The traces of the curated footprints (Fig. 2h; extract_curated.mat in the code) are factored by NMF into eight components (Methods), with multiplicative updates, 4 replicates and tolerances 1e-7 (ridgeline_nmf_grouping.m); each footprint goes to the component in which its weight ranks highest among footprints (same script). Footprints of a component are active in its event when their maximum within 25 s of the event time exceeds half their maximum over the recording (single_component_raster.m). The event times are open. The code lists eight times (820, 770, 990, 700, 400, 420, 1000 and 410 s) for components whose numbering a new NMF run does not reproduce. The readings are (a) the time of each component's largest temporal weight, (b) the code's eight times, each assigned one to one to the component with the largest temporal weights at those times. "From across the FOV" has no measure in the paper, and none is in the code. The plain reading used is that each event's active footprints lie in all four quarters of the field of view (split at the midlines of the extent of all footprint positions, taken as for fig2l-local-decoding-map). Reproduced when every component's event meets it under both event readings; not reproduced when some event fails it under both; otherwise methods_underspecified. The maps of active footprints (the code's Extended Data Fig. 8b, footprints thresholded at 10% of their maximum) and their counts are shown.

  • ed10-localized-activity

    Some footprints have activity localized to subregions of the maze.

    An examination of individual neural firing rate maps yielded footprints whose activity was localized to subregions of the maze (Extended Data Fig. 10).

    Main text, page 549, paragraph 1; Extended Data Fig. 10

    How it will be judged

    The paper selected footprints by eye "for large-scale structure in their receptive fields" (Extended Data Fig. 10 legend), a rule that cannot be redone. The maps are as in place_cells.m. Frames with speed above its 80th percentile; a footprint is active in a frame when its trace exceeds 1% of its maximum; the plotted value is the fraction of time active at each location, smoothed with a Gaussian of 10 video pixels. The code also computes, per footprint, the entropy of its mean activity over 100 bins of linear position between the 1st and 99th percentiles, and sorts footprints by the peak of the smoothed map. Neither the paper nor the code sets a threshold or a test for "localized", and the paper's selection by eye cannot be redone, so the claim cannot be judged by a method the paper fixes (methods_underspecified). The maps and the entropy and peak of every footprint are reported, with the code's 12 selected footprints (ifig in place_cells.m, indices into the curated set) when the data's footprint order can be matched to the curated set.
