    Results, paragraph 2; Methods, Identifying cue-responsive neurons

    How it will be judged

    As the Methods give it. For each neuron, cue (danger, uncertainty with shock and omission trials pooled, safety) and window (first 1 s, last 5 s of cue), a paired two-tailed t-test across trials of the window's mean rate (Hz) against the mean rate in the 10 s before that trial's cue onset; a neuron is cue-responsive when any of the six tests has p < 0.05, with no correction (the Methods say Bonferroni was not applied).
