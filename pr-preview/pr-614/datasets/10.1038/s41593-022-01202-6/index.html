  • allen-parcel-link

    The paper's Allen parcel masks, from its code, can be laid on the dandiset's ΔF/F pixels, so that the areas the paper names can be found in the data.

    How it will be judged

    The pixels with non-zero ΔF/F in the blue and green ΔF/F movies of every session used must equal the code's brainMask.mat under exactly one of the eight rotations and reflections (agreement of at least 99.9% of the 65536 pixels, all others below 99%). In one visual stimulus session per mouse, the calcium response to 100% contrast gratings (mean ΔF/F 0 to 1 s after onset minus -2 to 0 s, averaged over trials) is then computed for each of the 46 parcels. The code takes the left VISp parcel as the visually driven one. Reproduced when the mask match holds and left VISp has the largest response of the 46 parcels in at least 3 of 4 mice, with left VISp above right VISp in all 4. Partially reproduced when the mask match holds and the largest response is in a left visual parcel in at least 3 mice. Not reproduced otherwise.

  • fig2a-r2-differs-across-areas

    The variance of ACh3.0 and jRCaMP1b signals explained by a ridge regression on FaceMap PCs 1 to 25, locomotion speed and pupil area differs across cortical areas (Friedman's ANOVA p < 0.001 for both, n = 6 mice).

    For ACh3.0 and jRCaMp1b, the variance explained ... using this full model differed significantly across cortical areas

    Results, Calcium and cholinergic signals encode behavioral state, paragraph 1; Figure 2a,b

    How it will be judged

    Ten-fold cross-validated R2 (ten contiguous blocks) of a ridge model with the penalty chosen per parcel by marginal likelihood, as ridgeMML does, fitted per session on the 23 left parcels and averaged over each mouse's sessions. Friedman's test across the 23 parcels with mice as blocks. Reproduced when p < 0.05 for both signals; partially reproduced when for one. With 4 mice the paper's p < 0.001 is not required.

Reproduced when ACh has rho < 0 with p < 0.05 and calcium rho > 0 with p < 0.05; partially reproduced when one of the two holds; not reproduced otherwise.

  • fig2c-face-best-predictor-ach

    For ACh3.0, facial movement (FaceMap PC1) predicts activity better than locomotion or pupil, in single-variable models (Friedman p = 0.006, face against locomotion and against pupil p = 0.031) and as unique contribution (Friedman p = 0.003).

    Facial movements yielded the strongest prediction accuracy compared to locomotion and pupil variation for Ach 3.0

    Results, Calcium and cholinergic signals encode behavioral state, paragraph 1; Figure 2c

    How it will be judged

    Single-variable cross-validated R2 (face PC1, pupil area, wheel speed) averaged over the 23 left parcels, with negative values set to 0 as in the code, per mouse. Reproduced when Friedman's test across the three variables gives p < 0.05 and face PC1 has the highest R2 in every mouse. Partially reproduced when face PC1 has the highest mean R2 over mice but not in every mouse, or Friedman p >= 0.05. Not reproduced when face PC1 does not have the highest mean. The unique contributions (full model R2 minus the model with that variable shuffled in time; for face, PC1 is shuffled) are reported with the same rule and do not change the outcome.

  • s7-face-onset-transients

    Facial movement onset without locomotion is associated with significant whole-cortex transients in ACh (0.64 ± 0.09 ΔF/F, p = 0.001) and calcium (0.69 ± 0.15 ΔF/F, p = 0.006).

    Facial movement onset (quantified using FaceMap PC1) in the absence of locomotion was associated with desynchronization of local neuronal firing as estimated by ECoG and significant whole cortex-averaged transients in both ACh ... and calcium ... signals

    Results, Calcium and cholinergic signals encode behavioral state, paragraph 2; Figure S7

    How it will be judged

    Tolerances are the paper's stated spread (SEM over 6 mice). Face onsets as in the code (a high face state during sustained quiescence preceded by at least 4 s of low face state ending at most 0.5 s before). Per trial the mean of -4 to -2 s is subtracted; trials are averaged per mouse over sessions; the peak in 0 to 1 s is taken per parcel and averaged over the 23 left parcels. Reproduced when both means are within tolerance and both are above 0 by a one-sample t-test at p < 0.05 over mice. Partially reproduced when both are above 0 at p < 0.05, or positive in every mouse, but a mean is outside tolerance. Not reproduced otherwise.

  • fig3c-ach-sustained-face

    Results, Calcium and cholinergic signals encode behavioral state, paragraph 3; Figure 3c

    How it will be judged

Sustained states and epoch matching as in the code's SustainedStatesCorrelation.m; per epoch the mean ΔF/F, averaged over epochs and sessions per mouse, then over the 23 left parcels. Reproduced when both means are within tolerance and high exceeds low with LSD p < 0.05. Partially reproduced when high exceeds low in every mouse but a mean is outside tolerance or p >= 0.05. Not reproduced otherwise.
