  • fig2b-phc-scene-cuts

    Few parahippocampal neurons responded to scene cuts (25 of 373, 6.70%), but the modulation was consistent across the responsive neurons (cluster permutation test, p = 0.018).

    Comparatively few parahippocampal neurons responded to Scene Cuts (25/373, 6.70%), although there was nonetheless a consistent pattern of modulation across responsive neurons (p = 0.018, cluster permutation test)

    Results, Stimulus-aligned responsive neurons, paragraph 1; Figure 2b; Table S1

    How it will be judged

    The tolerance is the 95% binomial interval of a proportion of 6.70% among the paper's 373 units (1.96 times the standard error); 10% of the value (0.67 points) is smaller than the sampling error of a count of 25. Readings alpha 0.001 and 0.01. The cluster permutation test as in the Methods (z-scored trial-averaged rate per unit in 100 ms bins from -1000 to 1000 ms, two-sided independent t-test per bin between responsive and non-responsive units, clusters of adjacent bins with p < 0.005, summed absolute t, 1000 permutations of the unit labels, p of a cluster the fraction of permutations whose largest cluster sum exceeds it). Reproduced when, under one reading, the fraction is within tolerance and the largest cluster has p < 0.05; partially reproduced when one of the two holds; not reproduced otherwise.

  • fig3b-single-neuron-threshold

    Thresholding the activity of single neurons does not reliably predict whether Summer is on screen; most neurons perform near chance, below the population decoder.

    Decoding performances obtained by simply applying a threshold to single-neuron activity did not reliably predict the content, as the majority of neurons in the population performed near chance level (Fig. 3b). ... surpassing the performances achieved by individual neurons alone.

    Results, Decoding from neuronal activity, paragraph 1; Figure 3b; Methods, Single-neuron decoding performance

    How it will be judged

    For each unit, the summed spike count from 800 ms before to 800 ms after each sample; on each fold's validation set the threshold and its direction (above or below) that maximise kappa among up to 50 quantiles of the counts; kappa of that rule on the test set, averaged over the five folds. Reproduced when the median unit's kappa is below 0.05 and no unit reaches the paper's population kappa for Summer (0.31); partially reproduced when one of the two holds; not reproduced otherwise.
