Several main findings held up in this data, one did not, and most could not be tested. Hippocampus-amygdala reactivation in post-run NREM held when all cells were used (13 of 15 sessions). So did the lack of central nuclei reactivation and the higher BLA pyramidal firing in REM and NREM sleep, which rests on one rat. Ripple-peak reactivation was only partly tested, and unchanged REM firing in BLA interneurons did not hold (p = 0.020). The pyramidal-pair reactivation and ripple-subgroup findings could not be tested because the dataset lacks putative cell types. The air puff learning findings could not be tested because it lacks position tracking.

    habituation-over-days

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Description missingNew in this attempt. Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • BLA is the bla label (case ignored); units 0 and 1 of the files with a wrong spike index are left out.ripple-modulated-cells-larger-reactivation reactivation-peaks-at-ripples direction-all-cell-pairs
  • The dandiset does not say which of Rat08 to Rat11 are the paper's rats 1 to 4. Rat09, the rat without hippocampal units, is taken as rat 2 (whose hippocampal probe failed) and the others in order; this is an assumption.unit-counts
  • Units are counted as stored, one per session, including units 0 and 1 of the ten files whose spike index is wrong; location counts use the dandiset's labels read as the paper's structures.unit-counts
  • Laps are O1 reading c, the intervals between consecutive rewards at opposite ends with both rewards inside the run. Reading a has no lap intervals in the dandiset (its trials table is not laps) and reading b needs position, which it lacks. As answered, the claim is judged on reading c alone; the hand-picked 150-cm window of ClassifyLaps.m is not reproduced by any reading. A reading c lap runs from reward to reward, so it includes the time on the starting platform and any stop, which the M8 laps leave out; the count of laps is the same either way.habituation-over-days
  • The air puff direction of a day is the lap direction holding most puffs (1153 of 1197 puffs fall in run laps of that direction); the dandiset gives no direction. Rat08-20130709 has no puff inside a run lap and has no air puff count.habituation-over-days
  • Training day is the rank of the recording date within each rat, over the 40 files; the paper's 55 behaviour sessions include days the dandiset does not hold.habituation-over-days
  • Cells are the units the dandiset's cell_type column marks excitatory, read as the paper's monosynaptically identified pyramidal cells (O4 reading a). The column exists in five Rat08 files only, so the test uses one rat and about a fifth of the paper's cells. Reading b (all putative pyramidal cells) cannot be formed; as answered, it is not needed for the outcome.bla-pyr-sleep-firing-increase
  • Units 0 and 1 of files with a wrong spike index are left out (Rat08-20130710 and 20130711).bla-pyr-sleep-firing-increase
  • Cells are the units marked inhibitory in the five typed Rat08 files, read as the paper's identified interneurons (O4 reading a, which as answered decides alone); reading b cannot be formed.bla-int-sleep-firing
  • BLA is the bla label (case ignored); the wider amygdala labels are a variant.reactivation-all-cell-pairs
  • Units 0 and 1 of the files with a wrong spike index are left out.reactivation-all-cell-pairs
  • A 50-ms bin belongs to an epoch when its centre does, a standard choice.reactivation-all-cell-pairs
  • Central nuclei are the cen label. Units 0 and 1 of files with a wrong spike index are left out.cen-no-reactivation
  • Only the all-cell test is run; the pyramidal-pair test needs putative types the dandiset lacks.ripple-modulated-cells-larger-reactivation
  • Only the all-cell test is run; the pyramidal test needs putative types the dandiset lacks.reactivation-peaks-at-ripples
  • Laps are O1 reading c (between rewards at opposite ends); the dandiset has no lap intervals or position. The air puff direction of a day is the lap direction holding most puffs. These laps include the time on the starting platform after each reward and any stop, so the air puff and safe templates hold awake activity off the track that the M8 laps (position inside a 150-cm window) leave out.direction-all-cell-pairs
  • Ripples are the dandiset's rip annotations, counted in NREM when their peak is, a standard choice.ripple-rate-unchanged
