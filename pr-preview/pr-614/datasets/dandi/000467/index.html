Of the 17 findings tested here, 3 held up: 3-hexanone gives the broadest response, and the alarm pheromones activate few glomeruli, more of them at the higher concentration. Most others did not reproduce, including that the vehicle gives no response, a single glomerulus shared by all three panic pheromones, and the stereotyped positions of PGb, 6G and PGa, which were identified in only 5, 2 and 0 of 13 ants. The paper drew its regions of interest (ROIs) by eye, and the readings that would stand in for that disagree, so the counts depend on the reading adopted. The behaviour, transgenesis and confocal findings could not be tested because those data are not in the dataset. The single-glomerulus timing results rest on a second identification that found the third glomerulus (PGa) in only 4 ants.

Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Metadata errorThe planes' starting_time values step by 0.0335 s, so a 33-plane volume takes 1.105 s, while each file's imaging_rate is 0.83 volumes/s (1.205 s per volume) and the paper states 27.5 frames/s and a volume every 1.2 s. The data do not show which is right; the analysis times volumes by the imaging rate.
  • Description missingThe odor, concentration and trial are given only in the file and session names and the stimulus timing (onset 3 s, 5 s pulse) only in free-text descriptions; the files hold no stimulus or trial table. The bilateral files do not say which antennal lobe is the left one.
  • OtherEach file holds one plane of one trial as uncompressed float64, although the values are 13-bit integers (0 to 8191), so the dandiset is 14,685 files and 1.26 TB. A whole-lobe analysis of the alarm experiment must open about 11,600 files; this attempt read about half a terabyte through range requests.
  • Selection not documentedThe general odorant files hold the five odorants the paper analyses and the vehicle; the paper says 7 to 9 odorants were presented, and the dandiset does not say that the others were left out.
  • Methods not availableQuestions for the paper's authors. How were the ROIs drawn on the max projections (any size or shape rule, how adjacent glomeruli were separated), was an ROI's value its mean or its maximum, and was "responding" judged on the trial-mean value (Methods) or per trial and averaged (Figure S5 legend)? This attempt adopted the ROI's mean and the trial-mean rule, with glomerulus-sized ROIs for the focal glomeruli; the counts of responding glomeruli, the vehicle responses and the identification of PGb, 6G and PGa depend on these choices.
  • Methods not availableQuestions for the paper's authors. Which trials were excluded for motion artifacts, and by what rule? How was the centre plane of a glomerulus chosen, and what ROI was measured on the three-plane projection? What distance makes two glomeruli adjacent when identifying 6G and PGa?
  • OtherNo file or trial is deposited twice. All 14,685 assets have distinct content, and no two trials of an ant have identical baseline volumes in the maps computed.

Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • ROIs are the regions of at least 0.2 in a trial's map that recur in another trial of the odor (claims file, drawing a), a recurrence read as an overlap of at least half the smaller region, an assumption: the claims file does not say how much overlap makes a recurrence. In round 1 the pixels of at least 0.2 in two trials were taken instead, which broke the maps into fragments.fig3f-general-odorants-unique figs4a-bilateral-symmetry fig4b-vehicle-no-response fig4-large-lasting-responses figs5a-sparse-median-6 figs5a-concentration-more-glomeruli figs5b-shared-glomerulus figs5c-mho-rarely-overlaps fig5ab-focal-glomeruli
  • The single-glomerulus traces are computed for two identifications, bix (the adopted M2 reading) and aiiy (the reading that identifies PGa in the most ants, chosen in the notebook by that rule), for both plane readings and both of the claims file's ROIs (the 15 um disc and the region of at least 0.2 at the centre). aiiy uses the ROI's maximum and drawing a, the reading set aside for the counts of figs5a-sparse-median-6, and 356 of drawing a's 436 regions are under 10 um across; it is used only because it finds PGa in the most ants, and the PGa results rest on it.fig5c-s6-tuning fig5c-6g-slow figs7a-6g-extended figs7c-temporal-lme
  • Each plane's row band is stabilized (phase correlation against the baseline mean, as for the maps); the unstabilized disc trace is kept for comparison (PGb peak fold change, median absolute difference 0.035, largest 0.591, r = 0.98; 3 of 129 trials change side of 0.2). Volume times include the centre plane's offset in the volume scan (0.0335 s per plane, from the files' starting_time).fig5c-s6-tuning fig5c-6g-slow figs7a-6g-extended figs7c-temporal-lme
  • Stabilization by whole-pixel phase correlation in place of Fiji's Image Stabilizer; ROIs drawn by rule inside an AL mask (log-Otsu on baseline fluorescence), not by eye.fig3f-general-odorants-unique figs5a-sparse-median-6
  • The Minimum filter uses ImageJ's radius-2 kernel (21 pixels, RankFilters); round 1 used a 13-pixel disc.fig3f-general-odorants-unique fig3fg-hexanone-broad
  • "Closer" was read as a smaller mean distance to the same odorant's centroids in the other ants than to each other odorant's centroids; the claims file does not fix the distance rule.fig3f-general-odorants-unique
  • Area counted inside the AL mask; the whole field of view is shown as a variant and gives the same.fig3fg-hexanone-broad
  • The two ALs lie one above the other in the 1X images; the midline is the least fluorescent row between 30% and 70% of the height, and the files do not say which AL is the left one.figs4a-bilateral-symmetry
  • The vehicle was given once per ant in the dataset, so its ROIs come from the pheromone trials.fig4b-vehicle-no-response

  • Stabilization by whole-pixel phase correlation, not Image Stabilizer; residual motion could produce some of the negative regions, and the vehicle comparison removes only those it shares.fig4-no-decreases

  • The paper drew ROIs by eye.
