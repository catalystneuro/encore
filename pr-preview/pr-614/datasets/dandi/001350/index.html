Mostly untested: the hypomyelination of SRF knockout mice could not be checked, because the paper's hand scoring is not deposited and the masks cover one of five control mice. The sequencing findings largely held. The number of high-confidence peaks and of shared targets held only in part, as the paper does not say how peaks and motif matches were chosen. Optic nerve, staining, culture and single-nucleus findings could not be tested, because the data are missing or need an R pipeline that was unavailable.

    545 of 848 peaks (64.3%) are within 2 kb of a TSS by HOMER's annotation (the paper's tool), and 76.0% of the high-confidence peaks. In the -250 to +250 window around each peak centre, FIMO matches of the JASPAR SRF matrix MA0083.3 (p < 1e-4) are in 10.7% of peaks, against 5.2% of flank windows 3 kb away (odds 2.2, Fisher p < 0.0001) and 3.7% of shuffled peaks (p < 0.0001); in OPC peaks 8.9% against 5.2% (p = 0.0007), in OLG peaks 14.4% against 4.3%. The strict consensus CC(A/T)6GG gives 9.8% against 3.0% and 2.4%. Peak windows are GC-richer than flank windows (0.546 against 0.449); within GC quintiles the enrichment over flanks stays (Mantel-Haenszel odds ratio 2.14 for FIMO, 2.75 for the strict consensus), and the shuffled background keeps each peak's composition.

    fig4ab-rnaseq-degs

    DESeq2 (pydeseq2, default settings) on the RSEM gene results of GSE241560, KO against Flox per stage. The paper's normalized counts (Datasets S2 and S3, one stage each) are the expected counts divided by DESeq2's normalization factors from tximport's average-length offsets, computed per stage; with those factors the normalized counts here equal the paper's in every sample (spread of the log ratio over genes 0.0 to four decimals, median ratio 1.0001 and 1.0023), where size factors alone leave a spread of 0.09 to 0.21. So the paper used offsets (reading d). With the offsets and the DESeq2 vignette's pre-filter (at least 10 counts over a stage's samples), 156 genes have padj < 0.05 in OPCs (82 up, 74 down) and 263 in oligodendrocytes (92 up, 171 down), inside 157 +/- 15.7 and 264 +/- 26.4. They share 145 and 238 genes with the paper's tables, which hold 154 and 261 (Jaccard 0.88 and 0.83); p-values rank alike (Spearman 0.9996 and 0.998) and baseMean ratios are 1.000. Without a filter the offsets give 133 (outside the tolerance) and 266. Other readings, in this run, give 160 and 264 (b, length-scaled counts), 118 and 293 (a, rounded expected counts) and 130 and 418 (c, one model of all 15 samples). The round-2 run gave 117 and 294 for (a) and 130 and 423 for (c) with the same code and packages; a fit repeated on one machine is identical, and 15 to 44 genes per reading have padj between 0.045 and 0.055, so the difference most likely comes from the processor.

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • pydeseq2, the Python port of DESeq2, is used, as R is not available here. pydeseq2 takes only per-sample size factors; srf_geo.deseq_offsets puts DESeq2's gene-by-sample normalization factors where DESeq2 uses them (normalized counts, per-gene fits, Wald test). Given pydeseq2's own size factors it reproduces plain pydeseq2 exactly (printed).
    • The paper's tables list 13481 and 14851 genes, against 21688 and 27261 with any count here, so genes were filtered before testing by a rule the paper does not state. Assumed: the DESeq2 vignette's pre-filter (sum of counts at least 10). The outcome rests on it: without a filter the OPC count, 133, is outside the tolerance.

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingThe paper's hand scoring of the corpus callosum TEM (axons counted, diameters and myelin thickness) is not deposited. Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • The paper scored and measured axons by hand in Fiji; the dataset's segmentation masks (AxonDeepSeg pseudo-labels corrected by hand, made after the paper) stand in for that scoring (reading O1 of the claims file).fig2c-cc-percent-myelinated fig2d-cc-myelinated-density fig2e-cc-g-ratio figs3c-cc-myelin-thickness-unchanged figs3d-cc-larger-myelinated-diameter
  • The masks cover 8 full images and 2 small regions, from one SRF-Flox mouse (sub-370) and four SRF-cKO mice (sub-372, sub-373C, sub-374, sub-375), where the paper used 5 mice per genotype; sub-373C has 15 um2.fig2c-cc-percent-myelinated fig2d-cc-myelinated-density fig2e-cc-g-ratio figs3c-cc-myelin-thickness-unchanged figs3d-cc-larger-myelinated-diameter

  • The paper does not say how many replicates make 'consistently', nor which IgG peaks are excluded. Every reading is shown; none is adopted, as the paper's text does not single one out and the paper's peak lists (Dataset S1) do not hold the 329 peaks.fig3a-chip-peaks

Assumed: the paper's 'SRF CArG consensus' is the SRF matrix or the strict CC(A/T)6GG box, both of which are enriched; the one-mismatch variant is this attempt's own looser reading, not one the paper names, and is not enriched over all peaks.fig3bc-chip-tss-carg
