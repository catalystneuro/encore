    rnn-training-data-amount

    Under reading (a), the whole stored trial, the 9737 trials last 837.4 min (13 h 57.4 min), within 888 +/- 88.8 min; without target ids 8 and 9 they last 814.3 min, also within. The 7851 virtual trials last 540.0 min and the 1886 robot trials 297.3 min; without the robot trials the total is outside the tolerance. Trials last 1.80 to 11.09 s (virtual median 3.41 s, robot median 9.01 s). In trial-id order all virtual trials come first (ids 1 to 7851) and all robot trials after (7852 to 9737); the file does not say whether ids follow the recording order, so this does not show whether the robot trials are later fine-tuning data. Reading (b), the active portion, cannot be run as the file has no task states; it is at most reading (a). The channel-mean high gamma of the virtual trials stays at 0.830 to 0.843 (trial RMS 1) for the first 0.4 s and rises to 0.926 by 0.7 s, while the robot trials show no rise. This low start may be a period before the cue or the reaction time after it, which would lie inside the active (cue and control) portion; the outputs cannot tell which. In the worst case, if the first 0.5 s of every virtual trial were outside the active portion, reading (b) would be 771.9 min, outside the tolerance; this is a lower bound, not an estimate. The criterion asks for a match under one reading, which (a) gives. Whether the robot trials are the real-world practice with the MLP that the paper counts, or the later RNN fine-tuning data, and whether the stored trials are whole trials, is a question for the paper's authors; whether the criterion should require the readings to agree is asked of a reader in questions/001535.yaml.

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • Assumed that the dandiset's 9737 trials are the RNN training data the paper counts, including the 1886 robot trials as the real-world practice with the Kinova Jaco (Methods, RNN decoders based on spatiotemporal activity patterns, paragraph 2, "in the virtual 3D environment and with practice with the Kinova Jaco in the real-world using the MLP"). The dandiset description calls them training trials for decoders and points to the paper's biLSTM, but does not say they are exactly its training set, and the robot flag does not say which robot data they are.
    • Duration is the number of samples divided by 100 Hz; the last minus first timestamp gives 835.7 min, also within the tolerance.
    • Reading (b) of the criterion (active portion only) could not be run; the file has no task states. The notebook bounds it with the high-gamma time course at the start and end of the trials.

    figs3-spatial-pooling

    The file has no electrodes table, so the place of each channel on the 8x16 grid, which the 2x2 pooling needs, is not given. The right thumb trials are there (target 1 under both the Methods' map and the OSF code's names; 1357 trials) and so is the high gamma, but as the biLSTM input at 100 Hz, L2 normalized per trial, not the MLP pipeline's 200 ms band power, and with no session labels where the paper used one session. The online pilot blocks of the accuracy part are not in the dandiset (data_missing). A variant in the notebook bins the stored high gamma in 200 ms bins, applies a 1 s running average and pools 2x2. Pooling lowers the median per-channel variance to 0.499 of the raw value with channels in row-major order (z = 7.05, p = 1.9e-12) and 0.538 in column-major order (z = 7.12), and under all 200 random layouts (ratios 0.457 to 0.533, z 7.40 to 8.05, p < 0.05 in every one), against the paper's z = 8.721. Since random layouts give the same result, the variant shows only that averaging lowers variance and cannot test the claim about the grid.

Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds only participant B1's biLSTM training trials (high-gamma and low-frequency features at 100 Hz). Participant B3, the Figure 1 imagined-movement task, the delta, beta and high-gamma band-power features of the MLP decoders, the online decoder outputs, the ReFit-KF and IBID velocities and the robot task outcomes behind Figures 1 to 7 are not in it; 51 of the paper's 52 findings cannot be tested.
  • Description missingNo trial carries a day, session, block, or open-loop or closed-loop label; the trial table's start_time and stop_time are placeholders (the trial id and id plus one, described as DUMMY), and session_start_time is one date for all trials. Trials collected over many days cannot be grouped by day or context.
  • Description missingThe target ids 1 to 9 carry no names or directions in the file or the dandiset metadata. The paper's Methods name seven actions and directions, and its OSF code numbers them in two different orders.
  • Metadata errorThe dandiset description speaks of 7 directional targets; the trial table has 9 target ids, with ids 8 and 9 (132 trials each) only in virtual-environment trials.
  • Description missingThe 256 features are not described column by column. The data show columns 0 to 127 almost always positive and 128 to 255 signed, which fits high gamma then low frequency, and each block has an L2 norm of 1 per trial, which the units ("arbitrary") do not state. There is no electrodes table, so the channels cannot be placed on the 8x16 grid.
  • Description missingThe is_robot flag does not say whether a robot trial is the real-world practice with the MLP decoder or the later fine-tuning data of the RNN. The paper's 14 h 48 min of training data matches the stored total (837.4 min) only if the 1886 robot trials are counted (540.0 min without them). In trial-id order every robot trial follows every virtual trial, and nothing says whether the ids follow the order of recording.
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the paper's authors. Are the 9737 trials of the dandiset the data the biLSTM was trained on (the 14 h 48 min of the Methods), and are its robot trials the real-world practice with the MLP counted there, or the later fine-tuning of the RNN? Is each stored trial the whole trial or only its active portion? The virtual trials' high gamma rises only after about 0.4 s, which could be a period before the cue or the reaction time after it; in the worst case, excluding 0.5 s of each virtual trial would bring the total to 771.9 min.
  • OtherThe subject's age is P38Y, where the paper (STAR Methods, Participants) gives B1 as 41 years old; neither says at what date the age applies, so the two may refer to different dates.
