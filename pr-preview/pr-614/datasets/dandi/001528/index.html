The main findings largely held: STN and PPN stimulation excited SNr GABA and Vglut2 dopamine neurons, and drove opposite dopamine release in tail (increase) and dorsolateral striatum (decrease), scaling with frequency. Inhibition of Anxa1 dopamine neurons, the contrast with Vglut2 neurons, held in direction but was only partly testable, because the dataset lacks dorsolateral recordings from several of the paper's mice (5 and 4 mice here, 8 and 10 in the paper). The PPN duration effect on pan-dopamine responses did not reproduce, and the records give no cause. The human tissue findings could not be tested because those counts are not deposited. Minor claims, such as the 5 Hz dopamine rise, group sizes and rebounds, were mixed or failed.

Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingFor the Anxa1 mice the dandiset holds DLS terminal recordings for 5 (STN) and 4 (PPN) mice where the paper analyses 8 and 10. Nine mice (C5903, C5906, C5992, C6900, C6902, C7244, C7245, C7248, C7249) have only recordings labelled SNc, without a site suffix in the file name. Their signals resemble SNc recordings, and the paper's cell-body statistics are reproduced closely by the other nine mice's SNc recordings (p to two digits, t within 0.08), so the DLS recordings of these nine mice appear not to be deposited, and the nine SNc recordings were not used in the paper.
  • Selection not documentedThe dandiset includes Anxa1 SNc recordings from nine mice that the paper's cell-body analysis (n = 5 and 4) leaves out, and it does not say which recordings the paper used.
  • Data missingThe human RNAscope and immunofluorescence counts of Figure 1 and Figure S1 and the histology of Figure S2 are not in the dandiset, and the preprint has no data availability statement.
  • Data missingDA terminals in DLS have 8 mice where Figure S3 gives 9.
  • Paper inconsistentThe Figure 5 legend gives 10 mice for Vglut2+ neurons with STN stimulation, while the paper's Wilcoxon statistics (W = 41, 43, -5 with p = 0.012, 0.008, 0.82) are the exact values for 9 mice; the dandiset holds 9.
  • Data missingThree Vglut2 PPN mice (C3412, C3798, C4707) have no 5 Hz trains in their frequencies sessions, while the paper's Friedman statistic for PPN 1 s (24.8) needs at least 9 mice with all four frequencies.
  • Data missingSubject C4608 (SNr GABA, PPN) has no 4 s trains in its frequencies session; the conversion notes say they were recorded in a separate session, which is not in the dandiset.
  • Metadata errorThe dF/F series are stated in a.u. but hold percent (100 times (Gc - fit) / fit); a recomputation from the stored signals agrees with a median correlation of 0.9999 (durations) and 0.9988 (frequencies).
  • Metadata errorEvery session start time carries the offset -00:01, the local mean time of London that pytz gives when a time zone is attached with replace; the true offset is lost.
  • Metadata errorThe session description of the frequencies protocol gives an interval of 10 s between trains; the epochs and the Methods give 20 s.
  • OtherThirteen mice (SNr GABA and pan-DA, ids starting with B) have 10 trials per condition where the Methods state 5, and 30 mice have 16 s trains that the paper does not mention.
  • Published code disagreesThe conversion code names the 1 s block of the STN to SNr GABA frequencies sessions s1s10mW, suggesting 10 mW, while the epoch tables and the Methods give 5 mW.
  • Published code disagreesThe conversion repository's Python re-implementation of the dF/F forces the robust fit through the origin, saying the MATLAB version does so, while its own description of the MATLAB scripts includes an intercept.
  • Metadata errorThe files describe the modulation inconsistently. The excitation source descriptions give 465 nm modulated at 210 Hz and 405 nm at 330 Hz, as the Methods do, while the processed series descriptions give the calcium signal as demodulated at 330 Hz and the isosbestic signal at 210 Hz, as the conversion repository's description of the MATLAB scripts does. This attempt did not check which is right against the raw signal.
  • Description missingThe file names and session ids of nine Anxa1 mice carry no recording site, and every Anxa1 file lists both an SNc and a DLS fiber in its fiber photometry table, so the site rests on the name of the dF/F series alone.
  • OtherEight subject ids are written in lower case (c3015, c3016, c4379, c4708, c4709, c4977, c4978, c5113) while the others are upper case.
  • Paper inconsistentThe Results give F1,11 = 12.8 for the DLS frequency effect with 1 s trains for both STN (p = 0.0005) and PPN (p = 0.002); F(1, 11) = 12.8 gives p = 0.0043, and the data give F = 26.2 for STN. Two Friedman entries for Anxa1 terminals also do not fit their p-values (F = 11.5 with p = 0.0002, F = 15.5 with p = 0.015; with 3 df these give 0.0093 and 0.0014). F1,11 is also not possible for a one-way repeated-measures ANOVA in 7 or 9 mice. The Figure 5 legend says the Anxa1 STN frequency panels (N, O) show no significant effect of frequency, while the Results report significant effects for all three STN durations.
  • Methods not availableThe lab's MATLAB analysis scripts are not public; the preprint names custom MATLAB code and has no code availability statement. The conversion repository describes them.
  • OtherThe dandiset has no published version; this attempt used the draft as modified on 2026-09-01T15:27:47Z.

Reproduction Responses of SNr GABA and SNc dopamine neuron subtypes to STN and PPN input (Hadjas et al. 2025)

Tests the mouse fiber photometry findings of Hadjas et al. (2025) on dandiset 001528, which holds recordings from 120 mice during optogenetic stimulation of STN or PPN inputs to the substantia nigra. It compares responses of SNr GABA neurons, SNc pan-DA neurons, striatal dopamine release in TS and DLS, DA terminals, and Vglut2+ and Anxa1+ DA neurons across train durations and frequencies, using the lab's dF/F stored in the files. The human tissue counts of Figure 1 are not in the dandiset. All files
