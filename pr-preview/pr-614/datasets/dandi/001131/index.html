    fig6-rslds-fit-quality

    Under all four seeds the emissions reconstruction explains 59.4% of the variance in control and 58.4 to 58.7% in experimental mice (range 49.5 to 66.4% over mice), against 85 +/- 8.5%. The forward-prediction R2 does not differ between groups (p = 0.114 at 1 s and 5 s for every seed). Attack frames fall mostly in one state in 7, 8, 8 and 8 of the 8 mice with attack annotations (seeds 0 to 3), and at least 90% in one state in 3, 2, 3 and 5. Checks for seed 0 (rslds_checks.ipynb). Sigma-normalized fits explain 69.0% (control) and 63.6% (experimental), and their forward R2 differs between groups (p = 0.016). On held-out frames the reconstruction explains 47.9% and 41.0%, and forward predictions of the activity do not differ over 1 s (p = 0.26) but do over 5 s (0.371 against 0.198, p = 0.010). So the claim's "similar performance" part also fails on the held-out score at 5 s and on the sigma-normalized fits (p = 0.016 at both horizons); it holds on the in-sample score and the held-out score at 1 s. The 85% may be a measure other than the emissions R2; that is asked of a reader (questions/001131.yaml). The fits of mice 510 and 500 do not recover the authors' latent factor 1 (|Spearman r| at most 0.47 for dimension 1, 0.70 for any dimension). Seeds 0 and 1 are in rslds_seeds_0_1.ipynb and seeds 2 and 3 in rslds_seeds_2_3.ipynb, with the same cells and tags; rslds_checks.ipynb holds the seed-0 sigma-normalized fits and a held-out score.

Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds the miniscope data of 4 control and 6 experimental mice, where the paper reports 5 and 7 (STAR Methods, Micro-endoscopic data extraction). The female-intruder part of every imaging session is absent, as are the behaviour cohorts of Figures 2 and 3, the fiber photometry and the slice imaging, although the paper's data statement says the source data will be deposited at DANDI. 20 of the 36 claims cannot be tested for these reasons.
  • Description missingThe files mark neither the 5-min pre-intruder baseline, nor the intruder's entry and removal. The paper's activity measures are in units of sigma relative to that baseline, and Figure 6E uses the post-intruder period; here the baseline had to be assumed from the first annotated behaviour, and the post-intruder claim could not be tested. Only z-scored traces are given, not dF/F.
  • Metadata errorThe files of mice 510 and 500 describe their trace as "DF/F" with "GCaMP6m". The values are z-scores (every unit of mouse 510 has mean 0 and SD 1), and the paper says the imaged mice carried "an AAV vector that encodes both the gRNAs and a Cre-dependent GCaMP8s" (Results, Single cell CRISPRoscopy imaging).
  • Data missingThe files of mice 510 and 500 hold only sniffing annotations (series named "Ch1: sniffing"), with no attack or mounting, although the paper's exemplar mice attack. Mouse 510's sniffing bouts all fall between 179 and 279 s of a 1200-s recording.
  • OtherThe unit counts per group differ from the STAR Methods in the other direction from the mouse counts: 587 control units in 4 mice and 554 experimental units in 6 mice, against 585 in 5 and 546 in 7.
  • OtherThe two files of the earlier version 0.240826.1647 (subjects ControlMouse and MutantMouse) are the same recordings as mice 510 and 500 of version 0.260601.1203, with identical traces, sniffing bouts and rSLDS weights under different subject ids.
  • Description missingThe group of each mouse is given only by the prefix of its subject id (ctrl, mut) and, in eight files, a keyword; there is no genotype or description of the gRNA. Nine sessions carry the same start date (2023-02-01), apparently a placeholder.
  • OtherThe trace of mouse 500 is not z-scored over its file (unit SDs 0.65 to 1.05, means up to 0.23 away from 0), unlike the other nine, so it was z-scored over a longer recording and then cut.
  • OtherThe rSLDS weights deposited for mice 510 and 500 ("latent factor 1") are not recovered by fits with the settings of the claims file: over four seeds their |Spearman r| with the weights of the first fitted dimension is at most 0.47, and with those of any fitted dimension at most 0.70. The fits' time constants are hundreds of seconds and their variance explained 59%, against 85% in the paper.
  • Methods not availableQuestions for the authors. (1) Where does the pre-intruder baseline lie in each file, and when did each intruder enter and leave? (2) What are the "nested" Mann-Whitney and KS tests: GraphPad nested tests across mice, or tests on pooled units? The outcomes of fig4ef and figs4e depend on this. (3) For the rSLDS, which frames were fit, with which seeds and restarts, how were the cross-validation folds and horizon of the performance score formed, which state's dynamics matrix gives the time constants, and how are complex eigenvalue pairs (two equal time constants) treated in the line attractor score? (4) How is ACHW computed (which estimator of the autocorrelation, whether the window's mean is removed, over which window)? (5) How is the attack probability of Figure S4A(vii) computed and summarized per mouse? (6) Mouse 511 has two sniffing bouts of 22 and 40 s; is that annotation complete? (7) For Figure 1E, which cells of the Kim et al. 2019 atlas were counted (both sexes, or male cells only) and above what expression threshold? The outcome of fig1e depends on both.
  • Paper inconsistentThe Results give n=4 control mice for Figure 4E-F, while the Figure 4 and S4 legends and the STAR Methods give 5. The dandiset holds 4 control mice.
