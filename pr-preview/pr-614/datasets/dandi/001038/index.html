Most main findings held up in this data: Vglut2 and Calb1 axons rise after decelerations and respond to rewards and air puffs, Anxa1 axons rise after accelerations, ignore rewards and fall to air puffs, the subtypes cluster from these responses, and SNc somas track their axons. The locomotion recording counts did not reproduce (Anxa1 82 against 47), likely from exclusions the files do not record, so those findings used a larger set. Vglut2 deceleration timing and the DAT air puff response matched only in part, for unexplained reasons. The snRNA-seq clustering could not be tested without R software.

Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Metadata errorThe `coordinates` column of the fiber table is described as (AP, ML, DV) but holds (ML, AP, DV); the second value takes only the reference-slice AP positions and is negative in the tail of the striatum, and the paper's code builds the vector as [ML, AP, DV].
  • Metadata errorThe column `cross_correlation_with_acceleration` is described as the cross-correlation between dF/F and acceleration but holds the peak cross-correlation of the 405 nm control with acceleration (the code's Acc405, used for exclusion), and is missing where running was under 100 s.
  • Description missingThe dF/F series are normalized so that the largest transient is 1, which the files do not state (unit n.a., described as DF/F); the normalization factor is in the column `normalized_fluorescence`, which in 123 files holds two values per row.
  • Selection not documentedThe files carry the signal-to-noise ratio and 405 nm cross-correlation but not the other exclusion flags (running time, 405 nm artifacts) or which recordings each figure uses. Recomputed with the paper's code, the stimulus analyses give the paper's counts exactly, but the locomotion criteria select more striatal recordings than Figure 2 reports (Anxa1 82 against 47, Vglut2 50 against 42).
  • Data missingThe Events table lists consumed rewards only; rewards the mouse did not lick for are only in the raw 4 kHz Reward trace. The code excludes 5 s after every reward, so 911 acceleration and 767 deceleration onsets recomputed from the processed data are not in the stored WheelEvents.
  • Metadata errorThree subject ids (Anxa-I765L, Anxa-I767L, Calb-2246L) are recordings from the left hemisphere of mice that also have ids without the L, so the archive's 105 subjects are 102 mice. The age is a range (P8W/P16W) in every file.
  • Metadata errorIn the published version the related resource link to the paper is malformed (https://doi.org/https://doi.org/...), fixed in the draft; the asset summary lists measurementTechnique "analytical technique" and variableMeasured "ProcessingModule" only.
  • Description missing785 of 790 SNc fibers and 1281 of 2137 fibers overall have no coordinates.
  • Published code disagreesThe Methods integrate responses from +0.05 to +0.55 s minus -0.5 to 0 s; the code integrates 0 to +0.5 s minus -0.55 to -0.05 s. The paper's reward means equal those of the code's windows to the decimal (Vglut2 7.90, Calb1 12.40, DAT 5.88), as do its Vglut2, Calb1 and Anxa1 air puff means within tolerance; its DAT air puff mean (5.3) does not (2.46). The Methods' windows give about 10% more.
  • Published code disagreesThe code reports the trough of the cross-correlation 0.02 s, and the peak of triggered averages 0.01 s, later than the times the Methods define, and prints uncorrected p-values for Figures 2i and 7f, whose legends state Bonferroni correction.
  • Published code disagreesThe 405 nm artifact detection marks everything from the last noisy section to the end of a recording and from the start to the first, which the Methods do not describe.
  • Paper inconsistentThe Results give reward P = 2e-5 for Calb1 and 0.001 for Vglut2; the Figure 4g legend gives them the other way round. The Figure 2f legend gives 10 Anxa1 mice, the Methods 9. Extended Data Figure 9k gives striatal PCs explaining 52.4% and 25.2% of SNc variance, the Methods 53.2% and 24.3%.

Reproduction Functional responses of genetic subtypes of dopamine neurons (Azcorra, Gaertner et al. 2023)

Tests the fiber photometry findings of Azcorra, Gaertner et al. (2023) on dandiset 001038, which holds the recordings from striatal axons and SNc somas of Vglut2, Calb1, Anxa1, Aldh1a1 and DAT mice running on a treadmill and receiving rewards and air puffs. It covers locomotion signaling (Figures 2, 3 and 5), reward and air puff responses (Figure 4), clustering (Figure 6), soma and axon correlation (Figure 7) and Extended Data Figures 1 and 6 to 9. Two snRNA-seq claims of Figure 1 are tested on the paper's GEO matrices. All files
