Most of the paper's main findings could not be tested here, and those that could mostly did not hold. Cue responses and population trajectories of entorhinal neurons, histology, old-mouse behavior, and the optogenetic and L-DOPA rescues are untestable because the dandiset lacks odor times, images and those mice. The lower dopamine signal to the novel rewarded odor in APP-KI mice and the young APP-KI memory deficit were not significant, but only 10 of 27 mice have behavior and photometry rests on inferred timing. Higher firing rates in APP-KI mice did reproduce. I also tried a Gmail draft by mistake; it was denied and nothing was created.

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • The photometry traces are read as sampled at 1 kHz with the cue at sample 1000, so that a trial spans -1 to 5 s. The files give neither rate nor cue time. Every trial column has mean 0 and SD 1 over samples 0 to 999 and over no other span tested, as a trace z-scored to the 1 s before cue would (claims file, Shared method, Photometry, from ref. 16), and the noise peaks at 120 Hz and its harmonics under this rate. This is an assumption.
    • Odor codes 1 to 4 are read as Odor-A, Odor-B, Odor-1 and Odor-2, and action codes 1 to 4 as hit, miss, false alarm and correct rejection. The files give no names. The tables zscore_A to zscore_D of each file are block means of the trials of codes 1 to 4, and actions 1 and 2 occur only with codes 1 and 3.
    • Timepoints T1 to T5 are blocks of 10 trials of each odor starting at round(k (n - 10) / 4), the blocks the dataset's own tables hold (notebook, data check). This fits the claims file's T1, T3 and T5 and its reading T-a for T2 and T4.
    • The paper's units are hemispheres (10 WT, 8 APP-KI), which the files do not identify. The Fig. 4d legend's F(4,80) for 18 hemispheres and five timepoints leaves one value per hemisphere and timepoint, so a hemisphere's sessions are pooled. The mean over a mouse's sessions (5 WT and 5 APP-KI mice), the nearest pooled unit the files allow, is adopted as an assumption. Sessions as units (11 and 30), which count repeated recordings of a hemisphere as independent, are a variant. With 5 mice a two-sided signed-rank test cannot reach p < 0.05.
    • The trial columns are already z-scored to the precue second, so dF/F was not recomputed. They are smoothed with a Gaussian of sigma 200 ms (claims file, Shared method, Photometry).
    • Genotype comes from Supplementary Table 2 (link-photometry-mice); no file states it.
    • The two-way ANOVAs use type III sums of squares with sum-to-zero contrasts, the default of MATLAB's anovan (Methods, Data analysis, 'analyses were performed using MATLAB codes'). The interaction is the same under type II.
    • Session 00266_20190405 has no Odor-1/2 trial among trials 121 to 160, so under B1 it is neither a correct nor an error session.

    figs6d-dopamine-behavior-correlation

    All readings were run. With the precue SD (a), r = 0.30 to 0.73 over the 8 combinations of window (G1, G2), signal measure (window mean, window maximum) and trial count (among Odor-1 trials, among all trials). p < 0.05 in 6 of the 8, on 9 to 22 of 30 sessions. r is within 0.78 +- 0.078 only for the G2 window mean counted among Odor-1 trials (r = 0.73, p = 0.025, 9 sessions). With the session's SD (b), at most one session reaches the criterion, so no r can be computed. The paper does not say what the 3 SD is taken from, over which window, or whether the window mean or any sample must exceed it. No choice has a defensible basis over the others. The question is recorded for the authors (findings, methods_unavailable). Reading (b) takes the SD over every sample of every trial, post-cue responses included; its threshold (median 4.51 z) is above the 90th percentile of the Odor-1 1 to 4 s window means in every session (median 2.07 z). The window-mean readings under (a) keep 9 to 11 of 30 sessions, against the paper's 21.

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • The photometry traces are read as sampled at 1 kHz with the cue at sample 1000, so that a trial spans -1 to 5 s. The files give neither rate nor cue time. Every trial column has mean 0 and SD 1 over samples 0 to 999 and over no other span tested, as a trace z-scored to the 1 s before cue would (claims file, Shared method, Photometry, from ref. 16), and the noise peaks at 120 Hz and its harmonics under this rate. This is an assumption.
    • Odor codes 1 to 4 are read as Odor-A, Odor-B, Odor-1 and Odor-2, and action codes 1 to 4 as hit, miss, false alarm and correct rejection. The files give no names. The tables zscore_A to zscore_D of each file are block means of the trials of codes 1 to 4, and actions 1 and 2 occur only with codes 1 and 3.
    • The trial columns are already z-scored to the precue second, so dF/F was not recomputed. They are smoothed with a Gaussian of sigma 200 ms (claims file, Shared method, Photometry).
    • Genotype comes from Supplementary Table 2 (link-photometry-mice); no file states it.
