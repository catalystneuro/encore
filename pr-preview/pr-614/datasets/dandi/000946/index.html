Most of the paper's main findings could not be tested in this data, and none reproduced. The responses of regular and fast spiking units in S1 and POm to ultrasound, and the S1 to POm correlations, need spike waveforms and cell types, which the dataset lacks. The unit counts did not reproduce: POm has 352 units at 3000 Hz against 250 reported, perhaps because the paper's unit rule differs. The auditory cortex responses stayed undecided because the paper does not say how rates were normalized, and the readings gave different outcomes. Sham and cingulate controls could not be found.

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • The normalization (N1 or N2) and the baseline sample of the post hoc test (B1 or B2) are not fixed by the paper (Statistical analysis, "normalized to the pre-stim period (67 ms pre-stim)"); all four readings were run and they give different outcomes.
    • The Bonferroni factor is the five PRF levels of the panel (claims file header, reading C1); reading C2 applies only to a panel with a single comparison.
    • The dataset holds AC recordings at PD 200 us from 6 rats (7 AC rats in all) where the paper reports 12 S1-AC rats, and it gives 633 units with more than 2000 spikes where the paper reports 453; the paper's selection of AC units could not be matched.
    • Each PRF comes from 3 or 4 of the 6 rats, and the rats differ by PRF, so the Kruskal-Wallis test across PRFs mixes PRF with rat (for example BH538 averages 654.1% at 3000 Hz, 201 to 268 ms, against 310.5% pooled); the paper also pools units across rats.
    • Recordings deposited twice (identical spike times and trial starts) are counted once.
    • Trial start_time is taken as sonication onset; the clock check shows the firing changes after it and not 1 s later, which is consistent with this but does not prove there is no fixed delay.

Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingThe spike waveforms are not in the dataset, and the RSU/FSU cell types are not either: no file has waveforms or mean waveforms, and acquisition, analysis and processing are empty in all 1550 unit files. The paper types every S1 and POm unit from its waveform (Methods, Neuronal type classification), so 30 of the 33 claims (all those on RSU or FSU responses or their correlations) cannot be tested, and the RSU/FSU split of the two count claims cannot be tested either.
  • Metadata errorThe units column celltype_label is described as label 1 = RSU, label 2 = FSU, but it is 0 for every unit in the 1516 unit files that have it; 34 unit files have no such column.
  • Other734 of the 1550 unit files are second (or third) copies of another file, with identical spike times and trial starts and the same identifier, under session start times up to 57 s apart and different file names; 816 distinct recordings remain. Counting the files as they are would double most unit counts.
  • Data missingNo recording is marked as sham (no tFUS) or as tFUS aimed at CG1, the control conditions of Figure 5; every recording gives a PRF and a PD and a target of S1, M1 or POm. If the sham and CG1 sessions are in the dataset, they cannot be found.
  • Data missingThe dataset has 10 rats with POm recordings and 7 with AC recordings, where the paper reports 16 S1-POm rats and 12 S1-AC rats (Methods); 20 rats in all.
  • Selection not documentedThe AC recordings at PD 200 us give 633 units with more than 2000 spikes (112, 89, 138, 167 and 127 at 30 to 4500 Hz), where Figure 11 reports 453 (83, about 59, 107, 104 and 100), although the dataset has fewer AC rats than the paper. The POm totals exceed the paper's RSU + FSU counts by 36 to 44% under every selection of recordings by identifier (at 3000 Hz 352 against 250, at 30 Hz 315 against 232, over the five PRFs 1470 against 1019), with 10 POm rats where the paper reports 16. They come within 10% only if two of the POm rats are left out (9 of the 45 pairs, for example BH511 and BH513, which nothing marks for exclusion) or if only units with more than 8000 spikes are counted. The paper's unit selection cannot be repeated from its description.
  • OtherThe dataset also holds recordings of M1 and sessions with tFUS aimed at M1 (152 distinct recordings) or at POm (65), and a few recordings labelled S1BF (3), VPM (1), S1_R, S1_1, M1_R (1 each) and AC_1, AC_2 (1 each), none of which the paper describes; the dandiset description mentions only S1, POm and AC with tFUS at S1.
  • Metadata errorIn the 60 sessions at PD 200 us with tFUS at S1 that have files from two of S1, POm and AC (the only sessions checked), both files have identical trial start times, so the areas were recorded together, but their session_start_time values differ (median 15.5 min, up to 3964 min); session_start_time does not give the start of the recording.
  • Description missingThe recorded area and the tFUS target are given only in free text in general/notes, in two spellings; the electrodes table has location unknown and the electrode group location is 'brain area'. Five recordings of BH500 leave the area empty, and no other recording does. general/data_collection, which carries the PRF and PD, is empty in 33 recordings (BH494 25, BH499 6, BH500 2), where the identifier gives them; where both are given (782 recordings) they agree.
  • File format errorThe arrays of the STTC files do not have the shape their descriptions give: the descriptions list a dimension for nine dt values, while the arrays have the 10 time windows, the trials and the units of the two areas (for example STTC is 10 x 499 x 20 x 9) and no dt dimension. Which dt was used, and which units the rows and columns are, is not stated.
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the paper's authors: was the normalized spiking rate the ratio of a unit's mean rate in the window to its mean rate in the 67 ms before stimulation (over trials), or a per-trial ratio averaged over trials; and in the post hoc Wilcoxon test against baseline, what was the baseline sample (the normalized pre-stimulus rates, which are 100% under the first reading, or another pre-stimulus window)? The Statistical analysis says only that rates were normalized to the 67 ms before stimulation. For Figure 11B the readings give different outcomes, and the claim is blocked.

Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • Recordings deposited twice (identical spike times and trial starts) are counted once.fig4-unit-counts fig6-unit-counts fig11b-ac-responses
  • The RSU/FSU split cannot be made (no waveforms or cell types), so the claim is judged on the area totals, which are the same under every typing reading of the claims file header.fig4-unit-counts fig6-unit-counts
  • Which sessions count beyond the sessions of the S1 and POm rats is not fixed; every selection the identifiers allow is shown, and the outcome is the same under all of them because the POm totals are outside 10% under each.fig4-unit-counts
  • Which sessions count beyond the sessions of the S1 and POm rats is not fixed; every selection the identifiers allow is shown, and none brings either area total within 10%.fig6-unit-counts
  • The normalization (N1 or N2) and the baseline sample of the post hoc test (B1 or B2) are not fixed by the paper (Statistical analysis, "normalized to the pre-stim period (67 ms pre-stim)"); all four readings were run and they give different outcomes.fig11b-ac-responses
  • The Bonferroni factor is the five PRF levels of the panel (claims file header, reading C1); reading C2 applies only to a panel with a single comparison.fig11b-ac-responses
  • The dataset holds AC recordings at PD 200 us from 6 rats (7 AC rats in all) where the paper reports 12 S1-AC rats, and it gives 633 units with more than 2000 spikes where the paper reports 453; the paper's selection of AC units could not be matched.fig11b-ac-responses
  • Each PRF comes from 3 or 4 of the 6 rats, and the rats differ by PRF, so the Kruskal-Wallis test across PRFs mixes PRF with rat (for example BH538 averages 654.1% at 3000 Hz, 201 to 268 ms, against 310.5% pooled); the paper also pools units across rats.fig11b-ac-responses
  • Trial start_time is taken as sonication onset; the clock check shows the firing changes after it and not 1 s later, which is consistent with this but does not prove there is no fixed delay.fig11b-ac-responses
