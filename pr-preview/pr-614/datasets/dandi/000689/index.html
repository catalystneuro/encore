Almost none of the paper's main findings could be tested in this data, and none was confirmed. The dandiset holds only amygdala recordings and neurotensin sensor photometry, and no file says which mice are CRISPR or control, so the knockout behavior, anatomy, terminal photometry and group comparisons cannot be tested. The sensor's rise to sucrose after learning was significant only with all trials (p = 0.0046, against 0.093 with the first 16), and its fall to airpuff and its early dip were not reproduced. The seven functional clusters depend on an unreported smoothing window, and the units are not sorted single neurons. Behavior clustering matched only one of its two cluster counts.

Under the per-trial z-score (the reading adopted) the US-window response (2 to 7 s) is larger late than early under all four readings of the criterion. The criterion asks for p < 0.05 with late higher under every reading; it holds in 2 of the 4, so the criterion is not met, and only the direction holds in all four.     How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • The files mark one kind of cue, 2 s long, and no US. All cues are taken as CS-US trials with the US 2 s after CS onset (Methods, Head-fixed behavior, "The US ... was delivered 2 s after the onset of the CS"). The Methods say about half the CSs were unpaired; in the late sucrose sessions 49 to 58 of about 60 cues are followed by licking within 2 to 5 s, so most cues are paired, but unpaired cues, if any, cannot be removed. This is an assumption.
    • The median filter of 200 ms is applied at the native 20 Hz before averaging into 5 Hz bins, since at 5 Hz it would do nothing (Methods, Fiber photometry, Data analysis, "down-sampled to 5Hz and filtered ... using a median filter with a span of 200 ms").
    • Whether the residuals are z-scored against each trial's baseline or the session's pooled baseline is not said (Methods, "Residuals from the regression were z-transformed using data a baseline window of -5 s to 0 s relative to the start of the CS"). The per-trial baseline is adopted as an assumption (each trial's own -5 to 0 s window is the plainer reading); the session-pooled one is shown and gives no p < 0.05.
    • The 470 nm trace is regressed on the 415 nm trace over the trial-averaged -5 to 0 s window, as the Methods say ("Regression coefficients were computed using data averaged across trials in a session"). The fitted slopes run from 0.38 to 2.33 (correlation 0.25 to 0.99); a fit on the pooled baseline bins of every trial is shown as a variant.
    • The single photometry series interleaves three frame types at 60 Hz where the Methods give 40 Hz. Each channel is the phase of the three-frame order that the 415 or 470 nm LED labels mark before the first irregular frame interval. In Mouse6's late sucrose session the labels move by one frame after a 2.3 ms interval while the data keep their order (phase means 0.200, 0.019 and 0.057 before and after it), so the labels there would give the constant third frame type as the 470 nm channel.

    Run on the 1399 units of 24 mice with baseline of at least 0.3 Hz in sessions with all three CSs (163 units dropped for rate, 340 in the six sessions without neutral CSs, the whole of H17 for rate since its spikes stop at 249 s). Tests the findings of Li, Namburi, Olson et al. (2022) that dandiset 000689 can hold. It holds 31 sessions of BLA recordings during the three-cue discrimination task, with pose, and the NT sensor photometry of 5 mice during reward and punishment learning. The NT sensor learning effects, the neuron and tagged-unit counts, the functional clustering and the behavior clustering are tested; the group comparisons are blocked because no file says which mice are CRISPR and which control, and the other experiments of the paper are not in the dandiset. Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • The files mark one kind of cue, 2 s long, and no US. All cues are taken as CS-US trials with the US 2 s after CS onset (Methods, Head-fixed behavior, "The US ... was delivered 2 s after the onset of the CS"). The Methods say about half the CSs were unpaired; in the late sucrose sessions 49 to 58 of about 60 cues are followed by licking within 2 to 5 s, so most cues are paired, but unpaired cues, if any, cannot be removed. This is an assumption.fig3k-nt-sensor-sucrose-increase
  • The median filter of 200 ms is applied at the native 20 Hz before averaging into 5 Hz bins, since at 5 Hz it would do nothing (Methods, Fiber photometry, Data analysis, "down-sampled to 5Hz and filtered ... using a median filter with a span of 200 ms").fig3k-nt-sensor-sucrose-increase
  • Whether the residuals are z-scored against each trial's baseline or the session's pooled baseline is not said (Methods, "Residuals from the regression were z-transformed using data a baseline window of -5 s to 0 s relative to the start of the CS"). The per-trial baseline is adopted as an assumption (each trial's own -5 to 0 s window is the plainer reading); the session-pooled one is shown and gives no p < 0.05.fig3k-nt-sensor-sucrose-increase
  • The 470 nm trace is regressed on the 415 nm trace over the trial-averaged -5 to 0 s window, as the Methods say ("Regression coefficients were computed using data averaged across trials in a session"). The fitted slopes run from 0.38 to 2.33 (correlation 0.25 to 0.99); a fit on the pooled baseline bins of every trial is shown as a variant.fig3k-nt-sensor-sucrose-increase
  • The single photometry series interleaves three frame types at 60 Hz where the Methods give 40 Hz. Each channel is the phase of the three-frame order that the 415 or 470 nm LED labels mark before the first irregular frame interval. In Mouse6's late sucrose session the labels move by one frame after a 2.3 ms interval while the data keep their order (phase means 0.200, 0.019 and 0.057 before and after it), so the labels there would give the constant third frame type as the 470 nm channel.fig3k-nt-sensor-sucrose-increase
  • The files mark no airpuff, and the cue events last 2 s where the Methods give a 4 s CS. All cues are taken as CS-US trials with the airpuff 2 s after CS onset (Methods, Head-fixed behavior, "presented 2 s after the onset of the CS"). The Methods say about half the CSs were unpaired; with about 60 cues per airpuff session where the Methods give about 30 pairings, up to half the trials analysed may be unpaired, and the files cannot tell them apart. This is an assumption.fig3l-nt-sensor-airpuff-decrease
  • Same processing choices as fig3k-nt-sensor-sucrose-increase (median filter at 20 Hz, per-trial z-score adopted with the session one shown, regression on the trial-averaged baseline with the pooled fit shown, channels from the frame order).fig3l-nt-sensor-airpuff-decrease
  • Same trial assumption and processing choices as fig3k-nt-sensor-sucrose-increase and fig3l-nt-sensor-airpuff-decrease.fig3-nt-sensor-early-dip
  • The paper analyses sorted single units (Offline Sorter); the files' units are used as they are.fig4-recording-count
  • Which light block is the fake session is not labelled. Block 1 is taken as the fake one (Methods, In vivo electrophysiology, "a phototagging session was conducted after a 'fake' phototagging session"); the reverse, and either order in H31 to H42, are run as readings and agree.fig4c-photoidentification
  • The files hold light events of 5, 10 and 100 ms and none of 1 s; the 100 ms events stand in for the long light in reading (a). Reading (b) does not use them and gives the same outcome.fig4c-photoidentification
  • The count is compared as a total, since the projection labels are missing.fig4c-photoidentification
  • The smoothing window is run from 20 ms to 2 s, as the paper gives only MATLAB's smoothing factor (Methods, Functional hierarchical clustering, "a Gaussian-weighted moving average with a smoothing factor of 0.85"); each CS trace is smoothed separately.fig4d-seven-clusters
  • The six sessions without neutral CS trials (H09 to H14) are left out, since the clustered trace concatenates the three CSs (Methods, "Data from each experiment protocol (Sucrose CS, Neutral CS, Shock CS) were concatenated").fig4d-seven-clusters
  • The pose is put on the trial clock by a linear map fitted to the port entries (nb.fit_pose_clock), since the files give only a start time and a nominal 15 frames per second; this is an assumption. H11, H14, H17, H23, H25, H29, H39 and H40, where the map does not hold, and H28 and H32, whose recorded session has no pose, are left out.fig5dh-behavior-clusters
  • tsfresh 0.16.0 does not install with Python 3.13; tsfresh 0.21.2 with its comprehensive default feature set is used (6264 features per trial, the paper's "~6000").fig5dh-behavior-clusters
  • Features that are not finite or are constant are dropped. Whether the features were scaled before PCA is not said (Methods, Unsupervised trial-based classification of behaviors); both are run and give the same outcome. z-scoring is the reading adopted for the measured counts, since on the raw features 10 features carry all the variance of the 30 PCs and the counts change with the PCA solver and with changes of 1e-9 in the features.fig5dh-behavior-clusters
  • The criterion's "defaults of scikit-learn" are read as those of the installed release (1.9, PCA initialisation, automatic learning rate), as the criterion says; the defaults of the releases contemporary with tsfresh 0.16.0 (2020; random initialisation, learning rate 200) are run as a reading and give the same outcome. Under either set the tsfresh release is 0.21.2, not 0.16.0. The kernel density is seaborn's default (scipy gaussian_kde, Scott's bandwidth, a 200 by 200 grid reaching 3 bandwidths beyond the data); each local maximum is a watershed marker, so the count of maxima is the count of regions. Counting only maxima above 5% of the highest gives the same counts.fig5dh-behavior-clusters
