Most of this paper's main findings cannot be tested in this dataset, and the RNA-seq results that can be tested only partly hold. The dandiset's NWB files hold no images or measurements, and the behaviour videos have no labels or scored times, so the PNN loss in CA2 and the social memory deficits and their rescue could not be tested. In the RNA-seq counts from GEO, the 7 month immune upregulation and the PNN-gene and remodeling-gene directions held. The 3 month gene counts and learning and memory terms depend on undescribed DESeq2 and enrichment choices. At 7 months the 314 up genes held (284 to 320), but the 6 down genes did not (0 to 7). The ECM term claim rests on one marginal reading.

    fig4e-7m-go-immune-terms-up

    Under all 16 readings of model, fold change, background and term-size limit, five to eight of the top 10 terms name immune or defense responses or immune activation with positive z-scores, and the smallest top-10 z-score is 4.47. The notebook prints the top 10 of every reading.

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • The counts are GEO GSE317499 (GSE317499_counts.csv.gz, read and checked against its SHA-256), not data in the dandiset; 4 mice per age and genotype, from GEO, as the paper does not give n.
    • DESeq2 1.44.0 in R is replaced by pydeseq2 0.5.4 (Python), with DESeq2's defaults (Cooks refit, independent filtering, Benjamini-Hochberg); independent filtering is tuned at alpha 0.1, the default of R's results(), set explicitly as pydeseq2's default is 0.05. Its shrinkage is pydeseq2's apeglm-type prior. R cannot be pinned in the entry's locked environment, and the counts were not compared with R.
    • GO over-representation (hypergeometric, Benjamini-Hochberg, Biological Process) on the GO release 2024-06-17 (doi:10.5281/zenodo.12173881), a standard choice of a release near the paper's software versions; annotations propagated over is_a and part_of, NOT qualifiers dropped. The paper names GOplot but no enrichment tool, so the criterion's backgrounds (a) and (b) are both run.
    • The paper names R and GOplot but no enrichment tool (Methods 2.5), so the term-size limit is open; two readings are run, terms of 10 to 500 genes (clusterProfiler enrichGO's defaults) and no limit, giving 16 readings, which all agree with the DESeq2 readings and the two backgrounds.
    • Terms are counted as immune when their name contains immune, inflammatory or defense response, or an immune cell type followed by activation.
