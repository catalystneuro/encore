    table3-destabilizing

    Tested on the two sequence-based tools; the 15 values of mCSM, SDM and DUET need the I-TASSER models of Methods 2.3, which are not deposited. MuPRO (web server, sequence only) gives a negative DDG for all 5 variants and matches Table 3 for 3 of 5 (p.Leu740Phe -0.930, p.Asp423Glu -0.478, p.Arg131Cys -0.627); it gives p.Cys1019Tyr -0.887 (Table 3 -1.266) and p.Leu249Phe -1.2666 (Table 3 -1.525), so Table 3's p.Cys1019Tyr value is MuPRO's p.Leu249Phe value, which the Word document gives for p.Leu249Phe. I-Mutant 2.0 (web server, sequence, 25 C, pH 7) gives a positive, stabilizing DDG for 4 of 5 (p.Leu740Phe +0.19, p.Asp423Glu +0.88, p.Cys1019Tyr +0.14, p.Leu249Phe +0.55) and -1.22 for p.Arg131Cys; for IMPG2 these are Table 3's magnitudes (0.14, 0.55) with the sign reversed, and -1.22 is the Word document's value (Table 3 -1.13). Its sign classifier calls p.Asp423Glu an increase in stability (reliability 8 of 10). So under either I-Mutant output not every variant is destabilizing by every tool, and the claim's first part fails on a tool the paper names. The ranking cannot be tested on all five tools; p.Leu249Phe is the most destabilizing under MuPRO and p.Arg131Cys under I-Mutant. On Table 3's own values every entry is negative and p.Leu249Phe has the most negative mean (-1.25). The Word document holds 23 of the 25 values; 20 agree within 10%, and it gives SDM +0.58 (Stabilizing) for p.Asp423Glu where Table 3 prints -0.52. The outcome rests on the claims file's reading that iMutant is run from sequence; the paper does not name the mode, neither sequence output gives Table 3's IMPG1 values (-0.85, -0.76), and the regression and the classifier disagree in sign for p.Leu740Phe, p.Cys1019Tyr and p.Leu249Phe, so the paper may have used the structure mode on the undeposited models (asked of a reader in questions/001882.yaml).

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • mCSM, SDM and DUET were not run: they need the I-TASSER models, which are not deposited, and a rerun of I-TASSER (a queued web server with stochastic output) was not tried. 15 of the 25 values are therefore not computed.
    • MuPRO and I-Mutant 2.0 were run on their web servers as they answer today (no version can be pinned), on the UniProt sequences Q17R60 v129 and Q9BZV3 v146, the criterion's reading; I-Mutant at its default 25 C and pH 7, in both its DDG regression and sign classifier modes.
    • Assumption. iMutant is run from sequence, as the criterion says. The paper does not say so; Methods 2.4 gives only the server's address, its reference 46 is 'I-Mutant2.0, Predicting stability changes upon mutation from the protein sequence or structure', and Methods 2.3 built I-TASSER models 'for further analysis'. The structure mode cannot be run, since the models are not deposited, so the outcome rests on the sequence reading and on one tool, in one mode, on a server with no version. Under MuPRO alone every value is negative.
