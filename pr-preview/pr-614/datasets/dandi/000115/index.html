Gillespie et al (2021) Hippocampal replay reflects specific past experiences rather than a plan for subsequent choice

behavioral electrophysiological Norway rat Rat; norway rat; rats; brown rat 9.1 TB 57 files
  • ✓2 reproduced
  • ⊘31 blocked

Only the behavioural findings could be tested, and they held; the replay findings could not be tested. In all four rats the reward rate after a goal change started at or below chance and rose (from at most 0.133 on trial 1 to at least 0.584 by trial 15), and the session and block counts matched the paper's within 10%. The central findings that ripple replay favours the previous goal, persists across blocks and does not predict the next choice could not be tested. The dandiset holds only raw broadband, with no spikes, ripple times or decoder output, and streaming it was too slow.

Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds the raw 30 kHz broadband, port events, position and task logs, but none of the derived data the paper's findings rest on: no spike times or waveform features, LFP, ripple or multiunit event times, linearised position or decoder output. 31 of the paper's 33 claims need ripples or clusterless decoding recomputed from about 9.1 TB of raw data, which cannot be streamed within the registry's limits.
  • Description missingThe electrode table gives the location of all 128 channels as None in every file, so the CA1 cell-layer tetrodes used for ripple detection and decoding, and the corpus callosum tetrode used as LFP reference, cannot be identified from the data.
  • Selection not documentedThe files do not mark which run sessions the paper analysed. They hold 27, 32, 24 and 33 run epochs for jaq, roquefort, despereaux and montague, where the paper used 26 (24 after excluding sessions 1 and 3 of subject 1), 32, 23 and 33, and the excluded sessions are named only by number. Which jaq and despereaux epoch the paper did not count cannot be told: possible ones are jaq day 7 epoch 2 (6 rewards in 74 valid trials, no outerreps printed) and the epochs shorter than the Methods' 45 minutes (jaq day 6 epoch 4 and day 9 epoch 6, despereaux day 4 epoch 4). The paper's code repository (LorenFrankLab/Gillespie_Neuron_2021) holds a saved filter output with per-animal epoch lists that may identify them; it was not used here.

Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • Trials are rebuilt from the StateScript logs in the files (port entries, the printed goal, reward and timeout lines), not from the paper's preprocessed trial structures; rewards match the DIO pump onsets in 115 of 116 epochs.fig1d-sessions-blocks
  • Under session reading (b), the session with fewer than 75% of movement frames decoded on the same segment cannot be identified without decoding, which is blocked; every candidate is run and all give the same outcome.fig1d-sessions-blocks
  • Session selection by decode quality (the code's gooddecode) cannot be applied without decoding; all run epochs are counted, less the two excluded for jaq.fig1d-sessions-blocks
  • Trials are rebuilt from the StateScript logs, as for fig1d-sessions-blocks, with the same session readings.fig1ef-reward-rate
  • The criterion's "rises" is judged as trial 15 above trial 1 with non-overlapping S.E.M. bands and a positive Spearman correlation of rate with trial number; both are printed.fig1ef-reward-rate
