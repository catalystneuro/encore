Here is the paragraph so you can save it yourself: The main findings held in part in this single session of one monkey. Sigmoidal increments of produced intervals, more bias for the Long prior, and the sigmoidal mean neural projection held; opposite biases at the overlapping interval, slopes below one in every condition, and the inverted-U variance across intervals did not. The neural projection matched the Bayesian estimate, though random axes often fit nearly as well. The recurrent network results and monkey G could not be tested, because the dandiset holds one session of monkey H and no trained networks. Minor claims on speed and trajectory geometry gave mixed results.

    alt-speed-unmodulated

    Judged on the paper's test as the claims file fixes; signed-rank p = 0.125 for each prior, the smallest a test over 4 datasets can give, so the outcome carries no evidence for the absence of modulation. All 8 slopes are positive, but noise produces slopes of this size, as later bins average fewer trials. For Poisson units at constant rates with the same trials and Set times, the mean null slope is above the observed one in 8 of 8 and no observed slope exceeds the null's 95th percentile. With only the trials of the longest ts, which last the whole support, slopes are -0.106 to +0.529 per 100 ms and outside the null's 95% range in 1 of 8 (below it). The bootstrap intervals over trials are biased upward (the point estimate is below the interval in 2 of 8) and are not used. The observed slopes are below the null mean in 8 of 8 with all trials and in 8 of 8 with fixed trials, while the null's mean speed over the support is below the observed one in 7 of 8 (null / observed 0.65 to 1.18), so the null is not noisier than the data. This points to a speed that falls during the support, if the units' noise is no larger than Poisson; it was not tested further. The session shows no speed increase beyond what noise produces.

    glm-prior-ts

    36 of 48 units are prior-dependent (75.0%, 95% interval 61.2 to 85.1%) and 22 of 48 are ts-dependent (45.8%, interval 32.6 to 59.7%), against 65% and 27% for monkey H. Both are outside tolerance and higher; the interval of the prior fraction includes 65%, that of the ts fraction excludes 27%.     alt-threshold-monotonic

    With the components reaching 60% of the variance (1 to 3), the median time of the minimum distance is 210 ms before Go; 6 of 32 curves have their minimum in the last 40 ms and 14 in the last 200 ms. Distances do fall overall (the distance at Go is below that 800 ms earlier in 26 of 32). With 75% of the variance the median minimum is 80 ms before Go and in the full space 40 ms, which would meet the criterion, so the outcome depends on the number of components. With one component (eye datasets, 16 curves) the signed difference changes sign at the minimum in 12, so those minima are crossings; 4 have the minimum at Go (3 of them not crossings), and the distance at Go is more than 10 times the minimum in 5. With at least 2 components the median minimum is 230 ms before Go, with at least 3 it is 70 ms before Go.

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • The paper's test (one-tailed signed-rank, p = 1.5e-4) cannot be read unambiguously; the criterion on the median time of the minimum was fixed before the analysis.
    • The 60% of variance follows the figure legend; the 75% of the Methods is shown as a variant.

    gpfa-variance-inverted-u

    The quadratic coefficient is negative in 5 of 8 dataset-priors; one-tailed signed-rank p = 0.125 (paper 0.0273). Cross-validation chose 8 latent dimensions in all 4 datasets, the larger candidate. 12 latents, scored on the same folds as a variant, beat 8 in all 4 datasets; with them the coefficient is negative in 5 of 8, p = 0.125. After 100 EM iterations the training log-likelihood still rose by 0.064 to 0.085% over the last 25 iterations.

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • Latent dimensionality chosen only from 4 and 8, with 100 EM iterations, to keep the run short; the paper's cross-validation range is not stated.
    • One GPFA per effector and direction with both priors; the paper does not say how trials were grouped.

Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds one recording session (2016-12-11) of monkey H, with 54 units, where the paper analyses 17 sessions of H and 12 of monkey G (617 and 741 units recorded, 536 and 636 kept for analysis). The dandiset's description does not say that it holds one session of the paper's 29 or which one. The 48 units that pass the paper's rate rule match its count for the single session of H used for GPFA.
  • Data missingFollowing the Neural Latents Benchmark design, 283 trials of the session are in the test file without ts, tp, prior, effector or direction and without 14 of the 54 units, on a separate clock. These trials cannot be used for any analysis of the paper.
  • Metadata errorThe description of the `iti` column says 500 ms after Short prior trials and 1220 ms after Long ones; the values are the reverse (Short 1220, Long 500), as the paper states.
  • Description missing`tp` is described as the time between Set and Go, but is a median 17.9 ms longer than go_time minus set_time (5th to 95th percentile 1.1 to 29.8 ms, up to 200.6 ms in used trials). `ts` is the nominal interval, within 23.5 ms of ready_time minus set_time. Neither difference is documented.
  • Selection not documentedThe `is_outlier` flag is described only as a probabilistic mixture model. It flags 46 completed trials (4.47%); a refit of the Methods' Gaussian and uniform mixture per prior, ts, effector and direction flags 10 of these and no others, and the other 36 have tp from 0.53 to 1.60 times ts.
  • Description missingUnits carry no single-unit or multi-unit label and no electrode or probe; the electrode table has no positions or impedances and gives the filtering as unknown. The paper distinguishes 456 single units from 902 multi-units.
  • Methods not availableThe paper's analysis code is available only on request. The code the dandiset links (nlb_tools) loads the data and scores benchmark models, and holds none of the paper's analyses.
  • Paper inconsistentFor the single high-yield session of each monkey used for GPFA, the Results give the number of neurons as N = 48 for monkey H and N = 107 for monkey G, the Methods as N = 48 in H and 96 in G.

Reproduction Bayesian computation through cortical latent dynamics (Sohn et al. 2019)

Tests the findings of Sohn, Narain, Meirhaeghe and Jazayeri (2019) on dandiset 000130, the Neural Latents Benchmark's DMFC_RSG set, which holds one session of monkey H performing the Ready-Set-Go task with 54 units from dorsomedial frontal cortex. It tests the behaviour and the Bayesian observer model, single-unit sensitivity to the prior and elapsed time, the geometry and speed of population trajectories, the alternative mechanisms, and single-trial GPFA trajectories. Monkey G and the recurrent network models are not in the dandiset. All files
