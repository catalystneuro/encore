Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • OtherDataset KI is split across two dandisets. 001260 holds 74 recordings, 41 mice and 20,374 units; the other 25 recordings (25 mice, 9,408 units) are in 000473, whose title and metadata name a different paper (Esr1+ hypothalamic-habenula neurons). Neither dandiset says it holds part of dataset KI.
  • Selection not documentedThe two dandisets hold 29,782 units, the paper's count before its unit quality control, and no field marks the 24,248 units the paper kept. The paper's quality flags are only in its Zenodo metrics files.
  • Description missingThe units table's two-column `quality` is described only as "ISI violation, false positive rate". Column 0 runs from 0 to 1.15e5 (median 2), so it is not the ISI violation ratio of the Methods' threshold of 0.1, and its definition is not given.
  • Description missingThe behavioral setting of a recording is not a field; it can be read only from the session description and keywords. The flatmap position of each unit and the paper's ww/nw labels are not in the files and come from the paper's Zenodo record.
  • Metadata errorIn Detection and Context files the trials columns `lick` and `valve` are described as "sound amplitude"; in Detection files `Block` holds the trial outcome (miss, hit, correct rejection, false alarm), not a block. In a Context file `sound` is False for all 524 trials though 524 tones were played. Every electrodes column is described as "my description".
  • Metadata errorFor 283 of 20,374 units the location label in the files differs from the paper's Zenodo metrics files (207 of them in PL064_20200422-probe0), and the ww/nw label from the paper's differs for 3.0% of units when the Methods' thresholds are applied to the files' Peak_to_Trough.
  • Published code disagreesThe Methods detect sleep-like troughs below 0.2 of the geometric mean for longer than 5 ms; the code requires off periods of more than 50 ms and at most 0.6 s, pads only groups of at least 3 off periods by 1 s, fills free gaps of 1.5 s or less, and uses only PFC units with at least 75 of them. Both give more sleep-like time here (median 9.9% per session-pooled detection, 8.7% for the code's) than the paper's 6%.
  • OtherFollowing the Methods, sleep-like periods take a median 9.9% of each recording of 001260 (6% in the paper; per-probe detection gives 20.0%). The paper's own detection files on Zenodo give a median 5.3% over 73 recordings and mark 19 recordings as not using the detection (off_used = no), a selection step the Methods do not describe; where the paper used it, the Methods detector marks a median 15.9% of the time here.
  • Paper inconsistentThe Methods require "≥4 ISIs in at least 12 separate 3-s epochs" for unit quality and compute burstiness and memory in epochs "with at least six spikes (corresponding to at least four ISIs)", though six spikes give five ISIs. The Methods also say 99 probes were lowered and 24 recordings excluded, while Data availability gives 99 recorded sessions, the number the two dandisets hold.
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the authors. The paper numbers its spontaneous categories by its own Ward clusters (code, a memory ranking and a fixed permutation) and describes them in words; it gives no rule that carries the numbering to a retrained SOM. Under the code's rule and under a match to the text's descriptions the outcomes of 8 enrichment and hierarchy claims differ, and they are blocked. Projected on the paper's own trained SOM (Zenodo SOMs.zip), the units take categories that match the text, and 4 of the 8 claims hold. Should a reanalysis project units on the trained SOM, or which rule should it use?
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the authors. Which test supports "firing metric distributions were not significantly different across behavioral settings"? A mixed model with setting as a fixed effect finds differences here (for example Aversion against Passive in B, p = 0.0021).
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the authors. Which recordings had an inherent block structure for the stability analysis, and how is it stored in the files? The Block column holds block numbers in some settings and the trial outcome in Detection.
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the authors. The Methods say all sleep-like periods were reviewed visually; the paper's detection files (Zenodo quiet_active_detection.zip) mark 19 of 73 recordings off_used = no. What decided that a recording's detection was not used, and should a reanalysis repeat it from the EMG and LFP in the files?
