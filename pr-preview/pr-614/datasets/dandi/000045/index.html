The paper's main findings on consistency across laboratories largely held: mice in seven labs performed alike at proficiency, a classifier could not identify the lab, and mice used block priors in the full task. Learning speed was partly matched, with labs differing in days to proficiency but more weakly than reported. Only 83 mice reached basic proficiency against 140, because the dataset lacks mice trained before June 2019. Predicting slow learners from early sessions could not be tested, as the paper does not describe the method. Some secondary lab comparisons in the full task did not hold.

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds 178 mice and 6,615 sessions from 2019-06-11 to 2020-03-23. The paper's mice began training in January 2019 (206 mice, 10,903 sessions), so 41 mice of the project begin mid-training in the dandiset and the mice trained before June 2019 are missing.
  • Data missingThe training status of each session (in_training, trained_1a, trained_1b, ready4ephysrig and so on), on which every analysis of the paper selects sessions, is not in the files. It has to be recomputed from the trials, which leaves open details of the criteria.
  • OtherOne recording is deposited twice, under two mice (CSHL_013 and CSHL_015, 2019-07-15, identical trials and start time).
  • Metadata errorprobabilityLeft is described as the probability of a left stimulus for the current block, but in every basic-task session it varies from trial to trial between 0 and 1 (24% of basic-task trials are not 0.5), with the stimulus side following it.
  • Metadata errorsession_start_time is stated in UTC (+00:00) but holds local clock times; all seven labs, from London to Berkeley, have median start hours between 11.0 and 14.4 h.
  • File format errorIn 807 files the wheel position data and its timestamps differ in length (for example 181,623 samples against 340,799 timestamps); 16 files have no wheel series. In one session checked (ibl_witten_07), the wheel moves the opposite way to the choice on every trial.
  • Other257,021 trials (6%) have no stimulus onset time, and 185 sessions have none; 1,541 trials in 220 sessions have a response time before the stimulus onset (down to -5,638 s). No proficiency window of this attempt fails for the missing onsets alone.
  • OtherOne session (ZM_1367, 2019-06-11) uses contrasts of 4% and 8%, which the paper does not describe.
  • Paper inconsistentThe Methods give the 1a/1b performance criterion on easy trials and the Results give the lapse criterion as a maximum of 0.2 for the sum of the lapses, while Appendix 1, table 1d gives performance on 100% contrast and a bound on each lapse rate. After the answers of 2026-10-22 the claims file follows the pipeline's code for performance (easy trials) and the table for the lapses; with performance on 100% contrast only, 84 mice reach P1 against 83.
  • Description missingThe dandiset's metadata do not say which of its 178 mice are the paper's; one mouse (SWC_029) belongs to another project by its subject record.
