Most findings that could be tested held. Object location was represented mainly in MM, preparatory lick direction activity mainly in ALM, and PT neurons showed earlier lick direction selectivity than IT neurons, though only under one onset rule, bin size and bootstrap. The fraction of trial-type-selective neurons in MM did not match (26.3% against 23%). The shift of peak activity from MM to ALM did not appear when the peak was taken over the whole window, which the paper does not define. Findings that depend on layer could not be tested because the files hold no plane depth, and the wide-field, photoinhibition and silicon probe findings could not be tested because those data are not in the dataset.

Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • OtherEvery one of the 105 image planes is deposited twice, as two files with different names, identifiers and digests (for example an041_2014-08-23_21 and an041_2014-08-23_43) whose contents are otherwise identical. The archive's file count (210) is twice the number of recordings.
  • Metadata errorImagingPlane.location is "ALM" in every file, including the 47 planes whose experiment_description says left_vm1 (the paper's MM). The area per plane is given only by experiment_description, and with it the counts match the paper (8,837 ALM and 6,594 MM ROIs).
  • Description missingThe files hold no plane depth, no stereotactic coordinates of the planes or cells, and no identifier of the imaging location within a mouse; no text value in any of the 105 planes (descriptions, keywords and attributes included) names a depth, a coordinate or a layer. Four of the paper's findings rest on depth and cannot be tested, nor can the depth parts of three others; the 12 imaging locations cannot be counted.
  • Data missingThe dandiset holds only the two-photon imaging. The wide-field imaging, the whisker tracking, the lick times, the photoinhibition behaviour and the silicon probe recordings (567 units) are not in it. The paper's Key Resources Table names CRCNS doi 10.6080/K0S180F9 for its imaging and electrophysiology, and that DOI resolves to a placeholder record that says the data are not yet available.
  • Description missingThe ROI table's `included` column ("whether to include this roi into later analyses") marks 8,655 of 15,431 ROIs, without saying what excluded the others; the paper's counts use all 15,431.
  • Description missingThe trial table's start_time is 0.623 s before the sample event in every trial, while the paper's trial start is pole movement onset (the sample event, 4.2 s before the go cue). The offset is not described. Early-lick trials have instruction and outcome "non-performing", so their pole position is not recorded.
  • OtherWith the stored ROI and neuropil traces and the paper's r = 0.7, the baseline F0 is zero or negative on 0.26% of ROI-trials, in 1,262 ROIs, and dF/F reaches thousands; the mean dF/F of MM is dominated by these ROIs. The paper does not say how they were treated (a reader confirmed this in answer to a question).

Reproduction Anticipatory activity in mouse motor cortex (Chen et al. 2017)

Tests the findings of Chen, Li, Daie and Svoboda (2017) on dandiset 000015, which holds two-photon imaging of 105 image planes in left ALM and MM of six mice during a tactile delayed response task, with PT and IT labels in three of them. It tests the trial and neuron counts, the selectivity of single neurons for object location, lick direction and outcome, its map over ALM and MM, and the coding of PT and IT neurons. The plane depth, wide-field imaging, whisker tracking, photoinhibition and silicon probe data are not in the dandiset. Also uses DANDI:000010. All files
