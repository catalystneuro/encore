The paper's membrane recovery findings mostly hold in this data, which covers only its two-pulse experiment, so most other findings could not be tested. Test-pulse charging stays cosine-shaped and unchanged after mild conditioning pulses and falls after the strongest ones, as reported. The fall differs in size from the paper's at the cathodic pole, and at the anodic pole it is not significant after the weaker of the two strongest pulses. Full resealing after mild pulses and residual leak after strong ones hold, but the test pulse also finds a pole difference after the strongest pulse. These results depend on an automatic membrane trace, and the dye calibration, escalation, Gd3+ and calcium findings are absent from the dataset.

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • The perimeter trace is found automatically, where the paper draws "the 6-pixel-wide trace ... along the cell perimeter" by hand in MetaMorph (Methods 2.3). The membrane at each angle is the intensity peak within 8 px inside the cell's outer edge (the steepest outward fall of intensity), an assumption adopted after the first round's plain intensity peak was seen to fall inside two cells; that trace is shown as a variant, and so are windows of 4, 6, 10 and 12 px.
    • The analysed cell is the largest object in each subject's field (an assumption; the paper says one cell per experiment, Fig. 2 legend, and does not say how it was chosen).
    • The field direction in image coordinates is not given by the dataset; it is the A-weighted circular mean of cosine-fit phases over all 5-us files (-75.6 degrees, per-file sd 6.8), and from the control files alone as a variant (-73.9 degrees). 0 degrees is the depolarized pole, as Fig. 7A plots it.
    • Conditioning strengths are mapped by rank from the files' generator volts (100 to 350 V) to the Fig. 7A labels 360 to 1400 V/cm, and the single 50 V pulse, recorded first in every cell, is taken as the 150/150 benchmark; an assumption, as the files give no field strengths and no 150/150 pair. Read as steps of 100 V from 0.15 kV/cm at 50 V, the strengths in the files' general description would put the levels 0 to 11% lower, with the same order.
    • Six 5-us cells are in the dataset where the paper reports n = 5; all six are used, and each five-cell subset is shown.
    • Background and dark frames are open in the paper. Main reading adopted as an assumption, background removed (median of an annulus 1.6 to 2.2 radii) and frames without a laser flash dropped; the paper's own scale (Fig. 7A about 13% dF at 150/150; 9.1% dF per 100 mV in Results 3.3, about 16% dF at 150 V/cm) is met only with a background removed (12.6 to 12.9% against 5.6%), and the files' camera floor is about 209 counts under a membrane trace of 451 to 692.     • The criterion's pole dF is tested under the pole readings of fig7b-test-pulse-unchanged-to-760 (cathodic, anodic, mean of both), with its test, the unpaired two-sample t-test against the control group (the paired test shown as a variant); reading (a) compares the fit at each pole with the s.e. of the pole mean over cells, for the legend's gray outline.

Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds only the two-pulse Di-8-ANEPPS experiment of Fig. 7A and the bottom panel of Fig. 7B. The FluoVolt, single-pulse escalation, 0.3-us, Gd3+ and Fluo-4 calcium experiments behind Figs. 1 to 6 and the top panel of Fig. 7B are not in it; 22 of the paper's 27 claims cannot be tested here, and one only in part.
  • Description missingPulses are given in generator volts (50 to 350 V), not in the field strengths (V/cm) the paper reports and computes with Sim4Life, and no mapping is given. The mapping to the Fig. 7A labels (150, 360, 570, 760, 1000, 1200, 1400 V/cm) can only be inferred by rank.
  • Metadata errorEvery file gives the distance between electrodes as 1.00 mm; the paper (Methods 2.2) gives a spacing of 1.6 mm.
  • Metadata errorThe general description shared by every file lists field strengths of 0.15, 0.52, 0.9, 1.27 and 1.57 kV/cm, single pulses at each, and inter-pulse intervals of 1, 5, 25 and 125 us. The files hold single pulses only at 50 V, six conditioning amplitudes (100 to 350 V) and intervals of 1 and 5 us only.
  • OtherThe paper's benchmark for Fig. 7 is a 150 V/cm test pulse after an identical 150 V/cm conditioning pulse (Results 3.7); the files hold no 50 V plus 50 V pair, only a single 50 V pulse, which is the only candidate for the benchmark. Three cells (c4, c5, c6) have two single-pulse files.
  • Selection not documentedSix cells (c5 to c10) have the paper's 5-us interval where Fig. 7 reports n = 5, and nothing says which five were used. Four more cells (c1 to c4) have a 1-us interval, which the paper does not report.
  • Metadata errorThe image series description says exposures began 1 us after the start of recording; the images show the pulse from image 40 (6.7 s at 6 Hz), after 40 baseline images, as Methods 2.3 describe (best split at image 40 in 43 of 73 files, at 39 to 41 in 56).
  • Other70 of 73 files have a dark first image (field mean about 209 counts against 276), and 22 more dark images fall elsewhere in the sets; these are images with no laser flash. Nothing in the files marks them. Keeping or dropping them shifts the profile mean dF at the control by 2.17 percentage points under the main, background-subtracted reading (-2.09% kept, -4.26% dropped) and by 1.33 without a background, and the cosine amplitude by 0.0 to 0.3 points; the no-flash level (about 209 counts) shows a camera floor under every image that a dF without background subtraction includes.
  • Description missingThe files give no pixel size, no direction of the electric field in image coordinates, and no position of the analysed cell, while each field holds several cells. The field direction can be recovered from the polarization (per-file phases within 6.8 degrees sd).
  • Methods not availableFor the paper's authors, whether a background was subtracted from the potentiometric images and whether images without a laser flash were kept when the 40 baseline and 60 pulse images were collapsed. Background removal doubles the dF scale here (5.6% to 12.6% at 150/150), and only the background-subtracted scale matches the paper's.
  • OtherThe images fade through each set of 100. Over the 40 baseline images the membrane trace intensity (background removed) falls by about 0.10% per image in 43 of 44 files of the 5-us cells, which would predict a uniform dF of -5.16% over the 60 pulse images; the profiles' mean dF is -3.67% over these files and -4.3% at the control, and the two correlate weakly over files (r 0.18). The sign is that of the Di-8-ANEPPS bleaching the paper reports (Results 3.2), the size does not match, and the offset's source is not established; nothing in the files corrects or marks it.
