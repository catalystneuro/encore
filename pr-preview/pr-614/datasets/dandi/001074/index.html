    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • The dandiset gives no sensor labels. Labels are made from the identification traces by the Methods' procedure (Quantification and statistical analysis, paragraphs 2-3) with these choices, assumed where the Methods do not fix them. The jump artefact is an exponential with the Methods' 30 s decay scaled by the jump measured as in morse_figs3_rev cell 23. The exponential background is fitted only when two wells precede activation, with its decay bounded by the fitted segment. The ligand administration time course used to match clusters is a boxcar over the first minute of each well (a whole-well boxcar is shown as a variant), and a match is kept only when the cluster's mean derivative also peaks in that ligand's well. This peak rule is the author's own, not in the Methods; it leaves 47 cells unlabelled in reading (b) and 18 in reading (a), and results without it are shown.
    • The code's hand corrections of clusters (morse_figs3_rev cells 73-78) are not repeated, so the labelled cells are not the paper's 506.
    • The time base is the code's 31 frames per minute (93 per well); the files state 30 Hz.

Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Description missingNo file gives the sensor expressed by each cell, though the identities were made by post hoc dipping and every analysis of the paper rests on them. No file gives well, wash-on, KCl, optogenetic or injection times, the ligand of each well, the brain region of a slice or the injections of a mouse; there is no acquisition or stimulus data. Only the in vitro dipping of the ten revision probes could be labelled, from the paper's code.
  • Metadata errorEvery file states an imaging rate of 30 Hz and timestamps from 0 in steps of 1/30 s, the same session start (2023-01-01T12:30), the indicator OGB-1 and the location "brain". The Methods give about 0.5 volumes per second (31 per minute in the code for the 2025 recordings, 52 per minute for some 2023 ones), the sensors are GRAB sensors, and the dipping recordings are of HEK cells in a hydrogel, not of a mouse brain. The session dates are only in the subject names.
  • File format errorThe 3D image masks are stored scrambled. Each is the Fortran-order flattening of the (x, y, z) volume written into a C-order array of the same shape, so read as stored every mask spans the full width of the volume with equal weight in every plane; reordered with order="F" it is a compact cell.
  • Metadata error67 files belong to a subject named "0" with session names that carry no context. 16 of them repeat named files of 20230922-PL28, 20230829-SL1PL56 and 220408-PK27PL20; the other 51 cannot be placed.
  • Metadata errorThe six files of sub-250918-SL1ZS5-slice and of sub-250918-SL1ZS5-wells001 are identical in shape and values, so one recording of that probe is deposited under both names and the other is missing.
  • Metadata errorThe matched-file suffixes of probe 20230922-PL28 do not fit its pairs. wells201 holds a "matched" and a "matched1" file that are copies, and the "matched" file of wells401 has 96 cells, the count of wells001's "matched3", where wells001's "matched" has 82.
  • OtherThe matched pair of probe 251119-ZS16 has 143 cells in wells001 and 142 in wells101; the paper's code deletes row 53 of the identification file (morse_figs3_rev cell 6), which nothing in the files records.
  • Description missingThe accepted and rejected columns of every PlaneSegmentation are all false, so they carry no curation information, and the stage of each file (mr-raw, mr-m-raw, refit, matched<k>) is described only by the paper's code README.
  • OtherThe mask volumes have 16 to 26 planes, where the Methods describe 30 planes per volume (about 270 um); the files do not say whether planes were cropped.
  • Data missingThe deformation fields, raw two-photon imaging, Cellpose probability maps, fiber photometry, ELISA and mass spectrometry tables, choroid plexus imaging and histology are not deposited. The paper states that raw imaging and photometry are available on request; 13 claims cannot be tested for this reason.
  • Methods not availableQuestions for the authors. (1) Sensor identity. The Methods' clustering without the code's hand corrections (morse_figs3_rev cells 73-78) gives 384 or 559 labelled cells against 506 (382 in the first run); which corrections were made and why? (2) Dose-response normalization. The Methods say "corrected with an exponentially decaying background fit and then peak-normalized"; the code subtracts a baseline well and divides by a peak well, after per-cell background fits over hand-set windows (cells 285-293, 338-339). Which windows and wells were used, and is the noise cut of cells 343 and 365 part of the published fits? Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • The link claim and its criterion were written by the author during the analysis, after the frame counts were seen; its thresholds (more than half) are the author's.link-revision-well-order

  • Probes are pooled over all contexts (dipping, slice and in vivo recordings); Figure 1C shows one dipping probe.fig1c-cells-per-gel
  • The dandiset gives no sensor labels. Labels are made from the identification traces by the Methods' procedure (Quantification and statistical analysis, paragraphs 2-3) with these choices, assumed where the Methods do not fix them. The jump artefact is an exponential with the Methods' 30 s decay scaled by the jump measured as in morse_figs3_rev cell 23. The exponential background is fitted only when two wells precede activation, with its decay bounded by the fitted segment. The ligand administration time course used to match clusters is a boxcar over the first minute of each well (a whole-well boxcar is shown as a variant), and a match is kept only when the cluster's mean derivative also peaks in that ligand's well. This peak rule is the author's own, not in the Methods; it leaves 47 cells unlabelled in reading (b) and 18 in reading (a), and results without it are shown.fig1g-sensor-yield
