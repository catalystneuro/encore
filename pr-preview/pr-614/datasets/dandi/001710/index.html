Most of the paper's imaging and behavioral findings held in this data, a few did not, and several could not be tested. Cre mice licked less before the reward zone, place-code quality was unchanged, novelty-driven activity was lower in Cre mice, and reward cells were fewer. Narrower Cre fields held only partly, and the single-field and across-day backward shifts did not reproduce. Novelty preference and reward reversal could not be tested because those data are not deposited, and the synchronous-event and network-model findings were blocked by unspecified methods. All imaging results use dF/F, not the paper's deconvolved rates.
