Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingThe dandiset names this paper (IsPublishedIn) but holds none of its recordings. Its 292 files are FluoVolt strobe-imaging time courses at one 1-us pulse amplitude per cell, the protocol the paper attributes to its reference [20] (Semenov et al. 2025, Bioelectrochemistry 168, 109143). The paper's sets of 40 baseline and 60 pulse images at 900 ns with field escalation, and its Di-8-ANEPPS, 0.3-us, Gd3+, two-pulse and Fluo-4 Ca2+ recordings, are absent, so 33 of the 34 claims cannot be tested.
  • Description missingThe laser delay of each frame is not recorded; the files give only a start time and a nominal 6 Hz. The data show that the 70 frames are successive delays across the pulse (the response rises and falls over a median 19 frames at half peak, at frames 16 to 34 in forward files and at the same frames counted from the end in backward files), so the backward files hold the time course in reverse order. A step of about 50 ns is consistent with this but not shown.
  • Description missingPulses are given in volts across electrodes stated as 1.00 mm apart; no field strength is given. The paper uses electrodes 1.6 mm apart and field strengths from Sim4Life modelling, so its V/cm values cannot be assigned to these files. At the same 40 V the response differs between sessions (median peak index 25.9% on 2023-05-08, 11.9% on 2023-09-26).
  • Description missingEach subject has two files for each combination of recording order (forward, backward) and anode side, with nothing in the files to tell the two apart. Subject CHO-20230925-c1 has 4 files where the others have 8. Each file images a field of several cells (2 to 12 connected parts of the membrane mask; touching cells form one part), while the paper analyses one cell per experiment; no file says which cell, if any, the subject is.
  • Metadata errorFile sub-CHO-20230926-c1_obj-bcvqko shows a different field of view from the other files of its subject (median correlation of mean images 0.079, against 0.995 for files of one subject) and a session start of 2024-12-16, fourteen months after that subject's other files. Its response is weak (peak index 1.3%).
  • Metadata errorThe stated anode side does not fix the sign of the response. In 35 of 37 subjects the sign flips between the files stated as anode above and below, but 9 files differ in sign from the majority of the other files of their subject with the same stated anode side (groups with a tied majority are not counted), and across sessions files stated as anode above are brighter at the top of the cells (2023-05-08) or at the bottom (2023-09-25, 2023-09-26), and both in different subjects of 2023-10-10. The image orientation relative to the electrodes is not stated.
  • OtherIn 220 of 292 files frame 0 is less than 0.8 times as bright as the median frame (median ratio 0.59), which the description does not explain.
  • Metadata errorThe archive summary gives the species as 'Cricetulus griseus - Cricetulus aureus' for a CHO cell line (the files give NCBITaxon_10029, C. griseus only), and every subject has an age of P1D. The image series description mentions sine wave exposures, which no file holds.
  • Paper inconsistentEq. (2) with the constants of Methods 2.3 gives a 1.33% IMP reduction for a 10-fold rise of membrane conductivity, where Results 3.5 states about 2%; the 100-fold value (11.87%) matches the stated 12%.
