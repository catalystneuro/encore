The paper's local-circuit finding held in direction but not in its counts. Every VEN connection rate was below the matching pyramidal-cell rate, yet the data gave 26 connections against 31, and none of the four reported rates matched. The files carry no cell types or connection labels, so the counts rest on an annotation table from the paper's code that disagrees with the paper's totals. Lower sodium current, lower phase peak index, larger evoked responses and lower spontaneous event frequency in VENs reproduced on tables or sister dandisets. Soma distances could not be tested because no positions are stored, and the sequencing, morphology and modeling claims were not attempted. The file still needs to be created with this text.

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • Assumed. The Methods give the steps (-80 to 0 mV, 25 ms, leak subtracted online, 100 kHz) but neither a filter nor the window in which the peak is taken; unfiltered, the outcome changes between windows ending at 3 ms and at 5 or 10 ms. A 1 kHz low-pass (2nd-order Butterworth, forward and backward, the form of PatchAnaLab's filters; the paper's code applies filter_1000Lowpass to first-AP traces in extFig9ad_plotVENsubtypeAP_Cmp.m, line 35, and Fig4d_plotNaCurrent.m calls no filter, both printed in the notebook) is adopted because the peaks it gives reproduce the code's per-patch max_Na_current (r = 0.99); with it the window does not change the result.
    • Steps up to 0 mV and the baseline over the 2 ms before the step follow Fig4d_plotNaCurrent.m (nTraces = 17, test pulses from -80 to 0 mV; base over the first 2 ms of a snippet starting 2 ms before the step).
    • Reading (b), current per patch area from capacitance, cannot run; the files hold no capacitance. The Methods take every patch to be about 5 um2, so (b) divides every patch by the same area and gives the same test as (a).

Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Description missingThe files carry no cell type, no connection label and no soma position for any cell, and the stimulated cell of a file is not named correctly (below), so none of the Fig. 5 counts can be made from the dandiset alone. Types and connections exist only in the paper's code table metadata_multiPatch.xlsx, which links to the files by name.
  • Metadata errorEvery response series states a rate of 1 Hz; the data are sampled at 25 kHz (the stimulus series' rate, the code's 25 kHz, and the 50-sample pulse that is the Methods' 2 ms pulse). The stimulus series gives its rate of 25,000 with the unit millisecond.
  • Metadata errorThe stimulus amplitude is stored as 2 (2.5 or 3 in 14 files) with the unit amperes; the Methods inject 2 nA.
  • Metadata errorThe stimulus series of every file names electrode Elec_8 as the stimulated one, while the cell that fires on the pulse is on channel 8 in only 35 of 301 files; in 286 of the 288 files the code's table names, the firing channel is the table's presynaptic channel.
  • Metadata errorAll 301 files have the session start time 2018-01-01T01:00+08:00, while the slices are named for dates in 2019 and 2024, and every subject has the same age, P4Y/P6Y, and sex M.
  • Published code disagreesThe code's table metadata_multiPatch.xlsx disagrees with the paper's counts. It lists 298 cells, 1,416 ordered pairs (1,403 in files of the dandiset) and 26 connections, where the paper reports 377 neurons, 1,292 pairs and 31 connections; PC-to-PC 18 of 743 against 24 of 503, one VEN-to-PC connection (190315s2, channel 4 to 6, Excellent, with a 1 mV response in the data) where the paper reports none, and pair counts per class that differ from the paper's by more than 10% in 6 of 8 classes (all but VEN-to-IN and IN-to-VEN).
  • Published code disagreesThe code's table is inconsistent with itself and with the files. Ten of its presynaptic blocks name no NWB file (8 match a file of the dandiset by the firing channel; 190315s2 channel 7 and 20241014HS2 channel 8, 13 rows, match none), it gives 5 cells two types, labels 2 connections with a projection that names another postsynaptic channel (20240912HS5 4 to 1 labelled 4 to 3, 20241105HS16 6 to 3 labelled 8 to 5), and its Nr_Conn disagrees with its annotations in 4 of 53 slices. Six of its pairs name channels that read 0 mV in their file (slice 190314s1, channels 6 and 7). Eight of its Inh connections come from a PC, and 6 of its 12 Inh connections have a positive averaged response.
  • Other5 files of the dandiset, all of slice 20240912HS2 (23 to 28 sweeps), are not in the code's table, so it is not known whether the paper counted them.
  • OtherThe Methods average responses over more than 20 sweeps; 119 of 301 files hold more than 20 sweeps (median 20, range 1 to 30).
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the paper's authors. The paper and its code give no rule for calling a connection (amplitude, latency, noise or test), so the counts rest on the annotations of metadata_multiPatch.xlsx. Was the connection call made by eye on averaged traces, and with which criteria for Excellent, Good and OK quality? Do the 31 connections of the paper include the table's OK connections, and which of the table's pairs and cells make up the 1,292 pairs and 377 neurons?
  • Published code disagreessumData_StimWM.xlsx holds 23 VEN-PC pairs for Fig. 4j where the paper states 26, and AIScmp_PCvsVEN_Fig5c.xlsx holds 120 VENs and 500 PCs for Fig. 3c where the paper states 108 and 430; the AIS table has no slice or animal per cell, needed for the two-way ANOVA of the legend.
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the paper's authors. For the somatic Na+ currents of Fig. 4d, how was max_Na_current measured: which filter, and in which window after the step? Unfiltered peaks separate VENs from PCs when taken up to 3 ms after the step (p = 0.0074) and not up to 5 or 10 ms (p = 0.077 and 0.067); after a 1 kHz low-pass the peaks match the code's values (r = 0.99) and p = 0.0010 in every window. The attempt adopted the 1 kHz low-pass.
  • Published code disagreesThe code's processed tables for claims on other data differ in size from the paper. The Patch-seq mapping results hold 14 VEN-L and 15 VEN-S where the paper reports 10 and 11 mapped to L5 ET and L5/6 CT, and map the 273 excitatory neurons to 17 clusters where the paper states 18; the inhibitory Patch-seq mapping is not saved. PeakIdx_Fig5e.xlsx holds more cells per type than the Fig. 3e legend (VEN 32 against 22).
  • OtherThe paper quantifies 581 metabolites (Fig. 8a), while the MetaboLights deposit MTBLS13927 (revision MTBLS13927_01) holds 558 identified metabolites, the same as the code's Metabolic_data.xlsx; with them the AIC-V1 differential count is 175 to 195 under four readings, against the paper's 228.
  • OtherThe MetaboLights deposit MTBLS13927 and the code's Metabolic_data.xlsx disagree on which of two V1 samples is which: the intensities of V1-4-Q in the deposit are those of V1_4_S in the code's table, and the reverse. The deposit names the first AIC sample A1C-1-Q and stores the run mode (rppos, rpneg, hilicneg) in its charge column.
