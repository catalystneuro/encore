In this data the single-unit and circuit findings mostly held, but the evoked-potential extent (Figures 1 to 3) and the mouse detection task (Figure 7) could not be tested, since the raw AP band and behavior are not deposited. Direct responders were sparse, their response probability rose with amplitude, cortical recruitment grew with amplitude, and population dimensionality fell. Responders moved closer to the stimulation site with amplitude, but 5 and 100 uA recruiting distinct populations did not hold, because two recordings' contact coordinates differ from the paper's code. Fast-spiking versus regular-spiking differences and the cortical versus subcortical modulation difference held only under the code's classes and window, not the Methods'.

Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingThe raw AP band, LFP band and NI-DAQ files are not deposited (the paper says they are available on request), so the evoked-potential analyses of Figures 1 to 3, S1 and S2 cannot be repeated (11 claims blocked), and the comparisons of units with the evoked-potential boundaries (Figures 4F, 4I and 5I) can be made only with the boundaries the paper's code computed, taken here from its repository.
  • Data missingThe behaviour of the four trained mice (Figure 7 and S10) is not deposited on DANDI or elsewhere that the paper names; the paper's code reads it from local CSV files. 10 claims are blocked for this reason.
  • Published code disagreesThe stimulation contact coordinates of jlh31 and jlh32 in the trials table differ from those in the paper's code repository by 206 um (100, 150, -100) and 373 um (370, 0, -50); the other seven recordings agree exactly. With the code's coordinates the distance results of Figure 4H give the paper's statistics and most of its values (not the 50 uA extent, 868 against 965 um, or the 50 against 100 uA KS p, 0.8245 against 0.083); with the dataset's, the 5 against 100 uA KS test is no longer significant after correction.
  • Metadata errorThe file of session jlh49v1, which the paper and its code treat as a separate recording and mouse, is filed under subject jlh48. Its subject age (P381D) is one day more than that of the jlh48 file, which was recorded four months later.
  • Description missingThe electrodes table holds placeholders (location "none" for all 1152 channels, x 5 to 53, y 0, z 3820, impedance 0), so channel CCF coordinates and regions are not given; the paper's signed distances need them and here they are fitted from the units' coordinates. The stimulation contacts have coordinates but no region.
  • Description missingThe units table holds neither the waveform classes (FS, RS, up, axon) nor the stability flags the paper uses to select units; both are recomputed here (the stability filter gives the same number of units per cathodal and anodal condition as the code's intermediate table direct_df.csv, and 1 to 5 more or fewer per bipolar condition).
  • Selection not documentedThe dandiset holds ten recordings; the paper analyses nine. The tenth, jlh41 ("lots of noise issues ... not much analyzed yet here"), has no CCF coordinates or regions, and the dandiset does not say it was left out.
  • OtherCathodal single pulses are in seven of the nine recordings (jlh39 and jlh49v1 hold only bipolar pulses, jlh40 no bipolar), while the legends of the cathodal analyses give n = 9 recordings. jlh33 has 60 trials per condition, where the Methods state 75, and no anodal 50 uA single pulse. Trial and spike times of jlh40 and jlh53concat are negative in part.
  • Published code disagreesThe FS and RS classes of the paper's code are a 0.4 ms threshold on waveform duration (dlab classify_waveform_shape called with kmeans=0), where the Methods describe k-means on three normalised features. The two give 616 and 332 FS units and different outcomes for fig5j-fs-closer and figs6b-fsrs-early-firing.
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the authors. Were the FS and RS classes of Figures 5G to 5J and S6 assigned by k-means (Methods) or by the 0.4 ms duration threshold the code runs? This attempt assumed the threshold, whose classes give the paper's statistics exactly; k-means on the Methods' three features depends on its initialisation here (296 to 470 FS units over 20 runs), and under none of those runs are FS responders significantly closer than RS responders (p 0.087 to 0.401).
  • Published code disagreesFor Figure 6D the code's HVA group takes any CCF name with "layer" outside V1, which adds superior colliculus and dentate gyrus units to LM, AM, PM and other areas (151 good stable units in the nine recordings, 91 in the seven with cathodal pulses), and its subcortical list has no CA1 (315 good stable units); the legend names LM, AM and PM, and CA1, subiculum, dentate gyrus and superior colliculus. The paper's 15.82 comes from the code's groups; with the legend's groups HVA and subcortical modulation do not differ (13.91 against 13.26, Tukey p = 0.784).
  • Published code disagreesThe code's map_region_to_group looks for "posteromedial area", which matches no CCF name in the files ("posteromedial visual area"), so 16 PM units are left out of the cortical circuit responders of Figure 6E.
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the authors. The Methods compute the modulation index over 500 to 1000 ms with 5 ms bins, the Figure 6C legend over a 500 ms window from onset with 5 ms bins, and the code over 0 to 500 ms with 10 ms bins; the printed magnitudes are the code's. Which is meant? Over 500 to 1000 ms there is no cortical against subcortical difference (p = 0.457).
  • Metadata errorIn jlh53concat, a concatenated recording, the trials table's run numbers 0 to 7 are each used by a monopolar and a bipolar run, so a run number does not identify one condition; code that selects trials by run, as the paper's ZETA code does, takes 150 trials of both polarities.
  • OtherIn all nine recordings the population fires less in the 3 ms before the logged pulse onset, down to 0.07 to 0.20 spikes per trial from 2 to 1 ms before it (0.61 in jlh31), against 0.81 to 2.03 from 8 to 7 ms before; the trials table does not say whether the onset is logged after the pulse begins or spikes were removed around it.
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the authors. Was the 4 ms minimum peak width of the Figure 6F peak times meant to apply? The code's configuration sets it, but its conversion to 5 ms bins rounds it to one bin and skips it, and the Methods give no rule. Without it time to peak does not correlate with distance (r = -0.001 and 0.047); with it both correlate (r = 0.131, p = 0.001 and r = -0.115, p = 0.004).
