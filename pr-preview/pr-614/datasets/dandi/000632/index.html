The no-difference findings held in this data, the 770 kHz enhancement did not, and the Figure 2, 4 and 5 findings could not be tested. Co- and cross-directional pulses gave similar dye uptake at 1 Hz, cross-directional pulses at 770 kHz matched 1 Hz, and uptake did not depend on membrane area. Co-directional pulses gained 1.96-fold at 770 kHz on average but not significantly (p = 0.16), and did not significantly beat cross-directional ones. With few dishes the tests had little power, so they show only that no difference was found. The field-angle and voltage-imaging findings sit in other dandisets outside this attempt's scope.

    Under background reading (b) the co-directional dishes at 1 Hz (mean 665, n = 5) average 0.68 of the cross-directional ones (972, n = 6); two-tailed Student's t-test p = 0.32, so the test does not find a difference. Reading (a) gives ratio 0.75, p = 0.31; Welch's test and the variants (whole-disc footprint background, YP over MemBrite, center ROIs of 150 and 250 um, without dishes whose footprint centers are more than 400 um from a parallelogram, the deposit as labelled) give p from 0.28 to 0.68. By experiment day the comparison goes opposite ways, 0.45 on 2022-11-29 (p = 0.023, n = 3 and 3) and 1.43 on 2022-12-04 (p = 0.49, n = 2 and 3). The test has little power, so p >= 0.05 shows that no difference was found, not that the groups are equal. The 95% interval of the difference is -968 to 356 counts (co/cross 0.00 to 1.37), and the test would detect with 80% power only a difference of 922 counts, 95% of the cross-directional mean; at the observed difference (-306 counts, s.e. 293) its power is 16%. With the ROIs at the refined footprint centers the ratio is 0.72, p = 0.40.

  • OtherOne recording is deposited twice under two conditions. sub-BPAE-8unipolar-1Hz-04 (session Co-directional_1Hz) and sub-BPAE-8bipolar-1Hz-600ns-04 (session Cross-directional_1Hz) have identical chunk indexes, identical images and the same start time (2022-11-29 16:57). The start time and the image's pattern of uptake point to the cross-directional label, so the co-directional 1 Hz group holds 5 distinct dishes.
  • Metadata errorThe group sizes do not match the paper. By label the files are 6 co-directional 1 Hz, 6 cross-directional 1 Hz, 5 co-directional 770 kHz and 7 cross-directional 770 kHz (5, 6, 5 and 7 with the copy counted once), where the paper's Figure 3 reports n = 6 for each group. The 2022-12-04 series has four cross-directional and two co-directional dishes at 770 kHz, and lacks a number 04. One cross-directional dish more and one co-directional dish fewer than the paper's n = 6 at 770 kHz would follow from one mislabelled dish, but the four cross-directional 770 kHz dishes of 2022-12-04, the day with the extra dish, have anisotropy indexes of 0.06 to 0.18, below every co-directional dish (0.31 to 0.75), so the labels are used as deposited.
  • Metadata errorThe dandiset description and every session description say the electrodes were arranged in a triangular array. The images show four electrode footprints in a square, as the paper describes for this experiment (Methods 4.2, a 4-electrode square array).
  • Description missingThe files give no pixel size (the ImageSeries has no resolution or field of view) and name the channels only in a free-text description. A scale of 0.65 um per pixel had to be inferred from the camera and objective; under it the electrode footprints measure 1.59 mm against the 1.5 mm needles.
  • Description missingThe subject names carry undocumented tokens ('8bipolar', '8unipolar', '600ns'). 'bipolar' and 'unipolar' match the cross- and co-directional sessions, and '600ns' marks the files of 2022-11-29; the trailing numbers run 01 to 12 in order of start time within each day. None of this is described.
  • OtherThe dataset holds only the Figure 3 experiment. The data of Figures 2, 4 and 5 are in DANDI:000631, 000633 and 000634, whose metadata name the paper, but 000632 does not point to them, and the paper's Data Availability Statement says the data are available on request without naming any of the four dandisets.
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the paper's authors. Methods 4.4 says spontaneous YP uptake was measured within the electrode footprints but not where in the footprint, over what area, or how it entered the Figure 3 values (subtracted from the center ROI, or not). This attempt subtracts the mean of a 200 x 200 um ROI at the center of each footprint, and shows the raw values and a whole-disc variant; all give the same outcomes. Which pairs of groups the p-values printed in Figure 3 compare is also not stated in the text.

It measures YO-PRO-1 uptake in the 200 um ROI at the center of the array, with and without the electrode-footprint background, and compares the four groups by t-tests.
