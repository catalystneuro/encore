Most of the paper's main findings hold in this data. The share of wall-responsive PrV units, the split into proximity and map units, and the better distance decoding from map units all reproduced. The circuit model also reproduced: halving long-range inhibition loses map units. The whisker-cutting results could not be tested, because the paper's stable units cannot be identified and post-cut wall distances are not recorded. The SpVi-TeLC mouse experiments could not be tested because those mice are not in the dataset. The one clear miss was the count of units both activated and suppressed (92 against 29), where the paper's own Figure 2F disagrees with its text.

Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds only the wild-type silicon-probe recordings (67 sessions, 36 C57 mice, 1,358 units). The tungsten recordings, the SpVi-TeLC and GtACR mice, the anesthetized and single-row video sessions and all tracked whisker kinematics of the paper are absent, so 16 of the 41 claims cannot be tested here. The dandiset's description names local and long-range inhibition perturbations, which are not in it.
  • Description missingIn the 25 whisker-cutting sessions the processed files hold only the full-array passes, and trial_condition is empty in every file. The post-cut passes are listed only in the raw files' wall_pass_epochs, with no wall distance; the trial log holds the pre-cut sequence only, and the raw wall-actuator line, described as the only raw record of wall distance, takes the same two levels at every distance. The pass epochs have only start and stop times, and the distances come in blocks that are permutations of the session's distances, in an order that varies between sessions and blocks, so the post-cut distances cannot be inferred.
  • Selection not documentedThe paper's whisker-cutting population (195 units with stable waveforms from 18 mice) cannot be identified: the deposit has 25 cutting sessions from 25 mice with 445 units, no waveform-stability flag and no list of the sessions used.
  • Description missingThe processed files carry no isolation metrics (SNR, ISI false-alarm rate), no 'acceptable' flag and no waveforms or peak channels, so the paper's unit criteria (1,141 of 1,358) and unit depths can be recomputed only in part, or from the raw voltage. The deposit's wall_responsive labels already exclude units that fail isolation without saying so: no labelled-responsive unit has ISI false alarms above 2%, and the 18 units that pass ISI and firing and test responsive but are labelled unresponsive have median SNR 4.22 (12 below 5, computed here from the raw voltage) against 7.91 for the labelled-responsive units; by the same approximation 75 of the 768 labelled-responsive units passing ISI and firing are also below 5, so an SNR criterion in the labels is possible but not shown.
  • Metadata errorIn 20230711_sp_session_1 and 20230608_sp_session_1 the full-array trials table (200 passes each) runs past the longest gap between raw passes, in which the session notes place the cutting (49:18-57:15 and 51-62 min): its trials are raw passes 21 to 220 and 51 to 250 of 400, and 200 raw passes come before the gap, so 20 and 50 of the passes used for the full-array tuning were recorded after the cut, if the notes are right. The deposit's alignment of distances to passes is otherwise the one the spikes support: with the deposit's distances moved to raw passes 1 to 200, none of the 18 and 13 units of these sessions passing ISI and firing is distance-tuned, against 10 and 9 as deposited.
  • OtherThe Methods give the responsiveness test as one-tailed signed-rank tests at p < 0.05 per distance and do not say whether the p-values are corrected, while Fig2DEF_Pop_Heatmap_Hist_Pie.m corrects the nine p-values of each direction with BH-FDR. The paper's counts (768 responsive, Figure 2F) and the deposit's per-unit labels follow the corrected test; the uncorrected test marks 1,061 of 1,160 units responsive.
  • OtherIn the two 1-mm-step sessions (20221212_sp_session_2, 20221213_sp_session_2; 38 units) the deposit's stored tuning curves use, in each 2-mm bin, only the distance nearest the bin centre (reproduced exactly here), while the Methods bin all distance measurements into 2-mm bins. For the other 65 sessions the stored curves equal the rates computed from the spike times, except for one unit in each of 10 sessions.
