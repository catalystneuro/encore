None of the paper's main findings could be tested in this data: the task-anchored and task-independent grid cell firing classes, the trial-by-trial switching between them, the agreement among simultaneously recorded cells, and the link between task-anchored firing and successful reward localisation. The dandiset holds only raw tetrode signal and position, with no sorted units, and every finding rests on units the authors sorted with MountainSort. Re-sorting was not attempted, because streaming the raw signal alone would take about 2.3 hours against a 60-minute notebook limit. Even with units, the unnamed open arena position columns and an arena extent that differs from the stated size in some sessions could block grid cell classification.

Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingNone of the 734 files holds sorted units, a units table, a trials table, reward times or any processed data; each holds only the raw 16-channel tetrode signal (30 kHz) and a position series. Every claim of the paper rests on units sorted with MountainSort, so none can be tested without sorting about 976 GB of raw signal. The paper's data are deposited at Edinburgh DataShare (the paper's data availability statement); this attempt did not use or inspect them. The dandiset's variableMeasured lists only ElectrodeGroup and ElectricalSeries, which is accurate.
  • Metadata errorThe position series has the same description in every file, "position: cm, speed: cm/s, angle: degrees, trials: non-zero indexed, trial types: 0-beaconed, 1-nonbeaconed, 2-probe". The VR files hold three columns (position, trial number, trial type) and no speed or angle; the open arena files hold nine columns and no trials.
  • Description missingThe nine columns of the open arena position series are not named. From their ranges, columns 1 and 3 appear to be x and y in cm, 2 and 4 the same in camera pixels, 5 head direction in degrees, 7 speed in cm/s, 0 and 8 times in s; column 6 (session minima from 33.4, maxima up to 37,697) could not be identified.
  • Metadata errorThe files describe a 100 x 100 cm open arena, and in most sessions the x and y columns span 88.0 to 103.7 cm, but in 38 open arena sessions, 18 of M1's 41 and 20 of M2's 21, they span only 67.5 to 89.8 cm, so either the arena was smaller in these sessions or the position scaling differs; the files do not say which.
  • Data missingTwo VR files have position for only part of the raw recording, sub-M13 ses-M13-D27-2021-06-15-11-43-42 (687 s of 2168 s, 21 trials) and sub-M15 ses-M15-D5-2021-05-14-12-46-13 (881 s of 1779 s, 14 trials). In the other 353 VR files the position series ends within 0.1 s of the raw signal.
  • OtherIn open arena files the position series usually runs past the end of the raw signal, by a median 2.9 s (5th to 95th percentile 1.2 to 9.5 s), and in 20 of 379 files the two differ by more than 10 s (up to 451.9 s longer and 178.2 s shorter). The files do not say how the position was aligned to the raw signal (the data collection text describes LED pulses, which are not in the files).
  • Selection not documentedThe dandiset holds 12 mice, 379 open arena and 355 VR files, 29 of them on tracks longer than 200 cm; the paper uses nine mice and the 200 cm track. For the nine mice it has 242 usable 200 cm VR sessions (at least 10 trials) against the 215 of the paper's summary table, with more sessions than the table by more than 10% for M3 (30 against 26), M7 (28, 23), M10 (25, 16) and M12 (28, 21), and slightly more for M13 (27, 25). Nothing in the dandiset marks which sessions the paper used. 48 recording days have only an open arena file and 24 only a VR file.
