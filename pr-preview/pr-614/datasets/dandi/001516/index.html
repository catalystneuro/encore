    fig5b-conductivity-calibration

    Eq. (2) with the constants of Methods 2.3 gives f = 1.49798 for the intact membrane. A 10-fold rise in effective conductivity reduces IMP by 1.33% under reading (a), 1 - f/1.5, and by 1.20% under reading (b), relative to the intact f, both outside 2 +- 0.2%. A 100-fold rise gives 11.87% and 11.75%, within 12 +- 1.2%. A 2% reduction needs a 15.2-fold (a) or 16.2-fold (b) rise. With other radii and membrane thicknesses (5 to 12 um, 4 to 7 nm; a variant, not the criterion) the 10-fold reduction is 0.60 to 2.46%, within 2 +- 0.2% only for 10 um with 4 nm (2.06%) or 12 um with 5 nm (1.98%). The claim uses no recordings, so the outcome says nothing about the dataset. The notebook prints the Eq. (2) curve at 1 to 100000-fold (measured IMP 98.67% of IMPC at 10-fold, 88.13% at 100-fold, 42.62% at 1000-fold, 6.91% at 10000-fold) for comparison with the curve of Fig. 5B; it does not read the figure.

Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds none of the recordings behind the paper's figures. Its 72 files are one FluoVolt experiment of 2023-06-22 in 12 fields of view, single 1-us pulses (2 fields) and pulse pairs at intervals of 1, 5, 25, 125 and 250 us (2 fields each), at 150 to 1570 V/cm. The paper's Di-8-ANEPPS escalations (Figs. 1, 2B to D, 4B to D), its FluoVolt escalation (Figs. 2A, 3, 4A), the Gd3+ experiments (Fig. 5), the Fluo-4 calcium imaging (Fig. 6) and the Di-8-ANEPPS two-pulse recordings at a 5-us interval (Fig. 7) are absent. 31 of the 32 claims cannot be tested.
  • Metadata errorThe dandiset's metadata names the paper as the publication of its data (dcite:IsPublishedIn), but the paper reports no FluoVolt two-pulse experiment and no interval other than 5 us, and its Discussion names varying the interval as future work. The files' descriptions also differ from the paper's Methods on the electrode spacing (1.00 mm against 1.6 mm) and the frames per set (20 sham and 40 pulse against 40 and 60).
  • Metadata errorThe general description in every file gives the inter-pulse intervals as 1, 5, 25 and 125 us, but fields of view c5 and c12 hold pairs at 250 us.
  • File format errorThe ImageSeries starting_time holds the Unix time of each set's acquisition (1687481119 to 1687486631 s), the same instant as the file's session_start_time, where NWB defines it as the offset from the session's reference time; every recording thus starts about 53 years after its session.
  • Description missingThe pulse protocol of each set (sham, single or paired, amplitudes, interval) is only in the free text of session_description; there is no stimulus or trial table, and no frame is labelled as sham or pulse (the data confirm 20 sham frames, then 40). The field direction, the pixel size and the imaging delay per set are not recorded.
  • Description missingThe amplitudes are given in volts (50 to 250 V) and the description lists the field strengths 0.15, 0.52, 0.9, 1.27 and 1.57 kV/cm without mapping them to amplitudes. Read in order, these are not proportional to voltage (5 times the voltage for 10.5 times the field), while the paper states that the current scaled linearly with voltage.
  • Description missingThe 12 subjects (CHO_20230622_c1 to c12) are fields of view holding several cells each, some of them touching (29 whole, round cells, 2 or 3 per field of view, are measured in the notebook), and the files do not say which cell was the experiment's cell; the paper reports one cell per experiment.
  • Metadata errorSubject metadata describe a cell line as an organism, with age P1D and sex U, and the archive's summary gives the species as "Cricetulus griseus - Cricetulus aureus" for NCBITaxon_10029 (Cricetulus griseus).
  • OtherOn the cells, the 40 frames after the sham frames are 13.3 to 16.2% dimmer than sham frames 1 to 19 in the sham sets, with no pulse (9.4 to 16.8% with frame 0 in the baseline), and the per-frame pole dF falls within the pulse frames of the sham sets (by 12.1 and 12.2 points between pulse frames 20-29 and 50-59) and within their baseline (by 15.9 and 16.3 points between frames 1-9 and 10-19). In 65 of the 72 files the first frame is more than 10% dimmer than frames 1 to 19 (median 0.64, range 0.52 to 1.11 over files). This uniform offset enters the paper's collapsed dF measure at both poles.
  • Paper inconsistentResults 3.5 states that a 10-fold rise in effective membrane conductivity yields a ~2% IMP reduction by Eq. (2), but Eq. (2) with the constants of Methods 2.3 gives 1.33% (1.20% relative to the intact membrane); a 2% reduction needs a 15- to 16-fold rise. The 100-fold value (12%) agrees.
