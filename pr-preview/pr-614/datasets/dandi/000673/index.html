The paper's plot_PAC_neuron.m fits the three GLMs to the 20 aggregated bin counts of one neuron-channel pair with fixed effects only, as sbcat.py does, so neither a per-trial layout nor a random effect explains the difference.

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • Selection as in plot_PAC_neuron.m (20 theta-gamma bins, three Poisson GLMs, likelihood-ratio tests), fitted by IRLS in Python in place of fitglme (deviances checked against statsmodels). Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • File format errorSpike times are not sorted in four units of two files. In sub-13 ses-1, units 6, 14 and 15 jump back by up to 1,319 to 1,339 s (units 6 and 15 once, unit 14 three times), as if two spike trains had been concatenated; in sub-22 ses-2, unit 10 steps back 24 times by up to 3.3 ms. The analysis sorts them, which keeps the result of the paper's code (it selects spikes with logical masks).
  • Selection not documentedThe files do not mark the LFP trials, channels or regions that the paper's artefact rejection removed, most of it by visual inspection (20.4 +/- 13.9 trials per wire, and whole wires or regions). The automated rules of the Methods remove 11.4% (on the epochs) or 25.9% (on whole trials) of trials, and the remaining channels change several results. The pre-SMA has 17 channels with significant theta-high-gamma PAC where the paper has 5, and they decide three claims of absence. 269 hippocampal and 422 amygdala neurons have LFP in their bundle, where the paper analysed 212 and 358 ("pre-processing steps removed broadband LFPs for some of the neurons"). The paper's code ships the rejected trials of one example channel (artif_trials.mat, sub-26 session 1, channel 33, 17 trials), which shows the information exists for the paper's own analysis.
  • Description missingThe files do not identify the microwire bundles that the analyses pair neurons and LFPs within. Every wire is its own ElectrodeGroup, and the location names only area and hemisphere; some locations of a session hold up to 15 wires, and 26 of 285 location groups have more than one set of coordinates.
