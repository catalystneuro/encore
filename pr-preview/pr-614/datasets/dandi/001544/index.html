    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • The dandiset holds 24 ALL cells from 11 mice where the paper analysed 21 from 8, and does not say which were left out.     • The STAR Methods (Quantification and statistical analysis) say "Non-parametric tests were conducted if the data failed these tests" (Shapiro-Wilk and Kolmogorov-Smirnov). The claims file's readings do not include this rule, so the outcome follows readings (a) and (b), and Kruskal-Wallis and Mann-Whitney results are shown beside them; a question for a reader is in questions/001544.yaml.

The behaviour, immunohistochemistry counts, fiber photometry, chirp recordings and neuronal reconstructions are not in it, and the paper's data statement says all data will be shared by the lead contact on request. 24 of the 31 claims in the claims file cannot be tested.
  • Metadata errorEvery CurrentClampStimulusSeries is stated in amperes with conversion 1, but holds values from -60 to 500, the step amplitudes in picoamperes.
  • Description missingThe DC current that holds each cell near -65 mV is not recorded; `bias_current` (1e-11 A) and `bridge_balance` (2e7 Ohm) hold the same value in all 44 files, which a holding current set per cell cannot. No segment of any sweep is free of the holding current, so the resting potential the Methods define ("measured in the absence of a current injection") cannot be obtained.
  • Selection not documentedThe home-cage (ALL) group holds 24 cells from 11 mice, where the Figure 6 legend reports 21 cells from 8 mice; the dandiset does not say which cells the paper left out or why. No 8 of the 11 mice hold 21 cells (any 8 hold 14 to 20), so the paper's set is not the deposit less whole mice. The APP (12 cells, 6 mice) and AV (8 cells, 4 mice) groups match the legend.
  • Metadata errorThe six APP mice are 4 male and 2 female in the files, where the Figure 6 legend says 3 male and 3 female.
  • OtherFive ALL mice (CG354, CG355, CG382, CG538, CG539; 9 cells) are 5.7 to 6.6 weeks old in the files, younger than the 8 to 12 weeks the STAR Methods give for all mice.
  • Description missingEvery sweep's starting_time is 0, so the order in time and the 10 s between steps are not recorded; series resistance is not recorded, so that exclusion rule cannot be checked. Three files carry suffixes `_NSD` and `_FL` on their identifiers, with no description; the two CG538 files sharing recording number 003 are different cells (input resistance 192 and 802 MOhm), and the `_FL` files use 5 s sweeps where all others use 2 s.
  • OtherFive recordings (four APP, one ALL) reach -105.6 mV and stay there for 10 ms or more during the -60 pA step, flat at that value, which truncates the response; sag measured on these sweeps is underestimated. The value is not a floor in every file: one other APP file (20221214_001) has 7 samples at -105.62 mV and 1735 below -105.7 mV, down to -112.88 mV. Three sweeps hold NaN for their last 0.1 to 0.6 s, after the step.
  • Published code disagreesThe paper's code (v1.0.0) differs from the Methods in the spike threshold (10 against 20 mV/ms), spike latency (to the peak, not the threshold), AHP amplitude (minimum to the next spike on the first step with two spikes, not within 100 ms at rheobase) and tau (the -40 pA step, not -20 pA). Its downstroke window runs to the end of the step, and its test for a saturated sag response never fires (it compares with 1000 the count of the most frequent index among the unchanged samples, which is always 1).
  • Paper inconsistentThe STAR Methods say a nested ANOVA was run for the electrophysiology to account for cells from the same mouse, but the reported degrees of freedom (F(2, 38), t(31), t(27), t(18)) are those of cell-level tests on 41 cells.
  • Paper inconsistentThe Results say the convex hull of APP neurons was smaller than that of AV neurons in surface area and volume (Figure S13C), but the Figure S13 legend lists the volume of the convex hull among the features with no significant difference between the groups.
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the paper's authors. Which 21 of the 24 home-cage cells (and which 8 of the 11 mice) were analysed, and how was the rule on a 20% change of series resistance, input resistance or resting potential applied? The outcomes of figs12a-sag-higher and figs12-app-differs-from-all here change with the cell set (sag of AV against ALL p = 0.087 on all 24 cells, 0.0072 without the three `_FL` cells; in the set passing the 20% change rule, 3 of the 4 APP-specific features differ by reading (a) and 2 by reading (b)). And how was the membrane potential of Figure 6G measured "in the absence of a current injection", when the files hold only sweeps under the DC holding current?
