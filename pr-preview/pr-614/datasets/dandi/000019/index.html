Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Description missingNo electrode has a position relative to the central sulcus or the Sylvian fissure, the frame of the paper's spatial analyses. EC2 and GP31 have x, y, z coordinates with no unit or coordinate frame described; GP33 and EC9 have none. No electrode is labelled as on the guenon. Seven spatial findings of the paper cannot be tested.
  • Data missingThe microphone recordings were removed (dandiset description), so the acoustic onset, peak and offset of each syllable used by the paper cannot be computed; only hand-marked start, C-V transition and stop times are given.
  • Identifier missingSubjects GP31 and GP33 do not carry the paper's names (EC31, EC33), and nothing links them. GP31's coordinates fit a left hemisphere, as the paper's EC31; GP33 has no coordinates, so the right hemisphere of EC33 cannot be checked. The subject fields hold only species and id. The electrode group of EC2 and GP31 is `L256Grid electrodes`; that of GP33 is `auto_group`, with location `location`, so no field gives GP33's hemisphere.
  • OtherThe dandiset holds a fourth subject, EC9 (7 blocks, 1653 trials), that is not in the paper. It has extra syllables (/ch/, /dh/, /j/, /sk/), labels on 79 of 256 electrodes and no coordinates.
  • Selection not documentedRenditions per syllable range from 2 to 107 (GP31 /thi/ 2, EC2 /thi/ 10, GP31 up to 107) against the paper's 15 to 100, and GP31 lacks /tha/ and /thu/. The three subjects have 169 distinct syllables against the paper's 168 (57 + 57 + 54). The dandiset does not say which trials or syllables the paper used.
  • Metadata errorThe raw ElectricalSeries gives unit volt with a conversion factor of 0.001, but the stored values have an SD of a few 1e-5, which is a usual ECoG amplitude if read as volts and tens of nanovolts with the factor; the data are consistent with values already in volts.
  • Metadata errorTwo syllable labels are misspelt (`she` for `shee` in EC2 and EC9, `ha` for `haa` in GP33 and EC9). Electrode ids start at 0 in EC2 and GP31 and at 1 in GP33 and EC9.
  • OtherThe archive summary counts four subjects and 31 files; bad-channel flags differ between blocks of one subject (EC2 has none in two blocks and up to 10 in others), and 162 of 9370 analysed trials overlap intervals marked as ECoG artifacts (38 in GP31 block 63, whose rest period has a high-gamma SD of 0.3 times the block's). One file (EC2 block 1) holds a power spectrum of the 256 channels in a processing module; no other file has one.

Reproduction Speech articulation in human sensorimotor cortex (Bouchard et al. 2013)

Tests the findings of Bouchard, Mesgarani, Johnson and Chang (2013) on dandiset 000019, which holds raw ECoG and syllable annotations of subjects reading consonant-vowel syllables. High gamma is computed from the raw voltage. It tests the active electrodes, the articulator timing, the mixed tuning of electrodes and the cortical state-space analyses. The spatial maps cannot be tested, as the dandiset gives no electrode positions relative to the central sulcus and Sylvian fissure. All files
