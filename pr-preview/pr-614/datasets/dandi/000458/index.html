Most depth and state effects held in this data: superficial stimulation gave smaller ERPs and lower PCIst than deep, and PCIst was higher awake than under isoflurane at 30 of 30 sites. The late ERP peak was not absent with superficial stimulation (0 to 2 of 12 sites lacked it), and spiking outside the stimulated area was not limited to deep stimulation. Thalamus led cortex in the rebound by far less than reported, and burst correlations with cortical rate were weaker. Laminar CSD and stimulation depth could not be tested because the files hold no layers or electrode depth. Figure 4 unit counts are one third of the legend's, probably because it counts each unit once per current.

Over 128 combinations of readings MO exceeds VIS for all three signals in 112; the LFP order reverses in 4 of 32 LFP readings (high current, 1 Hz, -0.5 to 0 s baseline). No combination puts all six values within tolerance, and at most three are (medium current, 0.1 Hz, top 100 um left out, -2 to 0 s, epoch shuffle, 5 ms kernel, spiking as |z|; LFP 60.83 and 21.75, CSD 48.77 and 5.21, spiking 28.75 and 2.76, against 54.0, 18.0, 51.4, 4.8, 30.9 and 1.8). The LFP magnitude in MO is never within tolerance (7.57 to 77.16). Leaving out the top 100 um changes no ordering and moves the CSD values by up to 2.84. The spiking magnitude as the Methods describe it (signed z) is 0.31 to 3.96 in MO; integrating |z| gives 28.31 to 31.37 with the 5 ms kernel and the -2 to 0 s baseline, so the signed integral, in which the suppression cancels the excitation, most likely explains the earlier values far below 30.9 (verifier, round 2). Only the ordering is reproduced; the measured values are those of the combination named above, one of two with three values within tolerance.

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • Layer 1 is not labelled in the files; it is approximated by the channels in the top 100 um of each area (an assumed thickness), and the result is shown with and without the exclusion.
    • The Chebyshev channel rejection is applied once per channel (no channel was rejected), where the Methods apply it per sample and epoch; the CSD is computed from the trial-averaged LFP on rows of two channels, smoothed over 13 then 2 rows for the Methods' 26 then 4 channels; decimation and high pass are zero phase; the recording-wide shuffle draws onsets over the span of the awake pulses.
    • The spiking magnitude is shown both as the signed integral of the Methods and as the integral of |z| (Results, Global ERPs..., paragraph 1, "our magnitude metric accounts for both reductions as well as increases").

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingThe depth of the stimulating electrode in mm and its position are not in the files; the trial table gives only the target area and the label "superficial" or "deep". The paper's stimulation depths and the spatial analysis of the first ERP peak cannot be tested.
