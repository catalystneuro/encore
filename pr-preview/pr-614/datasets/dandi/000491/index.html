Several of the paper's central findings held in this data and several did not. The vessel radius peak near 28 um, the polynomial fit matching the segmented area ratio best, and the fit velocity ordering reproduced, mostly under only some analysis choices. The resistance power law held partially, while the area ratio and resistance distributions, ellipse parameters and PVS width asymmetry did not match. The likely reason is the paper's undescribed manual centerline cleaning and PVS selection, for which the agent used stand-ins. The validation against 2D images could not be tested because those images are not in the dataset.

Summary written by the agent that recorded the attempt, from its claims and the agents’ reports. A failure to reproduce may be a failure of the agent.

    • Starts from the deposited final segmentations, as the claims file says; the vessel centerline is skimage's 3D thinning in place of MATLAB bwskel. Added after the first verification, which found the uncleaned path 1.28 to 2.24 times the distance between its ends; 7534 of 9564 path points are kept at 0.648 um and 4974 of 6010 at 1 um. The cleaned path is still 1.13 to 1.78 times the distance between its kept ends and 1.08 to 1.44 times the arc of the fitted centerline at 0.648 um (E excepted), more than the zigzag of a 26-connected voxel path (at most about 8% on a straight line) explains, so it still meanders inside the vessel; the slices are oriented by the fitted centerline, so the meander changes how many there are more than where they lie.
    • Cross sections are sampled by linear interpolation of the masks, thresholded at 0.5, on planes normal to the fitted centerline, one per path point, with pixels of the P1 voxel size, in place of MATLAB obliqueslice (a standard choice).
    • Pixels in both the vessel and the PVS mask are counted as vessel. The other assignment was not run.
