Only the finding that granule and semilunar cells lose their electrical differences in the V321L mutant held in these recordings. The wild-type differences in resting potential, spike width and downstroke went the paper's way but were not significant (p = 0.08 to 0.35), and the separation of wild-type cell types by PCA and a trained classifier also failed. The morphology, soma position, Golgi and RNAScope findings could not be tested because those data are not in the dandiset. The sequencing findings on Penk+ cells and pseudotime could not be tested either, because the kept nuclei and cluster labels are not deposited and the pipeline needs R. The embryonic expression of the semilunar-cell genes held, judged by eye on Allen atlas images.

    figs9c-embryonic-sgc-genes

    Tested after verification (it was blocked as other, not attempted). The Allen Developing Mouse Brain Atlas holds one E18.5 ISH series per gene, all sagittal (Penk 100055886, Scg2 100083699, Nrg1 100057764, Cit 100077439). The criterion is a reading of the images by eye. Penk labels a band of cells along the dorsal (outer) edge of the dentate crescent in all three sections shown (45, 52, 59), and Nrg1 a dense band along the same edge (26, 33, 47). The atlas alignment puts the Scg2 crops off the crescent, so its two sections (29, 43) are also shown recentred by eye on their own crescent. There section 43 shows a band of labelled cells along the dorsal edge and section 29 a faint band with a few labelled cells; the expression mask covers 0.0032 and 0.0010 of the recentred crops (0.0008 in the off-centre section 29 crop), against 0.0123 for Penk and 0.0072 for Nrg1. Scg2 labels other regions strongly (whole-brain expression density 0.043). Cit (27, 41, 48) shows sparse, faint labelled cells along that edge (mask 0.0007). The criterion has one condition, labelled cells at the dorsal border for each of the four genes, and no partial rule, as for figs2f; all four meet it, Scg2 and Cit faintly, so the outcome is reproduced (changed from partially reproduced in round 3). The outcome rests on locating the dentate gyrus by eye, as the atlas does not annotate it at E18.5, and on a judgement by eye. Which images Fig S9C shows is asked of the paper's authors (methods_unavailable finding).

Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds only the current clamp recordings. The soma positions and the morphology from the biocytin fills (splay angle, branch points, dendritic length, 15 parameters), on which Fig 1F to I, Fig 2C to H, Fig S1 and the multiple regression rest, are not in it, and neither are the Golgi and RNAScope data; 23 of the paper's 56 claims cannot be tested for this reason.
  • Selection not documentedThe files hold 13 WT GCs and 9 WT SGCs, where the paper's electrophysiology has 14 and 9, and 12 V321L GCs and 14 V321L SGCs, where the paper has 10 and 15. Nothing in the files says which WT GC is missing or why the V321L cell types differ, so the paper's cell sets cannot be rebuilt.
  • Metadata errorEvery CurrentClampStimulusSeries stores the current in picoamperes (values -60 to 300) under the unit amperes with conversion 1.
  • Metadata errorThe electrode cell_id is VP neuron in all 48 files, though the cells are dentate granule cells.
  • Metadata errorThe general stimulus field says neurons were kept near -65 mV with a constant current and stepped from -60 to 200 pA. The voltage before the steps is -68 to -89 mV in every cell, 14 cells were stepped to 250 or 300 pA, and the bias_current attribute is 10 pA in every sweep, so whether a holding current was applied, and how much, cannot be told. Every sweep of every file also stores the same bridge balance (20 MOhm) and capacitance compensation (1 pF), which look like defaults.
  • Description missingThe genotype is coded only as VV or LL in the subject ids; the surgery field says VV is wild type and LL the valine-to-leucine mutant, and GEO agrees, but the cell type is given only as the end of the session description (semilunar or not semilunar), with no column or field of its own.
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the paper's authors. Was the four-class LDA of Fig 2M and Fig S2C fitted on the z-scored features or on principal components, as the classifier of Fig S2G is? The Methods' LDA section describes only the classifier. On this dandiset the two give different groups apart on LD1 (WT GC on the features, WT SGC on 9 PCs).
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the paper's authors. Which twenty features were extracted, and how were cells with a missing feature handled in the LDA, k-means and PCA? The Methods cite Kim et al (2024), Fig S2C lists eighteen, and the linked code returns twenty-one. Here 4 cells lack the code's AHP width and were dropped, which fits the 21 WT and 23 V321L cells of the Fig S2G matrices.
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the paper's authors. Which Allen Developing Mouse Brain ISH series and sections does Fig S9C show for Penk, Scg2, Nrg1 and Cit at E18.5? The atlas holds one sagittal E18.5 series per gene; in those, Penk and Nrg1 label cells along the dorsal edge of the dentate crescent clearly, and Scg2 and Cit only faintly.
  • Data missingGEO GSE280167, where the paper deposited the snMultiome data, holds the raw feature-barcode matrix of every barcode per mouse (genes and peaks) and a smoothed ATAC cut-site track per sample. The nuclei kept after filtering and doublet removal, the cluster labels the paper's snRNA-Seq figures rest on, and the per-nucleus ATAC fragments are not deposited.
