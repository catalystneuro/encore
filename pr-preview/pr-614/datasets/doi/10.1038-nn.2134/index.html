  • channel-region-link

    The dandiset's recording channels can be assigned to the prefrontal probe, the CA1 probe and the nose-poke sensors by the channel map of the CRCNS pfc-2 description of the same recordings (channels 1 to 64 prefrontal, 65 to 96 CA1, 97 right and 98 left nose-poke sensors), and the data agree with that map.

    How it will be judged

    Two checks in each of the eight sessions with the wideband signal, and the sensor check also in EE.088. Reproduced when both hold in every session with the CRCNS channel numbers; partially reproduced when both hold in every session only with a per-rat correction of the channel numbers found in the data; not reproduced otherwise.

  • fig2-field-size

    Neurons are active over similar standardized distances of the maze, 0.27 (62 cm) plus or minus 0.17 (39 cm) (mean and s.d.), from the 50% boundaries of the peak firing rate.

  • fig2b-trajectory-selective

    The fraction of neurons with significantly different firing on left and right trials is 16% (layer 2/3) and 18% (layer 5) in the central arm (positions 0 to 0.3), where the trajectories overlap, and 22% (layer 2/3) and 40% (layer 5) in the side arms (0.5 to 1).

    The fraction of trajectory-selective neurons in the side arms (positions 0.5–1) was almost twice as large in deep (layer 5, 40%) than in superficial (layer 2/3, 22%) neurons... a sizable but smaller fraction of neurons in both layer 2/3 and layer 5 (16% and 18%, respectively; Fig. 2b) was also differentially active in the central arm

    Results, Medial prefrontal cortical units predict behavioral choice, paragraph 4; Figure 2b; Methods, permutation tests and global bands

    How it will be judged

    The Methods' test; per-trial kernel densities of spike positions (sigma 0.05) on 101 points of the track, difference of left and right means, 1000 permutations of the trial labels, global band at alpha 0.05. A unit is selective in a segment when the difference breaks the global band anywhere in it. Units as in fig2-field-size. The chance rate is the same procedure on one random relabelling of each unit's trials. Per rat, reproduced when the central-arm fraction is within 14.4 to 19.8% (the range 16 to 18% widened by 10% of each bound) and the side-arm fraction is within 10% of the value of one layer (19.8 to 24.2% for layer 2/3, 36 to 44% for layer 5), with at most one rat in layer 5. The claim is reproduced when every rat is; partially reproduced when the pooled central-arm fraction is above the chance fraction (one-sided Fisher exact test, p < 0.05) but not every rat matches; not reproduced otherwise.

  • traj-diverge

    Left and right trajectories overlap in the central arm and begin to differ significantly at position 0.3.

    Left and right lap trajectories in segments 0 to 0.3 of the central arm overlapped; they began to differ significantly at position 0.3 (P < 0.01 with respect to differences in means; Supplementary Fig. 3 online).

    Results, Medial prefrontal cortical units predict behavioral choice, paragraph 4; Supplementary Figure 3

    How it will be judged

    Written in the first revision, after the verifier asked whether the x coordinate separates the sides and before x was compared between sides. The only coordinate is x1, the x of the first head LED in pixels; frames with x1 at or below 0 (-1, no tracking) are left out. Per trial, the mean x1 in 50 bins of 0.02 of the normalized position. Per session and bin, a Welch t-test of left against right trials. The divergence point of a session is the start of the first bin from which every bin up to 0.6 differs at p < 0.01. Blocked (data_missing) when x1 does not differ between sides (p < 0.01) over 0.5 to 1 in at least half the sessions, as then x1 does not carry the side. Otherwise, reproduced when the median divergence point over sessions is within 0.27 to 0.33 (10% of 0.3); partially reproduced when it is above 0.33, since one coordinate can separate the paths later than both would; not reproduced when it is below 0.27.

  • fig3-connection-rates

    Of all ordered pairs of simultaneously recorded neurons, 0.79% had short-latency, narrow peaks or troughs in their cross-correlograms (0.55% excitatory, 0.24% inhibitory), found with a jitter test.

    Of the 62,408 cell pairs (counting each literal pair twice, corresponding to the two directions), 495 (0.79%) had short latency (< 5 ms onset) and narrow significant peaks (≤ 2 ms) or troughs in their cross-correlograms... 0.55% excitatory connections and 0.24% inhibitory connections (single directions)

    Results, Characterization of mPFC neurons and their connections, paragraph 5; Figure 3a and 3e; Methods, identifying monosynaptic interactions

    How it will be judged

    Every ordered pair of PFC single units in a session, whole session. Cross- correlogram in 1 ms bins from -5 to 5 ms (the bin width is chosen here); 1000 surrogates with every spike jittered uniformly by +-5 ms; global bands at 99% from the maximum and minimum of each surrogate over the window. Excitatory when a bin from 1 to 4 ms is above the upper band and the run of bins above it is at most 2 ms wide; inhibitory when a bin from 1 to 4 ms is below the lower band and the pair is not excitatory in that direction. To save time, pairs whose correlogram is within the pointwise 1% Poisson quantiles of the exact jitter mean in every test bin are not given the Monte Carlo test; a random sample of them is, to show the screen misses nothing. Reproduced when all three rates are within tolerance; partially reproduced when one or two are; not reproduced otherwise.

    By their connections, 32.5% of units are putative pyramidal cells and 12.5% putative interneurons, a ratio of 2.82 plus or minus 0.51 (s.d.), and 50.7% of the postsynaptic targets of putative excitatory cells themselves inhibit other neurons.

    a sizable fraction of the recorded units could be classified as putative pyramidal cells (32.5%) or inhibitory interneurons (12.5%).     How it will be judged

    A unit is a putative pyramidal cell when it has at least one excitatory outgoing connection, a putative interneuron when it has at least one inhibitory one; a unit with both is counted in neither. Readings of the ratio; (a) the mean of per-session ratios (sessions with at least one interneuron); (b) the pooled ratio; (c) the mean of per-rat pooled ratios. Targets; distinct postsynaptic units of excitatory connections that are putative interneurons. Reproduced when all four values are within tolerance under one reading; partially reproduced when two or three are; not reproduced otherwise.

  • fig4-position-dependence

  • fig6-facilitation-depression

    About equal fractions of connected pairs show firing pattern-dependent depression (12.7%) and facilitation (10.7%), comparing the transmission of the first spike of a train with that of later spikes.

    we found that approximately equal percentages of neuron pairs showed firing pattern–dependent depressing (12.7%) and facilitating (10.7%) effects (Fig. 6c).

    Results, Behavioral modulation of monosynaptic interactions, paragraph 3; Figure 6c

    How it will be judged

    Excitatory pairs with at least 20 first spikes (after at least 200 ms of presynaptic silence) and 20 later spikes (less than 40 ms after the previous presynaptic spike). Transmission of a set of presynaptic spikes is the mean number of postsynaptic spikes 1 to 4 ms after each, minus its expectation under +-5 ms jitter of the postsynaptic spikes (chosen here, as the supplementary definition was not available). 1000 permutations of the first and later labels, two-sided P < 0.10; depressing when first spikes transmit more, facilitating when later spikes do. Reproduced when both fractions are within tolerance; partially reproduced when both are above the 5% that the test gives each side under its null (one-sided binomial test, p < 0.05) but not both within tolerance; not reproduced otherwise.

  • fig7-supralinear

    Coincident firing of two or more presynaptic neurons within 5 ms on a common target is more effective than the sum of their separate effects (supralinear), while spikes spread over windows longer than 10 ms add linearly.

    Coincident discharge of two presynaptic neurons within 5 ms was more effective than the sum of the effects of nonsynchronous spikes (Fig. 7a)... In contrast, spike occurrences of more than one neuron in time windows >10 ms showed only a linear additive effect

    Results, Behavioral modulation of monosynaptic interactions, paragraph 4; Figure 7

    How it will be judged

    Satellites are PFC single units with at least two excitatory presynaptic partners. Partner spikes are merged into events (a spike and every partner spike within 5 ms after it); for each event, whether the target fires 1 to 4 ms after its last spike, minus the chance of a target spike in any 3 ms window. Supralinear in a satellite when the excess for events with two partners exceeds twice that for events with one. Satellites with at least 20 two-partner events are tested. Reproduced when more than half of them are supralinear and a one-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test of the difference gives p < 0.05; partially reproduced when more than half are but p is 0.05 or more; not reproduced otherwise. The same with 20 ms events is shown as a variant for the linear part.
