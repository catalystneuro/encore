Reproducing Gao et al. (2018) from DANDI:000007 and CRCNS:alm-4¶

Paper. Gao Z, Davis C, Thomas AM, Economo MN, Abrego AM, Svoboda K, De Zeeuw CI, Li N (2018). A cortico-cerebellar loop for motor planning. Nature 563, 113-116. doi:10.1038/s41586-018-0633-x. The claims file (claims/gao-2018.yaml) was written from the version of record (PMC6212318) with its Methods, Extended Data legends and Supplementary Information; the legends of the main Figures 1 to 4 were not in the text read.

Datasets.

  • DANDI:000007, version 0.220126.1903 (doi:10.48324/dandi.000007/0.220126.1903). The paper cites no DANDI version (the dandiset was created in 2020, after the paper). This is the only published version; the draft has the same 54 assets and size, so the published one is pinned.
  • CRCNS:alm-4 (doi:10.6080/K0NS0S26), unversioned (descriptor version 0.6, 23 October 2018). Files read, with their MD5 sums from checksums.md5: ALM_recording_DCN_photoactivation/datafiles.tar.gz (12e9d7af630523ed2511826eeacf6b48), ALM_recording_DCN_photoinhibition/datafiles.tar.gz (5123dc96df58457f853e2deb5ce479c7), and, for method details, the two analyses_scripts.zip (601f4b52198614dbd4761a64d2658698 and 4639a8478bc307fb3a5a4b8f2a53fb58).

Both datasets hold the same 54 ALM recording sessions of 13 mice (shown below). The analysis runs on the CRCNS copy, which holds every trial, the GoodTrials flag and the laser traces; the DANDI copy is checked against it unit by unit and trial by trial, and the counts of the Fig. 2 claim are also given on the DANDI trial set.

Neither dataset holds the lesion experiments, the behavior-only sessions, the CN recordings, lick times, video, the VGAT-ChR2-EYFP experiments or the anatomy; the claims on those are blocked as data_missing (table below).

The CRCNS descriptor lists 34 sessions (9 mice) of ALM recording during fastigial or dentate photo-activation and 20 sessions (4 mice) during CN photo-inhibition; the files hold exactly these. The fastigial mice are 6 (ANM314746, ANM314757, ANM314758, ANM314760, BAYLORCD6, BAYLORNL15) and the dentate mice 3 (ANM314756, BAYLORNL12, BAYLORNL14), as Supplemental Table 1 gives (6 and 3). The paper reports 1309 ALM units (1194 putative pyramidal) in 10 mice over 39 sessions for Fig. 2, of which 912 pyramidal neurons in the 9 photo-activation mice, and 490 units (454 pyramidal) in 4 mice over 19 sessions for CN photo-inhibition. The counts above differ from both (printed): the files hold more units than the paper reports for the same mice.

def tp_(a, b):
    return stats.ttest_ind(a, b).pvalue if np.var(a) + np.var(b) > 0 else 1.0


def persistence_test(P):
    """Mean fraction significant in the post bins, against 1% and against the pre-onset bins."""
    f = (P < 0.01).mean(0)
    n = len(P)
    fpost, fpre = f[post].mean(), f[pre].mean()
    k, kp = int(round(fpost * n)), int(round(fpre * n))
    pa = stats.binomtest(k, n, 0.01, alternative="greater").pvalue
    z = sm.stats.proportions_ztest([k, kp], [n, n], alternative="larger")
    return f, n, fpost, fpre, k, pa, z

def lose_gain(grp, code_, keys, rng, n_sub=100):
    """Per neuron, E2 t-test on the window in control and photo-activation trials (instructed type),
    with all control trials and with control trials subsampled to the photo-activation counts of
    each type (mean over n_sub subsamples), which removes the difference in power."""
    kset = set(keys)
    out = []
    for wn, (a_rel, b_rel) in {"(a) light offset to go cue": (0.455, None), "(b) last 500 ms": (None, 0.5)}.items():
        for s in sessions:
            if s.group != grp:
                continue
            t = s.trials
            n = len(t)
            R = (t.instructed == "R").to_numpy(); L = (t.instructed == "L").to_numpy()
            po, cue = t.pole_out.to_numpy(), t.cue.to_numpy()
            a = po + a_rel if a_rel is not None else cue - b_rel
            for u in s.pyramidal():
                if (s.key, u["key"]) not in kset:
                    continue
                base = s.base_mask() & s.stable(u, n) & (t.correct | t.error).to_numpy()
                ctrl = base & (t.ptype == 0).to_numpy(); stim = base & (t.ptype == code_).to_numpy()
                c = s.counts(u, a, cue)
                cR, cL, sR, sL = c[ctrl & R], c[ctrl & L], c[stim & R], c[stim & L]
                pc, ps = tp_(cR, cL) < 0.05, tp_(sR, sL) < 0.05
                sub = np.array([tp_(rng.choice(cR, len(sR), replace=False), rng.choice(cL, len(sL), replace=False)) < 0.05
                                for _ in range(n_sub)])
                out.append(dict(window=wn, session=s.key, ctrl_sel=pc, lose=pc and not ps, gain=ps and not pc,
                                sub_sel=sub.mean(), sub_lose=(sub & (not ps)).mean(), sub_gain=(~sub & ps).mean()))
    return pd.DataFrame(out)
