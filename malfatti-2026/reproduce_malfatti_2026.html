Reproducing Malfatti, Velica et al. (2026) from dandiset 002056¶

Paper. Malfatti T, Velica A, et al. (2026). Increased layer 5 Martinotti cell excitation reduces pyramidal cell population plasticity and improves learned motor execution. eLife 14:RP109286, doi:10.7554/eLife.109286. The claims file was written from the eLife reviewed preprint v3 (2026-11-02), its Methods, legends and supplement (Supplemental Figures 1 to 7, Supplemental Tables 1 to 6); this attempt read the same text.

Dandiset. DANDI:002056, version 0.261113.1541. The paper cites no version of the dandiset. This is its only published version (2026-11-13, "First release"), and the draft has the same 566 assets and size (95.86 GB), so the published version is pinned.

Code. The paper's code, https://gitlab.com/malfatti/M1MartinottiOnMovement at commit 86efdf1 (the commit the claims file read), and the functions it calls from SciScripts (https://gitlab.com/malfatti/SciScripts) at commit ef25f53, the last before 86efdf1. The prehension detection and classification and the statistics follow them; m1mc.py says where each parameter comes from.

What the dandiset holds. 224 NWB files of 31 mice in three groups, and 342 raw miniscope AVI files (89.4 GB):

  • the behaviour-only learning group (BehaviourL5GqMC), 13 mice, DeepLabCut (DLC) tracking of sessions 1 to 10;
  • the miniscope group (L5PCMSMCGq), 7 mice, DLC tracking of sessions 1, 2, 5 and 6, and a OnePhotonSeries that points to the raw miniscope videos, with no extracted cells or traces;
  • the LFP group (LFPM1GqMC), 11 mice, sessions 8 to 13, DLC tracking and one LFP channel at 30 kHz.

No histology, no caspase-ablation group and no pasta handling or hanging wire data are in it.

Several mice reach the same bounds, -6389.6 and 6389.6, which looks like the amplifier's range. In every one of the 13 behaviour-only mice the deposited session 5 and session 6 hold the same DLC trace: one recording deposited twice. The Methods say that session 5 of this group held both halves ("After five minutes in the fifth session, the pellet was instead placed on a fixed position on the plate ... for five more minutes"), so session 6 of the paper is the second half of the deposited session 5, and the deposited session 6 is a copy. Each recording is counted once: session 5 is split at 300 s and the deposited session 6 is dropped (helper learning_table). The group then has 10 distinct sessions per mouse where the paper's statistics (F(10,110)) imply 11. The pellet's tracked position moves at 300 s in session 5 in all 13 mice (next cell, median 112 px), which supports the split.

The two session 2 recordings of 67111 have the same number of DLC frames and pellet positions that agree frame by frame (r = 0.98, median 0.97 px apart): the same behaviour video tracked twice, deposited with two different imaging recordings. Only Trial1 is used. Those of 67112 differ.

If the streams share a clock the potential is strongest near lag 0.

LFP band power¶

Band power as the Methods describe (helper band_power): second-order Butterworth band-pass, spectrogram, normalisation by the mean, mean over the bins in the band; mean power over the 1 s before (phase "before") and the 1 s after onset ("during"), averaged over the prehensions of each accuracy per mouse and session, sessions 11 to 13. Readings, all run: spectrogram window 1 s or 0.5 s (Hann, 50% overlap); normalisation per frequency bin over the recording (bin), over all bins (all) or by the running mean of each bin (cum, the literal "cumulative mean"); and the alignment: as deposited (the reading the outcomes use), with the four mice without a locked potential at lag 0 left out, or with their onsets moved by the lag found (circular, shown only for comparison; see the clock check). The file holds one channel per mouse ("All accepted MOp5 channels"), so reading (b) of the claims file, the channel, has one answer. Mice with prehensions of both accuracies in all three sessions are used.

The before and during windows take the spectrogram bins whose centres fall in them. With 1 s windows a bin centred just before onset covers up to 0.5 s after it, so the two phases overlap in time; the 0.5 s windows halve this. No judged contrast is between the phases.

        row["failed mean"] = round(d[d.acc == "failed"].power.mean(), 4)

How the analysis differs from the paper's. Prehensions and their accuracy come from the paper's code applied to the deposited DLC tracking; the paper's own per-prehension tables are not public, so the detection cannot be compared one by one. The behaviour-only group has 10 distinct sessions per mouse where the paper has 11; session 5 is split at the Methods' five minutes, as the code's per-mouse split times are not public, and its halves are counted per 10 min of the whole recording, as the code does; per 10 min of each half, session 5 would not fall below session 2. The LFP group lacks session 7. The LFP is decimated to 1 kHz before filtering, and the spectrogram window, the normalisation and the channel are not given by the paper; every reading is shown. The LFP claims are judged on the files as deposited; leaving out the four mice without a locked potential at lag 0 gives the same outcomes. Realigning them (circular, shown only for comparison) gives one genotype part of fig4fh-gamma-session13 in four of six readings, the criterion's partial outcome, and changes none of the other outcomes. The miniscope tracking detects far fewer prehensions than that of the other groups. The ANOVA-type statistic is a Python implementation of the nparLD formulas, not R's nparLD.

What was found about the dataset. The miniscope data are raw video only, with no cells or traces, so none of the imaging findings can be tested without rerunning the extraction. The behaviour-only group's session 6 is a copy of session 5 in all 13 mice. The two session 2 recordings of miniscope mouse 67111 hold the same behaviour video. The LFP of four control mice shows no prehension-locked potential at the deposited alignment and one about 8 s earlier, consistent with an offset that the files cannot confirm. The LFP files' session start times look like placeholders (23:59:5x on the last day of a month). The LFP is labelled volts but holds values in the hundreds to thousands, with one mouse at a scale 4 to 60 times the others. All mice are labelled female, against the paper's male miniscope mice, and one file gives a genotype as unknown. The histology and caspase-ablation data are not in the dandiset.
