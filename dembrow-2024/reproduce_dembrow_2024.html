Dembrow, Sawchuk et al. (2024) on dandiset 001065¶

Paper. Dembrow NC, Sawchuk S, et al. (2024). Areal specializations in the morpho-electric and transcriptomic properties of primate layer 5 extratelencephalic projection neurons. Cell Reports 43:114718. doi:10.1016/j.celrep.2024.114718.

Dataset. DANDI:001065, version 0.241028.1559 (doi:10.48324/dandi.001065/0.241028.1559): 294 MIES current clamp NWB files, one per neuron, from macaque temporal and motor cortex. The paper cites the dandiset without a version; 0.241028.1559 is its only published version (the draft has the same 294 assets), so it is pinned.

Claims. claims/001065/10.1016-j.celrep.2024.114718.yaml, written from the accepted author manuscript (PMC11488157) with its STAR Methods and supplement, and from the paper's code (Zenodo record 13749343). The author added one link claim, link-cells.

Outside inputs. The files hold no area, preparation or subclass labels. They come from the per-cell tables of the paper's code on Zenodo (doi:10.5281/zenodo.13749343): area, preparation, transcriptomic, retrograde and predicted subclass, and drug, from L5_physiology_121223.csv; the cells of the 4 nA protocol from the keys of MCx.pkl and TCx.pkl. Their feature values are used only to check the link. The morphology claims use the dendritic features of RawFeatureWide.csv (same record), as the reconstructions themselves are in the Brain Image Library.

Every feature is computed here from the voltage and command traces (helper dembrow.py); the paper's feature extraction code was not deposited.

Loading¶

Every file is streamed from the archive and reduced, as it loads, to the features of each cell (the helper dembrow.py says where each definition comes from), the 1 s FI curve, the series of 1 s steps to 4 nA where there is one, and the spike times and interspike segments of the 10 s steps. Labels come from the paper's per-cell tables on Zenodo (record 13749343), matched by the file's cell_id.

link-cells: the dataset's cells are the paper's cells¶

Claim (added by the author, protocol step 1.4): each file matches the paper's per-cell table by cell name, the matched records agree on donor and species, and the features computed here from the recordings agree with the table's values. Reproduced when every file matches a row, donor and species agree for at least 99% of cells, and the median over the 39 features of the Spearman correlation between the two is at least 0.9; partially reproduced when the match holds but the agreement is lower. The 4 nA cells of the code's MCx.pkl and TCx.pkl are matched the same way, and their spike counts compared step by step.

How the analysis differs from the paper's:

  • The paper's feature extraction was not deposited. All 39 features are computed here from the traces by the Methods' definitions, with standard choices where the Methods leave a detail open (helper docstrings). zap_tau, one of the code's 40 features, is not defined in the paper and is left out. SFA follows the Methods (second over last ISI); the paper's table holds the inverse.
  • Files hold several runs of each protocol; which sweeps the paper used is not stated. The first run is used, and for the 1 s steps the first series reaching 1 nA.
  • 294 of the 341 cells of the code's table are in the dataset, so every test has fewer cells than the paper's.
  • The oscillation analysis (Figure 7) was R code that was not deposited. It is rebuilt from the Methods, with the standard choices the claims file names, including a smoothing spline (chosen by generalized cross-validation) over the FFT spectra of each sweep's segments. The observations are cell by 5 mV bins for the frequency and strength claims (fig7, fig7k) and sweeps for the others (blockers, fig7l, figs7g). Oscillation strength is the amplitude of the Hann-windowed spectrum, a choice of this attempt.
  • Added after verification round 1: a check of the 1 s hyperpolarizing sweeps (13 sweeps of 10 cells rejected; none is left with a negative input resistance), a check of the chirp profile (one cell), type II tests valid on the rank-deficient four-way design, variants on the paper's table values for the same cells, and other spike width definitions.
  • Added after verification round 2: the UMAP count of fig3a over ten seeds, and the text of the one wash-on comment in the files, with a variant that counts that cell's earlier sweeps as without blockers.
  • The morphology claims use the paper's deposited features of 19 reconstructions, not the 23 reconstructions themselves.

What was found about the dataset:

  • The files carry no area, preparation, subclass, drug or retrograde label; these come only from the code's tables, matched by cell name, which every file has.
  • 47 cells of the code's L5 table (35 of them recorded by one user) are not in the dataset.
  • One file's subject (Q19.26.009) differs from the table's donor (Q19.26.012), and the two files of subject Q19.01.001 are M. mulatta in the files and M. nemestrina in the table.
  • One subject has sex U and one subject's age is 1 year, below the 2 to 19 years the paper reports.
  • Five MCx files hold 1 s command steps above 4 nA (up to 16 nA), beyond the 4 nA the Methods give as the headstage limit; they are left out here.
  • Files do not record when synaptic blockers were applied, except one cell's user comment (a wash-on at sweep 91); the blocker condition comes from the drug column of the code's table, per cell.
