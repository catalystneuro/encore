Paper. Xiao W, Severson KS, Zheng H, Chen K, Thompson PM, Levy MS, Choi S, Zhao S, Takatoh J, Prevosto V, Wang F (2026). Peri-head distance coding in the mouse brainstem. Neuron. doi:10.1016/j.neuron.2026.05.027. The claims file was written from the accepted author manuscript in PubMed Central (NIHMS2191201, PMC13336321) with its supplement file (legends of Figures S1 to S6, Tables S2 to S5); the version of record was not read.

Dandiset. DANDI:001687, version 0.260821.2126. The paper cites no version. This is the most recent published version; it was published with the release note "Added link to published article in Related Resources" and holds the same 191 assets as 0.260805.1529. The earlier version 0.260618.1741 holds 67 assets (426 MB, the processed files without the raw files). The analysis reads the 67 processed files and, from the raw files, the pass times of the whisker-cutting sessions and short stretches of raw voltage (for waveforms, unit depth and SNR), all present in the latest version, which also names the paper, so it is pinned.

What the dandiset holds. 67 processed silicon-probe sessions from 36 wild-type (C57) mice, 1,358 sorted PrV units, with spike times, the trials of the full-array wall passes (start time, measured wall distance), the authors' per-unit labels and tuning curves; and the raw files (broadband voltage, LFP, behaviour video, digital and analog sync lines, all wall-pass epochs). It holds no tungsten recordings, no SpVi-TeLC or GtACR mice, no tracked whisker kinematics and no anesthetized sessions. In the 25 whisker-cutting sessions the post-cut passes are in the raw files' pass epochs but carry no wall distance.

Outside input. The paper's circuit model (PrV_simulation, GitHub wanglab-neuro/2026_Xiao-Severson_Peri-head-distance at commit b1a9f3c), for the one elsewhere claim.

Claims. Main reading for unit classification: the claims file, as amended after the question in questions/001687.yaml, makes the responsiveness test with BH-FDR across the nine distances (q < 0.05, as in Fig2DEF_Pop_Heatmap_Hist_Pie.m) the main reading and the uncorrected test (p < 0.05 at any distance) the variant. An earlier run of this attempt judged the claims on the uncorrected test, as the claims file then had it. Both readings are shown everywhere.

Raw-voltage waveforms and duplicate units¶

The processed files hold no waveforms. The cell below reads each session's raw voltage (16 storage chunks of 5.2 s spread over the session's deposited passes, see prv.unit_waveforms) and computes each unit's mean waveform, its peak channel and depth (claim figs4j-ventral-location), and an SNR (Methods, Isolation of Single Unit Activity: "the mean waveform amplitude divided by the standard deviation (SD) of waveform noise"), taken here as the peak-to-trough amplitude on the peak channel over the SD of single spikes about the mean on that channel, an assumption (prv.waveform_measures). In the 25 cutting sessions the pre-cut window ends at the cut and a second window covers the post-cut passes, for the waveform-stability proxy of the cutting section. It also applies the Methods' duplicate rule (Pearson r > 0.1 of spike counts in 1-ms bins, prv.duplicate_pairs) within each session.

Whisker-cutting sessions (claims fig4-cut-baseline-unchanged, fig4b-cut-wall-firing-reduced, fig4b-cut-per-unit-changes)¶

The processed files hold only the full-array passes (trial_condition is empty). The raw file of each cutting session lists every wall pass (wall_pass_epochs), before and after the cut, on the same clock. The cut is taken as the longest gap between raw passes; the full-array passes are the deposit's trials before it, the single-whisker passes the raw passes after it. The post-cut passes have no wall distance, so only measures pooled over distances can be computed. Units: all units of these sessions that pass the ISI and firing criteria. The paper's population is the 195 units from 18 mice "demonstrating stable waveform properties both before and after cutting" (Methods, Isolation of Single Unit Activity); the deposit does not say which units or which mice these are, and the 25 cutting sessions here hold 384 such units from 25 mice. How the analysis differs from the paper's. The SNR isolation criterion is not applied to the analysed population, as the SNR here can only be approximated (the processed files hold no waveforms); units are those passing the ISI and firing criteria (1,160 against the paper's 1,141). An SNR computed from the raw voltage, an approximation, leaves 1,034 units at SNR >= 5, and the Methods' duplicate rule flags 9 pairs; both are shown as variants of the unit count only. Responsiveness is judged with BH-FDR across the nine distances, the claims file's main reading after the answer in questions/001687.yaml; it gives the paper's counts and the deposit's labels. The uncorrected test, the variant, marks most units responsive; every result is shown under both. Distance bins pool all passes inside each 2-mm bin (the deposit's curves use one distance per bin in the two 1-mm-step sessions). Paired tests truncate each bin to the smallest number of passes, as the code does for two sessions. The decoders are written here from the Methods and the code (the Neural Decoding Toolbox is MATLAB); the SVM uses scikit-learn's LIBSVM wrapper on every third 150-ms window. Unit depth uses mean waveforms over 16 raw chunks per session, not all spikes. The whisker-cutting claims could not be tested on the paper's units; on subsets chosen by session notes or by a waveform-similarity proxy the baseline result changes with the threshold (p from 0.0141 to 0.29), while the fall in wall firing holds on every subset.

What was found about the dataset. The deposit holds the wild-type silicon-probe recordings only: no tungsten, TeLC, GtACR or anesthetized sessions and no tracked whisker kinematics, although its description names local and long-range inhibition perturbations. The processed files carry no isolation metrics, no waveforms and no condition labels; the post-cut passes of the 25 whisker-cutting sessions are in the raw files' pass epochs without wall distance, and the raw wall-actuator line does not encode distance. In two cutting sessions the full-array trials table includes 20 and 50 passes recorded after the gap in which the notes place the cut. The distances come in blocks that are permutations of the session's distances, in an order that varies between sessions and blocks, so the post-cut distances cannot be inferred. The deposit's labels follow the BH-FDR test of the code; the Methods do not say the test is corrected. Four files say they are the same recording as another session; none of those partners is deposited, and no spike train or pass sequence recurs across files.
