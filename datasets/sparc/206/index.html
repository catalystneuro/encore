Dataset version 1 · tested 2026-11-18 · 8 open findings

  • Data missingThe dataset holds none of the histology (ATF3, CGRP, synaptophysin), c-Fos counts, post-mortem notes or behavioural observations behind Figures 4 and 5 and the safety claims. files/README.txt lists summary derived data from c-Fos activation mapping, which no file holds. Stimulation trains are deposited for four rats (9 trains), where the paper stimulated eight, and no file records whether urine was released after a train; the 8-week rats have no stimulation. Thirteen claims cannot be tested for these reasons; with electrode-failures, which lacks per-electrode impedances for 2 of the 10 arrays, 14 are blocked as data_missing.
    Found by the attempt on Payne et al. 2020 (payne-2020-01, 2026-11-18)
  • OtherThe ECAP files are sampled at 200 kHz and carry a 500 to 3000 Hz filter, where the Methods give 100 kHz and 300 to 5000 Hz. The acute files step the current on a logarithmic scale up to 1.6 mA (CL 80 to 250, and CL0 at 0.0175 mA), not 0 to 2 mA in 0.1 mA steps, and have no 0 mA trace. Several files stimulate E3-E4 and record E1-E2 (12-008, 12-050, 12-053, 12-056) or stimulate E2 to E1 (12-006, 12-007), where the Methods give stimulation on E1-E2.
    Found by the attempt on Payne et al. 2020 (payne-2020-01, 2026-11-18)
  • Description missingThe seven awake ECAP recordings are seven subject identifiers (four at day 0, three at day 7) with nothing linking a day-7 recording to the rat recorded at day 0, so the week 0 to week 1 comparison of the paper (n = 4) cannot be made. The pool_id column of subjects.xlsx is empty. The Methods give three awake rats and the Results four.
    Found by the attempt on Payne et al. 2020 (payne-2020-01, 2026-11-18)
  • OtherIn the colonic recording of 15-758 the pressure rises by about 0.65 mmHg during the 10 s before each recorded stimulus time and returns to baseline at that time, over 33 trains, so the recorded times are 10 s (one train) after the response. The files do not say whether the times mark the end of each train. The colonic stimulation in all three files (1.114 mA, 200 us, 4 or 5 s; 0.541 mA, 25 us, 15 Hz, 10 s) differs from the Methods (1 mA, 100 us, 10 Hz).
    Found by the attempt on Payne et al. 2020 (payne-2020-01, 2026-11-18)
  • OtherThe derivative cystometry-dataset.xlsx and the /Voids tables of the same rats give different values for most cycles (peak pressure agrees within 0.5 mmHg in 4 of 27 cycles), and neither the files nor the manifests say how either was computed. The derivative, and the dataset's own R notebook output from it, do not give Table 1's means (minimum pressure 11.74 against 10.3 mmHg; inter-void interval 6.66 against 7.6 min), though each lies within Table 1's CI, and it has HFPO frequencies for 7 of the 9 rats.
    Found by the attempt on Payne et al. 2020 (payne-2020-01, 2026-11-18)
  • Description missingThe manifests of the derivative, code, docs and protocol folders have no descriptions, and the columns of the /Voids group (for example ContractionDuration, RelaxationTimeConstant, PostBaselinePressure) and the CL scale of the ECAP stimuli are not defined. Some cystometry waves carry an AppliedOffset attribute with no description. Neither the ECAP files nor the ECAP schema give the amplifier gain (the Methods give 10^2) or define time zero, and the schema gives TimeUnits s where the files have ms.
    Found by the attempt on Payne et al. 2020 (payne-2020-01, 2026-11-18)
  • OtherThe dataset lists 29 subjects, where the paper reports 24 rats in all.
    Found by the attempt on Payne et al. 2020 (payne-2020-01, 2026-11-18)
  • OtherThe per-array impedance file reproduces the paper's impedance means, spreads and ranges exactly, from 12, 12 and 10 arrays; the paper describes them as 38 electrodes in 10 arrays, and its spreads are SEMs over arrays. Per-electrode impedances are deposited for 8 arrays only.
    Found by the attempt on Payne et al. 2020 (payne-2020-01, 2026-11-18)
