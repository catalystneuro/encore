    Data Records, paragraphs 1 and 2

    How it will be judged

    The tolerance of 3 rows is chosen here in place of 10% (655 rows), which would not test an exact count. The paper says both that the final dataframe has 6552 rows (Data Records) and that the dataframe is cleaned of duplicates to 6549 unique pages (Methods), so the deposit may hold either; 3 rows covers both. Reproduced when jecfa.rds has 6552 rows within tolerance and holds a column for each of the 17 fields of Table 1 (by the code's names in process_df.R, or an evident equivalent); partially reproduced when the row count holds and at most three fields are missing (a threshold chosen here). JECFA.csv is compared with jecfa.rds and any difference in rows or columns is reported.

  • unique-pages

    The 6552 scraped entries, cleaned of the duplicates of page 6316 and of test pages, give 6549 unique pages.

    We validated the effectiveness of this method by identifying 6552 entries, which were then cleaned to remove duplicates of (E)-N-[2-(1,3-BENZODIOXOL-5-YL)ETHYL]-3- (3,4-DIMETHOXYPHENYL)PROP-2-ENAMIDE ... and test pages, resulting in 6549

    Methods, Web-scraping, paragraph 2

    How it will be judged

    The tolerance of 2 rows is chosen here in place of 10% (655 rows), which would not test an exact count; the Technical Validation gives the same number as "6,551 minus a duplicate and a test page". The paper names page 5883 as the test page and the chemical of page 6316 as the duplicate, without saying how a duplicate is detected. Readings, (D1) rows with distinct URL; (D2) rows with distinct JECFA name, then removing the row of URL 5883; (D3) the 6552 rows less the rows of URL 5883 and less all but one row whose JECFA name is that of page 6316. Reproduced when one reading gives 6549 within tolerance and page 6316's chemical appears more than once in the 6552 rows.
