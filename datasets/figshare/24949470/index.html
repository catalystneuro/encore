Dataset version 1 · tested 2026-10-27 · 5 open findings

  • Metadata errorIn "VAA_party_positions coded.xlsx" the "Statement no." column disagrees with the statement's wording in 99 of 429 rows. For every party, the rows worded as statements 16 to 24 (Supplementary Table 1 and the data dictionary) are numbered 22, 23, 24, 16, 17, 18, 19, 20, 21, so the environment, multilevel governance and form of government statements are mislabelled. The recoded CSV and the paper's Figures 2 and 3 follow the wording. A user who joins positions to the user responses by "Statement no." gets the wrong statements.
    Found by the attempt on Mintal et al. 2024 (mintal-2024-01, 2026-10-27)
  • Metadata errorThe paper says the responses were gathered during the 26 days up to and including election day, but 432 rows (0.32%) are timestamped 1 to 9 October 2023, after the election. Table 1's sample entry (2023-10-09 08:57:38) is one of them. The R code has an optional filter that drops rows after 2023-09-30 22:42:30, but the deposited file includes them and the paper does not say so; the filter drops 451 of the rows it is applied to.
    Found by the attempt on Mintal et al. 2024 (mintal-2024-01, 2026-10-27)
  • Metadata errorThe paper's Data cleaning says "no opinion" answers are empty strings; in data_without_email.csv they are the string "NA" (183,459 values in S1 to S39). The README calls each row "a unique respondent", while EMAIL_ID repeats (30,775 distinct ids over 32,009 rows with an id), as the paper says it may.
    Found by the attempt on Mintal et al. 2024 (mintal-2024-01, 2026-10-27)
  • Description missingThe data dictionary lists no categories for GENDER, AGE and EDUCATION (they appear only in the R code's validation rules), does not give the time zone of TIME.STAMP, and lists a "Self-placement source" column that is empty in all 429 rows.
    Found by the attempt on Mintal et al. 2024 (mintal-2024-01, 2026-10-27)
  • OtherFigure 3 places PS at 54.5 on the economic axis, right of centre, while the deposited final positions give 47.7 by the same calculation that reproduces every other party's placement in Figures 2 and 3 to within 0.8. The figure and the deposited positions disagree for this one party.
    Found by the attempt on Mintal et al. 2024 (mintal-2024-01, 2026-10-27)
