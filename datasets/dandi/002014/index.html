  • OtherNewAll streams share one clock. The stored bipolar LFP matches the raw c7b minus c5c in 1 to 8 Hz at a lag of 0 to 1 ms (r 0.89 to 0.94 in four trials, over a search of 5 s either way); the raw c5d trough falls 8 samples after the thresholded spike times, within 2 samples of the stored waveforms' trough (sample 10); the spike-free gap sits 46 to 50 ms after each stored FSCV scan time throughout the session, consistent with the stated mapping of scans to within one scan; the trial-aligned oxidation current follows the raw current near 0.6 V (a fitted kernel over 36 trials is positive from about -2.8 to +0.2 s and largest at -0.2 to 0 s), though smoothed so that it matches the raw current best about 1.5 s earlier (finding 9); the large minus small dopamine difference appears only after V; and the spike rate is modulated at V far beyond random alignments. The offset of 11812.1 s reported by the claims file for an earlier version does not hold in this one (r -0.12 to -0.02 at that offset, 0.89 to 0.94 at 0 to 1 ms).#
    Checked: No finding is rated critical, and it would rate none critical; the clock offset does not hold in this version (cell 5). It noted that "no shift of more than a few tenths of a second" is true of the kernel's peak (lag 0), while cell 8 also shows the detrended correlation peaking with the raw current 1.5 s earlier (median; range 0.8 to 2.5 s), and that the kernel is positive from about -2.8 s (cell 8 prints +0.2 at -2.8 s and +1.3 at -2.6 s), not from -2.4 s.
    Found by the attempt on Choi et al. 2025 (choi-2025-02, 2026-11-24)

  • Description missingPartly fixedThe trial-aligned oxidation current (iox) is a smoothed version of the raw current near 0.6 V, and the file does not say so. A linear kernel from the raw current to iox, fitted over 36 trials, is positive from about 2.8 s before to 0.2 s after each sample, largest at -0.2 to 0 s, and negative from +0.4 s, and the detrended correlation peaks with the raw current 0.8 to 2.5 s earlier (median 1.5 s). The PCA dopamine estimate (da) follows iox by a further 0.1 to 0.4 s (median 0.3 s, 13 trials with no NaN), so it carries at least the same delay; matched directly with the raw current, da peaks 1.2 to 5.0 s earlier (median 3.1 s, with 5.0 s the edge of the search). Responses in the trial-aligned series are therefore spread over about 2 s after the raw change, and a latency measured on them comes out late by about 1.5 s or more. How the PCA components were derived from the scans is not documented.#
    Found by the attempt on Choi et al. 2025 (choi-2025-02, 2026-11-24)

Fixed since an earlier attempt 7

  • File format errorFixedThe raw ephys and the spike times are not on the clock of the trials, events, LFP and FSCV table. The raw ephys is stored with a starting time of 0 s and a rate, and the spike times count from its first sample. Locating the raw c7b minus c5c signal in the stored LFP puts raw time 0 at 11812.11 s on the trial clock (correlation 0.98 to 1.00 at eight points, drift about 4 ms per hour). Without this correction spikes cannot be aligned to the task. The dandiset description says all data are expressed relative to the start of the Neuralynx recording, which the data contradict.
    The raw ephys now starts at 4612.10 s on the session clock and lines up with the stored LFP at a lag of 1 ms at the first and last trial (r 0.972 and 0.872, earlier_findings.ipynb); choi-2025-02 finds the same over four trials (its finding 2). Found fixed on dataset version 0.261120.0528 by choi-2025-02, 2026-11-24.
  • Metadata errorFixedThe absolute times disagree. On the trial clock the raw samples run from 12:06:44 to 17:12:20, while the Neuralynx header gives the recording as opened at 08:49:52 (the session start) and closed at 15:12:24, two hours before the raw samples end. The header span (22952 s) is also longer than the raw samples (18336 s).
    The raw samples now run from 10:06:44 to 15:12:20, ending 3.9 s before the Neuralynx header's close at 15:12:24, and their start 4612 s after the recording was opened accounts for the longer header span (earlier_findings.ipynb). Found fixed on dataset version 0.261120.0528 by choi-2025-02, 2026-11-24.
  • OtherFixedThe stored spike times of the thresholded units are about 7.1 ms after the spike troughs in raw c5d (in one 300 s segment of 1633 spikes, per-spike trough lags of -7.4 to -6.9 ms from the 10th to the 90th percentile, 98.8% within 0.5 ms of -7.1 ms).
    The raw c5d trough now falls 0.000 to 0.344 ms after the stored spike times on average at the start, middle and end of the recording, close to the stored waveforms' trough at 0.312 ms (earlier_findings.ipynb); the 7.1 ms lag is gone. Found fixed on dataset version 0.261120.0528 by choi-2025-02, 2026-11-24.
  • Metadata errorFixedThe series "bipolar_lfp_series" is described as beta-band LFP but is broadband with line noise removed. Its values are already in volts, while it carries the raw int16 conversion factor (3.05e-8), which would scale them about 3e7 times too small.
    The series is no longer described as beta-band, and it carries a conversion of 1.0 in volts (median absolute value 24.2 uV, earlier_findings.ipynb). Its preprocessing, now described as not documented, is choi-2025-02's finding 4, a new finding. Found fixed on dataset version 0.261120.0528 by choi-2025-02, 2026-11-24.
  • Metadata errorFixedThe thresholded spike waveforms are stated in volts but hold microvolts (mean trough about -56), and the raw FSCV current is stated in amperes but holds nanoamperes (values around 1000).
    The waveforms now hold volts (mean trough -5.636e-05), and the raw FSCV current carries a conversion of 1e-09 to amperes on values around 1000 (earlier_findings.ipynb). Found fixed on dataset version 0.261120.0528 by choi-2025-02, 2026-11-24.
  • Metadata errorFixedThe subject description ("Two adult female rhesus monkeys were used for in vivo validation of the s-µIP device"), the session and experiment descriptions and the keyword "micro-invasive neural implant" describe the lab's micro-invasive probe study, not this paper's chamber.
    The subject, session and experiment descriptions and the keywords now describe this paper's chamber implant and the 09/27/2024 session of monkey T (earlier_findings.ipynb). Found fixed on dataset version 0.261120.0528 by choi-2025-02, 2026-11-24.
  • File format errorFixedThe event table stores each event once per overlapping 60 s trial window, about five copies of each (7221 stored trial-start events for 1412 distinct ones), and its event times are not sorted. The Plexon units table's spike times are not sorted either.
    The event table now stores 14117 events, 1412 trial starts, with no repeated time and no decreasing step within a label, and the Plexon spike times are sorted (earlier_findings.ipynb). Found fixed on dataset version 0.261120.0528 by choi-2025-02, 2026-11-24.
