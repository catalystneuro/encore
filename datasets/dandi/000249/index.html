Dataset version 0.230423.1416 · tested 2026-10-29 · 12 open findings

  • Data missingThe NWB files hold only raw, unaligned two-photon movies. There are no ROIs or fluorescence traces, no stimulus times, no trial tables and no record of which call each trial played (pseudorandom order, Methods), so none of the paper's 20 imaging findings can be tested from the dandiset. The figshare imaging items hold the same movies.
    Found by the attempt on Schiavo et al. 2020 (schiavo-2020-01, 2026-10-29)
  • Data missingThe behavioural data of Figs. 1c, 4c-e and Extended Data Figs. 2 and 8-9 (pup retrieval, latencies, y-maze) and the vocalization recordings of Fig. 1a are in neither the dandiset nor the figshare collection it links. The paper's figshare DOI 10.35092/yhjc.c.5037737 does not resolve.
    Found by the attempt on Schiavo et al. 2020 (schiavo-2020-01, 2026-10-29)
  • Description missingGroups (experienced or naive, dam, CH+575, opto-OT, cell type, hemisphere) are given only in free-text experiment descriptions; the indicator and location fields are "unknown" in every file, and the start of cohousing, needed for retrieval onset, is not recorded.
    Found by the attempt on Schiavo et al. 2020 (schiavo-2020-01, 2026-10-29)
  • Metadata errorThree recordings (sessions of 2018-03-08 named plus24hrs_postexp1) are deposited under both sub-NV58 and sub-NV63, with the same start times and frames; nothing says to which mouse they belong.
    Found by the attempt on Schiavo et al. 2020 (schiavo-2020-01, 2026-10-29)
  • Metadata errorSubjects EV56 and EV62 are described as NV56 and NV62 in their own experiment descriptions, and the five tones-only mice carry no experience group in theirs; the figshare item titles give it (EV56_Experienced_Exc and so on).
    Found by the attempt on Schiavo et al. 2020 (schiavo-2020-01, 2026-10-29)
  • Description missingThe in vivo whole-cell spreadsheet of experienced females does not say which cells come from dams, so the dams-against-EVs comparison of Extended Data Fig. 6g,h cannot be made.
    Found by the attempt on Schiavo et al. 2020 (schiavo-2020-01, 2026-10-29)
  • Metadata errorIn the in vivo whole-cell article of experienced females, file 19424013 is listed at -70 mV but carries about +560 pA throughout, as at a depolarized holding potential.
    Found by the attempt on Schiavo et al. 2020 (schiavo-2020-01, 2026-10-29)
  • Data missingFiles listed in the figshare spreadsheets but not deposited, 193260007 (naive in vivo, listed number with an extra digit) and 19o11005 (slice, Cell 20 at 575 ms).
    Found by the attempt on Schiavo et al. 2020 (schiavo-2020-01, 2026-10-29)
  • File format errorThe operant touch-time csv files print times with five significant digits, so after 1000 s of a session they step by 0.1 s and 99% of samples repeat the previous value; press durations can be resolved only to that step.
    Found by the attempt on Schiavo et al. 2020 (schiavo-2020-01, 2026-10-29)
  • OtherIn the in vivo whole-cell recordings the current almost never follows the played calls: the evoked measure is in the expected direction at p < 0.05 in 2 of 39 cell recordings (3 of 39 before the call), no cell responds 0-300 ms after onset, 13 of 110 files have a sweep-locked deflection (5.5 expected by chance) of which 3, from two cells (190326_e1 and 190313_e2), carry an inward current during the call from about 0.4 s after onset, and the Methods' inclusion rule keeps at most 1 of 39. The experienced recordings are about twice as noisy as the naive ones (baseline s.d. 11.4 against 5.3 pA), and the largest-trial measure gives the same experienced-naive difference before the call as during it.
    Found by the attempt on Schiavo et al. 2020 (schiavo-2020-01, 2026-10-29)
  • OtherThe imaging Methods say the calls were played in a pseudorandom order at 0.1 Hz. The movies recur every 40 frames (10.24 s, 0.098 Hz) and the tone files every 20 frames (5.12 s); in 31 of 38 three-call movies (one per subject) folding at three trial periods stands above two and four, as a fixed cycle of three calls would. No file says which call is played at which position of the cycle.
    Found by the attempt on Schiavo et al. 2020 (schiavo-2020-01, 2026-10-29)
  • OtherThe first syllable of many played calls is much quieter than the rest on the microphone channel of the in vivo recordings; an onset taken at the first loud syllable comes a median 193 ms late.
    Found by the attempt on Schiavo et al. 2020 (schiavo-2020-01, 2026-10-29)
