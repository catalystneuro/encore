    The 57 files described as ephys ALM, M1TJ, S1TJ, S1BF, M1B, S1L and ZZ hold 1917 units, within 10% of the paper's 1840. Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Description missingThe opto sessions do not name the inhibited region; 35 sessions at 4 mW and 25 at 2 mW, five per mouse, carry only the light times and LED drive. Figure 2 and Extended Data Fig. 3j-m cannot be tested.
  • Description missingThe control sessions do not mark the condition; which earplug session had earplugs, which half of each air session had the air flow on, and which numbing session was lidocaine are not stated.
  • Description missingThe recorded area and the kind of session are given only by the session description (the name of the lab's data folder); there is no electrodes table, unit location, depth or quality label, and the area of the zigzag (ZZ) and "ephys others" sessions is not given.
  • Data missingSpike waveforms are not included, so units cannot be classified as putative pyramidal or FS cells (Extended Data Fig. 3c-f), and the fibre distance of the efficiency recordings is not given.
  • File format error1446 spike times in 35 files are duplicated within a unit, away from trial boundaries (median 2.9 s). The Methods say these Phy duplicates were removed before analysis; the deposited spike times keep them.
  • OtherOne training recording is deposited twice; the MX181003 learning file of 2018-10-02 15:30:57 repeats all 4028 touches of the MX181002 file with the same start time and then continues, so it is unclear which mouse it belongs to.
  • File format errorIn the 574 learning files start_time and stop_time count from 0 while cue, touch, port and water times are on another clock (around 10^6 s), so trial intervals and events do not line up.
  • File format errorThe seqId column of the learning files holds the text of an unconverted MATLAB categorical object, not a sequence identity.
  • Description missingForce sensor and lick port series are in volts without a calibration, tongue landmarks are in pixels without a scale (the Methods give 32 um per pixel), and every trial column is described as "no description".
  • Selection not documentedThe Methods leave out 8 sessions whose touch offsets were not registered; the files do not mark them. Eight sessions with backtracking have touches of a few ms (5th percentile 3 to 7 ms), and leaving them out gives the paper's 47 sessions.
  • Metadata errorThe session description is cut short ("... se") in the files themselves, so the full name of the lab's data folder, the only place the area and kind of session are given, is lost.
  • Description missingThe trial column opto (values 0, 1 and 2) and the opto1 and opto2 series (unit "categorical") have no description, and no file has optogenetics metadata (light source, site, power).
  • Methods not availableThe paper says its MATLAB analysis code is available at GitHub but gives no address, and no repository was found under the authors' accounts.

Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • As for fig3c-length.fig3c-velocity fig3c-identity
  • The contamination estimate (Hill et al. 2011) uses a censored period of 0 and the mean rate over 1 s bins of the trial span holding a spike, with all of a unit's spikes in the count; the Methods give none of these.ephys-unit-counts
  • Units recorded outside the target structure were excluded in the paper; the files do not say which these are, so all units are counted.ephys-unit-counts
  • The reading that matches, all 57 sessions, is wider than the sessions the Results describe.fig3-units-per-session
  • The subset of most similar trials is found by a greedy search; the Methods do not say how it was searched.fig3a-trial-count
  • The inter-lick-interval standardisation of the Methods is not applied before the fits.fig3c-length
  • Fits use scikit-learn's elastic net path (l1 ratio 0.1, 30 penalties, standardised predictors, one-SE rule), not MATLAB's lasso; samples are assigned to folds at random, as MATLAB does by default. Random folds let the autocorrelation within a trial into the test folds; R2 values are higher than with folds that keep trials together, the orderings the same.fig3c-length
  • The fitting window is -0.5 to 0.5 s from the middle touch, read from the Methods' remark that 0.5 to 0.8 s of the decoding was new data; the paper does not state it.fig3c-length
  • The expected values were read from the figure image, as the text does not give them.fig3c-length
  • As for fig3c-length. The sign of theta (left or right) cannot be fixed from the files; R2 does not depend on it.fig3c-angle
  • As for fig3c-length. tau is the time from the middle touch without lick standardisation.fig3c-time
  • The bound of 0.3 is a choice; the paper gives no threshold.ed5b-variables-uncorrelated
  • VE is computed on -0.5 to 0.8 s from the middle touch of the selected standard trials; the paper says "during sequence execution".ed5e-variance-explained
  • The bound of 0.3 is a choice; the paper says "largely orthogonal".ed5f-axes-orthogonal
  • Decoded variables come from the fits of fig3c-length, without lick standardisation.fig3e-cca
  • The paper's null and confidence intervals come from a hierarchical bootstrap; here the null is 20 label shuffles per session, averaged over sessions.fig3f-backtracking-svm
  • Trials are balanced across directions by random subsampling.fig3f-backtracking-svm
  • The PCA before the SVM is fitted on all trials of a session, without labels, before the cross-validation.fig3f-backtracking-svm
  • Subsequences are aligned on touches (the seventh touch against the third or eleventh), not on the cross-correlogram of tongue angle, and the extra selection of trials closest to the median angle is not applied.fig4g-zigzag-context
  • The null mean over bins uses every fifth 5 ms bin.fig4g-zigzag-context
  • The paper's ITI window and trial set are not fully stated; the second before the cue of every standard trial is used.ed7f-target-position-alm
  • The paper gives no test; the bound of 0.1 was fixed in the criterion.ed7h-no-time-coding
  • Only the last second before the cue is used, where the paper may have used more of the interval.ed7h-no-time-coding
  • PETHs are not computed on standardised inter-lick intervals.ed4m-active-neuron-counts
  • The unit classification is that of ephys-unit-counts, with its open choices.ed4m-active-neuron-counts
  • The paper shows histograms of peak times without a test; the test was chosen in the criterion.ed4l-alm-initiation
  • Without lick standardisation the rhythm of trial-averaged PETHs is blurred away from the middle touch, which lowers r in every region.ed4o-rhythmicity
  • The paper shows example neurons and decoded tau; the test over units was chosen in the criterion.ed6-alm-reward-drop
  • The paper does not list the sessions of Extended Data Fig. 1h; recording sessions (except zigzag) and the opto sessions' trials without light are used.ed1h-sequence-duration
  • Proficiency was defined in the criterion, as the paper gives no definition.ed1l-proficiency-1500
  • The paper's own trial exclusions (first and last trial, unfinished videos) are not applied in the readings of the criterion; they are shown as a variant.ed2-backtracking-counts
  • Sessions are the Figure 3 sessions with backtracking, not the paper's 47.ed2e-missed-licks
  • The paper does not say whether licks or touches are counted or where the bout ends; the bout rule (1.5 times the median interval) is the Methods' rule for lick bouts.ed6b-consummatory-licks
  • The side of the port is taken from the angle of the lick that made the first touch, as the files do not say which side each position is on.ed7b-correct-side
