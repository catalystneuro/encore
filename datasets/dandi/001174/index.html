Dataset version 0.250331.2218 · tested 2026-11-01 · 6 open findings

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds no TTL or trial data and no raw traces. The Figure 2 and Figure S1 analyses, which align raw traces to TTL-marked target and movement onsets, cannot be run; six of the paper's findings are untestable. The Zenodo table of aligned activity holds the paper's z-scored windows, not the event times.
    Found by the attempt on Martel et al. 2025 (martel-2025-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Description missingThe trace in processing/ophys/Fluorescence/RoiResponseSeries is the CNMFe denoised trace (between events it decays by one constant factor per frame, in a median 90% and at least 77% of a file's samples), but is described only as "Fluorescence data associated with spatial footprints". EventAmplitude holds the deconvolved events as a dense frame by cell array without saying that zero means no event. ImagingPlane indicator and location are "N/A" in every file; the site is only in session_description.
    Found by the attempt on Martel et al. 2025 (martel-2025-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Identifier missingNo cross-session cell identity is deposited. The dandiset gives each file's cells 0..n-1; the paper's Zenodo table gives a global id only at a cell's first session, and the spreadsheet the code reads for identity (Metadata_all_monkeys.xlsx) is not public. The fraction of cells tracked across sessions (18%) cannot be checked.
    Found by the attempt on Martel et al. 2025 (martel-2025-01, 2026-11-01)
  • OtherThe dandiset's events differ from the paper's per-cell table. The cells match (centroids within 3 pixels for 93.8%), but event rates agree within 1% for only 40.9% of cell-conditions and the table's are a median 4.2% higher (up to 50% at the 95th percentile); one-frame inter-event intervals are rarer in the dandiset (3.9% against 11.9% of cells with a one-frame median IEI), so some events of the paper's table are missing from the dandiset's event arrays. U SMA 2023-03-07 and 2023-03-14 differ by factors of 0.04 to 20; in both the dandiset has far fewer spontaneous events and a higher amplitude in every cell, which is not a swap of conditions and looks like a different event detection. With the dandiset's amplitudes the U task M1 against SMA difference is not significant (p = 0.099), with the table's it is (p = 0.014), and without those two sessions p = 0.041.
    Found by the attempt on Martel et al. 2025 (martel-2025-01, 2026-11-01)
  • OtherU SMA 2023-03-14 is sampled at 20 Hz (both files), where the Methods give 10 Hz for all imaging.
    Found by the attempt on Martel et al. 2025 (martel-2025-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Metadata errorThe spontaneous file of Q's 2022-09-15 session is named sub-Q/sub-Q_ophys.nwb, without the session label the other 44 files carry.
    Found by the attempt on Martel et al. 2025 (martel-2025-01, 2026-11-01)
