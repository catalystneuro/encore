Findings about the data 6

What the attempts that used this dataset found about the data themselves, for its owner and the archive. The claims and their outcomes are on each paper’s page.

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds no output of the six spike sorters the paper compares, including the Kilosort2 run that the two experts curated. Every comparison of sorters in the paper, and the comparison of the curations with Kilosort2, can be repeated only by running the sorters again, three of them in MATLAB, and a new Kilosort2 run will not give the units the curators worked on.
    Found by the attempt on Buccino et al. 2020 (buccino-2020-01, 2026-10-22)
  • Description missingThe two curated files hold a Units table of ids and spike times only (description 'Autogenerated by NWBFile'), with no sampling rate, no unit labels, no curator name and no link to the Kilosort2 clusters they were curated from; which file is curator 1 and which curator 2 is not stated (the files differ only by session time, 15:55:42 and 15:55:43). Their spike times fall on whole frames at 30 kHz and match the mouse recording's clock.
    Found by the attempt on Buccino et al. 2020 (buccino-2020-01, 2026-10-22)
  • Metadata errorThe retina recording is stored as unsigned 16-bit values centred near 2048 and reaching 4096, consistent with raw counts of a 12-bit converter, while the file states a conversion of 1e-6 V per unit and no offset, which would put every channel near +2 mV. The converter's gain and offset are not in the file.
    Found by the attempt on Buccino et al. 2020 (buccino-2020-01, 2026-10-22)
  • Metadata errorThe mouse 412804 recording stores int16 values with a conversion of 1e-6 V and no gain or channel_conversion per channel. Read that way its band-passed noise SD is about 50 uV and the median curated trough about 455 uV, several times usual Neuropixels levels; at the Neuropixels 1.0 gain of about 0.195 uV per bit the noise would be about 10 uV. The values are consistent with raw counts given a conversion that treats them as microvolts; the file holds nothing to show which.
    Found by the attempt on Buccino et al. 2020 (buccino-2020-01, 2026-10-22)
  • Description missingThe electrode tables give location 'unknown' and filtering 'none' for every channel and placeholder group descriptions; the mouse recording has 248 channels where the paper says 246 are active, without marking which; the simulated file does not say which ground-truth cells are excitatory or inhibitory or what noise was added; the archive's species summary lists only house mouse though one recording is from a rat (its file gives 'rattus rattus').
    Found by the attempt on Buccino et al. 2020 (buccino-2020-01, 2026-10-22)
  • OtherEvery raw series is stored in chunks of one channel by 7911 to 26368 frames, so reading a stretch of all channels through h5py fetches one chunk per channel. Streamed from the archive at 0.087 to 0.155 s per chunk in this run, reading a whole recording that way would take 6.2 to 16.9 h; one bulk ranged read of a file ran at 72 MB/s, 1.4 to 6.5 min per recording. These times vary between runs.
    Found by the attempt on Buccino et al. 2020 (buccino-2020-01, 2026-10-22)
