Dataset version 0.260715.2016 · tested 2026-11-03 · 12 open findings

  • Description missingThe behavioural files give no position of the stimulation contacts in the volumetric imaging volume (the electrode table gives positions along the probe only) and no activation calls, and they hold per-ROI dF/F, not the voxel-level responses the activation rule uses. The paper's counts, densities and radii of activated neurons (Fig. 2B to 2G, fig. S2) cannot be formed.
    Found by the attempt on Kim et al. 2026 (kim-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Identifier missingPlanar ROIs are segmented separately in each session and carry no identity across sessions, and no mean or reference images are stored for alignment, so the neurons tracked across weeks in Fig. 3 and fig. S6 cannot be found.
    Found by the attempt on Kim et al. 2026 (kim-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Data missingEach unit has one mean waveform of 90 samples on one channel. Waveforms of spikes in and out of stimulation (Fig. 4D) and the multi-channel templates UnitMatch needs (fig. S8) are not in the files, nor are labels of the tracked units.
    Found by the attempt on Kim et al. 2026 (kim-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Description missingThe control survey imaging gives no voxel size or plane spacing, and 2 to 9 of its 32 stimulation contact positions sit at the origin with no stated mapping from Ripple channel number to contact index, so distances of ROIs from the stimulation site cannot be formed.
    Found by the attempt on Kim et al. 2026 (kim-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • OtherThe files hold 795 unit-sessions in the 46 sessions of weeks 0 to 4 (999 over all 55 behavioural sessions), where the paper reports 973 units; 24 control imaging sessions where the paper reports 18; and units in 14 sessions of 4 control mice where fig. S7 reports 4 animals and 15 sessions.
    Found by the attempt on Kim et al. 2026 (kim-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Description missingThe trials table gives no response time for miss trials except 98, so the movement-onset threshold of fig. S5 (25th percentile over hit and miss trials) cannot be formed as stated; and response_time is measured from trial start, which precedes the train onset by 0 to 85 ms depending on the session (56 to 85 ms in ICMS101 2023-10-27).
    Found by the attempt on Kim et al. 2026 (kim-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Data missingMouse 6 (ICMS83) has no catch trials in its files (currents 2 to 10 uA only), though the Methods give each block 10 catch trials; its thresholds cannot use the false-alarm point.
    Found by the attempt on Kim et al. 2026 (kim-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Metadata errorThe electrical_stimulation tables describe 700 ms, 70-pulse trains, but in sessions with planar imaging they also hold 37 to 90 trains of up to 242 pulses lasting seconds, after the end of the electrophysiology recording.
    Found by the attempt on Kim et al. 2026 (kim-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Metadata errorCell types are spelled 'Pyramidal Cell', 'Narrow Interneuron' and 'unknown' in ICMS83 and 'pyramidal' and 'interneuron' elsewhere; and the stored type disagrees with the 0.45 ms trough-to-peak rule applied to the stored mean waveform for 72 units (29 interneurons at 0.45 ms or more, 43 pyramidal below).
    Found by the attempt on Kim et al. 2026 (kim-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Metadata errorstim_channel and the electrode table's channel_name are both described as Ripple channel numbers, but they are not the same numbering. The paper's code places stimulation channel c at (32 - c) x 60 um on the probe; with that the contacts lie at 0 to 520 um depth and, pooled over mice, the fraction of modulated units falls with distance from them (not in Mice 5 and 6, nor in the nearest 60 um), while taking the electrode table's rel_y for channel_name = c puts them at -156 to 1559 um, some outside the electrode table, and shows no fall-off pooled. The mapping from stim_channel to a contact position is not stated in the files.
    Found by the attempt on Kim et al. 2026 (kim-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Description missingNo per-pulse stimulation times are stored; the trains have onsets and offsets only (every behavioural train lasts 700.00 ms), so pulse-locked analyses must place the pulses at their nominal 10 ms (20 ms in the controls) spacing.
    Found by the attempt on Kim et al. 2026 (kim-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Metadata errorIn the control files the trials table's trial_index is described as the trial index within the session, but it restarts in each recording block (rep_num), so the same index names up to several trials of a session, and the electrical_stimulation table's trial_index, which refers to it, cannot tell them apart without the block.
    Found by the attempt on Kim et al. 2026 (kim-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
