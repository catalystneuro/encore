Dataset unpublished draft (modified 2026-10-21) · tested 2026-10-21 · 5 open findings

  • Data missingStill present since pasquereau-2013-01. The monkey L (Leu) files mark no torque perturbation; torque_perturbation_type is not_available in every Leu trial, the Leu torque command is NaN in every pre-MPTP file, and 15 of 50 post-MPTP Leu files have no analog data. The draft's description and the files now say the Leu source files contain no perturbation times. The monkey L half of the paper (115 and 73 cells, and its EMG and kinematics) cannot be tested.
    Found by the attempt on Pasquereau et al. 2013 (pasquereau-2013-02, 2026-10-21)
  • OtherPartly fixed since pasquereau-2013-01. The torque command's 50 ms pulse still has three session sizes (about 46, 91 and 183 units above rest); most pre-MPTP monkey V sessions have the smallest (105 of 154) and most post-MPTP sessions the largest (125 of 137), post-MPTP pulses are followed by an opposite swing, and within post-MPTP sessions the step varies from trial to trial. The torque description now states all of this and that no conversion to newton-metres is available. The paper describes one 0.1 Nm, 50 ms impulse; whether the torque or only its command changed cannot be told, and movement and EMG results differ between command sizes.
    Found by the attempt on Pasquereau et al. 2013 (pasquereau-2013-02, 2026-10-21)
  • OtherPartly fixed since pasquereau-2013-01. The EMG description now gives the paper's processing (rectified, low-pass filtered at 100 Hz) where it said sample-hold integrated; the values are unchanged. The monkey L analog streams are still sampled at 100 Hz (the paper says 200 Hz) and the EMG is in uncalibrated A/D units, both now stated in the dandiset's description.
    Found by the attempt on Pasquereau et al. 2013 (pasquereau-2013-02, 2026-10-21)
  • OtherStill present since pasquereau-2013-01, now stated in the dandiset's description. The torque onset comes 1.696 to 1.732 s after center_target_appearance_time in 90% of monkey V perturbations, where the Methods put it 1 to 2 s after center capture at random.
    Found by the attempt on Pasquereau et al. 2013 (pasquereau-2013-02, 2026-10-21)
  • OtherNew. Three monkey V sessions (v1401, v1402, v1501) have 51 labelled perturbations but no pulse in the torque command, as the draft's torque description says. Their perturbations are kept here, as the command is not used to select trials.
    Found by the attempt on Pasquereau et al. 2013 (pasquereau-2013-02, 2026-10-21)
