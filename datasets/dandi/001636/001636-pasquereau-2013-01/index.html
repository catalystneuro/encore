Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingThe monkey L (Leu) files mark no torque perturbation; torque_perturbation_type is "none" in every Leu trial, the Leu torque command is NaN in every pre-MPTP file, and 15 of 51 post-MPTP Leu files have no analog data. The monkey L half of the paper (115 and 73 cells, and its EMG and kinematics) cannot be tested.
  • File format errorThe analog streams (ElbowAngle, ElbowTorque, ElbowVelocity, EMG) are not on the clock of the trials table and spikes. The conversion lays each trial's analog samples end to end by sample count, while trials and spikes use the behavioural trial end, about 5 ms later per trial, so the stored analog clock falls behind by up to 365 ms within a session. Moving each analog segment to its trial's start puts the torque command step 4 to 7 ms after every labelled onset; on the stored clock it falls up to 108 ms before it.
  • OtherThe torque command's 50 ms pulse has three sizes (about 46, 91 and 183 units above rest). Most pre-MPTP monkey V sessions have the smallest (108 of 157) and most post-MPTP sessions the largest (129 of 141), and post-MPTP pulses are followed by an opposite swing. The paper describes one 0.1 Nm, 50 ms impulse; the command's scale is not given, so the change may be of the torque or of the command's recording. Movement and EMG results differ between command sizes.
  • Metadata errorThe ElbowAngle reference_frame says positive is extension; active flexion movements and flexion torques raise the angle in both monkeys, so positive is flexion. The series are named Elbow for monkey L, whose task used the wrist.
  • Metadata errorFour monkey V files (v4527b, v4810b, v5604b, v5812) repeat a block of another file's trials. In two of them the same unit, with identical spikes in every repeated trial, is stored twice with different projection types (v4527 unit 2 not_tested and v4527b unit 2 a PTN; v5811 unit 2 a PTN and v5812 unit 2 no_response).
  • Metadata errorunit_also_in_session_id uses the prefixes V and L where session ids use Venus and Leu, and 35 of its 43 references name files that are not in the dandiset. Where the file is present, it holds a different unit (another unit number, spike count and often projection type), not the same neuron.
  • Metadata errorThe dandiset description says MPTP was given systemically, that both monkeys made elbow movements and that Leu was recorded in 1993. The files and the paper say unilateral intracarotid MPTP, monkey L used the wrist, and Leu files date from August 1992.
  • OtherThe monkey L analog streams are sampled at 100 Hz; the paper says 200 Hz. The EMG is described as sample-hold integrated over 10 ms in the files and low-pass filtered at 100 Hz in the paper, and is stored in uncalibrated A/D units.
  • OtherThe torque onset comes 1.70 to 1.73 s after center_target_appearance_time in 90% of monkey V perturbations, where the Methods put it 1 to 2 s after center capture at random. Either the column marks another event or the torque was predictable from the target's appearance.
  • Methods not availableNo analysis code is published. The response detection as described gives a false-positive rate near 0.2 over the 200 ms epoch, against the 0.01 its threshold implies, and stationary Poisson spike trains give 0.15, most of it, so the paper's exact procedure cannot be recovered, and single-trial latencies are not defined.
  • Paper inconsistentThe pre-MPTP PTN and CSN resting rates are 16.3 and 3.6 spikes/s in the text and 18.3 and 3.2 in Table 1. Table 1 marks the CSN change in the fraction of excitatory responses p < 0.01, but its own counts (23 of 24 against 19 of 22) give p = 0.54 with the Yates-corrected chi-square test that reproduces its other chi-square values.

Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • Monkey V only.baseline-rate ptn-faster-than-csn kinematic-latency-unchanged
  • The paper's location and isolation criteria cannot be applied; the units in the dandiset are taken as the selected units, which the matching counts support.cell-counts
  • The baseline is the mean SDF over the 500 ms before onset over all perturbation trials, a reading of the Methods.baseline-rate
  • Monkey V cells studied, against expected values that pool both monkeys.antidromic-latency
  • The detection compares the trial-averaged SDF with mean +/- t * SD of its baseline values, a reading of the Methods; a t-test across trials is shown as a variant.false-positive-rate
  • A response's onset is its first crossing within 20 to 60 ms; the Methods define the onset as the first crossing, so a variant takes the first crossing anywhere in 0 to 200 ms (6 of 202 responses have an earlier crossing).false-positive-rate
  • Categories are set from which directions respond and their signs, as fixed in the claims file; the paper words them by DSI value. The DSI value reading is shown as a variant.dsi-reduced
  • Monkey V only; the paper's ANOVA has animal as a third factor.kinematics-slower
  • The peak velocity and amplitude windows (0 to 200 and 0 to 300 ms) and the FIR length are choices; the Methods give the filter and velocity threshold only.kinematics-slower
  • Perturbations with the joint moving before onset (490 of 5413) are kept.kinematics-slower
  • Zero-phase (forward and backward) filtering; the Methods do not say how the filter was applied.kinematic-latency-unchanged
  • EMG in A/D units; the paper reports microvolts.emg-m2-larger
