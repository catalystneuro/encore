Dataset version 0.251012.1648 · tested 2026-11-05 · 10 open findings

  • Metadata errorThe sortings in the sub-003-fitted folder (dense probe, depth 1) are identical, spike for spike, to those in sub-004-fitted (depth 2) for all six sorters, and they match the depth 2 ground truth (78 of 131 Kilosort4 units with a best agreement of 0.5 or more) and not the depth 1 ground truth (none). The depth 1 sortings are therefore missing, and the depth 1 folder holds a copy of depth 2's.
    Found by the attempt on Laquitaine et al. 2024 (laquitaine-2024-01, 2026-11-05)
  • Data missingThe paper's results on Buccino's synthetic recording (S), on NS down-sampled by two (NS-2X) and on the 10-minute 40 kHz evoked recording rest on sortings that are not in the dandiset. S's traces and ground truth are deposited without sortings, NS-2X is absent, and the 10-minute evoked recording has traces and ground truth but no sortings. Seven claims cannot be tested, or only in part, for this reason.
    Found by the attempt on Laquitaine et al. 2024 (laquitaine-2024-01, 2026-11-05)
  • Description missingThe evoked recordings (sub-002, sub-002-fitted, sub-npx-evoked-sf-40Hz and their sortings) carry no stimulus table, trial intervals or stimulus description, so the stimulus direction and time of each presentation cannot be recovered from the dandiset. The paper's code rebuilds the schedule from parameters in its scripts. The five Figure 7 claims cannot be tested.
    Found by the attempt on Laquitaine et al. 2024 (laquitaine-2024-01, 2026-11-05)
  • Description missingThe ground truth tables of the dense probe simulations (sub-003 to sub-005, raw and fitted) hold only spike times and neuron names, without the soma positions, layers, synapse classes and electrical types that the Neuropixels ground truth tables carry. Only 56 of the 287 depth 1 neurons can be found by name in the Neuropixels tables.
    Found by the attempt on Laquitaine et al. 2024 (laquitaine-2024-01, 2026-11-05)
  • Metadata errorThe Horvath recordings (sub-vivo-horvath-depth-1 to 3) store int16 counts with an NWB conversion of 0.195, which declares 0.195 volts per count, and an electrode column gain_to_uV of 195000; neither is plausible, and they disagree. At 0.195 uV per count the median noise is 5.6 uV, while the dense probe model fitted to these data has 28.3 uV; in counts the noise is 28.7. The intended scale should be stated.
    Found by the attempt on Laquitaine et al. 2024 (laquitaine-2024-01, 2026-11-05)
  • Metadata errorThe Reyes probe traces of cell 3754013 (sub-biophy-isolated-traces-reyes and sub-biophy-summed-traces-reyes) both declare microvolts (conversion 1e-6), but the cell's spike-triggered peak-to-peak at the nearest electrode is 0.0424 in the isolated file and 37.9 in the summed file; the paper's code plots the isolated values in mV. The isolated file appears to be in millivolts. The files also lack the soma position of the cell, which the code hard-codes.
    Found by the attempt on Laquitaine et al. 2024 (laquitaine-2024-01, 2026-11-05)
  • Other78 of the 1,388 NS ground truth units (sub-001) and 213 of the 1,836 units of the 1-hour evoked recording (sub-002) have no spike in the first 10 minutes, though all fire later in their files, while the paper says that all model neurons fired at least once in the first 10 minutes.
    Found by the attempt on Laquitaine et al. 2024 (laquitaine-2024-01, 2026-11-05)
  • OtherThe depth 3 dense probe sortings (sub-005-fitted) barely agree with the depth 3 ground truth. 34.6% of Kilosort4 spikes fall within 0.65 ms of a ground truth spike, against 33.6% after a 50 ms shift (every sorter gives an excess of at most 1.1 points), no lag within 100 ms raises any sorter's coincidence rate more than 0.011 above its median over lags against the depth 1, 2 or 3 ground truth, no Kilosort4 unit reaches an agreement of 0.5, and one neuron in 1,123 is well sorted by any sorter. The deposit could not be shown to pair these sortings with any deposited ground truth.
    Found by the attempt on Laquitaine et al. 2024 (laquitaine-2024-01, 2026-11-05)
  • Description missingThe sorting tables' columns (KSLabel, KSLabel_repeat, layer, contact, ContamPct, Amplitude, firing_rates) have "No description", the contact column is a row index for the Neuropixels files and a 1-based channel name for the Horvath files, and the session descriptions of the Horvath and dense probe sortings contain an unformatted "{depth}" placeholder. Many raw files give related_publications as "doi:" with no identifier.
    Found by the attempt on Laquitaine et al. 2024 (laquitaine-2024-01, 2026-11-05)
  • Description missingThe Kilosort2 sorting of the evoked recording labelled 10 minutes (sub-002-fitted_ses-ks2-10m) ends at 240 s, as the Methods say Kilosort2 sorted only 4 minutes of the evoked recording, but neither its file name nor its metadata says so. Rates computed over 600 s would be 2.5 times too low.
    Found by the attempt on Laquitaine et al. 2024 (laquitaine-2024-01, 2026-11-05)
