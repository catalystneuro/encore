Findings about the data 15

What the attempts that used this dataset found about the data themselves, for its owner and the archive. The claims and their outcomes are on each paper’s page.

  • Metadata errorThe seizure label table (intervals/custom_intervals_table_name) of all 70 labelled EEG blocks gives start and stop times in minutes, while NWB prescribes seconds; the stops reach 62.7 in blocks of 3750 s. Read as minutes the labels match the EEG (30 to 80 Hz power 2.5 to 50 times higher inside them in all 136 block-hemispheres); read as seconds they do not. The EP and PLV files' interval tables are in seconds.
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2026 (gao-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Metadata errorThe five block files of sham rat BH635 are labelled BASELINE, SHAM, SHAM, BH636_post1 (another rat's number) and BH635_post2, with no post3, while every other sham rat has BASELINE, SHAM, post1, post2, post3. The files follow each other at 63 to 68 min like the other rats' blocks. BH649's post 3 file has the session id None in its path.
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2026 (gao-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Metadata errorEvery file gives the subject as "HSD:WI rat" (Envigo's Wistar code) of age P24W, while the paper used Sprague-Dawley rats (Envigo) of 3 to 6 months.
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2026 (gao-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Metadata errorThe ElectricalSeries of the EP files is described as "EEG data" though the files hold the 64 intracranial channels of left S1 (location Left S1, notes "LFP signal for EP test"). The PLV files are labelled 1Hz but 19 of 20 carry their pulses every 2 s.
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2026 (gao-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
