Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Metadata errorThe trial-table column rule_belief equals P(rule A) - P(rule B) in all 300 sessions, recomputed from the stored association matrix, and correlates negatively with the current rule being B (median r -0.73). The paper's Methods and the dataset's tutorial notebook both define it as positive for rule B. The other strategy columns match the Methods' formulas exactly.
  • Metadata errorIn the single-plane sessions the timestamps of the deconvolved activity, labelled seconds, are 2000 times the behaviour clock (for example 25356 against 12.678 s at the first frame), while the planes have exactly one frame per behaviour frame. Matching activity to behaviour by these timestamps puts every frame at the end of the session.
  • Selection not documentedThe paper's session counts cannot be recovered from the files. With session fraction correct excluding visually guided trials (the Methods' rule for all analyses) there are 291 sessions above 0.5, 154 above 0.65 and 100 layer 2/3 sessions above 0.65 (69,707 neurons), against the paper's 253, 216 and 141 (93,881 neurons). With all trials there are 297, 238 and 150 (97,989 neurons). Only the last matches the neuron counts within 10%. No file marks which sessions the paper analysed.
  • Paper inconsistentThe paper says the fraction of visually guided trials typically varied from 0 to 20%; in the files the median session has 23% and 222 of 300 sessions have more than 20% (up to 78%).
  • Data missingThe photoinhibition experiments (7 VGAT-ChR2 mice, 164 sessions; Figures 1J to 1M, S1K to S1M, S2M) are not in the dandiset, and the paper names no other archive for them. Three of the paper's findings cannot be tested.
  • OtherThe dandiset holds no GLM fits, encoding strengths or population decoders, on which 29 of the 43 claims rest. Refitting the GLM is estimated at no less than 652 CPU hours (a lower bound timed in the notebook, which varies between runs up to 1,191), so the 24 GLM claims cannot be checked against the deposit without the derived results or far more compute; the decoders were refit here on a subsample of sessions.
  • Other7 of 300 sessions have a 150-unit (1.5 m) maze stem where the paper describes a 200-unit stem; the TaskParam metadata records it. The analyses here use each session's own stem length.
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the paper's authors. How was the rule update per trial (Δ rule belief) of visually guided and normal trials computed (STAR Methods, Decision-making strategy variables)? Taking it as the change from trial t to t + 1 gives a larger update for visually guided trials (0.055 against 0.022, p < 0.001); from t - 1 to t gives none (0.030 against 0.027, p = 0.48); both give values 2 to 5 times the reported 0.013 and 0.0080.
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the paper's authors. Was the 65% session threshold applied to fraction correct over all trials, including visually guided ones? Only that reading comes near the 141 sessions and 93,881 neurons, but it gives 238 sessions where the supplement reports 216, and no reading gives the 253 sessions above 0.5.
  • Description missingThe Figure 8C legend has 698 decoders (100 in V1) from sessions with the cue offset at 0.76 of the maze, but the TaskParam cue delay length gives that offset (60 of 250 maze units) for only 8 of the 172 multi-plane layer 2/3 session files and 6 of the 142 in the decoder pool (fields RSC 2, A 2, PM 1, MM 1; 53 decoders), none with a field in V1 or AM; most sessions have a delay of 10 units (offset 0.96) or none.
