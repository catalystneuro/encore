Dataset version 0.251124.0550 · tested 2026-11-02 · 7 open findings

  • OtherIn version 0.250406.0045, the version the paper cites, every file of a mouse carries the behaviour (position, speed, licks, trials, rewards) of one and the same session: 152 files hold 11 distinct behaviour series, and the behaviour's length equals the imaging length in only 11 files. For example, every m11 file holds the behaviour of day 5 (zone A then C), whatever its scene name says. Imaging and behaviour cannot be aligned in the other 141 files. Version 0.251124.0550 has 152 distinct series, of the imaging's length (to one frame in 10 files), with zones that agree with the scene names. Version 0.250609.2249 has the same total size as 0.250406.0045.
    Found by the attempt on Sosa et al. 2025 (sosa-2025-01, 2026-11-02)
  • Description missingThe files' processing/ophys/Deconvolved series (unit 'lumens', no description) is not the deconvolved activity the paper analyses, which the Methods describe as OASIS on dF/F. Its median correlation with OASIS on dF/F computed from the files' F and neuropil is 0.47 per session (0.37 to 0.70). Spatial information on it marks 49.6% of cells as place cells, against 48.7% with OASIS on dF/F. The files hold no dF/F, so the paper's signals must be rebuilt from F and the code's parameters.
    Found by the attempt on Sosa et al. 2025 (sosa-2025-01, 2026-11-02)
  • Data missingThe files hold no cross-day ROI matching, although the Methods say cells were followed across days by the IoU of their pixels. Figure 5 and Extended Data Fig. 6 are tested here on matches made by this attempt, after a rigid alignment of the days' mean images.
    Found by the attempt on Sosa et al. 2025 (sosa-2025-01, 2026-11-02)
  • Data missingThe z-stack and the per-ROI depth estimates of single-plane mice (Methods, Estimation of anatomical location per ROI) are not in the dandiset, so the superficial-deep part of Extended Data Fig. 7 cannot be tested. The three fixed-condition mice (Extended Data Fig. 1d,f and 6d) are not in the dandiset either.
    Found by the attempt on Sosa et al. 2025 (sosa-2025-01, 2026-11-02)
  • Description missingOmitted rewards are not marked. The files give reward times, but an unrewarded trial can be an omission or a miss, which the paper treats differently (longer jitter after both, but only omissions in Figure 6). This attempt took unrewarded trials with licks in the zone as omissions; the trial sets with at least three omissions then match the paper's day counts per mouse exactly.
    Found by the attempt on Sosa et al. 2025 (sosa-2025-01, 2026-11-02)
  • Metadata errorThe ROI pixel and voxel masks name their coordinates x and y, but 'x' is consistent with indexing the image rows and 'y' the columns. In all 152 files 'y' reaches 630 to 788, beyond the 512 image rows, while 'x' stays at or below 508 (images 512 rows by 796 columns). The 'reward_zone' series is described as 'reward zone entry (binary)' but takes values 0 to 8. It rises in all 10,342 rewarded trials and in 52 of the 1,874 unrewarded ones, so it does not by itself mark omissions.
    Found by the attempt on Sosa et al. 2025 (sosa-2025-01, 2026-11-02)
  • Description missingThe files do not say how the image axes map onto the medio-lateral and antero-posterior axes, which Extended Data Fig. 7 uses.
    Found by the attempt on Sosa et al. 2025 (sosa-2025-01, 2026-11-02)
