Dataset version 0.261122.2159 · tested 2026-11-25 · 10 open findings

  • Metadata errorStill presentIn sub-Ca-EEG2-1 the behaviour sessions are timestamped about 1 h later than the offline recordings and the telemetry. The offline file's EEG is the Week file's EEG from the OfflineDay2Session1 epoch start (offset +0.002 s, difference 0 V), so they share one clock. On it the telemetry drops out (EEG constant, the mouse off its home-cage receiver) from 3780 to 2560 s before the NeutralExposure start time, 3710 to 2930 s before FC's and 3730 to 2850 s before Recall1's, and is normal during the FC epoch itself; each session fits in its dropout if it started 1 h before its timestamp. By the timestamps (session_start_time and the Week file's epochs), OfflineDay2Session1, described as recorded after fear conditioning, starts at 09:24:27, 47 min before FC (10:11:24), and 113 s after the telemetry dropout ends; OfflineDay2Session2 also precedes FC, and OfflineDay1Session1 to 4 precede NeutralExposure. The files do not say which clock is right. No analysis here combines sessions in time, but one that orders the offline recordings by FC's time would drop the first two after aversive encoding.#
    Found by the attempt on Zaki et al. 2024 (zaki-2024-02, 2026-11-25)
  • OtherStill presentThe sleep_state column of SleepIntervals in the offline files uses four states, "quiet wake", "wake", "sws" and "rem", where the paper scores wake, NREM and REM (Methods, Sleep recordings and sleep scoring) and names no quiet wake state. The column's description defines quiet wake as a waking state ("The animal is awake and alert but at rest"), and the Methods' sleep/wake split scores 64 of the 68 quiet-wake epochs of Ca_EEG2-1's offline recording as wake, so quiet wake is part of the paper's wake. A user who takes "wake" for the paper's wake leaves out 61 of 87 bursts in Ca_EEG2-1, and the overlap-neutral against overlap-aversive comparison of Fig. 5c reverses. (The first version of this finding said the column had no description of quiet wake; it has one.)#
    Found by the attempt on Zaki et al. 2024 (zaki-2024-02, 2026-11-25)
  • Data missingStill presentThe dataset holds 2 of the 9 mice of the calcium imaging and EEG/EMG experiment and one offline recording per offline period (for Ca_EEG2-1, 1 of the 24 recordings after aversive encoding, with cell-registration tables for 19 recordings of that day whose imaging is not deposited). Ca_EEG3-4 has no cell registration, Ca_EEG2-1 has no neutral or novel recall, and the deposited recordings hold no NREM or REM. The mice of the one-hour offline, behaviour-only, chemogenetic, cocaine, inhibitory tagging and no-shock experiments are not in it; 20 of the 26 claims cannot be tested, and the paper names no archive for the rest of its data.#
    Found by the attempt on Zaki et al. 2024 (zaki-2024-02, 2026-11-25)

  • OtherNewChecks that passed. The cell registration of Ca_EEG2-1 gives 97 neutral, 80 aversive, 166 overlap and 282 remaining cells in its offline recording, each of its 625 ROIs once; the freezing of both mice agrees with the paper's source data (reading b equal to two decimals for Ca_EEG3-4); the FC shock times of the description show in Ca_EEG2-1's motion (3387 to 3848 in the 2 s after each shock against a 95th percentile of 843 elsewhere); no asset shares a digest and no two sessions of equal length have correlated motion (largest r 0.131), so no recording is deposited twice; and the three files that changed since version 0.260825.1902 hold the same values in every array used here. A shared clock for imaging and behaviour could not be established in four sessions (Ca_EEG2-1 FC and NeutralExposure, Ca_EEG3-4 Recall2 and Recall3), where the correlation of population activity with motion peaks away from lag 0; no claim here uses imaging and behaviour together. The offline file's sleep labels of Ca_EEG2-1 agree with a scoring of the Week telemetry by the Methods' procedure (70 of 74 epochs labelled wake or quiet wake are scored wake).#
