Dataset version 0.220126.1903 · tested 2026-11-14 · 9 open findings

  • Data missingStill presentBoth datasets hold only the ALM recordings during CN photo-activation (34 sessions, 9 mice) and CN photo-inhibition (20 sessions, 4 mice). The lesion experiments, the behavior-only sessions (including response-epoch and 1.3 s photostimulation), the 878 CN units, lick times, video, the VGAT-ChR2-EYFP experiments, the anatomy, and the tenth mouse of the Fig. 2 ALM recordings are not deposited; 24 of the 46 claims in the claims file cannot be tested.
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2018 (gao-2018-02, 2026-11-14); also about CRCNS:alm-4
  • Data missingNewDANDI:000007 lacks trials that CRCNS:alm-4 holds with GoodTrials = 1 and a valid photostimulation code; ANM314746 2015-10-22 has 258 of 284 (trials 279 to 304, the last 26 such trials, missing) and ANM314757 2015-10-21 has 34 of 302 (trials 48 to 315 missing); in both, DANDI holds no trial after the first missing one. In the other 52 sessions DANDI holds exactly those trials, with the same codes and outcomes, and every unit's spike times are identical.
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2018 (gao-2018-02, 2026-11-14)
  • Metadata errorStill presentIn DANDI:000007 the trial columns cue_time and pole_in_time are described as relative to the trial start, but hold session times; trial start times are rounded (they differ from CRCNS:alm-4 by up to 0.05 s).
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2018 (gao-2018-02, 2026-11-14)
  • Metadata errorStill presentFor the photo-inhibition mice BAYLORCD12, BAYLORCD13 and BAYLORCD14 both datasets give the photostimulation site as the left fastigial nucleus and the ALM recording at ML -1.5 mm (left), on the same side (DANDI: optogenetics hemisphere left, ML -0.8 mm; electrode group ML -1.5 mm; for BAYLORCD8 right and -1.5 mm), while the paper, the CRCNS descriptor and the trial code descriptions say the CN was manipulated contralateral to the recorded ALM; Supplemental Table 1 lists BAYLORCD14 as recorded in right ALM. Which side was manipulated cannot be told from the files.
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2018 (gao-2018-02, 2026-11-14); also about CRCNS:alm-4
  • Description missingStill presentfound on promptIn DANDI:000007 the trial column photo_stim_id has no description beyond its type ("varchar(8) #") in all 54 files, and no file holds a laser or photostimulation trace (acquisition, stimulus and processing are empty). The codes are 1 and 2 in the fastigial, 3 and 4 in the dentate and 5 and 6 in the L7-cre x Ai32 sessions; which epoch, duration and power each stands for is not given in the files.
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2018 (gao-2018-02, 2026-11-14)
  • Identifier missingStill presentfound on promptIn DANDI:000007 the subjects anm00314746, anm00314756, anm00314757, anm00314758 and anm00314760 carry no other identifier, where the paper names these mice AN105 to AN109. By site, anm00314756 (the one dentate mouse of the five) and anm00314757 (the one with a left fastigial site and right ALM) can be matched; anm00314746, anm00314758 and anm00314760 share a right fastigial site and left ALM and cannot. The genotype of every mouse that is not L7-cre x Ai32 is "unknown", and session_description is empty in all 54 files.
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2018 (gao-2018-02, 2026-11-14)
  • Metadata errorStill presentfound on promptIn DANDI:000007 the units' cell_type column labels 60 units "pyramidal" whose mean waveform has a trough-to-peak width under the paper's 0.45 ms (33 under 0.35 ms), and 3 units "unknown". Every unit's waveform sampling rate is given as 20000 Hz, including the 34 sessions recorded with the 32-channel NeuroNexus probe, which the Methods say were sampled at 25,000 Hz; in 14 of those sessions the mean waveforms have 37 or 38 samples, against 30 in every other session.
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2018 (gao-2018-02, 2026-11-14)
  • OtherStill presentfound on promptIn 2 of the 54 sessions of DANDI:000007, BAYLORCD13 20171211 and 20171215, the spikes could not be shown to share the clock of the trial table, neither by a rate change after the onset of delay photostimulation nor by trial-type selectivity after sample onset. The first holds 15 trials for 38 units (8 with photostimulation, and 1 and 4 correct lick-right and lick-left control trials), the second 7 units. The other 52 sessions show it by one or both contrasts.
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2018 (gao-2018-02, 2026-11-14)
  • OtherStill presentfound on promptIn DANDI:000007 session BAYLORCD8 20171020 the behavior runs on after the recording ends. The obs_intervals column records this, but an analysis that does not apply it counts those trials as silent.
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2018 (gao-2018-02, 2026-11-14)
