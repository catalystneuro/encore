Dataset version 0.220126.1903 · tested 2026-11-10 · 9 open findings

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds only the ALM recordings (54 sessions, 13 mice). The CN recordings (878 units in 18 mice and 60 units in 2 L7-cre x Ai32 mice), lick times, the behavior-only sessions (lesions, 1.3 s and response epoch photo-activation, 0.5 s and response epoch photo-inhibition), the VGAT-ChR2-EYFP experiments and the anatomy are not in it; 26 of the 45 claims cannot be tested.
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2018 (gao-2018-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Description missingphoto_stim_id has no description, and the files hold no laser trace. The codes are 1 and 2 in fastigial, 3 and 4 in dentate and 5 and 6 in L7-cre x Ai32 sessions; from the ALM response, 1 and 3 start at sample onset and 2, 4, 5 and 6 at delay onset. Codes 5 and 6 both last through the delay; the ALM change to code 5 fades over the delay (mean absolute change 3.1 to 3.2 spikes/s early in the delay, 2.1 at its end) and to code 6 does not (2.95 to 3.00 early, 2.93 at its end), as two powers would give, but which is 1.5 mW and which 4.5 mW cannot be told, nor the photo-activation power. The ALM change to CN photo-inhibition starts about 30 ms after pole_out_time under both codes, so when the photostimulus starts relative to pole_out_time cannot be checked.
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2018 (gao-2018-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Metadata errorThe descriptions of pole_in_time, pole_out_time and cue_time say they are relative to the trial start; the values are on the session clock (pole_in_time - start_time is 0.57 s in most trials).
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2018 (gao-2018-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Metadata errorHemispheres disagree with Supplemental Table 1. The files place the manipulated CN of CD12 and CD13 on the left, the same side as the recorded ALM, where the table gives the right CN and the Methods say ALM was always recorded contralaterally; CD14 is recorded in the left ALM in the files and the right ALM in the table. Within each subject every session has the same coordinates.
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2018 (gao-2018-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Identifier missingSubjects anm00314746, anm00314756, anm00314757, anm00314758 and anm00314760 are named AN105 to AN109 in the paper; by site, anm00314756 is AN106 and anm00314757 is AN107, and the other three cannot be told apart. The genotype of the wild-type mice is "unknown", and session_description is empty.
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2018 (gao-2018-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Selection not documentedThe unit counts differ from the paper. By the paper's width rule the files hold 1082 putative pyramidal neurons in the 9 photo-activation mice (557 fastigial, 525 dentate) against 912 (446 and 466), and 676 in 20 sessions of the 4 photo-inhibition mice against 454 in 19 sessions. Nothing says which units or session the paper left out; the units table has no quality or isolation field (its columns are cell_type, sampling_rate, spike_times, obs_intervals, electrodes, electrode_group, waveform_mean and waveform_sd), and the paper gives no per-session counts. The files hold more slow neurons than the paper's counts allow (195 of the 676 fire under 0.5 spikes/s in the delay).
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2018 (gao-2018-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Metadata errorThe cell_type column labels 60 units "pyramidal" whose mean waveform has a trough-to-peak width under the paper's 0.45 ms (33 under 0.35 ms), and 3 units "unknown"; the waveforms are sampled at a stated 20 kHz in every file, including the 34 sessions recorded with the 32-channel NeuroNexus probe, which the Methods say were sampled at 25 kHz (the other 20 used the 64-channel probe).
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2018 (gao-2018-01, 2026-11-10)
  • OtherIn 5 of 54 sessions the response to delay photostimulation could not be shown to be locked to the trial events (BAYLORCD12 20180125 and 20180129, BAYLORCD13 20171211 and 20171215, anm00314756 20151023), mostly sessions with few units or photostimulation trials; the other 49 show it. Leaving them out changes no outcome.
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2018 (gao-2018-01, 2026-11-10)
  • OtherIn BAYLORCD8 20171020 the behavior runs on after the recording ends. The units' observation intervals end at 7221.8 s, and the last 127 of 462 trials, from 7223.1 to 8481.4 s, hold no spike of any unit. The obs_intervals column records this correctly, but an analysis that does not apply it counts those trials as silent (this attempt's first run did, which lowered the photo-inhibition sessions' mean rate).
    Found by the attempt on Gao et al. 2018 (gao-2018-01, 2026-11-10)
