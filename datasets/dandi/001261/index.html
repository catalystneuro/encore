  • Metadata errorThe recording CG608_FL_S4C7_B+G+_-63mV.mat is deposited twice with identical traces, once as a mouse cholinergic neuron (sub-CG608 obj-1ukhfn8) and once as a mouse noncholinergic neuron (sub-CG608 obj-17liqyi). Its name says G+ (GFP-positive, cholinergic). Counted once, the dandiset holds 45 distinct mouse noncholinergic recordings, not the paper's 46.
    Found by the attempt on Luo et al. 2024 (luo-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Metadata errorThe macaque noncholinergic recording NHP_20220420-03 of M#12 Shahruk is deposited twice with identical traces, under two session names (one of them with spaces in place of hyphens). Counted once, the dandiset holds 11 macaque noncholinergic neurons, as the paper says.
    Found by the attempt on Luo et al. 2024 (luo-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • File format errorIn the macaque file "2023_03_06_0002 R+B+A2C1 RMP -78.abf" (sub-M#19-29511 obj-1nl2qam) the voltage is 20 times too large (baseline -1557 "mV"; divided by 20 it is -77.9 mV, the value in its name), while its conversion factor is 1.
    Found by the attempt on Luo et al. 2024 (luo-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Metadata errorThe stimulus series are labelled amperes but hold pA (values -100 to 1200).
    Found by the attempt on Luo et al. 2024 (luo-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Metadata errorThe amplifier fields of every sweep of every file hold one value each (bias current 1e-11 A, bridge balance 2e7 Ohm, capacitance compensation 1e-12 F, gain 1), although the neurons were held at -65 mV by a bias current set per neuron (Methods 2.4); they are placeholders.
    Found by the attempt on Luo et al. 2024 (luo-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Description missingMost sweeps hold a short -20 pA test pulse besides the step, and nothing in the files marks which part of the stimulus is the step. In 8 files (six mouse cholinergic neurons of November 2021 to February 2022 and two macaque neurons of M#11) the pulse starts 0.7 s before the step in every sweep that holds a step; in the others it starts 1.3 s or more after the step onset. An analysis that takes the first non-zero stimulus sample as the step onset misses the step in those 8 files.
    Found by the attempt on Luo et al. 2024 (luo-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • OtherThe -60 pA responses of 8 neurons (seven mouse, one macaque) are clipped at -100 or -105.5 mV for 1144 to 4793 samples, the limit of the recording; their sag is measured on the next step up, as the paper's code intends.
    Found by the attempt on Luo et al. 2024 (luo-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Data missingThe resting potential (feature 1 of Methods 2.5, "measured in the absence of a current injection") is not recorded; every sweep carries the holding current. Some original file names, kept as the NWB identifier, carry a value (63 of 152 files).
    Found by the attempt on Luo et al. 2024 (luo-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Data missingThe morphology of the paper (13 features, Sholl counts, reconstructions of 31 and 44 mouse and 52 macaque neurons) is not in the dandiset, whose description says it holds the physiology data, and the paper names no other archive.
    Found by the attempt on Luo et al. 2024 (luo-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Description missingThe files record neither the quality-control measures (access resistance, offset current) nor the slice of each neuron; general/notes holds only a bregma position. The original file names carry labels such as R+, B+, G+ and B+R- that are not documented and do not follow session_description consistently (a macaque file named R+B+G+ is filed as noncholinergic, and of three named B+R- two are filed as cholinergic and one as noncholinergic).
    Found by the attempt on Luo et al. 2024 (luo-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Metadata errorTwo files in the folder of mouse CG608 (subject_id CG608) are named for mouse CG609 (CG609_FL_S7LC4_B+G+_-62mV.mat, CG609_FL_S6RC1_B+G+_-67mV.mat).
    Found by the attempt on Luo et al. 2024 (luo-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • OtherThe current steps differ between files and from the paper's description. The increments up to 200 pA are 10 to 50 pA in most files, include 5 pA in 24 files and are 100 pA in one, not the 10 pA of the Fig. S2-1 legend; 23 files go above 200 pA (one to 1200 pA); the steps last 500 or 1000 ms, not 500 ms.
    Found by the attempt on Luo et al. 2024 (luo-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • OtherThe 46 macaque cholinergic files come from 6 of the 9 macaques, while the paper reports 46 cholinergic neurons from nine macaques.
