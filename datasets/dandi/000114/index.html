    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • Units from 4 virgins and 7 sessions (194 units), against the paper's 147 units from more animals; which sessions the paper used is not stated.
    • OT-PVN and PVN units are pooled and the false discovery rate is applied over each session's units, as OT identity is not in the dandiset; the Methods apply it over OT-PVN or PVN units of a day.

Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Description missingThe behaviour labels ("Mom agressing", "Virgin in rest", "Mom retrieval", "Virgin Agressing", "Mother in nest" and others) have no description; the table's description, "events and states of pups", the same in every file, fits none of them. Shepherding, nest entry and dam retrieval can be named only from the paper's code, and "Virgin Agressing" not at all. One file (V116 03122018) carries both the code's labels and the paper's names.
  • Data missingThe optical tagging recordings and the identity of the 21 OT-PVN units are not in the dandiset, so no oxytocin-specific result can be tested. The behavioural cohorts of Figures 1 to 4, the unit recordings during observation, and the auditory cortex photometry and units are also missing. Units are present for 4 of the paper's 17 recorded virgins.
  • OtherUnits in V110 and V111 have a median 7.0 to 9.2% of inter-spike intervals under 2 ms, and 79 to 90% of their spikes lie within 0.1 ms of a spike of another unit. The Methods say spikes violating a 2 to 3 ms refractory period and spikes recorded at once on 10 or more channels were removed. These units look like multi-unit clusters or one neuron split over several wires. In V111 03032018 the 9 units activated by the dam's retrieval share a median 71% of their spikes pairwise within 0.1 ms, and the retrieval falls on a synchronous burst of about 16 units at 144 to 221 Hz, one of the 12 largest of the session's 3,325 windows of 5 s.
  • Metadata errorSession start times disagree with the dates in the session ids; V116 03082018 and 03102018 start in May 2018, V116 03092018 on 11 March, and V118 03182018 on 23 March, after V118 03192018.
  • File format errorIn V116 03102018 the raw voltage lasts 4.46 h, but spike times and annotations run to 13.4 h; in V118 03222018 annotations run to 2.52 h against 2.27 h of voltage. The spikes and events of these files cannot all come from the recording they are stored with.
  • Description missingThe units table holds spike times and a name only, with no channel, waveform, quality or cell type, and every electrode location is "unknown". V111's three sessions hold 31 units each with spike counts correlated 0.93 to 0.97 across days, and nothing says whether they are the same units.
  • Metadata errorEvery subject is described as "Virgin female co-housed with dam and pups", including the six photometry animals of the observation experiment. Photometry fibre location, fluorophore, wavelengths and gain are "unknown" or NaN; the trace is stated in watts with values near 1000; the commanded_voltage series, described as the LED command, varies from 0.07 to 482 within sessions and is not a command signal.
  • Description missingWhich photometry animals learned to retrieve is in the dandiset for 2 of 6 (ROV40 and ROV41, from their retrieval-test files), so the learner and non-learner groups of Fig. 4i cannot be formed.
  • OtherNo test here established that spikes and annotations share a clock; the firing change at annotation onsets was not larger at the stated times than at shifts of up to 2 minutes in any session.
  • Published code disagreesThe Methods cut nest entry episodes at 40 s and use the 10 s before shepherding and dam retrieval as baseline; the paper's significance code has the cap commented out and uses an equal-duration baseline for every behaviour. On this data the cap changes the fraction of units activated during nest entry from 1.0% to 21.6%.
  • Selection not documentedThe dandiset holds units from 4 virgins (228 units in 8 sessions), no units for V85, V116 03122018 and three V118 sessions, and annotations limited to "Mother in nest" in two V118 files, against 17 recorded virgins and 541 units in the paper. It does not say which recordings were deposited or why.

Reproduction PVN units and maternal behaviour in co-housed virgin mice (Carcea et al. 2021)

Tests the findings of Carcea et al. (2021) that dandiset 000114 can support. The dandiset holds PVN single units and video annotations from virgin mice co-housed with a dam and litter, and fibre photometry from virgins watching dam retrievals. The entry tests the fraction of units activated during nest entry, shepherding and dam retrieval, the firing after shepherded against voluntary nest entries, and the rates of shepherding and dam retrieval. OT-PVN identity, the behavioural cohorts and the auditory cortex data are not in the dandiset. All files

Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • Real episodes without spikes are left out as in the code, and V111's sessions counted separately, as for nest entry; both are also shown the other way.ed7a-shepherding-fraction ed7a-dam-retrieval-fraction
  • The Methods' 10 s baseline is used; the code's equal-duration baseline is shown as a variant.ed7a-shepherding-fraction ed7a-dam-retrieval-fraction
  • The names come from the paper's code, not from the dandiset; "Mom aggressing" in V85 is taken as the same label as "Mom agressing".label-link
  • Units from 4 virgins and 7 sessions (194 units), against the paper's 147 units from more animals; which sessions the paper used is not stated.ed7a-nest-entry-fraction
  • OT-PVN and PVN units are pooled and the false discovery rate is applied over each session's units, as OT identity is not in the dandiset; the Methods apply it over OT-PVN or PVN units of a day.ed7a-nest-entry-fraction
  • The baseline ends 0.5 s before the episode, from the code; the Methods do not give it.ed7a-nest-entry-fraction
  • V111's three sessions probably hold the same clusters (spike counts correlated 0.93 to 0.97) and are counted as separate units, as the paper counts units per day.ed7a-nest-entry-fraction
  • As in the code, real episodes with no spike in either window are left out of a unit's mean, while shuffled ones count as 0; counting both as 0 is shown as a variant, as is counting V111 once.ed7a-nest-entry-fraction
  • Units from 4 virgins and 7 sessions, OT-PVN and PVN pooled, as for nest entry.ed7a-shepherding-fraction
  • Units from 2 virgins and 5 sessions with 1 to 11 retrieval episodes each; OT-PVN and PVN pooled.ed7a-dam-retrieval-fraction
  • The paper does not say how a nest entry was classed as shepherded or which 18 units were used; the rule (entry starting from the start of a shepherding episode to 10 s after its end) and the unit set were fixed before the analysis.fig2j-shepherded-entry
  • Sessions are recordings of 2.5 to 15.8 h, not the paper's days of co-housing per dyad, and the dandiset does not say whether each session was annotated in full.fig1j-shepherding-rate
  • The comparison of 3.5 per hour comes from Extended Data Fig. 3b (N = 4 dyads); its dyads are not identified.fig1j-shepherding-rate
  • Rates from 5 cages over 10 sessions, against the paper's 14 dams over 4 days.ed8a-dam-retrieval-rate
