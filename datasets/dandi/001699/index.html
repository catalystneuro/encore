Dataset version 0.260917.2322 · tested 2026-10-19 · 8 open findings

  • Metadata errorThe paper's unit counts (406 units in 7 juvenile WT rats, 372 in 5 juvenile Fmr1-/y rats, 543 in 6 adult WT rats) and its range of 8 to 83 units per juvenile recording are matched exactly only if H3026, labelled WT, is a juvenile Fmr1-/y rat and H3029, whose date of birth makes it 97 days old, is a juvenile WT rat. With the archive's labels the juvenile groups have 5 WT and 4 Fmr1-/y rats, and the juvenile effects weaken (HD information p = 0.23, offsets p = 0.17).
    Found by the attempt on Moore et al. 2025 (moore-2025-01, 2026-10-19)
  • Metadata errorThe dates of birth of H3023 (2022-08-13) and H3026 (2022-09-13) are after their recordings in 2021, so the archive's asset metadata, which derive the age from the date of birth, give them negative ages (P-366D to P-344D). H3029 shares H4830's date of birth. No NWB file sets the subject's age.
    Found by the attempt on Moore et al. 2025 (moore-2025-01, 2026-10-19)
  • Data missingThe file of H4825 holds the recording of H4824 (identical waveforms, unit and spike counts, and spike times up to 1248 s), with its spike and tracking times shifted piece by piece onto H4825's epoch table and clipped at its end, where 131373 spikes share the last spike time and 12970 tracking frames repeat a timestamp. H4825's own recording is not in the dandiset, so the adult Fmr1-/y group has 5 rats and 440 units where the paper has 6 and 507.
    Found by the attempt on Moore et al. 2025 (moore-2025-01, 2026-10-19)
  • Selection not documentedThe units table holds all curated units with only spike times, mean waveforms, electrode group and sampling rate; no cell type, contamination or depth. The paper excluded cells recorded in the part of PoSub already recorded on the previous day, and the files give no probe depth per day, so that selection cannot be repeated.
    Found by the attempt on Moore et al. 2025 (moore-2025-01, 2026-10-19)
  • Metadata errorThe epochs and processing/tasks tables do not mark the exploration sessions reliably. In H3001 200202 task epoch 1 is called wake but lasts 94 min without tracking; in H3026 211003 epoch 1 spans 112 min of waking and sleep; in H3023 210813-1 epoch 2 runs from the end of the first session into the second; H4823 has a single epoch and no sleep task. Every file's session description mentions a cue rotation, and most files hold a third tracked session (`wake_cue_rot`), which version 1 of the paper does not describe.
    Found by the attempt on Moore et al. 2025 (moore-2025-01, 2026-10-19)
  • Metadata errorIn H3015 210417 and H4823 221108 position is stated in centimetres but spans about 300 and 700 units, so it is in pixels, and head direction runs from -pi to pi where the other files use 0 to 2 pi.
    Found by the attempt on Moore et al. 2025 (moore-2025-01, 2026-10-19)
  • File format errorOne unit of H3023 210812 has spike times out of order. 22 of the 24 juvenile files have one to three tracking frames whose timestamp does not increase.
    Found by the attempt on Moore et al. 2025 (moore-2025-01, 2026-10-19)
  • Metadata errorThe dandiset's `about` field lists "Fmr1 protein, rat" as an anatomy term with the MeSH identifier of Fragile X Syndrome (D005600).
    Found by the attempt on Moore et al. 2025 (moore-2025-01, 2026-10-19)
