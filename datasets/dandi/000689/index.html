Dataset version 0.240530.1923 · tested 2026-10-31 · 10 open findings

  • Description missingNo file says which recorded mouse is in the CRISPR group and which in the control group, nor whether its Cav2-Cre was injected in the NAc or the CeA; every subject's genotype is "Wild type". The paper's electrophysiology findings compare the groups and the tagged projectors, so 13 of its claims cannot be tested, and the phototagging count cannot be split by projection. Neither the units table (spike times and a unit name), the electrodes table (location "unknown"), any other text field of the files, nor the archive's subject metadata (age, sex, species, genotype) holds such a label.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2022 (li-2022-01, 2026-10-31)
  • File format errorThe units are not sorted single units, as the Methods describe (Offline Sorter). 21 of 31 sessions hold exactly 64 units on 32 channels, 1902 units in all against the paper's 685; in 1061 units more than 5% of inter-spike intervals are below 1 ms, and in each session 6 to 65% of spike samples recur in another unit. Both light blocks evoke a short-latency response in most of them (1353 and 1243 of 1902), so the light rule of the Methods with the fake-session test (91 to 256 tagged units, against 71 tagged neurons in the paper) cannot single out tagged neurons.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2022 (li-2022-01, 2026-10-31)
  • File format errorIn H17 the spike times of all 64 units fall within the first 249 s of a 12118 s recording, while its trial events cover the whole session, so its spikes cannot be aligned with its trials.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2022 (li-2022-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Description missingThe DeepLabCut pose has no timestamps, only a start time of 0 s and a nominal rate of 15 frames per second, and the mp4 videos give a constant 15 frames per second. Against the trial clock, port entries match the head's approach to the port at lags a median of 80 s and up to 192 s from zero that drift within 28 of 29 sessions. A linear map fitted to the port entries removes the drift in 21 sessions, with a rate factor of about 0.988 in 20 of them, as if the true frame rate were about 1.2% below 15; in 8 sessions no such map holds (lags of 2.8 to 19.2 s are left in some third). The videos of H07, H09 and H10 run 818 to 1528 s longer than their pose series, and that of H11 426 s longer; the others are within 10 s. Video timestamps, or the frame times of the original .asf files, would settle the alignment.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2022 (li-2022-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Description missing27 of the 31 ecephys files (all but H01, H02, H07 and H08) hold a stack of 8 to 42 confocal images (COFAImages) whose description names an Olympus FLUOVIEW file of the PVT. The images are named only by depth, with no channel, probe, stain or group label, so what they show cannot be read from the files.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2022 (li-2022-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Description missingEach session holds about 854 light events in two blocks of about 427, which fits the paper's "fake" phototagging session and the real one, but no label says which is which. In 12 sessions (H31 to H42) one block comes before the task, where the Methods put both after it. The two blocks evoke much the same short-latency responses (1353 and 1243 of 1902 units respond to the first and the second, and 88% of the units responsive to the second block also respond to the first), so neither behaves like a session with the patch cord disconnected. The files hold light events of 5, 10 and 100 ms and none of the 1 s light the Methods describe.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2022 (li-2022-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Metadata errorSix sessions (H09 to H14) hold 75 sucrose and 45 shock CSs and no neutral CS, while their session description says sucrose, shock and neutral CSs at 2 to 1 to 1.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2022 (li-2022-01, 2026-10-31)
  • OtherThe dandiset has recordings from 31 mice where the paper reports 30. For H28 and H32 the recorded session (Disc3, Disc5) is earlier than another discrimination day for which only pose is deposited (Disc4, Disc6), while the paper analyses only the last day.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2022 (li-2022-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Description missingThe photometry files store one RoiResponseSeries at 60 Hz that interleaves the 415 nm frames, the 470 nm frames and a third, undescribed frame type; the channels can be told apart only from the LED event labels, and in one session (Mouse6, late sucrose) the labels move by one frame after a 2.3 ms frame interval while the data keep their order, so after it they mark the constant third frame type as 470 nm. In Mouse1's early airpuff session the last three cues come 4.7 and 7.4 s apart, one lasting 4.7 s. The cue events last 2 s where the Methods give a 4 s CS, and no event marks the US or says whether a cue was paired. In two airpuff sessions the lick label is on for most of the session.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2022 (li-2022-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Data missingOnly the in vivo recordings of Figures 4 and 5 and the NT sensor photometry of Figure 3j-l are deposited. The behavior cohorts of Figures 1 and 2 and Extended Data Figure 10, the axon-terminal and somatic photometry with its head-fixed behavior (Figure 3b-f, Extended Data Figure 6), the anatomy and in situ data, the slice physiology and the sensor validation are not, so 19 of the paper's 39 findings in the claims file cannot be tested.
    Found by the attempt on Li et al. 2022 (li-2022-01, 2026-10-31)
