Fixed since an earlier attempt 7

  • Description missingFixedThe units tables hold only spike times. No unit has an electrode, shank, brain region, waveform or quality measure, and every electrode's location is "unknown". Units cannot be assigned to the antero-dorsal thalamus or the post-subiculum, which blocks 13 of the paper's 21 claims. The electrode groups and ripple annotations are consistent with Mouse24, Mouse25 and Mouse28 being the three dual-site mice, but the files do not say so.
    Every unit now has a region (853 Anterodorsal nucleus, 365 Postsubiculum, 216 Prelimbic area, 154 Field CA1, 22 Reticular nucleus of the thalamus, 72 unknown), an electrode group, a shank and a cluster_id, and no file has only unknown electrode locations (earlier_findings.ipynb). Units are labelled in both structures in sessions of Mouse20, 24, 25 and 28; finding 3 takes up the Mouse20 labels. Found fixed on dataset version 0.261106.1949 by peyrache-2015-02, 2026-11-15.
  • Description missingFixedThe open-field exploration (the paper's waking epoch) is not marked. The "Awake" state also covers waking in the home cage, and some sessions hold a second exploration or a larger second environment. The epoch had to be found from position.
    41 of 42 sessions have an open_field epoch, and in all 37 sessions with both the scored wake state lies wholly inside the open_field epochs (fraction 1.000). Only Mouse32-140820, which has no epochs (finding 1), lacks one. Found fixed on dataset version 0.261106.1949 by peyrache-2015-02, 2026-11-15.
  • File format errorFixedIn 36 of 40 files one unit's spike times are not sorted. Each such unit holds exactly two sorted runs, and in all 36 the first run ends at an index of the form k x 65536 - 1. The second run starts within 36 s of the start of the recording and the first between 24 and 13704 s, and the two end within 15 s of each other. In 26 units no time of the second run appears in the first; in 10, 821 to 22281 times do. This suggests an error in the conversion.
    No unit in the 42 files has unsorted spike times (0 of 1682). Found fixed on dataset version 0.261106.1949 by peyrache-2015-02, 2026-11-15.
  • OtherFixedIn Mouse12-120808 the position and the spikes do not share a clock. Head-direction tuning is best with position shifted by -1.5 s at the start of the exploration and -3.4 s at its end, and no unit passes the HD criteria as deposited; with position realigned by the best shift of each quarter, 13 units do. In all other sessions the best shift is -0.2 to 0 s.
    With the head direction shifted against the spikes, the tuning of the six most tuned ADn units of Mouse12-120808 is best at -0.1 to +0.1 s in each quarter of the wake interval, as in the 32 other sessions tested (-0.2 to +0.1 s), and the files mark 11 of its units as HD cells. Finding 6 is about the extent of the same session's wake interval, not its clock. Found fixed on dataset version 0.261106.1949 by peyrache-2015-02, 2026-11-15.
  • Metadata errorFixedThe raw LED positions in `acquisition` are stated in meters with a conversion of NaN but hold camera pixels (values in the hundreds).
    The raw LED series are now blue_led_position_pixels and red_led_position_pixels, with unit pixels and conversion 1.0 in all 84 series. Found fixed on dataset version 0.261106.1949 by peyrache-2015-02, 2026-11-15.
  • Description missingFixedThe annotation series `dow`, `hdD` and `rip` (labels such as "DOWN ON 5", "HD UP ON", "Ripple peak 1") have no description.
    The annotations are now the TimeIntervals tables down_states, hd_down_states and ripples (in 37, 13 and 21 files), each with a description and every column described; no series named dow, hdD or rip remains. Found fixed on dataset version 0.261106.1949 by peyrache-2015-02, 2026-11-15.
