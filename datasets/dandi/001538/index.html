Dataset version 0.260527.1302 · tested 2026-10-18 · 14 open findings

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds no patch-clamp recordings from unlesioned control or 6-OHDA-lesioned mice, which make up the control and 6-OHDA groups of Figures 1E, 1F, 1I, 3C, 3D and 3F (10, 12, 11 and 9 dSPNs; 21, 11, 14 and 9 iSPNs), nor the oxo-M line scans of Figure 7E or the open-field data of Figure 10. The paper's raw data on Zenodo (doi:10.5281/zenodo.17260198) list folders for all of these.
    Found by the attempt on Zhai et al. 2025 (zhai-2025-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Selection not documentedThe Sr2+-oEPSC files hold one cell per mouse (14 of the paper's 33 cells), the GRAB-ACh files one region per mouse (11 of 34 regions) and the AIM tables 7 of the paper's 44 mice plus two mice (4124, 5871) not in the paper. The dandiset does not say that it holds a subset or how the cells, regions and mice were chosen, and with these numbers the paper's tests cannot reach P < 0.05 for most comparisons.
    Found by the attempt on Zhai et al. 2025 (zhai-2025-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Metadata errorSession names state a drug (D1RaSch, D2RaSul) for files that also hold baseline on-state sweeps. 11 cells in such files have a baseline condition; 7 of them were also recorded with the drug and 4 are baseline only (dSPN 20190507 cell 1, iSPN 20190408 cell 1, 20190409 cell 1 and 20190508 cell 2). The condition of each sweep is only in the RecordingsIndexTable column condition_subfolder; grouping by session name puts baseline cells in the drug groups.
    Found by the attempt on Zhai et al. 2025 (zhai-2025-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Metadata errorThe genotypes of the CDGI behaviour mice contradict each other. Subject genotype is Wild-type CDGI and every session name says WT for all of them; the AIM table's genotype column says CDGI Knockout, Wild Type or unknown, changing between sessions of one animal; the video files of mice 1945, 2519, 2587, 2828 and 6572 say CDGI KO. The paper lists 2587 as wild type and the others as KO.
    Found by the attempt on Zhai et al. 2025 (zhai-2025-01, 2026-10-18)
  • OtherThe AIM session totals of the five Figure 8 mice in the dandiset differ from the paper's table in 20 of 25 sessions (mouse 1945 session 1, 33.5 against 27.0), while the two Figure 10 mice agree in all sessions. Some dandiset sessions have 4 time points and others 6.
    Found by the attempt on Zhai et al. 2025 (zhai-2025-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Metadata errorThree dendritic site series hold six trials, two sites under one name, while the cell has no series of the other compartment with that number (iSPN 20160526 cell 3 Distal1, iSPN 20160608 cell 1 Proximal1, dSPN 20170204 cell 1 Proximal1); their first and last three trials differ in baseline and area. Split, they give the paper's values for these cells. One series holds a single trial (iSPN 20160524 cell 2 Proximal1), one two and one four.
    Found by the attempt on Zhai et al. 2025 (zhai-2025-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Data missingTwo dendritic cells lack the sites the paper's values need. dSPN on+SCH 20190411 cell 1 has only its second pair of sites, which gives 0.584 against the paper's 0.228; with the paper's value the SCH test gives the paper's P (0.0133), without it P = 0.066. iSPN on+sul 20190508 cell 3 has only a distal site; with the paper's 0.217 the sulpiride test gives the paper's P (0.0545), without it P = 0.0097. Under the main basal-ratio rule these two cells decide fig1i-dspn-dendritic-sch-lower and fig3f-ispn-dendritic-sul-no-change; under the stricter rule (0.8 to 1.25) the sulpiride test gives P = 0.0663 without the cell. Reading the gap as data missing is an inference from the recovered P values, as the paper does not list each cell's sites.
    Found by the attempt on Zhai et al. 2025 (zhai-2025-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Description missingThe dandiset holds the raw spine image stacks and the rotation videos but no spine counts or rotation counts (none of the 86 spine files has a processing module or analysis entry), the measurements behind Figures 2B, 2C, 4B, 4C, 4K, 6F, 7H, 8A, 9F and 10A. They are only in the paper's Zenodo tables.
    Found by the attempt on Zhai et al. 2025 (zhai-2025-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Metadata errorThe stimulus series of every dendritic sweep (CurrentClampSeries_CellN_Dendritic_Stimulus) is all zero, though the three spikes per sweep show the 2 nA injections were delivered; and the index table gives no kymograph slice for 53 of 997 dendritic sweeps.
    Found by the attempt on Zhai et al. 2025 (zhai-2025-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Metadata errorIn the Sr2+-oEPSC files the light-pulse OptogeneticSeries is all zero in 69 of 140 sweeps, and the intracellular recordings table references no stimulus (its stimulus rows are empty). The pulse time (0.02 s) is only in the optogenetic epochs table.
    Found by the attempt on Zhai et al. 2025 (zhai-2025-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Metadata errorThe GRAB-ACh background reference images are described as taken at zero laser power, but their mean (381 to 525) is above the signal (F0 179 to 355), so they cannot be the PMT background the Methods subtract.
    Found by the attempt on Zhai et al. 2025 (zhai-2025-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Metadata errorThe files cite the bioRxiv preprint (related_publications) and describe the behaviour data with the preprint's figure numbers ("Figure 7J" for the CDGI AIM scores, "Figure 8" for the M1R study), which are Figures 8 and 10 in the published paper the dandiset names.
    Found by the attempt on Zhai et al. 2025 (zhai-2025-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Identifier missingPatch-clamp subjects are named by recording date (for example Subject20160523LIDOFFeGFP and SubjectRecordedAt20160523000000 for the same day), with no animal identifier, so recordings and spine stacks of one mouse cannot be linked, and the archive has 185 subject folders.
    Found by the attempt on Zhai et al. 2025 (zhai-2025-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Other18 somatic cells miss one or more current steps between 20 and 300 pA and 14 repeat a step; spike counts for these cells cannot be complete.
    Found by the attempt on Zhai et al. 2025 (zhai-2025-01, 2026-10-18)
