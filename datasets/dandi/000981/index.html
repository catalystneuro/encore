Dataset version 0.260910.1747 · tested 2026-11-10 · 10 open findings

  • Metadata errorFiles sub-20220403-h3 (hermaphrodite) and sub-20220403-m3 (male) hold the same 382-row ActivityTraces table, with identical values and labels. It is the traces of both animals, h3's (187 rows) followed by m3's (195 rows, with the male-specific CEM and MCM neurons), as the responses show when read with each file's stimulus order. Nothing in the files says which rows belong to which animal.
    Found by the attempt on Seyedolmohadesin et al. 2026 (seyedolmohadesin-2026-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Metadata errorThe subject sex field is "O" (other) in all 47 files; the sex is given only in the subject description ("Adult hermaphrodite." or "Adult male.") and the subject id. The strain field is "[]" in every file, though the paper names strain OH16230.
    Found by the attempt on Seyedolmohadesin et al. 2026 (seyedolmohadesin-2026-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Metadata errorThe StimulusInfo timestamps are labelled seconds but hold frame numbers (up to 4980 in a 31-minute recording at 2.66 volumes/s).
    Found by the attempt on Seyedolmohadesin et al. 2026 (seyedolmohadesin-2026-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Description missingThe activity traces have no unit and no description beyond "list of reading at each frame". Blocks of frames are blank (the first 96 frames in 13 recordings, other ranges of 29 to 514 frames in seven) without a note of why; they are consistent with the per-recording time ranges the paper's code ignores, whose spreadsheet is not public.
    Found by the attempt on Seyedolmohadesin et al. 2026 (seyedolmohadesin-2026-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Description missingThe identification confidence of each neuron, which the paper's code uses to keep only confident identities, is not in the dandiset.
    Found by the attempt on Seyedolmohadesin et al. 2026 (seyedolmohadesin-2026-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Data missingNo tail neuron has a trace, though the paper says tail neurons were recorded and the NeuroPAL segmentations identify them. The drop-test behaviour of Figure S1 is not in the dandiset.
    Found by the attempt on Seyedolmohadesin et al. 2026 (seyedolmohadesin-2026-01, 2026-11-10)
  • OtherThe stimulus annotations lie 1 to 3 frames after the fixed onset frames the paper's code uses for every recording (1323 presentations 2 frames later, 82 three and 5 one). The traces have 4979 frames, two fewer than the 4981 image volumes, and the stated rate is 2.66 volumes/s where the Methods give 2.67.
    Found by the attempt on Seyedolmohadesin et al. 2026 (seyedolmohadesin-2026-01, 2026-11-10)
  • OtherThe traces cover 189 distinct sex-shared neuron names across the 47 animals, where the paper says 196 sex-shared neurons were studied.
    Found by the attempt on Seyedolmohadesin et al. 2026 (seyedolmohadesin-2026-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Data missingThe tracked neuron ROIs (TrackedNeuronROIs, stored identically under processing/NeuroPAL and processing/CalciumActivity, "Neuron centers as tracked throughout GCaMP video. Formatted as (x, y, z, t)") have x = y = z = 0 in 99.75% of the 44,677,593 ROIs of the 47 files; the rest, in 19 files, have no coordinate above 2, where the image volumes of the two files listed are 512 by 300 or 400 by 23 voxels. Only the fourth field varies (0 to 4980 in sub-20220327-h2). The tracked labels include tail neurons (31 with one ROI per volume in sub-20220327-h2; 284,479 ROIs in 11 files, all zero), whose traces are not in the files, so the tracked positions that would let them be extracted from the raw volumes are missing.
    Found by the attempt on Seyedolmohadesin et al. 2026 (seyedolmohadesin-2026-01, 2026-11-10)
  • OtherThe connectome table the paper's code reads (prepared by S.J. Cook) is not in the repository or the dandiset, nor are neurons/neuron_classes.json, the per-recording spreadsheet of time ranges and ganglia to ignore, or the identification confidences.
    Found by the attempt on Seyedolmohadesin et al. 2026 (seyedolmohadesin-2026-01, 2026-11-10)
