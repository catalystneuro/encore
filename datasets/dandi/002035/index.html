  • a1-first-in-pathway

    In 5XFAD mice amyloid pathology in the central auditory pathway appears first in A1, as intracellular Abeta42 in layer 5 neurons by p21, before any label in MGB, IC, SOC, nLL or CN.

    Amyloid pathology in the central auditory pathway of Aβ42+ mice appeared first in A1

    Results, Primary auditory cortex, paragraph 1; Discussion, paragraph 1; Figure 1A

    How it will be judged

    For each auditory region (A1, MGB, IC, SOC, nLL, CN), the youngest age at which any 5XFAD mouse shows an Abeta42+ cell or a plaque, counted as in the opening comment. The paper gives A1 cells at p21 (one cell at p14), IC cells at p30, MGB, IC and A1 plaques at p90, and no age for SOC cells. "Appeared first" can be read as (a) the first age with label in any mouse, or (b) the first age with label in most mice of that age. Reproduced when, under one reading, A1 is the earliest region and no other auditory region shows label at an age earlier than A1.

  • cells-before-plaques

    Discussion, paragraph 3

    How it will be judged

    The first ages of cells and of plaques from the claims named under depends_on. The sampled ages make the lag one of a few values (the paper's own lags are 46 days for subiculum, p14 to p60, 69 for A1, p21 to p90, and 60 for IC, p30 to p90), so the 10% tolerance cannot apply and "roughly 2 months" is taken as 45 to 70 days, the span of the paper's own lags (the expected value and tolerance above encode that span). Reproduced when all three regions fall in the span under one reading.
