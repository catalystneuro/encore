  • ⊘1 blocked

None of the paper's main findings could be tested in this data, so none is confirmed or contradicted. These are the onset of intracellular Abeta42 in layer 5 of auditory cortex by p21, before the MGB and inferior colliculus, the later spread of plaques, earlier pathology in the subiculum, and no label in the lateral lemniscus or cochlear nuclei. The dataset records the brain region for only 864 of its 12394 images, with no section positions or atlas alignment, so no per-region count can be formed. The hand counts, boundaries and areas behind the figures are not deposited, and the wild-type controls were not imaged. The few region-labelled images were not scored, as their scale is unverified.

Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Description missingThe imaged region is recorded for 864 of the 12394 images (7.0%), and for 561 of the 7048 200X Abeta42 images, only where an original follow-up subfolder named it; 38 of the 62 mice have no region on any image, and the nucleus of the lateral lemniscus is never named. None of 20 sampled image headers and sidecars holds a stage position, section number or atlas coordinate, and samples.json says individual sections are not tracked. Fields can be matched to the low-magnification overviews before them for 26 of 66 sampled fields, but no overview is aligned to an atlas. The images of the main imaging pass cannot be assigned to a region, an A1 layer or a subdivision, so none of the paper's per-region values can be formed from the dandiset.
  • Data missingThe dandiset holds the raw images only: none of the cell and plaque markings, region and laminar boundaries, CCFv3 alignments, region areas or DAPI counts on which every figure and table of the paper rests.
  • Data missingThe 25 wild-type control mice of the paper are not in the dandiset; its README says they showed no detectable label and were not imaged. The paper's control claim cannot be tested.
  • OtherThe dandiset has 62 5XFAD mice, against the paper's 61: p120 has 9 mice (5 females, 4 males) and p45 has 3 females and 5 males, where the paper gives 4 males and 4 females at every age from p21 to p150. In all 30 females and 32 males. Sex, age and subject label agree with the original folder names for every mouse.
  • Paper inconsistentThe Methods (Mice) give 61 5XFAD mice as '31 females and 30 males', but the per-age numbers they give (4 and 4 from p21 to p150, 2 females and 3 males at p14) add up to 30 females and 31 males.
  • OtherMagnification is not recorded reliably by the acquisition software; the dandiset infers it from image content, and assigns 7125 Abeta42 and red-channel images to 200X by rule. On a seeded sample, DAPI nuclei measure about the same median diameter in the 100X and 200X classes (4.2 and 5.1 um from .czi files) and a 100X field holds 3.6 times as many, consistent with the stated ratio of the two classes.
  • Metadata errorThe magnification classes do not agree with the overviews. Of 66 sampled fields matched by cross-correlation to the 12.5X overviews of their block, 26 are placed, and none at its stated pixel size: against an overview at 5.16 um the best field pixel size is 2 times the stated one for 17 of the 26, 4 times for 6 and 8 times for 3. With the overviews between 12.5X and 50X, as the paper says of its low-magnification images, 3 images classed 200X by rule (the cochlear nucleus field of mouse 0136, the PRNc fields of the p14 mice BrTNR and LTN) have at least 0.645 um per pixel, twice the stated 0.3225 um; these three are best at 2.58 um, the largest size tried, so their true ratio may be higher than 8. Either the overview or the field class is wrong for the others. The pixel sizes of the sidecars and OME headers cannot be relied on for measurements in micrometres.
