  • fig1-conflict-decreases-certainty

    In the circular Kalman filter, an HD observation consistent with the current estimate increases certainty, while one that strongly conflicts with it (opposite direction) decreases certainty.

    HD observations increase the observer’s certainty if they are confirmatory ... if HD observations strongly conflict with the current estimate (e.g., if they point in the opposite direction), they actually decrease certainty

    Results, Circular Kalman Filtering, HD Observations, paragraph 1; Fig. 1 D and E

    How it will be judged

    The update is the vector addition of Fig. 1 D and E and the observation term of Eq. 9 (kappa changes by kappa_z cos(z - mu) dt). In the code's circKF step (vM_Projection in circularFiltering.py, kappa_phi = 1, kappa_v = 1, dt = 0.01) the observation enters as that vector addition on the natural parameters, followed by the prediction step. Take a state with certainty kappa_t (any of 1, 2, 5) and apply one step with an HD observation at z = mu (confirmatory) and one at z = mu + pi (opposite). The paper fixes no single reliability for this; use the per-step reliabilities the Methods give, xi_fun_inv(gamma_z dt) for gamma_z = 1/s (Fig. 3E) and 10/s (Fig. 2H). Compare (a) the certainty right after the observation update with the certainty before it (the update of Fig. 1 D and E alone, before the prediction step), and (b) the certainty after the full step with the certainty after the same step with no observation (kappa_z = 0). Reproduced when, under both comparisons, every kappa_t and both reliabilities, the confirmatory observation gives higher and the opposite one lower certainty.

  • fig2h-bump-tracks-circkf

    This network exhibits a spatially localized bump whose position tracks HD, with an accuracy similar to that of the circular Kalman filter itself ( Fig. 2 H ). The paper gives no number for "similar" or "accurately"; readings for both cue strengths: (a) the mean absolute circular difference between the network's and the circKF's HD estimates is smaller than the circKF's own mean absolute error to the true HD, over the whole run; (b) the Pearson correlation between bump amplitude and circKF certainty over the cue periods is at least 0.9.     First, it causes an unbiased random diffusion of μ t and, thus, an increasingly imprecise memory of the HD estimate. Second, it causes a positive drift and random diffusion of κ t

  • prediction-bump-amplitude-cue-reliability

    The paper predicts that the bump amplitude in the HD system is higher in the presence of reliable external HD cues, such as salient visual landmarks. The paper does not test this.

    Second, we predict that bump amplitude in the HD system should vary dynamically, with higher amplitudes in the presence of reliable external HD cues, such as salient visual landmarks.

    Discussion, paragraph 7

    How it will be judged

    The paper gives the measure and nothing else. Bump amplitude is the length of the population vector of the activity's first Fourier component, ordered by preferred HD (Eq. 3; Methods, Simulation parameters, decoder A = (cos phi, sin phi)), in the EPG population that holds the HD bump (SI Appendix, section 4A). The paper does not say how cue reliability is set in an experiment, which activity signal (raw fluorescence or dF/F) feeds the decoder, the time windows, the unit of comparison or a test. Comparison as the paper's text allows: bump amplitude while the fly sees the more reliable cue against the less reliable cue, as the dataset labels them. Readings for the test: (a) per fly, mean amplitude higher with the more reliable cue in more than half of the flies; (b) a one-sided paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test over flies, p below 0.05. Activity signal readings: (i) dF/F, (ii) raw fluorescence minus baseline. Reproduced when every combination finds higher amplitude with the more reliable cue; blocked as methods_underspecified when they differ. The second half of the prediction (a high bump amplitude makes the bump position less sensitive to new cues) has no measure in the paper and is not tested.
