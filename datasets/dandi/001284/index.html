  • fig1e-field-simulation

    In the simulation, 7.2 kV on one electrode gives 3.3 kV/cm in the centre of the array and 9.4 and 13.5 kV/cm at 6 and 7 mm from the centre toward the active electrode.

    Specifically, 7.2 kV applied to one electrode produced the electric field of 3.3 kV/cm in the center, and 9.4 and 13.5 kV/cm at 6 and 7 mm from the center towards the active electrode, respectively

    Methods 2.2, paragraph 4; Figure 1E

    How it will be judged

    The values are outputs of the paper's Sim4Life Lite model (Methods 2.2, built as in its references 16 and 18), which the paper does not deposit. The claim is tested only if a dataset holds the simulated field maps; a field model built by the author is not the paper's and is not a test. With the maps (1 kV on one electrode, scaled linearly to 7.2 kV as Methods 2.2 says), reproduced when the three values are within tolerance along the diagonal from the active electrode. Without them, blocked as data_missing.

  • fig2-heating-below-44c

    With packets at the maximum rate, heating followed the field and was largest near the electrodes; after 2 bursts it was confined to about 2 mm next to the electrodes and could exceed 30 C but not 44 C; after 6 bursts it spread further, exceeding 30 C even near the centre, yet never exceeded 44 C.

    With 2 bursts ( Fig. 2A ), heating was restricted to an approximately 2-mm zone next to electrodes, where it could reach over 30 °C but did not exceed 44 °C. With 6 bursts ( Fig. 2B ), heating was more widespread ... Temperature could exceed 30 °C even near the center of the array, but did not exceed 44 °C even near the electrodes.

    Methods 2.3, paragraph 1; Figure 2

    How it will be judged

    Set S5, film frames at the end of the 2nd and 6th burst (Methods 2.3). Temperature classes from the calibrated colour transitions, black to red, green, blue and back to black at 25, 26, 30 and 44 C (Methods 2.3). The hue limits of the colours are not given; readings (a) red below 45, green 75 to 165, blue 180 to 270 degrees of hue, (b) red below 30, green 90 to 150, blue 195 to 255 degrees, with black any pixel of HSV value below 0.2 (standard choices). Above 30 C is blue; above 44 C is black after blue, a dark pixel enclosed by a blue region outside the electrode footprints (dashed circles, Fig. 2 legend). "Near the center" is not given as a distance; readings (a) within 1 mm, (b) within 2 mm of the centre. Reproduced when, under every reading, the 2-burst image has blue pixels only within 2.2 mm of an electrode edge ("approximately 2-mm", widened by 10%), the 6-burst image has blue pixels near the centre, and neither image has a dark pixel enclosed by blue. Blocked as data_missing when no dataset holds the film images.

  • fig5-raised-electrodes-weaken-centre

    Since with MHz-CANCAN there was practically no YP staining near electrodes (a finding the text cites to Figure 4), the only clear effect of raising the electrodes 1 or 2 mm above the monolayer was a weakening of electroporation in the central spot.

    With MHz-CANCAN (CT26 cells, 2 bursts (x4), Fig. 4 ), there was practically no YP staining near electrodes to start with, so the only clear effect of raising electrodes was the weakening of electroporation in the central spot.

    Results 3.2, paragraph 1; Figure 5A, 5B

    How it will be judged

    Set S2, F1 to F3, F8, O1, O2. YP in percent of the mean 0-mm peak (Fig. 5B legend); the centre is x = 0. The paper gives no test. Reproduced when mean centre YP at 1 mm and at 2 mm elevation are each below that at 0 mm by a gap larger than the error bars (F8); two-sided t-tests with Bonferroni over the two comparisons (F7) are reported. The near-electrode part is the Figure 4 finding, judged in fig4b-40-fold-ratio; mean YP at 6 and 7 mm at each elevation, in % of the 0-mm centre, is reported.

  • fig5-killing-focused-centre

    With MHz-CANCAN, cell killing (propidium uptake 2 h after exposure) was produced at the centre of the array with negligible effect near the electrodes.

    Discussion, paragraph 1; Results 3.2; Figure 5A, 5B

    How it will be judged

The paper puts no number on "negligible"; Pr at 6 and 7 mm in % of the centre is reported, not judged. The 1 and 2 mm elevations are reported.

  • fig5b-gaussian-pr-lorentzian-yp

    For Pr data in the left panel of Fig. 5B , the coefficient of determination increased from 0.92 for Lorentzian fit to 0.96 for Gaussian fit. R2 is 1 - SSres / SStot over the fitted points. As 10% tolerances on R2 near 1 are loose, the orderings are part of the claim. Reproduced when every R2 is within tolerance and the ordering holds (Gaussian above Lorentzian for Pr, Lorentzian above Gaussian for YP) under every reading; partially when the values are within tolerance but an ordering fails. The other elevations are reported.

  • fig5c-nuclear-yp-centre

    Indeed, a closer examination of the central spot confirmed bright YP staining of cell nuclei ( Fig. 5C , zone 1), which is indicative of the irreversible membrane damage and cell death.     Results 3.2, paragraph 1; Figure 5C

    How it will be judged

Zone 1 is in the central spot; zone 3 straddles an electrode wall (its right portion lies inside the footprint, Results 3.2; the Fig. 5C legend marks the wall), so its outside part is the band within 0.5 mm outside a wall; zone 2 is not placed by the text, readings (a) 2 to 4 mm, (b) 3 to 5 mm from the centre along a diagonal; zone 1 is within 1 mm of the centre. Intensity part, on the whole-well images. Background-subtracted YP (F2) in zone 1 exceeds that in zones 2 and 3, footprint YP is below zones 2 and 3, and the footprint holds Hoechst-positive cells in the Hoechst image taken after the Pr image (Methods 2.4, re-imaged "to check for possible cell loss"), judged against the same footprint's cell-free lumen or, without one, against the camera offset (a standard choice; the paper names no reference level). Nuclear part, on the high-resolution images of Fig. 5C. Nuclei are Hoechst-positive objects (Otsu threshold, a standard choice) and the cytoplasm a ring around each, readings (a) 2 to 4 um, (b) 4 to 8 um; the median nucleus to cytoplasm YP ratio is higher in zone 1 than in zones 2 and 3. Reproduced when both parts hold under every reading; partially when the intensity part holds and the nuclear part is blocked because no dataset holds high-resolution images. Blocked as data_missing for the footprint part when no post-exposure Hoechst image is held.
