What the attempts that used this dataset found about the data themselves, for its owner and the archive. The claims and their outcomes are on each paper’s page.

  • Description missingThe trial table's `result` column says "Refer to the Discription in 'Result' column", but no such description is in the files. Codes 1 to 5 occur (33,243, 8084, 18,639, 141 and 104 trials). Codes 1 and 3 can be read from the other columns (rewarded or successful, and unrewarded choice); the error codes 2, 4 and 5 cannot be told apart with certainty, so the paper's error rates (0.39% and 0.73%) could not be recounted with certainty; the tone-A rate is not determined (0.27 to 0.81% under different counts), while every count of the tone-B rate gives 0.97 to 1.11%.
    Found by the attempt on Ito et al. 2009 (ito-2009-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Metadata errorThe no-reward tone of choice trials (500 Hz, 250 ms) is stored in the `SpTone Err` and `ViTone Err` series, named as the error tone, together with the error tone; the two are told apart only by their duration (about 0.3 s and 2 s).
    Found by the attempt on Ito et al. 2009 (ito-2009-01, 2026-11-03)
  • OtherThe error tone lasts about 2 s in the event series (8299 tones over 1.5 s), where the paper's Behavioral task gives 1000 ms.
    Found by the attempt on Ito et al. 2009 (ito-2009-01, 2026-11-03)

    Found by the attempt on Ito et al. 2009 (ito-2009-01, 2026-11-03)

    Found by the attempt on Ito et al. 2009 (ito-2009-01, 2026-11-03)

What initial value, or what rule, gave its normalized likelihood of 0.508? A non-zero initial value (0.1 to 1.0) was assumed and gives 0.504 to 0.508.

  • OtherThe cue tone probably stops when the rat leaves the center hole early. Some tone-A tones in the event series last under 1 s (1st percentile 0.46 s), against the task's 1000 to 2000 ms, and 96 of the 317 tone-A error trials with no L or R entry have a cue under 1 s (1st percentile 0.12 s), while the trials with result codes 5 and 4 have full-length cues (1st percentile 1.05 and 1.03 s). In the 96 short-tone trials the center exit falls within 10 ms of the trial table's tone offset (5th to 95th percentile -0.010 to -0.003 s), as it does in most trials with a full cue, so early exits cannot be told from held cues by the exit time. Cue duration marks only exits before 1 s, as a cue of 1 to 2 s cut short after 1 s looks like a full one. The files do not describe this.
    Found by the attempt on Ito et al. 2009 (ito-2009-01, 2026-11-03)
