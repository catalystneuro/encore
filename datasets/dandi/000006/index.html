Dataset version 0.220126.1855 · tested 2026-10-20 · 10 open findings

  • Data missingStill present (finding of economo-2018-01). 11 sessions of the paper's processed data (Figshare doi:10.25378/janelia.7007846) that hold tagged units are not in the dandiset. They hold 17 of the 61 PTupper and 13 of the 69 PTlower units the paper analysed, so the dandiset holds 44 and 56 under the code's rule. Every tagged unit the dandiset holds is in the processed data with the same label, inclusion decision and depth. The dandiset's description says some files were found to be broken and were left out, without naming them.
    Found by the attempt on Economo et al. 2018 (economo-2018-02, 2026-10-20)
  • Data missingNew. The missing sessions also hold unidentified units. The processed data have 517 good-quality unidentified units in the PTupper cohort and 547 in the PTlower cohort; the dandiset holds 232 (in 6 sessions) and 430 (in 12), against the 495 and 511 the paper analysed.
    Found by the attempt on Economo et al. 2018 (economo-2018-02, 2026-10-20)
  • Data missingStill present (finding of economo-2018-01). The dandiset holds the electrophysiology only; the single-cell and bulk sequencing, the axon reconstructions and the anatomy are on the Figshare record and at GEO (GSE119182). The smFISH data behind Fig. 3c are not in any public file.
    Found by the attempt on Economo et al. 2018 (economo-2018-02, 2026-10-20)
  • Data missingNew. No file holds video, so the reaction time the paper measured from 400 Hz videography cannot be tested, and the files hold no session manipulator depth, which the paper's code stores beside each unit's depth.
    Found by the attempt on Economo et al. 2018 (economo-2018-02, 2026-10-20)
  • File format errorStill present (finding of economo-2018-01). Spike times are not sorted in 1120 of 1149 units. They were written trial by trial as trial start + go cue time + the spike time from the go cue, and consecutive trials overlap on the file's clock, so some spikes of one trial lie in the next trial's early epochs (11.7% of the spikes counted in the baseline epoch, under 2% in later epochs, are detectably out of order). Trial membership of a spike cannot be recovered in full from the files. On the same 100 tagged units, every unit's baseline rate is higher in the files than in the paper's processed data (median by 0.99 Hz for PTupper and 0.97 Hz for PTlower units).
    Found by the attempt on Economo et al. 2018 (economo-2018-02, 2026-10-20)
  • File format errorNew. The lick times are stored the same way, trial by trial, and are out of order in 105 of 106 lick series (14,360 backward steps in 180,679 licks). The files do not assign licks to trials; rebuilt from the stored order, each trial's last lick matches the processed data's in 98.1% of correct trials.
    Found by the attempt on Economo et al. 2018 (economo-2018-02, 2026-10-20)
  • File format errorStill present (finding of economo-2018-01). The trial table's stop_time is NaN in every file, and pole_in_time, pole_out_time and cue_start_time are relative to the trial start without saying so. On the file's clock the go cue of a trial falls after the next trial's start_time in 13,306 of 15,096 trials.
    Found by the attempt on Economo et al. 2018 (economo-2018-02, 2026-10-20)
  • Description missingStill present (finding of economo-2018-01). The tagged unit labels PTupper and PTlower are not described (the cell_type column's description is the generic "cell type (e.g. wide width, narrow width spiking)"), and the projection targets (thalamus and medulla) that the paper uses to name the types are stated only in the paper's code.
    Found by the attempt on Economo et al. 2018 (economo-2018-02, 2026-10-20)
  • Description missingStill present (finding of economo-2018-01). Unit quality mixes two vocabularies (single, and Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor) and has undescribed empty values ([], 0, blank). Sex is U and genotype empty for every mouse, the electrode positions are all zero, and every electrode group gives layer 5 whatever the depth.
    Found by the attempt on Economo et al. 2018 (economo-2018-02, 2026-10-20)
  • OtherStill present (finding of economo-2018-01). In the paper's processed data, in every one of the 53 sessions the dandiset holds, the spike windows of the first ten trials start later relative to the pole than those of the other trials (session medians 1.73 to 5.84 s after the pole comes in, against -0.97 to -0.16 s); the paper's code leaves out the first ten trials of each unit, which hides this. The files carry the same shift, as their spike times equal the processed ones in every unit.
    Found by the attempt on Economo et al. 2018 (economo-2018-02, 2026-10-20)
