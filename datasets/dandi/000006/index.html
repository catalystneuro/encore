Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • The cluster table in Seq.zip (cluster_metadata.csv) is from an earlier version of the taxonomy, with other cluster names; the annotation file, which carries the paper's names, is used.pt-types-transcriptomic
  • Gene names that repeat in the tables keep their first row, as MATLAB's ismember does; counts per million are taken over all of a sample's rows before that.pt-bulk-sc-fold-change-correlation
  • The dandiset lacks 11 of the paper's sessions; the test runs on 44 PTupper and 56 PTlower units, not 61 and 69.pt-spike-rates-similar
