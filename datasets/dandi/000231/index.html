Dataset version 0.220904.1554 · tested 2026-10-29 · 11 open findings

  • Data missingThe analog input that carries the trial synchronization pulse in the neural stream is not deposited. Each session's Continuous_Data.openephys lists 100_ADC1 to 100_ADC8 and 100_AUX1 to 100_AUX6, but sourcedata holds only the 64 100_CH files, and all_channels.events holds no events, so the neural to video alignment cannot be checked from the raw data.
    Found by the attempt on Rodgers et al. 2022 (rodgers-2022-01, 2026-10-29)
  • Data missingThe behavioral (Arduino) timebase is not in the dataset, and neither are the curated whisker training sets and held-out predictions behind the tracking accuracy figures, which the abstract offers to users. The paper does not say where they are; the README of the original release (Zenodo doi:10.5281/zenodo.4743837) lists a trainset/ folder and per-session behavioral log files, which the paper does not mention.
    Found by the attempt on Rodgers et al. 2022 (rodgers-2022-01, 2026-10-29)
  • Metadata errorThe keywords of all 30 detection sessions include "shape discrimination", contrary to their experiment description ("whisker-based shape detection").
    Found by the attempt on Rodgers et al. 2022 (rodgers-2022-01, 2026-10-29)
  • Metadata errorIn every file the right reward series (described as the times of reward deliveries to the right) is identical to the right lick series, 315,782 times in all against 12,447 correct right-rewarded trials, so right reward times are not available.
    Found by the attempt on Rodgers et al. 2022 (rodgers-2022-01, 2026-10-29)
  • Description missing1,533 trials have stimulus "flatter convex" or "flatter concave", values that Table 6 does not list or describe.
    Found by the attempt on Rodgers et al. 2022 (rodgers-2022-01, 2026-10-29)
  • Metadata errorchoice_time is NaN on all 265 spoiled trials, where Table 6 says it is 45 s after the window opens. On the 22% of the other trials where it is not the first lick in the window (7,185 of 32,085), it falls 0 to 5 ms after the window opening on 7,183, with no lick within 5 ms of it on 94% of them, so it is not the first lick after the window as Table 6 defines it.
    Found by the attempt on Rodgers et al. 2022 (rodgers-2022-01, 2026-10-29)
  • Metadata error160 spoiled trials have ignore_trial False, where Table 6 says spoiled trials have it True; in the files ignore_trial equals "non-random or optogenetic" on every trial.
    Found by the attempt on Rodgers et al. 2022 (rodgers-2022-01, 2026-10-29)
  • Metadata errorThe neural recording has no timestamps array, which Table 2 and the paper's access code (.timestamps[:]) describe; its times are stored as a starting time and a rate.
    Found by the attempt on Rodgers et al. 2022 (rodgers-2022-01, 2026-10-29)
  • Other29 files hold three contacts tables, not the four the Data Records describe (no C0 table in 21, no C3 table in 8), without a note on whether the whisker made no contact.
    Found by the attempt on Rodgers et al. 2022 (rodgers-2022-01, 2026-10-29)
  • OtherThe dataset does not meet the paper's inclusion criteria as the files allow them to be checked. In 27 sessions more than 2% of a whisker's contacts hold an interpolated or absent frame (16 sessions for C1 to C3, up to 7.7%), C0 is labeled in fewer than 95% of frames in 3 to 7 sessions, and one session (228CR, 2019-07-15) has 84 trials with ignore_trial False.
    Found by the attempt on Rodgers et al. 2022 (rodgers-2022-01, 2026-10-29)
  • OtherThe paper's 18,514 trials cannot be recovered from the files, which hold 32,350 trials, 22,905 with ignore_trial False; no other combination of the trial flags tried gives it. The video frame total (63,979,800) and the 44 neural sessions match exactly.
    Found by the attempt on Rodgers et al. 2022 (rodgers-2022-01, 2026-10-29)
