    Dopamine delta AIC (constant minus changing) per animal -168.5 to 32.7, mean -18.7; t(6) = -0.73, p = 0.49 with k = 4 (paper t = -0.86, p = 0.42), and t = -0.65, p = 0.54 with k = 3.     How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • The model fit and the dF/F scale are as in fig4hi-delay-change-behavior-changes.
    • Assumed that the paper's fit applied the code's bounds (A -2 to 50, b -10 to 10) to dopamine values on a scale where they bind as they do here. Neither the Methods nor the code states the scale of the dF/F the Matlab scripts read from the authors' local files. The criterion fixes the bounds; the two scales the dandiset supports (fraction and percent) both give no change, and the paper's t (-0.86) is close to the one found here (-0.73), while the unbounded fit, outside the criterion, finds a change in every animal.

  • Description missingThe experimental condition of each session (Experiments 2 to 5) is not stated; only the first session of a condition carries a suffix in its name (8s, 8s-bgd6s, extinction), and the files of datHT-M6 and datHT-M7 do not name the task. On which scale of dF/F (fraction or percent, and with the AUC in ms) were the bounds of the dopamine changing model in cumsum_pavlovian.m (A -2 to 50, b -10 to 10) set, for the Fig 4I test? Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • Day numbers are read from the file names as load_dandi_url does; the Matlab scripts read them from folder names of the authors' local copies, which the dandiset does not hold.session-day-link
  • The criterion's every Day number from startday to endday is reported both for the listed days and literally for every integer day; the outcome uses the listed days, since integer days without a session can be days without training.session-day-link
  • Experiment 5 is counted as the sessions with background rewards in the event log, which equals the count in the range of cumsum_bgdrw_vs_extinction.m (7.86); condition 4 of cumsum_pavlovian.m gives 7.14, also within tolerance.methods-session-counts
  • The paper does not say how the percentages are averaged; both readings of the criterion are shown and both match.exp1-excluded-rewards
  • The window of the tests is given by neither the Methods, the legend nor the code; the three readings of the criterion are run, and they give different outcomes for the non-consummatory test.figs8e-lick-bout-responses
  • Peaks are found with scipy.signal.find_peaks (distance 50 samples, height and prominence as the code), in place of Matlab's findpeaks; NaN samples are set to the session minimum so that they form no peak.figs8g-baseline-unchanged
  • The changing model is fitted by bounded least squares (scipy) from 20 random start points with a fixed seed, keeping the best fit; Matlab's fit starts custom models at random points, so the code's fits are not repeated exactly.fig4hi-delay-change-behavior-changes
  • dF/F is divided by 100 before the dopamine AUC, as the files store percent and the Methods define dF/F as the fraction (470 - fitted 405)/fitted 405 (Methods, Fiber photometry). The scale for which the code's bounds were set is not stated; see fig4hi-delay-change-dopamine-unchanged.fig4hi-delay-change-behavior-changes
  • The model fit and the dF/F scale are as in fig4hi-delay-change-behavior-changes.fig4hi-delay-change-dopamine-unchanged
  • Assumed that the paper's fit applied the code's bounds (A -2 to 50, b -10 to 10) to dopamine values on a scale where they bind as they do here. Neither the Methods nor the code states the scale of the dF/F the Matlab scripts read from the authors' local files. The criterion fixes the bounds; the two scales the dandiset supports (fraction and percent) both give no change, and the paper's t (-0.86) is close to the one found here (-0.73), while the unbounded fit, outside the criterion, finds a change in every animal.fig4hi-delay-change-dopamine-unchanged
  • For reading (b) the trials after the lick change trial run to the end of the extinction sessions; the change trial is taken from both the code's and the Methods' lick measures, and both give every animal a positive mean after it (2.2 to 30.6).fig4jl-extinction-cue-response-positive
  • What the paired t-test compares is not in the Methods, legend or code; the three readings of the criterion are run and agree that background is not below extinction at p < 0.05.fig4p-background-faster-decay
  • HJ-FP-M4's first Experiment 5 session is missing from the dandiset (session-day-link).fig4p-background-faster-decay
  • The peak window and the baseline are not fixed by the paper; the four readings of the criterion are run and agree.figs11de-intermediate-cue-local-baseline
  • The Methods' baseline window is not given; both readings of the criterion are run and agree with the code's unsubtracted measure.fig6d-cues-increase-together
  • The claim's baseline is the 0.5 s before CS1; measure_inhibition_sequential.py takes it before every code-15 event, CS1 and CS2 alike (reftimes). Both are shown; the code's reproduces the paper's t values exactly.fig6g-inhibition-magnitude
  • For the 1 s reward window the 0.5 s baseline AUC is doubled, so that the baseline covers a window of equal length (claims header); a choice made here, which reading (b) does not involve.fig6g-inhibition-magnitude
  • The measure follows learnign_curve_sequential.py (session means of the CS1 AUC over 0 to 1.5 s minus the 1.5 s before, over the first conditioning session's mean reward response).fig6i-lower-cs1-asymptote
