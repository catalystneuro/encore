Dataset version 0.260923.2152 · tested 2026-11-02 · 9 open findings

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds only the whole-brain c-Fos light-sheet experiment (Figure 5, Table 4). The Homecage Scan behaviour, qRT-PCR, section immunohistochemistry, RNAscope and HCR images, body weights and telemetry behind 24 of the paper's 27 findings are not in it, nor in any other dandiset found.
    Found by the attempt on Bakalar et al. 2023 (bakalar-2023-01, 2026-11-02)
  • Selection not documentedThe dandiset holds 22 hemispheres of 13 mice (10 PACAP fl/fl from 6 mice, 12 PACAPko from 7). The paper analysed 8 PACAP fl/fl hemispheres from 6 mice and 11 PACAPko from 6 mice. Nothing in the dandiset says which hemispheres or which mouse were left out, or why; two PACAPko mice (45467ko, 45478ko) have one hemisphere each.
    Found by the attempt on Bakalar et al. 2023 (bakalar-2023-01, 2026-11-02)
  • Description missingThe FRST threshold used for the analysis is not marked. Each hemisphere has binary segmentations at 11 or 19 thresholds (2000 to 12000 for 4 hemispheres, 4000 to 40000 for the other 18), count tables at all of them, and one atlas-space heatmap whose threshold is not stated. The 18 hemispheres on the coarser series are 8 PACAP fl/fl from 6 mice and 10 PACAPko from 6 mice; nothing says why the other 4 were segmented on a different series.
    Found by the attempt on Bakalar et al. 2023 (bakalar-2023-01, 2026-11-02)
  • Data missingPer-region count tables exist for only 13 of the 22 hemispheres (one per mouse); for the other 9 the counts can only be recomputed from the segmentations and labels.
    Found by the attempt on Bakalar et al. 2023 (bakalar-2023-01, 2026-11-02)
  • Metadata errorREADME.md describes the root image stacks as downsampled to 25 um isotropic voxels, while SPIM.json and the files give 3.7 x 3.7 x 5 um voxels (2560 x 2160 pixels per plane). The README links CATNIP at github.com/AllenInstitute/CATNIP and a 2024 bioRxiv preprint, while dataset_description.json and the paper give github.com/snehashis-roy/CATNIP and the 2023 J Neuroendocrinol paper.
    Found by the attempt on Bakalar et al. 2023 (bakalar-2023-01, 2026-11-02)
  • Metadata errorThe atlas-space heatmaps state 3.7 x 3.7 x 5 um voxels in their OME metadata and 25 um in their sidecar (res-25um_SPIM.json).
    Found by the attempt on Bakalar et al. 2023 (bakalar-2023-01, 2026-11-02)
  • Metadata errorThe archive summary lists no species, participants.tsv spells the strain two ways (C57BL6/N for the PACAPko mice and C57BL/6N for the PACAP fl/fl mice), and the count tables have 1206 rows where the label table has 561 labels.
    Found by the attempt on Bakalar et al. 2023 (bakalar-2023-01, 2026-11-02)
  • Description missingThe Supplemental Methods ignore the counts of regions overlapping an artifact mask, but the dataset holds no artifact mask. HeatmapsAtlasSpaceCorrected holds corrected heatmaps for 2 hemispheres (45670ko Left, 45925flox Left) that set 54467 and 1095526 voxels to 0 (0.10% and 9.43% of the heatmap sum), with no description, and the dataset holds no atlas-space label image, so the regions excluded cannot be found.
    Found by the attempt on Bakalar et al. 2023 (bakalar-2023-01, 2026-11-02)
  • Description missing6 of the 22 hemispheres (45467ko Right, 45469ko Left and Right, 45476ko Right, 45670ko Right, 45930flox Left) have no AtlasLabelMasked, and nothing says why; their count tables use the unmasked label volumes. Where both exist, the masked labels hold 74% to 94% of the unmasked labelled voxels, so region densities depend on which is used.
    Found by the attempt on Bakalar et al. 2023 (bakalar-2023-01, 2026-11-02)
