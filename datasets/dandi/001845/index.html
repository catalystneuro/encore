Few of the main findings held up cleanly in this data. Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds only the cooperative sessions of the 5 WT and 5 Fmr1 pairs. The mixed WT-Fmr1 pairs, the visual-cue association task and the virtual-agent task are not in it, though the paper says all data underlying its results will be uploaded to this dandiset. Six of the paper's findings cannot be tested for this reason.
  • Description missingNo session is labelled sensory deprived (opaque barrier and white noise). The paper interspersed such sessions among the 50% sessions; the 646 files after the analysed sessions carry the same descriptions and fields as the others, and some have much lower performance (as low as 0.0), so the sessions may be in the dandiset without a label. Two findings (Figure 2B, the sensory-deprived part of Figure S2) cannot be tested.
  • OtherAbout 4.8% of pokes (82,245 of 1,716,630 in the analysed sessions) fall within 0.5 s of a poke by the same rat at another well, physically impossible for wells 47 cm apart, and they come in bursts (up to 128,979 pokes and 2,447 transitions in one rat session; 140 of 1,120 rat sessions affected, most in pairs FXM102-FXM103, FXM105-FXM107 and FXM108-FXM109). Used as recorded they inflate transition counts and change the performance of Eq. 1.
  • Description missingThe matched_poke events follow a rule the files do not describe. No match is at the well of the previous match (0 of 19,602 consecutive pairs), at 100% as at 50%, and matches cannot be recomputed from the poke events because only poke onsets are stored, not how long the nose is held.

  • OtherThe paper's code repository (JadhavLab/Social_CoOp_Behavior) holds only a README naming version 0.260519.1308, an early partial upload with 1,007 of the 2,649 assets of the latest published version; no analysis code is published.
