  • fig1b-accuracy-lower-doi

    Mice are less accurate in the olfactory search task in the DOI session than in the saline session, going more often to the incorrect side.

    Indeed, mice more often navigated to the incorrect side of the arena in the DOI condition ( Figure 1B ; Wilcoxon signed rank test, p = 0.024, n = 22)

    Results, DOI alters olfactory search behavior, paragraph 2; Figure 1B

    How it will be judged

    As the Methods (Figure 1) and fig1_cleaned.Rmd define it, accuracy is the percent of a session's odor trials (odor_setting 0) that were correct, sessions with fewer than 40 trials dropped. A two-sided paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test of DOI against saline accuracy over the mice. Reproduced when the median DOI minus saline difference is negative and p < 0.05; partially reproduced when it is negative with 0.05 <= p < 0.1. Omission-trial accuracy, which the code also tests, is reported as a variant.
