Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Metadata errorThe dandiset's metadata name the paper by its title but link it to doi:10.1162/neco_a_01676, which Crossref gives as "Energy Complexity of Convolutional Neural Networks". The paper is doi:10.1162/neco_a_01672.
  • Metadata errorThe CurrentClampSeries are stated in volts with conversion 1 but hold millivolts (-100 to +46), and the CurrentClampStimulusSeries are stated in amperes with conversion 1 but hold picoamperes (holding levels of 12 and -37, steps up to about 1000).
  • OtherThe patch timestamps are at 20.41 kHz (49 us) for every protocol except IDthres (20 kHz) and APWaveform (125 kHz, 8 us). The paper states digitization at 20 kHz, and APWaveform at high rates, typically 200 kHz. The intervals are regular and agree with the nominal step durations.
  • Metadata errorThe icephys sequential recordings table leaves out the first sweep of every protocol after IDthres in each run and every PosCheops sweep (35 of 300 recordings for cell 1, 28 of 240 for cell 2), so the grouping tables do not describe the protocols. The protocol_name and run columns of the intracellular recordings table are complete.
  • Description missingNo culture age, plating date or days in vitro is given in the files or the dandiset's metadata; the subject has only an id, a species and a description.
  • Description missingThe electrodes table's x, y and z are empty (NaN), location is "unknown" and impedance -1; electrode positions are only in the custom columns rel_x and rel_y, without a description of their frame.
  • Selection not documentedCell 2's file holds runs 3 to 6 of the recording, renamed run1 to run4; only the descriptions of its MEA series and the paper's export notebook say so. Runs 1 and 2 of cell 2 are not deposited, and the dandiset does not say why.
  • OtherIn every run 117 to 132 of the 675 to 678 MEA channels are constant for at least one of ten one-second segments (the channels the paper's code removes before computing templates); the deposited series keep them, with nothing marking them.
  • OtherIn the deposited sweeps the stimulus starts 50 ms earlier than the timings of the paper's feature code (350 ms against ton 400 ms for IDrest, 450 against 500 for firepattern), so the code's timings cannot be applied to the files as they are.
  • Paper inconsistentMethods 5.2 describe IV as 11 steps from -140 to 20% by 20%, which is 9 steps; the data hold 11 steps from -140 to 60%.
  • Paper inconsistentMethods 5.4 give 79 intracellular features for optimization and Table 1 gives 76; the code's feature files have 79 before it drops three sag features of IV -20%, and 76 after.
  • Methods not availableFor fig3b-ap-initiates-distal-ais, the paper does not say how the initiation site is read from Figure 3B or at what time resolution. The code's cut-outs (get_peak_cutout) are on a 0.05 ms grid, on which the distal and middle AIS tie at the -20 mV crossing and at the peak; they are ordered only between samples or in a finer simulation (5 to 7 us at the crossing, 20 to 25 us at the peak). Question for the authors - was the order read from a finer simulation than the plotted cut-outs?

Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • The 0% IV step of reading (a) has no relative tolerance; 10% of the IV increment (2% of the scale) was used. Its measured level is within 2% of holding in every run.ecode-protocol-structure
  • APWaveform's sweep count (6) and PosCheops' (1) are not stated in the Methods; 6 is the number of amplitudes from 200 to 350% by 30%.ecode-protocol-structure
  • The pre-stimulus window ends at the first change of the injected current found in each sweep (350 ms for most protocols), which in these files is 50 ms before the code's ton (400 ms for IDrest); the window ending at the code's ton is shown as a variant.holding-potential
  • The band-pass filter is applied forward and backward (sosfiltfilt), the default of the code's filtering library; the Methods give only the order and cut-offs. A standard choice that cannot move amplitudes of 159 uV and more below 50 uV.extracellular-spikes-above-50uv
  • Simulated with BluePyOpt 1.14.26 (NEURON 9) on the repository's model definition, in place of BluePyOpt 1.13.88; the main series uses its NEURON simulator, and the code's compute_rheobase_for_model on its LFPy simulator, as the notebook uses, is shown and agrees.gt-rheobase
  • Assumed. The paper does not say how the initiation site is read from Figure 3B (Results 3.2, "The AP is initiated at the distal end of the AIS and travels back toward the soma"), nor at what resolution. The times are read between the 0.05 ms samples of the code's cut-outs and checked on a fixed-step run at 0.005 ms, because a 0.05 ms grid cannot order two points 15 um apart that the simulation separates by 5 to 7 us; read at the sample, the two AIS points tie under both readings and the claim's strict order would not be met.fig3b-ap-initiates-distal-ais
  • Simulated with BluePyOpt 1.14.26 in place of 1.13.88; the code's protocol, recordings and get_peak_cutout run unchanged on its LFPy simulator. The fixed-step check uses its NEURON simulator (dt 0.005 ms, a standard choice).fig3b-ap-initiates-distal-ais
  • The AIS is placed with the segment midpoints of the instantiated LFPyCellModel (BluePyOpt 1.14.26, LFPy 2.3.7), where LFPy puts the soma midpoint at the origin. In the first round it was placed from NEURON's 3D points relative to the mean soma point, which puts the AIS midpoint at (1.7, -23.4) um, about 14 um off; that placement also gives channel 43 as nearest (23.3 um, next 26.8 um) and is shown as a variant.fig3a-largest-channel-at-ais
