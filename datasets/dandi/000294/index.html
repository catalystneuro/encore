  • recordings-two-cells-runs

    The experimental data are simultaneous patch-clamp and HD-MEA recordings of two cultured neurons, each stimulated with the eCode protocols repeated in four to six runs of about 200 s.

    We acquired paired patch-clamp and extracellular signals from two cells of cortical cultures in embryonic rats ... The eCode protocols were repeated during four to six runs (each run lasted approximately 200 s)

    Results 3.3, paragraph 1; Methods 5.2, last paragraph, and 5.6.5

    How it will be judged

  • ecode-protocol-structure

    Each run applies the eCode protocols with the sweep numbers, step durations and amplitudes (in percent of the estimated rheobase) that the Methods list.

    IDthresh: The purpose of this protocol was to finely screen the current levels to correctly reestimate Irheo before processing. It consisted of 21 sweeps with a 270 ms square pulse of increasing amplitudes from 50% to 130% with step increments of 4%

    Methods 5.2

    How it will be judged

    Per run, from the stimulus current series, count the sweeps of each protocol and measure step durations and amplitudes (current minus the pre-step holding level). The Methods give IDthres 21 sweeps of 270 ms, 50 to 130% by 4%; firepattern 2 sweeps of 3.6 s at 120 and 200%; IV 3 s steps; IDrest 11 sweeps of 1.35 s, 50 to 300% by 25%; APWaveform 50 ms pulses, 200 to 350% by 30%; HyperDepol 4 sweeps of 450 ms at -40 to -160% then 270 ms at 100%; sAHP 4 sweeps of 250 ms at 40%, 225 ms at 150 to 300% by 50%, 450 ms at 40%; PosCheops three ramps to 300% with up and down phases of 4, 2 and 1.33 s. The code's timings (ecodes_cell_timings in the feature notebooks) agree on every duration. For IV the Methods say 11 steps "from -140 to 20%" by 20%, which is 9 steps; the code's feature files list IV -140 to 60% (11 steps). Readings for IV (a) 11 steps, -140 to 60%; (b) 9 steps, -140 to 20%. The 100% scale is the IDthres scale (sweep k at 50 + 4k %), the rough rheobase from which the protocols were generated (Methods 5.6.5). Reproduced when, in every run of both cells, all eight protocols are present with these sweep counts and durations (within 10%) and every step amplitude is within 10% of its stated percentage of that scale (IV under either reading). Partially reproduced when counts and durations hold and amplitudes do not.
