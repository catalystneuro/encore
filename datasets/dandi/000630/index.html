Dataset version 0.230915.2257 · tested 2026-10-31 to 2026-11-22 · 19 open findings

  • Data missingDandiset 000630 holds the electrophysiology of 210 human interneurons from 53 donors, described as layer 1 neurons,, 85 of them with a usable chirp, where the paper's human acute sample has 542 cells from 170 donors (297 with a chirp) across all layers and four subclasses. It holds no mouse, cultured or ZD7288 recordings, no PV or SST cells, no transcriptomes and no morphologies, so most of the paper's findings cannot be tested on it alone; this attempt adds dandisets 000636 and 000020 for them. The dandiset was deposited for Chartrand et al. (2023) and is complete for that use; this describes its coverage of this paper.#
    Found by the attempt on Mansvelder et al. 2026 (mansvelder-2026-01, 2026-11-22)
  • Description missingThe files' subject records hold only the subject id, age, sex, species, genotype and a description; no subclass, t-type, donor name, brain structure, disease, layer or depth is read from the files. These labels had to be taken from the Chartrand et al. repository (linked by the session id in its nwb_path column) and from the paper's Table S1. The dandiset's description names the repository, so the labels can be found. The Chartrand table's Depth_from_Pia_microns column is 0 for all 210 dandiset cells, and its soma_depth_um is filled for 15 of them, so no pia-soma distance is available for most.#
    Found by the attempt on Mansvelder et al. 2026 (mansvelder-2026-01, 2026-11-22)
  • OtherMany sweeps were stopped before their stimulus ended and are zero-filled to full length without a flag in the file; over all 210 files, 50 chirp sweeps and 1763 hyperpolarizing long squares end in zeros (in 000636, 130 and 2561; in the 000020 files read, 73 and 201). No chirp sweep has zeros only before its chirp, so recordings with a leading zero are not what is dropped. An analysis that does not drop them reads 0 mV as data. The zeros are evident in the values.#
    Found by the attempt on Mansvelder et al. 2026 (mansvelder-2026-01, 2026-11-22)
  • Metadata errorThe species is spelled "Homo Sapiens" in 192 files and "Homo sapiens" in 18, and one file gives the donor's sex as "unknown" where the Chartrand table gives M.#
    Found by the attempt on Mansvelder et al. 2026 (mansvelder-2026-01, 2026-11-22)
  • OtherChecks that passed. Every file links to one row of the Chartrand table by its session id; subject ids and donor names map one to one; sex agrees for 209 of 210 files and age (days in the files, years in the table) within 0.06 years; all 53 donors are in the paper's Table S1; every one of the 1699 hyperpolarizing steps of the 210 files drives the voltage below its baseline inside the stimulus window; no two chirp cells share their impedance, sag and input resistance measures.#
    Found by the attempt on Mansvelder et al. 2026 (mansvelder-2026-01, 2026-11-22)
  • OtherFour cells are deposited in both dandiset 000630 and dandiset 000636 (the same session ids, with identical measures), and 41 donors have cells in both. An analysis that combines the two counts these cells twice unless it matches session ids, which both dandisets carry.#
    Found by the attempt on Mansvelder et al. 2026 (mansvelder-2026-01, 2026-11-22)
  • OtherDandiset 000630 describes its cells as human layer 1 neurons, but the Chartrand et al. table that labels them gives a target_layer of 2 for 34 of the 210 cells, 3 for 3 and 4 for 1, and none for 10 (its roi column agrees, for example TCx_L2). The files carry no layer. Whether these cells lie in layer 1 was not established; an analysis that selects layer 1 cells by the dandiset's description includes them.#
    Found by the attempt on Mansvelder et al. 2026 (mansvelder-2026-01, 2026-11-22)
  • OtherOne DANDI:000020 cell among the paper's mouse cells gives Q = 16.8 under chirp reading (b), where the next largest is 1.14 (mouse Q under reading (a) reaches 4.09). The value is evident in the measures; whether the recording is faulty was not established.#
    Found by the attempt on Mansvelder et al. 2026 (mansvelder-2026-01, 2026-11-22)
  • Metadata errorIn DANDI:000636, 21 files from 6 subjects (donors H19.03.004, H19.03.318, H19.03.319, H19.06.353, H19.26.402 and H20.03.304) give the donor's age as P0.0D, which is not an adult donor's age. It is evident from the values.#
    Found by the attempt on Mansvelder et al. 2026 (mansvelder-2026-01, 2026-11-22)
  • Description missingThe files carry no t-type, subclass, cortical layer, brain region or donor medical condition, and no specimen id of the paper's cell table. They can be linked to the paper's table only through the LIMS number in session_id, which equals the Ephys_Roi_Result number inside the table's internal nwb_path column; the dandiset's description points to the code repository but does not say how to link.#
    Found by the attempt on Chartrand et al. 2023 (chartrand-2023-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Identifier missingSix files (five of donor 1122692698, one of 1154304730) carry a time stamp such as 2021_08_17_112853 in general/source_script file_name, where the other 204 carry the cell name (for example H21.03.315.11.11.01.01).#
    Found by the attempt on Chartrand et al. 2023 (chartrand-2023-01, 2026-10-31)
  • File format errorIn 145 of 210 files, 2586 long square sweeps that stopped early hold zeros for the samples that were not acquired, where NWB readers such as IPFX expect missing values. IPFX's integrity check passes them and reads the step to 0 mV as a spike, which changes rheobase and spike features unless the sweeps are left out.#
    Found by the attempt on Chartrand et al. 2023 (chartrand-2023-01, 2026-10-31)
  • File format errorbias_current is stored in pA, with a unit attribute of pA, where the NWB schema fixes the unit of bias_current as amperes; a reader that follows the schema gets values 10^12 times too large.#
    Found by the attempt on Chartrand et al. 2023 (chartrand-2023-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Metadata errorThe two files of donor 922967568 state an age of P0.0D where the paper's table gives the age as unknown, and one file (session 977186103, cell H19.03.321.12.04.01.04) states sex unknown where the table gives M. Ages are stated as whole years times 365 days. The species is written as Homo Sapiens in 192 files and Homo sapiens in 18.#
    Found by the attempt on Chartrand et al. 2023 (chartrand-2023-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Data missingThe dandiset holds only the 210 Seattle cells of the paper's primary set. The 50 Amsterdam (22) and Szeged (28) cells of the strict L1 set, which the paper uses as held-out validation (Fig. S8B) and in its LAMP5 t-type analysis, are not in it, and the paper names no other deposit of them.#
    Found by the attempt on Chartrand et al. 2023 (chartrand-2023-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Selection not documentedThe dandiset holds exactly the paper's primary set P, the Seattle cells of the strict L1 set (soma in L1 or within 40 um of the L1/L2 border) whose long square features were extracted. The 12 Seattle cells of the strict set that failed feature extraction and the other Seattle cells of the paper's table are not deposited, and the dandiset's description does not say how the cells were selected.#
    Found by the attempt on Chartrand et al. 2023 (chartrand-2023-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Description missingThe donor medical condition is given in the public cell table for only 86 of the 194 cells of P in L1 t-types and 20 of the 71 reconstructed strict-set cells with an L1 t-type; the paper's code read it from the Allen Institute's donor database. The claims on medical condition cannot be repeated on the paper's cells.#
    Found by the attempt on Chartrand et al. 2023 (chartrand-2023-01, 2026-10-31)
  • OtherFor the mouse comparison (DANDI 000020 version 0.210913.1639, an outside input), 22 of the paper's 272 strict mouse L1 cells, 20 of them in VISp layer 1 and one of them a Lamp5 Krt73 cell, have no file in that version, and none of the paper's 15 mouse TEa cells does.#
    Found by the attempt on Chartrand et al. 2023 (chartrand-2023-01, 2026-10-31)
  • OtherNucleated-patch voltage clamp sweeps are in 38 human files of the dandiset and 25 strict mouse L1 files of DANDI 000020, but the patch surface areas the paper's conductance densities (Fig. 3F) need, measured from images, are not deposited.#
    Found by the attempt on Chartrand et al. 2023 (chartrand-2023-01, 2026-10-31)
