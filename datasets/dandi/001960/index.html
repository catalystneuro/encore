Findings about the data 4

What the attempts that used this dataset found about the data themselves, for its owner and the archive. The claims and their outcomes are on each paper’s page.

  • Identifier missingUnit ids are per session and nothing links the cells of a mouse's three sessions. Cells with the same id lie a median 1.1 to 1.3 mm apart, and only 6 to 26 of 320 cells have a cell of the other session within 10 um, so either the sessions image different cells or their coordinates are in different frames; the files do not say which. The gfp_positive flags of a mouse also differ between sessions (156, 171 and 171 NPY+ cells in M-YQ0201-27; 135, 144 and 157 in M-YQ0201-29), so the paper's per-mouse counts (166 and 145 of 320) cannot be traced to a session.
    Found by the attempt on Qi et al. 2026 (qi-2026-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Data missingThe tool-development experiments of Figure 1 and Supplementary Figures 1, 5, 6, 9 and 18 (JF585 against JF608, wide-field against targeted images, the 76-cell stimulation, the lattice off-target test, the spinning-disk z-stack, the 7.5x recordings) are not in the dandiset, and the raw-movie excerpt holds only projections of the structural stacks.
    Found by the attempt on Qi et al. 2026 (qi-2026-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Selection not documentedThe paper's connection and pair counts (802 of 7,280, 667 of 7,032, 3,553 and 2,199) are those of ordered pairs of passed_qc cells 60 to 400 um apart, and the passed_qc flag differs by session (the code documents its two rules); neither the band nor the rules are stated in the files.
    Found by the attempt on Qi et al. 2026 (qi-2026-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Description missingipsp_decay_source_units and its standard error carry no unit (the file says so); the values are consistent with seconds (median 0.035, capped at 0.4). membrane_time_constant_ms is exactly 0 for 7 cells of M-YQ0201-27, with no flag marking a failed fit. The x and y origin is undocumented. The x and y axes are not oriented on the brain (no anterior, posterior, medial or lateral direction is stored), so the directions of the wave analysis can be given only in the image frame.
    Found by the attempt on Qi et al. 2026 (qi-2026-01, 2026-11-06)
