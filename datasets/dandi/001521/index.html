Dataset version 0.261006.1845 · tested 2026-11-02 · 6 open findings

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds only the Fluo-4 imaging of Fig. 6. The pulsed-laser potentiometric imaging behind Figs. 1 to 5 and 7, which carries most of the paper's findings (21 of 27 claims), is not in it. Six other dandisets of the lab (001515, 001516, 001517, 001518, 001525, 001526) name the same paper and by their titles hold these experiments, but neither the paper's Data availability (on request) nor this dandiset's description points to them.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2026 (semenov-2026-05, 2026-11-02)
  • Description missingThe files give each pulse as a generator voltage (0 to 400 V); the paper reports only field strengths and gives no conversion. At about 3.9 V/cm per V the single-pulse voltages fall on the paper's field series and the six train voltages nearest the Fig. 6B fields have group sizes close to the figure's n (28, 38, 36, 68, 47, 39 against 28, 37, 34, 54, 35, 36). At about 3.3 V/cm per V the train voltages fall on the 230, 650 and 780 V/cm named in the text. The conversion is an assumption here.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2026 (semenov-2026-05, 2026-11-02)
  • Metadata errorEvery file states an electrode gap of 1.00 mm and an electrode diameter of 0.5 mm; the paper's Methods 2.2 give tungsten rods 500 um in diameter at 1.6 mm spacing.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2026 (semenov-2026-05, 2026-11-02)
  • OtherIn the train session (2025-05-02) each subject is one field of view recorded at 1 to 7 escalating voltages after a sham, so the same 82 cells account for 311 exposures to trains. The paper states that each cell was tested only once, 28 to 54 cells per field strength, 224 cells in all. Either the dandiset holds different recordings from Fig. 6B, or the paper's description is wrong.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2026 (semenov-2026-05, 2026-11-02)
  • Selection not documentedThe single-pulse session holds 27 cells in 6 fields of view, recorded at 0 to 400 V; Fig. 6A averages 14 cells over 0 to 1200 V/cm. Three different sets of fields of view hold 14 cells. The dandiset does not say which cells or recordings the paper used, and it holds no cell regions or traces, so the paper's selection cannot be repeated.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2026 (semenov-2026-05, 2026-11-02)
  • Metadata errorThe dandiset's description and title describe only single 1-us pulses with recordings of 5 min at 1 frame/s, but 33 of the 73 files are 60-pulse 6 Hz trains recorded for 3 min at 0.5 frame/s (the other 9 files of that session are shams). Subjects are fields of view of a cell line, yet each has an age of P1D, and the archive's species summary names two species, 'Cricetulus griseus - Cricetulus aureus', for the one taxon NCBITaxon 10029.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2026 (semenov-2026-05, 2026-11-02)
