    Found by the attempt on Peterson et al. 2021 (peterson-2021-02, 2026-11-25)

  • Metadata errorFixedIn three files (sub-01 ses-7, sub-04 ses-3, sub-06 ses-3) the ECoG holds 15.5 to 15.9 h and its starting_time is 0, while the reaches table and the pose run on a clock from midnight. The ECoG starts 29241 to 30682 s after midnight, the time missing from 24 h; ReachEvents and the session start time agree, and the movement response appears only with this shift (S06 day 3, precentral HFB +1.98 dB with it, -0.07 dB without). Read with starting_time as given, the event times point at the wrong ECoG or past its end.
    earlier_findings.ipynb finds the ECoG starting_time of the three files now set to 30682.414, 30048.322 and 29240.566 s, so all 55 files end at 86400 s on the clock of the events, and S06 day 3 gives precentral HFB +1.95 dB with the ECoG read from its starting_time against -0.10 dB from 0 s. Finding 2 of peterson-2021-02 names the same three files, but its helper reads their ECoG from 0 s (ajile.py, session_spectral), so the ECoG it reports as ending early starts late. Found fixed on dataset version 0.261123.1703 by peterson-2021-02, 2026-11-25.
