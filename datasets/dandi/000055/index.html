Dataset version 0.220127.0436 · tested 2026-11-07 · 10 open findings

  • Metadata errorIn three files (sub-01 ses-7, sub-04 ses-3, sub-06 ses-3) the ECoG holds 15.5 to 15.9 h and its starting_time is 0, while the reaches table and the pose run on a clock from midnight. The ECoG starts 29241 to 30682 s after midnight, the time missing from 24 h; ReachEvents and the session start time agree, and the movement response appears only with this shift (S06 day 3, precentral HFB +1.98 dB with it, -0.07 dB without). Read with starting_time as given, the event times point at the wrong ECoG or past its end.
    Found by the attempt on Peterson et al. 2021 (peterson-2021-01, 2026-11-07)
  • OtherThe files hold 8088 wrist movement events (211 to 996 per subject), where the data descriptor states 6931 verified events and the paper reports 151 to 947 per subject (median 640). No event is marked as a false positive or as removed, so the events the paper analysed cannot be selected.
    Found by the attempt on Peterson et al. 2021 (peterson-2021-01, 2026-11-07)
  • Description missingReach_angle_degrees is described only as "Reach angle in degrees". It holds the angle of the reach in image coordinates (y down, -180 to 180), so upward reaches are negative, while the paper's feature is the negated angle folded to -90 to 90.
    Found by the attempt on Peterson et al. 2021 (peterson-2021-01, 2026-11-07)
  • Metadata errorPose positions are labelled meters but hold pixels (the data descriptor gives pixels; the values span hundreds).
    Found by the attempt on Peterson et al. 2021 (peterson-2021-01, 2026-11-07)
  • Metadata errorBimanual_overlap is described as a fraction of the contralateral movement duration, but 1708 of 8088 values exceed 1 (up to 3.83); it behaves as seconds.
    Found by the attempt on Peterson et al. 2021 (peterson-2021-01, 2026-11-07)
  • Metadata errorsession_start_time is 19:00 at UTC-5 for files that start at local midnight (midnight UTC written in another zone), and 03:07 to 03:31 at UTC-5 for the three files whose ECoG starts later.
    Found by the attempt on Peterson et al. 2021 (peterson-2021-01, 2026-11-07)
  • Other124 of the event segments contain missing (NaN) ECoG samples on every electrode, in 30 of 55 files.
    Found by the attempt on Peterson et al. 2021 (peterson-2021-01, 2026-11-07)
  • Data missingThe subjects' sex and age, which the paper reports, are not in the files or the archive's metadata.
    Found by the attempt on Peterson et al. 2021 (peterson-2021-01, 2026-11-07)
  • Description missingThe electrode `good` flags differ between a subject's files, and nothing says which list the paper used; the paper's code takes the list of each subject's first file.
    Found by the attempt on Peterson et al. 2021 (peterson-2021-01, 2026-11-07)
  • Metadata errorThe electrode group descriptions and the data descriptor's Table 2 disagree for two subjects. S11 has groups LATD, LMTD and LTPD described as depth (18 electrodes), where the descriptor gives S11 106 surface and no depth electrodes; S10 has 78 electrodes in groups not described as depth and 48 in depth groups, where the descriptor gives 86 and 40.
    Found by the attempt on Peterson et al. 2021 (peterson-2021-01, 2026-11-07)
