Dataset version 0.241028.1559 · tested 2026-11-01 · 7 open findings

  • Description missingThe files carry no cortical area, brain region, slice preparation (acute or culture), transcriptomic or predicted subclass, retrograde label or drug condition, and session_description is a placeholder. These come only from the per-cell tables of the paper's code on Zenodo, matched by the electrode cell_id, which every file has. For the 51 L5 ET cells the table lists with synaptic blockers, only one file records when they were applied (cell QN23.26.013.4A.05.01, user comment "started washon of synaptic block" at sweep 91, after 13 of its 10 s sweeps); no other user comment, sweep comment or lab notebook text mentions a blocker or a wash, so for the other 50 cells when blockers were applied cannot be read from the files.
    Found by the attempt on Dembrow et al. 2024 (dembrow-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Data missing47 of the 341 cells of the code's L5 physiology table (35 of them recorded by one user) are not in the dandiset, which holds 294; the paper reports 333 cells in its physiology analysis, 33 of them from earlier studies.
    Found by the attempt on Dembrow et al. 2024 (dembrow-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Metadata errorFile sub-Q19-26-009 (cell Q19.26.009.2A.04.02) names subject Q19.26.009 where the paper's table gives donor Q19.26.012, and the two files of subject Q19.01.001 give Macaca mulatta (NCBITaxon 9544) where the table gives M. nemestrina.
    Found by the attempt on Dembrow et al. 2024 (dembrow-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Metadata errorOne subject has sex U and subject Q19.01.001 has age P1Y/ (1 year, in a malformed ISO duration), where the paper reports animals of 2 to 19 years; the files hold 57 subjects, 22 male, 34 female, against the paper's 58, 23 and 35.
    Found by the attempt on Dembrow et al. 2024 (dembrow-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • OtherFive files hold 1 s current command steps above 4 nA (up to 16 nA) in their 200 pA series, beyond the 4 nA that STAR Methods give as the headstage limit; whether these currents were delivered is not stated.
    Found by the attempt on Dembrow et al. 2024 (dembrow-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Selection not documentedFiles hold several runs of each protocol (repeats, series stopped early, a 50 pA and a 200 pA series, runs before and after drug application) and nothing marks which sweeps the paper's features use. For 61 of the 68 cells of the 4 nA analysis the steps in the files differ from those of the code's pickles.
    Found by the attempt on Dembrow et al. 2024 (dembrow-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • OtherIn 10 files the first run of 1 s hyperpolarizing steps holds sweeps that do not hyperpolarize the cell, start from a baseline above -40 mV or reach -20 mV (13 sweeps; some read 0 mV, as from a lost recording), and nothing in the files marks them. Used as they are, they gave negative steady-state input resistances for 3 cells.
    Found by the attempt on Dembrow et al. 2024 (dembrow-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
