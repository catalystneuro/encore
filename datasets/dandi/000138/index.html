Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds one session of monkey J's array recordings on the maze task, cut to 500 trials over 27 conditions for the Neural Latents Benchmark. The paper's J-array dataset had 108 conditions and a mean 2166 trials per neuron, and its single-electrode datasets of monkeys A, B and J and all EMG recordings are not in the dandiset, so two of the paper's main findings cannot be tested and the rest only on J-array.
  • Metadata errorEvery unit's electrode reference points into the PMd group of the electrodes table. The unit ids encode a prefix (1 for 83 units, 2 for 79) and a channel, and the referenced electrode is the channel minus one for both prefixes, so the 79 units of one array reference electrodes of the other. The heldout units split 20 and 20 between the prefixes.
  • Description missingWhich id prefix is the PMd array and which the M1 array is not stated, and all electrode coordinates (x, y, z) are NaN, so neither array membership nor position on the array can be read from the files. Anterior-posterior analyses here rest on an assumption.
  • Identifier missingNothing in the dandiset or the paper says whether the 2009-10-06 session is the one behind the paper's J-array dataset. The file has 162 units where the paper reports 146 isolations, and 27 of its 108 conditions.
  • OtherHand, cursor and eye position are at 1 kHz and only within trials, where the paper tracked the hand at 60 Hz and the eye at 240 Hz; the resampling is not described. Hand velocity agrees with the derivative of hand position (slope 1.000).
  • OtherEight trials have a delay of 0 ms or less (down to -2 ms), where the `delay` field is described as the delay between target presentation and go cue.
  • Paper inconsistentThe mean trials per neuron of the J-array dataset is 2166 in the Results and 2155 in the Methods.
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the paper's authors. Is the PCA space (peri-movement and kinematic) computed once from all conditions, with only the PD regression leaving a condition out, or recomputed without the left-out condition? Computed once, performance peaks at k = 10 and falls beyond (as the paper reports); recomputed, it keeps rising to k = 14, and the outcomes of fig6-each-dimensionality, fig6-dimensionality-trend, fig7a and fig7c change.   • Methods not availableQuestion for the paper's authors. How was the maze proto-trial defined, over which window, and did the horizontal and vertical profiles each need r > 0.9 or the two together?   • Methods not availableQuestion for the paper's authors. Tests the findings of Churchland, Cunningham, Kaufman, Ryu and Shenoy (2010) on dandiset 000138 (MC_Maze_Large), which holds 500 maze-task trials over 27 conditions and 162 array units from one session of monkey J. It compares preparatory and peri-movement tuning, and how well a preferred direction captures preparatory tuning in peri-movement, kinematic and task spaces, with the controls of Figure 7 and the oscillator of Figure 8. The single-electrode datasets of monkeys A, B and J and the EMG are not in the dandiset. All files

Requiring each axis to pass keeps 81% of trials and leaves 2 or 3 trials in some conditions, as one velocity component of nearly vertical or horizontal reaches is mostly noise; the concatenated test keeps 96%, and the paper reports "typically ~90%" with outliers only occasional. Movement onset is the trial table's `move_onset_time`; the paper refers to Churchland et al. 2006c, not available here, and the speed-threshold reading (10% of peak speed, a median 14 ms later) is also run.fig4d-tuning-correlation-j-array
