Dataset version 0.220126.2143 · tested 2026-10-21 · 8 open findings

  • Metadata errorIn 36 files the SpikeTrain timestamps run 10 ms before the CurrentClampSeries timestamps of the same samples (the spike samples line up with the voltage one for one); in 16 files they agree. Under the spike timestamps the median L4 C2 touch-response peak is 8 ms in the files without the offset and 12 ms in those with it (rank sum p = 0.016); under the voltage timestamps it is 8 and 22 ms. The data are consistent with the voltage timestamps of the 36 files being 10 ms late, and a residual 4 ms difference under the spike timestamps is not explained.
    Found by the attempt on Hires et al. 2015 (hires-2015-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Metadata errorIn 38 files the CurrentClampSeries data, with the stated conversion of 0.001 to volts, span only 2 to 26 microvolts, so the conversion is off by 1000; in the other 14 they span 3 to 28 mV.
    Found by the attempt on Hires et al. 2015 (hires-2015-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Metadata errorIn 38 files theta_filt, described as theta_at_base filtered 6 to 60 Hz, is not the signal the stored amplitude and phase come from (up to 46 deg apart); in 14 it equals amplitude times cos(phase). The stored amplitude and phase match a 6 to 60 Hz Butterworth band-pass and Hilbert transform of theta_at_base.
    Found by the attempt on Hires et al. 2015 (hires-2015-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Metadata errorset_point is described as the amplitude of the Hilbert transform of theta_at_base but follows theta minus its band-passed part (r 0.96 to 1.00), and holds missing values in 23 files.
    Found by the attempt on Hires et al. 2015 (hires-2015-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Metadata errorpole_position is described in mm, but its values range over several scales between files, 0.2 to 1.6 in some, 992 to 13888 (motor steps) in others, about 1e8 in others, and up to 1.4e12 in one.
    Found by the attempt on Hires et al. 2015 (hires-2015-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Description missingThree subjects (anm211295, anm215592, anm217330; six cells) have no genotype, the paper's VGAT-ChR2 mice by their counts. The files hold 23 subjects where the paper reports 21 mice, and the C57BL6 and Ai32 x PV-Cre counts differ from the paper's by one cell each, so the mapping of subjects to the paper's animals is not given.
    Found by the attempt on Hires et al. 2015 (hires-2015-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Other30 files are named with an ogen suffix, but no file holds an optogenetic stimulus series and stim_present is 0 in every trial.
    Found by the attempt on Hires et al. 2015 (hires-2015-01, 2026-10-21)
  • Otherfirst_lick_time disagrees with the lick onset series in some trials, by up to 3.1 s; 47 trials have licks in the series and no first_lick_time, and 136 have a first_lick_time and no lick in the series.
    Found by the attempt on Hires et al. 2015 (hires-2015-01, 2026-10-21)
