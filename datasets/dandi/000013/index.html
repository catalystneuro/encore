Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • The sample epoch is read from the `pole_available` series, cut at the end of each 4.5 to 5 s recording; the paper's own measure may extend past it.behavior-sample-epoch
  • The pre-touch windows are those ending at or before onset (starts -50 to -10 ms), a choice the claims file does not fix; the example cell's pre-touch rate is far from the paper's under any window.example-cell-ff-around-touch
  • Reading (1b) leaves out windows within 60 ms before or 100 ms after a touch onset, a standard choice.fig4a-touch-detection-c2
  • Ties between thresholds of equal training percent correct are broken at the middle one, a choice the claims file does not fix; the lowest one is shown as a variant.fig4a-touch-detection-c2
  • The smaller pseudocount of the variant, 0.01, is a value of our choosing; the criterion gives none.fig4b-touch-time-precision
  • The floor rate, one spike over all of a cell's training cycles and bins, is a value of our choosing, as the paper gives none. Cells without a phase fit take preferred phase 0. Tests use 50 samples per phase and phase differences in 6 deg steps.fig4c-phase-decoding
  • Pre-touch velocity is taken as the frame before onset, fig5c's "pretouch" reading; the claims file does not repeat which reading applies here.fig6s1b-clusters-less-variable
  • The PSTH used for the free rate starts 10 ms before onset so that the integral over the preceding 2.3 ms is defined at the first window.fig6d-not-refractory
