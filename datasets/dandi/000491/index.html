Several of the paper's central findings held in this data and several did not. The vessel radius peak near 28 um, the polynomial fit matching the segmented area ratio best, and the fit velocity ordering reproduced, mostly under only some analysis choices. The resistance power law held partially, while the area ratio and resistance distributions, ellipse parameters and PVS width asymmetry did not match. The likely reason is the paper's undescribed manual centerline cleaning and PVS selection, for which the agent used stand-ins. The validation against 2D images could not be tested because those images are not in the dataset.

    • Starts from the deposited final segmentations, as the claims file says; the vessel centerline is skimage's 3D thinning in place of MATLAB bwskel. The other assignment was not run.
    • Assumed, the PVS of a cross section is the two largest PVS regions (8-connected) that come within one vessel radius, sqrt(area / pi), of the vessel region, the vessel being the connected region at or nearest the slice center.
