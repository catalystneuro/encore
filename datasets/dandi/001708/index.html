    Both ON- and OFF-cells exhibited clear ... low-frequency spectral peaks, often accompanied by har ... istic period of approximately five minutes (TON ... peak = 286.86 ... s, full width at half maximum (FWHM) = 16.92 s; TOFF ... 298.33 s, FWHM = 16.12 s)

    How it will be judged

    As Panel_3.ipynb and the supplement ("Spike train power spectra"): each cell's 1500 s segment (ongoing-cell-counts) binned at 5 ms, its periodogram with a Hann window over one segment, zero-padded (the code's FFT length is 50 times the spike count of the first ON cell, a resolution of 1.65e-4 Hz), DC removed, normalized to unit area (get_auto_correlation_freqs_with_rate); the class PSD is the mean over cells; the peak is the highest local maximum of the mean PSD between 0.0001 and 0.1 Hz (get_summary_psd_stats), and the period its inverse. Reproduced when both periods are within tolerance and the FWHMs match, under one reading; partially reproduced when only the periods do. Whether peaks near 600 s and 150 s (harmonics) are present is reported.
