Problems that change results 2

The data are wrong in a way the files do not show, so an analysis that takes them as given comes out wrong. Each was checked against the notebook’s outputs by someone other than the attempt’s author.

  • File format errorChanges resultsIn all 335 files units/spike_times_index holds the offset where each unit's spikes start, not where they end as NWB requires; standard readers (pynwb) therefore give every unit the spikes of the unit before it. The per-unit, per-trial spike counts in 'unit and trial pairs' agree with the start-offset reading in every file.
    Checked: reproduce_kaku_2025.ipynb cell 6 prints that spike_times_index read as start offsets matches the per-trial spike counts in 335 of 335 files and read as end offsets in 0. The test runs in a helper module, so only the summary count is printed, and the pynwb behaviour is stated in markdown and follows from the index layout; it was not run.
    Found by the attempt on Kaku et al. 2025 (kaku-2025-01, 2026-11-05)
  • Metadata errorChanges resultsThe 3D tracking's idx field matches the DLC trial number in 154 of 173 sessions and the trial number minus one in 19 sessions (15 subjects), judged by the correlation of the 2D and 3D tongue tracks (median 0.98 at the matching offset); the files do not say how idx counts trials.
    Checked: reproduce_kaku_2025.ipynb cell 11 prints offset 0 as best in 154 sessions and offset -1 in 19 sessions, with 15 subjects listed for -1 and a median correlation of 0.983 at the offset used. The evidence is a summary of 2D against 3D track correlations with a minimum of 0.354 at the chosen offset, so a few sessions are assigned with weak support, but a reader who takes idx as the trial number pairs the 3D tracks with the wrong trial in one of the two groups.
    Found by the attempt on Kaku et al. 2025 (kaku-2025-01, 2026-11-05)

Other findings 8

  • File format errorIn 19 files the units/histology table (region and CCF position) has more rows than the units (for example 680 against 613 in DL034 session 20220205 probe p2), so its rows cannot be matched to units with certainty; the paper's code, and this attempt, take them by position.
    Found by the attempt on Kaku et al. 2025 (kaku-2025-01, 2026-11-05)
  • Metadata errorThe subject age field reads P210W to P303W (210 to 303 weeks) for every file, which does not fit adult mice in an acute recording study; the general/stimulus field says 'photostim' in every file, but no file holds photostimulation or cue data.
    Found by the attempt on Kaku et al. 2025 (kaku-2025-01, 2026-11-05)
  • Metadata errorThe 'left'/'right' hemisphere text of unit_location and of the insertion's recordable_brain_regions puts 99.3% of units on the side opposite to the one ccf_x gives if it increases to the right (midline 5,700 um); the files do not name the CCF axes or their direction, so the side of a unit cannot be fixed.
    Found by the attempt on Kaku et al. 2025 (kaku-2025-01, 2026-11-05)
  • Description missingThe 3D tracking (jaw_side, tongue_bottom, lickport_bottom) has no stated axes, origin or units, and the trial tables have no target position, so the lick direction of Extended Data Fig 8 cannot be computed; along the 3D x axis licking units prefer opposite sides in the two hemispheres, but which side is ipsilateral cannot be named.
    Found by the attempt on Kaku et al. 2025 (kaku-2025-01, 2026-11-05)
  • OtherEvery trial in the trial tables starts at 0 and spikes, tracking and airflow are stored per trial relative to its start, so trials cannot be placed on one session clock and inter-trial periods are absent.
    Found by the attempt on Kaku et al. 2025 (kaku-2025-01, 2026-11-05)
  • OtherIn every two-probe session (162) the airflow and DLC tracking of the two files are the same recording deposited twice (identical samples and lengths). The 3D tracking holds one trial fewer than the trial table in 211 files, and the side-camera DLC rows differ from trials x 1471 in 25 files.
    Found by the attempt on Kaku et al. 2025 (kaku-2025-01, 2026-11-05)
  • OtherIn six subjects (DL029 to DL034) the airflow-tongue cross-correlation peaks 20 to 30 ms later than in the other subjects (tongue lag median 103.7 against 78.2 ms), and fewer in-bout inspirations fall inside a tongue protrusion (8.5% to 14.6% against 22% to 42%). The files do not say how the airflow and video clocks were aligned, so a per-subject offset between them cannot be excluded.
    Found by the attempt on Kaku et al. 2025 (kaku-2025-01, 2026-11-05)
  • Data missingThe dandiset holds only the multi-directional licking task recordings (335 insertions, where the paper reports 339). The cued licking task, the ALM, latSC and brainstem photostimulation experiments, the rat recordings, the raw videos used by the autoencoder and the premotor tracing data are not in it, and the paper names no other deposit; 18 of the 48 claims are blocked for missing data.
    Found by the attempt on Kaku et al. 2025 (kaku-2025-01, 2026-11-05)
