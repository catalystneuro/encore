Dataset version 0.260520.1603 · tested 2026-11-05 · 5 open findings

  • Metadata errorThe imaging rate stated in the ImagingPlane metadata is wrong in 21 files: 30 Hz (concentration session of sub-2400) and 50 or 100 Hz (all GNG_2023b sessions) where the plane timestamps give 4.51 to 4.52 Hz per plane.
    Found by the attempt on Daste et al. 2025 (daste-2025-01, 2026-11-05)
  • Selection not documentedThe Go/NoGo experiment holds 8 mice, 4 OB-tagged and 4 mPFC-tagged, where the paper has 7 (4 OB-p, 3 mPFC-p). The extra mPFC mouse is presumably sub-668, the only one without tracking identifiers, with 24 to 58 cells per session; its day 3 performance (90% correct) passes the paper's exclusion threshold of 60%, and nothing in the dandiset says why it is left out.
    Found by the attempt on Daste et al. 2025 (daste-2025-01, 2026-11-05)
  • Selection not documentedThe paper's counts are matched only when planes without red cells are left out, as the paper's code does; over all planes the identity experiment has 2328 untagged neurons, against 1937 with the rule and 1978 in the paper. In sub-164 and sub-8 the plane without red cells nearly repeats plane 0 (mean-image r = 0.994 and 0.996), consistent with the code's comment about duplicated planes. The files do not mark these planes.
    Found by the attempt on Daste et al. 2025 (daste-2025-01, 2026-11-05)
  • OtherFour identity sessions (sub-8, sub-9, sub-163, sub-164, recorded in 2020) have 80 trials of 30 s with odor onset about 10 s into the trial, while the other six have 100 trials of 22 s with onset at 5.1 s; the paper describes 22 s trials with a 5 s baseline.
    Found by the attempt on Daste et al. 2025 (daste-2025-01, 2026-11-05)
  • OtherUnder the paper code's criterion, the 46 tracked OB-p neurons come from 3 OB-p mice (sub-664 contributes none), not 4.
    Found by the attempt on Daste et al. 2025 (daste-2025-01, 2026-11-05)
