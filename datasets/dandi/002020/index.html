  • dishes-analysed

    Seven dishes met the paper's inclusion rule (spontaneous activity observed continuously for more than 10 days) and were summarized.

    Averaging data across seven dishes revealed that power law in PCA was consistently established around 15 DIV

    Results, Self-organization of power law in PCA, paragraph 3; Methods, Data processing and principal component analysis, paragraph 1

    How it will be judged

    Count the dishes in which recordings with activity above 0.03 Hz (reading S1 or S2) run continuously, without a day missing activity, over more than 10 days (Methods, Data processing, paragraph 1, "continuously observed for more than 10 days"). A count of dishes is exact; tolerance 0 for that reason.

  • activity-onset

    Spontaneous activity became detectable at about 2 to 3 DIV.

    Spontaneous activity became detectable approximately 2 – 3 days in vitro (DIV)

    Results, Self-organization of power law in PCA, paragraph 1

    How it will be judged

    For each dish, the earliest DIV with a recording whose activity exceeds 0.03 Hz (readings S1, at least one electrode above 0.03 Hz, or S2). Reproduced when the median over dishes lies within the reported range 2 to 3 widened by 10% of each bound (1.8 to 3.3, the expected value and tolerance above). The paper gives no detection rule beyond the 0.03 Hz selection, which is used for that reason. If the dataset starts each dish later than this, the claim cannot be tested.

  • fig1b-async-to-bursts

    As the cultures matured, spiking changed from asynchronous firing to synchronous bursting.

    During this transition from exponential to power-law decay, spiking activity evolved from asynchronous firing to synchronous bursting

    Figure 1B; Results, Self-organization of power law in PCA, paragraphs 1 and 4

    How it will be judged

    The paper shows this in raster plots only and defines no burst or synchrony measure. Readings, each over all recordings of all dishes against DIV, (a) the mean pairwise Pearson correlation of the 100 ms spike counts (the bins of the Methods), (b) the largest fraction of electrodes active within one 100 ms bin, as a share of electrodes. Reproduced when under each reading the Spearman correlation with DIV is positive with p < 0.05, and the dish median at 15 DIV and later exceeds that at 3 to 5 DIV. The test and threshold are a reading of "evolved" chosen here, as the paper gives none.

The 0.1 boundary is the paper's own threshold between its Exponential and Power-law groups (Results, Co-occurrence, paragraph 3). The first three DIVs, the 15 DIV boundary (from Results paragraph 3) and the majority of dishes are readings chosen here, as the paper gives no rule for Figure 1D.

  • fig1d-shuffle

    Temporal shuffling leaves the variance spectrum unchanged early in development but, once the power law has emerged, disrupts it and returns it to exponential decay.

    During the initial development period, shuffling did not alter the variance spectra. However, after the emergence of the power law in PCA, shuffling significantly disrupted the power law, reverting them to exponential decay

    Figure 1D, dashed lines and legend; Results, Self-organization of power law in PCA, paragraph 4

    How it will be judged

    Shuffle each recording as in the Figure 1D legend and fit b to the original and the shuffled spectra (F1 and F2). The paper reports no test. Early recordings are those in the Exponential group (b at or above 0.1); late ones those in the Power-law group. Reproduced when (i) in early recordings the median ratio of shuffled to original b lies within 0.9 to 1.1 (the registry's default 10%), and (ii) in late recordings the shuffled b is at or above 0.1 in a majority and exceeds the original b, with a one-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test over recordings at p < 0.05. The test and the 10% band are readings of "did not alter" and "significantly" chosen here, as the paper gives none; the effect sizes are reported.

  • no-temporal-structure

    The cultures did not show the in vivo temporal structure of PCs, in which dominant PCs have longer time scales than minor PCs.

As the paper names no test, the outcome is reported under both readings, and the claim is blocked as methods_underspecified where they disagree.
