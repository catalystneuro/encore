Dataset version 0.261006.1856 · tested 2026-11-02 · 7 open findings

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds only the 1-us escalation strobe images (6 FluoVolt and 13 Di-8-ANEPPS cells). The alternating 150/230 V/cm sets of Fig. 2C/D, the 0.3-us pulses of Fig. 4D, the Gd3+ cells and controls of Fig. 5A, the Ca2+ imaging of Fig. 6 and the two-pulse experiments of Fig. 7 are not in it; 15 of the paper's 31 claims cannot be tested.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2026 (semenov-2026-03, 2026-11-02)
  • Description missingThe pulse amplitude is given only as generator volts inside a free-text session description (25 to 350 V). The paper reports field strengths in V/cm from Sim4Life modelling (43 to 1400 V/cm), which are not proportional to the volts, and no file or dandiset field gives the conversion; the nine steps had to be matched in order.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2026 (semenov-2026-03, 2026-11-02)
  • Description missingThe files hold raw images only: each holds one ImageSeries with the frames, their rate and a general description, and no ImagingPlane, Device, field of view or grid spacing. The pixel size, the field direction, the cell outline or ROIs, and which frames are baseline and which carry the pulse are not stored; the 40/60 split is stated in the paper and is consistent with the data.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2026 (semenov-2026-03, 2026-11-02)
  • Metadata errorEvery session description gives an electrode distance of 1.00 mm; the paper (Methods 2.2) and its reference [20] give 1.6 mm.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2026 (semenov-2026-03, 2026-11-02)
  • Metadata errorThe dandiset description says Di-8-ANEPPS was used, replacing FluoVolt, but 68 of the 220 sets (6 cells) are stated in their session descriptions to be FluoVolt.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2026 (semenov-2026-03, 2026-11-02)
  • OtherFrame 0 of 137 of 141 sets examined and 290 further frames hold no laser flash (frame mean near the camera floor) and are not flagged. Apart from frame 0 they are five times as frequent among pulse frames (257 of 8460) as among baseline frames (33 of 5499). Averaging them in changes dF by several percent (the Di-8-ANEPPS 43 V/cm offset goes from -5.1 to -2.2%).
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2026 (semenov-2026-03, 2026-11-02)
  • Selection not documentedThe dandiset has eight Di-8-ANEPPS cells with the nine-step escalation where Fig. 2B and 4B report n = 5, five FluoVolt cells with it (one with an extra 350 V set first) and a sixth with a finer escalation where Fig. 2A and 4A report n = 4, and five Di-8-ANEPPS cells recorded on 2025-01-22 with other steps that the paper does not describe. Which cells the paper used is not stated.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2026 (semenov-2026-03, 2026-11-02)
