Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Metadata errorIn every file the units' electrode_group labels are swapped. Units 0 to 95 are labelled PMd but their electrodes column points to electrode rows of group 1 (M1), and units 96 to 191 are labelled M1 but point to rows of group 2 (PMd), the rows the PMd LFP uses. The data agree with the electrodes column; in R the array labelled PMd has 29 silent channels and carries almost no target information (at most 6 significant channels per session in the delay window and decoding at most 0.03 above chance), though 40 of its 96 channels regress on target position when R's sessions are pooled; this is as expected of the M1 array the paper calls unusable. The conversion code assigns electrode_group by the order of a dictionary of groups.
  • File format errorIn every J file the boundary between units 0 and 1 in units/spike_times_index is misplaced. Unit 0 stops 1323 to 3120 s into sessions of 3083 to 8106 s, and unit 1 holds two to five times the median number of spikes, with spike times that run to the session's end and restart near 0.
  • Metadata errorHand, cursor, eye and decoded positions are stated in meters with conversion NaN; the values are in mm (targets at up to 120, hand reaching them at about the same values).
  • Description missingtask_id is 2 in every trial and version_id is one value per monkey, so the files do not say which target configuration a session used; it must be inferred from target_pos, which has no unit. Electrode positions are NaN and impedances -1.
  • Metadata errorThe trials column is_successful is described as "if monkey started before the mandatory delay period after target shown"; it holds the rig's trial success flag.
  • Metadata errorThe dandiset description says the files hold sorted spikes; the units are threshold crossings of each electrode (one unit per electrode, built from the rig's spike rasters).
  • Metadata errorThe archive's assetsSummary for version 0.220124.2156 gives 15 files and 187.5 GB; the version holds 12 files and 39.5 GB.
  • Data missingThe dandiset holds 5 of the 16 sessions the paper uses for monkey J. All four horizontal sessions, three of four ring sessions and two of four in each other configuration are missing; the paper's own data link returns HTTP 403.
  • OtherThe files place R's horizontal targets on 2017-01-15 and 16 and vertical targets on 2017-01-17 and 18; the Methods give these dates the other way round. Grouped by the files' geometry, R's trial counts match the Methods' counts for horizontal and vertical better (within 4% and 3%) than grouped by date (12% and 5%).
  • OtherR's horizontal targets lie at 5.2 + 10.4 k mm from the center, not 1 cm apart as stated, and R's 2017-01-17 session holds 69 successful trials to six ring targets outside its layout.
  • OtherR's delays run from 400 to 1000 ms, against 400 to 900 ms in the Methods, and R has only 26 trials without a delay (all on 2017-01-19), against 10% in the Methods; J has 19.8%.
  • Selection not documentedR's 3-rings session holds 1340 successful delayed center-out trials where the paper used 912; neither the paper nor the dandiset says how the trials were selected.
  • OtherIn R's 2017-01-19 the cursor differs from the hand by more than 1 m in over 1% of samples; in the other sessions they agree to within 3 mm in 99% of samples.
  • Paper inconsistentThe Results give J's distance errors as "3.03 cm v.s. 2.64 cm" (projected, then full) and R's as "2.78 cm v.s. 3.27 cm", while stating that projection raises the error in both; the R values are printed in the reverse order or contradict the text.
  • Methods not availableThe paper names no analysis code, does not report its SVM hyperparameters or the nulls of its p-values, and its data link returns HTTP 403.

Reproduction Structure and variability of delay activity in premotor cortex (Even-Chen et al. 2019)

Tests the findings of Even-Chen, Sheffer, Vyas, Ryu and Shenoy (2019) on dandiset 000121, which holds 12 sessions of delayed center-out reaching with Utah arrays in two monkeys (all 7 of R, 5 of the 16 of J). It checks the sessions and trial counts, the behaviour, the low-dimensional structure of PMd delay activity (PCA, the spatial plane, its time course, the speed axis), the variability of single-trial neural states on and off the reach axis, and SVM decoding of reach direction and distance. Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • J is 5 of its 16 sessions.channels-task-correlated single-trial-variance
  • The configuration is inferred from the target positions, as the files' task_id is 2 in every trial.session-configurations
  • Which trials the paper counted is not stated; three readings were tested.r-trial-counts
  • Movement onset (10% of peak speed) and maximum speed are defined here; the paper does not define them. Onsets at 5% and 20%, and onset searched only after the go cue, are shown.delays-and-times
  • Reach time is undefined in the paper; two readings were tested and (b) matches.delays-and-times
  • PMd is taken as units 96 to 191, from the data check, against the units' electrode_group labels.channels-task-correlated
  • The day's average rate is the session's rate over its recorded time; the paper does not say how it was computed. The other choice is shown.pca-variance
  • J is 5 of its 16 sessions and 96 of its 120 conditions.pca-variance
  • J's plane is fit on 96 of its 120 conditions and its horizontal task is absent.spatial-plane-variance
  • Two definitions of the share and two namings of R's horizontal and vertical tasks were tested.spatial-plane-variance
  • The thresholds, bins and smoothing are chosen here; the paper reads the times from a figure.plane-timecourse
  • The position of maximum speed comes from hand positions smoothed here (Gaussian, SD 20 ms).onoff-predicts-kinematics
  • J has only vertical sessions here, against horizontal and vertical in the paper.speed-axis-within-distance
  • R's horizontal targets lie at 5.2 + 10.4 k mm, so distances are matched by rank (1 to 12) within each task.speed-axis-within-distance
  • The paper does not say how the correlation was tested; a 10-fold cross-validated linear regression was chosen.distance-after-speed
  • The paper does not define the distance dimension or the projection; both are defined here.speed-distance-projection
  • J's ring task is 1 of 4 sessions.on-greater-than-off
  • The test is not named; three readings were tested.on-greater-than-off
  • SVM hyperparameters are sklearn's defaults; the paper's tuned values are not reported. A tuned variant is shown.svm-ring-and-distance
  • The tolerance is 10%, not the paper's SEM over folds (see the claims file).svm-ring-and-distance
  • J ring is 1 of 4 sessions; J distance is the vertical task only.svm-ring-and-distance
  • SVM hyperparameters are sklearn's defaults.svm-3rings-by-ring
  • The paper's "F test" was read as a one-way ANOVA of per-trial arc errors; a variance test (Levene) and the restricted ANOVA are shown.arc-error-distance-independent
  • The paper prints R's values in an order that contradicts its text; two readings were listed and (a) matches.distance-error-and-plane-projection
  • J's distance classification uses the vertical task only.distance-error-and-plane-projection
  • The plane is fit on all trials, including the test folds of the plane-projected classifier.distance-error-and-plane-projection
