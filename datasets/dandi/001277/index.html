Reproduction Remote focusing of electroporation by MHz pulse packets (Silkuniene et al. 2025)

Tests the findings of Silkuniene, Silkunas, Pakhomova, Semenov and Pakhomov (2025) on dandiset 001277, which holds whole-well YO-PRO-1, Hoechst and propidium images of CT26 monolayers from the paper's pulse-duration experiment (Figure 6). It measures dye uptake along the diagonals of the four-electrode array, fits the Lorentzian of the Methods per well, and compares the central peak and its width across 300, 600 and 900 ns pulses, and the YP and propidium spots at 600 ns. The packet-rate, raised-electrode, burst-number and thermometry experiments are not in the dandiset. All files

Method differences

  • The paper reports n=5 per duration; the dandiset has 7 wells at 600 ns and does not say which five were analysed. Assumed that the Fig. 6 experiments are the five plate rows that each hold one well of 300, 600 and 900 ns (P1 row A, P2 rows A and B, P3 rows A and B); plate P1 row B holds the sham well and two more 600-ns wells.   • The background is the mean of 3 ROIs in the darkest electrode footprint (Methods 2.5 says 3 ROIs within a footprint, not which one); the mean over all four footprints is shown as a variant and changes no outcome.fig6b-peak-900ns-not-significant

  • When the two s.e. bars differ in length the gap is compared with their mean (Cumming et al. 2007, cited by Methods 2.5, uses the average arm); the longer bar is shown as a variant and gives the same outcome.fig6c-normalized-decline-similar
