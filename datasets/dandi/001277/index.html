Dataset version 0.250701.1610 · tested 2026-11-01 · 7 open findings

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds only the pulse-duration experiment of Fig. 6 (18 wells of CT26 cells on three plates, 2 bursts (x4) with packets delivered with no interval). The packet-rate series and controls (Figs 3 and 4), the raised-electrode wells (Fig. 5), the burst-number series and the CHO cells (Fig. 7) and the thermometry (Fig. 2) are not in it, and the paper names no other public deposit.
    Found by the attempt on Silkuniene et al. 2025 (silkuniene-2025-03, 2026-11-01)
  • Metadata errorEvery well's subject description says "Protocol consisted of 9 packets of pulses delivered at 0.2MHz frequency", including the 300-ns and 900-ns wells and the sham well. The dandiset description and the paper (Fig. 6A legend) give 18, 9 and 6 packets for 300, 600 and 900 ns, delivered with no interval (0.4, 0.2 and 0.14 MHz).
    Found by the attempt on Silkuniene et al. 2025 (silkuniene-2025-03, 2026-11-01)
  • Selection not documentedThe dandiset has 5 wells at 300 ns, 7 at 600 ns and 5 at 900 ns, where the paper reports n=5 per duration (Fig. 6B legend). Nothing says which five 600-ns wells were analysed, and the Fig. 6D trend depends on it. With the five plate rows that hold one well of each duration, the FWHM trend is significant under 28 of 48 readings (p 0.002 to 0.004, close to the paper's 0.003, with contiguous ROIs fitted to the 15 per-well values); with all 7 it is absent (p 0.83 to 1.00). Nor does anything say whether the 600-ns wells, with their propidium images, are also the 0-mm group of Fig. 5; propidium images exist also for three 300-ns and three 900-ns wells.
    Found by the attempt on Silkuniene et al. 2025 (silkuniene-2025-03, 2026-11-01)
  • OtherTwo 600-ns wells, P1_20240926_B2 and P1_20240926_B3 (plate P1 row B, with the sham well), show YO-PRO-1 uptake over the whole electrode array (YP at 6 and 7 mm is 78% and 36% of the centre, against 3 to 11% in the other 15 exposed wells) and Lorentzian FWHMs of about 18 and 5 mm. P1_20240926_B3 also shows a loss of Hoechst-stained cells over the centre at 2 h (central to whole-image median 0.78, against 1.25 to 1.69 in the other 600-ns wells) and a propidium image without a central spot. The metadata give no reason for the difference.
    Found by the attempt on Silkuniene et al. 2025 (silkuniene-2025-03, 2026-11-01)
  • OtherThe propidium and Hoechst image of well P2_20240925_B3 is deposited twice, as sub-P2-20240925-B3_image.nwb and sub-P2-20240925-B3_obj-w6fqmq_image.nwb. The pixel data are identical (same size; checked on 1500 rows of both channels); the files differ in NWB identifier and session start time.
    Found by the attempt on Silkuniene et al. 2025 (silkuniene-2025-03, 2026-11-01)
  • Metadata errorThe OME metadata in every file give 12 significant bits per pixel, but the YP images hold values far above 4095 (the largest 25 x 25 pixel block means are 3500 to 22600), so the stored values are not raw 12-bit camera counts. The ImageSeries unit is "n.a." and no scaling is described.
    Found by the attempt on Silkuniene et al. 2025 (silkuniene-2025-03, 2026-11-01)
  • Description missingThe dandiset gives no electrode positions, ROI coordinates or measured profiles, so the paper's ROI placement cannot be repeated exactly; the footprints had to be found in the images. The plate layout (wells A1 to B3 on plates P1 to P3) is recoverable only from the subject ids.
    Found by the attempt on Silkuniene et al. 2025 (silkuniene-2025-03, 2026-11-01)
