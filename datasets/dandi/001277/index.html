  • fig6b-peak-900ns-not-significant

    The central peak of YP uptake is highest for 900-ns pulse packets, but its difference from 300- and 600-ns packets is not statistically significant.

    The central peak of YP uptake was higher for 900-ns pulses, but the difference from the other two pulse durations was not statistically significant

    Results 3.3, paragraph 2; Figure 6B

    How it will be judged

    Per experiment (well, n=5 per pulse duration, Fig. 6B legend), the background-subtracted YP intensity at the centre ROI (0 mm), as in Methods 2.5. The paper does not name the test for this comparison; the Methods allow ANOVA or two-sided t-tests with Bonferroni correction, and the matching comparison at the array centre in Results 3.1 (Fig. 4B) used t-tests with Bonferroni correction. Readings are (a) two-sided t-tests of 900 ns against 300 ns and against 600 ns, Bonferroni over the three pairwise comparisons, and (b) a one-way ANOVA over the three durations, each at p < 0.05. The claim of absence is reproduced when the test finds no significant difference and the 900-ns mean is the highest; not reproduced when the test finds a difference. If (a) and (b) disagree the claim is blocked as methods_underspecified. The centre value from the Lorentzian fit (parameter a) is shown as a variant. Effect sizes are reported.
