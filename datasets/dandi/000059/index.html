  • File format errorNewMeasured MS temperature traces hold artefacts far outside any brain temperature, with no description of them: raw values up to 1354 C (MS21-180625), 1276 C (MS22-180720) and 801 C (MS21-180803) and down to -1751 C (MS22-180712) and -257 C (MS22-180623); 11 of the 31 measured traces hold values outside 5 to 45 C, and three MS22 traces hold values near 5 and 10 C outside the trials.
    Found by the attempt on Petersen et al. 2020 (petersen-2020-02, 2026-11-15)
  • Metadata errorPartly fixedThe 2018-07-19 sessions of MS21 and MS22 are labelled as cooled (cooling intervals and Cooling trials), but their measured MS temperature does not fall below 37.4 and 35.2 C. In MS22-180719 theta frequency still falls during the labelled cooling (8.68 to 8.27 Hz), in MS21-180719 it rises (7.53 to 8.13 Hz).
    Found by the attempt on Petersen et al. 2020 (petersen-2020-02, 2026-11-15)
  • Data missingPartly fixedTwenty sessions have no measured MS temperature, only the lab's modelled curve (all MS10 sessions, 7 of 9 MS12 sessions, 2 of 5 MS13, MS21-180627 and MS22-180628), so 31 of 51 sessions support temperature analyses and MS10 none.
    Found by the attempt on Petersen et al. 2020 (petersen-2020-02, 2026-11-15)
  • Data missingPartly fixedThe hippocampal temperature of the paper's control experiment (Figure S1H to K), unit waveforms, and the paper's place fields are not in the dandiset. 23 of the 51 sessions hold no curated units, and 5 hold units without a cell type.
    Found by the attempt on Petersen et al. 2020 (petersen-2020-02, 2026-11-15)
  • Metadata errorNewThirteen sessions have two cooling epochs; in MS12-170717 and MS12-170718 the second cooling interval ends at 25683 s and 28409 s, long after the last trial, and four sessions have a no-cooling interval that ends before it starts (left out by the conversion, as its description says).
    Found by the attempt on Petersen et al. 2020 (petersen-2020-02, 2026-11-15)
  • Metadata errorNewThe lab's theta channel of MS13-171130 (channel 82) is marked bad in the electrodes table. The epochs table names the environment Linear_Track_Peter for theta maze epochs (paradigm ThetaMaze_AlternativeRunning) in 2 sessions, such as MS21-180629.
    Found by the attempt on Petersen et al. 2020 (petersen-2020-02, 2026-11-15)
  • Data missingPartly fixedfound on promptFive of the paper's 53 behavioural sessions are not in the dandiset (MS12-170712, MS13-171204, MS13-171205, MS13-171206 and MS21-180806). The release notes of version 0.261115.0311 name the first four as not in the dandiset and do not mention MS21-180806.
    Found by the attempt on Petersen et al. 2020 (petersen-2020-02, 2026-11-15)
  • OtherStill presentfound on promptIn MS21-180621 and MS22-180621 the good units carry no more spatial information than with their spikes shifted by 60 s (median 0.170 and 0.119 bits/spike, against 0.214 and 0.152 shifted), while in MS21-180625 and MS22-180629 the information falls from 0.842 and 1.037 to 0.061 and 0.083 bits/spike with the shift. The clock of spikes and position could not be established in these two sessions.
    Found by the attempt on Petersen et al. 2020 (petersen-2020-02, 2026-11-15)

The 2018-07-19 files say that the septum does not appear to have been cooled, and MS10-170307 has Pre, Cooling and Post trials like the analysed sessions.
    Found by the attempt on Petersen et al. 2020 (petersen-2020-02, 2026-11-15)
