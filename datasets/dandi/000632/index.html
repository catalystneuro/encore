    At 1 Hz, co-directional and cross-directional (90 degree) paired pulses in the square array have the same electroporation efficiency, measured as YP uptake at the array center.

    Results, 2.2, paragraph 1; Figure 3

    Under background reading (b) the co-directional dishes at 1 Hz (mean 665, n = 5) average 0.68 of the cross-directional ones (972, n = 6); two-tailed Student's t-test p = 0.32, so the test does not find a difference. Reading (a) gives ratio 0.75, p = 0.31; Welch's test and the variants (whole-disc footprint background, YP over MemBrite, center ROIs of 150 and 250 um, without dishes whose footprint centers are more than 400 um from a parallelogram, the deposit as labelled) give p from 0.28 to 0.68. By experiment day the comparison goes opposite ways, 0.45 on 2022-11-29 (p = 0.023, n = 3 and 3) and 1.43 on 2022-12-04 (p = 0.49, n = 2 and 3). The test has little power, so p >= 0.05 shows that no difference was found, not that the groups are equal. The 95% interval of the difference is -968 to 356 counts (co/cross 0.00 to 1.37), and the test would detect with 80% power only a difference of 922 counts, 95% of the cross-directional mean; at the observed difference (-306 counts, s.e. 293) its power is 16%. With the ROIs at the refined footprint centers the ratio is 0.72, p = 0.40.

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • The center ROI is placed automatically at the mean of the four electrode footprint centers, located by template matching on a reduced YP image (epimg.find_footprints); the paper placed it by hand in MetaMorph (Methods 4.4). The located footprints and ROI are shown for every dish. As a check the footprints are located again on a finer image (5.2 um blocks, epimg.refine_footprints); over the 23 distinct dishes the refined array center lies a median 55 um (6 to 193 um) from the one used, and placing the ROIs there is run as a variant with the same outcomes.
    • Assumption. The files give no pixel size. 0.65 um per pixel is assumed (the 6.5 um ORCA-Flash4 pixel behind the 10x objective named in Methods 4.4); under it the footprints measure a median 1.59 mm across against the 1.5 mm needles (Methods 4.2), and on the finer image a median 1.66 mm. If the footprints are 1.5 mm across, the true pixel is about 0.59 to 0.61 um and the 308-pixel center ROI covers about 180 to 190 um, smaller than the paper's 200 um. The 0.5 mm lumen cannot be seen in the images (YP inside it is 0.97 to 1.13 times the YP around it), so it gives no second check. Center ROIs of 150 and 250 um sides are run as variants and give the same outcomes.
    • Background reading (b) subtracts the mean YP of a 200 x 200 um ROI at the center of each of the four footprints. Methods 4.4 says only that measurements were made within the footprints 'to account for spontaneous YP uptake'; the ROI size is the paper's, its place a stated choice, and a variant over the whole disc inside 0.6 of the footprint radius gives the same outcome.
    • Assumption. The dataset holds 23 distinct dishes, not 24. The file labelled co-directional 1 Hz, sub-BPAE-8unipolar-1Hz-04, is a copy of the cross-directional 1 Hz file sub-BPAE-8bipolar-1Hz-600ns-04 and is counted once, as cross-directional (its start time is in the 2022-11-29 series, where number 04 is otherwise missing, and its anisotropy index is in the cross-directional range). Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • The center ROI is placed automatically at the mean of the four electrode footprint centers, located by template matching on a reduced YP image (epimg.find_footprints); the paper placed it by hand in MetaMorph (Methods 4.4). The located footprints and ROI are shown for every dish. As a check the footprints are located again on a finer image (5.2 um blocks, epimg.refine_footprints); over the 23 distinct dishes the refined array center lies a median 55 um (6 to 193 um) from the one used, and placing the ROIs there is run as a variant with the same outcomes.fig3-1hz-co-equals-cross fig3-770khz-co-enhanced fig3-770khz-cross-same-as-1hz fig3-770khz-co-above-cross fig3-no-area-dependence
  • Assumption. The files give no pixel size. 0.65 um per pixel is assumed (the 6.5 um ORCA-Flash4 pixel behind the 10x objective named in Methods 4.4); under it the footprints measure a median 1.59 mm across against the 1.5 mm needles (Methods 4.2), and on the finer image a median 1.66 mm. If the footprints are 1.5 mm across, the true pixel is about 0.59 to 0.61 um and the 308-pixel center ROI covers about 180 to 190 um, smaller than the paper's 200 um. The 0.5 mm lumen cannot be seen in the images (YP inside it is 0.97 to 1.13 times the YP around it), so it gives no second check. Center ROIs of 150 and 250 um sides are run as variants and give the same outcomes.fig3-1hz-co-equals-cross fig3-770khz-co-enhanced fig3-770khz-cross-same-as-1hz fig3-770khz-co-above-cross fig3-no-area-dependence
  • Background reading (b) subtracts the mean YP of a 200 x 200 um ROI at the center of each of the four footprints. Methods 4.4 says only that measurements were made within the footprints 'to account for spontaneous YP uptake'; the ROI size is the paper's, its place a stated choice, and a variant over the whole disc inside 0.6 of the footprint radius gives the same outcome.fig3-1hz-co-equals-cross fig3-770khz-co-enhanced fig3-770khz-cross-same-as-1hz fig3-770khz-co-above-cross fig3-no-area-dependence
