Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds the 12 Neuropixels sessions only. The 2p imaging (V1L2/3, V1L4, mesoscope) and 2p holographic optogenetics recordings behind Figures 1h to 1k, 2b, 2c, 3e and 4 to 6 and several Extended Data figures are not in it, and the paper names no public archive for them; 21 of the paper's findings cannot be tested.
  • Metadata errorThe units tables hold units whose quality is labelled noise (539 of 3,026 units in sub-633229, 322 of the V1 units across sessions), while the Methods state that all units not classified as noise were packaged into the NWB files.
  • Paper inconsistentThe Methods' Animals list 14 Neuropixels mice (Sst-IRES-Cre;Ai32 n = 10, PV-IRES-Cre;Ai32 n = 4); the files hold 12 sessions from 12 mice (9 Sst, 3 Pvalb), as the Results and legends state.
  • Metadata errorThe eye tracking pupil position (pupil_tracking/data) is stated in meters; its values (pupil center near 300 to 400, pupil half-width near 20) are camera pixels, and no conversion to visual degrees is given, so the Methods' 8 degree fixed-gaze criterion cannot be applied from the files.
  • Paper inconsistentThe Methods say eye video was acquired at 30 Hz; the eye tracking timestamps in the files are 16.7 ms apart (60 Hz), which is what the paper's code assumes.
  • Description missingThe IC block tables give each image only as a frame index (0 to 21) with no image names; the names come from the paper's code, and the image templates stored in the files allow them to be checked by eye.
  • OtherThe last eye tracking timestamp of each session with eye tracking is NaN.
  • Methods not availableFor the authors. The Methods do not say how the SVM hyperparameters were set; the code calls fitcecoc with OptimizeHyperparameters auto. Which coding, box constraint and kernel scale were chosen per session and area? The significance of PM and AM in Fig. 3d depends on it here (four areas significant with C chosen by inner cross-validation, five with C = 1), and with C chosen by inner cross-validation it also depends on the cross-validation partition (five areas with each of two further seeds, PM significant with one and AM with the other).
  • Methods not availableFor the authors. Extended Data Fig. 2 counts sessions whose fixed-gaze RF trials left at least one trial in each position. Do inverse-patch trials count towards that coverage? Counting them gives the paper's 9 sessions; counting standard trials only gives 8.
  • Methods not availableFor the authors. Extended Data Fig. 6b counts 87 V1 IC-encoders and 636 segment responders. Are these all units in the NWB files, including those whose quality column says noise? All units give exactly 87 and 636; dropping the units labelled noise gives 79 and 566.

Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • Assumption. Reading (b), "all units not classified as noise", is taken as every unit in the files, because the Methods say "all units not classified as noise were packaged into Neurodata Without Borders" files. The exact match of both counts under (b1) supports the assumption. Asked of the authors under findings (methods_unavailable).ed6b-npx-v1-group-counts
  • The 8 degree threshold of the Methods cannot be applied, as the files give pupil position in camera pixels with no conversion; the code's 20 pixels (criterion reading b) is used.ed2b-npx-fixed-gaze
  • Assumption. The criterion and the Extended Data Fig. 2 legend require that fixed-gaze RF trials cover all 9 positions ("at least one trial in each position") without saying whether inverse trials count. Rule (r), any RF trial, is adopted, as neither text restricts the trials to standard patches; it gives the paper's 9 sessions, where standard trials only give 8. A valid session with no fixed-gaze standard trial at a position gets p = 1 there. Asked of the authors under findings (methods_unavailable).ed2b-npx-fixed-gaze
  • The shuffle 95th percentile of 1,000 permutations is the criterion's standard choice for the null of the match.ed2b-npx-fixed-gaze
  • The paper names no test for the absence of systematic differences; the criterion's Friedman test is a reader's choice.ed2a-npx-gaze-confined
  • The per-pair normalisation by the square root of the spike counts is left out; it does not change the comparison of a pair's peak with its own flank.fig2d-npx-ccg-hva-input
