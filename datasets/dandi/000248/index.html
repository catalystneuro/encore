Dataset version 0.240502.2344 · tested 2026-10-28 to 2026-11-21 · 8 open findings

  • Description missingThe ICwcfg1 presentations table lists every 400 ms frame of the block, including the gray inter-trial frames, under one column frame. The gray frames show the same image as the Blank stimulus (template 0), and nothing marks which of them are Blank trials. The image codes 0 to 21 have no names in the files or the dandiset metadata; the names come only from the paper's code. The trials can be recovered from the sequence (60 leading gray frames, then trial and gray frame alternating, 400 Blank trials in every session), and the templates agree in kind with the code's names.#
    Found by the attempt on Kim et al. 2025 (kim-2025-01, 2026-11-21)
  • Description missingThe units table describes none of its columns (quality, isi_violations, firing_rate and the others read "no description").#
    Found by the attempt on Kim et al. 2025 (kim-2025-01, 2026-11-21)
  • OtherThe spikes share the clock of the ICwcfg1 table. The V1 population rate rises 75 ms after image onsets and not after Blank onsets, and the image identity is decoded from the 400 ms after onset (0.576, chance 0.083) and not from the 400 ms before (0.089). No session is deposited twice. All 11 sessions the paper's code lists are present, with a twelfth (sub-619293) that it does not list.#
    Found by the attempt on Kim et al. 2025 (kim-2025-01, 2026-11-21)
  • Data missingThe dandiset holds the 12 Neuropixels sessions only. The 2p imaging (V1L2/3, V1L4, mesoscope) and 2p holographic optogenetics recordings behind Figures 1h to 1k, 2b, 2c, 3e and 4 to 6 and several Extended Data figures are not in it, and the paper names no public archive for them; 21 of the paper's findings cannot be tested.#
    Found by the attempt on Shin et al. 2025 (shin-2025-01, 2026-10-28)
  • Metadata errorThe units tables hold units whose quality is labelled noise (539 of 3,026 units in sub-633229, 322 of the V1 units across sessions), while the Methods state that all units not classified as noise were packaged into the NWB files.#
    Found by the attempt on Shin et al. 2025 (shin-2025-01, 2026-10-28)
  • Metadata errorThe eye tracking pupil position (pupil_tracking/data) is stated in meters; its values (pupil center near 300 to 400, pupil half-width near 20) are camera pixels, and no conversion to visual degrees is given, so the Methods' 8 degree fixed-gaze criterion cannot be applied from the files.#
    Found by the attempt on Shin et al. 2025 (shin-2025-01, 2026-10-28)
  • Description missingThe IC block tables give each image only as a frame index (0 to 21) with no image names; the names come from the paper's code, and the image templates stored in the files allow them to be checked by eye.#
    Found by the attempt on Shin et al. 2025 (shin-2025-01, 2026-10-28)
  • OtherThe last eye tracking timestamp of each session with eye tracking is NaN.#
    Found by the attempt on Shin et al. 2025 (shin-2025-01, 2026-10-28)
