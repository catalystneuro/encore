Findings about the data 15

What the attempts that used this dataset found about the data themselves, for its owner and the archive. The claims and their outcomes are on each paper’s page.

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds 966 of the paper's 1214 ALM units (47 of 57 sessions), 789 of 790 thalamic units (72 of 73 sessions) and 181 of 227 SNr units (18 of 23 sessions; apparently only the putative GABAergic units). Behaviour-only sessions, the muscimol experiments, the weak (0.5 mW) thalamus photoinhibition of Fig. 5, non-behaving whole-cell recordings with current injection, M1 whole-cell recordings, Olig3-cre recordings and the histology are not deposited.
    Found by the attempt on Guo et al. 2017 (guo-2017-01, 2026-10-20)
  • Data missingSpikes are stored only from each trial's start to about 4.8 s after pole onset, although trials last about 9 s, and not between trials. The end is fixed to pole onset, not to the go cue, so in trials with delays above about 2 s the response epoch is not stored; spike rates over whole trials or sessions cannot be computed.
    Found by the attempt on Guo et al. 2017 (guo-2017-01, 2026-10-20)
  • Description missingMost units hold spikes over only part of their session (median 70% of its trials; 1358 of 1936 units below 90%), and the Units table gives no observation interval, so the trials in which a unit was recorded must be inferred from its first and last spikes.
    Found by the attempt on Guo et al. 2017 (guo-2017-01, 2026-10-20)
  • File format errorThe mean waveforms are cut short (29 to 38 samples at 25 kHz, trough at sample 15 to 19), so trough-to-peak widths above about 0.5 ms cannot be measured; in 78% of ALM units labelled 'Wide width spiking' the peak is the last sample.
    Found by the attempt on Guo et al. 2017 (guo-2017-01, 2026-10-20)
  • Selection not documentedThe Units `cell_type` labels ('Wide width spiking', 'Narrow width spiking', 'Cannot be determined') have no stated rule; they cannot be derived from the deposited waveforms but give class sizes close to the paper's.
    Found by the attempt on Guo et al. 2017 (guo-2017-01, 2026-10-20)
  • Description missingThe whole-cell trial tables leave every photostimulus column empty, and `stim_present` marks the photostimulus trials without description. The laser time is held only in an OptogeneticSeries (stimulus/presentation/photostim_on_*) whose description is 'no description'. It starts between the two delay onsets the trial times allow, 0.10 to 0.17 s after 1.3 s past pole_in_time and 0.03 to 0.10 s before 0.1 s past pole_out_time (1.40 s after pole onset in mouse anm300476, 1.47 s in the others), and it is on in one trial not marked by stim_present (cell_21_1).
    Found by the attempt on Guo et al. 2017 (guo-2017-01, 2026-10-20)
  • Metadata errorIn the whole-cell file of cell_21_1 (anm300476) the trial start and stop times are shifted by one row against the pole and cue times (85 rows), and the table is not in time order.
    Found by the attempt on Guo et al. 2017 (guo-2017-01, 2026-10-20)
  • Metadata errorThe whole-cell Vm series state unit 'mV' with conversion 0.001, but the values are already in mV; applying the conversion gives values a thousand times too small.
    Found by the attempt on Guo et al. 2017 (guo-2017-01, 2026-10-20)
  • Metadata errorEvery electrode group, in thalamus and SNr as well as ALM, carries the ALM coordinates (AP 2.5, ML -1.5, DV 0); the SNr units of anm00261336 are placed at AP -1.56 mm, outside SNr; photostimulus sites give ML negative for the left hemisphere while units give it positive.
    Found by the attempt on Guo et al. 2017 (guo-2017-01, 2026-10-20)
  • Description missingThe Units column unit_y is described only as "y-coordinate of this unit (mm)", with no reference, so whether it is depth below the pia is not stated; session_start_time is the date of conversion (all sessions on 2017-06-27), not of recording, and no sorter is named, so the ALM sessions missing from the dandiset cannot be identified.
    Found by the attempt on Guo et al. 2017 (guo-2017-01, 2026-10-20)
  • Description missingThalamic units have no nucleus label (brain_subregion 'N/A'), so VM/VAL units can be selected only by distance from a centre the paper does not give in coordinates.
    Found by the attempt on Guo et al. 2017 (guo-2017-01, 2026-10-20)
  • Metadata errorThe 9 Gad2 thalamic sessions give the genotype 'Ai35D x GAD2_Cre' (an Arch line), where the paper used Gad2-IRES-Cre with ChR2 injected in TRN, and mark no photostimulus trial, though their 84 units match the optrode units of Extended Data Fig. 2c.
    Found by the attempt on Guo et al. 2017 (guo-2017-01, 2026-10-20)
  • Metadata errorThe VGAT-ChR2-EYFP thalamic sessions give the photostimulus power as 2.23 mW where the paper gives 10 mW (Extended Data Fig. 2d); the thalamic fibre site is one nominal coordinate in every session.
    Found by the attempt on Guo et al. 2017 (guo-2017-01, 2026-10-20)
  • Description missingPhotostimulation at galvo position (0.5, 0.5) mm in mouse anm00264943, interleaved with ALM photoinhibition, is not named; trials labelled 'delay' with power 0 are not explained; the trial columns photo_stim_period, photo_stim_type and is_good have no description.
    Found by the attempt on Guo et al. 2017 (guo-2017-01, 2026-10-20)
  • OtherThe dandiset holds data the paper does not describe, 21 ALM sessions of PV-Cre x Ai32 mice with thalamic photostimulation at 10, 22.5 and 45 mW and 12 thalamic sessions of C57BL6 mice without photostimulation.
    Found by the attempt on Guo et al. 2017 (guo-2017-01, 2026-10-20)
