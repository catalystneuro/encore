Dataset version 0.260825.1909 · tested 2026-11-10 · 7 open findings

  • Selection not documentedThe dandiset holds only the sessions that pass the paper's inclusion criterion (it was converted from the lab's A_Structs_Final), which neither its description nor its files say. The sessions that fail it, which the paper's learning analyses (Fig. 4) and pre-cue analyses (Fig. 5) use, are not in it.
    Found by the attempt on Mah et al. 2023 (mah-2023-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Description missingNothing marks the sessions run on the pre-initiation cue task. The 16 rats of Fig. 5 are among the 291, their pre-cue sessions are included, and all sessions have protocol RWTautowait2. The switch dates in the paper's Figure5.m mark them for 14 of the 16 rats, as initiation times depend on the current offer only after the date.
    Found by the attempt on Mah et al. 2023 (mah-2023-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Metadata errorOne session is deposited twice, sub-L011_ses-RWTautowait2-20221004-095612.nwb and the same name with a "-x" suffix, with identical trial data.
    Found by the attempt on Mah et al. 2023 (mah-2023-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Description missingThe processed trial table has no catch probability. The conversion maps the A struct field under the misspelled name prob_cacth, so prob_catch is dropped; the value is only in the raw trial table (catch_percentage), which has the same rows.
    Found by the attempt on Mah et al. 2023 (mah-2023-01, 2026-11-10)
  • OtherThe raw trial table's is_catch differs from the processed is_catch on 176,009 trials; the processed flag is reward_delay == 100, as the lab's parser defines it, and the raw flag is set on trials with ordinary delays. Neither description says which to use.
    Found by the attempt on Mah et al. 2023 (mah-2023-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Description missingThe subject age is empty in 21,853 of 23,034 files and is the range P6M/P24M in the others, so no rat's age is given.
    Found by the attempt on Mah et al. 2023 (mah-2023-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Other360 sessions of 8 rats have catch probability 0.3, which is not among the five cohorts the paper lists (0.1, 0.15, 0.2, 0.25, 0.35).
    Found by the attempt on Mah et al. 2023 (mah-2023-01, 2026-11-10)
