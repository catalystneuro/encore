Reproduction Human single neurons during a recognition memory task (Chandravadia et al. 2020)

Tests the data descriptor of Chandravadia et al. Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • The exact count differs from the paper's by one unit; the tolerance is the protocol's 10%.counts-neurons-subjects
  • The criterion did not give a number for most files in a partial result; 78 of 87 was taken as most.ttl-codes
  • The tolerance of the mean is the stated spread (0.10), as the protocol prescribes; the spread itself was held to 10%.fig5c-auc-all
  • The paper names no measure or test for asymmetry; the z-ROC slope of the paper's code and a t-test against 1 were fixed in the criterion.fig5b-roc-asymmetric
  • Sessions whose interior z-ROC points do not allow a fit are left out of the mean and the tests, after the second verification round; all 87 sessions are a variant.fig5b-roc-asymmetric
  • Three sessions without responses in one confidence group are left out; the paper says all sessions.fig5d-confidence-accuracy
  • The main result uses the Methods' 1 s window; the paper's code uses 1.5 s, shown as a variant.vs-cells-proportion
