Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Metadata errorEvery photometry series states a sampling rate of 9 Hz (starting_time rate 8.999 to 9.0). Each series has as many samples as the behavior series, which steps at 55.6 ms (18 Hz), and the Methods and code use 18 Hz; at the stated rate the recordings would last twice as long as their timestamps.
  • Description missingNo field in the files states which experiment (spontaneous, unpredicted reward, Pavlovian learning or extinction, control, stimulation) a session belongs to, or which Pavlovian phase. Cohorts can be told only from subject names and indicators, and phases only by counting rewarded light cues (1.0, 0.8 or 0.2). The paper's Zenodo record has a table of this mapping outside the dandiset.
  • Data missingThe Pavlovian files have light cue events but no tone cue events, although each session has 30 tone cues (the 68 rewards of a learning session are 30 light, 30 tone and 8 uncued). Tone trials cannot be analysed.
  • Data missingSession sub-UG27_ses-240313r lacks the high-pass, artifact-removed green series that all other UG sessions hold, so it cannot enter the analyses that use it.
  • Metadata errorThe device description of ExcitationSource470 describes 470 nm and 405 nm (isosbestic) light alternated at 100 Hz, and that of ExcitationSource570 reads "405 nm"; the Methods alternate 470 and 570 nm at 36 Hz. The mutant sensor is named ACh4.3mut in the files where the Methods and Key Resources Table name ACh3.0-mut. The session description gives 9 uL rewards at 5 to 30 s, where the Methods give 5 uL at 10 to 30 s, and stores the micro sign mis-encoded.
  • Description missingThe code keeps only sites with signal in both channels (a `sig` flag per site) for the transient analyses; the flags are not in the files. All 174 included sites gave the paper's count, so this did not change the result here.
  • Published code disagreesIn fig4_pav.m (commit 833fa3d) the loop over a phase's last three sessions loads exp_dirs{d} in place of task_dirs{d}, so it reads the mouse's first three recordings for every phase, and the extinction correlation is called with polarity 1 (the DA peak) although the legend and Methods describe the DA trough. The notebook follows the evident intent and the Methods.
  • Published code disagreesThe code departs from the Methods in several parameters; triggered-average significance uses the 0.5th and 99.5th percentiles (Methods 2.5th and 97.5th), the minimum locomotion bout is 2.2 s (Methods 2.27 s), paired-transient magnitude correlations are Pearson r over all pairs (Methods a 100-pair bootstrap), and the stimulation cohort's exclusion of reward and stimulation periods combines the three conditions with AND, so a sample is dropped only when all three hold. hotspot_comparison.m places the second hotspot's random volumes with the first grid's dimensions.
  • Paper inconsistentFor extinction, the Results give 41 of 74 sites and the Figure 4g legend 48 sites in 5 mice, while the Methods give 3 Pavlovian mice and the files hold 75 sites in 3 mice. For learned cues the Results give 39 of 75 negative and 26 of 75 positive sites, which sum to 65.
  • Paper inconsistentIn the Figure 8 source data, the Figure 8b DLS group holds 6 slice labels from 4 mouse labels (slice_B_4 holds only stimulus 5) where the legend states 5 slices from 5 mice.
  • OtherThe ex vivo slice data of Figure 8 are not in the dandiset; their source data are in the paper's Zenodo record (doi:10.5281/zenodo.18202873).
