Dataset version 0.260928.2230 · tested 2026-11-06 · 9 open findings

  • Data missingThe published version 0.260928.2230 does not mark which units are the paper's LC-NE neurons or which are cortex-projecting, and holds no per-unit stable recording windows; the Neuropixels files hold all 14,126 sorted units. The draft (modified 2026-10-06) adds opto_NE_paper, antidromic_tier_1, antidromic_tier_2, antidromic_jitter, antidromic_latency, p_antidromic, p_collision, ephys_cut_start, ephys_cut_end and firing_rate_stability to the units tables; publishing it would make the selection and the cortex-projecting claims testable.
    Found by the attempt on Su et al. 2026 (su-2026-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Selection not documentedReconstructing the selection from the code's criteria files gives 252 units from 21 mice and 112 sessions, against 241 units and 19 mice; the code's model-based session criteria (aN, bias), its drift cuts and its 0.2 ISI bound for cortex-projecting units need data the published version lacks. The draft's opto_NE_paper marks 267 units and agrees with this selection under the same criteria on 223.
    Found by the attempt on Su et al. 2026 (su-2026-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Data missingThe per-trial spike amplitudes used as a drift regressor in every spike-rate regression, and the Q-learning model's per-trial variables (chosen value, RPE) and parameters, are not in the dandiset.
    Found by the attempt on Su et al. 2026 (su-2026-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Data missingThe anatomy, transcriptomics, MERFISH, retrograde tracing and patch-seq data of Figures 1 to 3 and S1 to S11 are not in the dandiset, which its title and description match; the paper points to a Code Ocean collection without a DOI or other pinned identifier.
    Found by the attempt on Su et al. 2026 (su-2026-01, 2026-11-06)
  • File format errorNumeric unit columns are stored as strings in some files, isi_violations_ratio in 4 files and peak_to_valley in 54 (for example sub-751004 session 2024-12-23).
    Found by the attempt on Su et al. 2026 (su-2026-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Description missingTetrode units lack the quality columns of the Neuropixels files (isi_violations_ratio among them, which the selection needs; it is recomputed here from the spike times, matching the Neuropixels column at r = 0.9993), and have no CCF coordinates. The pupil_diameter series states no unit.
    Found by the attempt on Su et al. 2026 (su-2026-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Metadata errorThe description of acquisition/right_lick_time reads "The time of left licks".
    Found by the attempt on Su et al. 2026 (su-2026-01, 2026-11-06)
  • OtherTwo files of subject 763360 (kind ecephys) have no trial table, so they cannot be used in task analyses; the archive counts 42 subjects of which 41 have task data.
    Found by the attempt on Su et al. 2026 (su-2026-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Data missingThe antidromic stimulation tables the code reads to identify cortex-projecting neurons ({session}_opto_session.csv) and the pupil edge confidence are not in the dandiset, and the trial table's ITI_duration column does not hold the generated inter-trial intervals the Methods describe (it is 0.5 s in every photometry trial).
    Found by the attempt on Su et al. 2026 (su-2026-01, 2026-11-06)
