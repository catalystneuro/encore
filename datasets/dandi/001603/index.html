Dataset version 0.260923.0623 · tested 2026-11-17 · 4 open findings

  • Data missingThe baseline sorted recording of murine organoid MO12 is not in the dandiset (its gabazine and NBQX with R-CPP sorted files are), and no longitudinal murine organoid recording (Supplementary Fig. 2) is in it; every MO file has age P45D.#
    Found by the attempt on Van Der Molen et al. 2025 (van-der-molen-2025-01, 2026-11-17)
  • Identifier missingUnits have positions but no identifier linking them across recordings of one organoid (0 h and 4 h, Supplementary Fig. 8; drug conditions, Extended Data Fig. 4). The ordered unit positions are identical for HO2 at 0 h and 4 h (90 units), for HO3 once the 4 h file's unit 24 is left out (116 units), and for MO10, MO11 and MO14 between baseline and gabazine, which is consistent with joint sorting, but not for MO13, nor between the NBQX with R-CPP recording and any other condition of an organoid (printed with ed4c-gabazine-participation). Positions alone are not unique (61 distinct positions for 90 HO2 units).#
    Found by the attempt on Van Der Molen et al. 2025 (van-der-molen-2025-01, 2026-11-17)
  • OtherThe HO2 and HO3 recordings at 6, 7, 8 months and 8 months plus 4 h are each deposited twice under different session names, and the 7-month ones a third time as HO2_sorted.mat and HO3_sorted.mat, with identical spike matrices.#
    Found by the attempt on Van Der Molen et al. 2025 (van-der-molen-2025-01, 2026-11-17)
  • Metadata errorThe dandiset description says only spike-sorted data are included for the sourced human organoids, but raw ecephys files are present for HO1 to HO4 (sixteen files). The slice files say "Sorting/curation not specified".#
    Found by the attempt on Van Der Molen et al. 2025 (van-der-molen-2025-01, 2026-11-17)
