Dataset version 0.260915.2309 · tested 2026-11-10 · 11 open findings

  • Metadata errorThe trial-segmented traces under processing/Ophys/dFoF_* are named and typed as Delta F/F (DfOverF), but their values are the normalised inferred spiking activity the paper analyses: sparse, 90 percent of values below 1e-6, each neuron's maximum 1.
    Found by the attempt on Najafi et al. 2020 (najafi-2020-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Data missingNo fluorescence trace (Delta F/F or CNMF denoised) is in the files, so the paper's analysis of raw calcium traces (Figure S5) cannot be repeated; the raw movies and the histology are not in the dandiset either.
    Found by the attempt on Najafi et al. 2020 (najafi-2020-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Metadata errorThe starting_time of every trial series carries an attribute named rate whose value, 0.0324, is the frame interval in seconds, not a rate; the imaging plane states 30 Hz.
    Found by the attempt on Najafi et al. 2020 (najafi-2020-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Data missingTrial times are relative to each trial's own start (start_time -0.48 to 0.21 s) and the files hold no session clock, so inter-trial intervals, which the paper's behavioural model uses, cannot be computed.
    Found by the attempt on Najafi et al. 2020 (najafi-2020-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Description missingThe lick trace (firstSideTryAl_lick) is 0 or 1 per ms with no spout identity, and no field gives the side of the choice; the side must be inferred from trial_type and the response, and centre-spout licks cannot be separated.
    Found by the attempt on Najafi et al. 2020 (najafi-2020-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Description missingThe NWB session_id is the timestamp of the CNMF run (for example 161227154657), not the recording; the recording date and file number are only in session_description and session_start_time.
    Found by the attempt on Najafi et al. 2020 (najafi-2020-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Othertrial_pulse_rate is an integer in some files and a non-integer rate (for example 5.335 Hz) in others, with no description of the difference.
    Found by the attempt on Najafi et al. 2020 (najafi-2020-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Description missingThe ROI table columns fitness, roi2surr_sig and offsets_ch1_pix have empty descriptions, and roi2surr_sig and offsets_ch1_pix hold the minimum int64 as a fill value.
    Found by the attempt on Najafi et al. 2020 (najafi-2020-01, 2026-11-10)
  • OtherThe dandiset metadata lists no species and only a behavioural measurement technique, and its related publication in the files is the bioRxiv preprint (10.1101/354340), not the Neuron paper.
    Found by the attempt on Najafi et al. 2020 (najafi-2020-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Description missingThe trial table has no column for helped trials or for the first trials of a session, and trial_is_good is true exactly for a correct or incorrect response, so the paper's code's trial exclusions (setTrs2rmv.m) cannot be repeated.
    Found by the attempt on Najafi et al. 2020 (najafi-2020-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Description missing16 Hz trials are rewarded on a random side, and their trial_type is always High-rate, so the side the mouse licked on these trials cannot be recovered from the files.
    Found by the attempt on Najafi et al. 2020 (najafi-2020-01, 2026-11-10)
