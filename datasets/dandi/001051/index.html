Dataset version 0.260922.1827 · tested 2026-11-01 · 6 open findings

  • Metadata errorIn 6 of the 87 sessions the paper analysed (1176989662, 1178460518, 1178693650, 1179911454, 1180266229, 1181096406) the units under each unit id differ from the paper's units table at the cited code commit (max_drift and firing_rate differ for 55 to 99.8% of units, quality for 14 to 38%, and receptive-field p values recomputed from the files are uncorrelated with the table's; the files' spike counts fit the files' firing rates but not the table's). The dandiset seems to hold a different spike sorting or unit assignment for these sessions than the one the paper used.
    Found by the attempt on Bennett et al. 2024 (bennett-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Data missingIn 5 of those sessions (1176989662, 1178693650, 1179911454, 1180266229, 1181096406) the paper's units table has 328 units, 133 of them in CP, that the session files do not hold. Each session's are on one probe of the table (probe_id 88, 46, 121, 133 and 139, with 38 to 114 of these units) whose probe_id is not among the file's and on which the file has no unit; none of their peak channels is a peak channel of a file's unit.
    Found by the attempt on Bennett et al. 2024 (bennett-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Selection not documentedThe 87 sessions passing ephys QC are listed only in the paper's code; the files and the dandiset metadata do not mark them, and the 12 other sessions are not explained. In the 87 sessions the files also hold 40 probe recordings (2004 units, 1873 on unannotated channels) that the paper's tables leave out, and nothing marks them.
    Found by the attempt on Bennett et al. 2024 (bennett-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Description missingNo file records the implant hole or probe orientation of an insertion, which probes were sharpened, which sessions had probes retracted 100 um, Dura-Gel use, or failed insertion attempts, so Figures 4B (trajectories), S4B, S4D to S4H and S3D cannot be tested.
    Found by the attempt on Bennett et al. 2024 (bennett-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Data missingThe dandiset holds none of the Figure 3 material (surgery records, ISI images, histology, MRI, training histories), the dual-hemisphere sessions of Figure 5, or the VGAT-ChR2 and Vglut2-Cre mice of Figure 6; its 27 mice are all single-hemisphere change-detection mice.
    Found by the attempt on Bennett et al. 2024 (bennett-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Data missingSessions 1187475832 and 1202438738 have no eye tracking. In the others the running and pupil streams follow the stimulus and trial clock (running slows after hits from a median 0.225 s after the change, the pupil constricts after white flashes from a median 0.35 s), but in 5 sessions for running and 3 for the pupil the contrasts are too weak to show it, and 2 sessions (1173189336, 1230732154) have no running contrast, as they have no hit or no miss trials; in all, alignment of at least one stream is not shown in 12 of the 99 sessions.
    Found by the attempt on Bennett et al. 2024 (bennett-2024-01, 2026-11-01)
