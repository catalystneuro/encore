  • expert-sessions

    The expert sessions of the behavioural data come from 41 mice and number 201, with an average of 23 context blocks per session.

    Mice were trained in this task until they reached expert performance, completing an average of 23 blocks per session

    Results, Rapid behavioral adaptation to sensory stimuli in a changing context, paragraph 2; Figure 1C legend; Figure 1 figure supplement 1A and 1B

    How it will be judged

    The Methods (Behavioral task and training curriculum) define a block contrast, for each block but the first and last, as the mean of the absolute differences in whisker hit rate between the block and its two neighbours, and call a session expert when its contrasts are significantly above 0.375. The test is that of the code, the lower bound of a 95% bootstrap interval (scipy.stats.bootstrap, 10,000 resamples, defaults otherwise) of the mean contrast above 0.375 (process main_analysis/figure1_supp_analysis.py:53-187), with early-lick trials dropped. Blocks are the context epochs of the session (the Methods); the code cuts blocks of 20 non-early-lick trials (figure1_supp_analysis.py:72-85), shown as a variant. Blocks per session are counted from the context epochs. Applied to every behavioural session of the dataset, reproduced when the three counts are within tolerance; the overlap with the code's list sessions_Context_sessions_expert.yaml is reported.
