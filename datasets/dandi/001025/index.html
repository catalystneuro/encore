  • Data missingThe dandiset holds only the 21 monolayer images of Figure 7. The images of Figures 5, 6, 8 and 9 appear, from their titles and descriptions, to be in four other dandisets that cite the same paper (000686, 000875, 001027 and 001028), which the dandiset does not mention, and the single-cell time-lapse data of Figure 4 are in no dandiset that cites the paper.
    Found by the attempt on Pakhomov et al. 2023 (pakhomov-2023-03, 2026-11-01)
  • Description missingNo pixel size is given (the ImageSeries resolution attribute is 96 or 1, not a pixel size), the array (small or large) is not stated, and the electrode positions, the electrode labels e1 to e4 and the paper's ROI measurements are not included. The array can be inferred from the electrode spacing, and it matches the protocol in every file.
    Found by the attempt on Pakhomov et al. 2023 (pakhomov-2023-03, 2026-11-01)
  • Description missing7 of the 21 files name no detector, objective or channel, and the 5 large-array files hold a second channel labelled DAPI that the paper does not describe.
    Found by the attempt on Pakhomov et al. 2023 (pakhomov-2023-03, 2026-11-01)
  • Metadata errorSubject ids do not identify dishes. sub-15 and sub-25 each hold two dishes imaged on different days under different protocols (2.4 kV 1Px4 and 4.8 kV 10Px4; 5.6 kV and 3.2 kV 1Px4), so the archive counts 19 subjects for 21 dishes, while each dish was used in one experiment (Section 2.1).
    Found by the attempt on Pakhomov et al. 2023 (pakhomov-2023-03, 2026-11-01)
  • Selection not documentedFigure 7 states n = 3 for every voltage. The dandiset has 4 dishes at 2.4 kV, 3 at 3.2, 4.0, 4.8 and 5.6 kV on the small array, and for the large array only 4.8 kV (2 dishes) and 6.4 kV (3 dishes). Which 2.4 kV dish the paper left out, and the other large-array voltages, are not documented.
    Found by the attempt on Pakhomov et al. 2023 (pakhomov-2023-03, 2026-11-01)
  • Description missingThe session descriptions name the CANCAN protocol with 12.5% decrements but not the packet (Packets 1, 2 and 3 all use that decrement); Packet 2 is inferred from the dandiset's title and Figure 7.
    Found by the attempt on Pakhomov et al. 2023 (pakhomov-2023-03, 2026-11-01)
