  • maintenance-cells-mfc

    Technical Validation, Proportion of selective cells, paragraph 3

    How it will be judged

    Per Sternberg unit, all trials, the mean rate 0 to 2500 ms after maintenance onset against the rate 0 to 500 ms after fixation cross onset, by a one-sided permutation test (increase) at P < 0.05 (Methods; the Methods give the 500 ms baseline length, and the code, NWB_calcSelective_SB.m, places it at fixation onset; the fixation cross lasts 900 to 1000 ms). Non-preferred trials are read as (i) trials whose Encoding 1 image is not the preferred image (the code's intent), and (ii) trials in which the preferred image was not shown in any encoding period. 2000 permutations, as in the code's comment. The code's paired one-sided t-tests are a variant. Percent over MFC Sternberg units (dACC, pre-SMA, vmPFC; 453 by sternberg-unit-yield). Reproduced when count and percent are within tolerance under all readings; blocked as methods_underspecified when the readings disagree.
