  • OtherThe four agarose groups carry an Agarose_Percentage series, the agarose under the fly in every frame, which the other groups lack; its label changes only with the island. The 0.8 against 1.4% chambers were loaded both ways round (island 0 is 0.8% for 18 of 24 flies without sucrose and 12 of 24 with 200 mM on both sides), so the island number alone does not give the hardness, but the series does. The first version of this attempt read only the island and sucrose series, missed it, and reported the hardness as unrecorded (metadata_insufficient, major); that was wrong, and this finding replaces it.#
    Found by the attempt on Vijayan et al. 2022 (vijayan-2022-01, 2026-11-25)
  • Data missingThe paper says all fly tracking data it used are on DANDI 000212, but the dandiset lacks the movable-boundary chambers (Fig. 1D, E), the glued flies (Fig. 1F), the 115-line Gal4 screen (Fig. 7A), the Gal80 rescues (Fig. 7L to N), nine of the 13 receptor genotypes of Fig. 8A, the 200/500 mM and agarose chambers of the mutants and DDC>Kir flies (Fig. 8J, K), four of the six agarose conditions of Fig. 6B and the BDSC 84714 mutant. Some of these may have been scored by egg counts without tracking; the paper does not say. The feeding-latency videos (Figs. 7B, 8B) are not tracking data and are also absent.#
    Found by the attempt on Vijayan et al. 2022 (vijayan-2022-01, 2026-11-25)
  • Description missingThe two Dop1R2 groups are named 'Dop1R2-mutant-1' and '-2', with genotypes that name only the paper each mutant came from (Keleman et al. 2012, Deng et al. 2019). The paper's allele names (Dop1R2 attP, Dop1R2-) follow from its references and the egg counts, not from the files.#
    Found by the attempt on Vijayan et al. 2022 (vijayan-2022-01, 2026-11-25)
  • Description missingEvery file has the same placeholder session start time (2021-01-01 01:01:01 -05:00), so the files hold no recording dates and experiments cannot be ordered or matched to batches.#
    Found by the attempt on Vijayan et al. 2022 (vijayan-2022-01, 2026-11-25)
  • Other48 of 29531 intervals between successive eggs of a fly are under 10 s (12 are a single frame), against the paper's statement that eggs are at least 1 min apart; in 47 both eggs are on one island and in 34 the fly moves no faster than 0.3 mm/s between them, so they may be double annotations. They are listed in the data check and kept.#
    Found by the attempt on Vijayan et al. 2022 (vijayan-2022-01, 2026-11-25)
  • OtherThe dandiset has never been published: only the draft exists, so it has no fixed version or DOI, and an attempt on it can only record the draft's modification time (2023-06-20T00:56:15Z).#
    Found by the attempt on Vijayan et al. 2022 (vijayan-2022-01, 2026-11-25)
  • OtherEgg counts per group agree with the egg-aligned counts of the paper's legends within 2 eggs in 16 of 19 groups (for example 1863 against 1862 for CS 0/200, 533 for DDC>Kir 31 C, 831 and 795 for the same-agarose chambers). The files hold 596 eggs against 586 for Dop1R2-mutant-1, 220 against 214 for D. suzukii and 670 against 675 for the bounce chamber; the legends' counts are of egg-aligned averages that leave out eggs near the ends of a recording, and those built here hold 586, 216 and 670. The ref. 16 chambers hold 95 flies and 3978 eggs (paper 95, 3979), no recording appears twice, the islands follow the y position and the sucrose label changes only with the island, and flies speed up before an annotated egg and pause at it while shifted times show no such contrast, so the egg annotations share the position clock.#
    Found by the attempt on Vijayan et al. 2022 (vijayan-2022-01, 2026-11-25)
  • OtherTwo Dop1R2-mutant-1 recordings (sub-0-200-Dop1R2-mutant-1-fly#-11 and -12) have 6 and 4 eggs annotated in their last 19 s, 0.5 to 10.5 s apart, while the fly walks at up to 8.7 and 7.0 mm/s; they are unlikely to be eggs. They make up 7 of the 48 intervals under 10 s, and the group holds 596 eggs in all. The paper's legend count for the group, 586, is of an egg-aligned average that leaves out eggs near the ends of a video, so it does not show whether the paper counted them. They are kept; they change no outcome (fig8fg egg-count p-values 0.34 to 0.69 without them).#
    Found by the attempt on Vijayan et al. 2022 (vijayan-2022-01, 2026-11-25)
