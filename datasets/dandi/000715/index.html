What the attempts that used this dataset found about the data themselves, for its owner and the archive, under the version of the dataset each was found on.

Dataset version 0.241009.1514 · tested 2026-10-31 · 7 open findings

  • Metadata errorEvery file gives strain OH16230 (the NeuroPAL;GCaMP6s strain of the whole-brain recordings), a spinning disk confocal, a worm "in microfluidic chip", birth on 2021-01-05 and imaging on 2021-01-07. The files are the heads of the paper's ten otIs669 (OH15262) atlas worms imaged on a Zeiss LSM 880; the volumes of 7_YAaLR and 76_YAaDV are identical, voxel for voxel, to the Zenodo volumes of those otIs669 worms, and the ten identifiers match.
    Found by the attempt on Yemini et al. 2021 (yemini-2021-02, 2026-10-31)
  • Metadata errorThe third channel is labelled GFP-GCaMP, with GFP emission settings, but its mean is 17,000 to 21,000 in every file, far above the fluorescence channels, and the Zenodo copies mark that channel as the DIC (transmitted light) channel. The worms carry no GCaMP.
    Found by the attempt on Yemini et al. 2021 (yemini-2021-02, 2026-10-31)
  • Data missingThe dandiset holds only the heads. The tails of the same ten worms, used for the paper's tail atlas and tail accuracies, are on the paper's Zenodo record, and so is the whole-brain activity of Figure 6. Nine of the claims tested here need the tails in part or in full.
    Found by the attempt on Yemini et al. 2021 (yemini-2021-02, 2026-10-31)
  • Description missingThe files carry no per-neuron colors (the Zenodo files have a color readout per neuron), no orientation of the worm, and do not say whether the stored voxel indices are 0- or 1-based. The five heads imaged in dorsal-ventral view are stored rotated by about 90 degrees about the long axis relative to the five imaged in left-right view, which only the subject names (ending DV or LR) hint at. Comparison with the Zenodo annotations shows that each stored voxel is the Zenodo position made 0-based and rounded. Colors had to be recomputed from the volumes.
    Found by the attempt on Yemini et al. 2021 (yemini-2021-02, 2026-10-31)
  • Description missing0 to 6 of the 189 to 196 stored neurons per file have an empty name, with no description of what they are; the paper reports 195 annotated head neurons per worm.
    Found by the attempt on Yemini et al. 2021 (yemini-2021-02, 2026-10-31)
  • OtherNeuron names are stored as a ragged array of single characters (ID_labels with ID_labels_index), so each name must be joined from its characters.
    Found by the attempt on Yemini et al. 2021 (yemini-2021-02, 2026-10-31)
  • Identifier missingThe dandiset metadata name no related publication (relatedResource is empty); the description and the files' related_publications give the paper's title only, without a DOI.
    Found by the attempt on Yemini et al. 2021 (yemini-2021-02, 2026-10-31)
