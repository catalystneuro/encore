Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingNo unit is labelled light-responsive (birthdated), and no optogenetic pulse times are stored in the processed or the raw files, so the paper's comparisons of same- and different-birthdate neurons and its birthdate profiles (22 of the 35 claims listed here) cannot be tested. The labels exist in the lab's CellExplorer files (optoTag, putativeCellType), which are not public at a pinned identifier.
  • Description missingUnits carry no putative cell type and no waveforms, though the paper classifies 7,411 pyramidal cells and 2,060 interneurons.
  • Data missingSix of the 17 mice (e13_1f1, e13_1m1, e14_1m1, e15_4m1, e15_4m2, e15_5m1; 39 files) have sleep states and ripples but no units and no position. The dandiset holds 8,201 units where the paper reports 9,471.
  • Data missingOf the 16 novel environment explorations, only 9 have position and trials, and no open field has position. The novel-cohort sessions hold 7 further epochs of 10 to 50 min without position or video that may be the missing explorations.
  • Metadata errorThe subject sex differs between files of the same mouse. e13_1f1 is Female in 3 files and Male in 2; e13_1m1 is Male in 9 and Female in 3. The value in most of each mouse's files gives the paper's 12 males and 5 females.
  • Metadata errorThe trials column is_familiar_maze is True only for the first two trials of sessions with a single maze recording, where every trial is on the familiar maze (112 True, 1,182 False).
  • Metadata errorIn several novel recordings (e13_26m1 211019, 211116, 211119; e15_13f1 220120; e16_3m2 211213) x and y lie between 0 and 1 though stated in cm.
  • Metadata errorTwelve of the 13 raw files give a session start of 2011-08-18 for sessions recorded in 2021 and 2022.
  • Metadata errorThe processed file of e16_3m1_210201 holds a copy of the 1250 Hz LFP of the raw file, labelled with a rate of 30,000 Hz and a conversion factor of 3.05e-13 V.
  • Identifier missingUnits have no link to the electrodes table, and their group_id values ("Group3") do not match the electrode group names ("Group 3") in 64 of 65 files, so a unit's shank and position on the probe cannot be recovered.
  • Description missingThe embryonic day of electroporation, the variable the paper is about, is stored only as the prefix of each subject id (e13 for E13.5); the age is given as P3M/P6M for every mouse.
  • OtherTwo files of e13_1f1 (200305, 200307) hold a single Awake interval for the whole session, so sleep was not scored, though both hold ripples.
  • OtherOnly 38 of 65 familiar-maze sessions have ten or more trials on each visited arm, and 6 have fewer than 20 trials, where the Methods require ten per arm in every session; 19 of 1,942 trials have no start or stop time.
  • Paper inconsistentThe Methods give 92 sessions, while Figure 1e (30, 24, 24 and 18 sessions) and Figure 7f give 96. The dandiset's files of the 15 homecage mice are 96 sessions that match Figure 1e by birthdate and the Methods' duration bounds exactly (113.7 to 475.6 min).
  • Paper inconsistentThe paper calls the novel-environment cohort of three mice separate, but one of them (e13_26m1) is also among the nine figure-eight mice, in the dandiset and in the paper's code.

Method differences

  • The criterion took sex from the subject metadata without saying how to combine files that disagree; the value in most of a mouse's files is used, chosen after the per-file values were seen (the first round of this attempt read the first file and gave 13 and 4).cohort-birthdates
