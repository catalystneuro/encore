Dataset version 0.261113.1035 · tested 2026-11-19 to 2026-11-20 · 13 open findings

  • Metadata errorThe version the paper cites (0.230630.2304) has no putative cell type and no light-responsive label for its units, which the paper's selection of pyramidal cells and its ChR2 analysis need, and its trial table marks 28 of the 30 familiar-maze trials of e16_3m1_210201 as not familiar. Version 0.261113.1035 adds the labels and corrects the flag; the spike times of that session are identical in the two versions.
    Found by the attempt on Zheng et al. 2024 (zheng-2024-01, 2026-11-20)
  • Data missingSession e13_26m1_210913, one of the paper's 46 familiar sessions, is not in the dandiset.
    Found by the attempt on Zheng et al. 2024 (zheng-2024-01, 2026-11-20)
  • Description missingOn the linear mazes the trials' visited_arm holds the running direction without saying so; left is a run toward higher linearised position in all 108 such trials and right toward lower in all 110.
    Found by the attempt on Zheng et al. 2024 (zheng-2024-01, 2026-11-20)
  • OtherThe paper requires at least 50 putative pyramidal cells per session, but by the files' CellExplorer labels six of its familiar sessions have fewer (e14_2m3_201118 39, e14_2m3_201121 40, e15_10m1_200803 47, e15_9f1_200715 42, e15_9f1_200720 49, e16_2m1_201121 48). The paper's labels may differ from the files'.
    Found by the attempt on Zheng et al. 2024 (zheng-2024-01, 2026-11-20)
  • OtherSpikes share the position clock; the pyramidal cells' spatial information is lower with spikes shifted by 5 s or 30 s in all 53 analysed tasks, and highest unshifted in 52 (in e16_3m2_211212's linear maze a 1-s shift is slightly higher). Every familiar trial starts in the delay zone. In e16_3m1_210201 the wide-band signal shares the spikes' clock.
    Found by the attempt on Zheng et al. 2024 (zheng-2024-01, 2026-11-20)
  • Data missingWide-band signal is deposited for 11 of the 45 familiar sessions (raw_ecephys files of 52 to 175 GB); the others have only spike times and mean waveforms, so spike amplitudes around a switch (Figure S1) can be measured in those 11 only.
    Found by the attempt on Zheng et al. 2024 (zheng-2024-01, 2026-11-20)
  • Data missingStill presentSessions e13_1f1_200305 and e13_1f1_200307 have only wake in their SleepStates table, with no non-REM or REM sleep, while the paper says every recording included a homecage period of sleep and wake. A third session has no REM. Either the sleep scoring of these sessions is missing from the files or they held no sleep.
    Found by the attempt on Huszar et al. 2022 (huszar-2022-02, 2026-11-19)
  • Metadata errorNewIn e16_3m2_211211 the position series named for the linear_manu subsession (LinearizedSpatialSeries3_linear_manu and SpatialSeries3_linear_manu) has timestamps from 9,575 to 11,203 s, inside the openField_thomas epoch (9,559 to 12,002 s), and none inside the linear_manu epoch (15,124 to 16,771 s). The series or its epoch is mislabelled or mistimed; the attempt leaves both subsessions out.
    Found by the attempt on Huszar et al. 2022 (huszar-2022-02, 2026-11-19)
  • Description missingPartly fixedPositions outside the familiar figure-eight are in the lab's units with no spatial scale, as the files' own descriptions say, and the units differ between environments: five novel figure-eight and linear-track series span 0 to 1, while three others give 0.85 to 0.93 cm per unit when their speeds are set against the simultaneous linearized position in cm. Linearized positions of the novel figure-eight and linear tracks are in cm, but the open fields (7 of the 16 novel explorations) have only the 2D position, so their 1 cm bins rest on an assumed scale, and one novel ratemap outcome (Fig. 6c) depends on it.
    Found by the attempt on Huszar et al. 2022 (huszar-2022-02, 2026-11-19)
  • Data missingNewThe units table has no cluster isolation metric. The paper uses each unit's L-ratio (Extended Data Figs 2h and 5), so its L-ratio controls cannot be repeated on the dataset.
    Found by the attempt on Huszar et al. 2022 (huszar-2022-02, 2026-11-19)
  • OtherNewThe paper leaves out two E13.5 mice from the per-animal comparison because only a single light-responsive neuron per session was recorded in them (Extended Data Fig. 4 legend). In the files these mice, e13_1f1 and e13_1m1, have sessions with two light-responsive cells, giving 1 and 2 same-birthdate pairs.
    Found by the attempt on Huszar et al. 2022 (huszar-2022-02, 2026-11-19)
  • OtherStill presentThe Methods say animals ran at least ten trials along each arm in every session. In the trials table, 27 of the 57 familiar figure-eight sessions hold fewer than ten trials of one visited arm, and 6 hold fewer than 20 trials in all (fewest 7 left and 5 right, e16_2m1_201116). Trials may be missing from the table, or the paper's rule may describe training.
    Found by the attempt on Huszar et al. 2022 (huszar-2022-02, 2026-11-19)
  • Description missingNewIn 25 files (9 mice) the electrodes table gives probe_side Front or Back with shank numbers 1 to 4 on each, without saying whether these are two probes or two faces of one probe. Both faces have the same x positions, and the paper's pair counts by shank (2,561 SBD pairs within 500 um, 1,205 SBD and 13,494 DBD on the same shank in the maze, 1,743 and 15,996 in the homecage, 347 and 3,588 of them more than 60 um apart) are found only when both faces count as one shank, so they are read here as faces of the same shanks.
    Found by the attempt on Huszar et al. 2022 (huszar-2022-02, 2026-11-19)

Fixed since an earlier attempt 10

  • Data missingFixedNo unit is labelled light-responsive (birthdated), and no optogenetic pulse times are stored in the processed or the raw files, so the paper's comparisons of same- and different-birthdate neurons and its birthdate profiles (22 of the 35 claims listed here) cannot be tested. The labels exist in the lab's CellExplorer files (optoTag, putativeCellType), which are not public at a pinned identifier.
    Every processed file now has a light_responsive column, with opto_p_salt and opto_h_reliable beside it, and an OptogeneticPulses table (477,092 pulses in 104 files); 664 units are light-responsive in the 15 homecage mice (earlier_findings.ipynb). Found fixed on dataset version 0.261113.1035 by huszar-2022-02, 2026-11-19.
  • Description missingFixedUnits carry no putative cell type and no waveforms, though the paper classifies 7,411 pyramidal cells and 2,060 interneurons.
    Every units table now holds putative_cell_type and waveform_mean; the 15 homecage mice give 7,411 pyramidal cells, 1,556 narrow and 504 wide interneurons and 1 Unknown (earlier_findings.ipynb). Found fixed on dataset version 0.261113.1035 by huszar-2022-02, 2026-11-19.
  • Data missingFixedSix of the 17 mice (e13_1f1, e13_1m1, e14_1m1, e15_4m1, e15_4m2, e15_5m1; 39 files) have sleep states and ripples but no units and no position. The dandiset holds 8,201 units where the paper reports 9,471.
    All 104 files have units, the six mice included (202 to 1,432 units each), 11,656 in all and 9,472 in the 15 homecage mice. The six still have no position, which no claim of the paper asks of them (earlier_findings.ipynb). Found fixed on dataset version 0.261113.1035 by huszar-2022-02, 2026-11-19.
  • Data missingFixedOf the 16 novel environment explorations, only 9 have position and trials, and no open field has position. The novel-cohort sessions hold 7 further epochs of 10 to 50 min without position or video that may be the missing explorations.
    The 12 novel-cohort sessions now hold 16 position series outside the familiar maze, 7 of them in the three open fields, so every novel exploration has position (earlier_findings.ipynb). Found fixed on dataset version 0.261113.1035 by huszar-2022-02, 2026-11-19.
  • Metadata errorFixedThe subject sex differs between files of the same mouse. e13_1f1 is Female in 3 files and Male in 2; e13_1m1 is Male in 9 and Female in 3. The value in most of each mouse's files gives the paper's 12 males and 5 females.
    Every mouse has one sex in all of its files (e13_1f1 F in 5, e13_1m1 M in 12), giving 12 males and 5 females (earlier_findings.ipynb). Found fixed on dataset version 0.261113.1035 by huszar-2022-02, 2026-11-19.
  • Metadata errorFixedThe trials column is_familiar_maze is True only for the first two trials of sessions with a single maze recording, where every trial is on the familiar maze (112 True, 1,182 False).
    is_familiar_maze is True for all 1,294 trials of single-recording sessions and for every trial of a session's first recording, False for the 362 others (earlier_findings.ipynb). Found fixed on dataset version 0.261113.1035 by huszar-2022-02, 2026-11-19.
  • Metadata errorFixedTwelve of the 13 raw files give a session start of 2011-08-18 for sessions recorded in 2021 and 2022.
    The version holds 11 raw files, and each gives the date of its file name as its session start (earlier_findings.ipynb). Found fixed on dataset version 0.261113.1035 by huszar-2022-02, 2026-11-19.
  • Metadata errorFixedThe processed file of e16_3m1_210201 holds a copy of the 1250 Hz LFP of the raw file, labelled with a rate of 30,000 Hz and a conversion factor of 3.05e-13 V.
    The LFP of e16_3m1_210201 is labelled 1250 Hz with a conversion of 1.95e-07 V, as in all 104 processed files (earlier_findings.ipynb). Found fixed on dataset version 0.261113.1035 by huszar-2022-02, 2026-11-19.
  • Identifier missingFixedUnits have no link to the electrodes table, and their group_id values ("Group3") do not match the electrode group names ("Group 3") in 64 of 65 files, so a unit's shank and position on the probe cannot be recovered.
    In all 104 files the units table has an electrodes column with indices inside the electrodes table, and its electrode_group references resolve to the file's electrode groups (earlier_findings.ipynb). Found fixed on dataset version 0.261113.1035 by huszar-2022-02, 2026-11-19.
  • Description missingFixedThe embryonic day of electroporation, the variable the paper is about, is stored only as the prefix of each subject id (e13 for E13.5); the age is given as P3M/P6M for every mouse.
    The day is still held only in the subject id and the age is still P3M/P6M, but the subject description now states that the e-number of the id is the electroporation day and that no source gives another record of it or each mouse's age (earlier_findings.ipynb).
