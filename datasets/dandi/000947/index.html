Dataset version 0.240510.2211 · tested 2026-10-18 · 11 open findings

  • Selection not documentedThe dandiset holds more GPi and VLa units than the paper analysed (268 GPi and 279 VLa units under its own labels and the Methods' rate criteria, against 209 and 185) and does not say which units the paper kept. The paper's unit list is only in its Dryad deposit.
    Found by the attempt on Schwab et al. 2020 (schwab-2020-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Identifier missingNo identifier links a neuron recorded over consecutive files. 97 of the paper's 394 units span 2 to 5 files, and the dandiset stores them as separate units.
    Found by the attempt on Schwab et al. 2020 (schwab-2020-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Metadata errorLocation labels are inconsistent. GPi and VLa are written GPI and VLA in some files, 54 Gaia thalamic units are labelled only "Thalamus" (25 of them are VLa units in the paper), and 33 Isis units have an empty location and no channel id.
    Found by the attempt on Schwab et al. 2020 (schwab-2020-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Data missing30 of the 394 units the paper analysed are not among the curated units. 21 of 254 pre-MPTP files have no curated units. 13 of them hold sorted clusters in processing/ecephys/units (124 in all, 16 in G-140813-2), whose only columns are spike times, unit name and location, so there is no channel or sort letter to match them; 8 have no such table.
    Found by the attempt on Schwab et al. 2020 (schwab-2020-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Metadata errorIn all 254 pre-MPTP files the session_start_time is two days after the date in the session_id.
    Found by the attempt on Schwab et al. 2020 (schwab-2020-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Description missingSort labels are letters in some files and numbers in others, and the unit quality column is missing from some files and holds free text (for example "B -> C") in others, without a description of its values.
    Found by the attempt on Schwab et al. 2020 (schwab-2020-01, 2026-10-18)
  • OtherError trials are flagged only in files with an error_onset_time column (127 of 253 files with trials), and movement ends are missing from 87 files, all of them Gaia (32 of 119 Gaia files have them), so the Methods' trial exclusion can be applied only in part.
    Found by the attempt on Schwab et al. 2020 (schwab-2020-01, 2026-10-18)
  • File format errorOne pre-MPTP file (I-160616-4) has no trials table, though it has 5 curated units and raw data, and one spike train is stored twice in I-170518-2, as two M1-S units on channels 40 and 43.
    Found by the attempt on Schwab et al. 2020 (schwab-2020-01, 2026-10-18)
  • OtherThe pre-MPTP NWB files name the paper only by its bioRxiv preprint (doi:10.1101/546598); the dandiset metadata names the published paper.
    Found by the attempt on Schwab et al. 2020 (schwab-2020-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Data missingThe EMG of S3 Fig and the stimulation mapping of Fig 1 are not in the dandiset; the EMG is in the paper's Dryad deposit and the mapping is not public.
    Found by the attempt on Schwab et al. 2020 (schwab-2020-01, 2026-10-18)
  • Description missingThe electrode table's coordinates relative to the anterior commissure (acx_y, acx_z) put the electrodes labelled VLa ventral of those labelled VLp, if z is ventral positive as the column description says, where the paper places VLa dorsal of VLp. The files do not say how the VLa and VLp labels were assigned, so it cannot be told whether the description of the z axis or the labels differ from the paper.
    Found by the attempt on Schwab et al. 2020 (schwab-2020-01, 2026-10-18)
