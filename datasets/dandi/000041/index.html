Dataset version 0.261104.0300 · tested 2026-11-15 · 5 open findings

  • Data missingNewDANDI:000041 holds 22 of the paper's 27 sessions. The three sessions of rat B22 (CRCNS 20140526_277um, 20140527_421um, 20140528_565um) and the ACC probes of Dino 061914 and 062014 (52 and 24 units) are not in it, so it holds 10 of 11 rats, 850 of 995 pE and 95 of 126 pI cells.#
    Found by the attempt on Watson et al. 2016 (watson-2016-03, 2026-11-15)
  • Data missingNewDino-061914 in DANDI:000041 has no LFP; the release notes say the lab's source file does not divide into whole channels at 128. The CRCNS .eeg of both Dino 061914 folders divides into 142 channels, which the .xml gives and the stream's own autocorrelation confirms.#
    Found by the attempt on Watson et al. 2016 (watson-2016-03, 2026-11-15)
  • Data missingNewDANDI:000041 holds only Awake, Non-REM and REM epochs. The paper's analysis units (WAKE, SLEEP and WAKE-SLEEP cycles, packets, MAs, WAKE interruptions, nonREM and REM episodes), its UP/DOWN states and spindles, and the motion and EMG signals, all in CRCNS:fcx-1, are not in it, and its only ElectricalSeries is the 1250 Hz LFP, so no EMG can be derived from a wideband signal. Built here from the epochs by Table S1's rules they differ from the lab's, 47 cycles against 51, one session without a cycle, 181 MAs in cycles against 434, median packet 144 s against 111 s, median REM episode 64 s against 98 s.#
    Found by the attempt on Watson et al. 2016 (watson-2016-03, 2026-11-15)
  • Metadata errorNewIn 7 DANDI sessions whose spike trains are identical, train for train, to the CRCNS stable units, the cell_type labels differ from the lab's _CellIDs (EAll and IAll), for example BWRat20-101513 with 63 pE and 3 pI against 60 and 6. The CRCNS labels give the paper's 995 and 126; the DANDI labels describe cells the paper classed otherwise.#
    Found by the attempt on Watson et al. 2016 (watson-2016-03, 2026-11-15)
  • Description missingNewThe DANDI unit tables of Dino-072314 and Dino-072414 hold 10 and 15 units more than the CRCNS stable set (26 against 16, 61 against 46), Dino-061814 one more, and Dino-072114 16 units of which 13 match the 18 CRCNS units. No column marks which units are the paper's stable units, so the paper's cell set cannot be selected from DANDI alone.#
    Found by the attempt on Watson et al. 2016 (watson-2016-03, 2026-11-15)
