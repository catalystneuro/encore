    Appendix B (Composition, errors and noise)

    657 of 23,810 frames (2.8%) have a body length outside the session's quartiles widened by 3 IQR. In the fastest 10% (at least 389 px/s) it is 0.010 against 0.030 (difference -0.020, interval -0.033 to -0.008), the reverse of the claim. Both directions hold at 5% and 20% and with a factor of 1.5. Error fractions by label are 0.045 (investigation), 0.105 (mount) and 0.009 (other), so a hunched posture in mount could give real outliers. Checked against the MARS confidence of the same frames, flagged close frames have a lower confidence than unflagged close frames (median 0.574 against 0.657, difference -0.082, interval -0.107 to -0.050), and in each of eight flagged close frames drawn at random and shown with the video, at least one segment visibly runs across the other mouse or the background (judged by eye). Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingThe MARS confidence scores, which CalMS21 distributes with every keypoint, are missing from the dandiset's CalMS21 file; the confidence field of all 14 pose series is present but all 333,340 values are NaN. CaltechDATA's copy of the same sequence has them (0.03 to 0.98).
  • Selection not documentedFor CalMS21 the dandiset holds one sequence, the Task 1 test sequence mouse071, out of 574 sequences on CaltechDATA. Its description presents a collection of datasets and does not say that only one CalMS21 sequence is included or why this one was chosen.
  • Description missingThe behavior catalogue (CalMS21_ethogram) has an empty definition for attack, investigation, mount and other, though the paper defines the three behaviors (Appendix D.2) and states that annotations refer to behaviors initiated by the resident.
  • Description missingThe two mice are stored as one subject with strain C57BL/6, sex unknown and age P0D/; the intruder's strain (BALB/c) and the residents' sex (male) are known from the paper but not recorded, and which series belongs to the black or white mouse is given only by the series names.
  • Metadata errorThe session start time is 2024-01-01T00:00, a placeholder; CalMS21 gives no recording dates.
  • Description missingThe `centroid_speed` series, and the bout columns mean_speed, peak_speed and immobility_fraction that reduce it, are the speed of the mean of all 14 keypoints, which is the midpoint of the two mice, not the speed of either animal. The description ("the pose centroid, mean over keypoints") does not make this clear.
  • OtherThe dandiset's mouse071 video (184 MB) is a re-encoding of CaltechDATA's (964 MB). It has the same frame count, size and rate, and its motion follows the pose at lag 0, but the dandiset does not say it was re-encoded.
  • Description missingThe dandiset links the CalMS21 dataset DOI (10.22002/D1.1991) as IsDerivedFrom, but not the paper that describes the dataset and its benchmarks (arXiv 2104.02710, NeurIPS 2021 Datasets and Benchmarks).
  • OtherOn CaltechDATA, 574 CalMS21 sequences hold 450 distinct recordings (identical keypoints over the whole sequence). 93 of the 109 Task 3 test sequences are Task 1 training recordings, five Task 1 test recordings (mouse071 among them) appear as six Task 3 training sequences, and four Task 2 recordings are in the training set of one of annotators 3 and 4 and the test set of the other. The paper describes each sequence as an independent observation and does not mention the overlap, and the CaltechDATA readme says that training and test sets are split by animal identity.
  • Paper inconsistentThe paper's datasheet says the dataset does not include the raw videos, while Section 3.1 says all source videos of Task 1 are released, and the deposit holds them. The datasheet also gives the licence as CC-BY-NC-SA, while the CaltechDATA record and the dandiset give CC-BY 4.0.
  • OtherTable 5 gives 139,112 training frames for Task 2 annotator 1; the deposited file holds 139,122. The other 47 counts of Table 5 match.
  • Methods not availableThe baseline code the paper links (gitlab.aicrowd.com/aicrowd/research/mab-e/mab-e-baselines) is not reachable and no archived copy of the repository was found; the code of the challenge's top entries is not linked. The paper leaves the architectures' settings to that code, so none of the benchmark results can be repeated.
