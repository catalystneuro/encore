Dataset version 0.241130.1903 · tested 2026-11-10 · 8 open findings

  • Data missingThe 69 files hold raw HD-MEA voltage only. The sorted and curated units on which almost every result of the paper rests are not in the dandiset, nor public elsewhere, and the histology and patch-clamp data are not deposited. 27 of the 28 claims on the paper's recordings cannot be tested.
    Found by the attempt on Andrews et al. 2024 (andrews-2024-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Data missingThe light ON and OFF times, which the Methods say were stored in the MaxWell file's GPIO bit and in a stimulation log, are not in the files, nor are the light intensities. They can be recovered from a step the LED leaves in the voltage in the eight 3C recordings (on every channel) and in 4C-1, 4C-3, 7D-0, 7D-2, 7D-5 and 7D-7 (on part of the array, steps of 40 to 88 counts). No channel group of any 5C, 8D, 10F or 11G recording shows a 10 s light step, including 10F, which the paper illuminated for 10 trials, and 9E and 12G show none in the first channel group.
    Found by the attempt on Andrews et al. 2024 (andrews-2024-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Description missingNo file says which medium, drug or light protocol it was recorded under (physiologic, bicuculline, 0-mg, 0-mg plus kainic acid, closed loop, intensity sweep), which opsin its slice carries, or which of the paper's recordings it is. The session descriptions are empty and the files keep no original recording names, so the paper's per-condition analyses cannot be assigned to files.
    Found by the attempt on Andrews et al. 2024 (andrews-2024-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Metadata errorEach file's subject_id is the recording (for example 3C-7), so the archive counts 69 subjects where the paper has 7 patients and 12 slices. Every session_start_time is 2024-09-28, the date of the NWB conversion, not of the recording.
    Found by the attempt on Andrews et al. 2024 (andrews-2024-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Description missingThe NWB fields for the experiment are empty or absent in every file. general/ holds no virus, pharmacology, protocol, notes, optogenetics, slices or surgery field and no subject genotype, strain or description; experiment_description is the paper's title in all 69 files, and the ElectricalSeries' description and comments are the converter's defaults.
    Found by the attempt on Andrews et al. 2024 (andrews-2024-01, 2026-11-10)
  • OtherIn the 3C recordings, light onset drives 81 to 94% of the channels in the first 64-channel group to the ends of the 0 to 1023 range in the first 0.5 s, and some stay there through the light period. In at least one 5C recording, 34% of that channel group sits at the ends of the range in the median 100 ms bin.
    Found by the attempt on Andrews et al. 2024 (andrews-2024-01, 2026-11-10)
  • Description missingElectrode location is "unknown" for every channel, and nothing places the array over the slice's anatomy (granule cell layer, hilus), which the paper's GCL and propagation analyses use.
    Found by the attempt on Andrews et al. 2024 (andrews-2024-01, 2026-11-10)
  • OtherThe conversion to volts is 6.29 uV per count for slices 1A to 8D and 1.57 uV per count for 9E to 12G, a gain difference that the dandiset does not describe.
    Found by the attempt on Andrews et al. 2024 (andrews-2024-01, 2026-11-10)
