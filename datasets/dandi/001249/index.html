What the attempts that used this dataset found about the data themselves, for its owner and the archive. The claims and their outcomes are on each paper’s page.

  • Metadata errorEach NWB file stores the z planes of a brain as the frames of an ImageSeries (acquisition/TRAP and acquisition/background) with starting_time rate 4.0, the z step in micrometres given as a sampling rate, resolution 3.6 and unit "lumen". No voxel size, orientation, channel wavelength or description is given; the descriptions are "no description".
    Found by the attempt on Ishii et al. 2025 (ishii-2025-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Data missingThe dandiset holds only the raw volumes of Figure 2. The registration to the atlas, the cell segmentation, the per-region counts and densities, and the trained ilastik classifier are not in it, so none of the Figure 2 results can be computed without rerunning the whole pipeline, and the classifier, trained on manual labels, cannot be rebuilt as it was. The per-region densities are only on Figshare (tdTomato_summary.csv), with no link to the volumes.
    Found by the attempt on Ishii et al. 2025 (ishii-2025-01, 2026-11-01)
  • OtherThe volumes are stored uncompressed and unchunked, 1.38 TB for 14 brains. At the 35.5 to 67.4 MB/s measured in runs of this notebook, streaming the TRAP channels once takes 2.8 to 5.4 h and both channels 5.7 to 10.8 h, which puts any analysis of Figure 2 out of reach of a streamed notebook. A full-width band of rows can be read in one range request (20.4 MB/s in the executed notebook), a crop only row by row (2.5 MB/s).
    Found by the attempt on Ishii et al. 2025 (ishii-2025-01, 2026-11-01)
  • OtherThe five consummatory (Mount) brains have session start dates of 2024-02-07, a year after the appetitive and post-ejaculatory brains (2022-12 to 2023-06), and their middle-plane TRAP intensities are lower (means 20 to 35 against 47 to 143). The dandiset does not say whether this is a separate imaging batch; it bears on the group comparisons of Figure 2.

    Found by the attempt on Ishii et al. 2025 (ishii-2025-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Methods not availableThe decay length of Figure 6C is defined in the Methods as "the time the z-scored signal was smaller than 0", and the code reads it precomputed. The deposited values equal the time from the peak after onset to the first sample below 0 for 207 of 219 cells, which gives the paper's result, while the time from onset to the first sample below 0, or the total time below 0, give Vgat no longer than Vglut2. The deposited table gives 120 s to each of the 44 responders whose trace never falls below 0 after the peak; with the time to the end of the window, or with these cells left out, Vgat is not longer at p < 0.05. The questions for the authors are whether the decay length is measured from the peak, what value a trace that never returns below 0 takes and why, and how the other 12 cells are computed.
