Problems that change results 3

The data are wrong in a way the files do not show, so an analysis that takes them as given comes out wrong. Each was checked against the notebook’s outputs by someone other than the attempt’s author.

  • OtherChanges resultsIn 18 of the 21 mPFC-LH files and one ephys file the rescaled spike times match the ElectricalSeries only after an offset of 5 to 28 s, and in 3 mPFC-LH files (20191025-chrimson2-3-1v3, 20191029-chrimson1-1-1v2, 20191029-chrimson1-2-1v2) at no offset within 60 s, so in those files the sorted spikes and the raw recording do not share a start.#
    Checked: reproduce_padilla_coreano_2022.ipynb cell 16 prints the spike/raw cross-correlation per file: with times x 1.5, 19 files peak above 10 SD only at a lag beyond 0.1 s (18 mPFC-LH files at 5.9 to 28.4 s and ephys3-4-2v4 at 4.9 s), and the three named mPFC-LH files have no peak above 10 SD within 60 s. The ephys and BLA files peak at about 2 ms, so the method resolves a shared start where there is one.
    Found by the attempt on Padilla Coreano et al. 2022 (padilla-coreano-2022-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Description missingChanges resultsThe offset between the neural recording and the behaviour clock of the tone times, port entries and labels is in no file, though the dandiset description says a video_gap variable holds it. Estimated from the units' responses to port entries it is 4 to 45 s, differing by session (294 s in one file), and it cannot be estimated for the 10 ephys files, which have no port entries, or for 12 of the 38 files with them. An analysis aligning spikes to the task can rely on the files alone only where the port entries give a clear offset (23 of the 34 labelled files).#
    Checked: reproduce_padilla_coreano_2022.ipynb cell 6 prints an empty list for paths containing video_gap, and cell 18 prints port-entry offsets with a clear peak in 26 of 38 files, from 4.0 to 44.6 s plus one at 294 s, none at offset 0; cell 3 shows the 10 ephys files have no port entries and cell 24 prints 23 of 34 labelled files with a clear offset. The dandiset description's mention of video_gap is quoted only in markdown, and 5 mPFC-LH files from 20191024 also lack port entries, which the finding does not say.
    Found by the attempt on Padilla Coreano et al. 2022 (padilla-coreano-2022-01, 2026-11-03)
  • OtherChanges resultsThe ElectricalSeries of sub-20191025-chrimson2-3-1v3 is the same signal as that of sub-20191029-chrimson2-3-1v3 (identical samples at three points, same length), while their units differ and the 20191025 file's units match its recording at no offset; the 20191025 file most likely carries the 20191029 recording in place of its own.#
    Checked: reproduce_padilla_coreano_2022.ipynb cell 19 prints the same md5 of a 500-sample block and the same length (61438265 samples) for sub-20191025-chrimson2-3-1v3 and sub-20191029-chrimson2-3-1v3, identical samples at three further points, 49 against 45 units and different session starts. Cell 16 shows the 20191025 file's spikes match the recording at no lag (5.4 SD) while the 20191029 file's do (38.8 SD), which supports the 20191025 file as the one carrying the wrong recording.
    Found by the attempt on Padilla Coreano et al. 2022 (padilla-coreano-2022-01, 2026-11-03)

Other findings 12

  • File format errorThe spike_times of every units table are two thirds of the time of the same spikes in the ElectricalSeries. Multiplied by 1.5 they line up with threshold crossings of the recording (cross-correlation peaks of 14 to 95 SD in 50 of 53 files, none above 7 SD unscaled), and the last spike of every file falls at 0.63 to 0.68 of the recording length. The times look divided by 30 kHz where the logger samples at 20 kHz.#
    Found by the attempt on Padilla Coreano et al. 2022 (padilla-coreano-2022-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Description missingEvery one of the 5,879 Klusta clusters is labelled unsorted, where the paper analyses 998 manually curated cells. The quality column exists but no cluster carries a curation label, so the paper's cells cannot be told from multi-unit and noise clusters, and none of the single-unit findings can be tested.#
    Found by the attempt on Padilla Coreano et al. 2022 (padilla-coreano-2022-01, 2026-11-03)
