Dataset version 0.260311.1727 · tested 2026-11-03 · 15 open findings

  • File format errorThe spike_times of every units table are two thirds of the time of the same spikes in the ElectricalSeries. Multiplied by 1.5 they line up with threshold crossings of the recording (cross-correlation peaks of 14 to 95 SD in 50 of 53 files, none above 7 SD unscaled), and the last spike of every file falls at 0.63 to 0.68 of the recording length. The times look divided by 30 kHz where the logger samples at 20 kHz.
    Found by the attempt on Padilla Coreano et al. 2022 (padilla-coreano-2022-01, 2026-11-03)
  • OtherIn 18 of the 21 mPFC-LH files and one ephys file the rescaled spike times match the ElectricalSeries only after an offset of 5 to 28 s, and in 3 mPFC-LH files (20191025-chrimson2-3-1v3, 20191029-chrimson1-1-1v2, 20191029-chrimson1-2-1v2) at no offset within 60 s, so in those files the sorted spikes and the raw recording do not share a start.
    Found by the attempt on Padilla Coreano et al. 2022 (padilla-coreano-2022-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Description missingThe offset between the neural recording and the behaviour clock of the tone times, port entries and labels is in no file, though the dandiset description says a video_gap variable holds it. Estimated from the units' responses to port entries it is 4 to 45 s, differing by session (294 s in one file), and it cannot be estimated for the 10 ephys files, which have no port entries, or for 12 of the 38 files with them. An analysis aligning spikes to the task can rely on the files alone only where the port entries give a clear offset (23 of the 34 labelled files).
    Found by the attempt on Padilla Coreano et al. 2022 (padilla-coreano-2022-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Description missingEvery one of the 5,879 Klusta clusters is labelled unsorted, where the paper analyses 998 manually curated cells. The quality column exists but no cluster carries a curation label, so the paper's cells cannot be told from multi-unit and noise clusters, and none of the single-unit findings can be tested.
    Found by the attempt on Padilla Coreano et al. 2022 (padilla-coreano-2022-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Data missingThe dandiset holds no alone sessions, no phototagging light pulses, no optogenetic experiments, no unimplanted cohort, no ex vivo recordings, no AlphaTracker evaluation frames, no body weights and no port location.
    Found by the attempt on Padilla Coreano et al. 2022 (padilla-coreano-2022-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Data missingThe 10 ephys files hold no port entries, labels or poses, and in all 22 mPFC-BLA files every pose value is missing; 17 of the 53 competitions have no labels of either mouse.
    Found by the attempt on Padilla Coreano et al. 2022 (padilla-coreano-2022-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Description missingPoses are given for Mouse1 and Mouse2 with nothing saying which is the file's subject, and without the nose that the Methods say was tracked (ears, tail base and logger only).
    Found by the attempt on Padilla Coreano et al. 2022 (padilla-coreano-2022-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Metadata errorReward deliveries are 0.49 s after tone onset in every file, where the Methods give 2 s. The tone times are the program's schedule, the same in all files of a cohort (all 53 share the first 17 onsets), not times recorded per session; in 7 of the 10 ephys files the last tones fall after the end of the neural recording.
    Found by the attempt on Padilla Coreano et al. 2022 (padilla-coreano-2022-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Metadata errorpair_in_dataset is 'not included' in all 53 files, but for 20 sessions the competitor's file is in the dandiset.
    Found by the attempt on Padilla Coreano et al. 2022 (padilla-coreano-2022-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Metadata errorsub-20200121-BLA2-1-1v2 has a session start of 2020-01-22 13:30, while its labels mirror those of its competitor's file in the 2020-01-21 13:15 session; the subject description of sub-20200123-BLA1-3-2v3 names mouse 2 against mouse 3, where the file name says mouse 3 against mouse 2 (its ranks are those of the file name's mice).
    Found by the attempt on Padilla Coreano et al. 2022 (padilla-coreano-2022-01, 2026-11-03)
  • OtherThe ElectricalSeries of sub-20191025-chrimson2-3-1v3 is the same signal as that of sub-20191029-chrimson2-3-1v3 (identical samples at three points, same length), while their units differ and the 20191025 file's units match its recording at no offset; the 20191025 file most likely carries the 20191029 recording in place of its own.
    Found by the attempt on Padilla Coreano et al. 2022 (padilla-coreano-2022-01, 2026-11-03)
  • OtherWhere both mice of a session are labelled, one file's labels are the other's seen from the competitor (self and other in port swapped, a push opposite displaced or resist), in every bin for most sessions; in 20200121-BLA2-3v4 and 20200122-BLA1-3v4 only 76% and 68% of position bins agree.
    Found by the attempt on Padilla Coreano et al. 2022 (padilla-coreano-2022-01, 2026-11-03)
  • OtherThe 34 labelled files hold 965 trials from 14 mice, the size of the paper's HMM-GLM dataset (965 trials, 32 sessions, 13 mice), and the 53 files hold the 53 competitions of ED Fig. 5b (12, 11, 15 and 15 per rank against 12, 12, 15 and 14).
    Found by the attempt on Padilla Coreano et al. 2022 (padilla-coreano-2022-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Selection not documentedIn 18 of the 34 labelled files the labels hold fewer trials than the file has tones, and nothing says which tones were scored. Against the port entries, several files match only when the label trials start at a later tone (20200122-BLA2-1-1v4 at r = 0.76 from the third tone, against -0.02 from the first), and in others no start gives a clear match.
    Found by the attempt on Padilla Coreano et al. 2022 (padilla-coreano-2022-01, 2026-11-03)
  • OtherThe paper's processed HMM-GLM input (Tyelab/HMMGLM) matches the dandiset's labelled trials (for the 512 trials used for the HMM-GLM, median label agreement with the best-matching trial 0.91, against 0.70 for the second best), and against it the spikes of all 34 labelled files peak at one offset, which agrees within 1 s with the port-entry offset in 20 of the 23 files where both are clear. So the missing video_gap can be recovered for the labelled files from the paper's own input; the files alone give it for 23.
    Found by the attempt on Padilla Coreano et al. 2022 (padilla-coreano-2022-01, 2026-11-03)
