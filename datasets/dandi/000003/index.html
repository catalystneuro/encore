    CA3 pyramidal neurons are most active during wake and least active during REM.

    CA3 pyramidal neurons were most active during wake and least active during REM

    Results, Physiological properties of granule cells and mossy cells, paragraph 1; Figure 2A

    What the paper reports

    • across states: Kruskal-Wallis test, p < 10⁻⁴
    • pairwise between states: Bonferroni post hoc test, p < 10⁻⁴, Bonferroni-corrected

    CA3 pyramidal cells fire least in REM (0.287 Hz; adjusted P = 1.1e-15 against NREM and 5.4e-09 against wake), but the NREM median (0.459 Hz) is above the wake median (0.441 Hz awake, 0.433 Hz with quiet wake) and the two do not differ (adjusted P = 0.77 and 0.59). With home-cage awake time as wake (a variant), the wake median is 0.412 Hz and NREM against wake gives adjusted P = 0.14; CA3 units fire at similar rates awake in the home cage and during behaviour (0.424 and 0.415 Hz).

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • The paper's CA3 units come from a separate classification (Figure S2); here the dandiset's `pyramidal cell` label is used.
