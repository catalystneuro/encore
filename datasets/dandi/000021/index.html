What the attempts that used this dataset found about the data themselves, for its owner and the archive, under the version of the dataset each was found on.

Dataset version 0.251116.2246 · tested 2026-11-21 · 11 open findings

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds 13 of the 25 wild-type sessions the paper analysed (the Brain Observatory 1.1 sessions); the other 12 (766640955, 767871931, 768515987, 771160300, 771990200, 774875821, 778240327, 778998620, 779839471, 781842082, 793224716, 847657808), with 638 of the paper's 1204 units, are not among its 32 session files, and none of those 638 unit ids is in any of them. The paper's Data availability statement names only this dandiset for the in vivo data. It also holds 5 of the 8 Pvalb and 6 of the 12 Sst opto-tagging mice.
    Found by the attempt on Wei et al. 2023 (wei-2023-01, 2026-11-21)
  • OtherEvery one of the 566 Source Data units of the 13 held sessions is a VISp unit of quality good in the units tables, with the per-session counts of Source Data sheet Fig 1d; recomputed from the files' mean waveforms, TPW and REP equal the Source Data values for 85% of them, and the drifting-grating metrics and the code's firing-rate columns are reproduced exactly.
    Found by the attempt on Wei et al. 2023 (wei-2023-01, 2026-11-21)
  • Description missingThe files do not say which units the paper analysed or give its cluster labels (FS/RS and FS1 to RS3); these come only from the paper's Source Data, which names units by the same ids as the units tables.
    Found by the attempt on Wei et al. 2023 (wei-2023-01, 2026-11-21)
  • Metadata errorThe units table's waveform_mean is labelled volts, but its values (amplitudes of about 5 to 500) are microvolts.
    Found by the attempt on Wei et al. 2023 (wei-2023-01, 2026-11-21)
  • Metadata errorThe electrodes tables' CCF coordinates (x, y, z) hold NaN in 6 of the 24 sessions used (every value in 3 of them, 1152 to 5760 of 6912 values in the other 3), and in the other 18 y equals z on every channel, so the coordinates cannot place a channel in the CCF and the CCF layer of a unit cannot be read (the location column gives the structure, VISp, without the layer).
    Found by the attempt on Wei et al. 2023 (wei-2023-01, 2026-11-21)
  • Description missingThe units table holds every sorted unit (74658), with a `quality` column marking 20182 as `noise` and the AllenSDK quality metric columns (isi_violations, amplitude_cutoff, presence_ratio and others), none of which has a description in the files. The paper's unit counts are matched when the `noise` units are left out, as the AllenSDK's units table does; nothing in the files says that they should be, and keeping them changes the thalamic decay results.
    Found by the attempt on Fritsche et al. 2022 (fritsche-2022-01, 2026-11-21)
  • OtherThree sessions (715093703, 719161530, 721123822) hold 598 drifting gratings in place of the 600 the paper and the stimulus design give; one or two direction and frequency conditions there have 14 presentations.
    Found by the attempt on Fritsche et al. 2022 (fritsche-2022-01, 2026-11-21)
  • OtherThirteen sessions list invalid intervals tagged with a probe, totalling 12 to 2931 s, and several overlap the drifting or static grating epochs (375 drifting presentations in 5 sessions, 7001 static in 10). Their meaning is not described in the files; the paper does not mention them, and they are not applied in the main analysis. Leaving those presentations out changes the 1-back ratios by at most 0.014.
    Found by the attempt on Fritsche et al. 2022 (fritsche-2022-01, 2026-11-21)
  • Description missing3723 of the units that pass the quality metrics sit on channels whose area is `grey` and 686 on `VIS`, labels that name no structure; they reach 50 responsive units, the paper's rule for a region, and are left out here.
    Found by the attempt on Fritsche et al. 2022 (fritsche-2022-01, 2026-11-21)
  • OtherChecks that passed. 32 mice, one session each, with the genotypes the paper gives; stimulus parameters, durations and spacing as the Methods describe; no recording or unit deposited twice; the V1 orientation tuning is locked to the stimulus tables in every session (true labels against labels shifted by whole presentations).
    Found by the attempt on Fritsche et al. 2022 (fritsche-2022-01, 2026-11-21)
  • OtherStatic blank sweeps number 189 to 197 of 6000 presentations per session, about one in 31, where the Methods say roughly one in 25.
    Found by the attempt on Fritsche et al. 2022 (fritsche-2022-01, 2026-11-21)
