  • figs14k-waveform-spinal-vs-cortex

    In slices, the action potential waveforms of LC-NE neurons projecting to spinal cord differ significantly from those of neurons projecting to isocortex (p < 0.01).

    Neurons projecting to spinal cord had significantly different action potential waveforms than those projecting to isocortex or cerebellum

    Results, Spatial variation of LC-NE activity during action-outcome learning, paragraph 5; Figure S14k, left, legend

    How it will be judged

    The measure is fixed by the code (LCNE-patchseq-figures, code/spikes.py), as the paper says only "action potential waveforms". Per cell, among long-square rheobase sweeps (X3LP_Rheo_DA_0 and X5LP_Rheo_DA_0) whose main stimulus pulse lasts at least 0.8 s, the sweep with the smallest absolute amplitude that has a spike peak during the pulse; peaks found as the code does (voltage interpolated at the sampling interval, pairs of strict upward and downward crossings of -10 mV, the maximum between them); the raw voltage from -5 to 10 ms around every peak of that sweep, averaged. Each average is min-max normalised over -2 to 4 ms; PCA (mean-centred, by SVD) over -3 to 6 ms across all cells; PC1 per cell. The legend's "paired" t-test cannot apply to two groups of different cells of unequal size; the code's two-sided Welch t-test of PC1, spinal cord against cortex cells, is the test, and Student's two-sample t-test is shown as a variant. Cells and sweeps follow the Methods' inclusion criteria (sweep criteria applied before choosing the rheobase sweep, where the dataset holds the values they need); the code's choice, all 96 cells and no sweep criteria, is a variant. Reproduced when the Welch test gives p < 0.01; partially reproduced when 0.01 <= p < 0.05; not reproduced otherwise. The group means and SDs of PC1 are reported.

  • figs14k-tau-cortex-vs-spinal

    In slices, LC-NE neurons projecting to isocortex have significantly longer membrane time constants than those projecting to spinal cord (p < 0.001).

    Neurons projecting to isocortex had significantly longer membrane time constants than those projecting to cerebellum or spinal cord

    Results, Spatial variation of LC-NE activity during action-outcome learning, paragraph 5; Figure S14k, right, legend

    How it will be judged

    The membrane time constant per cell as the IPFX package computes it (Methods, Patch-seq recordings), from the dataset's hyperpolarising long-square subthreshold sweeps, with IPFX's long-square analysis at its default settings. The paper gives neither the IPFX version nor its settings, and the code reads frozen values from the Allen Institute's internal database; the defaults are a standard choice and are recorded as a deviation. The code's frozen per-cell values (code/data/LCNE_patchseq_S14_cell_table.csv at commit 79fa20a, missing for 14 cells) are shown as a variant and compared with the recomputed values cell by cell. Cells without a time constant are left out. Sweep inclusion as in the Methods, the code's choice (none) a variant. Two-sided Welch t-test (the code's; the legend's "paired" t-test cannot apply to independent groups), Student's t-test a variant. The IPFX settings are the reader's standard choice, not the paper's, so they cannot decide the outcome alone. The test is also run on the code's frozen values (the paper's own IPFX output); if that gives a different outcome from the recomputed values, the claim is blocked as methods_underspecified with both shown. Reproduced when the cortex mean exceeds the spinal cord mean and p < 0.001; partially reproduced when the cortex mean is higher with 0.001 <= p < 0.05; not reproduced otherwise. The group means and SDs and the number of cells with a value are reported.
