  • data-scale

    The paper made 19 paired spine Ca2+ imaging and HD-MEA recordings in 10 cultures, tested 20 spines (one per recording, two in one recording), and the recordings have a mean of 102 ± 43 sorted units each.

    we conducted 19 paired spine Ca 2+ imaging and HD-MEA recording experiments in 10 different cell cultures ... based on 19 recordings with a mean of 102 ± 43 units per recording ... (in one of recordings two spines were chosen)

    Results, 3.4 Performance evaluation on experimental data, paragraphs 1 and 2; Table 1

    How it will be judged

    Counts of recordings, cultures and spines are exact, as they describe the data collected. Units are the curated sorted units of each recording. The paper does not say over which set the mean and spread are taken or which SD is meant; Table 1 settles it, as 19 recordings (rows 9 and 10 counted once) with the population SD (ddof 0) give 101.6 ± 42.6, while 20 rows give 105.5 ± 44.8 and the sample SD gives 43.8. That reading is used, and the other is shown. The per-row unit counts of Table 1 are compared with the files as well, each within 10%.

  • table1-best-r

    For each of the 20 tested spines, the best-R (the Pearson R of the demixed spine trace with the convolved trace of its best-matching unit, outside network bursts) has the value given in Table 1, from 0.148 to 0.765.

    How it will be judged

    The pipeline of the opening comment, from the Methods 2.4 to 2.6 and notebook 1 of the code, with each detail cited there. Each spine is matched to its Table 1 row by culture and unit count; a spine's best-R matches when it is within 10% of the Table 1 value under at least one of the readings of the open details (p_thresh 96 to 99, hyperactive strategy (i) or (ii)), as the paper chose p_thresh per data set without reporting it. Every reading's value is shown. Reproduced when all 20 spines match; partially reproduced when 15 to 19 match; not reproduced otherwise. The lowess variant (frac 0.0002) and the code's default pair (97, (i)) are shown.

  • fig1e-best-r-gap

    In the example recording of Figure 1(e), the best-R shows the largest increase over the next ordered R value among all units.

    Moreover, the R value for the best-matching unit ( best-R ) shows the largest increase with respect to the next (ordered) R value among all units

    Results, 3.1 Identifying the presynaptic unit of a dendritic target spine, last paragraph; Figure 1(e)

    How it will be judged

    The paper does not say which recording is the example. For each spine, the R values of all units are sorted and the gap between best-R and the second R is compared with every other gap between consecutive sorted values. Reproduced when the first gap is the largest for at least one spine (the example is one of them); the number of spines for which it holds is reported. A claim about one unnamed example, so a weak test.

  • table1-optimal-thresholds

    For each recording, simulated ground-truth connections of every unit give an F-score surface over the quantile pair (Q_test, Q_GT), whose maximum and optimal quantiles are those of Table 1 (max_F 0.485 to 0.870).

    How it will be judged

    As in Results 3.3, Methods 2.8 and notebook 3 of the code. For every unit, 1'000 surrogates with the recording's noise and signal estimate (opening comment); for each surrogate the R with every unit's convolved trace over the kept frames; the test-Rs of unit i are the R of the surrogates of each surrogate's second-best unit with unit i's convolved trace (pooled over all 1'000 surrogates, imaging_tools.compute_test_r); the GT-Rs of unit i are the R of its own surrogates with its own trace. For each grid pair (100 values from 0 to 1, np.linspace rounded to 3 decimals, which gives the Table 1 quantiles), the threshold is the larger of the two quantiles; with unit i present, a surrogate whose best unit is i and whose best R passes is TP, else FN; with unit i removed, the best R of the other units passing is FP, else TN. F with beta 0.5, summed over all units. The Methods and the code disagree on a surrogate whose best unit is not i in the test with i present; the Methods count it FP if it passes and TN if not (main reading), the code counts it FN (variant). The noise estimate uses the background ROI of claim acceptance-9-of-20; for recording 3 (two spines) both spines' estimates are run. Each recording's max F matches when within 10% of Table 1; ties of the maximum are all reported, and Q_GT and Q_test match when one maximum lies within 10% of the Table 1 values. Reproduced when max F matches for at least 17 of the 19 recordings; partially for 10 to 16; not reproduced below. The quantiles are reported for each recording.
