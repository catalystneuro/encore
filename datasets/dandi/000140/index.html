    For the J-array dataset, the correlation across conditions between a unit's preparatory and peri-movement firing rates is positive on average but small (mean Pearson r = 0.13, p = 0.03; mean Spearman r = 0.11).

    Across the four datasets the mean correlation was positive (p=0.01, p=0.01, p=0.12, p=0.03) but only very modestly so (mean r = 0.21, 0.19, 0.06 and 0.13). Non-parametric correlations were similarly modest (mean Spearman's r = 0.20, 0.20, 0.06 and 0.11)

    How it will be judged

    Per unit, the vector of condition-mean preparatory rates (200 ms before to 100 ms after the go cue) is correlated with the vector of condition-mean peri-movement rates (100 ms before to 350 ms after movement onset), Methods. Units with > 5 spikes/s of preparatory tuning and peri-movement tuning at least 1.5 times as strong (Methods), under readings O1 and O2; trials with delay > 400 ms; trial filter under O4; movement onset under O3. The test of the mean against zero is not named; readings are (a) a two-sided one-sample t-test on r, (b) the same on Fisher z-transformed r, (c) a two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test. Reproduced when, under one reading, both means are within tolerance and the mean is positive at p < 0.05; partially reproduced when the mean is positive at p < 0.05 and below 0.3 but outside tolerance. Fewer conditions than the paper's 108 make r noisier per unit; the number of conditions and units used is reported.

  • fig6d-perimovement-best-j-array

    For the J-array dataset, the cross-validated performance of a preferred direction in capturing preparatory tuning is highest when the PD is expressed in the PCA-based peri-movement space, compared with the kinematic space and the task spaces.

    For every dataset, the highest level of performance was achieved ... when PDs were expressed in the peri-movement space

    Results, PD performance in a variety of spaces, paragraphs 1 to 3; Figure 6D

    How it will be judged

    Methods, Using a PD to capture preparatory tuning, PCA-based spaces and PD performance. Per unit (> 5 spikes/s preparatory tuning under O1, no peri-movement criterion), the preparatory rate vector is regressed on a c x k matrix S of condition locations plus an offset, with leave-one-condition-out cross-validation; performance is one minus the mean squared prediction error divided by the variance of the preparatory rates across conditions; averaged over units. The peri-movement space is the first k PC scores of the c x nt matrix of condition-mean rates from 150 ms before to 800 ms after movement onset of all units except the one fitted, not normalised, columns mean-centred (Supplemental Text). The kinematic space is the PC scores of a c x 2t-style matrix of horizontal and vertical hand position, velocity and acceleration from 150 ms before movement onset to the end of the reach, each variable scaled to unit range across times and conditions; the Methods say each neuron contributed one kinematic variable so the matrix matches the neural one in size, which is read as (a) the six variables each repeated equally to about n columns, or (b) the six variables once, as the stated scaling makes repetition change only the relative weights; the end of the reach is taken as 600 ms after movement onset (Figure 1 legend, reaches last about 200 to 600 ms) or as 800 ms, the neural window. Task spaces for the maze task as listed in Methods, Task spaces (initial velocity, endpoints + maximum speed, endpoints + orthogonal excursion, endpoints + viapoint, endpoints + maximum velocity, endpoints + halfpoint, segment directions, kitchen sink of 11 dimensions with barrier and distractor flags). PCA dimensionalities tested are not listed; readings are k = 1 to 14 (the range the Results give) and k = 1 to 20. Time step under O5. Reproduced when, under each reading, the best peri-movement performance over k exceeds the best kinematic performance and every task space; partially reproduced when it exceeds all but one. No EMG space can be built for J-array (no EMG recorded).
