  • fig4i-rmp-hyperpolarization

    In acute hippocampal slices, PAGER-Gi-expressing CA1 neurons show a greater hyperpolarizing shift in resting membrane potential with 100 nM DCZ plus 1 uM mCherry than with DCZ alone (mixed linear model, Z = 3.17, P = 0.0015).

    Neurons displayed a greater hyperpolarizing shift in RMP when presented with DCZ plus mCherry than with DCZ alone

    Results, Customized cell behaviours driven by PAGER G, paragraph 3; Fig. 4i and legend

    How it will be judged

    Measures from the Methods (Slice electrophysiology). Per sweep, RMP is the mean membrane potential over the 500 ms before any current injection. Rin is the steady-state voltage change to the -100 pA, 200-ms step (step voltage minus that sweep's RMP) divided by -100 pA; the steady-state window is not given, and the readings are the last 50 ms and the last 100 ms of the step. Cells whose initial Rin (first sweep) exceeds 500 megaohms are excluded (the paper prints "mΩ"), and sweeps with RMP above -50 mV or with no action potential during the ramp. Action potential detection is not given; the readings are (A1) an upward crossing of 0 mV and (A2) dV/dt above 20 V/s. The change "upon 2-min DCZ addition" is not defined beyond "before, during and after" the application; the readings are (W1) mean over included sweeps from DCZ onset to 2 min after onset minus the mean over included sweeps before onset, and (W2) mean over included sweeps from onset to the end of the recording minus the same baseline. Onset is the application time the dataset records. The test is a mixed linear model (statsmodels, as the Methods name) with treatment as fixed effect and a random intercept per mouse (Methods, Statistical analysis); the unit is not given, and the readings are (M1) one change per cell and (M2) every included sweep's change from baseline, with cell as a variance component within mouse. Under each combination of readings, the result is reproduced when the DCZ plus mCherry change is more negative than the DCZ change, the treatment effect has p < 0.05, and |Z| is within tolerance; partially reproduced when direction and p < 0.05 hold but |Z| is outside tolerance; not reproduced otherwise. The outcome is the one all combinations give; if they differ, the claim is blocked as methods_underspecified with every result shown. Group means and s.e.m. of the change are reported, as the figure plots them.
