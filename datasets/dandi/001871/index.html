Dataset version 0.260630.1657 · tested 2026-11-12 · 8 open findings

  • Description missingThe complex-plume behavior files come in two sets, 'smoke-1a' (24 files) and 'smoke-2a' (24), recorded on the same days and vials, each with 12 flipped and 12 unflipped projections, whose session descriptions name two different movies (smoke_1a and smoke_2a) and whose signals differ (99th percentile of the intensity 171 and 252; the turn-direction coefficient of motion is positive in one set and negative in the other, in both projection flips). Nothing in the files or the dandiset says what smoke_2a is; the dandiset's description says the deposited smoke_1a_orig plume was analysed throughout the paper, the code selects smoke-2a only, and the Yale Dataverse deposit lists both as complex plume. The two sets' intensity layouts across y are not mirrored relative to each other, and the mean gradient cue of neither follows its mean intensity profile, so a sign convention differing between the sets cannot be ruled out from the files.
    Found by the attempt on Brudner et al. 2026 (brudner-2026-01, 2026-11-12)
  • Description missingThe turn_events table gives direction as +1 or -1 ("Turn direction label") without saying which is left or right, and the heading is described as in "standardized arena coordinates" but is atan2(-vy, vx), so y is reversed relative to the heading. Left and right turns cannot be named, which the paper's rightward bias towards the projector needs.
    Found by the attempt on Brudner et al. 2026 (brudner-2026-01, 2026-11-12)
  • Description missingThe turn events are not described. Their start comes a median of 4 frames (about 67 ms) after the turn onset the SI defines (20% of the peak rotational speed on the rising edge) and a median of 1 frame after the crossing of half the peak; they stop a median of 1 frame before the falling crossing of half the peak and 4 frames before the falling 20% crossing, and last 5 to 11 frames (all but 8 of 15841 sampled events 5 to 9). Turn displacements over the events (median about 21 degrees) are smaller than the paper's 34 degrees, which the turns bounded by the 20% crossings give.
    Found by the attempt on Brudner et al. 2026 (brudner-2026-01, 2026-11-12)
  • Description missingThe odor signals in the behavior files cannot be aligned with the deposited complex plume window. No file gives the movie frame shown at a trajectory frame or the trial's start in the movie.
    Found by the attempt on Brudner et al. 2026 (brudner-2026-01, 2026-11-12)
  • Description missingodor_velocity (the motion cue) is NaN on 95 to 98% of frames, which its description ("estimated from frame-to-frame correlation of antenna-segment odor profiles") does not explain; the paper's code holds the last value through the gaps.
    Found by the attempt on Brudner et al. 2026 (brudner-2026-01, 2026-11-12)

  • Description missing205 of 7235 trajectories have no position at all (every x and y NaN) and are stored at full length, with nothing to say why; in 47 files some trajectories carry timestamps and others a starting time and rate.
