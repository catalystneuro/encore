Problems that change results 2

The data are wrong in a way the files do not show, so an analysis that takes them as given comes out wrong. Each was checked against the notebook’s outputs by someone other than the attempt’s author.

  • Metadata errorChanges resultsTwo recordings are deposited twice under different days. sub-JF-dLight-ramp2-F2 Day3 and Day4 hold identical photometry (381,921 samples), cues and licks, as do sub-JF-dLight-ramp2-F4 Day5 and Day6 (330,189 samples). An analysis that counts sessions counts each of them twice, and one of each pair is filed under a day on which, as far as the files show, no other recording exists.#
    Checked: Shown, and critical is right. Cell 9 shows identical 470 hashes, sample counts (381,921 and 330,189), cue counts and lick counts for F2 Day3/Day4 and F4 Day5/Day6. Cue and lick identity is shown only through the counts. One recording deposited as two, labelled as different days, is critical by definition.
    Found by the attempt on Garud et al. 2026 (garud-2026-01, 2026-11-25)
  • Metadata errorChanges resultsThe photometry and the event log are on different clocks and the files do not say so. Every photometry series states a start of 0 (at most 8 microseconds), while the event log has its own zero; aligned by the TTL2 channel, the photometry starts 3.6 to 251 s before the event log's zero. The only link is photometry_ttl2, described in every file only as "raw photometry channel ttl2", whose rising edges fall on the cue onsets (event code 15). A user who takes both time bases as given places dopamine seconds to minutes away from the cues and rewards.#
    Checked: Shown in substance, and critical is right. Cell 7 shows TTL2 edges fitting cue times within 0.094 s, and the photometry starting 3.6 to 251.3 s before the event-log zero. "Starts at time 0" and the TTL2 description are not printed. Signals on different clocks with no stated link are critical; the 64 files with the wrong rate would make the problem visible, but the other 292 sessions would not.
    Found by the attempt on Garud et al. 2026 (garud-2026-01, 2026-11-25)
