Findings about the data 12

What the attempts that used this dataset found about the data themselves, for its owner and the archive. The claims and their outcomes are on each paper’s page.

  • Data missingThe Data availability statement says the functional MRI data are deposited in this dandiset, but it holds only derived values (seed connectivity of the voxels of 17 target ROIs for monkeys N, T and D, and three group-average connectomes). There are no fMRI or DWI images, no whole-brain seed maps, no seed connectivity for the nonsurgical controls B, W and H, and no per-monkey connectomes, so the voxelwise cluster maps of Fig. 5a, Fig. 6d and Extended Data Figs. 7, 8 and 10d cannot be tested.
    Found by the attempt on Fujimoto et al. 2026 (fujimoto-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Description missingThe connectomes in "Source Data FC matrix.xlsx" (123 x 123) carry no ROI names. The Fig. 4 script reads them from atlas key and reorder files (CHARM_key_4.txt, SARM_key_4.txt, reorder_rois.txt) that are not deposited, so no ROI can be named and the network sorting of Fig. 4c cannot be repeated.
    Found by the attempt on Fujimoto et al. 2026 (fujimoto-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Metadata errorThe legend in dbs_behavior_data_a.xlsx codes foraging as yes 2 and no 1, but every foraging value is 0 or 1; read as 1 yes, the values give the paper's statistics. The same legend gives group codes 1 (DBS) and 0 (non-stimulation control), while monkey D's rows carry group 2.
    Found by the attempt on Fujimoto et al. 2026 (fujimoto-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • OtherIn CC1_MCC_CB_data_a.xlsx two fields of monkey T's control side (section T423, fields 3 and 4) carry CC1/DAPI ratios that do not match their counts (44.9 and 35.4 % against 35.4 and 44.2 % from the counts); field 4's ratio is field 3's. Two MCC-CB fields have more CC1-positive than DAPI-positive cells (ratio above 100 %). The paper's script uses the ratio column; with the ratio from the counts the side effect changes from F = 9.00 to 9.03.
    Found by the attempt on Fujimoto et al. 2026 (fujimoto-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Metadata errorIn dbs_EMgratio_MCC_CB_data_a.xlsx the column headed "diameter of axon(pixel)" holds half the mean of the long and short diameters (a radius), and "diameter of axon(nm)" the same in nm. The g-ratio column is consistent with the Methods' definition.
    Found by the attempt on Fujimoto et al. 2026 (fujimoto-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Published code disagreesThe Fig. 4 script counts an ROI as changed when `ranksum` of its changes against a vector of zeros gives P < 0.05/nrois (nrois = 123), while the Methods and the Fig. 4b legend give a rank-sum test at "P < 0.01 with Bonferroni correction". The script's threshold reproduces the 69 and 44 ROIs of Supplementary Tables 1 and 2, whose largest listed P is 3.9e-4, above 0.01/122.
    Found by the attempt on Fujimoto et al. 2026 (fujimoto-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Paper inconsistentThe Fig. 6a legend gives the PrePost x side x tract interaction as F(1,1238) = 6.02, P = 0.0025. The data give F = 6.02 and P = 0.0025 with 2 numerator df, as three tracts imply.
    Found by the attempt on Fujimoto et al. 2026 (fujimoto-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Methods not availableFor fig6e-monkey-d-no-network-reduction, the paper does not say whether "the widespread reductions ... were not observed" in monkey D covers the control hemisphere. Its own Fig. 6e legend lists a significant control-side CEN difference, which the data show to be a decrease (P = 0.0089). The Methods list LIM without area 25, while the script and the legend's voxel counts include it. The authors could say which hemisphere the sentence refers to.
    Found by the attempt on Fujimoto et al. 2026 (fujimoto-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Description missingThe dandiset's metadata names the paper only in its title, with no related resource, and gives no species, approach, measurement technique or variables measured. The sheets have no data dictionary beyond the legends inside some of them.
    Found by the attempt on Fujimoto et al. 2026 (fujimoto-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Methods not availableThe seed connectivity tables hold one value per voxel and session, and the file names say "concatenated". The paper does not say whether a session's seed map was computed on concatenated runs or averaged over per-run maps, and no per-run maps are deposited.
    Found by the attempt on Fujimoto et al. 2026 (fujimoto-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Published code disagreesThe FigE1 script computes the foraging standard errors from column 2, the movement column (lines 91 to 96), so the error bars of the foraging plot in Extended Data Fig. 1 are those of movement.
    Found by the attempt on Fujimoto et al. 2026 (fujimoto-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • OtherTwo scripts are deposited twice under two names with identical text (`Fig4 Fujimoto_NatNeuro_2026_FCmatrix.m` and `Fig4andE10C_Fujimoto_NatNeuro_2026_FCmatrix.m`; `Fig6DandE10D_dbs_FC_25norm_mkD_analysisscript_a.m` and `Fig6DandE10Ddbs_FC_25norm_mkD_analysisscript_a.m`).
    Found by the attempt on Fujimoto et al. 2026 (fujimoto-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
