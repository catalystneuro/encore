  • fig6df-epsp-ipsp-at-rest

    At the resting membrane potential, photostimulation of DRN-DAT terminals evoked both excitatory and inhibitory post-synaptic potentials in downstream cells of the BNST, CeA and BLP (19, 36 and 48 cells recorded).

    Optical stimulation at the resting membrane potential evoked both excitatory and inhibitory post-synaptic potentials (EPSPs and IPSPs) in downstream cells

    Results, DRNDAT input has divergent effects on downstream cellular excitability, paragraph 2; Figure 6D-F

    How it will be judged

    Counts are compared exactly (tolerance 0) because they are cell counts the dataset either holds or not; a count that differs is reported, and the claim is then judged on the cells present. Per cell, the average of the light sweeps at rest (Methods, Ex vivo electrophysiology); the EPSP peak and IPSP trough are measured against the 5 s before stimulation (Methods). A cell is 'excitation' with an EPSP only, 'inhibition' with an IPSP only, 'mix' with both, 'none' otherwise (Methods; Figure 6D-F legend). The paper gives no threshold for calling a deflection an EPSP or an IPSP; the readings run are a peak (EPSP) or trough (IPSP) of the average trace within 0-5.5 s of light onset (the window the Methods use for total voltage area) beyond (a) 2 or (b) 3 standard deviations of the 5 s baseline of the average trace. Reproduced when, under the readings, each of the three regions has at least one cell with an EPSP and the pooled cells include cells with an IPSP; the proportions of the four classes per region are reported. If the readings disagree the claim is blocked as methods_underspecified.
