  • fig1b-delta-decrease

    In amygdalae outside the SOZ, delta power (1-4 Hz) is lower during AVERSIVE than during NEUTRAL sequences, and the reduction lasts through the AVERSIVE sequence.

    In nSOZ amygdalae, DELTA band power was consistently reduced throughout the duration of the AVERSIVE sequence (Fig. 1B)

    Results 2.1, paragraph 1; Figure 1B

    How it will be judged

    The iEEG power method of the opening comment (Methods 4.8 and 4.11), run under each reading listed there (z-scoring, amygdala set, cluster statistic, mixed model or the unpaired t-test variant). Reproduced when, under the mixed model, at least one cluster with AVERSIVE below NEUTRAL has permutation p < 0.05 and the AVERSIVE mean delta power is below the NEUTRAL mean over the 24 s block, under every reading of the open details; blocked as methods_underspecified when the readings disagree. The Results say "throughout the duration" and Discussion 3.1 says the suppression "lasted for most of the AVERSIVE sequence"; no number is given in the main text (the abstract says "lasting up to 18 s"). "Most" is read as more than half of the 24 s block, and the claim is partially reproduced when the significant clusters cover less than half of it. The extent of the clusters is reported, and compared with the 18 s of the abstract.
