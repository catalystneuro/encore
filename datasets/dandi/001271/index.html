Dataset version 0.250805.1352 · tested 2026-11-01 · 11 open findings

  • Metadata errorDANDI:001271 states in every file that 1 V translates to 36 V/cm, but its stated voltages give the fields marked in Figure S2 at 18 V/cm per V (45, 63, 72; 90, 108, 117; 54, 72, 81 V/cm match neurons tested at 2.5, 3.5, 4; 5, 6, 6.5; 3, 4, 4.5 V before and after the change) and at no voltages at 36 V/cm per V. The voltages are likely peak-to-peak, which the 001271 descriptions do not say (001032 to 001035 say peak-to-peak; 001030 says one polarity).
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2025 (semenov-2025-02, 2026-11-01)
  • Metadata errorThe descriptions of the 71 TTX sessions of DANDI:001271 say TTX abolished the action potentials and "caused sustained depolarization during application of stimulating waves". The paper and the dandiset's own description say TTX prevented it, and the data agree with them (mean dF over the last 100 ms of the stimulus below zero after TTX at every voltage up to 10 V).
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2025 (semenov-2025-02, 2026-11-01)
  • Description missingDANDI:001271 does not mark the solution change. Sessions before it carry the same label, "without tetrodotoxin (vehicle control)", as vehicle sessions after it; the change of the 11 vehicle neurons was placed from gaps between session start times, and 3 vehicle-labelled neurons (13_20240731, 17_20240801, 4_20240730) show no such gap.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2025 (semenov-2025-02, 2026-11-01)
  • Description missingThe files of 001271, 001272 and 001032 to 001035 hold 16 x 16 frames, where the paper describes 64 x 64 images after 4 x 4 binning, with no soma ROI, no stimulus record and no stimulus onset; the timing (50 ms onset, 250 ms) is from the Methods and agrees with the aliased stimulus at 10 and 12 V (52 to 299 ms) and with the AP times.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2025 (semenov-2025-02, 2026-11-01)
  • OtherIn every file of 001271, 001030 and 001032 to 001035 the first frame is 1.5 to 24 times brighter than the rest and the last frame a few percent brighter than the one before, which no description mentions.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2025 (semenov-2025-02, 2026-11-01)
  • OtherAll 178 data arrays of DANDI:001034 are identical to those of DANDI:001032, with the same start times, under other subject identifiers (1_3 for 1_1 and so on); the same recordings are deposited twice as parts 1 and 3.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2025 (semenov-2025-02, 2026-11-01)
  • Metadata errorSome subjects of DANDI:001032 hold sessions of more than one cell. The 20-kHz sessions of 13_1 show the same image as the sessions of 14_1 (correlation 0.997) and correlate 0.09 with 13_1's 2-kHz sessions; 9 subjects of 001032, 001033 and 001035 have pairs of sessions whose mean frames correlate below 0.8.

    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2025 (semenov-2025-02, 2026-11-01)
