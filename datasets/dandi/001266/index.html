Dataset version 0.250710.0758 · tested 2026-11-01 · 8 open findings

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds the example recordings of the figures (the 83 rows of the Source Data index, 84 files), not the pooled samples behind the paper's statistics: the 17 neurons of Fig. 3, the 43 branches of Fig. 4b, the 28 cells of Fig. 5b, the drug series of Figs. 6 and S16 to S18, the temperature series, the patch recordings of Fig. S1 and the calibration series of Figs. S3 to S5. 29 of the 55 claims cannot be tested for this reason.
    Found by the attempt on Park et al. 2025 (park-2025-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Description missingThe files hold only raw camera movies and DAQ channels. They carry no pixel size, no structural (2P or HiLo) or eYFP images, no stimulus masks, and no regions of interest, so distances from the soma, stimulus positions and the user-drawn regions of the paper cannot be recovered from the files. The pixel size used here (0.65 µm) is from the Methods.
    Found by the attempt on Park et al. 2025 (park-2025-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Metadata errorEvery file is its own subject: subject_id is the file name (81 subjects for 84 files), date_of_birth is 1900-01-01 and sex is U. Animals and cells can be told apart only through the file names (for example PP070-P16-Cell2).
    Found by the attempt on Park et al. 2025 (park-2025-01, 2026-11-01)
  • OtherThree recordings are deposited twice with identical sizes and first frames: sub-153924PP070-P27-Cell1-soma-step-5ms (one copy named without a session), sub-000919PP070-P17-V2-11-freq-opto and sub-000820PP070-P17-V2-11-freq-opto-2khz (two copies each, with different obj- suffixes).
    Found by the attempt on Park et al. 2025 (park-2025-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Metadata errorCamera frames are 1.03 ms apart (971 Hz; 1.04 ms in one file) where the paper states 1 kHz, and 0.52 ms (1923 Hz) where it states 2 kHz. The movie and the DAQ record span the same time, so the timestamps are consistent; the stated rates are rounded.
    Found by the attempt on Park et al. 2025 (park-2025-01, 2026-11-01)
  • Description missingThe camera series has "no description", and the DAQ channels are named only by short labels that differ between files for the same signal (the EFS trigger is stim1, stim1 (distal), EC, EC_elec or EC_stim). The amplifier gains are given only in the dandiset description, not in the files.
    Found by the attempt on Park et al. 2025 (park-2025-01, 2026-11-01)
  • OtherFile names and figure legends name stimulus sites differently: the Fig. 2 legend's "D1 stim" is the file named D3, and the Fig. 3 legend's D2 is the file named D4 (its bAP count, 84, matches the legend's 49 + 35). The Source Data index resolves the first; counts resolve the second.
    Found by the attempt on Park et al. 2025 (park-2025-01, 2026-11-01)
  • OtherIn the Fig. 2 recording 7 to 10 of the 54 trials have three somatic spikes, where the paper says each stimulus evoked two, and most dSpikes ride on the third spike, where the paper says they always emerged from the second.
    Found by the attempt on Park et al. 2025 (park-2025-01, 2026-11-01)
