Dataset version 0.260825.0222 · tested 2026-11-03 · 7 open findings

  • Description missingThe files carry no cell type, VEN subtype or transcriptomic type, although the dandiset's description says the recordings are annotated with transcriptomic cell types. These labels exist only in the paper's code repository (metaInfo_ephysio_patchSeq.csv), matched by file name.
    Found by the attempt on Liu et al. 2026 (liu-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Metadata errorFive pairs of files hold identical voltage data in every sweep under different cell IDs, four of them under different subjects (s640 and n5, B722 and B726, s653 and n29, s485 and s676, B289 and n175). In the pair B289 and n175 the cell table gives the two cells different transcriptomic clusters (16 and 8). The dandiset holds 457 distinct recordings in 462 files.
    Found by the attempt on Liu et al. 2026 (liu-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Data missingThe dandiset holds 10 subjects, where the paper says the Patch-seq experiments used 12 animals, and it holds only the Patch-seq cells. The additional whole-cell recordings without RNA (281 VENs and 306 L5 PCs of Fig. 4a-c, 85 VEN-L and 41 VEN-S of Extended Data Fig. 9c,d and Supplementary Table 8) are not in it, and the paper does not say where they are.
    Found by the attempt on Liu et al. 2026 (liu-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Metadata errorEvery file has the same session start time (2018-01-01 01:00 +08:00), a placeholder. Two session IDs carry the date of another subject (sess_180920d2 under Mfa181204, sess_220419d1HS under Mfa220428).
    Found by the attempt on Liu et al. 2026 (liu-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Metadata errorThe stimulus series are stated in amperes but hold pA (values -200 to 1,480 in steps of 20), and their starting time is NaN. Four files whose device is AXON recording system are sampled at 25 kHz like the HEKA files, and one HEKA file at 50 kHz.
    Found by the attempt on Liu et al. 2026 (liu-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Identifier missingThe 62 AIS distances and peak indices of Fig. 4i (NeuronPeakIdx_summary.mat in the paper's code) carry no cell IDs, and their values match none of the per-cell tables that have IDs, so they cannot be linked to the recordings. The code's morphology table gives an AIS distance for 153 Patch-seq cells, so it does not say which 62 were used either.
    Found by the attempt on Liu et al. 2026 (liu-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • OtherThe Methods put the Patch-seq recordings on a HEKA amplifier and the recordings without RNA on a MultiClamp 700B, but 139 files with a transcriptomic type have the device AXON recording system; the device follows the recording year (mostly Axon for the 2018 subjects, HEKA for 2022). AP half-width and tau are longer in the Axon files in every group, in the computed values and in the paper's feature table (half-width 3.13 against 2.08 ms, tau 41 against 26 ms), and bringing Axon files to the HEKA sampling rate does not change them, so the recording conditions differ between the two sets. The groups are unevenly spread over them (L5 IT FEZF2 93 of 125 Axon files, L2/3 IT HPCAL1 6 of 30), and the narrow APs and short tau of L2/3 IT HPCAL1 are not significant within either set.
    Found by the attempt on Liu et al. 2026 (liu-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
