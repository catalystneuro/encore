  • cell-classes-counts

    PoSub units split into 1,835 putative excitatory cells and 427 putative FS cells by firing rate and waveform, and 1,602 excitatory cells (87%) were HD cells with HD information above 0.2 bits per spike; the vertical recordings gave 931 units from 14 mice and the parallel ones 1,999 units from 18 mice.

    The probe was implanted either vertically ( n = 931 units from 14 mice, range ... or parallel to PoSub cell layers ( n = 1,999 units from 18 mice ... PoSub units were subdivided into putative excitatory cells ( n = 1,835) and putative PoSub-FS cells ( n = 427) based on mean firing rate and waveform shape ... were classified as PoSub-HD cells (>0.2 bits per spike, n = 1602, 87% of excitatory cells

    Results, High-density recordings in the PoSub of freely moving mice, paragraph 1; Methods, Spike sorting and unit classification, and HD tuning curves and tuning metrics

    How it will be judged

    Classification as fixed in the opening comment (Methods). The 0.4 ms trough-to-peak cut is the main reading and the code's 0.35 ms a variant; the HD threshold is read as 0.2 bits per spike (Results, Methods and code) and as the 99th percentile of the excitatory cells' time-reversed HD information (Methods' definition). The fraction can be 87% (Results) or 85% (Methods, "85% of putative excitatory cells"); either value within tolerance counts. Unit counts are over all units of each probe orientation as the dandiset's metadata gives it. The dandiset holds 31 of the 32 sessions, so a count below the paper's by one session's cells is reported as such. If the dandiset carries its own cell-type labels, they are compared with the recomputed classes and both counts are shown.
