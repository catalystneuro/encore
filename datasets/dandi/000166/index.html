  • fig1f-sink-b-deeper

    The layer 3/4 border sink of the DOWN-UP transition of non-REM slow oscillations (landmark b) lies slightly but significantly deeper than the early light-evoked sink (b') in the same mice, by 39 ± 49 um.

    Sink b, associated with the “UP” state of slow oscillation during non-REM sleep, was slightly but significantly deeper than the light-evoked sink b′ in the layer 3/4 border (39 ± 49 μm

    Results, Anchoring Mesoscopic Patterns to Physiological Landmarks, paragraph 2; Figure 1F

    How it will be judged

    Per mouse with light stimulation, the average CSD of DOWN-UP transitions (onsets from the UP and DOWN detection of the STAR Methods, as in buzcode DetectSlowWaves) and of light onsets, both as the second spatial difference of the LFP along the 64 sites (Figure 1D, 1E). The paper does not fix the time window or how a sink's depth is read from the map; the readings are the depth of the most negative CSD near the layer 3/4 border within (a) 0 to 100 ms after onset (the 100 ms pulse of the Results) and (b) 0 to 50 ms after onset (the early sink). Reproduced when, under a reading, b is deeper than b' with a two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank p < 0.05 and the mean difference is within tolerance (the paper's SD). CSD spatial smoothing is not given; none is applied (standard choice; it shifts a sink by less than a site).

  • fig2b-six-coherence-clusters

    Clustering the 64 sites by their pairwise gamma (30-100 Hz) coherence with a gradient-descent algorithm gives six clusters whose five boundaries are consistent across mice in normalized depth.

    the recording sites were clustered on the basis of their resulting cross-coherence matrix, resulting in six clusters whose five boundaries were consistent across animals when projected into normalized depth coordinates

    Results, Anchoring Mesoscopic Patterns to Physiological Landmarks, paragraph 3; Figures 2A, 2B

    How it will be judged

    Coherence 30-100 Hz between all site pairs during waking (Figure 2A), clustered by the energy-gap rule of the STAR Methods (Gamma coherence and gradient descent clustering), from random starts, repeated (the paper says several times; 20 repeats is a standard choice) keeping the lowest energy. The paper does not say how the number of clusters reaches six (the algorithm merges to a local minimum). Readings are (a) the number of clusters at convergence, and (b) six clusters imposed. The boundary consistency test is unclear in what it compares; readings are (a) each boundary's normalized depth against the matching gamma IC boundary in the same mouse, and (b) each mouse's boundary depths against the across-mouse median. Reproduced when, under some reading, six clusters result in every mouse and no boundary differs at p < 0.05 by the signed-rank test.

  • fig2c-six-gamma-ics

    ICA of the 30-100 Hz LFP yields six main independent components whose spatial loadings are highly consistent across mice and whose depth distribution is related to the anatomical layers.

    ICA yielded six main independent components (ICs) that were highly consistent across animals (p < 0.001, Spearman’s correlation for ICs spatial loadings)

    Results, Anchoring Mesoscopic Patterns to Physiological Landmarks, paragraph 3; Figure 2C

    How it will be judged

    Per mouse, the LFP band-passed 30-100 Hz, reduced by PCA to its first ten PCs, then runica (EEGLAB, Bell and Sejnowski; STAR Methods, Independent Component Analysis of LFPs); ICs ranked by variance explained, loadings as the inverse mixing matrix projected on depth. The paper does not say which six of the ten are the main ones or how ICs are matched across mice. Readings are (a) the six with the highest variance, matched by the depth of peak loading, and (b) the six whose loadings are confined in depth (not spread over all sites), matched likewise. The correlation test is (a) each mouse's loading against the across-mouse mean, or (b) all pairs of mice, in normalized depth. The brain state the ICA is run on is not given (Figure S2H compares IC variance across states, which suggests one decomposition per session); readings are the whole session and waking only. Reproduced when, under a reading, the median Spearman correlation is positive with p < 0.001 for each of the six ICs.
