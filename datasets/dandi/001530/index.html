Dataset version 0.260605.0601 · tested 2026-11-02 · 6 open findings

  • Description missingThe dandiset gives each unit a probe location and an Allen CCF label but not which CCF labels make up the paper's five areas. Only with data_helpers/Area_list.mat from the Zenodo record do the unit counts match the paper (10,294); without it 12,066 units sit on the five areas' probes.
    Found by the attempt on Ghaderi et al. 2026 (ghaderi-2026-01, 2026-11-02)
  • Metadata errorIn 12 optogenetic sessions (11 of mouse PG061 and one of PG021) the subject genotype is C57BL/6JRj, which carries no ChR2, although the paper inactivates VGAT-ChR2 mice and light trials in these sessions reduce licking as in the others; the other sessions of PG021 give VGAT_ChR2-eYFP.
    Found by the attempt on Ghaderi et al. 2026 (ghaderi-2026-01, 2026-11-02)
  • Metadata errorThe units' spike_width is described as "Trough to peak in ms" but holds seconds (0.0001 to 0.0012); times 1000 the RS and FS flags fit the Methods' thresholds (RS 0.467 to 1.200 ms, FS 0.100 to 0.333 ms).
    Found by the attempt on Ghaderi et al. 2026 (ghaderi-2026-01, 2026-11-02)
  • Metadata errorSeveral trial-table descriptions do not fit their columns, for example opto_area ("laser light duration", holds an area name), opto_stim_start_time ("Pulse duration in s"), opto_stim_stop_time ("Individual pulse timestamps") and context ("Boolean for sound", holds go_tone, nogo_tone or no_tone).
    Found by the attempt on Ghaderi et al. 2026 (ghaderi-2026-01, 2026-11-02)
  • OtherEleven trials of sub-PG028_ses-20210318T163504 have trial_type 0, outside the five types, and sub-PG076_ses-20220502T140636 has no No-tone trials. 343 trials have no video frames in their -1 to 2 s window, at least one in every session and most in sub-PG061_ses-20220212T233813 (243) and sub-PG038_ses-20210316T152753 (29), so they cannot be classed as quiet. sub-PG038_ses-20210317T180556 has no unit that passes the CCF filter.
    Found by the attempt on Ghaderi et al. 2026 (ghaderi-2026-01, 2026-11-02)
  • Data missingThe detected lick times and lick mask used for the lick coding direction (Fig. 8b, Supplementary Fig. 18) are only in the Zenodo record (Lick_times.mat); the dandiset holds the raw piezo lick trace and the jaw trace, and its BehavioralEvents hold only Valve_OpeningTime in all 35 sessions (printed in reproduce_ghaderi_2026.ipynb, fig8b-lick-coding-direction).
    Found by the attempt on Ghaderi et al. 2026 (ghaderi-2026-01, 2026-11-02)
