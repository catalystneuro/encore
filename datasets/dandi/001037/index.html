  • ed1n-engaging-x1-slower

    In freely behaving mice engaging in aggression, rSLDS fits show an integration dimension x1 with a longer time constant than the orthogonal dimension x2, an approximate line attractor.

    revealed an approximate line attractor under both conditions, exhibiting ramping and persistent activity aligned and maintained across both performed and observed attack sessions

    Extended Data Fig. 1n and 1o; Results, Line attractor for observing aggression, paragraph 1

    How it will be judged

    For each mouse, an rSLDS with 2 latent dimensions fit to the engaging session (number of states by fivefold cross-validation, Methods, or 3 states with the sample code's settings; both readings run). The cvR2 of each fit is reported; no mouse is excluded by the Methods' 70% rule, since the paper reports n = 5 in both conditions while the mean observation cvR2 it gives (67.2%) is below 70%. Time constants from the eigenvalues of each state's dynamics matrix as the sample code computes them; x1 is the dimension with the larger time constant in the state that holds the slow dynamics (the state with the largest time constant). Reproduced when the x1 time constants exceed the x2 time constants across the 5 mice by a two-tailed unpaired Mann-Whitney test at p < 0.05. The line attractor score, log2 of the largest over the second largest time constant (Methods), is reported per mouse.
