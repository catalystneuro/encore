Findings about the data 10

What the attempts that used this dataset found about the data themselves, for its owner and the archive. The claims and their outcomes are on each paper’s page.

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds the 21 OH16230 head recordings of Figure 6 only. The 21 OH16230 tail recordings, the 7 OH15500 heads (Table S6), and the imaging behind Figures 1 to 5 and 7 are not in it. The tails, the OH15500 heads and 10 atlas worms are in the paper's Zenodo deposit (doi:10.5281/zenodo.3906530).
    Found by the attempt on Yemini et al. 2021 (yemini-2021-01, 2026-10-30)
  • Description missingThe files do not say which AWC neuron is ON and which is OFF in each head, which the paper's AWC asymmetry result needs. The NeuroPAL image holds it: CyOFP1, the srsx-3 fluorophore (Table S1), is brighter relative to TagRFP-T in the AWC that the paper's Zenodo deposit marks as OFF (is_AWCL_OFF) in 19 of 21 heads.
    Found by the attempt on Yemini et al. 2021 (yemini-2021-01, 2026-10-30)
  • Metadata errorThe calcium imaging volume (CalciumImVol) states a grid spacing of 0.267, 0.267 and 1.5 um, the same as the NeuroPAL volume, but in all 21 files the calcium segmentation's x and y coordinates are half those of the same neurons in the NeuroPAL segmentation (fitted ratio 1.94 to 2.09; z 0.97 to 1.07), and the movie is 256 x 128 pixels against 512 x 256. The movie is binned 2 x 2, and its x and y spacing is 0.533 um.
    Found by the attempt on Yemini et al. 2021 (yemini-2021-01, 2026-10-30)
  • Metadata errorThe two trace series in each file, SignalRawFluor ("Raw fluorescence activity") and SignalRawFluordNMF ("Raw fluorescence activity data computed using dNMF"), hold the same numbers in all 21 files.
    Found by the attempt on Yemini et al. 2021 (yemini-2021-01, 2026-10-30)
  • Metadata errorNeuronIDs, described as neuron labels, include three cells that are not neurons (AMSOL, AMSOR, amphid sheath glia; HMC, the head mesodermal cell) and, in head 20190929_06, DD6, a motor neuron of the posterior ventral cord that cannot be in a head volume. None of these four names is in the paper's deposit.
    Found by the attempt on Yemini et al. 2021 (yemini-2021-01, 2026-10-30)
  • Selection not documented126 named traces in the dandiset have no trace in the paper's deposit (AVL in 11 heads, I5 in 8, and others), 113 of them with finite values and 13 all NaN; 101 of them have an empty slot in the deposit. The dandiset does not mark which traces the paper excluded (near duplicates, empty traces).
    Found by the attempt on Yemini et al. 2021 (yemini-2021-01, 2026-10-30)
  • Metadata errorThe stimulus table's description gives 2-butanone as "10^-4mM" (2,3-pentanedione as "10^-4") and the order as random; the paper gives both odors as 10^-4 dilutions and the order as rotated, and the files show three orders, seven heads each.
    Found by the attempt on Yemini et al. 2021 (yemini-2021-01, 2026-10-30)
  • OtherThe dandiset's traces are one frame (0.25 s) earlier than the paper's deposited traces in all 21 files: deposit frame k + 1 correlates best with dandiset frame k. Each dandiset trace has one frame fewer than its movie. Which of the two is aligned with the stimulus table is not stated.
    Found by the attempt on Yemini et al. 2021 (yemini-2021-01, 2026-10-30)
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the paper's authors. Of two neurons within 5 um whose traces correlate at 0.95 or more, were both removed, or one (and which), and on which traces was the correlation computed? The STAR Methods say they "were deemed too similar and removed". Run on the dandiset (with the corrected spacing), the rule removes 338 named traces keeping one member and 651 removing both, while the paper's deposit lacks 126, and only 20 and 27 of those are among the removed. Keeping one member comes closest to the per-class sample counts of Table S6, though agreement with its p values is about the same under every reading. Three results here depend on the choice (fig6-asj-ric, fig6gi-stimulus-specific-correlations and the head median of fig6-sample-sizes); one member is removed in this attempt, as an assumption.
    Found by the attempt on Yemini et al. 2021 (yemini-2021-01, 2026-10-30)
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the paper's authors. How were traces normalised for the response tests ("When comparing neuronal activity across animals, we used dNMF to normalize activity traces per neuron class")? The p values of Table S6 cannot be recomputed from the deposited dF/F0 traces with the test the STAR Methods describe (rank correlation of log p 0.57 to 0.75); tests on traces scaled one by one come much closer (0.87 to 0.96 with z-scores) and min-max scaling matches the size of the paper's confidence intervals, but none reproduces the table. Two outcomes here depend on the choice (fig6-nacl-unknown-interneurons, fig6-asj-ric); z-scores are used, as an assumption. How a head was judged to show the published ASE and AWC responses is also not described: under the claims file's rules 19 of the 21 deposited heads pass, not 21.
    Found by the attempt on Yemini et al. 2021 (yemini-2021-01, 2026-10-30)
