Most main findings that could be tested hold, but the 40-fold off-target suppression did not reproduce. Central YO-PRO-1 uptake rises with burst number (borderline in CT26), the one-burst peak is the sharpest of the study, and focusing improves from 1 Hz to 0.2 MHz packets. The center-to-electrode ratios came out at 32 and 45 against the reported 40 to 50, and they depend on which electrode footprint sets the background, which the paper does not specify. The log dependence on packet rate, raised electrodes, pulse duration, heating and the field simulation could not be tested because those data are not in this dandiset. The unipolar control also showed no decline toward the center.

Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds only the burst-number series of Fig. 7 and CANCAN and control wells at a packet rate of 1 Hz. The paper's 1 kHz and 10 kHz rates, raised electrodes, propidium images, post-exposure Hoechst, 300 and 900 ns pulses and thermometry are not in it. Three other dandisets citing the paper (001047, 001277, 001284) appear to hold several of these series, but 001276 does not point to them. None of the four names a 1 kHz or 10 kHz rate in its archive metadata.
  • Description missingThe files give no electrode positions and no ROI coordinates. Pixel size (1.625 um), exposure (200 ms for YO-PRO-1, 600 ms for Hoechst) and stage position are only in OME-XML appended to the free-text description of the image series, and one YO-PRO-1 file (P2_20240827_A3) has none. Cell line is in the subject strain, but protocol, packet rate, number of bursts, channel and phase are only in free-text descriptions. The stated inter-electrode distance of 10.0 mm fits the paper's 15.3 mm diagonal only as the edge-to-edge distance along a side; the electrode footprints in the images are 12.8 mm apart center to center at 1.625 um per pixel.
  • Selection not documentedThe dandiset has 3, 6, 3 and 3 CT26 wells and 5, 8, 7 and 8 CHO wells for 1, 2, 4 and 6 bursts (x4), where Fig. 7 reports n = 3 to 5 and n = 4, and CHO wells at 1 Hz that the paper does not report. Nothing records which wells the paper used.
  • Metadata errorEvery file's session_start_time is 2024-12-12, the conversion date; the experiment dates (June to September 2024) are only in the subject ids. Each subject is a culture well, but its age is given as P1D.
  • OtherTwo Hoechst files (P1_20240821_A1 and the 12.5 MB file of P1_20240821_A2) hold a second image plane that the description does not mention, with values near the camera offset. The 12.5 MB file covers 1% of the well and matches the same region of the full Hoechst image of the same well (r = 0.89), a fragment deposited twice. Well P1_20240702_A1 has no Hoechst file.
  • OtherIn three YO-PRO-1 images (P1_20240627_B1, P1_20240821_A1, P1_20240827_A1) the electrode footprints are too faint, or lie partly outside the stitched area, to locate the array, so the paper's center cannot be placed from the image; in some others the array is off-center and its lower footprints touch the edge of the stitched area.
  • Methods not availableThe paper does not give the spacing of the ROIs along the diagonals, the fit range used for Fig. 7, or which electrode footprint holds the background ROIs. With 1 mm spacing the 1-burst CT26 FWHM is 1.00 mm and with 0.5 mm spacing 1.09 mm, which decides whether the 7.3% and 2.3% figures are reproduced. The background footprint decides whether the 40 to 50-fold and 40-fold ratios are reproduced, since YP near the electrodes is about as small as the spread between footprints. Question for the authors, what ROI spacing and fit range were used for the FWHM values of Fig. 7, which footprint gave the background in each image, which wells of the dandiset form each group, and what background the Hoechst intensity inside the footprints was compared with (Fig. 5C).
