  • inhalation-sorting

    Inhalation durations appear bimodal, and a Gaussian-mixture and hierarchical clustering of inhalation waveforms sorts them into slow and fast inhalations; fast inhalations are shorter and have steeper airflow changes than slow ones.

    Since the distribution of inhalation durations appeared bimodal, we developed a semi-unsupervised method that sorted inhalations into slow and fast depending on their airflow rate waveforms ... Fast inhalations had shorter duration and steeper changes in airflow rate than slow inhalations.

    Results, Sniffing causes unstable odor responses in the PCx, paragraph 1; Figure 1C; STAR Methods, Inhalation clustering

    How it will be judged

    Per STAR Methods, Analysis of the breathing signal and Inhalation clustering (the code does not hold this step). Breathing signal band-passed 0.5 to 50 Hz, smoothed with a Gaussian of SD 25 ms; peaks at local minima, peaks closer than 65 ms to the next dropped; onset and offset at the zero crossings around the peak. Waveforms of 100 ms centred on the peak, normalised by their 2-norm, PCA to the fewest PCs explaining 98% of variance, GMM (full covariance, regularisation 0.05, 15 replicates, 10,000 iterations) with the number of clusters by the Calinski-Harabasz index, centroids merged by a correlation-distance hierarchical dendrogram into two clusters, the one with shorter mean inhalation length named fast. Where the dataset gives slow and fast labels, they are compared with this output (fraction of agreement reported). Reproduced when, in every mouse, the fast class has a shorter median inhalation length and steeper negative and positive inhalation slopes (10% to 90% of peak amplitude, Methods) than the slow class, and the leave-one-mouse-out accuracy is within tolerance. Open, and named as readings, is how the dendrogram is cut when it gives three clusters ("two clusters were similar and stemmed from the same branch" was decided by visual inspection) - (a) merge the two clusters sharing the nearest parent; (b) cut the dendrogram at two clusters. Which eight mice formed the cross-validation is not stated; all awake mice with breathing are used, and the eight-mouse sets are a variant when the count differs.
