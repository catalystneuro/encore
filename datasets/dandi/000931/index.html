Dataset version 0.261011.1559 · tested 2026-10-31 · 6 open findings

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds one NWB file from one of the paper's 22 mice, with the spike times of 1013 sorted units and their labels. The four signals the Methods say were recorded besides the spikes (the nasal airflow, the odor signal of the photo-ionization detector, the odor-trial TTL and the inhalation-onset TTL), the artificial airflow pulses, the unit waveforms and the brain area of each unit are not in it, and neither are the other 21 mice. All 37 claims need some of these, so none can be tested. The paper says its processed data are available from the lead contact on request, and its code deposit (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.10381293) loads preprocessed files it does not hold.
    Found by the attempt on Dehaqani et al. 2024 (dehaqani-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Description missingThe file gives no brain area for its units and has no electrodes table. Phy-good units lie along the whole probe (depth 80 to 3840 for the 359 good units, with 97, 127, 53 and 82 of them in the four 1000-um bands), so they include structures above the piriform cortex, and the paper's selection of PCx layer 2 and 3 units cannot be repeated. The experiment the mouse belongs to is given only by the keyword "Concentration Series", and the odors given are not stated.
    Found by the attempt on Dehaqani et al. 2024 (dehaqani-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Metadata errorKSLabel is "good" for all 1013 units and ContamPct is 100 for all of them. Kilosort labels a cluster good when its estimated contamination is low, so the two columns contradict each other and say nothing about the units. The Phy curation (quality) is good for 359 units, mua for 10 and missing for 644.
    Found by the attempt on Dehaqani et al. 2024 (dehaqani-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • Description missingOf the unit table's columns only quality, spike_times and unit_name have a description. The others (KSLabel, sh, ch, depth, Amplitude, amp, ContamPct, fr, n_spikes, original_cluster_id) say "No description.", and depth, Amplitude and amp have no unit. The session description is the converter's default.
    Found by the attempt on Dehaqani et al. 2024 (dehaqani-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • OtherThe file's related_publications names the bioRxiv preprint (10.1101/2023.04.04.535405), not the published paper, and the two 2024 versions of the dandiset (0.240305.0939 and 0.240305.0945) do not name the paper; version 0.261011.1559 and the draft do. All versions hold the same asset.
    Found by the attempt on Dehaqani et al. 2024 (dehaqani-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
  • OtherWhat could be checked is consistent with the paper. The Phy-good units have ISI violation rates (median 0.0066, Q1 0, Q3 0.029, by the Hill et al. formula at 1.5 ms) and presence ratios (1 in every quartile) in the ranges the Methods report, all fire above the 0.5 Hz threshold, no unit is duplicated, and the summed activity of the good units nearest the probe tip (by the standard Neuropixels 1.0 channel map, which depth follows; the file names no probe) peaks at 2.78 Hz, within the range of mouse breathing; the check of the half frequency leaves that peak at 2.78 Hz.
    Found by the attempt on Dehaqani et al. 2024 (dehaqani-2024-01, 2026-10-31)
