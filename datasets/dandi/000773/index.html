  • fig1c-old-faster-alarm

    For the pooled alarm pheromone stimuli, separate logistic curves for young and old ants fit better than a single curve, and old ants leave the nest faster.

    For the pooled alarm pheromone stimuli, the preferred model incorporated separate curves for young and old ants, with old ants responding faster

    Results, paragraph 1; Figure 1C; Table S1, row "Pooled alarm pheromone stimuli"

    How it will be judged

    From the Methods, Table S1 and alarm_behavior_calculations.py (commit 1aef6bc). For each trial of the three alarm stimuli and each time point from -10 to 60 s in 10 s steps, the proportion of young ants outside the nest (young outside over the colony's young, as the code computes it; the Methods' "by the total number within the arena" is settled by the code) and the same for old ants. The logistic Y = YM*Y0/((YM-Y0)*exp(-K*x)+Y0) of Table S1 is fit by least squares to all per-trial points, once shared (3 parameters) and once per age (6), and compared by the extra sum-of-squares F test; the residual degrees of freedom should be 314. Prism's starting values are not given; any start that converges is a standard choice. Reproduced when separate curves are preferred at p < 0.05, old ants are faster, and the six parameters are within tolerance. "Faster" is not defined; readings (a) K old above K young, (b) the old curve above the young curve at every time from 0 to 60 s. Partially reproduced when the test and direction hold but a parameter is out of tolerance.

  • fig2e-heptanone-breadth-falls

    At 48%, 4-methyl-3-heptanone activates a few more glomeruli in young than in old ants (medians of 4 against 2 responding ROIs), while the count for 4-methyl-3-heptanol does not differ with age.

    While there was no significant difference for 4-methyl-3-heptanol, 4-methyl-3-heptanone activated a few more glomeruli in young ants than in old ants (medians of 4 versus 2 responding ROIs)

    Results, paragraph 4; Figure 2E

    How it will be judged

    From the Methods (Image processing). For each 48% ant, ROIs of the size and shape of single glomeruli that respond in at least two trials, from the peak response maps; per ROI and trial the mean peak fold change; an ROI responds to an odor when its mean over that odor's trials is >= 0.2. The ROIs were drawn by hand; here they are the connected regions of a trial's map at >= 0.2 that recur, overlapping, in at least two trials of any odor, split at glomerulus size, a choice the paper does not fix, so the counts are also compared ant by ant with Data S2. This ROI procedure is a choice made here, not the paper's; when its outcome differs from the outcome the paper's counts in Data S2 give, the claim is blocked as methods_underspecified, with both shown. Motion-artifact trials under both readings of the shared open details. Wilcoxon rank-sum test (R defaults) of young against old per pheromone. Reproduced when 4-methyl-3-heptanone gives p < 0.05 with young higher and medians within tolerance, and 4-methyl-3-heptanol gives p >= 0.05. Partially reproduced when the direction and the 4-methyl-3-heptanol result hold but the medians or the significance do not.
