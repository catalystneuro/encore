  • Description missingStill present from yu-2016-01. No L4 file names the cell type of its neuron; the cell types the paper compares can only be inferred from light tagging, waveforms and light inactivation.
    Found by the attempt on Yu et al. 2016 (yu-2016-02, 2026-10-20)
  • Description missingStill present from yu-2016-01. No file marks the 9 ION-transected mice, and the files hold no surgery or notes field; the ION-cut claims cannot be tested and their cells sit in every control group.
    Found by the attempt on Yu et al. 2016 (yu-2016-02, 2026-10-20)
  • Metadata errorStill present from yu-2016-01. The genotype is missing for the 6 VPM mice and 3 L4 mice (anm260074, anm223517, anm250509), and the 5 SOM-IRES-Cre x Ai32 mice are given as Ai32.
    Found by the attempt on Yu et al. 2016 (yu-2016-02, 2026-10-20)
  • Description missingStill present from yu-2016-01. Every L4 file gives cortical layer 4 (depth 0.42 to 0.62 mm), so the layer of each cell cannot be checked.
    Found by the attempt on Yu et al. 2016 (yu-2016-02, 2026-10-20)
  • Description missingStill present from yu-2016-01. The optogenetic conditions are not described. photo_stim_mode and type have empty descriptions and the mode labels do not map to one kind of light, photo_stim_power is NaN in every L4 file, and no file names the opsin (eNpHR3.0 in the PV-IRES-Cre mice).
    Found by the attempt on Yu et al. 2016 (yu-2016-02, 2026-10-20)
  • Metadata errorStill present from yu-2016-01. The session description gives the wrong recording mode for 24 of 130 L4 files, all from PV-IRES-Cre mice; by the trace PV-IRES-Cre has 9 whole-cell and 39 cell-attached cells where Supplementary Table 2 has 5 and 42.
    Found by the attempt on Yu et al. 2016 (yu-2016-02, 2026-10-20)
  • Metadata errorStill present from yu-2016-01. In the L4 files the trial table's first_touch_time is the trial start plus the first-touch latency divided by 1000 (it matches the touch series in 0.956 of trials once multiplied by 1000); in the VPM files it is correct.
    Found by the attempt on Yu et al. 2016 (yu-2016-02, 2026-10-20)
  • Description missingStill present from yu-2016-01. The L4 trace is sampled at 10 kHz (92 files) or 20 kHz (38 files), which only the timestamps state.
    Found by the attempt on Yu et al. 2016 (yu-2016-02, 2026-10-20)
  • Description missingStill present from yu-2016-01. 8 files name no principal whisker, and the VPM files give one principal whisker per file though the session description says each unit has its own; 11 of 20 units in files with several tracked whiskers respond most to another whisker.
    Found by the attempt on Yu et al. 2016 (yu-2016-02, 2026-10-20)
  • OtherStill present from yu-2016-01. In VGAT-ChR2 mice the light at the site labelled VPM is given as 594 nm, and isolated pulses at the site labelled vS1 (594 nm) drive 13 VGAT-ChR2 cells at 1.6 to 4.6 ms; ChR2 needs blue light, so the wavelengths look wrong.
    Found by the attempt on Yu et al. 2016 (yu-2016-02, 2026-10-20)
  • Metadata errorNew. The injected current (CurrentClampStimulus) has unit amperes and conversion 1e-6, but the end-of-trial test pulse, -0.1 nA in the Methods, is stored as about -0.1 (in 14 of 57 whole-cell files), so the stored values are in nA and the conversion is wrong by a factor of 1,000. 17 cell-attached files of VGAT-ChR2 mice carry a constant stored current of about 1.9 in every trial, which no description explains.
    Found by the attempt on Yu et al. 2016 (yu-2016-02, 2026-10-20)
  • Data missingNew. The dandiset lacks data the paper reports from these experiments, namely the 6 L4 excitatory neurons recorded with depolarizing current (Fig. 3; only one whole-cell file, of the PV-ChR2 cell, has trials held near 0 mV), the 17 thalamic multiunit recordings during photoinhibition (Fig. 6b,c), and the rabies tracing histology (Supplementary Fig. 10).
    Found by the attempt on Yu et al. 2016 (yu-2016-02, 2026-10-20)
  • OtherNew. The pulse trains at the site labelled VPM run at 50 Hz in 12 of 33 files, where the Methods give 100 Hz.
    Found by the attempt on Yu et al. 2016 (yu-2016-02, 2026-10-20)
  • Description missingNew. The no-lick trial type is spelled Nogo in some files and NoGo in others, and the type and response columns have empty descriptions.
    Found by the attempt on Yu et al. 2016 (yu-2016-02, 2026-10-20)
