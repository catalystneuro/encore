Dataset version 0.260227.1824 · tested 2026-11-03 · 8 open findings

  • Data missingThe 9 files of the 1-minute paradigm hold only the stimulation command channel ('S1r 1', the TDT stream uS1r), one series per trial. The multi-unit recordings, the LFP and the stimulation monitor that the paper analysed are not in them, so none of the paper's 1-minute findings (the parameter exploration, Tables 1 to 3, Figures 3 to 10) can be tested.
    Found by the attempt on Bellvila et al. 2026 (bellvila-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Data missingNo file holds sorted units, although the paper's results rest on SpyKING CIRCUS output. For the 20-minute paradigm the recordings could be sorted again, but at about 6.5 minutes per rat in this run, about 85 minutes for the 13 (68 to 85 in the runs so far); the published spike times would make the 20-minute findings testable.
    Found by the attempt on Bellvila et al. 2026 (bellvila-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Data missingThe sham recording (one F344 nTg female rat, 80 minutes under propofol) is not in the dandiset.
    Found by the attempt on Bellvila et al. 2026 (bellvila-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Metadata errorEvery series is labelled in volts with a conversion of 1e-6. In the 1-minute files the values are the stimulation current in uA (peaks of 49.98 to 399.87 for 50 to 400 uA); in the 20-minute files they are already in volts (median magnitude about 0.1 mV, spike troughs about 100 uV), so the conversion would make them a million times too small.
    Found by the attempt on Bellvila et al. 2026 (bellvila-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Description missingThe 20-minute stream (channels 'aw_ 1' to 'aw_ 16', 12207 Hz) matches neither stream of Methods 2.3. It has the LFP's rate but is not low-passed at 300 Hz, and it carries spikes. In 8 of 13 rats the 60 Hz line stands 10 to 1099 times above its neighbours, so these are not notch-filtered; in the other five the ratio is 1.0 to 9.6 (no line at all in sub-2023-05-04 and sub-2023-05-05), so a notch cannot be ruled out there. The files and the dandiset description do not say which TDT stream it is or how it was filtered; the description gives 24 kHz for all data.
    Found by the attempt on Bellvila et al. 2026 (bellvila-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Description missingThe electrodes table has no positions (location 'unknown'), so the 4 x 4 layout of the array, which the paper's field estimate needs, is not documented. The 20-minute files have no epochs or stimulation times; the stimulation is found only from its artifact. Session descriptions are empty.
    Found by the attempt on Bellvila et al. 2026 (bellvila-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Selection not documentedThe paper gives 20 trials per 1-minute rat; the files hold 15 to 23 (185 in all), with repeats of some combinations, three rats missing some of the 16 analysed combinations (one lacks all four at 400 uA), and one trial at 100 Hz and 20 uA outside the paper's design. Which trials the paper analysed is not documented.
    Found by the attempt on Bellvila et al. 2026 (bellvila-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • OtherThe 20-minute subjects are 6 male and 7 female in the files; Methods 2.1 say 7 male and 6 female. The 1-minute subjects (4 male, 5 female) agree with the paper.
    Found by the attempt on Bellvila et al. 2026 (bellvila-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
