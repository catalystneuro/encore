  • efield-1min-medians

    The electric fields estimated from the stimulation artifact in the 1-minute experiments had medians of 0.77, 1.42, 2.86 and 6.04 mV/mm for 50, 100, 200 and 400 uA.

    The magnitude of electric fields was assessed from sACS artifacts in electrophysiological recordings, and had medians of 0.77 ± 0.29, 1.42 ± 0.60, 2.86 ± 1.20, and 6.04 ± 1.68 mV/mm for 50, 100, 200, 400 μA sACS amplitudes, respectively

    Results 3.1, paragraph 1; Figure 1C

    How it will be judged

    Method from Methods 2.8. For each rat and amplitude, every channel of every trial of that amplitude is band-pass filtered at the stimulation frequency (5, 10, 20 or 40 Hz); the peak voltage of each stimulation cycle is averaged over the stimulation period per channel; differences between neighbouring electrodes along each array axis are divided by the 0.5 mm spacing (Methods 2.3), the two orthogonal fields combined by vector addition, outliers removed by Grubbs' test (alpha 0.05), and the field averaged over the frequency trials of the amplitude. Tolerances are the paper's stated spread. Open, with readings run (a) the median over rats of each rat's average field, (b) the median over all channel-pair estimates of all rats (Fig. 1C left shows these); "maximal" field per axis read as (i) each neighbour pair or (ii) the largest pair per axis and rat. The band-pass filter is not named; a zero-phase Butterworth band of +/- 1 Hz around the stimulation frequency on the LFP stream is the standard choice and is stated as one. Reproduced when all four medians are within tolerance under one reading and rise with amplitude.
