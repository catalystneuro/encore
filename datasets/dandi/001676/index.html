Dataset version 0.251205.2137 · tested 2026-11-03 · 8 open findings

  • File format errorThe tuning_curves table (processing/ophys/tuning_curves) of every imaging file holds one curve repeated for every ROI. In 401 of 402 sessions it is the curve of ROI 0, smoothed with a kernel of about 4 bins (median correlation 0.995). The table's description says it holds the tuning curve of each ROI.
    Found by the attempt on Skromne Carrasco et al. 2026 (skromne-carrasco-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Identifier missingEach protocol's files carry a CellReg table for that protocol only, and nothing links ROIs across protocols. The probe trials of Fig. 3 (the first week of the protocol "4 environments are visited weekly in a random order, for 4 weeks") cannot be matched to the last visits of the two-week protocol, so Fig. 3 cannot be tested. A cross-protocol registration table would fix this.
    Found by the attempt on Skromne Carrasco et al. 2026 (skromne-carrasco-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Data missingThe electrophysiology files hold spike times and electrode groups only, with no waveforms; the waveform features of Extended Data Fig. 7f-h cannot be computed.
    Found by the attempt on Skromne Carrasco et al. 2026 (skromne-carrasco-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Description missingThe probe week of Fig. 3 is not labelled as such. It is the first week of the random-order protocol, whose description (four weeks of random visits) also covers the three weeks of Extended Data Fig. 9, where the paper describes a week of probe trials followed by four further weeks.
    Found by the attempt on Skromne Carrasco et al. 2026 (skromne-carrasco-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • OtherThe dandiset holds 402 imaging sessions (367 for mice 1 to 5) where the paper reports 388, and random-order sessions of mouse 5, whose field of view the paper says was lost before the probe trials. Sessions last 902 to 1799 s where the Methods state 20 min. The circular protocol has 19, 20, 19, 19 and 20 sessions, as Extended Data Fig. 1e states.
    Found by the attempt on Skromne Carrasco et al. 2026 (skromne-carrasco-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • OtherEach electrophysiology file holds two explorations of each of the three environments, separated by sleep, where the paper describes one. The files hold 347 units, 254 of them on PoSub shanks (the paper's n of 254 cells) and the rest in cortex or CA1.
    Found by the attempt on Skromne Carrasco et al. 2026 (skromne-carrasco-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • OtherThe CellReg scores (scratch/Cell registration scores) include values below 0, 102 of 5,190 rows over all protocols and mice, down to -1.24, which a CellReg confidence score (0 to 1) cannot take. The files do not describe the score.
    Found by the attempt on Skromne Carrasco et al. 2026 (skromne-carrasco-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
  • Metadata errorThe dandiset's related publication and each file's related_publications point to the bioRxiv preprint (10.1101/2024.06.13.598909), not the Nature article.
    Found by the attempt on Skromne Carrasco et al. 2026 (skromne-carrasco-2026-01, 2026-11-03)
