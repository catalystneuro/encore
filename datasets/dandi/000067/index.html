    The dandiset's recording channels can be assigned to the prefrontal probe, the CA1 probe and the nose-poke sensors by the channel map of the CRCNS pfc-2 description of the same recordings (channels 1 to 64 prefrontal, 65 to 96 CA1, 97 right and 98 left nose-poke sensors), and the data agree with that map.

    Records are matched by channel number. In rats EE and FF the CRCNS map holds in every session; the CA1 channels have a theta ratio 1.9 to 2.7 times that of channels 1 to 64, and the more active sensor during the nose poke is the trial's side in 0.97 to 1.00 of trials. In rat GG channels 97 and 98 are not sensors (agreement 0.35 to 0.42); the sensors are on 65 and 66 and the CA1 probe on 70 to 101, and with that correction both checks hold in every session (agreement 1.00). The sensor on 66 is active on trials labelled Right, so in GG the order of the sensors, or the side labels, is reversed relative to EE and FF. Channels 1 to 64 have a lower theta ratio than the CA1 channels in every session (0.16 to 0.27 against 0.37 to 0.54), so the prefrontal probe is placed as the description says in all three rats; every claim that uses PFC units depends on that part, which holds.

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • The map is used with the per-rat correction found in the data, not as published.
    • The sensor check of EE.088 uses its LFP only; it has no wideband signal for the theta check.

Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds three rats (EE, FF, GG); the paper recorded four. Of the 13 working memory sessions in the CRCNS session table, EE.152, EE.165 and EE.188 are not in the dandiset, and EE.210 has no trial table or position. EE.188 is the paper's example session (Figures 2a, 3b to 3d, 4 and 5).
  • Description missingThe units table holds spike times only. It has no channel, shank, brain region, cell type or quality label, and mixes prefrontal units, CA1 units, multi-unit clusters (cluster 1 of each shank, kept by the conversion) and clusters of the sensor signals. Units can be placed only from their waveforms in the wideband signal, which EE.042 and the ten sessions of 2008-06-27 (EE.080 to EE.089, among them the working memory session EE.088) do not have.

Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • The map is used with the per-rat correction found in the data, not as published.channel-region-link
  • The sensor check of EE.088 uses its LFP only; it has no wideband signal for the theta check.channel-region-link
  • Units are counted per session; the paper's way of counting is not stated.rats-and-units
  • Well-isolated units are selected by a refractory rule chosen here.rats-and-units
  • The layer of each rat is not known; values are matched rat by rat under the layer readings.fig2c-active-fraction
  • The threshold for "relatively constant" (a factor of 2) is chosen here.fig2c-active-fraction
  • The mean's tolerance is the paper's stated s.d. of the neurons, as the protocol asks, which accepts a wide range of means; the s.d. carries the test.fig2-field-size
  • The 1 Hz peak rule is the CRCNS "Cells" rule, not the paper's.fig2-field-size
  • The paper does not say how the 50% boundaries are taken or which trajectory is used; three readings are shown.fig2-field-size
  • The number of permutations (1000) and the 101-point grid are chosen here.fig2b-trajectory-selective
  • Layers are matched rat by rat, as the layer of each rat is not known.fig2b-trajectory-selective
  • In rat GG the side labels may be swapped (see channel-region-link); this does not change a left-right test.fig2b-trajectory-selective
  • Only the x coordinate is available; the paper used the two-dimensional position.traj-diverge
  • The test (Welch t-test per bin of 0.02, p < 0.01, divergence from the first bin of a run up to 0.6) is chosen here; the paper's is in a supplementary figure that was not available.traj-diverge
  • The criterion was written in the first revision, after the verifier's report.traj-diverge
  • The paper names no test; a Mann-Whitney test of per-trial rates was chosen.odor-nosepoke
  • The nose-poke period is from the start of the first nose poke to the end of the last (median 4.66 s), as the trial table gives; the paper's window is not stated in the main text.odor-nosepoke
  • Bins of 1 ms (not stated in the main text) and a Poisson screen before the Monte Carlo test.fig3-connection-rates
  • A trough beside a peak in the same direction is not counted as inhibition (chosen here). Troughs that mirror a peak in the other direction are counted, as the criterion was written; the rates without them are shown as a variant.fig3-connection-rates
  • The set of well-isolated units is selected here, not by the paper.fig3-connection-rates
  • Units with both kinds of connection are counted in neither class (chosen here).fig3-cell-classes
  • Distance is the shank separation only; positions within a shank were not estimated.fig3f-distance
  • The band is mean plus c times s.d. of the surrogates, not the Methods' rank band, because the rank band cannot reach a one-sided 0.01 with 1000 surrogates on 101 points (it is empty when more than 1% of data sets reach the extreme rank somewhere). This was found after a first run of the rank version gave 1.0% and a synthetic test showed that it could not detect a strong position-limited coupling; the rank result is shown as a variant.fig4-position-dependence
  • Event positions are kept at the original presynaptic spike in the surrogates.fig4-position-dependence
  • Transmission measured as excess spikes from 1 to 4 ms over the jitter expectation, chosen here in place of the paper's rate-normalized peak height.fig6-facilitation-depression
  • Later spikes are any spike within 40 ms of the previous one (the Figure 6 legend), not only spikes inside trains that began after 200 ms of silence.fig6-facilitation-depression
  • Satellites, events and the excess measure are defined here.fig7-supralinear
  • Layers are matched rat by rat; trajectory specificity is the test of fig2b-trajectory-selective over the whole track.fig3-trajectory-by-class
