Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • OtherThe spikes may be misaligned with the trial table. Workload information in the spikes (squared t of low against high workload, summed over units) is at chance in every session when the spikes of trial window i are paired with the label of trial i (p = 0.12 to 0.94 against shuffled labels). Pooled over the 7 sessions with all 192 trials it peaks when they are paired with the label of trial i + 3 (corrected p = 0.005), and decoding workload from all neurons gives 0.46 as deposited, below scrambled labels, against 0.60 with every session moved by three trials. Session by session the shift is supported less: of the 7 complete sessions only P03S1 and P06S1 have p < .05 at a shift of three trials, P02S1 has p = 0.95 there and four others peak at other shifts, and the pooled peak is weighted toward P03S1 and P06S1 (52 and 96 units). In the 9 sessions with fewer trials no single shift carries the information, and the trial table holds no source numbering that would show where trials were left out. A single offset is therefore not established. Within trials, the spikes are consistent with the epochs.
  • Metadata errorThe location of each unit is the atlas label of the macro contact whose number equals the unit's microwire number (for example the units on wire AHL6 get the label of contact AHL6, in the middle temporal gyrus). The microwires protrude from the tip of the electrode, so these are not the units' locations; 614 of 809 units get a label outside hippocampus, parahippocampal gyrus and amygdala.
  • Description missingThe dandiset does not hold the assignment of units to regions the paper used, nor the location of the microwire tips that Figure 1d shows. By electrode name the units are 361 hippocampal, 217 entorhinal and 226 amygdala; by the atlas label of the tip contact 362 hippocampal, 168 parahippocampal, 146 amygdala and 133 elsewhere; with those 133 placed by the nearest contact with an MTL label (or the electrode name where there is none) 467, 196 and 146; the paper reports 454, 209 and 146. Five units are on bundles (PCL, HL, HR) that the subject descriptions do not list as implanted.
  • Metadata errorParticipant 12's two sessions are each deposited twice, in four files with the same session start time (2016-12-17 10:38); the pairs are identical, and only the identifiers (session01, session02) tell the sessions apart.
  • Metadata errorThe response timestamps put 1385 of 3038 responses before the test array appears. The conversion adds the trial start and a 1.7 s event offset to the stored response time; read that way, the stored values are response times from test onset of 0.41 to 3.15 s. The response series does not say what its timestamps mean.
  • Selection not documentedSessions hold 174 to 192 trials (3038 in all), where the paper and the files' task description state 192 per session. Trial ids run without gaps, and which trials were left out, and why, is not documented.
  • OtherSpikes are stored only inside trial windows (fixation onset to 2 s after test onset), so nothing between trials, including the inter-trial interval, can be analysed. The fixation period is a fixed 0.9 s in the epochs, where the task description has a jittered black dot.
  • Paper inconsistentThe Results give the response-time ANOVA (F = 48.47) on mean response time; on this dataset that F is obtained exactly from per-session median response times (mean gives 61.36), which Figure 1c plots. The 118 ms/item slope is not obtained by any of three readings on the set size (69 to 76); it equals the slope of the median response times on the condition step (1 to 4), 118.1 ms per step.

Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • The sessions hold 3038 trials (174 to 192 per session), where the paper states 192 per session; the 2678 correct trials equal the paper's count.behavior-accuracy-by-set-size
  • Participant 12's sessions are each deposited twice; each is counted once, by the session number in the file identifier.table1-capacity-and-neurons
  • RT is the stored response timestamp minus trial start minus 1.7 s, read as the time from test onset (data check; the conversion adds 1.7 s to the stored response time). The slopes and F values do not depend on this offset.behavior-rt-set-size
  • Reading (a) takes the location of the microwire tips from the atlas labels of each bundle's contacts, as the microwires protrude 4 mm from the electrode tip (Methods 2.3) and Figure 1d shows the "Location of the microelectrodes at the tip of the depth electrodes" color-coded by region. The files hold no location for the wires themselves. PhG (parahippocampal gyrus) is read as entorhinal cortex. The tip contact (contact 1) gives the region where it has an MTL label. Assumption (a2), for the 133 units whose tip contact is labelled unspecific or FuG (all on hippocampal electrodes), the label of the contact nearest the tip with an MTL label, and the electrode name where no contact has one (60 units). It is preferred to the electrode name (a1) because it uses the bundle's own atlas labels before its target name; both are shown, and Figure 1d colours every site as one of the three regions. The grounding is weak: (a2) differs from (a1) only for the 28 units on PHR of participant 8, and it places them by contacts 2 and 3, which lie further from the protruding wire tips than contact 1. Under (a1) the entorhinal count is 168, below the tolerance floor of 188.1.neurons-recorded
  • Reading (i) of the ISI, the whole recording, cannot be computed, as the files hold spikes only inside trial windows. Reading (b) of the SNR, a noise level stored by the spike sorter, cannot be computed, as the Units table has none.spike-sorting-quality
  • Reading (ii) of the trial, from the warning signal to the end of the inter-trial interval, cannot be computed, as no spikes are stored between trials.firing-rates-by-period
  • The p value of each per-neuron test is (number of permutation t values at least as large in absolute value + 1) / (number of permutations + 1), with 1000 permutations for the region tests and 5000 for the counts, the claims file's standard choice and its check.fig2c-maintenance-neurons
  • Reading (b), the cluster-based test, uses rates in 500 ms windows stepped by 50 ms (the Figure 2a legend gives 500 ms bins with a 1 ms step), a two-sided t threshold at p < .05 and the 95th percentile of the largest cluster mass over 1000 label permutations; its counts use 99 label shuffles, not 499, for time.fig2d-workload-modulated-maintenance-neurons
  • The significance of reading (b) uses 99 within-trial shuffles of the three period labels with 200 permutations per neuron and test, for time.fig3b-test-neurons
  • Spike counts are binned at 10 ms before smoothing, not 1 ms; with a 167 or 200 ms SD kernel this is a standard choice that changes the smoothed rates very little. The smoothing is renormalised at the trial edges, where no spikes are stored.   • Reading (b) uses 100 runs, not 500, for time.fig4b-speed-lowest-in-maintenance
