Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds 2 of the paper's 9 analysed wild type rats (14 or 15 of its 15 Grin2b+/-), so genotype comparisons rest on two WT, one of which has no SWDs. The pharmacology and intrathalamic infusion recordings (Figures 6, 7, S11, S12), western blots (Figures 1, S2, S3), development measures (Figure S4) and the visual scoring used to validate the scorer (Table S1) are not in it. The paper says raw data are available on request.
  • Description missingGRIN2B-132 has no genotype and sex U, so it cannot be placed in either group. The conversion repository marks both as still to be confirmed by the lab.
  • Selection not documentedThe paper analysed 15 Grin2b+/- and 9 WT rats and excluded 4 and 9 descendants of one founder. The dandiset does not say which of its rats the paper analysed, and holds a Grin2b+/- rat (GRIN2B-228) with 1 SWD per day, unlike the paper's statement that all Grin2b+/- rats had SWDs at wake-NREM transitions.
  • Metadata errorThe sleep_states description gives code 4 for SWD epochs, but no file holds code 4; the stored scores are the scorer's output before SWD epochs are set, so SWD epochs are scored as wake, NREM or REM.
  • Metadata errorswd_epoch_counts is described as the number of SWD events per 5-s epoch but holds the scorer's flags (6 for an SWD epoch, 4 otherwise). The flagged epochs cover a median of 51% (0 to 73%) of the epochs SWD events overlap, mostly the last epoch of each SWD, and only about a tenth of SWD onsets fall in a flagged epoch.
  • Metadata errorIn seizure_totals_by_zt the per-hour SWD counts are in spike_wave_discharge, while number_of_seizure_events and Day hold the day's total in the first row and 0 elsewhere; the description itself marks the columns as unconfirmed.
  • Metadata errorsleep_state_power_spectra is described as a baseline-corrected PSD normalized to the average over states, with units to be confirmed. The lab's scorer writes the mean log10 multitaper amplitude referenced to each frequency's 5th percentile over the day, which is what the values (0 to about 1.6) fit; the paper's Welch spectra are not in the dandiset.
  • File format errorThe raw EEG and EMG are stored as unsigned 12-bit codes with offset 0, so every channel reads about +8 mV of DC in volts. Signal loss is written as 32767 on all channels at once, and some files hold other values outside 0 to 4095; neither is described. In the windows read, 21 to 73% of 5-s epochs per rat contain such samples (73% for GRIN2B-238).
  • Metadata errorThe dandiset's assets summary counts 16 files and 16 subjects, while the draft holds 17 NWB files of 17 rats.
  • Published code disagreesThe Methods exclude SWD times from the sleep states, while the lab's scorer replaces only the flagged epochs, which cover about half of the epochs SWD events overlap. The paper's wake results depend on this; with the stored scores (SWD epochs as wake) the wake bout differences reverse and the correlation of SWD time with wake time disappears.
  • Paper inconsistentIn the Figure 5 legend, NREM time gives T = -1.47 with R = -0.13 and df = 13, which implies T = -0.47; REM bouts give T = 0.704 with R = -0.19, of opposite sign; and REM bout duration and wake bouts have identical statistics (T = 0.61, R = 0.17, p = 0.55). The Figure 5A ANOVA gives genotype F = 1 with p = 0.98, which do not match for df = 1.
  • Paper inconsistentThe Figure 3 legend says no net differences between genotypes were observed for wake, while reporting a genotype effect on hourly wake time of F = 7.14, p = 0.014, and the Results say Grin2b+/- rats spent less time awake.
  • Description missingThe Supplementary Methods chose, by eye and per animal, the S1 EEG channel (left or right) and the EMG channel with fewer artefacts for sleep scoring; the dandiset does not record which channels were used.
  • Methods not availableThe paper does not give its frequency band limits, Welch segment length, bout definition or the rule for wake-NREM transitions, and neither linked repository holds the statistics or figure code.
