Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Data missingThe dandiset holds one of the twelve sessions analysed in the paper, and none from the two-workspace experiment, so four of the paper's main findings cannot be tested from it.
  • Data missingThe positions of the arm markers, which the paper's whole-arm model is built on, are not included. Joint angles and muscle lengths derived from them are.
  • Metadata errorThe text descriptions of hand position and hand velocity give centimetres while the stated unit is metres. The stated unit, with the file's conversion factor, is correct.
  • Selection not documentedThe dandiset's metadata names the paper without saying which part of it the data covers. It was packaged for the Neural Latents Benchmark, not as the paper's data release.

Reproduction Active and passive arm movements in somatosensory area 2 (Chowdhury et al. 2020)

Tests the active/passive findings of Chowdhury, Glaser and Miller (2020) on dandiset 000127, which holds one of the paper's twelve sessions. It compares hand and arm kinematics between active reaches and passive bumps, measures how well single neurons tell the two apart, and fits hand-only and whole-arm models of each neuron's firing. The paper's two-workspace experiment cannot be tested because those sessions are not in the dandiset. All files
