Dataset version 0.250805.1356 · tested 2026-11-01 · 10 open findings

  • Description missingThe dandiset holds only the raw images. The ROIs over the de- and hyperpolarized poles, the debris ROI used to scale out laser-flash fluctuations, and the shape and orientation group of each experiment (elongated parallel, elongated perpendicular, random) are not in it, so the group comparisons of Figure 4D and 4E cannot be made and the time constants cannot be recomputed as the paper did.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2025 (semenov-2025-04, 2026-11-01)
  • Description missingThe electrode axis is given for the January 19 and March 11 experiments, but the January 17 files say "Electrode position not specified", and no file says which electrode was the cathode or how the camera image is oriented relative to the named directions.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2025 (semenov-2025-04, 2026-11-01)
  • Selection not documentedThe files form 22 runs of 7 or 8 replicates (one run of cell 1 of January 17 has 7) plus six single files at 100 V; the paper reports 19 experiments. Nothing says which runs the paper used or why the others were left out. The nominal amplitudes vary (40, 60, 80, 100 and 130 V) where the paper gives one field, 140 V/cm, and no file gives the field.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2025 (semenov-2025-04, 2026-11-01)
  • Description missingFiles recorded "in a backward manner" are stored in acquisition order, so their frames run back in time; the data show this (the backward files match the forward ones only once reversed), but the files do not say it beyond the stepping direction in prose.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2025 (semenov-2025-04, 2026-11-01)
  • Metadata errorThe time base of the series (50 ns per frame, pulse 1 us after the first image) is given only in prose. The ImageSeries rate is the camera's 6 Hz, and starting_time holds the session start as a Unix time (about 1.7e9 s) where NWB expects seconds after the session start.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2025 (semenov-2025-04, 2026-11-01)
  • Metadata errorThe subjects (13) are cells identified by number and date, not animals, and their age is P18D with reference birth, for neurons dissected at E18 and cultured about one week.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2025 (semenov-2025-04, 2026-11-01)
  • OtherThe first image acquired in 156 of 175 files of the experiments is about half as bright as the rest (a failed or partial flash); 204 frames in all were dropped as dim.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2025 (semenov-2025-04, 2026-11-01)
  • OtherThe paper's other dandisets were surveyed from their metadata and images (survey_other_dandisets.ipynb). DANDI:001034 holds the same 178 image arrays as DANDI:001032, byte for byte, with the same session descriptions, under other subject ids (1_1 against 1_3, and so on for all 14). Every session description of 001032 to 001035 names the NaCl solution, so the sodium gluconate thresholds of Figure 3B do not appear to be deposited.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2025 (semenov-2025-04, 2026-11-01)
  • Data missingDANDI:001272, used here for Figure S1, holds three Di-8-ANEPPS neurons (subject ids 2, 3 and 4 of 2024-08-06) where the paper reports eight, and its files do not give the stimulus timing.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2025 (semenov-2025-04, 2026-11-01)
  • Metadata errorIn DANDI:001030, used here for Figure 3C, every file gives the species as Mus musculus, where the paper used E18 rat hippocampal neurons; two of its 17 sessions (cell c5 at 1.5 and 2.0 V) end at 1.91 s, before the 2-s stimulus does.
    Found by the attempt on Semenov et al. 2025 (semenov-2025-04, 2026-11-01)
