    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • Data elsewhere, in DANDI:001030, as the claims file assigns from the dandiset's title.
    • F is the mean over the whole 16 x 16 frame. At this resolution the soma the Methods name ("emission intensity over the entire cell soma") cannot be outlined.
    • The bleaching trace is the only session without APs during the stimulus under both readings (c7 at 1.0 V), used for every cell (Methods, "corrected for bleaching by subtracting a trace without APs"). Uncorrected results are shown too.
    • The first 20 frames (6.4 ms) of each file are dropped, a standard choice. The first frame is 2.6 to 7.6 times brighter than the rest, and the next ones recover from it.
    • The criterion does not say how cells are combined. Each cell's field series is judged on its own, as Figure 3C shows one cell ("Sustained firing and depolarization in a neuron", Figure 3 legend). Assumed that the claim is reproduced when the criterion holds in most cells, partially reproduced when it holds in at least one cell or its firing parts hold in most, and not reproduced otherwise. The Results present the neuron of Figure 3C as what was observed ("we observed sustained firing for the entire duration of the sine wave"), so a typical cell should show it, which a single cell does not establish. The outcome rests on this rule. Judged on any one cell the claim would be reproduced (c7); judged on most cells it would not.
    • AP detection runs on a 1-ms running mean of the trace; a reading (a) peak must stand 5% dF above its surroundings, and peaks of both readings must be at least 5 ms apart. These are standard choices (APs last about 10 ms, Methods). The first run used no smoothing and a prominence of 2.5%, which counted noise on a depolarized plateau as APs; it is shown as a variant.

Findings about the data

What attempts found about the dataset itself, for its owner and the archive.

  • Description missingThe dandiset holds only the raw images. The ROIs over the de- and hyperpolarized poles, the debris ROI used to scale out laser-flash fluctuations, and the shape and orientation group of each experiment (elongated parallel, elongated perpendicular, random) are not in it, so the group comparisons of Figure 4D and 4E cannot be made and the time constants cannot be recomputed as the paper did.
  • Description missingThe electrode axis is given for the January 19 and March 11 experiments, but the January 17 files say "Electrode position not specified", and no file says which electrode was the cathode or how the camera image is oriented relative to the named directions.
  • Selection not documentedThe files form 22 runs of 7 or 8 replicates (one run of cell 1 of January 17 has 7) plus six single files at 100 V; the paper reports 19 experiments. Nothing says which runs the paper used or why the others were left out. The nominal amplitudes vary (40, 60, 80, 100 and 130 V) where the paper gives one field, 140 V/cm, and no file gives the field.

Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • The ROIs are not in the dataset. The extreme pixels are taken from the RATIO image rebuilt as the Methods describe (Pulsed laser fluorescence microscopy, CONTROL, POLARIZED and RATIO images, 12 x 12 pixel low-pass filter), within the soma, defined as the connected region of the filtered CONTROL image brighter than halfway between the image median and the brightest central pixel (a standard choice; the neuron was centred in the field, Methods, Sine wave and square pulse stimulation). The centroid is that of the soma region.fig4-polarized-poles-face-electrodes
  • Assumed that "Up and Bottom" in the session description is the image's vertical axis and "Right and Left" its horizontal axis. The files do not give the orientation of the camera image relative to the stage; this is the plain reading of the description.fig4-polarized-poles-face-electrodes
  • The 8 January 17 experiments have "Electrode position not specified" and are left out.fig4-polarized-poles-face-electrodes
  • The test is run over the 22 experiments in the dataset (set A) and over the 15 at 40 V (set B); the paper's 19 cannot be identified.fig4-no-pole-difference
  • Unscaled data only; the debris ROI used for scaling is not in the dataset (claims file, F2).fig4-no-pole-difference
  • ROIs rebuilt from the RATIO image under the claims file's readings O1 (a) and (b), within the soma region described under fig4-polarized-poles-face-electrodes.fig4-no-pole-difference
  • Fits that fail or end at a bound (tau 0.005 or 50 us, amplitude 200%) are dropped, a standard choice; the counts are printed. Fits are least squares in SciPy, not Origin 8.0.fig4-no-pole-difference
  • The ROIs and the debris ROI are not in the dataset. The polarized ROIs are rebuilt from the RATIO image (O1 a and b), and the traces are unscaled (F2). The paper scaled to the debris ROI to remove flash-to-flash laser fluctuations, which this attempt could not do; the whole-frame scaling shown as a variant is not the paper's.fig4-pooled-time-constants
  • 22 experiments in the dataset against the paper's 19; set A uses all 22, set B the 15 at 40 V.fig4-pooled-time-constants
  • Dim frames (below 70% of the file's median frame mean, mostly the first image acquired) are dropped, a standard choice; 204 of 28,000 frames.fig4-pooled-time-constants
  • The time base is the Methods' (frame 20 is the pulse onset, 50 ns per frame); the onset estimated from the data has median 19.9 frames (18.0 to 21.0), and the fits are repeated with it as a variant.fig4-pooled-time-constants
  • Fits are kept up to tau 50 us, a standard choice; values beyond the fitted window are poorly constrained, so a variant drops them.fig4-pooled-time-constants
  • Data elsewhere, in DANDI:001030, as the claims file assigns from the dandiset's title.fig3c-sustained-firing-2s
  • F is the mean over the whole 16 x 16 frame. At this resolution the soma the Methods name ("emission intensity over the entire cell soma") cannot be outlined.fig3c-sustained-firing-2s
  • The bleaching trace is the only session without APs during the stimulus under both readings (c7 at 1.0 V), used for every cell (Methods, "corrected for bleaching by subtracting a trace without APs"). Uncorrected results are shown too.fig3c-sustained-firing-2s
  • The first 20 frames (6.4 ms) of each file are dropped, a standard choice. The first frame is 2.6 to 7.6 times brighter than the rest, and the next ones recover from it.fig3c-sustained-firing-2s
  • The criterion does not say how cells are combined. Each cell's field series is judged on its own, as Figure 3C shows one cell ("Sustained firing and depolarization in a neuron", Figure 3 legend). Assumed that the claim is reproduced when the criterion holds in most cells, partially reproduced when it holds in at least one cell or its firing parts hold in most, and not reproduced otherwise. The Results present the neuron of Figure 3C as what was observed ("we observed sustained firing for the entire duration of the sine wave"), so a typical cell should show it, which a single cell does not establish. The outcome rests on this rule. Judged on any one cell the claim would be reproduced (c7); judged on most cells it would not.fig3c-sustained-firing-2s
  • AP detection runs on a 1-ms running mean of the trace; a reading (a) peak must stand 5% dF above its surroundings, and peaks of both readings must be at least 5 ms apart. These are standard choices (APs last about 10 ms, Methods). The first run used no smoothing and a prominence of 2.5%, which counted noise on a depolarized plateau as APs; it is shown as a variant.fig3c-sustained-firing-2s
  • Data elsewhere, in DANDI:001272, as the claims file assigns from the dandiset's title. Three neurons where the paper reports eight.figs1-di8-anepps-control
  • F is the mean over the whole 16 x 16 frame (the soma cannot be outlined), inverted, with the first 20 frames dropped (standard choices).figs1-di8-anepps-control
  • The stimulus timing (50 ms before, 250 ms during) is from the Methods ("The standard imaging protocol started 50 ms before sine wave stimulation and continued for 250 ms during"); the files do not give it.figs1-di8-anepps-control
  • The criterion does not fix how "latency falls with field" or the window of "ends in depolarization" are judged. Latency falls when the Spearman correlation of field and first-AP latency over a neuron's sessions with APs is negative. A neuron ends in depolarization when its highest-field sessions have no APs in the last 100 ms of the stimulus and mean dF above 5% there, the window the claims file uses for 250-ms stimuli (fig2-modulated-locks-to-beats). Both are standard choices; no window gives the 5% level, as no session reaches it over its last 100 ms.figs1-di8-anepps-control
  • AP detection runs on a 1-ms running mean, and a peak of reading (a) or (c) must stand above its surroundings by the detection level (standard choices, as for fig3c-sustained-firing-2s). The first run's detector is a variant.figs1-di8-anepps-control
