  • Data missingStill presentThe food-reward photometry experiments (Methods, 6 mice), the open-loop dLight calibration experiments (2 dLight+ChrimsonR mice) and the dLight+ChrimsonR closed-loop sessions with 2-s and 3-s stimulation are not in the dandiset; only two 2-s excitation sessions of the dLight+ChrimsonR mice are. The release notes of 0.261113.1911 state the first and last are missing.
    Found by the attempt on Markowitz et al. 2023 (markowitz-2023-02, 2026-11-19)
  • OtherStill presentThe dandiset holds 10 DMS mice where the paper reports 8; dms-dlight-7 and dms-dlight-9 have no photometry. Of the photometry session files, 92 of 167 DMS and 22 of 97 dLight-only DLS files, and 10 of 236 dLight+ChrimsonR files, have behaviour but no photometry series, without a note saying why. dls-dlight-6 and dls-dlight-8 have no photometry, consistent with the two mice the paper excluded.
    Found by the attempt on Markowitz et al. 2023 (markowitz-2023-02, 2026-11-19)
  • Selection not documentedNewThe DLS photometry experiments without stimulation number 164 (dLight-only mice and the dLight+ChrimsonR files that are a baseline day of some target) or 204 with the pure post-stimulation days, against 216 in Fig. 1b and 378 baseline experiments in the Methods. The DMS experiments with photometry number 75 under the Methods' quality control and 64 under the code's rule, which matches the paper. Which files form the paper's DLS sets is not recorded. 38 dLight+ChrimsonR files are the baseline day of one target and the post day of another (two rows of their TargetSessions table), and two 2-s excitation sessions carry session numbers 0 and 4, those of a baseline and a post day.
    Found by the attempt on Markowitz et al. 2023 (markowitz-2023-02, 2026-11-19)
  • Metadata errorNewThe OptogeneticSeries description says onsets are recorded as 1 and offsets as 0, but the onset values are the power, 0.01 W.
    Found by the attempt on Markowitz et al. 2023 (markowitz-2023-02, 2026-11-19)
  • OtherNewOver the stimulation files 70.7% of target detections are stimulated, where the Methods give 75%.
    Found by the attempt on Markowitz et al. 2023 (markowitz-2023-02, 2026-11-19)
  • OtherNewOne dLight+ChrimsonR session of dlight-chrimson-6 has a dF/F of up to 10,804% and a dLight-reference correlation of 0.998, and is not marked excluded in its TargetSessions table; it fails the Methods' quality control. One dls-dlight-7 session has a raw dF/F of up to 228 (22,798%); it passes the Methods' rule and fails the code's.
    Found by the attempt on Markowitz et al. 2023 (markowitz-2023-02, 2026-11-19)

  • Description missingNewThe velocity-modulation files do not record whether stimulation targeted the fastest or the slowest quartile of the target's velocity, except in the session descriptions of mice 5891 to 5894 ('Stim session -up', '-down'); the five dls-ai32jr mice's 50 files and the remaining files of the other four mice do not say. The dandiset has 9 mice with such files, the paper 5. The direction can be read from the velocities of the stimulated instances once their detection times are corrected (next finding); this agrees with the stated direction in 16 of 17 files.
    Found by the attempt on Markowitz et al. 2023 (markowitz-2023-02, 2026-11-19)
  • Metadata errorNewIn the velocity-modulation files the TargetDetections times and the OptogeneticSeries light onsets are 2700.03 s later than the clock of the syllable labels and the other behavioural series. 2,845 of 3,423 detections and 1,901 of 2,479 light onsets fall after the last syllable onset of their file, and 1.0% of detections fall on an instance of the target syllable; shifted by -2700.03 s, 99.4% do. In the reinforcement files the detections fall on target instances without a shift (median 99.7%).
    Found by the attempt on Markowitz et al. 2023 (markowitz-2023-02, 2026-11-19)
  • OtherNewThe syllable-onset dLight averages differ between the two DLS groups. The peak of the shuffle-normalised average from 0 to 0.3 s has a median of 3.83 in the 6 dLight-only mice and -0.10 in the 8 dLight+ChrimsonR mice (reinforcement-photometry files; U = 48 of 48, P = 0.001), and the average reaches its maximum within 0 to +1 s at 0.30 to 0.33 s in every dLight-only mouse but at 0.97 to 1.0 s in 5 of the 8 dLight+ChrimsonR mice. A label shift check gives the same best shift (-3 samples) in both groups, so a clock offset does not explain it, and the files do not say what else differs.
    Found by the attempt on Markowitz et al. 2023 (markowitz-2023-02, 2026-11-19)

The one snc-eyfp mouse (3155) says its genotype is not given in the source. Found fixed on dataset version 0.261113.1911 by markowitz-2023-02, 2026-11-19.

Found fixed on dataset version 0.261113.1911 by markowitz-2023-02, 2026-11-19.
