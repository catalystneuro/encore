Dataset unversioned files (the archive keeps no versions) · tested 2026-11-08 · 4 open findings

  • Metadata errorThe derived data's time axes put each segment's first sample at -1.5 s, and that sample is the trial table's epoch start (encmain at column H, first stimulus onset; proc 0.3 s before column I, test cue offset; pre about 0.1 s after column G), so the task events lie 1.5 s earlier on the time axes than 0 s suggests. The deposited scripts cut their windows from the time axes as if 0 s were stimulus onset, test cue offset minus 0.3 s and start screen minus 0.9 s. The data side with the table: the 900 and 1150 ms delay trials part where the table puts the first delay-dependent event of each segment (proc ERPs from -0.25 to 0.75 s, p down to 0.005 against 200 permutations) and not where the scripts' windows put it (proc 1.0 to 2.0 s, p 0.29 to 0.94).
    Found by the attempt on Johnson et al. 2018 (johnson-2018-01, 2026-11-08)
  • Metadata errorThe response times of docs/summary_file.csv, which the descriptor says are "calculated on all correct trials", differ from the mean over correct trials of the per-trial tables in 7 of 10 subjects (s1 identity 1948 against 2348 ms), and no other summary of the trial tables tried (medians, all or kept trials, other subjects' rows, blocks of 40 trials, trial type) gives them. The paper's condition means are those of summary_file.csv. Its accuracies do equal the per-trial tables.
    Found by the attempt on Johnson et al. 2018 (johnson-2018-01, 2026-11-08)
  • Metadata errorFor s10 the trial table's epoch columns are not on the derived data's sample grid (offsets of 97.1 to 98.1 samples from column G, about 114 for two trials, and fractional offsets for H and I), and its trialinfo does not equal the trial table's columns.
    Found by the attempt on Johnson et al. 2018 (johnson-2018-01, 2026-11-08)
  • Metadata errorThe descriptor gives fsample 1000 for the derived structures; s8 and s9 are at 512 Hz (as its note on the trial table says).
    Found by the attempt on Johnson et al. 2018 (johnson-2018-01, 2026-11-08)
