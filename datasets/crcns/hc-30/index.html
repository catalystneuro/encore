What the attempts that used this dataset found about the data themselves, for its owner and the archive, under the version of the dataset each was found on.

Dataset unversioned files (the archive keeps no versions) · tested 2026-11-11 · 7 open findings

  • Data missingThe data set holds only the 110 working sessions of the 239 recorded, and no positions of the cells in the field of view, so the paper's test that noise correlations do not depend on the distance between cells cannot be run. There is no reward or trial record; trials and task performance are rebuilt from the position.
    Found by the attempt on Hazon et al. 2022 (hazon-2022-01, 2026-11-11)
  • OtherThe traces array holds event onsets (single frames with the event amplitude), as the descriptor says, not the alpha-convolved and normalised event trace the paper decodes from; the trace must be rebuilt with the Methods' kernel. Rebuilt this way, the response variability matches the paper's 0.7263 +/- 0.6117 exactly.
    Found by the attempt on Hazon et al. 2022 (hazon-2022-01, 2026-11-11)
  • Description missingNo sampling rate, frame times or spatial scale are stored; position is in camera pixels. 20 Hz and 118 cm over the 5th to 95th percentile of position are taken from the paper and its code.
    Found by the attempt on Hazon et al. 2022 (hazon-2022-01, 2026-11-11)
  • Metadata errorThe descriptor and the paper describe sessions of about 20 min; at 20 Hz the sessions last a median 30.1 min (20.1 to 46.0).
    Found by the attempt on Hazon et al. 2022 (hazon-2022-01, 2026-11-11)
  • OtherIn six sessions the event trace does not track the position at lag 0 (a quick decoder gives 14.7 to 44.0 cm mean error, against a median 5.6 cm). In Mouse2012 141832 and Mouse2011 142436 the events lead by 80 to 90 frames (best lag error 5.4 and 2.8 cm); Mouse2011 105556 and 104511 show no position information at any lag within 200 frames; Mouse2026 115639 and Mouse2021 132808 are weaker at lag 0 (14.7 and 15.3 cm). The paper's code trims the first 350 frames of Mouse2026 115639, which suggests a known offset there. Four of them (Mouse2012 141832 and the three of Mouse2011) have flat IMSE curves and the other two low, slowly rising ones, so the IMSE fits give meaningless I0 and N: Mouse2011 105556 gives I0 = 260 with 4 subsets and 241 with 80 (against a median 1e-4), and 104511 a negative I0 and N with 4 subsets and I0 = 231 with 80.
    Found by the attempt on Hazon et al. 2022 (hazon-2022-01, 2026-11-11)
  • File format errorFour sessions have negative positions (Mouse2012 141832 down to -3.7e7 pixels over 795 frames, Mouse2022 093722 down to -1.5e5 over 79 frames, Mouse2024 090858&091446 a ramp from -12,628 over the first 503 frames, and Mouse2028 105610 down to -23 over 6 frames, where the track spans about 60 to 700 pixels). The first valid trial of Mouse2022 093722 holds the 79 frames; no valid trial of the other three holds any.
    Found by the attempt on Hazon et al. 2022 (hazon-2022-01, 2026-11-11)
  • Description missingTwo session IDs join two recordings (Mouse2024 090858&091446 with 55,247 frames, and 130536&130559) without saying how; the first has 77.6 events per neuron per minute against a median 12.0, and its rate rises from 38.6 in the first quarter of the frames to 103.1 in the last.
    Found by the attempt on Hazon et al. 2022 (hazon-2022-01, 2026-11-11)
