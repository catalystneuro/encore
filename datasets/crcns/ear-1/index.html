Dataset unversioned files (the archive keeps no versions) · tested 2026-11-06 · 9 open findings

  • Description missingThe data set stores CL, QTm, V0.5 and alphaB per cell, but not the lateral wall capacitance CLW, the stereocilia bundle capacitance CSB or the non-lateral-wall capacitance CNLW that the paper's Eq. 8a uses, so the paper's CLW and charge density cannot be taken from it. Computed here with the CSB rule of Corbitt et al. (2012) on the stored area, CLW reproduces the Fig. S1 legend's value (18.30 against 18.56 pF), but several results (the XE991 fits, the band of Fig. 1, the lower C_Leq limit) turn on the CLW of the smallest cells, which this rule makes as small as 0.48 pF.
    Found by the attempt on Farrell et al. 2020 (farrell-2020-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Description missingThe linear capacitance at negative voltages, CLn, and the rule for when it could be estimated are not stored; neither are the linear-region indices in the HDF5 files (the MATLAB files keep them as indposit_ramplin).
    Found by the attempt on Farrell et al. 2020 (farrell-2020-01, 2026-11-06)
  • File format errorIn every HDF5 file with DC steps (19 cells), `transformed data set/.../I_mean` has shape (N, 2) but stores its values in MATLAB column order (the N means, then the N SDs). Read as given, the mean and SD columns are interleaved; reshaped to (2, N) it equals the (mean, SD) columns of the original MATLAB files.
    Found by the attempt on Farrell et al. 2020 (farrell-2020-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Metadata errorFile sensory_cell_#52.h5 holds the membrane at +0.06 V before and after the ramp, where the stimulus description in every file says -0.06 V.
    Found by the attempt on Farrell et al. 2020 (farrell-2020-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Description missing`cell_surface_area` follows two formulas, 2 pi r l for the 34 cells with a cell length and 2 pi r LW + 2 pi r^2 (with the lateral wall length) for 50 others, and the description does not say so or which length is which measurement; `cochlear_outer_hair_cell_length` is stored for 35 cells and `cochlear_outer_hair_cell_lateral_wall_length` for 83.
    Found by the attempt on Farrell et al. 2020 (farrell-2020-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Selection not documentedThe cells behind the paper's tables are not marked. The data set gives 27 pigmented cells without XE991 with 144 mM chloride (paper 28), 17 pigmented XE991 cells with 144 mM chloride split 5 and 12 by region (Table 1 row a, 4 and 11), 69 Fig. 2 cells (legend 70), 46 cells for Fig. 3b by the rule used here (legend 44).
    Found by the attempt on Farrell et al. 2020 (farrell-2020-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Description missingThe original MATLAB field `toxins` labels 15 of the 20 XE991 cells `XE991` and 5 `yes`; the HDF5 files store 60 uM for all 20, while the Methods give 30 to 60 uM.
    Found by the attempt on Farrell et al. 2020 (farrell-2020-01, 2026-11-06)
  • Identifier missingThe `original cell number` is not unique: 13 numbers are used by two cells each, from different dates and mostly different researchers and colonies. The file number is the only unique identifier, and the paper names cells by original number (Fig. S1, cell 99).
    Found by the attempt on Farrell et al. 2020 (farrell-2020-01, 2026-11-06)
  • OtherThe largest conductance at V0.5 among XE991 cells is 8.5 nS from the DC steps and 7.0 nS from the admittance, about 25 times the 335 pS the paper reports; the unit or the measure in the paper may differ from the data set's.
    Found by the attempt on Farrell et al. 2020 (farrell-2020-01, 2026-11-06)
