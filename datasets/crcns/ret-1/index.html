  • fig1-broad-expression

    Pcdh-gamma proteins (Pcdh-gamma-GFP in fusg mice) are present in all five retinal layers and in all six retinal cell types (photoreceptors, horizontal, bipolar, amacrine, RGCs and Mueller glia).

    As judged by localization of GFP in Pcdh-γ fusg mice, Pcdh-γs are present in all five retinal layers ... All six cell types were Pcdh-γ-positive ... Thus, Pcdh-γs are expressed in all cell types of the neural retina.

  • fig9a-response-variety

    RGCs in mutant and control retinas show a similar variety of responses to small flashing spots, including sustained and transient ON, OFF and ON-OFF responses.

    RGCs in Pcdh-γ mutant and control retinas showed a similar variety of responses to small flashing spots, including sustained and transient ON, OFF and ON-OFF responses

    Results, Functional visual circuits form in the absence of Pcdh- γ s, paragraph 3; Figure 9A

    How it will be judged

    The paper shows example rasters only and defines no classes. ON, OFF and ON-OFF are read from the On-Off index of Figure 9C (spikes in the on-phase over all spikes); the paper gives no cut-offs and none for transient against sustained. Readings for the ON/OFF cut-offs are (a) index below 1/3 OFF, above 2/3 ON, otherwise ON-OFF; (b) below 0.2 OFF, above 0.8 ON. Transient against sustained is read as the ratio of the spike rate in the second half of a phase to that in the first, with readings (a) below 0.5 transient, (b) below 0.3 transient. Reproduced when, under every reading, the mutant group and the control group each hold cells of each of the three polarities and of both time courses; the class counts per group are reported.

  • fig9b-direction-selective

    Both ON and ON-OFF direction-selective RGCs are found in mutant retinas.

    Both ON and ON-OFF direction-selective cells were encountered in mutant retinas ( Figure 9B and data not shown)

    Results, Functional visual circuits form in the absence of Pcdh- γ s, paragraph 3; Figure 9B

    How it will be judged

    The stimulus is from the Figure 9B legend, a grating moving in 8 directions (wavelength 664 um, speed 664 um/s), with the average firing rate per direction. The paper defines no direction selectivity index and no threshold. Readings are (a) the vector-sum index |sum r e^(i theta)| / sum r above 0.3; (b) (preferred minus opposite) / (preferred plus opposite) above 0.5; in each also requiring the index to exceed the 95th percentile of 1000 shuffles of the direction labels over trials. ON and ON-OFF are taken from the flash readings of fig9a-response-variety. The Results also name moving bars ("moving bars and gratings", with "data not shown"), whose parameters are not given; mutant moving-bar recordings are analysed the same way, per direction. Reproduced when, under every reading, at least one mutant ON cell and one mutant ON-OFF cell are direction selective. Blocked when the data set holds no moving-grating or moving-bar recordings from mutant retinas.

  • fig9d-rf-size-similar

    Receptive field centre size varies greatly among RGCs, but its distribution is similar in control and mutant retinas.

    The size of the receptive field center varied greatly among RGCs, but the distribution was similar in control and mutant retinas ( Figure 9D )

    Results, Functional visual circuits form in the absence of Pcdh- γ s, paragraph 4; Figure 9D

    How it will be judged

    A claim of absence. Methods, Electrophysiology, Eqns 1 and 2, and Figure 9D legend. The spike-triggered average of the flickering-bar stimulus, intensity with its time average subtracted, over all spikes of the cell; its length in time is not given, and a window of 0.5 s (30 frames) before each spike is used as a standard choice that holds the response. The separation h(x, t) = b(x) a(t) is not specified; readings are (i) the leading singular vectors of h, (ii) b(x) as h at the time of its largest absolute value and a(t) as h at the bar of its largest absolute value. Centre size is the full width at half maximum of |b(x)| around its peak, in um from the bar width (8.3 or 16.6 um) of each recording, with linear interpolation between bars as a standard choice. Reproduced when the 95% bands of the two cumulative histograms overlap under every combination of the CI readings (header) and the separation readings; not reproduced when they separate under all; blocked as methods_underspecified otherwise. The medians and a Kolmogorov-Smirnov test are shown as a variant.

  • fig9g-threshold-unaltered

    In a Linear-Nonlinear model fitted to the flicker responses, the response threshold of mutant RGCs is unaltered.

    Based on fitting with a Linear-Nonlinear model ... we found that the threshold is unaltered, but the gain is reduced in mutants ( Figures 9G-H )

    Results, Functional visual circuits form in the absence of Pcdh- γ s, paragraph 4; Figure 9G

    How it will be judged

    A claim of absence. Methods, Eqn 3, the generator y(t) is the stimulus filtered by the cell's spike-triggered average h(x, t) (Eqn 1), summed over bars and scaled by Z to unit variance; the rate is N(y) = B for y below theta and B + G y above. The fitting procedure is not given; readings are (a) least squares of the model against the firing rate in bins of one stimulus frame (16.6 ms); (b) least squares against the mean rate in bins of y (the empirical nonlinearity of Chichilnisky 2001), with 20 bins of equal count as a standard choice. The printed form B + G y is discontinuous at theta unless B is adjusted; the continuous form B + G (y - theta) is a second reading of the nonlinearity. The filter and the fit use the same data, as the Methods describe no split. Judged on the paper's comparison, the overlap of the 95% bands of the cumulative histograms of theta. Reproduced when they overlap under every combination of the CI, fit and form readings; not reproduced when they separate under all; blocked as methods_underspecified otherwise. Medians and a Kolmogorov-Smirnov test are shown as a variant.
