The paper's electrophysiology findings largely held up in this data, while its behavioural and imaging findings could not be tested because those recordings are not in the dataset. ALM selectivity matched: 912 of 1,245 pyramidal neurons were selective, contra- and ipsi-preferring neurons were equally common, and activity on error trials followed the licking direction. Pyramidal tract neurons, unlike intratelencephalic neurons, were mostly contra-preferring when only selective neurons were counted, and their late contralateral selectivity matched in size but not in onset or in its comparison with all selective neurons. The licking effects of photoinhibiting ALM and of photoactivating pyramidal tract neurons are among the missing data. Minor claims on spike widths, fast-spiking neurons and antidromic jitter held in part.

Reproduction A motor cortex circuit for motor planning and movement (Li et al. 2015)

It tests trial-type selectivity and its time course, response categories, error trials, contra and ipsi preference, fast-spiking neurons, antidromic latencies, and the contralateral selectivity of PT neurons. Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • Measured on the 19 electrophysiology mice in the data set; the paper's figure is over 42 mice, and the behaviour-only mice are not deposited.task-performance
  • No-lick trials are left out of the denominator, as the data set's Demo_get_performance.m does; the Methods say only that lick early trials are excluded.task-performance
  • Waveforms are the deposited 29-sample snippets at 19531.25 Hz, so widths come in steps of 0.0512 ms; the paper does not say whether its widths were interpolated.unit-counts
  • The t-test is on spike rates (count over each trial's epoch length), as the data set's analysis_compile_all_data.m runs it (lines 217 to 221 and 300, printed in the notebook); the Methods say spike counts, which differ where an epoch is not 1.3 s (3,707 trials), and the test on counts is shown as a variant.fig2-selective-fraction
  • Trials with axonal photostimulation (types 1 to 5 and NaN) and trials with GoodTrials 0 are left out, and each unit's trials are limited to the span from its first to its last trial with a spike, as the data set's analysis_compile_all_data.m does; the paper does not state either.fig2-selective-fraction
  • The text and Fig. 2d disagree on which of 389 and 304 is the peri-movement-only count; the data match the figure (recorded as a paper inconsistency).fig2d-response-categories
  • The 10 trials that set each neuron's preference are drawn at random with a fixed seed; the preference uses spike counts from sample onset to the end of the response epoch, as the data set's compile script does. Neurons need 11 trials of each type for the split (848 with more than 15, as the script requires, give the same).fig2c-selectivity-time-course
  • The 0.5 bound is the claims file's assumption read from Fig. 2c; the paper gives no threshold.fig2c-dynamic-single-neurons
  • The paper does not say how antidromic spikes are told from spontaneous ones. Assumed - the first spike within 10 ms of a pulse, kept when within +/-1 ms of the neuron's modal latency (0.2 ms bins). Between 0.5 and 1 ms from the mode the IT neurons have 783 spikes where the spontaneous background (latencies more than 2 ms from the mode) predicts 69.7. This shows the spikes are locked to the pulse, not that they are antidromic - several IT neurons show humps several ms wide, and the four broadest hold 316 of the 783 spikes. The window rests instead on the readings that need none (fits of a narrow peak plus background, with and without a broad component), which give IT jitter near the +/-1 ms value. The +/-0.5 ms window, adopted before verification, is shown as a variant.antidromic-latency-jitter
  • Pulse onsets are read from the AOM input trace at 10 kHz, so they are quantised to 0.1 ms, and any delay between the AOM input and the light is not known.antidromic-latency-jitter
  • IT tagging trials are type 2; types 3 to 5, undocumented, are included as a variant.antidromic-latency-jitter
  • Antidromic spikes are selected as in antidromic-latency-jitter.ed5g-it-second-pulse-jitter
  • The paper does not say how the first movement is found in the photodiode trace, and the trace's baseline (0.07 to 4.83 V), scale and sign differ between sessions. Two rules are run, the noise rule (k = 3, 5, 10; floor 2 or 5 mV; 5 ms hold) and the peak rule (5% to 50% of the trial's peak excursion). No choice among them has a basis in the paper, and they give different outcomes.reaction-time
  • Measured on the 19 electrophysiology mice; the paper's mice for this number are not stated.reaction-time
  • The paper does not define a large difference or contra-suppression; the claims file's readings are used, with the 0.5 s before sample onset as baseline (the data set's compile script's baseline) and the whole pre-sample period as a variant.contra-ipsi-selectivity-forms
