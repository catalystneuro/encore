  • lesion-directed-song-abolished

    After complete bilateral Uva lesions, birds presented with a female court her but do not sing; they produce only sporadic short sounds like introductory notes without their rhythm.

    However, consistent with previous reports [ 26 , 27 ], lesioned birds failed to sing and only produced sporadic short sounds, acoustically similar to introductory notes but without their characteristic rhythmicity

    Results, Uva is necessary for adult stereotyped song, paragraph 1; S1 Video

    How it will be judged

    The paper shows this by video and gives no measure. Open; the reading used here (not the paper's) is, on post-lesion directed-song recordings segmented by the Segmentation method, that no bout of the bird's pre-lesion motif occurs, judged with the spectral correlation of the Maturity index method (no bout of at least 700 ms whose correlation with pre-lesion bouts reaches the range of pre-lesion bout pairs), in every lesioned bird recorded in the directed context. The rhythm of the short sounds is not tested. Untestable unless the dataset marks which recordings were directed.
