Dataset unversioned files (the archive keeps no versions) · tested 2026-11-09 · 7 open findings

  • Data missingThe data descriptor gives 343 cells, 219 from monkey X and 124 from monkey B. Every session with a trial record has monkey X's target layout (targets about 9.2 cm apart horizontally and 7.4 cm vertically, middle row at y near -4 cm, depth near 10 cm, as in Table 1 for monkey X), in every month from October 2008 to November 2009; none has monkey B's square layout at a depth near 14.5 cm. Monkey B's data appear not to be in the data set.
    Found by the attempt on Shi et al. 2013 (shi-2013-01, 2026-11-09)
  • Selection not documentedThe data set holds 359 monkey X sessions with the paper's complete design (5 completed trials per target and condition) and 485 sorted units in them (83 sessions have 2 units, 21 have 3 or 4), against the paper's 219 monkey X sessions and neurons, plus 47 sessions with an incomplete design. All were recorded on 92 days before the paper was submitted, and consecutive sessions of a day do not look like one neuron recorded again (median tuning correlation 0.04, against -0.01 across days). Which sessions and units the paper analysed is not documented.
    Found by the attempt on Shi et al. 2013 (shi-2013-01, 2026-11-09)
  • Description missingThe condition of a trial (Vision or No Vision) is coded only as 4 or 7 in field 14 of an undocumented ASCII record strobed after each trial. Nothing says which is Vision. Code 7 trials are aborted more often after the end of the reach (5,688 against 4,423) and not before it (6,040 against 6,006), which suggests 7 is No Vision; this attempt assumes so. Question for the data owners, which code is Vision.
    Found by the attempt on Shi et al. 2013 (shi-2013-01, 2026-11-09)
  • Description missingThe event codes are not described. EventCodes.txt is a generic list (TAPE = 1, DISPLAY = 10 and so on) that does not match the codes in the files (0, 1, 55, 3 to 8, the target codes 44 to 66, 10, 12, 13, 14, 255). Their meaning, and that of the 19, 22 or 24 fields of the per-trial text record (task parameters, target, condition, hand position, two further values), had to be read from their timing and values.
    Found by the attempt on Shi et al. 2013 (shi-2013-01, 2026-11-09)
  • Description missingThe analog channels (in .plx and .plxAnaAD files) are named AD01 to AD16 without description, the descriptor does not say what they record, and it lists the variables of the .plxAnaAD.mat files but not the layout of the .plxAnaAD.dat files that hold the samples. No continuous arm or eye position could be identified; the trial record gives one hand position per trial.
    Found by the attempt on Shi et al. 2013 (shi-2013-01, 2026-11-09)
  • Description missingThe descriptor gives no units for the time stamps. In the .mat files spike times are in milliseconds and event times in seconds; they agree with the .plx file of the session checked (010609_1).
    Found by the attempt on Shi et al. 2013 (shi-2013-01, 2026-11-09)
  • OtherNine early files (d1 to d14.mat, 2008) have a 19 field record without hand positions and a minimum hold (record field 11) of 500 or 600 ms where the other sessions have 800 to 1,100 ms, below the paper's 800 ms hold.
    Found by the attempt on Shi et al. 2013 (shi-2013-01, 2026-11-09)
