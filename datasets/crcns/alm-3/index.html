  • models-monolithic-fail

    Monolithic network models (fixed-point attractor, integrators, trained recurrent networks) do not recover their unperturbed trajectory after transient silencing of half of their neurons.

    Overall, all “monolithic” models consisting of one network were unable to explain robustness

    Results, Robust model networks, paragraph 1; Figure 1c; Extended Data Fig. 1f-i, 4c

    How it will be judged

    A simulation that uses no recorded data (the data set's title says it holds simulations as well); parameters from the Methods and ED Fig. 1 legend (simple integrator N = 100, tau 100 ms, one eigenvalue 0.99; corrective-feedback integrator with the synaptic time constants given; FORCE network N = 400, tau 200 ms, g 1.5, p 0.1, 30 iterations; tamed chaos, 20 iterations). Silencing holds half the neurons at zero for 0.2 s < t < 1.0 s (50 random neurons in the simple integrator, 200 in the FORCE network); in the corrective-feedback integrator it is a large current into the inhibitory neuron with negative rates disallowed (ED Fig. 1g), its amplitude open.
