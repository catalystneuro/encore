The spike counts confirm the shift (P11C2 has 327 spikes in stable1, 52 in rotation, and is listed with a stable2 file of 21567).
    Found by the attempt on Park et al. 2019 (park-2019-02, 2026-11-13)

Stable2 of 1015R029 and of 1025R029, and the room-frame rotating trial of 1211R039 and of 1212R039, whose arena-frame tracking differs. One trial of each pair holds another day's tracking.
