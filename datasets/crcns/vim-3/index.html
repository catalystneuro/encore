Dataset unversioned files (the archive keeps no versions) · tested 2026-11-08 · 6 open findings

  • Data missingThe data set holds the raw functional runs, event sequences, task records and stimulus screenshots, but not the anatomical images, cortical surfaces, retinotopic mapping or V1, V2 and V3 definitions that every response measure of the paper uses. The descriptor says so and asks users to request them on the CRCNS forum. 15 of the 16 claims are blocked, 14 of them for this reason.
    Found by the attempt on Mannion et al. 2015 (mannion-2015-01, 2026-11-08)
  • Description missingThe data set holds the 120 stimulus screenshots, but not the measured CIE coordinates of the monitor's primaries needed to convert their pixel values to DKL space, in which the image statistics of Figure 5 and the radial-bias test are computed; nor are they in the paper or its code (a local PsychoPy monitor profile and the unlinked package monitors.conversions). The patches are in colour (R, G and B differ in most pixels of 118 of the 120 patches), so the chromatic channels cannot be formed without them.
    Found by the attempt on Mannion et al. 2015 (mannion-2015-01, 2026-11-08)
  • Metadata errorThe data set's README.txt says it contains fMRI measurements in areas V1, V2 and V3. The files are whole volumes of a 35-slice occipital slab with no area labels, so no measurement in V1, V2 or V3 can be formed from the data set alone.
    Found by the attempt on Mannion et al. 2015 (mannion-2015-01, 2026-11-08)
  • Description missingThe NIfTI files are named by scanner series number (for p1003 series 3, 4, 6 to 13, for p1010 series 4 to 13), while the sequence and task files are named by run, and the descriptor does not give the mapping. The participant configurations of the paper's code take the series in order as runs 1 to 10; a leave-one-run-out fit supports this for six participants, and for p1011 it could not be confirmed.
    Found by the attempt on Mannion et al. 2015 (mannion-2015-01, 2026-11-08)
  • OtherFor p1011 the timing of the event sequences and the run order could not be confirmed from the data without motion correction; the held-out runs barely follow their own sequences (mean t 0.7 and 1.1, against 2.6 to 4.9 in the others), and a coarse motion index reaches 3.6 mm by run 10, against at most 1.8 mm in the other participants. Its upper-field responsive voxels also lie above its lower-field ones, the reverse of the other six.
    Found by the attempt on Mannion et al. 2015 (mannion-2015-01, 2026-11-08)
  • Description missingFor p1004 runs 1 to 5 the target column of the task lookup does not describe the task the participant performed; the paper's code redefines the targets as white 9 and black 5 for those runs (the participant misunderstood the instructions). The descriptor does not say so, and the four button keys used in the task files are not described.
    Found by the attempt on Mannion et al. 2015 (mannion-2015-01, 2026-11-08)
