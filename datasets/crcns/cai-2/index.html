Dataset unversioned files (the archive keeps no versions) · tested 2026-11-13 · 5 open findings

  • OtherTwo jRCaMP1a files, data_20150526_cell2_001.mat and data_20150526_cell2_002.mat, hold the same recording, with identical soma fluorescence and raw ephys, the same 75 spikes. Counted twice it adds 7 one-spike events and 12 noise traces to the jRCaMP1a analysis.
    Found by the attempt on Dana et al. 2016 (dana-2016-01, 2026-11-13)
  • Identifier missingThe files name the cell and the recording date but not the animal. The paper reports 6 and 5 mice, which match the number of recording dates, but that each date is one mouse cannot be checked.
    Found by the attempt on Dana et al. 2016 (dana-2016-01, 2026-11-13)
  • OtherIn data_20150707_cell1_001.mat (jRCaMP1a, 304 spikes) the corrected soma fluorescence shows no relation to the spikes (peak correlation of spike counts with the fluorescence rise 0.028 at a lag of 14 frames, below the 0.050 of shifted spike trains), and in data_20150605_cell7_003.mat (15 spikes) it falls just below it (0.047 against 0.048). The shared clock of spikes and frames could not be established for these two; all other 44 recordings peak at lags of -1 to +1 frame, above the null. The first contributes 31 of the 162 jRCaMP1a one-spike events.
    Found by the attempt on Dana et al. 2016 (dana-2016-01, 2026-11-13)
  • OtherThe analysis script data_analysis_for_CRCNS_jRGECO1a.m calls rawf2df_f and fit_dynamic_fitting, which are not in the data set, so it cannot run as deposited; and there is no script for the jRCaMP1a files.
    Found by the attempt on Dana et al. 2016 (dana-2016-01, 2026-11-13)
  • Data missingcai-2 holds only the simultaneous imaging and cell-attached recordings of Figure 4, as its description says, and not the visual stimulus of those recordings. The cultured neuron screen recordings, the purified protein data, the V1 grating imaging (Figures 3, 5, 6 and the Figure 4 supplements), and the Drosophila, zebrafish and C. elegans imaging are not in it, and the paper names no other deposit of them; the eLife site holds only summary tables (Figure 2, source data 1; Figure 7, source data 1 to 3). 24 of the paper's 35 claims are blocked as data missing.
    Found by the attempt on Dana et al. 2016 (dana-2016-01, 2026-11-13)
