Dataset unversioned files (the archive keeps no versions) · tested 2026-11-13 · 5 open findings

  • Data missingThe data set holds no voltage-clamp recording. The paper analyses 29 probes in 14 of 42 voltage-clamped A1 cells (Figures 5 and 6), but every one of Asari's 346 files is a whole-cell current-clamp recording in mV or a cell-attached recording, and Summary.mat lists only current-clamp, cell-attached and unpublished groups. The conductance findings cannot be tested.
    Found by the attempt on Asari et al. 2009 (asari-2009-01, 2026-11-13)
  • Data missingThe example recording of Figure 7 (a natural sound probe after DMRs in 0.625-2.5, 2.5-10 and 10-40 kHz) is not in the data set; no recording has DMR conditioning stimuli in those single bands. The DMR frequency variants in it are 2-octave bands from 0.25 to 32 kHz in 1-octave steps.
    Found by the attempt on Asari et al. 2009 (asari-2009-01, 2026-11-13)
  • OtherThe data set holds 145 A1 whole-cell cells and 14 MGB cells, against the 194 current-clamp and 16 MGB cells the paper recorded. Its natural sound and MGB groups hold exactly the 39 cells with 305 probes and the 14 cells with 93 probes the paper analysed. The synthetic sound group holds 96 cells; with the natural sound group, 133 cells meet the paper's rule for analysis against its 123, and the ensembles split differently from the paper's list (DMR 15 cells against 6, MHT 26 against 39, and 14 cells with SoundTrans variants of natural sounds).
    Found by the attempt on Asari et al. 2009 (asari-2009-01, 2026-11-13)
  • Description missingWhich conditioning stimuli of a synthetic sound recording vary which sound property (the sets behind Table 1 and Figure 8) is listed in the notes of only the 19-stimulus MHT recordings. For the others it has to be rebuilt from the stimulus parameters and descriptions. Rebuilt with one test per set for AM and FM, the sets give the five probe totals of Table 1, but the split by probe type does not match: 82 of the 96 AM tests and all 82 FM tests have a natural sound probe, and only 2 recordings have MHT probes, where Table 1 gives 71 and 57 MHT-probe tests.
    Found by the attempt on Asari et al. 2009 (asari-2009-01, 2026-11-13)
  • Metadata errorSummary.mat lists two cells, 20060314-ha1-001 and 20060320-ha3-003, in both the natural sound and the synthetic sound group, so their tuning-curve files are named twice. The recordings are the same files; no two files hold identical traces.
    Found by the attempt on Asari et al. 2009 (asari-2009-01, 2026-11-13)
