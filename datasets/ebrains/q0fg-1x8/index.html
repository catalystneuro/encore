Tests the findings of Nagelhus, Andersson, Gonzalo Cogno, Moser and Moser (2023) on EBRAINS dataset 10.25493/Q0FG-1X8 (v1), which holds spike times and tracking of CA1 and CA3 cells in rats foraging in an open field with and without an object, from tetrodes and Neuropixels probes.
