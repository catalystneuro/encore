Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • The face and scene parcels are the ones deposited in derivatives/ROIs of the snapshot (rSTS_masked, lSTS, rOFA, lOFA, rFFA, lFFA; rRSC, lRSC, rTOS, lTOS, rPPA, lPPA), which the dataset's README names as the Julian et al. 2012 parcels; the claims file treats them as an outside input, but no outside data were used.   • Assumed O2a (one regressor per artifact timepoint), as deposited in the paper's own nuisance files, and O3a (all 168 TRs), since the Methods restrict the timepoints to TRs 11 to 168 only for the reverse correlation and maturity analyses (paragraphs 9 and 13), not for the inter-region correlation (paragraph 7).
