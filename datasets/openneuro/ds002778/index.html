Dataset version 1.0.5 · tested 2026-10-27 · 4 open findings

  • Data missingThe manual artifact marks (rejected samples) and the list of excluded noisy electrodes that the paper's preprocessing used are not in the dataset; every channels.tsv marks all channels good and the events files hold only a start trigger. The paper's code loads them from rejectscombined.mat, which is not public. Analyses here run without rejection.
    Found by the attempt on Jackson et al. 2019 (jackson-2019-01, 2026-10-27)
  • Description missingThe questionnaire fields of the beh JSON files (Total UPDRS, UPDRS 18-26, Brady kinesia UPDRS, Rigidity UPDRS, Left and Right UPDRS, H&Y, Beck) have no description; the only beh sidecar describes a dummy trial column. Which part of the UPDRS "Total UPDRS" covers has to be inferred from the values.
    Found by the attempt on Jackson et al. 2019 (jackson-2019-01, 2026-10-27)
  • OtherThe sub-pd16 ses-on recording, which participants.tsv says was exported from a preprocessed EEGLAB file, has electrode standard deviations about six times those of every other recording after the same preprocessing (median 62.6 uV), about 100 times their power, and no alpha peak; its means are near zero but its channels are not average referenced. The dataset does not say how it was processed or scaled. The sub-pd6 ses-on file, also exported from EEGLAB, is average referenced and lasts 289 s.
    Found by the attempt on Jackson et al. 2019 (jackson-2019-01, 2026-10-27)
  • Description missingThe events files hold one trigger (value 1) a few seconds into most recordings, and other values in sub-pd16 ses-on (130817) and sub-pd17 ses-off (a second 65536); what the trigger marks is not described.
    Found by the attempt on Jackson et al. 2019 (jackson-2019-01, 2026-10-27)
