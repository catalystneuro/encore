    How it will be judged

    Welch power spectra as in the Methods (window 256 samples, overlap 128, nFFT 450, at 512 Hz; pwelch's default Hamming window), averaged over electrodes and participants of each group (off, on, controls). Partially reproduced when the alpha peak is present in all groups and the beta peak in some.
