Dataset version 1.0.1 · tested 2026-10-28 · 9 open findings

  • OtherThe dataset holds 68 sessions with 3353 trials (15 sessions with fewer than 50 trials), where the paper reports 71 sessions of 50 trials; the 3045 correct trials equal the paper's count. The README says each session includes about 50 trials and gives no session count.
    Found by the attempt on Dimakopoulos et al. 2022 (dimakopoulos-2022-01, 2026-10-28)
  • Metadata errorIn every channels.tsv, low_cutoff is 1000 and high_cutoff is 0.5, the reverse of the 0.5 to 1000 Hz passband given in the ieeg.json and eeg.json sidecars and the paper.
    Found by the attempt on Dimakopoulos et al. 2022 (dimakopoulos-2022-01, 2026-10-28)
  • Description missingThe EDF headers leave the physical dimension of every signal blank; only channels.tsv gives µV. The derivative virtual sensors carry no unit, and their sampling rate varies from 200 to 4096 Hz across subjects without description.
    Found by the attempt on Dimakopoulos et al. 2022 (dimakopoulos-2022-01, 2026-10-28)
  • Metadata errorIn sub-11 the contacts named PHL lie at positive MNI x (right) and those named PHR at negative x, and the atlas labels follow the coordinates (PHL1 at x = 33.6, "Right Hippocampus"; PHR1 at x = -31.1), so the electrode names give the wrong hemisphere; 3 contacts of sub-12 have the same mismatch, and in sub-01 the atlas label names the hemisphere opposite to the x coordinate for 12 contacts (for example ECL1 at x = -24, "Right Amygdala").
    Found by the attempt on Dimakopoulos et al. 2022 (dimakopoulos-2022-01, 2026-10-28)
  • Metadata errorparticipants.tsv gives a pathology different from Table 1 of the paper for eLife participants 5 (hippocampal sclerosis against brain contusion), 6 (brain contusion against hippocampal sclerosis) and 15 (dysembryoplastic neuroepithelial tumor against hippocampal sclerosis); 5 and 6 look swapped.
    Found by the attempt on Dimakopoulos et al. 2022 (dimakopoulos-2022-01, 2026-10-28)
  • Metadata errorThe README lists the virtual sensors as temporal superior lobe, lateral prefrontal cortex, occipital cortex, posterior parietal cortex and Broca, but the derivative files hold DLPFC, OFC, PPC, AC and V1 (no Broca), plus the scalp channels, which the README does not mention.
    Found by the attempt on Dimakopoulos et al. 2022 (dimakopoulos-2022-01, 2026-10-28)
  • Identifier missingThe derivative LCMV files carry no trial numbers. Their trials are the correct trials of set sizes 6 and 8 in session order (shown here by correlating their scalp channels with the raw EEG), without the artifact-flagged trials in 13 subjects and with them in sub-13 and sub-14.
    Found by the attempt on Dimakopoulos et al. 2022 (dimakopoulos-2022-01, 2026-10-28)
  • Data missingThe derivative files hold sources for correct trials only, and the beamformer filters, electrode alignment and head model are not deposited, so the paper's source statistics (Table 1) cannot be repeated from the dataset. The incorrect-trial sources of Figure 4f are not deposited either; they can be approximated because each virtual sensor is an exact linear combination of the file's own scalp channels, which gives the correct-trial filters.
    Found by the attempt on Dimakopoulos et al. 2022 (dimakopoulos-2022-01, 2026-10-28)
  • Other17 trials (16 in sub-14, 1 in sub-12) have SetSize 0, no Match, ResponseTime 0 and the artifact flag, apparently placeholders; ProbeLetter is n/a for every trial of sub-10 to sub-15. Neither is described in the events sidecar.
    Found by the attempt on Dimakopoulos et al. 2022 (dimakopoulos-2022-01, 2026-10-28)
