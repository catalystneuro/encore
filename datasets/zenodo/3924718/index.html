Dataset version 3924718 · tested 2026-11-25 · 9 open findings

  • Data missingThe record holds spike annotations and raw data only for the evoked preparations. The nine preparations with spontaneous gastric mill rhythms (Figure 2 and its supplement) and the nine with the pyloric pacemaker hyperpolarized (Figure 4, figure supplement 2) are not in it, although the paper's data statement names only this record for its raw data and spike annotations. Four of the paper's findings cannot be tested.#
    Found by the attempt on Powell et al. 2021 (powell-2021-01, 2026-11-25)
  • Data missing941_003.zip and 941_006.zip hold raw ABF recordings of two preparations recorded on 11 and 12 June 2019 (ABF header dates), with no spike annotations and no description. Neither is among the ten evoked preparations of evoked_data.mat, and they belong to none of the three sets the Methods describe (2013, 2017, 2020). 941_006 is one of the four preparations of Figure 3, figure supplement 1, so that claim can be tested only on the other three.#
    Found by the attempt on Powell et al. 2021 (powell-2021-01, 2026-11-25)
  • Data missingThere is no zip of raw recordings for 901_005, one of the ten evoked preparations; its spike times, temperature and mask are in evoked_data.mat.#
    Found by the attempt on Powell et al. 2021 (powell-2021-01, 2026-11-25)
  • Description missingThe record has no README and its description is the paper's abstract. The fields of evoked_data.mat are not described; the mask (0 in runs of about 7 s, stimulation by the paper's code) and the 1 kHz sampling of temperature and mask are evident only from the values and the code. The preparation and file names are MATLAB categorical objects, readable outside MATLAB only by decoding the file's MCOS subsystem, and the fields T and time_offset are empty in every preparation. Zenodo types the record as a journal article, not a dataset.#
    Found by the attempt on Powell et al. 2021 (powell-2021-01, 2026-11-25)
  • Other932_151, one of the ten evoked preparations, was recorded on 2 May 2019 by the header of 932_151_0000.abf, while the Methods say the evoked preparations came from crabs caught in 2017. 901_046 and 901_098 carry 2017 dates as described.#
    Found by the attempt on Powell et al. 2021 (powell-2021-01, 2026-11-25)
  • OtherSeven zips hold raw recordings as crabsort .crab files and four as ABF files. The .crab zip of 901_062 holds 101 files, while evoked_data.mat stacks 58 files of that preparation; the other zips hold as many files as are stacked.#
    Found by the attempt on Powell et al. 2021 (powell-2021-01, 2026-11-25)
  • OtherIn the data, 9 of the 10 evoked preparations have an LG rhythm at every temperature step from 7 to 21 C at which they were held, as the paper says, when 901_062, whose last step was 19 C, is counted; 901_086 was held and stimulated at 21 C for 330 s with no LG spike. The record does not say why 901_062 stopped at 19 C.#
    Found by the attempt on Powell et al. 2021 (powell-2021-01, 2026-11-25)
  • Otherevoked_data.mat matches the paper's analysis. Its ten preparations are those of getEvokedData.m, no recording is deposited twice, the stimulation mask and LG spikes and the temperature and PD spikes share their clocks, and with the code's burst rule and computations the paper's reported numbers are obtained exactly (Q10 paired t-test t = 1.27, p = 0.235; 3799 cycles and Spearman p = 0.88 in Figure 5f; LG phase trend rho = -0.31, p = 0.46; 3816 LG burst starts).#
    Found by the attempt on Powell et al. 2021 (powell-2021-01, 2026-11-25)
  • File format errorThe DG spike times in evoked_data.mat are not sorted in nine of the ten preparations (1 to 50 times per preparation, 129 in all, a spike time is smaller than the one before it); the PD and LG spike times are sorted. The paper's code (getEvokedData.m) sorts PD and LG on loading but not DG; taking DG in the file's order changes the DG bursts of the code's rule by -2 to +6 per preparation (3675 against 3667 in all). This attempt sorts all three.#
    Found by the attempt on Powell et al. 2021 (powell-2021-01, 2026-11-25)
