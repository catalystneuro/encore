  • At 11 degrees C and -110 mV, Ih in PD and LP cells was 1.5 nA in saline and 0.23 nA in Cs+, a reduction of 93% (4 PD and 3 LP cells, paired t-test p = 0.0005).

    Results, The steady-state frequency increase in response to elevated temperature was decreased by Cs+, paragraph 1; Figure 1-figure supplement 1A and 1Cfigs1-ih-reduced-11c
    From the paper

    At 11°C and –110 mV, I h was 1.5±0.83 nA in saline and 0.23±.5 nA in Cs + , a reduction of 93±30% (4 PD cells, 3 LP cells, paired Student’s t-test; p=0.0005)

    Criterion

    Ih is the difference between the initial current needed to hold the step to -110 mV and the steady-state current (Results, paragraph 1; Figure 1-figure supplement 1 legend). The paper does not fix the windows. Readings are (a) initial current as the mean over the first 100 ms after the capacitive transient, steady state as the mean over the last 1 s of the 12 s step; (b) initial current as the extreme current in the first 500 ms after the transient, steady state as the last 1 s. Reduction per cell is 100 * (1 - Ih Cs / Ih saline), averaged over cells; the ratio of the means is a variant. Reproduced when, under one reading, the values are within tolerance and a paired t-test of saline against Cs+ gives p < 0.05.

  • The difference between a preparation's frequency in saline and in Cs+ increased significantly as temperature increased.

    Results, The steady-state frequency increase, paragraph 3; Figure 1D legendfig1d-difference-grows-with-temperature
    From the paper

    As temperature increased, the difference between a preparation’s frequency in saline and Cs + significantly increased (one-way repeated measures ANOVA [F(5,85) = 43.259, p<0.001, N=21])

    Criterion

    Per experiment, saline minus Cs+ mean frequency at each of the six holding temperatures (within 0.3 degrees C of 11, 13, 15, 17, 19 and 21 degrees C); a one-way repeated-measures ANOVA on temperature. The printed degrees of freedom, F(5,85), imply 18 experiments, not 21; readings are (a) every experiment with values at all six temperatures in both conditions; (b) the 21 experiments, with any missing value handled as MATLAB ranova does (dropping the experiment). Reproduced when, under one reading, the temperature effect has p < 0.05 and the mean difference at 21 degrees C exceeds that at 11 degrees C. The number of experiments entering is reported.

  • In an example preparation in Cs+, the frequency decrease right after the temperature step from 13 toward 15 degrees C came from an elongation of the PD depolarizing phase, not of the bursting, repolarization or inhibitory phases; a Cs+ + PTX example showed the same.

    Results, Cs+ revealed dynamic transitory frequency response, paragraph 1; Figure 2C legend; Results, In Cs+, follower neurons determined frequency-temperature sensitivity, paragraph 3, and Figure 4G for Cs+ + PTX; Discussion, The jags in frequency, paragraph 2fig2c-decrease-by-depolarizing-phase
    From the paper

    Alignment of each PD cycle during this window to the first PD spike reveals that the decrease in frequency was due to elongation of the depolarizing phase and not the bursting, repolarization, or inhibitory phases

    Criterion

    The paper shows this in one example preparation, not identified, for the step from 13 toward 15 degrees C, and gives no measure; the Discussion treats it as general ("the frequency decrease is associated with an elongation of the depolarizing phase", Figures 2C and 4G). The phase boundaries and windows are open; the paper names the phases (bursting, repolarization, inhibitory, depolarizing in the Results; bursting, repolarization, depolarizing in the Figure 2C legend) without defining them. Readings for the phases on the intracellular PD trace, per cycle, are (a) three phases as in the legend, burst (first to last PD spike), repolarization (last PD spike to the voltage minimum), depolarization (voltage minimum to the next first PD spike); (b) four phases as in the Results, burst (first to last PD spike), repolarization (last PD spike to the first LP spike), inhibition (first to last LP spike), depolarization (last LP spike to the next first PD spike). Windows, from the Discussion's "~30 s of temperature increase", are the cycles of the 30 s before the 13 to 15 degrees C step against the cycles of the first 30 s of warming whose period exceeds the pre-step mean. In each Cs+ experiment with intracellular PD, the share of the period increase carried by each phase. Reproduced when, under one reading, in most such experiments the depolarizing phase accounts for more than half of the period increase; partially reproduced when this holds only in the experiments with the largest frequency decrease. Not testable without intracellular PD traces.

  • In cycles of matched period between saline at 11 degrees C and Cs+, PD bursts were 51 ms shorter in Cs+ while LP fired 91 ms earlier and PY latency did not change (1.9 ms), and the PD offset advance did not correlate with the LP onset advance.

    Results, Phase of PD and LP advanced in Cs+, paragraph 3matched-period-lp-earlier
    From the paper

    Although PD burst duration was only 51±30 ms shorter in Cs + , LP fired 91±68 ms earlier. Conversely, PY latency did not change in these matched frequency samples (1.9±100 ms earlier).     Criterion

    The paper does not say how periods were matched. Readings are (a) per experiment, the Cs+ holding temperature whose mean period is nearest the saline 11 degrees C mean period, with latencies and durations as means over those holds; (b) per experiment, Cs+ cycles whose period is within 2% of the saline 11 degrees C mean period (a standard choice, the paper gives no bound). Latencies are from the first PD spike to the first LP or PY spike (O6); advance is saline minus Cs+. Reproduced when, under one reading, the three means are within tolerance, LP advance exceeds PD shortening, and the Pearson correlation across experiments of PD offset advance (ms) with LP onset advance (ms) has p >= 0.05 (a claim of absence, the paper's test). The printed r(21) implies 23 pairs by the degrees-of-freedom convention, while r = 0.26 with p = 0.2928 fits about 18 pairs; the number of pairs used is reported.
