Conserved brain-wide emergence of emotional response from sensory experience in humans and mice: Most of the paper's main findings held up in this data. Ketamine cut late but not early eye closure in humans and mice, lowered persistent activity along the coding dimension in humans and mice, and shortened the human intrinsic timescale.
