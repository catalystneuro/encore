Most of the paper's behavioural findings held up in this data, and its optogenetic maps could not be tested. Licking to the whisker stimulus, the first-trial switch at context changes, and the modelled behaviour all reproduced, as did most of the widefield and photoactivation-control findings, though the expert-session count did not (318 sessions in 49 mice against 201 in 41) because the paper's set is a fixed list. The seven optogenetic map claims are blocked because the methods give a different shuffle null in different places and no threshold for an affected site. The retrosplenial context-discrimination ranking is also blocked, because the paper states two baselines that give different results, and the retrosplenial tracing is untestable because the images are not in the dataset. The Figure 4 trajectory finding did not reproduce, and the Figure 4 connectivity claims were never run.

Reproduction Context-dependent sensorimotor transformation and retrosplenial cortex (Bech, Dard et al., eLife)

Tests the findings of Bech, Dard et al. on dandiset 001847, which holds the behaviour, orofacial tracking, optogenetic grid inactivation, muscimol and widefield calcium imaging sessions of the paper. It tests the behavioural context modulation and its time course, the optogenetic and pharmacological inactivation maps, the widefield dynamics on grid ROI traces, and the effects of inhibition on cortical activity and its low-dimensional trajectories. Mouse lines and session groups come from the session lists of the paper's processing code, as the files do not record them. All files

Environment: Python 3.13, 38 packages pinned in requirements.lock.txt; CI runs each notebook from a new environment built from it.

  • The transition time is the start of the context epoch in which the first trial of the new block falls; the code's transition table is not in the files.fig1h-temporal-integration

  • Jaw is dropped for 32 of 97 sessions by the Methods' 70% rule at 60% likelihood.fig1i-pupil-larger-wplus
  • Jaw opening is jaw_y minus its 5th percentile (50% likelihood) in pixels over 28.fig1i-posture-before-lick
  • Whisker angle is computed from the particle, whisker base and nose points as the Methods give it, filtered at 80% likelihood of whisker base and particle; the stored series is a variant.fig1i-posture-before-lick
  • Speed is the absolute first difference times each session's frame rate (89 sessions at 200 Hz, 8 at 100 Hz).fig1j-jaw-speed-context
  • The trial level of the bootstrap is drawn as Binomial(n, p)/n per mouse, which has the same distribution as resampling n binary trials with replacement; the random stream differs from the code's.supp2-muscimol-ws1-rsc
  • Session 1 and 2 are ranked by date within mouse and condition, third sessions dropped, as figure2_supp_stats.py does.supp2-muscimol-ws1-rsc
  • Assumption, the trials pooled for the onset are the correct whisker trials of Figure 3B (Methods, Figures 3B and 3C); the other trial sets give onsets below the tolerance.fig3c-jaw-onset
  • The 70% session rule of Figures 1I-J is applied, as the Methods for Figure 3B say "As in Figures 1I&J"; the variant without it is shown.fig3c-jaw-onset
  • d' at each time is over the mouse's trials, with untracked points left out at that time; the variant over session means is shown.fig3c-jaw-onset
  • The pre-stimulus baseline is run under the paper's two readings, 50 ms (Methods, Figure 3H, citing the d' of Figure 3F, and Methods, Figures 3E&F) and 250 ms (Methods, Computation of ΔF/F0), and the code's 100 ms as a variant.fig3h-rsc-first-context-discrimination
  • The pixel version reads only the frames from 250 ms before to 120 ms after the stimulus of the W+ hits and W- misses; ROI circles are the file's grid masks (grid_areas/brain_grid_area_segmentation); smoothing is applied to the mouse-average images.fig3h-rsc-first-context-discrimination
  • The measure (peak 50 to 120 ms, a decrease being more than the control SD over mice) is the claims file's; the paper gives none.fig4c-inhibition-propagates-forward
  • Closeness is mean squared distance on PC3, the claims file's measure; the paper gives none.fig4d-trajectories-follow-lick
