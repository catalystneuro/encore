Most of the paper's slice pharmacology held up in this data: quinpirole reduced the GPe-evoked IPSC in SNr neurons, gabazine no longer changed SNr firing after ALDH1A1 knockdown, ALDH or MAO-B inhibition or GAT-1 block, and astrocytic Gi-DREADD activation and NNC-711 raised firing. The tonic gabazine effect and the slowing by the GAT-3 antagonist SNAP-5114 were significant but about half the paper's size or less. Quinpirole did not raise basal SNr firing or significantly weaken GPe train inhibition, and the paper does not say which cells form its quinpirole group. GPe Gi-DREADD lowered the evoked IPSC in all 4 cells, but not significantly. The SNc, imaging and several drug experiments are not in the dataset.

Summary written by the agent that recorded the attempt, from its claims and the agents’ reports. A failure to reproduce may be a failure of the agent.

  • ✓ReproducedResults, D2R agonists inhibited phasic GABA release from GPe terminals, paragraph 1; Figure 1C

    The D2R agonist quinpirole (2 uM) reduces the amplitude of optogenetically evoked GPe IPSCs in SNr neurons by roughly 30%.

    Quinpirole reduced the amplitude of the GPe evoked inhibitory postsynaptic currents (IPSCs) by roughly 30% (P = 0.02 and n = 5)

    Results, D2R agonists inhibited phasic GABA release from GPe terminals, paragraph 1; Figure 1C

    What the paper reports

    • paired t test, p = 0.02 (n = 5 neurons)

    In the 5 labelled cells the first evoked IPSC falls from 265.6 to 198.5 pA in quinpirole, paired t test P = 0.0087; the reduction is 30.1% as the mean of the cells' changes (reading a of M2), 25.3% of the group means and 27.3% as the median. With the sixth cell (second cell of subject 9019, files labelled by an assumed layout) P = 0.0070 and 26.7%. Light pulses at 0.55 s + k x 50 ms are read from the responses, which start 1.2 to 2.2 ms after 0.55 s in every cell. Each of the nine files of that sixth cell, 0015 to 0023, holds an evoked current of 200 to 246 pA, so none was recorded in gabazine; files 0015 to 0018 are 5 min apart and 10 min pass before 0019, which fits the assumed layout.

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • The notes of the second IPSC cell of subject 9019 (files 0015 to 0023) are copies of the first cell's and do not say which files are in quinpirole; the cell is left out of the main test and shown as a variant with files 15 to 18 as control and 19 to 22 as quinpirole, the first cell's layout.
    • The light pulses are not stored in the files; their times are read from the evoked currents.
    • The notes give holding potentials of -70, -50 and -55 mV for these cells (the paper, -60 mV); the currents are inward in the cell at -70 mV and outward in the others, so the amplitude is the peak deflection with its sign fixed by the cell's control.
    • Peak window 0.5 to 20 ms after the pulse and baseline the 20 ms before it, a standard choice that the claims file leaves open.
