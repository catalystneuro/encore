Summary: Most of this paper's main findings could not be tested, because the dataset holds only 2 of the 9 mice of one experiment and none behind the retrospective memory linking, burst recruitment and recall results. The overlap ensemble co-bursting more with the neutral than the aversive ensemble during offline wake held only weakly in one mouse. It reversed when the files' quiet wake, a state the paper does not mention, was counted as wake. The brief slowing of movement before population bursts did not reproduce with telemetry, the files' only movement measure. Of the minor claims, higher freezing after a strong shock and offline bursts above shuffled data held, the burst frequency only under a lenient reading.

    Passage. Extended Data Fig. 3l legend, "Neuron activities were circularly shuffled 1000 times relative to one another and the mean population activity was re-computed each time.   • One mouse per group of the paper's 5 high-shock and 4 low-shock mice; bins within a session stand in for mice, as the criterion says.ed9b-aversive-freezing-high-vs-low
