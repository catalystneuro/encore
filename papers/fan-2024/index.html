Fan et al. 2024 · Neuron

Closed-loop microstimulations of the orbitofrontal cortex during real-life gaze interaction enhance dynamic social attention

doi:10.1016/j.neuron.2024.05.004 · paper key fan-2024

Datasets

  • DANDI:000959

The central OFC findings held up in this data, but the causal-strength analyses could not be tested and the Eyes versus Mouth comparison did not reproduce. OFC stimulation brought gaze closer to the partner's eyes (p = 0.0002 over 27 days), mostly in the contralateral hemifield, and shortened the inter-looking interval and reciprocation latency, with no such effects for dmPFC or ACCg. The six causal-strength and slope findings were blocked because the decomposition needs at least 159 CPU hours. The Eyes versus Mouth bootstrap did not reproduce, and the paper does not say how its p-value is computed. Among the controls, the saccade results rest on an assumed detector.
