Tingley et al. 2018 · Neuron

Transformation of a Spatial Map across the Hippocampal-Lateral Septal Circuit

doi:10.1016/j.neuron.2018.04.028 · paper key tingley-2018

Datasets

  • DANDI:000213

The main finding that lateral septum spike phase predicts position better than firing rate held in this data, with lower phase error across 3,660 conditions. Most other findings did not hold or held only in part: significant phase coding in 52% of neurons against 89%, equivalence of rate and phase in hippocampus, the depth and CA3 coherence effects, and hippocampal-septal assemblies. Rare septal place fields matched the paper, but hippocampal place fields were fewer than reported. Assembly-linked phase coding, speed independence and maze rotation could not be tested. Many gaps probably reflect the dandiset lacking the paper's linearized position, trial conditions and theta channel, which the agent rebuilt.
