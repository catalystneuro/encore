Lefebvre et al. 2008 · Other journals

Gamma-Protocadherins regulate neuronal survival but are dispensable for circuit formation in retina

doi:10.1242/dev.027912 · paper key lefebvre-2008

Datasets

  • CRCNS:ret-1

Most of the paper's central histology could not be tested, and the part that could be tested gave a mixed picture. The data set holds only white-noise spike recordings, so the paper's thinned retinal layers, extra apoptosis, Bax rescue and normal laminar targeting could not be tested, nor could the flashing-spot, flash and moving-stimulus responses of Figure 9A to 9C. Of the Figure 9 findings that could be tested, the slower time course and lower firing rate in mutant retina held up, while the unaltered threshold did not (mutant thresholds were higher). Similar receptive field size and reduced gain could not be settled, because the paper does not specify its band construction or its model's nonlinearity. The data set's unit counts also differ from the paper's (200 mutant and 122 control units against 115 and 143).
