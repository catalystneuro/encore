None of the paper's main findings could be tested in this data set, which holds only current-step recordings of 17 pyramidal cells and 6 basket cells. The larger basket cell inhibition of deep pyramidal cells, the running-evoked axon activity, the stronger superficial-to-basket excitation and the projection-specific results need paired recordings, imaging and anatomy that are not deposited. The network model result was too costly to run, at about 450 core-hours. Among the minor intrinsic-property claims, the lack of firing-curve and input-resistance differences and the larger superficial sag held, and rheobase matched only in part. Resting potential could not be tested because the holding current was not recorded.

  • ✓ReproducedResults, PVBCs Preferentially Innervate Subpopulations of CA1 PCs, paragraph 2; Figure S1E and legendfigs1e-if-curves

    Superficial and deep CA1 PCs do not differ in firing frequency as a function of injected current (two-way ANOVA, p > 0.4; 8 sPCs, 9 dPCs).

    Two-way ANOVA of firing frequency on group and step (0 to +550 pA), reading (a) of the rate (spikes per second…

    There were no differences between dPCs and sPCs in terms of their firing frequency as a function of intracellularly injected current (Figure S1E)

    Results, PVBCs Preferentially Innervate Subpopulations of CA1 PCs, paragraph 2; Figure S1E and legend

    What the paper reports

    • two-way ANOVA, p > 0.4 (n = 8 sPCs and 9 dPCs)

    Two-way ANOVA of firing frequency on group and step (0 to +550 pA), reading (a) of the rate (spikes per second of the 1 s step), group F = 0.68, p = 0.411, eta squared 0.0005, interaction p = 0.997; reading (b) p = 0.866. The mixed ANOVA with step within cell gives p = 0.759 and 0.951. At +550 pA, sPC 31.62 and dPC 30.56 Hz under reading (a), 33.63 and 31.24 Hz under reading (b), all within tolerance. Reading (a) also gives the paper's p > 0.4. Spike counts change by under 1% between thresholds of -20, 0 and +10 mV. Reading (b) gives 0 Hz to a sweep with one spike; 2 PC sweeps have one spike and none has two.

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • The files record no injected current; the step levels are those of the data descriptor's table (-400 to +550 pA in 50 pA steps), and the sweep order is checked against the voltage responses in the data check.
    • The paper says only "two-way ANOVA"; the model with the group by step interaction is the main test, and the additive model (group p = 0.400) and the mixed ANOVA are shown. All agree.
    • Under rate reading (b), a sweep with fewer than two spikes has no interval and is given 0 Hz, a standard choice that affects only sweeps at the rheobase.
    • The data set has no animal or slice identifiers, so the result is broken down by cell, not by animal.

    The experimental superficial and deep PCs have the same rheobase, +150 pA, at the 50 pA resolution of the step protocol.

    Per-cell rheobases are 100 to 250 pA for sPCs and 100 to 300 pA for dPCs.

    Not quoted: Values from Table S1 (Experimental Rheobase, dPC +150, sPC +150 pA); no sentence of the text states them.

    Per-cell rheobases are 100 to 250 pA for sPCs and 100 to 300 pA for dPCs. Reading (a), the median, gives sPC 200 and dPC 150 pA, so only dPC matches Table S1. Reading (b), the mean rounded to 50 pA, gives 200 pA for both (means 187.5 and 177.8 pA). By the criterion the groups share a step under (b) but it is not +150 pA, which is partial. No cell's rheobase changes with a spike threshold of -20 or +10 mV, or under the definition of SimTracker's CellData tool (least positive step with an upward crossing of -20 mV anywhere in the sweep).     How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • Step levels from the data descriptor; steps are added to the holding current, so the tonic current is excluded as the Table S1 footnote says.
    • The paper does not define rheobase or its spike threshold. The data descriptor names the CellData tool of SimTracker for the cells' intrinsic properties; its rheobase, "Least depolarized step with spikes" with spikes as crossings of -20 mV (tools/CellData.m), is run as a variant and gives the same per-cell values.

Reproduction Intrinsic properties of superficial and deep CA1 pyramidal cells and PV+ basket cells (Lee et al. 2014)

It compares firing, input resistance, sag and rheobase between the sublayers and the firing of a basket cell. The paired recordings, anatomy, imaging and model behind the paper's main figures are not in it. All files

Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • The files record no injected current; the step levels are those of the data descriptor's table (-400 to +550 pA in 50 pA steps), and the sweep order is checked against the voltage responses in the data check.figs1e-if-curves
  • The paper says only "two-way ANOVA"; the model with the group by step interaction is the main test, and the additive model (group p = 0.400) and the mixed ANOVA are shown. All agree.figs1e-if-curves
  • Under rate reading (b), a sweep with fewer than two spikes has no interval and is given 0 Hz, a standard choice that affects only sweeps at the rheobase.figs1e-if-curves
  • The data set has no animal or slice identifiers, so the result is broken down by cell, not by animal.figs1e-if-curves
  • Step levels from the data descriptor, as for figs1e-if-curves. The pre-step baseline for reading (b) is the 0.2 s before onset, a standard choice.figs1f-input-resistance
  • Step levels from the data descriptor, as for figs1e-if-curves.figs1h-sag
  • Step levels from the data descriptor; steps are added to the holding current, so the tonic current is excluded as the Table S1 footnote says.tables1-rheobase
  • The paper does not define rheobase or its spike threshold. The data descriptor names the CellData tool of SimTracker for the cells' intrinsic properties; its rheobase, "Least depolarized step with spikes" with spikes as crossings of -20 mV (tools/CellData.m), is run as a variant and gives the same per-cell values.tables1-rheobase
  • The 1 s step of 50 pA increments is assumed by the criterion; the data show a 1.000 s step and the descriptor gives 50 pA increments for the six PVBCs.figs3f-pvbc-firing
  • PV34 is deposited twice with identical bytes; it is counted once.figs3f-pvbc-firing
