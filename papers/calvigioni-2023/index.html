    How it will be judged

    Sessions are matched by subject and date in the file name (for example 258412_20200915). Per session, units are selected as in Fig4g_GLM_tuning.m, which follows the Methods (In vivo electrophysiological data analysis) and adds one rule, units in ACAd, PL, ILA or ORBm by the location of their main channel (units electrodes, 0-indexed), ISI violation count over spike count < 0.01, mean session firing rate > 0.1 Hz, and mean spike count per 100 ms bin divided by 0.2 greater than 1. The mean session firing rate is computed from the dataset (spike count over recording duration) and compared with analysis/Mean_FR; the per-unit tuning scores in GLM_fitted may be used as an outside check of the tuning claims below.

  • fig4e-opto-modulation-genotypes

    Optogenetic activation strongly modulates mPFC units in Vglut2-cre and Esr1-cre mice but not in Npy-cre mice, while the auditory stimuli and air puffs modulate mPFC units in all four groups.

    We found strong modulation of mPFC units in response to optogenetic activation only in Vglut2-cre and Esr1-cre and not in Npy-cre mice, while air puffs and auditory stimuli modulated mPFC activity in all four mouse groups

    Results, Pathway-specific aversive signals in the PFC, paragraph 2; Figure 4e and Extended Data Figure 7a

    How it will be judged

    The paper shows this as PSTHs and heatmaps and reports no test. The measure is the paper's own unit-level test, the GLM tuning of the Methods (Generalized linear model, Tuning scores; regressors of 200 ms for each sound, 500 ms after light onset and 500 ms after air puff onset, 100 ms bins, Poisson glmfit, 1,000 circular shifts of the session, a unit tuned when its beta exceeds the 99.9th or falls below the 0.01th percentile of the shifted betas). Reproduced when Vglut2 and Esr1 have a higher opto-tuned fraction than Ctl at p < 0.05, Npy does not, and all four genotypes exceed chance for both sound 1 and air puff at p < 0.05. As a second reading, the block-2 population PSTH of all units (10 ms bins, z-scored to the 1 s pre-tone baseline, Methods) averaged over 0.7 to 1.2 s after trial start, compared with baseline per genotype by a Wilcoxon signed-rank test over units at p < 0.05, is shown.
