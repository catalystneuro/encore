  • ◐1 partially reproduced

On DANDI:000067: None of the main findings reproduced in full, and several could not be tested. Trajectory selectivity, odor-dependent firing and the fall of connection probability with distance appear in this data, but with higher fractions of selective units than the paper's. The cell classes from connections, facilitation and depression, and supralinear summation did not match. Position-dependent coupling could not be tested because its methods are in the supplement, which was unavailable. Task performance, where trajectories diverge and the example session could not be tested because error trials, the y coordinate and that session are missing from the dandiset.

On CRCNS:pfc-2: Few of the paper's main findings held up in this data. Neurons fired over similar stretches of the maze and connected cells were mostly trajectory specific, but far more cells told left from right trials in the side arms than reported (54.3% in layer 2/3 against 22%), and the population did not fire uniformly along the maze. The connection results did not reproduce: counts, cell types and the hub circuit differed, position-dependent transmission was rarer, and depression, facilitation and supralinear coincidence were not seen. Likely reasons are detection details the paper leaves open and missing data, as one of four rats and the layer 5 example session are absent. Minor claims, such as no link with running speed, held.
