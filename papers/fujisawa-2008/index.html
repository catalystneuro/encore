Summary: In this data the paper's trajectory-specific firing held in part: many prefrontal neurons fire differently on left and right trials, but in larger fractions than reported. Its monosynaptic connection results did not reproduce, as the jitter test here found far more excitatory connections and far fewer interneurons, with a bin width and test band the paper does not give. Position-dependent transmission was above chance but in 6.4% of connections against 19.5%, and the split into depressing and facilitating connections and the supralinear effect of coincident inputs did not reproduce. Task performance could not be tested, as only correct trials are deposited. Smaller results on field size, retrospective coding, running speed and spatial clustering reproduced.
