Powell et al. 2021

Coupling between fast and slow oscillator circuits in Cancer borealis is temperature-compensated

Outcomes of 22 claims

  • ✓10 reproduced
  • ◐4 partially reproduced

  • ⊘4 blocked

Summary: Most of the paper's findings on evoked rhythms hold in this data: gastric mill and pyloric periods shorten with temperature in all 10 preparations, their Q10s do not differ, and LG stays integer-coupled to PD from 7 to 21 C while DG does not. Two did not reproduce: a Q10 of about 2 in most preparations (1 of 10 by the Methods' formula; the paper's count follows its code's per-burst Q10), and a drop in LG spiking near the PD burst end at higher temperatures. LG phase locking held in part. The spontaneous rhythms from 11 to 30 C and the rhythm without the pyloric pacemaker could not be tested, as those recordings are not in the deposit.

Summary written by the agent that recorded the attempt, from its claims and the agents’ reports. A failure to reproduce may be a failure of the agent.

    The LG spike p.d.f. at the mean PD burst-end phase (about 0.19 to 0.22) is below 1 at every temperature with at least 1000 spikes (reading a, 0.45 to 0.81 from 7 to 21 C, 0.23 at 23 C), but its Spearman correlation with temperature is positive (reading a, rho = 0.150, p = 0.70; reading b, 0.167, p = 0.67; without 23 C, 0.64 and 0.67). The odd-temperature filter leaves out 3.6% of LG spikes with a PD phase (even rounded temperatures).

Notes on the paper

What the attempts found about the paper itself: a method or rule it does not give, a place where it disagrees with itself, published code that does something else. What they found about the data is on each dataset’s page.

  • Published code disagreesThe Methods keep bursts of at least n spikes (5 for LG and DG, 2 for PD), but the paper's burst detection, crabsort.computePeriods, compares n with the number of inter-spike intervals in a burst, keeping bursts of at least n + 1 spikes. The paper's reported numbers follow the code's rule (for example 3799 cycles in Figure 5f, against 3850 under the Methods' rule, and 3228 DG burst starts, which the Methods' rule exceeds by more than 10%).#
  • Published code disagreesThe Methods test the first 40 LG and DG burst starts of each preparation and temperature for non-uniformity, but the reported ranges (LG p < 4e-4, 7.5 < z < 45; DG z up to 13) are those of the first 50, the sample of the later code (marderlab/gastric commit 7a82b1f). The archived commit the paper cites (fa07d36) pools all starts of a temperature over preparations, which gives z of 67 to 577. With 40 starts z cannot exceed 40.#
  • Published code disagreesThe Methods compute the Q10 from the mean burst frequency at each temperature against 11 C, but fig_burst_period.m computes a Q10 for every burst at its own temperature, against the bursts within 0.1 C of 11 C, removes Q10s above 10 and averages per preparation. The reported count of 8 of 10 preparations near 2 and the paired t-test (t = 1.27, p = 0.235) follow the code; the Methods' formula gives 1 to 3 of 10 and LG Q10s up to 39 from temperatures crossed during ramps.#
  • Paper inconsistentFigure 3, figure supplement 1 says that 4 of the 10 evoked preparations had spontaneous rhythms, but one of the four preparations its script uses (941_006) is not among the ten evoked preparations and was recorded in June 2019, not 2017. In evoked_data.mat, 3 of the 10 evoked preparations (901_062, 901_080, 901_086) have LG bursting before their first stimulation at 11 C, which with 941_006 makes the four of the script. A fourth of the ten, 932_151, has 16 LG burst periods before stimulation at 9.83 to 9.93 C, so the paper's 4 of 10 may count it in place of 941_006.#
  • Methods not availableThe paper says rhythms were evoked 'across a temperature range of 7 to 21 C in 9 of 10 preparations', with steps of 2 or 4 C 'until a gastric mill rhythm could no longer be evoked'. It does not say whether a preparation whose warmest step was 19 C (901_062, never brought to 21 C) counts among the nine, nor whether temperatures crossed only between steps (9, 13 and 17 C for most preparations) count. The attempt assumed that only bins held at least 2 minutes count and that a preparation passes with a rhythm at every step it had, which gives the paper's 9; requiring 21 C gives 8, and counting every bin crossed gives 3. Which did the authors count?#

Reproduction Coupling of the gastric mill and pyloric rhythms across temperature (Powell et al. 2021)

Tests the findings of Powell, Haddad, Gorur-Shandilya and Marder (2021) on Zenodo record 3924718, which holds the spike times, temperature and stimulation mask of the ten preparations in which gastric mill rhythms were evoked, and raw recordings. It tests the temperature dependence of the LG and PD burst periods, their Q10s, the slowing after stimulation, integer coupling of LG and DG with PD, and the phase of LG and DG in the PD cycle. The spontaneous recordings and those with the pyloric pacemaker hyperpolarized are not in the record. All files

Environment: Python 3.13, 28 packages pinned with their hashes in requirements.lock.txt; CI runs each notebook from a new environment built from it.

Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • The claims file leaves open which bins a preparation 'was held at'. A bin counts as held when the recording spends at least 120 s in it, the 2-minute minimum step of the Methods ('a given temperature step was held for at least 2 minutes') and the code's (BurstPeriodsVsTime.m, 'temperature held for less than 2 minutes'). Counting every bin with any time in it gives 3 of 10, so the outcome rests on this choice, which is an assumption (assumed: true); the question is put to the paper's authors as a methods_unavailable finding.fig3-evoked-7-to-21-in-9-of-10
  • A preparation whose warmest held bin is 19 C (901_062) passes when it has a rhythm at every bin it was held at, as the criterion is worded; the count requiring 21 C as well (8 of 10) is shown beside it.fig3-evoked-7-to-21-in-9-of-10
  • 941_006 has no spike times in the record, so three of the four preparations are tested; the outcome does not depend on it under the Methods burst rule, since one similar of three allows at most two of four.fig3s1-spontaneous-and-evoked-similar
  • The spike times of a raw file are those within its samples in evoked_data.mat (the filename categorical), the stacked form of the same crabsort annotations the script reads per file.fig3s1-spontaneous-and-evoked-similar
  • The claims file does not say which temperatures enter the Methods' Q10. Two readings were run, every bin with bursts and only the bins held at least 120 s (the temperature steps of the Methods, "Spontaneous and evoked gastric mill rhythm temperature ranges"); both give the same outcome. T is the bin centre, f_T the mean of 1/period over bursts starting in the bin.fig4c-q10-about-2
  • PD periods above 5 s and LG periods above 50 s are removed before the Q10, as fig_burst_period.m does (the outlier filter the claims file cites for fig4b-periods-decrease-all-preps).fig4c-q10-about-2
  • A stimulation block is taken as running to the end of the recording when no stimulation follows, as MATLAB's temperature(stim:[]) is empty in BurstPeriodsVsTime.m; the mask's meaning (stimulation where 0) is taken from the code (findStimulationTimes.m, BurstPeriodsVsTime.m), as the record does not describe it.fig4s1-slowdown-after-stimulation
  • The paper used one shuffle draw per temperature; here the median D and p over 1000 draws are reported, as the criterion sets. p is the asymptotic p of MATLAB's kstest2.fig5d-lg-pd-integer-coupled
  • Bins with a single preparation have no SEM; their interval is taken as the value itself (the code draws them without an error bar).fig5e-lg-area-exceeds-dg
  • The reported ranges match the sample of the later code (commit 7a82b1f, first 50 starts), not the Methods' first 40, as the claims file anticipated; the outcome follows the Methods, as the criterion sets.fig6b-lg-phase-locked
  • The claims file's reading (a) does not say over which combinations the fraction is taken; both those with any start and all 90 are shown. The outcome is decided by the DG total, whichever is used.fig6b-lg-start-counts
