A motor cortex circuit for motor planning and movement

doi:10.1038/nature14178 · paper key li-2015

DANDI:000010: Most findings on ALM activity held, but the photoinhibition and photostimulation experiments could not be tested because the dandiset holds none of those data. Task accuracy, the large share of selective neurons (912 of 1,245) and their rise through the delay all reproduced. Contra-preferring pyramidal tract neurons outnumbered ipsi-preferring ones only when tagging-light trials were kept. The split into preparatory and peri-movement neurons (272 to 294 peri-movement-only neurons against 389), early contralateral selectivity in pyramidal tract neurons, the imaging cell counts and the spatial intermingling of imaged cells did not reproduce. The layer 5 imaging result could not be tested because the planes have no recorded depth. You'll need to save the paragraph there.

CRCNS:alm-1: The paper's electrophysiology findings largely held up in this data, while its behavioural and imaging findings could not be tested because those recordings are not in the dataset. ALM selectivity matched: 912 of 1,245 pyramidal neurons were selective, contra- and ipsi-preferring neurons were equally common, and activity on error trials followed the licking direction. Pyramidal tract neurons, unlike intratelencephalic neurons, were mostly contra-preferring when only selective neurons were counted, and their late contralateral selectivity matched in size but not in onset or in its comparison with all selective neurons. The licking effects of photoinhibiting ALM and of photoactivating pyramidal tract neurons are among the missing data. Minor claims on spike widths, fast-spiking neurons and antidromic jitter held in part.
