Qi et al. 2026 · Preprint

Large-scale all-optical electrophysiology links excitability and connectivity to circuit dynamics in vivo

doi:10.64898/2026.10.05.756595 · paper key qi-2026

Datasets

  • DANDI:001960

Most of the paper's main findings hold up in this data: the intrinsic excitability groups, the synaptic and gap-junction connection counts (802 of 7,280 pairs in one mouse, exactly as published), the subthreshold dynamics, the link from excitability to spontaneous firing, and the NPY subtype differences. The synaptic length constant came out much longer than published, and the spikes lost or gained through connections did not match. Traveling waves and the interaction kernel did not reproduce, where the paper gives few method details. A small NPY gap-junction subtype effect appeared where the paper reports none, and single-spike SNR was lower than published. The Figure 1 tool-development claims could not be tested because those recordings are not in the dataset.
