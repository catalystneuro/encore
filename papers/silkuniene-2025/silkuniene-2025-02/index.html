MHz compression of pulse packets facilitates remote focusing of electroporation

Summary: Most main findings that could be tested hold, but the 40-fold off-target suppression did not reproduce. Central YO-PRO-1 uptake rises with burst number (borderline in CT26), the one-burst peak is the sharpest of the study, and focusing improves from 1 Hz to 0.2 MHz packets. The center-to-electrode ratios came out at 32 and 45 against the reported 40 to 50, and they depend on which electrode footprint sets the background, which the paper does not specify. The log dependence on packet rate, raised electrodes, pulse duration, heating and the field simulation could not be tested because those data are not in this dandiset. The unipolar control also showed no decline toward the center.

  • Methods not availableThe paper does not give the spacing of the ROIs along the diagonals, the fit range used for Fig. 7, or which electrode footprint holds the background ROIs.
