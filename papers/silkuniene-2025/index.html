DANDI:001047: The dandiset does not show the paper's main finding, a sharp central spot of YO-PRO-1 uptake with about 40-fold suppression near the electrodes at 0.2 MHz packet rate, and all seven central tests that could be run failed except one weak partial. Centre enhancement, the rate-dependent suppression near the electrodes, the Lorentzian profile and the 40-fold ratio did not reproduce: the 0.2 MHz wells are unstained at the burst label read as the paper's 2 bursts (centre -1.4 a.u.), and no label gives a ratio near 40. The one comparison available, no rate effect at the centre, held (p = 0.93), but neither rate stained the centre. Rate trends beyond 1 Hz and 0.2 MHz, pulse duration, raised electrodes, heating and the burst series could not be tested because those data are missing. The files' stated voltage and electrode spacing differ from the paper's, so these images may be other experiments.

DANDI:001276: Most main findings that could be tested hold, but the 40-fold off-target suppression did not reproduce. Central YO-PRO-1 uptake rises with burst number (borderline in CT26), the one-burst peak is the sharpest of the study, and focusing improves from 1 Hz to 0.2 MHz packets. The center-to-electrode ratios came out at 32 and 45 against the reported 40 to 50, and they depend on which electrode footprint sets the background, which the paper does not specify. The log dependence on packet rate, raised electrodes, pulse duration, heating and the field simulation could not be tested because those data are not in this dandiset. The unipolar control also showed no decline toward the center.

DANDI:001277: In this data, the one central Fig. 6 finding that could be tested did not hold, and most of the rest could not be tested. The paper says normalized YO-PRO-1 profiles decline similarly across 300, 600 and 900 ns pulses. In this data the profiles separate, with the 900 ns profile falling faster near the centre. Two findings held up: the 900 ns centre peak is highest but not significantly so, and the 900 ns spot is about 1.7 mm wide. The sharper peak with longer pulses was blocked, because the dandiset has 7 wells at 600 ns where the paper used 5 and does not say which five. The packet-rate, raised-electrode, burst-number and heating findings could not be tested because that data is not in the dandiset.
