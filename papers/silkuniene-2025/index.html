Summary: The paper's central finding held in this data: MHz pulse packets focused electroporation at the centre of the electrode array in both cell lines, killing stayed there, and one burst gave the sharpest spot, about 1.1 mm wide. The 40-fold excess of central over near-electrode uptake was met only for some choices of background footprint, which the paper does not name. Narrowing with packet rate could not be tested, as the intermediate rates are not deposited. Narrowing with longer pulses held only when two wells were left out, one for the breadth under test, and raised electrodes did not clearly weaken the centre. The electroporation spot was narrower than the killing spot; the paper reports it slightly larger.
