Simulated mouse primary motor cortex activity during control and decreased pyramidal tract neuron excitability in the parkinsonian condition

Here is the paragraph to save as that file: The paper's main findings mostly held: beta peaks at rest in both conditions, the control gamma peak, and higher PT5B firing, beta power and synchrony in the parkinsonian rest state. The parkinsonian activated gamma peak did not (27 Hz against 43 Hz), nor did burst rates, duty cycles, coincident-spike shares or unitary-event durations, probably because the paper does not state its definitions or which cells it analysed. Reduced PT5B excitability, network size and subthreshold oscillations could not be tested: single-cell runs and silent cells are missing, and no detection rule is given. Minor IT rate claims held only when averaging over cells that fired at least once.
