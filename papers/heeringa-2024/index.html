  • The ABR threshold is not readable as written; general/notes ends with one character, or NaN. It is read as the character's code point (for example '#' is 35), an interpretation the data support (all values are multiples of 5 from 10 to 85 dB SPL, the range of Table 1, and the group means and SDs then equal Table 1's) but do not prove.table1-age-abr-weight fig3-abr-age
  • The click delay is not in the files (the trial table gives NaN for CLICK trials). The 2-bin latency of Clickextract_func.m and getFSL2bins.m needs it; 7.75 ms, the delay of every tone recording in the dataset, is assumed, and 5 ms is shown as a variant.recording-type-counts fig7b-click-latency-bf
  • Vector strength and its p-value are not in the NWB files. They are recomputed per level of the PH recording with PHextract_func.m and TytoView_calcVS.m (Methods, 'The analysis procedures were carried out by the function PHextract_func.m'), spikes between tone onset and offset, p = exp(-N vs^2), NaN when N < 50 as the Methods say ('When N < 50, the p-value is NaN'); the code's rule (ntot > 50, NaN when N is 50 or fewer) is shown as a variant. The silent trials' value, which the code also stores, is left out.fig6d-max-phase-locking-bf fig6d-highest-vs-low-bf-low-sr
  • The CF of units without a BF recording is recomputed (see fig7a-cf-matches-bf); it does not change any bound.table1-bf-range
  • The stored CF and click latency are not in the files; reading 1 uses the values recomputed with the paper's functions.recording-type-counts
  • A recording is taken as a recording type of the unit; each type occurs once per unit in the files.isi-exclusion
  • The two readings of 'highest' differ; the claims file's criterion takes the claim as reproduced under either, and only (b) holds.fig6a-young-two-sensitive-regions
  • The stored CF is not in the NWB files. CF is recomputed from the CF recording's spike times with CFextract_func.m, getSpikeCounts.m and getQ10dB.m (named in the Methods, 'The analysis procedures were carried out by the function CFextract_func.m'), automatic first pass only. The code asks the experimenter to accept or change the response criterion and the CF ('T could be manually adjusted when needed', Methods); those manual choices are not in the files.fig7a-cf-matches-bf
  • The stored 2-bin latency is not in the files; it is recomputed with Clickextract_func.m and getFSL2bins.m (named in the Methods).fig7b-click-latency-bf
  • The claims file's criterion can be read two ways when the CF and CLICK subsets give different results; the reading taken is stated in the notes.fig7ab-representative-sampling
