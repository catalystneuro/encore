An internal expectation guides Drosophila egg-laying decisions

Vikram Vijayan, Zikun Wang, Vikram Chandra, Arun Chakravorty, Rufei Li, Stephanie L. Sarbanes, Hessameddin Akhlaghpour, Gaby Maimon

Summary: In this data the paper's central finding holds up: after leaving a better substrate, flies hold back egg laying on a worse one and release it with time, across chamber layouts, strains and agarose hardness. Kir2.1 in DDC-Gal4 neurons erodes the sucrose choice, and D1-like receptor mutants show flat rate curves. Not reproduced are an unchanged egg-laying pause, as pauses measured by the Methods come in 0.5-s steps, and the lack of preference in D. suzukii, where one time bin differs without correction for multiple tests. The Gal4 screen, Gal80 rescues, feeding tests and most receptor mutants could not be tested, as those flies are not in the dataset. Some timing details hold only in part.
