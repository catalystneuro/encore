Geiller et al. 2020 · Neuron

Large-Scale 3D Two-Photon Imaging of Molecularly Identified CA1 Interneuron Dynamics in Behaving Mice

doi:10.1016/j.neuron.2020.09.013 · paper key geiller-2020

Datasets

  • DANDI:000625

Findings on locomotion coupling mostly held in this one mouse, while those on spatial tuning mostly did not, and several could not be tested. Most interneurons correlated positively with velocity, CCK cells were enriched among the negatively correlated ones, and subtypes differed. Weaker modulation of IvC/NGFC, the fraction of place-selective cells, position decoding from them and the greater stability of PVBC did not reproduce, and the paper's place-field rule selected 1.0% of cells against 17.5%. Depth, ripple, novel-belt, reward and VIP findings could not be tested because the dataset lacks those recordings. The dataset holds one of 16 mice, and the imaging rate had to be refitted for two sessions.

  • ◐Partially reproduced About 227 interneurons were imaged per mouse (226.6 ± 27.3, mean ± std over 16 mice), and 121.0 ± 28.4 per mouse were assigned a putative subtype.
  • ⊘Blocked The layer distribution of each subtype along the CA1 depth axis is consistent with earlier anatomical studies.
  • ✓Reproduced The majority of interneurons were positively correlated with running velocity, and about 10% were negatively correlated, across all subtypes (156 negative and 1429 positive in Figure 2D).
  • ✓Reproduced Negatively correlated cells were significantly enriched in CCKC, where nearly half of cells were more active during immobility.
  • ✓Reproduced The correlation with velocity differs between the six subtypes (one-way ANOVA, p < 1e-10, with Tukey's post hoc test).
  • ?Not reproduced IvC/NGFC were less modulated by locomotion than other subtypes.
  • ⊘Blocked Negatively correlated cells were enriched in stratum oriens and positively correlated cells in stratum pyramidale.
  • ?Not reproduced The activity of positively correlated cells followed changes in velocity by up to several seconds, while the activity of negatively correlated cells was more tightly linked in time to changes in velocity.
  • ◐Partially reproduced Run-start and run-stop responses show that immobility-triggered activation is specific to CCKC.
  • ⊘Blocked A deep network classified PV-expressing, SOM-expressing, CCK and IvC/NGFC cells from dF/F, velocity and depth with accuracies of 64, 71, 60 and 59%.
  • ⊘Blocked During sharp-wave ripples PVBC were generally strongly modulated while most SomC were not.
  • ⊘Blocked AAC and BiC showed bimodal modulation during sharp-wave ripples, and all subtypes but CCKC and IvC/NGFC split into a modulated and a non-modulated group.
  • ?Not reproduced 17.5 ± 6.4% (mean ± sem over 6 mice, 3 sessions each) of interneurons, blind of subtype, passed the test for significant spatial selectivity.
  • ?Not reproduced 12.7 ± 8.3% of interneurons (mean ± sem) had negative spatial selectivity, slightly fewer than had positive selectivity.
  • ?Not reproduced Position decoded from the small subset of spatially selective interneurons was about as accurate as from all interneurons, and more accurate than from as many randomly chosen interneurons or with position shuffled.
  • ⊘Blocked BiC had a higher probability than other subtypes of being spatially modulated again in other sessions.
  • ✓Reproduced The selectivity index of spatially selective cells did not differ between subtypes (one-way ANOVA, p = 0.35).
  • ?Not reproduced Spatially selective PVBC had a more stable within-session recruitment (odd-even lap correlation of tuning curves) than spatially selective SomC.
  • ◐Partially reproduced Within-session stability of tuning curves differed between subtypes, PVBC showing the highest and CCKC the lowest odd-even correlations.
  • ◐Partially reproduced Tuning curve correlations of the same cells after 1 hour and after 24 hours were lower than within-session correlations, and still higher than chance.
  • ⊘Blocked Interneuron tuning curves decorrelated on exposure to a novel belt, more than with the passage of time, and context could be decoded from population activity.
  • ⊘Blocked PVBC, SomC, BiC and AAC decreased their activity in the first laps on a novel belt; CCKC were not modulated.
