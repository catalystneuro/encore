Summary: Most main findings hold up: granule and mossy cells separate as described, mossy cells fire more, and granule cells fire most in NREM sleep, lock more strongly to theta and gamma, excite mossy cells with burst facilitation and carry less spatial information. Two did not reproduce: weaker granule-to-mossy transmission in NREM sleep, which depends on the authors' manual curation of pairs, and less open-field remapping in granule than mossy cells (P = 0.69). Optogenetic tagging matched the paper only under a per-bin reading of the suppression rule. Some smaller claims, such as CA3 cells firing most in wake, did not hold. The distance between connected cells and dendritic backpropagation were untested, as the claims file gives no criterion.

  • The interneurons are the dentate ones, as the paper's counts are ("narrow-waveform (n = 248) and wide-waveform (n = 268) putative interneurons ...
