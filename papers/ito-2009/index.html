Ito et al. 2009 · The Journal of Neuroscience

Validation of Decision-Making Models and Analysis of Decision Variables in the Rat Basal Ganglia

doi:10.1523/jneurosci.6157-08.2009 · paper key ito-2009

Datasets

  • DANDI:001641

Most behavioral findings held: choices depend on reward history, with switching after non-reward. The advantage of time-varying F-Q and DF-Q models held in part (0.5374 and 0.5369 against the paper's 0.5375 and 0.5368). The tests comparing the models could not be settled, because the Q update equation and the training and test split are missing from the paper. In NAc and VP, state, action and reward coding reproduced, and the ventral pallidum's larger share of action value neurons held only in part. Reward information was above threshold before the poke, which the test criterion did not allow. The core versus shell comparison could not be tested, because the dataset does not label them.
