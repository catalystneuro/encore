Dembrow et al. 2024 · Cell Reports

Areal specializations in the morpho-electric and transcriptomic properties of primate layer 5 extratelencephalic projection neurons

doi:10.1016/j.celrep.2024.114718 · paper key dembrow-2024

Datasets

  • DANDI:001065

The core electrophysiology held up. L5 ET neurons had higher resonant frequency, lower input resistance and faster spikes than other cells, and motor cortex differed from temporal cortex in resonance, afterhyperpolarization and input resistance. The transcriptomic findings could not be tested because the sequencing data are not in the dataset and were not processed in the run. Corticospinal neurons grouping with L5 ET cells in the physiology map did not reproduce, nor did the ranking of resonance and input resistance as the largest effects. Classifier and subthreshold oscillation findings held only in part, the latter on a pipeline rebuilt without the authors' code. Spike-width ratio and rheobase claims failed.

  • ⊘Blocked In the Patch-seq data, 47 ion channel, neurofilament and axonal targeting genes differ between L5 ET and non-ET neurons (partial eta squared at least 0.05), 21 of them also found in the snRNA-seq data, including HCN1 and PEX5L higher in L5 ET.
  • ⊘Blocked In the Patch-seq data, 43 curated genes differ between L5 ET neurons of MCx and TCx (partial eta squared at least 0.05), 10 of them also found in the snRNA-seq data, among them KCNN2, KCNN3, KCNC2, DPP6 and CACNA1A.
  • ⊘Blocked In the human and macaque snRNA-seq references, 29 curated genes differ between L5 ET and non-ET subclasses (at least 0.5 log2-fold, partial eta squared at least 0.05), including KCNN1, KCNN2, SCN4B, HCN1 and NEFH.
  • ⊘Blocked Within the L5 ET subclass, 30 curated genes differ between M1 and MTG in the snRNA-seq references, including SK channel genes, KCNC2 and KCNIP1.
