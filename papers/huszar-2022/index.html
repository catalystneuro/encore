Summary: The paper's main finding held up in this data: CA1 pyramidal cells' firing, ripple participation, theta modulation and spatial information vary with embryonic birthdate, and cells born on the same day cofire more in ripples and theta, share place fields and remap together, though several values differ. Same-birthdate pairs also share more interneuron targets, but the connection detector, which the paper does not fully describe, finds far more connections than reported, and transmission did not peak at gamma intervals. The birthdate trend in REM theta phase shifts and higher map correlations in novel environments did not reproduce, the latter resting on unscaled open-field positions. The assembly analyses and network model were not run in the attempt's time.
