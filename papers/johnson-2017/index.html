The paper calls the model "logit mixed-effects" in the Results and "linear mixed-effects" in the Methods. Readings (a) a Gaussian (linear) model on the cell accuracies; (b) a binomial logit model on the cells, weighted by trial counts; (c) condition as a three-level factor instead of one number. Group means are over each subject's accuracy, read (a) over all the subject's trials or (b) as the mean of its six cells. Reproduced when, under one reading, both means are within tolerance and the group effect of the group-only model has p < 7 x 10^-5 with patients lower; partially reproduced when the means hold and p < 0.0071 (the paper's Bonferroni level). The full model's group effect is reported as well.
