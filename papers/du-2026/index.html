A Context-Conditional Audit of Trial-Pairing-Dependent Neural Gain in Motor-Cortex Decoding

Zonghan Du, Zhongyuan Lai, Liang Hu, Tangwei Ye

Outcomes of 33 claims

  • ✓33 reproduced

Summary: The paper's main findings held up in this data. In the LINK finger-task sessions, motor-cortex spiking-band power improved velocity decoding beyond task phase, target geometry and recent finger position (mean gain 0.02009, positive in all 18 sessions), and pairing each trial with the neural activity of the closest matched trial turned the gain negative. Both results held in the 20 held-out confirmation sessions and in the MC_RTT and MC_Maze reaching datasets. The exact confirmation and MC_Maze values match only under the paper's own random split and seed, and many move outside tolerance under others, while the pattern holds. The synthetic calibration, alternative estimators and timing and capacity checks also reproduced.

What the attempts found about the paper itself: a method or rule it does not give, a place where it disagrees with itself, published code that does something else. What they found about the data is on each dataset’s page.

In MC_RTT and in MC_Maze (hand_vel, cursor_pos) the series carry unit m/s or meters with conversion 0.001, so the stored values are mm/s and mm, as the descriptions say; the fields agree once the conversion is applied.
  • OtherThe paper's released code, ported here, reproduces its reported values at their printed precision for almost every claim tested. It re-implements the paper's eight decoders (linear regression, KFobs, KFlatent static and dynamic, Wiener filter, UKF, rEFH static and dynamic) from its Methods and code, and compares them at four bin widths, three amounts of training data and four fractions of the units. The paper's tests across its 49 sessions cannot be run on one session. All files

Environment: Python 3.13, 13 packages pinned in requirements.lock.txt; CI runs each notebook from a new environment built from it.

Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • Seed sensitivity was run for SBP only.nested-geometry-gain nested-history-gain
  • The data are generated by a port of the paper's synthetic.py (commit b042fbe) with the parameters of run_synthetic_calibration.py, which match Methods 4.8; no dataset is involved.synthetic-template-null
  • The neural-only penalty is chosen as in the release's evaluate_decoders_nested (inner 80/20 split, random state seed plus 11), the choice the claims file names; no release script computes this R2.link-neural-only-r2
  • The protocol's five-seed sensitivity was run for the geometry-by-phase and plus-history rungs (in other claims), not for the phase-only, direction-by-phase and additive-geometry rungs, to keep the notebook within its time limit.link-context-ladder-gain
  • The bootstrap generator is seeded from the claim (protocol), not with the release's 20260802.link-task-contrast-by-context
  • The random source is a uniform draw among the other same-condition test trials, with generators seeded from the claim (a standard choice the claims file names); five draws are shown.replacement-ranks-lower
  • The match holds under the lower-middle reading of the session tie only; the criterion names both readings, and the release's figure code uses the lower middle.representative-trial
  • The block Ridge is solved exactly (Cholesky) where the release uses lsqr with tolerance 1e-6, to fit the time limit. On one session (2021-10-15 CO, SBP, geometry by phase) the two solvers choose the same penalties and give gains of 0.0261224 and 0.0261222 (03_link_estimators.ipynb); the other sessions were not run under lsqr.estimator-family-link
  • Reading (b) of the nonlinear context, every setting of Methods 4.6 (27 combinations), was not run; estimated about 9 h on four processes. The outcome rests on reading (a), the release's setting.estimator-family-link
  • One window start, 280 ms after the start bin, for every offset (the claims file's reading of holding the window fixed); 367 to 375 trials per session are usable there.temporal-offset
  • Block Ridge solved exactly in place of lsqr (as in estimator-family-link); nonlinear context under the release's setting only.estimator-family-nlb
  • Assumption. The criterion states no reproduction rule ("Not on this attempt's datasets"). The rule used is that of rtt-gain, the claim of the same Table 1 on the other NLB dataset, every expected value within its tolerance. The outcome rests on this rule; the values under it and under other estimator seeds are printed in 07_mc_maze.ipynb.maze-gain
  • Tested in the revision after verification round 1; the first version of the attempt had blocked it without reading the file. The loader is a port of the release's mc_maze.py (template context, neural_lead_ms 80, as run_primary_analyses.py _run_nlb calls it).maze-gain
  • The conditional interval uses 500 bootstrap resamples, the default of run_primary_analyses.py; the paper gives no count.maze-gain
  • Tested in the revision after verification round 1, as maze-gain.rff-maze
