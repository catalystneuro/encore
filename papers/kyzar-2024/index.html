Most of the paper's main findings hold in this data, one does not, and one could not be settled. Unit yields, working memory behavior (slower and less accurate with load), spike sorting quality, the concentration of concept cells in the medial temporal lobe rather than medial frontal cortex, and the share of maintenance cells in medial frontal cortex all reproduced. Persistent firing of concept cells during maintenance did not: preferred means were about 3 Hz against the reported 4.76 Hz, and the reported values match the encoding window, as the released code computes them. The medial frontal probe cell count could not be settled because the Methods do not say how the permutation tests were paired. The text's screening unit count for the temporal lobe also appears to be the Sternberg count.

Notes on the paper

What the attempts found about the paper itself: a method or rule it does not give, a place where it disagrees with itself, published code that does something else. What they found about the data is on each dataset’s page.

  • Paper inconsistentThe text gives 449 MTL units for the screening task. The screening files hold 421 MTL units (159 hippocampus, 262 amygdala), which agrees with the paper's own screening percentages (138 is 32.78% of 421); 449 is the Sternberg MTL count.
  • Paper inconsistentThe persistent activity values in the text (4.76 plus or minus 4.71 against 2.67 plus or minus 3.66 Hz, preferred against non-preferred trials "during the maintenance period") are reproduced exactly by the rates 200 to 1000 ms after Encoding 1 onset of all 119 concept cells of the code's parametric test, split by whether the Encoding 1 image is the preferred one. In the maintenance window the same cells fire at 3.05 against 2.70 Hz. The released code computes these Encoding 1 rates under the names hzPref and hzNonPref, with a mask that, as written, selects every trial as preferred (per the claims file's reading of NWB_calcSelective_SB.m).
  • Methods not availableQuestion for the authors. The Methods name permutation tests for the concept, maintenance and probe cells, while the released code runs parametric tests (anovan, paired t-tests) and keeps the permutation versions in comments; the parametric tests reproduce every published count exactly here (138, 26, 94, 144, 122). Were the published counts computed with the parametric tests? If permutation tests were used, how many permutations, and were the probe and maintenance comparisons paired by trial (sign flipping) or unpaired (label shuffling)? For the probe cells the choice decides whether the count matches (118 or 111 paired, 109 or 104 unpaired, against 122), and the encoding rate (all encoding periods shown, or Encoding 1 only) is also not stated.
  • Selection not documentedThe reported ISI violation spread (0.41 plus or minus 0.50%) is matched only after leaving out units with 4% or more violations, as NWB_QA_graphs.m does (one unit, at 11.74%); over all units it is 0.43 plus or minus 0.63%. The reaction time ANOVA uses correct trials only, as the code does; neither filter is stated in the paper.
