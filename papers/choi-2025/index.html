Choi et al. 2025 · Other journals

Aseptic, semi-sealed cranial chamber implants for chronic multi-channel neurochemical and electrophysiological neural recording in nonhuman primates

doi:10.1016/j.jneumeth.2025.110467 · paper key choi-2025

Datasets

  • DANDI:002014

Of the paper's main findings, the larger dopamine rise after large than small reward cues and the spiking locked to the value cue held up in this data. Stronger beta suppression for large cues held only in direction (p = 0.108). The two-monkey recordings and the electrode yield, culture, drainage and bone growth results could not be tested, because the dandiset holds one session of one monkey. Spikes line up with the task only after their times are shifted by 11812 s, an offset measured in the data. The single-trial dopamine example and the single-unit waveform panels pass, but on weak criteria.
