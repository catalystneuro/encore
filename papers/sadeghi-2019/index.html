Summary: Most of the paper's main neural findings held up: inferior colliculus multiunit correlations identify sounds and categories in single trials well above chance, and scrambling channel order hurts the temporal classifier. With spectrum-equalized sounds the correlation classifiers stayed above chance and the spectrum classifier fell, though the values did not match. In the cochlear model the category correlation widths and diversity reproduced, but the stationarity values did not, the index's formula being lost from the text. The model classifiers reached similar maxima, their optimal resolutions held only in part, and the spectrum classifier tied the temporal one, on a component count the paper does not give. Two supplementary analyses could not be tested, lacking tone-pip and raw voltage recordings.
