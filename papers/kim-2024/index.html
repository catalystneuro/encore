Kim et al. 2024 · Cell Reports

Distinct subpopulations of ventral pallidal cholinergic projection neurons encode valence of olfactory stimuli

doi:10.1016/j.celrep.2024.114009 · paper key kim-2024

Only the patch-clamp findings could be tested, and higher maximum firing in odor-activated than home-cage neurons held. Greater hyperpolarization of aversive-odor neurons, their smaller afterhyperpolarization and lower excitability of appetitive-odor neurons did not.
