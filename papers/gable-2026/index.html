Tuft Dendrite Spikes Are Accompanied by Selective Input From Distinct Functional Networks

Jacob Gable, Zachary L Newman, Sarah Young, Jackson Scheib, Savannah Bliese, Nicole Miller, Aaron Kerlin

Summary: None of the paper's main findings could be tested in this data. Its central results, that tuft dendrite spikes come with elevated, dendrite-specific glutamate input and that synapses grouped by population coupling differ in activity and task encoding, rest on the authors' own two-photon imaging, which is not public. The dataset holds earlier ALM silicon-probe recordings that the paper reanalyses only in a supplementary figure. There, the lowest-coupling neurons were more correlated with each other under every reading tried, but the effect's size and the higher and middle coupling results turned on firing-rate and trial choices the paper does not describe. The paper's selection could not be rebuilt (9 mice against its 11), and many units are stored twice.

Jitter is the s.d. over the pulses kept, per neuron, averaged over neurons.
