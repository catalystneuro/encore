Mu et al. 2019 · Cell

Glia Accumulate Evidence that Actions Are Futile and Suppress Unsuccessful Behavior

doi:10.1016/j.cell.2019.05.050 · paper key mu-2019

Datasets

  • DANDI:000350

Two of the paper's main findings held in this data: fish swim harder just after a switch to open loop, and whole-brain neuronal activity is lower during passivity. Passivity was not higher in open loop than in closed loop under the preset comparison. Glial calcium rose at passivity onset in only 2 of 4 fish, and two of the files have unexplained odd traces. Epoch durations and swim intervals matched only in part. The astrocyte ablation, activation and noradrenergic experiments could not be tested because they are not in the dataset. The L-MO and NE-MO region results could not be tested either, because the ROIs have no registration or region labels.
