2026-10-30 · on DANDI:000402

  • ✓Reproduced The calcium imaging data comprise 14 scans of one mouse, collected between P75 and P81, spanning a volume of about 1,200 x 1,100 x 500 um.
  • ✓Reproduced Eleven scans were imaged at 6.3 Hz with four depths (eight fields), two at 8.6 Hz with three depths (six fields) and one at 9.6 Hz with two depths (four fields); in the 11 scans the four planes span roughly 300 to 400 um.
  • ✓Reproduced In total 125,413 masks were segmented across the 14 scans, of which 115,372 were classified as somatic by a trained classifier (8.1% excluded).
  • ⊘Blocked Consolidating masks imaged in more than one scan by their 3D position in the structural stack gives an estimated 75,909 unique functionally imaged neurons.
  • ⊘Blocked The imaging volume is roughly 50% VISp and 50% higher visual areas (VISlm, VISrl and VISal), with each functional unit assigned an area from its position in the stack.
  • ⊘Blocked All scans drifted less than 10 um in z over the recording, and most less than 5 um.
  • ⊘Blocked A two-channel structural stack (green GCaMP and red vasculature) of 1,412 x 1,322 x 670 um at 0.5 pixels per um was collected after Texas Red injection.
  • ✓Reproduced Each scan's stimulus lasted about 84 min and was made of 6 oracle clips of 10 s repeated 10 times (10 min), 144 unique natural clips of 10 s (24 min), 90 natural clips of 10 s shown twice (30 min), and 20 Trippy and 20 Monet2 seeds of 15 s each shown twice (10 min each).
  • ◐Partially reproduced The oracle clips, the 90 twice-repeated clips and 10 of the 20 seeds of each parametric stimulus were the same in every scan; the 144 unique clips and the other 10 seeds differed between scans.
  • ✓Reproduced The six oracle clips were repeated in the same order ten times per scan.
  • ◐Partially reproduced Each scan was preceded by 0.15 to 5.5 min with the monitor on and followed by 8.3 to 21.2 min with the monitor off, to record spontaneous activity.
  • ✓Reproduced Each 15 s Monet2 block holds 16 equal periods of motion, one for each of 16 directions between 0 and 360 degrees, at 42.8 degrees per second.
  • ✓Reproduced Neurons differ in how reliably they respond to the repeated oracle clips; units with high oracle scores give repeat-to-repeat responses that are consistent, and units with low scores do not.
  • ✓Reproduced Monet2 drives direction-tuned responses; neurons can be found preferring each of the 16 directions, and their trial-averaged responses peak at their preferred direction.
  • ◐Partially reproduced Treadmill velocity, and the pupil position and diameter from video of the left eye, were recorded throughout each scan.
  • ⊘Blocked The binary flip code of the photodiode signal, used to align stimulus and behaviour clocks, was recovered for 89% of trials.
  • ⊘Blocked Experts manually matched 19,181 functional ROIs from 14 scans to 15,439 individual EM neurons.
  • ⊘Blocked Units independently matched to the same EM neuron in two scans have higher signal correlation than each matched unit with the nearest unmatched unit in the other scan, more clearly for units with oracle score above 0.2.
  • ⊘Blocked The fiducial-based automatch assigns 84,198 functional ROIs to 37,364 EM neurons with 83% precision against manual matches; dropping the bottom 30% of separation scores gives 90% precision with 59,934 ROIs and 31,042 neurons.
  • ⊘Blocked The vessel-based automatch, without fiducials, assigns 75,856 functional ROIs to 34,712 EM neurons with 84% precision; dropping the bottom 30% of separation scores gives 90% precision with 53,248 ROIs and 28,233 neurons.
  • ⊘Blocked Keeping only the matches on which both automated methods agree gives 89% agreement with manual matches, with 60,091 ROIs and 29,620 EM neurons.
  • ⊘Blocked A co-registration transform built from 2,934 expert-matched fiducials (1,994 somata and 942 blood vessels) has an average residual of 3.8 um; the staged transform lowers the residual from about 10 um to 5.6, 4.6, 3.9, 3.5, 3.1 and 2.9 um.
  • ◐Partially reproduced The block was cut into 27,972 sections of 40 nm, 26,652 were imaged by five autoTEMs, and an 800 um region of sections 7,931 to 27,904 was processed, split into two subvolumes of about 35% and 65% of the sections.
  • ◐Partially reproduced Automated detection found 524 million synaptic clefts (186 million in subvolume 35, 337 million in subvolume 65), with 96% precision and 89% recall against 8,611 manually annotated synapses in 70 subvolumes, and 98% partner assignment accuracy on 191 held-out synapses.
  • ✓Reproduced Nuclei were automatically segmented in subvolume 65, 144,120 in all.
  • ✓Reproduced An SVM on nucleus features detects neurons with 96.9% precision and 99.6% recall, predicting 82,247 neurons in subvolume 65, of which 69,957 have well-segmented somata; a multilayer perceptron separates excitatory from inhibitory neurons with 97% accuracy (96% precision and 94% recall for inhibitory calls).
  • ⊘Blocked The released segmentation holds 1,046,656 proofreading edits as of 16 September 2024; 1,433 neurons have proofread axons, 85 excitatory neurons have fully proofread axons and dendrites, and 1,188 excitatory neurons have proofread dendrites.
  • ⊘Blocked Comparing proofread with automated dendrites, 99% of inputs assigned to a postsynaptic soma in the automated segmentation were correct.
  • ⊘Blocked The longest proofread excitatory axon measures 18.9 mm with 2,483 synaptic outputs; inhibitory axons range from 1.1 to 32.3 mm in length with a mean of 2,754 outputs (range 99 to 14,019).
  • ⊘Blocked The automated segmentation had 7,050 objects merging 17,753 neurons; after manual and automated splitting the total of individually segmented neurons is 84,035, and NEURD made more than 164,000 edits.
  • ⊘Blocked Between 60% and 95% of the output synapses of proofread axons map to postsynaptic targets with a known soma, depending on cell type and location.
