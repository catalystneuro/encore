Distinct value computations support rapid sequential decisions

Andrew Mah, Shannon Sandra Schiereck, Veronica Bossio, Christine M. Constantinople

Summary: Most of the paper's main findings held up in this data, and every claim could be tested: wait and trial initiation times both track the hidden reward blocks, initiation times follow recent rewards while wait times do not, and an inferential model fits wait times better than a retrospective one. Slower initiation after small offers held only on trials after violations, where the task repeats the offer; on the trials the Methods name, it reverses. Two model results did not reproduce, the inferential model's insensitivity to the previous offer and wait time differences between rats with good and poor inference, on refits made without the unpublished fitting code. Training effects, tested on the authors' Zenodo deposit, mostly held.
