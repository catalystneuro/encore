Carcea et al. 2021 · Nature

Oxytocin neurons enable social transmission of maternal behaviour

doi:10.1038/s41586-021-03814-7 · paper key carcea-2021

Datasets

  • DANDI:000114

The main findings held up only in part. Virgin PVN neurons were activated by nest entry and by the dam's retrievals, though in fewer units than reported. Activation by shepherding did not reproduce (1.5% of units against 11.9%), and neither did stronger firing after shepherded nest entry. The dam's shepherding and retrieval rates were also well below the paper's. Oxytocin-specific results, learning by observation, silencing and the auditory cortex work could not be tested, because the tagging, behaviour cohorts and cortex recordings are missing. The dandiset has units from only 4 of 17 virgins.
