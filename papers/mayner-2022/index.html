Measuring Stimulus-Evoked Neurophysiological Differentiation in Distinct Populations of Neurons in Mouse Visual Cortex

The central finding held: unscrambled movies evoked higher neural differentiation than scrambled ones in layer 2/3 and in areas AL and AM, as did the multivariate, decoding and clip-difference results. The claim that this survives nearly all analysis settings did not reproduce (36 of 108 settings agree), though the paper's own outputs also fail the rule used. The pupil, calcium-event and mediation analyses could not be tested, since the dataset has no eye tracking or events. The locomotion correlation held, and one minor effect-size range held only in part.
