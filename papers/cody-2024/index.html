Cody et al. 2024 · The Journal of Neuroscience

Cortical Zinc Signaling Is Necessary for Changes in Mouse Pupil Diameter That Are Evoked by Background Sounds with Different Contrasts

doi:10.1523/jneurosci.0939-23.2024 · paper key cody-2024

Datasets

  • DANDI:001256

Most of the paper's central findings hold up in this data, but several do not. The inverse-U relation between baseline pupil diameter and tone response, reliability and contrast gain holds. So does the baseline-by-contrast interaction. Evoked pupil responses to high contrast were never significantly larger over the whole sound, and cortical responses did differ by contrast where the paper found none. The zinc-blocker (ZX1) pupil effects partly held and the Figure 5 widefield findings could not be tested. Widefield imaging is missing from the dataset, and the paper's pupil cleaning is underspecified, so the agent's own cleaning choices (an assumption) drive many outcomes. The minor claims on pupil drift and the likelihood-ratio test mostly did not match.
