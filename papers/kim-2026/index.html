Kim et al. 2026 · Science Advances

Chronic electrophysiology and two-photon calcium imaging of mouse primary somatosensory cortex during intracortical microstimulation learning

doi:10.1126/sciadv.aef0343 · paper key kim-2026

Datasets

  • DANDI:001868

The paper's main behavioural and electrophysiology findings largely held up in this data. Thresholds fell with learning (median 5.39 to 3.88 uA), and modulation rose over training in pyramidal and pulse-locked units but not in non-pulse-locked units. Population coupling rose in pulse-locked units. Several findings did not hold: pulse-locking identity was stable across currents in only 79% of unit-channel pairs (the paper says over 80%), and the hit-miss separation (KL divergence) and the hit-miss divergence onsets did not match. All imaging findings (activation density and radius, tracked neurons) could not be tested, because the files lack stimulation-contact positions in the imaging volume and any identity of a cell across sessions. The same gap blocks the waveform and unit-tracking findings, since each unit has only one mean waveform. Conduction velocity at 4 uA fell just below the expected range.
