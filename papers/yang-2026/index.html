Summary: The paper's main findings on ALM photoinhibition held up in this data. In all five regions the early population difference lies mostly outside the choice and action axes, lick decoding rises after Go, and a difference remains after matching laser and control trials on lick direction and reaction time. Finer values matched only in part, among them the early decoding accuracies, the choice and action shares before the lick and the ratio to control-trial splits. In the example session the displayed plane held 70 to 97% of the early difference against 25%, by a computation the paper does not describe.
