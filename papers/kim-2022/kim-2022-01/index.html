    Results 3.1, paragraph 1; Figure 2A

    Mean YP rises with field for all five amplitudes (Spearman rho 0.98 to 0.99 over 16 ROI from 5.13 to 15.38 kV/cm). Strongest against weakest ROI, YP is higher with p < 0.05 for 50, 75 and 100% under both readings (paired p 0.011, 0.041, 0.003; unpaired p 0.0008, 0.014, below 0.0001) and not for unipolar (2 dishes; paired p 0.17, unpaired p 0.11) or 25% (3 dishes; paired p 0.19, unpaired p 0.15). On the top side, with the scale from the electrodes, with the right footprint and with the field scaled to 15.1 kV/cm at the centre the outcome is the same. On the bottom side of bb' alone, or with YP_0 and MB_0 from the left footprint alone, the paired test passes for 2 amplitudes and the unpaired for 3, which the criterion would call methods_underspecified.     How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • The ROI positions are not in the dataset or the paper. 200 um ROI are tiled contiguously along bb' from the midpoint of the electrode centres out to 3 mm, and the two ROI at the same distance are averaged per dish (the claims file's reading a). This is assumed as the reading the paper best supports, since Methods 2.3 and Fig. 1C treat bb' as one line through the centre; one side only is shown for each side.
    • YP_0 and MB_0 are averaged over discs of 0.5 mm radius in both footprints (Methods 2.5 says "within the electrodes' footprints"); each footprint alone is shown as a variant.
    • The field per ROI is recomputed from the electrode geometry (Methods 2.3) in closed form for two cylinders in the plane, which gives 15.38 kV/cm at the centre against the 15.1 kV/cm the paper cites. The pixel size, absent from the files, is taken as 0.65 um (6.5 um camera pixel, 10x objective, Methods 2.5); a scale set from each dish's electrode distance is a variant.
    • Unipolar pulses have 2 dishes, not 3, because sub-Fig2-0-03 is left out (its YP channel is a copy of sub-Fig2-25-04's).
    • The ROI with the weakest field is the outermost tiled ROI (3.0 mm, 5.13 kV/cm); the paper does not give how far its ROI extend. This is an assumption (bipolar.py, N_ROI_SIDE), and the notebook shows the test with the weakest ROI at 2.0 and 2.4 mm.
