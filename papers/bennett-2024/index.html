Bennett et al. 2024 · Neuron

SHIELD: Skull-shaped hemispheric implants enabling large-scale electrophysiology datasets in the mouse brain

doi:10.1016/j.neuron.2024.06.015 · paper key bennett-2024

Datasets

  • DANDI:001051

Most Figure 4 findings held: units per session, drift, QC pass fraction, and stable unit yield, receptive fields and visual responses across days. The QC metric distributions were not comparable by the paper's test. Of the alpha-event findings, VISp events locking to stimulus offset held, but the claim of no hippocampal engagement and the replay correlation (r at most 0.63) did not. The cortical and striatal coupling networks could not be settled, since the paper does not fully define coherence and events and the readings disagree. The surgery, histology, MRI, bilateral and optogenetics findings could not be tested because that material is not in the dataset.
