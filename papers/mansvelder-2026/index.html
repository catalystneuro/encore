Summary: The paper's species finding held in part: human interneurons had higher resonance frequency and strength than mouse ones and PV cells were the most often resonant, but fewer human cells were resonant than reported (25.5% against 32.7%), under a chirp analysis the paper does not fully describe. Slice culture raised resonance in most subclasses; the subclass-specific links of sag ratio and input resistance to resonance frequency did not hold, and of the gene correlations only HCN1 held. PV and SST cells could not be compared across species, as those mouse cells are not public. The depth, HCN blocker and model findings could not be tested: the depth and blocker data are not public, and the models were not run.
