  • fig1d-pv-decorrelation

    The correlation between population vectors of place cells decreases with the lag between trials, in both familiar and novel environments (within-session drift).

    The correlation decreased as a function of the lag between trials, suggesting drift within both the familiar and the novel environment

    Results, Within-session representational drift, paragraph 1; Figure 1C and 1D

    How it will be judged

    Per session and turn (figure-8) or direction (linear maze), the population vector of a trial is the concatenation of the place cells' trial rate maps (Figure 1C legend; code compare amplitude change and location drift notebook, raw rates, place cells only); Pearson correlation between every pair of trials. The slope of correlation on lag is fitted by linear regression over all trial pairs with lags 1 to 10 (code pv_corr_analysis.py, get_decay_slope_linregress). The paper reports no test of the decrease itself; its default one-sample test is the two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test (STAR Methods, Quantification and statistical analysis). Reproduced when the slopes are below zero by that test at p < 0.05 in familiar and in novel units separately; partially reproduced when this holds in one environment only.
