  • signaling-network-size

    The binarized signaling network of the 188 head neurons, with an edge for each pair whose q-value is below 0.05, has 1151 edges and a density of 0.033.

    We consider binarized directed connectome and signaling networks of the same head neurons (N = 188 head neurons from a total of 302 neurons in the entire nervous system), but each having different numbers of edges (corresponding to M = 1151 for signaling

    Results, paragraph 1; Methods, The Networks

    How it will be judged

    Directed edges from stimulated to responding neuron among the 188 head neurons, kept when q < 0.05 (Methods). Density is edges over 188 x 187 possible directed edges, which gives 0.0327 for 1151 edges. The q-values are read (a) recomputed from the dataset with the method of Randi et al. 2023 (reference 27), or (b) taken from that paper's released atlas; reproduced when both numbers are within tolerance under one reading.

    How it will be judged

Reproduced when both values are within tolerance and sigma > 1.

    How it will be judged
