Kohus et al. 2016 · Other journals

Properties and dynamics of inhibitory synaptic communication within the CA3 microcircuits of pyramidal cells and interneurons expressing parvalbumin or cholecystokinin

doi:10.1113/jp272231 · paper key kohus-2016

Datasets

  • CRCNS:hc-7

The paper's main findings on short-term plasticity largely held up here: uIPSC kinetics and amplitude differ by presynaptic cell type, PV+ interneuron synapses depress more at higher frequencies, and CCK+ synapses facilitate during short bursts. The late onset of asynchronous release at CCK+ synapses did not reproduce, likely because the paper does not describe its onset rule and the rules tried also flag PV+ pairs the paper calls synchronous. CCK+ basket cell to pyramidal cell trains facilitated in only 1 of 6 pairs, and the plasticity models reproduced in part. The interneuron firing clusters, connection probabilities, spontaneous IPSCs and sharp-wave ripple results could not be tested, as those recordings are not in the dataset.

  • ⊘Blocked Hierarchical clustering of 67 CA3 interneurons on principal components of 40 electrophysiological features gives two clusters that match PV+ and CCK+ cells with more than 98% overlap; a single PV+ cell falls in the CCK+ cluster.
  • ⊘Blocked Reclustering the 26 PV+ cells gives two groups, one mostly PV+BCs (13 of 16) and one mostly AACs (8 of 10), an overall match of about 83%.
  • ⊘Blocked Clustering the CCK+ cells reveals no well-separated subgroups matching CCK+BCs and CCK+DTIs, which form a physiological continuum.
  • ⊘Blocked PV+ and CCK+ interneurons differ in single-cell physiology; PV+ cells have a lower input resistance, fire more APs at most and have narrower APs than CCK+ cells.
  • ⊘Blocked Morphologically identified PV+BCs and AACs differ in input resistance, rheobase, maximal AP number, AP width and after-hyperpolarization; AACs have a higher input resistance, a lower rheobase and fire more APs at most.
  • ⊘Blocked Connection probability is high among PV+ cells, 50% (22 of 44 pairs); 66.6% among PV+BCs (20 of 30, six unidirectional and seven bidirectional pairs); PV+BCs contact AACs (2 of 4) but AACs contact neither PV+BCs nor AACs, a highly significant difference.
  • ⊘Blocked Connection probability among CCK+ cells is 28% (14 of 50), with frequent bidirectional connections, but only within CCK+BCs (42.85%) and within CCK+DTIs (45.45%); none between the two subtypes, a significant preference for the same partner type.
