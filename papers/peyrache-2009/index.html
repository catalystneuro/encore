Peyrache et al. 2009 · Nature Neuroscience

Replay of rule-learning related neural patterns in the prefrontal cortex during sleep

doi:10.1038/nn.2337 · paper key peyrache-2009

Datasets

  • CRCNS:pfc-6

The central finding held in this data: awake activity patterns were reactivated more strongly in slow-wave sleep after the task than before it, in all three encoding-strength groups. Two related findings did not hold. The link to encoding strength was positive but weaker than reported (r squared 0.38 against 0.61), and the heavier-tailed post-task distributions appeared in only one group. The ripple, delta-wave, spindle and learning findings could not be tested, because the data set has no LFP or event times. The paper's session and cell counts and its rule-learning counts were not matched, partly because the data set holds fewer sessions of one rat and does not say which sessions were analysed. Several controls held only in part.
