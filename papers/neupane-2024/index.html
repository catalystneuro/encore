Neupane et al. 2024 · Nature

Mental navigation in the primate entorhinal cortex

doi:10.1038/s41586-024-07557-z · paper key neupane-2024

Datasets

  • DANDI:000897

Behaviour, the absence of eye and hand periodicity and single-neuron coding of distance held up in this data, but the central finding of widespread periodic entorhinal firing did not. Under the main reading under 16% of units were periodic, against 37% in the paper, and the Methods do not say how the null becomes a firing rate. The peak near 0.65 s held in one monkey, and offset coding beat onset coding in one monkey. The comparison with the inter-trial interval and the generalization result could not be tested, because the interval is unmarked and the training sessions are not deposited. Only 15 of the paper's 32 sessions are in the dataset.
