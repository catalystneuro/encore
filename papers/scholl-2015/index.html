  • fig1e-pv-tdtomato-colocalization

    tdTomato and endogenous PV co-localize broadly in V1 of PV-Cre;Ai14 mice; 1,274 of 1,613 PV+ cells expressed tdTomato (79.4% +/- 7.8% SD) and 1,274 of 1,354 tdTomato cells expressed PV (94.3% +/- 3.6% SD), in 3 animals.

    Broad co-localization of tdTomato and endogenous PV in V1 was established using post hoc immunostaining (1,274 of 1,613 PV+ expressed tdTomato: 79.4% ± 7.8% SD; 1,274 of 1,354 tdTomato cells expressed PV: 94.3% ± 3.6% SD, n = 3 animals

    Results, Binocular Integration in PV+ and PV- cells of Mouse V1 Binocular Zone, paragraph 1; Figure 1E

    How it will be judged

    Histology (anti-PV immunostaining of fixed sections, four fields of view in V1, layers 2 to 5, 3 mice; Experimental Procedures, Immunohistochemical Analysis). Tolerances are the paper's SD across animals. The Methods state the two percentages the other way round ("Most (94.3%) PV+ neurons expressed tdTomato, whereas ~80% tdTomato neurons were PV+"), so the readings are (a) the Results' assignment and (b) the Methods' assignment; the claim holds when the two percentages match under either. The data set is described as imaging data; if it holds no histology the claim cannot be tested.

    Results, Binocular Integration in PV+ and PV- cells of Mouse V1 Binocular Zone, paragraph 3

    How it will be judged

    Responsiveness by the Methods' criterion, (mean stimulus - mean blank) / (SE stimulus + SE blank) >= 1 on the 0.5 s window response. The Methods require this for at least one monocular and one binocular stimulus, but the Results count monocular and binocular responsiveness separately and note binocular responses without monocular ones, so the count is read as cells with at least one monocular stimulus passing, and cells with at least one binocular stimulus passing, each counted on its own (counting only cells that meet the joint criterion would make the two counts equal, which the reported numbers rule out). The neuropil requirement (responses above the neuropil's 95% confidence interval) is applied where neuropil traces are available and the result without it is shown; the paper does not define the neuropil region for this test. The counts hold when all six values are within tolerance.
