Summary: The paper's group photostimulation results hold in this data: activating x1 neurons drives integration along the x1 dimension, and activating x2 neurons does not. The slow integration dimension that defines the line attractor in rSLDS fits did not reproduce, with x1 slower than x2 in 5 of 7 two-photon mice; the paper's fitted models are not deposited, and new fits change with the random seed. That stimulation moves activity along the attractor holds only in part. The single-neuron stimulation and connectivity findings could not be tested because those sessions are not in the dataset, and the network model could not be run as the Methods describe it. Smaller claims on neuron overlap and variance explained did not reproduce.
