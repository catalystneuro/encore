Guo et al. 2017 · Nature

Maintenance of persistent activity in a frontal thalamocortical loop

doi:10.1038/nature22324 · paper key guo-2017

Datasets

  • DANDI:000009

Most of the paper's main findings hold in this data, but its exact numbers mostly do not. ALM photoinhibition biases licking ipsilaterally, thalamus photoinhibition nearly silences ALM, thalamus drives ALM more strongly than M1 or contralateral ALM, and selectivity rises over the delay in both areas. Thalamic activity under ALM photoinhibition falls to about half of control, where the paper reports a quarter. The 18.7 mV hyperpolarization of ALM neurons comes out smaller, and the files leave the photostimulus start unclear. The muscimol, thalamic behaviour, weak-photoinhibition and anatomy findings could not be tested because those data are not deposited. Unit counts fall short because the dandiset holds fewer units than the paper.
