Luo et al. 2024 · Other journals

Comparative Physiology and Morphology of BLA-Projecting NBM/SI Cholinergic Neurons in Mouse and Macaque

doi:10.1002/cne.70001 · paper key luo-2024

Datasets

  • DANDI:001261

The paper's main electrophysiology findings mostly held in this data, some did not, and all morphology findings could not be tested. Mouse cholinergic and noncholinergic neurons differ in 13 of 17 features, the lowest count the paper's range allows, LDA separated them with AHP amplitude weighted highest, and macaque neurons were more excitable. The AHP and membrane time constant differences did not reproduce as the Methods define them, though the paper's code settings matched. The near-complete UMAP separation also failed, with at least 4 neurons misplaced against 2. Morphology was untestable because the dandiset holds only current-clamp recordings.
