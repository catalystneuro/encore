Most central findings held up in this data, one only in part, and several could not be tested. Optogenetic dopamine pairing raised target syllable use over controls (U = 198, paper 197), and dLight differed by syllable without identifying it. Dopamine also tracked syllable usage and transition entropy across syllables, and predicted later use at 6 of 10 windows. The catch-trial finding of no movement effect could not be tested, because the files do not mark catch trials. The food-reward data are missing, and seven analyses were skipped for time, including the reinforcement-learning model.
