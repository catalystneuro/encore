On DANDI:000946: Most of the paper's main findings could not be tested in this data, and none reproduced. The unit counts did not reproduce: POm has 352 units at 3000 Hz against 250 reported, perhaps because the paper's unit rule differs. On DANDI:001199: No main finding of the paper could be confirmed in this data. The cell-type results for S1 and POm, including responses to repetition rate and duty cycle and the cross-area correlations, could not be tested, because the files hold spike times only, with no waveforms or cell-type labels, and only one pulse duration. The auditory cortex responses did not reproduce: 194 units against the paper's 453, with no significant dependence on repetition rate, resting on assumed voltage and trial timing for the newer sessions. The rats are fewer than the paper reports and were recorded after its preprint, so this dandiset probably holds different recordings.
