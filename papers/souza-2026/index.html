Summary: Most of the paper's main findings on reward coding hold up in this data. Reward-modulated units are mostly suppressed, more in VISp than VISam and in narrow-spiking and layer 4 units, and their summed activity is less variable than the rest. Three results did not reproduce: how much better mixtures of response archetypes fit units than single ones, how little areas differ in them, and the variability change around image changes, for which the paper gives no windows. Some counts and controls, including those for layer 4, hold only in part or not. All results rest on an assumed choice of sessions and units, as the paper does not say which 103 of 153 sessions it used.
