    Results, paragraph 5; fig. S1B to S1D and legend

    How it will be judged

    Tolerances are the s.e.m. the fig. S1 legend gives. Per rat and condition, the mean train velocity over the travel (from tracked head position on the linearized track), the number of completed laps and the duration of the travel session. The paper does not say how velocity is averaged; readings are (a) over all position samples of the session, (b) over samples above 5 cm/s, the Methods' threshold for awake analyses. A claim of absence, judged on the paper's test, a repeated-measures ANOVA over the 5 rats for each measure. Reproduced when all three ANOVAs give P > 0.05 and all nine means are within tolerance; partially reproduced when the ANOVAs hold but some means do not. The effect sizes are reported.

  • fig1d-no-remapping-p1-active

    Firing fields did not remap between Passive 1 and Active. Left: Mean unsigned proportional shift s (vertical gray line; **P < 0.01) and s distribution for n = 2000 bootstrapped remapping data sets

    Fig. 1D and legend; Results, paragraph 5

    How it will be judged

    Following the Methods (Firing fields, paragraph 2) and FMAToolbox TestRemapping and FieldShift. For each place cell, the shift is the mode of the spatial cross-correlogram of its two firing curves divided by its mean field size; the mean unsigned shift is compared with 2000 shuffles of cell identity in the test condition; p is the proportion of shuffled means at or below the observed one. Then peak locations as angles on the obround track, differences tested with a Rayleigh test and a V-test against 0. Open, with readings run each; (a) cells with a field in both conditions, (b) cells with a field in either; the cross-correlogram (a) circular, as the track is a closed loop, (b) linear, TestRemapping's default; no repeat-control correction, as the Methods mention none (TestRemapping with the control given twice). Reproduced when all three tests reach their legend bounds under one reading; partially reproduced when the bootstrap test and the V-test are significant at 0.05 only.
