The dandiset itself holds none of the paper's data, so the checks ran on the nine dandisets the paper lists, using an assumed soma region. Reproduced: pure sine waves fire more action potentials than modulated ones, and firing locks to the beats of modulated waves. Partly reproduced: AP peak height held but duration did not, and modulated thresholds came out higher with only one of three tests significant. Not reproduced: the excitation block at the highest fields. Blocked: the 20 kHz versus 2 kHz threshold ratio, which needs voltage calibration that is not deposited, and the low-conductivity thresholds, whose sessions are missing. The Figure 4 to 6 membrane-charging findings were also blocked, because the strobe files hold no ROIs or cell identities.

On DANDI:001271: Two main findings held up, several did not, and the tetrodotoxin results could not be tested. Pure sine waves evoked more spikes than 20-Hz modulated waves, and modulated firing locked to the beats, both on an assumed reading of the criterion. Firing rates stayed well below the reported 60 to 90 Hz, and 20-kHz thresholds were not about twice the 2-kHz ones. Sustained depolarization was not detected, though the measure may miss plateaus this small. The tetrodotoxin effects stayed untested because the paper does not say how the soma was outlined on the deposited crops, the low-conductivity recordings are absent, and the pulse-kinetics findings were not attempted.

On DANDI:001272: Some of the paper's main findings held in this data, others did not, and two could not be tested. Unmodulated waves evoked more spikes than 20-Hz modulated waves, firing mostly locked to the beats, and tetrodotoxin abolished spikes. Excitation of every neuron with delayed spikes, the doubled 20-kHz threshold, higher thresholds for modulated waves and the membrane charging times did not reproduce; the amplitude calibration and the debris ROI are not deposited. The low-conductivity finding has no deposited data and the sine-versus-pulse finding was not attempted. The control holds 3 neurons, against 8 in the paper.

On DANDI:001273: Of the paper's main findings on membrane charging by 2-us pulses, two held here, the pooled time constants did not, and the shape and alignment effects could not be tested without shape labels in the dataset. Polarization at the electrode-facing poles held (13 of 14 experiments), as did equal charging at both poles, though that test has little power. Pooled discharging times came out longer than reported, and the paper's ROIs and fit settings are not given. Sustained firing partly held and the Di-8-ANEPPS control did not, under a 5% level set for another dye. The sine wave excitation and threshold findings are in other dandisets and were not tested for lack of time.

On DANDI:001274: This is the paragraph for that file: Most of the paper's main findings on sine wave excitation of neurons did not hold up in this data, and the polarization findings could not be tested. Unmodulated waves evoked more action potentials than modulated ones, and modulated thresholds were higher, though only in part. The delayed onset of firing, the firing rate ceiling, the excitation block and the twofold higher 20-kHz thresholds missed, and the 2-kHz threshold was 75.6 against 57.7 V/cm. The generator calibration behind the field values is not deposited, which may explain the threshold misses. The polarization and sodium gluconate findings could not be tested because the dataset has no ROIs, cell identifiers or gluconate sessions.
