The amplitude and noise of the simulated traces rise with depth as in vivo, and Kilosort4 yields far fewer single units than the model predicts, mostly degraded ones. The in vivo amplitude-to-noise range, power-law spectra, six WaveMap profiles (8 clusters in the simulation), log-normal firing rates and the layer 5 selection bias did not reproduce. The accuracy, curation and predictor claims could not be tested, as sortings of the synthetic and 10-minute evoked recordings are missing or analyses were too large. The five manifold claims could not be tested, as the dandiset has no stimulus table.
