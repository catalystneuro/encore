Sargolini et al. 2006 · Science

Conjunctive Representation of Position, Direction, and Velocity in Entorhinal Cortex

doi:10.1126/science.1125572 · paper key sargolini-2006

Datasets

  • DANDI:000582

Grid cells occur in every layer and most often in layer II, as the paper reports. However, the deeper layers hold far more grid cells than the paper's counts, and layer II is no longer ahead of layer III under a stricter grid threshold. Head-direction cells cover the full range of directions and stay stable across trials, and gridness and directional tuning form a continuum. The finding that layer II has no head-direction or conjunctive cells did not hold up: a few layer II cells passed both tests, all from two normally tracked files of one rat. Whether running speed modulates the cells stayed unsettled, because method details the paper does not give changed the result, and the dataset lacks the cue-rotation trials and the recording-site positions needed to test grid scaling along the dorsoventral axis.
