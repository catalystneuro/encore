  • fig1ab-raf-patterns

    Human layer 1 interneurons show two forms of retroaxonal firing (RAF) after induction, persistent RAF with barrages of RA-APs at high frequency (median 88 RA-APs, 13.9 Hz; duration median 17.1 s, mean 68.4 s, from 0.18 to 2809 s) and sporadic RAF with a few RA-APs at low frequency (median 8 RA-APs, 0.31 Hz).

    persistent RAF showed barrages of 88 ± 1828 RA-APs with high frequencies (13.9 ± 17.3 Hz), (RAF duration: min: 0.18 s, max: 2809 s, mean: 68.4 s, and median: 17.1 ± 202.9 s); however, only a few (8 ± 6) individual RA-APs emerged at low frequencies (0.31 ± 1.5 Hz) during sporadic RAF

    Results, paragraph 1; Figure 1A and 1B

    How it will be judged

    Human induction recordings only. RA-APs are the APs of the continuous recording after induction, classed by readings (a) to (c) of the header. Per the Methods, an RAF epoch's duration runs from its first to its last RA-AP, its frequency is RA-APs over duration, and an epoch is persistent above 5 Hz and sporadic below. The Methods do not say how a recording is cut into epochs, nor what duration an epoch of one RA-AP has. Readings are (i) one epoch per cell, all RA-APs after induction; (ii) one epoch per induction run, when a cell was induced more than once (Figure 1E has cells with both patterns). Epochs of a single RA-AP are left out, as their frequency is undefined. The sentence places the duration values (minimum, maximum, mean, median) under persistent RAF; whether they cover all RAF epochs is not certain, so they are also shown over all epochs. Reproduced when, under one reading, at least six of the eight values are within tolerance and the two groups both occur; partially reproduced for four or five. The SDs are reported beside the medians.
