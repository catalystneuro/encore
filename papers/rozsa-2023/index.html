Summary: The paper's finding that retroaxonal action potentials (RA-APs) have a lower somatic threshold and pre-AP slope than AIS-APs held up in this data, and its sag findings held in part. The share of cells with retroaxonal firing did not: 0.98 of human cells in the deposited files have RA-APs against 0.226, and the dataset does not say how its cells were selected. Nor did the reduction of retroaxonal firing by ZD7288, under epoch definitions the paper does not give, or the bleb-first arrival of RA-APs. Spontaneous RA-APs in active slices and in vivo could not be tested, as the files carry no AP labels and the paper does not give its boundary between the two classes.
