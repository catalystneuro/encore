Chartrand et al. 2023 · Science

Human L1 patch-seq electrophysiology

doi:10.1126/science.adf0805 · paper key chartrand-2023

Datasets

  • DANDI:000630

Most of the paper's human electrophysiology findings held in this data, several did not, and the transcriptomic claims were not tested. Subclass differences in sag and firing rate, strong sag in MC4R cells, bursting PAX6 TNFAIP8L3 cells and the classifier held, though the firing rate result rests on a sweep noise criterion the paper does not describe. The sag and adaptation fit (R2 0.33 against 0.74), the LAMP5 sag gradient and irregular firing limited to one MC4R type did not hold. Human and mouse differences held only in direction. The held-out classifier test was blocked because the dandiset lacks those cells.
