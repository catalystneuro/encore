Seyedolmohadesin et al. 2026 · Current Biology

Whole-brain chemosensory responses of both C. elegans sexes

Maedeh Seyedolmohadesin, Xingyang Fu, Mahdi Torkashvand, Sina Rasouli, Samuel Lang, Liangzi Li, Cristine A. Kalinski, Steven J. Cook, Frank C. Schroeder, Eviatar Yemini, Vivek Venkatachalam

Outcomes of 33 claims

  • ✓24 reproduced
  • 4 not reproduced
  • ⊘5 blocked

Summary: Most of the paper's main findings held up in this data: nearly all head neuron classes respond to the cues, most respond differently in the two sexes, sensory neurons are the most dimorphic layer, functional connectivity is similar between the sexes (R2 = 0.806), and functional dimorphism does not track synaptic dimorphism. Three finer findings did not hold: fewer sensory classes than stated were both dimorphic and monomorphic, AWA did not respond to copper, and ASER's low-salt dimorphism fell just short (p = 0.055). Whether the most functionally dimorphic pairs lack synapses could not be tested, as it depends on a connectome form the paper does not name. Tail neurons were untestable: the dataset holds only head traces.

    Results, Most sensory neurons in the head exhibit sexually dimorphic responses, paragraph 9; Figure 5B

    86 of 89 canonical classes are responsive under exclusion (a) and 85 under (b), at p <= 0.05 and at p < 0.05 alike (not responsive AVD, I4 and RMG, and AQR under (b)). Each subclass has 32 tests (8 cues, 2 windows, 2 sexes), and 22.1% of all tests are significant, so the count is near its ceiling; this is the paper's test as written. The paper states no chance level. With 32 uncorrected tests per class, a class with no effect would be called responsive with probability up to 1 - 0.95^32 = 0.81 if the tests were independent, so about 72 of 89 classes could be expected by chance and the count is weakly diagnostic.

    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • The code's identification-confidence filter (conf == 1) cannot be applied; the dandiset has no confidence values. Its time ranges to ignore are already blank in the traces; its ganglia to ignore per recording cannot be checked.
    • Onset frames are the code's (config.py ON_stim), as the claims file says; the dandiset's annotations lie 1 to 3 frames later.

(2026) on dandiset 000981, which holds whole-head calcium imaging of 22 hermaphrodites and 25 males during repeated presentation of ten chemosensory stimuli.
