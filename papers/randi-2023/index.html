The paper's example responses (AVJR to AVDR, AVER to AVAR, SAADL to OLLR) and the spatial specificity of stimulation held up in this data, though two of the examples rest on very few trials. The inhibitory fraction did not reproduce (15.1% here against the paper's 11%), and the per-pair values matched the published ones only partly. Everything built on q values, including the functional connections, the anatomy comparisons and the unc-31 screen, could not be tested because the unstimulated control recordings are not public. The RID result held for one of the three neurons in wild type and only weakly in unc-31.
