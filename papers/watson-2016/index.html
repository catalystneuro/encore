Summary: The paper's central finding, that sleep evens out pyramidal cell firing rates, with fast cells slowing and slow cells speeding up across sleep and within nonREM packets, held up in the CRCNS copy of the full data set. State durations, firing rates by state and UP/DOWN state durations mostly matched too. Not reproduced were most of the sub-state effects of Figure 5, such as rate changes across microarousals or REM between packets, the decline of spindle and UP/DOWN incidence over sleep, and the rate-group correlations of Figure 2F. For the microarousal effects, the deposited microarousals do not match the paper's (434 within sleep against 790). The DANDI copy is a subset and lacks the lab's sleep sub-state intervals.
