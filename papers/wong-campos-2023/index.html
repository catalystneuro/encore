Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • The pulse windows are inferred from the light-evoked depolarization of the traces, as a 1 s period square wave with the paper's 0.5 s pulses (Results, Dendritic voltage mapping, "0.5 s pulses of widefield blue light at increasing intensity"); the period is read from the trace. This is an assumption, as the file records no stimulus.fig1d-light-intensity-spike-rate
  • The traces are the deposited z-scored PCA traces, not dF/F.fig1d-light-intensity-spike-rate
  • Spike and dendritic event detection use a 10 Hz high-pass filter and a 4 ms minimum separation, standard choices the paper does not give.fig1d-light-intensity-spike-rate
  • Assumed sub-frame peak timing by a parabola (reading (b) of fig2f-delay-range), since the dandiset's own Fig. 2 delays are given to 0.01 ms at 2 ms frames; under the sample reading no dendrite qualifies.fig1fg-dendrite-peaks-after-soma
  • The amplitudes are the deposited per-site values (peak -dF/F of the STA), not recomputed from traces, which are not in the dandiset.fig2e-half-amplitude-distance
  • Assumed that the preprint's 30 sites are the 30 LR sites of the dandiset's 12-neuron table; the preprint describes only the Lucy-Rho CheRiff construct (Fig. 1a) and gives n = 30, which equals the LR count. No neuron identifiers allow selecting the paper's 5 neurons.fig2e-half-amplitude-distance
  • The delays are the deposited ones; the criterion's two peak-timing readings cannot be run without the traces.fig2f-delay-range
  • The measured values are for the 32 LR sites of the dandiset's 12-neuron table, not the paper's 30 sites of 5 neurons.fig2f-delay-range
  • Deposited delays; the files do not say which neuron a site belongs to, so only the pooled fit (reading (a)) is run.fig2f-conduction-speed
  • Assumed the LR site set, as for fig2e-half-amplitude-distance.fig2f-conduction-speed
  • The paper's 23 pairs of 3 neurons cannot be identified. The values match for the Fig. 3 neuron (the barrel1 files) and not for the three neurons the dandiset records in both states; the outcome rests on that choice of pairs.fig3d-dendrite-cross-correlation
  • In the figure3i recordings, whether column 1 is the soma is not stated; both readings are shown.fig3d-dendrite-cross-correlation
  • The recordings are the 11 of the dandiset's Fig. 4a-d file (5 mice), which follows a later version of the paper; the paper's 3 neurons cannot be identified.fig4a-doublet-isi
  • Spike detection by the claims' readings (5 and 4 robust s.d. of the 10 Hz high-pass soma trace); the deposited spike times are shown as a variant.fig4a-doublet-isi
  • Both detection thresholds of the criterion (5 and 4 robust s.d.) decide, as the criterion asks for every reading; 4 s.d. recovers more of the spike times deposited with these recordings (mean recall 0.72 against 0.41, precision 0.95 against 0.98).fig4bc-doublet-first-fails
  • Amplitudes are measured per doublet and averaged per branch, not read off an average waveform, since the second spike's time varies between doublets.fig4bc-doublet-first-fails
  • Branches are the 31 dendrite columns of 11 recordings, not the paper's 10 branches; the file does not say which columns are the same branch.fig4bc-doublet-first-fails
  • Both detection thresholds of the criterion decide, as for fig4bc-doublet-first-fails.fig4d-accelerating-amplifies
  • Each spike's bAP amplitude is measured from the 5 ms before that spike, in the criterion's windows, and normalized by the branch mean (Methods, Analysis of bAP failures during spontaneous activity). The criterion gives the window of fig4bc and not its baseline; the fig4bc baseline (before the first spike of the trio) is shown as a variant.fig4d-accelerating-amplifies
  • 31 dendrite columns of 5 mice, not the paper's 10 branches of 3 neurons.fig4d-accelerating-amplifies

  • The average is smoothed over 10 ms and must stay within the band for 50 ms to count as returned; standard choices, shown with no smoothing and 20 ms.fig4l-sensory-plateau
