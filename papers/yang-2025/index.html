  • tables1-network-stable-short-term

    In every rat the cf_w vectors of seizure onset correlate more strongly between seizures of the same baseline group than between seizures of different baseline groups (Pearson r, intra-group against inter-group, Mann-Whitney).

    Methods C, paragraph 2; Table S1; Figure S3

    How it will be judged

    Tolerances are the SDs Table S1 reports (mean +- SD over seizure pairs). From Methods C and the Figure S3 legend, for each seizure of the four baseline groups the cf_w vector (4 nodes) over the first 5 s after onset; Pearson r between the vectors of every two seizures; intra-group pairs are those within one baseline group, inter-group pairs those across groups; Mann-Whitney test of intra against inter per rat. Open, with readings named, are (a) each unordered pair once, or (b) every off-diagonal cell of the matrix (each pair twice), and whether self-pairs (r = 1) enter the intra-group set, (c) excluded or (d) included; the cf_w sign and window-combination readings of the opening comment apply. Reproduced when, under one reading, intra-group r exceeds inter-group r with Mann-Whitney p < 0.05 in all 7 rats and at least 10 of the 14 means lie within tolerance; partially reproduced when the direction and significance hold in at least 5 rats.
