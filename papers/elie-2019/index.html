  • units-404

    The analysis uses 404 of the 914 isolated single units, those from which call type could be decoded above chance in the authors' earlier study.

    The data of neural responses from 404 out of 914 isolated single units were used in this study. The 404 neurons were selected based on a prior analysis that showed that this subset of units were not only auditory but also contained information about call-types

    Methods, Neurophysiological and histological procedures, last paragraph

    How it will be judged

    The selection is the decoding analysis of Elie & Theunissen 2015 (Eur J Neurosci 41:546-567, reference 40), which this paper does not describe. Readings (a) a list of the 404 units from the data set's documentation or the 2015 paper, (b) the 2015 decoding selection redone as that paper describes it. Reproduced when the data set holds 914 single units and a selection of 404 +- 40 units can be made under one reading; the units, not only the count, are carried to later claims. Blocked as methods_underspecified when neither reading can be carried out.

Reproduced when the count is within tolerance under one reading.

  • s1-temporal-resolution

    Low-pass filtering at 50 Hz (10 ms bins) keeps 96.7% +- 6.9% of the coherence-based information of the spike trains, only 88 of 404 units lose more than 5%, and 98.8% +- 1.6% of the power of the KDE rates lies below 50 Hz.

    Over all units, 96.7% ±6.9% (mean ± SD) of information was conserved by a lowpass filtering at 50Hz and only 88 out of 404 cells had information losses greater than 5%. ... On average across units, the cumulative power reached 98.8%±1.6% (mean ± SD) at 50Hz

    Methods, Time-varying spike rate estimation, paragraph 4; S1 Fig

    How it will be judged

    Tolerances on the two percentages are the stated SDs (protocol). Coherence, per unit the coherence between the PSTHs of two halves of the trials over all stimuli, converted to the coherence between one spike train and the mean response by the formula of the Methods (lost from the text read; reference 99, Hsu, Borst and Theunissen 2004), and I_AR = -integral of log2(1 - coherence) over frequency, from 0 to 50 Hz against 0 to 500 Hz (S1 Fig legend). Spike trains at 10 kHz as the Methods say (code, organiz_SpikeArray4coherence.m; the coherence function is not in the repository). Power, per unit, the KDE rates of all stimuli, each Hanning-windowed over 600 ms and concatenated, Welch periodogram in 200 ms windows at 1 kHz, cumulative power normalised to the total, read at 50 Hz (Methods; code, Semantic_NeuroInfo_Poisson_savio.m). Reproduced when all three values are within tolerance; partially when one or two are.

  • fig2a-poisson-test

    For most units (240 of 404) the spike counts in 10 ms bins cannot be told from a Poisson distribution in 90% of the bins, only 5 of 404 deviate in 40% of the bins, and on average 10.5% of bins are rejected.

    For the large majority of cells (240/404), the empirical spike count could not be distinguished from a Poisson distribution for 90% of the time bins. For a small number of neurons (5/404) non-Poisson spike count statistics could be observed in 40% of the time bins. The mean number of rejected time bins was 10.5%.

    Results, Avian auditory neurons have approximate Poisson statistics, paragraph 2; Figure 2A

    How it will be judged

    Per unit, stimulus and 10 ms bin, the Poisson log-likelihood of the trial counts with the delete-one (jackknife) KDE mean of each trial, against 1000 log-likelihoods of 10 Poisson samples with the full KDE mean; two-tailed p; Benjamini-Hochberg at 0.05 within each unit over all its stimulus-bin tests (Methods, Testing the poisson assumptions). The code (PoissonTestNeuralResponses.m) takes a one-tailed p, 1 minus the fraction of simulated log-likelihoods above the data's, has no FDR step, and uses bins from 10 to 610 ms; the Methods are followed and the code's p is a variant. Open, the cut-offs; readings (a) rejected in at most 10% of bins and in at least 40%, (b) rejected in under 10% and over 40%. Reproduced when the three values are within tolerance under one reading; the count of 5 is held to +- 1 unit, as 10% of 5 is below one unit. Partially reproduced when the mean is within tolerance and one count is.
