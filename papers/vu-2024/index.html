Vu et al. 2024 · Neuron

Targeted micro-fiber arrays for measuring and manipulating localized multi-scale neural dynamics over large, deep brain volumes during behavior

doi:10.1016/j.neuron.2023.12.011 · paper key vu-2024

Datasets

  • DANDI:001084

Most main findings held up in this data. Light evoked widespread dopamine increases, the tone drove increases in the posterior tail and decreases anteriorly, both latency gradients appeared, and release from one stimulated fiber fell with distance. Two parts did not hold: light increases were not less prevalent in the tail, and tone decreases were more ventral but not more lateral. Chronic stability could be tested only to week 8, because the task variables for later sessions are missing. The freely moving and jaw movement results were not tested, because their data are only on Zenodo. Latency means matched the paper only when counted as its code counts them.

  • ✓Reproduced In a D1-cre mouse expressing GCaMP7f in D1 spiny projection neurons, fluorescence under 470 nm excitation shows Ca2+ transients across fibers that are absent under 405 nm (isosbestic) excitation.
  • ✓Reproduced Pairwise correlations of D1 SPN Ca2+ signals are generally low but variable, and drop off significantly with the distance between fiber tips.
  • ✓Reproduced After accounting for distance in the brain, signal correlations do not depend on the distance between fibers on the bundle surface, so nearby fibers in the bundle do not cross-talk.
  • ◐Partially reproduced Dopamine release to unpredicted water reward is reliable and of comparable amplitude over at least 12 weeks after implantation (median over 44 fibers, normalized to week 5).
  • ✓Reproduced The dandiset's DF/F traces, fibers and stimulus times are those behind the paper's salient-stimulus analysis, so that its per-fiber responses can be linked to the paper's.
  • ✓Reproduced The light evokes transient increases in dopamine release across many striatal locations.
  • ◐Partially reproduced Light-evoked dopamine increases follow an amplitude gradient from the anterior striatum into the posterior tail, where increases are less prevalent and smaller.
  • ✓Reproduced The tone evokes the strongest dopamine increases at sites in the posterior tail, while anterior to the tail it evokes primarily decreases.
  • ◐Partially reproduced Tone-evoked decreases are slightly more prevalent in the anterior lateral and ventral striatum, with many sites showing both increases to the light and decreases to the tone.
  • ◐Partially reproduced The modality-specific release patterns are consistent within and across mice.
  • ◐Partially reproduced Locomotor changes to the stimuli are variable and bi-directional, while the dopamine release patterns to light and tone are consistent, so the patterns are stimulus and not movement driven.
  • ◐Partially reproduced With isosbestic (415 nm) excitation, large fluorescence changes and spatial patterns to light and tone are not observed, ruling out a hemodynamic origin of the release patterns.
  • ◐Partially reproduced Latencies to half-maximum are shorter for dopamine increases to light than to tone, and shorter for increases than decreases to tone (light increase 221.6 ms, SD 42.5; tone increase 305.4 ms, SD 100.0; tone decrease 428.3 ms, SD 197.1).
  • ✓Reproduced Dopamine release latencies to light follow an anterior-posterior gradient, with faster increases in the anterior striatum, consistent with wave-like dynamics.
  • ✓Reproduced Dopamine release latencies to tone follow a different gradient, faster in the posterior striatum than in the anterior striatum.
  • ◐Partially reproduced The spatial latency gradients are also present in individual mice.
  • ◐Partially reproduced In the mouse with ChR2 in dopamine axons and rDAm3.0 in the striatum, 24 recording locations lie within or on the border of the striatum; 18 of the 24 stimulated locations evoke a significant response at another location, and 22 of the 24 recording locations respond to at least one stimulation.
  • ✓Reproduced Stimulating dopamine axons through one fiber evokes release at neighbouring non-stimulated sites that drops off sharply with distance in all three dimensions.
  • ◐Partially reproduced Evoked release drops off less with distance dorsal to the stimulated fiber than ventral to it, consistent with dorsally extending dopamine axon branches.
  • ✓Reproduced In control mice expressing tdTomato in place of rDAm3.0, single-fiber stimulation evokes no significant fluorescence increase at non-stimulated sites (0 of 48 sites, 26 stimulations in 2 mice), so the release is not a hemodynamic effect.
  • ✓Reproduced In mice expressing rDAm3.0 without opsin, light through one fiber raises fluorescence at the stimulated site by photoswitching, but at no other site (0 of 22 non-stimulated sites for 3 stimulations).
