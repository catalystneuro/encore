Hiroki Asari, Anthony M. Zador

Summary: The paper's central finding held in this data: A1 responses depend on the preceding sound for seconds, the context-dependent power decaying with a time constant of about 1 s as reported, while in the thalamus (MGB) the effect fades fast. Fewer probes showed significant context dependence than reported, the bound on short-window models rose more slowly, and longer STRF windows did improve predictions, under a fold averaging the paper does not describe. Amplitude and frequency changes outlasted modulation and higher-order changes, but the proportions did not match, on stimulus groupings rebuilt because the paper does not list them. The synaptic conductance findings could not be tested, as the data set holds no voltage-clamp recordings.
