Reach-dependent reorientation of rotational dynamics in motor cortex.

David A. Sabatini, Matthew Tyler Kaufman

Summary: Much of the paper's picture holds in this data: shared rotations in reach-specific planes explain 94.1 to 96.9% of the variance, the planes reorient between reaches, the state at movement onset predicts them, and models built on them encode reaches better than standard models. Two central findings did not reproduce: the rotation frequencies sit above the paper's 0.5, 1.5 and 2.5 Hz and differ between reaches more than estimation noise, and one linear dynamical system fits all reaches much better than reported. A possible reason is that the dataset holds one unlabelled unit per electrode, where the paper used sorted units. Hand position decoding and several dimensionality and control analyses held only in part or not at all.
