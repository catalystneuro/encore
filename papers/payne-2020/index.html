Payne et al. 2020

Recording of Electrically Evoked Neural Activity and Bladder Pressure Responses in Awake Rats Chronically Implanted With a Pelvic Nerve Array

Outcomes of 31 claims

  • ✓9 reproduced
  • ◐1 partially reproduced

Summary: Evoked compound action potentials were recorded in all five anaesthetised rats, and the awake voiding parameters of Table 1 held, but the latencies, conduction velocities and threshold order of the three fibre populations did not. Those results rest on peak-picking rules the paper does not describe, and its 0.05 uV threshold level lies within the noise of most files. Impedance rose as reported to 2 weeks, while the awake latencies did not reproduce and the week-1 threshold rise could not be tested, as no recording is linked to a rat. Stimulation-evoked voiding could not be tested, since no file records urine release and trains exist for four of eight rats. No histology or c-Fos data are deposited.

Notes on the paper

What the attempts found about the paper itself: a method or rule it does not give, a place where it disagrees with itself, published code that does something else. What they found about the data is on each dataset’s page.

  • Methods not availableThe paper does not say how the three ECAP peaks were identified in each recording (which local maxima count, from what time after the artefact, at which current), nor where latency is measured from or at which current. Question for the authors, how were P1, P2 and P3 picked, and at which stimulus level and time origin were their latencies read? This attempt ran four rules (first three peaks, three largest, the triple inside the paper's ranges, first three of the inverted traces); under the first, the anaesthetised medians are 1.60, 2.50 and 4.19 ms, and no rule gives the printed latencies.
  • Methods not availableThe ECAP threshold is defined as a response of at least 0.05 uV in both recordings, which lies below three noise SDs in most deposited files (noise SD 0.02 to 0.7 uV), and the paper does not say how the response amplitude was measured. Question for the authors, was the amplitude measured from baseline or peak to trough, was the 0.05 uV level applied to the deposited traces in their units, and is Dunn's P (0.034) adjusted for multiple comparisons?
  • Methods not availableThe paper does not define the colonic pressure increase or a response, and the deposited trains differ from the Methods. Question for the authors, which rat and which 13 responses give 2.6 mmHg, and how was the increase measured?
