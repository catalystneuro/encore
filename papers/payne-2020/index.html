    How it will be judged

    Threshold per rat and peak is the lowest stimulus current giving a response amplitude of at least 0.05 uV in both of the two recordings (Methods, ECAP and Impedance Testing). Open, and named here. The amplitude is (a) the positive peak voltage relative to the pre-stimulus baseline, or (b) peak to the preceding trough. Reproduced when a Friedman test over P1, P2 and P3 with rat as block gives P < 0.05 and the central values rise from P1 to P3; the three values are also checked against tolerance under each combination of readings, and all are shown.
