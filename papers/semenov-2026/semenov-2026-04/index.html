    How the test differed from the paper’s method

    • The pulse is given in volts; the factor to V/cm is assumed to be 5.6 V/cm per V (1400 V/cm at 250 V, the top mapping). A two-dimensional model of the electrodes of Methods 2.2 (rods 0.5 mm in diameter, 1.6 mm apart) gives 4.64 to 7.19 V/cm per V at the midpoint, depending on whether 1.6 mm is the gap or the centre distance. The bottom mapping (43 V/cm at 25 V, 1.72 V/cm per V) matches the paper's dye sensitivities, which fig3-dye-sensitivity tests, and is run as a variant. Every criterion is also run over factors from 1.5 to 8 V/cm per V.
    • Each field of view holds 3 to 6 cells; the paper used one cell per experiment and does not say which. Experiment means (adopted), the cell nearest the image centre and all cells are run.
    • The membrane trace is found automatically (peak of the baseline image along rays from the cell centre, 6 samples 1 pixel apart), not drawn by hand; 360 points at equal angles stand for the resampling along the perimeter (Methods 2.3). In some cells the trace is drawn inwards by bright spots inside the cell, and one object in c7 is two touching cells; a trace that follows the cell edge is run as a variant over every criterion and changes no result at the adopted factor.
    • Pixel size 0.267 um (16-um camera pixels over the 60x objective of Methods 2.3) is assumed; the files give none. The mean radius is 6.7 um with the peak trace and 9.9 um with the edge trace, against the about 8 um the paper uses in Eq. (2).
    • Frame 0 and other dark frames (laser misfires) are left out, so most baselines have 39 images.
    • Background subtraction (median of cell-free pixels of the same image) is adopted; the paper states it only for Ca2+ imaging (Methods 2.4). No subtraction and the camera offset alone are run as variants.

Reproduction Membrane charging measured with FluoVolt and Di-8-ANEPPS (Semenov et al. 2026)

Tests the findings of Semenov, Silkuniene, Silkunas, Pakhomova, Bixler, Ibey, Kochunov and Pakhomov (2026) on dandiset 001518, which holds ten escalations of 1-us pulses from 25 to 250 V, five with each dye, imaged by pulsed laser microscopy. Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • The pulse is given in volts; the factor to V/cm is assumed to be 5.6 V/cm per V (1400 V/cm at 250 V, the top mapping). A two-dimensional model of the electrodes of Methods 2.2 (rods 0.5 mm in diameter, 1.6 mm apart) gives 4.64 to 7.19 V/cm per V at the midpoint, depending on whether 1.6 mm is the gap or the centre distance. The bottom mapping (43 V/cm at 25 V, 1.72 V/cm per V) matches the paper's dye sensitivities, which fig3-dye-sensitivity tests, and is run as a variant. Every criterion is also run over factors from 1.5 to 8 V/cm per V.fig3-dye-sensitivity
  • Each field of view holds 3 to 6 cells; the paper used one cell per experiment and does not say which. Experiment means (adopted), the cell nearest the image centre and all cells are run.fig3-dye-sensitivity
  • The membrane trace is found automatically (peak of the baseline image along rays from the cell centre, 6 samples 1 pixel apart), not drawn by hand; 360 points at equal angles stand for the resampling along the perimeter (Methods 2.3). In some cells the trace is drawn inwards by bright spots inside the cell, and one object in c7 is two touching cells; a trace that follows the cell edge is run as a variant over every criterion and changes no result at the adopted factor.fig3-dye-sensitivity
  • Pixel size 0.267 um (16-um camera pixels over the 60x objective of Methods 2.3) is assumed; the files give none. The mean radius is 6.7 um with the peak trace and 9.9 um with the edge trace, against the about 8 um the paper uses in Eq. (2).fig3-dye-sensitivity
  • Frame 0 and other dark frames (laser misfires) are left out, so most baselines have 39 images.fig3-dye-sensitivity
  • Background subtraction (median of cell-free pixels of the same image) is adopted; the paper states it only for Ca2+ imaging (Methods 2.4). No subtraction and the camera offset alone are run as variants.fig3-dye-sensitivity
  • Points, line and readings as in fig3-dye-sensitivity (adopted reading); LOWESS with delta of 1% of the IMPC range, a standard speed-up.fig3-fluovolt-nonlinear
  • The fit through the origin keeps the uniform bleaching offset of each profile, as the paper reports bleaching in its own Di-8-ANEPPS dF (Results 3.2, paragraph 3); removing it is run as a variant.fig3-fluovolt-nonlinear
  • The mean of per-image dF over the pulse images equals the collapsed-image dF, as both use the same F0; the Fig. 2 and Fig. 4 measures coincide.fig2a-fluovolt-saturation
  • FluoVolt n is 5 experiments here, against 4 in the paper.fig2a-fluovolt-saturation
  • The sets are those nearest the paper's levels; all 19 sets are shown as a variant.fig2a-fluovolt-stable
  • Under the top mapping the 43 V/cm level has no set, so the steps are tested from 150 V/cm.fig2b-di8-climbs-to-570
  • The text does not name the pole; reading (c), the cathodic pole, is assumed, as the paper places the damage at the cathodic pole (Results 3.1, the cathodic pole dims at 780 V/cm; Results 3.4, the reduction is more pronounced at the cathodic pole).
