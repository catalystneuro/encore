Most of this paper's behavioural and decoding findings that could be tested held up in this data, but the neuron-class counts did not reproduce, and several central tests could not be run. The licking increase without sound, PPC's larger share of goal-distance neurons, ramping activity in PPC after-reaching neurons, and lower decoder uncertainty with sound in PPC reproduced. The neuron-class counts (12 to 41% above the paper's for most groups), time to reward (9.3 s against 7.4 s) and the log-normal decoder's advantage did not reproduce. The muscimol experiments could not be tested because the dandiset lacks those mice. Findings that depend on the unstated baseline of the task-relevance test, or on the chance level of the decoder, were blocked because the readings disagree.

Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • The trial start is taken as the first frame of each trial record, where sound_on_off marks the start tone; the paper does not say from which event its 7.4 s is measured.behaviour-timing
  • The files hold a lick frequency (1/inter-lick interval, capped at 10 Hz, held between licks), not lick times. The first lick is the first frame beyond 33 cm from the start where this frequency changes to a non-zero value or stays at the cap. Two variants are shown, the first frame of non-zero frequency beyond 33 cm and the first rise from 0 Hz beyond 33 cm.lick-goal-correlation
  • Lick counts per bin are the integral of the lick frequency over the frames in the bin (the files hold no lick times). Bin edges are multiples of 4.2 cm from the goal, as the criterion says; the bins are labelled 29.4-25.2 cm where the claims file writes 29.3-25.1.fig1c-licking-without-sound
  • Lick frequency is the files' lick_rate series. Both readings of O9 for speed and for zones are run.supp1-licking-speed
  • k-means (two clusters) is run once on the after-reaching group pooled over all sessions, with seed 0 and 20 starts; the paper does not say whether it clustered per session. A per-session variant is shown, for the counts and the supp8 claims.neuron-classes
  • The inter-trial interval of baseline O1 b is the last 7 s of each trial record, from the Methods' "The inter-trial interval (ITI) was 7 s"; the records run 7.9 to 8.2 s past the reward.neuron-classes
  • Distance-aligned activity is interpolated at 0.42 cm bin centres between the frames from the start to the goal (a standard choice; most 0.42 cm bins hold no frame at the mice's speeds). Moving averages are 15 frames (500 ms) and 20 bins (8.4 cm).neuron-classes
  • Distance preference is the goal distance of the peak of the smoothed distance-aligned average over 67.2 to 0 cm (O4).fig2d-layer-distance-preference
  • The free width is profiled over a grid of 25 log-spaced values from 0.5 to 200 (cm or dB), with the linear coefficients fitted by least squares at each, and refined by a bounded scalar optimizer of the log width between the neighbouring grid values, in place of an optimizer from four initial values (Online Methods, Regression analysis). The basis centre is the value of the variable at the peak of the neuron's activity averaged in 0.42 cm (1 dB for Sound) bins and smoothed over 20 bins (3 for Sound).supp6-distance-not-sound
  • Sound intensity follows the Methods (100 dB SPL at 25 cm, 6 dB lower per doubling); Step holds the goal distance on entering each no-sound zone. Zones are computed from the condition and distance, as the files hold no task sound.supp6-distance-not-sound
  • The fit is the distance-only set of equation (2), y = b0 + b1 phi(distance), with the width profiled over a grid as in supp6-distance-not-sound.tuning-similar-ppc-pm
  • Both trace readings of the claims file are run, the 0.42 cm bins and the 8.37 cm moving average.supp8a-pm-no-sound
  • Distance preference per condition is the peak of the condition's trial-averaged 0.42 cm trace after the 8.4 cm moving average.supp9-preserved-representation
  • Decoder details from the claims file and Methods; the positive shift (O7) is run at 0.01 and 0.1. Frames are those from the start to the goal with goal distance 67.2 to 0 cm. Both chance readings of the claims file are run.fig4-above-chance
  • 'Closer' compares each area's median of per-trial mean MAP with the bin centre.fig4d-map-near-goal
  • Frames within 33 cm of the start, which always have sound, are left out of zones that are silent in their condition.fig5a-map-decreases-without-sound
  • The Gaussian decoder uses raw activity with per-bin mean and s.d., smoothed and resampled as for the log-normal (claims file).lognormal-decoder-better
