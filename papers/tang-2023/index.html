Tang et al. 2023 · Cell Reports

Geometric transformation of cognitive maps for generalization across hippocampal-prefrontal circuits

doi:10.1016/j.celrep.2023.112246 · paper key tang-2023

Datasets

  • DANDI:000447

The central finding that PFC maps generalize across rooms while CA1 remaps held up in this data, as did the manifold distance relations in the UMAP analysis. Decoding held only in part: CA1 error was higher in the familiar room but worse than the shuffle, and PFC error differed between rooms. The CCGP abstraction results could not be settled, because the paper's smoothing width is garbled and the outcomes change with it. The faster-learning results could not be tested because the de novo sessions are not in the dandiset, and familiar above novel performance did not reproduce (p = 0.383). Minor ramping-cell results did not reproduce either.
