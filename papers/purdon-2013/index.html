Purdon et al. 2013 · Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences

Electroencephalogram signatures of loss and recovery of consciousness from propofol

doi:10.1073/pnas.1221180110 · paper key purdon-2013

Datasets

  • DANDI:001285

None of the paper's main findings could be tested in this data, because the dandiset holds the EEG of one of the ten volunteers and none of the task data. The loss of response to clicks before verbal stimuli, the timing of unconsciousness, the beta and gamma rise before it, the frontal alpha mode and the phase-amplitude shift all need the button-press responses, which are not deposited. The 64 channels also lack names and positions, so spatial results cannot be built. All 22 claims were blocked, and none reproduced or failed. The one subject's band-power course tests no claim.

  • ⊘Blocked In all 10 subjects, during induction, the probability of response to clicks was below that to verbal stimuli in the transition between EON and LOC.
  • ⊘Blocked The transition from EON to LOC took a median of 23.9 min across subjects (range 6.7 to 32.8 min, quartiles 16.4 and 28.6 min).
  • ⊘Blocked At the group level, aligned at LOC, Pclicks was below Pverbal during the transition to LOC, and the difference grew as subjects approached LOC.
  • ⊘Blocked During emergence, at the group level aligned at ROC, Pclicks was below Pverbal, while individual curves were more variable.
  • ⊘Blocked During emergence 6 of 10 subjects did not reach EOFF, as Pclicks did not stay above 0.95 for 5 min or longer.
  • ⊘Blocked The transition from ROC to EOFF (or the end of the recording when EOFF was not reached) took a median of 25.5 min (range 4.9 to 47.6 min, quartiles 12.8 and 38.0 min).
  • ⊘Blocked Gamma and beta power rose significantly above baseline in the 30 min before LOC and stayed elevated during unconsciousness.
  • ⊘Blocked At LOC, alpha (8 to 12 Hz) and low-frequency (0.1 to 1 Hz) power both increased significantly.
  • ⊘Blocked Fifteen minutes after LOC, the alpha power increase was concentrated in frontal channels and the low-frequency increase was spread broadly over temporal and parietal channels.
  • ⊘Blocked During emergence the changes reversed with a similar time course, frontal alpha and low-frequency power decreasing at ROC while gamma and beta power stayed elevated after ROC.
  • ⊘Blocked Low-frequency power increased more than 10-fold in the unconscious state compared with baseline.
  • ⊘Blocked In the transition before LOC, the median frequency of the frontal spectrum fell from 23.1 to 12.0 Hz and its bandwidth (interquartile range) from 17.4 to 9.1 Hz.
  • ⊘Blocked In the transition after ROC, the median frequency rose from 11.8 to 21.9 Hz and the bandwidth from 9.9 to 12.8 Hz.
  • ⊘Blocked Power at the median frequency had a frontal distribution at each behavioral time point.
  • ⊘Blocked Gamma and beta power were frontal from Pre-LOC through LOC; during unconsciousness the frontal power moved into the beta and alpha bands; during emergence it shifted back toward beta and gamma, and frontal gamma power persisted through EOFF.
  • ⊘Blocked At baseline the principal mode of the alpha band is spatially coherent over occipital channels; its modal projection dissipates at LOC and returns at ROC, remaining prominent after ROC.
  • ⊘Blocked During unconsciousness the principal alpha mode (about 10 Hz) is spatially coherent over frontal channels; its modal projection begins after LOC and ceases at ROC.
  • ⊘Blocked Low frequencies showed no prominent principal mode, although low-frequency power rose more than 10-fold in unconsciousness.
  • ⊘Blocked From about 20 min before LOC to about 10 min after it, alpha/beta (8 to 14 Hz) amplitude was largest at the troughs of the low-frequency (0.1 to 1 Hz) oscillation (trough-max), significant in 9 of 10 subjects within 10 min of LOC.
  • ⊘Blocked During profound unconsciousness, 10 to 40 min after LOC, the modulation shifted by 180 degrees, alpha/beta amplitude being largest at low-frequency peaks (peak-max), significant in 8 of 10 subjects.
  • ⊘Blocked During emergence, the modulation reverted to trough-max about 15 min before ROC, significant in 9 of 10 subjects.
  • ⊘Blocked The low-frequency phase modulates a broad range of higher frequencies, from theta through gamma, in both the trough-max and the peak-max regimes.
