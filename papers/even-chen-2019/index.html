Even Chen et al. 2019 · PLOS Computational Biology

Structure and variability of delay activity in premotor cortex

doi:10.1371/journal.pcbi.1006808 · paper key even-chen-2019

Datasets

  • DANDI:000121

Some of the paper's main findings on premotor delay activity hold in this data and others do not. Variability ellipses align with the condition mean, on-axis and off-axis variability predict reach kinematics, and distance is decodable after removing speed. The plane's variance shares, the classifier accuracies and the distance-independent arc error did not reproduce, nor did the speed and distance projection result. Only 5 of monkey J's 16 sessions are in the dandiset, so J's results rest on partial data. The files also swap R's horizontal and vertical target configurations relative to the Methods.

  • ◐Partially reproduced The sessions are those the Methods list for each target configuration; for monkey R, ring on 2017-01-24 and 25, horizontal on 2017-01-17 and 18, vertical on 2017-01-15 and 16, and 3-rings on 2017-01-19; for J, ring, vertical and 3-rings on the dates listed.
  • ◐Partially reproduced Monkey R performed 3577 ring trials over two sessions, 3699 horizontal over two, 4040 vertical over two, and 912 3-rings trials in one session.

  • ◐Partially reproduced Delays were uniform from 300 to 700 ms for J and 400 to 900 ms for R, 10% of trials had no delay; average reaction time was 346 ms (J) and 311 ms (R), average reach time 582 ms (J) and 482 ms (R), and reaction time was negatively correlated with delay (p < 1e-4 for both).
  • ✓Reproduced Of the 96 PMd electrodes, 74 for J and 80 for R had activity significantly correlated with the x and y location of the target (p < .05, Bonferroni over 96).
  • ?Not reproduced The top six PCs of the condition-averaged delay activity explain cumulatively 58, 82, 88, 92, 94 and 95% of the variance for J (120 conditions) and 81, 89, 92, 94, 95 and 96% for R (132 conditions).
  • ?Not reproduced The spatial plane captures a large part of the variance of the condition-averaged activity for the ring, horizontal, vertical and 3-rings tasks; 66.8, 64.4, 47.7 and 68.2% for J and 33.7, 30.7, 31.0 and 30.1% for R (p < 1e-4).
  • ◐Partially reproduced After target onset the neural state in the spatial plane starts at the center, diverges after 50 ms (R 75 ms) and converges to the steady delay state after 300 ms (R 400 ms).
  • ✓Reproduced On single trials, on-axis and off-axis deviations of the neural state in the spatial plane are positively correlated with the on-axis and off-axis deviations of the hand position at maximum speed (on-axis p = 1e-3 J, 4e-3 R; off-axis p = 4e-4 J, 2e-4 R).
  • ◐Partially reproduced The speed axis of delay activity varies with maximum speed for reaches to the same target distance, for 11 of 12 distances in J and 11 of 12 in R (p < 0.05), so it encodes speed independently of distance.
  • ✓Reproduced After regressing out speed from the neural activity, a positive correlation with target distance remains (p < 1e-4 for J and R).
  • ?Not reproduced Projected onto the top PCs, the distance dimension is about half as large as the speed dimension.
  • ?Not reproduced Across all single trials the speed dimension explains 2% (J) and 1% (R) of the variance of the neural state, and the spatial plane 20% (J) and 10% (R).
  • ✓Reproduced The distributions of single-trial neural states for each condition are elliptical, and for most conditions their major axis points along the direction of the mean neural state from the origin.
  • ◐Partially reproduced The mean absolute on-axis (distance) deviation of the neural state is larger than the off-axis (direction) deviation, in each task (J p = 9e-7 ring, 4e-7 horizontal, 1e-8 vertical, 3e-14 3-rings; R 8e-3, 4e-7, 2e-7 and 0.19).
