Summary: The main findings tested on this data held up only in part. Structural connectivity from unsegregated or reciprocal synapses matched functional connectivity better than random synapses, and walking activated LAL, FB, IPS and GNG, but walking suppression of WED and AVLP and the link to cholinergic output did not appear. FlyEM matched better than FlyWire, though both correlations fell below the paper's, most likely because this attempt did not apply the motion correction the paper uses, whose settings it does not give. The findings on preprocessing and ROI number could not be tested, as the paper's ROI atlases are not published. Several connectome-only findings held in the paper's deposited tables.

Method differences

How the tests differed from the paper’s method or data, and the claims each applies to.

  • Registration (claims file O2). The paper gives no SPM12 or ANTs settings. No SPM12 motion or slice-timing correction was applied (claims file M1 requires it), so the data are uncorrected. The raw series moves: a rigid in-plane shift estimated per volume from the tdTomato channel has a median of 0.6 to 3.2 um and a 95th percentile of 2.8 to 6.1 um per fly, against an in-plane voxel of about 2.6 um (motion cell). Regressing the two shift series out moves the fig3d values by 0.005; that check does not stand in for a realignment and was not applied to the other claims. The mean of three blocks of 152 green volumes was registered to the resized FDA template with antspy (affine start, the better of mean squares and Mattes mutual information, then SyN with defaults), and the ROI means were computed through the registration's linear interpolation map, which equals warping each volume and averaging (extractROItimeseries.m). The registration is seeded through ants.config (antspy 0.6.3 ignores the random_seed argument that the earlier runs passed, so they were unseeded) with ITK on one thread, and it now repeats exactly; two runs of the notebook gave identical output. It is unstable across seeds: a registration with seed 11 moved native voxels by a median of 2.5 to 44 um per fly, and its ROI series correlate with the first at a median of 0.18 to 0.98 (data check).
