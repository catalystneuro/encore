  • ✓Reproduced The RP mutations are all missense, dominant in IMPG1 and recessive (compound heterozygous) in IMPG2; all VMD mutations are dominant (heterozygous), either missense or nonsense.
  • ✓Reproduced BCVA (OD and OS) is F32 20/25 and 20/25; M83 20/70 and 20/30; M75 20/40 and 20/40; M82 20/30 and 20/25; M73 20/100 and 20/50; F57 20/20 and 20/20; M34 20/20 and 20/20.
