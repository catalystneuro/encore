Summary: The paper's main findings held up in this data: spatial attention in DLPFC, stimulus identity and attended value in OFC, and accept/reject and left/right value in ACC at cue 1, the OFC comparison of attended against stored value, and the ACC belief-confirmation signal that predicts reaction time. Some held under only a few of the readings tried, such as the ACC action ramp and OFC leading in value-difference neurons. Figure 8's action-value neurons in ACC and DLPFC pass their tests, but their fractions do not both fall near the paper's 18%, a gap the attempt puts down to permutation noise. Among minor claims, the supplementary latency order and equal cue weights in monkey M did not reproduce.
