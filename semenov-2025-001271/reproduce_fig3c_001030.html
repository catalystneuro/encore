Method details fixed from the paper, used by the Figure S2 criteria.
(F1) F is the mean emission over the entire cell soma per frame, F0 the mean over an interval
before the stimulus, dF = 100% (F - F0)/F0 (Methods, Recording of the optical APs). FluoVolt
emission rises with depolarization, so traces are not inverted.
(F2) Traces are not filtered or modified in any way (Methods); no detrending or smoothing.
(F3) The stimulus is a 250-ms, 2-kHz unmodulated sine wave (Figure S2 legend), starting 50 ms into
the session (Methods); the onset, the field and the solution of each session are taken from the
dataset, and the 50 ms is used when the onset is not given there.
(F4) Sessions after the change are those about 5 min after the solution was replaced by TTX or
vehicle (Figure S2 legend); the solution of each session is taken from the dataset.
(F5) The maximum firing rate of a session is the inverse of the peak-to-peak interval between its
first two APs (Methods).
(F6) The two-sided Student's t test, paired or unpaired as appropriate, at p < 0.05, is the paper's
test where a criterion needs one (Methods, Quantification and statistical analysis). The paper
reports no test for Figure S2.

Details the paper leaves open, with the readings named. Every Figure S2 criterion runs every
combination of the readings below; a claim is decided when all give its outcome, and blocked as
methods_underspecified when they give different outcomes.
(O1) The soma outline is not given. ROIs are taken from the dataset when it holds them. When it
does not, readings (a) the connected component nearest the image centre (the Methods centre the
neuron) of an Otsu threshold of the mean pre-stimulus frame, (b) that mask eroded by 2 pixels, to
leave out the neurite bases at its edge. Otsu and the 2 pixels are standard choices.
(O2) The length of the F0 interval is not given ("a time interval preceding the electrical
stimulation"); readings (a) all frames before the onset (about 50 ms), (b) the last 20 ms before
the onset. This is unlikely to change an outcome.
(O3) AP detection is given only as "brief emission upticks", in neurons selected for APs above 5%
dF lasting about 10 ms (typical 11.3% and 8 ms). An AP is a local maximum of dF with width at half
height between 2 and 20 ms ("about 10 ms"; the bounds are a standard choice) and at least 5 ms
from a larger maximum (standard choice; the paper's highest rates, 60 to 90 Hz, give intervals
over 11 ms). Readings of the height (a) above 5% dF (the Methods' selection level), (b) above 5 SD
of dF before the onset. After TTX, (a) and (b) are applied with the level of the same neuron's
sessions before the change, so that a drop of the baseline noise cannot create APs.
(O4) "Sustained depolarization" is not defined by a measure. It is no AP in the last 100 ms of the
stimulus while the mean dF over that window is above, reading (a) 5 SD of dF before the onset,
(b) half the median AP height of the neuron before the change. The 100-ms window is a standard
choice. "Sustained firing" is, as a standard choice, at least three APs with one in the last 50 ms
of the stimulus.
(O5) Which session pairs are compared before and after the change is not stated beyond the
legend's "recorded first ... and again"; readings (a) every field tested both before and after,
(b) only the highest field tested both before and after.

fig3c-sustained-firing-2s¶

In the low-conductivity solution, a 2-s unmodulated 2-kHz sine wave ramped over 250 ms evoked firing for its whole duration, rising with field until replaced by a firing block with sustained depolarization.

Criterion: traces corrected for bleaching by subtracting a trace without APs (Methods), AP readings of the header. Reproduced when an intermediate field has APs in the last 250 ms of the stimulus, the AP count rises with field up to some field, and the highest field shows sustained depolarization (O4, over the last 250 ms of the stimulus).

Details taken here, each a choice the criterion leaves open:

  • The trace without APs is the neuron's own session with no AP under the reading. Only cell c7 has one (1.0 V). For the others, readings: (a) no correction, (b) subtract the dataset's only AP-free trace, c7 at 1.0 V. The reference session is not itself judged.
  • Subtracting one raw trace adds its frame noise, which raises the pre-onset SD by about 1.4 times and with it the 5-SD levels. Reading (c) subtracts the reference after a 101-ms running median, which keeps the slow bleaching and drops the noise and any single AP: the cell's own AP-free session, or else c7's lowest field (1.0 V), used for c7 as well when its 1.0-V session has one AP.
  • "Rises with field up to some field": the AP count at the field with the most APs is larger than at the lowest field, and that field is not the lowest (a standard choice).
  • Which neurons: Figure 3C shows one neuron. Readings: (a) at least one neuron meets all three parts, (b) every neuron with at least three fields does.
  • O4(b) uses the median AP height of all the neuron's sessions (there is no change of solution).
  • APs are counted during the stimulus (25 to 2,025 ms, or the end of the recording).

def roi_trace(d, roi):
    on = int(round(ONSET_S * 3134.8))
    m = sw.soma_masks(d[1:on].astype(float).mean(0))[roi]
    if not m.any():
        return None
    tr = d[:, m].astype(float).mean(1)
    tr[0] = np.nan
    tr[-1] = np.nan
    return tr


def run(roi, f0r, apr, sdr, bleach):
    out = []
    xs = {}
    for i, r in S.iterrows():
        tr = roi_trace(r["data"], roi)
        if tr is None:
            continue
        rate = r["rate"]
        on = int(round(ONSET_S * rate))
        lo = 1 if f0r == "all" else on - int(round(0.020 * rate))
        f0 = np.nanmean(tr[lo:on])
        xs[i] = (100 * (tr - f0) / f0, lo, on)
    # APs on uncorrected traces first, to find each cell's AP-free session
    raw_aps = {}
    for i, (x, lo, on) in xs.items():
        r = S.loc[i]
        off = min(int(round((ONSET_S + STIM_S) * r["rate"])), len(x) - 1)
        sd = np.nanstd(x[lo:on], ddof=1)
        lvl = 5.0 if apr == "5pct" else 5 * sd
        raw_aps[i] = len(sw.detect_aps(x, r["rate"], lvl, on, off))
    ref = {}
    for cell, g in S.groupby("cell"):
        free = [i for i in g.index if i in xs and raw_aps[i] == 0]
        ref[cell] = min(free, key=lambda i: S.loc[i, "volt"]) if free else None
    c7_ref = ref.get("c7")
    c7_low = min((i for i in xs if S.loc[i, "cell"] == "c7"), key=lambda i: S.loc[i, "volt"], default=None)
    for i, (x, lo, on) in xs.items():
        r = S.loc[i]
        rate = r["rate"]
        if bleach == "smooth":
            use = ref[r["cell"]] if ref[r["cell"]] is not None else c7_low
        else:
            use = ref[r["cell"]] if ref[r["cell"]] is not None else (c7_ref if bleach == "c7" else None)
        if use is not None and use != i and use in xs:
            xr = xs[use][0]
            if bleach == "smooth":
                # bleaching is slow: a 101-ms running median keeps it and drops the frame noise and any single AP
                xr = pd.Series(xr).rolling(int(0.101 * S.loc[use, "rate"]) | 1, center=True, min_periods=1).median().to_numpy()
            n = min(len(x), len(xr))
            y = x[:n] - xr[:n]
        else:
            y = x.copy()
        off = min(int(round((ONSET_S + STIM_S) * rate)), len(y) - 1)
        w0 = int(round((ONSET_S + STIM_S - 0.25) * rate))
        pre_sd = np.nanstd(y[lo:on], ddof=1)
        lvl = 5.0 if apr == "5pct" else 5 * pre_sd
        aps = sw.detect_aps(y, rate, lvl, on, off)
        out.append(dict(i=i, cell=r["cell"], volt=r["volt"], is_ref=(use == i) or (ref[r["cell"]] == i),
                        corrected_by=None if use is None or use == i else f"{S.loc[use, 'cell']} {S.loc[use, 'volt']:g} V",
                        n_ap=len(aps), ap_last250=int((aps["frame"] >= w0).sum()),
                        last250_mean=float(np.nanmean(y[w0:off])), pre_sd=pre_sd,
                        peaks=aps["peak"].to_numpy(), truncated=off < int(round((ONSET_S + STIM_S) * rate)), y=y))
    o = pd.DataFrame(out)
    med_h = o.groupby("cell")["peaks"].apply(lambda v: np.median(np.concatenate(list(v))) if sum(map(len, v)) else np.nan)
    o["sd_level"] = 5 * o["pre_sd"] if sdr == "5sd" else 0.5 * o["cell"].map(med_h)
    o["sustained"] = (o["ap_last250"] == 0) & (o["last250_mean"] > o["sd_level"])
    return o


def judge(o):
    rows = []
    for cell, g in o[~o["is_ref"]].groupby("cell"):
        g = g.sort_values("volt")
        if len(g) < 2:
            rows.append(dict(cell=cell, fields=len(g), inter_ap_last250=False, rises=False, top_sd=False))
            continue
        inter = g.iloc[1:-1] if len(g) >= 3 else g.iloc[0:0]
        peak_i = g["n_ap"].to_numpy().argmax()
        rows.append(dict(cell=cell, fields=len(g), inter_ap_last250=bool((inter["ap_last250"] > 0).any()),
                         rises=bool(peak_i > 0 and g["n_ap"].iloc[peak_i] > g["n_ap"].iloc[0]),
                         top_sd=bool(g["sustained"].iloc[-1])))
    j = pd.DataFrame(rows)
    j["all3"] = j["inter_ap_last250"] & j["rises"] & j["top_sd"]
    return j


res, store = [], {}
for roi in ("otsu", "otsu_eroded", "frame"):
    for f0r in ("all", "last20"):
        for apr in ("5pct", "5sd"):
            for sdr in ("5sd", "half_ap"):
                for bleach in ("none", "c7", "smooth"):
                    o = run(roi, f0r, apr, sdr, bleach)
                    j = judge(o)
                    store[(roi, f0r, apr, sdr, bleach)] = (o, j)
                    elig = j[j["fields"] >= 3]
