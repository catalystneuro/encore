Description of one session:
 2 kHz stimulation of hippocampal neurons under 1.25V amplitude (measured as one polarity, V; peak-to-peak would be two-fold larger). Sine wave amplitude is gradually increased during the initial 0.25 s, then stays constant at the indicated voltage. Sine wave stimulation begins at 25 ms and lasts 2 s. For the employed electrode configuration, 1V translates into 36 V/cm. All experiments were performed in a low-conductance physiological solution containing (in mM): 140 NaGluconate, 5.4 KCl, 2 CaCl2, 1.5 MgCl2, 10 D-glucose, and 10 HEPES (pH 7.3, 290-300 mOsm/kg, 0.8 S/m).

The 17 sessions come from four cells (c5 to c8), each at several amplitudes; two sessions of cell c5 stop at 1.91 s, before the 2.025-s end of the stimulus. The files give the species as Mus musculus, where the paper used rat neurons, and the images are 16 x 16 pixels. The first frame is several times brighter than the others in every file, and the next frames recover from it over a few ms; the first 20 frames (6.4 ms) are dropped. Amplitudes are in volts (one polarity), 36 V/cm per volt by the description.

Clock. Each file is one image series with no other stream; the stimulus timing (25 ms to 2.025 s) comes from the description. The check below shows the response starts with the stimulus: the AP counts (reading b) in the 25 ms before and the 25 ms after the stated onset.

fig3c-sustained-firing-2s¶

In the low-conductivity solution, a 2-s unmodulated 2-kHz sine wave ramped over 250 ms evoked firing for its whole duration, rising with field until replaced by a firing block with sustained depolarization.

Criterion: traces corrected for bleaching by subtracting a trace without APs (Methods); AP detection readings (a) and (b). Reproduced when an intermediate field has APs in the last 250 ms of the stimulus, the AP count rises with field up to some field, and the highest field has no APs after 250 ms with mean dF above F0 by more than 5% over the rest of the stimulus. The criterion does not say whether it applies per cell; the field series of each cell is judged on its own, as Figure 3C shows one cell, and every cell is reported.

def judge(sub, r):
    sub = sub.sort_values("amplitude_v")
    counts = sub[f"{r}_after_ramp"].to_numpy()
    top = sub.iloc[-1]
    intermediate = sub.iloc[1:-1]
    late = bool((intermediate[f"{r}_last_250ms"] > 0).any())
    k = int(np.argmax(counts))
    rises = k > 0 and bool(np.all(np.diff(counts[:k + 1]) >= 0))
    block = top[f"{r}_after_ramp"] == 0 and top.mean_dF_after_ramp > 5
    return {"late_firing_at_intermediate_field": late, "count_rises_to_a_field": rises,
            "block_at_highest_field": bool(block), "holds": late and rises and bool(block),
            "highest_field_aps_after_ramp": int(top[f"{r}_after_ramp"]),
            "highest_field_mean_dF": round(float(top.mean_dF_after_ramp), 2)}

Result: partially reproduced. With the revised detector and the bleaching correction, in all four cells under both readings an intermediate field fires in the last 250 ms of the stimulus and the AP count after the ramp rises with field. Cell c7 meets the whole criterion under both readings: at 2.3 V it has no AP after the ramp and a corrected mean dF of 6.66%. Cells c5 and c8 stop firing at their highest field (1 and 0 APs after the ramp under reading (a), 0 under (b)), but their mean dF there is 3.48% and 3.63%, below the 5% level. Cell c6 keeps firing at its highest field, 1.45 V (32 APs after the ramp). So the criterion holds in 1 of 4 cells. The criterion does not say how cells are combined. Figure 3C shows one neuron, which the Results present as typical ("we observed sustained firing for the entire duration"), so this attempt assumes the claim is reproduced when the criterion holds in most cells, partially reproduced when it holds in at least one or its firing parts hold in most, and not reproduced otherwise. Judged on any one cell it would be reproduced (c7); judged on most cells it is not. With the first run's detector it holds in none: reading (a) counts 197 noise peaks on the c7 plateau and reading (b) one, at 1,707 ms. Without the bleaching correction it holds in no cell under either detector, as the corrected mean dF of c7 at 2.3 V, 6.66%, is 3.30% uncorrected.

Claim Outcome
fig3c-sustained-firing-2s partially reproduced: firing rising with field in all four cells; the block with more than 5% depolarization in one (c7), a block with less depolarization in two

A failure to reproduce here may be a failure of this attempt, not of the paper: see the reasoning and the differences from the paper's method.

How the analysis differs from the paper's: F is the whole 16 x 16 frame, as the soma cannot be outlined at this resolution; the bleaching trace is the dataset's only session without APs (cell c7 at 1.0 V), used for every cell; the judgement is per cell, and the outcome is assumed to need most cells for reproduced and one cell, or the firing parts in most, for partially reproduced; AP detection on a 1-ms running mean, with the prominence of a reading (a) peak equal to the 5% level (standard choices).

What was found about the dataset: the species is given as mouse for neurons the paper describes as rat; the images are 16 x 16 pixels; two sessions end before the stimulus does; the first frame of every file is an artefact.
