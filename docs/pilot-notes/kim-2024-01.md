# 001544, Kim et al. 2024

Three claims reproduced (one on an assumption), none partially, four not
reproduced (one on an assumption) and 24 blocked, all for missing data. All 31
claims are on the dandiset. The first verification round agreed with 30 of the
31 outcomes as they stood and the second with all 31, and nothing was proposed
after the second, so there was no third round. It pins version 0.260827.1410,
the only published version; the paper cites none. The paper, Kim, Ananth, Desai,
Role and Talmage, "Distinct subpopulations of ventral pallidal cholinergic
projection neurons encode valence of olfactory stimuli" in Cell Reports, tests
mice for odor preference in a Y-maze with an appetitive (APP) and an aversive
(AV) odor, counts cFos and ChAT in the ventral pallidum, records its cholinergic
neurons by fiber photometry, inhibits them chemogenetically and labels the
neurons an odor activates and reactivates, and compares by whole-cell current
clamp APP-odor-activated neurons (12 cells from 6 mice), AV-odor-activated
neurons (8 cells from 4 mice) and home-cage neurons ("ALL", 21 cells from 8
mice), with their morphology from neurobiotin fills. The claims were written
from the NIH author manuscript (PMC11080946) without the supplement or the
figures, and from the paper's code (nsdesai/patch_clamp_analysis at v1.0.0,
commit 4ee1125, and rokim01/Fiber-Photometry-analysis at commit 49dcfd4); the
check made eleven corrections and left three points open. The seven claims on
the current-clamp recordings of Figure 6 and Figure S12 were tested. Reproduced
are `fig6f-max-firing-rate` (ALL 12.5, APP 50.5, AV 49.25 Hz, F(2, 41) = 10.83
against the paper's 10.71, both pairs holding in every cell set and under both
readings), `figs12-other-features-shared` (9 of the 10 features the Figure S12
legend names as similar show no difference, 7 of 10 without the `_FL` cells and
10 of 10 without the cells whose resistance changed by more than 20%) and
`figs12-app-differs-from-all` (input resistance, spike width, downstroke and f-I
slope all differ between APP and ALL under both readings), which rests on using
all 24 ALL cells, as without the changed cells input resistance does not differ
(p = 0.20 and 0.19). Not reproduced are `figs12a-sag-higher`, where AV does not
exceed ALL under either reading (p = 0.087, mixed model p = 0.24) and which also
rests on the cell set, `fig6g-av-more-hyperpolarized` (APP against AV p = 0.60,
the paper's t(18) = 2.72), measured under the holding current, so that it says
little about the paper's resting potential, `fig6h-av-smaller-ahp` (p = 0.68 for
reading (a), the opposite direction for reading (b)) and
`fig6-app-less-excitable` (latency 153.2 against 74.5 ms, p = 0.074, with
rheobase in the opposite direction). The other 24 claims are blocked because
the behaviour, immunohistochemistry counts, fiber photometry and
reconstructions are not in the dandiset. The author's first run, on 30 claims,
gave 2 reproduced, 1 partially, 3 not reproduced and 24 blocked, with
`figs12a-sag-higher` partially reproduced and `figs12-other-features-shared`
judged on 14 features (9 of 14 on all cells, 7 of 14 without the `_FL` cells,
so resting on an assumption). The author asked a reader whether that claim
should be judged on the ten features the Figure S12 legend, read from the
supplement after the claims were written, calls similar; the reader agreed,
amended the claim and moved the other four features to a new claim,
`figs12-app-differs-from-all`, and the author-answers run tested both. Round 1
disagreed with `figs12a-sag-higher`, which it would have called not
reproduced, and found that the Methods' switch to non-parametric tests when
normality fails was not applied, that four of the five sag responses clipped at
-105.6 mV were APP cells, that the mixed-model p-values used a normal reference
with 4 to 11 mice per group, that the membrane potential reading is taken under
the holding current, and that the paper's 21 ALL cells cannot come from the 24
by dropping whole mice; in reply the sag outcome moved to not reproduced, the
Mann-Whitney and Kruskal-Wallis results, contrasts against t on mice minus 3
df (the downstroke then marginal, p = 0.039), the code's saturation test, a
variant without the five clipped cells and a per-sweep table of the 20% change
rule (whose largest-departure reading would remove 30 of 44 cells) were added,
and a question on the normality rule was filed for a reader in
`questions/001544.yaml`. Round 2 agreed with all 31 and found statements the
outputs did not support (3 of 6 APP mice above every ALL mouse where it is 4 of
6, a single sample at -112.9 mV where the count was not printed, a
methods_unavailable finding out of date, a run time of about four minutes where
302 s was printed, and the rank tests used to decide the sag outcome while the
deviations say it follows readings (a) and (b)), all corrected, and two places
where the claims file misreads Figure S13, which became a `paper_inconsistency`
finding and two questions for a reader. The author's run took about 41 minutes
(01:21 to 02:02 UTC) and nothing failed; the notebook ran in about 200 s at
first, 187 s after the author-answers run, 137 s after round 1 and 345 and 348
s at the end. Untracked entries at the repository root (`.bashrc`, `scripts`
and others), which the author did not create, were left alone.

The dandiset holds only the current-clamp step recordings of Figure 6 and
Figure S12, 44 files; the behaviour, immunohistochemistry counts, fiber
photometry, chirp recordings and neuronal reconstructions are not in it, and the
paper's data statement says all data will be shared by the lead contact on
request, so 24 of the 31 claims cannot be tested. The ALL group holds 24 cells
from 11 mice where the Figure 6 legend reports 21 from 8, and nothing says which
were left out; no 8 of the 11 mice hold 21 cells (any 8 hold 14 to 20), while
the APP (12 cells, 6 mice) and AV (8 cells, 4 mice) groups match the legend. The
DC current that holds each cell near -65 mV is not recorded, `bias_current`
(1e-11 A) and `bridge_balance` (2e7 Ohm) hold the same value in all 44 files,
and no segment of any sweep is free of the holding current, so the resting
potential the Methods define cannot be obtained. Every
CurrentClampStimulusSeries is stated in amperes with conversion 1 but holds
values from -60 to 500, the step amplitudes in picoamperes. The six APP mice are
4 male and 2 female in the files where the legend says 3 and 3, and five ALL
mice (CG354, CG355, CG382, CG538, CG539; 9 cells) are 5.7 to 6.6 weeks old,
younger than the 8 to 12 weeks the STAR Methods give. Every sweep's
starting_time is 0, so the order in time and the 10 s between steps are not
recorded, and series resistance is not recorded, so that exclusion rule cannot
be checked; three files carry suffixes `_NSD` and `_FL` with no description, the
two CG538 files sharing recording number 003 are different cells (input
resistance 192 and 802 MOhm), and the `_FL` files use 5 s sweeps where all
others use 2 s. Five recordings (four APP, one ALL) reach -105.6 mV and stay
there for 10 ms or more during the -60 pA step, which truncates the response
and underestimates their sag, though one other APP file (20221214_001) goes
down to -112.88 mV; three sweeps hold NaN for their last 0.1 to 0.6 s. The
paper's code (v1.0.0) differs from the Methods in the spike threshold (10
against 20 mV/ms), spike latency, AHP amplitude and tau, its downstroke window
runs to the end of the step, and its test for a saturated sag response never
fires. Of the paper itself, the STAR Methods say a nested ANOVA was run for the
electrophysiology, but the reported degrees of freedom (F(2, 38), t(31), t(27),
t(18)) are those of cell-level tests on 41 cells, and the Results say the convex
hull volume of APP neurons was smaller than that of AV neurons while the Figure
S13 legend lists it among the features with no significant difference.
Questions are put to the paper's authors: which 21 of the 24 home-cage cells
(and which 8 of the 11 mice) were analysed and how the 20% change rule was
applied, and how the membrane potential of Figure 6G was measured "in the
absence of a current injection".

The author's points on the protocol: it covers duplicates but not a dataset
holding more cells than the paper analysed with no way to tell which, so all
cells were used with the alternatives run and the affected outcomes marked
assumed; it does not say whether a supplement that arrives after the claims
were written makes the claims file's note a misreading, so a reader was asked;
an exclusion rule the criterion says to apply would, read through the test
pulse, remove cells from groups whose counts already match the paper, so it was
made a variant; the example notebook's bold header labels conflict with the
no-bold instruction, so they were removed; and it is unclear whether code read
only for its method belongs under `inputs`, so it was cited in the deviations
and the helper's docstring and `inputs` left empty.
