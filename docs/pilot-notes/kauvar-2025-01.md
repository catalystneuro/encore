The author's points on the protocol: the literal scan in `tools/entry.py`
backtracks without end on long integer lists, so `check` hung until one list
was split; the checker rejects `data_missing` for `elsewhere` claims even when
the named archive lacks the data, and `other` was used; fig4b, fig5b and figs34c
are marked `data: dataset` though their data are in 001347, and the field was
left unchanged; link claims should go in the dependent claims' `depends_on`,
which the author may not edit, so the dependency was written in the notes;
link-claim criteria can only be written after seeing the data, and they were
marked as added by the author; `too_large` is defined for computation and was
used for claims the five hours did not reach; some criteria name a main reading
with variants while the protocol takes the outcome all readings give, and the
main reading was judged with the variants shown; and the protocol says nothing
of delegating work to a subagent, which was done for the 001347 notebook.
