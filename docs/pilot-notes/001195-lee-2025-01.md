# 001195, Lee et al. 2025

Five claims reproduced, five partially, two not reproduced and 26 blocked, all
on the dataset. The first verification round agreed with 36 of the 38 outcomes
as they stood and the second with 37, and a third round, held on the one
change of outcome proposed after the second, agreed with it. It pins version
0.250408.1733, the only published version; the paper cites none, and the draft
has the same 230 assets and size. The paper, Lee, Matthews, Lemieux et al.,
"Separable Dorsal Raphe Dopamine Projections Mimic the Facets of a
Loneliness-like State" in eLife, traces the projections of dorsal raphe (DRN)
dopamine neurons to the BNST, CeA and BLP (Figure 1), tests them with
behavioural optogenetics (Figures 2 to 4 and 8) and RNAscope (Figure 5),
records the responses of BNST, CeA and BLP cells to DRN terminal stimulation in
ex vivo patch clamp (Figure 6, Figure 7 supplement 1), and images CeA cells in
vivo during three-chamber sessions with and without ChrimsonR stimulation of
DRN dopamine terminals and after isolation (Figure 7). The claims were written
from the eLife reviewed preprint, version 2, with its Methods, legends and
figure supplements; the paper links no analysis code. The dandiset holds only
the patch-clamp recordings and the in vivo imaging, so the 12 tested claims
are all ex vivo. `fig6df-epsp-ipsp-at-rest` reproduces on the cells present
(15, 38 and 56 ChR2 cells with usable rest sweeps against the paper's 19, 36
and 48), resting on the contrast with a light-free window of the same traces,
since the criterion's 2 and 3 SD rule also finds responses there (an EPSP in
94, 80 and 40% of cells and an IPSP in 67, 30 and 5% at 2, 3 and 5 SD).
`fig6-s1h-cea-cluster-properties` and `fig6-s1i-blp-cluster-properties`
reproduce, the BLP split depending on single cells (without P10, P19, P29 or
P43 the delay no longer differs, p = 0.053 to 0.063).
`fig6-s1jk-cluster-voltage-area` reproduces on the assumption that the cells of
the three mice labelled female belong in the analysis; with male mice only its
BLP tests are not significant (area p = 0.11, EPSP p = 0.099).
`fig7-s1ef-red-vs-blue` reproduces on the assumption that the '+0.3mW blue
light' and '+0.2mW blue light' sweeps carry blue light alone (F(2,16) = 25.6
against the paper's 200.1). `fig6gi-firing-responses` is partial, the pattern
holding with 3, 13 and 13 firing cells against 5, 20 and 17.
`fig6j-epsp-amplitude-region` is partial, only reading (a) at 3 SD being
significant (p = 0.049), and not without cell C15 (p = 0.075).
`fig6k-ipsp-amplitude-region` is partial on the assumed 3 SD reading (F(2,55) =
6.88, p = 0.0021), with 58 cells against 34 +- 3.4 and 2 SD failing (p =
0.76). `fig6pq-blp-clusters` is partial, the direction holding with 64.7% or
58.8% of the pyramidal cluster inhibited against 93 +- 9.3%.
`fig7-s1hi-red-during-blue` is partial, the amplitude smaller during blue light
(p = 0.0072) and the area not significantly smaller. `fig6no-cea-clusters` is
not reproduced, 2 or 3 cells of the late-firing cluster being inhibited in all
16 readings, and `fig6-s1ln-combined-clusters` is not reproduced, the excited
combined cluster differing between CeA and BLP. `fig6-s1de-monosynaptic` is
blocked as `metadata_insufficient`, and the 8 in vivo claims of Figure 7 and
the 17 claims of Figures 1 to 5 and 8 as `data_missing`. The author's first
run gave fig6j as reproduced on an assumption, fig6k as not reproduced and
fig6no as partial. The author asked a reader no questions, so the answer run
and the following author run changed nothing. Round 1 disagreed with fig6j and
fig6no, found that the 2 and 3 SD detection rule finds responses in noise, and
asked for the cells of mice labelled female, rest sweeps that fire before the
light, the failed sweeps of P10 and the blue-light reading of fig7-s1ef to be
examined; in reply fig6j became partial, fig6k became partial on the 3 SD
assumption, fig6no was kept with the disagreement recorded, the light-free
window, 5 SD labels and a male-only variant were added, rest sweeps firing
before the light and P10's failed sweeps were left out, and a question was put
to a reader on whether the fig6-s1ln criterion misreads Figure 6 supplement 1N,
which appears to show the pattern found here. Round 2 disagreed with fig6no,
asked that fig6-s1jk be marked assumed and that the fig6k assumption rest on
its agreement with 5 SD, and found sentences the outputs did not support (P10's
sweep count, a P10-removed BLP clustering that was not run, capacitance reading
low by the same factor in every cell, a male-only sentence and the sex of three
subjects), which were corrected; the change of fig6no to not reproduced was
proposed and round 3 agreed, noting that the claims file may read the paper's
"excited" late-firing cells too strictly, which was put to a reader. The
author's run took about 1 h 50 min (07:32 to 09:19 UTC) with nothing failing,
loading the patch-clamp files taking about 13 minutes of the notebook's 15. The
notebook ran in 883 s at first and 707 s after the answer, the entry run took
22 minutes after round 1, and the notebook ran in 1544 s after round 2 and 877
s after round 3.

The dandiset holds the ex vivo patch-clamp recordings (Figure 6, Figure 7
supplement 1) and the in vivo CeA imaging of Figure 7; the tracing, behavioural
optogenetics and RNAscope data, 17 of the paper's 38 findings in the claims
file, are not in it. The imaging files hold traces, footprints, the raw movie
and a link to the behaviour video, but no pose estimates, cup interactions,
position, habituation and test epochs, or record of which cup held the
juvenile, and the SLEAP model is not published, so none of the 8 in vivo
findings can be tested. They hold 869, 913 and 854 ROIs for the ChrimsonR mice
in GH off, GH on and SI off against the paper's 429, 441 and 484 cells, and 35,
138 and 89 for the TdTomato mice against 39, 61 and 76, with nothing marking
which ROIs the paper kept. The imaging is stated at 25 Hz, but its 19,211
frames last 961 s at the Methods' 20 frames/s, within 4 s of the video in 40 of
41 sessions, and every imaging file describes another experiment (a GRIN lens
in the mPFC, Chrimson in the BLA, 589 nm light at 20 Hz). The patch-clamp files
do not record when the light was on, the stimulus series of the light sweeps
being zeros and the protocol named only in 103 distinct free-text
descriptions, so the light time was read from the responses; no sweep or
session is marked as recorded in TTX/4AP; and the injected current is stored
with unit volts and the voltage-clamp seal test as an IZeroClampSeries. The
holding current named in the sweep descriptions disagrees with the measured
current in 88 ChR2 light sweeps, and 148 of 2437 rest sweeps, in 28 cells, fire
before the light. The dataset holds 15, 38 and 56 ChR2 cells with usable 30 s
rest sweeps against the paper's 19, 36 and 48, and 3, 13 and 13 with firing
sweeps against 5, 20 and 17, 19 ChR2 cells being recorded only with other light
protocols. Three subjects (23, C137 and C150; 17 ChR2 cells) are labelled
female where the paper says all experiments were in male mice; the mice
labelled male give 48 BLP cells with rest sweeps, the paper's count. Two cells
named with the BLP letter (P4x and P42) are labelled BNST, 51 light sweeps of
P10 are failed sweeps that are not marked, and subject ids such as '4484' and
'4484?' leave open whether there are 17 subjects or 20. The ChrimsonR sweeps
described '+0.3mW blue light' and '+0.2mW blue light' begin with the
description of the 635 nm red protocol. Questions are put to the paper's
authors: what decided that a cell had an EPSP or an IPSP at rest and whether
Figure 6J includes mix cells; whether the cells of the mice labelled female
were analysed; and the capacitance code, a custom MATLAB Q-method on a 5 mV,
250 ms step that is not published and whose step is not in the files.

The author's points on the protocol: it does not say how to handle a stimulus
time missing from the files, and the light time was taken from the data with a
check and recorded as a deviation and a finding without `assumed: true`; some
count criteria judge on the cells present and others do not, and
`partially_reproduced` was used where the pattern held but the counts failed;
several criteria define only reproduced or blocked when readings disagree, and
`partially_reproduced` was used where every reading failed the same way and the
effect held in part; and it does not say how to quote the dataset id in YAML,
which was quoted.
