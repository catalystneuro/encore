# haley-2018, Haley et al. 2018

Eleven claims reproduced, two partially, eleven not reproduced and six blocked,
three as `metadata_insufficient` and three as `methods_underspecified`; every
tested outcome rests on an assumption. The first verification round agreed with
26 of the 30 outcomes and gave `fig3c-stg-rhythmic-fraction`,
`fig3d-stg-frequency`, `fig3f-stg-duty-cycle` and `fig6-cg1-measures` as not
reproduced where the record had partially reproduced, since none of their
criteria has a partial condition; the author took all four. The second round
agreed with all 30, and nothing was proposed after it, so there was no third
round. It pins the OSF version of 2019-01-08T14:49:50. The paper, Haley, Hampton
and Marder, "Two central pattern generators from the crab, Cancer borealis,
respond robustly and differentially to extreme extracellular pH" (eLife
7:e41877), steps the saline from pH 7.8 to 5.5 (acid) or 10.4 (base) in steps
of about 0.5, records the pyloric rhythm of the stomatogastric ganglion (STG,
15 preparations) and the cardiac ganglion (CG, 15 preparations), with
intracellular PD and LP recordings under picrotoxin (PTX), and reports that
both rhythms are robust from pH 6.1 to 8.8, that they respond differently at
the extremes, and that gastric-like rhythms appear at the extremes and on recovery. The
dataset holds 408 files, 385 raw .abf files and 23 READMEs. The claims were
written from the version of record (PMC6328273) and the paper's code at commit
83f5904; the four statistics tables are images, so only p < 0.05 bounds are
recorded. The manual state annotations and the Spike2 output are not deposited,
so spike thresholds were set automatically and the rhythmic states were read
from the spikes, with PD bursting and lvn firing between PD bursts for the STG,
which cannot tell LP from PY. The example STG preparation, PD spikes per burst
at 5.5, the PTX membrane potential claims, the depolarization of intact PD and
LP at 5.5, the robustness from 6.1 to 8.8 and the CG duty cycle reproduce; PTX
spike amplitude and frequency are partial. Not reproduced are the STG fraction
rhythmic (13 preparations against 15, and 10.4 not significant, p = 0.31), STG
frequency (acid ANOVA p = 1.6e-07 in the Methods' last 8 min, p = 0.061 in the
code's window), the duty cycle increase at 6.1, the CG example of Figure 6, the
CG fraction rhythmic, frequency and spikes per burst, the ganglion comparison
of Figure 8A, the Figure 8B counts (STG ceased firing at 5.5 in 8 of 13 against
14 of 15), the cross-predictive claim and the baseline frequency correlations.
Several CG outcomes depend on the isolation of the Large Cell unit: without the
four units of doubtful isolation, 10.4 is significantly slower in Figure 7C and
Figure 7B would be partial. The state transition claims and Figure 3A are
blocked because states 1 and 2 cannot be told apart, and the three gastric
claims because the paper gives no criteria for a gastric-like rhythm. Round 1
found that the state reading counted state 3 as rhythmic, that the LC isolation
was unvalidated, that missing steps entered as 0, and that the PD label of
903_052, the only preparation meeting Figure 4A, was doubtful; in reply the lvn
condition and an isolation check were added, the zero-fill was traced to the
paper's code with leaving it out as a variant, a check allowing for the
conduction delay confirmed the PD label, and three questions went to a reader
in `questions/haley-2018.yaml`. Round 2 found sentences the outputs did not
support, which were corrected, and asked for variants without three more
doubtful CG units, which were added. The author's run took about 1.5 h (01:05
to about 02:37 UTC), and the clean run took 989 s with 3.4 GB peak memory. The
OSF API returned 500 errors and timeouts and one listing timed out, and in the
first revision osf.io answered with HTTP 429, which cost two failed development
runs, so files are now read through WaterButler with a throttle.

In 903_046_0002 the PD and LP channel names are swapped relative to the other
ten files of the experiment: the input named LP3 is followed by a pdn spike 38
ms later for 1.00 of its spikes, while the input named PD4 matches at 0.13
against 0.15 by chance, and nothing in the file shows it; round 2 raised this
to critical. The manual state annotations (stateData_STG.xlsx,
stateData_CG.xlsx), the Spike2 spike and burst output and the gastric-rhythm
annotation behind Figures 3, 5, 7 and 8 are not deposited, so the state
transition analyses and the gastric counts cannot be repeated and every burst
measure depends on thresholds set again. Two experiments the paper's code
analyses are not in the project, STG 877_127 and CG 887_017 (unit CG3L). The
nine PTX folders (903_046 to 903_064) have no README, so the pH of each file was
inferred from the recorded bath pH. Seven experiments have no file for one pH
step, without the READMEs saying so. In 903_058 and 903_060 the bath pH shows
9.3 applied before 8.8. Several READMEs disagree with their folders, among them
887_145's, which lists its files as 887_149_0001.abf to 887_149_0014.abf.
877_101 has no pdn, so its PD unit was left out. The bath pH electrode of
903_052 responds at -37.6 mV per pH unit against -45.2 to -46.7 in the other
eight, so its 10.4 and 5.5 files read as pH 9.75 and 5.87 on the common slope.
The project links a GitHub add-on that returns HTTP 403 to an anonymous reader.
In the paper, pHCumulative.m compares a file length in seconds with a sample
count, so its window is the 480 s after the first burst, where the Methods say
the last 8 min, and the paper's 15 CG preparations are 15 Large Cell units from
10 experiments, treated as independent. Six questions are put to the paper's
authors: the per-file thresholds and burst parameters, how a missing pH step
entered the statistics, which PTX file is which pH and whether 903_058 and
903_060 received 9.3 before 8.8, which file stands for repeated controls or
acid steps, whether 877_101's PD was measured on the lvn, and the criteria for
a gastric-like rhythm.

The author's points on the protocol: its sha256 check cannot apply to partial
reads, and the STG and CG files were read whole and checked while the PTX
files were not hashed; the target list names OSF Storage only while the API
also lists a GitHub add-on, recorded as a minor finding; OSF API failures in
the listing, which feeds no result, were retried and print "not checked";
`assumed: true` falls on every tested outcome and no longer separates them;
a criterion's own partial condition was applied strictly and the protocol's
general definition used where there is none; `measured` is awkward when the
expected value is a sample size, and 13 was recorded; the label swap sat
between critical and major in the severity table, and major was chosen at
first; and the clean-run time varied from 10 to 16.5 minutes with network
speed, so `runtime_minutes` was left at 13.
