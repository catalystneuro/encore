# 000469, Kyzar et al. 2024

18 claims reproduced, none partially, one not reproduced and one blocked, and
both verification rounds agreed with all 20 outcomes. It pins version
0.240123.1806, the latest; the paper cites no version, and all four published
versions and the draft hold the same 41 assets. The paper, Kyzar, Kaminski,
Brzezicka, Reed, Chung, Mamelak and Rutishauser in Scientific Data, is a data
descriptor of single neurons recorded with hybrid macro-micro depth electrodes
in 21 patients with drug-resistant epilepsy during an image screening task and
a Sternberg working memory task, and reports the unit yield, behaviour, spike
sorting quality and the fractions of concept, maintenance and probe cells. The
claims were written from the version of record in Europe PMC (PMC10796636) and
from the paper's code (rutishauserlab/workingmem-release-NWB at commit
f8594e0), whose MATLAB scripts run parametric tests (anovan, paired t-tests)
where the Methods name permutation tests, and keep the permutation versions in
comments; the Methods gave the main criteria, with the 2000 permutations of the
code's comment, and the code's tests were run as variants. All 20 claims were
tested and no outcome rests on an assumption. The subject, session and unit
counts, the behaviour and the quality metrics match, `isi-violations` only with
the code's filter of units below 4% (0.416 and 0.498% against 0.428 and 0.625%
over all 902 units). `screening-concept-mfc` passes near the tolerance bound:
27 (5.56%) on the main seed, 29 (5.97%), outside, on one of three other seeds,
and 26 (5.35%) when the 142 MFC units with a p-value between 0.02 and 0.10 are
re-tested with 20000 permutations. `persistent-activity` is not reproduced: in
the maintenance window concept cells fire more on preferred trials (every
paired one-sided p below 1e-6), but the preferred means are 2.57 to 3.13 Hz
against the paper's 4.76. `probe-cells-mfc` is blocked as
`methods_underspecified`, as sign flipping gives 118 (26.05%) and 111 (24.50%),
within tolerance, and label shuffling 109 (24.06%) and 104 (22.96%), outside
it, against 122, which the code's paired one-sided t-tests give. The author
asked a reader no questions, so the answer run and the following author run
changed nothing. Round 1 found eight problems, none affecting a conclusion;
they led to a variant counting ties as exceedances (permutationTest.m counts
with a strict inequality, which with discrete spike counts makes the tests
slightly anti-conservative), under which the maintenance counts stay within
tolerance (140, 139, 142 and 141) and the probe readings still disagree, to
three more seeds for `sternberg-concept-mtl` (95 to 97 two-sided, 99 to 100
one-sided) and to a check of short fixation periods that found a third odd
trial. Round 2, whose verifier could not run code as numpy was not installed,
found statements the outputs did not support, among them the summary's "every
number in the paper's quality and behaviour sections is recovered" and column
descriptions no cell printed, which were narrowed or given new outputs, so
there was no third round. The author's run took about 45 minutes; two notebook
runs failed during development, on a dict with float keys passed as `**kwargs`
and on p-values printed as 0 by rounding, and were fixed. The notebook ran in
322 s at first and in 223 s in the last run.

The paper's persistent activity values (4.76 plus or minus 4.71 against 2.67
plus or minus 3.66 Hz "during the maintenance period") are reproduced exactly
by the rates 200 to 1000 ms after Encoding 1 onset of the code's 119
parametric concept cells in all areas (4.764 plus or minus 4.709 against 2.673
plus or minus 3.656 Hz), split by whether the Encoding 1 image is the preferred
one, while in the maintenance window the same cells fire at 3.05 against 2.70
Hz; the released code computes these Encoding 1 rates, and its preferred and
non-preferred trial masks, as written, select every trial and no trial. The
code's parametric tests reproduce every published cell count exactly (138, 26,
94, 144, 122), though the Methods name permutation tests. The text gives 449
MTL units for the screening task, which is the Sternberg MTL count (190 + 259);
the screening files hold 421 (159 hippocampus, 262 amygdala), which agrees with
the paper's own percentages (138 is 32.78% of 421). The reported ISI violation
spread (0.41 plus or minus 0.50%) needs the code's exclusion of one unit at
11.74%, and the reaction time ANOVA uses correct trials only, as the code does;
neither filter is stated in the paper. Four units have spike times out of
order, each with one backward step (0.3 to 12.4 ms), in the screening files of
subjects 11 and 5 and the Sternberg file of subject 2. `waveforms_mean_proj_dist`
is described as the isolation distance, `loadsEnc3_PicIDs` as "Picture ID for
Enc1 loads", and every column of the electrodes table but `id` as "my
description". Subjects 13 and 14 have the native IDs P39CS and P39CS_2, the
same age (26), sex, recording year and diagnosis, with different units and
electrodes, and nothing says whether they are one patient, so the 21 patients
may be 20. Three Sternberg trials have implausible timestamps: one of subject 9
has no maintenance period, another of subject 9 has fixation and Encoding 1
onsets 0.03 ms apart, and one of subject 21 has a 14.07 s fixation; leaving
them out does not change the persistent activity result. Ages in the files run
from 17 to 70, as in Table 1, while the text gives 17 to 71. One question is
put to the paper's authors: whether the published counts were computed with the
parametric tests and, if permutation tests were used, how many permutations,
whether the probe and maintenance comparisons were paired by trial, and which
encoding periods form the probe cells' encoding rate.

The author's points on the protocol: step 1.6 lets the author adopt the
best-supported reading while the probe-cells criterion says to block when the
readings disagree, and the criterion was followed; persistent-activity's effect
holds in direction but is much weaker, which the protocol would call partially
reproduced, while its criterion says not reproduced otherwise, and the
criterion was followed; the protocol does not say what to do when a permutation
count sits at a tolerance bound, and the 20000-permutation re-test was recorded
as a deviation; the clock check peaks away from the true onsets in 18 of the 41
files, mostly those with few units, which the notebook lists as not
established; and the example attempt the prompt points to (001453) records
protocol `pilot-5`, older than `pilot-12`.
