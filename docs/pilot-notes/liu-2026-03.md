The files carry no cell type, no connection label and no soma position, so none
of the Fig. 5 counts can be made from the dandiset alone; types and connections
exist only in the code's table metadata_multiPatch.xlsx, which links to the
files by name, and neither the paper nor its code gives a rule for calling a
connection. The table disagrees with the paper: it lists 298 cells, 1,416
ordered pairs (1,403 in files of the dandiset) and 26 connections, where the
paper reports 377 neurons, 1,292 pairs and 31 connections, PC-to-PC 18 of 743
against 24 of 503, one VEN-to-PC connection (190315s2, channel 4 to 6,
Excellent, with a 1 mV response in the data) where the paper reports none, and
pair counts that differ from the paper's by more than 10% in 6 of 8 classes. It
is also inconsistent with itself and with the files: ten of its presynaptic
blocks name no NWB file (8 match a file by the firing channel; 190315s2 channel
7 and 20241014HS2 channel 8, 13 rows, match none), it gives 5 cells two types,
labels 2 connections with a projection that names another postsynaptic channel,
its Nr_Conn disagrees with its annotations in 4 of 53 slices, six of its pairs
name channels that read 0 mV (slice 190314s1, channels 6 and 7), eight of its
Inh connections come from a PC and 6 of its 12 Inh connections have a positive
averaged response; 5 files of slice 20240912HS2 are not in it. Every response
series states a rate of 1 Hz where the data are sampled at 25 kHz, and the
stimulus series gives its rate of 25,000 with the unit millisecond; the
stimulus amplitude is stored as 2 (2.5 or 3 in 14 files) with the unit amperes
where the Methods inject 2 nA; every file names electrode Elec_8 as the
stimulated one, while the cell that fires is on channel 8 in only 35 of 301
files and is the table's presynaptic channel in 286 of the 288 files it names;
and all 301 files have the session start 2018-01-01T01:00+08:00 although the
slices are named for dates in 2019 and 2024, with every subject of age
P4Y/P6Y and sex M. The Methods average over more than 20 sweeps, and 119 of 301
files hold more than 20 (median 20, range 1 to 30). Of the other data,
sumData_StimWM.xlsx holds 23 VEN-PC pairs where the paper states 26,
AIScmp_PCvsVEN_Fig5c.xlsx 120 VENs and 500 PCs against 108 and 430, with no

14 VEN-L and 15 VEN-S against 10 and 11 and 17 excitatory clusters against 18,

the legend's 22. The MetaboLights deposit MTBLS13927 holds 558 identified
metabolites where the paper quantifies 581, the same as the code's
Metabolic_data.xlsx, which swaps the labels of two V1 samples; the deposit
names the first AIC sample A1C-1-Q and stores the run mode in its charge

called by eye on averaged traces, with which criteria for Excellent, Good and

The author's points on the protocol: it does not say whether marking claims
`data: with_labels` and adding `depends_on` counts as changing the claims
file, and both were done with statements, expected values and criteria
unchanged; connection annotations are expert judgements, yet had to be counted
as `with_labels`, so it was recorded that they decide the outcome; claims on
data elsewhere not tried for lack of scope have no fitting block reason, so
`other` was used with a note on each; some such claims name a raw archive while
the paper's code holds per-cell tables of the same data, which were used and
recorded as a deviation; there is no rule for a claim only partly testable, so
`patchseq-mapping` was blocked with the excitatory numbers in its notes; and it
is not clear when unmatched denominators make an outcome partial, so partial
was used where the connection counts matched and only the pair counts were
off.
