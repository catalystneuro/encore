# 000467, Hart et al. 2023

Three claims reproduced, three partially, 11 not reproduced and eight blocked
on the dandiset; the seven claims on data in other archives, counted apart,
are blocked too. The verification rounds agreed on every outcome but one:
the verifier holds `figs4a-bilateral-symmetry` to be not reproduced, and the
disagreement is recorded. It pins version 0.261004.1655, the only published
version; the paper cites none. The paper, Hart, Frank, Lopes et al. in Cell,
expresses GCaMP6s in the olfactory sensory neurons of the clonal raider ant
Ooceraea biroi, images the antennal lobe (AL) with two photons while the ants
smell general odorants and four alarm pheromones, and shows that the
pheromones are encoded sparsely and stereotypically, implicating a core
glomerulus for alarm behaviour. The claims were written from the accepted
author manuscript in PubMed Central (PMC10334690) and from the lab's
repository Hart_Kronauer2023 at commit 72d041a, which holds a TIFF converter
and the ImageJ macro for the peak maps; the repository the Key Resources Table
links returns 404. The 17 imaging claims were tested; the behaviour,
transgenesis and pupa image claims are blocked as `data_missing`, the four
Brain Image Library claims as `other`, as the retrieve API answered "no entry
found" and the stacks were not located within the run's five hours, and the
glomerulus counts, the ablation volumes (manual segmentation) and the genome
resequencing (17 Gbases to align) as `not_runnable`. The paper drew its ROIs
by eye, and the claims file gives eight readings (two drawings, the ROI's mean
or maximum, two counting rules); the attempt adopted glomerulus-sized ROIs,
the mean and the Methods' trial-mean rule (bix). 3-hexanone has the broadest
response, 2.57 times ethylpyrazine's median area, the median count of
responding glomeruli is at most 3, and more glomeruli respond at the higher
concentration in 28 of 52 ant and pheromone pairs (2 fewer, 22 ties), the
last two resting on that reading. Among the not reproduced outcomes, 10 of 27
ant and odorant cases are closest to the same odorant in other ants in
`fig3f-general-odorants-unique`, `fig4b-vehicle-no-response` is met under
biy only, 594 odor-specific decreases appear, 487 of them touching the AL
border, which points to motion, the median peak fold change is 0.453 or 1.016 where the
criterion asks for both in 0.9 to 2.2, and under bix PGb, 6G and PGa are
identified in 5, 2 and 0 of 13 ants. The single-glomerulus claims rest on a
second identification (aiiy) that finds PGa in 4 ants; `fig5c-s6-tuning` has
5 of 6 named combinations responding, the PGa part of `fig5c-6g-slow` passes
trivially, 6G's plateau lasts 2.4 to 3.6 s at 90% of its peak against about
30 s in `figs7a-6g-extended`, and in `figs7c-temporal-lme` the time-to-maximum
interaction reaches p < 0.05 in 9 of 12 readings, where the paper reports
p = 0.88, with PGa from 3 ants against 8. The author's first run gave three
reproduced, seven partially, six not reproduced and 16 blocked. The author
asked a reader no questions, so the answer run and the following author run
changed nothing. Round 1 agreed with 24 of 32 outcomes and gave
`data_missing` for the seven claims on data elsewhere, which the author kept
as `other` or `not_runnable`; it found that the traces were computed only
under bix, which finds no PGa, that a try/except hid the failed figs7c fits,
that the traces were not stabilized though shifts reached 12 um, that drawing
a returned 387 ROIs on pure noise, and that the Minimum filter used 13 pixels
where ImageJ's kernel has 21. Fixing the filter moved `fig3fg-hexanone-broad`
from a ratio of 1.91 to reproduced; `fig4-large-lasting-responses`,
`figs5b-shared-glomerulus` and the imaging half of `fig6-shared-panic-pathway`
moved to not reproduced and `figs7c-temporal-lme` from blocked to not
reproduced (assumed); drawing a was rebuilt (8 to 40 ROIs per ant), and a
question to a reader on its size was put during the revision. Round 2 agreed
with 30 of 32, disputing figs4a and `figs5c-mho-rarely-overlaps`, whose
criterion asks for every reading while the four maximum-value readings fail;
it found sentences the outputs did not support, among them directions in
fig5d said to hold by construction and "All fits converged", which were
corrected, and noted that 356 of drawing a's 436 regions are under 10 um
across and that 618 of 1,684 region-ROI traces fell back to the disc. The
author proposed figs5c as not reproduced, a third round agreed, without
"assumed", and its point that the maximum reading on fragments may make a
claim of rarity close to untestable became a second reader question. The
author's run took about 1 h 50 min, ending at 13:01 UTC; a second
identification tried during development was dropped as it would have pushed
a notebook to about 27 of its 30 minutes, and the figs7c fits failed with one
glomerulus level. The three notebooks ran in 7.3, 19.6 and 20.8 min at first,
and the last entry run took about 47 minutes.

Each file holds one plane of one trial as uncompressed float64, although the
values are 13-bit integers (0 to 8191), so the dandiset is 14,685 files and
1.26 TB; a whole-lobe analysis of the alarm experiment must open about 11,600
files, and this attempt read about half a terabyte through range requests.
The planes' starting_time values step by 0.0335 s, so a 33-plane volume takes
1.105 s, while each file's imaging_rate is 0.83 volumes/s (1.205 s per volume)
and the paper states a volume every 1.2 s; the analysis times volumes by the
imaging rate. The odor, concentration and trial are given only in the file and
session names and the stimulus timing (onset 3 s, 5 s pulse) only in free-text
descriptions, with no stimulus or trial table, and the bilateral files do not
say which AL is the left one. The general odorant files hold the five
odorants the paper analyses and the vehicle, though the paper says 7 to 9 were
presented, and the dandiset does not say the others were left out. Behaviour
videos, confocal stacks and the genome sequence are not in the dandiset. No
file or trial is deposited twice. Questions are put to the paper's authors:
how the ROIs were drawn, whether an ROI's value was its mean or its maximum,
whether "responding" was judged on the trial-mean value or per trial, which
trials were excluded for motion artifacts and by what rule, how the centre
plane of a glomerulus was chosen and what ROI was measured on it, and what
distance makes two glomeruli adjacent.

The author's points on the protocol: reading with remfile and h5py was too
slow for 1.26 TB, so HTTP range reads at the byte offsets h5py reports were
used and checked against h5py; the claims file judges M2 claims under each
reading while step 1.6 says to adopt the best-supported one, and step 1.6 was
followed with those outcomes marked assumed; there is no rule for claims that
depend on a claim tested but not reproduced that only partly identifies the
glomeruli; no `blocked_by` category fits data elsewhere that cannot be
located, and `other` was used; conclusions can only be written after the run,
so the Result text was edited in the entry and executed copies without a
re-run; and the 30-minute notebook limit meant splitting the work into three
notebooks.
