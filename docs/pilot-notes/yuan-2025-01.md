# 001882, Yuan et al. 2025

Eight claims reproduced, seven partially (one on an assumption), none not
reproduced and three blocked, all three for missing data; the eight claims that
also use outside inputs, the in silico analyses of the variants, are counted
apart: one reproduced, two partially, three not reproduced (one on an
assumption) and two blocked for missing data. The first verification round
agreed with all 26 outcomes, giving data_missing as the reason for the three
claims the record then had blocked as not runnable, and the second agreed with
all 26, with a reservation on `table3-destabilizing`; nothing was proposed
after the second, so there was no third round. It pins version 0.260701.1757,
the only published version, whose 13 assets the draft holds unchanged; the
paper cites none. The paper, Yuan, Chatterjee, Leys, Odom and Salido,
"Prevalence of IMPG1 and IMPG2 Mutations Leading to Retinitis Pigmentosa or
Vitelliform Macular Dystrophy in a Cohort of Patients with Inherited Retinal
Dystrophies" in Genes, describes seven patients of a cohort of 480 at West
Virginia University with IMPG1 or IMPG2 variants, two with retinitis pigmentosa
(RP) and five with vitelliform macular dystrophy (VMD), and scores the variants
with in silico tools; the dandiset holds a spreadsheet of the seven patients, a
Word document of tool outputs and the paper's Figures 1 to 4 as image files,
and no recording. The claims were written from the version of record in Europe
PMC (PMC11764596) without a PDF; the paper links no code, and its Data
Availability Statement says "No new data were created or analyzed in this
study". The spreadsheet looks like the authors' source for Table 1, and the
images are the paper's own annotated panels, so the Table 1 and phenotype
claims test the deposit's agreement with the paper, not independent evidence,
and every phenotype feature was judged by the author's visual reading of the
panels. Reproduced are `cohort-prevalence` (7 carriers, 1.458%, 0.417% and
1.042% over the paper's 480), the four Table 1 claims (7 of 7 patients, 14 of
14 eyes), `vmd-acuity-preserved` (M73 the only reduced patient under all four
readings), `f32-sector-rp-phenotype`, `rp-superior-field-no-cme` and, of the
claims with outside inputs, `gnomad-frequencies` (all 6 values within 10%
under gnomAD v4.1 joint counts read as 1/(2 x allele frequency)). The six
other phenotype claims are partial, with 2 to 6 of each claim's features
visible: M75's left macula is hyper-autofluorescent as the text says, not hypo
as the legend says, M34's maculae are hypo-autofluorescent (0.64 and 0.59 of
the surround), the legend's reading, not the text's, and F57's "more
pronounced in the left eye" is not shown. `m73-f57-similar-lesions` is partial
on the assumption of one of five readings of a bright FAF focus, under which
M73's right eye shows only a speck no brighter than the perimacular ring (64%
of background discs are as bright); under two of the other readings all three
parts of its criterion would hold. `variant-domains` is partial (no reading
places all 7; UniProt, InterPro and the PROSITE profiles place 6, Pfam and
SMART 4) and `table4-impg1-physicochemical` partial (14 of 18 values, the four
misses all in the p.Asp423Glu column). Not reproduced are
`table2-pathogenicity` (the IMPG2 SIFT and PolyPhen-2 HumVar scores match
exactly, the IMPG1 scores and the Pathogenic call for p.Asp423Glu do not, CADD
matches at most 2 of 7 values and no FATHMM or MutationTaster score matches),
`asp423glu-novel`, as the variant is ClinVar variation 1000102 in all 4 ClinVar
VCF releases of November 2024, and `table3-destabilizing`, on the claims
file's reading that iMutant is run from sequence, as I-Mutant 2.0 gives a
positive DDG for 4 of 5 variants while MuPRO gives a negative one for all 5.
`all-caucasian`, `m73-eog-normal` and `other-gene-variants` are blocked for
missing data, and so are the two DynaMut interaction claims, as the I-TASSER
models are not deposited. The author's first run gave the same outcomes except

runnable, with no outcome on an assumption. The author asked no questions of a

The author's points on the protocol: the protocol assumes NWB files with a
time base, which this dataset lacks, so the clock check could not apply;
`entry.py check` rejects any `imread(` or `Image.open(` though here the images
are the data, so they were read with `tifffile` and `imagecodecs`, and the
rule needs an exception for image data from the archive; criteria resting on
visual reading conflict with backing every sentence by a printed number, so
the readings were printed as a table with the panels each rests on; there is
no guidance for claims tested on the paper's own figures, so the circularity
was flagged in each claim's notes and deviations; outside services cannot be
pinned by a DOI or a commit, so the release each printed was recorded; there
is no guidance for personal data found in a dataset, so it was kept out of the
outputs and reported as a finding; the ten-minute target could not be met, as
the notebook takes 13 to 15 minutes, almost all of it waiting for VEP; one
criterion (m73-f57-similar-lesions) gives no rule for a partial result;
`pysam` saves ClinVar index files into the working directory, so it was run in
a temporary directory; and `measured` must be numbers, so the cohort size of
480 taken from the paper was recorded with a deviation.
