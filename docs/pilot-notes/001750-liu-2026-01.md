# 001750, Liu et al. 2026

No claim on the dandiset reproduced: all three are blocked, two as methods
underspecified and one for missing data. Of the 34 claims, one is a link check,
`na-cell-table-link`, partially reproduced, and 30 have their data elsewhere,
in the paper's code repository, MetaboLights, other dandisets, GEO,
NeuroMorpho.org or the model, and are counted apart: three reproduced, one
partially, one not reproduced and 25 blocked. The first verification round
agreed with 32 of the 34 outcomes as they stood and the second with all 34, and
nothing was proposed after the second, so there was no third round. It pins
version 0.260825.0222, the only published version; the paper cites none. The
paper, Liu, Huang, Shen, Shao, Jing et al., "An atlas of primate insular cortex
reveals a signal-processing strategy in von Economo neurons" in Nature Cell
Biology, has its main Patch-seq recordings in DANDI:001746; this dandiset holds only its
outside-out recordings of somatic Na+ currents, 35 patches from one macaque in
two sessions, for the Fig. 4d finding that the currents are lower in von
Economo neurons (VENs, n = 14) than in L5 PCs (n = 21). The claims were written
from the version of record in Europe PMC (PMC13457102) without a PDF, the
Supplementary Information or the Source Data, and from the paper's code
(AIC_proj at commit 44cd8a6 and PatchAnaLab 1.0); the check made nine changes,
four of them new claims, and left four points open. `fig4d-sodium-current-lower`
is blocked as methods underspecified: the VEN median is lower under all eight
readings, but the rank-sum p is 0.045 (a1b1c1) and 0.025 (a1b1c2), the two
readings that keep each patch's holding current, and 0.077 to 0.29 under the
six others. `model-na-ratio` depends on it and is blocked with it, and
`na-patch-capacitance` for missing data. The link is partial because all 35
files match the code table by file name and cell ID, while under the eight
readings at most 2 of 35 computed maxima are within 10% of the table, against
the 28 required. Of the claims on other data, `fig4j-eepsp-amplitude` (repeated
measures p = 0.0068, and p = 0.0033 on the sheet's "re-orgnized data" block
against the paper's 0.0034), `ven-subtypes-similar-physiology` (p = 0.55, 0.27
and 0.958) and `fig3e-phase-peak-index` (VEN median 0.117, Kruskal-Wallis p =
4.9e-08) are reproduced on the authors' per-cell tables in the code repository,
which checks the statistics, not the measurements. `fig8de-glycolytic-bias` is
partially reproduced (2-ketobutyrate lower, ratio 0.37, but p = 0.18) and
`fig8a-metabolite-gradient` is not reproduced (175 and 84 metabolites against
expected 205.2 to 250.8 and 97.2 to 118.8). The five model claims are blocked as
not runnable, as the model code is not in the repository and its outputs are in
a multi-part RAR archive of about 11.8 GB on Zenodo; nine are blocked as too
large, the sequencing claims on GEO and the claims on DANDI:001746, which would
take about 1.9 to 3.2 hours to stream; nine are blocked as other, those whose
criteria hand the test to the claims files of DANDI:001751 and 001752, the
NeuroMorpho.org morphology claims, not attempted in the run's time, and
`fig4j-eepsp-latency`, whose table holds no latencies; and
`ven-firing-rate-variability` (methods underspecified) and
`fig3c-ais-distance-length` (metadata insufficient) are blocked too. The
author's first run gave the same outcomes except that
`ven-subtypes-similar-physiology` and `fig3e-phase-peak-index` were blocked;
in it one `grep -o` on the paper's text hung for the full 60-minute tool
timeout, which cost about an hour in which some blocked claims could have been
tested. The author asked no questions of a reader, so the answer and
author-answers runs changed nothing. Round 1 disagreed on `model-na-ratio`,
which it gave partially reproduced, and on `ven-subtypes-similar-physiology`,
part of which it found testable, and found that the maximum takes the single
most negative sample of unfiltered 100 kHz traces, that the a1 readings mostly
measure the holding offset, that the smoothing variant was tuned to the table,
that noise and repetitions were not shown by cell type, that the session
dependence was not discussed, that the re-organized eEPSP block was not used as
a cross-check and that glutamate has two entries; in reply per-cell
diagnostics found that in 10 cells the maximum is a brief transient, filtered
and short-window variants that decide nothing were added, the subtype claim was
tested and reproduced, the per-cell phase peak index table was found while
writing a reason for each blocked claim and `fig3e` tested on it,
`model-na-ratio` was kept blocked under the protocol's rule for dependent
claims with the disagreement recorded, and a question on a filtered reading was
filed in `questions/001750.yaml`, after the reader's run. Round 2 agreed with
all 34, found that the Bessel variant was run forward and backward, doubling its
order, that the AS session reversal coincides with the transients, and
sentences the outputs did not support (that the readings without baseline find
the effect, where 2 of 4 do, that the table "matches" a smoothed peak, the
streaming time, a stray fragment and an inference about capacitive transients);
in reply the filter was run once forward (13 of 24 baseline-subtracted variants
significant, from 17), the session contrast without the transient cells was
added and the text was corrected. The author's run took about 2 h 25 min (13:18
to about 15:43 UTC), with one YAML quoting error in the record fixed; the
notebook ran in 164 s at first, 208 s after the author-answers run, about 4
minutes after round 1 and about 3.5 minutes at the end. Untracked dotfiles and
`scripts/` at the repository root were left alone.

The files carry no cell type, so VENs and PCs can be told apart only through
the paper's code table metadata_outSideOutSodiumCurrentRec.csv, whose file
names and cell IDs agree for all 35 patches. How the maximal Na+ current was
measured from the traces is not stated in the paper or its code: plain peaks of
the repetition-averaged traces, with or without a pre-step baseline, do not
match the code table (at most 2 of 35 within 10%), a peak after 0.75 ms
smoothing comes closest (26 of 35, at a width fitted to the table), and with a
2.9 kHz Bessel low-pass or a 0 to 3 ms window the VEN against PC difference is
significant under every baseline-subtracted reading. The traces keep holding
currents of -131 to +92 pA before the step although the Methods say they were
leak subtracted online, and one file (cell HS018, a VEN) has a positive holding
current and noise near 57 pA SD. In at least 10 of the 35 files the averaged
trace holds brief biphasic transients 0.07 to 0.16 ms wide at half height,
larger than the Na+ current in some patches; under reading a2 b1 c1 they set
the maximum in 6 of the 14 VENs and 4 of the 21 PCs, 9 of the 10 in session
221206AS, and without them session AS keeps 2 VENs and 6 PCs, too few to tell.
No file holds the patch capacitance, series resistance or compensation values,
so the patch size of about 50 fF and 5 um2 and current densities cannot be
computed. Files hold 28 or 34 steps (to +55 or +85 mV) and 1 to 10
repetitions, where the Methods describe steps to 0 mV, every file gives the
session start 2018-01-01 01:00 while the session ids and cell IDs date the
recordings to 6 December 2022, and the series starting_time values (0 to 3 s)
carry no order. The code table's own values give a VEN to PC ratio of 0.58
(mean) and 0.51 (median), outside the tolerance around the model's 55 against
80 pS/um2. Of the outside data, the MetaboLights table of MTBLS13927 holds 558
metabolites where the Results report 581, and one AIC sample is named A1C-1-Q.
In the code repository, AIScmp_PCvsVEN_Fig5c.xlsx has 500 PC and 120 VEN values
where the Fig. 3c legend gives 430 and 108, with no slice or animal column for
its two-way ANOVA; sumData_StimWM.xlsx labels its stimulation values 0.5, 1 and
2 mA where the Methods give 0.05 to 0.2 mA, gives no unit for the amplitudes,
and its two blocks of Fig. 4j amplitudes agree in 69 of 78 rows and differ in
9; PeakIdx_Fig5e.xlsx differs from the Fig. 2d-f counts in four types (29 VENs
against 22); and extFig9ad_plotVENsubtypeAP_Cmp.m loads VENsubtype_APs.mat from
a folder VENsubtypeCmp_extFig9ab that is not in the repository, which holds it
under VENsubtypeCmp_extFig8ab. A question is put to the paper's authors: which
baseline, filter or fit, steps and window gave the maximal currents of Fig. 4d.

The author's points on the protocol: it has no block reason for a claim whose
criterion hands the test to another dandiset's claims file, so `other` was
used; it has no reason for a claim not attempted for lack of time, so
`too_large` was used only where the streaming rate gave an estimate and `other`
elsewhere; a criterion that blocks when readings disagree conflicts with step
1.6, which allows adopting the best-supported reading, and the criterion was
followed; it does not say whether a dependent claim blocked automatically
should still have its numbers shown, and they were shown; and it does not say
how to label variants found after looking at the data, so the smoothing was
said to be post hoc and to decide nothing.
