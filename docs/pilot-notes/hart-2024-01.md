# 000773, Hart et al. 2024

Two claims reproduced, one partially, none not reproduced and three blocked
on the dataset, with one more reproduced, two more partially, five more not
reproduced and one more blocked that also need outside data. The first
verification round agreed with 19 of the 22 outcomes and the second with all
22, so there was no third round. It pins version 0.261004.1511, the only
published version; the paper cites none. The paper, Hart, Lopes, Frank and
Kronauer in Current Biology, counts young and old clonal raider ants leaving
the nest after alarm pheromones and images the calcium responses of olfactory
sensory neuron axons (Orco-QF2, 15xQUAS-GCaMP6s) in the right antennal lobe
(AL) of young (15 to 20 days) and old (60 to 61 days) ants to two alarm
pheromones and five general odorants at 3% or 48%, as whole-AL maps, counts
of responding glomerulus-sized ROIs and the responses of six focal glomeruli
(PGb, PGa, G1 to G4). The claims were written from the accepted author
manuscript (NIHMS1997610, PMC11265976) with supplement MMC1 and from the
paper's two Zenodo code records; no code for the glomerulus ROIs, the AUC, the
responding-ROI counts or the statistics is published. The five behaviour
claims were tested on Data S1 of the paper and all reproduce with the values
of Table S1 (for example F(3, 314) = 8.006, p = 3.7e-05 for
`fig1c-old-faster-alarm`); they and `fig2b-gcamp-baseline-unchanged`, whose
confocal stacks are in the Brain Image Library, are left out of the counts,
and fig2b is blocked as `other`, the download server having timed out on
every try. The whole-AL claims use an outline found by Otsu's threshold in
place of the paper's hand-drawn one: `fig2d-whole-al-alarm-no-age-effect` and
`figs3b-whole-al-other-no-age-effect` reproduce (smallest p 0.061 and 0.062),
and `figs3b-whole-al-general-decline` is partial, both declines holding (p =
2.0e-05 and 0.0085) but the isopropanol ratio being -5.68 against 51.3, as its
old mean is negative. `fig2e-heptanone-breadth-falls`, `fig2-alarm-sparse` and
`figs3c-general-breadth-no-age-effect` are blocked as
`methods_underspecified`: the paper drew its ROIs by hand, and the claims
file's automated ROIs give medians for 4-methyl-3-heptanone of 4 and 2, 1 and
0, and 0 and 0 at 25, 35 and 50 pixels, with no size giving a significant
difference (p = 0.22, 0.35 and 0.71). For the focal glomerulus claims each
glomerulus was placed where the dandiset's per-trial responses best correlate
with Data S2's, under a link claim the author added
(`data-s2-glomerulus-link`, partial, 71 of 108 triples agreeing at r >= 0.7,
66% against the 80% needed, and 44 of 108 held out), and the held-out
placement (leave one odor out), an 8-pixel disk ROI and fold-change reading
(a) were adopted as assumptions. On them
`figs2a-mean-six-glomeruli-no-age-effect` reproduces (p = 0.58 and 0.23), a
null the notes call weak evidence; `fig3b-g2-heptanone-weaker-old` (G2 p =
0.86), `fig3d-pg-stronger-old` (PGb with 4-methyl-3-heptanone p = 0.14),
`figs4a-general-odorants-in-focal-glomeruli` (56 of 60 cells, the 3-hexanone
pattern broken), `fig4-propionic-acid-g2-g3` (p = 0.60 and 0.17) and
`pg-opposite-modulation`, which needs fig3d, are not reproduced;
`fig4-general-odorants-decline` is partial, 9 of 10 declines being
significant with two p-values just under 0.05 and none of the eight other
variants reaching that level, and `figs4d-pgb-hexanone-dynamics` is partial,
the early window being larger in young ants (p = 0.0026) but the late window
not larger in old ants (p = 0.67). Data S2's own values give every focal
glomerulus claim except pg-opposite-modulation.
`fig3d-pg-60-vs-90-days-same` is blocked as `methods_underspecified`: reading
(a), with the 90-day ants of DANDI 000467, finds no difference but only 1 of 8
of the 90-day PGa placements is above chance, and reading (b) compares AUCs
measured here with Data S2's, two pipelines, not two ages. The author's first
run gave figs3c as reproduced on an assumption, fig3d-pg-60-vs-90-days-same as
reproduced under reading (a), fig4-general-odorants-decline as not
reproduced, pg-opposite-modulation as partial and fig2b as blocked as
`data_unreadable`. The author asked a reader no questions, so the answer run
and the following author run changed nothing; the latter noted that it was
started with no questions file. Round 1 disagreed with figs3c, a null from
automated ROIs that miss the paper's effect and give implausible counts (17
to 173 ROIs per ant), with fig3d-pg-60-vs-90-days-same and with
pg-opposite-modulation, whose criterion states only when the claim is
reproduced, and found that the placements were not tested against chance and
were chosen on the trials they were then tested on, that reading (b) let a
pixel with a tiny baseline dominate (old-ant G1 means of 39 to 57), that the
whole-AL measure has a negative offset and that the motion-exclusion reading
(b) excluded nothing. In reply the three were changed as round 1 gave, fig2b's
reason became `other`, a permutation chance level (all 42 young and 24 of 39
old placements above chance) and the held-out placement were added, the
latter making fig4 partial, reading (b) was given a background floor, and two
questions were put to a reader, on the contradiction between the criteria of
pg-opposite-modulation and fig4-propionic-acid-g2-g3 and on reading (b) of
the 60 against 90 day claim. Round 2 agreed with all 22 and found sentences
the outputs did not support (4 old ants where 5 were printed, timeouts and
shell attempts in no output, Data S2 said to give every focal glomerulus
claim, the whole-AL measures said to need no hand-drawn ROI, a run time of
about 30 minutes where 43.9 was printed, ten dependent claims where there are
twelve, and p >= 0.080 where 0.07996 was printed), which were corrected; the
direction of the placement bias and the held-out 60-day values against the
90-day ones were added at its request, and a third question for a reader, on
the missing rule for readings of fig3d-pg-60-vs-90-days-same that disagree.
The author's run took about 2 h 35 min, with four full notebook runs of 19 to
23 minutes each, and only the Brain Image Library download failed; for the
final text fixes the Markdown of the executed copy was edited directly. The
notebook ran in 22.4 min at first, 1695 s after the answer, 30 min after
round 1 and 36.2 min after round 2.

The dandiset holds no ROI positions, glomerulus identities or responding-ROI
counts, so the six focal glomeruli and the responding ROIs of Figures 2E and
S3C cannot be located from it alone, and twelve claims depend on them; Data
S2 holds the traces of the focal glomeruli but not their positions. At
positions matched to Data S2, PGb alarm AUCs measured from the imaging are
well below Data S2's in the 60-day ants of this dandiset (5.15 against 20.66
for 4-methyl-3-heptanone) and in the 90-day ants of DANDI 000467 (11.59
against 24.99), although the trace shapes often match, and every young-ant
glomerulus is placed above chance but 15 of 39 old-ant glomeruli are not; on
held-out trials only 9 of 52 old-ant triples agree, so old-ant values are
read partly where the glomerulus is not, which pushes claims that need old
ants lower toward passing and those that need them higher toward failing.
The behaviour data of Figure 1 and S1 are only in Data S1, the confocal
stacks of Figure 2A and 2B only in the Brain Image Library
(doi:10.35077/g.1153), whose landing page lists 15 brains (7 young, 8 old)
and notices that the repository is under review and that data would be
unavailable from October 5 to 16, and the 90-day ants of Figure 3C and 3D in
DANDI 000467. The dandiset holds all 21 ants (7 young and 7 old at 48%, 3 and
4 at 3%), with ages matching Data S2. Stimulus timing is stored only in the
series description ("odor stimulus triggered at 3s with duration 5s"), with
which the traces are consistent. The timing metadata disagree: the
description gives 40 cycles in 48.0 s (1.2 s per volume), the series rate is
1.2097 Hz (0.83 s per volume, the Methods' value), and the imaging plane's
imaging_rate is 0.83, a period stored as a rate. One trial (ant1-m2-d24-y2023,
trial 135) spells ethylpyrazine "ethylyprazine" in its 33 file names. The
Methods say a small number of trials were excluded from the responding-ROI
counts for motion artifacts, but neither the dandiset nor Data S2 says which.
The whole-AL mean of the peak fold change map is negative for a
non-responding AL (paraffin oil median -0.23), from the minimum filter on
noisy per-pixel fold change, so the fold declines of Figure S3B are sensitive
to the baseline; with each ant's paraffin oil value subtracted the ratios are
6.0 and 9.5. In Data S2 the trailing number of a trial name is the repeat for
most ants but the recording's trial number for the 90-day PGb traces, and one
of those 96 numbers does not match the 000467 trial of that odor and repeat.
Raw fluorescence is stored as uncompressed float64, 1.28 TB for 21 ants in
15,246 files of one plane of one trial each. Questions are put to the paper's
authors: how the responding ROIs of Figures 2E and S3C were drawn and what
the per-ant counts are; and where the six focal glomeruli are in each ant,
what size their ROIs were, whether fold change was computed on the ROI mean
or per pixel, and whether the PGb and PGa alarm traces of Figure 3C and 3D
were quantified on the same ROIs and planes, with the same processing, as the
general odorant traces of Figure 4.

The author's points on the protocol: the fig2e criterion blocks the claim
when its result differs from the paper's counts in Data S2, which holds no
counts, so three glomerulus sizes were run and the claim blocked as
`methods_underspecified` when they differed; there is no rule for when an
author may change a claim's `data` field or `depends_on`, and the focal claims
were set to `with_inputs` and made to depend on the link claim with
statements, values and criteria unchanged; nothing says how to choose between
readings that differ only in where an outside comparison value comes from, and
the reading that measures both ages the same way was adopted as an
assumption; no `blocked_by` reason is given for an unreachable outside
archive, and `data_unreadable` was used; the pg-opposite-modulation criterion
states only when the claim is reproduced, and partial was given with the
reason in the notes; and for the final text-only fixes the Markdown of the
executed copy was edited directly in place of a 23-minute re-run.
