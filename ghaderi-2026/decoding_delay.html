Ghaderi, Crochet & Petersen (2026) from dandiset 001530: population decoding and clustering¶

Paper. Ghaderi P, Crochet S, Petersen CCH (2026), Nature Communications 17, 5982, doi:10.1038/s41467-026-73622-y; claims written from the version of record (PMC13346651), main text, legends and Methods.

Dandiset. DANDI:001530, version 0.260605.0601, the only published version (the draft has the same assets and was last modified with the publication); the paper cites no version. The data check, the trial and unit selection and the claims on behaviour, optogenetics and single neurons are in reproduce_ghaderi_2026.ipynb; this notebook loads the same sessions through the same helper.

Outside inputs. data_helpers/Area_list.mat (CCF labels of each area) and processed_data/Data_Clustering.mat (the paper's cluster labels, linked by claim cluster-label-link in the first notebook), from Zenodo doi:10.5281/zenodo.17973874.

Run size. The paper's decoding repeats each fit over 100 neuron subsets (Fig. 5b) or 5 runs (Fig. 7d), and its GMM uses 1,000 initialisations per k. The Methods' grid search fits 36 SVMs for every one of the code's decoder (7 values of C in an inner 5-fold search, then the refit), and the run times printed below put a full run of either reading beyond what fits here, so both are run with fewer repetitions (constants in the next cell); each section prints what was run, its run time and the estimate for the full run.

fig5b-context-decoding-delay¶

With 50 neurons per area, context (Hit against Nogo-tone Whisker CR) is decoded in the last 200 ms of the delay above baseline in every area, but far better in wM2 (64 +/- 1%) and ALM (61 +/- 1%) than in A1 (52 +/- 1%), wS1 (52 +/- 1%) and wS2 (52 +/- 0.4%).

As the criterion: per 10 ms bin, a linear SVM on the counts of 50 random neurons, majority class undersampled, z-scored, stratified 5-fold, mean over subsets. Delay = bins labelled 0.8 to 1.0 s, baseline -1 to 0 s (Methods) and -0.8 to 0 s (code). Reading (b) is the code's nu-SVC, reading (a) the Methods' C-SVM with nested grid search; (a) is run on one subset per session and on every 5th baseline bin.

def session_classes(s, cols, rule="median", counts="c10"):
    q = g.quiet_mask(s, rule)
    X = s[counts][:, :, cols]
    return X[g.trial_mask(s, 1, True) & q], X[g.trial_mask(s, 3, False) & q]

Summary¶

A failure to reproduce here may be a failure of this attempt, not of the paper: see the reasoning and the differences from the paper's method.

Claim Outcome
fig5b-context-decoding-delay partially reproduced
fig5b-decoding-time-course reproduced

How the analysis differs from the paper's. 5 random subsets of 50 neurons per session (3 for 5 to 45 neurons) in place of 100 with the code's decoder, with four more seed sets on the delay bins to show the spread, and one subset on every 5th baseline bin (and the cue and delay bins) with the Methods' grid search, whose grid (C from 10^-3 to 10^3) is the claims file's. Quiet trials by the Methods' median absolute deviation; the code's mean absolute deviation is run with the code's decoder only. sklearn's NuSVC and SVC (both libsvm) stand in for the MATLAB libsvm interface; folds and undersampling use their own random draws.

What was found about the dataset. The area-sessions with at least 50 neurons are exactly the paper's (8, 17, 19, 18 and 16 sessions in 6, 9, 9, 8 and 6 mice).
