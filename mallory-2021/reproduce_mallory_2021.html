Reproducing Mallory, Hardcastle et al. (2021) on dandiset 000053¶

Paper. Mallory CS, Hardcastle K, Campbell MG, Attinger A, Low IIC, Raymond JL, Giocomo LM (2021). Mouse entorhinal cortex encodes a diverse repertoire of self-motion signals. Nature Communications 12, 671. doi:10.1038/s41467-021-20936-8. The claims file (claims/mallory-2021.yaml) was written from the version of record (Europe PMC full text of PMC7844029) with its Methods, legends and Supplementary Information.

Dandiset. DANDI:000053, version 0.210819.0345 (doi:10.48324/dandi.000053/0.210819.0345). The paper cites no DANDI version (its data statement points to figshare). This is the only published version; the draft lists the same 359 assets, so the published version is pinned.

What the dandiset holds. 329 open field files (25 mice, 1200 units, tetrodes) with body position, body speed, azimuthal head direction and azimuthal head velocity, and no pitch or roll; 20 head-fixed Neuropixels files (9 mice, 5120 units) with VR position, speed, eye position and eye velocity, and 10 behaviour-only copies of head-fixed sessions. The paper's head-fixed data are 11 of the 20 files (link claim below); of the other 9, six hold gain and contrast trials and three only gain 1 and contrast 100% (their trial tables are printed after the link).

Outside input. The paper's own deposit, figshare Dataset 3 (doi:10.6084/m9.figshare.13426103.v1), read in place, names which dandiset files are the paper's 11 recordings. It brings in no measurement.

The LN model, under the main readings¶

The forward search of the claims file's LN pipeline is run for every unit of the 11 sessions, with open detail (1) under reading (a), 10-s sections assigned to folds in turn (compute_test_train_ind.m), and open detail (2) under reading (a), bins outside any variable's inner 98% dropped. The other reading of detail (1) is run, with every downstream analysis, in readings_folds_mallory_2021.ipynb; reading (2b) was not run, for lack of time. For each encoding cell the reduced models (one variable removed, 10 folds), the model-derived tuning curves (Methods eq. 10, M = 200) and the half-session refits for stability are computed in the same pass.

def med(x):
    x = np.asarray(x, float)
    x = x[np.isfinite(x)]
    if len(x) == 0:
        return (np.nan, np.nan, np.nan, 0)
    return (round(float(np.median(x)), 3), round(float(np.percentile(x, 25)), 3), round(float(np.percentile(x, 75)), 3), len(x))


def ranksum(a, b):
    a, b = np.asarray(a, float), np.asarray(b, float)
    a, b = a[np.isfinite(a)], b[np.isfinite(b)]
    return stats.ranksums(a, b).pvalue if len(a) and len(b) else np.nan


class _NoTest:
    statistic = np.nan
    pvalue = np.nan


def kruskal(*groups):
    g = [np.asarray(x, float)[np.isfinite(np.asarray(x, float))] for x in groups]
    g = [x for x in g if len(x)]
