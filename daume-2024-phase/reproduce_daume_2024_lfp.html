Daume et al. (2024) on dandiset 000673: LFP phase-amplitude coupling¶

Paper. Daume J, Kaminski J, Schjetnan AGP, Salimpour Y, Khan U, Kyzar M, Reed CM, Anderson WS, Valiante TA, Mamelak AN, Rutishauser U (2024). Control of working memory by phase-amplitude coupling of human hippocampal neurons. Nature 629, 393-401. doi:10.1038/s41586-024-07309-z.

Dandiset. DANDI:000673, version 0.240118.2135, the version the paper cites (reference 59); see the header of reproduce_daume_2024.ipynb for the later versions.

Text the claims were written from. The version of record (PMC11078732) with its Methods, legends and Supplementary Information, and the paper's code (SBCAT-release-NWB at a25e8cc3).

This notebook tests the claims on LFP phase-amplitude coupling (PAC). Each channel's LFP is streamed, cut into -0.5 to 3 s epochs around maintenance onset, filtered trial by trial as the paper's code does, and Tort's modulation index (MI) is z-scored against 200 trial-shuffled surrogates (sbcat.py says where each parameter comes from). Every result is shown under both readings of the artefact rejection (O1): (a) on the epochs, (b) on whole trials. Where they disagree, (a) is adopted: on the one channel whose rejected trials the paper's code ships (artif_trials.mat, sub-26 session 1, channel 33), every trial (a) removes is among the paper's, while (b) removes 5 trials the paper kept (data check below).

def slopes(m, term, ind="load1"):
    """The term's slope in load 3 trials, in load 1 trials and averaged over the loads, with Wald z and p.
    load1 is an indicator of load 1 trials, so load 3 is the reference: the paper's coding, as Tables
    S2 to S4 name the terms "Load1" and "PAC : Load1" and say "Load 1 are tested against load 3 trials".
    The model's own PAC (or SC) term is then the load 3 slope."""
    V = m.cov_params()
    b, i = term, f"{term}:{ind}"
    out = {}
    for name, w in [("L3", 0.0), ("L1", 1.0), ("mean", 0.5)]:
        est = m.params[b] + w * m.params[i]
        z = est / np.sqrt(V.loc[b, b] + w * w * V.loc[i, i] + 2 * w * V.loc[b, i])
        out.update({f"{name} est": est, f"{name} t": z, f"{name} p": 2 * stats.norm.sf(abs(z))})
    out.update({"Load1 est": m.params[ind], "int est": m.params[i], "int p": m.pvalues[i], "converged": m.converged})
    return out


def rt_frame(rd, a):
    """Correct, clean trials of the area's significant PAC channels under artefact reading rd. RT in s,
    log10 RT, and RT z-scored over the session's correct trials (the paper gives no unit or transform)."""
    d = ch[(ch.reading == rd) & (ch.area == a) & (ch.z_thetaHG > 1.64)]
    parts = []

        idx = c["trial_index"]

        rt = tr.rt.to_numpy()[idx][keep]
